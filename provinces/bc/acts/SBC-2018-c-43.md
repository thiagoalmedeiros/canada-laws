# Advanced Education Statute Repeal Act

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/18043/xml) before relying on it.

SBC 2018, c 43

### Repeal

1 The Public Education Flexibility and Choice Act, S.B.C. 2002, c. 3, is repealed.

### Commencement

2 This Act comes into force on the date of Royal Assent.
