# National Day for Truth and Reconciliation Act

SBC 2023, c 4

### National Day for Truth and Reconciliation in British Columbia

1 The National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, observed on September 30, is a public holiday.

### Commencement

5 This Act comes into force on the date of Royal Assent.
