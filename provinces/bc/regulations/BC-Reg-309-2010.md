BC Reg 309/2010

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Ports Property Tax Act.

### Interpretation of Schedule

2 In the Schedule,

(a) an assessment roll number set out in Column 1 of the Schedule is the number on the assessment roll prepared by the British Columbia Assessment Authority for the 2026 taxation year, as at December 31, 2025, and

(b) Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 345/2012, s. 1 (b).]

(c) the municipality and terminal names set out in Columns 2 and 3 of the Schedule are listed for ease of reference only and do not affect the designation by assessment roll number.

### Designated property

3 The land identified by the assessment roll numbers listed in Column 1 of the Schedule, and the improvements on that land, are designated for the purposes of

(a) section 3 of the Act, and

(b) section 4 of the Act.
