# Minister's Requirement for Approval of Bylaws Regulation

BC Reg 27/2017

### North Coast Regional District

1 For the purposes of section 585 (1) of the Local Government Act, approval by the minister is required before North Coast Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 608, 2017, may be adopted by the North Coast Regional District.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Local Government Act, R.S.B.C. 2015, c. 1, s. 585]
