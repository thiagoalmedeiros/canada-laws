BC Reg 220/94

### Transfer of lands

1 The Capital Regional District may transfer lands, identified as surplus on the attached map, from the Capital Regional District's solid waste local service to the Capital Regional District's regional parks extended service.

## Map

The map is exempt from publication and may be inspected at the offices of the

Capital Regional District, 524 Yates Street, Victoria, B.C. V8W 2S6

[Provisions of the Local Government Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 323, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 799]
