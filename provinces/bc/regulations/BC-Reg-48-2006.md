BC Reg 48/2006

### Louis Burgart — remuneration per day

1 Louis Burgart, as a member of the commission, is remunerated at $430 a day, in accordance with the policy set out in section 7 of Treasury Board Directive 1/04.

### Remuneration for expenses

2 The commission members are paid reasonable travelling and living expenses at the Group 3 Rate established by Treasury Board Order 88.
