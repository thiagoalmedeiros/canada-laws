BC Reg 359/94

### Transfer of assets and liabilities

1 The assets and liabilities of a society that has been designated as a hospital under the Hospital Act may be transferred to a board by

(a) the society agreeing to the transfer by special resolution as defined in section 1 of the Societies Act, and

(b) the board agreeing to assume the assets and liabilities by bylaw passed by a majority of not less than 75% of the votes of the members of the board by voting personally or by proxy after receiving at least 14 days' notice of the proposed bylaw.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Health Authorities Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 180, s. 21 (2).]
