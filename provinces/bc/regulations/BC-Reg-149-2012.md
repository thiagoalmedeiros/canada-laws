# Family Day Regulation

BC Reg 149/2012

### Day prescribed as Family Day

1 The third Monday of February is to be observed as "Family Day".

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Family Day Act, S.B.C. 2012, c. 24, s. 2]
