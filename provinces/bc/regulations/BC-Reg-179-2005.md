BC Reg 179/2005

# Small Claims Court
Monetary Limit Regulation

### Prescribed amount of claims the court may hear

1 For the purposes of section 3 of the Small Claims Act, the prescribed amount is $35 000.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Small Claims Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 430, section 21]
