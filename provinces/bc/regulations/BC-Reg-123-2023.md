# Mineral Land Tax Regulation

BC Reg 123/2023

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Mineral Land Tax Act.

### Information sharing – prescribed enactments

2 For the purposes of section 19.1 (5) (j) of the Act, the following enactments are prescribed:

(a) the Business Number Act;

(b) section 8 of the Financial Administration Act.
