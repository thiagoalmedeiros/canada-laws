BC Reg 122/2003

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Forest Act;

*reduced volume condition* means a condition on a licence that, under section 14 (1) (g.2) of the Act, reduces a portion of the allowable annual cut available to the holder of the licence.

### Annual rent

1.1 (1) For the purposes of sections 111 and 112 of the Act, the holder of a licence, agreement or permit described in Column 1 of the following table must pay to the government annual rent at the rate set out in Column 2 immediately opposite that licence, agreement or permit:

| Item | Column 1 Description of Licence, Agreement or Permit | Column 2 Rate of Annual Rent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Community forest agreement | $0.37/m3 of allowable annual cut determined for the community forest agreement area |
| 2 | First nations woodland licence | $0.12/m3 of allowable annual cut determined for the first nations woodland licence area |
| 3 | Forest licence, other than a supplemental forest licence | $0.37/m3 of allowable annual cut specified in the forest licence |
| 4 | Cutting permit issued for a supplemental forest licence | $0.37/m3 of volume of timber authorized to be harvested under the cutting permit |
| 5 | Timber licence | $1.85/ha |
| 6 | Tree farm licence | $0.57/m3 of allowable annual cut determined for the tree farm licence area |
| 7 | Woodlot licence | $0.60/m3 of allowable annual cut determined for the woodlot licence area |

(2) Despite subsection (1), the holder of a licence with a reduced volume condition must pay an amount that corresponds to the portion of the allowable annual cut that is not reduced by the reduced volume condition.

### Woodlot Product Development Council payment

2 With respect to a woodlot licence, $0.25/m3 of the rate set out in Column 2 of the table in section 1.1 is allocated under section 112.1 of the Act to represent money payable to the Woodlot Product Development Council.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Forest Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 157, ss. 11, 112, 112.1 and 151.]
