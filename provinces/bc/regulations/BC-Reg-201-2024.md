BC Reg 201/2024

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Vancouver Charter.

### Prescribed meaning of "First Nations corporation"

2 For the purposes of section 523E of the Act, *First Nations corporation* means a company that satisfies both of the following requirements:

(a) the company is incorporated under federal or provincial law;

(b) all of the shares in the company are directly or indirectly held by one or more First Nations.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Vancouver Charter, S.B.C. 1953, c. 55, ss. 523E and 523Q (1).]
