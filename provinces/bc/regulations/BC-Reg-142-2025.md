# Mineral Land Reserve (Harmony Gold) Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/142_2025/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 142/2025

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*land* includes foreshore and land covered by water;

*reserve* means the mineral reserve established under section 2;

*reserve period* means the period beginning on the date this regulation comes into force and ending on March 1, 2030;

*site number* means the site number given in the registry to a particular area of land shown on an electronic map in the registry.

### Temporary reserve established

2 A mineral reserve for all minerals is established for the reserve period over the area of land identified by site number 1007094 and the site name Harmony Gold Project.

### No registration of claims in reserve

3 A free miner must not register a claim in the reserve.

### No exploration, development or production in reserve

4 A person must not conduct the following activities in the reserve:

(a) the search for a mineral;

(b) the exploration and development of a mineral;

(c) the production of a mineral.

### Term of claim

5 The expiry date of a mineral claim located in the reserve is extended to November 1, 2030.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Mineral Tenure Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 292, s. 22.]
