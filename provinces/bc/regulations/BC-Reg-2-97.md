# Superintendent of Motor Vehicles Records Regulation

BC Reg 2/97

### Interpretation

1 In this regulation *Act* means the Motor Vehicle Act.

### Records prescribed for section 82 (11) of the Act

2 For the purpose of section 82 (11) of the Act, the records described below are prescribed:

(a) those records, each of which sets out or describes, with respect to a person, the information required by section 95 (4) of the Act;

(b) those records, each of which sets out or describes, with respect to a person, particulars of or pertaining to a prohibition from operating a motor vehicle imposed under section 91, 92, 93, 94.2, 98, 99, 100, 215, 215.43 or 251 (4) of the Act or section 7 (2) (f) or (3) of the Young Offenders (British Columbia) Act;

(c) those records, each of which sets out or describes, with respect to a person, particulars of or pertaining to a suspension imposed before September 1, 1982 under section 29, 90, 94, 95 or 102 of the Act;

(d) those records, each of which sets out or describes, with respect to a person, particulars of or pertaining to vehicle impoundments imposed under section 215.46 or 251 of the Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Motor Vehicle Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 318, s. 82.]
