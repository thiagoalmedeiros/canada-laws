BC Reg 243/2007

### Definitions and interpretation

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Income Tax Act;

*complete level 1*, in relation to a program, means the completion of the level 1 requirements established by SkilledTradesBC and as amended from time to time for the program;

*complete level 2*, in relation to a program, means the completion of the level 2 requirements established by SkilledTradesBC and as amended from time to time for the program;

*complete level 3*, in relation to a program, means the completion of the level 3 requirements established by SkilledTradesBC and as amended from time to time for the program;

*complete level 4*, in relation to a program, means the completion of the level 4 requirements established by SkilledTradesBC and as amended from time to time for the program;

*graduate* means in relation to an eligible recognized program set out in Schedule 1, to receive one of the following for completing the program: a Certificate of Qualification; if a Certificate of Qualification is not granted for that program, a Certificate of Apprenticeship, in relation to an eligible training program set out in section 4 (5) or in Schedule 2, to receive one of the following for completing the program: a Certificate of Qualification; if a Certificate of Qualification is not granted for that program, a Certificate of Apprenticeship; an Inter-Provincial Red Seal Endorsement, and in relation to an eligible apprenticeship program set out in Schedule 3, to receive both of the following for completing the program: a Certificate of Qualification; a Certificate of Apprenticeship;

*level 1 technical training*, in relation to an eligible apprenticeship program set out in Schedule 3, means level 1 of technical training established by SkilledTradesBC for that program, and shown as completed on a transcript from SkilledTradesBC;

*level 2 technical training*, in relation to an eligible apprenticeship program set out in Schedule 3, means level 2 of technical training established by SkilledTradesBC for that program, and shown as completed on a transcript from SkilledTradesBC;

*work-based hours*, in relation to a number of work-based hours completed, means work-based hours shown as completed on a transcript from SkilledTradesBC.

### Prescribed eligible recognized programs

2 The recognized programs set out in Column 1 of Schedule 1 are prescribed for the purposes of the definition of "eligible recognized program" in section 116 of the Act.

### Prescribed eligible training programs

3 The accredited programs and recognized programs set out in Column 1 of Schedule 2 are prescribed for the purposes of the definition of "eligible training program" in section 116 of the Act.

### Prescribed eligible apprenticeship programs

3.1 The programs set out in Column 1 of Schedule 3 are prescribed for the purposes of the definition of "eligible apprenticeship program" in section 120 of the Act.

### Level requirements

4 (1) The level 1 requirements, if any, established for an eligible recognized program itemized in Column 1 of Schedule 1 are the requirements set out opposite that item in Column 2.

(2) The level 2 requirements, if any, established for an eligible recognized program itemized in Column 1 of Schedule 1 are the requirements set out opposite that item in Column 3.

(3) The level 3 requirements, if any, established for an eligible training program itemized in Column 1 of Schedule 2 are the requirements set out opposite that item in Column 2.

(4) The level 4 requirements, if any, established for an eligible training program itemized in Column 1 of Schedule 2 are the requirements set out opposite that item in Column 3.

(5) The level 5 requirement established for the following eligible training programs is to graduate from the program:

(a) hydraulic service mechanic.

(b) Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 181/2011, App. 2, s. 1.]

(c) Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 118/2015, App. 2, s. 1.]

### Apprenticeship level requirements

4.1 (1) The level 1 apprenticeship requirement, if any, established for an eligible apprenticeship program itemized in Column 1 of Schedule 3 is the requirement set out opposite that item in Column 2.

(2) The level 2 apprenticeship requirement, if any, established for an eligible apprenticeship program itemized in Column 1 of Schedule 3 is the requirement set out opposite that item in Column 3.

### Repealed

5 Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 299/2010, s. (b).]
