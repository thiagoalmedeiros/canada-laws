# Employer Health Tax Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/268_2018/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 268/2018

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Employer Health Tax Act.

### Calculation of interest

2 For the purposes of section 66 of the Act, interest payable to the government under the Act must be

(a) calculated at the rate prescribed under the Interest Rate under Various Statutes Regulation, B.C. Reg. 386/92, and

(b) calculated on a daily basis and compounded monthly.

### Instalments — prescribed amount

3 For the purposes of section 41 of the Act, the amount prescribed is $2 925.

### Certificate of lien form

4 The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of section 84 (2) (a) of the Act.

### Communication of information — prescribed enactments

5 For the purposes of section 90 (5) (j) of the Act, the following enactments are prescribed:

(a) the Business Number Act;

(b) section 8 of the Financial Administration Act.
