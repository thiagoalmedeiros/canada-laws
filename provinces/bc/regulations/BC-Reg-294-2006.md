BC Reg 294/2006

### Section 53 of the Utilities Commission Act does not apply

1 Section 53 of the Utilities Commission Act does not apply in respect of Terasen Gas (Squamish) Inc. or Terasen Gas Inc. in relation to an amalgamation of those 2 corporations.
