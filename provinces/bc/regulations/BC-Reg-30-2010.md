BC Reg 30/2010

# Improvement District Letters Patent

 Amendment Regulation

### Minister authorized to amend letters patent

1 The minister is authorized to make orders amending letters patent of an improvement district.

[Provisions of the Local Government Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 323, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 734 (1)]
