### Pacific Coast Fishermen's Mutual

Marine Insurance Company

1 Pacific Coast Fishermen's Mutual Marine Insurance Company is designated as a mutual company for the purposes of Division 4 of Part 6 of the Financial Institutions Act.

[Provisions of the Financial Institutions Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 141, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: sections 188 and 289 (4) (p)]
