# BC Hydro Integrated Resource Plan Regulation

BC Reg 266/2018

### Prescribed submission date

1 For the purposes of section 3 (6) (b) of the Clean Energy Act, February 28, 2021 is prescribed as the submission date for an integrated resource plan.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Clean Energy Act, S.B.C. 2010, c. 22, s. 35 (h)]
