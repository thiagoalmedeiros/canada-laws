# Access to Services (COVID-19) Regulation

BC Reg 6/2022

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Access to Services (COVID-19) Act.

### Designated policing unit

2 For the purposes of the definition of "police officer" in section 1 of the Act, the designated policing unit known as the South Coast British Columbia Transportation Authority Police Service is prescribed.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Access to Services (COVID-19) Act, S.B.C. 2021, c. 33, s. 6.]
