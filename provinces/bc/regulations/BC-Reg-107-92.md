# Credit Union Minimum Level of Capital Regulation

BC Reg 107/92

# Credit Union Minimum Level
of Capital Regulation

### Interpretation

1 In this regulation *Act* means the Financial Institutions Act.

### Amount prescribed for section 277 (2) (d) of the Act

2 For the purpose of section 277 (2) (d) of the Act, the amount prescribed is 5% of the calculated value of the credit union's risk weighted assets.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Financial Institutions Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 141, ss. 277 (2) (d) and 289.]
