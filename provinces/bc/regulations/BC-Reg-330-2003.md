BC Reg 330/2003

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Community Charter.

### Responsible ministers designated

2 The minister set out in Column 1 of the Table is designated as the minister responsible in relation to the matters set out opposite in Column 2 for the purposes of section 9 of the Act:

## Table

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Column 1 Responsible Minister | Column 2 Matter |
| The minister responsible for the administration of the Public Health Act except sections 3, 4 and 66 of that Act as those provisions relate to mental health and addictions | Bylaws under section 8 (3) (i) of the Act |
| The minister responsible for the administration of the Environmental Management Act | Bylaws under section 8 (3) (j) of the Act |
| The minister responsible for the administration of the Wildlife Act except (a) section 3 (b) of that Act as that provision relates to the portfolio of the Minister of Environment and Climate Change Strategy, and (b) sections 6 and 108 (2) (a) of that Act | Bylaws under section 8 (3) (k) of the Act in relation to wildlife |
| The minister responsible for the administration of the Ministry of Energy and Mines Act | Bylaws under section 8 (3) (m) of the Act that prohibit soil removal |
| The minister responsible for the administration of the Environmental Management Act | Bylaws under section 8 (3) (m) of the Act that prohibit the deposit of soil or other material by reference to the quality of the soil or material or to contamination |
