BC Reg 188/2004

### Additional purpose prescribed

1 For the purpose of section 3 (2) (b) of the Emergency Communications Corporations Act, an emergency communications corporation named in Column 1 of the following table is authorized to have the additional purpose set out opposite in Column 2:

| Column 1 | Column 2 | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E-Comm Emergency Communications for British Columbia Incorporated | to enter into agreements with telephone services providers by which 9-1-1 telephone access to emergency services to members of the public will be provided by the telephone service providers | |

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Emergency Communications Corporations Act, S.B.C. 1997, c. 47, section 14 (2) (b)]
