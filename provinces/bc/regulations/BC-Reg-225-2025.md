# School Tax Exemptions (Solar Power Projects) Regulation

BC Reg 225/2025

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the School Act;

*solar power project* means a power project that uses photovoltaic solar panels to generate electricity.

### Approved class of alternative energy power projects

2 Solar power projects

(a) are approved alternative energy power projects for the purposes of section 131.1 of the Act, and

(b) are exempt from school taxes to the extent set out in section 3 of this regulation.

### Specified improvements exempt from school taxes

3 The following improvements in respect of a solar power project are exempt from school taxes:

(a) a racking system used to support one or more photovoltaic solar panels;

(b) a foundation for a racking system referred to in paragraph (a).

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: School Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 412, s. 131.1.]
