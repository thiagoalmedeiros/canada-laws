# Sustainable Environment Fund Regulation

BC Reg 100/2026

### Purpose

1 This regulation prescribes, for the purposes of section 4 (g) of the Sustainable Environment Fund Act, revenue derived under the following Acts:

(a) Environmental Management Act ;

(b) Integrated Pest Management Act .

### Revenue under Environmental Management Act

2 In respect of the Environmental Management Act, the prescribed revenue is all revenue that the government derives from any of the following under that Act:

(a) application fees and annual charges for approved waste management plans;

(b) application fees and annual charges for registrations under section 3 of the Asphalt Plant Regulation, B.C. Reg. 217/97;

(c) application fees and annual charges for operational certificates;

(d) fees under Part 4 of the Contaminated Sites Regulation, B.C. Reg. 375/96;

(e) application fees and annual charges for registrations under Division 2 of Part 2 of the Municipal Wastewater Regulation, B.C. Reg. 87/2012;

(f) annual charges for authorizations to discharge under the Oil and Gas Waste Regulation, B.C. Reg. 254/2005;

(g) annual charges for registrations under section 4 of the Waste Discharge Regulation, B.C. Reg. 320/2004.

### Revenue under Integrated Pest Management Act

3 In respect of the Integrated Pest Management Act, the prescribed revenue is all revenue that the government derives from any of the following under that Act:

(a) fees for licences under section 4 ;

(b) fees for certificates under section 5 ;

(c) fees for permits under section 6 ;

(d) fees for pesticide use notices under section 7 .

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Sustainable Environment Fund Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 445, s. 6.]
