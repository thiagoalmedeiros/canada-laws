# Crown Agency Employers Exemption Regulation

BC Reg 177/2007

### Exemptions

1 For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of "public sector employer" in the Public Sector Employers Act, the following corporations and unincorporated boards, commissions, councils, bureaus, authorities and similar bodies are exempted:

British Columbia Emergency Health Services

Organized Crime Agency of British Columbia Society

Regulatory colleges within the meaning of the Health Professions and Occupations Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Public Sector Employers Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 384, ss. 1 and 15 (2).]
