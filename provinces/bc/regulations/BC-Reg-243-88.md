# shíshálh Nation Government District Municipal Benefits Regulation

BC Reg 243/88

### Municipal benefits

1 The shíshálh Nation Government District is entitled to or eligible for the following municipal benefits:

(a) grants under the Local Government Grants Act;

(b) subject to an agreement being entered into under section 2 of the Provincial-Municipal Partnership Act, incentives for economic development and participation in economic development programs of the government under that Act.

1. Section 7 of the Provincial-Municipal Partnership Act was consolidated as section 319 (1) of the Municipal Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 323. Sections 1 to 6 of the Provincial-Municipal Partnership Act were not consolidated and the Act was subsequently repealed by 1997-25-219 (c), effective July 28/97.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: shíshálh Nation Government District Enabling Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 416, s. 4.]
