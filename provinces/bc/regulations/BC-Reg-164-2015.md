# Time Period for Review Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). BC Reg 164/2015

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Workers Compensation Act.

### Prescribed time period

2 For the purposes of section 270 (1) (b) of the Act, the time period to file a request for a review of any of the following is 45 days:

(a) a Board decision or order referred to in section 268 (1) (a) of the Act;

(b) a Board decision referred to in section 268 (1) (c) (iii) (A) of the Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Workers Compensation Act, R.S.B.C. 2019, c. 1, s. 109 (3).]
