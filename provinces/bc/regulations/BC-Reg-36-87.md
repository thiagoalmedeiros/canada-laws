### Provisions of Family Law Act applicable to authority

1 Sections 239 and 244 (1) (a) and (d) of the Family Law Act apply to the British Columbia Hydro and Power Authority.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Hydro and Power Authority Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 212, section 32 (6)]
