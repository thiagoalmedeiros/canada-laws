BC Reg 427/2003

### Definition of "wildlife"

1 For the purposes of the Community Charter, *wildlife* has the same meaning as in the Wildlife Act.

[Provisions of the Community Charter, S.B.C. 2003, c. 26, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 282]
