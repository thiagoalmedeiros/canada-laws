# Insurance Commission Exemption Regulation

BC Reg 149/97

### Interpretation

1 In this regulation *Act* means the Financial Institutions Act.

### Exemption from section 178 (3)

2 A person described in section 2 (1) (b.1), (c), (g) and (i) and (2) of the Insurance Licensing Exemptions Regulation, B.C. Reg. 328/90, while acting only as described in those provisions, is a person prescribed for the purposes of section 178 (3) of the Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Financial Institutions Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 141, s. 289 (4) (o).]
