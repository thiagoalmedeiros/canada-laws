# Residential Property (Short-Term Holding) Profit Tax Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/281_2024/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 281/2024

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Residential Property (Short-Term Holding) Profit Tax Act.

### Calculation of interest

2 Interest payable on amounts due to the government under the Act must be

(a) calculated at the rate prescribed under section 8 of the Interest Rate Under Various Statutes Regulation, B.C. Reg. 386/92, and

(b) calculated on a daily basis and compounded monthly.

### Certificate of lien form

3 The form set out in the Schedule is prescribed for the purposes of section 79 (2) (a) of the Act.

### Communication of information – prescribed enactment

4 For the purposes of section 87 (5) (k) of the Act, section 8 of the Financial Administration Act is a prescribed enactment.
