BC Reg 566/2004

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Financial Institutions Act.

### Insurance Council authority to suspend,

 cancel, restrict licences and impose fines

2 For the purpose of section 231 (1) (e.1) of the Act, sections 2, 3, 3.1 and 3.2 of the Financial Products Disclosure Regulation are prescribed provisions of the regulation.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Financial Institutions Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 141, s. 289.]
