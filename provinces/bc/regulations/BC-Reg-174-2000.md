### Recreation commission

4 A person authorized by the district manager to collect a fee referred to in section 24 (2) of the Forest Recreation Regulation, B.C. Reg. 58/99, may retain a commission equal to 100 percent of the amount collected.

[Provisions of the Financial Administration Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 138, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 14 (2)]
