# Short Term Borrowing Limit Regulation

BC Reg 368/2003

### Short term capital borrowing limit

1 The limit prescribed for the purposes of the debt referred to in section 178 (2) of the Community Charter, for a municipality, is the amount obtained by multiplying $150 by the population of the municipality.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Community Charter, S.B.C. 2003, c. 26, ss. 178 and 282.]
