# Required Consent (Simpcw First Nation — Reviewable Mine Projects) Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/133_2026/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 133/2026

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *consent agreement* means the agreement between the government and the Simpcw First Nation titled "Consent Decision-Making Agreement for Mine Projects", negotiated and entered into under section 7 (1) (b) of the Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act and dated July 15, 2026.

### Consent agreement prescribed

2 For the purposes of section 7 (b) (ii) of the Environmental Assessment Act, the consent agreement is prescribed as it relates to the Yellowhead Copper Project.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Environmental Assessment Act, S.B.C. 2018, c. 51, s. 7.]
