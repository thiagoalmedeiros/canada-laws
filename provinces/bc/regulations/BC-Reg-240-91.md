BC Reg 240/91

# Regional District of

Kootenay Boundary Regulation

### Transit

1 The Regional District of Kootenay Boundary is granted the additional power to provide transit as a local service.

### Emergency Programs

2 The Regional District of Kootenay Boundary is granted the additional power to provide Emergency Programs as an extended service under the Emergency Program Act.

### Firecrackers and fireworks

3 The Regional District of Kootenay Boundary is granted the additional power to, by bylaw, regulate, prohibit and impose requirements in relation to the use of firecrackers and fireworks.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Local Government Act, R.S.B.C. 2015, c. 1, s. 296.]
