### University of Victoria Pension Plan

2 University of Victoria Pension Plan is designated as an institution with which the Minister of Finance may enter into agreements under section 78 (1) of the Financial Administration Act.

### Columbia Basin Trust

3 The Columbia Basin Trust is designated as an institution with which the Minister of Finance and Corporate Relations may enter into agreements under section 78 (1) of the Financial Administration Act.
