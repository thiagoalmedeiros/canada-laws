# FortisBC Inc. Exemption Regulation

BC Reg 103/2026

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Utilities Commission Act.

### Exemption

2 (1) FortisBC Inc. is exempt from section 45 (5) of the Act in respect of the following extensions of its system:

(a) upgrades at the DG Bell substation;

(b) a new substation in the North End neighbourhood of the City of Kelowna.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to an extension that, on the date FortisBC Inc. decides to construct the extension, cannot reasonably be expected to come into service by June 30, 2036.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Utilities Commission Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 473, s. 22.]
