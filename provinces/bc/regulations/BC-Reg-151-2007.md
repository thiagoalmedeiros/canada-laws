# Resort Timber Administration Act (Specified Enactment) Regulation

BC Reg 151/2007

### Enactments specified

1 The following are specified enactments for the purposes of section 4 (2) (b) of the Resort Timber Administration Act:

(a) section 47.4 (2) (a) of the Forest Act;

(b) section 47.5 (1) (b) of the Forest Act;

(c) section 47.6 (2) of the Forest Act;

(d) section 76 of the Forest Act;

(e) section 77 of the Forest Act;

(f) section 3 of the Forestry Licence to Cut Regulation, B.C. Reg. 221/2006;

(g) section 91 (1) (a) (ii) of the Forest Planning and Practices Regulation, B.C. Reg. 14/2004, but only in relation to the granting of exemptions from section 46 of the regulation;

(h) section 105 (1) (a) of the Forest Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Resort Timber Administration Act, S.B.C. 2006, c. 30, section 4 (2) (b)]
