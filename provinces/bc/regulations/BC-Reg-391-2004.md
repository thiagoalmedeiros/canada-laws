BC Reg 391/2004

### Employment Standards Branch officers

1 Officers of the Employment Standards Branch to whom powers under section 117 of the Employment Standards Act have been delegated are designated commissioners for taking affidavits for British Columbia for the purposes of exercising the powers and performing the duties of their office.

[Provisions of the Evidence Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 124, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 60 (1)]
