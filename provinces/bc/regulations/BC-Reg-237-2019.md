# Witness Security Regulation

BC Reg 237/2019

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Witness Security Act.

### Amendment of service plan

2 For the purposes of section 24 (3) of the Act, the prescribed amount is $10 000.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Witness Security Act, S.B.C. 2019, c. 21, s. 58.]
