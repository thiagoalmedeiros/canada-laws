# New Era Hydro Corporation Exemption Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/46_2012/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 46/2012

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Utilities Commission Act.

### Exemption

2 New Era Hydro Corporation is exempt from Part 3, except for section 22, of the Act in respect of the sale of electricity generated at Fraser, British Columbia, to the Yukon Government.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Utilities Commission Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 473, section 22]
