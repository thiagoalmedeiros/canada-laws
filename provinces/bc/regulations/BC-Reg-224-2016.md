# Regional Health Board Records Regulation

BC Reg 224/2016

### Health records if services provided

1 Section 11.1 of the Hospital Act Regulation applies to a board for the purposes of the treatment of, or the provision of services to, a person by a facility or institution owned, operated, managed or funded, in whole or in part, by the board.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Health Authorities Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 180, section 19]
