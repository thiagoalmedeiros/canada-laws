# Commission Expenses Regulation No. 2

BC Reg 52/2006

### Stewart Ladyman — remuneration

1 Stewart Ladyman, as a member of the commission, is remunerated at $430 a day, in accordance with the policy set out in section 7 of Treasury Board Directive 1/04.

[Provisions of the Electoral Boundaries Commission Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 107, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: sections 2 and 4]
