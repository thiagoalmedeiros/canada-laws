## Part 1 — Interpretation

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Firearm Violence Prevention Act;

*Canadian Forces* means the Canadian Forces as defined in section 2 of the Criminal Code;

*police service* means any of the following within the meaning of the Police Act: the provincial police service; a municipal police department; a designated policing unit.

### Descriptors for federal enactments

2 In this regulation, if a reference to a federal enactment is followed by italicized text in square brackets that describes the subject matter of the federal enactment, the text in square brackets

(a) is not part of this regulation, and

(b) is to be considered to have been added editorially for convenience of reference only.

## Part 2 — Matters Prescribed for Provisions of Act

### Devices prescribed as vehicles

3 The following devices are prescribed as vehicles for the purposes of paragraph (h) of the definition of "vehicle" in section 1 of the Act:

(a) a designated motorized device as defined in section 1 of the Motor Vehicle Act;

(b) a sleigh or wagon that is not a vehicle as defined in section 1 of the Motor Vehicle Act.

### Requirements for transporting, carrying or storing in vehicles or boats

4 (1) For the purposes of section 4 (2) of the Act and unless subsection (2) of this section applies, if a low-velocity firearm or imitation firearm is being transported, carried or stored in or on a vehicle or boat,

(a) the low-velocity firearm or imitation firearm must be inside a trunk or compartment, and

(b) the trunk or compartment must be locked.

(2) If the vehicle or boat does not have a trunk or compartment that can be locked, or if the low-velocity firearm or imitation firearm cannot, because of its size, be transported, carried or stored in the trunk or compartment, the low-velocity firearm or imitation firearm must be inside an opaque container that is locked.

### Persons prescribed as wildlife officers

5 For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition of "wildlife officer" in section 10 (1) of the Act, the prescribed officers or employees of the government are as follows:

(a) biologists, veterinarians, licensed science officers and scientific technical officers described in section 1 (a) of the Designation of Officers Regulation, B.C. Reg. 305/2002;

(b) managers described in section 1 (c) of the Designation of Officers Regulation.

### Requirements for credible false identification

6 For the purposes of the exemption set out in section 12 (1) of the Act, 2 pieces of identification must be produced, as follows:

(a) one piece must be in the form of

(i) a passport,

(ii) a driver's licence that displays a photograph and date of birth, or

(iii) an identification card, issued by a government of any jurisdiction, that displays a photograph and date of birth;

(b) the other piece of identification must display

(i) a name, and

(ii) a signature or photograph.

## Part 4 — Exemptions for Game Farmers, Livestock Farmers and Veterinarians

### Definitions for Part 4

19 In this Part:

*authorized purpose* means any of the following purposes: in relation to a game farmer, euthanizing, immobilizing, medicating, sedating or vaccinating the game farmer's fallow deer, bison or reindeer; in relation to a livestock farmer, euthanizing, immobilizing, medicating, sedating or vaccinating the livestock farmer's alpacas, cattle, equines, goats, llamas, sheep or swine; in relation to a veterinarian, euthanizing, immobilizing, medicating, sedating or vaccinating an animal, or conducting research in respect of an animal;

*game farmer* means a licensed game farmer within the meaning of the Game Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 5/2015;

*livestock farmer* means a person who operates a farm or ranch on which alpacas, cattle, equines, goats, llamas, sheep or swine are kept;

*veterinarian* means any of the following: a person who is registered under the Veterinarians Act; a person who is exempt by reason of any of the following provisions of the Veterinarians Act from the prohibition set out in section 46 (2) of that Act: section 46 (3) (d) (ii) ; section 46 (3) (e) ; section 46 (3) (g) .

### Exemptions from sections 4 (1) and 5 of Act for game farmers, livestock farmers and veterinarians

20 (1) A game farmer, livestock farmer or veterinarian who takes an action referred to in a provision of the Act listed in subsection (3) is exempt from the listed provision, in relation to that action, if the following conditions are met:

(a) the action is taken for an authorized purpose;

(b) the firearm to which the action relates is a non-restricted firearm or low-velocity firearm;

(c) the game farmer, livestock farmer or veterinarian has the licences, permits and authorizations required, if any, under the federal Act;

(d) in the case of an action taken with a firearm that is in or on a vehicle, and subject to subsection (2), the action is taken while the vehicle is not in motion.

(2) The action described in subsection (1) (d) may be taken while the vehicle is in motion if

(a) the firearm is a low-velocity firearm, and

(b) the game farmer, livestock farmer or veterinarian is not operating the vehicle.

(3) This section applies in relation to the following provisions of the Act:

(a) section 4 (1) ;

(b) section 5 (1) .

### Exemptions from sections 4 (2) and 14 of Act for game farmers, livestock farmers and veterinarians

21 (1) A game farmer, livestock farmer or veterinarian who takes an action referred to in a provision of the Act listed in subsection (2) is exempt from the listed provision, in relation to that action, if the action is taken for an authorized purpose.

(2) This section applies in relation to the following provisions of the Act:

(a) section 4 (2) of the Act;

(b) section 14 (1) and (2) .

### Exemption from section 6 (2) (a) of Act for game farmers, livestock farmers and veterinarians

22 A game farmer, livestock farmer or veterinarian who operates a vehicle if there is a non-restricted firearm in or on the vehicle is exempt from section 6 (2) (a) of the Act, as that provision relates to a non-restricted firearm, if the following conditions are met:

(a) the non-restricted firearm is in or on the vehicle for an authorized purpose;

(b) the game farmer, livestock farmer or veterinarian, or another occupant of the vehicle, has the authorization referred to in section 7 (a) of the Act.

### Exemption from section 8 of Act for veterinarians

23 A veterinarian who takes an action referred to in section 8 (2) of the Act is exempt from that section, in relation to that action, if the action is taken for an authorized purpose.

### Exemptions for persons working under direction of game farmer, livestock farmer or veterinarian

24 (1) The exemptions in this Part for a game farmer also apply to a person acting under the direction of the game farmer.

(2) The exemptions in this Part for a livestock farmer also apply to a person acting under the direction of the livestock farmer.

(3) The exemptions in this Part for a veterinarian also apply to a person acting under the direction and close personal supervision of the veterinarian.

## Part 6 — Exemptions for Artistic Activities

### Definitions for Part 6

30 In this Part:

*artistic activity* means an activity carried out in creating a film, painting, photograph or similar artistic work, but does not include an activity carried out in providing or performing in a performing arts event as defined in section 32 of the Firearm Violence Prevention (Designated Property) Regulation;

*firearm safety coordinator*, in relation to an artistic activity, means a person who is responsible for the safe use, transportation and storage of firearms, imitation firearms or prohibited devices used for the purposes of the artistic activity;

*local police service*, in relation to an artistic activity, means the police service that is responsible for policing and law enforcement in the area in which a firearm, imitation firearm or prohibited device is to be used for the purposes of the artistic activity.

### Exemptions from sections 4 (2) and 14 of Act for artistic activity

31 (1) A person who takes an action referred to in a provision of the Act listed in subsection (2) is exempt from the listed provision, in relation to that action, if the following conditions are met:

(a) the action is taken for the purposes of an artistic activity;

(b) a firearm safety coordinator has given written notice to the local police service in accordance with section 33 of this regulation.

(2) This section applies in relation to the following provisions of the Act:

(a) section 4 (2) ;

(b) section 14 (1) and (2) .

### Exemption from section 6 (2) (a) and (c) of Act for artistic activity

32 A person who operates a vehicle if there is, in or on the vehicle, a firearm or prohibited device referred to in section 6 (2) (a) or (c) of the Act is exempt from that section, in relation to that action, if the following conditions are met:

(a) the action is taken for the purposes of an artistic activity;

(b) a firearm safety coordinator has given written notice to the local police service in accordance with section 33 of this regulation;

(c) in the case of a vehicle operated when there is a firearm in or on the vehicle, the firearm is not loaded;

(d) the person or another occupant of the vehicle has the authorizations referred to in section 7 (a) and (b) of the Act.

### Giving notice to local police service

33 (1) This section sets out the requirements for giving notice to a local police service under section 31 (1) (b) or 32 (b).

(2) The notice must set out or describe the following:

(a) the artistic activity that will be taking place, including the dates on which it will be taking place;

(b) the firearm, imitation firearm or prohibited device proposed to be used for the purposes of the artistic activity, including the dates on which the firearm, imitation firearm or prohibited device will be used;

(c) the location where the firearm, imitation firearm or prohibited device is proposed to be used, including the names of relevant streets;

(d) the name and telephone number of the firearm safety coordinator.

(3) The notice must be given within 5 to 10 days before the first date on which the firearm, imitation firearm or prohibited device will be used.

## Part 7 — Exemptions for Paintball, Laser Tag, Airsoft and Similar Activities

### Exemptions from sections 4 and 5 of Act for paintball, laser tag, airsoft or similar activity

34 (1) A person who takes an action referred to in a provision of the Act listed in subsection (2) is exempt from the listed provision, in relation to that action, if the following conditions are met:

(a) the action is taken in or on a vehicle that is in or on the premises of a facility that provides paintball, airsoft, laser tag or a similar sporting or recreational activity involving the use of low-velocity firearms or imitation firearms;

(b) in the case of an action taken with a firearm, the firearm is a low-velocity firearm;

(c) the owner or operator of the facility permits persons to take the action while using vehicles;

(d) in the case of an action taken with a low-velocity firearm,

(i) the firearm is not discharged in, on or from the vehicle when that vehicle is in motion, and

(ii) the firearm is not discharged at another vehicle when that other vehicle is in motion.

(2) This section applies in relation to the following provisions of the Act:

(a) section 4 (1) and (2) ;

(b) section 5 (1) .

### Exemptions from section 14 of Act for paintball, laser tag, airsoft or similar activity

35 A minor who takes an action referred to in section 14 (1) or (2) of the Act is exempt from that section, in relation to that action, if

(a) the action is taken in or on the premises of a facility that provides paintball, airsoft, laser tag or a similar sporting or recreational activity involving the use of low-velocity firearms or imitation firearms, and

## Part 8 — Permitted Devices and Other Exemptions

### Division 1 — Permitted Devices

### Meaning of "permitted device"

36 In this Division, *permitted device* means any of the following:

(a) a starter pistol or similar device designed to fire blank cartridges at athletic events;

(b) a bear banger, bird banger or similar device designed to produce a loud sound to scare wildlife away from persons or property;

(c) a captive bolt pistol, captive bolt rifle, free bolt stunner or similar device designed for use in slaughtering livestock;

(d) a flare gun or similar device designed to send distress signals;

(e) a pneumatic line thrower, pyrotechnic line thrower or similar device designed to project lines or ropes to distant positions or to positions that cannot be safely reached by other means;

(f) a nail gun, pneumatic tool, power-actuated tool or similar device designed for use in construction or manufacturing to fasten or join materials.

### Exemptions for permitted devices

37 (1) A person who takes an action referred to in a provision of the Act listed in subsection (2) is exempt from the listed provision, in relation to that action, if the following conditions are met:

(a) the action is taken with a firearm or imitation firearm that is a permitted device;

(b) the action is taken for the purposes of using the permitted device exclusively for the purposes for which it is designed.

(2) This section applies in relation to the following provisions of the Act:

(a) section 3 ;

(b) section 4 (1) and (2) ;

### Exemptions from section 11 of Act for toy guns

40 (1) In this section, *toy gun* means an object, other than an object produced in whole or in part from a 3-dimensional printer, that

(a) is an imitation firearm,

(b) is designed and produced for children 13 years of age or younger for use in learning or play, and

(c) has not been altered since the date of manufacture.

(2) A person who sells or offers to sell a toy gun to a minor is, in relation to that action, exempt from section 11 (1) of the Act, as that section relates to imitation firearms.

(3) A person who rents or offers to rent, or supplies or offers to supply, a toy gun to a minor is, in relation to that action, exempt from section 11 (3) (b) of the Act.

### Exemptions from section 14 of Act for cadet programs

41 (1) In this section, *cadet program* means a cadet program or cadet activity of any of the following organizations:

(a) the Air Cadets, Army Cadets, Junior Canadian Rangers or Sea Cadets, as operated by the Canadian Forces;

(b) the Navy League of Canada;

(c) a police service.

(2) A minor who takes an action referred to in section 14 (1) or (2) of the Act is exempt from that section, in relation to that action, if the following conditions are met:

(a) the action is taken for the purposes of participating in a cadet program;

(b) the action is taken under the close personal supervision of an adult who is a member, employee, contractor or volunteer of,

(i) in the case of a cadet program of an organization referred to in subsection (1) (a), the Canadian Forces,

(ii) in the case of a cadet program of the Navy League of Canada, the Navy League of Canada, or

(iii) in the case of a cadet program of a police service, the police service;

(c) the minor does not carry ammunition, other than ammunition for a low-velocity firearm, while taking the action.
