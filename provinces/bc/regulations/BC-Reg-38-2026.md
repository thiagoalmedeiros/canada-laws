# Intimate Image Claims Regulation

BC Reg 38/2026

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Civil Resolution Tribunal Act.

### Application

2 This regulation applies to intimate image claims under Division 8 of Part 10 of the Act.

### Maximum intimate image claim amount

3 For the purposes of the definition of "tribunal limit amount" in section 136.1 of the Act, $75 000 is the maximum amount that the tribunal may award in an intimate image claim in accordance with section 136.2 (1) (b) .

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Civil Resolution Tribunal Act, S.B.C. 2012, c. 25, s. 93.]
