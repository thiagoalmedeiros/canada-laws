# School Tax Exemptions

(Wind Power Projects) Regulation

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the School Act;

*wind power project* means a power project that uses a wind turbine to generate or supply electricity from the wind.

### Approved class of alternative energy power project

2 Wind power projects are approved alternative energy power projects for purposes of section 131.1 of the Act and are exempt from school taxes to the extent set out in section 3 of the regulation.

### What property is tax exempt?

3 The following improvements in respect of a wind power project are exempt from school taxes:

(a) any tower used to support a wind turbine;

(b) the foundation for a tower referred to in paragraph (a).

[Provisions of the School Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 412, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 131.1]
