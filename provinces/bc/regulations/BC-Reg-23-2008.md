# Comox Valley Regional District Regulation

BC Reg 23/2008

### Regional growth strategy

1 (1) The Comox Valley Regional District, except that part within the area of the Islands Trust, is designated as an area for which a regional growth strategy must be developed and adopted.

(2) Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 350/2010.]

### Declared nuisances

2 The Comox Valley Regional District is granted the additional power to exercise the powers conferred on the council of a municipality under Division 12 of Part 3 of the Community Charter, except the powers conferred under sections 73 and 75, subject to the terms and conditions set out in that Division.

[Provisions of the Local Government Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 323, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: sections 799 and 852]
