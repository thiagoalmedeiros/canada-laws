# Mount Polley Investigation and Inquiry Regulation

BC Reg 158/2014

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Ministry of Energy and Mines Act;

*minister* means the minister responsible for the administration of the Mines Act.

### Minister is authorized

2 The minister, with respect to the breach of the tailings storage facility at Mount Polley Mine on August 4, 2014, being a matter arising under the Mines Act, is authorized to do everything referred to in section 8 (2) and (3) (a) of the Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Ministry of Energy and Mines Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 298, section 8 (2)]
