BC Reg 488/88

### Incompatible provisions

1 Sections 18 to 29, 36 to 41, 53 to 60, 89 and 93 of the Indian Act (Canada) are specified to be incompatible with the Torrens system.

1 provisions cited are those as of December 1988

[Provisions of the Land Title Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 250, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 367 (2) (a) (ii)]
