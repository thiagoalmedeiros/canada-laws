BC Reg 301/2021

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Income Tax Act.

### Communication of information — prescribed enactments

2 For the purposes of section 64 (5) (j) of the Act, the following enactments are prescribed:

(a) the Business Number Act;

(b) section 8 of the Financial Administration Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Income Tax Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 215, s. 48.]
