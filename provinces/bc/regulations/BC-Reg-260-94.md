# Woodworker Lien Fee Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/260_94/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 260/94

### Definitions

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Woodworker Lien Act.

### Fees

2 There must be paid at the time of filing a statement of lien under section 3 of the Act a fee of $60.

[Provisions of the Woodworker Lien Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 491, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 3]
