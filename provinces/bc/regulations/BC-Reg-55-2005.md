BC Reg 55/2005

### College of the Rockies Students' Association

1 The College of the Rockies Students' Association is the designated student society under the College and Institute Act for the College of the Rockies.

[Provisions of the College and Institute Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 52, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: sections 21 (1) (b) and 68]
