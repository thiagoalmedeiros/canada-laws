# Tribunal Small Claims Regulation

Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/232_2018/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 232/2018

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Civil Resolution Tribunal Act.

### Application

2 This regulation applies to tribunal small claims under Division 3 of Part 10 of the Act.

### Maximum tribunal small claim amount

3 For the purposes of section 118 (1) of the Act, the maximum tribunal small claim amount prescribed is $5 000, exclusive of interest and any expenses referred to under section 49 of the Act.

### Repealed

4 Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 147/2022, s. (a).]

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Civil Resolution Tribunal Act, S.B.C. 2012, c. 25, s. 93.]
