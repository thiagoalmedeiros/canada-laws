BC Reg 217/2012

### Special Act insurance companies

1 Sections P7 to P15 of the Pre-existing Company Provisions prescribed under section 442.1 of the Business Corporations Act do not apply to a special Act insurance company.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Financial Institutions Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 141, section 2 (4)]
