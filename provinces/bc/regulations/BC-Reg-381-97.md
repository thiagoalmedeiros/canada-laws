BC Reg 381/97

### Prescribed agencies

1 The following are prescribed agencies for the purposes of Part 7 of the Medicare Protection Act:

(a) Insurance Corporation of British Columbia;

(b) Workers' Compensation Board;

(c) Office of the Superintendent of Motor Vehicles.

[Provisions of the Medicare Protection Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 286, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 36 (1)]
