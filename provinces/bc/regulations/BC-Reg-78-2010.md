# Conflicts of Interest Exceptions (Sun Peaks Mountain Resort Municipality) Regulation

BC Reg 78/2010

# Conflicts of Interest Exceptions
(Sun Peaks Mountain Resort Municipality) Regulation

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Community Charter;

*corporation* means the Sun Peaks Resort Corporation;

*councillor* means a councillor appointed to the council of Sun Peaks Mountain Resort Municipality under section 25 (1) (b) (i) of the Local Government Act.

### Nature of pecuniary interest

2 A pecuniary interest in the nature of remuneration, expenses, benefits, dividends, profits or other amounts payable to a councillor as a result of the councillor being a director, senior officer or shareholder or an employee of the corporation, or of a subsidiary or parent of the corporation, is prescribed for the purposes of section 104 (1) (e) of the Act.

### Exception to restriction on use of insider information

3 Section 108 of the Act does not apply to a councillor in respect of a pecuniary interest of the nature prescribed under section 2 during the term of the councillor's appointment.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: the Community Charter, S.B.C. 2003, c. 26, ss. 104 and 282.]
