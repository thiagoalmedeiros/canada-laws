BC Reg 475/98

### Notice of debt

1 The prescribed form referred to in section 17 (4) of the Mines Act for a notice of debt is the applicable form approved by the Director of Land Titles under the Land Title Act.

[Provisions of the Mines Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 293, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: sections 17 (4) and 38]
