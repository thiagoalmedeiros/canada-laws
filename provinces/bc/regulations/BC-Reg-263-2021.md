# Shulus Exemption Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/263_2021/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 263/2021

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Utilities Commission Act;

*Kingsvale pump station* means the pump station owned and operated by Trans Mountain Corporation in Kingsvale, south of the City of Merritt, and supplied by the Kingsvale transmission line;

*Kingsvale transmission line* means the approximately 24 km, 138 kV transmission line located south of the City of Merritt that connects the Kingsvale pump station to the authority's transmission system at circuit 1L251 and is designated by the authority as 1L251KPS;

*Shulus* means Shulus Electricity Transmission Line LP.

### Exemption

2 (1) Shulus is exempt from Part 3 of the Act in respect of

(a) its ownership and operation of the Kingsvale transmission line, and

(b) the provision of service respecting the transmission of electricity from the authority's transmission system to the Kingsvale pump station.

(2) Despite subsection (1), Shulus is not exempt from section 42 of the Act in relation to orders of the Commission respecting safety issued to Shulus under section 23, 25, 37 or 49 of the Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Utilities Commission Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 473, s. 22.]
