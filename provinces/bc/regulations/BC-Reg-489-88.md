# shíshálh Nation Designation Regulation

BC Reg 489/88

### Nation designated

1 The shíshálh Nation referred to in the shíshálh Nation Self-Government Act (Canada) is designated for the purpose of applying the Torrens system to Indian land of the Nation.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Land Title Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 250, s. 367 (2).]
