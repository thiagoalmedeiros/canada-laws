BC Reg 215/2009

### Declared nuisances

1 The Strathcona Regional District is granted the additional power to exercise the powers conferred on the council of a municipality under Division 12 of Part 3 of the Community Charter, except the powers conferred under sections 73 and 75, subject to the terms and conditions set out in that Division.
