# Creditor Assistance Fee Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/237_94/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 237/94

### Definitions

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Creditor Assistance Act.

### Fees

2 A fee of $30 must be paid to the district registrar

(a) for the filing of an affidavit of claim under section 8 (1) of the Act, or

(b) for the issue or renewal of a certificate.

[Provisions of the Creditor Assistance Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 83, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 51]
