# Mineral Land Reserve (Dasiqox Teáºtan Area) Regulation

BC Reg 36/2025

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*land* includes foreshore and land covered by water;

*reserve period* means the period beginning on the date this regulation comes into force and ending on June 30, 2025;

*site number* means the site number given in the registry to a particular area of land shown on an electronic map in the registry.

### Temporary reserve established

2 A mineral reserve for all minerals and placer minerals is established for the reserve period over the area of land identified by site number 1007088 and the site name Dasiqox Teáºtan Area.

### No registration of claims in reserve

3 A free miner must not register a claim in the reserve established under section 2.
