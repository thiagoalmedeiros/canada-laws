# Marriage Act Fees Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official British Columbia website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/516_81/xml) before relying on it.

BC Reg 516/81

### Interpretation

1 In this regulation,

*Act* means the Marriage Act.

### Fees

2 The following fees are payable under the Act:

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| | (a) for a marriage licence (section 15)................... | $100 |
| | (b) for solemnization of a marriage (section 20)..... | $75 |
| | (c) to lodge a caveat (section 23)......................... | $25 |

### Commission — issuer of marriage licences

3 An issuer of marriage licences who is not a salaried member of the public service is entitled to receive $10 as commission for each marriage licence issued.

### Commission — marriage commissioner

4 A marriage commissioner who is not an employee as defined in the Public Service Act is entitled to receive $50 as commission for each civil marriage ceremony performed.

[Provisions of the Marriage Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 282, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: sections 15, 20, 23 and 36]
