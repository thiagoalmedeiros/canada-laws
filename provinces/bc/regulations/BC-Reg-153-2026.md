# Safe Access to Schools Regulation

BC Reg 153/2026

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Safe Access to Schools Act.

### Access zones

2 Access zones are established for the following schools:

(a) schools operated by a board of education, other than the Qwam Qwum Stuwixwulh Community School in the City of Nanaimo;

(b) schools operated by a francophone education authority;

(c) schools operated by an independent school authority, other than the schools set out in column 2 of the Schedule to this regulation.

### Designated policing unit

3 For the purposes of the definition of "police officer" in section 1 of the Act, the designated policing unit known as the Stl'atl'imx Tribal Police Service is prescribed.
