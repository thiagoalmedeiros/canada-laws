# Regional District Service Withdrawal Regulation

BC Reg 398/2000

### Services that are not subject to withdrawal

1 The following services are exempted from withdrawal under Division 6 of Part 10 of the Local Government Act:

(a) an emergency telephone system, including an emergency 911 system;

(b) a transit service;

(c) a regional parks service established under the Local Government Act;

(d) the service of the regulation, storage and management of municipal solid waste and recyclable material.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Local Government Act, R.S.B.C. 2015, c. 1, s. 361 (6)]
