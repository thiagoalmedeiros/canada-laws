# Treaty First Nation Definition Regulation

BC Reg 179/2024

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Offence Act;

*Yuułuʔiłʔatḥ* means the Ucluelet First Nation as defined in section 29.1.1 of the Maa-nulth First Nations Final Agreement.

### Treaty first nation

2 The following are prescribed for the purposes of the definition of "treaty first nation" in section 1 of the Act:

(a) Tsawwassen First Nation;

(b) Huu-ay-aht First Nations;

(c) Ka:'yu:'k't'h' /Che:k'tles7et'h' First Nations;

(d) Toquaht Nation;

(e) Uchucklesaht Tribe;

(f) Yuułuʔiłʔatḥ;

(g) Tla'amin Nation.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Offence Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 388, s. 132 (2).]
