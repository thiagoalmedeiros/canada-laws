# Act respecting artistic, literary and scientific competitions

Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c C-51*

*Updated to 2025-06-07*

**1** The Minister of Culture and Communications may establish annual artistic and literary competitions, and determine the conditions thereof.

The Minister of Economy, Innovation and Energy may establish annual scientific competitions, and determine the conditions thereof.

**2** The minister responsible for the competition shall appoint, for each competition that he establishes, a jury which shall confer, if it deems it expedient, rewards on the prize-winners.

The jury, under the direction of the minister responsible, shall fulfill the duties that may be assigned to it.

**3** The minister responsible for the competition shall fix each year the number of the members of each jury and their remuneration.

Each jury shall be composed of at least three members.

**4** The conditions of each competition shall be published in due time in the *Gazette officielle du Québec.*

**5** (This section ceased to have effect on 17 April 1987).
