**132** Matters pending before the former Commission are continued and decided in accordance with this Act by the Conseil du trésor, the Commission de la fonction publique established by this Act, the Office or the Minister of Civil Service, in accordance with their respective jurisdictions under this Act, but if an inquiry provided for by section 61 of the Civil Service Act replaced by this Act has begun before a delegate designated by the former commission, it continues before that same delegate.

The Conseil du trésor, the Commission de la fonction publique established by this Act, the Office or the Minister of Civil Service, as the case may be, may exercise all the powers of the former Commission, including the power to revise or revoke decisions, orders or certificates as if it or he were the author thereof.

**133** (Omitted).

**134** (Omitted).

**135** (Omitted).

**136** The Commission de la fonction publique established by this Act becomes a party to any proceeding to which the former Commission was a party on 31 March 1979, without continuance of suit.

**137** (Omitted).

**138** The regulations and decisions made under the Civil Service Act (1965, 1st session, chapter 14) remain in force, to the extent that they are in conformity with this Act, until they are repealed or until they are replaced or amended by regulations or decisions made in accordance with this Act.

**139** The eligible lists presently in force remain valid and shall be used in the application of this Act, as the Office may determine.

**140** Any reference in any Act, proclamation or commission, order in council or other document to the Civil Service Act or to a provision of that Act is a reference to this Act or the corresponding provision of this Act; and, in particular, any reference to section 61 of the Civil Service Act is a reference to section 87 or 97 of this Act, as the case may be.

**141** The moneys appropriated for the purposes of the former Commission are transferred to the Commission de la fonction publique established by this Act, the Office, the Minister of Civil Service or the Conseil du trésor, as the Government may determine.

**142** The moneys required for the application of this Act are taken for the fiscal year 1978/1979 out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, and for subsequent fiscal years, out of the moneys granted each year for that purpose by Parliament.

**143** The Minister of Civil Service is responsible for the application of this Act.

**144** (Omitted).
