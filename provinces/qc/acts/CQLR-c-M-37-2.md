# Act to proclaim Hispanic Heritage Month

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Gouvernement du Québec. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Quebec. Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/M-37.2) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c M-37.2*

*Updated to 2018-06-15*

AS Québec’s Hispanic community represents an important social group within Québec society;

AS many individuals in Québec report that they are of Latin American or Spanish origin and that Spanish is their mother tongue;

AS Québec’s Hispanic community contributes to Québec’s economic development;

AS the cultural and social heritage of Québec’s Hispanic community is rich and diversified;

AS this cultural heritage includes the contributions of the Indigenous peoples of the Americas;

AS this cultural heritage includes the contributions of the communities of African descent;

AS celebrating Hispanic Heritage Month would help recognize the contributions of Québec’s Hispanic community to Québec society;

THE PARLIAMENT OF QUÉBEC ENACTS AS FOLLOWS:

> The Minister of Immigration, Francization and Integration is responsible for the application of this Act. Order in Council 1640-2022 dated 20 October 2022, (2022) 154 G.O. 2 (French), 6514.

**1** October is proclaimed Hispanic Heritage Month.

**2** (Omitted).
