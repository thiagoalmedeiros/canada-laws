# Regulation to suspend certain requirements of holders of a special permit authorizing the operation of a combination of vehicles including a hauled dolly

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Gouvernement du Québec. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Quebec. Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/C-24.2,%20r.%2032.1) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c C-24.2, r 32.1*

*Enabling statute: Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2, s. 633.2)*

*Updated to 2026-03-12*

**1** This Regulation applies to the holder of a special permit issued under the Regulation respecting special permits (chapter C-24.2, r. 35) authorizing the operation of a combination of vehicles including a hauled dolly and belonging to any of the following classes or combinations of road vehicles:

(1) a combination of classes 1 and 5;

(2) a combination of classes 1, 2, 3 and 5;

(3) class 5;

(4) a combination of classes 5 and 7;

(5) class 6 of the general category for all public highways;

(6) a combination of class 6 of the general category for all public highways and class 1 or 7.

**2** The application of the following subparagraphs of the first paragraph of section 5 of the Regulation respecting special permits (chapter C-24.2, r. 35) is suspended in respect of a permit holder if the combination of road vehicles is travelling without a hauled dolly:

(1) subparagraphs 7 and 9;

(2) paragraph 10 as regards the requirement that permit holders make sure that all the characteristics of the vehicle used provided for in paragraphs 9 and 14 of section 4 of the Regulation are identical to the characteristics mentioned in the permit and that all the characteristics provided for in paragraph 10 of the same section are identical or superior to the characteristics mentioned on the permit.

**3** The suspension provided for in section 2 applies to combinations of road vehicles travelling northward on Route 389 following the itinerary provided in Schedule 1, between the following two places:

(1) the parking area situated at chain number 389-02-150-000C 0+800, before the Manic-5 interpretation centre, the boundaries of which are identified by the following road sign:

[Image: C-24.2R32.1_EN_001_001.jpg]

(2) the parking area situated at chain number 389-02-160-000C 0+050, immediately after the main spillway of the Manic 5 station, the entrance to which is identified by the appropriate road sign.

Despite the first paragraph, the driver of a combination of road vehicles may drive up to 100 m past the place referred to in subparagraph 2 in order to back up.

**4** To avail themselves of the suspension under section 2, permit holders must comply with the provisions of sections 5 to 8.

**5** Combinations of road vehicles must be coupled and uncoupled at the places identified in the first paragraph of section 3.

**6** The permit holder must make sure that the combination of road vehicles has the following characteristics:

(1) the overall height, including the load and equipment, of the combination of road vehicles exceeds by not more than 20 cm the height applicable under the permit;

(2) the load weight on the steer axle exceeds by not more than 20% the weight indicated on the permit;

(3) the load weight on the group of rear axles of a tractor is not more than 17,400 kg per axle;

(4) the minimum spacing between the centre of the last axle of the tractor and the centre of the first axle of the semi-trailer is equal to or greater than the minimum spacing indicated on the permit between the centre of the single axle of the hauled dolly, or the last axle if it has more than one, and the centre of the first axle of the semi-trailer.

**7** The permit holder must make sure that the driver drives at a speed equal to or under 15 km/h.

In addition, the permit holder must make sure that the combination of road vehicles is followed by an escort vehicle for the entire duration of the journey and is present during the coupling and uncoupling of the combination of road vehicles if there is a risk that those operations could impede or obstruct traffic.

**8** The application of section 418 of the Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2) is suspended in respect of the driver of a combination of road vehicles who, after making sure that it can be done safely, stops the vehicle on the shoulder on the part of the road opposite the Manic-5 interpretation centre or the part of the road situated opposite the brake verification area to allow for and facilitate the passing of other vehicles.

**9** (Omitted in part).

This Regulation is revoked on the day of the fifth anniversary of its coming into force.
