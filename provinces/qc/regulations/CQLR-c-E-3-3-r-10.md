*CQLR c E-3.3, r 10*

*Enabling statute: Election Act (chapter E-3.3, ss. 337 and 549)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** An elector may produce either of the following documents as identification under the second paragraph of section 337 of the Election Act (chapter E-3.3):

(1) a Certificate of Indian Status issued to persons registered in the Indian Register under the Indian Act (R.S.C. 1985, c. I-5); or

(2) a Canadian Forces identification card issued under order CFAO 26-3 of the Department of National Defence.

**2** (Omitted).
