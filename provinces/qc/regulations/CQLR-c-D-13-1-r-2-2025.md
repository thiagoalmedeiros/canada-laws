*CQLR c D-13.1, r 2 (2025)*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting hunting and fishing rights in the James Bay and New Québec territories (chapter D-13.1, s. 78, 1st par., subpar. f, and 3rd par)*

*Updated to 2025-12-04*

**1** The upper limit of kill for moose allocated to the Native people and non-Natives in Area 17 determined by the Regulation respecting fishing and hunting areas (chapter C-61.1, r. 34) is 104 moose for the period from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.

**2** (Omitted).
