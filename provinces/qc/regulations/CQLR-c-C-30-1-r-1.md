# Regulation under section 2 of the Act to facilitate the trade of goods and the mobility of labour from the other provinces and the territories of Canada

*CQLR c C-30.1, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act to facilitate the trade of goods and the mobility of labour from the other provinces and the territories of Canada (chapter C-30.1, s. 2, par. 2, and s.13)*

*Updated to 2026-06-30*

## I — DEFINITION AND SCOPE

**1** For the purposes of the Act to facilitate the trade of goods and the mobility of labour from the other provinces and the territories of Canada (chapter C-30.1), **goods** means a good classified under the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System of the World Customs Organization.

**2** The phrase “without any further manufacturing, production, preparation, composition, classification, content, performance or container capacity requirement” in the first paragraph of section 2 of the Act does not operate to exclude or limit the application of an Act or any statutory instrument that governs in particular:

(1) persons and partnerships authorized to commercialize or acquire goods, in particular goods whose sale is governed by the Professional Code (chapter C-26), legislation respecting the professions, or regulations made under the Code or such legislation; and

(2) the requirement to obtain a permit, licence or another similar authorization as well as the use of goods, its labelling, installation, storage, transport, distribution or accessibility.

## II — GOODS AND CLASSES OF EXCLUDED GOODS

**3** The following goods and classes of goods are excluded from the application of the first paragraph of section 2 of the Act to facilitate the trade of goods and the mobility of labour from the other provinces and the territories of Canada (chapter C-30.1):

(1) cannabis, including the classes of cannabis and accessories to which the Cannabis Regulation Act (chapter C-5.3) and the regulations made under that Act apply, and any object if a name, logo, distinguishing guise, design, image or slogan that is directly associated with cannabis, a brand of cannabis, the Société québécoise du cannabis or a cannabis producer appears on the object;

(2) tobacco, any product containing tobacco, electronic cigarettes and any other devices of that nature that are put to one’s mouth to inhale any substance that may or may not contain nicotine, including their components and accessories, and any other product or class of product to which the Tobacco Control Act (chapter L-6.2) and the regulations made under that Act apply, and any object if a name, logo, distinguishing guise, design, image or slogan that is directly associated with tobacco, a tobacco product, a brand of tobacco product or a manufacturer of tobacco products appears on the object;

(3) alcoholic beverages referred to in paragraph 5 of section 2 of the Act respecting offences relating to alcoholic beverages (chapter I-8.1);

(4) plants grown for commercial purposes other than plants destined for reforestation;

(5) threatened or vulnerable plant species designated respectively in sections 2 and 3 of the Regulation respecting threatened or vulnerable plant species and their habitats (chapter E-12.01, r. 3) and the invasive exotic plant species listed in Schedule I to the Regulation respecting invasive exotic plant species (O.C. 675-2026, 2026-05-06);

(6) plant seeds;

(7) pesticides;

(8) halocarbons;

(9) petroleum products referred to in section 2 of the Petroleum Products Act (chapter P-30.01);

(10) single-use plastics, including polystyrene;

(11) live domestic animals and other animals kept in captivity or deemed to be kept in captivity referred to respectively in the second and third paragraphs of section 1.5 of the Animal Health Protection Act (chapter P-42), fertilized eggs and ova of those animals, any part of those animals taken from a live animal and any non-domestic insect used for commercial pollination purposes;

(12) flesh of animals referred to in the first paragraph of section 1 of the Regulation respecting the sale, importation, possession and disposal of an animal or wildlife by-product (chapter C-61.1, r. 33.1), bear gall-bladders and bile as well as any fluid, excretion or secretion from a cervid and any product derived therefrom;

(13) animals kept in captivity that are carriers of or affected by a pathogen listed in Schedule 3 to the Regulation respecting animals in captivity (chapter C-61.1, r. 5.1);

(14) raw dairy products and food containing raw milk or raw cream intended for human consumption;

(15) live fish except for fish of a saltwater species, dead fish of a species listed in the first paragraph of section 30 of the Regulation respecting aquaculture and the sale of fish (chapter C-61.1, r. 7), and live or dead baitfish;

(16) houses used for bees or for non-domestic insects used for commercial pollination purposes and equipment used for apiculture or commercial pollination;

(17) air bag modules, seat belts with pretensioners, air bag and seat belt electronic control modules, and devices that simulate the presence or proper functioning of air bags and seat belts with pretensioners;

(18) radar warning devices;

(19) equipment or parts, devices and apparatus designed to increase the power or maximum speed of a moped to a level greater than that originally provided by the manufacturer;

(20) exhaust systems of maintenance vehicles and off-highway vehicles within the meaning of section 2 of the Act respecting off-highway vehicles (chapter V-1.3) and equipment aimed at or having the effect of bypassing those exhaust systems or affecting their proper functioning as well as exhaust systems of motor vehicles within the meaning of section 4 of the Highway Safety Code;

(21) devices for tampering with a pollution control system for a motor vehicle within the meaning of section 4 of the Highway Safety Code;

(22) booklets, cards and tickets to participate in a lottery scheme, tickets for a sports event within the meaning of section 1 of the Act respecting safety in recreation and sports (chapter S-3.1) as well as amusement machines and video lottery machines.

## III — REQUIREMENTS THAT REMAIN APPLICABLE

**4** The requirements stated in Schedule I that apply to manufacturing, production, preparation, composition, classification, content, performance or container capacity remain applicable to the goods concerned that are manufactured, prepared, cultivated, raised or sold for commercial purposes in another province or a territory of Canada in compliance with the standards applicable in that province or territory.

## IV — FINAL PROVISION

**5** (Omitted).
