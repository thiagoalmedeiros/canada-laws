# Regulation respecting the application of certain provisions of Chapter III of the Act respecting legal aid and the provision of certain other legal services

*CQLR c A-14, r 3*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting legal aid and the provision of certain other legal services (chapter A-14, s. 83.17)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The cost of a legal service referred to in section 83.16 of the Act respecting legal aid and the provision of certain other legal services (chapter A-14) includes

(a) the fees of an advocate;

(b) the fees of any bailiff or any stenographer who acts as such on behalf of the person to whom the legal service was rendered;

(c) the fees and costs of any expert who, with the prior authorization of the director general, acts for the person to whom the legal service was rendered; and

(d) the other expenses.

**2** (Omitted).
