# Regulation respecting access to surgical services

*CQLR c G-1.021, r 0.01*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the governance of the health and social services system (chapter G-1.021, s. 72)*

*Updated to 2026-01-15*

**1** Where a mechanism for access to surgical services is put in place pursuant to section 71 of the Act respecting the governance of the health and social services system (chapter G-1.021), all private providers referred to in the second paragraph must comply with the mechanism.

The following are private providers:

(1) specialized medical centres;

(2) private institutions operating a hospital centre.

Every private provider is required to provide services to a user referred to the private provider by the person responsible for managing access to specialized or superspecialized services within the time determined in the mechanism for access.

**2** Where a system for the distribution and referral of users is put in place regarding surgical services pursuant to section 71 of the Act respecting the governance of the health and social services system (chapter G-1.021), all dentists and physicians offering surgical services must use the system.

**3** Where there are wait times for access to surgical services, users awaiting services must be kept informed by Santé Québec of the progress of their request, including the priority having been granted to the request and the average wait times associated with it, in accordance with the mechanism for access. The use of technological means to inform users, with the users’ consent, of the progress of their request should be favoured.

**4** Sections 1 and 2 do not apply to a mechanism for access or a system for the distribution and referral of users put in place before 31 December 2025.

**5** (Omitted).
