# Regulation respecting the Québec Aquarium

*CQLR c C-61.1, r 8*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the conservation and development of wildlife (chapter C-61.1, ss. 77 and 78)*

*Updated to 2024-09-12*

**1** No visitor may be admitted inside the Québec Aquarium building if he is accompanied by a dog or any other animal unless it is a guide dog or a service dog.

**2** No visitor to the Québec Aquarium may:

(1) touch the aquatic mammals;

(2) put his hand or foot into the pool of an aquatic mammal;

(3) feed an aquatic mammal;

(4) throw any objects into the enclosure of an aquatic mammal;

(5) frighten an aquatic mammal.

**3** Within the Québec Aquarium property limits it is not allowed to:

(1) pick flowers or plants, damage bushes or trees;

(2) move, deface or damage signs;

(3) move or damage the benches, tables and shelters;

(4) move or damage movable property or damage immovable property.

Moreover, painting, altering or removing rocks or any other natural formations are not allowed.

**4** Inside the Québec Aquarium building no visitor may wear only a swimsuit or a lower body garment.

**5** Smoking is not allowed in the passages and halls or in the auditorium of the Québec Aquarium.

**6** Filming or taking pictures in the passages and halls of the Québec Aquarium is allowed only if no artificial lighting is used.

**7** Within the Québec Aquarium property limits, it is not allowed to:

(1) use loud speakers or any other sound producing instrument or sound amplifier;

(2) hand out or post up notes, pamphlets or folders;

(3) offer anything for sale.

**8** Any visitor to the Québec Aquarium must park his vehicle in the areas provided and identified for that purpose.

**9** Camping or lighting a fire on the Québec Aquarium property are not allowed.

**10** Climbing trees, walls, fences or any other structure located within the Québec Aquarium property limits is not allowed.

**11** Discarding bottles, papers or garbage, elsewhere than in the trash cans and other containers provided for that purpose is not allowed.

**12** The sale and trade of goods within the Québec Aquarium property limits are authorized only when such activities are carried out under a franchise contract entered into under paragraph 3 of section 78 of the Act respecting the conservation and development of wildlife (chapter C-61.1).

**13** The director may temporarily prohibit access to part or all of the Québec Aquarium property:

(1) when the safety of visitors may be endangered;

(2) when the capacity of visitors allowed in an area is reached or may be exceeded.

**14** (Omitted).
