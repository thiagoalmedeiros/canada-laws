*CQLR c A-4.1, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the acquisition of farm land by non-residents (chapter A-4.1, s. 35)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** Pursuant to section 21 of the Act, the information that must be contained in an application for registration of the acquisition of any farm land must be included in one of the clauses of the application for registration under the description “Declarations respecting the Act respecting the acquisition of farm land by non-residents (chapter A-4.1)”.

**2** (Implicitly revoked).
