# Order in Council concerning exclusion of certain goods and materials from the definition of “motor freight transportation”

*CQLR c T-12, r 5*

*Enabling statute: Transport Act (chapter T-12, s. 48.11.1)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** In accordance with section 48.11.1 of the Transport Act (chapter T-12) the goods and materials that fall under any of the categories listed below are excluded from the definition of “motor freight transportation”:

(1) dairy products, from the farm of a dairy producer to the dairy plant, where their transport requires a permit;

(2) sand, earth, gravel, stone, bituminous concrete including planed asphalt and recyclable and non-recyclable asphalt, snow and ice, ore that has not been altered to increase its concentration, farm, agricultural and fish products transported from the location where cut, harvested or extracted to the primary processing plant or to the market, firewood and coal;

(3) the timber referred to in the Regulation respecting forest transport contracts (chapter T-12, r. 2) and any other timber referred to in the Act respecting the marketing of agricultural, food and fish products (chapter M-35.1);

(4) the goods and materials transported by a person registered in the bulk trucking register referred to in subdivision 4.2 of the Transport Act.
