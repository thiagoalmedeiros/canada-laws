# Ministerial Order respecting the types of construction that the holder of an exclusive exploration right, a mining exploration licence or a licence to explore for surface mineral substances may erect or maintain on lands of the domain of the State without ministerial authorization

Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/M-13.1,%20r.%203) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c M-13.1, r 3*

*Enabling statute: Mining Act (chapter M-13.1, s. 304)*

*Updated to 2024-11-29*

**1** On lands of the domain of the State, the types of construction that the holder of an exclusive exploration right, a mining exploration licence or a licence to explore for surface mineral substances may erect or maintain without ministerial authorization on the land subject to the right are temporary, portable shelters that can be dismantled and are made of pliable material stretched over rigid supports.

**2** The types of construction referred to in section 1 shall clearly show the registration number or alphanumeric code of the mining right in respect of the territory where the construction is erected or maintained.

**3** (Omitted).
