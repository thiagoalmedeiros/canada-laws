*CQLR c C-61.1, r 45*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the conservation and development of wildlife (chapter C-61.1, s. 122)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The territory, the plan of which appears in the Schedule, is established as a wildlife preserve under the name Pierre-Étienne-Fortin Wildlife Preserve.

**2** (Omitted).
