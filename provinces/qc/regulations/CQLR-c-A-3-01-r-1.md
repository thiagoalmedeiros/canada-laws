*CQLR c A-3.01, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the accreditation and financing of students’ associations (chapter A-3.01, s. 2)*

*Updated to 2021-07-01*

**1** The following are educational institutions within the meaning of the Act respecting the accreditation and financing of students’ associations (chapter A-3.01):

(1) the Conservatoire de musique et d’art dramatique du Québec, established under the Act respecting the Conservatoire de musique et d’art dramatique du Québec (chapter C-62.1), together with its branches and affiliated superior schools of music or dramatic art;

(2) the Institut de technologie agroalimentaire du Québec;

(3) (paragraph revoked);

(4) the Institut du tourisme et d’hôtellerie du Québec.

**2** (Omitted).
