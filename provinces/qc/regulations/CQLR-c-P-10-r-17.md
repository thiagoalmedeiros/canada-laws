# Regulation respecting the supply of medications to ambulance technicians by an institution

*CQLR c P-10, r 17*

*Enabling statute: Pharmacy Act (chapter P-10, s. 37)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** An institution operating a centre in which a pharmacist practises his or her profession may supply medications to an ambulance technician governed by the Regulation respecting the professional activities that may be engaged in within the framework of pre-hospital emergency services and care (chapter M-9, r. 2), for the purposes of the professional activities authorized therein, if the technician engages in those activities mainly on the territory of the health and social services agency responsible for that institution.

**2** The medications supplied must be determined in a clinical protocol developed and approved in accordance with section 3 of the Act respecting pre-hospital emergency services (chapter S-6.2) and be covered by a prescription.

**3** (Omitted).
