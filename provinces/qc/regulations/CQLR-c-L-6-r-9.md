# Regulation respecting persons who must respect the conditions for issue and maintenance of a licence relative to video lotteries

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Gouvernement du Québec. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Quebec. Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/L-6,%20r.%209) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c L-6, r 9*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting lotteries and amusement machines (chapter L-6, s. 119)*

*Updated to 2023-10-27*

**1** When a legal person must hold a licence to manufacture or repair video lottery machines, the directors of that person, and the shareholders holding 10% or more of the shares with full voting rights must also respect the conditions for the issue and maintenance of such a licence, provided in section 30 of the Rules concerning video lottery machines (chapter L-6, r. 3).

**2** When a legal person must hold a video lottery machine site operator’s licence, the directors of that person, the shareholders holding 10% or more of the shares with full voting rights, and the person responsible for the administration referred to in subparagraph 4 of section 33 of the Rules respecting video lottery machines (chapter L-6, r. 3) must also respect the conditions for the issue and maintenance of such a licence provided for in section 31 of those rules.

**3** (Omitted).
