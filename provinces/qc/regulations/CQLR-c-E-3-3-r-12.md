*CQLR c E-3.3, r 12*

*Enabling statute: Election Act (chapter E-3.3, ss. 40.20 and 550)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

## I — GENERAL

**1** The Interpretation Act (chapter I-16) applies to this Regulation.

## II — ENUMERATOR’S BADGE

**2** Each enumerator shall, when enumerating, wear in a conspicuous fashion a circular plastic-covered metal badge 8 cm in diameter with a pin on the back.

**3** The enumerator’s badge shall include the following particulars:

(a) identification of the Chief Electoral Officer;

(b) the enumerator’s name and title;

(c) the badge number.

Each badge shall have a different number.

**4** Enumerators shall return their badges to the returning officer when they have completed their duties.
