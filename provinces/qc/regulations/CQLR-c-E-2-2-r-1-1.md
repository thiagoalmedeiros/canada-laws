*CQLR c E-2.2, r 1.1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting elections and referendums in municipalities (chapter E-2.2, s. 465, 4th par.)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

**1** Each amount provided for in section 465 of the Act respecting elections and referendums in municipalities (chapter E-2.2) is adjusted on 1 April of each year according to the change in the average Consumer Price Index for the preceding year, based on the index established for the whole of Québec by Statistics Canada.

Any amount adjusted in accordance with the first paragraph is rounded off to the nearest dollar in the case of the base amount, or to the nearest cent in the case of the amount by which the base amount is increased. An equidistant result is rounded up to the nearest dollar or cent.

**2** (Implicitly revoked).

> See the notice published in Part 1 (French) of the Gazette officielle du Québec of 28 February 2026, page 155.

**3** (Omitted).
