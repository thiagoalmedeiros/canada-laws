*CQLR c V-1.2, r 0.1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting off-highway vehicles (chapter V-1.2, s. 47, 1st par., subpar. 5)*

*Updated to 2020-01-01*

**1** In addition to the off-highway vehicles mentioned in section 33.1 of the Act respecting off-highway vehicles (chapter V-1.2), off-highway vehicles equipped with the following engines or motor are authorized to be operated in the places listed in paragraphs 1 to 4 of section 12.1 of the Act:

(1) a two-stroke engine;

(2) an electric motor;

(3) a hybrid engine.

**2** (Omitted).
