# Regulation respecting the stopping of a road vehicle in a traffic lane reserved exclusively for certain vehicles

*CQLR c C-24.2, r 29.1*

*Enabling statute: Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2, s. 633.2)*

*Updated to 2026-01-15*

**1** The application of paragraph 7.1 of section 386 of the Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2) is suspended with respect to the driver of a road vehicle who stops in a traffic lane reserved exclusively for certain vehicles, hereinafter a “reserved lane”, in order to load and unload goods.

**2** This Order applies in a reserved lane located on the right-hand side, if there are signs that comply with the Regulation respecting road signs (chapter C-24.2, r. 41) with respect to P-160 road signs and that contain one of the following silhouettes:

[Image]

This Order applies, under the same conditions, in a reserved lane located on the left-hand side in the case of a one-way road.

**3** To benefit from the suspension provided for in section 1, the driver of a road vehicle must comply with sections 4 to 7.

**4** The driver must stop the road vehicle in the zone delimited by the road signs referred to in section 2.

**5** Before entering the reserved lane to stop a road vehicle therein, the driver must ascertain that this manoeuvre can be done safely.

**6** The driver must stop the road vehicle not more than 30 cm from the near edge of the roadway and facing the same way as the traffic.

If there are 2 contiguous reserved lanes that are not separated by a median strip or any other raised device, the driver must stop the vehicle in the right-hand lane or the left-hand lane, as applicable, in compliance with the provisions of the first paragraph.

Stopping is allowed only during the hours indicated on the signs referred to in section 2 and may not exceed the time required to load or unload goods. The driver must activate the flashing emergency lights of the vehicle for the entire duration of the stop.

**7** After loading or unloading goods, the driver must re-enter the lane contiguous to the reserved lane after signaling that intention by means of the turn-signal lights and ascertaining that this manoeuvre can be done safely.

**8** (Omitted).
