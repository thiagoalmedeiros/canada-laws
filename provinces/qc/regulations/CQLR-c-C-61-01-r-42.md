*CQLR c C-61.01, r 42*

*Enabling statute: Natural Heritage Conservation Act (chapter C-61.01, s. 43)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** **Establishment of the ecological reserve:** The island described in section 2 is established as an ecological reserve under the name of Réserve écologique Marcel-Léger.

**2** **Description:** The ecological reserve established pursuant to section 1 consists of an island called Île aux Sternes situated in the St. Lawrence River downstream from Lac Saint-Pierre, opposite lots 10 to 22 in the Grand-Chemin Range of the official cadaster of the Parish of La Visitation de la Pointe-du-Lac.
