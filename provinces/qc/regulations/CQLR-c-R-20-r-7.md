# Regulation respecting the financing of the administration costs of the Commission de la construction du Québec for the compulsory annual vacation and public holiday fund

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Gouvernement du Québec. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Quebec. Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/R-20,%20r.%207) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c R-20, r 7*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting labour relations, vocational training and workforce management in the construction industry (chapter R-20, s. 123)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The Commission de la construction du Québec may use the interests on the funds respecting compulsory annual vacations and general holidays up to a sum equal to 25% of those interest plus $965,000, in order to meet the administration and collection costs of the plan.

**2** (Obsolete).

**3** For its administration, the Commission may use a sum of $1,980,000 that is holds in trust as a reserve created from the interests on the compulsory annual vacation and public holiday fund.
