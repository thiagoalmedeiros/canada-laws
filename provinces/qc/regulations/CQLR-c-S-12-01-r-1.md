*CQLR c S-12.01, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the Société de télédiffusion du Québec (chapter S-12.01, s. 20)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The Société de télédiffusion du Québec must obtain the authorization of the Government for any financial commitment of or greater than $1,000,000 except for contracts under regulations made or deemed to be made under the Act respecting contracting by public bodies (chapter C-65.1).

**2** (Omitted).

**3** (Omitted).
