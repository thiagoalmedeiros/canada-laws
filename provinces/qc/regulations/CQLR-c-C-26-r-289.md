## I — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**1.01** In this Regulation, unless the context indicates otherwise,

(a) “Order” means the Ordre professionnel des travailleurs sociaux et des thérapeutes conjugaux et familiaux du Québec;

(b) “secretary” means the secretary of the Order.

## II — TERMS AND CONDITIONS FOR ISSUING PERMITS

**2.01** The board of directors issues permits to persons who send an application to the secretary, accompanied by the following items:

(a) an official attestation that the person holds a diploma recognized by the Office des professions du Québec under the first paragraph of section 12.0.1.1 of the Professional Code (chapter C-26) or recognized as equivalent by the board of directors;

(b) (paragraph revoked).
