# Regulation respecting discrimination on the basis of territory applicable to certain contracts of municipal bodies involving an expenditure equal to or greater than $20,000,000

Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c C-65.01, r 2*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01, s. 136, 1st par., subpar. 1)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

**1** The awarding of a contract involving an expenditure equal to or greater than $20,000,000 and referred to in any of section 7, to the extent that it is a contract referred to in subparagraph *b* of paragraph 2 of section 6, and sections 9 to 11 of the Regulation respecting discrimination on the basis of territory allowed when awarding certain contracts of municipal bodies or during a process for certification or qualification (chapter C-65.01, r. 3), must be authorized by the Government, unless the documents of the call for tenders published for the awarding of the contract include measures to discriminate on the basis of territory provided for with regard to the contract by any of those sections.

To obtain the authorization provided for in the first paragraph, a municipal body must show by a serious analysis that the measures to discriminate on the basis of territory provided for with regard to the contract by any of the sections referred to in that paragraph would so restrict procurement that there is a real risk of no tender being submitted.

**2** (Omitted).
