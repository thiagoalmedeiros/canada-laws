# Regulation respecting fees and tariffs payable under the Money-Services Businesses Act

*CQLR c E-12.000001, r 2*

*Enabling statute: Money-Services Businesses Act (chapter E-12.000001, ss. 60.1 and 62)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

## I — FEES PAYABLE

**1** The fees payable by a money-services business for a licence application filed with the Minister of Revenue for each class of licence are:

(1) $826 for currency exchange;

(2) $826 for funds transfer;

(3) $826 for the issue or redemption of traveller’s cheques, money orders or bank drafts;

(4) $826 for cheque cashing;

(5) $272 for the operation of each automated teller machine;

(6) $577 for the operation of each cryptoasset automated teller machine.

**1.1** If a money-services business has more than 25 establishments in which one or more of the money services provided for in subparagraphs 1 to 4 of the second paragraph of section 1 of the Money-Services Businesses Act (chapter E-12.000001) are offered, additional fees of $514 for each establishment beginning with the 26th establishment are payable for a licence application filed with the Minister of Revenue for one or more classes of licence provided for in subparagraphs 1 to 4 of the first paragraph of section 4 of the Act.

For the purposes of the first paragraph, **establishment** includes an establishment of a mandatary of a money-services business through which the money-services business offers one or more of the money services provided for in subparagraphs 1 to 4 of the second paragraph of section 1 of the Act.

**2** Fees are also payable for a licence application in the amount of $163 for each person covered by the security clearance report issued under section 8 of the Act.

**3** The money-services business must, by March 31 of each year, pay to the Minister the fees prescribed under section 1 with respect to each class of licence and the fees prescribed under section 1.1, as applicable.

## II — TARIFFS PAYABLE

**4** The charges payable with respect to the issue of a new security clearance report are $163 for each person or entity referred to in section 27 of the Act.

**7** The fees, charges and costs prescribed under this Regulation are not refundable.

**8** The fees, charges and costs payable are adjusted annually on 1 April in accordance with the rate corresponding to the annual change in the average all-items Consumer Price Index for Québec, excluding alcoholic beverages, tobacco products and recreational cannabis, for the 12-month period ending on 30 September of the preceding year. Fees, charges and costs are rounded down to the nearest dollar if they include a fraction of a dollar lower than $0.50 and rounded up to the nearest dollar if they include a fraction of a dollar that is equal to or greater than $0.50.

The result of the annual indexation is published annually in the *Gazette officielle du Québec* and on Revenu Québec’s website.
