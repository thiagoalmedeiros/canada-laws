# Regulation to impose conditions for the awarding of certain supply contracts by municipal bodies

Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c C-19, r 2.2*

*Enabling statute: Cities and Towns Act (chapter C-19, s. 573.3.1.1, 1st par., subpar. 1 and 2nd par.)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

**1** For the purposes of this Regulation,

**establishment** means a place where an enterprise carries on its activities on a permanent basis, clearly identified under its name and accessible during regular business hours.

**2** This Regulation applies to supply contracts for

(1) computer hardware and software;

(2) medical supplies and equipment;

(3) pharmaceutical products; and

(4) scientific instruments.

**3** Call for tender documents whose object is the awarding, according to an open procedure, of a contract referred to in section 2 must, for the sole purpose of determining the successful tenderer, impose a penalty in the form of an increase by 10% to 25% of the price submitted by an enterprise having an establishment in the United States of America but not in Québec or in a territory covered by an applicable intergovernmental agreement.

**4** The percentage of increase determined under section 3 must be specified in the call for tender documents.

**5** Call for tender documents whose object is the awarding, according to a procedure by written invitation, of a contract referred to in section 2 must be sent only to enterprises having an establishment in Québec or in a territory covered by an applicable intergovernmental agreement.

**6** A contract referred to in section 2 may be awarded by mutual agreement only to an enterprise having an establishment in Québec or in a territory covered by an applicable intergovernmental agreement, except with the prior authorization of the body’s council.

**7** Sections 3 and 4 do not apply to a public call for tenders that was published on the electronic tendering system before 6 March 2025.

**8** Sections 1 to 7 cease to have effect on 5 March 2027.

**9** (Omitted).
