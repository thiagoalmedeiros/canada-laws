# Regulation respecting the threshold and time periods applicable when awarding certain contracts of municipal bodies

Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c C-65.01, r 4*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01, ss. 29, 45, 83 and 263)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

## I — OBJECT AND DEFINITIONS

**1** This Regulation determines the expenditure threshold from which a supply, construction, services or partnership contract must be awarded according to an open procedure by a municipal body and the minimum time period for the receipt of tenders by the municipal body following, in the case of an open procedure, the publication of the notice on the electronic tendering system or, in the case of a procedure by written invitation, the transmission of invitations to tender.

For the purposes of this Regulation, a municipal body that is not subject to an intergovernmental agreement is considered to be a local municipality for the purposes of the rules set out in the agreement.

**2** For the purposes of this Regulation,

**Canada-European Union Agreement** means the Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement between Canada and the European Union and its Member States;

**services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement** means a services contract for:

(1) courier or mail services, including email;

(2) fax services;

(3) real estate services;

(4) computer services, including consultation services for the purchase or installation of computer software or hardware, and data processing services;

(5) maintenance or repair services for office equipment;

(6) management consulting services, except arbitration, mediation and conciliation services with regard to human resources management;

(7) engineering services, except engineering services related to a single transportation infrastructure design and construction contract;

(8) architectural services, including architectural landscaping;

(9) land use and planning services;

(10) test, analysis or inspection services for quality control;

(11) building cleaning services, including the interior;

(12) machinery or equipment repair services;

(13) purification services;

(14) garbage removal services; and

(15) road services.

## II — THRESHOLD

**3** The expenditure threshold from which a contract must be awarded according to an open procedure is the minimum threshold provided for in an intergovernmental agreement applicable to the municipal body.

## III — TIME PERIODS

**4** The minimum time period for the receipt of tenders when awarding a supply, construction or partnership contract according to an open procedure other than a procedure by means of a request for a quotation addressed to qualified enterprises is

(1) 15 days, in the case of a contract involving an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract;

(2) in the case of a contract involving an expenditure equal to or greater than the threshold set in paragraph 1,

(a) 25 days, if the tenders may be submitted on the electronic tendering system; and

(b) 30 days, in other cases.

**5** The minimum time period for the receipt of tenders when awarding a services contract according to an open procedure other than a procedure by means of a request for a quotation addressed to qualified enterprises is

(1) 15 days, when

(a) it is a contract involving an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract; or

(b) it is a contract other than a services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement; and

(2) in the case of a services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement involving an expenditure equal to or greater than the threshold set in subparagraph *a* of paragraph 1,

(a) 25 days, if the tenders may be submitted on the electronic tendering system; and

(b) 30 days, in other cases.

**6** The minimum time period for the receipt of tenders when awarding a contract according to a procedure by written invitation other than a procedure by means of a request for a quotation addressed to qualified enterprises is 8 days.

**7** The minimum time period for the receipt of tenders with a view to awarding a contract by means of a request for a quotation addressed to qualified enterprises is 10 days, unless a shorter time period is provided for in the document referred to in the first paragraph of section 22 of the Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01) published to establish the process for the qualification of enterprises prior to the awarding of the contract.
