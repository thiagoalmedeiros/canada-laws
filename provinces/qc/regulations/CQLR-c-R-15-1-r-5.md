*CQLR c R-15.1, r 5*

*Enabling statute: Supplemental Pension Plans Act (chapter R-15.1, s. 110.1)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The limit to the costs for producing the statement of benefits that a member and his spouse are entitled to obtain in the cases mentioned in sections 108 and 110 of the Supplemental Pension Plan Act (chapter R-15.1) and the limit to the expenses incurred for effecting a transfer of benefits between spouses are fixed as follows:

Act Pension Plan Limit (1) for the issuance of a statement of benefits defined contribution plan $150 combination plan $325 any other plan $250 (2) *(paragraph revoked)* (3) for effecting the transfer of benefits defined contribution plan $100 combination plan $200 any other plan $150

**2** (Omitted).
