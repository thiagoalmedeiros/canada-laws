# Regulation respecting participation in a tasting show or in an exhibition held to present or discover alcoholic beverages

Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/S-13,%20r.%206.01) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c S-13, r 6.01*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the Société des alcools du Québec (chapter S-13, s. 28.1, 1st and 2nd pars., s. 37, 1st par., subpar. 8.1)*

*Updated to 2021-08-05*

**1** The holder of a permit issued under the Act respecting the Société des alcools du Québec (chapter S-13) may, in accordance with section 28.1 of that Act, as made by section 110 of the Act to modernize the legal regime applicable to liquor permits and to amend various other legislative provisions with regard to alcoholic beverages (2018, chapter 20), with the authorization of the Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux, participate in a tasting show or an exhibition held, in whole or in part, to present or discover alcoholic beverages, and sell for consumption on the premises, at the tasting show or exhibition, the alcoholic beverages the permit holder makes and holds in inventory.

**2** (Omitted).
