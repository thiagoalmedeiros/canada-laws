*CQLR c A-29, r 8*

*Enabling statute: Health Insurance Act (chapter A-29, s. 72.1)*

*Updated to 2026-07-01*

**1** The tariff applicable to hearing devices and insured services appears as Schedule I to this Regulation, with respect to the hearing devices and services set out therein.

That tariff is made as a complement to the Regulation respecting hearing devices and insured services (chapter A-29, r. 2), made by the Government under subparagraph *h*.2 of the first paragraph of section 69 of the Health Insurance Act (chapter A-29).

**2** (Omitted).
