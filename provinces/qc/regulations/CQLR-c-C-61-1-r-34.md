*CQLR c C-61.1, r 34*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the conservation and development of wildlife (chapter C-61.1, s. 84.1)*

*Updated to 2015-09-24*

**1** The territory of Québec is divided into fishing and hunting areas, delimited as described in Schedules I to XXIX.

However, the territory delimited as described in Schedule XXV constitutes only a fishing area.

The parts of Areas 8, 10 and 13 that are included in Area 25 are excluded from their respective area in the case of fishing.

**2** (Omitted).

**3** (Omitted).
