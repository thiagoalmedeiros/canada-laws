*CQLR c M-9, r 23*

*Enabling statute: Medical Act (chapter M-9, s. 20. 1st par., subpar. f)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** (Revoked).

**2** A physician wishing to practise acupuncture shall prove to the credentials committee of the Collège des médecins du Québec that he has completed a training program in acupuncture that is in accordance with the present requirements of the profession and comprises a minimum of 300 hours of theoretical and practical training. For this purpose, the credentials committee shall include at least 1 physician-acupuncturist.

**3** (Omitted).
