# Regulation respecting regulatory measures for activities under the responsibility of municipalities carried out in bodies of water and on flood protection works

## I — GENERAL

### I — SCOPE

**1** This Regulation provides, primarily as a complement to the rules set out in other Acts and regulations and in municipal by-laws, rules that apply to various activities carried out in bodies of water described in section 46.0.2 of the Act and on flood protection works situated in the territory governed by the municipalities.

The foremost purpose of the rules is to provide greater protection for those bodies of water and to reduce the vulnerability of persons and property exposed to flooding or watercourse channel migration.

This Regulation applies to reserved areas and agricultural zones established under the Act respecting the preservation of agricultural land and agricultural activities (chapter P-41.1).

**2** Local municipalities are responsible for the enforcement of this Regulation, except sections 132 to 155, which are under the Minister’s responsibility.

For the purposes of this Regulation, a regional county municipality whose territory includes an unorganized territory is considered to be a local municipality with respect to that territory.

**3** This Regulation does not apply to

(1) activities carried out by a government department or public body;

(2) activities carried out pursuant to an order made under the Act or a notice of execution issued under the Act respecting certain measures enabling the enforcement of environmental and dam safety legislation (chapter M-11.6);

(3) construction and maintenance of a domestic waste water management and treatment facility to which the Regulation respecting waste water disposal systems for isolated dwellings (chapter Q-2, r. 22) applies;

(4) activities regulated by the Regulation respecting the sustainable development of forests in the domain of the State (chapter A-18.1, r. 0.01), except

(a) construction, widening and straightening of a road that is under the management of the Minister responsible for the Act respecting roads (chapter V-9) and is classified as an autoroute or a national, regional or collector route; and

(b) construction, improvement and repair of a road or a route that skirts a lake or watercourse, encroaching on its bed or riparian ecotone within the meaning of section 2 of that Regulation;

(5) activities carried out in a natural setting or an area designated under the Natural Heritage Conservation Act (chapter C-61.01), if the activities require authorization under that Act;

(6) activities carried out in a wildlife preserve to which the Act respecting the conservation and development of wildlife (chapter C-61.1) applies;

(7) activities carried out in the habitat of a threatened or vulnerable plant species identified under section 11 or 12 of the Act respecting threatened or vulnerable species (chapter E-12.01), if the activities require authorization under that Act or under the Act respecting the conservation and development of wildlife; or

(8) activities involving the use of pesticides regulated under the Pesticides Management Code (chapter P-9.3, r. 1) and under the Regulation respecting permits and certificates for the sale and use of pesticides (chapter P-9.3, r. 2).

**4** Section 118.3.3 of the Act does not apply to a municipality that regulates an activity regulated by this Regulation or that establishes boundaries for a lakeshore or riverbank that are wider than a width specified in the definition of “lakeshore” and “riverbank” in section 5.

### II — DEFINITIONS

**5** In this Regulation, unless the context indicates a different meaning,

**Act** means the Environment Quality Act (chapter Q-2); (*Loi*)

**body of water** means an area meeting the criteria set out in section 46.0.2 of the Act, characterized in particular by the permanent or temporary presence of water that may occupy a bed and may be stagnant or in movement, such as a lake or watercourse, and including their littoral zones, shores and banks, channel migration zones and flood zones; (*milieu hydrique*)

**boundary of the littoral zone** means the boundary separating the littoral zone from the lakeshore or riverbank, determined using the methods set out in Schedule I; (*limite du littoral*)

**building** means a fixed, mobile or floating structure that is not designed for ground, rail or water traffic, has a roof and is used or intended to be used to shelter, house or receive persons, animals, foodstuffs or any other thing, except a structure that is part of or attached to a dam; a structure, works or equipment adjoining a building, such as a terrace, a garage or a balcony, is considered to be part of the building; (*bâtiment*)

**channel migration zone** means an area in which the bed of the watercourse may shift as a result of various physical processes, including erosion and sedimentation, and whose boundaries are established in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act; (*zone de mobilité*)

**culvert** means a structure built under embankments that allows water to flow under a road, railway or similar infrastructure and whose length corresponds to the width of the infrastructure above; (*ponceau*)

**ditch** means a ditch along a public or private road, a common ditch or a drainage ditch, as defined in subparagraphs 2 to 4 of the first paragraph of section 103 of the Municipal Powers Act (chapter C-47.1); (*fossé*)

**flooded land** means the area flooded during the spring floods of 2017 or 2019, lying within the perimeter delimited pursuant to subparagraph 6 of the first paragraph of Schedule II and, where applicable, lying outside the boundaries of the low-velocity and high-velocity zones identified using one of the means referred to in subparagraphs 1 to 3 of the first paragraph of Schedule II; (*territoire inondé*)

**flood protection works** means a flood protection works within the meaning of section 1 of the Flood Protection Works Regulation (chapter Q-2, r. 34.2), extending over 3 m from its downstream toe and upstream toe, calculated horizontally from the works, except in the following cases:

(1) where the upstream toe of the works is situated within the 2 year flood recurrence level, in which case, on that side, it ends at the upstream toe;

(2) where the upstream toe of the works is situated less than 3 m from a littoral zone, in which case it extends to the boundary of the littoral zone; (*ouvrage de protection contre les inondations*)

**flood zone** means an area that is likely to be occupied by the water of a lake or watercourse during flood periods, whose boundaries are established in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act or, if the boundaries have not been established, are established as provided in Schedule II; (*zone inondable*)

**fording site** means a site laid out in the bed of a watercourse enabling the watercourse to be crossed; (*passage à gué*)

**forest development activity** means an activity referred to in paragraph 1 of section 4 of the Sustainable Forest Development Act (chapter A-18.1), carried out elsewhere than on land in the domain of the State and specifically intended to develop and conserve forest land; (*activité d’aménagement forestier*)

**green water management infrastructure** is an infrastructure other than a vegetated filter marsh consisting in whole or in part of vegetation and intended to reduce the flow of water running off into a drainage network or the receiving environment, and to improve water quality by means of interception, capture, storage, treatment, infiltration or evapotranspiration; (*infrastructure végétalisée de gestion des eaux*)

**high-velocity flood zone** means a part of the flood zone associated with a 20 year flood recurrence; a flood zone in which high-velocity and low-velocity zones are not identified is considered to be a high-velocity flood zone; (*zone inondable de grand courant*)

**ice jam flood zone** means an area that, because of the accumulation of ice in a section of a lake or watercourse during flood periods, may be occupied by water because of the impoundment of water upstream of the lake or watercourse and whose boundaries are established in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act or, if the boundaries have not been so established, are established as provided in Schedule II; (*zone d’inondation par embâcle de glaces*)

**invasive exotic plant species** means a plant introduced outside its natural distribution area that may constitute a threat to the environment, biodiversity, human health, the economy or society; (*espèce floristique exotique envahissante*)

**lakeshore** and **riverbank** mean the strip of land bordering a lake or watercourse and whose width is measured horizontally inland from the boundary of the littoral zone. Its width must be

(1) 10 m if the average slope is less than 30% or, if the slope is 30% or greater, it has a bank that is not higher than 5 m;

(2) 15 m if the average slope is 30% or greater and is continuous or it has a bank higher than 5 m; (*rive*)

**littoral zone** means the part of a lake or watercourse that extends from the boundary separating it from the lakeshore or riverbank towards the centre of the water body; (*littoral*)

**low-velocity flood zone** means a part of the flood zone, beyond the boundaries of the high-velocity zone, associated with a 100 year flood recurrence; flooded land is considered to be such a zone; (*zone inondable de faible courant*)

**Minister** means the Minister responsible for the administration of the Environment Quality Act; (*ministre*)

**professional** means a professional within the meaning of section 1 of the Professional Code (chapter C-26); any person authorized by a professional order to perform an activity reserved for members of the order is also considered to be a professional; (*professionnel*);

**public body** means a body to which the Government or a minister appoints the majority of the members, to which, by law, the personnel is appointed in accordance with the Public Service Act (chapter F‐3.1.1), or at least half of whose capital stock is derived from the Consolidated Revenue Fund; (*organisme public*);

**public road** means a public highway within the meaning of section 4 of the Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2); (*voie publique*)

**rut** means a track on the surface of the ground, measuring at least 4 m in length, created by the wheels or crawlers of a motorized or non-motorized machine; on organic soil, a rut is considered to be the torn plant cover and on mineral soil, a track having a depth of more than 20 cm measured from the litter surface is considered to be a rut; (*ornière*)

**sewer system** means any works used to collect, store, transport or treat wastewater, in whole or in part of domestic origin, before being discharged into the environment, with the exception of

(1) a sewer line serving a single building, connected to a sewer system, located within the property line for the building;

(2) a storm water management system to collect wastewater of domestic origin from an overflow, or treated wastewater; and

(3) equipment or a device to treat water, other than wastewater of domestic origin, that is not operated by a municipality; (*système d’égoût*)

**storm water management system** means any man-made works used to collect, store, transport or treat storm water, including a ditch, with the exception of

(1) a sewer system;

(2) a line serving a single building, connected to a storm water management system, located within the property line for the building;

(3) equipment or a device to treat water other than storm water; and

(4) a watercourse; (*système de gestion des eaux pluviales*)

**watercourse** means any mass of water running along a bed in a regular or intermittent flow, including a bed created or altered by human intervention, showing signs or traces of waterflow, including the St. Lawrence River, the estuary and the Gulf of St. Lawrence and all the seas surrounding Québec, excluding a ditch; (*cours d’eau*)

**waterworks system** means a mains, a system of mains or a facility or equipment used to treat, store or supply water intended for human consumption, with the exception of,

(1) in the case of a building connected to a waterworks system, a mains or any other equipment serving the building and that is located within the property line for the building; and

(2) in the case where more than one building is served by the system, a mains or any other equipment located within the buildings if the buildings and the system belong to the same owner; (*système d’aqueduc*)

**wetland** means an area meeting the criteria set out in section 46.0.2 of the Act, characterized in particular by hydromorphic soils or vegetation dominated by hygrophilous species, such as a pond, marsh, swamp or peatland. (*milieu humide*)

For the purposes of the definition of “watercourse” in the first paragraph, where a watercourse follows the bed of a ditch for a portion of its course, it is considered to be a watercourse for the entirety of its course.

Despite the first paragraph, the following are not considered to be a pond, a marsh, a swamp, a peatland, a lake, a watercourse or any other wetland or body of water:

(1) flood protection works;

(2) the following man-made works:

(a) an irrigation pond;

(b) a water management or treatment facility referred to in subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph of section 22 of the Act;

(c) a water retention body containing water pumped from a quarry or a sand pit, if the quarry or sand pit has not been restored;

(d) a commercial fishing pond;

(e) a pond for the production of aquatic organisms;

(f) a basin reserved for fire-fighting purposes;

(g) a basin the bottom of which was created using artificial materials and is used for recreational purposes such as bathing, games and sports;

(3) a wetland in which the vegetation is dominated by reed canary grass (*Phalaris arundinacea L.*) or the introduced sub-species of common reed (*Phragmites australis* (Cav.) Trin. ex Steud. subsp. *australis*), and the soil of which is not hydromorphic.

For the purposes of subparagraph 2 of the third paragraph,

(1) the works must be situated on land, in a flood zone or a long-term channel migration zone, excluding the littoral zone, a lakeshore and riverbank, a short-term channel migration zone and a wetland;

(2) the works must still be in use or, if not in use, must have remained unused for less than 10 years;

(3) a water environment resulting from work under a program to restore and create wetlands and bodies of water developed under the Act to affirm the collective nature of water resources and to promote better governance of water and associated environments (chapter C-6.2) or under the Regulation respecting compensation for adverse effects on wetlands and bodies of water (chapter Q-2, r. 9.1) is not considered to be man-made works; and

(4) a wetland or body of water into which storm water is discharged cannot be considered to be a water management or treatment facility.

**6** The flood zones, whose boundaries are established in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act, are grouped into the following 4 classes of flood hazard intensity that reflect in particular the likelihood of occurrence and the above-ground flood depth during flood periods:

(1) very high flood hazard zones;

(2) high flood hazard zones;

(3) moderate flood hazard zones;

(4) low flood hazard zones.

**7** The channel migration zones, whose boundaries are established in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act, are grouped into the following 2 classes of channel migration hazard intensity that reflect in particular the erosion rate and meander cutoff:

(1) short-term channel migration zones;

(2) long-term channel migration zones.

**8** Unless otherwise provided, for the purposes of this Regulation,

(1) an ice jam flood zone identified on a map referred to in subparagraph 3 of Schedule II is considered to be a very high flood hazard zone;

(2) a reference to an area or length is a reference to the cumulative area or length for the type of environment in which the activity takes place and includes, if applicable, the planned footing under a structure;

(3) an area or length calculated with respect to an environment that includes another environment must be included in the calculation of the area or length regarding that other environment;

(4) a distance is calculated horizontally

(a) from the 2 year flood recurrence level, for a lake or a watercourse, if a flood protection works is in whole or in part within the 2 year flood recurrence level;

(b) in all other cases, from the boundary of the littoral zone for a lake or a watercourse;

(5) minor soil grading involves levelling soil to create a uniform surface, free from depressions and irregularities, including the replacement of a thin layer of unconsolidated deposits;

(6) management of vegetation consists in cutting, pruning, removing, planting and seeding vegetation but excludes cultivation of non-aquatic plants and mushrooms, and forest development activities;

(7) construction of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment consists in its siting, replacement, reconstruction, substantial modification and relocation;

(8) reconstruction or replacement consists in demolition, dismantling or removal of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment in order to build or put in place, in whole or in part, another infrastructure, works, building or equipment of a similar nature, provided the construction or replacement work began within not more than 3 years after the demolition, dismantling or removal, and the encroachment area of the infrastructure, works, building or equipment is equal to or less than the initial encroachment area; in the case of a building, any construction, refurbishment or repair work involving 50% or more of the structural components of the existing building are considered to be reconstruction work, provided the conditions above are met and the work is carried out within not more than 5 years;

(9) a substantial modification consists in a change to the structural or functional characteristics, or a reduction or increase in the size of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment;

(10) the relocation of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment consists in moving or installing it at a site different from the site on which it was previously located, including where the relocation is partial;

(11) the maintenance of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment consists in inspection, refurbishment and repair not involving a change to its structural or functional characteristics; it is carried out in the immediate vicinity of the infrastructure, works, building or equipment;

(12) dismantling or demolition of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment includes waste management and site restoration; in the case of a building, any work involving the removal of more than 50% of the structural components of the building is considered to be demolition; the removal of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment in order to relocate it is considered to be dismantling or demolition;

(13) an adaptation measure implemented with regard to an infrastructure, works, building or equipment consists in an intervention to improve flood resilience and to reduce its vulnerability and that of persons and other property; its primary purpose is to minimize or forestall submersion, prevent water from entering a building or allow water to enter in a controlled manner;

(14) a stabilization works is a works to increase the mechanical resistance of the soil or an infrastructure so as to protect against erosion and landslides;

(15) a crossing works for a lake or a watercourse includes bridges, culverts, walkways and fording sites;

(16) a road is an infrastructure permitting travel and whose right of way may include a roadway, shoulders, ditches and turning circles, but excludes stabilization works, a railway, bridge, culvert or any other works enabling access to or the crossing of a lake or watercourse; subject to those exclusions, the following are considered to be a road:

(a) a trail that is not laid out as part of a forest development activity and any other works permitting travel, such as cycle paths;

(b) an infrastructure or works permitting travel to access an infrastructure, works, non-residential building, equipment or site, such as a vehicular entrance or pedestrian walkway, including a parking area;

(17) an infrastructure, works, building or equipment is considered to be temporary if it is put in place for a maximum period of 3 years;

(18) every building other than a residential building or a building accessory to a residential building is considered to be a non-residential building;

(19) a building is considered to be a residential building if it includes at least one part used or intended to be used as a main or secondary private residence by a natural person, including when the residence is occasionally offered for rent to tourists;

(20) a works or a building accessory to a residential building consists in any works, building, equipment or structure detached from the building and situated on the same grounds, excluding works enabling access to or the crossing of a lake or a watercourse, residential accesses, electrical wires, waste water discharge, collection or disposal systems, water withdrawal facilities, mains, as well as anchored, open pile or wheeled structures that float on or extend into the water such as a quay or a boat shelter;

(21) a residential access consists in any infrastructure or works giving access to a residential building or its accessory works and buildings, such as a vehicular entrance or pedestrian walkway, including a parking area;

(22) extension of a building consists in side extensions to the building and any extension above and below ground, with or without further encroachment on the ground;

(23) the following are considered to be land:

(a) a unit of assessment within the meaning of section 34 of the Act respecting municipal taxation (chapter F-2.1), except for an immovable that is the subject of a declaration of co-ownership pursuant to article 1052 of the Civil Code, in which case the land is the land on which are situated all the components that are part of the divided co-ownership;

(b) land described in a lease granted under the Act respecting the lands in the domain of the State (chapter T-8.1);

(24) the term **motorized vehicle** does not include a boat.

### III — MUNICIPAL PERMIT

**9** This Regulation provides among other things that a permit must be obtained from a local municipality before certain activities may be carried out in a body of water or on a flood protection works situated in the territory of the municipality.

A provision in this Regulation creating such a requirement does not apply to activities carried out by a municipality.

**10** A local municipality may revoke a permit it has issued under this Regulation for the reason that its holder does not comply with a provision of the permit or this Regulation, provided the authorized activity has not been fully completed. The municipality nonetheless remains responsible for applying the penalties applicable under Division II of Chapter VI for non-compliance with the provision.

**11** The holder of a permit issued under this Regulation is required to ensure that the permit activity carried out complies with all other laws, regulations or by-laws, in particular by obtaining all required authorizations.

**12** The holder of a permit issued under this Regulation must begin the permit activity within the time set in the permit or, if the permit does not set a time, within 2 years after the issue of the permit. That failing, the permit is automatically cancelled.

**13** If the construction of a residential building, its accessory works and buildings or a residential access requires a municipal permit under this Regulation and is taking place in a wetland, the relevant local municipality may authorize the activity only if

(1) authorization from the Minister has been obtained for the construction pursuant to subparagraph 4 of the first paragraph of section 22 of the Act;

(2) the construction is covered by a declaration of compliance in accordance with the Regulation respecting the regulatory scheme applying to activities on the basis of their environmental impact (chapter Q-2, r. 17.1); or

(3) the construction is exempted from authorization under the Regulation respecting the regulatory scheme applying to activities on the basis of their environmental impact.

**14** A permit application made under this Regulation must contain the following information and documents in addition to the requirements of the local municipality:

(1) the name and contact information of the person wishing to carry out the activity and that of the person’s representative, if any;

(2) the cadastral designation of the lots on which the activity will be carried out or, if there is no cadastral designation, the most precise identification of the place where the activity will be carried out;

(3) the location of the planned activity, including the boundaries of the wetlands and bodies of water on the activity site, the areas of land affected by the activity as well as the precise location on the flood protection works and any related encroachments;

(4) identification of any flood protection works involved;

(5) a detailed description of the planned activity;

(6) if the activity consists in construction of a building, its type, whether residential or non-residential and, if the building is of both types, a description showing how each type is divided;

(7) a declaration by the person wishing to carry out the activity or the person’s representative stating that

(a) the activity is not covered by section 3;

(b) the conditions applying to the activity under this Regulation will be complied with while the work is being carried out;

(8) an attestation by the person wishing to carry out the activity or the person’s representative stating that all the information and documents provided are accurate and complete.

If the work consists in demolition or dismantling of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment, the boundaries of the wetlands and bodies of water required under subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph are not required to support the application.

If the work is part of a forest development activity, the boundaries of the bodies of water required under subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph are not required to support the application.

## II — BODY OF WATER

### I — GENERAL

**15** This Chapter applies to activities carried out in a body of water.

The rules prescribed apply according to the type of body of water and may apply to an environment present within a different type of environment. As a general rule and unless otherwise provided,

(1) a rule applicable to a littoral zone, lakeshore or riverbank also applies to any other of body of water present;

(2) a rule applicable to a flood zone does not apply to other bodies of water present;

(3) a rule applicable to a channel migration zone applies to a flood zone, but does not apply to any littoral zone, lakeshore or riverbank present.

### II — STANDARDS APPLICABLE TO ANY ACTIVITIES CARRIED OUT IN A BODY OF WATER

#### 1 — General

**16** This Division applies to activities carried out in a body of water, irrespective of whether they require a municipal permit pursuant to Division III.

#### 2 — Management of vegetation

**17** Cutting, pruning and removing vegetation in a littoral zone or on a lakeshore or riverbank are prohibited, except in the following cases:

(1) they are required for work authorized under Division III and meet the conditions applicable under Division IV;

(2) they are required for work other than the work referred to in subparagraph 1;

(3) they are part of the maintenance of an infrastructure, works, residential building or non-residential building referred to in section 53, or the maintenance of equipment and meet the conditions set out in section 31.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, the cutting, pruning and removing of vegetation required in a body of water for the carrying out of an activity requiring a permit under this Regulation cannot be undertaken before the permit is issued.

**18** Landscaping work associated with a residential building in a body of water must meet the following conditions:

(1) the work must involve only seeding and planting of vegetation;

(2) the work must be carried out outside the littoral zone;

(3) the work must be carried out outside a wetland, except if it is associated with a building referred to in subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph of section 345 of the Regulation respecting the regulatory scheme applying to activities on the basis of their environmental impact (chapter Q-2, r. 17.1);

(4) if the work is carried out on a lakeshore or riverbank, or in a short-term channel migration zone, it must be carried out

(a) without tree clearing;

(b) over an area that does not exceed 20 m^2;

(c) without backfilling or excavation other than minor ground leveling; and

(d) using native species adapted to the environment;

(5) if the work is carried out in a flood zone, it must not involve backfilling or excavation other than minor ground leveling.

**19** Seeding and planting of invasive exotic plant species is prohibited.

#### 3 — Operation of motorized vehicles

**20** Races, rallies and other motorized vehicle competitions are prohibited in a littoral zone, on a lakeshore or riverbank and in a short-term channel migration zone.

**21** Motorized vehicles may be operated in a littoral zone, on a lakeshore or riverbank and in a short-term channel migration zone only in the following cases:

(1) off-road vehicles if there is ice cover or if the load-bearing capacity of the ground permits it, in such manner as to not create ruts;

(2) on a road or crossing works;

(3) the operation is required for a lawful hunting, fishing or trapping activity;

(4) the operation is required to access a property and there is no road or crossing works to enable access;

(5) the operation is required to carry out work that is authorized by this Regulation and complies with the conditions in sections 67 and 69;

(6) the operation is required to carry out work other than the work referred to in paragraph 5.

#### 4 — Infrastructures, works and equipment

**22** The siting in a body of water of an underground parking garage associated with a residential building is prohibited.

**23** The construction of a backfill embankment to floodproof a residential building and its accessory works and buildings or a non-residential building is prohibited.

Despite the first paragraph, construction of a backfill embankment in a flood zone to raise the ground level under a building is permitted on the following conditions:

(1) the work is warranted to floodproof a residential building that is situated in a flood zone and was present on the land on 1 March 2026 or, if the boundaries of a new flood zone are established pursuant to sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act on land where no flood zones had previously been established, on the date of coming into force of the establishment of the boundaries of the new flood zone;

(2) the adaptation measures set out in section 100 cannot be applied to floodproof a residential building;

(3) the construction of a backfill embankment is deemed to be an appropriate measure by a professional.

#### 5 — Buildings and accessory works and buildings

**24** The following is prohibited in a littoral zone:

(1) siting and relocation of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings;

(2) reconstruction of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings, except reconstruction required because of damage caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration;

(3) conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a non-residential building to a residential building;

(4) addition of a dwelling in a building situated in such a zone.

**25** The following is prohibited on a lakeshore or riverbank:

(1) siting of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings;

(2) reconstruction of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings, except reconstruction required because of damage that was not caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration;

(3) conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a non-residential building to a residential building;

(4) addition of a dwelling in a building situated in such an environment.

**26** Siting, reconstruction and relocation of a residential building on backfilled land in a flood zone, including any body of water present, without first obtaining the necessary authorizations are prohibited.

**27** The following is prohibited in a very high flood hazard zone:

(1) siting of a residential building and its accessory buildings;

(2) reconstruction of a residential building and its accessory buildings, except reconstruction required because of damage that was not caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration;

(3) conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a non-residential building to a residential building;

(4) addition of a dwelling in a building situated in such a zone.

The first paragraph applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a very high flood hazard zone.

**28** The following is prohibited in a high or moderate flood hazard zone:

(1) siting of a residential building;

(2) conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a non-residential building to a residential building;

(3) addition of a dwelling in a building situated in such a zone.

The first paragraph applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a high or moderate flood hazard zone.

**29** The following is prohibited in a high-velocity flood zone:

(1) siting of a residential building;

(2) reconstruction of a residential building, except reconstruction required because of damage that was not caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration;

(3) conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a non-residential building to a residential building;

(4) addition of a dwelling in a building situated in such a zone.

The first paragraph applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a high-velocity flood zone.

**30** The following is prohibited in a short-term channel migration zone:

(1) siting of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings;

(2) reconstruction of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings, except reconstruction required because of damage that was not caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration;

(3) conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a non-residential building to a residential building;

(4) addition of a dwelling in a building situated in such a zone.

**31** Maintenance of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment whose construction requires a municipal permit under Division III of this Chapter and maintenance of a culvert, the total opening of which does not exceed 1.2 m and whose construction complies with the conditions in paragraphs 2 to 4 of section 44, if carried out in a body of water, must comply with the following conditions:

(1) excavation and backfill work must be limited to what is necessary to maintain the infrastructure, works, building or equipment in good condition allowing for its use in an optimal way according to the use for which it was designed;

(2) the work must be carried out with no waterweed cutting;

(3) the work cannot involve construction of a temporary works requiring excavation or backfill work in the littoral zone or, if such work is involved, the construction is covered by a declaration of compliance in accordance with section 336 of the Regulation respecting the regulatory scheme applying to activities on the basis of their environmental impact (chapter Q-2, r. 17.1);

(4) the management of vegetation, other than the vegetation referred to in paragraph 5, must take place only in the immediate vicinity of the infrastructure, works, building or equipment;

(5) if the management of vegetation consists only in cutting, pruning and removal of the vegetation in a littoral zone or on a lakeshore or riverbank, those activities must comply with the following conditions:

(a) for a residential building, they must be carried out less than 3 m from the building;

(b) in all other cases, they must be carried out less than 1.5 m from the infrastructure, works, non-residential building, building accessory to a residential building or equipment;

(6) for a culvert, the work must be carried out at a distance of not more than 9 m upstream and downstream from the culvert;

(7) the conditions in sections 60 to 64, 66 to 72, 78 and 79 of this Regulation.

**32** The demolition or dismantling of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment whose construction requires a municipal permit under Division III of this Chapter and maintenance of a culvert, the total opening of which does not exceed 1.2 m and whose construction complies with the conditions in paragraphs 2 to 4 of section 44, if carried out in a body of water, must comply with the conditions in sections 60 to 64, 66 to 72 and 78.

**33** Except for enclosures restricting access to a swimming pool, the erection of a fence associated with a residential building in an ice jam flood zone, including any body of water present, is prohibited.

**34** The construction of a culvert, in a littoral zone, the total opening of which does not exceed 1.2 m and whose construction complies with the conditions in paragraphs 2 to 6 of section 44, must also comply with the conditions in sections 60 to 64, 66 to 69, 74, 75 and 78.

**35** The erection of a low wall, in a body of water, to flood-proof a residential building, its accessory works and buildings or a non-residential building that complies with the conditions in subparagraphs *a* and *b* of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph of section 53 is prohibited.

For the purposes of this section, a window well is not considered to be a low wall.

**36** Despite any provision contrary to this Division, work relating to improving accessibility to the building for mobility impaired persons and work relating to fire safety measures is permitted.

**37** Despite any contrary provision, if a residential building sustains damage caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration in a littoral zone, on a lakeshore or riverbank, in a very high flood hazard zone, a high-velocity flood zone or a short-term channel migration zone, the following rules apply:

(1) reconstruction of the residential building is prohibited if the value of the damage represents less than 50% of the new build cost, excluding costs relating to accessory works and buildings and siting improvements;

(2) if the value of the damage represents 50% or more of the new build cost, excluding costs relating to accessory works and buildings and siting improvements, maintenance work is considered to be reconstruction work.

The first paragraph also applies to a long-term channel migration zone present in a very high flood hazard zone or a high-velocity flood zone.

**38** For the purposes of section 37, the new build cost is established as provided in Part 3E of the Manuel d’évaluation foncière du Québec and is adjusted on 1 July of the year preceding the year in which the building was affected by flooding, submersion or watercourse channel migration.

### III — MUNICIPAL PERMIT

#### 1 — Permit requirement

**39** No person may carry out, in a body of water, an activity to which this Division applies without first obtaining a permit from the relevant local municipality.

Such a permit is issued if the conditions specific to each activity and those applicable under Division IV of this Chapter are complied with.

No permit may be issued if the activity is prohibited under Division II of this Chapter.

**40** Management of vegetation and minor soil grading required to carry out another activity requiring a municipal permit under this Division are considered to be part of that activity for the purposes of this Division.

**41** Creation of visual openings in a littoral zone or on a lakeshore or riverbank requires a municipal permit.

**42** Construction of a road in a body of water requires a municipal permit and must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the road surface must not be impervious;

(2) the total cumulative width of the roadway and shoulders must not exceed 6.5 m;

(3) the width of the right of way for the road must not exceed

(a) 20 m in the case of a temporary road; and

(b) 10 m in other cases;

(4) for the laying out, extension or widening of a road, other than a temporary road required to carry out another activity, in a littoral zone, on a lakeshore or riverbank or in a short-term channel migration zone,

(a) the road must have a water crossing works; and

(b) the sole purpose of the road must be to cross the body of water.

The conditions in the first paragraph do not apply to an infrastructure or a works providing access to an infrastructure, works, non-residential building, equipment or an existing site if such an infrastructure or works is required to provide accessibility for a mobility impaired person.

The conditions in subparagraphs 1 and 2 and subparagraph *b* of subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph do not apply to a road situated in a littoral zone or on a lakeshore or riverbank, except any flood zone present, if the work for the road includes work relating to a bridge or a culvert situated in those environments and is eligible for or exempted from a declaration of compliance under this Chapter.

If the construction of a road is incidental to a forest development activity,

(1) the condition in subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph does not apply to work carried out on a lakeshore or riverbank, in a channel migration zone or in a flood zone; and

(2) the conditions in subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph do not apply, but if the right of way of the road is situated on a lakeshore or riverbank, it cannot exceed 15 m in width.

This section does not apply to any wetland present in a flood zone or a long-term channel migration zone.

**43** Construction of a residential access in a body of water requires a municipal permit.

**44** Construction of a culvert or a stabilization works associated with a culvert in a littoral zone or on a lakeshore or riverbank requires a municipal permit and must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the total opening of the culvert must be greater than 1.2 m but not exceed 4.5 m;

(2) the culvert must not have more than 2 pipes;

(3) the culvert must be covered by fill not more than 3 m thick;

(4) work involving a watercourse must not result in alteration of the natural watercourse alignment;

(5) if the work is carried out in a littoral zone or on a lakeshore or riverbank, the work other than work relating to the stabilization works must cover a distance of not more than 9 m upstream and downstream of the culvert;

(6) the stabilization works must not exceed a length equal to twice the width of the culvert, upstream and downstream of the culvert.

**45** Construction of a stabilization works, other than a retaining wall or a works associated with a bridge or a culvert, in a body of water requires a municipal permit and must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the work must not be carried out in a channel migration zone, including any wetland or body of water present in the zone;

(2) if phytotechnology is used, the stabilization works must not exceed a length of 130 m;

(3) if inert materials are used,

(a) in the case of work involving a lake, the stabilization works must not exceed a length of 30 m; and

(b) in the case of work involving a watercourse, the stabilization works must not exceed a length of 30 m or 5 times the width of the watercourse, whichever is more restrictive;

(4) if phytotechnology and inert materials are used for the same works, the stabilization works must not exceed a total length of 130 m or the lengths provided for in subparagraph 3 for inert materials.

For the purposes of the first paragraph, if the work is intended to extend or join stabilization works, the extension or junction must not result in an extension of the total length of the works beyond the lengths determined in that paragraph. Stabilization works situated less than 2 m from each other are considered to be joined.

This section does not apply to any wetland present in a flood zone.

**46** Construction of a drainage system, waterworks system, sewer system or storm water management system in a body of water requires a municipal permit and must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the work must be on the underground components of the systems or the following components:

(a) a ditch;

(b) a green water management infrastructure connected to one of the systems;

(c) a fire hydrant;

(d) an outflow;

(2) work carried out in the littoral zone must be for the sole purpose of discharging water into the area;

(3) work carried out on a lakeshore or riverbank or in a short-term channel migration zone must be for the sole purpose of crossing the area or discharging water into the area;

(4) if the system has a pipe for discharging water into a lake or a watercourse, the invert of the outlet pipe must be at a height of at least 30 cm calculated from the deepest part of the bed of the lake or watercourse.

Work referred to in the first paragraph carried out in connection with cultivating non-aquatic plants and mushrooms does not require a municipal permit.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, a green water management infrastructure is an infrastructure other than a vegetated filter marsh consisting in whole or in part of vegetation and intended to reduce the flow of water running off into a drainage network or the receiving environment, and to improve water quality by means of interception, capture, storage, treatment, infiltration or evapotranspiration.

For the purposes of this section, a reference to a system does not include the treatment facility.

This section does not apply to any wetland present in a flood zone or a long-term channel migration zone.

**47** The laying out of an access to a lake or a watercourse in a body of water requires a municipal permit.

**48** Construction of a structure, other than a building, that is anchored, on piles or wheels and floats on or extends into water, such as a quay or boat shelter, requires a municipal permit if the total encroachment area of the structures in a littoral zone and on a lakeshore or riverbank, including any structures already present in such environments on the land, does not exceed 30 m^2, excluding the anchor points.

**49** The laying out, in a body of water, of a fording site not exceeding a width of 10 m and not connected to a trail laid out as part of a forest development activity, requires a municipal permit.

**50** Construction of a structure enabling the crossing of a watercourse or providing access to an infrastructure, works, building or equipment situated in the littoral zone requires a municipal permit and must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the construction must be carried out with no support in the littoral zone;

(2) the structure must not exceed 5 m in width.

**51** Construction of a temporary bridge having a right of way not exceeding 10 m on a lakeshore or riverbank and not connected to a trail laid out as part of a forest development activity, requires a municipal permit.

**52** Construction and demolition of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings in a body of water requires a municipal permit.

**53** The following activities involving a non-residential building require a municipal permit:

(1) in a flood zone or a long-term channel migration zone, the siting, relocation or substantial modification creating further encroachment in the zone, on the following conditions:

(a) they do not involve excavation, including for foundations or to bury equipment, mains or wires;

(b) the surface area of the building on the same land does not exceed 40 m^2;

(2) in a body of water, the substantial modification if it results in further encroachment in such an environment;

(3) on a lakeshore or riverbank, in a flood zone or a channel migration zone, the reconstruction of a building on the following conditions:

(a) it does not involve excavation, including for foundations or to bury equipment, mains or wires;

(b) the surface area of the building does not exceed

(i) 40 m^2 in a flood zone or a long-term channel migration zone; or

(ii) 5 m^2 on a lakeshore or riverbank or in a short-term channel migration zone;

(4) work to improve accessibility to the building for mobility impaired persons and work relating to fire safety measures.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, if the work involves siting, the surface area of the building referred to in subparagraph *b* includes the surface area of existing buildings on the land in that zone.

**54** Work to convert a non-residential building or a building accessory to a residential building to a residential building requires a municipal permit if the building is situated

(1) in a long-term channel migration zone, excluding any very high, high or moderate flood hazard zone present;

(2) in a low flood hazard zone; or

(3) in a low-velocity flood zone.

**55** Construction in a littoral zone, on a lakeshore or riverbank or in a short-term channel migration zone of mains or any other equipment serving a residential building or its accessory buildings, connected to a waterworks system, sewer system or storm water management system and situated within the property line of the residential building, requires a municipal permit.

**56** Construction of a backfill embankment to raise the ground level under a residential building situated in a flood zone requires a municipal permit.

**57** Any activity that may be carried out in connection with a management plan referred to in Chapter IV, implemented through by-law made pursuant to section 79.1 of the Act respecting land use planning and development (chapter A-19.1) and approved by the Minister of Municipal Affaires, Regions and Land Occupancy pursuant to section 79.17 of that Act, requires a municipal permit.

#### 2 — Content of an application

**58** In addition to the general content prescribed by section 14, a permit application under this Chapter must contain all information or documents required by the local municipality as well as the following information and documents:

(1) if the application is for construction of a residential building or its accessory works and buildings in a body of water also situated in a flood zone or a long-term channel migration zone and

(a) the work requires authorization from the Minister pursuant to subparagraph 4 of the first paragraph of section 22 of the Act, or is eligible for a declaration of conformity under the Regulation respecting the regulatory scheme applying to activities on the basis of their environmental impact (chapter Q-2, r. 17.1), the authorization issued by the Minister pursuant to the Act or the declaration of compliance filed in accordance with that Regulation, as applicable;

(b) the work is exempted from ministerial authorization pursuant to the Act, in accordance with the Regulation respecting the regulatory scheme applying to activities on the basis of their environmental impact, a declaration by the applicant stating that the work is exempted from ministerial authorization pursuant to the Act, citing the provision of the Regulation respecting the regulatory scheme applying to activities on the basis of their environmental impact applicable to the applicant’s situation;

(2) if the application is for construction, in a flood zone, of a residential building whose structure or any part of the structure is situated below the protection objective applicable under Schedule III, an opinion signed by a professional showing that on completion of the work, the building will be able to resist a 350 year flood;

(3) if the application is for construction, in a flood zone, of a backfill embankment to raise the ground under a residential building, an opinion signed by a professional attesting that a backfill is an appropriate adaptation measure to replace the measures provided for in section 100, which cannot be applied, and the conditions in the second paragraph of section 23 and in section 80 will be complied with;

(4) if the application is for relocation of a residential building in an ice jam flood zone, an opinion signed by a person qualified in the field attesting that the relocation does not increase exposure to ice;

(5) if the application is for reconstruction because of flood damage sustained, or for relocation or substantial modification of a building referred to in subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph of section 99, in a flood zone or a channel migration zone,

(a) an opinion signed by a professional showing that the work ensures the safety of persons and property, in particular by the implementation of adaptation measures; and

(b) if the adaptation measures set out in section 100 affect the heritage interest of the immovable, an opinion signed by a professional showing that the measures affect that interest and that the measures proposed by the applicant offer equivalent protection for persons and property;

(6) if the application is for a building that has sustained damage caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration, an opinion signed by a professional indicating the value of the damage;

(7) if the application is for underground siting, reconstruction or extension of a residential building in a channel migration zone, an opinion signed by a person qualified in the field,

(a) describing the hydrogeomorphologic characteristics of the sector; and

(b) characterizing the vulnerability of persons and property to a watercourse channel migration;

(8) if the application is for construction of embankment stabilization works, including rockfill with inert materials, an opinion signed by a person qualified in the field showing that the proposed stabilization method is the technique most likely to reflect the natural character of the site, while ensuring the safety of persons and property;

(9) if the application is for construction of a 2-pipe culvert, an opinion signed by a person qualified in the field showing that the use of a 2-pipe culvert is necessary for technical reasons taking into account the characteristics of the site, in particular the topography, the width of the watercourse and the amount of backfill needed;

(10) if the application is for construction of a residential access that does not respect the original topography of the land, an opinion, signed by a professional, attesting that the access must be raised to the requested height to allow for the evacuation of the occupants of the residential building and to ensure the safety of persons, and that the conditions in the second paragraph of section 82 will be complied with.

### IV — CONDITIONS APPLICABLE TO THE CARRYING OUT OF ACTIVITIES REQUIRING A MUNICIPAL PERMIT

#### 1 — General

**59** Unless otherwise provided, this Division applies only to activities requiring a municipal permit under Division III of this Chapter.

**60** Work carried out in a body of water must comply with the following conditions:

(1) materials appropriate to the site must be used;

(2) measures to control erosion, sediments and suspended matter must be put in place.

#### 2 — Site restoration and management of vegetation

**61** On completion of an activity requiring a municipal permit in a body of water, the following measures must be applied:

(1) unless otherwise provided, all temporary works must be dismantled and removed from the site;

(2) all debris and other residual materials must be removed, unless consisting of wood waste present outside the littoral zone;

(3) embankments must be stable and protected against erosion, preferably by means of the technique most conducive to maintaining the natural character of the site;

(4) the site must be restored within the year following completion of the activity, including, as applicable,

(a) soil restoration; and

(b) in the dewatered zone, revegetation of the areas affected if they have been stripped of vegetation or soil, except

(i) after sample taking, conducting surveys, making technical surveys, carrying out archaeological excavations or taking measurements, as regards the tree stratum; or

(ii) if the revegetation jeopardizes the stability or safety of works, as regards the tree and shrub stratum;

(5) all stabilization works must be vegetated, except in areas where it is impossible for vegetation to grow or vegetation jeopardizes the stability or safety of the works.

For the purposes of subparagraphs 1 and 2 of the first paragraph, works and materials in the ground such as pilings or anchors may be left in place, except the foundations of a building situated on a lakeshore or riverbank or in a short-term channel migration zone.

**62** If soil restoration is required under section 61, it must comply with the following conditions:

(1) it must be carried out with the excavated materials or, if that is not possible, with substitute materials of the same nature as the original substrate;

(2) the organic part of the soil must be returned to the surface of the soil profile;

(3) the original drainage conditions must be restored or equivalent drainage conditions put in place;

(4) the restoration must preserve to the extent possible the original topography of the site.

**63** If revegetation is required under section 61, it must comply with the following conditions:

(1) it must be carried out using native species that belong to the same strata as those affected and are adapted to the environment;

(2) the survival rate of the vegetation or cover must be 80% in the year following revegetation; that failing, dead vegetation must be replaced.

**64** Management of vegetation in a littoral zone, on a lakeshore or riverbank or in a short-term channel migration zone must be undertaken without stump removal.

The first paragraph does not apply to activities whose nature necessarily entails stump removal, such as road construction or maintenance, burial or anchoring of certain pieces of equipment, or construction of a building.

**65** A visual opening may be created only by the pruning of vegetation present in the shrub stratum and it may not exceed a width of 5 m or 10% of the total length of the lakeshore or riverbank situated on the land concerned, including existing visual openings on the land.

#### 3 — Excavation and backfilling

**66** Excavation and backfilling in a body of water must be limited to what is necessary to carry out work such as road construction or maintenance, burial or anchoring of certain equipment, or construction of a building.

Such excavation and backfilling may create temporary encroachments in bodies of water if carried out in the footing of the infrastructure, works, building or equipment, or in the immediate work zone.

At the end of any activity, spoil and excess materials must be disposed of outside the bodies of water and managed so as to forestall sediment movement toward the bodies of water.

#### 4 — Operation and use of vehicles and machinery

**67** The operation of vehicles and machinery in a body of water is permitted on the following conditions:

(1) in the littoral zone, vehicles or machinery may be operated only in a dewatered or drained area of the zone or if there is ice cover;

(2) if ruts are created, the area must be restored to its original condition, or a condition close thereto, in particular by seeding stripped soil, decompacting compacted soil and levelling depressions.

Subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph does not apply if the operation is necessary for

(1) construction of temporary works;

(2) making preliminary technical surveys;

(3) taking samples; or

(4) taking measurements.

**68** Refuelling and maintenance of vehicles or machinery in a body of water must comply with the following conditions:

(1) in a littoral zone, the work must be carried out only in a dewatered or drained area of the zone or if there is ice cover;

(2) the vehicle or machinery must be equipped with a system for collecting fluid leakage and spillage, or with a spillage prevention device.

**69** Where work requires a vehicle or machinery to be operated across a watercourse and there is no fording site or crossing works, the operation of the vehicle or machinery in the watercourse must comply with the following conditions:

(1) it is limited to only one back-and-forth crossing;

(2) the crossing point minimizes the impacts on the watercourse.

#### 5 — Dewatering and narrowing of the littoral zone of a watercourse

**70** The dewatering or temporary narrowing of the littoral zone of a watercourse may not be carried out in the same part of the watercourse more than twice in a 12-month period.

Dewatering or narrowing work may in no case last for more than 30 consecutive days and, in addition to the condition in the first paragraph, must meet the following conditions:

(1) in the case of work lasting for not more than 10 days, the dewatering or narrowing may be total if the watercourse is less than 5 m in width and the water is redirected in its entirety to the watercourse downstream of the work;

(2) in other cases, the dewatering or narrowing must not exceed one-third of the width of the watercourse.

**71** Dewatering or narrowing work in the littoral zone of a watercourse must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the equipment and materials used must make it possible to limit the discharge of suspended matter into the littoral zone;

(2) if the pumped water contains suspended matter visible to the naked eye, it must be discharged into an area of vegetation located more than 30 m from the littoral zone, such as a field of grasses or forest litter, provided the point of discharge is regularly shifted to a new location.

**72** All works used for dewatering or narrowing the littoral zone of a watercourse must be dismantled first by removing the materials situated inside the dewatered area and then advancing from the area downstream of the works toward the upstream area.

#### 6 — Infrastructures, works and equipment

**73** The laying out, in a body of water, of an access to a lake or a watercourse must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the work must be carried out using one or more of the following means:

(a) the management of vegetation not more than 5 m wide;

(b) the construction of stairs or a walkway on piles not more than 2 m wide;

(c) the construction of a pathway not more than 2 m wide using non-granular material such as slabs or stones;

(2) if there is already an access to the lake or watercourse on the land, the work must not result in the addition of another access to the same lake or watercourse on the same land;

(3) the access must be created so as to forestall sediment movement towards the lake or watercourse;

(4) the work must not involve fill or excavation, except minor soil grading.

**74** Installation of a culvert in a body of water must not result in the raising or lowering of the bed level of a lake or a watercourse compared to its initial state.

**75** Construction of permanent works or installation of permanent equipment in the littoral zone of a watercourse must not cause the watercourse to be widened beyond the boundary of the littoral zone, unless the purpose of the construction or installation is to restore the natural width of the watercourse or to reduce the embankment slope.

The littoral zone of a watercourse may not be permanently narrowed by more than 20% of its width or, as applicable, by a width greater than the narrowing resulting from works or equipment present in the watercourse at that location, if the narrowing is already greater than 20% of the watercourse width.

This section does not apply to culvert lining and sleeving.

**76** A waterworks system or sewer system may be sited or extended in a flood zone only in the following cases:

(1) the system is intended to serve an infrastructure or a building that

(a) was constructed in the flood zone before 23 June 2021; or

(b) is not prohibited in a flood zone where the construction work is to take place;

(2) the system is intended to serve an infrastructure, a building or a sector outside a flood zone and it is not possible to avoid crossing a flood zone to connect it;

(3) for work carried out in the right of way of a public road.

The first paragraph also applies in a short-term channel migration zone, with the necessary modifications.

This section does not apply to any long-term channel migration zone present in a flood zone.

**77** Construction in a littoral zone, on a lakeshore or riverbank or in a short-term channel migration zone of mains or any other equipment serving a residential building and its accessory buildings, connected to a waterworks system, sewer system or storm water management system and situated within the property line of the building may take place only if either of the following conditions is met:

(1) the work is carried out only if doing so is not possible elsewhere on the land without encroaching on one of those areas; or

(2) the sole purpose of the work is to cross the area, to discharge water into the area or to connect the system to a building situated in such an environment.

**78** Storage, including temporary storage, of a structure or equipment in a body of water must take place without tree clearing.

**79** Stabilization works in a littoral zone, a flood zone or on a lakeshore or riverbank must not result in raising the ground level.

**80** Construction of a backfill embankment in a flood zone to raise the ground level under a residential building is permitted on the following conditions:

(1) the presence of backfill will not increase the exposure to flooding for the infrastructures, works or buildings likely to be affected by the presence of the backfill embankment;

(2) the backfill embankment ensures only the immediate protection of the building and does not extend to the entire land on which the building is situated;

(3) the height of the backfill embankment does not exceed the applicable protection objective under Schedule III;

(4) the average slope downward from the top of the fill next to the protected building or works must be at least 33⅓%.

This section applies to any body of water present in a flood zone.

**81** The laying out of a road in a flood zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) it must not exceed the protection objective applicable under Schedule III;

(2) if the road does not respect the original topography of the site, culverts must be installed to allow water to flow freely.

This section applies to any body of water present in a flood zone.

**82** Construction of a residential access in a body of water must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the access must not have an impervious surface;

(2) the access cannot be wider than 6.5 m, except for work involving a parking area;

(3) the work must respect the original topography of the site to the extent possible;

(4) for siting or extension in a littoral zone, on a lakeshore or riverbank or in a short-term channel migration zone,

(a) the access must include a crossing works; and

(b) the sole purpose of the access is to cross the area;

(5) for siting of a residential access as part of the construction of a residential building, the access must not slope downward toward the building.

Despite subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph, the access height may be increased sufficiently to allow for the building occupants to be evacuated if it is shown that that measure is necessary to guarantee the safety of persons and if the access meets the following conditions:

(1) the presence of backfill will not increase the exposure to flooding for the infrastructures, works, buildings or equipment likely to be affected by the presence of the backfill embankment;

(2) the backfill embankment ensures only the evacuation of the occupants of the building and does not extend to the whole of the land;

(3) the height of the backfill embankment does not exceed the applicable protection objective under Schedule III;

This section applies to any body of water present in a flood zone.

#### 7 — Non-residential buildings and residential buildings and their accessory works and buildings

**83** The reconstruction of a residential building in a littoral zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) it is carried out because of damage that was not caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration;

(2) it is carried out only if the building cannot be relocated elsewhere on the land without encroaching in a littoral zone or a very high flood hazard zone.

**84** Substantial modification of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings situated in a littoral zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the work does not involve the addition of a concrete base;

(2) for an extension,

(a) if the building or works is in a flood zone present in a littoral zone, the work involves only relocating rooms used by a person as living spaces or facilities essential to the building, with a view to reducing the vulnerability of persons and property;

(b) it does not result in further encroachment in the littoral zone; and

(c) it does not involve adding a basement or a structure attached to the building such as a deck or balcony.

The conditions in subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

**85** Relocation, reconstruction and substantial modification of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings on a lakeshore or riverbank must comply with the following conditions:

(1) in the case of reconstruction,

(a) unless the initial encroachment does not allow for it, a vegetation strip at least 5 m in width, measured from the boundary of the littoral zone, must be preserved in a natural or restored state so as to re-establish at least 2 strata of herbaceous, shrubby or arborescent vegetation;

(b) the reconstruction is carried out only if the building or works cannot be relocated elsewhere on the land without encroaching on a lakeshore or riverbank;

(2) in the case of relocation, it must not result in moving the residential building and its accessory works and buildings closer to the littoral zone;

(3) in the case of substantial modification of a residential building,

(a) it does not result in further encroachment on the lakeshore or riverbank; and

(b) it does not involve adding a basement or a structure attached to the building such as a deck or balcony;

(4) for work involving a residential building’s accessory works or buildings, the following conditions must be met:

(a) in the case of reconstruction, the total encroachment area on the lakeshore or riverbank of the accessory works or buildings, including existing works and buildings, must not exceed 30 m^2, or is of an area equal to that of the initial encroachment area of the accessory works or building if that area was 30 m^2 or less; and

(b) the work does not require backfilling or excavation other than minor ground leveling.

For the purposes of subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph, if the work is to replace the foundation, the building must be relocated off the lakeshore or riverbank if the space on the land permits it.

The conditions in subparagraph *a* of subparagraph 3 and in subparagraph *a* of subparagraph 4 of the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures, which must meet the following conditions:

(1) the work results in further encroachment into a lakeshore or riverbank that does not exceed 5 m^2; and

(2) the work is carried out avoiding to the extent possible any encroachment into a lakeshore or riverbank.

**86** If the work involves a residential building in a very high flood hazard zone, it must comply with the following conditions:

(1) in the case of reconstruction, it is carried out only if the building or works cannot be relocated elsewhere on the land without encroaching into a very high flood hazard zone;

(2) in the case of extension,

(a) the work involves only relocating rooms used by a person as living spaces or facilities essential to the building, with a view to reducing the vulnerability of persons and property;

(b) it does not entail further encroachment in the zone; and

(c) it does not involve adding a basement or a structure attached to the building such as a deck or balcony;

(3) in the case of relocation,

(a) it must not result in moving the building closer to the littoral zone or short-term channel migration zone, as applicable; and

(b) the relocation is to a new site at an elevation higher than the original site.

The conditions in the first paragraph do not apply to any long-term channel migration zone present in a very high flood hazard zone.

**87** Work involving works or buildings accessory to a residential building in a very high flood hazard zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) except in the case of relocation, the work must not result in a total encroachment area exceeding 50 m^2 in any flood zone, including the encroachment area of existing works and buildings;

(2) in the case of relocation, substantial modification or reconstruction because of damage that was not caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration, the work is carried out without backfilling, except for minor ground leveling.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, an encroachment created by a pool or fence is not included in calculating total encroachment area.

The condition in subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph does not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

This section does not apply to any long-term channel migration zone present in a very high flood hazard zone.

**88** Work involving a residential building in a high flood hazard zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) in the case of reconstruction, it is carried out only if the building cannot be relocated elsewhere on the land without encroaching on a high flood hazard zone;

(2) in the case of extension,

(a) it involves only relocating rooms used by a person as living spaces or facilities essential to the building, with a view to reducing the vulnerability of persons and property;

(b) it does not result in further encroachment in the zone; and

(c) it does not involve adding a basement or a structure attached to the building such as a deck or balcony;

(3) in the case of relocation,

(a) it must not result in moving the building closer to the littoral zone or short-term channel migration zone, as applicable; and

(b) the relocation is to a new site at an elevation higher than the original site.

The conditions in the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

Subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a high flood hazard zone and subparagraphs 2 and 3 of the first paragraph apply to any body of water present in a high flood hazard zone.

**89** Work involving works or buildings accessory to a residential building in a high flood hazard zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) except in the case of relocation, the works must not result in a total encroachment area exceeding 50 m^2 in any flood zone, including the encroachment area of existing accessory works and buildings;

(2) the work is carried out without backfilling, except for minor ground leveling.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, an encroachment created by a pool or fence is not included in calculating total encroachment area.

Subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph does not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

This section applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a high flood hazard zone.

**90** Work involving a residential building in a moderate flood hazard zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) in the case of reconstruction, it is carried out only if the building cannot be relocated elsewhere on the land without encroaching on a moderate flood hazard zone;

(2) in the case of extension, if the work results in further encroachment in the zone, that encroachment may not exceed 15 m^2 and may occur only once;

(3) in the case of relocation,

(a) it must not result in moving the building closer to the littoral zone or short-term channel migration zone; and

(b) the relocation is to a new site at an elevation higher than the original site.

The conditions in the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

Subparagraphs 1 and 2 of the first paragraph apply to any long-term channel migration zone present in a moderate flood hazard zone and subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph applies to any body of water present in a moderate flood hazard zone.

**91** Work involving works or buildings accessory to a residential building in a moderate flood hazard zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) except in the case of relocation, the work must not result in a total encroachment area exceeding 50 m^2 in any flood zone, including the encroachment area of existing works and buildings;

(2) the work is carried out without backfilling, except for minor ground leveling.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, an encroachment created by a pool or fence is not included in calculating total encroachment area.

The conditions in subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

This section applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a moderate flood hazard zone.

**92** Work for the siting of a residential building in a low flood hazard zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the land on which the work is to be carried out must meet the following criteria:

(a) it must be served by a municipal waterworks or sewer system;

(b) if the land is situated outside an urbanization perimeter contained in a land use and development plan, it must be situated between 2 pieces of land on which there is an existing building;

(2) green storm water management and runoff infrastructures must be installed on the land on which the work is to be carried out;

(3) at least 30% of the area of the land on which the work is to be carried out must be pervious;

This section applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a low flood hazard zone.

**93** Work involving works or buildings accessory to a residential building in a low flood hazard zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) except in the case of relocation, the work must not result in a total encroachment area exceeding 50 m^2 in any flood zone, including the encroachment area of existing works and buildings;

(2) the work is carried out without backfilling, except for minor ground leveling.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, an encroachment created by a pool or fence is not included in calculating total encroachment area.

The conditions in subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

This section applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a low flood hazard zone.

**94** Work involving a residential building in a high-velocity flood zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) in the case of extension,

(a) it involves only relocating rooms used by a person as living spaces or facilities essential to the building, with a view to reducing the vulnerability of persons and property;

(b) it does not result in further encroachment in the zone; and

(c) it does not involve adding a basement or a structure attached to the building such as a deck or balcony;

(2) in the case of relocation,

(a) it must not result in moving the building closer to the littoral zone or short-term channel migration zone, as applicable; and

(b) the relocation is to a new site at an elevation higher than the original site; and

(c) the work is carried out without backfilling, except for minor ground leveling.

The conditions in the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

This section applies to any body of water present in a high-velocity flood zone, except subparagraph 2, which does not apply to a littoral zone present in a high-velocity flood zone.

**95** Work involving works or buildings accessory to a residential building in a high-velocity flood zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) except in the case of relocation, the work must not result in a total encroachment area exceeding 50 m^2 in any flood zone, including the encroachment area of existing works and buildings;

(2) if the work involves an accessory building, it is carried out without backfilling, except for minor ground leveling.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, an encroachment created by a pool or fence is not included in calculating total encroachment area.

The conditions in subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

This section applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a high-velocity flood zone.

**96** If the work involves the siting of a residential building in a low-velocity flood zone, the land on which the work is to be carried out must meet the following criteria:

(1) it must be situated within an urbanization perimeter contained in a land use and development plan;

(2) it must be served by a municipal waterworks and sewer system;

(3) it must be situated between 2 pieces of land on which a building is already present on 23 June 2021;

(4) it does not result from a land subdivision after 23 June 2021.

The conditions in the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

This section applies to any long-term channel migration zone present in a low-velocity flood zone.

**97** Work involving works or buildings accessory to a residential building in a low-velocity flood zone must comply with the following conditions:

(1) except in the case of relocation, the work must not result in a total encroachment area exceeding 50 m^2 in any flood zone, including the encroachment area of existing works and buildings;

(2) if the work involves an accessory building, it is carried out without backfilling, except for minor ground leveling.

For the purposes of subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph, an encroachment created by a pool or fence is not included in calculating total encroachment area.

The conditions in subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures.

**98** If carried out in a short-term channel migration zone, the relocation, reconstruction and substantial modification of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings must comply with the following conditions:

(1) in the case of reconstruction,

(a) it is carried out only if the building cannot be relocated elsewhere on the land without encroaching on a short-term channel migration zone; and

(b) is carried out because of damage that was not caused by a flood, submersion or watercourse channel migration;

(2) in the case of substantial modification of a residual building,

(a) it must not result in further encroachment in a short-term channel migration zone; and

(b) it must not involve extension below ground or the addition of a structure attached to the building such as a deck or balcony;

(3) for work involving relocation, it must not move the residential building and its accessory works and buildings closer to the littoral zone;

(4) if the work involves a residential building’s accessory works or buildings,

(a) in the case of reconstruction, the total encroachment area in the short-term channel migration zone of the accessory works or buildings must not exceed 30 m^2, or an area equal to that of the initial encroachment area of the accessory works or building if that area was 30 m^2 or less; and

(b) the work does not require backfilling or excavation, other than minor ground leveling.

For the purposes of subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph, if the work is to replace the foundation, the building must be relocated outside the short-term channel migration zone if the space on the land permits it.

The conditions in subparagraph *a* of subparagraph 2 and in subparagraph *a* of subparagraph 4 of the first paragraph do not apply to work the purpose of which is to improve accessibility for mobility impaired persons or to work relating to fire safety measures, which must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the work creates further encroachment that does not exceed 5 m^2; and

(2) the work is carried out avoiding to the extent possible any encroachment in that zone.

Subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph applies to any body of water present in a short-term channel migration zone.

**99** Despite any contrary provision, reconstruction of a building in a flood zone or channel migration zone required because of flood damage sustained may take place on the following conditions:

(1) the reconstruction involves any of the following immovables:

(a) a recognized or classified heritage immovable, as applicable;

(b) an immovable situated in a recognized, classified or declared heritage site under the Cultural Heritage Act (chapter P-9.002); or

(c) an immovable in an inventory made pursuant to section 120 of the Cultural Heritage Act and that was in the inventory before the date of the flood;

(2) the work has been authorized by the Minister of Culture and Communications or by the competent municipality, as applicable;

(3) an opinion signed by a professional shows that the work ensures the safety of persons and property, in particular through the implementation of adaptation measures.

Relocation and substantial modification of a building referred to in the first paragraph may take place on the following conditions, except if they are carried out in a low flood hazard zone or a long-term channel migration zone:

(1) the work does not result in further encroachment in a flood zone of an area that exceeds 30 m^2;

(2) the work has been authorized by the Minister of Culture and Communications or by the competent municipality, as applicable.

The adaptation measures set out in section 100 do not apply to work referred to in the first and second paragraphs if an opinion signed by a professional shows that the measures affect the heritage interest of the immovable and that the measures proposed offer equivalent protection for persons and property.

**100** Construction of a building in a flood zone, including any body of water present, must comply with the following adaptation measures, as applicable:

(1) in the case of siting, reconstruction, extension and relocation of a building, as well as for the substantial modification of its foundation, only storage and parking areas may be laid out below the protection objective applicable under Schedule III;

(2) in the case of substantial modification other than a modification of the building’s foundation, the floor and ground floor must be situated above the protection objective applicable under Schedule III, except if that is impossible, in which case the following conditions must be met:

(a) adaptation measures must be implemented;

(b) an emergency exit and a refuge area must be provided for and situated above the determined protection objective under Schedule III;

(3) openings such as windows and doors in areas used for sleeping and meal preparation, as well as areas that are not designed to be resistant or resilient to contact with water, must be situated above the protection objective applicable under Schedule III;

(4) areas situated below the protection objective applicable under Schedule III must be made using materials and assemblies having a good overall resilience performance, as applicable;

(5) drains and vent ducts must be equipped with check valves;

(6) a major component in the building’s mechanical system, such as an electrical system, plumbing system, heating system or ventilation system, must be installed above the protection objective applicable under Schedule III, unless the nature of the system requires it to be below that protection objective in which case protection measures must be put in place.

For the purposes of subparagraph 4 of the first paragraph, good overall resilience performance of materials refers to

(1) the water resistance of materials;

(2) the capacity of materials and assemblies to dry and be cleaned; and

(3) the capacity of materials to retain their original size and structural integrity after a flood.

## III — FLOOD PROTECTION WORKS

### I — GENERAL

**101** This Chapter applies to activities carried out on flood protection works.

### II — STANDARDS APPLICABLE TO ACTIVITIES CARRIED OUT ON FLOOD PROTECTION WORKS

**102** This Division applies to activities carried out on flood protection works, irrespective of whether the activities require a municipal permit pursuant to Division III.

**103** The siting and reconstruction on flood protection works of the following works and buildings is prohibited:

(1) a non-residential building, other than an ancillary building necessary for the proper operation of the works, whose encroachment area, on the same land, does not exceed 40 m^2;

(2) a residential building and its accessory works and buildings.

**104** The following is prohibited on flood protection works:

(1) the addition of a dwelling in a building;

(2) the conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a residential building to a residential building.

**105** Races, rallies and other motorized vehicle competitions are prohibited on flood protection works.

**106** The operation of motorized vehicles on flood protection works is permitted only in the following cases:

(1) on trails or roads laid out for that purpose;

(2) to carry out work, if the conditions set out in section 123 are complied with;

(3) in an emergency to ensure the safety of the works.

**107** Seeding and planting of invasive exotic plant species on flood protection works are prohibited.

### III — MUNICIPAL PERMIT

#### 1 — Permit requirement

**108** No person may, on flood protection works, carry out an activity to which this Division applies without first obtaining a permit from the relevant local municipality.

Such a permit is issued if the conditions specific to each activity and those applicable under Division IV of this Chapter are complied with.

No permit may be issued if the proposed activity is prohibited under Division II of this Chapter.

**109** Substantial modification and relocation of the following infrastructures, works and buildings on flood protection works require a municipal permit:

(1) a residential building and its accessory works and buildings;

(2) a non-residential building, other than a building necessary for the proper operation of a flood protection works, provided

(a) there is no excavation, including for foundations or to bury equipment, mains or wires; and

(b) the area of the building on the same land does not exceed 40 m^2.

**110** Construction on flood protection works of the following infrastructures and works requires a municipal permit:

(1) a residential access;

(2) an infrastructure or works providing access to an infrastructure, works, non-residential building, equipment or site, such as a vehicular entrance or pedestrian walkway, provided

(a) the infrastructure or works is not impervious;

(b) the infrastructure or works does not exceed 6.5 m in width; and

(c) there is no other means of accessing the infrastructure, works, non-residential building, equipment or site.

**111** The dismantling and demolition of the following infrastructures, works and buildings, if situated on a flood protection works, require a municipal permit:

(1) a residential building, its accessory works and buildings and residential accesses;

(2) a non-residential building referred to in paragraph 2 of section 109;

(3) an infrastructure or works giving access to an infrastructure, works, non-residential building, equipment or site, such as a vehicular entrance or pedestrian walkway referred to in paragraph 2 of section 110.

**112** Maintenance of the infrastructures, buildings and works referred to in section 111 requires a municipal permit if the work requires backfill of 30 cm or more.

**113** The construction, on a flood protection works, of a structure, other than a building, that is anchored, on piles or wheels and floats on or extends into water, such as a quay or boat shelter, is exempted from authorization pursuant to this Division if the total encroachment area of the structures, including any structures already present on the land, does not exceed 30 m^2, excluding the anchor points.

#### 2 — Content of an application

**114** In addition to the general content prescribed by section 14 as well as any information or document required by the local municipality, a permit application under this Chapter must contain a technical report signed by an engineer

(1) specifying the measures to put in place so that the work does not jeopardize the safety of the works during and after the work, in particular as regards restoring the site to its original condition;

(2) attesting, if the activity involves construction of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment, that the construction does not restrict access to the flood protection works nor impedes travel on the works or the carrying out of maintenance and monitoring activities; and

(3) indicating, if backfilling and excavating is required, that such work has no impact on the stability and integrity of the flood protection works.

### IV — CONDITIONS APPLICABLES WHEN CERTAIN ACTIVITIES ARE CARRIED OUT

#### 1 — General

**115** This Division applies only to activities requiring a municipal permit under Division III of this Chapter.

**116** No person may carry out work, construction or any other activity on flood protection works if doing so is likely to compromise the safety of the works.

**117** A person carrying out work on flood protection works

(1) must not prevent or impede access to the works;

(2) must not prevent or impede maintenance, inspection and monitoring activities related to the works; and

(3) must, in the event of breakdown or incident affecting the flood protection works, inform as soon as possible the local municipality concerned and restore the works to its former condition in compliance with the instructions of an engineer to ensure the safety of the works.

**118** Excavation and fill work on a flood protection works are limited to what is necessary to carry out work such as construction of an infrastructure, works, building or equipment. In such a case, the activities must not alter the topography or elevation of the flood protection works.

#### 2 — Site restoration and management of vegetation

**119** On completion of an activity carried out on flood protection works, the following measures must be applied:

(1) all temporary works must be dismantled and removed from the site;

(2) the embankments must be stable and protected against erosion, preferably by means of the technique most conducive to maintaining the original conditions of the site;

(3) the site must be restored within the year following completion of the activity, including, as applicable,

(a) soil restoration; and

(b) in the dewatered zone, revegetation of any area that has been stripped of vegetation or soil, except in the following cases:

(i) after sample taking, conducting surveys, making technical surveys and taking measurements;

(ii) if the revegetation jeopardizes the stability or safety of the flood protection works.

**120** Soil restoration required pursuant to section 119 must comply with the following conditions:

(1) if water is present, the restoration must make use of the stabilized original substrate, unless it is not appropriate for ensuring the stability of the works;

(2) debris and other residual materials must be removed;

(3) the restoration must respect the original topography of the flood protection works.

**121** Revegetation required under this Chapter must comply with the following conditions:

(1) it must use native species adapted to the environment;

(2) the survival rate of the vegetation or plant cover must be 80% in the year following revegetation; that failing, dead vegetation must be replaced.

**122** Management of vegetation on flood protection works, including revegetation required under this Chapter, must not involve seeding or planting trees and shrubs.

#### 3 — Operation and use of vehicles and machinery

**123** Motorized vehicles and machinery required for work on flood protection works may be operated on the following conditions:

(1) the vehicles or machinery operate only in a dewatered or drained area of the flood protection works or if there is ice cover;

(2) if ruts are created, the area is restored to its original condition, or a condition close thereto.

The condition in subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph does not apply if the operation is necessary for

(1) construction of temporary works;

(2) making preliminary technical surveys;

(3) taking samples; or

(4) taking measurements.

**124** Refuelling and maintenance of vehicles or machinery must meet the following conditions:

(1) the work must be carried out only in a dewatered or drained area of the flood protection works or if there is ice cover;

(2) the vehicle or machinery must be equipped with a system for collecting fluid leakage and spillage, or with a spillage prevention device.

#### 4 — Dewatering on flood protection works

**125** Temporary dewatering upstream of a flood protection works may not take place more than twice in a 12-month period.

Dewatering works may in no case exceed a period of 30 consecutive days.

**126** Dewatering work on flood protection works must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the equipment and materials used must make it possible to limit the discharge of suspended matter into the lake or watercourse;

(2) if the pumped water contains suspended matter visible to the naked eye, it must be discharged into an area of vegetation located more than 30 m from the littoral zone and elsewhere than on the flood protection works, such as a field of grasses or forest litter, provided the point of discharge is regularly shifted to a new location.

**127** Works used for dewatering on flood protection works must be dismantled by first removing the materials situated inside the dewatered area and then moving from the area downstream of the works used for dewatering toward the area upstream.

#### 5 — Infrastructures, buildings, accessory works and buildings and residential access

**128** Substantial modification and relocation, on flood protection works, of a non-residential building referred to in paragraph 2 of section 109 and of a residential building and its accessory works and buildings must comply with the following conditions:

(1) in the case of relocation, the purpose must be to relocate the building or works off the flood protection works or, if that is not possible, to reduce encroachment on the flood protection works as far as it is feasible to do so;

(2) in the case of substantial modification, it must not result in further encroachment on and into the flood protection works;

(3) in the case of extension, it consists only in relocating rooms used by a person as living spaces or facilities essential to the building, with a view to reducing the vulnerability of persons and property;

(4) the work on a residential building and a nonresidential building referred to in paragraph 2 of section 109 must comply with the adaptation measures set out in section 100.

The conditions in the first paragraph also apply to substantial modification and relocation, on a flood protection works, of a residential access, infrastructure or works providing access to an infrastructure, works, nonresidential building, equipment or site, such as a vehicular entrance or pedestrian walkway, with the necessary modifications.

**129** The siting and reconstruction on flood protection works of an infrastructure or works providing access to an infrastructure, works, non-residential building, equipment or site, such as a vehicular entrance or pedestrian walkway, may be carried out only if doing so is not possible elsewhere on the land without encroaching on the flood protection works.

**130** Construction of a residential access must comply with the following conditions:

(1) the access must not be impervious;

(2) the access must be no wider than 6.5 m;

(3) the construction is not possible elsewhere on the land without encroaching on the flood protection works.

**131** Despite any provision contrary to this Division, work relating to improving accessibility for mobility impaired persons and to fire safety measures is permitted.

## IV — FLOOD RISK MANAGEMENT PLAN

### I — OBJECTIVES AND CRITERIA

**132** A regional county municipality may prepare a flood risk management plan, which is implemented through by-law made pursuant to section 79.1 of the Act respecting land use planning and development (chapter A-19.1) and approved by the Minister of Municipal Affairs, Regions and Land Occupancy pursuant to section 79.17 of that Act, in order to establish a development strategy for all or part of its territory regarding existing flood zones. The development strategy must take into consideration the territorial particularities and the various activities carried out in the territory, seek to better manage development of the territory and long-term public safety, and enhance environmental benefits.

Regional county municipalities whose territories are contiguous and which share a watercourse may draft a joint flood risk management plan. Each regional county municipality that is party to the plan is responsible for the plan’s implementation in its territory.

**133** For the purposes of this Chapter, with the necessary modifications, every local municipality whose territory is not included in the territory of a regional county municipality is considered to be a regional county municipality.

Despite the foregoing, if the territory of a local municipality referred to in the first paragraph is included in the territory of an urban agglomeration within the meaning of the Act respecting the exercise of certain municipal powers in certain urban agglomerations (chapter E-20.001), the functions assigned by this Chapter to a regional county municipality come under the jurisdiction of the agglomeration.

**134** A flood risk management plan may, to the extent provided in section 139, allow a regional county municipality and the local municipalities that are part of it to authorize the activities referred to in that section in the flood zones whose boundaries are established in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act, despite the activities being prohibited or regulated by conditions or restrictions, including by permitting work to be carried out in certain sectors for the purpose of, as applicable,

(1) their consolidation, namely completing and structuring the existing built framework, such as a residential sector, through coherent interventions compatible with the built framework, with a view to enhancing its physical qualities; and

(2) their reclassification, namely modifying the physical qualities of the existing built framework in a sector in the territory through interventions giving it new uses as a means of reducing the vulnerability of persons and property to flooding and thereby enabling the sector to be minimally functional in terms of services and infrastructures.

The provisions of this Regulation other than section 139, to the extent provided in that section, continue to apply despite the implementation of a flood risk management plan.

A regional county municipality may also include in its flood risk management plan various activities or measures that form part of its development strategy but are not exclusively regulated by laws, regulations and by-laws under its responsibility. Their implementation remains, however, subject to the applicable Acts and regulations, in particular as regards the prior environmental authorizations required.

**135** The flood risk management plan may apply to any flood zone in the territory, with the exception of

(1) a low or high velocity flood zone; and

(2) any flood zone downstream of flood protection works for which a municipality has been declared responsible under section 46.0.13 of the Act.

If the boundaries of a flood zone in a territory covered by an applicable flood risk management plan are modified, as a result of which a flood zone is situated upstream of a flood protection works referred to in subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph, the activities covered by the plan may no longer be carried out in that zone as of the date of publication of the notice referred to in the fourth paragraph of section 46.0.2.1 of the Act specifying the establishment of the new boundaries. The same applies to the establishment of new boundaries providing for a channel migration zone in a territory.

**136** The measures in a flood risk management plan prepared by a regional county municipality must meet the following criteria:

(1) the choices of measures in the strategic development are justified by benefits for the community that are greater than their impacts on flood-related risks and on the environment;

(2) within an urbanization perimeter, the buildings in the territory covered by the flood risk management plan are served by waterworks and sewer services before the work proposed in the plan may be carried out;

(3) outside an urbanization perimeter, either of the following 2 conditions:

(a) the territory is served by waterworks and sewer services before the work proposed in the plan may be carried out; or

(b) the buildings in the territory covered by the flood risk management plan are equipped with a system for the discharge, collection or disposal of domestic waste water, grey water or toilet effluents and with withdrawal facilities that comply with the Act and the regulations and are to the greatest extent possible situated outside a flood zone;

(4) the measures proposed are consistent with the regional wetlands and bodies of water plan and the water master plan developed pursuant to the Act to affirm the collective nature of water resources and to promote better governance of water and associated environments (chapter C-6.2), as well as with any other climate plan covering the same territory as the flood risk management plan;

(5) the hydraulic implications generated by the activities covered by the flood risk management plan and any other activity carried out in the flood zone are taken into account and measures are planned to not obstruct the flow of water, taking into particular consideration the topography of the land and its hydrographic characteristics;

(6) access to the sectors in the territory covered by the flood risk management plan in the flood period is assessed and contingency measures are set in place for any inaccessible portions of the territory;

(7) flood prevention and public awareness measures are put in place, such as

(a) establishment of a flood monitoring and forecasting system, including visual flood level markers;

(b) installation of an early warning alert system and efficient communication strategies; and

(c) preparation of an evacuation plan, including designation of a refuge area;

(8) consolidation and reclassification comply with the conditions set out respectively in sections 137 and 138 and are planned on the basis of the following parameters:

(a) in a very high or high flood hazard zone, existing urban sectors may be reclassified without construction of a new residential building;

(b) in a moderate or low flood hazard zone, existing urban sectors may be reclassified and consolidated;

(9) if the flood risk management plan provides for consolidating or reclassifying a sector, the regional county municipality must provide for resilience measures aimed at reducing the vulnerability of persons and property;

(10) the measures proposed will result in permanent environmental benefits, obtained in particular by

(a) improving water management, for example through the creation of water retention basins, blue or green corridors and green ditches;

(b) reducing soil imperviousness, for example by reducing the width of streets and sidewalks, parking areas and vehicular entrances to a minimum, using pervious materials and creating green ditches;

(c) focusing on the protection and restoration of natural ecosystems and enhancing biodiversity, for example by creating a dedicated biodiversity sector, restoring or creating wetlands and bodies of water and reconstituting the forest cover; and

(d) allowing floods to expand so as to reduce the flood hazard intensity, for example by reconnecting bodies of water and creating green buffer zones integrating a variety of plant strata.

**137** To be included in a flood risk management plan, consolidation must comply with the following conditions:

(1) be implemented in sectors in the territory

(a) where the proportion of built-up land is equal to or greater than 65% in moderate and low flood hazard zones;

(b) where land covered by the consolidation and land contiguous thereto are not exposed to natural constraints other than flood zones;

(c) that do not contain

(i) a landfill site;

(ii) a site where industrial activities likely to contaminate storm water are carried on;

(iii) an outdoor bulk storage site likely to contaminate storm water;

(iv) a site where hazardous materials, chemical products or salts are loaded or unloaded;

(v) a site where activities to repair or clean heavy vehicles or railway vehicles likely to contaminate storm water are carried on; or

(vi) a site where vehicle recycling, long-term storage, crushing and shredding activities are carried on;

(2) it must provide for subdivision of the territory covered by the consolidation if the subdividing has not already occurred;

(3) the projected density of the part of the sector covered by the consolidation must be consistent with the density provided for in the land use and development plan;

(4) residential accesses sloping downward toward a building are prohibited;

(5) the siting of a main residential building and conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a residential building to a main residential building to permanently house vulnerable persons, mobility impaired persons or persons with special needs is prohibited, as is adaptation of a main residential building to permanently house such persons;

(6) if mature trees are present on land covered by the consolidation, they must be preserved, subject to those occupying the area of the building to be built, or if there are no mature trees on the land, at least 2 trees for the first area equal to or less than 250 m^2 of area must be planted as well as at least 2 trees per 250 m^2 of additional area;

(7) for non-built-up land, the siting and reconstruction of buildings on a slope of 30% or greater is prohibited;

(8) the consolidation promotes improved land water management by

(a) integrating at least one green management infrastructure for storm water and runoff, such as a green roof, a living wall, a rain garden or a filter strip;

(b) maintaining at least 30% pervious surface area for an area of land 500 m^2 to 749 m^2, and a 50% pervious surface for an area of land greater than 749 m^2; and

(9) the extension of an existing road is possible in the following cases:

(a) its purpose is to connect 2 existing roads over a maximum distance of 300 m between each;

(b) it involves construction of a dead end with a turning radius or a manoeuvring area not exceeding 120 m in length.

**138** To be included in a flood risk management plan, reclassification must comply with the following conditions:

(1) it must be intended for anthropized sectors, namely sectors for which at least one activity or use has altered the natural functions of the territory or the ground occupancy, particularly through land being cleared or made impervious;

(2) it renders the highest risk infrastructures more resilient, for example by establishing sustainable water management infrastructures or creating wetlands;

(3) it favours land planning that does not obstruct the flow of water, although without creating other issues in neighbouring sectors;

(4) it must provide for subdivision of the territory covered by the reclassification if the subdivision has not already occurred;

(5) the projected density of the part of the sector covered by the reclassification must be consistent with the density provided for in the land use and development plan.

**139** A flood risk management plan may permit the following activities:

(1) despite the restrictions set out in subparagraph 4 of the first paragraph of section 27, the addition of a dwelling as part of the reclassification of a sector, in a high flood hazard zone;

(2) despite section 28,

(a) the siting or reconstruction of a residential building, as part of the consolidation of a sector and situated along an existing road or a road that was extended in accordance with paragraph 9 of section 137, in a moderate flood hazard zone;

(b) the conversion of a non-residential building or a building accessory to a residential building to a residential building as part of the reclassification of a sector, in a high or moderate flood hazard zone; and

(c) addition of a dwelling as part of the reclassification of a sector, in a high or moderate flood hazard zone;

(3) despite the restrictions set out in subparagraphs *a* and *c* of subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph of section 86, the extension of a residential building on reclassification of a sector, in a very high flood hazard zone;

(4) despite the restrictions set out in subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph of section 88, the extension of a residential building on reclassification of a sector, in a high flood hazard zone;

(5) despite the restrictions set out in subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph of section 90, the extension of a residential building on reclassification of a sector, in a moderate flood hazard zone.

**140** If the boundaries of a flood zone in a territory for which a flood risk management plan is applicable are modified in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act as a result of which the intensity class of a flood zone in the territory is raised, the activities covered by the plan that have not begun and no longer meet the flood zone intensity class required to be carried out under this section may no longer be carried out in that zone as of the date of publication of the notice referred to in the fourth paragraph of section 46.0.2.1 of the Act specifying the establishment of the new boundaries.

### II — EXPERT EVALUATION

**141** For the preparation of a flood risk management plan, a regional county municipality must first obtain an expert evaluation from a professional determining the flood risks for the territory of the sectors covered by the plan. The evaluation must contain a characterization of the flood hazard and a vulnerability assessment conducted pursuant to this Division.

The professional must provide the regional county municipality with a copy of all information and documents used in the expert evaluation.

The regional county municipality must send the expert evaluation conducted under this Division to the Minister as soon as it has been completed.

**142** For characterization of the flood hazard, the expert evaluation must use the boundaries of the flood zones and channel migration zones established in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act for existing watercourses or segments of watercourses in the territory of the sectors that are covered by the flood risk management plan.

**143** To assess the level of human, territorial and environmental vulnerability and the vulnerability related to accessibility to persons and property, the expert evaluation must among other things factor in the following elements for the territory of the sectors covered by the flood risk management plan and determine their degree of exposure, their value or the strategic importance of the exposed elements, and their degree of sensitivity to floods:

(1) the human implications, in particular based on the demographic portrait, for example, according to the number of persons exposed to flooding, the proportion of sensitive age individuals, that is, young children and seniors, or the proportion of individuals with communication difficulties;

(2) the territorial implications, in particular based on

(a) the number of exposed residential buildings;

(b) the number of each type of public institution, establishment or facility, including health and social service institutions, educational institutions, correctional facilities and tourist establishments, as defined in section 3 of the Regulation respecting the regulatory scheme applying to activities on the basis of their environmental impact (chapter Q-2, r. 17.1);

(c) the number of public security establishments, including fire stations and police stations; and

(d) the proportion of each type of infrastructure, works and buildings providing public services, such as operating stations or current transforming stations, bridges, dams, city halls, municipal garages, grocery stores, pharmacies, service stations, hardware stores, wastewater treatment works and drinking water treatment plants;

(e) the number of heritage immovables among the following:

(i) a recognized or classified heritage immovable, as applicable;

(ii) an immovable situated in a recognized, classified or declared heritage site under the Cultural Heritage Act (chapter P-9.002);

(iii) an immovable in an inventory made pursuant to section 120 of the Cultural Heritage Act;

(3) the environmental implications, in particular based on

(a) the number of contaminated sites or sites described in subparagraph *c* of paragraph 1 of section 137;

(b) the percentage of anthropogenic areas having generally impervious surfaces such as parking areas and non-vegetated public areas;

(c) the percentage and types of wetlands;

(d) the percentage of protected areas, wildlife and plant habitats and other similar areas under protection; and

(e) the percentage of forest environments;

(4) the implications associated with accessibility, based in particular on

(a) the number of kilometres in the overall road network in the territory likely to be flooded by more than 30 cm of water;

(b) the number and types of buildings, for example residential or non-residential, likely to be isolated because of a flooded portion of the road network;

(c) the number of isolated kilometres in the overall road network in the territory, namely the portions not connected to at least 2 other portions; and

(d) the maximum height of flood water likely to cover the road network based on a 20, 100 and 350 year flood recurrence.

**144** The expert evaluation must determine the level of flood risks, weighing the flood hazard characterization against the assessment of the 4 types of vulnerability referred to in section 143, and must provide the flood-risk evidence for each of those vulnerabilities.

### III — CONTENT OF THE FLOOD RISK MANAGEMENT PLAN

**145** A flood risk management plan must contain the following elements:

(1) the territorial boundary, the local municipalities covered by the plan and the classes of flood zones present;

(2) a description of the development strategy specifying, in particular,

(a) the objectives, such as sector consolidation or reclassification, setting out the type of construction or activity that could be authorized according to class of flood zone and the applicable conditions that will be implemented through by-law made pursuant to section 79.1 of the Act respecting land use planning and development (chapter A-19.1);

(b) a description of the activities included in the development strategy, stating, as applicable, whether they must be authorized by the Minister or by another government authority; and

(c) the flood-risk mitigation measures that will be implemented, such as building adaptation or green management infrastructure development;

(3) the implementation timetable for the development strategy;

(4) a description of the public awareness measures the regional county municipality intends to implement regarding flood risks.

**146** The regional county municipality must send the flood risk management plan to the Minister as soon as it is completed.

### IV — RETENTION OF INFORMATION AND DOCUMENTS

**147** The regional county municipality must retain all information and documents that have been used to prepare the flood risk management plan and the expert evaluation, along with the expert evaluation and flood risk management plan, for the entire time the plan is being implemented and thereafter for a minimum period of 10 years following completion of the last activities under the plan.

The information and documents must be provided on request to the Minister within the time the Minister specifies.

### V — REVIEW OF A FLOOD RISK MANAGEMENT PLAN

**148** A regional county municipality must review its flood risk management plan at the earliest of

(1) each 10-year period;

(2) the time at which the boundaries of the flood zones in its territory are modified; and

(3) after any flooding, unless the plan was reviewed less than 5 years earlier.

The review must

(1) be based on the boundaries of the flood zones in effect at the time of the review;

(2) be based on an expert evaluation current at the time of the review, including the flood hazard characterization, assessment of the 4 types of vulnerability and the resulting flood-risk evidence;

(3) validate the congruence between the current evidence and the development strategies in the flood risk management plan; and

(4) in the case of incongruence or with a view to improvement, review the development strategy in the flood risk management plan and the measures described in paragraph 12 of section 136.

**149** Every review of a flood risk management plan is implemented through by-law made pursuant to section 79.1 of the Act respecting land use planning and development (chapter A-19.1), in accordance with section 79.17 of that Act.

### VI — CRITERIA FOR APPROVAL OF A REGIONAL BY-LAW IMPLEMENTING A FLOOD RISK MANAGEMENT PLAN

**150** To be approved by the Minister of Municipal Affairs, Regions and Land Occupancy pursuant to section 79.17 of the Act respecting land use planning and development (chapter A-19.1), a by-law made pursuant to section 79.1 of that Act to implement a flood risk management plan must meet the following criteria:

(1) provide the precise boundaries and a detailed description of the sectors in the regional county municipality’s territory that will be covered in the flood risk management plan;

(2) meet the criteria set out in section 136 for the flood risk management plan;

(3) contain the elements required by section 145 for the flood risk management plan;

(4) determine the residential and non-residential uses, constructions and work that may be authorized under the regional by-law and the conditions that are to apply for all the sectors of the territory covered by the flood risk management plan, according to class of flood zone;

(5) establish that any authorization requirements other than those issued by the municipality for activities to be carried out, if applicable, have been taken into consideration for the implementation of the regional by-law;

(6) determine the control measures the regional county municipality may use to verify compliance of the activities authorized under the regional by-law;

(7) determine the penalties applicable for breach of the regional by-law;

(8) determine the situations entailing a review of the regional by-law set out in the first paragraph of section 148.

## SCHEDULE I — s. 5 — BOUNDARY OF THE LITTORAL ZONE

The boundary of the littoral zone is determined based on various factors such as the presence of works or special ecological conditions.

To that end, the following methods must be used in the order determined below, according to the cases described:

(1) the eco-geomorphological method must be used for coasts and islands in the following places:

(a) the Gulf of St. Lawrence;

(b) the baie des Chaleurs;

(c) the rivière Saguenay within the boundaries of the Saguenay–St. Lawrence Marine Park;

(d) the portion of the St. Lawrence River downstream of the territories of the municipalities of Saint-Louis-de-Gonzague-du-Cap-Tourmente, Saint-Vallier and Saint-François-de-l’Île-d’Orléans;

(2) in the presence of a water retaining structure greater than 1 m in height, the boundary of the littoral zone is situated at the maximum operating level of the hydraulic structure for the part of the body of water upstream from the structure, within its zone of influence;

(3) if the 2 year flood recurrence level has been established under subdivision 2 of Division V.1 of Chapter IV of Title 1 of the Act, the boundary of the littoral zone is determined using that recurrence level;

(4) if indicator plant species are present, the botanical method must be used;

(5) in other cases, the boundary of the littoral zone must be determined by hydraulic modelling of the 2 year flood recurrence level.

The first paragraph does not operate to modify the boundary of the littoral zone of the St. Lawrence River situated in the territory of Municipalité régionale de comté de La Côte-de-Beaupré applicable under the Act to delimit the high water mark of the St. Lawrence River in the territory of Municipalité régionale de comté de La Côte-de-Beaupré (1999, chapter 84).

## SCHEDULE II — ss. 5, 8, 160 and 161, and Sch. III — FLOOD ZONE WHOSE BOUNDARIES ARE DETERMINED ON ANOTHER BASIS

In the absence of a flood zone established in accordance with sections 46.0.2.1 to 46.0.2.3 of the Act, the flood zones are those whose boundaries on 25 March 2021 have been established using one of the following means:

(1) a map approved under an agreement on mapping and flood zone protection between the Gouvernement du Québec and the Government of Canada;

(2) a map published by the Gouvernement du Québec;

(3) a map integrated into a land use and development plan or an interim control by-law;

(4) the 20 year or 100 year, or both, flood recurrence levels established by the Gouvernement du Québec;

(5) the 20 year or 100 year, or both, flood recurrence levels referenced in a land use and development plan or an interim control by-law;

(6) any perimeter indicated on a map referred to in Schedule 2 to Order in Council 817-2019 dated 12 July 2019, as amended by Order in Council 1260-2019 dated 18 December 2019 and by orders of the Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing dated 2 August 2019, 23 August 2019, 25 September 2019, 23 December 2019 and 12 January 2021, excluding the territories listed in Schedule 4 to Order in Council 817-2019 dated 12 July 2019.
