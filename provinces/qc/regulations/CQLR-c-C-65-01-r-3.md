# Regulation respecting discrimination on the basis of territory allowed when awarding certain contracts of municipal bodies or during a process for certification or qualification

*CQLR c C-65.01, r 3*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01, s. 8, 3rd par., s. 22, 2nd par., s. 39, 1st par., subpar. 2, ss. 83 and 263)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

## I — OBJECT AND DEFINITIONS

**1** This Regulation determines the cases in which and the conditions on which the following documents may discriminate on the basis of territory:

(1) the tender documents published or sent by a municipal body for the awarding of a supply, construction, services or partnership contract according to an open procedure or by written invitation;

(2) the documents referred to in the first paragraph of section 22 of the Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01) published by a municipal body for establishing a process for the certification of goods or the qualification of enterprises for the awarding of a contract referred to in subparagraph 1.

For the purposes of this Regulation, a municipal body that is not subject to an intergovernmental agreement is considered to be a local municipality for the purposes of the rules set out in the agreement.

**2** For the purposes of this Regulation,

**Canada-European Union Agreement** means the Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement between Canada and the European Union and its Member States;

**services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement** means a services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement within the meaning of the Regulation respecting the threshold and time periods applicable when awarding certain contracts of municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01, r. 4).

## II — DISCRIMINATION ON THE BASIS OF TERRITORY ALLOWED WHEN AWARDING A CONTRACT ACCORDING TO AN OPEN PROCEDURE OR DURING A PROCESS FOR CERTIFICATION OR QUALIFICATION

### I — GENERAL

#### 1 — Discrimination on the basis of territory related to the territory from which the tenders or applications for the granting of the certification or qualification originate

**3** The tender documents published for the awarding, according to an open procedure, of a supply or partnership contract and the documents published for establishing a process for the certification of goods or the qualification of an enterprise for the awarding of such a contract may provide that only the tenders or applications presented by the following enterprises are accepted:

(1) enterprises that have an establishment in Canada when the contract to be awarded involves an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract;

(2) enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement when the contract to be awarded involves an expenditure equal to or greater than the threshold set in paragraph 1.

**4** The tender documents published for the awarding, according to an open procedure, of a construction contract and the documents published for establishing a process for the qualification of an enterprise for the awarding of such a contract may provide that only the tenders or applications presented by the following enterprises are accepted:

(1) enterprises that have an establishment in Québec or in Ontario when the contract to be awarded involves an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canadian Free Trade Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract;

(2) enterprises that have an establishment in Canada when the contract to be awarded involves an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract;

(3) enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement when the contract to be awarded involves an expenditure equal to or greater than the threshold set in paragraph 2.

**5** The tender documents published for the awarding, according to an open procedure, of a services contract and the documents published for establishing a process for the qualification of an enterprise for the awarding of such a contract may provide that only the tenders or applications presented by the following enterprises are accepted:

(1) enterprises that have an establishment in Canada, when

(a) the contract to be awarded involves an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract; or

(b) the contract to be awarded is a contract other than a services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement;

(2) enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement when the contract to be awarded is a services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement that involves an expenditure equal to or greater than the threshold from which the municipal body must offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract.

#### 2 — Discrimination on the basis of territory related to the origin of goods, services or enterprises participating in the performance of the contract

**6** The tender documents published for the awarding, according to an open procedure, of a contract and the documents published for establishing a process for the certification of goods or the qualification of an enterprise for the awarding of such a contract may provide that only the tenders or applications providing that all or part of the goods or services provided must be Canadian or all or part of the enterprises participating in the performance of the contract must have an establishment in Canada in the following cases are accepted:

(1) their object is the awarding of a supply, construction or partnership contract or the certification of goods or the qualification of an enterprise for the awarding of such a contract and the contract to be awarded involves an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract;

(2) their object is the awarding of a services contract or the qualification of an enterprise for the awarding of such a contract and

(a) the contract to be awarded involves an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract; or

(b) the contract to be awarded is a contract other than a services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement.

**7** The tender documents whose object is the awarding, according to an open procedure using an overall criteria evaluation system, a system in which knowledge of the price is deferred or a system adapted to the equipment or infrastructure project, of a contract referred to in section 6 and the documents published for establishing a process for the certification of goods or the qualification of an enterprise for the awarding of such a contract may provide an evaluation criterion according to which part of the goods or services must be Canadian or according to which part of the enterprises participating in the performance of the contract must have an establishment in Canada. The maximum number of points that may be assigned to the evaluation criterion may not be greater than 10% of the total number of points for all the criteria.

**8** For the purposes of sections 6 and 7, goods are deemed to be Canadian goods if assembled in Canada, even if some of their parts do not come from Canada.

### II — PROVISIONS SPECIFIC TO CERTAIN CATEGORIES OF CONTRACTS

**9** The tender documents whose object is the awarding, according to an open procedure, of a single contract providing for the design and construction of a transportation infrastructure may require that the engineering services related to the contract be provided by an enterprise having an establishment in Canada.

**10** The tender documents whose object is the awarding, according to an open procedure, of a contract for the operation of equipment or infrastructure may require that a service intended for the public be provided by an enterprise having an establishment in Canada.

**11** The tender documents whose object is the awarding, according to an open procedure, of a contract for the acquisition of mass transit vehicles may require that a percentage that does not exceed 25% of the total contract be subcontracted in Canada and that the vehicles’ final assembly be included in the subcontracted work.

For the purposes of the first paragraph, assembly includes

(1) the installation and interconnection of the following parts:

(a) engine, propulsion control system and auxiliary power;

(b) transmission;

(c) axles, suspension or differential;

(d) brake system;

(e) ventilation, heating or air conditioning system;

(f) frames;

(g) pneumatic or electrical systems;

(h) door system;

(i) passenger seats and handrails;

(j) information and destination indicator system and remote monitoring system;

(k) wheelchair access ramp;

(2) the vehicles’ final inspection, test and final preparation for delivery.

## III — DISCRIMINATION ON THE BASIS OF TERRITORY ALLOWED WHEN AWARDING A CONTRACT BY WRITTEN INVITATION

**12** The tender documents sent for the awarding of a contract by written invitation may provide a criterion that discriminates on the basis of territory, in particular as regards the origin of all or part of the goods or services required for the performance of the contract.
