# Regulation respecting the conditions and procedures for the use of detection systems

*CQLR c C-24.2, r 9*

*Enabling statute: Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2, s. 519.82)*

*Updated to 2025-03-29*

**1** A detection system approved by the Minister of Transport and the Minister of Public Security pursuant to the second paragraph of section 519.79 of the Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2) is the subject of

(1) a validation

(a) in the 6 months preceding the date of its use;

(b) by a peace officer who has received appropriate training;

(c) allowing to ensure

(i) using an external device or system, that the accuracy of the speed it records complies with the manufacturer’s specifications for the device or system;

(ii) that the information referred to in section 3 or 4 of the Regulation respecting the application of various provisions concerning detection systems (chapter C-24.2, r. 1.001), as the case may be, other than speed, and that appears on the images obtained by the device or system is accurate;

(2) (paragraph revoked);

(3) testing

(a) in the 36 hours preceding its use and in the 36 hours following its use;

(b) the result of which, read by a peace officer who has received appropriate training, shows that it is in good working order at the place where it is used;

(4) (paragraph revoked).

**2** Each approved detection system used in accordance with section 1 must be registered in a register kept by the Sûreté du Québec, which must contain the following information in respect of each device or system:

(1) the make, the manufacturer’s name, as well as the model, where applicable;

(2) the identification number;

(3) the date of each validation referred to in paragraph 1 of section 1, the result obtained and the name of the peace officer who carried out the validation;

(4) (subparagraph revoked);

(5) the date and time of each test referred to in paragraph 3 of section 1, the result obtained and the name of the peace officer who read the result;

(6) the date and result of the inspections made to ensure the good working order of the device or system, as well as the date and a description of any repairs made;

(7) the identity of the author of each entry in the register.

The documents related to the validation, testing, inspections and repair of the device or system are entered in a register kept by the Sûreté du Québec.

Only peace officers may make entries in a register that must be kept under this section.

**3** (Revoked).

**4** (Omitted).
