## I — ANNUAL FEES

**1** The fees payable for the issuance and renewal of a representative’s certificate are $116 for each sector or sector class in which a representative is authorized to act.

Where a representative is authorized to act in the sector of mortgage brokerage, he must pay an additional fee of $302 for the issuance of his certificate and an additional annual fee of $302 for its renewal.

**2** The fees payable to register as a firm or an independent partnership with the Autorité des marchés financiers and to maintain such registration annually are $116 per sector for each representative through which the firm or independent partnership pursues or intends to pursue activities.

**3** The fees payable to register as an independent representative with the Authority and to maintain such registration annually are $116 per sector or sector class in which the representative is authorized to act.

## I.1 — CONTRIBUTION TO THE FONDS D’INDEMNISATION DES SERVICES FINANCIERS

**3.1** The contribution to be paid by an independent representative and by a firm or an independent partnership to the Fonds d’indemnisation des services financiers for each representative through whom the firm or the independent partnership pursues or intends to pursue its activities is, for each sector in which the representative is authorized to pursue activities,

(1) $160 for each of the damage insurance, the insurance of persons and the group savings plan brokerage sectors; and

(2) $100 for the other sectors.

If a representative operates in more than one sector, the contribution is discounted by $75 for each additional sector.

## II.1 — FEES PAYABLE FOR THE COMPULSORY PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT OF MORTGAGE BROKERS

**22.1** The fees for an application for recognition referred to in the Regulation respecting the compulsory professional development of mortgage brokers (chapter D-9.2, r. 13.2), including an application for recognition as a provider of professional development activities or an application for recognition of a training activity, are $264.

**22.2** The fees for any modification or renewal relating to a recognition contemplated in the Regulation respecting the compulsory professional development of mortgage brokers (chapitre D-9.2, r. 13.2), are $132.

## III — INDEXATION

**23** Annual fees and other fees payable are adjusted annually on 1 January of each year in accordance with the rate of increase of the general consumer price index for Canada for the period ending on 30 September of the preceding year, as determined by Statistics Canada. They are rounded down to the nearest dollar if they include a fraction of a dollar lower than $0.50 and rounded up to the nearest dollar if they include a fraction of a dollar that is equal to or greater than $0.50.

The result of the annual indexation is published yearly in Part 1 of the *Gazette officielle du Québec* and in the Bulletin referred to in section 193 of the Act.
