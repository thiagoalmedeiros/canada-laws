# Regulation respecting the location of old car dumps, and the installations of posters, signboards and luminous signs along autoroutes

*CQLR c V-9, r 2*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting roads (chapter V-9, s. 41)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The distance within which land may not be used as old car dumps along an autoroute or a connecting highway is 300 m from the right-of-way of the autoroute or connecting highway.

**2** (Revoked implicitly by 1988, chapter 14, s. 34).
