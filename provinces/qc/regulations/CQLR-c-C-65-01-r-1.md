# Regulation respecting the awarding of certain contracts of municipal bodies according to a procedure by written invitation or by mutual agreement

Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/C-65.01,%20r.%201) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c C-65.01, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01, s. 33, 1st par., subpar. 5, and s. 261)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

## I — OBJECT AND DEFINITIONS

**1** This Regulation determines the conditions on which a contract may be awarded, despite sections 29 and 30 of the Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01), according to a procedure by written invitation or by mutual agreement and the categories of enterprises to which a contract may be awarded according to such a procedure.

For the purposes of this Regulation, a municipal body that is not subject to an intergovernmental agreement is considered to be a local municipality for the purposes of the rules set out in the agreement.

**2** For the purposes of this Regulation,

**Canada-European Union Agreement** means the Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement between Canada and the European Union and its Member States;

**services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement** means a services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement within the meaning of the Regulation respecting the threshold and time periods applicable when awarding certain contracts of municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01, r. 4).

## II — CONDITIONS ON WHICH CERTAIN CONTRACTS MAY BE AWARDED BY WRITTEN INVITATION OR BY MUTUAL AGREEMENT

**3** A supply contract may be awarded by written invitation or by mutual agreement if

(1) it is awarded to a non-profit organization and involves an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract; or

(2) it is related to the cultural sector.

**4** A construction contract or a service contract may be awarded by written invitation or by mutual agreement if

(1) it is a contract whose object is the performance of work to remove, move or reconstruct mains or installations for waterworks, sewers, electricity, gas, steam, telecommunications, oil or other fluids and is awarded to the owner of the mains or installations, or to a public utility for a price corresponding to their fair market value; or

(2) it is a contract whose object is the performance of work on a railway right-of-way used as such and is awarded to the owner or operator of the railway for a price corresponding to their fair market value.

**5** A services contract may be awarded by written invitation or by mutual agreement if

(1) it is awarded to a non-profit organization and

(a) it is not a services contract covered by the Canada-European Union Agreement; or

(b) it involves an expenditure below the threshold from which the municipal body must, under the Canada-European Union Agreement, offer to enterprises that have an establishment in a territory covered by the agreement to submit a tender for such a contract;

(2) it is awarded to a solidarity cooperative designated by the Minister under subparagraph 5 of the first paragraph of section 4 of the Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01) whose articles include a clause prohibiting the allotment of rebates or the payment of interest on any category of preferred shares unless the rebate is allotted or the interest is paid to a municipality, the Union des municipalités du Québec or the Fédération québécoise des municipalités locales et régionales (FQM);

(3) it is related to the cultural sector;

(4) it is a contract whose object is the supply of media space for the purposes of a publicity campaign or for promotional purposes;

(5) it is a bulk trucking contract awarded through the holder of a brokerage permit issued under the Transport Act (chapter T-12);

(6) it is a financial services contract that is not an insurance contract;

(7) it is a contract whose object is the supply of professional services necessary for the purposes of a proceeding before a tribunal, or a body or person exercising judicial or adjudicative functions;

(8) it is a contract whose object is the supply of professional services that may only be rendered by a health and social services professional who is a member of a professional order referred to in the Professional Code (chapter C-26); or

(9) it is an insurance contract awarded by a public transit authority or by the Réseau de transport métropolitain.

**6** Supply or cloud services contracts may be awarded by mutual agreement to an enterprise that is a party to a framework agreement entered into with the Minister of Cybersecurity and Digital Technology when

(1) the contract concerns goods or services referred to in the framework agreement;

(2) the term of the contract, including any renewal, does not exceed 3 years;

(3) the enterprise retained is the one whose tender is the most advantageous based on the price of the contract or any other criteria related to the object of the contract, such as technological compatibility, accessibility of goods or services, performance and technical assistance; and

(4) the goods or services referred to in the framework agreement take into account the applicable criteria related to security, levels of services and compliance.

**7** A contract whose object is the supply of professional services that may only be rendered by an advocate or a notary may be awarded

(1) by written invitation if the contract involves an expenditure equal to or above the threshold determined for the purposes of section 29 of the Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01); or

(2) by mutual agreement if the contract involves an expenditure below that threshold.

**8** A contract for professional services may be awarded by mutual agreement to an enterprise that drew up plans and specifications for the municipal body under a contract that was awarded to the enterprise according to an open procedure or by written invitation if the object of the contract is the adaptation or modification of those plans and specifications for the carrying out of the work for the purposes of which they were drawn up or the supervision of the work related to such adaptation or modification, or an extension of the duration of the work.

**9** A supply or services contract involving an expenditure below the threshold determined for the purposes of section 29 of the Act respecting contracting by municipal bodies (chapter C-65.01) may be awarded by mutual agreement to a solidarity cooperative whose articles include a clause prohibiting the allotment of rebates or the payment of interest on any category of preferred shares unless the rebate is allotted or the interest is paid to a municipality, the Union des municipalités du Québec or the Fédération québécoise des municipalités locales et régionales (FQM).

## III — FINAL

**10** The order of the Minister of Municipal Affairs and Greater Montréal dated 10 December 1999 (1999, G.O. 2, 6945, French only) and the order of the Minister of Municipal Affairs, Sports and Recreation dated 1 September 2004 (2004, G.O. 2, 2622) are revoked.

**11** (Omitted).
