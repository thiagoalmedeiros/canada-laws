# Regulation respecting the promise and awarding of grants

*CQLR c A-6.01, r 6*

*Enabling statute: Public Administration Act (chapter A-6.01, s. 57)*

*Updated to 2026-06-04*

**1** This Regulation applies to departments and budget-funded bodies.

**2** (Revoked).

**3** Subject to section 4, any award or any promise of grant must be submitted for authorization:

(a) to the Government, on the proposal of the Conseil du trésor, where the amount of such award or promise is equal to or greater than $3,000,000;

(b) of the Conseil du trésor, where the amount of such award or promise is less than $3,000,000 but greater than $100,000.

**4** The award or promise of grant does not require the authorization prescribed in section 3 in the following cases:

(a) where a legislative provision establishes the amount thereof;

(b) where it is made in accordance with standards approved by the Government or the Conseil du trésor.

Notwithstanding subparagraph *b* of the first paragraph, the awarding or promise of a grant shall be authorized in accordance with section 3 in either of the following cases:

(a) where a grant of more than $100,000 is awarded or promised to a profit-oriented organization that has more than 100 employees and where the standards approved do not include the obligation to implement an affirmative action program that complies with the Charter of human rights and freedoms (chapter C-12); or

(b) (subparagraph revoked);

(c) where a grant, intended for the carrying out of construction work involving an expenditure equal to or greater than the lowest minimum threshold from among those provided for in any intergovernmental agreements and that are applicable to departments and budget-funded bodies for construction work contracts, is awarded or promised, except to a public body within the meaning of section 4 of the Act respecting contracting by public bodies (chapter C-65.1), and where the standards approved do not include the obligation to make a public call for tenders for awarding the contract, unless the standards expressly provide that the obligation to make a public call for tenders does not apply.

**5** The awarding or promise of a grant made in accordance with standards approved by the Government or the Conseil du trésor before 1 October 2008 is not subject to the authorization now required under subparagraph *c* of the second paragraph of section 4 where the obligation to make a public call for tenders is imposed in the conditions of the awarding or promise of a grant.
