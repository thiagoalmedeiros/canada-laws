# Regulation authorizing certain psychiatric establishments to receive and treat persons detained under the Criminal Code or any penal law

Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/M-19.2,%20r.%201) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c M-19.2, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the Ministère de la Santé et des Services sociaux (chapter M-19.2, s. 10.2)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** Hôpital Louis H. Lafontaine and Centre hospitalier Robert Giffard, in Montréal and Québec City respectively, are authorized to receive and treat persons detained under the Criminal Code (R.S.C. 1985, c. C-46) or any penal law, the whole in accordance with section 10.2 of the Act respecting the Ministère de la Santé et des Services sociaux (chapter M-19.2).

> The Institut Philippe Pinel has the same authorization (O.C. 539-73).
