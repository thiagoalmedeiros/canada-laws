# Regulation respecting the issuance and renewal of permits for institutions

*CQLR c S-5, r 2*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting health services and social services for Cree Native persons (chapter S-5, s. 173)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

## I — GENERAL

**1** Application for the issue or renewal of a permit to operate an institution shall be made to the Minister of Health and Social Services by filling the form prescribed in Schedule I.

Application for the issue of a permit must be accompanied by a statement of the objects pursued and of the means to attain them. In the case of a legal person, a resolution of the board of directors authorizing the application must be attached to the form and, in the case of a partnership or a person operating an enterprise under a name which does not include the person’s surname and given name, a certified copy of the declaration required under the Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises (chapter P-44.1) dated less than a month before the date of the application must also be attached.

**2** Application for renewal of a permit must be submitted at least 6 months before the date of its expiry.

**3** Any applicant for the issue or renewal of a permit must:

(1) be a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident within the meaning of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (S.C. 2001, c. 27);

(2) be domiciled in Québec;

(3) be solvent;

(4) not have received a sentence under the Criminal Code (R.S.C. 1985, c. 46) which has not been satisfied;

(5) forward to the Minister any information he may require.

**4** Application for a first operating permit for a private institution not covered by sections 176 and 177 of the Act respecting health services and social services for Cree Native persons (chapter S-5) must be accompanied by budget estimates for the first 2 years of operation.

**5** A legal person may not apply for the issue or renewal of a permit if any of its members is:

(1) a person whose sentence under the Criminal Code (R.S.C. 1985, c. 46) has not been satisfied;

(2) a person who, during the preceding 3 years, has been the subject of disciplinary measures under section 170 of the Act or of a fine under sections 179 and 180 of the Act.

**6** The permit holder must post his permit in the institution.

He must also notify the Minister of any change of address.
