# Regulation respecting the rate per cubic metre of timber applicable to the computation of the contribution payable to a regional agency for private forest development by holders of a wood processing plant operating permit

Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/A-18.1,%20r.%2013) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c A-18.1, r 13*

*Enabling statute: Sustainable Forest Development Act (chapter A-18.1, s. 173)*

*Updated to 2013-04-01*

> The former alphanumerical designation of this Regulation was: chapter F-4.1, r. 14.

**1** The rate per cubic metre of timber applicable to the contribution payable to a regional agency for private forest development by holders of a wood processing plant operating permit under section 162 of the Sustainable Forest Development Act (chapter A-18.1) shall be $1.

**2** The report referred to in section 163 of the Act shall be filed by the permit holder on the first of each month.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, a permit holder who has purchased a volume of timber of less than 100 m^*3* originating from the territory of an agency during a reference period for which he must normally file a report referred to in the first paragraph, may file his report only at the end of the reference period during which he completes that minimum purchase. However, he shall send his report not later than on 1 March.

**3** The first report of a permit holder following the establishment of an agency on the territory of which he has purchased timber shall be filed not later than on 1 March following such establishment, if the second paragraph of section 2 applies to him.

**4** (Omitted).
