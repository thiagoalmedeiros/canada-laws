# Group of Seven Day Act, 2024

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/statute/24g35) before relying on it.

S.O. 2024, c. 35

His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of the Province of Ontario, enacts as follows:

### Group of Seven Day

1 July 7 in each year is proclaimed as Group of Seven Day.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Act).

3 Omitted (enacts short title of this Act).
