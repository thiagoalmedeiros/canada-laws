# Ontario University Athletics Week Act, 2025

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/statute/25o18) before relying on it.

S.O. 2025, c. 18

His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of the Province of Ontario, enacts as follows:

### Ontario University Athletics Week

1 The first full week of October in each year is proclaimed as Ontario University Athletics Week.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Act).

3 Omitted (enacts short title of this Act).
