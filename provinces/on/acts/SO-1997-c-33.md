# Geographic Township of Creighton-Davies Act, 1997

S.O. 1997, c. 33

### Name changed

1 The name of the geographic township of Creighton in the Territorial District of Sudbury is changed to Creighton-Davies.
2 Omitted (amends or repeals other Acts).
3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Act).
4 Omitted (enacts short title of this Act).
