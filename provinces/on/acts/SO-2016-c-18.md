# Treaties Recognition Week Act, 2016

S.O. 2016, c. 18

### Treaties Recognition Week

1 The week beginning on the first Sunday in November in each year is proclaimed as Treaties Recognition Week.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Act).
3 Omitted (enacts short title of this Act).
