# DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY — CONDOMINIUM DESCRIPTIONS — TOWN OF ST. MARYS

O. Reg. 419/01

### Delegation

1 The authority of the Minister under section 9 of the Condominium Act, 1998, or a predecessor of it, to approve or exempt a condominium description is delegated to the council of the Town of St. Marys with respect to applications whose file numbers are set out in the Schedule.

### Subdelegation

2 (1) If the authority delegated under section 1 is in turn delegated to a committee of the council or to an appointed officer under subsection 5 (1) of the Act, the council shall forward to the Minister a certified copy of the delegating by-law within 15 days of its passing.
(2) The delegation of authority set out in this Regulation is not terminated by reason only that the condition set out in subsection (1) is not complied with.
3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
