# COUNCIL COMPOSITION — REGIONAL MUNICIPALITY OF DURHAM

O. Reg. 3/12

### Municipality may exercise power

1 The council of The Regional Municipality of Durham may exercise its power under section 218 of the Act to change the method of selecting the head of the council.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
