# RESTRICTED TOWING ZONES

O. Reg. 217/25

### Restricted towing zones

1 (1) The following highways or parts of highways are designated as restricted towing zones in each direction of travel:
1. Highway 401 from Highway 400 to Highway 404.
2. Highway 401 from Highway 404 to Morningside Avenue.
3. Highway 404 from Highway 401 to Major Mackenzie Drive.
4. Highway 401 from Morningside Avenue to Highway 412.
5. Highway 401 from Dixie Road to James Snow Parkway.
6. Highway 401 from James Snow Parkway to Highway 6 South.
7. Highway 410 from Highway 401 to Hurontario Street.
8. Highway 403 from the QEW to Highway 401.
9. Highway 400 from Highway 401 to Highway 9.
10. Highway 401 from Dixie Road to Islington Avenue.
11. Highway 401 from Islington Avenue to Highway 400.
12. Highway 409 from Highway 427 to Highway 401.
13. Highway 427 from Highway 409 to the QEW.
14. Highway 427 from Highway 409 to Major Mackenzie Drive.
15. QEW from Highway 427 to Third Line.
16. Highway 403 from Third Line to Highway 6 North.
17. QEW from Highway 403 to Fifty Road.
(2) A restricted towing zone referred to in subsection (1) includes all ramps to and from a highway or part of a highway designated in that subsection, other than ramps to and from the private toll highway known as Highway 407, but does not include any ONRoute rest stop, truck inspection station or carpool lot.
