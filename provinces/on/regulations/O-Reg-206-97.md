# WASTE DISPOSAL SITES, WASTE MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS AND SEWAGE WORKS SUBJECT TO APPROVAL UNDER OR EXEMPT FROM THE ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT ACT

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/970206) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 206/97

1 A waste disposal site, waste management system or sewage works is exempt from being the subject matter of a hearing under section 20.15 of the Environmental Protection Act if it is or forms part of an undertaking or project that,

(a) is subject to Part II.3 of the Environmental Assessment Act; or

(b) is subject to Part II.1 of the Environmental Assessment Act under section 15 of that Act.

2 Revoked: O. Reg. 247/11, s. 3.
