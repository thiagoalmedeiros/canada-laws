# Application of Not-for-Profit Corporations Act, 2010

O. Reg. 389/95

### Borrowing

1 For the sole purpose of borrowing money from the Province of Ontario or a provincial agency, subsection 15 (1) of the Not-for-Profit Corporations Act, 2010 applies to the Centre.
