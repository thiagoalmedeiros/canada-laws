### Designation

1 The following products, in all their forms and however they are grown, are designated as agricultural products for the purposes of the Act:

1. Apple trees.

2. Apples, other than crab apples.

3. Asparagus.

4. Barley.

5. Apis Mellifera (bees).

6. Bok choy.

7. Broad beans.

8. Broccoli.

9. Brussels sprouts.

10. Cabbage.

11. Canola, including rapeseed.

12. Carrots.

13. Cauliflower.

14. Celeriac.

15. Celery.

16. Cherries.

16.1 Cherry trees.

17. Chinese cabbage.

18. Coloured beans.

19. Corn.

20. Cucumbers.

21. Eggplant.

22. Flaxseed, including solin.

23. Forage.

24. French shallots.

25. Gai lan.

26. Garlic.

27. Ginseng.

28. Grapes.

29. Grapevines.

30. Green and wax beans.

31. Green peas.

32. Hemp.

33. Honey.

34. Kale.

35. Leeks.

36. Lettuce.

37. Lima beans.

38. Melons.

39. Mesclun.

40. Mustard greens.

40.1 Nectarine trees.

41. Nectarines.

42. Oats.

43. Onions.

44. Parsnips.

44.1 Peach trees.

45. Peaches.

46. Peanuts.

46.1 Pear trees.

47. Pears.

48. Peppers.

48.1 Plum trees.

49. Plums.

50. Potatoes.

51. Pumpkins.

52. Radishes.

53. Red Beets.

54. Rutabagas.

54.1 Rye.

55. Soybeans.

56. Spinach.

57. Squash.

58. Strawberries.

59. Sugar beets.

60. Sunflowers.

61. Sweet corn.

62. Sweet potatoes.

63. Tobacco.

64. Tomatoes.

65. Turnips.

66. Watermelons.

67. Wheat.

68. White beans.

69. Winter spelt.

70. Yellow mustard.

71. Yu choy.

72. Zucchini.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
