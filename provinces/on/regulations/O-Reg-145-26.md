# EXCLUSION OF CERTAIN LANDLORDS

O. Reg. 145/26

### Meaning of landlord, exclusions

1 Despite subsection 1 (2) of the Act, a person is not a landlord for the purposes of the Act in respect of the following premises:
1. Premises providing homelessness prevention and support services, including emergency shelter providers and providers of health and social services.
2. A retirement home, as defined in subsection 2 (1) of the Retirement Homes Act, 2010.
3. A long-term care home, as defined in subsection 2 (1) of the Fixing Long-Term Care Act, 2021.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
