# PRESCRIBED MATTERS — SUBSECTION 15 (5) OF ACT

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/060587) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 587/06

### Prescribed matter

1 The following matter is prescribed for the purposes of subsection 15 (5) of the Act:

1. Any matter with respect to which a by-law could have been passed under the Act, other than sections 8 and 11, as it read on the day before section 11 of Schedule A to the Municipal Statute Law Amendment Act, 2006 came into force.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
