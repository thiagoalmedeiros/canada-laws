# NUMBER OF SHARES ISSUED TO THE HOSPITALS

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/990503) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 503/99

1 For the purpose of subsection 4 (2) of the Act, the number of common shares of the Corporation that are deemed under subsection 4 (1) of the Act to have been issued to each of the Hospitals is as follows:

1. To Mount Sinai Hospital, 427 common shares.

2. To Sunnybrook and Women’s College Health Sciences Centre, 258.8 common shares.

3. To The Hospital for Sick Children, 625.6 common shares.

4. To University Health Network (formerly named The Toronto Hospital), 688.6 common shares.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
