# DEEMING ORDER (CITY OF HAMILTON)

O. Reg. 100/13

### Deemed zoning by-law, O. Reg. 138/10

1 Ontario Regulation 138/10, as it read immediately before this Regulation comes into force, shall be deemed to be and to always have been a by-law passed under section 34 of the Act by the City of Hamilton.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
