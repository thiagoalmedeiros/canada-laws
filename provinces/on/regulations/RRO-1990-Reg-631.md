R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 631

1 The intersections on highways in territory without municipal organization that are described in paragraph 1 of each Schedule are designated as intersections at which yield right-of-way signs shall be erected to face traffic travelling in the direction referred to in paragraph 2 of each Schedule.

2 Where a highway is referred to in a Schedule by a number or name, the reference is to that part of the highway in a territory without municipal organization that is known thereby.
