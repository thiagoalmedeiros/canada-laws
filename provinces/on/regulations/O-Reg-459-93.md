# CAPITAL GRANTS AND LOANS

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/930459) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 459/93

1 In this Regulation,

“eligible capital costs” are capital costs that are eligible, under the policies of the Ministry of Health, for funding under this Regulation.

2 (1) The Minister may pay provincial aid by way of a grant to a hospital for the eligible capital costs of a project.

(2) The Minister may make a loan to a hospital for the eligible capital costs of a project.

3 A grant or loan may be made only if the project has been approved by the Minister.

4 (1) Except in the circumstances set out in subsection (2), the amount of a grant or loan may not exceed two-thirds of the eligible capital costs of the project.

(2) The amount of a grant or loan may be up to the eligible capital costs of the project if the project is eligible, under the policies of the Ministry of Health, for funding above the level allowed under subsection (1).

5 (1) A grant or loan may be paid in a lump sum or in instalments.

(2) Payments may be made before or after the eligible capital costs are incurred.

6 It is a term of every loan that the Province pay to the hospital the amount of each payment required to repay the loan.
