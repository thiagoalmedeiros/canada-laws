# NON-APPLICATION OF PART III OF THE MUNICIPAL AFFAIRS ACT

O. Reg. 338/02

### Non-application

1 For the purposes of section 5 of the Act, Part III of the Municipal Affairs Act ceases to apply to the Town of Moosonee.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
