O. Reg. 77/22

### Period when no tolls payable

1 No tolls are payable on Highway 407 East during the period from June 1, 2025 to the day the Highway 407 East Act, 2012, is repealed.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
