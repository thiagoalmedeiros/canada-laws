# WARD DESCRIPTIONS

O. Reg. 403/25

### Wards, as of January 1, 2026

1 For the period commencing on January 1, 2026 and ending on the day before the day the new council of the City of Barrie is organized following the 2026 municipal election, Wards 1, 3, 4 and 5 of the City of Barrie are altered as described in Schedule 1 to this Regulation.

### Wards, after 2026 municipal election

2 (1) On the day the new council of the City of Barrie is organized following the 2026 municipal election, Wards 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 of the City of Barrie are altered as described in Schedule 2 to this Regulation.

(2) For greater certainty, the City of Barrie may divide and redivide the municipality into wards, or dissolve existing wards, in accordance with the Municipal Act, 2001 and, in that case, the ward boundaries of any such wards shall be altered or dissolved accordingly.

(3) Despite section 1, the 2026 municipal election shall be conducted as if the ward boundaries have already been altered in accordance with subsection (1) of this section.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
