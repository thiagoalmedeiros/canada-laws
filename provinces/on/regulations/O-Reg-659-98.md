# WITHDRAWAL OF DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY — CITY OF BRANTFORD

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/980659) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 659/98

1 The authority of the council of the City of Brantford under the following provisions is removed with respect to all applications made on or after December 25, 1998 for land in the City of Brantford:

1. Subsection 50 (18) of the Act, to give approvals.

2. Section 51 of the Act, to approve a plan of subdivision.

3. Sections 50 and 53 of the Act, to give consents.

4. Section 57 of the Act, to issue a certificate of validation.

5. Section 50 of the Condominium Act, to approve or exempt a condominium description.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
