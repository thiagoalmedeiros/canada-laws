# TURKEY — EXTENSION OF POWERS OF THE CANADIAN TURKEY MARKETING AGENCY

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/980070) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 70/98

1 In this Regulation,

“turkey” means a turkey, any class of turkey or part of a turkey produced in Ontario.

2 The Canadian Turkey Marketing Agency is authorized to regulate the marketing within Ontario of turkey in the manner set out in section 3.

3 For the purpose of regulating the marketing of turkey within Ontario, the Canadian Turkey Marketing Agency may exercise, with respect to the turkey marketed, the powers that it may exercise with the respect to turkey marketed in interprovincial or export trade under clauses 22 (1) (d), (j), (k) and (m) of the Farm Products Agencies Act (Canada).
