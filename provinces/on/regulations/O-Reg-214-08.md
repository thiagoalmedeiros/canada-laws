# Reliability Standards

O. Reg. 214/08

### Standards Authority

1 Northeast Power Coordinating Council, Inc. is designated as a standards authority for the purposes of the definition of “standards authority” in subsection 2 (1) of the Act.

### Board initiated review

2 For the purposes of subsection 36.2 (4) of the Act, the prescribed period is 120 days.
3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
