# WILDLIFE SCHEDULES

O. Reg. 669/98

1 The species listed in Schedule 1 are prescribed as furbearing mammals.
2 The species listed in Schedule 2 are prescribed as game mammals.
3 The species listed in Schedule 3 are prescribed as game birds.
4 The species listed in Schedule 4 are prescribed as game reptiles.
5 The species listed in Schedule 5 are prescribed as game amphibians.
6 The species listed in Schedule 6 are prescribed as specially protected mammals.
7 The species listed in Schedule 7 are prescribed as specially protected raptors.
8 The species listed in Schedule 8 are prescribed as specially protected birds.
9 The species listed in Schedule 9 are prescribed as specially protected reptiles.
10 The species listed in Schedule 10 are prescribed as specially protected amphibians.
11 The species listed in Schedule 11 are prescribed as specially protected invertebrates.
