# Ontario Local Forest management Corporations

O. Reg. 111/12

### Nawiinginokiima Forest Management Corporation established

1 An Ontario local forest management corporation to be known in English as the Nawiinginokiima Forest Management Corporation and in French as Société de gestion forestière Nawiinginokiima is established as a corporation without share capital.

### Temagami Forest Management Corporation established

1.1 An Ontario local forest management corporation to be known in English as the Temagami Forest Management Corporation and in French as Société de gestion forestière Temagami is established as a corporation without share capital.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
