# GAS WITHOUT CHARGE OR AT A REDUCED RATE

O. Reg. 100/99

1 (1) No person shall use or consume any gas that was supplied without charge or at a reduced rate under an agreement entered into with a gas distributor for which the supplying of gas without charge or at a reduced rate is a consideration.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to an agreement or renewal of an agreement made before January 1, 1955.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
