### Services that cannot be identified as a special service

1 For the purposes of clause 326 (5) (a) of the Act, the following services are prescribed as services that cannot be identified as a special service under clause 326 (1) (a) of the Act:

1. Health programs and services under Part II of the Health Protection and Promotion Act.

2 Omitted (revokes other Regulations).

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
