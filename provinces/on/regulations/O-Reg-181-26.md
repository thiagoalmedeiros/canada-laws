# ENGLISH-LANGUAGE DISTRICT SCHOOL BOARDS - DIRECTORS OF EDUCATION

O. Reg. 181/26

### Not member of board

1 The director of education of an English-language district school board is deemed not to be a member of the board for the purposes of paragraph 3 of subsection 171 (1) of the Act.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
