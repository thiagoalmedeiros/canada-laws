O. Reg. 151/26

### Interpretation

1 In this Regulation,

“local transit system” has the same meaning as in the Metrolinx Act, 2006; (“réseau local de transport en commun”)

“special constable employer” has the same meaning as in the Community Safety and Policing Act, 2019. (“employeur de constables spéciaux”)

### Definition of “officer” in the Act

2 The following provincial offences officers are prescribed for the purposes of the definition of “officer” in the Act if their employer is a special constable employer:

1. Special constables employed by Metrolinx.

2. Special constables employed by the operator of a local transit system.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
