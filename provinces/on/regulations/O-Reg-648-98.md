# DESIGNATION OF THE GENERATION CORPORATION AND THE SERVICES CORPORATION

O. Reg. 648/98

1 Ontario Power Generation Inc., incorporated under the Business Corporations Act on December 1, 1998 pursuant to subsection 48 (1) of the Electricity Act, 1998, is hereby designated as the Ontario Electricity Generation Corporation for the purposes of the Electricity Act, 1998.
2 Hydro One Inc. (formerly Ontario Hydro Services Company Inc.), incorporated under the Business Corporations Act on December 1, 1998 pursuant to subsection 48 (1) of the Electricity Act, 1998, is hereby designated as the Ontario Electric Services Corporation for the purposes of the Electricity Act, 1998.
