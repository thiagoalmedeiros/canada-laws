(2) The date prescribed in respect of the land described in paragraph 1 of subsection (1) is February 13, 2026.
(3) In respect of the land described in paragraphs 2 and 3 of subsection (1), the following apply:
1. The date of vesting is February 13, 2026 and the vesting period shall end on March 31, 2029.
2. At the end of the vesting period, the land, including any improvements to it, shall vest in the City of Toronto.

### Combined sewer outflow

3 (1) Any structure, fixture or other thing that is part of or associated with combined sewer outflow infrastructure located within the land described in paragraph 2 of subsection 2 (1) that was, immediately before the date mentioned in subsection 2 (2), under the control of the City of Toronto shall remain under the City’s control.
(2) The City of Toronto shall take the following actions with respect to the vested real property described in subsection (1):
1. Any action required to maintain, operate, repair or replace any of the vested real property.
2. Ensure that all permits, licences and approvals issued by the City that are necessary to complete the actions described in paragraph 1 are issued.
(3) Despite subsection (2), no action requiring access to the land described in subsection 2 (1) shall be taken without the written permission of the Minister.
(4) If the Crown incurs costs in respect of any of the actions described in subsection (2), the Crown may recover such costs from the City of Toronto.
