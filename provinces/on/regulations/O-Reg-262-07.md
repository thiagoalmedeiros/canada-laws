O. Reg. 262/07

### Fees for incorporation

1 The following fees shall be paid for the incorporation of an organization under the Act:

1. $100 for agricultural associations.

2. $25 for agricultural societies.

3. $25 for horticultural societies.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
