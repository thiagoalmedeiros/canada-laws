# general

R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 831

1 (1) The fee for a commission appointing a licensee under the Law Society Act as a notary public is $145.
(2) The fee for a commission appointing a person other than a licensee under the Law Society Act as a notary public is $110.
(3) The fee for a commission reappointing a person other than a licensee under the Law Society Act as a notary public is $75.
2 Section 1 does not apply to a commission appointing as a notary public for Ontario a person who is an employee of,
(a) the Government of Canada;
(b) the Government of Ontario;
(c) a municipality in Ontario; or
(d) the band of a First Nation in Ontario.
3 A 10-year period is prescribed for the purposes of subsections 5 (1) and (2) of the Act.
