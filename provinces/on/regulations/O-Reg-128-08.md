# INVESTMENT AND LENDING ACTIVITIES — FIRE MUTUALS GUARANTEE FUND

O. Reg. 128/08

### Old investment rules apply

1 The assets of the Fire Mutuals Guarantee Fund shall be invested in the same manner and subject to the same limitations that apply to a joint stock insurance company, other than a company licensed to transact the business of life insurance, under the provisions of the Acts and the regulations that are the old investment rules for the purposes of section 431.1 of the Act.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
