# FARMLAND AND MANAGED FOREST LAND FOR 1998

O. Reg. 497/98

1 For the purposes of subsection 23 (6) of the Act,
“farmland” means land that is farmland for the purposes of subsection 21 (5) of the Provincial Land Tax Act;
“managed forest land” means land that is managed forest land for the purposes of subsection 21 (5) of the Provincial Land Tax Act.
