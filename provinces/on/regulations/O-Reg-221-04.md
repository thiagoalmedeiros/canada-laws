# DISSOLUTION OF ST. MICHAEL’S HOSPITAL CROWN FOUNDATION

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/040221) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 221/04

### Dissolution of foundation

1 The crown foundation established by order of the Lieutenant Governor in Council dated December 4, 1996 under section 2 of the Act and known as the “St. Michael’s Hospital Crown Foundation” is hereby dissolved.

### Transfer of assets

2 Upon the dissolution of the “St. Michael’s Hospital Crown Foundation” under section 1, the assets of the foundation become the assets of the St. Michael’s Hospital Foundation.
