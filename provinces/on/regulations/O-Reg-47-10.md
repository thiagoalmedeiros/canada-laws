O. Reg. 47/10

### Penalties re notices of contravention

1 (1) The following penalties are prescribed for the purposes of section 27 of the Act:

| Item | Column 1 Contravention | Column 2 Penalty, in dollars |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | If the notice relates to a contravention of subsection 9 (1) or (2) of the Act in respect of a passport or work permit | 100,000 |
| 2. | If the notice relates to the second contravention in a three-year period of subsection 9 (1) or (2) of the Act in respect of a passport or work permit | 150,000 |
| 3. | If the notice relates to the third or subsequent contravention in a three-year period of subsection 9 (1) or (2) of the Act in respect of a passport or work permit | 200,000 |
| 4. | If the notice relates to a contravention of a provision of the Act other than subsection 9 (1) or (2) in respect of a passport or work permit | 250 |
| 5. | If the notice relates to the second contravention in a three-year period of a provision of the Act other than subsection 9 (1) or (2) in respect of a passport or work permit | 500 |
| 6. | If the notice relates to the third or subsequent contravention in a three-year period of a provision of the Act other than subsection 9 (1) or (2) in respect of a passport or work permit | 1,000 |
| 7. | If the notice relates to a contravention of subsection 9 (1) or (2) of the Act in respect of a passport or work permit and the contravention affects more than one foreign national | 100,000, multiplied by the number of foreign nationals affected |
| 8. | If the notice relates to the second contravention in a three-year period of subsection 9 (1) or (2) of the Act in respect of a passport or work permit and the contravention affects more than one foreign national | 150,000, multiplied by the number of foreign nationals affected |
| 9. | If the notice relates to the third or subsequent contravention in a three-year period of subsection 9 (1) or (2) of the Act in respect of a passport or work permit and the contravention affects more than one foreign national | 200,000, multiplied by the number of foreign nationals affected |
| 10. | If the notice relates to a contravention of a provision of the Act other than subsection 9 (1) or (2) in respect of a passport or work permit or section 14 or 15, and the contravention affects more than one foreign national | 250, multiplied by the number of foreign nationals affected |
| 11. | If the notice relates to the second contravention in a three-year period of a provision of the Act other than subsection 9 (1) or (2) in respect of a passport or work permit or section 14 or 15, and the contravention affects more than one foreign national | 500, multiplied by the number of foreign nationals affected |
| 12. | If the notice relates to the third or subsequent contravention in a three-year period of a provision of the Act other than subsection 9 (1) or (2) in respect of a passport or work permit or section 14 or 15, and the contravention affects more than one foreign national | 1,000, multiplied by the number of foreign nationals affected |

(2) Revoked: O. Reg. 349/15, s. 1 (2).

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
