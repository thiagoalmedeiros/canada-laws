# DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY — PARRY SOUND AREA PLANNING BOARD

O. Reg. 418/01

### Delegation

1 The Minister’s authority under section 51 of the Act to approve a plan of subdivision is delegated to the Parry Sound Area Planning Board with respect to applications whose file numbers are set out in the Schedule.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
