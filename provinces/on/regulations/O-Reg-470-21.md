# AUTHORIZED PRESCRIBERS

O. Reg. 470/21

### Authorized prescribers

1 A physician or nurse who is authorized under the laws of Manitoba or Quebec to give a prescription within the scope of their practice is prescribed as an authorized prescriber for the purposes of section 16 of the Act.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
