O. Reg. 325/03

### Dissolution of foundation

1 The crown foundation established by order of the Lieutenant Governor in Council under section 2 of the Act and known as the “CIAR” foundation is hereby dissolved.

### Transfer of assets

2 Upon the dissolution of the “CIAR” foundation under section 1, the assets of the foundation become the assets of the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research.
