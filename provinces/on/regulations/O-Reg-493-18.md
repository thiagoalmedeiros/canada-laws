# THE 2019 ROUND OF COLLECTIVE BARGAINING

O. Reg. 493/18

### Application and Timing

### Application

0.1 This Regulation applies with respect to the round of collective bargaining that begins in 2019.

### Dates re employee bargaining agencies

1 (1) For the purposes of subsection 20 (5) of the Act, the deadline for applying to the Ontario Labour Relations Board for advice and notifying the Minister that an application has been made is January 9, 2019.
(2) For the purposes of subsection 20 (8) of the Act, the deadline for applying to the Ontario Labour Relations Board for an order and notifying the Minister that an application has been made is January 28, 2019.
(3) For the purposes of subsection 20.1 (5) of the Act, the period within which the notices mentioned in subsections 20.1 (2), (3) and (4) of the Act may be given starts on the day this Regulation is filed and ends on December 31, 2018.

### Notice of desire to bargain

2 A notice of desire to bargain under section 59 of the Labour Relations Act, 1995 may be given on or after April 29, 2019.

### Central Tables — Employees Other Than Teachers

### CUPE central table

3 (1) The Canadian Union of Public Employees (“CUPE”) is designated under subsection 20 (2) of the Act as the employee bargaining agency for the employees of every school board in every bargaining unit for which CUPE or one of its affiliated trade unions is the bargaining agent.
(2) A council of the following trustees’ associations is designated under subsection 21 (6) of the Act as the employer bargaining agency for a central table for central bargaining with respect to employees in the bargaining units for which CUPE is designated under subsection (1) of this section as the employee bargaining agency:
1. L’Association des conseils scolaires des écoles publiques de l’Ontario.
2. L’Association franco-ontarienne des conseils scolaires catholiques.
3. Ontario Catholic School Trustees’ Association.
4. Ontario Public School Boards’ Association.
(3) A central table is established under subsection 23 (2) of the Act for central bargaining by CUPE with respect to all of the bargaining units for which it is designated under subsection (1) of this section as the employee bargaining agency.
