# BLUE BOX WASTE

O. Reg. 386/16

### Blue box waste materials

1 Waste that consists of any of the following materials, or any combination of them, is prescribed as blue box waste for the purposes of the Act:
1. Glass.
2. Metal.
3. Paper.
4. Plastic.
5. Textiles.
2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
