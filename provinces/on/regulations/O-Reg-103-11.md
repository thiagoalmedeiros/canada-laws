# DEEMING ORDER (CENTRAL ALMAGUIN PLANNING BOARD)

O. Reg. 103/11

### Deemed zoning by-law, O. Reg. 501/96

1 (1) Ontario Regulation 501/96, as it read immediately before this Regulation comes into force, shall be deemed to be and to always have been a by-law passed under section 34 of the Act by the Central Almaguin Planning Board.

(2) The deemed by-law shall be referred to as by-law number 2011-01 of the Central Almaguin Planning Board.

### Deemed zoning by-law, O. Reg. 516/98

2 (1) Ontario Regulation 516/98, as it read immediately before this Regulation comes into force, shall be deemed to be and to always have been a by-law passed under section 34 of the Act by the Central Almaguin Planning Board.

(2) The deemed by-law shall be referred to as by-law number 2011-02 of the Central Almaguin Planning Board.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
