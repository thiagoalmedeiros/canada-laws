# TRANSFER OF FUNCTIONS

R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 998

1 Revoked: O. Reg. 383/16, s. 1.

2 (1) The functions of the Director of Land Registration referred to in the sections set out in Column 2 of Schedule 2 of the Acts set out opposite thereto in Column 1 of Schedule 2 are transferred to the Director of Titles.

(2) Despite subsection (1), the Director of Land Registration shall retain concurrent authority to perform the functions transferred under subsection (1).
