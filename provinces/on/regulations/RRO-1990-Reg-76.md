R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 76

1 The following fees are payable to the Crown by a commissioner appointed under section 4 of the Act:

1. For an appointment, $75.

2. For a renewal of an appointment, $50.

2 Section 1 does not apply to the appointment or the renewal of an appointment of a commissioner who is an employee of,

(a) the Government of Canada;

(b) the Government of Ontario;

(c) a municipality in Ontario;

(d) a children’s aid society under the Child, Youth and Family Services Act, 2017; or

(e) the band of a First Nation in Ontario.

3 A 10-year period is prescribed for the purposes of subsections 4 (3) and (3.1) of the Act.
