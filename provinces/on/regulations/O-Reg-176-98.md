# COST OF LIVING ADJUSTMENTS — METHODS OF CALCULATION

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/980176) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 176/98

1 The following manners of calculating cost of living adjustments required by a support order or a support deduction order are prescribed for the purposes of subsections 7 (4) to (7) of the Act:

1. A calculation made by applying cost of living adjustment factors derived from any part of the Consumer Price Index.

2. A calculation made by applying either the greater or the lesser of,

i. a percentage change in the payor’s or recipient’s income, and

ii. a percentage change in the Consumer Price Index,

as specified in the support order or support deduction order.

3. A calculation made by applying a rate of increase or decrease specified in the support order or support deduction order.

4. A calculation made in accordance with the methods specified in Quebec legislation dealing with cost of living adjustments to support orders.

5. A calculation made by applying the indexing factor applied by an income source to the payor’s wages, salary, pension or other income.
