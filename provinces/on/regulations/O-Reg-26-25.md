### Activities exempted

1 For greater clarity, for the purposes of the definition of “supervised consumption site” in section 1 of the Act, the activities in respect of which the exemption has been granted must include the supervised consumption of controlled substances or precursors.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
