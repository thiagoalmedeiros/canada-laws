O. Reg. 212/23

PRESCRIBED PERSONS

### Prescribed persons, “physician”

1 A person who is exempted from the requirement to be a member of the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario under subsections 9 (1) and (3) of the Medicine Act, 1991 by a regulation made under that Act is a prescribed person for the purposes of clause 1 (1) (b) of the Act.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
