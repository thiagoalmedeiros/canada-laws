# CALCULATION OF 2022 PREMIUMS

O. Reg. 665/21

### Prescribed date

1 For the purposes of subsection 88.1 (1) of the Act, December 31, 2022 is prescribed as the later date.

### Prescribed amount

2 For the purposes of clause 88.1 (1) (b) of the Act, $100,422 is the amount prescribed for the period beginning January 1, 2022.
3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
