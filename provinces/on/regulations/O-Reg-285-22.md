O. Reg. 285/22

### Information related to accreditation to be provided on request

1 Upon request, a person who operates a laboratory in which a test described in subsection 2 (1) of the Act is conducted shall provide the Minister with information relating to the accreditation of the laboratory.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
