# regulated activities: additional prescribed activities

O. Reg. 406/07

### Administering mortgages

1 The following activities are prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 2 of subsection 5 (1) of the Act as activities that constitute administering mortgages:
1. Taking steps, on behalf of another person or entity, to enforce payment by a borrower under a mortgage.
2 O mitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
