### Public bodies

1 Each body that is listed in Column 1 of Table 1 is prescribed as a public body for the purposes of the Act.

### Commission public bodies

2 A public body that is listed in Column 1 of Table 1 is prescribed as a Commission public body for the purposes of the Act if so indicated in the corresponding row in Column 2.

3 Omitted (revokes other Regulations).

4 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
