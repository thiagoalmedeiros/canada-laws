# FEES FOR TRANSCRIPTS AND STATEMENTS OF STANDING AND FOR DUPLICATES OF DIPLOMAS, CERTIFICATES AND LETTERS OF STANDING

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/900293) before relying on it.

R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 293

1 (1) The fee for a transcript of standing, or for a duplicate of a diploma or certificate, obtained in Ontario by a pupil is $20.

(2) The fee set out in subsection (1) shall be adjusted as follows:

1. As of October 1, 1993, to $21.

2. As of July 1, 1994, to $22.

3. As of July 1, 1995, to $23.

4. As of July 1, 1996, to $24.

2 (1) The fee for a duplicate of an Ontario Teacher’s Certificate is $37.

(2) The fee set out in subsection (1) shall be adjusted as follows:

1. As of October 1, 1993, to $39.

2. As of July 1, 1994, to $41.

3. As of July 1, 1995, to $43.

4. As of July 1, 1996, to $45.

3 (1) The fee for a duplicate of a letter of standing, or of a certificate of qualification as a teacher other than an Ontario Teacher’s Certificate, is $20.

(2) The fee set out in subsection (1) shall be adjusted as follows:

1. As of October 1, 1993, to $21.

2. As of July 1, 1994, to $22.

3. As of July 1, 1995, to $23.

4. As of July 1, 1996, to $24.

4 (1) The fee for the preparation by the Ministry, on the request of a teacher, of a statement of standing obtained, and a description of courses completed, at a teacher’s education institution in Ontario is $20.

(2) The fee set out in subsection (1) shall be adjusted as follows:

1. As of October 1, 1993, to $21.

2. As of July 1, 1994, to $22.

3. As of July 1, 1995, to $23.

4. As of July 1, 1996, to $24.
