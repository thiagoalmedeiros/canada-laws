# TORONTO STOCK EXCHANGE INC.

O. Reg. 261/02

### Restriction on shareholdings

1 Ten per cent is prescribed, for the purposes of subsection 21.11 (1) of the Act, as the maximum percentage of any class or series of voting shares of The Toronto Stock Exchange Inc. that any person or company or combination of persons or companies acting jointly or in concert is permitted to beneficially own or exercise control or direction over, without the prior approval of the Commission.
