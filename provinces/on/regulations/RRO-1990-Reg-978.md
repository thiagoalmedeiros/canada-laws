# INTERSECTIONS IN UNORGANIZED TERRITORY

R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 978

1 Section 7 of the Highway Traffic Act and the regulations made thereunder do not apply in intersections of private roads with the King’s Highway in territory without municipal organization.
