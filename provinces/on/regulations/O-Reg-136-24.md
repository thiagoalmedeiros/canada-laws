# SPECIFIED LOCATE REQUESTS

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/240136) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 136/24

### Definition

1 In this Regulation,

“large excavation or dig site” means an excavation or dig site that is comprised of,

(a) at least two properties or parts of properties, each having its own municipal address, or

(b) one or more properties or parts of properties, at least one of which has no municipal address.

### Specified requests

2 The following types of locate requests are specified requests:

1. A locate request that is in regard to a large excavation or dig site.

### Time periods for specified request

3 For the purposes of clause 6 (2) (a) of the Act, a time period set out in Column 2 of the Table to this section is prescribed in respect of the specified request set out opposite the time period in Column 1 of the Table.

### TABLE

| Item | Column 1 Specified request | Column 2 Time period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | A specified request described in paragraph 1 of section 2 | 10 business days |
