# DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY OF MINISTER TO REGIONAL MUNICIPALITY OF YORK: AMENDMENTS TO OFFICIAL PLANS

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/960156) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 156/96

1 All authority of the Minister in respect of those amendments to the official plans for the local municipalities of the Regional Municipality of York that are set out in the Schedule is hereby delegated to the Council of the Regional Municipality of York.

2 All authority of the Minister in respect of applications for amendments to the official plans for the local municipalities of the Regional Municipality of York that were made to the local municipalities before March 28, 1995 and that were not adopted by the councils of the local municipalities before the day this regulation comes into force is hereby delegated to the Council of the Regional Municipality of York.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
