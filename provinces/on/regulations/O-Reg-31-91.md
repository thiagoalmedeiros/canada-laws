# DESTRUCTION OF SECURITIES

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/910031) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 31/91

1 (1) The Minister of Finance may destroy securities issued and held by the Province of Ontario if the securities are of no further value because they have been paid, cancelled or redeemed by the Province.

(2) The Minister of Finance shall prepare and retain a destruction certificate listing the destroyed securities.
