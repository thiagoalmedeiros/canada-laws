# FORMS

O. Reg. 111/18

### Direction to seize

1 A direction to seize an article under subsection 14 (1) of the Act shall be in Form 1.

### Application to Small Claims Court

2 An application to the Small Claims Court under section 23 of the Act shall be in Form 2.

### Application under s. 24 of the Act

3 An application under section 24 of the Act shall be in Form 3.

### Initial certificate

4 An initial certificate under subsection 24 (5) of the Act shall be in Form 4.

### Notice of objection by respondent

5 A notice of objection by a respondent under subsection 24 (6) of the Act shall be in Form 5.

### Final certificate

6 A final certificate under subsection 24 (7) of the Act shall be in Form 6.

### Writ of seizure

7 A writ of seizure under subsection 24 (9) of the Act shall be in Form 7.

### Receipt for article that is released

8 A receipt under subsection 24 (11) of the Act for an article that a respondent releases to an applicant in compliance with an initial or final certificate shall be in Form 8.

### Receipt for article that is seized

9 A receipt under subsection 24 (11) of the Act for an article that is seized by a sheriff or bailiff under a writ of seizure shall be in Form 9.

### Waiver of further claim

10 A waiver of further claim under subsection 24 (11) of the Act shall be in Form 10.

### Irrevocable letter of credit

11 An irrevocable letter of credit is prescribed as a type of security for the purposes of subsections 24 (4) and (7) of the Act and it shall be in Form 11.

### Financial guarantee bond

12 A financial guarantee bond is prescribed as a type of security for the purposes of subsections 24 (4) and (7) of the Act and it shall be in Form 12.

### Access to forms

13 In this Regulation, when a form is referred to by number, the reference is to the form with that number that is described in the Table of Forms at the end of this Regulation and is available on the website of the Government of Ontario Central Forms Repository.
