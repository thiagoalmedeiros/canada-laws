# OPEN SEASONS — WILDLIFE

O. Reg. 670/98

1 The areas, open seasons, times, classes or types of firearm, daily bag limits, possession limits and age limits, where applicable, set out in each Table to this Regulation are prescribed for the species to which the Table applies.
2 A reference in this Regulation to a period of time that begins and ends on specific dates shall be deemed to include the first and last dates of the period.
3 Revoked: O. Reg. 85/17, s. 1.
4 A reference in this Regulation to a wildlife management unit (WMU) is a reference to that unit as referred to in Schedule 1 to Part 6 of Ontario Regulation 663/98 (Area Descriptions) made under the Act except that if a wildlife management unit is referred to by whole number only, the whole number includes a reference to all of the wildlife management units referred to in Schedule 1 by that number used in combination with a letter, or a letter and another number.
5 In this Regulation,
“deer” means white-tailed deer.
6 A reference in this Regulation to a class of firearm is a reference to the class of firearm prescribed by section 69 of Ontario Regulation 665/98 (Hunting).
