# FORMS

O. Reg. 381/12

### Reference to forms

1 In this Regulation, when a form is referred to by number, the reference is to the form with that number that is described in the Table of Forms at the end of this Regulation and is available on the website of the Government of Ontario Central Forms Repository under the listing for the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs.

### Petition for drainage works

2 The petition for a drainage works referred to in subsection 4 (2) of the Act,

(a) shall be in Form 1 where it is filed by a person or persons referred to in clause 4 (1) (a) or (b) of the Act;

(b) shall be in Form 2 where it is filed by a person referred to in clause 4 (1) (c) of the Act; or

(c) shall be in Form 3 where it is filed by the Director under clause 4 (1) (d) of the Act.

### Notice of on-site meeting

3 The notice of an on-site meeting referred to in subsection 9 (1) of the Act shall be in Form 4.

### By-laws

4 (1) The by-law adopting the engineer’s report referred to in subsection 45 (1) of the Act,

(a) in the case of municipalities not within a regional municipality, within the County of Oxford or within The District Municipality of Muskoka, shall be in Form 5; and

(b) in the case of municipalities within a regional municipality, within the County of Oxford or within The District Municipality of Muskoka, shall be in Form 6.

(2) The borrowing by-law for use by a regional municipality, the County of Oxford or The District Municipality of Muskoka shall be in Form 7.

### Notice of compensation

5 The statement of the amount of compensation paid referred to in section 68 of the Act shall be in Form 8.

### Minor improvement project

5.1 A notice of request for a minor improvement project under subsection 78 (5) of the Act shall be in Form 9.

6 Omitted (revokes other Regulations).

7 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
