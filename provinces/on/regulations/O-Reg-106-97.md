# LAW ENFORCEMENT EXEMPTION

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/970106) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 106/97

### Exemption

1 The Act does not apply to the Ministry of the Attorney General as an employer with respect to those of its employees who are determined by the deputy minister to be employees the disclosure of whose names, salaries and positions could endanger their safety in carrying out the duties of their position or to the Ministry of Community Safety and Correctional Services as an employer with respect to those of its employees who are determined by the deputy minister to be employees the disclosure of whose names, salaries and positions could endanger their safety in carrying out their duties in law enforcement for which the Ministry is responsible.

2 Revoked: O. Reg. 34/12, s. 2.
