O. Reg. 144/26

### Prescribed offences

1 An offence under the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act (Canada) for contravening paragraph 7.1 (1)(a) of that Act is a prescribed offence for the purposes of the Act.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
