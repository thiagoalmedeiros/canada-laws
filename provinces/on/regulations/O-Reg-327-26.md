### Bicycle lanes, Bloor Street West

1 Despite section 195.6 of the Act, the Minister shall remove all bicycle lanes located on Bloor Street West between Clissold Road and Royal York Road in the City of Toronto and may, in connection with the removal of the bicycle lanes, remove or reconfigure related features or adjacent infrastructure.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
