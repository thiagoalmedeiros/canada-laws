# Associate Judges — QUALIFICATIONS

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/960535) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 535/96

1 Ten years is prescribed, for the purposes of subsection 86.1 (2) of the Act, as the period of time for which a person must have been a member of the bar of a province or territory or a judge in order to be appointed as an associate judge.
