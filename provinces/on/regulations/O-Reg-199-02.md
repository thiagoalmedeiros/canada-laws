# Hydro One Inc.

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). O. Reg. 199/02

### Prescribed communities, conditional

1 (1) The following communities are prescribed for the purposes of subsection 48.1 (1) of the Act:

1. Armstrong.

2. Bearskin Lake First Nation.

3. Biscotasing.

4. Deer Lake First Nation.

5. Fort Severn First Nation.

6. Hillsport.

7. Kasabonika Lake First Nation.

8. Keewaywin First Nation.

9. Kiashke Zaaging Anishinaabek.

10. Kingfisher Lake First Nation.

11. Kitchenuhmaykoosib Inninuwug.

12. Marten Falls First Nation.

13. Muskrat Dam First Nation.

14. Namaygoosisagagun First Nation.

15. Neskantaga First Nation.

16. North Caribou Lake First Nation.

17. North Spirit Lake First Nation.

18. Oba.

19. Pikangikum First Nation.

20. Poplar Hill First Nation.

21. Sachigo Lake First Nation.

22. Sandy Lake First Nation.

23. Sultan.

24. Wapekeka First Nation.

25. Wawakapewin First Nation.

26. Webequie First Nation.

27. Whitesand First Nation.

28. Wunnumin Lake First Nation.

(2) Hydro One Inc. shall not, directly or through a subsidiary, own or operate generation facilities and distribution systems in and shall not distribute electricity within a community mentioned in subsection (1) unless, after the Board has received an application for a licence amendment from Hydro One Remote Communities Inc., the Board has issued an order amending the licence to include that community within its service area.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
