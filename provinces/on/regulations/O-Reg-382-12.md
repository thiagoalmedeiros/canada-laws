# BORROWING BY-LAWS, DEBENTURES AND LOANS

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/api/v2/legislation/en/doc-search/regulation/120382) before relying on it.

O. Reg. 382/12

### Reference to forms

1 In this Regulation, when a form is referred to by number, the reference is to the form with that number that is described in the Table of Forms at the end of this Regulation and is available on the website of the Government of Ontario Central Forms Repository under the listing for the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs.

### Borrowing by-laws

2 (1) The borrowing by-law referred to in subsection 2 (1) of the Act, for use by municipalities not within a regional municipality, within the County of Oxford or within The District Municipality of Muskoka, shall be in Form 1.

(2) The borrowing by law referred to in subsection 2 (1) of the Act, for use by municipalities within a regional municipality, within the County of Oxford or within The District Municipality of Muskoka, shall be in Form 2.

(3) The borrowing by-law referred to in subsection 2 (1) of the Act, for use by a regional municipality, the County of Oxford or The District Municipality of Muskoka, shall be in Form 3.

### Debentures

3 The debenture referred to in subsection 2 (1) of the Act shall be in Form 4.

### Affidavit of clerk

4 The affidavit of the clerk of the municipality referred to in subsection 2 (3) of the Act shall be in Form 5.

### Application for loan

5 The application for a loan referred to in section 3 of the Act shall be in Form 6.

### Inspection and completion certificate

6 The inspection and completion certificate referred to in section 4 of the Act shall be in Form 7.

### Offer to sell

7 The offer to sell a debenture referred to in subsection 5 (8) of the Act,

(a) in the case of use by a municipality not within a regional municipality, within the County of Oxford or within The District Municipality of Muskoka, shall be in Form 8; and

(b) in the case of use by a regional municipality, the County of Oxford or The District Municipality of Muskoka, shall be in Form 9.

### Rating by-law

8 The rating by-law referred to in section 8 of the Act shall be in Form 10.

9 Omitted (revokes other Regulations).

10 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
