# PRESCRIBED ENTITIES — SUBSECTIONS 8 (3) AND (4) OF THE ACT

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Ontario website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). O. Reg. 193/24

### Prescribed entities, subs. 8 (3) of the Act

1 The following entities are prescribed for the purposes of paragraph 3 of subsection 8 (3) of the Act:

1. A not-for-profit corporation incorporated or continued under the Not-for-Profit Corporations Act, 2010 or a predecessor of that Act.

2. A not-for-profit corporation incorporated or continued by or under a special Act of the Legislature.

3. A not-for-profit corporation incorporated or continued under the laws of another province or a territory of Canada that is operating or carrying on business in Ontario.

4. A not-for-profit corporation incorporated or continued under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act or a predecessor of that Act that is operating or carrying on business in Ontario.

5. A not-for-profit corporation incorporated or continued under a special Act of the Parliament of Canada that is operating or carrying on business in Ontario.

6. A registered charity as defined in subsection 248 (1) of the Income Tax Act (Canada).

7. An Indigenous governing body that is a council, government or other entity that is authorized to act on behalf of a First Nation, Inuit, Métis or other Indigenous group, community or people that holds rights recognized and affirmed by section 35 of the Constitution Act, 1982.

8. Any entity, other than a governing body mentioned in paragraph 7, that serves the interests of or provides services to First Nation, Inuit, Métis or other Indigenous peoples.

9. A Local Services Board established under Part I of the Northern Services Boards Act.

10. An entity described in any of paragraphs 1 to 9, together with another one or more of those entities.

11. The council of any one municipality, together with one or more of the entities described in paragraphs 1 to 9.

### Prescribed entities, subs. 8 (4) of the Act

2 The following entities are prescribed for the purposes of subsection 8 (4) of the Act:

1. An entity described in any of paragraphs 1 to 9 of section 1.

2. An entity described in any of paragraphs 1 to 9 of section 1, together with another one or more of those entities.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
