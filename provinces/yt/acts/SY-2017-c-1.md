## ACT

### Preamble

Whereas

The Constitution of Canada recognizes the existing

rights of the Aboriginal Peoples of Canada;

Indigenous Peoples have a long and rich history in

Yukon and have made, and continue to make,

important contributions to Yukon and it is considered

appropriate that there be a day to mark and celebrate

these contributions and to recognize the different

cultures of the Aboriginal Peoples of Yukon and Canada;

Many Indigenous Peoples celebrate the summer

solstice, which has an important symbolism within

their cultures; and

The Government of Canada has proclaimed June 21 as

National Indigenous Peoples Day (formerly named

National Aboriginal Day).

The Commissioner of Yukon, by and with the advice

and consent of the Legislative Assembly, enacts as

follows

### National Indigenous Peoples Day established

1 June 21 of each year is to be known as “ National

Indigenous Peoples Day ” and is to be observed as a

holiday.
