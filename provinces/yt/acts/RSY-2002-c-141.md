RSY 2002, c 141

### Interpretation

1 Any expression in this Act which is defined in the

Liquor Act shall have the same meaning given to it in

the Liquor Act .

### Tax

2 There shall be levied on all liquor purchased from the

Yukon Liquor Corporation a tax in an amount equal

to 12 per cent of the amount paid to the Yukon Liquor

Corporation for its own use for the sale of the liquor.

### Transfer of tax to Y.C.R.F.

3 (1) The Yukon Liquor Corporation shall transfer the

amount of liquor tax revenue monthly from the Liquor

Corporation Fund of the Yukon Consolidated Revenue

Fund to the Deputy Head of the Department of Finance

and adjust the amount to be transferred in the final

month of each financial year so that the total amount

to be transferred in each financial year shall equal the

liquor tax revenue of that financial year as established

by audit.

(2) The Yukon Liquor Corporation shall account to the

Treasurer with respect to the tax as required.
