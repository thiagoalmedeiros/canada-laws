RSY 2002, c 23

## ACT

### Indemnity agreements

1 (1) The Minister of Health and Social Services may, on

behalf of the Government of the Yukon, agree with one

or more of the governments of Canada, the provinces

or the territories to indemnify and save harmless from

personal liability

(a) former and present directors and officers of the

Canadian Blood Agency and members of its

Scientific Advisory Committee; and

(b) former and present directors, officers,

members, and staff of the Canadian Blood

Service

for anything done or not done by those persons while

carrying out their duties or exercising their powers in

good faith in the administration of the National Blood

Supply Program.

(2) In conjunction with an agreement under

subsection (1), the Minister may contribute to a fund to

be used for the indemnification of persons referred to

in subsection (1) and may make agreements with one

or more of the governments of Canada, the provinces

or the territories for the creation and administration of

the fund.

### Limitation

2 The indemnity may apply only to the extent that similar

or adequate protection against personal liability is not

provided under insurance coverage maintained by the

Canadian Blood Agency or Canadian Blood Services.

### Payments

3 Payments by the Government of the Yukon under an

agreement authorized by this Act must not exceed the

allocation of the operating expenses of the Canadian

Blood Agency, or of Canadian Blood Services, to the

Yukon in place at the time of the occurrence giving rise

to a claim.

### Inconsistency with Act

4 A provision of an agreement inconsistent with this Act

is not enforceable against the Government of the

Yukon.
