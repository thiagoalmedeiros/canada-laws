RSY 2002, c 237

### Loan

1 (1) The Yukon Development Corporation is hereby

authorized to borrow up to $21,000,000 to be used

consistently with the Yukon Development Corporation

Act for the construction of an electrical transmission

line from the Mayo Dam to the City of Dawson.

(2) The Yukon Development Corporation may authorize

the Yukon Energy Corporation to borrow and use on

behalf of the Yukon Development Corporation some or

all of the amount referred to in subsection (1).

### Guarantee by Minister

2 The Minister of Finance is hereby authorized to

guarantee on behalf of the Government of the Yukon

the repayment of part or all of the money borrowed by

the Yukon Development Corporation under section 1.

### Documents

3 The Yukon Development Corporation and the Minister

of Finance may execute any agreements and other

documents necessary for the borrowing of the money

and the guaranteeing of its repayment.
