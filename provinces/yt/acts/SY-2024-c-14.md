## ACT

The Commissioner of Yukon, by and with the advice

and consent of the Legislative Assembly, enacts as

follows:

## PART 1

## ELECTORAL DISTRICTS ESTABLISHED

### Purpose

1 This Act establishes the electoral districts in Yukon for

the election of members to the Legislative Assembly.

### Electoral Districts and authoritative maps

2 The following electoral districts are established, each

having an area and boundaries as shown for that

electoral district in the Official Digital Version of the

Electoral District Maps of the Yukon 2024 published by

the Chief Electoral Officer:

(a) Electoral District of Copperbelt North;

(b) Electoral District of Copperbelt South;

(c) Electoral District of Klondike;

(d) Electoral District of Kluane;

(e) Electoral District of Lake Laberge;

(f) Electoral District of Marsh Lake-Mount Lorne-

Golden Horn;

(g) Electoral District of Mayo-Tatchun;

(h) Electoral District of Mountainview;

(i) Electoral District of Porter Creek Centre;

(j) Electoral District of Porter Creek North;

(k) Electoral District of Porter Creek South;

(l) Electoral District of Riverdale North;

(m) Electoral District of Riverdale South;

(n) Electoral District of Southern Lakes;

(o) Electoral District of Takhini;

(p) Electoral District of Watson Lake-Ross River-

Faro;

(q) Electoral District of Vuntut Gwitchin;

(r) Electoral District of Whistle Bend North;

(s) Electoral District of Whistle Bend South;

(t) Electoral District of Whitehorse Centre;

(u) Electoral District of Whitehorse West.

### Maps for convenience

3 For convenience only, maps of the electoral districts

referred to in section 2 are included in the Schedule.

### Availability

4 The Chief Electoral Officer must ensure that the Official

Digital Version of the Electoral District Maps of the Yukon

2024 is published in a manner that the Chief Electoral

Officer considers appropriate and is available to the

public without charge.
