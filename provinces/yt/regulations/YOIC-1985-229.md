YOIC 1985/229

*Made under the Constitutional Questions Act*

Pursuant to section 5 of the Constitutional Questions

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

A. The Commissioner in Executive Council hereby refers

to the Court of Appeal for the Yukon Territory, for

hearing and consideration, the following questions:

1 (Withdrawn by O.I.C. 1986/142)

2 Is it within the legislative competence of the

Legislative Assembly of the Yukon Territory to enact a

residency requirement of one year before persons can

vote in a Yukon Territorial election?

3 If the answer to question 2 is no, is it within the

legislative competence of the Legislative Assembly of

the Yukon Territory to enact a residency requirement

of six months before persons can vote in a Yukon

Territorial election?

4 If the answer to questions 2 and 3 is no, is it

within the legislative competence of the Legislative

Assembly of the Yukon Territory to enact a residency

requirement of residency in the Yukon Territory on the

31st day of December in the year preceding any Yukon

Territorial election before persons can vote in such an

election?

5 Is it within the legislative competence of the

Legislative Assembly of the Yukon Territory to enact a

residency requirement for Yukon Territorial elections

for any period beyond what is required by the Chief

Electoral Officer for administrative purposes?

B. Order-In-Council 1985/227 is revoked.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 21

day of October, A.D. 1985.

Commissioner of the Yukon/Commissaire
