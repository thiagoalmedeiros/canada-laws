YOIC 1990/78

*Made under the Public Service Act*

Pursuant to section 209 of the Public Service Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 This order may be cited as the Exemption For

Federal Program Transfers.

2 Appointment to the public service is exempt from

sections 99, 105, 108, 113 and 115 of the Public Service Act

when all of the following conditions are met:

(a) a program of the Government of Canada is

transferred to the Government of the Yukon,

(b) the individual who is to be appointed was

employed by the Government of Canada in that

program, and

(c) in the agreement pertaining to the transfer of

that program, the Government of the Yukon has

agreed to offer the individual appointment to the

public service.

3 Where the appointment is one for which an

exemption has been made under section 2 and under the

agreement between the Government of Canada and the

Government of the Yukon pertaining to the transfer the

individual who is to be appointed is entitled to be

appointed at a rate of pay higher than the maximum rate

for the class the position is in, then the appointment of

that individual is exempt of subsection 74(1) of the Public

Service Act .

4 Notwithstanding the generality of section 2, where

the appointment is one for which an exemption has been

made under section 2 and the individual who was

appointed was still on probation when their employment

with the Government of Canada ended, then that

individual will remain on probation as an employee of the

Government of the Yukon for the same period as they

would have remained on probation if they had remained as

an employee of the Government of Canada.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory this 24th

day of May, A.D. 1990.
