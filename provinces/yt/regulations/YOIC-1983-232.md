YOIC 1983/232

*Made under the Canada Student Loans Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of Section 2(1) of the Canada

Student Loans Act, the Commissioner in Executive

Council orders as follows:

1 Roane State Community College, Harriman,

Tennessee is designated a specified educational

institution for the purpose of the Canada Student Loans

Act .

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 29th

day of November, A.D., 1983.
