YOIC 2003/210

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The portion of Road shown on Plan 42265 CLSR,

20941 LTO, Deep Creek Subdivision, Yukon, as separating

Lots 8 and 9, and Lots 10 and 11 on the said plan, is hereby

closed and declared to no longer be highway.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 9th July 2003.
