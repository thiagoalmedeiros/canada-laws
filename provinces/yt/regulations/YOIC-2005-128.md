YOIC 2005/128

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The portion of the old Alaska Highway, Plan

40367 CLSR, 23461 LTO lying southerly of the access road

shown on Plan 43481 CLSR, 23048 LTO and southerly of

the Alaska Highway, Plan 43508 CLSR, 23076 LTO, is

hereby closed and declared to no longer be highway.

2 The portion of Y. T. Highway No. 1, shown as

Old Alaska Highway on Plan 75028 CLSR, 93-41 LTO and

that portion of Y. T. Highway No. 1 shown as Old Alaska

Highway on Plan 40368 CLSR, 22310 LTO lying

northeasterly of Lot 1000, Quad 105 C/2, Plan 66280 CLSR,

57582 LTO and southwesterly of Y. T. Highway No. 1,

shown as Present Alaska Highway on Plan 75028 CLSR,

93-41 LTO, is hereby closed and declared to no longer be

highway.

3 The portion of the Alaska Highway between

Monuments H1361 and H1382, Plan 40376 CLSR, 22304

LTO, shown on Plan 68357 CLSR, 66310 LTO; and the

portion of the Alaska Highway between Monuments H1382

and H1414, Plan 40377 CLSR, 22305 LTO, shown on Plan

68358 CLSR, 66309 LTO, are closed and declared to no

longer be highway.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this August 9 2005.
