## MINISTERIAL ORDER

Certain Yukon Oil and Gas Lands (Final Recommended

Dawson Regional Land Use Plan) is made.

March 23, 2026

Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources/Ministre

Section 1

## ORDER WITHDRAWING FROM

## DISPOSITION CERTAIN YUKON OIL

## AND GAS LANDS (FINAL

## RECOMMENDED DAWSON REGIONAL

## LAND USE PLAN)

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to withdraw the Yukon oil

and gas lands described in Schedule 1 and shown in

Schedule 2 from disposition in order to implement a

recommendation in the Final Recommended Dawson

Regional Land Use Plan.

### Lands withdrawn from disposition

2 The Yukon oil and gas lands described in Schedule 1 and

shown in Schedule 2 are withdrawn from disposition

until April 1, 2028.
