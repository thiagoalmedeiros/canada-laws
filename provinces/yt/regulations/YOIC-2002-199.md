YOIC 2002/199

*Made under the Housing Corporation Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation

“ facility ” means the seniors’ supportive housing facility

known as Normandy Living located at 468 Range Road

in Whitehorse, Yukon; « établissement »

“ resident ” means an individual who occupies, within the

facility, a housing unit that is the object of a tenancy

agreement with the corporation. « résident »

### Program assignment

2 (1) A seniors ’ supportive housing program is

established at the facility for the purpose of providing

some or all of the services described in subsection (3) to

residents.

(2) The corporation is to carry out the program.

(3) The services referred to in subsection (1) are the

following:

(a) food services;

(b) housekeeping services;

(c) laundry services;

(d) telephone, internet, and television services; d)

(e) social and recreational opportunities; e)

(f) transportation services; f)

(g) concierge services; g)

(h) maintenance services; h)

(i) 24-hour security monitoring services; i)

(j) monitoring of well-being and presence; j)

(k) professional on-site management. k)
