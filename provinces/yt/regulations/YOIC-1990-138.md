YOIC 1990/138

*Made under the Education Act*

Pursuant to section 87 of the Education Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The annexed form for the notice of nominations of

candidates for the offices of trustees of a school board

or members of a council is hereby made and

established and shall be published in newspapers which

are circulated territory-wide.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 11th

day of October, A.D. 1990.

Administrator of the Yukon/
