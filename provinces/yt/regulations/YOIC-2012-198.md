## PART 1 – INTERPRETATION

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,

“ Act ” means the Registered Nurses Profession Act ;

« loi »

“ applicant ” means a person who has applied for

registration under section 4;« candidat »

“ criminal record check ” means a record check to

obtain information from a law enforcement agency in

Canada as to whether an individual has, under any

enactment of Canada or a province, any

(a) conviction or finding of guilt in relation to

which no pardon or record suspension is in

effect, or

(b) outstanding charge awaiting court

disposition; « vérification du casier

judiciaire »

“ international criminal record check ” means a record

check to obtain information from an agency recognized

by the registrar in a jurisdiction outside Canada as to

whether an individual has any conviction or finding of

guilt, or outstanding charge awaiting court disposition,

under a law of that jurisdiction. « vérification

internationale du casier judiciaire »

### Nursing education programs

2 (1) An education program in nursing, including an

entry, graduate or re-entry program, that is approved

by another nursing regulatory body in Canada is

approved as a nursing education program.

(2) If an education program in nursing is offered in

Yukon, the board may approve that program as a

nursing education program by passing a further

regulation or amending this regulation, in accordance

with subsection 7(1) of the Act.

## PART 2 – MEMBERSHIP, REGISTRATION AND

## LICENSING

## DIVISION 1 – CLASSES OF MEMBERSHIP

### Classes of membership

3 (1) The following classes of membership for registered

nurses are created:

(a) full;

(b) interim;

(c) virtual;

(d) courtesy.

(2) The following classes of membership for nurse

practitioners are created:

(a) full;

(b) interim;

(c) virtual;

(d) courtesy.

(3) The student nurse class of membership is created.

(4) The following classes of membership are

continued:

(a) non-practising;

(b) honourary.

### Registration and licensing periods

3.1 The registrar may establish registration periods and

licensing periods for the purposes of this Regulation.

## DIVISION 2 – REGISTRATION

### Application for registration

4 (1) An applicant for registration in any class of

membership, other than honourary, must provide to

the registrar

(a) a completed form for registration in the

applicable class, accompanied by any

documents required by the form;

(b) a declaration satisfactory to the registrar that

the person will comply with the code of ethics

established under the bylaws;

(b.1) evidence satisfactory to the registrar that the

applicant has completed training in cultural

safety and humility approved by the registrar;

(b.2) subject to subsection (4), satisfactory results of

a criminal record check and, if the applicant

has resided or practised outside Canada in the

two-year period before the date of the

application, of an international criminal record

check, carried out within three months before

the date of the application;

(c)

[Paragraph 4(1)(c) repealed by O.I.C 2024/144]

(d) if the applicant is or was registered in another

jurisdiction, a document, dated within 6

months of the date of application, from the

nursing regulatory body in the jurisdiction

where the applicant is or was most recently

registered, and, if required by the board, any

jurisdictions where the applicant was

previously registered

(i) certifying that, in the applicable jurisdiction,

the applicant’s entitlement to practise

nursing has not at any time been suspended,

limited, restricted, or made subject to

conditions and the applicant has not at any

time been disqualified from the practice of

nursing, or specifying the particulars of any

such suspension, limitation, restriction,

conditions, or disqualification,

(ii) certifying that there is no investigation,

review, or other proceeding underway in the

applicable jurisdiction that could result in the

applicant’s entitlement to practise nursing

being suspended, limited, restricted, or

made subject to conditions, or the applicant

being disqualified from the practice of

nursing, or specifying the particulars of any

such investigation, review, or other

proceeding, and

(iii) certifying that the applicant’s entitlement to

practise nursing has not been voluntarily

relinquished in the applicable jurisdiction

with the effect of preventing the filing of a

complaint or the commencement or

completion of an investigation, review, or

other proceeding that could have resulted in

the applicant’s entitlement to practise

nursing in the applicable jurisdiction being

suspended, limited, restricted, or made

subject to conditions or the applicant being

disqualified from the practice of nursing, or

specifying the particulars of any such

relinquishment;

(e) in the case of an applicant for registration in a e)

class of membership for registered nurses or

nurse practitioners or in the student nurse

class of membership, evidence satisfactory to

the registrar of the person’s proficiency in the

English language; and

(f) the applicable fee established under the bylaws.

(2) The registrar may require the applicant to provide

any additional documents or information that the

registrar considers necessary to assess whether the

applicant meets the requirements for registration.

(3) A person may apply for registration in the full class

of membership for registered nurses or nurse

practitioners

(a) before a document referred to in

paragraph (1)(a) or (d) or subsection (2) that is

required to be submitted by a person other

than the applicant has been received by the

registrar; or

(b) while

(i) they are waiting to write or awaiting the

results of the examination established or

approved by the board to qualify as a

registered nurse or nurse practitioner, or

(ii) they are waiting to take or awaiting the

results of a bridging program.

(4) The registrar may waive the requirement under

paragraph (1)(b.2) if the applicant provided satisfactory

results of a criminal record check and, if applicable, an

international criminal record check, carried out within

five years before the date of the application

(a) to the registrar; or

(b) to a nursing regulatory body in another

province.

### Disqualification from registration — protection of public

4.1 Despite any other provision of this Regulation, the

registrar must refuse to register an applicant if the

registrar considers it necessary to protect the public

(a) because of a continuing investigation, review or

other proceeding in any jurisdiction that could

result in the applicant being disqualified from

the practice of nursing in the jurisdiction;

(b) because the applicant has been disqualified

from the practice of nursing in any jurisdiction;

(c) because the applicant’s entitlement to practise

nursing in any jurisdiction has been suspended,

limited or restricted or made subject to

conditions and the registrar considers that

imposing conditions on the applicant’s

registration in Yukon would not be effective to

protect the public; or

(d) because of a continuing criminal proceeding, or

a conviction or finding of guilt, relating to the

applicant’s conduct, character or competence to

practise nursing.

### Registrar may impose conditions on registration

5 (1) The registrar may impose conditions on the

registration of a member if

(a) the member’s entitlement to practise nursing in

another jurisdiction is limited, restricted or

made subject to conditions or the member is

disqualified from the practice of nursing in

another jurisdiction;

(b) an investigation, review or other proceeding is

ongoing in Yukon or another jurisdiction that

could result in

(i) the member’s entitlement to practise

nursing in Yukon or the other jurisdiction

being suspended, limited, restricted or made

subject to conditions, or

(ii) the member being disqualified from the

practice of nursing in Yukon or the other

jurisdiction;

(c) the member has voluntarily relinquished their

entitlement to practise nursing in another

jurisdiction with the effect of preventing the

filing of a complaint or the commencement or

completion of an investigation, review, or other

proceeding that could have resulted in

(i) the member ’s entitlement to practise

nursing in the jurisdiction being suspended,

limited, restricted, or made subject to

conditions, or

(ii) the member being disqualified from the

practice of nursing in the jurisdiction;

(d) the registrar considers it necessary to protect

the public as a result of continuing criminal

proceedings, or convictions or findings of guilt,

in any jurisdiction relating to the member’s

conduct, character or competence to practise

nursing.

(2) The registrar must

(a) impose conditions on the registration of a

member registered in an interim, virtual or

courtesy class of membership or the student

nurse class of membership to reflect the

applicable limits, restrictions and conditions on

the member’s practice imposed by subsections

9.1(2) and (3), 9.2(2), (3) and (4), 9.3(2), (3)

and (4) and 9.4(2) and (3), respectively; and

(b) note the conditions on the member’s licence.

### Registration in classes of membership for registered nurses

6 (1) An applicant who has applied for registration in the

full class of membership for registered nurses and who

has met the requirements under section 4 is eligible to

be registered in that class of membership in any of the

following cases:

(a) the applicant is registered, or within the six-

month period before the date of the application

was registered, to practise nursing as a

registered nurse, or the equivalent of a

registered nurse, in the full class or the

equivalent of the full class of membership in a

province;

(b) the applicant

(i) was registered to practise nursing as a

registered nurse, or the equivalent of a

registered nurse, in the full class or the

equivalent of the full class of membership in

a province more than six months before the

date of the application, but is no longer

registered in a province,

(ii) passed the examination established or

approved by the board to qualify as a

registered nurse, and

(iii) has provided evidence satisfactory to the

registrar that the applicant has a

combination of experience and education to

practise as a registered nurse;

(c) the applicant

(i) either

(A) is registered, or within the six-month

period before the date of the application

was registered, to practise nursing as a

registered nurse, or the equivalent of a

registered nurse, in the equivalent of the

full class of membership in a jurisdiction

outside Canada, or

(B) is practising, or within the six-month

period before the date of the application

was practising, as a nurse in a jurisdiction

outside Canada that does not provide for

the registration of the equivalent of

registered nurses and, in their practise,

was exercising powers and carrying out

duties and responsibilities equivalent to

those of a registered nurse registered in

the full class of membership,

(ii) successfully completed a program in nursing

education at an institution satisfactory to the

board in a jurisdiction outside Canada,

(iii) successfully completed a competency

assessment, and, if required by the registrar

because of the results of the competency

assessment, a bridging program, established

or approved by the registrar to qualify as a

registered nurse, and

(iv) passed the examination established or

approved by the board to qualify as a

registered nurse;

(d) within the three-year period before the date of

application, the applicant

(i) graduated from a nursing education

program to qualify as a registered nurse,

and

(ii) passed the examination established or

approved by the board to qualify as a

registered nurse;

(e) the applicant

(i) graduated

(A) from a nursing education program more

than three years before the date of the

application, or

(B) from a program in nursing education at

an institution satisfactory to the board in

a jurisdiction outside Canada,

(ii) passed the examination established or

approved by the board to qualify as a

registered nurse,

(iii) is not otherwise eligible to be registered

under paragraphs (a) to (d), and

(iv) within the three-year period before the date

of the application, successfully completed a

competency assessment, and, if required by

the registrar because of the results of the

competency assessment, a bridging

program, established or approved by the

registrar to qualify as a registered nurse.

(2) An applicant who has applied for registration in the

full class of membership for registered nurses and to

whom one of the following circumstances applies but

who has otherwise met the requirements under

section 4 and subsection (1) of this section may, at the

discretion of the registrar, be registered in the interim

class of membership for registered nurses:

(a) the applicant is registered to practise nursing

as a registered nurse, or the equivalent of a

registered nurse, in the equivalent of the

interim class of membership in a province;

(b) a document referred to in paragraph 4(1)(a) or

(d) or subsection 4(2) that is required to be

submitted by a person other than the applicant

has not yet been received by the registrar;

(c) the applicant is waiting to write or awaiting the

results of the examination established or

approved by the board to qualify as a

registered nurse;

(d) the applicant is waiting to take or awaiting the

results of a bridging program.

(3) An applicant who has applied for registration in the

virtual class of membership for registered nurses and

who has met the requirements under section 4 is

eligible to be registered in that class of membership if

(a) the applicant is registered to practise nursing

as a registered nurse, or the equivalent of a

registered nurse, in the equivalent of the full

class of membership in a province;

(b) the applicant is employed by an employer

approved by the registrar for the purpose of

providing, from another province, nursing

services approved by the registrar to patients

located in Yukon;

(c) the applicant is applying for registration as a

registered nurse in Yukon for the purpose of

providing the nursing services referred to in

paragraph (b);

(d) the registrar is satisfied that the applicant is

competent to practise nursing for that purpose;

and

(e) the applicant either

(i) provides evidence satisfactory to the

registrar that the applicant is covered by

professional liability insurance in respect of

the applicant’s practice of nursing in Yukon,

or

(ii) participates in the professional liability

insurance made available by the Association.

(4) An applicant who has applied for registration in the

courtesy class of membership for registered nurses and

who has met the requirements under section 4 is

eligible to be registered in that class of membership if

(a) the applicant is registered to practise nursing

as a registered nurse, or the equivalent of a

registered nurse, in the equivalent of the full

class of membership in a province;

(b) the applicant is applying for registration as a

registered nurse in Yukon on a temporary basis

to practise nursing for a specified purpose

approved by the registrar;

(c) the registrar is satisfied that the applicant is

competent to practise nursing for the specified

purpose; and

(d) the applicant either

(i) provides evidence satisfactory to the

registrar that the applicant is covered by

professional liability insurance in respect of

the applicant’s practice of nursing in Yukon,

or

(ii) participates in the professional liability

insurance made available by the Association.

(5) A person who is registered as a registered nurse,

or the equivalent of a registered nurse, with a body

that regulates registered nurses that is a party to an

agreement entered into under section 12.1 of the Act

may, at the discretion of the registrar, be registered in

a class of membership for registered nurses if

(a) under the terms of the agreement the person

may be registered in that class of membership

in Yukon; and

(b) any requirements respecting the person’s

registration and practice provided for by the

agreement are met.

any of the following cases:

month period before the date of the application

was registered, to practise nursing as a nurse

practitioner, in the full class or the equivalent

(b) the applicant

(ii) passed the examination established or

practitioner, and

(iii) has provided evidence satisfactory to the

the application, the applicant

### Registration in student nurse class of membership

8.1 An applicant who has applied for registration in the

student nurse class of membership and who has met

the requirements under section 4 may, at the

discretion of the registrar, be registered in that class of

membership if

(a) the applicant

(i) is enrolled in a nursing education program

to qualify as a registered nurse or nurse

practitioner,

(ii) is required to practise nursing as part of the

program, and

(iii) intends to practise nursing in Yukon as part

of the program; and

(b) the institution at which the applicant is enrolled

in the nursing education program referred to in

subparagraph (a)(i) has confirmed

(i) the identity of the applicant,

(ii) that the applicant is in good standing with

the institution, and

(iii) that the applicant is covered by the

institution’s insurance in respect of the

applicant’s practice of nursing as part of the

program.

### Registration in non-practising class of membership

8.2 An applicant who has applied for registration in the

non-practising class of membership and who has met

the requirements of subsections 4(1) and (2) is eligible

to be registered in the non-practising class of

membership if the applicant is registered, or within the

three-month period before the date of the application

was registered,

(a) to practise nursing as a registered nurse or a

nurse practitioner, or the equivalent of a

registered nurse or nurse practitioner, in the

full class or the equivalent of the full class of

membership in a province; or

(b) in the non-practising class of membership or

the equivalent of the non-practising class of

membership in a province.

### Full members

9 (1) Registered nurses and nurse practitioners

registered in the full class of membership are eligible

(a) to attend, participate in and vote at all

meetings of the Association;

(b) to hold office as a member of the board;

(c) to be appointed as a member or the chair of a

committee of the Association; and

(d) for any professional liability insurance made

available by the Association.

(2) A registered nurse registered in the full class of

membership may use the title “Registered Nurse” or

the designation “Reg. N.” or “R.N.” or another title,

designation, description, or abbreviation that indicates

that they are a registered nurse.

(3) A nurse practitioner registered in the full class of

membership may use the title “Nurse Practitioner” or

“Registered Nurse Practitioner” or the designation

“Reg. N. P.” or “N.P.” or another title, designation,

description, or abbreviation that indicates that they are

a nurse practitioner.

### Interim members

9.1 (1) Registered nurses and nurse practitioners

registered in the interim class of membership are

eligible

(a) to attend and participate in all meetings of the

Association;

(b) to be appointed as a member of a committee of

the Association; and

(c) for any professional liability insurance made

available by the Association.

(2) Subject to subsection (4), a registered nurse

registered in the interim class of membership must not

practise nursing except under the direct supervision of

a registered nurse or a nurse practitioner who is

registered and in good standing in the full class of

membership.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), a nurse practitioner

registered in the interim class of membership must not

practise nursing except under the direct supervision of

(a) a nurse practitioner who is registered and in

good standing in the full class of membership;

or

(b) a medical practitioner.

(4) For the purposes of subsections (2) and (3), the

registrar may determine that supervision rather than

direct supervision is sufficient in the case of a

particular member.

(5) The registration of a member in the interim class

of membership expires on the date determined by the

registrar.

(6) A person may be registered in the interim class of

membership for more than one registration period,

but, unless subsection (7) applies, the person must not

be registered in that class of membership

(a) for more than a total of six months, in the case

of a person registered in the interim

membership class under paragraph 6(2)(b) or

8(2)(b); or

(b) for more than a total of three years, in any

other case.

(7) Subject to subsection (9), if a person has been

registered in the interim class of membership for the

applicable maximum period set out in subsection (6),

the registrar may, if the registrar considers it

appropriate in the circumstances, register the person

in the interim class of membership for one additional

period of not more than one year.

(8) If a registered nurse or nurse practitioner who is

registered in the interim class of membership has not

passed the examination referred to in

subparagraph 6(2)(c) or 8(2)(c), respectively, and is not

eligible to rewrite the examination, the registered

nurse’s or nurse practitioner’s registration and licence

expire seven days after the day on which the body that

administers the examination notifies the registrar that

the registered nurse or nurse practitioner has not

passed the examination.

(9) A person whose registration in the interim class of

membership expires under subsection (8) is no longer

eligible to be registered in the interim class of

membership.

(10) A registered nurse registered in the interim class

of membership

(a) must use the title “interim registered nurse”;

and

(b) must not use the titles, designations,

descriptions or abbreviations referred to in

subsection 9(2).

(11) A nurse practitioner registered in the interim

class of membership

(a) must use the title “interim nurse practitioner”;

and

(b) must not use the titles, designations,

descriptions or abbreviations referred to in

subsection 9(3).

(12) If a member registered in the interim class of

membership for registered nurses meets the

requirements for registration in the full class of

membership, the registrar must remove the member

from registration in the interim class of membership

and register them in the full class of membership.

(13) If a member registered in the interim class of

membership for nurse practitioners meets the

requirements for registration in the full class of

membership, the registrar must remove the member

from registration in the interim class of membership

and register them in the full class of membership.

### Virtual members

9.2 (1) Registered nurses and nurse practitioners

registered in the virtual class of membership are

eligible for any professional liability insurance made

available by the Association.

(2) A registered nurse registered in the virtual class of

membership must not practise nursing except to

provide the nursing services approved by the registrar

as referred to in paragraph 6(3)(b).

(3) A nurse practitioner registered in the virtual class

of membership must not practise nursing except to

provide the nursing services approved by the registrar

as referred to in paragraph 8(3)(b).

(4) A member who is registered in the virtual class of

membership must remain registered and in good

standing in the province referred to in

paragraph 6(3)(a) or 8(3)(a), and if their registration in

that province is suspended or cancelled, their

registration in the virtual class of membership is

cancelled.

(5) A registered nurse registered in the virtual class of

membership may use the title “Registered Nurse” or

the designation “Reg. N.” or “R.N.” or another title,

designation, description, or abbreviation that indicates

that they are a registered nurse.

(6) A nurse practitioner registered in the virtual class

of membership may use the title “Nurse Practitioner”

or “Registered Nurse Practitioner” or the designation

“Reg. N. P.” or “N.P.” or another title, designation,

description, or abbreviation that indicates that they are

a nurse practitioner.

### Courtesy members

9.3 (1) Registered nurses and nurse practitioners

registered in the courtesy class of membership are

eligible for any professional liability insurance made

available by the Association.

(2) A registered nurse registered in the courtesy class

of membership must not practise nursing except for

the specified purpose approved by the registrar as

referred to in paragraph 6(4)(b).

(3) A nurse practitioner registered in the courtesy

class of membership must not practise nursing except

for the specified purpose approved by the registrar as

referred to in paragraph 8(4)(b).

(4) A member who is registered in the courtesy class

of membership must remain registered and in good

standing in the province referred to in

paragraph 6(4)(a) or 8(4)(a), and if their registration in

that province is suspended or cancelled, their

registration in the courtesy class of membership is

cancelled.

(5) The registration of a member in the courtesy class

of membership expires on the earlier of

(a) the day set out in the registration; and

(b) the 30 th day after the day on which they are

registered.

(6) A person who is or was registered in the courtesy

class of membership may apply for a new registration

in that class of membership.

(7) A registered nurse registered in the courtesy class

of membership may use the title “Registered Nurse” or

the designation “Reg. N.” or “R.N.” or another title,

designation, description, or abbreviation that indicates

that they are a registered nurse.

(8) A nurse practitioner registered in the courtesy

class of membership may use the title “Nurse

Practitioner” or “Registered Nurse Practitioner” or the

designation “Reg. N. P.” or “N.P.” or another title,

designation, description, or abbreviation that indicates

that they are a nurse practitioner.

### Student nurse members

9.4 (1) A member registered in the student nurse class of

membership who is entitled to practise within the

scope of practice of a registered nurse must not do so

except under the direct supervision of a registered

nurse, or a nurse practitioner, who is registered and in

good standing in the full class of membership.

(2) A member registered in the student nurse class of

membership who is entitled to practise within the

scope of practice of a nurse practitioner must not do so

except under the direct supervision of

(a) a nurse practitioner who is registered and in

good standing in the full class of membership;

or

(b) a medical practitioner.

(3) A member registered in the student nurse class of

membership must not practise nursing except within

the parameters of the nursing education program in

which they are enrolled as referred to in

subparagraph 8.1(a)(i).

(4) The registration of a member in the student nurse

class of membership expires on the date determined by

the registrar.

(5) A person who is or was registered in the student

class of membership may apply for a new registration

in that class of membership.

(6) A member registered in the student nurse class of

membership must use the title “student registered

nurse” or “student nurse practitioner” as set out in

their registration.

## DIVISION 3 – LICENSING

### Registrar to issue licence

17 Subject to section 18, the registrar must issue a licence

for a licensing period to a member who is registered in

a class of membership for registered nurses or nurse

practitioners or in the student nurse class of

membership and who has applied for a licence if

(a) the member’s entitlement to practise nursing

has not been revoked or suspended in any

jurisdiction;

(b) the member has paid the applicable fee

established under the bylaws; and

(c) the member has completed any continuing

competence requirements established by the

board for the applicable class of membership.

### Criminal record check

17.1 The registrar may, at any time the registrar considers

advisable, require an applicant for a licence to provide

satisfactory results of a criminal record check and, if

the applicant has resided or practised outside Canada

in the two-year period before the date of the request,

of an international criminal record check, carried out

within the three-month period before it is submitted to

the registrar.

### Board may impose conditions

18 (1) The board may impose training, practise,

examinations, or assessments as a condition of issuing

a licence if

(a) in the case of a member who has applied for a

licence as a registered nurse, the member has

not practised as a registered nurse within the

five-year period immediately preceding the

date their application is received by the board;

or

(b) in the case of a member who has applied for a

licence as a nurse practitioner, the member has

not practised as a nurse practitioner within the

three-year period immediately preceding the

date their application is received by the board.

(2) For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a), a member

shall be considered not to have practised as a

registered nurse within the five-year period unless,

during that period, the member has

(a) worked 1,125 hours as a registered nurse in

any jurisdiction; or

(b) successfully completed a nursing education

program.

(3) For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), a member

shall be considered not to have practised as a nurse

practitioner within the three-year period unless, during

that period, the member has

(a) worked 900 hours as a nurse practitioner in

any jurisdiction; or

(b) successfully completed a nursing education

program to qualify as a nurse practitioner.

### Board discretion to issue licence with or without conditions

19 Despite subsection 18(1), the board may grant a

license, with or without conditions, to a member who

has not met the criteria set out in subsection 18(2)

or (3) for the licence for which they have applied if the

board is satisfied that the member

(a) has experience in the practice of nursing or

education or a combination of experience and

education considered by the board to be

substantially equivalent to

(i) the requirements of paragraph 18(2)(a) or

(b), in the case of an application for a licence

for a registered nurse, or

(ii) the requirements of paragraph 18(3)(a) or

(b), in the case of an application for a licence

for a nurse practitioner; and

(b) has demonstrated an effort to meet the

requirements of subsection 18(2) or (3), as

applicable, but has been unable to do so due to

special circumstances that the board considers

sufficient.

## PART 5 – SCOPE OF PRACTICE OF NURSE

## PRACTITIONERS

### Nurse practitioner advisory committee

25 (1) The nurse practitioner advisory committee is

established, consisting of at least four individuals

appointed by the board as follows

(a) one individual who is licensed to practise as a

nurse practitioner in Yukon;

(b) one other individual who is a member of the

Association;

(c) one individual who is licensed to practise

medicine in Yukon;

(d) one individual who is licensed to practise as a

pharmacist in Yukon; and

(e) such other individuals as the board may

determine.

(2) If, after making reasonable inquiries, the board

determines that no individual described in

paragraph (1)(a), (c) or (d) who is acceptable to the

board is able or willing to serve on the committee, the

board may appoint an individual who is licensed in

Canada to practise in the health provider category

named in the paragraph.

(3) The terms of office of individuals appointed to

serve as members of the committee shall be

determined by the board in accordance with the

bylaws.

(4) The board may rescind the appointment of a

member of the committee.

(5) The committee shall be governed by the rules and

procedures set out in the bylaws.

### Nurse practitioner practice

26 (1) A nurse practitioner is entitled to practise nursing

within the scope of practice of a registered nurse and

within the scope authorized under subsection 13.1(2) of

the Act.

(2) For the purposes of subsection 13.1(2) of the Act,

the scope of practice of a nurse practitioner is subject

to the exceptions, conditions and restrictions set out in

the attached Schedule.

(3) Subject to the exceptions, conditions and

restrictions set out in the Schedule, the following acts

are within the scope of practice of a nurse practitioner

(a) admission to and discharge of a patient from a

hospital; and

(b) admission to and discharge of an individual

from an institution, facility or program.

NURSE PRACTITIONER PRACTICE

PART 1 – GENERAL EXCEPTIONS, CONDITIONS AND

RESTRICTIONS

Practice subject to education, competence and

imposed conditions

1. The scope of practice of a nurse practitioner under

subsection 13.1(2) of the Act is subject to the condition

that the practice must

(a) be within the nurse practitioner’s educational

preparation and individual competence;

(b) utilize appropriate consultation and referral

mechanisms to meet patient needs; and

(c) comply with any conditions imposed by the

registrar under section 5 of the Regulation on

the nurse practitioner’s entitlement to practise.

Practice in hospital or facility

2. A nurse practitioner may admit or discharge

patients or other individuals to or from a hospital,

institution, facility or program if

(a) the operator of the hospital, institution, facility

or program has granted admission and

discharge privileges or authority to the nurse

practitioner; and

(b) the admission or discharge is made in

accordance with the rules, policies and

procedures of the operator of the hospital,

institution, facility or program.

PART 2 – EXCEPTIONS, CONDITIONS AND

RESTRICTIONS RESPECTING DRUGS

Conditions on supplying drugs

3. A nurse practitioner’s entitlement under

paragraph 13.1(2)(d) of the Act to supply drugs is

subject to the following conditions

(a) the nurse practitioner reasonably believes that

the patient

(i) otherwise lacks reasonable access to the

drug,

(ii) is unable to pay for the drug or does not

qualify for appropriate support programs, or

(iii) will not otherwise receive the drug; and

(b) if the drug is a compound prepared by the

nurse practitioner, the compound is simple and

uncomplicated.

Exceptions, conditions and restrictions on prescribing

drugs

4.(1) In subsection (2)

(a) the letter “E”, for excepted, indicates that a

nurse practitioner may not prescribe the drug;

(b) the letter “M”, for maintenance therapy,

indicates that a nurse practitioner may not

prescribe the drug as an initial prescription,

but may renew the prescription, prescribe an

adjustment to the dose initially prescribed,

discontinue the drug, and perform other

maintenance therapy; and

(c) the letter “R”, for restricted, indicates that a

nurse practitioner may prescribe the drug only

within the specified restriction.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), a nurse practitioner’s

entitlement in paragraph 13.1(2)(d) of the Act

to prescribe drugs and treatments is subject to

the following exceptions, restrictions and

conditions

(a) Anti-infective agents:

E Anti-tuberculosis therapy,

M Chronic hepatitis drugs,

M Human immunodeficiency virus drugs,

except when prescribed for HIV pre-exposure

prophylaxis or post-exposure prophylaxis;

(b) Anti-neoplastic agents:

E Anti-neoplastic agents, except

(i) Methotrexate, and

(ii) Hormone active anti-neoplastic agents;

(c) Autonomic agents: c)

E Depolarizing and non-depolarizing skeletal

muscle relaxants,

E Ergot alkaloids,

M Anti-parkinsonism agents;

(d) Blood formers and coagulators: d)

R Thromboembolytic enzymes to clear plugged

venous lines and pleural tubes,

M Hematopoietic growth factor;

(e) Cardiovascular drugs: e)

R Anti-arrhythmic therapy to a patient over

the age of 18 years, if the nurse practitioner

uses the same clinical guideline or protocol

that is used in the practice setting to

manage the care of the patient;

(f) Central nervous agents: f)

E General anesthetics, except topical

formulations;

(g) Eye, ear, nose and throat preparations: g)

E Ophthalmologic steroids,

M Antiglaucoma agents;

(h) Gold Compounds: h)

E All gold compounds;

(i) Hormones and synthetic substitutes: i)

E Infertility drugs,

M Human growth hormone;

(j) Serums, toxoids and vaccines:

R Botulinum toxin for use in cosmetic

applications;

(k) Skin and mucous membrane agents:

M Psoralens ;

(l) Other drugs:

E Immunosuppressants, except topical

formulations for dermatologic conditions.

(3) The exceptions, restrictions and conditions set out

in subsection (2) on a nurse practitioner’s entitlement

to prescribe drugs and treatments do not apply if

(a) the nurse practitioner prescribes the drug in

accordance with a treatment plan for the

patient that has been developed with and

approved by a medical specialist within the

meaning of the Medical Profession Act ; or

(b) the nurse practitioner prescribes the drug

(i) as a treatment in providing gender affirming

care, or

(ii) in providing the patient with medical

assistance in dying.
