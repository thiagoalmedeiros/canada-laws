YOIC 2017/167

*Made under the Legislative Assembly Retirement Allowances Act, 2007*

1 (1) The actuarial equivalent of a particular assigned

benefit under section 15 of the Act is to be calculated

on the basis of the actuarial assumptions used in the

relevant report prepared and delivered most recently

before the calculation is made.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), the relevant

report is the report containing an actuarial valuation of

the plan required for the continuing registration of the

plan under the Income Tax Act (Canada).
