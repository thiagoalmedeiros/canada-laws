YOIC 1988/43

*Made under the Municipal Act*

Pursuant to section 328 of the Municipal Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council is pleased hereby to

make the following order:

1 Whereas the Council of the City of Whitehorse has

adopted a subdivision Control bylaw approved by the

Executive Council Member with responsibility as Minister

of Community and Transportation Services, the Council

shall be an approving authority within the limits of the

City of Whitehorse, from the date of this order.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 21st

day of March, A.D. 1988.
