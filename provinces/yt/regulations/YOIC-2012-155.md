# Establishes classification and lot prices in Whistle Bend Subdivision

YOIC 2012/155

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act and section 21

of the Lands Regulations , the Commissioner in Executive

Council orders as follows

### Definition

1 In this order “development cost” has the same

meaning as in the Lands Regulations .

### Classification of lots in Whistle Bend

### Subdivision (Phase 1, partial)

2 The lots in the Whistle Bend Subdivision

(Whitehorse) listed in the attached schedule are declared to

belong to the classes shown.

### Sale price and method for duplex lots

3 (1) Lots 18 to 31 (classified as special) shall be

offered for sale to the general public

(a) by lottery;

(b) for a price reflecting development cost for the

Whistle Bend Subdivision, with each lot bearing

an equitable portion of the cost; and

(c) in pairs, as follows

(i) Lots 18 and 19,

(ii) Lots 20 and 21,

(iii) Lots 22 and 23,

(iv) Lots 24 and 25,

(v) Lots 26 and 27,

(vi) Lots 28 and 29, and

(vii) Lots 30 and 31.

(2) Lots 32 and 33 (classified as special) shall be offered

for sale to Habitat for Humanity Yukon Society for the

price of $1.00.

### Sale price and method for multi-family lots

4 Lots 1 to 4, 53, 54 and 114 (classified as special)

(a) shall be offered for sale to the general public

by a call for tenders which shall specify a

minimum bid price reflecting development cost

of the Whistle Bend Subdivision, with each lot

bearing an equitable portion of the cost; and

(b) may be sold at a price determined by the

tendering of bids (as long as the price is equal to

or greater than the specified minimum bid price).

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, August 16, 2012.
