YOIC 1986/150

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 3 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

Those portions of the streets and lanes shown on

survey plans CLSR 42228 and more particularly

described as follows are formally closed to

accommodate completion of the lands/loan exchange

with British Yukon Railway Company.

All lanes in Blocks 28, 29, 30, 31, 43, 44, 45 and 46,

9th Street from Too-Chi Avenue in a North-Easterly

direction until it passes Block 46,

10th Street from Too-Chi Avenue in a North-Easterly

direction until it passes Block 46,

Taku Avenue from 9th Street in a Westerly direction

until it passes Block 30,

Teslin Avenue from 9th Street in a Westerly direction

until it passes Block 30,

Takhini Avenue from 9th Street in a Westerly

direction until it passes Block 44.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 19th

day of September, A.D., 1986.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
