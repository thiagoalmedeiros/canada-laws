YOIC 1994/230

*Made under the Yukon Land Claim Final Agreements, An Act Approving*

## AN ACT APPROVING YUKON LAND CLAIMS

## AN ACT APPROVING YUKON LAND

## CLAIMS FINAL AGREEMENTS

Pursuant to section 3 of An Act Approving Yukon Land

Claims Final Agreements , the Commissioner in Executive

Council orders as follows:

1 The attached Teslin Tlingit Final Agreement

between the Teslin Tlingit Council, the Government of

Canada, and the Government of the Yukon made the 29th

day of May, 1993 is hereby approved.

2 The attached First Nation of Nacho Nyak Dun Final

Agreement between the Government of Canada, the First

Nation of Nacho Nyak Dun, and the Government of the

Yukon made the 29th day of May, 1993 is hereby

approved.

3 The attached Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation Final

Agreement between the Government of Canada, the

Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation, and the Government of the

Yukon made the 29th day of May, 1993 is hereby

approved.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

22nd day of December, 1994.

_____________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

(Copies of the above-mentioned agreements are available from

the Yukon Government Land Claims Secretariat Office, 2nd

Floor, 204 Main Street, Box 2703, Whitehorse, Yukon Y1A

2C6 Canada)
