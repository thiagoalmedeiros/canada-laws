YOIC 2017/17

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

## 2016-17

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders

1 An amount of up to $29,385,000 to be paid from the

consolidated revenue fund in accordance with the attached

Schedules A and B is authorized in addition to the net sum

of $1,391,649,000 provided for in the First Appropriation

Act, 2016-17.

2 Payments made under this special warrant may be

made for the purpose of financing government operations

for the period January 23, 2017 to March 31, 2017 as set

forth in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, January 23, 2017.
