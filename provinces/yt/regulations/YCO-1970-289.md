YCO 1970/289

*Made under the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS by subsection (1) of section 8 the Reciprocal

Enforcement of Judgments Act , chapter 4 of the Ordinances of

the Yukon Territory, 1956 (2nd session), the Commissioner

of the Yukon Territory, when satisfied that reciprocal

provisions have been or will be made by a province of

Canada for the enforcement therein of judgments given in

the Territory, he may by order declare it to be a

reciprocating jurisdiction for the purposes of the aforesaid

Act; and

WHEREAS the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory is

satisfied that the Province of Manitoba will make reciprocal

provisions for the enforcement therein of judgments

within the meaning of the aforesaid Act given in the

Territory;

NOW THEREFORE, by virtue and in exercise of the

powers of the said Act vested in the Commissioner of the

Yukon Territory, the Commissioner is pleased to declare

and doth hereby order as follows:

1 The Province of Manitoba is hereby declared to be a

reciprocating jurisdiction for the purpose of the Reciprocal

Enforcement of Judgments Act .

2 This Order shall come into force and effect on and

from the 9th day of October, A.D. 1970.

IN TESTIMONY WHEREOF I, James Smith,

Commissioner of the Yukon Territory, have this 9th day of

October A.D., 1970, set my hand and caused the seal of the

Yukon Territory to be hereunto affixed at the City of

Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory.

Commissioner of the Yukon
