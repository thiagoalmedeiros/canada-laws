YMO 1993/12

*Made under the Economic Development Act*

Pursuant to section 9 of the Economic Development Act ,

the Minister of Economic Development orders as

follows:

1 There is hereby established an Energy

Infrastructure Loans for Resource Development

Application Review Committee comprised of the

following three members:

(a) two members of the public service to be designated

by the Minister of Economic Development; and

(b) an officer or employee of the Yukon Development

Corporation, to be designated by that Corporation.

(Subsections (a) and (b) replaced by M.O. 1997/09)

2 The Committee is to act in an advisory capacity

and its function is to review applications for loans

under the Energy Infrastructure Loans for Resource

Development Regulation and to recommend to the

Minister in accordance with that Regulation how the

applications should be dealt with.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 8th

day of December, 1993.

Minister of Economic Development
