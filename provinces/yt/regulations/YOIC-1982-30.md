YOIC 1982/30

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 17(1) of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The streets and lanes in Block 50, Carcross as shown

on the Plan of Survey are closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 16th

day of February, A.D., 1982.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
