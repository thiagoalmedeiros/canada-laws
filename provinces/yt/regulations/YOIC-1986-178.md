YOIC 1986/178

*Made under the Public Utilities Act*

Pursuant to section 18 of the Public Utilities Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The Board shall adjourn the hearing of the Yukon

Electric Company Limited rate application now before

the Board until the negotiations between the Yukon

Government and the Government of Canada regarding

the transfer of the Northern Canada Power Commission

have been completed and a rates policy directive has

been issued by the Commissioner in Executive Council.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 21st

day of November, A.D., 1986.
