YOIC 1987/133

*Made under the Yukon Act (Canada)*

Pursuant to subsection 23(2) of the Yukon Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The Yukon Human Rights Commission is granted

permission to start a bank account using public money.

2 The Bank designated where the Yukon Human

Rights Commission account shall be kept is the Bank of

Montreal.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 2nd

day of July, A.D. 1987.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
