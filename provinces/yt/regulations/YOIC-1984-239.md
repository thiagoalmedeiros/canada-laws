*Made under the Energy Conservation Assistance Act*

## REGULATIONS RESPECTING YUKON

1 (1) These Regulations may be cited as the Yukon

Energy Alternatives Grants Regulations .

2 (1) In these Regulations

“ grant ” means a grant made under subsection 5(1) of

the Act. « subvention »

3 (1) Any individual who is a resident of Yukon and

any corporate body that is incorporated, continued, or

registered under the Business Corporations Act or the

Societies Act and is carrying on business or pursuing

its objects in Yukon may apply for a grant.

(2) Applications for a grant must be presented to an

official of the Department of Economic Development

and Tourism.

(3) A grant may be made only after the application has

been approved and the grant has been authorized by

the Executive Council Member.

4 (1) Any person who applies for a grant must supply

enough information to enable the Executive Council

Member and his officials to ascertain and evaluate

(a) the technical description of the project to be

studied,

(b) the description of the energy market to be

supplied, including the quantities of petroleum

likely to be displaced and the likely consumers,

(c) socio-economic impacts of the study and the

project, including energy price impacts and

impacts on the local economy,

(d) impacts of the study and the project on the

environment,

(e) the likely costs of the study and the project,

(f) procedures for obtaining the expert services

needed for the study and the project, and

(g) such other information as may be relevant to

determine whether the study is one for which a

grant should be made.

5 (1) Studies for which a grant is sought will be

evaluated according to relevant criteria including

(a) the potential of the project for displacing

petroleum with other energy forms that are

indigenous to Yukon,

(b) the scope of the study and the project,

including whether its primary impacts will be in

relation to all of Yukon, or a community or part

of a community in Yukon,

(c) the cost effectiveness of each aspect of the

study and the project,

(d) the potential benefits of the study and the

project,

(e) the ability of the applicant to implement the

project to which his proposed study relates.

6 (1) It is a condition of every grant that the Yukon

Government will hold the copyright to information and

other results of the study for which the grant is made,

unless the Executive Council Member expressly agrees

otherwise.
