YOIC 2009/251

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 That portion of the Road, Quad 105 A/02, Plan

53701 CLSR, 29782 LTO located between Lot 1030, Plan

71052 CLSR, 85193 LTO and Lot 1-5, Plan 53701 CLSR,

comprising approximately 0.153 hectares and shown

“hatched” on the map attached as an Appendix, is closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 16 December 2009.
