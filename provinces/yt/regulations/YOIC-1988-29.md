YOIC 1988/29

*Made under the Liquor Act*

### Preamble

Whereas the Commissioner in Executive Council has

received a resolution duly passed by the Council of the

Town of Watson Lake requesting an area enforcement

order prohibiting public drinking in Watson Lake,

therefore pursuant to subsection 105(3) of the Liquor Act

the Commissioner in Executive Council is pleased hereby

to make the following order:

1 Public drinking of liquor is prohibited within the

municipal boundaries of the Town of Watson Lake

commencing the date of this order.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 5th day

of February, A.D., 1988.
