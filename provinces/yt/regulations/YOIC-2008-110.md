# Order Respecting the Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Lands in Yukon (Ddhaw Ghro Habitat Protection Area)

YOIC 2008/110

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to withdraw certain lands

from disposal for the public purpose of facilitating the

establishment of the Ddhaw Ghro Habitat Protection

Area.

### Lands withdrawn from disposal

2 The tracts of lands described in the schedule, including

all mines and minerals, other than oil and gas under

the Oil and Gas Act , and the right to work them are

withdrawn from disposal for the period beginning on

the day on which this Order is made and ending on

August 31, 2011 or on the day a Management Plan for

Ddhaw Ghro Habitat Protection Area is approved

pursuant to Section 187 of the Wildlife Act , whichever is

earlier.

### Exceptions

3 Section 2 does not apply to the disposition of

(a) substances or materials that may be disposed

of under the Territorial Quarrying Regulation

under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act ;

(b) timber under the Timber Regulation under the

Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act ;

(c) timber which may be disposed of pursuant to

section 30 of the Lands Act ; or

(d) materials that may be disposed of under the d)

Quarry Regulation under the Lands Act .

### Existing rights and interests 4.

4 For greater certainty, section 2 does not apply to Il est

(a) recorded claims in good standing acquired a)

under the Yukon Placer Mining Act (Canada)

,the Yukon Quartz Mining Act (Canada), the

Placer Mining Act or the Quartz Mining Act ;

(b) existing oil and gas rights and interests b)

granted under the Canada Petroleum Resources

Act (Canada) or the Oil and Gas Act ; and

(c) existing rights acquired under section 6 of the c)

Territorial Lands Act (Canada) or section 6 of

the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act .
