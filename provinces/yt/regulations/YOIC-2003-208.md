YOIC 2003/208

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The portion of the old Alaska Highway, lying

easterly of the southerly extension of the easterly boundary

of Lot 457, Group 804, Plan 51666 CLSR, 26288 LTO and

continuing to the intersection of the old Alaska Highway

with the northerly boundary of the Alaska Highway, Plan

56901 CLSR, 34791 LTO, is hereby closed and declared to

no longer be highway.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 9th July 2003.
