YOIC 2021/143

*Made under the Yukon Act (Canada)*

Pursuant to section 36 of the Yukon Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders

1 The Commissioner, with the consent of the

Executive Council, makes the following request to the

Auditor General of Canada:

Pursuant to section 36 of the Yukon Act , I am

requesting that the Auditor General undertake the

annual audit of the accounts of Yukon University, in

each fiscal year, in accordance with the auditing

standards recommended by the Chartered

Professional Accountants of Canada.

In conducting the audit, the Auditor General is

asked to express their opinion as to whether the

consolidated financial statements present fairly, in all

material respects and in accordance with generally

accepted accounting principles, the financial position

of Yukon University at the end of its fiscal year and

the results of its operations and the changes in its net

assets and its cash flows for the fiscal year.

I request that these annual engagements begin for

Yukon University’s fiscal year ending March 31, 2021

and continue until I revoke this request with the

consent of the Executive Council.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, October 14 2021.
