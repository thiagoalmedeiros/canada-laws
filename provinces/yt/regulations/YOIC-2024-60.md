## THE ANIMAL PROTECTION AND

### Assignment of administration

1 This Regulation assigns responsibility for the

administration of the Animal Protection and Control Act .

### Minister of Environment

2 Except as otherwise provided by section 3, the Minister

of Environment is assigned responsibility for the

administration of the Animal Protection and Control Act .

### Minister of Environment and Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources

3 The Minister of Environment and the Minister of Energy,

Mines and Resources are assigned responsibility for the

administration of the following provisions of the Animal

Protection and Control Act :

(a) subparagraph 1(4)(b)(ii);

(b) section 2;

(c) sections 4, 5 and 6;

(d) sections 46 and 47;

(e) section 52;

(f) section 54;

(g) sections 56 and 57;

(h) subparagraphs 70(1)(a)(iii) to (vii) and h)

paragraphs 70(1)(b) to (d).
