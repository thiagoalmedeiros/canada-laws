YOIC 2016/84

*Made under the Miners Lien Act*

1 The forms in the Schedule to this Regulation are

prescribed for the purposes of the Act.

2 A claim of lien for work or services performed or

materials furnished is to be in Form 1.

3 A receipt for a discharge of a claim of lien is to be in

Form 2.

4 An affidavit verifying a claim of lien or a discharge of

a lien is to be in Form 3.
