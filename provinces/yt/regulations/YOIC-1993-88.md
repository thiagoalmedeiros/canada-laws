YOIC 1993/88

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this special warrant:

1 To authorize an amount of up to $6,025,000 for

Capital to be paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in

accordance with the attached Schedules A and B.

2 To stipulate that payments made under it be made:

(a) for the purpose of financing government

operations referred to in an appropriation Act

already enacted for the fiscal year 1993-94 or in

Schedules A and B to this order;

(b) in the fiscal year 1993-94.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 12th

day of July, 1993.

____________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
