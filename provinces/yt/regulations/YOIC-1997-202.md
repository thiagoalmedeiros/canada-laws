YOIC 1997/202

*Made under the Public Service Act*

Pursuant to section 209 of the Public Service Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 Notwithstanding sections 36 and 63 of the Public

Service Act , the Public Service Commissioner may,

under such circumstances as he or she considers

appropriate, permit a classification or reclassification

of a position to take effect on a date earlier than that

provided under sections 36 and 63 of the Act.

2 Where the Public Service Commissioner permits a

classification or reclassification of a position to take

effect on a date earlier than that provided in the Act,

the affected sections of the Act do not apply to such a

case.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 22

day of December, 1997.
