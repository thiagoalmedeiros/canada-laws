## W.C.H.S.B.O. 2000/04

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS' COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to subsection 101(1) of the Workers'

Compensation Act , the Board orders as follows:

1 The maximum assessable earnings for the year 2001

shall be $62,400.00 per annum.

2 This order shall come into force on January 1, 2001.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 23

day of November, 2000.
