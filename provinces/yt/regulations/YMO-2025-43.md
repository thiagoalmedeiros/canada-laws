YMO 2025/43

*Made under the Education Act*

### Whistle Bend Elementary School renamed

1 Whistle Bend Elementary School Council of Attendance

Area #30 is continued under the name Dámbü Tän

Kets'ádań K ų̀ — Cliff Trail Elementary School Council of

Attendance Area #30.

### Attendance Areas and Assignment of Schools Order amended In the Attendance Areas and Assignment of Schools Order (a) the heading to section 19 is replaced with the following:

2 Attendance area #30 — Dámbü Tän Kets'ádań K ų̀ —

Cliff Trail Elementary School

(b) in subsection 19(2), the expression “ Whistle

Bend Elementary School ” is replaced with the

expression “ Dámbü Tän Kets'ádań K ų̀ — Cliff

Trail Elementary School ”.

### Ministerial Order 1991/001 amended In Schedule A to Ministerial Order 1991/001

3 (a) the expression “ Whistle Bend Elementary School

Council of Attendance Area #30 ” is repealed;

### and

(b) the expression “ Dámbü Tän Kets'ádań K ų̀ — Cliff

Trail Elementary School Council of Attendance

Area #30 ” is added in alphabetical order.

### Ministerial Order 1991/002 amended In the Schedule to Ministerial Order 1991/002 (a) the following item is repealed:

4 Whistle Bend Elementary School Council of Attendance

Area #30 5

(b) the following item is added in alphabetical

### order:

Dámbü Tän Kets'ádań K ų̀ — Cliff Trail Elementary

School Council of Attendance Area #30 5
