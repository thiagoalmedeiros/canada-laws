# Relinquishes administration and control of public property (Kluane National Park) to the Minister responsible for Parks Canada

YOIC 2005/14

*Made under the Yukon Act (Canada)*

Pursuant to section 46 of the Yukon Act (Canada), the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The administration and control of the public real

property described in the attached Schedule is hereby

relinquished to the Minister responsible for Parks Canada.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this January 21st 2005.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon

from the Commissioner to the Minister responsible for
