### Definition

1 In this Regulation

“ taxation year ” has the same meaning as in

subsection 249(1) of the Income Tax Act (Canada).

### Provision of notice

2 (1) A notice referred to in subsection 6(5) of the Act

must be

(a) provided in writing;

(b) signed and dated by the attorney; and

(c) sent to each person to whom notice must be

provided as soon as practicable after the

enduring power of attorney comes into effect or

is brought into effect.

(2) Notice must be sent by registered mail or by

electronic communication in a manner that creates and

preserves a record confirming that the notice was

provided.

(3) Notice sent by registered mail is deemed to be

provided upon the issuance of a receipt and tracking

number.

(4) Notice sent by electronic communication is deemed

to be provided when sent.

(5) The attorney must maintain records regarding

notices for the duration of the period during which they

are empowered to act on behalf of the donor, including

(a) copies of all notices sent under subsection 6(5)

of the Act; and

(b) documentation of all efforts made to provide

such notices.

### Contents of notice

3 (1) A notice referred to in subsection 6(5) of the Act

must include the following information:

(a) the attorney’s full name, postal address, email

address and telephone number;

(b) the donor’s full name , postal address, email

address and telephone number;

(c) the date that the enduring power of attorney

comes into effect or is brought into effect and

the specified contingency;

(d) a copy of sections 8 to 9.02 of the Act setting out

the attorney’s authorities and duties;

(e) a list of the duties, authorities, powers and

functions that the attorney assumes under the

enduring power of attorney.

(2) The notice must advise the recipient that failure to

acknowledge receipt of the notice does not prevent the

attorney from acting.

### Records of financial transactions

4 (1) An attorney acting under an enduring power of

attorney must

(a) make a reasonable effort to ascertain the

donor’s property, funds and liabilities as of the

date on which the attorney first acts on behalf of

the donor; and

(b) prepare an initial list of the donor’s property,

funds and liabilities as of the date referred to in

paragraph (a).

(2) The attorney must maintain the following records of

financial transactions:

(a) a current list of the donor’s property, funds and

liabilities; and

(b) records sufficient to provide a complete account

of the attorney’s activities under the enduring

power of attorney.

(3) The attorney must

(a) maintain the records referred to in

subsection (2) during the period in which the

attorney is acting under the enduring power of

attorney; and

(b) retain those records for a period of six years

after the end of the taxation year in which the

attorney ceases to act.

(4) Records required under this section must be

maintained in a manner that ensures that they can be

produced within 30 days upon request and without

imposing significant cost or burden on the donor.

### Report to the Public Guardian and Trustee

5 (1) A report to the Public Guardian and Trustee under

section 21 of the Act must include

(a) the full name, postal address, email address and

telephone number of the person making the

report;

(b) the full name, postal address, email address and

telephone number of the attorney, if known;

(c) the full name, postal address, email address and

telephone number of the donor, if known;

(d) if the report involves allegations that a person

attempted to induce the donor to make or revoke

an enduring power of attorney, the full name,

postal address, email address and telephone

number of that person, if known; and

(e) a concise statement of the material facts

supporting the report.

(2) The report may be made in writing, verbally, or by

any other means.

### Coming into force

6 This Regulation come into force on the later of the

following days:

(a) the day on which the Enduring Powers of

Attorney and Related Amendments Act (2020)

comes into force;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of

regulations under the Regulations Act .
