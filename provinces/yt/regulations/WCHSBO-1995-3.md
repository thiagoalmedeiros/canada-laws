## W.C.H.S.B.O 1995/003

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to section 54 of the Workers’ Compensation

Act , the Board orders as follows:

1 The following assessment classification of industries

shall be established:

Class 1 Mining

Class 2 Construction, Trucking

and Related Industries

Class 3 Business or Public

Administration

Class 4 Specified Trades

Class 6 Service Industries

Class 8 Government

2 Workers’ Compensation Health and Safety Board

Order 1993/02 is revoked.

3 This Order shall be deemed to be into force January

1, 1995.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 30th

day of October, 1995.
