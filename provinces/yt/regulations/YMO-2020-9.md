## ORDER WITHDRAWING FROM

## DISPOSITION CERTAIN YUKON OIL AND

## GAS LANDS (PEEL WATERSHED REGIONAL

## LAND USE PLAN)

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to withdraw certain

Yukon oil and gas lands from disposition as required to

implement the Peel Watershed Regional Land Use Plan.

### Interpretation

2 (1) In this Order

“Peel Watershed Regional Land Use Plan” means the

regional land use plan developed and recommended

by the Peel Watershed Planning Commission for the

Peel Watershed Planning Region and approved in

August 2019 by Tr’ondëk Hwëch’in, First Nation of

Na-cho Nyäk Dun, Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation,

Gwich’in Tribal Council and Government of Yukon.

« Plan régional d’aménagement du bassin hydrographique

de la rivière Peel »

(2) A reference in this Order to a numbered LMU

(Landscape Management Unit) is a reference to the LMU

with that number under the Peel Watershed Regional Land

Use Plan as more particularly shown and described in the

Schedule.

### Lands withdrawn from disposition —

### interim

3 LMU 1, LMU 4, LMU 6, LMU 10, LMU 15, and LMU

16 (each of which is designated as a Wilderness Area or a

Wilderness Area-Boreal Caribou under the Peel Watershed

Regional Land Use Plan) are withdrawn from disposition

until January 1, 2030.

### Lands withdrawn from disposition —

### indeterminate

4 LMU 2, LMU 8, LMU 9, LMU 11, LMU 12 and LMU

14 (each of which is designated as a Special Management

Area under the Peel Watershed Regional Land Use Plan) are

withdrawn from disposition.
