WCHSBO 1993/11

*Made under the Workers’ Safety and Compensation Act*

## W.C.H.S.B.O. 1993/011

## WORKERS' COMPENSATION HEALTH AND SAFETY

## BOARD ORDER

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to section 90 of the Workers’ Compensation

Act , the Board orders as follows:

1 The maximum wage rate for the year 1993 shall be

forty thousand dollars ($40,000) per annum.

2 This maximum wage rate is to be applied to workers

who are entitled to compensation as a result of a disability

caused in 1992 or earlier.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 9th

day of December, 1993.
