YOIC 2000/121

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section11 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The portion of “Road” adjacent to Lot 33, Plan

65468 CLSR, 56420 LTO, Judas Creek Subdivision, Yukon is

hereby closed and declared to no longer be highway.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 24

day of July, 2000.

____________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
