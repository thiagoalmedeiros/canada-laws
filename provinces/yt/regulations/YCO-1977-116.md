YCO 1977/116

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 That portion of the Kluane Wagon Road located

within Lot 2, Group 15 (803) and registered in the Yukon

Land Titles Office in Whitehorse under number 47660

which is shown cross-hatched on Appendix A and

Appendix B as to location is closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 21st

day of June, A.D., 1977.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
