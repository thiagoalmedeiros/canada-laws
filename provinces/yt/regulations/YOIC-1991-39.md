YOIC 1991/39

*Made under the Education Act*

1 Pursuant to subsection 157(5) of the Education Act ,

every member of the Education Appeal Tribunal,

including the Chair, shall swear the oath or affirm the

declaration outlined in the attached Form “Q”.

## SCHEDULE

## FORM Q

Section 157(5)

OATH OR DECLARATION OF NON-DISCLOSURE FOR

EDUCATION APPEAL TRIBUNAL MEMBER

I, _______________________, do swear/solemnly declare

that

1. I am a member of the Education Appeal Tribunal

and have access to information relating to appeals

under the Education Act .

2. I will not knowingly disclose to any person any

information obtained in my capacity as a member of

the Education Appeal Tribunal, except as permitted by

the Act.

SWORN/AFFIRMED by me, at ___________________, Yukon

,

this ____ day of____________________ , 199_____ .

____________________________

Deponent/Affiant

____________________________________

(Person administering the oath

or affirmation)
