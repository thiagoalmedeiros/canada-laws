## SPECIAL WARRANT (NO. 1) 2020-21

Pursuant to the Financial Administration Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders

1 An amount of up to $39,382,000 to be paid from

the consolidated revenue fund in accordance with the

attached Schedules A and B is authorized in addition

to the net sum of $1,716,705,000 provided for in the

First Appropriation Act 2020-21 and Second

Appropriation Act 2020-21 .

2 Payments made under this special warrant may

be made for the purpose of financing government

operations for the period March 12, 2021 to

March 31, 2021 as set forth in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, March 12, 2021.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon
