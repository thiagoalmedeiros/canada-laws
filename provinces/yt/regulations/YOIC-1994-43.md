YOIC 1994/43

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this special warrant:

1 To authorize an amount of up to $63,730,000 for

Operation and Maintenance to be paid from the Consolida-

ted Revenue Fund in accordance with the attached Schedu-

les A and B.

2 To stipulate that payments made under it be made

for the purpose of financing government operations for the

period April 1, 1994 to April 30, 1994 as set forth in Sched-

ules A and B to this order;

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

22nd day of March, 1994.

____________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
