YOIC 1991/60

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby orders this special warrant:

1 To authorize an amount of up to $10,999,000 for

Operation and Maintenance and $655,000 for Capital to be

paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in accordance

with the attached Schedules A et B.

2 To stipulate that payments made under it be made:

(a) for the purpose of financing government

operations as required in the Third Appropriation

Act , 1990-91 and for the purpose of defraying

wage increases attributable to that year.

(b) in the fiscal year 1990-91.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 21st

day of March, 1991.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
