YOIC 2013/31

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

## 2012-13

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows

1 An amount of up to $5,641,000 to be paid from the

consolidated revenue fund in accordance with the attached

Schedules A and B is authorized in addition to the net sum

of $1,202,777,000 provided for in the First Appropriation

Act, 2012-13 and Second Appropriation Act, 2012-13 .

2 Payments made under the special warrant shall be

made for the purpose of financing government operations

for the period March 07, 2013 to March 31, 2013 as set

forth in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, March 7 2013.
