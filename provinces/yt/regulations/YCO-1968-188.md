YCO 1968/188

*Made under the Motor Vehicles Act*

## MOTOR VEHICLE ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of the Motor Vehicle Act , the

Commissioner of the Yukon Territory is pleased to and

doth hereby order as follows:

1 The Dawson Whitehorse Road right-of-way as

shown on plan of survey of record number 52635 in the

Canada Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa is hereby declared

closed to the public. Said right-of-way is more particularly

described as follows:

Commencing at survey monument marked L33Gl0

A180 as shown on said plan; thence in a northerly

direction along the easterly limit of said right-of-way

to survey monument marked A179; thence to survey

monument marked A178; thence to survey monument

marked 1L33G10 as shown on said plan; thence in a

straight line to survey monument 5L22G10RS as

shown on said plan; thence in a southerly direction

along the westerly limit of said right-of-way to survey

monument 3L22Gl0.R; thence to survey monument

L22G10.R; thence southerly along said right-of-way to

the corner directly opposite the point of

commencement; thence in a straight line to the point

of commencement.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 18th

day of October, A.D. 1968.
