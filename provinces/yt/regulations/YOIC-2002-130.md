YOIC 2002/130

*Made under the Motor Vehicles Act*

1 The program described in the publication entitled

“Yukon Alcohol Ignition Interlock Device Program”,

approved by the Director, Transport Services Branch on

March 1, 2002, as that publication may be amended or

replaced from time to time, is established as the Yukon

Alcohol Ignition Interlock Device Program for the

purposes of the Act and the Criminal Code (Canada).
