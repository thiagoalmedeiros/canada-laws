YMO 1993/11

*Made under the Criminal Code (Canada)*

Pursuant to section 672.1 of the Criminal Code

(Canada), the Minister of Health and Social Services

orders as follows:

1 The following places are designated as hospitals for

the custody, treatment or assessment of an accused in

respect of whom an assessment order, a disposition or

a placement decision is made under the Criminal Code

(Canada):

(a) Whitehorse General Hospital, Whitehorse,

Yukon;

(b) Mental Wellness and Substance Use Services,

Whitehorse, Yukon;

(c) Whitehorse Correctional Centre, Whitehorse,

Yukon.

(Added by M.O. 1994/009)

(Section 1 replaced by M.O.2025/48)

(Paragraph 1(c) added by M.O. 2026/03)

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 25th

day of October, 1993.

Minister of Health and Social Services/Ministre
