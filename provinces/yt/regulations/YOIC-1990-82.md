YOIC 1990/82

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to section 15 of the Financial Administration

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 That a remission be granted to Curragh Resources

for the amount of $176,000 (plus applicable interest)

related to the costs of construction of pull-outs on the

South Klondike Highway.

2 Order-in-Council 1989/44, dated March 27, 1989 is

revoked.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 13th

day of June, A.D., 1990.

Commissioner of the Yukon
