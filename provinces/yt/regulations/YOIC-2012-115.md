# Establishes that no part of described land (Upper Liard) is deemed to be reserved for the Crown

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Government of Yukon. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Yukon. Text obtained from the [official source](https://laws.yukon.ca/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2012/2012-0115/2012-0115_1.pdf) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

YOIC 2012/115

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

Pursuant to section 11 of the Territorial Lands (Yukon)

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows

1 If at any time before 2015 notification under

subsection 7(2) of the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act is

issued for the first time in respect of a property described in

the attached schedule, no part of the property (or, if the

notification applies to only a portion of the property, no

part of that portion) is at and after that time to be deemed

under section 11 of the Act to be reserved to the Crown.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, June 21, 2012.
