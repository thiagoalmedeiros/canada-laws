YOIC 2024/154

*Made under the Legislative Assembly Act*

### Accommodation reimbursement

1 The maximum amount that a member may be

reimbursed for their actual expenditures for

accommodation under subsection 46(2) of the Act is

$2,104 per month.

### Transitional

2 A member who made actual expenditures for

accommodation under subsection 46(2) of the Act prior

to the coming into force of this section is entitled to be

reimbursed to a maximum of $2,104 per month for

expenditures made on or after May 15, 2025.
