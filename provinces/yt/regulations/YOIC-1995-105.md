YOIC 1995/105

*Made under the Assessment and Taxation Act*

Pursuant to section 56 of the Assessment and Taxation

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 The attached Rural Electrification And Telephone

Local Improvement Tax Regulation, 1995, Schedule 1, is

hereby established.

2 Schedule 1 of the Rural Electrification and

Telephone Local Improvement Tax Regulations, 1993 is

amended as follows for Region 2 - Henderson Corner:

under the heading “Description” the description “Lots 15-

1, 15-2, 16-1, 16-2, 18-1, 18-2, 20-1, 20-2, 21-1, 21-2” is

substituted for the description “Lots 15 to 21, Plan 89-32”

(Lots 17 and 19 remain as in the 1993 submission).

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 15th

day of June, 1995.

__________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
