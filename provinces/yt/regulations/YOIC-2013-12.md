YOIC 2013/12

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 That portion of the North Klondike Highway,

comprising approximately 47.28 square metres and located

within the boundaries of the City of Dawson, which is

described in Appendix A and outlined in bold on the map

attached as Appendix B, is closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, January 18, 2013.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon

## APPENDIX A

In Yukon , lying within Quad 116 B/3, that parcel of land

located within the City of Dawson, more particularly

described as follows

Bearings are UTM grid and are referred to the central

meridian of UTM Zone 7 (141° west longitude) as indicated

on Plan 96-48 LTO, Plan 78636 CLSR;

Starting from the shown monument marking the south

west corner of Lot 20, Block HA, marked BL HA, L20, R, R,

1963 running southerly along the easterly limit of Front

(First) Avenue a distance of 94.55 m to the point marking

the south westerly corner of Lot 1 Block HC, said point

being the point of commencement;

From the point of commencement following a bearing of

302° 55’ 24” a distance of 3.00 metres;

Thence 17° 44’ 24” a distance of 15.76 metres;

Thence on a bearing of 122° 55’ 24” a distance of 3.00

metres;

Thence in a southerly direction along the westerly limit of

Lot 1 Block HC a distance of 15.76 metres to the point of

commencement;

Said parcel containing approximately 47.28 square metres

more or less.
