## ORDER APPROVING THE

## REMUNERATION OF PERSONS

## APPOINTED, EMPLOYED OR RETAINED

## BY THE ELECTIONS OFFICE ON BEHALF

## OF THE ELECTORAL DISTRICT

## BOUNDARIES COMMISSION

### Approval

1 The remuneration, reimbursement for expenses and

conditions of employment of persons appointed,

employed or retained by the Elections Office on behalf of

the Electoral District Boundaries Commission under

subsection 413(1) of the Elections Act , as set out in the

Schedule, are approved.

### Order-in-Council 2017/188 repealed

2 Order-in-Council 2017/188 is repealed.

Section 1

## SCHEDULE

1 Remuneration

The remuneration of a person appointed, employed or

retained under subsection 413(1) of the Act to provide

one or more of the following services is as follows:

Administrative support Up to $45 per hour

services

Communications services Up to $180 per hour

Legal services Up to $425 per hour

Geomatic and mapping Up to $110 per hour

services

Other technical and Up to $125 per hour

advisory support services

2 Reimbursement for expenses

A person appointed, employed or retained under

subsection 413(1) of the Act is to be reimbursed in

accordance with Management Board Directive #13/84 as

amended or replaced from time to time for expenses

that they incur in the provision of their services as if they

were a public servant and in the amount that applies at

the time that the services were provided.

### Conditions of employment

3 The conditions of employment of a person employed

under subsection 413(1) of the Act are to be in

accordance with the Collective Agreement between the

Government of Yukon and the Public Service Alliance of

Canada in effect at the time of employment, as if they

were a public servant.
