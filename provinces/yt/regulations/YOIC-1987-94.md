YOIC 1987/94

*Made under the Young Persons Offences Act*

Pursuant to section 36 of the Young Persons Offences

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 The Justice of Peace Court is designated as a

youth court for the purposes of the Young Persons

Offences Act .

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 11th

day of May A.D., 1987.
