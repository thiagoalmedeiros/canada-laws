YOIC 2023/44

*Made under the Public Service Labour Relations Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation

“ National Joint Council ” means the National Joint

Council whose establishment was authorized by Order-

in-Council P.C. 3676 (Canada), dated May 16, 1944.

« Conseil national mixte »

### Remuneration

2 Members of a conciliation board are paid the following

remuneration for performing their duties as conciliation

board members:

(a) $600 per hour for the chair of the conciliation

board;

(b) $400 per hour for the other members of the

conciliation board.

### Expenses

3 Members of a conciliation board are reimbursed for

travel and living expenses that they incur in connection

with the performance of their work away from their

ordinary place of residence in accordance with the Travel

Directive of the National Joint Council that is in force at

the time that the work is performed.
