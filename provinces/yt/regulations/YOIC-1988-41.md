YOIC 1988/41

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of Sections 19(2) and (3) of

the Financial Administration Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council is pleased to and doth hereby order a

special warrant as follows:

1 An amount of up to $6,757,000 O & M and

$28,460,000 Capital to be paid from the Consolidated

Revenue Fund in accordance with the attached schedule.

2 The payments to be made for the purpose of

financing government operations as required in the Fourth

Appropriation Act , 1987-88.

3 The payments to be made in the fiscal year 1987-88.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 11th

day of March, A.D., 1988.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
