## 2021-22

Pursuant to the Financial Administration Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders

1 An amount of up to $401,054,000 may be paid

from the consolidated revenue fund in accordance

with the attached Schedules A and B.

2 Payments made under this special warrant may

be made for the purpose of financing government

operations for the period April 1, 2021 to

May 31, 2021 as set forth in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, March 12, 2021.
