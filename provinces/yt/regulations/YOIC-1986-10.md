YOIC 1986/10

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of section 17(1) of the

Highways Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows:

1 The roads in the community of Carcross, as shown

crosshatched on Schedule A attached hereto, are closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 24th

day of June, A.D., 1986.

______________________________

Administrator of the Yukon
