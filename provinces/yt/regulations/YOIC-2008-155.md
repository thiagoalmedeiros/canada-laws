YOIC 2008/155

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 That portion of the Right-of-Way of the Old Alaska

Highway adjacent to Lot 1295 Quad 105 D/14, CLSR Plan

75815, LTO Plan 93-180 as shown “cross-hatched” on the

schedule is closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 23 October 2008.
