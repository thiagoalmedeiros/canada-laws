# Order in respect of Liard First Nation Settlement Land Selection Parcel R-137B

YOIC 2025/224

*Made under the Placer Mining Act*

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to prohibit entry onto Liard

First Nation settlement land selection parcel R-137B and

the lands identified as Area 1 on the map attached as a

Schedule to this Order, as described in section 3, to

facilitate consultation respecting

(a) Liard First Nation settlement land selection

parcel R-137B; and

(b) the inclusion of lands within the Ross River Area.

### Definitions

2 In this Order

“ parcel R-137B ” means the tract of land identified as R-

137B on map sheets 105 I/05, 105 I/06, 105 I/11 and

105 I/12, all dated June 21, 2002, on file with Crown-

Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs Canada at

Whitehorse in Yukon, copies of which have been

deposited with the Manager, Lands Client Services,

Department of Energy, Mines and Resources,

Government of Yukon at Whitehorse and copies of which

have been deposited with the Mining Recorders at

Whitehorse, Watson Lake, Mayo and Dawson, in Yukon;

« parcelle R-137B »

“ recorded claim ” means

(a) a recorded placer claim, acquired under the

Yukon Placer Mining Act (Canada) or the Placer

Mining Act , that is in good standing, or

(b) a recorded mineral claim, acquired under the

Yukon Quartz Mining Act (Canada) or the Quartz

Mining Act , that is in good standing. « claim

inscrit »

“ Ross River Area ” means the tract of land identified as

the Ross River Area and shown cross-hatched and

outlined in bold on the map attached as Appendix 1 to

the Prohibition of Entry on Certain Lands (“Ross River

Area”) Order . « région de Ross River »

### Prohibition of entry

3 Subject to section 4, no person shall enter onto parcel R-

137B or the lands identified as Area 1 on the map

attached as a Schedule to this Order during the period

starting on the date this Order is made and ending on

November 30, 2026, for the purpose of

(a) locating a claim or prospecting for gold or other

precious minerals or stones under the Placer

Mining Act ; or

(b) locating a claim or prospecting or mining for

minerals under the Quartz Mining Act.

### Existing rights and interests

4 Section 3 does not apply to entry onto parcel R-137B or

the lands identified as Area 1 on the map attached as a

Schedule to this Order for the purposes of prospecting

or mining on a recorded claim by the owner or holder of

the recorded claim.
