YOIC 2013/143

*Made under the Judicature Act*

Pursuant to paragraph 41(1)(a) and section 51 of the

Judicature Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows

1 The offices of the clerk and sheriff shall be open

from 9:00 a.m. until 4:00 p.m. on each day except on

Saturdays, Sundays and holidays.

2 Order-in-Council 1983/066 is repealed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, July 9, 2013.
