YMO 2017/28

*Made under the Area Development Act*

Pursuant to section 3 of the Area Development Act and

the Ministerial Rezoning Regulation , the Minister of Energy,

Mines and Resources orders

1 In addition to the accessory uses permitted by

section 17 of Schedule A to the Golden Horn Development

Area Regulation , a permitted accessory use of Lot 1178,

Quad 105 D/10, Plan 91390 CLSR, 2006-0132 LTO is up to

three guest cabins.
