YOIC 1992/50

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to section 77 of the Financial Administration

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council, on the

recommendation of the Executive Council Member,

orders as follows:

1 The Department of Finance is, in relation to the

loan of public money to Curragh Resources Inc., of an

amount up to $5,000,000, exempt from the provisions

of the Financial Administration Act.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 7th

day of April, 1992.
