# Yukon Hospital Corporation Board of Trustees Remuneration Regulation

YOIC 2010/111

*Made under the Hospital Act*

### Remuneration for chair

1 The chair of the Yukon Hospital Corporation Board of

Trustees shall be paid

(a) a stipend of $2000 per month; and

(b) in respect of each meeting of the Board of

Trustees, of a committee of the Board of

Trustees or of the First Nations Health

Committee under section 6 of the Act, an

honorarium equal to the lesser of

(i) the total of

(A) $600 for each day on which the meeting

runs for four hours or more,

(B) $300 for each day on which the meeting

runs for less than four hours, and

(C) $300 for time spent in preparing for the

meeting; and

(ii) $900.

### Remuneration for members

2 A member of the Yukon Hospital Corporation Board of

Trustees (other than the chair and the Chief Executive

Officer) shall, in respect of each meeting of the Board

of Trustees, of a committee of the Board of Trustees or

of the First Nations Health Committee under section 6

of the Act, be paid an honorarium equal to the lesser of

(a) the total of

(i) $400 for each day on which the meeting

runs for four hours or more,

(ii) $200 for each day on which the meeting

runs for less than four hours, and

(iii) $200 for any time, regardless of its duration,

spent in preparing for the meeting; and

(b) $600.

### Remuneration – travel to Board meetings

3 A member of the Yukon Hospital Corporation Board of

Trustees (other than the Chief Executive Officer but for

greater certainty including the chair) shall, in respect of

each meeting of the Board of trustees, be paid an

honorarium equal to the lesser of

(a) $50 per hour for time spent in travel from the

community where the member resides to a

Board meeting held in another Yukon

community; and

(b) $500.

### Remuneration – training, etc.

4 A member of the Yukon Hospital Corporation Board of

Trustees (other than the Chief Executive Officer but for

greater certainty including the chair) shall, in respect of

each event that is a conference, a workshop, a seminar

or a training course and that the member attends in

relation to the member’s duties on the Board, be paid

an honorarium equal to the total of

(a) $400 for each day on which the event runs for

four hours or more; and

(b) $200 for each day on which the event runs for

less than four hours.

### Expenses

5 The chair and other members of the Yukon Hospital

Corporation Board of Trustees shall be reimbursed for

expenses incurred in relation to their duties on the

Board in accordance with the Management Board

Government Travel directive.

### Exception – public servants

6 Despite any other provision of this Regulation, no

person employed in the public service of the Yukon who

is appointed to the Yukon Hospital Corporation Board of

Trustees under paragraph 5(1)(h) of the Act is to be

remunerated under any of sections 1 to 4.
