# Order Prohibiting Entry on Certain Lands in Yukon (Ts’alwnjik Chu - Nordenskiold Wetland Habitat Protection Area)

YOIC 2010/142

*Made under the Placer Mining Act*

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to prohibit entry to certain

lands to facilitate the establishment of the Ts’alwnjik -

Nordenskiold Wetland Habitat Protection Area.

### Interpretation

2 In this Order,

“ recorded claim ” means

(a) a recorded placer claim that is in good standing

acquired or continued under the Placer Mining

Act ;

(b) a recorded mining claim that is in good

standing acquired or continued under the

Quartz Mining Act .

### Prohibition

3 No person shall enter on the lands set out in the

schedule for the purpose of

(a) locating a claim, or prospecting for gold or

other precious minerals or stones, under the

Placer Mining Act , or

(b) locating a claim, or prospecting or mining for

minerals under the Quartz Mining Act .

### Existing rights and interests

4 Section 3 does not apply to entry for the purposes of

prospecting or mining on a recorded claim by the

owner or holder of the recorded claim.

## SCHEDULE

Lands on which entry is prohibited (Ts’alwnjik Chu -

Nordenskiold Wetland Habitat Protection Area)

The area for the Ts’alwnjik Chu - Nordenskiold Wetland

Habitat Protection Area is shown on Plan 88225 CLSR,

dated December 9, 2003, being the Administrative Plan

showing Nordenskiold Wetland Habitat Protection Area

(NWHPA), a copy of which is on file with the Surveyor

General Branch of the Department of Natural Resources

Canada in Whitehorse.

Saving and Excepting

Lot numbered 1000 in Quad numbered 115H/16 in the

Yukon Territory, as said lot is shown on a plan of

survey of record number 78079 in the Canada Lands

Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in

the Land Titles Office for the Yukon Land Registration

District at Whitehorse under number 95-129;

Base Map 115H/16, having an area of approximately 41

hectares;

shown on a plan of survey of record number 41592 in

number 22709;

survey of record number 62530 in the Canada Lands

Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in

District at Whitehorse under number 48358;

shown approximately by a double solid line designated

as Access Road on Territorial Resource Base Map

115I/01.
