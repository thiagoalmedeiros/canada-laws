YOIC 2003/128

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

Pursuant to section 21 of the Territorial Lands (Yukon)

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows

1 The annexed Withdrawal of Certain Lands (Haines

Junction, Y.T.) from Disposal Order is hereby made.

2 This Order comes into force April 1, 2003.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 25th day

of March, 2003.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## WITHDRAWAL OF CERTAIN LANDS (HAINES

## JUNCTION) FROM DISPOSAL ORDER

### Withdrawal from disposal

1 It is hereby ordered, for the purpose of encouraging

land applicants to reside in the registered subdivision that

has been established at Haines Junction in the vicinity of

Mile 1017 of the Alaska Highway by promoting subdivision

control in the adjoining area, to withdraw from disposal

under the Act the lands described in the schedule.

## SCHEDULE

In the Yukon Territory, at Haines Junction; that parcel

described as follows

COMMENCING at iron post numbered 4L30 in the easterly

limit of Lot 30, Group 803, according to a plan of survey of

record number 43243 in the Canada Lands Surveys Records

at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the Land Titles Office

for the Yukon Land Registration Districts at Whitehorse

under number 22673;

THENCE, 346°39’ northwesterly, 4370.7 feet;

THENCE, 250°19’ southwesterly, 6303.9 feet;

THENCE, 160°19’ southeasterly, 7159.4 feet;

THENCE, 70°19’ northeasterly, 5509.3 feet, approximately

to a line bearing 166°39’ from the point of

commencement;

THENCE, 346°39’ northwesterly, 2832.7 feet,

approximately to the point of commencement;

SAVING AND EXCEPTING thereout and therefrom the

following according to plans in said Records and Office

listed hereunder;

Haines Junction Subdivision, plans 41519 and 43245

Lot 30, Group 803, and road, plan 43243

Lot 32, Group 803, plan 43383 those parts, lying within the

aforedescribed parcel, of Haines Cut-off Road, plan 42118

Alaska Highway, plan 40905

said bearings being astronomic and referred to the

meridian through an iron post numbered H1827 in the

northerly limit of the Alaska Highway, according to plan

40904 in said Division; the remainder containing

approximately 770 acres.
