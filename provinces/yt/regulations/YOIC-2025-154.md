# Health Authority Board of Directors Remuneration Regulation

YOIC 2025/154

*Made under the Health Authority Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation

“ approved meeting ” means

(a) in respect of a member of the board, a board

meeting, or other meeting in connection with

the performance of their duties under the Act as

a member of the board that is approved by the

board or the chair of the board, and

(b) in respect of a member of a committee, a

meeting of the committee, or other meeting in

connection with the performance of their duties

under the Act as a member of the committee

that is approved by the chair of the committee;

« réunion approuvée »

“ approved training event ”, in respect of an individual,

means a conference, workshop, seminar or other

training event or program approved by the board, that

the individual attends in connection with the

performance of their duties under the Act as the chair of

the board, member of the board, the chair of a

committee or member of a committee, as the case may

be; « activité de formation approuvée »

“ board ”, for greater certainty, includes the initial board

within the meaning of section 96 of the Act; « conseil »

“ chair of the board ”, for greater certainty, includes the

initial chair within the meaning of section 96 of the Act;

« président du conseil »

“ committee ” means the finance, audit and risk

committee or the quality assurance, patient safety and

cultural safety committee; « comité »

“ finance, audit and risk committee ” means the

committee of the board required to be established by the

board under paragraph 25(1)(a) of the Act; « comité des

finances, de la vérification et du risque »

“ full day ” means a period or periods that total more

than four hours in one day; « journée complète »

“ member of a committee ” does not include the chair of

the committee; « membre d’un comité »

“ member of the board ” does not include the chair of the

board; « membre du conseil »

“ quality assurance, patient safety and cultural safety

committee ” means the committee of the board required

to be established by the board under paragraph 25(1)(b)

of the Act; « comité de l’assurance de la qualité, de la

sécurité des patients et de la sécurisation culturelle »

“ travel” , in relation to an individual, means travel

between the community where the individual ordinarily

resides and another community; « déplacement »

“ year ” means the financial year of the health authority

as set out in section 50 of the Act. « exercice »

### Remuneration of chair of the board

2 (1) The chair of the board is to be paid a retainer of

$58,500 per year for the performance of all their duties

under the Act as chair of the board, other than

attendance at meetings and attendance at approved

training events, and related travel, which are

remunerated separately under subsection (3) and

sections 6 and 7.

(2) For greater certainty, the retainer referred to in

subsection (1) includes, but is not limited to,

remuneration for preparing for meetings, preparing

agendas, undertaking research and writing, preparing

or responding to correspondence and engaging in other

communications.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), the chair of the board is

to be paid

(a) $750 for a full day for time spent attending a

board meeting or other meetings in connection

with the performance of their duties under the

Act as chair of the board; and

(b) $375 for time spent attending a board meeting

or other meetings in connection with the

performance of their duties under the Act as

chair of the board, for a period or periods less

than a full day.

(4) The maximum amount the chair of the board is to

be paid under subsection (3) is $26,250 per year.

### Remuneration of members of the board

3 (1) A member of the board is to be paid a retainer of

$28,600 per year for the performance of all their duties

under the Act as a member of the board, other than

attendance at approved meetings and attendance at

approved training events, and related travel, which are

remunerated separately under subsection (3) and

sections 6 and 7.

(2) For greater certainty, the retainer referred to in

subsection (1) includes, but is not limited to,

remuneration for preparing for approved meetings,

undertaking research and writing, preparing or

responding to correspondence and engaging in other

communications.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), a member of the board is

to be paid

(a) $750 for a full day for time spent attending an

approved meeting; and

(b) $375 for time spent attending an approved

meeting, for a period or periods less than a full

day.

(4) The maximum amount a member of the board is to

be paid under subsection (3) is $18,750 per year.

### Remuneration of chair of a committee

4 (1) The chair of a committee is to be paid a retainer of

$11,141 per year for the performance of all their duties

under the Act as chair of the committee, other than

attendance at meetings and attendance at approved

training events, and related travel, which are

remunerated separately under subsection (3) and

sections 6 and 7.

(2) For greater certainty, the retainer referred to in

subsection (1) includes, but is not limited to,

remuneration for preparing for meetings, preparing

agendas, undertaking research and writing, preparing

or responding to correspondence and engaging in other

communications.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), the chair of a committee

is to be paid

(a) $750 for a full day for time spent attending a

meeting of the committee or other meetings in

connection with the performance of their duties

under the Act as the chair of the committee; and

(b) $375 for time spent attending a meeting of the

committee or other meetings in connection with

the performance of their duties under the Act as

the chair of the committee, for a period or

periods less than a full day.

(4) The maximum amount the chair of a committee is

to be paid under subsection (3) is $6,000 per year.

### Remuneration of members of a committee

5 (1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), a member of a

committee is to be paid

(a) $750 for a full day for time spent attending an

approved meeting; and

(b) $375 for time spent attending an approved

meeting for a period or periods less than a full

day.

(2) The maximum amount a member of a committee is

to be paid under subsection (1) is $6,000 per year.

(3) No remuneration is to be paid under subsection (1)

to an individual who is not a member of the board but

who is appointed to sit as a member of the quality

assurance, patient safety and cultural safety committee

under subsection 25(5) of the Act if

(a) they are employed by a government, association

or any other organization and are appointed to

the committee as part of their employment

duties; and

(b) they receive remuneration from their employer

for the time spent attending approved meetings.

### Remuneration for training

6 (1) Subject to subsection (2), the chair of the board, a

member of the board, the chair of a committee or a

member of a committee is to be paid

(a) $750 for a full day for time spent attending

approved training events; and

(b) $375 for time spent attending approved training

events, for a period or periods less than a full

day.

(2) No remuneration is to be paid under subsection (1)

to an individual who is not a member of the board but

who is appointed to sit as a member of the quality

assurance, patient safety and cultural safety committee

under subsection 25(5) of the Act, if

(a) they are employed by a government, association

or any other organization and are appointed to

the committee as part of their employment

duties; and

(b) they receive remuneration from their employer

for the time spent attending approved training

events.

### Remuneration for travel

7 (1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), the chair of the

board, a member of the board, the chair of a committee

or a member of a committee is to be paid

(a) $750 for a full day for time spent for travel to

attend meetings referred to in subsections 2(3),

3(3), 4(3) or 5(1), as the case may be, or for

travel to attend approved training events; and

(b) $375 for time spent for travel to attend meetings

referred to in subsections 2(3), 3(3), 4(3) or 5(1),

as the case may be, or for travel to attend

approved training events, for a period or periods

less than a full day.

(2) To be eligible for remuneration under

subsection (1), the chair of the board, a member of the

board, the chair of a committee or a member of a

committee must have travelled 200 km or more in one

day.

(3) No remuneration is to be paid under subsection (1)

to an individual who is not a member of the board but

who is appointed to sit as a member of the quality

assurance, patient safety and cultural safety committee

under subsection 25(5) of the Act, if

(a) they are employed by a government, association

or any other organization and are appointed to

the committee as part of their employment

duties; and

(b) they receive remuneration from their employer

for the time spent for travel to attend approved

meetings or approved training events.

### Reimbursement of travel expenses

8 (1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), travel expenses

incurred by the chair of the board, a member of the

board, the chair of a committee or a member of a

committee to attend meetings referred to in

subsections 2(3), 3(3), 4(3) or 5(1), as the case may be,

or to attend approved training events are to be

reimbursed as follows:

(a) if there is a directive of the management board

in force concerning payment of travel expenses

to members of boards of the Government of

Yukon, in accordance with the directive, as

amended or replaced from time to time;

(b) otherwise, in accordance with the policy for

payment of travel expenses to employees of the

Government of Yukon, as amended or replaced

from time to time.

(2) To be eligible for reimbursement of travel expenses

under subsection (1), the chair of the board, a member

of the board, the chair of a committee or a member of

a committee must have travelled 70 km or more one

way in one day.

(3) No reimbursement is to be paid under subsection (1)

to an individual who is not a member of the board but

who is appointed to sit as a member of the quality

assurance, patient safety and cultural safety committee

under subsection 25(5) of the Act, if

(a) they are employed by a government, association

or any other organization and are appointed to

the committee as part of their employment

duties; and

(b) they receive reimbursement from their

employer of their travel expenses to attend

approved meetings or approved training events.

### Maximum daily amounts

9 (1) Despite any other provision of this Regulation, no

remuneration in excess of $750 for any one day is to be

paid to the chair of the board, a member of the board,

the chair of a committee or a member of a committee

for time spent attending meetings or approved training

events.

(2) Despite any other provision of this Regulation, no

remuneration in excess of $750 for any one day is to be

paid to the chair of the board, a member of the board,

the chair of a committee or a member of a committee

for time spent for travel to attend meetings or approved

training events.

### Proration of retainers

10 (1) If the chair of the board, a member of the board or

the chair of a committee is appointed part way through

a year, or their appointment expires or is revoked part

way through a year, the retainer to be paid for that year

under subsections 2(1), 3(1) or 4(1), as the case may be,

is to be prorated for the number of days in the year they

are appointed as chair of the board, a member of the

board or chair of the committee.

(2) If the chair of the board, a member of the board or

the chair of a committee is unavailable to perform their

duties under the Act for more than 30 consecutive days

in a year, the retainer to be paid for that year under

subsections 2(1), 3(1) or 4(1), as the case may be, is to

be prorated to take account of the number of

consecutive days of their unavailability.

### Retainer for vice-chair

11 If the chair of the board is unavailable to perform their

duties under the Act for a period of more than

30 consecutive days in a year, or if the office of chair of

the board is vacant for a period of more than

30 consecutive days in a year, and if the vice-chair

selected under subsection 17(2) of the Act performs the

duties of the chair of the board during one or more of

those periods, the vice-chair is to be paid the retainer

for the chair of the board under subsection 2(1) prorated

for the number of consecutive days in the year the vice-

chair performs the duties of chair of the board.

### No reimbursement for professional fees

12 There is to be no reimbursement to the chair of the

board, a member of the board, the chair of a committee

or a member of a committee for any professional,

association or membership dues or fees.

### Publication of remuneration

13 (1) Within six months of the end of a year, the board

must make public the following information:

(a) the budgeted amounts for the year for the board

and each of the committees respecting

(i) retainers,

(ii) remuneration for attendance at meetings,

(iii) remuneration for attendance at approved

training events,

(iv) remuneration for travel, and

(v) reimbursement of travel expenses;

(b) the actual amounts paid in the year to the chair

of the board, each member of the board, the

chair of each committee and each member of a

committee respecting the items referred to in

subparagraphs (a)(i) to (v).

(2) The information referred to in subsection (1) must

be included in the board’s annual report prepared under

section 61 of the Act and must be published on a health

authority public-facing website.

### Review

14 The Minister must, before the fifth anniversary of the

coming into force of this Regulation, begin a review of

the provisions of this Regulation and, following

completion of the review, may make recommendations

to the Commissioner in Executive Council for any

amendments the Minister considers appropriate.

### Application during transition

15 For greater certainty, to ensure an orderly and effective

transition under Part 11, Division 2 of the Act, this

Regulation also applies in the period before the coming

into force of provisions of the Act under

subsection 108(1).
