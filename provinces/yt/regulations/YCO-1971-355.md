YCO 1971/355

*Made under the Expropriation Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Expropriation Act , the

Commissioner of the Yukon Territory is pleased to and

doth hereby order as follows:

1 The lands and premises delineated, described and

set out in Registered Plan Number 34533 are hereby

expropriated for the public purposes of the Territory.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 21st

day of September, A.D., 1971.
