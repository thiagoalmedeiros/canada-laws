YOIC 2000/5

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to subsection 14(3) of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The portion of the old Blind Creek Road between

the gates at Kilometres 9 and 10 as shown on Schedule A

hereto is hereby closed between January 1 and May 31 and

between November 1 and December 31 of each year to

protect the Fannin sheep winter range.

2 This order is deemed to have come into force on

November 29, 1999.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 10

day of January, 2000.

____________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
