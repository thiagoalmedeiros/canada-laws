## MENDENHALL ACCESS ROADS COST

## RECOVERY LOCAL IMPROVEMENT

## TAX REGULATION

1 In these regulations, "local improvement" means

the construction of access roads to serve the properties

listed in Schedule 1.

2 The properties listed in Schedule 1 are declared to

benefit from the local improvement.

3 To recover the cost of the local improvement there

is levied on each property listed in Schedule 1 a local

improvement tax consisting of the principal amount and

the interest for that property in Schedule 1.

The local improvement tax

(a) shall be shown at a separate item on the tax

notice for the property;

(b) is due and payable in the annual instalments

shown on Schedule 1 at the same time as the

general tax;

(c) is part of the total tax payable for the

property; and

(d) may be paid in advance by paying the

outstanding balance together with the interest

accrued at the time of payment.
