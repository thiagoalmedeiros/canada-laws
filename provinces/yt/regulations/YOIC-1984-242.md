YOIC 1984/242

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

Pursuant to subsection 86(5) of the Employment

Standards Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

is pleased to and doth hereby order as follows:

1 Remuneration payable to the Members of the

Employment Standards Board shall be $100.00 for each

day’s sitting or portion thereof.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 24

day of August, A.D., 1984.
