YOIC 1999/183

*Made under the Health Act*

1 The Department of Health and Social Services may

supply to third parties who are not clients of the

Workers' Safety and Compensation Board the same

occupational rehabilitation services as it offers to clients

of the Board under the agreement between the Board

and the Department signed July 5, 1999.

2 The fees to be charged for the services supplied

under section 1 are:

For an Isernhagen Certified

Functional Capacity Evaluation (FCE)

- six hours plus administration and $1,200

reporting

For additional follow-up or

consultation - based on the time $120/hour

each therapist spends with the

per therapist

client
