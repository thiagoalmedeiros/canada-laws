YOIC 2019/128

*Made under the Public Service Labour Relations Act*

1 In this regulation

“ National Joint Council ” means the National Joint

Council whose establishment was authorised by Order-

in-Council P.C. 3676 (Canada) dated May 16, 1944.

« Conseil national mixte »

2 An adjudicator is to be paid $730 per day for

performing their duties as an adjudicator.

3 An adjudicator is to be reimbursed for travel and

living expenses that are incurred in the performance of

their duties as an adjudicator in accordance with the

Travel Directive of the National Joint Council that is in

force at the time that the duties are performed.
