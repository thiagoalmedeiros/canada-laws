## YUKON ACT (CANADA)

### and

## YUKON ACT (CANADA)

Pursuant to sections 12 and 31 of the Lands Act and

subsection 47(3) of the Yukon Act (Canada) , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The administration, control and management of

the lands herein described is hereby transferred to Her

Majesty the Queen in Right of Canada:

Lots 41 and 42, Plan 27954, Ross River,

Lots 56 and 72, Plan 29803, Ross River.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

13th day of May, 1998.
