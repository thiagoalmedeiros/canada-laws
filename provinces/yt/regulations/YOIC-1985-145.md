YOIC 1985/145

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of Sections 20(2) and (3) of

the Financial Administration Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council is pleased to and doth hereby order a

special warrant as follows:

1 An amount of up to $29,765,000 to be paid from

the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

2 The payments to be made for the purpose of

financing government operations for the months of July

and August, 1985.

3 The payments to be made in the fiscal year 1985-86.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 27th

day of June, A.D., 1985.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
