*Made under the Public Service Labour Relations Act*

## PUBLIC SERVICE STAFF RELATIONS ACT

## ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of the Public Service Staff

Relations Act of the Yukon Territory, the Commissioner

of the Yukon Territory is pleased and doth hereby order

as follows:

1 The Board, as defined in the Public Service Staff

Relations Act , shall be:

(Amended by C.O. 1973/258, 1974/9, 1974/10 and 1974/11)

2 The term of office for the Chairman and Vice-

Chairman shall be five years from the date of

appointment, and for the members representing

employer interest and the members representing

employee interest, three years from the date of

appointment.

(Added by C.O. 1970/344)

3 The remuneration to be paid to each member of

the Yukon Public Service Staff Relations Board, except

the Chairman and the Vice-Chairman, shall be

calculated on a prorata basis of his per diem

remuneration as member of the Yukon Public Service

Staff Relations Board for the time he is engaged on the

work of the Yukon Public Service Staff Relations Board

together with actual travelling and living expenses

incurred while absent from his normal place of

residence in connection with his duties for which the

usual detailed accounts will be submitted. The member

is not entitled to charge travelling and living expenses

if he is reimbursed from other sources for such

expenses.

(Added by C.O. 1970/344)

4 Authority is delegated to the Chairman of the

Yukon Public Service Staff Relations Board to pay to

Board members, arbitrators, adjudicators, conciliators

and members of the conciliation board and such other

persons as the Board deems necessary for the

performance of its duties under the Act.

(a) such remuneration per day as the Chairman

deems appropriate; and

(b) travelling and living expenses incurred while

absent from the person’s normal place of

residence in connection with his duties, either

subject to submission of the usual detailed

accounts or in accordance with the rate of

subsistence allowance currently paid to

members of the Public Service of the Territory,

such remuneration in (a) above to be in accordance

with Federal Government practice.

(Amended by C.O. 1975/74)

5 Authority is delegated to the Chairman of the

Yukon Public Service Staff Relations Board to appoint

the officers to have signing authority with respect to

the operations of the Yukon Public Service Staff

Relations Board including the power to sign documents

related to the banking operations of the Yukon Public

Service Staff Relations Board, including deposits,

withdrawals and banking charges, but excluding loans,

overdrafts or guarantees.

(Amended by C.O. 1976/274)

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 22nd

day of July, A.D., 1970.

Commissioner of the Yukon/Commissaire
