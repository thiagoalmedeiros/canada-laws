YOIC 1990/123

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Financial Administration

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 The Deputy Head of the Department of Finance is

authorized to open or close bank accounts for the

Government of the Yukon for the receipt, deposit and

transfer of public funds and of trust money.

2 Order-In-Council 1987/15 is revoked.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 30th day of August,

A.D., 1990.
