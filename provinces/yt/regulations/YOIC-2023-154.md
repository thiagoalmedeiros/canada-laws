# Regulation Prescribing the Remuneration of Members of the Electoral District Boundaries Commission

YOIC 2023/154

*Made under the Elections Act*

### Remuneration for attendance or preparation: retired judge

1 A retired judge appointed under paragraph 408(1)(b) of

the Act to be the chair of the Commission is to be paid

remuneration as follows:

(a) $600 per day of attendance for more than four

hours at a meeting or public hearing held by the

Commission;

(b) $300 per day of attendance for four hours or less

at a meeting or public hearing held by the

Commission;

(c) $75 per hour of preparation for a meeting or a

public hearing held by the Commission to a

maximum of 20 hours per term of membership

in the Commission.

### Remuneration for attendance or preparation: member appointed under paragraph 408(1)(c) of the Act

2 Each member of the Commission appointed under

paragraph 408(1)(c) of the Act is to be paid

remuneration as follows:

(a) $450 per day of attendance for more than four

hours at a meeting or public hearing held by the

Commission;

(b) $225 per day of attendance for four hours or less

at a meeting or public hearing held by the

Commission;

(c) $56.25 per hour of preparation for a meeting or

a public hearing held by the Commission to a

maximum of 20 hours per term of membership

in the Commission.

### Remuneration while travelling

3 (1) Subject to subsection (2), a member of the

Commission appointed under paragraph 408(1)(b) or (c)

of the Act is to be paid remuneration in an amount of

$140 per day spent travelling to or from a meeting or

public hearing of the Commission held in a location away

from their ordinary place of residence.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to a day on which the

member of the Commission is eligible to receive

remuneration under section 1 or 2.

### Regulation Prescribing the Remuneration of Members of the Electoral District Boundaries Commission repealed

4 The Regulation Prescribing the Remuneration of

Members of the Electoral District Boundaries

Commission (Order-in-Council 2017/129) is repealed.
