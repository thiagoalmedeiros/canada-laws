## NI’IINLII’ NJIK (FISHING

## BRANCH)WILDERNESS PRESERVE

## WITHDRAWAL AND DISPOSITION

## RESTRICTION ORDER

Pursuant to paragraphs 17(1)(a) and (b) of the Oil and

Gas Act , the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources orders

as follows

1 The Yukon oil and gas lands described in the

Schedule to this Order [the Ni’iinlii’ Njik (Fishing Branch)

Wilderness Preserve] are withdrawn from disposition under

the Oil and Gas Act for a period of 20 years commencing

April 1, 2003.

2 Effective April 1, 2023, the issuance of dispositions

in respect of Yukon oil and gas lands described in the

Schedule to this Order [the Ni’iinlii’ Njik (Fishing Branch)

Wilderness Preserve] is restricted as follows

Where the surface area of the location of a proposed

disposition is wholly or partly within the area of the

Wilderness Preserve, the disposition when issued is subject

to the conditions that

(a) the site of any well drilled into the location of

the disposition must be situated outside the

Wilderness Preserve, with the well being drilled

directionally or horizontally into the location;

(b) any oil and gas facilities required in

connection with the drilling, completion,

operation, maintenance, shutting in, suspension,

abandonment, or re-abandonment of the well

must be constructed or installed outside the

Wilderness Preserve; and

(c) any oil and gas activity related to a well

referred to in paragraph (a) or an oil and gas

facility referred to in paragraph (b) must be

carried on in a manner that does not result in any

adverse effect within the Wilderness Preserve.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 4th day

of March, 2003.
