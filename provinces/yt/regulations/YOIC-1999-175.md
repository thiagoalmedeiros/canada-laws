YOIC 1999/175

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 11 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The portions of highway which were formerly part

of the Haines Road, Highway #3 right-of-way, and are

shown as Areas A, B, C, D, and E on the annexed extracts

from map CLSR # 73326, and Area F from map CLSR #

73325, are closed and declared to no longer be highway.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 18

day of October 1999.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
