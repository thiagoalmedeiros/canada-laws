WCHSBO 1993/15

*Made under the Workers’ Safety and Compensation Act*

## W.C.H.S.B.O. 1993/015

## WORKERS' COMPENSATION HEALTH AND SAFETY

## BOARD ORDER

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to subsection 101(1) of the Workers’

Compensation Act , the Board orders as follows:

1 The maximum wage rate for the year 1994 shall be

fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00) per annum.

2 This maximum wage rate is to be applied to workers

who are entitled to compensation as a result of a disability

caused after January 1, 1993.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 21st

day of December, 1993.
