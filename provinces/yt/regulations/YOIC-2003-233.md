YOIC 2003/233

*Made under the Continuing Consolidation of Statutes Act*

Pursuant to sections 8, 12 and 19 of the Continuing

Consolidation of Statutes Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council orders as follows

1 Having been advised by the Chief Legislative

Counsel that the 2002 general statute revision is

complete, the attached copy of the revision is hereby

deposited in the office of the Clerk of the Legislative

Assembly as the official copy of the revision.

2 The 2002 general statute revision is deemed to

have come into force on January 1, 2003.

3 Subject to the Appendixes to the revision, all

statutes of the Yukon enacted before January 1, 2003

are deemed to have been repealed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this July 24th 2003.
