YOIC 1997/163

*Made under the Assessment and Taxation Act*

Pursuant to section 56 of the Assessment and Taxation

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 The attached Rural Electrification And Telephone

Local Improvement Tax Regulation, 1997, Schedule 1, is

hereby established.

2 The charges imposed on Lot 35, Plan 89-32 by

Orders-in-Council 1990/49 and 1993/61 are hereby

terminated. These charges have been allocated by this

order to Lots 35-1, 35-2 and 35-3, Plan 95-44.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 1st

day of October, 1997.
