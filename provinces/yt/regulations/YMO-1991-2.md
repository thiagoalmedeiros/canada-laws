*Made under the Education Act*

Pursuant to section 67 of the Education Act , the

Minister of Education orders as follows:

1 The number of members on each School Council

shall be as set out in the attached schedule.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 4th

day of February, 1991.

Minister of Education/
