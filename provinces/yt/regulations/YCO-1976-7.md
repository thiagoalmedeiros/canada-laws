YCO 1976/7

*Made under the Chiropractors Act*

## CHIROPRACTIC ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of the Chiropractic Act , the

Commissioner of the Yukon Territory is pleased to and

doth hereby order as follows:

1 Commissioner’s Order 1971/69 is hereby revoked.

2 The Schedule of fees under the Chiropractic Act

shall be as follows:

(a) Registration Fee $100.00

(b) Annual Licence Fee $ 50.00

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 31st

day of December, A.D., 1975.

Commissioner of the Yukon/Commissaire
