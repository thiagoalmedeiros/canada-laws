COMMISSIONER'S AGENT PERMITTEE

CONDITIONS

1 The permittee shall

a) restrict cutting to the quantity, area and timber described hereon;

b) Utilize all merchantable material in the timber cut;

c) dispose of all logging and sawmill waste in such a manner as to prevent the start or spread of forest fire

and in accordance with instructions from the forest officer;

d) repair or pay for any damage to government improvements or experimental projects that may result

from his operations;

e) cut and remove timber in a manner satisfactory to the forest officer supervising the cutting and removal;

f) submit returns on the prescribed form to the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory with dues at the rates

set out in the Yukon Timber Regulations for materials cut: (dues paid in advance will be credited on

submission of the final return);

g) obtain the approval of the forest officer before constructing any roads, buildings or works necessary for

the removal of timber from the area; and

h) cease any logging or sawmill operations on the permit area immediately on receipt of notice in writing

from the forest officer that there has been a violation of any of the terms and conditions on his permit or

the authority under which it is issued.

2 The permittee shall not

a) cause unnecessary damage to young growth or trees reserved from cutting by the Commissioner of the

Yukon Territory; or

b) cut any trees higher than 12 inches from the ground measured at the lower side except with the written

permission of the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory.
