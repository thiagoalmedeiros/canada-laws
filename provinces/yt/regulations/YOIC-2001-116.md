YOIC 2001/116

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 Lots 35, 36, 37, and 38, Callison Industrial

Subdivision, Plan 99-0223 LTO, and a portion of road, Plan

99-0223 LTO, shall be sold to the Yukon Energy

Corporation for the following prices

Lot 35 $36,042.12

Lot 36 $21,195.78

Lot 37 $18,067.77

Lot 38 $24,323.79

Road $14,299.48

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 16th day

of July, 2001.

Commissioner of the Yukon
