# Exempts Finance Minister from subsection 65(1) of the Act in respect of the Yukon College or the Yukon Hospital corporation to obtain letters of credit for the purposes of the Pension Benefits Standards Act, 1985 (Canada)

YOIC 2011/62

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to section 81 of the Financial Administration

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows

1 The Minister of Finance is exempt from subsection

65(1) of the Act in respect of any guarantee given by

the Minister, on behalf of the Government of Yukon, in

order to enable Yukon College or the Yukon Hospital

Corporation to obtain one or more letters of credit for

the purposes of the Pension Benefits Standards Act,

1985 (Canada).

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 19 May 2011.
