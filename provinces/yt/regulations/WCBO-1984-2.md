*Made under the Workers’ Safety and Compensation Act*

## WORKERS' COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of Section 80(2) of the

Workers’ Compensation Act , the Board is pleased to and doth

hereby order as follows:

1 The maximum wage rate for the year 1985 shall be

Twenty-eight Thousand, Five Hundred Dollars per annum.

2 This order shall be deemed to be in force January 1,

1985.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 24th

day of October, A.D., 1984.
