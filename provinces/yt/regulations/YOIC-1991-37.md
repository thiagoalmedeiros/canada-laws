YOIC 1991/37

*Made under the Education Act*

### Auditor to possess professional designation

1 An auditor appointed pursuant to section 293 of the Act

must possess one or more of the following professional

designations:

- chartered accountant

- certified general accountant

- certified management accountant

### □

2 A School Board shall provide the Minister with the

name and qualifications of the person appointed as

auditor.
