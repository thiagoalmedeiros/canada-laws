YOIC 1989/94

*Made under the Wildlife Act*

1 These Regulations may be cited as the Trapping

Concession Area Boundary Regulations.

2 The boundaries for trapping concession areas in the

Yukon are set out on the following maps which can be

found on the Registered Trapping Concessions map

series of the Department of Environment.

A set of digital 1:250,000 scale maps derived from the

National Topographic Data Base, and maintained by the

Department of Environment, Information Management

and Technology Branch at 10 Burns Road in

Whitehorse, Yukon. Maps are designated as

Department of Environment (ENV) Registered Trapping

Concessions (RTC).
