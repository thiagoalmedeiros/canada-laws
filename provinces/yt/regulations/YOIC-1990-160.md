YOIC 1990/160

*Made under the Municipal Act*

Pursuant to section 19 of the Municipal Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The “Hamlet of Ibex Valley” is hereby established

effective December 7, 1990 or upon swearing in of the first

Advisory Council, whichever is the later.

2 The boundaries of the Hamlet of Ibex Valley are as

set out in Schedule I hereto. Inclusion of lands within the

boundaries of the hamlet is without prejudice to these

lands subsequently being selected by a Yukon First Nation

in its First Nation Final Agreement and being removed

from the hamlet at that time.

3 The Hamlet Advisory Council shall be responsible

for, but not limited to, advising the Commissioner in

Executive Council on matters that would normally be

under the jurisdiction of a Municipal Council. The council

shall also advise the Commissioner in Executive Council

on matters of local government in areas adjacent to the

hamlet that may affect the hamlet.

4 In accordance with Part 3 of the Municipal Act, the

first election of members of the Advisory Council for the

Hamlet of Ibex Valley shall be held on December 6, 1990.

5 (a) The returning officer for the election to be

held on December 6, 1990, shall be Florence

Roberts, and;

(b) The returning officer shall be paid an

honorarium of $15.00 per hour.

6 Nominations for members of the Advisory Council

shall be accepted by the returning officer from 10:00 A.M.

until 12:00 noon on November 26, 1990.

7 The poll, if required, shall be held on Thursday,

December 06, 1990, from the hours of 10:00 A.M. until

8:00 P.M.

8 Election procedures shall be conducted in

accordance with provision of the Municipal Act modified as

required for this election.

(i) For future elections, the Advisory Council shall

appoint a returning officer a deputy returning

officers as it deems necessary prior to a hamlet

election,

(ii) The Advisory Council shall pay to the election

officials such honoraria as it deems appropriate,

(iii) The provisions of Part 3 of the Municipal Act

shall apply to future elections in the Hamlet of

Ibex Valley in the same manner as if Ibex Valley

were a municipality.

9 The elected Advisory Council of the hamlet of Ibex

Valley shall hold office from 8:00 A.M. Friday, December 7,

1990, or upon their swearing in, whichever is later, until

regular municipal elections in 1991.

10 The Ibex Valley Advisory Council shall be the

recipient of any funding from the Government of Yukon

that may, from time to time, be provided to the hamlet for

its purposes. The Advisory Council, in managing its

financial affairs, shall adhere to all applicable requirements

and deadlines as set forth for a Village in Part V, Division 1,

of the Municipal Act .

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 20th

day of November, A.D. 1990.
