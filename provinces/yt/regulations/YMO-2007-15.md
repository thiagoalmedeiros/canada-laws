Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources

## MINISTERIAL ORDER RESPECTING THE

## WITHDRAWAL FROM DISPOSITION OF

## YUKON OIL AND GAS LANDS

(LOT 1498, QUAD 105 D/14)

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to withdraw certain

Yukon oil and gas lands from disposition to provide for

wetland habitat protection.

### Definition

2 In this Order,

“area” means Lot 1498, Quad 105 D/14, shown on

Plan 92102 in the Canada Lands Survey Records in

Ottawa and Plan 2007-0012 in the Land Titles Office

of the Yukon Land Registration District. « zone »

### Lands withdrawn from disposition

3 The Yukon oil and gas lands in the area are

withdrawn from disposition under the Oil and Gas Act .

### Exceptions

4 Section 3 does not apply to any disposition of

Yukon oil and gas lands underlying the area which may be

accessed directionally from a location outside of the area,

provided that the disposition does not require access to the

surface of the area or would result in any reasonable

likelihood of disturbance of the surface of the area.
