YOIC 1986/93

*Made under the Assessment and Taxation Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of sections 33 and 37 of the

Assessment and Taxation Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council orders as follows:

1 An Assessment Appeal Board is hereby established.

2 The following persons are appointed as members of

the Assessment Appeal Board to hold office during

pleasure:

Anne King - Chairperson

Truska Correll - Vice Chairperson

Martin Blackwell - Member

3 Marion Morrison is appointed as Secretary of the

Assessment Appeal Board.

4 The remuneration to a member of the board shall

be $100.00 per day together with all transportation,

accommodation and living expenses incurred in

connection with the performance of his or her duties as a

member of the board away from his or her ordinary place

of residence, provided that the payment of such expenses

shall conform as nearly as possible in all respects to the

payment of such expenses for members of the public

service of the Yukon.

5 Orders-in Council 1981/79, 1983/45, 1983/119 and

1985/51 are hereby revoked.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 30th

day of May, A.D., 1986.

Commissioner of the Yukon
