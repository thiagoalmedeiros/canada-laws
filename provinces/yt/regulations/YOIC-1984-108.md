*Made under the Wildlife Act*

These Regulations are made pursuant to

subsection 178(1) of the Wildlife Act .

1 (1) These Regulations may be cited as the “Game

Management Subzone Regulations”.

2 The boundaries for game management subzones in

the Yukon are set out on maps listed below. They are

derived from the National Topographic Data Base, and

maintained by the Department of Environment,

Information Management and Technology Branch at 10

Burns Road in Whitehorse, Yukon. Maps are designated

as Department of Environment (ENV) Game

Management Subzones (GMS).

EXPLANATORY NOTE ON THE MAPS (This note is not part

of the Regulation)

Because of their size and complexity, the maps setting

out the boundaries for game management subzones in

the Yukon are not included with this order. Copies may

be obtained from the Department of Environment

4 (1) Where the boundary of a Game Management

Subzone appears to follow the line represented by a

road, creek, river, lake or the channel of a river, it

shall, unless otherwise clearly indicated on the map, be

deemed to follow the center of that line.

(2) Despite this Regulation, portions of subzones 6-04,

6-06, 6-07 and 6-08 as shown on map ENV.041.002,

and entitled “Special Sheep Guiding Opportunity Area”,

shall be open for hunting as set out in Chapter 22 of

the Kluane First Nation Final Agreement.
