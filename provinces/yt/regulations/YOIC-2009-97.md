YOIC 2009/97

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The hatched portion of Old Bay View Road, Plan

51533 CLSR, 26014 LTO, as shown on the map attached as

the Appendix is closed and is declared to no longer be a

highway.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 14 May 2009.
