## NETWORK DESIGNATION ORDER

### Designation

1 The following electronic information systems are

designated as parts of the Yukon health information

network:

(a) the Yukon electronic health information system

which consists of

(i) the Yukon Hospital Corporation’s hospital

information system, and

(ii) the parts of the continuing care information

system operated by the Department of Health

and Social Services in respect of the following

facilities:

(A) Copper Ridge Place,

(B) Thomson Centre,

(C) McDonald Lodge,

(b) the drug information system operated by the

Department of Health and Social Services;

(c) the client registry system operated by the

Department of Health and Social Services.

### Repeal 2

2 Ministerial Order 2016/31 is repealed. L’Arrêté
