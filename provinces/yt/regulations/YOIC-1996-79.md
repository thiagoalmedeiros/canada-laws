YOIC 1996/79

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The annexed Highway Closure (Lowey’s Lane)

Order is made.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 24th

day of May, 1996

______________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## HIGHWAY CLOSURE

(LOWEY’S LANE) ORDER

1 The following highway is closed:

All that portion of the road described as Lowey’s Lane

on Canada Land Survey Records Plan No. 51787 which

is registered in the Yukon Land Titles Office as Plan

No. 26426 and shown with Lots 1260 and 1261, Quad

105D/14, Echo Lake, Yukon.
