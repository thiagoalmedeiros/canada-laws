*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to Section 13 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council hereby orders as

follows:

1 The lots in Schedule A attached hereto are classed

Industrial, and shall be sold at the price listed in Schedule

A.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

11th day of May, A.D., 1987.
