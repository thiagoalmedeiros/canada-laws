# Designates the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources as the Minister to whom reference is made in the definition of “Minister” in subsection 1(1) of the Oil and Gas Act

YOIC 2004/72

*Made under the Government Organisation Act*

Pursuant to section 4 of the Government Organisation

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows

1 The Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources is

designated as the Minister to whom reference is made

in the definition of “Minister” in subsection 1(1) of the

Oil and Gas Act .

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this April 5th 2004.
