### Section 3 amended Section 3 is replaced with the following:

3 3 Conditions

(1) The exemptions in section 2 are subject to the

conditions in this section.

(2) The operator must, at all times, post the following

in accordance with subsections (3) and (4):

(a) a notice, with a font size of at least 36 points,

stating “ The Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs is

temporarily exempted from most

requirements of the Public Pool Regulations

under the Public Health and Safety Act. Please

see the exemption order that is available on

the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs website and at

the front entrance door and front desk of

Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs. ”;

(b) an electronic copy of this Order;

(c) a paper copy of this Order.

(3) The operator must post the notice set out in

paragraph (2)(a) in a prominent location readily

visible and legible to a customer in each of the

following places:

(a) on the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs website,

immediately below the heading “Rates” in the

“R ates & Classes” portion of the website;

(b) immediately beside the front entrance door of

Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs; and

(c) on the customer side of the front desk of

Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs.

(4) The operator must post the copies of this Order

as set out in paragraphs (2)(b) and (c) in a prominent

location readily visible and legible to a customer in the

following manner:

(a) an electronic copy of this Order must be

posted on the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs

website, immediately below the heading

“Rates” in the “Rates & Classes” portion of the

website;

(b) a paper copy of this Order must be posted

immediately beside the front entrance door of

Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs; and

(c) a paper copy of this Order must be posted on

the customer side of the front desk of Eclipse

Nordic Hot Springs.

### Section 4 amended Section 4 is replaced with the following:

4 4 Expiry

This Order expires on May 11, 2029.

### Conditions The operator must, at all times, post copies of this Order in a prominent location readily visible and legible to a customer, in the following manner: (a) an electronic copy of this Order must be posted on the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs website, immediately below the heading “Rates” in the “Rates & Classes” portion of the website ; (b) a paper copy of this Order must be posted immediately beside the front entrance door of Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs; and (c) a paper copy of this Order must be posted on the customer side of the front desk of Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs.

5

### Coming into force This Order comes into force on May 12, 2024.
