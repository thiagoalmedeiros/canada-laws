YMO 2013/2

*Made under the Securities Act*

Pursuant to sections 169, 170 and 171 of the Securities

Act , the Minister of Community Services orders as follows

1 The attached Rule to Make Amendment No. 5 to the

Rule Respecting the Implementation of CSA Instruments and

Policies (Local Rule 11-802) (Ministerial Order 2009/07) is

made.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, February 21 2013.

______________________________________________________

Minister of Community Services

## RULE TO MAKE AMENDMENT NO. 5 TO

## THE RULE RESPECTING THE

## IMPLEMENTATION OF CSA INSTRUMENTS

## AND POLICIES (LOCAL RULE 11-802)

1 This rule amends the Rule Respecting the

Implementation of CSA Instruments and Policies (Local Rule 11-

802) .

### Schedule amended

2 The following instruments and companion policies

listed in the schedule attached to Local Rule 11-802 are

repealed as of the dates shown

(a) National Instrument 33-102 – Regulation of

Certain Registrant Activities and Companion Policy

33-102CP – Regulation of Certain Registrant

Activities , as of September 28, 2009;

(b) National Instrument 55-101 – Insider Reporting

Exemptions and Companion Policy 55-101CP –

Insider Reporting Exemptions , as of April 30, 2010;

and

(c) Multilateral Instrument 55-103 – Insider

Reporting for Certain Derivative Transactions (Equity

Monetization) and Companion Policy 55-103CP –

Insider Reporting for Certain Derivative Transactions

(Equity Monetization) , as of April 30, 2010.

3 The following companion policies are added in

numerical order to the schedule attached to Local Rule 11-

802 as of the dates shown

(a) Companion Policy 23-102CP – Use of Client

Brokerage Commission , as of June 30, 2010; and

(b) Companion Policy 51-105CP – Issuers Quoted

in the U.S. Over-the-Counter Markets , as of July 31,

2012.
