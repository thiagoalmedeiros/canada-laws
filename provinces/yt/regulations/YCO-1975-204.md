*Made under the Municipal Finance and Community Grants Act*

## COMMUNITY ASSISTANCE ACT

## COMMUNITY ASSISTANCE ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of the Community Assistance

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 Where alternative materials or methods of

construction of a project which is eligible for funding by

the Territory in whole or in part pursuant to the

Community Assistance Act is available, the Commissioner

shall determine the materials to be used or methods of

construction acting on the best information available at

the time for the purpose of establishing the cost base for

applying applicable cost sharing formulas.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 29th

day of August, A.D., 1975.
