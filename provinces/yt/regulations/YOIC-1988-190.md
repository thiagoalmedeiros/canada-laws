YOIC 1988/190

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council is please to make the

following order:

1 The forty-four lots in Schedule A attached hereto

are residential and shall be sold at the prices listed in

Schedule A.

2 The nine lots in Schedule B attached hereto are

classed as indicated and shall be sold at the prices listed in

Schedule B.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 14th

day of November, A.D., 1988.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
