YOIC 2025/80

*Made under the Assessment and Taxation Act*

### Local improvement taxes

1 To recover the cost of local improvements with respect

to the regions and projects listed in the Schedule, a local

improvement tax is levied on each property for the local

improvement associated with that property consisting of

the principal amount and the interest specified for that

property in the Schedule.

### Tax payable, etc.

2 The local improvement tax

(a) shall be shown as a separate item on the tax

notice for the property;

(b) is due and payable in the annual instalments

shown in the Schedule at the same time as the

property taxes under the Act are due and

payable;

(c) is part of the total tax payable for the property;

and

(d) may be paid in advance by paying the

outstanding balance of principal together with

interest accrued at the time of payment

### Minister may enter into agreements

3 (1) The Minister may enter into a non-renewable

agreement deferring the imposition of a local

improvement tax for up to 15 years without interest, for

electrical utility services, telephone utility services or

other telecommunication services.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), a non-renewable deferral

agreement terminates on the earlier of

(a) the expiry date of the agreement; and

(b) the date on which the utility service is connected

to the property subject to the tax.

(3) If the property subject to the tax is disposed of

before the earlier of the dates described in

paragraphs (2)(a) and (b), the agreement is terminated

on the disposition of the property, unless the agreement

provides otherwise.
