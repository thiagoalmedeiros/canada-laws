*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to Section 17 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council is pleased to and doth

hereby order as follows:

52922 and lots 23 and 24 C.L.S.R. plab 52922 and lot 216

on Schedule A attached hereto is closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 31st

day of August, A.D., 1984.
