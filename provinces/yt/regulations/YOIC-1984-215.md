YOIC 1984/215

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12(1) of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The administration and control of lot 1018, Quad

115K/7 in Beaver Creek is hereby transferred to the

Government of Canada.

2 If at any time lot 1018, Quad 115K/7 in Beaver

Creek is no longer used as the site of a Customs Station, the

administration and control shall revert to the

Commissioner of Yukon.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 13th

day of July A.D., 1984.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
