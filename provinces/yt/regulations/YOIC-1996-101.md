## YUKON ACT

## YUKON ACT

Pursuant to sections 12 and 31 of the Lands Act and

section 47 of the Yukon Act , the Commissioner in Executive

Council orders as follows:

1 The administration, control and management of:

Lot 1009, Quad 105D/2, Plan 63779,

Carcross, Yukon

is hereby transferred to Her Majesty the Queen in Right of

Canada.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 28th

day of June, 1996.

Administrator of the Yukon
