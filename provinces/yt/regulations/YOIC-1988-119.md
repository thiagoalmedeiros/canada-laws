# Public drinking prohibited within a five kilometre radius of Pelly Crossing Community Centre

YOIC 1988/119

*Made under the Liquor Act*

### Preamble

Whereas the Commissioner in Executive Council has

received a resolution duly passed by Council of the Selkirk

Indian Band requesting an area enforcement order

prohibiting public drinking within a five (5) kilometre

radius from the Pelly Crossing Community Centre.,

therefore pursuant to subsection 105(4) of the Liquor Act

the Commissioner in Executive Council is pleased hereby

to make the following order:

1 Public drinking of liquor is prohibited within a five

(5) kilometre radius from the Pelly Crossing Community

Centre commencing the date of this Order.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 4th day

of July, A.D., 1988.
