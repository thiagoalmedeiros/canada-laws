# Department of Economic Development, Tourism and Culture Establishment Regulation; Repealed by O.I.C. 2026/95

YOIC 2026/37

*Made under the Government Organisation Act*

### Department of Economic Development, Tourism and Culture established

1 The Department of Economic Development, Tourism and

Culture is established.

### Departments disestablished

2 The following Departments are disestablished:

(a) the Department of Economic Development;

(b) the Department of Tourism and Culture.

### Transfer of departmental positions, duties and functions

3 (1) The positions in the Department of Economic

Development and the positions in the Department of

Tourism and Culture are transferred from those

departments to the Department of Economic

Development, Tourism and Culture.

(2) The duties and functions of the Department of

Economic Development and the duties and functions of

the Department of Tourism and Culture are transferred

from those departments to the Department of Economic

Development, Tourism and Culture.

### Transfer and assignment of Ministerial powers, duties and functions

4 All of the powers, duties and functions given or imposed

by law to or on the Minister of Economic Development

and all of the powers, duties and functions given or

imposed by law to or on the Minister of Tourism and

Culture are transferred and assigned to the Minister of

Economic Development, Tourism and Culture.

### References to Ministers in enactments, orders and documents

5 Unless the context otherwise requires, where, in any

enactment, order or document there is a reference to

the Minister of Economic Development or to the Minister

of Tourism and Culture, the Minister of Economic

Development, Tourism and Culture is designated as the

Minister to whom that reference is made.

### Coming into force

6 This Regulation comes into force on the later of the

following days:

(a) April 1, 2026;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of

regulations under the Regulations Act .
