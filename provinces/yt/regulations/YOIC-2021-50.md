## LOCAL IMPROVEMENT TAX

## REGULATION, 2021

1 To recover the cost of local

improvements with respect to the regions and

projects listed in the Schedule, a local

improvement tax is levied on each property

for the local improvement associated with that

property consisting of the principal amount

and the interest specified for that property in

the Schedule.

2 The local improvement tax

(a) shall be shown as a separate item on

the tax notice for the property;

(b) is due and payable in the annual

instalments shown in the Schedule at the

same time as the property taxes under the

Act are due and payable;

(c) is part of the total tax payable for the

property; and

(d) may be paid in advance by paying the

outstanding balance of principal together

with interest accrued at the time of

payment.

3 (1) The Minister may enter into a non-

renewable agreement deferring the imposition

of a local improvement tax for up to 15 years

without interest, for electrical utility services,

telephone utility services or other

telecommunication services.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), a non-

renewable deferral agreement terminates on

the earlier of

(a) the expiry date of the agreement; and

(b) the date on which the utility service is

connected to the property subject to the

tax.

(3) If the property subject to the tax is

disposed of before the earlier of the dates

described in paragraphs (2)(a) and (b), the

agreement is terminated on the disposition of

the property, unless the agreement provides

otherwise.
