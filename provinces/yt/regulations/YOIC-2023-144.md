*Made under the Public Utilities Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation

“ ATCO Electric Yukon ” means The Yukon Electrical

Company Limited doing business as ATCO Electric

Yukon. « ATCO Electric Yukon »

### Excluded undertaking

2 (1) The Lower Post Photovoltaic System Project consists

of

(a) the installation of a photovoltaic electricity

generating system at the Daylu Dena Council ’s

multipurpose cultural centre located at

27 Riverside Road, in Lower Post, British

Columbia;

(b) the transmission of the electricity generated by

the photovoltaic electricity generating system

from the centre referred to in paragraph (a) to

the ATCO Electric Yukon distribution system in

Lower Post; and

(c) the sale by the Daylu Dena Council, to ATCO

Electric Yukon, of electricity generated in the

course of the Daylu Dena Council’ s operation of

the photovoltaic electricity generating system

referred to in paragraph (a).

(2) The Lower Post Photovoltaic System Project is

prescribed as an excluded undertaking.
