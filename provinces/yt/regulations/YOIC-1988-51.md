YOIC 1988/51

*Made under the Change of Name Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Change of Name

Regulations .

### Schedule of fees

2 The fees set out in Schedule A annexed hereto are

prescribed as the fees for those services as set out in

the Schedule.

### Forms

3 The forms annexed hereto are hereby prescribed as the

forms for use under the Change of Name Act .
