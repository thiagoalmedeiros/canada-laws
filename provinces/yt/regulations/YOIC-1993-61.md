## RURAL ELECTRIFICATION AND

## TELEPHONE LOCAL IMPROVEMENT TAX

## REGULATION, 1993

1 In these regulations, “local improvement” means

the extension of an electrical power line or of telephone

service to serve the properties in the regions listed in

Schedule 1.

2 The regions listed in Schedule 1 are declared to

benefit from the local improvement.

3 To recover the cost of the local improvement there

is levied on each property in each region listed in Schedule

1 a local improvement tax consisting of the principal

amount and the interest specified for that property in

Schedule 1.

The local improvement tax

(a) shall be shown as a separate item on the tax

notice for the property;

(b) is due and payable in the annual instalments

shown on Schedule 1 at the same time as the

general tax;

(c) is part of the total tax payable for the property;

and

(d) may be paid in advance by paying the

outstanding balance of principal together with

interest accrued at the time of payment.
