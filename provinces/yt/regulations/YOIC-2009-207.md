YOIC 2009/207

*Made under the Health Act*

1 (1) The chair of the Yukon Advisory Committee on

Nursing shall be paid an honorarium of $300 per day.

(2) A member of the Yukon Advisory Committee on

Nursing, other than the chair, shall be paid an

honorarium of $200 per day.

2 The honoraria referred to in section 1 and expenses

incurred by the chair and members of the Yukon

Advisory Committee on Nursing shall be paid in

accordance with the policies of the Government of

Yukon for payment of remuneration to boards and

committees.
