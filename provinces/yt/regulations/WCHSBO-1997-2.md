## W.C.H.S.B.O. 1997/002

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to section 90 of the Workers’ Compensation

Act , R.S.Y. 1986, c. 180 and section 90(1)(b) of the Workers’

Compensation Act , S. Y. 1992, c. 16, the Board orders as

follows:

1 The maximum wage rate for the year 1995 shall be

forty one thousand dollars ($41,000.00) per annum.

2 The maximum wage rate is to be applied to workers

who are entitled to compensation as a result of a disability

caused in 1992 or earlier.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 24

day of April, 1997.
