YOIC 1987/55

*Made under the Public Service Act*

## PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION ACT

Pursuant to subsection 210(1) of the Public Service

Commission Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows:

1 The recruitment for park rangers at Herschel Island

under the COPE Agreement is excluded from the operation

of sections 99 and 100 of the Public Service Act.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 9th

day of March, A.D.,1987.
