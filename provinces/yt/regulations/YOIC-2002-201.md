YOIC 2002/201

*Made under the Yukon Act (Canada)*

### Accept transfer of land

1 The transfer by the Governor in Council of administration

and control of the following parcels of public real

property is accepted:

(a) the whole of Parcel P and the whole of Parcel V

as those parcels are shown on Administrative

Plan CLSR 41780 YT filed in the Canada Lands

Survey Records, Ottawa, a copy of which is filed

in the Land Titles Office at Whitehorse under LTO

Plan No. 20364;

(b) the surface parcel with the legal land description

stated on the Certificate of Title as “ Parcel

#100091652; or Lot 21 Block HB, Harper Estate,

City of Dawson, YT, Plan No 91- 15”.
