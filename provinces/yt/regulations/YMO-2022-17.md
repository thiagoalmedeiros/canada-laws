## FIRST NATION SCHOOL BOARD ORDER (2022)

1 The whole of Yukon is established as an

education area to be known as education area #29.

2 The First Nation School Board and its trustees are

established as a corporation under the name of “First

Nation School Board of Trustees of Education Area

#29”.

3 The category of residents for whom education

area #29 is established are those residents of the

Yukon whose ancestral language is one or more of the

following:

(a) Gwitchin;

(b) Hän;

(c) Kaska;

(d) Northern Tutchone;

(e) Southern Tutchone;

(f) Tagish;

(g) Tlingit; or

(h) Upper Tanana.

4 The number of trustees of the First Nation

School Board is five.
