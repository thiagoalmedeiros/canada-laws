YOIC 1998/219

*Made under the Health Act*

1 The following fees shall be charged for services

supplied to clients of the Continuing-Care Day-Program

at the Thomson Centre:

For each meal (including up to two snacks) $5.00

For each one-way trip to or from the Thomson Centre

on the Centre's bus $1.20

2 There shall be no fee charged under this Regulation

to recipients of assistance under the Social Assistance

Act .
