## REGULATION TO AMEND THE SCHEDULE

## TO THE PARKS AND LAND CERTAINTY ACT

(2018)

1 This Regulation amends the Schedule to the Act.

### Item 3 amended

2 Item 3 of the Schedule is replaced with the

following:

3 Big Creek Campground Recreation Park To provide a highway

campground

### Item 26 amended

3 In item 26 of the Schedule, the expression “Quiet

Lake Campground” is replaced with the expression “Quiet

Lake Campground North”.

following:

destination

campground
