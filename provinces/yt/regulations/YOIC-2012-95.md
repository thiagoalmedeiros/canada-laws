YOIC 2012/95

*Made under the Child Care Act*

1 The following are exempt from the Act

(a) a program for children that

(i) has a primary purpose other than providing

child care, and

(ii) operates for a period of 12 consecutive

weeks or less; and

(b) a program for children if at least one parent of

each child in attendance

(i) remains on the same premises as where the

program operates, and

(ii) is accessible at all times to attend to the

child’s needs.
