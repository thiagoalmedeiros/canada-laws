# Order Prohibiting Entry on Certain Lands in Yukon (Final Recommended Dawson Regional Land Use Plan)

YOIC 2026/40

*Made under the Placer Mining Act*

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to prohibit entry on lands

within the areas described in Schedule 1 and shown in

Schedule 2, being areas within the Dawson Planning

Region, because

(a) the described and shown lands are based on

areas that are identified in the Final

Recommended Dawson Regional Land Use Plan

as conservation areas; or

(b) prohibiting entry on the described and shown

lands is required to implement the

recommendation in the Plan that the lands be

protected from the locating of claims or the

prospecting or mining of minerals during the

planning period.

### Definition

2 In this Order

“ recorded claim ” means

(a) a recorded placer claim that is in good standing,

acquired under the Yukon Placer Mining Act

(Canada) and continued under the Placer Mining

Act , or acquired under the Placer Mining Act , or

(b) a recorded mineral claim that is in good

standing, acquired under the Yukon Quartz

Mining Act (Canada) and continued under the

Quartz Mining Act , or acquired under the Quartz

Mining Act . « claim inscrit »

### Prohibition of entry

3 (1) Subject to section 4, a person must not enter on

lands within the areas described in Part 1 of Schedule 1

and shown in Part 1 of Schedule 2, before April 1, 2028,

for the purpose of

(a) locating a claim, or prospecting for gold or other

precious minerals or stones, under the Placer

Mining Act ; or

(b) locating a claim, or prospecting or mining for

minerals, under the Quartz Mining Act .

(2) A person must not enter on lands within the area

described in Part 2 of Schedule 1 and shown in Part 2 of

Schedule 2, before April 1, 2028, for the purpose of

locating a claim, or prospecting or mining for minerals,

under the Quartz Mining Act .

### Existing rights and interests

4 (1) Section 3 does not apply to entry on a recorded

claim by the holder of the recorded claim.

(2) Paragraph 3(1)(a) does not apply to entry on lands

in respect of which a lease has been granted under

section 92 of the Placer Mining Act where entry is by the

lessee in accordance with the term of the lease.

### Repeal

5 The Order Prohibiting Entry on Certain Lands in Yukon

(Recommended Dawson Regional Land Use Plan) is

repealed.
