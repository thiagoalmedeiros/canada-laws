## ORDER-IN-COUNCIL 2023/102

Pursuant to the Highways Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council orders

1 In this Order

“ access road ” means the unsurveyed and unmaintained

access road that

(a) is approximately 150 m long, begins on the west

side of Highway No. 8 (Tagish Road) near

kilometre 22.5, intersects the Tagish

Community Association lease lands, terminates

at the easterly boundary of Lot 1077-1, CLSR

96605, LTO 2010-0017, and

(b) is outlined in bold on the map attached as the

Schedule to this Order; « voie d'accès »

“ Tagish Community Association lease lands ” means the

following lands, which are the subject of a lease between

the Government of Yukon and the Tagish Community

Association (Land Disposition No. 105D08-012):

(a) all that parcel of land comprising one hectare,

more or less, southeast of Lot 1096, CLSR 78749,

LTO 96-53, near kilometre 22.5 of Highway No.

8 (Tagish Road), in Quad 105 D/08, and

(b) all that parcel of land comprising two hectares,

more or less, adjoining Lot 1077-1, CLSR 96605,

LTO 2010-0017, near kilometre 22.5 of Highway

No. 8 (Tagish Road), in Quad 105 D/08. « terres
