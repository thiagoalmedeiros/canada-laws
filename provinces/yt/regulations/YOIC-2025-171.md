YOIC 2025/171

*Made under the Exemptions Act*

### Interpretation

1 (1) In this Regulation

“ companion animal ” means an animal that is kept

primarily for the purpose of companionship; « animal

de compagnie »

“ date of the initial bankruptcy event ” has the same

meaning as in the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act

(Canada); « ouverture de la faillite »

“ service animal ” means an animal that is trained to

provide assistance to a person with a disability that

relates to that person ’ s disability, and includes a retired

service animal. « animal d ’ assistance »

(2) For the purposes of paragraph (f) of the definition

“dependant” in the Act, the following persons are

prescribed as classes of persons:

(a) a child of the debtor who is conceived but not

yet born at the date of the writ of execution in

the case of a money judgment or at the date of

the initial bankruptcy event in the case of a

bankruptcy proceeding under the Bankruptcy

and Insolvency Act (Canada);

(b) a grandparent, parent or descendant of the

debtor who was dependent on the debtor for

maintenance and support for a period of at least

three years immediately before the date of the

writ of execution in the case of a money

judgment or the date of the initial bankruptcy

event in the case of a bankruptcy proceeding

under the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act

(Canada);

(c) a person who is either divorced from the debtor

or a former common law spouse of the debtor

and who was dependent on the debtor for

maintenance and support for a period of at least

three years immediately before the date of the

writ of execution in the case of a money

judgment or the date of the initial bankruptcy

event in the case of a bankruptcy proceeding

under the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act

(Canada).

### Determination of applicable law for debtor exemptions

2 (1) In a proceeding to enforce a money judgement, a

debtor’s exemptions are determined in accordance with

the law that is in force on the date on which the writ of

execution is issued.

(2) In a bankruptcy proceeding under the Bankruptcy

and Insolvency Act (Canada), a debtor’s exemptions are

determined in accordance with the law that is in force

on the date of the initial bankruptcy event.

### Clarification of exemptions and exceptions

3 For the purposes of paragraph 2(1)(i) of the Act, the

following are exempt from seizure under a writ of

execution:

(a) a companion animal, the animal’s food and any

equipment used to maintain the animal;

(b) a service animal, the animal’s food and any

equipment used to maintain the animal or used

in its function as a service animal;

(c) a prepaid or inherited burial plot;

(d) prepaid funerals within the meaning of the

Funeral Directors Regulations.

### Prescribed limits

4 Subject to the annual adjustment in section 6, the

following are the prescribed limits, for the purposes of

subsection 2(1) of the Act, in respect of property, or

interests in property, exempt from seizure under a writ

of execution:

(a) $15,000 for the combined value of tools,

instruments, books, vehicles and other personal

property, ordinarily used by the debtor in the

debtor’s business, calling, profession, trade or

occupation;

(b) $15,000 for the combined value of tools,

instruments, vehicles, watercraft and other

personal property ordinarily used in, and needed

by the debtor for use in, hunting, trapping or

fishing for food;

(c) $35 ,000 for the debtor’s interest in the ir

principal residence;

(d) $10,000 for a motor vehicle or other vehicle used

for personal transport.

### Election and selection

5 A person may, on behalf of the debtor, make the election

referred to in subsection 3(1) of the Act or the selection

referred to in subsection 6(1) of the Act if

(a) in the case of a debtor who is not mentally

incapacitated or infirm, the person is named as

the attorney in a valid power of attorney signed

by the debtor; or

(b) in the case of a debtor who is mentally

incapacitated or infirm, the person is named as

the attorney in a valid enduring power of

attorney signed by the debtor or by the person

appointed by the court as the debtor’s guardian.

### Annual adjustment of limits

6 (1) In this section

“ adjusted amount ” means an amount calculated in

September of the current year by increasing a

prescribed limit in effect for that month by the annual

percentage increase in the All-Items Consumer Price

Index for Whitehorse published by Statistics Canada, if

any, for the period from September 1 of the immediately

preceding year to August 31 of the current year.

« montant rajusté »

“ amount ” means an amount expressed in dollars or in

dollars and cents; « montant »

“ current year ” means, in respect of any particular

calculation being made by the Minister under

subsection (2), the year in which the calculation is made;

« année courante »

(2) In September of each year, the Minister must

calculate the adjusted amount for each prescribed limit

set out in section 4 and publish the adjusted amounts on

a Government of Yukon website or by any other means

that the Minister considers appropriate.

(3) From November 1 in each year until October 31 of

the immediately following year, the prescribed limits set

out in section 4 are deemed to be replaced by the

corresponding adjusted amounts calculated under

subsection (2) and those adjusted amounts are the

prescribed limits in effect for those months.

### Coming into force

7 This Regulation comes into force on the later of the

following days:

(a) the day on which the Exemptions Act , S.Y. 2024,

c. 5, comes into force;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of

regulations under the Regulations Act.
