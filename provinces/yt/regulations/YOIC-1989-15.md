YOIC 1989/15

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of Sections 19(2) and (3) of

the Financial Administration Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council is pleased to and doth hereby order a

special warrant as follows:

1 An amount of up to $13,082,000 Operation and

Maintenance and $11,347,000 Capital to be paid from the

Consolidated Revenue Fund in accordance with the

attached schedule (Schedule A).

2 The payments to be made for the purpose of

financing government operations as required in the Third

Appropriation Act, 1988-89 and for the purpose of defraying

general election expenses.

3 The payments to be made in the fiscal year 1988-89.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 31st

day of January, A.D., 1989.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
