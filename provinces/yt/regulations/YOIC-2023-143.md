### Definitions

1 In this Regulation

“ ATCO Electric Yukon ” means The Yukon Electrical

Company Limited doing business as ATCO Electric

Yukon; « ATCO Electric Yukon »

“ electrical grid ” means the interconnected network of

equipment and infrastructure, owned by Yukon Energy

Corporation and ATCO Electric Yukon, for the generation,

transmission and delivery of electricity in Yukon.

« réseau de distribution d’électricité »

### Excluded undertaking

2 (1) The Atlin Hydro Expansion Project consists of

(a) the expansion of the Tlingit Homeland Energy

Limited Partnership’s hydroelectric facility in

Atlin, British Columbia, including the installation

of a new substation at the facility;

(b) the installation of a new interconnection

substation at Jakes Corner, Yukon;

(c) the installation of a new transmission line from

the substation referred to in paragraph (a) to

the interconnection substation referred to in

paragraph (b);

(d) the sale by the Tlingit Homeland Energy Limited d)

Partnership, to the Yukon Energy Corporation, of

electricity generated and transmitted in the

course of the Tlingit Homeland Energy Limited

Partnership’s operation of the hydroelectric

facility referred to in paragraph (a); and

(e) the upgrades to the electrical grid to e)

accommodate the expansion, installations, and

sales described in paragraphs (a) to (d).

(2) The Atlin Hydro Expansion Project is prescribed as (2) Le

an excluded undertaking. d’Atlin
