YOIC 2011/81

*Made under the Cabinet and Caucus Employees Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation

“ cabinet employee position ” means a cabinet

employee position established by section 2. « poste

d’employé du cabinet »

### Cabinet employee positions

2 The cabinet employee positions set out in the Schedule

are established for the purposes of section 3 of the Act.

### Identification of positions

3 A cabinet employee position may be identified for any

purpose by a title, number or other designation that is

different from its designation in the schedule.

### x

4 [Section 4 repealed by O.I.C. 2026/21]

### Classification

5 For the purposes of subsection 3(2) of the Act the

Premier, in consultation with the deputy head

responsible for the Executive Council Office, may assign

classification levels to the cabinet employee positions.
