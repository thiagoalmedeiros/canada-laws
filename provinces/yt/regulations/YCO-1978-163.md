YCO 1978/163

*Made under the Liquor Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory

has received a Resolution duly passed by the Council of the

City of Dawson requesting that Section 103 of the Liquor

Ordinance be put into operation in respect of the

Municipality of City of Dawson,

NOW THEREFORE, the Commissioner of the Yukon

Territory is pleased to and doth hereby order:

(1) Section 103 of the Liquor Ordinance shall come

into full force and effect in the City of Dawson on 15 July

1978.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 11th day

of July, A.D., 1978.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon Territory
