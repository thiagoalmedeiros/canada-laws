YOIC 1991/218

*Made under the Education Act*

1 The fees for distance education courses of

instruction shall be those established from time to time

by the Distance Learning and Continuing Education

Branch of the British Columbia Ministry of Education, or

any successor agency. For the 1991/92 school year,

the fees are set at $350 per course or $2,000 per

grade.
