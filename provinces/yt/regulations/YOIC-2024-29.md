YOIC 2024/29

*Made under the Cannabis Control and Regulation Act*

### Redesignation

1 The Yukon Liquor Corporation is re-designated as the

distributor corporation for an indeterminate period.

### Name of distributor corporation

2 The Yukon Liquor Corporation is to be known as the

Yukon Liquor Corporation when performing its functions

under the Act.

### Repeal

3 Order-in-Council 2018/107 is repealed.

### Coming into force

4 This Order comes into force on April 1, 2024.
