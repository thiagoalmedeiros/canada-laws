YOIC 2000/159

*Made under the Highways Act*

1 (1) For each bridge mentioned in Schedule 1, the

maximum allowable gross vehicle weight is the weight

in tonnes mentioned in Schedule 1 under the heading

“Weight Restriction” for the vehicle's configuration.

(2) When the temperature at the bridge is colder than

minus 35 degrees Celsius the maximum allowable

gross vehicle weight is the weight in tonnes mentioned

in Schedule 1 under the heading “Cold Weather Weight

Restriction” for the vehicle's configuration.

2 No person shall operate on a bridge mentioned in

Schedule 1, or move onto it, a vehicle or object whose

weight exceeds the limit established by section 1,

unless they hold a permit issued under the Highways

Act or the Highways Regulations authorizing them to do

so.
