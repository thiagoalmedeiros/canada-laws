YOIC 1996/48

*Made under the Motor Vehicles Act*

Pursuant to subsection 38(2) of the Motor Vehicles Act ,

the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 The following classes of motor vehicles are

exempted from registration under the Motor Vehicles

Act when they are operated within a highway

construction zone or are being moved under a

Highways Act permit:

(a) off-highway trucks commonly known as

“rock trucks”;

(b) rubber-tired motor scrapers.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 22nd

day of April, 1996.
