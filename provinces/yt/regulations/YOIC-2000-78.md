YOIC 2000/78

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this special warrant

1 To authorize an amount of up to $212,370,000 to

be paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in

accordance with the attached Schedules A and B.

2 To stipulate that payments made under it be made

for the purpose of financing government operations for the

period April 1, 2000 to May 31, 2000 as set forth in

Schedules A and B to this order.

3 To stipulate that payments be made under it by the

Yukon Legislative Assembly for the expenses incurred

either before or after April 1, 2000 for the general election

to be held on April 17, 2000.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 17

day of March, 2000.
