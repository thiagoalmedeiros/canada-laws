# Minister responsible for the Executive Council Office is designated as the territorial minister for the purposes of the Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment Act (Canada)

YOIC 2016/25

*Made under the Government Organisation Act*

Pursuant to subsection 2.2(1) of the Government

Organisation Act , the Commissioner in Executive

Council orders as follows

1 The Minister responsible for the Executive Council

Office is designated as the territorial minister for the

purposes of the Yukon Environmental and Socio-

economic Assessment Act (Canada).

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, February 24, 2016.
