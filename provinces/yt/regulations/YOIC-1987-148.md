## PARKS ACT

## PARKS ACT

Pursuant to Section 7 of the Parks Ac t, the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 Herschel Island is hereby established as Herschel

Island Territorial Park, with the classification of “nature

preserve”.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 30th

day of July, A.D., 1987.
