WCHSBO 2008/9

*Made under the Workers’ Safety and Compensation Act*

## W.C.H.S.B.O. 2008/09

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to paragraph 127(1)(a) of the Workers'

Compensation Act , S.Y. 2008, c.12 and subsection 80(2) of

the Workmen's Compensation Ordinance 1973, as

amended, the board of directors orders as follows

1 The maximum wage rate for the year 1998 shall be

$45,000 (actual rate: $39,000).

2 The maximum wage rate is to be applied to workers

who are entitled to compensation as a result of a work-

related disability caused in 1992 or earlier.

3 This Order is deemed to have come into force on

January 1, 1998.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 23 December 2008.
