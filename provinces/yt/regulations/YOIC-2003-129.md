YOIC 2003/129

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

Pursuant to section 21 of the Territorial Lands (Yukon)

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows

1 The annexed Withdrawal of Certain Lands (Vangorda

Creek, Y.T.) from Disposal Order is hereby made.

2 This Order comes into force April 1, 2003.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 25th day

of March, 2003.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## WITHDRAWAL OF CERTAIN LANDS

(VANGORDA CREEK Y.T.) FROM

## DISPOSAL ORDER

### Withdrawal from disposal

1 It is hereby ordered, for the purpose of the

construction of mill and mine buildings, of an airfield, and

areas for the disposal of tailings, to withdraw from disposal

under the Act the surface only of the lands described in the

Schedule, without prejudice to the rights of the holders of

recorded mineral claims in good standing.

## SCHEDULE

All that parcel of land in the Vangorda Creek area of the

Yukon Territory, as the said parcel is shown, bordered in

red, on a sketch plan of record on file 8-3-955-0-1, in the

Territorial Division of the Department of Indian Affairs and

Northern Development.
