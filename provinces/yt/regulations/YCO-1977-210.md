*Made under the Insurance Premium Tax Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Insurance Premium

Tax Act , the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory is

pleased to and doth hereby order as follows:

1 The annexed Insurance Premium Tax Regulations

are hereby made and established.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 2nd

day of November, 1977.

## REGULATIONS

“ Insurance Premium Tax Regulations .”

“ Insurance Premium Tax Act ,” and the word “returns”,

means Form IPT-1, annexed.
