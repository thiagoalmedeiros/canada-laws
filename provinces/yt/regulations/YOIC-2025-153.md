YOIC 2025/153

*Made under the Health Authority Act*

### Health authority — transition

1 For greater certainty, in the period before the coming

into force of provisions of the Act under

subsection 108(1)

(a) the health authority is established under the

provisions of Part 11, Division 2 of the Act for

the purpose of, and with the responsibility for,

undertaking actions and exercising powers of

the health authority under the Act, including in

respect of the operations and financing of the

health authority and entering into contracts,

that are necessary or advisable to ensure an

orderly and effective transition and to make

provisions of the Act effective upon the coming

into force of those provisions under

subsection 108(1) of the Act;

(b) the health authority must enter into agreements

as are necessary or advisable with the

Government of Yukon, the Minister, the Yukon

Hospital Corporation, the Yukon First Nations’

health committee or other persons to ensure an

orderly and effective transition under Part 11,

Division 2 of the Act, and to make provisions of

the Act effective upon the coming into force of

those provisions under subsection 108(1) of the

Act; and

(c) the provisions of the Act respecting the

operations and financing of the health authority

apply as necessary or advisable in order for the

health authority to carry out its transition

responsibilities under paragraphs (a) and (b).

### Initial board, initial chair and initial chief executive officer — transition

2 (1) For greater certainty, in the period before the

coming into force of provisions of the Act under

subsection 108(1), the initial board, the initial chair and

the initial chief executive officer

(a) are responsible for undertaking actions and

exercising powers under the Act of the health

authority, the board, the chair or the chief

executive officer, as the case may be, including

in respect of the operations and financing of the

health authority, the board, the chair or the chief

executive officer, and entering into contracts on

behalf of the health authority, that are necessary

or advisable to ensure an orderly and effective

transition and to make provisions of the Act

effective upon the coming into force of those

provisions under subsection 108(1) of the Act;

and

(b) must enter into agreements as are necessary or

advisable with the Government of Yukon, the

Minister, the Yukon Hospital Corporation, the

Yukon First Nations’ health committee or other

persons to ensure an orderly and effective

transition under Part 11, Division 2 of the Act,

and to make provisions of the Act effective upon

the coming into force of those provisions under

subsection 108(1) of the Act.

(2) For greater certainty, in the period before the

coming into force of provisions of the Act under

subsection 108(1), the provisions of the Act respecting

the operations and financing of the board, the chair and

the chief executive officer apply to the initial board, the

initial chair and the initial chief executive officer, as the

case may be, as necessary or advisable for them to carry

out their transition responsibilities under subsection (1).
