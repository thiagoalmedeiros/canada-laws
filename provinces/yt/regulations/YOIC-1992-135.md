YOIC 1992/135

*Made under the Environment Act*

### Recycling Fund

1 There is hereby established a special fund to be known

as the Recycling Fund.

### Payments from Fund

2 The Minister of Community Services may consider

representations from any person or organization

respecting the purposes for which money from the

fund may be used.

### Annual financial statement

3 The Minister of Community Services shall cause to be

prepared an annual financial statement and report on

the activities of the fund.
