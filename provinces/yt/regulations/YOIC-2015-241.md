YOIC 2015/241

*Made under the Notaries Act*

### Interpretation

1 In this Regulation, “ applicant ” means a person who

has applied for enrolment or renewal of enrolment as a

notary public under section 2 or 6 of the Act, as the

case may be.

### Fees payable to the Consolidated Revenue Fund

2 The fees set out in sections 4 and 8 are payable to the

Yukon Consolidated Revenue Fund.

### Application for enrolment

3 An application for enrolment as a notary public must be

in the form attached as Schedule A.

### Application fee

4 The fee for making an application for enrolment or for

renewal of enrolment as a notary public is $75.00.

### Proof that requirements met

5 The proof that an applicant has met the requirements

of section 5 of the Act must be set out in the form

attached as Schedule A.

### Oath of office

6 The oath of office for a notary public must be in either

of the following forms

(a) “I, [name of the applicant], do solemnly and

sincerely promise that I will faithfully and

honestly fulfil the duties of a Notary Public, and

that I will keep confidential any matter that

comes to my knowledge through the exercises

of those duties”; or

(b) “I, [name of the applicant], do solemnly and

sincerely swear that I will faithfully and

honestly fulfil the duties of a Notary Public, and

that I will keep confidential any matter that

comes to my knowledge through the exercises

of those duties. So help me God.”

### Certificate of commission

7 A certificate of commission under section 12 of the Act

must be

(a) if the notary public is enrolled under section 5

of the Act, in the form attached as

Schedule B; or

(b) if the notary public is enrolled under section 16

of the Act, must be in the form attached as

Schedule C.

### Fee for certificate of commission

8 The fee for issuing a certificate of commission under

section 12 of the Act is $50.00.

### Entitlement to receive fees

9 A notary public acting in the office of a notary public is

entitled to receive, for exercising a power or

performing a duty, any reasonable fee agreed upon in

advance.
