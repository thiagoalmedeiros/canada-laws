*Made under the Yukon Act (Canada)*

## CREEK)

### Administration and control relinquished

1 The administration and control of the public real

property described in the attached schedule is

relinquished, in perpetuity, to His Majesty the King in

right of Canada as represented by the Minister of

Northern Affairs.

### Coming into force

2 This Regulation comes into force on the day the

Governor in Council approves the relinquishment of the

public real property referred to in section 1.

## SCHEDULE

In the Yukon; in Quad 116 C/07; the surface parcel which

includes the Clinton Creek abandoned mine area, being

more particularly described as follows:

Commencing at the legal survey monument at the

northeast corner of Mineral Claim Ford #2, Lot 92 with

markings “3L92”, as shown on Plan 51946 CLSR.
