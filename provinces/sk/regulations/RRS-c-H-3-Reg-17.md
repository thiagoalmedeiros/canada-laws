# The Provincial Highway Designation Regulations, 1990

RRS c H-3 Reg 17

*Made under The Highways and Transportation Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Provincial Highway Designation

Regulations, 1990.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “official highway map” means the Official Highway

Map that appears in Part I of the Appendix and includes the Regina Bypass Official

Highway Map that appears in Part II of the Appendix.

### Provincial highways designated

3(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), the public highways shown on the official

highway map under the heading “Provincial Highways” are designated as provincial

highways.

(2) If a public highway shown on the official highway map as a provincial

highway passes through a city or a rural municipality, that portion of the

public highway that lies within the city or the rural municipality is a provincial

highway except for the portions of public highways listed below:

(a) any portion that lies within the boundaries of a city except for any portion

of the Regina Bypass shown in Part II of the Appendix that lies within the

boundaries of the City of Regina;

(b) the following portions of public highways:

(i) Town of Assiniboia:

(A) Highway 2 South (Centre Street) from the south edge of Sunset

Drive to the south edge of Highway 13 East (1st Avenue);

(B) Highway 2/Highway 13 (1st Avenue) from the east edge of

Leeville Street - to the Railroad;

(ii) Town of Canora:

(A) Highway 5 West (Railway Avenue) from the west edge of

Highway 9 to the Railroad;

(B) Highway 9 from the south edge of 8th Avenue to the north edge

of Highway 5 West (Railway Avenue);

(iii) Town of Esterhazy, Highway 22 (Park Avenue) from the west edge

of Short Street to the west edge of James Street (west urban limit);

(iv) Town of Fort Qu’Appelle, Highway 210 (Broadway Street) from the

west edge of Highway 35 to the west urban limit (30.52m west along the

south edge of Highway 210 from the west corner of the intersection of

Qu’Appelle Crescent with Highway 210);

(v) Town of Gravelbourg:

(A) Highway 43 (1st Avenue) from the east urban limit (132.87m

east of the east property line of parcel A on Highway 43 East) to

the west urban limit (303.03m west of the west property line of

parcel AA on Highway 43 West);

(B) Highway 58 North (Magnan Street) from the north edge of

Highway 43 (1st Avenue) to the north urban limit (54.01m north of

the north property line of parcel R on Highway 58 North);

(vi) Town of Kamsack:

(A) Highway 5 (Queen Elizabeth Boulevard) from the start

of 4-lane to the east edge of Highway 8 North (Edmonton Avenue);

(B) Highway 8 South (Railway Avenue) from the west edge of 7th

Avenue to the south edge of Highway 5 (Queen Elizabeth Boulevard);

(vii) Town of Kerrobert, Highway 21 (Pacific Avenue) from the north

edge of Highway 21 South to the south edge of Peters Street;

(viii) Town of Maple Creek, Highway 21 (Pacific Avenue) from the east

edge of Highway 21 South to the Railroad;

(ix) Town of Moosomin, Highway 8 South (Main Street) from the south

edge of Wright Road to the north edge of Old Highway (Park Avenue);

(x) Town of Nipawin:

(A) Highway 55 East (Nipawin Road) from the east edge of

Evergreen Drive to the west edge of Highway 35 (1st Avenue);

(B) Highway 35 (1st Avenue) from the north edge of Old Highway 35

to the south edge of Highway 55 East (Nipawin Road);

(xi) Town of Outlook, Highway 15 (Saskatchewan Avenue and McKenzie

Street) from the east edge of Christie Street to the south edge of Hall

Avenue;

(xii) Town of Preeceville, Highway 49 (Highway Avenue) from the east

edge of 7th Street North East to the west edge of 3rd Street North West;

(xiii) Town of Regina Beach, Highway 54 (Centre Street) from the north

edge of Highway 54 East to the north edge of Railway Avenue;

(xiv) Town of Rosetown, Highway 4 from the north edge of Highway 7

(Railway Avenue) to the north edge of Marshall Avenue;

(xv) Town of Tisdale, Highway 35 (100th A Street and 100th Street/

Main Street) from the north edge of Highway 3 to the north edge of 112th

Avenue;

(xvi) Town of Wadena:

(A) Highway 5 (Pamela Wallin Drive) from the west edge of 8th

Street North East to the west urban limit (the east edge of 4th

Street North West);

(B) Highway 35 (Main Street) from the north edge of 1st Avenue

South to the Railroad;

(xvii) Town of Watrous, Highway 2 East (1st Avenue) from the east urban

limit (the west edge of Highway 365) to the west edge of 3rd Street West;

(xviii) Town of Wilkie, Highway 29 (2nd Avenue) from the east edge

of 5th Street West to the west edge of 6th Street East.

(3) Any portion of a public highway, as shown on the official highway map, that

lies or is situated wholly within the boundaries of the Prince Albert National Park

is deemed not to be a provincial highway.

(4) Within the boundaries of the Rural Municipality of Sherwood, north of the City

of Regina, the following are designated as provincial highways:

(i) the portion of Inland Drive from the intersection of Provincial

Highway 6 east to the intersection with Range Road 2193;

(ii) the portion of Range Road 2193 from the intersection of Inland Drive

south 800 metres to Provincial Highway 0460RUA.

### R.R.S. c.H-3 Reg 16 repealed

4 The Provincial Highway Designation Regulations, 1989 are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on April 1, 1990.
