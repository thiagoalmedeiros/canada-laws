RRS c P-22.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Prairie and Forest Fires Act, 1982*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Forest Fire Contingency Fund Regulations.

### Escaped fire

2 For the purposes of clause 2(e.1) of The Prairie and Forest Fires Act, a fire greater

than 100 hectares in area is prescribed as an escaped fire.

### Coming into force

3 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations, but are retroactive and are deemed to have been in force

on and from April 1, 2001.
