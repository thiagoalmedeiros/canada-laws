RRS c P-22.2 Reg 1

*Made under The Pre-judgment Interest Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Pre-judgment Interest Regulations.

### Interest rates

2 The interest rate for each three-month period commencing with the three-month

period that begins on January 1, 1986 is the average yield announced by the Bank

of Canada in respect of the last weekly Government of Canada tender of 91-day

Canada Treasury Bills that occurs prior to the commencement of the interest period.

### Publication of interest rates

3(1) In this section, “three-month period” means a three-month period

mentioned in section 2.

(2) As soon as is practicable after the interest rate for a three-month period is

determined pursuant to section 2, the Inspector of Legal Offices shall publish in

Part I of the Gazette a notice of that interest rate together with the interest rates

for the three preceding three-month periods.

(3) The Inspector of Legal Offices shall cause the Queen’s Printer to publish on

the Queen’s Printer website a cumulative notice of all interest rates determined

pursuant to section 2 commencing with the three-month period that begins on

January 1, 1986.
