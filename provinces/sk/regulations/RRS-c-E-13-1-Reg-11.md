RRS c E-13.1 Reg 11

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Trade and Export Development

Regulations.

### Ministry continued

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry of

Export and Trade Development is continued as the Ministry of Trade and Export

Development.

### Objects and purposes

3 The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Trade and Export Development

are to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities and

functions of the Minister of Trade and Export Development may be exercised and

carried out in relation to the following:

(a) to co-ordinate, develop, promote, and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to encouraging the growth and

expansion of the Saskatchewan economy;

(b) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan that foster innovation and the development

of new ideas, products and processes to ensure the growth and expansion of

the Saskatchewan economy;

(c) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan that provide business information and

advice and investment advice to businesses, local governments, persons, co-

operatives, organizations and associations;

(d) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan that will provide Saskatchewan businesses

with the skills and information they require to be able to excel in the competitive

tendering environment inside and outside Saskatchewan;

(e) to research and investigate economic issues, strategies and opportunities;

(f) to collect and disseminate information respecting the growth and expansion

of Saskatchewan’s economy;

(g) to co-ordinate all efforts by the Government of Saskatchewan related to

economic issues, strategies and opportunities within Canada, other jurisdictions

in North America and the world;

(h) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan related to trade opportunities and priorities;

(i) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan that foster and advance economic

development in northern Saskatchewan;

(j) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to the economic development of

First Nations and Métis peoples.

### RRS c E-13.1 Reg 8 repealed

4 The Ministry of Export and Trade Development Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
