RRS c V-2.1 Reg 8

*Made under The Vehicle Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Vehicle Impoundment (Financial

Responsibility) Regulations.

### Prescribed amount

2 For the purposes of subsection 68(3) of The Vehicle Administration Act, the

prescribed amount of property damage is $1000.
