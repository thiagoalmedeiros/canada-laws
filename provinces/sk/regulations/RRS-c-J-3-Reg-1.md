RRS c J-3 Reg 1

*Made under The Judgments Extension Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Judgments Extension Regulations.

### Extension of Act

2 The Judgments Extension Act extends to:

(a) the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland; and

(b) the States of Victoria and New South Wales in the Commonwealth of

Australia.

### Repeal

3 Orders in Council 238/28, 1590/28 and 1648/28 are repealed.

### Coming into froce

4 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
