RRS c T-20.2 Reg 1

*Made under The Trespass to Property Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Trespass to Property Regulations.

### Definitions

2(1) In these regulations, “Act” means The Trespass to Property Act.

(2) For the purposes of the Act, “person” includes the Crown in right of Canada.

### Occupier’s authorized representative

3(1) An occupier may authorize an individual or an organization, orally or in

writing, as the occupier’s representative for the purpose of exercising a power or

performing a duty of the occupier pursuant to the Act.

(2) An occupier’s representative who is authorized in accordance with subsection (1)

is prescribed as an occupier under subclause 2(c)(iii) of the Act in relation to a

power exercised or a duty performed by the occupier’s representative on behalf of

the occupier.

### Deemed notice of prohibited activities

3.1(1) For the purposes of subsection 11(1) of the Act, notice given “by any other

means prescribed in the regulations” includes notice deemed to have been given

pursuant to subsection (2).

(2) The following activities in or on all or part of any premises are deemed to be

prohibited activities for which notice is deemed to have been given pursuant to

section 10 of the Act:

(a) public intoxication;

(b) use of a controlled substance in contravention of the Controlled Drugs

and Substances Act (Canada);

(c) threatening to cause physical harm to any person or property, whether

the threat is communicated through verbal, physical or other means;

(d) public urination or defecation;

(e) intentionally causing damage to the premises or to any personal property

located on the premises;

(f) any activity that constitutes an offence pursuant to the Criminal Code.

### Coming into force

4 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
