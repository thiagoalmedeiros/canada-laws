# The 2023 Farm and Ranch Water Infrastructure Program Regulations

RRS c F-8.001 Reg 52

*Made under The Farm Financial Stability Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The 2023 Farm and Ranch Water Infrastructure

Program Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“account” means the 2023 Farm and Ranch Water Infrastructure Program

Account established pursuant to section 4;

“Act” means The Farm Financial Stability Act;

“approved project” means an eligible project that has been approved by

the minister;

“eligible applicant” means an applicant who meets the requirements set

out in section 6;

“eligible expenditures” means expenditures for items and activities that

are determined to be eligible pursuant to section 8;

“eligible project” means a project that meets the requirements set out in

section 7;

“fund” means the Saskatchewan Agricultural Stabilization Fund continued

pursuant to section 23 of the Act;

“Indian band” means a band as defined in the Indian Act (Canada), and

includes the council of a band;

“irrigation” means irrigation within the meaning of The Irrigation Act, 2019

for primary agricultural purposes;

“ministry” means the ministry over which the minister presides;

“person” includes a partnership, association, Indian band or other organization;

“policy” means program eligibility, administration and payment criteria as

set out in the 2023 Farm and Ranch Water Infrastructure Program Policy

established by the minister, dated April 1, 2023, as amended from time to time;

“program” means the 2023 Farm and Ranch Water Infrastructure Program

established pursuant to section 3;

“program payment” means a payment made to an eligible applicant;

“program year” means the period commencing on April 1 in one year and

ending on March 31 of the following year;

“secure and sustainable water supply” means the supply of a long-term

source of water.

### Program established

3(1) The 2023 Farm and Ranch Water Infrastructure Program is established.

(2) The purpose of the program is to provide payments to eligible applicants to

support the adoption of practices and projects that:

(a) develop a secure and sustainable water supply in Saskatchewan;

(b) increase irrigation capacity in Saskatchewan;

(c) improve energy and water efficiency for existing irrigation infrastructure;

or

(d) the minister considers to be in the interest of the agricultural and

environmental priorities of the Government of Saskatchewan.

### Account established

4(1) Pursuant to clause 24(2)(a) of the Act, the 2023 Farm and Ranch Water

Infrastructure Program Account is established in the fund.

(2) The Minister of Finance is authorized to deposit into the account:

(a) all contributions from the Government of Canada that are directed to

the account for the purposes of the program pursuant to an agreement made

pursuant to subsection 22(2) of the Act; and

(b) from moneys appropriated by the Legislature, all contributions of the

Government of Saskatchewan to the program pursuant to an agreement made

pursuant to subsection 22(2) of the Act.

(3) The account consists of:

(a) all contributions mentioned in clause (2)(a);

(b) all moneys appropriated by the Legislature:

(i) for the purposes of the program; or

(ii) for any other farm income stabilization purpose, if the Minister of

Finance directs that those moneys are to be paid into the account;

(c) all earnings on investments of moneys in the account; and

(d) all other moneys received in the account for the purposes of the program.

(4) All program payments are to be paid from the account.

(5) Any surplus remaining in the account when the program is completed is to be

returned to the Government of Canada and the Government of Saskatchewan in

proportion to each government’s contribution to the account.

(6) The fiscal year of the account is the program year.

### Minister to administer account and program

5(1) The minister shall administer the account and the program in accordance

with these regulations.

(2) For the purposes of administering the account and the program, the minister

may:

(a) exercise the powers given to the minister pursuant to the Act; and

(b) do any other thing that the minister considers necessary to administer

the account or the program.

(3) Without limiting the generality of subsection (2), for the purpose of administering

the account or the program, the minister may:

(a) enter into any agreement that the minister considers advisable with any

person, agency, organization, association, institution or body;

(b) undertake research, conduct studies and provide information to

agricultural producers, eligible applicants or persons who, in the minister’s

opinion, may become eligible applicants in relation to eligible projects or the

program;

(c) use any moneys received in the account:

(i) to pay for the administration of the account and the program; and

(ii) to make program payments;

(d) invest any moneys in the account that are not presently required for the

purposes of the program in any securities or class of securities authorized

pursuant to The Financial Administration Act, 1993 for the investment of

moneys in the general revenue fund; and

(e) dispose of any investment made pursuant to clause (d), subject to the terms

of the investment, in any manner, on any terms and in any amount that the

minister considers expedient.

### Program eligibility

6(1) Any of the following may apply to the program with respect to an eligible

project:

(a) an individual who is a Saskatchewan resident and at least 18 years of age;

(b) a body corporate, co-operative, partnership or other entity if it is authorized

to carry on business in Saskatchewan and is subject to Saskatchewan income

tax;

(c) an Indian band that has reserve land in Saskatchewan;

(d) a rural municipality;

(e) an irrigation district within the meaning of The Irrigation Act, 2019;

(f) a Crown land pasture grazing association.

(2) An applicant must meet any other eligibility criteria set out in the policy,

including:

(a) in the case of an applicant mentioned in clause (1)(a) or (b):

(i) the gross income requirement; and

(ii) participation in regulatory programs that the minister considers to

be in the interest of the agricultural and environmental priorities of the

Government of Saskatchewan; or

(b) in the case of an applicant mentioned in subclause clause (1)(c), (d), (e)

or (f), the criterion mentioned in subclause (a)(ii).

(3) If the minister receives an application pursuant to this section and is satisfied

that the application is complete, that the eligible applicant meets the criteria set out

in these regulations and that it is appropriate to do so, the minister may approve

the application.

### Eligible projects

7(1) For the purposes of these regulations, an eligible project is a project that

meets the following requirements:

(a) the project is located in Saskatchewan;

(b) the project is undertaken by an eligible applicant;

(c) the project involves the development or improvement of a secure and

sustainable water supply that, in the opinion of the minister, is substantially

for environmental or agricultural purposes;

(d) the project:

(i) meets requirements contained in the agricultural funding framework

as agreed to between the Government of Saskatchewan and the

Government of Canada; or

(ii) in the case of projects that are not funded by the Government of

Canada, meets the requirements of the policy, or the part of the policy,

for that type of project;

(e) the project meets the criteria set out in the policy;

(f) in the case of a project pre-approved pursuant to The 2018 Farm and

Ranch Water Infrastructure Regulations, the minister approves that project

for transfer into the program.

(2) No eligible applicant shall supply any false or misleading information to the

minister on any application form or in response to any request for information

from the minister.

(3) An eligible applicant shall provide the minister with any changes to the

information on the applicant’s application form as soon as is practicable after that

information has changed.

### Eligible expenditures

8(1) In this section, “fair market value” means the fair market value as

determined by the minister based on the report of a qualified person or another

person who is satisfactory to the minister.

(2) Subject to these regulations, the minister may approve the following

expenditures if the minister is satisfied that those expenditures are incurred by an

eligible applicant for use in an eligible project:

(a) the cost of capital items;

(b) construction costs;

(c) licensing, permitting and consulting costs.

(3) Subject to these regulations, in determining the eligible amount of an applicant’s

expenditures for the purposes of an eligible project, the minister shall use the lesser

of the applicant’s actual costs and the fair market value of the services rendered.

(4) For the purpose of subsection (2), the applicant must provide evidence

satisfactory to the minister that the eligible project, or a portion of the eligible

project, was completed in the manner claimed by the applicant.

(5) The minister may approve expenditures and costs not specifically referenced

in these regulations if the minister is satisfied that those costs are necessary for

the functionality or sustainability of the project.

### Claim for program payments

9(1) An eligible applicant must, for the purposes of making a claim for a program

payment, submit a request, in a form and manner approved by the minister, to the

minister by:

(a) December 31, 2028; or

(b) any other date determined by the minister.

(2) A request made pursuant to subsection (1) is to include:

(a) a summary of all eligible expenditures with respect to the claim; and

(b) any other information that the minister reasonably requires for the

purpose of evaluating the claim.

(3) An eligible applicant shall provide the minister with any changes to

the information that was submitted pursuant to subsection (2) as soon as is

practicable after the eligible applicant becomes aware of those changes.

(4) An eligible applicant shall allow the minister, or any person designated by the

minister, to inspect:

(a) projects that are the subject of an application; and

(b) any records, regardless of their location, that the minister considers

necessary or relevant to evaluate the claim or ensure compliance with these

regulations and the policy.

### Program payments

10(1) If the minister is satisfied that an eligible applicant has completed an eligible

project, or a portion of the eligible project, and has provided the minister with any

information that the minister may reasonably require, the minister may make a

program payment to the eligible applicant in accordance with this section respecting

the eligible project that is the subject of the application.

(2) The minister may impose any terms and conditions that the minister considers

appropriate on a program payment, including with respect to:

(a) eligible project costs;

(b) program funding caps;

(c) minimum claim amounts;

(d) items that are not eligible for funding as part of an eligible project; and

(e) the date of commencement for eligible expenditures.

(3) An eligible applicant shall comply with any terms and conditions imposed by

the minister on a program payment.

(4) If an eligible applicant fails to comply with these regulations and the policy,

the minister may deny the claim for a program payment.

(5) If the minister denies a claim for a program payment, the minister shall notify

the applicant.

(6) An applicant mentioned in subsection (5) may request, in a form and manner

approved by the minister, that the minister reconsider the decision mentioned in

that subsection.

### Overpayments

11(1) The minister may determine any program payment made to an eligible

applicant to be an overpayment if the minister is satisfied that:

(a) the applicant has knowingly made a false or misleading statement with

respect to a material fact on any form or in any information or document

provided to the minister pursuant to these regulations;

(b) the applicant has knowingly omitted to make a statement or to provide

any information or document if the omission results in a statement with respect

to a material fact being misleading; or

(c) the applicant has failed to comply with these regulations and the policy.

(2) If the minister determines a program payment to be an overpayment, the

amount of the overpayment is deemed to be a debt due and owing to the Government

of Saskatchewan and may be recovered from the eligible applicant in any manner

authorized pursuant to The Financial Administration Act, 1993 or in any other

manner authorized by law.

### Termination of approvals

12(1) Notwithstanding any other provision of these regulations, if the minister

determines that moneys in the account have been fully allocated to approved projects:

(a) the minister may refuse to approve any application of an eligible applicant

that is received by the minister after the date of that determination; and

(b) an eligible applicant mentioned in clause (a) is not eligible to receive any

program payment in that program year with respect to that application.

(2) If the minister makes a determination pursuant to subsection (1), the minister

may cause the effective date of the determination:

(a) to be posted on the ministry’s website; and

(b) to be made public in any other manner that the minister considers

appropriate.

(3) The effective date of the determination may be an earlier date than the date

on which the minister makes public the effective date of the determination.

### Coming into force

13(1) These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with

the Registrar of Regulations.

(2) These regulations expire on March 31, 2029.
