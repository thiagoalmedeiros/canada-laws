RRS c R-8.1 Reg 3

*Made under The Regional Colleges Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Regional Colleges Programs and Services

Regulations, 1997.

### Delivery of programs

2 A regional college may:

(a) provide employment services, programs and activities associated with

career, educational and training services, programs and activities, pursuant to

agreements with the Minister of Post-Secondary Education and Skills Training

or New Careers Corporation; and

(b) on behalf of and pursuant to agreements with the Minister of

Post-Secondary Education and Skills Training:

(i) administer training allowances that relate to educational services or

programs provided through the regional college; and

(ii) determine eligibility for and provide employment, career counselling

and job replacement services.

### Coming into force

3 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
