RRS c W-1.3 Reg 1

*Made under The Wanuskewin Heritage Park Act, 1997*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Wanuskewin Heritage Park Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Wanuskewin Heritage Park Act, 1997.

### Allowance and reimbursement

3 For the purposes of subsection 5(8) of the Act, the authority may pay the members,

other than members who are employees of the Government of Saskatchewan:

(a) an allowance for their services at the rates approved by the Lieutenant

Governor in Council; and

(b) reimbursement for their expenses incurred in the performance of their

duties at the rates paid to members of the public service of Saskatchewan.

### Borrowing limits

4 For the purposes of section 12 of the Act, the authority may borrow money to a

maximum of:

(a) $250,000 for operating purposes;

(b) $500,000 for capital purposes; and

(c) subject to the approval of the Lieutenant Governor in Council, $10,000,000

for capital purposes related to the Thundering Ahead Capital Redevelopment

Project, if the borrowing occurs in either or both of the 2020-2021 and 2021-2022

fiscal years.

### Fiscal year

5 For the purposes of section 15 of the Act, the fiscal year of the authority is the

period commencing on April 1 in one year and ending on March 31 in the following

year.

### R.R.S. c.W-1.2 Reg 2 repealed

6 The Wanuskewin Heritage Park Appointment of Members Regulations are

repealed.
