RRS c R-16.3 Reg 1

*Made under The Regulatory Modernization and Accountability Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Regulatory Modernization and

Accountability Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Regulatory Modernization and

Accountability Act.

### Reporting requirements

3 For the purposes of subsection 3(1) of the Act, the report prepared by the minister

must contain information respecting the measures undertaken or proposed to be

undertaken to modernize regulations during the reporting period.

### Coming into force

4(1) These regulations come into force on the day on which section 1 of The

Regulatory Modernization and Accountability Act comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day

on which section 1 of The Regulatory Modernization and Accountability Act comes

into force, these regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with

the Registrar of Regulations.
