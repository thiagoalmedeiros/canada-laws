RRS c H-3.01 Reg 1

*Made under The Highways and Transportation Act, 1997*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Highways and Transportation Act

Regulations.

### Prescribed distance

2 For the purposes of section 32 of The Highways and Transportation Act, 1997 the

prescribed distance from the surveyed limit of a provincial highway is 90 metres.

2.1 Repealed. 26 Aug 2022 SR 66/2022 s2.

### Coming into force

3 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
