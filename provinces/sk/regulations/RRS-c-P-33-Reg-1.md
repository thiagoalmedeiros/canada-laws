RRS c P-33 Reg 1

*Made under The Provincial Mediation Board Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Provincial Mediation Board (Fees)

Regulations.

### Amount of fee payable

2 The amount of the fee payable by a creditor to the Provincial Mediation Board,

pursuant to section 6 of The Provincial Mediation Board Act, is equal to 10 % of the

total amount to be paid by the debtor under an agreement between the debtor and

the creditor.

### Amount of fee payable by municipality

2.1 The amount of the fee payable by a municipality to the Provincial Mediation

Board, pursuant to subsection 7.1(2) of The Provincial Mediation Board Act,

is $20.
