RRS c N-8 Reg 1

*Made under The Notaries Public Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Notaries Public Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Notaries Public Act.

### Fee

3(1) Subject to subsection (2), the fee payable for an appointment pursuant to the

Act as a notary public is $200.

(2) Where an employee of the Government of Saskatchewan or The Saskatchewan

Legal Aid Commission is required to be a notary public in connection with his duties,

no fee is payable for the appointment.

### Repeal

4 Saskatchewan Regulations 191/72 are repealed.
