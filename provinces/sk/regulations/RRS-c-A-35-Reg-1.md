# The Saskatchewan Auto Fund Designation of Assets and Liabilities Regulations

RRS c A-35 Reg 1

*Made under The Automobile Accident Insurance Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Saskatchewan Auto Fund Designation of

Assets and Liabilities Regulations.

### Assets

2 The assets listed in Part I of the Appendix are the assets as of January 1, 1984 of

Saskatchewan Government Insurance which are to form assets of the Saskatchewan

Auto Fund.

### Liabilities

3 The liabilities listed in Part II of the Appendix are the liabilities as of January

1, 1984 of Saskatchewan Government Insurance which are to form liabilities of the

Saskatchewan Auto Fund.

### Short-term investments

4 The items listed in Part III of the Appendix are the short-term investments

mentioned in Part I of the Appendix.

### Bonds and debentures

5 The items listed in Part IV of the Appendix are the bonds and debentures

mentioned in Part I of the Appendix.

### Mortgage investments

6 The items listed in Part V of the Appendix are the mortgage investments

mentioned in Part I of the Appendix.

### Equity investments

7 The items listed in Part VI of the Appendix are the equity investments mentioned

in Part I of the Appendix.

### Capital lease

8 The details of the capital lease mentioned in paragraph 4 of Part I of the Appendix

are shown in Part VII of the Appendix.

### Land

9 The land listed in Part VIII of the Appendix is the land mentioned in Part I of

the Appendix listed by municipal address, with each municipal address having been

assigned a lot group number to cover the group of lots comprised in the municipal

address.

### Buildings

10 The buildings listed in Part IX of the Appendix are the buildings mentioned

in Part I of the Appendix listed by municipal address with each municipal address

bearing the same lot group number as it bears in Part VIII of the Appendix.

### Legal descriptions

11 Part IX of the Appendix shows the legal descriptions of the land mentioned in

Part I of the Appendix grouped according to the lot group number shown in Part VIII

of the Appendix.
