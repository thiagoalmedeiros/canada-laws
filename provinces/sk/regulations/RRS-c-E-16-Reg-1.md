RRS c E-16 Reg 1

*Made under The Expropriation Procedure Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Expropriation Procedure Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Expropriation Procedure Act.

### Form C varied

3 Form C of the Act is varied by striking out “(Signature of expropriating authority)”

under the signature line and substituting “(Signature of expropriating authority or

person acting for the expropriating authority)”.
