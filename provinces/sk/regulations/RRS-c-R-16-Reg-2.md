### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Regulation Chapter Number Assignment

Regulations.

### New chapter numbers

2 The regulations set forth in Column 1 of the Table of Chapter Numbers in the

Appendix are amended by repealing their chapter numbers set forth in Column 2

opposite the respective regulation and assigning the new chapter numbers set forth

in Column 3 opposite the respective regulation.
