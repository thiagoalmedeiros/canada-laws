RRS c H-2.2 Reg 1

*Made under The Heritage Property Act*

## PART 1

### Preliminary Matters

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Heritage Property Regulations, 2016.

### Definitions

2(1) In these regulations, “Act” means The Heritage Property Act.

(2) For the purposes of the Act and in these regulations:

“boulder effigy” means any site that contains or contained lines, clusters,

configurations or patterns of stones, rocks, cobbles or other lithic objects

that:

(a) depict zoomorphic or anthropomorphic figures or other

representations; and

(b) are or were constructed for ceremonial or other non-domestic use;

“burial object” means any archaeological objects that are or were found

in association with a burial place;

“burial place” or “burial mound” means any site that contains or

contained or on inspection can be reasonably expected to contain human

skeletal material or burial objects;

“human skeletal material” means all osseous parts or elements of the

human anatomy;

“medicine wheel” means a site that contains or contained lines, clusters,

configurations and circular or semi-circular patterns of stones, rocks, cobbles

or other lithic objects that are or were constructed for ceremonial or other

non-domestic use;

“petroglyph” means any picture, design, symbol, art work or other

representation pecked, carved or incised on a natural rock surface for at

least 75 years;

“pictograph” means any picture, design, symbol, art work or other

representation painted on a natural rock surface for at least 75 years.

## PART 2

### Review Board

### Definition for Part

3 In this Part, ‘review board member’ means a member of the Foundation who

is appointed to the review board pursuant to subsection 5.3(2) of the Act.

4 Repealed. 21 Sep 2018 SR 70/2018 s4.

### Duties of review board members

5 In conducting the business and carrying out the duties of review board members,

each review board member shall:

(a) comply with the provisions of the Act and these regulations; and

(b) exercise the care, diligence, skill and impartiality that a reasonably

prudent person would exercise in comparable circumstances.

### Filing of referrals

6 Every referral to the review board pursuant to the Act is to be in writing and to

be filed with the secretary of the review board at the address set out on the review

board’s website.

### Request for review

7 A request for a review must state the affected property, the person requesting

the review and the reasons for the request.

### Notification of hearing

8 The secretary of the review board shall notify the applicant in writing on

receipt of the request of the date, location and time of the public hearing.

9 Repealed. 21 Sep 2018 SR 70/2018 s9.

### Decision

10 Every decision of the review board must state the date it is issued.

### Adjournment of public hearing

11(1) On the request of a party to a public hearing and the designating

authority, the review board may adjourn any public hearing and, in that case,

the review board shall:

(a) not prepare a decision; and

(b) notify all parties participating in the hearing and any other person

who requests the notice of the adjournment.

(2) The chairperson of the review board may adjourn a public hearing if the

review board considers it necessary or appropriate to do so.

(3) If a public hearing is adjourned, the review board shall notify all parties

to the public hearing of the date, location and time the public hearing will be

recommenced.

## PART 3

### Permits

### Application fee

11.1(1) For the purposes of subsection 67(2) of the Act, the application fee for the

following permits is $300 for each permit:

(a) Archaeological Impact Assessment Investigation Permit;

(b) Archaeological Mitigation Investigation Permit;

(c) Palaeontological Impact Assessment Investigation Permit;

(d) Palaeontological Mitigation Investigation Permit.

(2) The application fee mentioned in subsection (1) is non-refundable.

### Cancellation of permit

12(1) For the purposes of subsection 68(8) of the Act, the permit holder, in a

request for the cancellation of a permit, shall:

(a) provide, in writing, an explanation of the reason for the request;

(b) provide, in writing, a description of the activities completed in

accordance with that permit;

(c) describe all measures required to:

(i) complete the activities proposed in the application for that permit;

and

(ii) if applicable, return, insofar as it is possible, the site to its original

state; and

(d) provide reasonable assurance that the work described in clause (c)

has been completed.

(2) At the time that a request is made for the cancellation of a permit, the

permit holder shall:

(a) provide the curatorial repository identified in that permit with copies

or, if required by the minister, originals of all records, maps, forms and

photographs amassed; and

(b) provide to that repository all objects recovered and acquired in the

course of the permitted work.

## PART 4

### Repeal and Coming into Force

are repealed.

### Coming into force
