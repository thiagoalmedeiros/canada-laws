RRS c S-65 Reg 2

*Made under The Surface Rights Acquisition and Compensation Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Surface Rights Acquisition and

Compensation Regulations, 2023.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Surface Rights Acquisition and Compensation Act;

“minister” means the minister responsible for the administration of the

applicable regulatory enactment.

### Definition of “mineral”

3 For the purposes of clause 2(e) of the Act, “mineral” includes:

(a) a subsurface mineral as defined in The Subsurface Mineral Tenure

Regulations; and

(b) helium.

### Maximum board award for loss or damage re tortious act

4 For the purposes of subsection 63(5) of the Act, the prescribed amount is $30,000.

### Proof of payment re subsection 86.1(8) of the Act

5 For the purposes of subsection 86.1(8) of the Act, an operator shall provide proof

to the minister, in a form and manner approved by that minister, that payment has

been issued by the operator and has been accepted by the owner.

### RRS c S-65 Reg 1 repealed

6 The Surface Rights Acquisition and Compensation Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

7(1) These regulations come into force on the day on which section 1 of The Surface

Rights Acquisition and Compensation Amendment Act, 2023 comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day on

which section 1 of The Surface Rights Acquisition and Compensation Amendment

Act, 2023 comes into force, these regulations come into force on the day on which

they are filed with the Registrar of Regulations.
