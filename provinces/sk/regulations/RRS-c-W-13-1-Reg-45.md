# The Wildlife Management Zones and Special Areas Boundaries Regulations, 1990

RRS c W-13.1 Reg 45

*Made under The Wildlife Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Wildlife Management Zones and Special

Areas Boundaries Regulations, 1990.

### Interpretation

2(1) In these regulations, “Northern Provincial Forest” means the Northern

Provincial Forest described in Part III of the Appendix to The Forest Resources

Management Regulations.

(2) Any reference in these regulations to a provincial highway, provincial road or

municipal road is a reference to the provincial highway, provincial road or municipal

road as published in the most recent version of the Saskatchewan Official Road Map

that is based on data maintained by the ministry over which the minister responsible

for the administration of The Highways and Transportation Act, 1997 presides.

(3) Any reference in these regulations to a provincial highway, provincial road,

municipal road, road or trail as a wildlife management zone boundary means the

centreline of the referenced provincial highway, provincial road, municipal road,

road or trail is deemed to be the boundary.

(4) Any reference in these regulations to a provincial highway containing four lanes

as a wildlife management zone boundary means the centreline of the westbound or

southbound lane is deemed to be the boundary.

(5) In these regulations, references to Sections and Legal Subdivisions by number

are inclusive.

### Wildlife management zones

3(1) Subject to subsection (2), the wildlife management zones described in Part I

of the Appendix are constituted as areas for managing, harvesting, controlling or

regulating wildlife.

(2) If all or part of any of the following areas is contained within the boundaries of

a wildlife management zone, that area or part is not part of the wildlife management

zone:

(a) any game preserve, road corridor game preserve or wildlife refuge

constituted by these regulations;

(b) any provincial park, protected area, recreation site, historic site or park

land reserve constituted pursuant to The Parks Act;

(c) any regional park established pursuant to The Regional Parks Act, 1979;

(d) Repealed. 2 Jan 2009 SR 130/2008 s3.

(e) Fort a la Corne Wildlife Management Unit.

### Wildlife management units

4 The wildlife management units described in Part II of the Appendix are

constituted as areas for protecting, propagating, managing, harvesting, controlling

or regulating wildlife or its habitat.

### Game preserves

5 The game preserves described in Part III of the Appendix are constituted as

areas for protecting, propagating, managing, controlling, regulating or enhancing

wildlife or its habitat.

### Road corridor game preserves

6 The road corridor game preserves described in Part IV of the Appendix are

constituted as areas for protecting, propagating, managing, controlling, regulating

or enhancing wildlife or wildlife habitat.

### Wildlife refuges

7 The wildlife refuges described in Part V of the Appendix are constituted as

areas for protecting, propagating, perpetuating, managing, controlling, regulating

or enhancing wildlife or its habitat.

7.1 Repealed. 11 Apr 2014 SR 15/2014 s4.

### c.W-13.1 Reg 25 repealed

8 The Wildlife Management Zones and Special Areas Boundaries Regulations are

repealed.
