# The Seniors’ Angling Licence Fee Remission Regulations

RRS c F-13.4 Reg 35

*Made under The Financial Administration Act, 1993*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Seniors’ Angling Licence Fee Remission

Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “angling licence” means an angling licence required pursuant to The

Fisheries Regulations;

(b) “2006-07 angling season” means:

(i) in the case of an angling licence for the southern and central zones,

the season ending on March 31, 2007;

(ii) in the case of an angling licence for the northern zone, the season

ending on April 15, 2007;

(c) “fee” means the fee prescribed in Table 1 of The Fisheries Regulations, as

that Table existed immediately before the coming into force of The Fisheries

Amendment Regulations, 2006, for an angling licence for the 2006-07 angling

season;

(d) “minister” means the member of the Executive Council to whom for the

time being the administration of The Fisheries Act (Saskatchewan), 1994 is

assigned;

(e) “remission” means a remission of a fee already paid for an angling licence

for the 2006-07 angling season;

(f) “senior” means a Saskatchewan resident who is 65 years of age or older.

### Remission

3 Every person who was a senior at the time he or she acquired an angling licence

for the 2006-07 angling season is granted a remission of the fee paid for the angling

licence by the senior.

### Condition

4 The remission granted pursuant to section 3 is granted on the condition that

the senior:

(a) apply for the remission to the minister on or before September 30, 2006;

and

(b) provide the minister with information to establish to the minister’s

satisfaction that the applicant:

(i) acquired his or her angling licence for the 2006-07 angling season

before the day on which The Fisheries Amendment Regulations, 2006

came into force or paid a fee for his or her angling licence for the 2006-07

angling season on or after the day on which The Fisheries Amendment

Regulations, 2006 came into force; and

(ii) is entitled to the remission.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
