RRS c S-64 Reg 6

*Made under The Superannuation (Supplementary Provisions) Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The New Careers Corporation Designation

Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Superannuation (Supplementary Provisions) Act;

(b) “New Careers Corporation” means the New Careers Corporation

established by Order in Council 598/84 pursuant to The Crown Corporations

Act, 1978.

### Designation

3 The New Careers Corporation is designated as an institution for the purposes

of section 16 of the Act.
