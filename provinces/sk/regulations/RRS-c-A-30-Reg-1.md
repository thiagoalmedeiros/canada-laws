RRS c A-30 Reg 1

*Made under The Assignment of Wages Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Assignment of Wages Regulations.

### Valid assignment

2 An employee may authorize deductions from his wages as mentioned in section

4 of The Assignment of Wages Act for the purpose of making payments due pursuant

to a maintenance order as defined in The Enforcement of Maintenance Orders Act.
