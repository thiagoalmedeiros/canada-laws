RRS c C-29.01 Reg 1

*Made under The Constitutional Questions Act, 2012*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Constitutional Questions Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Constitutional Questions Act, 2012; (« Loi »)

“Form” means a form set out in the Appendix. (« formule »)

### Application for court-appointed lawyer

3(1) An application made pursuant to section 15.3 of the Act for a court-appointed

lawyer must be:

(a) made in Form A; and

(b) accompanied by:

(i) a letter from Legal Aid Saskatchewan stating that the applicant is

not eligible for legal aid; or

(ii) a completed waiver in Form B.

(2) Subject to subsections 17(1) and (2) of the Act, notice of the application must

be served in one of the following manners:

(a) by ordinary mail;

(b) by registered mail;

(c) by facsimile transmission;

(d) by any other manner of electronic messaging that produces a written

record.

### Coming into force

4(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day on which

section 9 of The Queen’s Bench Amendment Act, 2016 comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day on

which section 9 of The Queen’s Bench Amendment Act, 2016 comes into force, these

regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the Registrar

of Regulations.
