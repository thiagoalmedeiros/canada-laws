# The Relief for Long-waiting Joint Replacement Patients Regulations

RRS c H-0.0001 Reg 1

*Made under The Health Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Relief for Long-waiting Joint Replacement

Patients Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Health Administration Act;

“arthroplasty” is an orthopedic surgical procedure to replace the joint of the

hip, knee or shoulder, performed to manage pain and improve mobility for

persons suffering from advanced arthritis or other conditions;

“clinical criteria” means the criteria established by an out-of-province

surgical facility, and approved by the minister, that the facility considers

necessary to ensure that persons selected for arthroplasty are physically fit

and can safely receive the surgery in the facility and be discharged within 24

hours after surgery;

“long-waiting patient” means a beneficiary who, as at April 1, 2023, has

been on the Saskatchewan Surgical Registry wait list for arthroplasty for 12

months or more;

“out-of-province surgical facility” means a health care facility in Canada

but outside Saskatchewan that has entered into an agreement with the minister

for the purpose of providing arthroplasty to persons designated pursuant to

section 3.

### Designation of eligible persons

3(1) Persons who meet the criteria set out in subsection (2) are designated pursuant

to clause 6.1(a) of the Act as persons for whom the minister may pay part or the

whole of the cost of the arthroplasty provided to those persons at out-of-province

surgical facilities.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), the person must:

(a) reside in Saskatchewan;

(b) be a long-waiting patient;

(c) meet the clinical criteria for the out-of-province surgical facility that

will provide the arthroplasty;

(d) be selected, assessed and recommended to the minister by the

Saskatchewan Health Authority as being eligible for arthroplasty at an

out-of-province surgical facility; and

(e) be accepted by the minister as being a person for whom part or the whole

of the cost of the arthroplasty provided to that person at an out-of-province

surgical facility will be paid by the minister.

### Agreements

4 The minister may enter into agreements with out-of-province surgical facilities

for the provision of arthroplasty pursuant to these regulations.

### Payments

5(1) Subject to subsection (2), the minister may make payment for arthroplasty

provided to persons described in section 3 at out-of-province surgical facilities

between April 1, 2023 and September 30, 2024.

(2) The total amount paid by the minister to all out-of-province surgical facilities

for arthroplasty pursuant to these regulations shall not exceed $10,000,000.

### Coming into force

6 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
