RRS c D-25.01 Reg 1

*Made under The Dependants’ Relief Act, 1996*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Dependants’ Relief Trust Fund

Regulations, 1997.

### Trust fund limit

2 A trust fund established pursuant to subsection 9(2) of The Dependants’ Relief

Act, 1996 is not to exceed $100,000.

### R.R.S. c.D-25 Reg 1 repealed

3 The Dependants’ Relief Trust Fund Regulations are repealed.
