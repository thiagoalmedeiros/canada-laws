RRS c L-11.1 Reg 2

*Made under The Legislative Assembly and Executive Council Act*

Order in Council 1875/81,dated December 15, 1981

His Honour the Lieutenant Governor in Council, onthe recommendation of

the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 58.1 of The Legislative Assembly

and Executive Council Act, makes regulations in accordance with the attached

Schedule, effective the date of this order.

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Members of the Legislative Assembly

Benefits Regulations.

### Schemes established

2 The plans or schemes established for the benefit of members in accordance with

section 58.1 of The Legislative Assembly and Executive Council Act are:

(a) the disability income plan for certain public employees in the province; and

(b) the group life insurance plan for certain public employees in the province.
