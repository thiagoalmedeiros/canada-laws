RRS c E-0.011 Reg 1

*Made under The Economic and Co-operative Development Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Information Technology Office Service

Regulations.

### Prescribed public agencies

2 For the purposes of clause 2(d) of The Economic and Co-operative Development

Act, the following are prescribed as prescribed public agencies:

(a) Saskatchewan Grain Car Corporation;

(b) Saskatchewan Municipal Board;

(c) Saskatchewan Housing Corporation;

(d) the Saskatchewan Apprenticeship and Trade Certification Commission;

(e) The Saskatchewan Legal Aid Commission;

(f) the Public Guardian and Trustee of Saskatchewan;

(g) Repealed. 21 Nov 2014 SR 91/2014 s2.

(h) the Financial and Consumer Affairs Authority of Saskatchewan;

(i) Technical Safety Authority of Saskatchewan;

(j) Global Transportation Hub Authority;

(k) Repealed. 9 Mar 2012 SR 9/2012 s3.

(l) the Crown Investments Corporation of Saskatchewan;

(m) the Liquor and Gaming Authority;

(n) the Water Security Agency;

(o) the Physician Recruitment Agency of Saskatchewan;

(p) SaskBuilds Corporation.

2.1 Repealed. 9 Mar 2012 SR 9/2012 s4.

### Coming into force

3 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
