RRS c H-6 Reg 1

*Made under The Horned Cattle Purchases Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Horned Cattle Purchases

Regulations, 1983.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Horned Cattle Purchases Act;

(b) “department” means the Department of Agriculture.

### Interpretation

3 For the purposes of the Act:

(a) “dehorned” means cattle with horns removed in such a manner as to

ensure that no more than 50 mm of horn or boney tissue projects beyond the

surface of the skull of the animal;

(b) “owner” means a person who owns one or more head of cattle.

### Returns

4(1) A return pursuant to section 4 of the Act for moneys required to be deducted

pursuant to section 3 or subsection 5(2) of the Act is to be in Form A of the Appendix.

(2) The form mentioned in subsection (1) is to be:

(a) prepared by the department on the basis of the information submitted on

livestock manifests pursuant to “The Livestock Inspection and Transportation

Regulations, 1978”; and

(b) issued to the dealer.

### Commissions

5(1) Every dealer is entitled to receive a commission of 3 per each head of cattle

with horns with respect to which he deducts or collects the amount to be deducted

pursuant to section 3 or subsection 5(2) of the Act.

(2) Every operator of an auction market in Alberta that is prescribed as an approved

inspection point in Alberta pursuant to subsection 6(2) is entitled to receive a

commission of 10¢ per each head of cattle with horns with respect to which the

operator deducts or collects the amount to be deducted pursuant to section 3 or

subsection 5(2) of the Act.

### Out-of-province inspection points

6(1) For the purposes of section 5 of the Act, the approved inspection points for

Manitoba are stockyards:

(a) with respect to which the department has negotiated agreements for the

collection of statutory deductions from Saskatchewan livestock owners; and

(b) where cattle are inspected prior to sale.

(2) For the purposes of section 5 of the Act, the approved inspection points for

Alberta are inspected auction markets licensed pursuant to The Livestock and

Livestock Products Act (Alberta) and The Licensing and Bonding of Livestock Dealers

and Livestock Dealers’ Agents Regulations made pursuant to that Act.

### Fiscal year of fund

7 The fiscal year for the administration of the Horned Cattle Fund is the period

commencing on April 1 in one year and ending on March 31 in the next year.

### Repeal

8 Saskatchewan Regulations 220/78 are repealed.
