RRS c E-0.2 Reg 30

*Made under The Education Act, 1995*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Education Emergency Pandemic Support

Program Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Education Act, 1995;

“applicant” means an applicant for a program payment;

“application” means an application for a program payment;

“approved” means approved by the minister;

“eligible expense” means an expense that the minister may approve pursuant

to section 7;

“historical high school” means a historical high school as defined in The Registered

Independent Schools Regulations;

“prior annual costs” means an applicant’s prior annual costs as assessed by the

minister pursuant to subsection 7(3);

“program” means the Education Emergency Pandemic Support Program

established pursuant to section 3;

“program payment” means a payment made to an approved applicant pursuant

to section 9 or 9.1;

“qualified independent school” means a qualified independent school as defined

in The Registered Independent Schools Regulations.

### Program established

3(1) The Education Emergency Pandemic Support Program is established.

(2) The purpose of the program is to provide financial assistance to eligible

applicants for eligible expenses incurred directly as a result of the COVID-19

pandemic.

(3) The following moneys are available for the program:

(a) $20 million from the Government of Saskatchewan for eligible expenses

incurred directly as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic;

(b) $10 million from the Government of Saskatchewan for eligible expenses

to enhance non-classroom learning options for pupils with weakened immune

systems;

(c) moneys allocated by the Government of Saskatchewan from the

Government of Canada for the purposes of the program.

### Eligible applicants

4 Only the following are eligible to apply for financial assistance pursuant to the

program:

(a) boards of education;

(b) the conseil scolaire;

(c) historical high schools;

(d) qualified independent schools;

(e) Cornwall Alternative School.

### Application

5(1) Every applicant for a program payment must apply to the minister in a form

and manner satisfactory to the minister.

(2) An application pursuant to subsection (1) must include evidence satisfactory to

the minister that the applicant has incurred or will incur eligible expenses directly

as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic.

(3) The minister may require an applicant to provide the minister with any

additional information that the minister may reasonably require to evaluate the

application and to ensure compliance with these regulations, including original

receipts for expenses claimed.

(4) No applicant shall supply any false or misleading information to the minister

on any application or in response to any request for information from the minister.

(5) An applicant shall provide the minister with any changes to the information

on any application as soon as possible after that information changes.

(6) An applicant shall allow the minister or any person designated by the minister

to inspect:

(a) any item that is being claimed by the applicant as an eligible expense; and

(b) any records that the minister considers necessary or relevant to evaluate

the application or to ensure compliance with these regulations.

### Application deadlines

6(1) An applicant may apply to the program for the 2020-2021 school year based

on planned eligible expenses to support the applicant’s school re-opening plan.

(2) Applicants may apply to the program during the 2020-2021 school year, within

the times specified by the minister, with respect to:

(a) eligible expenses for previously unanticipated items; and

(b) actual eligible expenses for staffing costs if actual costs have exceeded

the applicant’s prior annual costs for staffing.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), all applications must be received by the minister on

or before July 31, 2021.

(4) The minister may consider an application received after July 31, 2021 if the

minister is satisfied that extenuating circumstances made it unreasonable or

impossible for the application to have been received by July 31, 2021.

### Eligible expenses

7(1) For the purposes of the program, the minister may approve the following as

eligible expenses:

(a) expenses that the applicant has incurred or will incur directly as a result

of the COVID-19 pandemic, including:

(i) increased cleaning and sanitization supplies and equipment;

(ii) increased school transportation cleaning costs;

(iii) increased supplies and equipment for pupils for the purposes of

lowering shared usage among pupils;

(iv) actual substitute staffing costs that exceed the applicant’s prior

annual costs;

(v) additional temporary, short-term staff for exceptional circumstances;

(vi) any other expense for which the Government of Canada has provided

funding for the purposes of the program; and

(vii) any other expense that the minister considers acceptable for

pandemic safety purposes;

(b) expenses to enhance the applicant’s non-classroom learning options for

pupils with weakened immune systems, including:

(i) increased technology requirements that support non-classroom

learning;

(ii) additional temporary, short-term staff for exceptional circumstances;

(iii) any other expense for which the Government of Canada has provided

funding for the purposes of the program; and

(iv) any other expense that the minister considers acceptable to enhance

non-classroom learning options.

(2) The following are not to be approved as eligible expenses:

(a) costs that could be eligible to be paid from the applicant’s preventative

maintenance and renewal funding;

(b) any other expense that the minister determines is ineligible.

(3) The minister may consider the following factors in assessing an applicant’s

prior annual costs:

(a) the applicant’s actual expenses for the 2018-2019 school year;

(b) the applicant’s budgeted and forecasted expenses for the 2019-2020 school

year;

(c) adjustments to enrolment;

(d) adjustments related to the teacher salary increases in accordance with

the applicable teachers’ collective bargaining agreement;

(e) any other factor that the minister considers appropriate in the

circumstances.

### Approval

8(1) On receipt of an application pursuant to these regulations, the minister shall:

(a) review the application to verify that the applicant has met the requirements

of the program;

(b) determine which expenses claimed by the applicant are eligible expenses,

if any; and

(c) subject to subsection 3(3) and section 13, make a recommendation to the

Minister of Finance as to the amount of the program payment that an approved

applicant is eligible to receive.

(2) The minister may approve different levels of funding to different applicants

for approved eligible expenses based on:

(a) the number of COVID-19 cases and outbreaks within an applicant’s

geographical area; and

(b) any additional factors that the minister considers appropriate in order to

adapt to the changing COVID-19 environment and the differing levels of need

resulting from the COVID-19 pandemic.

(3) No program payment to an approved applicant shall exceed the total amount

of the applicant’s approved eligible expenses.

(4) If the minister rejects an application for failure to meet the program

requirements, the minister shall notify the applicant in writing.

### Program payment

9(1) Subject to subsection 3(3) and section 13, the Minister of Finance may make

a program payment to an approved applicant in the amount:

(a) recommended by the minister; and

(b) authorized by the Minister of Finance.

(2) The Minister of Finance may rely on the minister’s approval of the application

and the applicant’s eligible expenses for the purposes of certifying pursuant to The

Financial Administration Act, 1993 the lawfulness, propriety and accuracy of the

program payment and that the applicant is eligible for the program payment.

(3) Every program payment that is paid to an approved applicant pursuant to

these regulations is subject to the following terms and conditions:

(a) the approved applicant and its schools must be organized, operated and

maintained in accordance with the Act and the regulations;

(b) the approved applicant must comply with every term and condition that

may be imposed on the program payment by the Act, The Executive Government

Administration Act, these regulations or the minister;

(c) the approved applicant shall use the program payment only for the purpose

for which the program payment is made.

### Program payment for 2021-2022 school year

9.1(1) If there are moneys remaining in the program after moneys have been

allocated pursuant to the program for the 2020-2021 school year, every eligible

applicant that was approved for a program payment for the 2020-2021 school year

is eligible to receive a program payment for the 2021-2022 school year, for approved

eligible expenses, in the amount:

(a) recommended by the minister; and

(b) authorized by the Minister of Finance.

(2) The amount of an eligible applicant’s program payment for the 2021-2022

school year is to be calculated based on the eligible applicant’s proportionate share

of budgeted operating grants paid by the minister for the 2020-2021 school year.

(3) Unless requested by the minister, no further application is required for an

applicant to be eligible to receive a program payment for the 2021-2022 school year.

### Audit

10 Every approved applicant who receives a program payment pursuant to these

regulations shall provide, at the minister’s request and within the period specified

by the minister, any information or record that the minister may require to audit

the applicant’s financial affairs.

### Overpayment

11(1) The minister may declare all or any part of a program payment made to an

approved applicant pursuant to these regulations to be an overpayment if, in the

opinion of the minister:

(a) the applicant has knowingly made a false or misleading statement with

respect to a material fact on any form or in any information or record provided

to the minister pursuant to these regulations;

(b) the applicant has knowingly omitted to make a statement or to provide

any information or record to the minister pursuant to these regulations that

results in a statement with respect to a material fact being misleading; or

(c) the applicant has failed to comply with these regulations.

(2) If the minister declares all or any portion of a program payment to be an

overpayment, the amount of the overpayment is deemed to be a debt due and owing

to the Government of Saskatchewan and may be recovered from the approved

applicant in any manner authorized pursuant to The Financial Administration

Act, 1993 or in any other manner authorized by law.

### Powers re overpayments

12(1) The Minister of Finance, on the request of the minister, may collect any

overpayment declared by the minister pursuant to section 11.

(2) For the purposes of collecting overpayments, the Minister of Finance may

exercise any power conferred on the Minister of Finance pursuant to The Revenue

and Financial Services Act.

### Termination of program if moneys fully utilized

13(1) Notwithstanding any other provision of these regulations, if the minister

determines that moneys available for the purposes of the program have been fully

allocated, the minister may refuse to approve any application, and the applicant is

not eligible to receive any program payment with respect to that application.

(2) If the minister makes a determination pursuant to subsection (1), the minister

may cause the effective date of the determination:

(a) to be posted on the ministry’s website; and

(b) to be made public in any other manner that the minister considers

appropriate.

(3) The effective date of the determination may be an earlier date than the date

on which the minister makes public the effective date of the determination.

(4) The program is terminated on the effective date of the determination as made

public by the minister.

### Immunity

14(1) No action or proceeding lies or shall be commenced against the Government

of Saskatchewan, the Minister of Education, the Minister of Finance, any ministry

of the Government of Saskatchewan, or any officer or employee of any ministry, if

that person is acting pursuant to the authority of these regulations, for anything in

good faith done, caused, permitted or authorized to be done, attempted to be done

or omitted to be done, by that person or by any of those persons pursuant to or in

the exercise or supposed exercise of any power conferred by these regulations or in

the carrying out or supposed carrying out of any duty imposed by these regulations.

(2) The decisions or actions of any of the persons mentioned in subsection (1) are

final and conclusive and are not reviewable by any court of law or restrained by

any injunction, prohibition, mandamus, certiorari or other proceeding whatsoever.

### Coming into force

15 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
