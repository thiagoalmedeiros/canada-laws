RRS c T-14 Reg 2

*Made under The Time Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Time Act General Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Time Act;

(b) “court” means the Court of Queen’s Bench.

### Certain references in Act

3 For the purposes of the Act and of adapting the provisions of The Local

Government Election Act to the Act:

(a) a reference to a school division is deemed to be a reference to the school

division as it existed on December 31, 2005; and

(b) a reference to a board is deemed to be a reference to the board as it

currently exists.

### Retention of certain records

4 Unless ordered to do otherwise by a judge of the court, the Chief Electoral Officer

shall:

(a) retain for two months from the date of the vote the ballots and all other

papers used in connection with a vote conducted pursuant to the Act; and

(b) after the expiry of the two-month period mentioned in clause (a), destroy

the ballots and other papers.

### Sask. Reg. 188/66 repealed

5 Saskatchewan Regulations 188/66 are repealed.

### Coming into force

6 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
