RRS c H-3.1 Reg 2

*Made under The Highway Traffic Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Public Service Vehicle Identification

Regulations, 1987.

### Prescribed identification

2 For the purposes of section 21 of The Motor Carrier Act, a truck or power unit

registered as a public service vehicle is to display identification on each side of the

vehicle showing:

(a) in clear and easily read letters that are not less than 5 centimetres in

height, the name and address of the holder of the certificate of registration

for that vehicle;

(b) in a clear manner and not less than 15 centimetres in height, the registered

trade mark of the holder of the certificate of registration for that vehicle; or

(c) any lettering or logo that may be allowed by the board.
