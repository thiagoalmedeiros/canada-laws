RRS c E-13.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Justice Regulations, 2016.

### Ministry continued

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry of

Justice is continued.

### Objects and purposes

3 The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Justice are to provide the structure

wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities and functions of the Minister

of Justice and Attorney General may be exercised and carried out in relation to

the following:

(a) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan related to the office of the Minister of

Justice and Attorney General in accordance with The Justice and Attorney

General Act;

(b) to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers,

responsibilities and functions of Keeper of the Great Seal of Saskatchewan

may be exercised and carried out;

(c) to co-ordinate, develop, promote, implement and enforce policies

and programs of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to consumer

protection;

(d) to develop, promote, implement and enforce policies and standards

with respect to pension plans in Saskatchewan;

(e) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to licensing, inspecting and

regulating business corporations, non-profit corporations, co-operatives

and other businesses in Saskatchewan to achieve public policy objectives;

(f) to co-ordinate, develop, promote, implement and enforce policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to land surveys

and land titles and to the use of the Land Titles system to achieve public

policy objectives;

(g) to co-ordinate, develop, promote, implement and enforce policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to personal property

transactions and to the use of the Personal Property Registry system to

achieve public policy objectives;

(h) Repealed. 9 Feb 2018 SR 13/2018 s2.

(i) Repealed. 9 Feb 2018 SR 13/2018 s2.

(j) Repealed. 9 Feb 2018 SR 13/2018 s2.

### R.R.S. c.G-5.1 Reg 157 repealed

4 The Ministry of Justice Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with

the Registrar of Regulations.
