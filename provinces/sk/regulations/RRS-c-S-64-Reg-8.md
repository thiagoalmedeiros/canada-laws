RRS c S-64 Reg 8

*Made under The Superannuation (Supplementary Provisions) Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Tourism Authority Designation Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Superannuation (Supplementary Provisions) Act;

(b) “Tourism Authority” means the Tourism Authority established by The

Tourism Authority Act.

### Designation

3 The Tourism Authority is designated as an institution for the purposes of

section 16 of the Act.
