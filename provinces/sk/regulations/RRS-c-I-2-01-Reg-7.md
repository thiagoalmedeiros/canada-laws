RRS c I-2.01 Reg 7

*Made under The Income Tax Act, 2000*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Saskatchewan Low-income Tax Credit

Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Income Tax Act, 2000.

### Determination of C re subsection 39(6.3) of the Act

3 For the purposes of determining the variable C in the formula set out in

subsection 39(4) of the Act in accordance with subsection 39(6.3) of the Act for

the 2026 taxation year, the amount C is equal to 3.029% of the amount, if any, by

which the individual’s adjusted income for that taxation year exceeds the amount

D calculated in accordance with the following formula:

$460

D = $54,532 −

0.03029

.

### Coming into force

4 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with

the Registrar of Regulations.
