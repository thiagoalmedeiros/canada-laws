RRS c S-29 Reg 18

*Made under The Saskatchewan Medical Care Insurance Act*

## PART I

### Title and Interpretation

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Medical Care Insurance Peer Review

Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Saskatchewan Medical Care Insurance Act;

(b) “director” means an employee of the department who is designated by

the minister as the director of professional review.

## PART II

### Joint Medical Professional Review Committee

### Interpretation of Part

3 In this Part:

(a) “board” means the board of directors of the Saskatchewan Medical

Association;

(b) “chairman” means the chairman of the committee;

(c) “committee” means the joint medical professional review committee

established pursuant to subsection 49(1) of the Act;

(d) “council” means the council of The College of Physicians and Surgeons

of the Province of Saskatchewan.

### Membership

4(1) Subject to subsections (2) to (5) and section 5, the committee shall consist of

six members of whom:

(a) two members shall be appointed by the council;

(b) two members shall be appointed by the board; and

(c) two members shall be appointed by the minister, at least one of whom

shall be an employee of the department.

(2) No person is eligible to be appointed as a member of the committee unless

that person:

(a) is resident in Saskatchewan or in Lloydminster, Alberta;

(b) is a duly qualified medical practitioner; and

(c) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to clause (1)(a) or (b), is

currently practising medicine in Saskatchewan.

(3) An appointment of a member pursuant to clause (1)(a) or (b) is effective on the

day that written notice of the appointment by council or the board, as the case may

be, is received by the director.

(4) A member of the committee who ceases to meet the criteria for eligibility

prescribed in subsection (2) immediately ceases to be a member and his position is

deemed to be vacant.

(5) The members of the committee shall choose one of their number as chairman

and one as vice-chairman.

### Temporary members

5(1) From time to time, the committee may, by a unanimous vote of all members

of the committee who are present, appoint temporary members for the purpose of

investigating a matter referred to the committee.

(2) A member appointed pursuant to subsection (1) is entitled, with respect to

the purpose for which he is appointed, to all the rights and privileges of a member

appointed pursuant to subsection 4(1) other than the right to be chairman or vice-

chairman.

(3) Section 6 does not apply to a member appointed pursuant to subsection (1).

### Term of office

6(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), the term of office of a member of the

committee is to be determined at the time of appointment by the person or body

that appoints him.

(2) The term of office of a member of the committee shall not exceed three years

from the effective date of the member’s appointment.

(3) The term of office of each of the two members of the committee appointed by

the council, the board or the minister shall be set so that the expiry of the term of

one member is separated by at least 12 months from the expiry of the term of the

other member.

(4) Subject to subsection 6(4.1), a member of the committee holds office until his or

her successor is appointed and is eligible for reappointment, but no member shall

hold office for more than two consecutive terms.

(4.1) Where the board, the council and the minister unanimously agree that a

member’s continued presence on the committee is vital to the ongoing work of the

committee, that member may be appointed to a further term of up to 12 months

beyond the expiry of the member’s second consecutive term.

### Vacancy

7(1) A vacancy in the membership of the committee does not impair the ability of

the remaining members to fulfill their duties or exercise their powers.

(2) Where a vacancy in the membership of the committee occurs, the person or

body that appointed the person who ceased to be a member to create the vacancy

shall appoint another person as soon as it is practicable to do so.

### Quorum

8 A quorum consists of a majority of the members.

### Majority

9(1) Except for the making of an order pursuant to subsection 49.2(5) or (7) of the

Act, a decision of a majority of members present at a meeting constitutes a decision

of the committee respecting any matter brought before that meeting.

(2) In the case of the making of an order pursuant to subsection 49.2(5) or (7)

of the Act, a decision of the majority of members of the committee present at the

meeting constitutes a decision of the committee unless more than one of the members

present dissents.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), if there is a dissenting vote from two or more

members on a matter mentioned in subsection (2), the matter shall be returned to

the committee for further discussion and a new decision.

(4) No matter shall be returned to the committee pursuant to subsection (3) more

than once.

### Meetings

10 The committee shall meet at the call of:

(a) the chairman;

(b) in the absence of the chairman, the vice-chairman; or

(c) where there is no chairman or vice-chairman, the director.

### Remuneration

11(1) Each member of the committee is entitled to be paid any remuneration for

the member’s services and allowances for expenses necessarily incurred in attending

meetings and in otherwise attending to the affairs of the committee in the amounts

that the minister shall determine.

(2) In determining the amount of remuneration and allowances for the purposes

of subsection (1), the minister shall consider, with respect to each member:

(a) attendance at meetings;

(b) travel time;

(c) preparatory time; and

(d) the preparation and presentation by the member of reports for the

committee.

(3) The chairman is entitled to remuneration in addition to the remuneration

provided in subsection (1) in an amount that the minister shall determine.

(4) The chairman may request the minister to pay a special remuneration to

any member in recognition of extraordinary work by that member and, where the

minister considers it appropriate, he may pay that special remuneration.

### Report

12 Where the committee makes an order pursuant to section 49.2 or section 49.22

of the Act:

(a) the chairman shall forward a written report of the order and the

information on which it was based to the director; and

(b) the director shall forward a copy of the report to:

(i) the physician who is the subject of the order;

(ii) the council; and

(iii) the board;

and may submit a report to the Department of Justice of the Government of

Saskatchewan.

### Attendance

13 The director or his designate and one other department employee, one

employee of the council and one employee of the board may attend all meetings of

the committee.

### Clerical and other support

14(1) The minister may provide clerical and other support for the committee.

(2) Without restricting the generality of subsection (1), the minister may, at the

committee’s request, provide the services of a person to assist the committee during

the conduct of a hearing.

## PART III

### Joint Optometric Professional Review Committee

### Interpretation of Part

15 In this Part, “committee” means the joint optometric professional review

committee established pursuant to subsection 49(3) of the Act.

### Membership

16(1) Subject to subsections (2) to (5), the committee shall consist of not more than

five members of whom:

(a) not more than three members may be appointed by the council of the

Saskatchewan Association of Optometrists; and

(b) not more than two members may be appointed by the minister, at least

one of whom shall be an employee of the department.

(2) No person is eligible to be appointed as a member of the committee unless

that person is:

(a) resident in Saskatchewan; and

(b) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to clause (1)(a), registered

and licensed pursuant to The Optometry Act, 1985 and whose licence has not

been suspended or cancelled.

(3) An appointment of a member pursuant to clause (1)(a) is effective on the day

that the written notice of the council of the Saskatchewan Association of Optometrists

is received by the director.

(4) A member of the committee who ceases to meet the criteria for eligibility

prescribed in subsection (2) immediately ceases to be a member and his position is

deemed to be vacant.

(5) The members of the committee shall choose one of their number as chairman.

### Attendance at meetings

17 The director or his designate may attend all meetings of the committee.

### Term of office

18(1) Subject to subsections (2) to (5), the term of office of a member of the

committee is to be determined at the time of appointment by the person or body

that appoints him.

(2) The term of office of a member of the committee shall not exceed three years

from the effective date of the member’s appointment.

(3) Where the council of the Saskatchewan Association of Optometrists appoints

more than one member of the committee, the council shall set the term of office of

two of the members that it appoints so that the expiry of the term of one member is

separated by at least 12 months from the expiry of the term of the second member.

(4) Where the minister appoints two members of the committee, the minister shall

set the term of office of each of the two members so that the expiry of the term of one

member is separated by 12 months from the expiry of the term of the second member.

(5) A member of the committee holds office until his successor is appointed and

is eligible for reappointment, but no member shall hold office for more than two

consecutive terms.

### Decision

19 A decision of a majority of members of the committee present at a meeting

constitutes a decision of the committee respecting any matter brought before that

meeting.

### Application of certain sections

20 Subsection 7(1) and sections 8, 10 to 12 and 14 apply mutatis mutandis to the

committee.

## PART IV

### Joint Chiropractic Professional Review Committee

### Interpretation of Part

21 In this Part, “committee” means the joint chiropractic professional review

committee established pursuant to subsection 49(2) of the Act.

### Membership

22(1) Subject to subsections (2) to (5) and section 23, the committee shall consist

of not more than five members of whom:

(a) not more than three members may be appointed by the board of The

Chiropractors’ Association of Saskatchewan; and

(b) not more than two members may be appointed by the minister, at least

one of whom shall be an employee of the department.

(2) No person is eligible to be appointed as a member of the committee unless

that person:

(a) is resident in Saskatchewan; and

(b) in the case of a member appointed pursuant to clause (1)(a), is registered

as a chiropractor pursuant to The Chiropractic Act and that registration has

not been suspended or cancelled.

(3) An appointment of a member pursuant to clause (1)(a) is effective on the day that

the written notice of the board of The Chiropractors’ Association of Saskatchewan

is received by the director.

(4) A member of the committee who ceases to meet the criteria for eligibility

prescribed in subsection (2) immediately ceases to be a member and his position is

deemed to be vacant.

(5) The members of the committee shall choose one of the members appointed

pursuant to clause (1)(a) as chairman.

### Temporary members

23(1) From time to time, the committee may, by a unanimous vote of all members

of the committee who are present, appoint temporary members for the purpose of

investigating a matter referred to the committee.

(2) A member appointed pursuant to subsection (1) is entitled, with respect to

the purpose for which he is appointed, to all the rights and privileges of a member

appointed pursuant to subsection 22(1) other than the right to be chairman.

(3) Section 25 does not apply to a member appointed pursuant to subsection (1).

### Attendance at meetings

24 The director or his designate may attend all meetings of the committee.

### Term of office

25(1) Subject to subsections (2) to (5), the term of office of a member of the

committee is to be determined at the time of appointment by the person or body

that appoints him.

(2) The term of office of a member of the committee shall not exceed three years

from the effective date of the member’s appointment.

(3) Where the board of The Chiropractors’ Association of Saskatchewan appoints

more than one member of the committee, the board shall set the term of two of the

members that it appoints so that the expiry of the term of one member is separated

by at least 12 months from the expiry of the term of the second member.

(4) Where the minister appoints two members of the committee, the minister shall

set the term of office of each of the two members so that the expiry of the term of

one member is separated by at least 12 months from the expiry of the term of the

second member.

(5) A member of the committee holds office until his successor is appointed and

is eligible for reappointment, but no member shall hold office for more than two

consecutive terms.

### Decision

26 A decision of a majority of members of the committee present at a meeting

constitutes a decision of the committee respecting any matter brought before that

meeting.

### Application of certain sections

27 Subsection 7(1) and sections 8, 10 to 12 and 14 apply mutatis mutandis to the

committee.
