RRS c I-13.2 Reg 1

*Made under The Intestate Succession Act, 2019*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Intestate Succession Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Intestate Succession Act, 2019.

### Prescribed amounts

3(1) For the purposes of section 6 of the Act, if the net value of the estate does not

exceed $200,000, the entirety of the estate shall be distributed to the spouse.

(2) For the purposes of subclause 6(1)(b)(i) of the Act, if the net value of the estate

exceeds $200,000, the spouse is entitled to the greater of the following amounts:

(a) $200,000;

(b) ½ of the net value of the estate.

### Coming into force

4(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day on which

section 1 of The Intestate Succession Act, 2019 comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day

on which section 1 of The Intestate Succession Act, 2019 comes into force, these

regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the Registrar

of Regulations.
