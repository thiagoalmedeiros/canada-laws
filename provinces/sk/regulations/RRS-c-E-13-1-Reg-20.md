RRS c E-13.1 Reg 20

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Health Regulations, 2020.

### Ministry continued

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry of Health

is continued.

### Objects and purposes

3 The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Health are the following:

(a) to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities

and functions of the Minister of Health and the Minister of Mental Health

and Addictions, Seniors and Rural and Remote Health may be exercised and

carried out;

(b) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to the office of the Minister of

Health in accordance with The Health Administration Act;

(c) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan related to the following:

(i) health services in Saskatchewan;

(ii) medical services and medical education programs;

(iii) early childhood development;

(iv) drug plan and extended benefits;

(v) public health research;

(vi) training of health personnel;

(vii) health systems and health technology;

(d) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan related to the following:

(i) health promotion;

(ii) substance abuse and addictions;

(iii) healthy eating;

(iv) mental health;

(e) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement social, cultural, economic

and other policies and programs for the benefit of Saskatchewan seniors.

### RRS G-5.1 Reg 139 repealed

4 The Ministry of Health Regulations, 2007 are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
