RRS c E-7.3 Reg 2

*Made under The Emergency 911 System Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Public Safety Answering Point

Regulations, 2011.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “area of coverage” means an area served by a public safety answering

point by receiving emergency 911 telephone calls placed by connected customers;

(b) “connected customer” means a connected customer as defined in The

Sask911 Fees Regulations, 2003.

### Public safety answering points

3 The following are prescribed as public safety answering points:

(a) Repealed. 8 May 2015 SR 42/2015 s3.

(b) Regina Public Safety Answering Point;

(c) Saskatoon Public Safety Answering Point;

(d) Prince Albert Public Safety Answering Point.

4 Repealed. 8 May 2015 SR 42/2015 s4.

### Regina Public Safety Answering Point

5 The area of coverage of the Regina Public Safety Answering Point is the area

that lies within the boundaries of the City of Regina.

### Saskatoon Public Safety Answering Point

6 The area of coverage of the Saskatoon Public Safety Answering Point is the area

that lies within the boundaries of the City of Saskatoon.

### Prince Albert Public Safety Answering Point

7 The area of coverage of the Prince Albert Public Safety Answering Point is all

areas within the boundaries of Saskatchewan that are not included in the areas

mentioned in sections 5 and 6.

### R.R.S. c.E-7.3 Reg 1 repealed

8 The Public Safety Answering Point Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

9 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
