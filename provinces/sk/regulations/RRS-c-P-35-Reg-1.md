RRS c P-35 Reg 1

*Made under The Provincial Secretary’s Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Provincial Secretary’s Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Provincial Secretary’s Act.

### Fees

3 The fee payable pursuant to the Act is $50 for:

(a) a certificate authenticating the appointment, or the appointment and

signature, of any notary public or other official whose commission, letters

patent, licence or other instrument or document effecting his or her appointment

is or is required to be in the possession of the Provincial Secretary;

(b) a certified copy of an instrument or document; or

(c) a certified extract from a register.
