RRS c R-22.01 Reg 1

*Made under The Revenue and Financial Services Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Public Employees Benefits Agency

Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “superannuation Act” means an Act or part of an Act

listed in Table 1 of the Appendix.

### Designation of plans

3 Every superannuation plan established under a superannuation Act and the

group life insurance plan, the disability income plan and the dental plan established

by the Government of Saskatchewan for employees of a public agency are designated

as programs to be administered by the Public Employees Benefits Agency pursuant

to subsection 31(2) of The Department of Revenue and Financial Services Act.
