RRS c R-8.2 Reg 7

*Made under The Regional Health Services Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Health Centres (Hospital Standards

Adoption) Regulations.

### Standards applicable to health centres

2(1) The following provisions of The Hospital Standards Regulations, 1980, being

Saskatchewan Regulations 331/79, are adopted, as amended from time to time

and with any necessary modification, for the purpose of governing the operations

of facilities that are health centres within the meaning of The Facility Designation

Regulations:

(a) Repealed. 3 Jne 2011 SR 26/2011 s2.

(b) section 12;

(c) section 13;

(d) section 14;

(e) section 15;

(f) Repealed. 3 Jne 2011 SR 26/2011 s2.

(g) section 17;

(h) section 18;

(i) section 22;

(j) section 34;

(k) section 53;

(l) Repealed. 3 Jne 2011 SR 26/2011 s2.

(m) Repealed. 3 Jne 2011 SR 26/2011 s2.

(2) If a term defined in section 1 of The Hospital Standards Regulations, 1980, being

Saskatchewan Regulations 331/79, is used in any of the provisions adopted pursuant

to subsection (1), that definition is adopted, with any necessary modification, for the

purpose of applying the adopted provision to health centres.

### Coming into force

3 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
