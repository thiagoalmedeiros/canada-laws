# The Electronic Equipment Stewardship Program Regulations

RRS c E-10.22 Reg 10

*Made under The Environmental Management and Protection Act, 2010*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Electronic Equipment Stewardship

Program Regulations.

### Definitions and interpretation

2(1) In these regulations:

“Act” means The Environmental Management and Protection Act, 2010;

“approval” means an approval of a product stewardship program by the

minister pursuant to section 7;

“brand owner” means the person who is the owner or licensee of intellectual

property rights or a trademark with respect to electronic equipment that is

supplied or distributed into or in Saskatchewan, whether or not, in the case

of a trademark, the trademark is registered or is used in association with or

marked on the electronic equipment;

“electronic equipment” means, subject to subsection (2), any new product

listed in Table 1 of the Appendix and includes all embedded components;

“marketplace” means a physical or electronic entity that supplies electronic

equipment;

“marketplace facilitator” means a person, whether or not that person

receives consideration in exchange for services, who does all or any of the

following:

(a) makes or facilitates a marketplace for retail sales by marketplace

sellers;

(b) directly or indirectly collects payment from a consumer or user and

remits payment to a marketplace seller;

“marketplace seller” means a person who supplies and sells electronic

equipment through a marketplace operated, owned or controlled by the person

or a marketplace facilitator;

“producer” means the person who is determined to be the producer in

accordance with section 3;

“producer responsibility organization” means a person who operates a

product stewardship program on behalf of producers;

“product stewardship program” means a program for the collection and

recycling of electronic equipment;

“recycle” means to collect electronic equipment under a product stewardship

program for the purpose of selling that equipment as a commodity that will be

reprocessed into raw materials for use as inputs into new products;

“trademark” means a trademark as defined in the Trademarks Act (Canada).

(2) For the purposes of the definition of “electronic equipment” in subsection (1),

electronic equipment does not include a product listed in Table 1 of the Appendix

if the product:

(a) is powered by gas, alternative fuel or compressed air or liquid and does

not require electrical or battery power;

(b) is a large-scale stationary industrial tool not designed to be moved;

(c) requires professional installation;

(d) cannot readily and safely be deinstalled, transported or recycled;

(e) contains mercury;

(f) has been implanted in a person;

(g) requires decontamination before recycling;

(h) has come into contact with blood or other biological contaminants; or

(i) is an air treatment device containing refrigerant.

### Determination of producer

3(1) Subject to subsection (2), the producer is:

(a) if the person is a resident of Canada, a manufacturer, distributor or

brand owner of electronic equipment that is sold, offered for sale or otherwise

distributed into or in Saskatchewan;

(b) if there is no person as described in clause (a), the person who first imports

the electronic equipment for distribution or resale into or in Saskatchewan;

(c) if there is no person as described in clause (a) or (b), the retailer who is

selling the electronic equipment for use in Saskatchewan; or

(d) if there is no person as described in clauses (a) to (c), any person

who assembles electronic equipment from parts that were imported into

Saskatchewan for resale or supply.

(2) In the case of a business operated wholly or in part as a franchise, the producer

is the franchisor if that franchisor has franchisees that are resident in Saskatchewan.

(3) If the producer determined in accordance with subsection (1) is a retailer and

that retailer is a marketplace, the producer is:

(a) the marketplace seller who supplies electronic equipment into or in

Saskatchewan; or

(b) if there is no person as described in clause (a), the producer is the

marketplace facilitator who supplies electronic equipment into or in

Saskatchewan.

### Prescribed product

4 Electronic equipment is a prescribed product for the purposes of section 46 of

the Act.

### Guidelines

5(1) For the purposes of approving a product stewardship program pursuant to

these regulations, the minister may establish guidelines respecting any additional

terms and conditions that a producer or producer responsibility organization must

comply with.

(2) If the minister establishes guidelines pursuant to subsection (1), the minister

shall cause the guidelines to be made available to the public in any form or manner

that the minister considers appropriate, including publishing them on the ministry’s

website.

### Product stewardship program required

6(1) No producer shall fail to:

(a) operate a product stewardship program approved by the minister; or

(b) enter into an agreement with a producer responsibility organization to

operate a product stewardship program approved by the minister.

(2) No producer responsibility organization shall fail to operate a product

stewardship program in accordance with:

(a) the program as approved by the minister; and

(b) these regulations.

(3) No producer who has entered into an agreement with a producer responsibility

organization shall fail to ensure that the product stewardship program is operated

in accordance with:

(a) the program as approved by the minister; and

(b) these regulations.

### Approval to operate product stewardship program

7(1) A producer or producer responsibility organization that operates or intends

to operate a product stewardship program shall:

(a) apply to the minister for approval of that program in the form and manner

acceptable to the minister;

(b) comply with any requirements set out in the guidelines established by

the minister pursuant to section 5;

(c) submit an implementation schedule for that program; and

(d) submit any additional information or material to the minister that the

minster requests and considers relevant to the application.

(2) The minister shall not approve a product stewardship program unless the

product stewardship program contains details that are satisfactory to the minister

respecting all of the following:

(a) the management structure of the program;

(b) the composition of the board of directors of the program;

(c) the creation of an advisory committee to the producer or producer

responsibility organization;

(d) the role of the advisory committee in relation to the implementation and

operation of the program;

(e) the manner in which Saskatchewan interests will be represented on the

advisory committee;

(f) the public education or public awareness and communication strategy for

the program;

(g) the manner in which financial disputes related to the program will be

resolved;

(h) the manner in which electronic equipment will be collected in all areas

of Saskatchewan;

(i) the policies and procedures to be followed by any person collecting or

processing electronic equipment pursuant to the program;

(j) how the product stewardship program will be funded and how funds under

the program will be expended;

(k) the quality control and quality assurance aspects of the program, including

the mechanisms to collect, track and audit information for the purposes of the

program;

(l) the management of contracts with collectors, processors and other third-

party contractors, including the policies and procedures to be followed to

ensure that all collectors, processors and other contracted parties comply with

all applicable laws;

(m) how program effectiveness, efficiency, accessibility and participation

rates and resident feedback will be determined and measured;

(n) the manner in which electronic equipment will be managed by the

program in accordance with the order of preference in the waste management

hierarchy set out in the guidelines established by the minister pursuant to

section 5, including providing evidence satisfactory to the minister that the

management options chosen will minimize the impact of electronic equipment

on the environment;

(o) a schedule of targets set by the producer or the producer responsibility

organization operating the program, as the case may be, to be achieved each

year for the product stewardship program period that is satisfactory to the

minister, for each of the following performance indicators:

(i) public awareness of the availability and functioning of the program;

(ii) percentage of the population of Saskatchewan with access to the

program;

(iii) any other performance indicators proposed by the producer or

producer responsibility organization;

(p) a description of how the schedule of targets mentioned in clause (o) was

determined;

(q) the manner in which the program will manage greenhouse gas emissions

related to the collection and recycling of electronic equipment;

(r) any collaborative initiatives undertaken or to be undertaken with other

product stewardship programs approved pursuant to these regulations or

any other regulations made pursuant to section 46 of the Act for the purposes

of achieving public convenience and greater product stewardship program

effectiveness and efficiency;

(s) any other matter the minister considers appropriate.

(3) If the minister is satisfied that a proposed product stewardship program

complies with the Act and these regulations and that it is in the public interest to

do so, the minister may:

(a) approve the project stewardship program; and

(b) impose any terms and conditions on the approval that the minister

considers appropriate.

(4) The minister shall cause notice of any approval pursuant to clause (3)(a) to be

made public in any manner the minister considers appropriate, including publishing

it on the ministry’s website.

### Review of product stewardship program

8 Every 5 years after the date of approval of a product stewardship program

pursuant to clause 7(3)(a), the producer or producer responsibility organization that

operates the product stewardship program shall:

(a) review the approved terms and conditions of the program;

(b) submit the results of the review to the minister in the form and manner

and within the period specified by the minister; and

(c) either:

(i) request approval in writing from the minister of any proposed

amendments to the program; or

(ii) notify the minister, in writing, if no amendments to the program

are proposed.

### Changes to product stewardship program

9 No producer or producer responsibility organization operating a product

stewardship program shall make any changes to the program without obtaining

the minister’s prior written approval of the proposed changes.

### Amendment, suspension or cancellation of approvals

10(1) Subject to subsection (2), if, in the minister’s opinion, a product stewardship

program is not being operated in accordance with the Act, these regulations or the

terms and conditions of the approval, or if, in the minister’s opinion, it is in the

public interest to do so, the minister may:

(a) amend the approval by imposing new or additional terms and conditions;

(b) suspend the approval; or

(c) cancel the approval.

(2) Before amending, suspending or cancelling an approval pursuant to

subsection (1), the minister shall provide the producer or producer responsibility

organization operating the product stewardship program with:

(a) written notice of the minister’s intended action together with reasons for

that intended action; and

(b) an opportunity to make written representations to the minister,

within 30 days after the written notice mentioned in clause (a) is served, with

respect to why the intended action should not be taken.

(3) The minister is not required to give an oral hearing to any producer or

producer responsibility organization to whom a notice has been provided pursuant

to subsection (2).

(4) After considering the representations made pursuant to subsection (2), the

minister shall issue a written decision and shall serve a copy of the decision together

with reasons on the producer or producer responsibility organization operating the

product stewardship program.

(5) Notwithstanding subsection (2), if the minister considers that it is necessary in

order to protect the public interest, the minister may immediately amend, suspend

or cancel an approval without giving the producer or producer responsibility

organization mentioned in subsection (2) an opportunity to make written

representations, but the minister shall give the producer or producer responsibility

organization an opportunity to make written representations within 15 days after

the date on which the minister takes any of those actions.

### Annual reporting

11(1) In this section, “reporting period” means:

(a) the period commencing on January 1 and ending on December 31 of that

same year; or

(b) if a product stewardship program has not been operated for the period

set out in clause (a), the period commencing on the date on which the minister

approved the program and ending on December 31 of that same year.

(2) On or before June 30 in each year, every producer or producer responsibility

organization that operates a product stewardship program shall prepare and submit

to the minister a written annual report that describes the activities of the product

stewardship program during the previous reporting period.

(3) A written annual report mentioned in subsection (2) must be in a form

satisfactory to the minister and include:

(a) the amount of electronic equipment:

(i) sold or supplied into or in Saskatchewan by the producer on whose

behalf the program is being operated;

(ii) collected and sent to a sorting facility for diversion;

(iii) recycled;

(iv) recovered for energy; and

(v) disposed of in a landfill;

(b) the number and location of collection services by service type and any

changes from the previous reporting period;

(c) the performance indicators set out in the product stewardship program,

including:

(i) the level of public awareness of the availability and functioning of

the product stewardship program;

(ii) the percentage of the population with access to a collection location;

and

(iii) any other performance indicators proposed by the producer or

producer responsibility organization;

(d) a comparison of the performance for the year against the performance

indicator targets set out in the product stewardship program;

(e) a description of educational materials and educational strategies used to

advertise the product stewardship program and educate the Saskatchewan

public;

(f) a description of the efforts taken by or on behalf of the producers to reduce

environmental impacts throughout the product life cycle and to increase

reusability or recyclability at the end of the product life cycle;

(g) the independently audited financial statements detailing:

(i) the fees charged to producers participating in the product stewardship

program for each product of electronic equipment, including an explanation

of any significant changes to those fees;

(ii) the total amount of fees collected from producers who are participating

in the product stewardship program to fund the program;

(iii) the amount spent to operate the product stewardship program in

Saskatchewan;

(iv) the amount paid out in recycling incentives, if any;

(v) the costs incurred to administer the product stewardship program;

and

(vi) the amount spent on public education or public awareness and

communication in Saskatchewan; and

(h) any other information that the minister may reasonably require.

### RRS c E-10.22 Reg 6 repealed

12 The Electronic Equipment Stewardship Regulations are repealed.

### Transitional

13(1) In this section, “former regulations” means The Electronic Equipment

Stewardship Regulations, as those regulations existed on the day before the coming

into force of these regulations.

(2) Subject to subsections (3) and (4) but notwithstanding any other provision of

these regulations, if a product stewardship program was approved pursuant to the

former regulations and the approval for that product stewardship program is valid

and not under suspension or cancellation on the day on which these regulations

come into force:

(a) the product stewardship program is deemed to be an approved product

stewardship program for the purposes of these regulations until the minister

receives an application pursuant to subsection (3) and approves the product

stewardship program that is the subject of that application pursuant to these

regulations; and

(b) the product stewardship program may be dealt with pursuant to these

regulations as if approved pursuant to these regulations but is subject to

the same terms and conditions that were imposed pursuant to the former

regulations.

(3) Any producer operating a product stewardship program mentioned in

subsection (2) or any producer responsibility organization operating a product

stewardship program mentioned in that subsection on a producer’s behalf shall

apply to the minister for approval of a product stewardship program pursuant to

subsection 7(1) within 180 days after the day on which these regulations come into

force.

(4) Any producer required to operate a product stewardship program or any

producer responsibility organization that has entered into an agreement to operate

a product stewardship program on a producer’s behalf pursuant to the former

regulations shall, if the product stewardship program has not been approved by the

minister pursuant to the former regulations before the coming into force of these

regulations, apply to the minister for approval of a product stewardship program

pursuant to subsection 7(1) within 180 days after the day on which these regulations

come into force.

### Coming into force

14 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
