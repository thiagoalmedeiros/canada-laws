RRS c S-50 Reg 1

*Made under The Slot Machine Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Slot Machine Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “peace officer” means a peace officer as defined in The

Summary Offences Procedure Act.

### Seizure and taking possession of slot machines

3 A peace officer may at any time with a warrant seize or otherwise take possession

of a slot machine found within Saskatchewan.

### Warrant

4 Where a justice of the peace is satisfied by information upon oath of a peace

officer that there are reasonable grounds for believing that a slot machine is on

any premises, he may at any time issue a warrant under his hand authorizing the

peace officer to enter and search the premises and, for that purpose, to break open

the door, lock or fastening and to seize the slot machine.

### Disposing of slot machines

5 The Attorney General shall determine the disposition of any slot machine seized

or otherwise taken or received by any person on behalf of the Crown in right of

Saskatchewan.

### Repeal

6 Order in Council 1300/43 as published in the Gazette dated December 7, 1943

is repealed.
