RRS c C-45.3 Reg 1

*Made under The Credit Union Central of Saskatchewan Act, 2016*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Credit Union Central of Saskatchewan

Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Credit Union Central of Saskatchewan

Act, 2016.

### Fees

3 For the purposes of clause 18-1(a) of the Act, CUDGC must pay the registrar

a fee of $25,000 by June 30 of each year.

### Coming into force

4(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on April 1, 2017.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after

April 1, 2017, these regulations come into force on the day on which they are

filed with the Registrar of Regulations.
