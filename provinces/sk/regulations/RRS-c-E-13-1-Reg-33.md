RRS c E-13.1 Reg 33

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Community Safety Regulations.

### Ministry continued

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry

of Corrections, Policing and Public Safety is continued as the Ministry of

Community Safety.

### Objects and purposes

3 The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Community Safety are to provide

the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities and functions of

the Minister of Community Safety may be exercised and carried out in relation to

the following:

(a) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan relating to the custody, detention, support

and rehabilitation of young offenders and adult offenders;

(b) to plan, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of the

Government of Saskatchewan related to policing;

(c) to plan, develop, furnish, equip, administer, manage, operate, maintain

and repair facilities for the care or treatment of persons to whom section 36

of The Summary Offences Procedure Act, 1990 or subsection 30(1) or 42(2) of

the Youth Criminal Justice Act (Canada) applies;

(d) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement public safety policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to:

(i) emergency planning, coordination and services;

(ii) disaster assistance; and

(iii) fire commissioner services;

(e) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement initiatives, policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to community safety.

### RRS c E-13.1 Reg 23 repealed

4 The Ministry of Corrections, Policing and Public Safety Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with

the Registrar of Regulations.
