Sask Reg 98/2023

*Made under The Saskatchewan Firearms Act*

## PART 1

### Preliminary Matters

### Title

1-1 These regulations may be cited as The Saskatchewan Firearms Regulations.

### Definitions

1-2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Saskatchewan Firearms Act;

“significant shareholder” means an individual who, directly or indirectly,

owns 25% or more of a corporation’s outstanding shares.

## PART 2

### Regulation of Firearms and Related Activities

### Non-application of section 3-3 of the Act

2-1 Subsection 3-3(1) of the Act does not apply to a seizure agent who is licensed

pursuant to Part 4 of the Act, in the course of the seizure agent’s duties as a seizure

agent.

### Prescribed form re alternative penalties

2-2 An order pursuant to section 3-18 of the Act is to be in Form A of the Appendix.

2-3 Repealed. 1 May 2026 SR 25/2026 s3.

### Medical professionals

2-4 The following categories of medical professionals are prescribed for the purposes

of section 3-21 of the Act:

(a) individuals who hold a valid licence to practise issued by one of the

following regulatory bodies:

(i) the Saskatchewan College of Psychologists;

(ii) the Saskatchewan Association of Social Workers;

(iii) the College of Registered Nurses of Saskatchewan;

(iv) the College of Registered Psychiatric Nurses of Saskatchewan;

(v) the College of Licensed Practical Nurses of Saskatchewan;

(vi) the Saskatchewan Association of Optometrists;

(b) individuals who provide counselling services or therapy services but who

are not mentioned in clause (a).

### Designations re section 117.08 of the Criminal Code

2-5(1) For the purposes of subsection 2-2(5) of the Act, a designation by the

commissioner may only be with respect to the following:

(a) possession of a firearm, a prohibited device or prohibited ammunition

pursuant to paragraph 117.08(a) of the Criminal Code;

(b) alteration of a serial number on a firearm pursuant to paragraph 117.08(g)

of the Criminal Code, but only for the purpose of conducting forensic and

ballistic training in relation to the activities of the commissioner.

(2) A designation by the commissioner pursuant to subsection 2-2(5) of the Act

may contain any terms or conditions that the commissioner considers appropriate.

## PART 3

### Seizure Agents

### Definition of specified law

3-1 For the purposes of sections 4-1, 5-1 and 6-1 of the Act, the definition of

“specified law” includes section 4.1 of the Conditions of Transferring Firearms and

Other Weapons Regulations (Canada).

### Application for licence

3-2(1) For the purposes of section 4-5 of the Act, an application for a licence is to

include:

(a) a fee of $150;

(b) confirmation of the following:

(i) the applicant has obtained an insurance policy in accordance with

section 4-14 and subsection 4-22(2) of the Act;

(ii) if the licence is with respect to an individual, the individual holds

a valid licence issued by the chief firearms officer in accordance with

clause 4-15(a) of the Act;

(iii) if the licence is with respect to a person other than an individual,

the person holds a valid licence or licences issued by the chief firearms

officer in accordance with clause 4-15(b) of the Act;

(iv) the person to be licensed has successfully completed training in

accordance with section 4-16 of the Act; and

(c) if the licence is with respect to:

(i) a person other than an individual, or a sole proprietor who is an

individual:

(A) a current list of all the applicant’s employees; and

(B) a copy of a criminal record check, dated not more than 30 days

before the date on which the application is submitted, with respect to:

(I) if the applicant is a sole proprietor who is an individual,

the individual;

(II) if the applicant is a sole proprietor that is a corporation,

each significant shareholder, director and officer of the

corporation;

(III) if the applicant is a corporation, each significant

shareholder, director and officer of the corporation;

(IV) if the applicant is a partnership, each partner of the

partnership who is an individual; or

(V) if the applicant is a co-operative, each director of the

co- operative; or

(ii) an individual:

(A) a recent photograph of the person to be licensed; and

(B) a copy of a criminal record check of the person to be licensed

dated not more than 30 days before the date on which the application

is submitted.

### No refund

3-3 Application fees mentioned in section 3-2 are non-refundable.

## PART 4

### Compensation and Testing re Firearms

### Firearm amnesty programs

4-1(1) In this section, “firearm amnesty program” means a program operated

by a police service or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police that allows a person to

voluntarily surrender firearms to the police service or the Royal Canadian Mounted

Police.

(2) A police service or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police must obtain the approval

of the commissioner before operating a firearm amnesty program.

(3) The commissioner may approve a firearm amnesty program pursuant to

subsection (2) on any terms and conditions the commissioner considers appropriate,

including terms and conditions respecting:

(a) advertising and communications respecting the firearm amnesty program;

(b) the collection of firearms by the police service or the Royal Canadian

Mounted Police; and

(c) the destruction or deactivation of firearms by the police service or the

Royal Canadian Mounted Police.

(4) If a police service or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police takes possession of

a firearm pursuant to a firearm amnesty program, the police service or the Royal

Canadian Mounted Police must, before destroying or deactivating the firearm, offer

the firearm to the commissioner for:

(a) destruction;

(b) any scientific, research or educational purpose;

(c) any forensic or ballistic testing or tracing of the firearm to determine if

the firearm may have been used in the commission of an offence pursuant

to any Act, Act of the Parliament of Canada or regulation made pursuant to

any Act or Act of the Parliament of Canada, including preserving evidence of

any potential offence pursuant to any Act, Act of the Parliament of Canada or

regulation made pursuant to any Act or Act of the Parliament of Canada; or

(d) preservation as a historical firearm.

(5) Parts 4 and 5 of the Act do not apply in the case where a police service or the

Royal Canadian Mounted Police takes possession of a firearm pursuant to a firearm

amnesty program that is conducted in accordance with this section.

## PART 5

### Coming into Force

### Coming into force

5-1(1) Subject to subsections (2) to (4), these regulations come into force on the day

on which section 5-2 of The Saskatchewan Firearms Act comes into force.

(2) Subject to subsections (3) and (4), if these regulations are filed with the Registrar

of Regulations after the day on which section 5-2 of The Saskatchewan Firearms

Act comes into force, these regulations come into force on the day on which they are

filed with the Registrar of Regulations.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), section 2-2 and the Appendix come into force on the

day on which section 3-18 of The Saskatchewan Firearms Act comes into force.

(4) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day on

which section 3 18 of The Saskatchewan Firearms Act comes into force, section 2-2

and the Appendix come into force on the day on which these regulations are filed

with the Registrar of Regulations.
