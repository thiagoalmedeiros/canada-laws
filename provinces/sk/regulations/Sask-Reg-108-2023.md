Sask Reg 108/2023

*Made under The Accessible Saskatchewan Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Accessible Saskatchewan Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Accessible Saskatchewan Act.

### Public sector bodies

3 The bodies listed in Table 1 of the Appendix are prescribed public sector bodies

for the purposes of the Act.

### Coming into force

4(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day on which

section 1-1 of The Accessible Saskatchewan Act comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day

on which section 1-1 of The Accessible Saskatchewan Act comes into force, these

regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the Registrar

of Regulations.
