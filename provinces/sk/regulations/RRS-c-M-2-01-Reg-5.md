*Made under The Management and Reduction of Greenhouse Gases Act*

## PART 1

### Preliminary Matters

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Management and Reduction of Greenhouse

Gases (Reporting and General) Regulations, 2025.

### Definitions and interpretation

2(1) In these regulations:

“Act” means The Management and Reduction of Greenhouse Gases Act;

“annual report” means a report that includes:

(a) the quantified greenhouse gas emissions from a regulated facility

for a particular year; and

(b) any other information specified in each applicable standard;

“facility” means:

(a) all buildings, units, equipment, structures, on-site transportation

machinery and stationary items that:

(i) are located on a single site, on multiple sites or between multiple

sites that are owned or operated by the same person; and

(ii) function as a single integrated site; or

(b) a pipeline transportation system;

but does not include public roads;

“regulated facility” means, unless otherwise specified, a facility owned or

operated by a regulated emitter described in section 3;

“standard” means:

(a) The Management and Reduction of Greenhouse Gases (Reporting)

Standard, as amended from time to time; or

(b) any other standard developed or adopted pursuant to subsection 6(1).

(2) Part 3 of The Management and Reduction of Greenhouse Gases (General and

Electricity Producer) Regulations applies, with any necessary modification, to these

regulations.

### Application of regulations

3(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), every person who owns or operates a facility

that emits 10,000 tonnes or more of CO e each year is a regulated emitter with

2

respect to the facility and is subject to these regulations.

(2) If the baseline emissions intensity has been established for a regulated

industrial facility in accordance with The Management and Reduction of Greenhouse

Gases (Standards and Compliance) Regulations, 2023, these regulations do not

apply to the regulated industrial facility.

(3) These regulations do not apply to a regulated electricity facility within the

meaning of The Management and Reduction of Greenhouse Gases (Standards and

Compliance) Regulations, 2023.

(4) For greater certainty, if a regulated emitter described in subsection (1) is

not subject to other reporting requirements respecting greenhouse gas emissions

pursuant to other regulations made pursuant to the Act, the regulated emitter must

comply with the requirements set out in these regulations.

## PART 2

### Reporting of Greenhouse Gases by Regulated Emitters

### Emissions measured in tonnes of CO e

### 2

4 For the purposes of the Act and these regulations, greenhouse gas emissions

must be measured, calculated and expressed in tonnes of CO e.

2

### Submission of annual report

5(1) For the purposes of section 21 of the Act, a regulated emitter must submit to

the minister an annual report, in the manner established by all applicable standards,

for each regulated facility that the regulated emitter owns or operates.

(2) A regulated emitter must submit an annual report to the minister on or before:

(a) June 1 of the year following the year to which the annual report relates;

(b) another date that is requested by the regulated emitter and that the

minister is satisfied is appropriate; or

(c) another date that the minister may direct in writing.

### Adoption of standard

6(1) The minister may develop or adopt one or more standards respecting the

following matters:

(a) annual reports;

(b) quantification methodologies for greenhouse gases;

(c) audits and inspections;

(d) any other matter dealt with in these regulations.

(2) In addition to publishing notice of each standard in accordance with

subsection 7(4) of the Act, the minister shall publish notice of each standard on the

ministry’s website.

(3) The minister may consult with any regulated emitter before amending an

applicable standard.

### Compliance with standard

7 Every regulated emitter must comply with the standards developed or adopted

pursuant to these regulations.

## PART 3

### General

### Administrative penalties

8(1) For the purposes of section 78 of the Act, the minister may assess a penalty

for a contravention of a provision of the Act or these regulations that is set out in

the Appendix.

(2) The maximum penalty that the minister may assess with respect to each

contravention is $10,000.

### Record keeping

9 Every person who submits an annual report to the minister pursuant to these

regulations must retain and make available for inspection by the minister all

documents and information used to prepare the annual report, including a record

of each methodology, procedure and instrument used, for 7 years after the date on

which the annual report is submitted.

### Confidentiality requests

10(1) For the purposes of subsections 61(3) and (4) of the Act, requests to the

minister to keep confidential all or part of an annual report must be made in writing

in conjunction with the submission of the annual report.

(2) After receiving a request for confidentiality pursuant to subsection (1),

the minister shall, as soon as is practicable, provide a written response to the

regulated emitter indicating if the request has or has not been accepted together

with reasons for the minister’s decision.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), during the period in which the minister is

considering a request for confidentiality made pursuant to subsection (1), the

information contained in the annual report to which the request relates shall

be kept confidential.

(4) The following information contained in an annual report is not subject to the

confidentiality requirement mentioned in subsection (3):

(a) the name of the regulated facility;

(b) the ownership of the regulated facility;

(c) the location of the regulated facility;

(d) the annual emissions for the regulated facility;

(e) any other information specified in the applicable standard that the

minister is satisfied should not be kept confidential.

(5) If the minister does not accept the request for confidentiality pursuant to

subsection (2), the minister shall give the regulated emitter 7 business days after

the date of receipt of the written response mentioned in that subsection to make

written representations to the minister.

(6) After reviewing any written representations made pursuant to subsection (5)

or, if no written representations are made, after the expiry of the 7-day period

mentioned in that subsection, the minister shall:

(a) make a final decision to accept or not accept the request for confidentiality;

and

(b) provide written notice to the person of the minister’s decision.

### Audit and inspection

11 For the purposes of section 67 of the Act, an audit or inspection of a regulated

facility must be undertaken in accordance with the applicable standard.

## PART 4

### Repeal and Coming into Force

### RRS c M-2.01 Reg 2 repealed

12 The Management and Reduction of Greenhouse Gases (Reporting and General)

Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

13 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations, but are retroactive and are deemed to have been in force

on and from June 1, 2025.
