Sask Reg 59/2026

*Made under The Heritage Recognition (From Many Peoples, Strength) Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Heritage Recognition (Japanese Heritage

Month) Regulations.

### Designation

2 The month of September is designated as Japanese Heritage Month.

### Recognition

3 The designation of Japanese Heritage Month is in recognition of the following:

(a) Japanese heritage in Saskatchewan reflects a story of courage,

determination and community building, beginning with early settlers who

forged new lives on the Prairies, continuing with those affected by internment,

dispossession and displacement and carried forward by successive waves of

immigrants and their descendants who have chosen to call Saskatchewan home;

(b) historical ties between Japan and Saskatchewan, which are woven into

the geography, communities and ongoing cultural and educational exchanges,

continue to foster friendship, understanding and international cooperation;

(c) September holds historical significance as the month, in 1988, in which

the Japanese Canadian Redress Agreement was signed by the Government

of Canada and the National Association of Japanese Canadians, marking an

important step in acknowledging past injustice and reflecting the enduring

leadership and commitment of the Japanese community to advancing justice,

reconciliation and human rights while contributing to the ongoing work of

building a more just and equitable society for all;

(d) celebrating Japanese Heritage Month honours the stories, contributions

and cultural legacies of all individuals who help shape the province’s past,

present and future;

(e) the contributions of Saskatchewan’s Japanese community to the cultural

richness, resilience and shared prosperity of Saskatchewan;

(f) the traditions, values and cultural expressions of Saskatchewan’s Japanese

community, including a deep respect for harmony, learning, family and

community, which have enriched Saskatchewan’s social, artistic and economic

landscape.

## SR 59/2026 (JAPANESE HERITAGE MONTH)

### Coming into force

4(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day

on which section 1 of The Heritage Recognition (From Many Peoples, Strength)

Act comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day

on which section 1 of The Heritage Recognition (From Many Peoples, Strength)

Act comes into force, these regulations come into force on the day on which they

are filed with the Registrar of Regulations.
