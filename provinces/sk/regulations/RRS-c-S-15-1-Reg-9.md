RRS c S-15.1 Reg 9

*Made under The Saskatchewan Employment Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Health Labour Relations Reorganization

Regulations, 2024.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Saskatchewan Employment Act.

### Designated employers’ organization

3(1) For the purposes of Division 14 of Part VI of the Act and any regulations

made pursuant to that Part for the purposes of that Division, the Saskatchewan

Association of Health Organizations Inc. is designated as the designated employers’

organization for health sector employers.

(2) Every health sector employer is to be a member of the designated employers’

organization for the purposes of bargaining collectively.

### Health sector employer

4 For the purposes of Division 14 of Part VI of the Act and any regulations made

pursuant to that Part for the purposes of that Division, the following are prescribed

as health sector employers:

(a) La Ronge Health Centre;

(b) eHealth Saskatchewan.

### RRS H-0.03 Reg 2 repealed

5 The Health Labour Relations Reorganization Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

6 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
