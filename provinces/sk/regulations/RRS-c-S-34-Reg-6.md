RRS c S-34 Reg 6

*Made under The Saskatchewan Telecommunications Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Saskatchewan Telecommunications Fees

Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Saskatchewan Telecommunications Act;

(b) “corporation” means Saskatchewan Telecommunications continued

pursuant to section 3 of the Act;

(c) “wireless customer” means a customer of the corporation’s wireless

service who has the capacity to place emergency 911 telephone calls through

the Sask911 system and who is assigned a telephone number associated with

Saskatchewan.

### Requirement to pay fee

3 To reimburse the corporation for the costs mentioned in clause 9(1)(d.3) of the

Act, every wireless customer is required to pay the corporation a fee of $0.21 per

working telephone number per month.

### Coming into force

4(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on October 15, 2008.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after

October 15, 2008, these regulations come into force on the day on which they are

filed with the Registrar of Regulations.
