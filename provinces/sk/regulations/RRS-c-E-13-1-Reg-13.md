RRS c E-13.1 Reg 13

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Government Relations

Regulations, 2019.

### Ministry continued

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry of

Government Relations is continued.

### Objects and purposes

3(1) The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Government Relations are the

following:

(a) to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities

and functions of the Minister of Government Relations and the Minister

Responsible for First Nations, Métis and Northern Affairs may be exercised

and carried out;

(b) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan related to cities, urban municipalities, rural

municipalities and northern municipalities, and matters of an intermunicipal

nature and, without limiting the generality of this object and purpose, to

coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs relating

to the following:

(i) community planning in, and development and diversification in and

of, cities and urban, rural and northern municipalities;

(ii) transportation;

(iii) coordinating and carrying out of joint ventures relating to or

affecting municipalities with:

(A) a municipal council;

(B) a board responsible for health, education or other services

either inside or outside the municipalities; or

(C) any ministry or agency of the Government of Canada, the

Government of Saskatchewan or the government of any other

province or territory of Canada;

(iv) providing financial, administrative, technical and other support

services to municipalities and to associations of municipalities;

(v) community facilities;

(c) to coordinate, develop, implement and manage the programs and policies

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to local government elections;

(d) to act as a contact for First Nations and Métis peoples with the Government

of Saskatchewan;

(e) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan with respect to matters affecting First

Nations and Métis peoples;

(f) to coordinate, manage and implement policies and programs of the

Government of Saskatchewan relating to First Nations and Métis governance

and land matters;

(g) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan that foster and advance development in

northern Saskatchewan;

(h) to coordinate with other ministries and agencies the negotiation and

administration of surface lease agreements for mines in northern Saskatchewan;

(i) to coordinate programs and activities of the Government of Saskatchewan

to monitor uranium mining developments and operations in northern

Saskatchewan;

(j) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan relating to building, accessibility and other

safety standards;

(k) to enhance the protection of the public by:

(i) coordinating, promoting, developing, implementing and enforcing

safety standards with respect to boilers, pressure vessels, gas equipment

and installations, electrical equipment and installations, elevators and

amusement rides; and

(ii) licensing and regulating persons associated with the matters

described in subclause (i).

(2) The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Government Relations are to provide

the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities and functions of

the Minister Responsible for the Provincial Capital Commission may be exercised

and carried out in relation to the following:

(a) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan relating to:

(i) conserving, preserving, managing, protecting and developing

Saskatchewan’s capital city as a source of pride for the people of

Saskatchewan; and

(ii) informing and educating, and collecting and disseminating

information, with respect to the importance of Saskatchewan’s capital

city and its place in the life and history of Saskatchewan and of Canada;

(b) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan relating to:

(i) the promotion of the Legislative Building and the land on which it

is situated;

(ii) international, national, provincial and regional special events and

anniversaries to be commemorated within Saskatchewan’s capital city;

and

(iii) the Saskatchewan Centre of the Arts and Wascana Centre Authority;

(c) to be responsible for the operation and management of Government House

and the Territorial Building;

(d) to work together with business, communities, governments, agencies,

ministries and the people of Saskatchewan to support the objects and purposes

of the ministry.

### RRS G-5.1 Reg 156 repealed

4 The Ministry of Government Relations Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
