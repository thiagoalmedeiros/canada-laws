# The Urgent Action on Breast Cancer Diagnosis and Treatment Regulations

RRS c H-0.0001 Reg 2

*Made under The Health Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Urgent Action on Breast Cancer Diagnosis

and Treatment Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Health Administration Act;

“clinical criteria” means the criteria established by an out-of-province facility,

and approved by the minister, that the facility considers necessary to ensure

that persons selected for procedures are physically fit and can safely receive the

procedure in the facility and be discharged within 24 hours after the procedure;

“eligible patient” means a beneficiary:

(a) who is on a wait list in Saskatchewan for a procedure urgently

required for the diagnosis or treatment of breast cancer; and

(b) who, because of that wait list:

(i) has waited for longer than the clinically-prescribed time for

the procedure; or

(ii) will wait for longer than the clinically-prescribed time for the

procedure if the procedure is to be performed in Saskatchewan;

“out-of-province facility” means a health care facility in Canada but outside

Saskatchewan that has entered into an agreement with the minister for the

purpose of providing procedures to persons designated pursuant to section 3;

“procedure” means a diagnostic or surgical procedure related to breast health

that may include diagnostic mammography, image-guided biopsy or other

surgical or diagnostic modalities.

### Designation of eligible persons

3(1) Persons who meet the criteria set out in subsection (2) are designated pursuant

to clause 6.1(a) of the Act as persons for whom the minister may pay part or the whole

of the cost of the procedures provided to those persons at out-of-province facilities.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), the person must:

(a) reside in Saskatchewan;

(b) be selected and recommended to the minister by the Saskatchewan Health

Authority as an eligible patient;

(c) meet the clinical criteria for the out-of-province facility that will provide

the procedure; and

(d) be accepted by the minister as being a person for whom part or the whole

of the cost of the procedure provided to that person at an out-of-province facility

will be paid by the minister.

### Agreements

4 The minister may enter into agreements with out-of-province facilities for the

provision of procedures pursuant to these regulations.

### Payments

5(1) Subject to subsection (2), the minister may make payment for procedures

provided to persons described in section 3 at out-of-province facilities between

December 1, 2023 and March 31, 2027.

(2) The total amount paid by the minister to all out-of-province facilities for

procedures pursuant to these regulations shall not exceed $4,400,000.

### Coming into force

6 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
