RRS c P-1.1 Reg 7

*Made under The Parks Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Contact Lake Surface Lease Agreement

Regulations, 1991.

### Surface lease agreement

2 The Minister of Parks and Renewable Resources and the Minister of Community

Services are hereby authorized to enter into and execute an agreement granting

surface rights to Cameco Corporation, Uranerz Exploration and Mining Limited

and Westward Explorations Ltd. substantially in accordance with the provisions

set out in the form of the agreement contained in the Appendix.

### Rental payment

3 Notwithstanding any other regulations made pursuant to The Parks Act, the

minister may determine the amount of rental payments under the agreement

mentioned in section 2 in accordance with the provisions of that agreement.

## EXEMPTION FROM PUBLICATION

Re: The Contact Lake Surface Lease Agreement Regulations, 1991

Pursuant to the authority vested in me by subsection 7(2) of The Regulations Act, 1989,

the Appendix referred to in the above-mentioned regulations is hereby exempted from

publication in The Saskatchewan Gazette.

Dated at Regina, Saskatchewan, June 25, 1991.

Lynn A. Minja

Registrar of Regulations
