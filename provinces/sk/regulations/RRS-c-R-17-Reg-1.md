RRS c R-17 Reg 1

*Made under The Rehabilitation Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Vocational Rehabilitation Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Rehabilitation Act;

(b) “applicant” means a person who has requested a service under the Act

by completing the application form prescribed by the director for that purpose;

(c) Repealed. 18 Dec 92 SR 144/92 s3;

(d) “department” means the Department of Social Services;

(e) “director” means a person appointed by the minister to administer the

Act and these regulations;

(f) Repealed. 18 Dec 92 SR 144/92 s3;

(g) “referral agency” means any private agency or any department of the

Government of Saskatchewan designated by the director to present applications

on behalf of applicants;

(h) “service” means any rehabilitation service which may be provided

pursuant to section 3 of the Act for the rehabilitation of a disabled person;

(i) Repealed. 18 Dec 92 SR 144/92 s3.

### Application for service

3 A disabled person may apply to the director or to a referral agency for a service.

### Requirements of application for service

4 Where a person makes an application pursuant to section 3, he shall:

(a) complete the application form and any other forms or documents prescribed

by the director; and

(b) furnish any evidence of proof of eligibility and need that the director may

require.

### Process for reviewing applications

5 The minister shall establish a process for reviewing applications for services

and for determining the eligibility of applicants.

### Approval

6 The minister may, on any terms and conditions that the minister considers

appropriate, approve the financial sponsorship of the whole or any part of any service

that the minister considers advisable for the rehabilitation of a disabled person who

displays a potential for vocational rehabilitation and employment.

### Director’s powers

7 The director may:

(a) prescribe any forms that he considers necessary in connection with the

administration of the Act and these regulations;

(b) designate any private agency or department of the Government of

Saskatchewan as a referral agency for the purpose of these regulations;

(c) subject to section 4 of the Act, enter into arrangements with public or

private agencies for providing rehabilitation services;

(d) establish any policy or procedure that he considers necessary for the

administration of these regulations.

### Minister’s power

8 The minister may:

(a) prescribe the conditions of eligibility for a service;

(b) require ministerial approval for the furnishing of a service;

(c) subject to section 4 of the Act, enter into arrangements with public or

private agencies for sharing the costs of providing services; and

(d) do any other thing that he considers necessary for the proper administration

of the Act and these regulations.

### Records

9 The director and every referral agency shall maintain and keep a record of all

services provided pursuant to these regulations and any other records that the

director considers necessary.

### Information confidential

10 All information regarding an applicant is confidential and no person shall

disclose information regarding an applicant to any person other than the minister,

the deputy minister or a person employed by the department, without the written

consent of the minister.

### Coming into force

11 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
