# The Wildlife Habitat Lands Disposition and Alteration Regulations

RRS c W-13.2 Reg 1

*Made under The Wildlife Habitat Protection Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Wildlife Habitat Lands Disposition and

Alteration Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Wildlife Habitat Protection Act;

(b) “cutline” means a pathway cleared through a wooded area;

(c) “disposition” means an instrument that grants an interest in or the right

to enter on Crown land for any purpose, but does not include a disposition of

minerals granted pursuant to The Crown Minerals Act;

(c.1) “forest harvesting” means cutting, picking, gathering, collecting,

accumulating or removing forest products as defined in The Forest Resources

Management Act by any means;

(d) “slash” means the debris resulting from the clearing of cutlines.

### Crown corporation

3 The Saskatchewan Wetland Conservation Corporation is prescribed as a Crown

corporation for the purposes of subsection 6(4) of the Act.

### Permitted dispositions

4(1) Subject to subsection (2) and section 5, for the purposes of subsection 6(2) of

the Act, the following are permitted dispositions:

(a) dispositions that permit or relate to the exploration for or the extraction

of oil, natural gas or non-hydrocarbon gases and activities incidental to the

exploration or extraction;

(b) dispositions that permit the grazing of livestock and activities incidental

to the grazing of livestock;

(c) dispositions that permit haying for agricultural purposes and activities

incidental to haying for agricultural purposes;

(d) dispositions that permit cultivation and activities incidental to cultivation

for agricultural purposes of Crown land that is under cultivation on the day

the Crown land is designated as wildlife habitat land;

(e) dispositions that permit forest harvesting and activities incidental to

forest harvesting;

(f) dispositions that permit the exploration for or extraction of sand, gravel

or clay and activities incidental to the exploration or extraction.

(2) Notwithstanding any other provision of the Act or these regulations, no

disposition that permits the exploration for or extraction of sand, gravel or clay and

activities incidental to the exploration or extraction shall be granted for any wildlife

habitat land described in the Appendix.

### Restriction on alterations

5 Subject to section 9, no disposition holder shall make any alteration to wildlife

habitat lands other than an alteration that is permitted pursuant to these regulations

or authorized by the minister in writing.

### Alterations of subsurface permitted

6(1) In this section, “disposition” means a disposition of minerals granted

pursuant to The Crown Minerals Act.

(2) Every person who holds a disposition may make any alteration of the subsurface

of wildlife habitat lands where the minerals that are the subject of the disposition

are located if the alteration is necessarily incidental to exercising the person’s rights

pursuant to the disposition.

### Oil and natural gas

7(1) For the purposes of this section “motor vehicle” means a vehicle that is

required to be registered pursuant to The Vehicle Administration Act.

(2) Subject to subsections (3) to (5) and section 9, every person who holds a

disposition of wildlife habitat lands described in clause 4(a) may make any alteration

to the wildlife habitat land that is necessarily incidental to exercising that person’s

rights pursuant to that disposition.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), a person may make an alteration pursuant to

subsection (2) where:

(a) the number of wells on the wildlife habitat land does not exceed four wells

per quarter section of land;

(b) the person breaks slash windrows at least once in every 100 metres with

an opening that is at least five metres long;

(c) the person does not make cutlines that exceed 10 metres in width; and

(d) if the cutlines are not being used for the purpose for which they were made,

the person blocks the cutlines so as to prevent access to them by motor vehicles.

(4) The minister may give notice in writing to the person who was granted the

disposition requiring that person to perform rehabilitative measures that the

minister considers necessary as a result of the alterations to the wildlife habitat

land that the person has carried out on the wildlife habitat land that was the subject

of the disposition.

(5) Where a person is given a written notice pursuant to subsection (4), that person

shall perform the rehabilitative measures that are prescribed in the notice in the

manner and in the period specified in the notice.

(6) The minister may, on any terms and conditions that he or she may determine,

modify any requirement of clause (3)(a), (b), (c) or (d) in the case of a particular

disposition where the minister is satisfied that:

(a) meeting that requirement will cause unnecessary hardship to the

disposition holder; and

(b) failure to meet that requirement by the disposition holder will not seriously

jeopardize the wildlife habitat land.

### Mineral dispositions

8 Subject to sections 7 and 9, every person who, on or after the day this section

comes into force, holds a disposition to Crown lands designated as wildlife habitat

lands permitting or relating to the exploration for or the extraction of any mineral

may make any alteration to the wildlife habitat land that is necessarily incidental

to exercising that person’s rights pursuant to that disposition.

### No alterations on certain lands without minister’s approval

9 Without the prior written approval of the minister, no person shall make any

alteration of the surface of any wildlife habitat land described in the Appendix

pursuant to a disposition permitting or relating to the exploration for or the

extraction of any mineral activities incidental to that exploration or extraction.

### Grazing and haying

10(1) Subject to subsection (2), the following persons who hold the following

dispositions of wildlife habitat lands may make any alteration to the wildlife

habitat land that is necessarily incidental to exercising their rights pursuant to

those dispositions:

(a) persons who hold dispositions permitting the grazing of livestock and

activities incidental to that grazing; or

(b) persons who hold dispositions permitting haying for agricultural purposes

and activities incidental to that haying.

(2) The persons mentioned in subsection (1) shall not engage in any clearing

or breaking of the wildlife habitat land without the prior written approval of the

minister.

### Cultivation

11 Every person who holds a disposition permitting cultivation, and activities

incidental to that cultivation, for agricultural purposes of Crown land that is under

cultivation on the day the Crown land is designated as wildlife habitat land may

make any alteration to the wildlife habitat land that is necessarily incidental to

exercising that person’s rights pursuant to that disposition.

### Forest harvesting

11.1(1) No person shall, without the prior written approval of the minister, make

any alteration of the surface of any wildlife habitat land pursuant to a disposition

permitting or relating to forest harvesting or activities incidental to forest harvesting.

(2) The minister may provide prior written approval if he or she is satisfied that

the proposed forest harvesting activities will not impair the usefulness of, or will

improve, the wildlife habitat.

(3) The written approval provided pursuant to subsection (2) may contain any

terms and conditions the minister considers necessary respecting:

(a) the size and configuration of the harvest area;

(b) the methods of harvest;

(c) the location of the harvest;

(d) the location, construction and use of roads; and

(e) any other matters the minister considers appropriate.

(4) Notwithstanding any other provision of the Act or these regulations, the

minister shall not provide written approval pursuant to subsection (2) for any wildlife

habitat land described in the Appendix unless those lands require forest harvesting

to prevent the loss of wildlife habitat.

(5) No person shall fail to comply with the terms and conditions of a prior

written approval.

### Sand, gravel and clay dispositions

11.2(1) No person shall, without the prior written approval of the minister, make

any alteration to the surface of any wildlife habitat land pursuant to a disposition

permitting or relating to the exploration for or the extraction of sand, gravel or clay

or activities incidental to the exploration or extraction.

(2) The minister may provide prior written approval if, after considering the

following, he or she is satisfied that it is appropriate to do so:

(a) whether or not there is an existing sand, gravel or clay disposition;

(b) whether or not there is an alternate source of sand, gravel or clay in the

vicinity;

(c) whether or not the proponent is prepared to undertake rehabilitative

measures;

(d) any other information the minister considers appropriate.

(3) The written approval provided pursuant to subsection (2) may contain any

terms and conditions the minister considers necessary respecting:

(a) the size and configuration of the quarrying area;

(b) the location, construction and use of access roads;

(c) the use of native seeds or plants in the rehabilitation of the area after the

quarrying is completed; and

(d) any other matters the minister considers appropriate.

(4) No person shall fail to comply with the terms and conditions of a prior written

approval.

### Provincial roads

12(1) For the purposes of this section, “road” means a road that is designated as

a provincial highway or a provincial road that appears on the Saskatchewan Official

Highway Map 1993.

(2) Every person who has the authority to maintain or rebuild any road may make

any alteration of wildlife habitat land that is necessarily incidental to maintaining

or rebuilding that road on the condition that no alteration is made more than 40

metres from:

(a) in the case of an undivided road, the centre-line of the roadway; or

(b) in the case of a divided road, the road shoulder that is nearest to the

proposed alteration.

### Municipal roads

13(1) For the purposes of this section, “road” means a municipal road within the

meaning of The Rural Municipality Act, 1989.

(2) Every person who has the authority to maintain or rebuild any road may make

any alteration of wildlife habitat lands that is necessarily incidental to maintaining

or rebuilding that road on the condition that no alteration is made more than 25

metres from the centre-line of the road.

### Radio and telecommunications

14 Every person who has the authority to maintain radio or telecommunication

facilities or equipment may make any alteration of wildlife habitat lands that is

necessarily incidental to that maintenance.

### Transmission of electricity, oil or natural gas

15(1) Every person who has the authority to maintain facilities or equipment

used or involved in the transmission of electricity, oil, natural gas, water or refined

hydrocarbon substances may make any alteration of wildlife habitat lands that is

necessarily incidental to that maintenance.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) restricts a disposition holder mentioned in

subsection 7(2) from making any permitted alteration that is necessarily incidental

to exercising his or her rights pursuant to that disposition.

### R.R.S. c.C-47.1 Reg 2 repealed

16 The Critical Wildlife Habitat Lands Disposition and Alteration Regulations

are repealed.
