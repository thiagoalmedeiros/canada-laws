RRS c W-11.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Weed Control Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Weed Control Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Weed Control Act.

### Definition of domestic animal

3 For the purposes of clause 2(1)(g) of the Act, “domestic animal” means any

animal, bird, reptile, amphibian or fish that is under the care of a person.

### Definition of machine

4(1) In this section, “emergency vehicle” means an emergency vehicle as defined

in The Vehicle Equipment Regulations, 1987.

(2) For the purposes of clause 2(1)(n) of the Act, “machine”:

(a) includes:

(i) a vehicle as defined in The Traffic Safety Act;

(ii) a conveyance, an aircraft, a boat or other watercraft; and

(iii) any accessory attached to a vehicle, conveyance, aircraft, boat or

other watercraft mentioned in subclause (i) or (ii); and

(b) does not include any emergency vehicle.

### Form of agreement

5 Every agreement entered into pursuant to subsection 20(1) of the Act is to be in

Form A of the Appendix.

### Form of order

6 Every order issued pursuant to section 21, 23, 26, 29 or 31 of the Act is to be in

Form B of the Appendix.

### Recovery from owner or occupant – maximum amount

7 The maximum amount that may be recovered by a municipality from the owner

or occupant of the land pursuant to subsection 37(2) of the Act is:

(a) if the area of the land is 0.25 hectares or less, $400; and

(b) if the area of the land is greater than 0.25 hectares, $1250 per hectare or

part of a hectare to a maximum of $10,000 per quarter section of land.

### Deposit on appeal

8 A notice of appeal pursuant to section 34 of the Act must be accompanied by a

deposit in the amount of $550.

### Service by courier

9(1) In addition to the means of service described in section 41 of the Act, any

document required by the Act or the regulations to be given or served may be

delivered by courier to the last known address of the person being served.

(2) A document served by courier is deemed to have been received on the second

business day following the day it was delivered by the courier, unless the person to

whom it was sent establishes that, through no fault of that person, he or she did

not receive the document or that he or she received it at a later date.

### R.R.S. c.N-9.1 Reg 1 repealed

10 The Noxious Weeds Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

11 These regulations come into force on the day on which section 1 of The Weed

Control Act comes into force.
