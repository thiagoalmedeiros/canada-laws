## PART I

### Title, Interpretation and Application

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Municipal Police Clothing and Rank

Regulations, 1991.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Police Act, 1990;

“clothing” includes all articles of uniform and accessories worn with a uniform;

“commissioned officer” means a rank of a police service from inspector to

chief, inclusive;

“rank” means an appointment or position held in a police service from constable

to chief, inclusive;

“specialized uniform” means an alternative uniform worn during:

(a) training;

(b) specialized services operations, including the following:

(i) police communications;

(ii) detention;

(iii) forensic identification;

(iv) bike patrol;

(v) motorcycle patrol;

(vi) crowd management and public order;

“uniform” means, unless the context requires otherwise, a basic working

uniform worn during a normal tour of duty.

### Application

3 These regulations apply to all police services established pursuant to the Act

and supersede any regulation or order governing a police service in matters covered

by these regulations that is inconsistent with them.

## PART II

### Uniforms and Accessories

### For all ranks

4(1) Uniforms for all ranks are to include the following items:

(a) tunic;

(b) trousers;

(c) shirt, long or short sleeve;

(d) tie, clip-on style;

(e) tie-clip;

(f) black, closed-toe footwear;

(g) forage cap;

(h) weather-appropriate headwear;

(i) police baseball cap;

(j) gloves;

(k) weather-appropriate jacket;

(l) standard issue coats and rainwear, but with respect to commissioned

officers, coats and rainwear that are:

(i) trench-coat style; and

(ii) knee length;

(m) socks.

(2) Accessories for all ranks are to include the following:

(a) forage cap badge;

(b) badge;

(c) gloves, white nylon or cotton and black leather unlined gloves for

commissioned officers;

(d) belt, waist;

(e) lanyard, nylon;

(f) service and rank insignias;

(g) plastic cap cover;

(h) equipment belt;

(i) holster;

(j) handcuff pouch;

(k) if the member has been issued a pistol:

(i) double-magazine carrier, vertical or horizontal mount, except in the

case of a plain clothes member; and

(ii) single-magazine carrier, in the case of a plain clothes member;

(l) badge and identification card holder;

(m) shoulder flash may be included in accessories for commissioned officers

and may be of a distinctive design for commissioned officers of police services

with more than five sworn personnel.

(3) The following items of clothing and accessories are optional and are to be

supplied if required by a member in the performance of duty:

(a) task-appropriate gloves;

(b) flashlight;

(c) sweater;

(d) equipment pouches;

(e) helmet, breeches and boots or leggings to be used by members assigned

to motorcycle duty;

(f) reflective attire.

### Consistent appearance

4.1 All clothing and accessories issued to members of a police service pursuant to

section 4 must be consistent in appearance and approved by the chief.

### Colour of buttons and accessories

5 The colour of buttons, metal badges and fittings, rank insignia and other

accessories or trim is to be either gold or silver or a combination of both for all ranks

provided that chiefs may exercise a local option as to the colour or combination of

colours to be used on the uniforms of their personnel.

### Commissioned officers

6(1) The uniform for commissioned officers is to be as follows:

(a) cloth - good quality, dark navy blue or black;

(b) tunic design - tailored, form fitting, single-breasted front closing with four

buttons, notched lapel, two patch-type breast pockets with flap and button, two

pouch-type pockets below the waistline with flap and button, epaulets, belt

loops or metal belt holder on either side, cloth belt with a military style buckle;

(c) trouser design - full cut slack design, two side pockets, two rear pockets

with tabs to button, waistband with belt loops, special pockets as required,

a red stripe with a minimum width of 2.54 centimetres extending from the

waist to the bottom of the trouser leg, no cuffs;

(d) Sam Browne belt – black colour, with a cross strap and of good quality

with no tooling, consisting of:

(i) a level 3 security holster;

(ii) handcuff pouch;

(iii) magazine pouches.

(2) The chief may, on any terms and conditions the chief considers appropriate,

authorize additional accessories mentioned in clauses 14(i), (j), (k), (l) and (m) to be

worn on the Sam Browne belt mentioned in clause (1)(d).

### Members other than commissioned officers

7 The uniform for members other than commissioned officers is to be as follows:

(a) cloth - good quality, dark navy blue or black;

(b) tunic design - tailored, form fitting, single-breasted front closing with four

buttons, notched lapel, two patch-type breast pockets with flap and button,

epaulets, belt loop or metal belt holder on either side, cloth belt with military

style buckle;

(c) trouser design - full cut slack design, two side pockets, two rear pockets,

special pockets as required, waistband with belt loops, a red stripe with a

minimum width of 2.54 centimetres extending from the waist to the bottom

of the trouser leg, no cuffs.

### Trouser design for Saskatchewan Marshals Service

7.1 Notwithstanding clauses 6(c) and 7(c) that require a red stripe, all commissioned

officers and other members of the Saskatchewan Marshals Service shall wear a

trouser design:

(a) with a green stripe; and

(b) that meets all other requirements set out in clause 6(c) or 7(c), as the

case may be.

8 Repealed. 1 Sep 2017 SR 95/2017 s8.

### Outer garments

9(1) Outer garments for all ranks are to be as follows:

(a) weather-appropriate jacket – dark navy blue or black colour, two-way

front zipper closure, epaulets, and a drawstring in the liner;

(b) topcoat – for commissioned officers only, and dark navy blue or black

colour, trench-coat style, knee length, with a quilted nylon zip-in lining, 2 slash

side pockets, and fly-type button front;

(c) reflective attire – high visibility with reflective bands and “POLICE”

graphics;

(d) wind pants – for winter wear, as authorized by the chief.

(2) The outer material of the garments mentioned in subsection (1) is not to be

of a composition that is either leather or material that approximates leather in

appearance.

### Headwear

10(1) The headwear of commissioned officers is to be as follows:

(a) forage cap – dark navy blue or black colour having a red band of,

minimally, 2.54 centimetres surrounding the base of the cap, a black plastic

peak, 2 side buttons, a cap strap with municipal cap badge:

(i) the chief is to wear 2 rows of embroidery oak leaves on the cap peak;

(ii) the Deputy Chief of Police is to wear one row of embroidery oak

leaves on the cap peak;

(iii) commissioned officers, other than the chief and the Deputy Chief of

Police, are to wear one bar embroidery on the cap peak;

(iv) a chief having an establishment of 50 or less is to wear one row of

embroidery oak leaves on the cap peak;

(b) optional fur or synthetic fur cap – klondike style with ear flaps held in

upright position with a tie string and a cloth crown that is to be of navy blue

or black material.

(2) Subject to subsection (5), the headwear for members is to be as follows:

(a) forage cap – dark navy blue or black colour having a red band of,

minimally, 2.54 centimetres surrounding the base of the cap, a black plastic

peak, 2 side buttons, a black strap, and a municipal cap badge;

(b) police baseball cap – dark navy blue or black colour, police service name

or crest on the front panel and ‘POLICE’ embroidered on either or both the

front and side panels in silver, grey, gold or white, adjustable size;

(c) optional fur or synthetic fur cap – klondike style with ear flaps held in

upright position with a tie string and a cloth crown that is to be of navy blue

or black material.

(3) All headwear is to be worn professionally, squarely on the head with the

underside of the peak approximately 3 finger widths above the bridge of the nose.

(4) A police baseball cap mentioned in clause (2)(b) shall not be worn with a tunic.

(5) Members assigned to specialized duties may wear task-appropriate headwear

as authorized by the chief.

(6) Notwithstanding clauses (1)(a) and (2)(a) that require a red band, all

commissioned officers and other members of the Saskatchewan Marshals Service

shall wear forage caps:

(a) with a green band; and

(b) that meets all other requirements set out in clause (1)(a) or (2)(a), as the

case may be.

### Shirts

11 Shirts for members are to be as follows:

(a) commissioned officers - top quality white, midnight blue or black dress

shirt with epaulets and two breast pockets with button-down flap and pen slot;

(b) other ranks - top quality midnight blue or black dress shirt with epaulets

and two breast pockets with button-down flap and pen slot, in either long

sleeves or short sleeves;

(c) for all ranks, shirts worn under a body protector shall:

(i) be worn in accordance with clause (a) or (b), as the case may be; or

(ii) be long or short sleeved, polo-style, have epaulets, shoulder flashes

and be consistent in colour with clauses (a) and (b).

### Hosiery

12 All ranks are to wear navy blue or black socks.

### Footwear

13(1) Subject to subsection (2), all ranks are to wear a plain black closed-toe shoe

or black closed-toe ankle style boot.

(2) Members assigned to specialized duties may wear task appropriate footwear

as authorized by the chief.

### Accessories

14 Unless otherwise authorized by the commission, all accessories are to be black

in colour and of good quality with no tooling, consisting of:

(a) gloves for all ranks of good quality black leather with a good quality lining

for winter wear;

(b) mitts of plain black leather of good quality, with a good quality lining for

winter wear;

(c) a waist belt for all ranks with an eye buckle;

(d) an equipment belt;

(e) a level 3 security holster, except in the case of plain clothes members;

(f) in the case of plain clothes members, holsters:

(i) that are pistol specific if worn inside the pants;

(ii) that are custom form if they are shoulder holsters; and

(iii) that have a paddle with a thumb break, custom form;

(g) handcuff pouches;

(h) magazine pouches to be attached to the equipment belt;

(i) baton holders with a belt loop;

(j) a flashlight holder;

(k) a folding knife or tool holder;

(l) a conducted energy device holster; and

(m) an Oleoresin Capsicum spray holder.

### Alternative items of uniform

14.1(1) The chief may, on any terms and conditions the chief considers

appropriate, authorize the following alternative items of uniform for members

or commissioned officers that are to be worn in accordance with section 4.1 or

subsection 10(3), as the case may be:

(a) midnight blue or black mock turtleneck;

(b) midnight blue or black T-shirt;

(c) midnight blue or black military style toque;

(d) midnight blue or black sweater;

(e) maternity uniform;

(f) fleece wear or other undergarments;

(g) campaign style hat.

(2) The chief may, on any terms and conditions the chief considers appropriate,

authorize a member or commissioned officer to wear or carry articles in accordance

with religious requirements sincerely believed by the member.

(3) The chief may, on any terms and conditions the chief considers appropriate,

authorize members or commissioned officers to wear one or both of the following

on their uniforms:

(a) the flag of Saskatchewan;

(b) the flag of Canada.

(4) Notwithstanding anything else in this Part or Part III, the commission may, on

any terms and conditions the commission considers appropriate, approve alternative

items of uniform or waive the requirement to wear particular items of uniform for

members or commissioned officers.

### Specialized uniforms

14.2(1) The chief may, on any terms and conditions the chief considers appropriate,

authorize specialized uniforms for members or commissioned officers while those

members or commissioned officers, as the case may be, are actively engaged in the

following:

(a) training;

(b) specialized services operations;

(c) emergency services response.

(2) The chief may, on any terms and conditions the chief considers appropriate,

authorize emergency services response members to wear specialized uniforms while

performing general patrol duties.

(3) Specialized uniforms must be:

(a) black, green, grey, brown, or brown-grey; and

(b) a consistent, non-disrupted colour pattern.

(4) Sections 21, 22, 25, 26 and 28.1 apply to members or commissioned officers

authorized to wear specialized uniforms pursuant to this section.

## PART III

### Orders of Dress

### Clothing to be maintained

15 All ranks are to ensure that items of uniform and accessories issued to them

are properly fitted and worn and maintained in clean and tidy condition at all times.

16 Repealed. 4 Oct 2024 SR 76/2024 s15.

### Tunics

17(1) Uniformed members are to wear tunics when attending the following

functions in uniform:

(a) formal parades and other occasions that are ordered by the chief;

(b) recruit graduation at the Saskatchewan Police College;

(2) A chief attending the Saskatchewan Police College for recruit training is to wear

the uniform and accessories of a constable and will be considered a police constable

during the period in training and graduation.

### Ties

18 All ranks are to wear ties, dark navy blue or black in colour, at all times when

in uniform, or while wearing a tunic, storm coat or parka.

## PART IV

### Ranks, Insignias, Service and Identification Badges

### Ranks

20 A service may have all or any of the following police ranks but no others:

(a) chief;

(b) Deputy Chief of Police;

(c) Superintendent;

(d) Inspector;

(e) Staff Sergeant;

(f) Sergeant;

(g) Corporal;

(h) Constable; and

(i) Special Constable.

### Insignia for chiefs

21(1) Subject to subsection (2), members of the following ranks are to wear on

their shoulder straps the insignia described as follows:

(a) chief, crown above three stars;

(b) Deputy Chief of Police, crown above two stars;

(c) Superintendent, crown above one star;

(d) Inspector, crown.

(2) The chief of a police service that has an establishment of:

(a) not more than 50 but not less than 26 persons is to wear crown above two

stars as an insignia;

(b) not more than 25 but not less than six persons is to wear a crown above

one star as an insignia; and

(c) five persons or less shall wear a crown as an insignia.

### Display of insignia

22(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), the following insignia must be worn on the

upper part of the right sleeve of the shirt, between the elbow and shoulder seam:

(a) in the case of a Sergeant, three chevrons and a crown;

(b) in the case of a Corporal, two chevrons;

(c) in the case of a senior constable of more than 10 years’ service, a single

chevron.

(2) A staff sergeant shall wear an insignia of four inverted chevrons on the right

sleeve of the shirt between the cuff and elbow.

(3) The insignias mentioned in subsections (1) and (2) are to be displayed on

epaulets for shirts and jackets worn by the members.

(4) A special constable shall display the words “Special Constable” on epaulets or

beneath shoulder flashes.

23 Repealed. 4 Oct 2024 SR 76/2024 s18.

### Lapel badges

24 Every member is to wear a badge on the lapel of the tunic depicting the

municipal crest of the municipality or a crest designated by the commission.

### Shoulder flashes

25 Every member other than a commissioned officer is to wear, and every

commissioned officer may wear, shoulder flashes:

(a) on the uppermost portion of both sleeves of all garments with sleeves

except rain wear; and

(b) identifying the police service of which he or she is a member.

### Design of shoulder flashes

26 The shoulder flashes to be worn by members of a police service are to be of a

design approved by the board.

### Service badges

27(1) A member may wear on the member’s tunic or jacket one or more badges:

(a) in the design of a maple leaf with one badge representing five years of

service; and

(b) fastened 8.89 centimetres from the bottom of the left sleeve.

(2) A member who has been awarded federal or provincial recognition for long

service may wear the badge or other indication of long service in addition to or

instead of the badges mentioned in subsection (1).

### Single Chevron

27.1 A senior constable of more than 10 years’ may wear a single chevron on a

coat or shirt epaulette.

### Identification badges and numbers

28(1) Every member shall be issued a badge bearing the identification number of

the member’s police service after the member’s appointment.

(2) With respect to every member, other than a commissioned officer, who is in

uniform:

(a) the member shall prominently display the member’s identification number

above the left breast pocket of the shirt, jacket or body protector; and

(b) the member’s identification number must be displayed in silver, grey,

gold or white.

### Recognition insignia

28.1 The chief may authorize a member to wear an insignia that recognizes:

(a) any special duty of the member; or

(b) any award provided to the member by his or her police service.

### Firearm badges

29 A constable or non-commissioned officer may wear a badge indicating proficiency

in the use of firearms:

(a) attached to the left sleeve of the tunic or jacket; and

(b) above any badges described in section 27 worn by the member.

### Undress ribands

29.1 All ranks may wear, immediately above the left breast pocket of the shirt,

undress ribands of Orders, Decorations and Medals recognized by the Sovereign

and listed in the Canadian Order of Precedence of Orders, Decorations and Medals.

### Identification card

30(1) Subject to subsection (2), the board of a police service shall issue to each

member after his or her appointment a wallet-sized identification card that is tamper

resistant, which shall be signed by the chief of the police service.

(2) In the case of the chief, the identification card described in subsection (1) shall

be signed by the chairperson or vice-chairperson of the board of the police service

instead of the chief.

(3) An identification card issued pursuant to subsection (1) or (2) must contain

the following information:

(a) the name of the municipality that established the board;

(b) the police service;

(c) the name and rank of the member;

(d) a photograph of the member;

(e) the date of issuance of the identification card.

(4) The specific design of an identification card issued pursuant to this section

must be approved by the commission.

(5) A new identification card is to be issued to each member on a change of rank

of the member or every five years, whichever comes first.

### Holders

31 A black wallet-size holder designed to carry an identification card and badge

shall be issued to all members.

## PART V

### General

### Styles, etc., of dress

32 The styles and character of formal and dress uniforms of members are at the

discretion of the chief and subject to the approval of the commission.

### Supply of clothing and accessories

33(1) Any articles of clothing and accessories required by these regulations are to

be supplied to each member of a police service by the municipality, or other authority

authorized to establish a police service pursuant to the Act, that is served by the

police service.

(2) The replacement of articles of clothing and accessories is to be in accordance

with the terms of any local agreement between the board and the police service.

### Dress of Director, etc.

34 The Director of Training and the Assistant Director of Training and any other

employees at the Saskatchewan Police College that are designated by the commission

may wear articles of clothing and rank insignia described in these regulations when

approved by the commission if the clothing bears a clearly visible identification

with the college.

### Dress of special constables

35 Unless authorized by the commission, a Special Constable appointed under

section 76 of the Act to enforce bylaws only shall not wear the articles of uniform

or the accessories described in these regulations.

### Sask. Reg. 90/81 repealed

36 Saskatchewan Regulations 90/81 are repealed.
