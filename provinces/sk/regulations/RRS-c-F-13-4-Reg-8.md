RRS c F-13.4 Reg 8

*Made under The Financial Administration Act, 1993*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Gaming Licensing Exemption and

Remission Regulations.

### Exemption granted

2 Exemption is granted to holders and former holders of:

(a) a class B bingo licence from liability to pay any amount pursuant to

subclause 3(1)(b)(ii) of The Gaming Licensing Regulations;

(b) a class C bingo licence from liability to pay any amount pursuant to

subclause 3(1)(c)(ii) of The Gaming Licensing Regulations;

(c) a licence to manage and conduct a break-open lottery from liability to

pay any amount pursuant to subclause 3(1)(d)(ii) of The Gaming Licensing

Regulations;

(d) a licence to manage and conduct a raffle in which the total value of all

prizes awarded is in excess of the lesser of $2,000 and any amount prescribed

pursuant to subclause 98.2(1)(b)(ii) of The Alcohol and Gaming Regulation

Act from liability to pay any amount pursuant to subclause 3(1)(e)(ii) of The

Gaming Licensing Regulations;

(e) a bingo licence from liability to pay any amount incurred on or after

April 1, 1994 pursuant to subclause 3(1)(a)(ii) of The Saskatchewan Gaming

Regulations;

(f) a break-open ticket licence from liability to pay any amount incurred on

or after April 1, 1994 pursuant to subclause 3(1)(b)(ii) of The Saskatchewan

Gaming Regulations; and

(g) a raffle licence from liability to pay any amount incurred on or after

April 1, 1994 pursuant to subclause 3(1)(c)(ii) of The Saskatchewan Gaming

Regulations.

### Remission granted

3 Remission is granted to holders and former holders of:

(a) a class B bingo licence for any amount paid pursuant to sub-

clause 3(1)(b)(ii) of The Gaming Licensing Regulations;

(b) a class C bingo licence for any amount paid pursuant to sub-

clause 3(1)(c)(ii) of The Gaming Licensing Regulations;

(c) a licence to manage and conduct a break-open lottery for any amount

paid pursuant to subclause 3(1)(d)(ii) of The Gaming Licensing Regulations;

### QP

(d) a licence to manage and conduct a raffle in which the total value of all

prizes awarded is in excess of the lesser of $2,000 and any amount prescribed

pursuant to subclause 98.2(1)(b)(ii) of The Alcohol and Gaming Regulation Act

for any amount paid pursuant to subclause 3(1)(e)(ii) of The Gaming Licensing

Regulations;

(e) a bingo licence for any amount paid on or after April 1, 1994 pursuant to

subclause 3(1)(a)(ii) of The Saskatchewan Gaming Regulations;

(f) a break-open ticket licence for any amount paid on or after April 1, 1994

pursuant to subclause 3(1)(b)(ii) of The Saskatchewan Gaming Regulations; and

(g) a raffle licence for any amount paid on or after April 1, 1994 pursuant to

subclause 3(1)(c)(ii) of The Saskatchewan Gaming Regulations.

### References to regulations

4(1) A reference in these regulations to The Gaming Licensing Regulations is a

reference to those regulations as they existed on the day before the coming into force

of The Gaming Licensing Amendment Regulations, 1995.

(2) A reference in these regulations to The Saskatchewan Gaming Regulations is

a reference to those regulations as they existed prior to the coming into force of The

Gaming Licensing Regulations.
