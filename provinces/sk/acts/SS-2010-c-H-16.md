SS 2010, c H-16

An Act respecting Hunting, Fishing and Trapping

### Preamble

### Preamble

WHEREAS hunting, fishing and trapping have played important roles in shaping

Saskatchewan’s social, cultural and economic heritage;

WHEREAS hunters, fishers and trappers have made important contributions to

the understanding, conservation, restoration and management of Saskatchewan’s

fish and wildlife resources;

WHEREAS the best traditions of hunting, fishing and trapping should be valued

by future generations;

AND WHEREAS, it is desirable to observe a day to celebrate hunting, fishing and

trapping as an integral part of Saskatchewan’s heritage;

THEREFORE HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative

Assembly of Saskatchewan, enacts as follows:

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Hunting, Fishing and Trapping Heritage Act.

### Right to hunt, fish and trap

2(1) A person has the right to hunt, fish and trap in accordance with the law as it

exists from time to time.

(2) The reference to the law in subsection (1) includes The Wildlife Act, 1998, The

Fisheries (Saskatchewan) Act, 2020, the Fisheries Act (Canada), the Migratory Birds

Convention Act, 1994 (Canada) and the regulations made pursuant to those Acts.

### Non-derogation of Aboriginal rights

3 Nothing in this Act abrogates or derogates from any existing Aboriginal and treaty

rights to hunt, fish or trap of the Aboriginal peoples of Canada that are recognized

and affirmed by section 35 of the Constitution Act, 1982.

### Recognition of Hunting, Fishing and Trapping Heritage Day

4 November 15 in each year is designated as “Hunting, Fishing and Trapping

Heritage Day” in Saskatchewan.

### Coming into force

5 This Act comes into force on assent.
