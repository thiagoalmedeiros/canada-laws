SS 2002, c F-13.101

An Act to recognize Fetal Alcohol Syndrome Day in Saskatchewan

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Fetal Alcohol Syndrome Awareness Day Act.

### Recognition of Fetal Alcohol Syndrome Day

2 September 9 of each year is designated as Fetal Alcohol Syndrome Awareness

Day in Saskatchewan.

### Coming into force

3 This Act comes into force on assent.
