SS 1992, c T-1.2

An Act to declare a Day of Appreciation

for Scottish Clans in Canada

### Preamble

### Preamble

WHEREAS April 6th was chosen as “Tartan Day” by the Federation of Scottish

Clans in Nova Scotia;

AND WHEREAS this date is commemorative of the Scots who came to Canada,

many in duress, and went on to establish a great country and became good citizens;

AND WHEREAS this date is recognized in Nova Scotia and Ontario by Acts of those

respective Assemblies;

AND WHEREAS it is appropriate that Saskatchewan join in honouring these

Canadians:

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Tartan Day Act.

### April 6 Tartan Day

2 April 6 each year is hereby declared to be “Tartan Day” in Saskatchewan in

appreciation of the Scottish Clans in Canada.
