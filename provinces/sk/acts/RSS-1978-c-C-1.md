RSS 1978, c C-1

An Act respecting the Canadian Institute of Management

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Canadian Institute of Management (Saskatchewan

Division) Act.

### Incorporation

2 The Canadian Institute of Management (Saskatchewan Division), hereinafter

called the institute, is hereby continued as a body corporate and politic.

### Head office

3 The head office of the institute shall be at Saskatoon or at such other place in

the province as may be determined by bylaw.

### Power to establish branches

4 The institute may establish chapters or branches in various centres within the

province for the social and educational welfare of its members, with such powers

as may, from time to time, be determined by bylaw.

### Power to hold land

5 The institute may acquire, hold and dispose of such real and personal property

or any interest therein as is required for its purposes.

### Power to borrow

6 The institute may borrow money for the purposes of the institute and give security

for any money so borrowed on any of the real or personal property of the institute

by way of mortgage, pledge, charge or in any other manner.

### Objects

7 The general objects of the institute shall be to promote and encourage the

advancement of knowledge in the field of management and administration and to

maintain and improve the standards of achievement in the field of management

and administration by means including educational courses, research, periodical

conferences and publications.

### Power to establish lectures and classes

8 The institute may establish lectures and classes for students, or may enter into

agreements with the governing body of any university or college in Saskatchewan

for the participation of students in such lectures and classes and in correspondence

courses, as may come within the ambit of subjects prescribed by the bylaws.

### Benevolent fund

9 The institute may establish and administer a benevolent fund for the benefit of

any members or the families of members who may require financial assistance, and

for that purpose may make and receive contributions and donations.

### Power to affiliate

10 The institute may enter into contracts, in accordance with the bylaws, to affiliate

with any body or bodies of managers or administrators incorporated by or under any

Act of the Government of Canada or of any province thereof for the mutual benefit

of its members and the establishment of uniform qualifications and examinations.

### Membership

11(1) The membership of the institute shall be classified as general members and

registered members, with such other classification of members as may be authorized

by the bylaws.

(2) General membership shall be available to anyone eighteen years of age or over

who is engaged or interested in management and administration and complies with

the bylaws.

(3) Registered members shall consist of those general members who pass

examinations of the institute as prescribed under section 16 or otherwise fulfil the

requirements of the bylaws.

### Record and register of members

12(1) A record should be kept at the head office containing the names of all general

members.

(2) A register shall be kept at the head office in which shall be registered the names

and addresses of all registered members.

(3) The record and register shall be open for inspection by the public at all

reasonable times.

(4) The register mentioned in subsection (2) may be made available in any other

manner acceptable to the board, including an electronic format.

### Officers and Board of Directors

13(1) The officers of the institute shall consist of a president, first vice-president,

second vice-president and third vice-president, secretary and a treasurer, who shall

be directors, and who shall perform such duties as may be assigned to them from

time to time by the bylaws.

(2) The affairs and business of the institute shall be administered by a Board of

Directors, hereinafter called the board, consisting of at least ten members, including

the officers, five of whom shall constitute a quorum and who shall hold office for a

term of one year or until their successors are elected; and any member of the board

may be re-elected.

### Meetings, annual and special

14(1) A general meeting of the members of the institute shall be held annually

at such time and place and upon such notice as is provided in the bylaws for the

purpose of electing the board and officers and for such other business as may be

brought before the meeting.

(2) Special general meetings of the members may also be called and held in

accordance with the bylaws of the institute.

### Institute to have powers of a society

15 In addition to the powers by law vested in a corporation the institute shall have

all the powers given to societies incorporated under The Societies Act.

### Power to pass bylaws

16(1) The board may pass bylaws and from time to time pass amendments to bylaws

but no such bylaw or amendment thereto shall come into force until approved by

an annual general meeting of the institute, or at a special general meeting thereof,

called for the purpose of considering the bylaw or amendment.

(2) Any bylaw passed by the board may be repealed, amended, varied or otherwise

dealt with by the institute at any annual general meeting or at a special general

meeting called for the purpose.

(3) Without limiting the generality of its powers, the board may pass bylaws:

(a) to prescribe a curriculum and the courses of studies to be pursued by

members of the institute in management and administration and all matters,

conditions and requirements respecting examinations and the subjects upon

which candidates for admission to registered membership shall be examined

and for granting certificates to registered members;

(b) to establish and to collaborate with educational institutions in establishing

lectures, classes and examinations for members of the institute in the field of

management and administration;

(c) to authorize agreements between the institute and any university, college

or school for the establishment of lecturers, classes and examinations;

(d) to prescribe the rights and obligations of its various classes of membership;

(e) to regulate the admission, the qualification, the conduct, the suspension

and the expulsion of members of the institute;

(f) to fix the admission and annual fees to be paid by the members and the

examination fee to be paid by applicants for the institute’s examinations;

(g) to provide for the appointment, functions, duties, remuneration and

removal of officers and employees of the institute;

(h) to establish chapters of the institute within Saskatchewan and to enrol

members of the institute;

(i) to affiliate with any other body, corporate or unincorporate, having objects

similar to those of the institute;

(j) to govern the election of members of the board and the officers;

(k) to regulate the calling and holding of meetings of members of the institute

and of its board and the procedure in all things at such meetings; and

(l) generally to carry out the purposes of this Act.

(4) The secretary of the institute shall send a copy of every bylaw to the Registrar

of Corporations within one week after the passing thereof.

### Right to use designation “Certified Industrial Manager”

17(1) A registered member of the institute in good standing has the right to use

the designation “Certified Industrial Manager” and may use after his name the

letters “C.I.M.”, indicating that he is a certified industrial manager.

(2) Every person who is not a registered member of the institute in good standing

and who:

(a) uses or makes use of the designation “Certified Industrial Manager” or

the letters “C.I.M.”; or

(b) uses or makes use of any name, title or description that implies that he

is a registered member of the institute;

is guilty of an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding

twenty-five dollars.

### Right to practise as manager or administrator not affected

18 This Act does not affect or interfere with the right of any person to act or to

carry on the business in Saskatchewan as a manager or administrator or to designate

himself as such.

### Profits to be retained by institute

19 Subject to the provisions of section 8, any profits derived from carrying on the

affairs and business of the institute shall be devoted and applied solely in promoting

and carrying out the objects and purposes of the institute and shall not be divided

among its members.

20 Repealed. 1979, c69, s5.

### Effect of ceasing to be a member

21 If a persons ceases, for any cause whatsoever, to be a member of the institute,

he shall not, nor shall his representatives, have any interest in or claim against

the funds and property of the institute because or by reason of his membership in

the institute.
