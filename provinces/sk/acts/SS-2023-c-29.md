SS 2023, c 29

An Act respecting King’s Counsel and Precedence at the Bar

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The King’s Counsel Act.

### Appointment

2 The Lieutenant Governor in Council may authorize the issue of letters patent

under the Great Seal for the appointment during pleasure of members of the Bar

of Saskatchewan residing in Saskatchewan as provincial officers under the name

of His Majesty’s counsel learned in the law for Saskatchewan.

### Exception and limitation

3 Other than a person who holds or has held the office of Attorney General or

Solicitor General of Canada or Attorney General or Deputy Attorney General for

Saskatchewan, a person is not eligible to be appointed pursuant to section 2 unless

that person has been entitled during 10 years to practise in the superior courts

of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland or of any province

or territory of Canada or for a portion of that period in one and for the remaining

portion of that period in another or others of those courts.

### Precedence of certain officials

4 The following members of the Bar of Saskatchewan have precedence in the courts

of Saskatchewan in the following order:

(a) the Attorney General for Saskatchewan for the time being;

(b) the members of the Bar of Saskatchewan who have filled the office of

Attorney General of Canada or of Attorney General for Saskatchewan according

to seniority of appointment as Attorney General;

(c) the members of the Bar of Saskatchewan who have filled the office

of Solicitor General of Canada according to seniority of appointment as

Solicitor General.

### Precedence of King’s Counsel

5 Members of the Bar of Saskatchewan appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in

Council to be His Majesty’s counsel learned in the law for Saskatchewan severally

have the precedence in the courts of Saskatchewan that is assigned to them by the

Lieutenant Governor in Council under the Great Seal.

### Precedence of other members of the Bar

6 The remaining members of the Bar of Saskatchewan as between themselves

have precedence in the courts of Saskatchewan in the order of their enrolment.

### Crown counsel

7(1) Nothing in this Act affects or alters any right of precedence that applies to a

member of the Bar of Saskatchewan when acting as counsel for His Majesty or for

any Attorney General of His Majesty in any matter in the name of His Majesty or

of the Attorney General before the courts.

(2) The right and precedence mentioned in subsection (1) remains as if this Act

had not been passed.

### Revocation of appointment

8 If a person who has been appointed to be one of His Majesty’s counsel learned in

the law for Saskatchewan is disbarred pursuant to The Legal Profession Act, 1990 for

any cause other than non-payment of fees payable by that person to the law society:

(a) the appointment is, on disbarment, automatically revoked and that person

shall promptly forward the person’s letters patent to the Attorney General for

Saskatchewan; and

(b) if the person fails to forward the letters patent pursuant to clause (a),

the person is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine

not exceeding $50.

### RSS 1978, c Q-2 repealed

9 The Queen’s Counsel Act is repealed.

### Coming into force

10 This Act comes into force on assent.
