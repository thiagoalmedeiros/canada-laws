SS 2006, c P-15.02

An Act respecting a Memorial Day to honour Police and Peace Officers

### Preamble

### Preamble

WHEREAS Saskatchewan police and peace officers make valuable contributions to

the safety of Saskatchewan citizens;

AND WHEREAS Saskatchewan is proud of the selfless contributions of fallen

police and peace officers and honours those police and peace officers who serve

Saskatchewan;

THEREFORE HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative

Assembly of Saskatchewan, enacts as follows:

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Police and Peace Officers’ Memorial Day Act.

### Last Sunday in September to be memorial day

2 The last Sunday of September of each year is designated as “Police and Peace

Officers’ Memorial Day” in Saskatchewan.

### Coming into force

3 This Act comes into force on assent.
