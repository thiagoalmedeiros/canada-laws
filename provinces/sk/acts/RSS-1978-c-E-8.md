RSS 1978, c E-8

An Act respecting Emergency Medical Aid

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Emergency Medical Aid Act.

### Interpretation

2 In this Act:

(a) “physician” means a legally qualified medical practitioner within the

meaning of the law of any province of Canada;

(b) “registered nurse” means a person who is a registered nurse under The

Registered Nurses Act.

### Non-liability for the provision of emergency

### medical services or first-aid assistance

3 Where, in respect of a person who is ill, injured or unconscious as a result of an

accident or other emergency:

(a) a physician or registered nurse voluntarily renders emergency medical

services or first-aid assistance and the services or assistance are not rendered at

a hospital or other place having adequate medical facilities and equipment; or

(b) a person other than a person mentioned in clause (a) voluntarily renders

emergency first-aid assistance and that assistance is rendered at the immediate

scene of the accident or emergency;

the physician, registered nurse or other person is not liable for damages for injuries

to or the death of that person alleged to have been caused by an act or omission on

the part of the physician, registered nurse or other person rendering the medical

services or first-aid assistance, unless it is established that the injuries or death

were caused by gross negligence on his part.
