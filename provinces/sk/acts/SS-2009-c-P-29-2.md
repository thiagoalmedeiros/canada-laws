SS 2009, c P-29.2

An Act to protect the Wild Ponies of the Bronson Forest

### Short title

1 The Protection of the Wild Ponies of the Bronson Forest Act.

### Protection

2 The wild ponies of the Bronson Forest are hereby protected.

### Prohibition

3 No person shall in any way willfully molest, interfere with, hurt, capture or kill

any of the wild ponies of the Bronson Forest.

### Exception

4 Notwithstanding section 3, the Minister of Environment may make any order

that the minister deems appropriate with respect to the wild ponies of the Bronson

Forest.

### Penalty

5 Every person who violates section 3 is guilty of an offence and is liable on

summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $1,000 or to imprisonment for a term

not exceeding two months, or to both.

### Aboriginal rights

6 Nothing in this Act shall be construed or interpreted so as to abrogate or derogate,

directly or indirectly, any treaty or aboriginal rights recognized and affirmed by

subsection 35(1) of the Constitution Act, 1982.

### Coming into force

7 This Act comes into force on assent.
