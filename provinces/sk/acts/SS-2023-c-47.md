SS 2023, c 47

An Act respecting Remembrance Day and Observing a

period of Remembrance in Saskatchewan

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Saskatchewan Remembrance Observance Act.

### Definitions

2 In this Act:

“employer” means a person, firm, association or body that has one or more

workers in the service of the person, firm, association or body and includes any

person, firm, association or body prescribed in the regulations;

“poppy” means a poppy recognized by the Royal Canadian Legion;

“worker” means an individual who is engaged in the service of an employer

or a member of a category of individuals prescribed in the regulations.

### Right to wear a poppy

3(1) Every worker in Saskatchewan has the right to wear a poppy in the

workplace during the period in each year commencing on November 1 and ending

on November 11.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply if the wearing of a poppy may pose a danger or

hazard to the health, safety or welfare of any person.

### Regulations

4 The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations:

(a) defining, enlarging or restricting the meaning of any word or expression

used in this Act but not defined in this Act;

(b) prescribing persons, firms, associations or bodies for the purposes of the

definition of “employer” in section 2;

(c) prescribing categories of individuals for the purposes of the definition of

“worker” in section 2;

(d) prescribing any matter or thing required or authorized by this Act to be

prescribed in the regulations;

(e) respecting any other matter or thing the Lieutenant Governor in Council

considers necessary to carry out the intent of this Act.

### Coming into force

5 This Act comes into force on assent.
