CNLR 982/96

*Made under the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and Young Persons Offences Act*

Under the authority of subsection 24.1(1) and section 7 of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and subsection 16(2) of the Young Persons Offences Act, the Newfoundland and Labrador Youth Centre at Whitbourne is designated as a secure custody facility and a place of temporary detention within the meaning of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and as a facility of custody and a place of temporary detention within the meaning of the Young Persons Offences Act.

Under section 2.1 of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and paragraph 2(d) of the Young Persons Offences Act, the Administrator of the Community Corrections Services and the Administrator of the Newfoundland and Labrador Youth Centre are Provincial Directors for the purposes of administrating those Acts.

Under the authority of subsection 7(1) of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and section 16 of the Young Persons Offences Act, the Whitbourne Youth Centre is designated as a place of temporary detention within the meaning of the Young Offenders Act (Canada).

Under the authority of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and the Young Persons Offences Act, the Director of Youth Corrections is designated as the Provincial Director for the purposes of those Acts.

Under the authority of section 2 of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and the Young Persons Offences Act, the Whitbourne Training School is designated as a secure custody facility for the purposes of those Acts.

Under the authority of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and the Young Persons Offences Act, foster homes in the province are designated as open custody facilities for the purposes of those Acts.

The Director of Child Welfare appointed under the Child Welfare Act is designated as a provincial director for the purpose of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and the Young Persons Offences Act.

The following Orders are repealed:

Order re: Designation of Facility of Secure Custody and Designation of Facility of Temporary Detention, Newfoundland Regulation 255/91;

Order re: Designation of Provincial Directors, Newfoundland Regulation 254/91;

Order re: Designation of Facility of Temporary Detention, Newfoundland Regulation 175/89;

Order re: Provincial Director for the Purposes of the Young Offenders Act (Canada) and The Young Persons Offences Act, Newfoundland Regulation 282/85;

Order re "Secure Custody Facility", Newfoundland Regulation 115/85;

Order designating foster homes as "open custody facilities", Newfoundland Regulation 67/85.
