CNLR 744/96

*Made under the Judicature Act*

Section 132 of the Judicature Act, respecting the resealing by the Supreme Court of foreign grants of probate and of administration, shall apply to the Canadian Provinces of British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia and Prince Edward Island and to the Northwest Territories and the Yukon Territory.

The Resealing of Foreign Grants of Probate and Administration Order, Newfoundland Regulation 194/87, is repealed.
