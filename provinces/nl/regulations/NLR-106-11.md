NLR 106/11

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Order Respecting a Feasibility Report for Benton.

### Feasibility report

2 (1) A feasibility report shall be prepared to consider the annexation of the Local Service District of Benton to the Town of Gander.

(2) George Whey of Whey Consulting is appointed to prepare the report.

(3) The report shall be prepared according to the provisions of the Municipalities Act, 1999 and the Terms of Reference for the Report.
