CNLR 790/96

*Made under the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act*

States declared to be reciprocating states:

Alberta

Ashmore and Cartier Islands Territory

Australian Antarctic Territory

Australian Capital Territory

British Columbia

Coral Sea Islands Territory

Heard and McDonald Islands Territory

Jervis Bay Territory

Manitoba

New Brunswick

New South Wales

Northern Territory of Australia

Northwest Territories

Nova Scotia

Nunavut

Ontario

Prince Edward Island

Queensland

Saskatchewan

South Australia

Tasmania

United Kingdom

Victoria

Western Australia

Yukon Territory

The List of Reciprocating States, Newfoundland Regulation 125/82, and the Order re: Reciprocating States, Newfoundland Regulation 110/92, are repealed.
