# Notice of Wellhead Protected Water Supply Area for the Local Service District of Ship Cove - Lower Cove - Jerry's Nose, Well Number 2

NLR 99/04

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Port au Port abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Local Service District of Ship Cove - Lower Cove - Jerrys Nose water supply Number 2 Well, having UTM co-ordinates northing 5,375,910 metres and easting 356,828 metres.

All co-ordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection
