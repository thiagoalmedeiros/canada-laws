NLR 43/26

*Made under the Wild Life Regulations and the Wild Life Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Open Seasons Hunting and Trapping Order, 2026-2027.

### Definition

2 In this Order, "regulations" means the Wild Life Regulations.

### Generally

3 A person shall not hunt, take or kill fur bearing animals, big game, small game or coyotes at any time unless

(a) the person holds a valid licence for the species being hunted;

(b) the person holds a valid licence for the area in which the person is hunting; and

(c) it is during the open season prescribed in this Order for the species and the area.

### Fur bearing animals – Newfoundland

5 (1) In this section, "fur management area" means the fur management areas described in Schedule A of the Fur Bearing Animals and Coyote Trapping and Shooting Order, Newfoundland and Labrador.

(2) The open season for the trapping and shooting of the following fur bearing animals and coyotes on the Island of Newfoundland in all fur management areas shall be:

| Beaver | October 20, 2026 to April 15, 2027 |
| --- | --- |
| Coyote | October 20, 2026 to February 1, 2027 |
| Ermine, weasel | October 20, 2026 to February 28, 2027 |
| Fox, coloured | October 20, 2026 to February 1, 2027 |
| Fox, white | October 20, 2026 to February 1, 2027 |
| Mink | November 1, 2026 to February 28, 2027 |
| Muskrat | October 20, 2026 to March 15, 2027 |
| Otter | October 20, 2026 to March 15, 2027 |
| Squirrel | October 20, 2026 to February 28, 2027 |
| Wolf | October 20, 2026 to February 1, 2027 |

(3) The open season for the trapping and shooting of lynx on the Island of Newfoundland in the following fur management areas shall be:

(a) January 1, 2027 to February 1, 2027 in the Lynx Zone A: Northern Region (Open); and

(b) January 15, 2027 to February 1, 2027 in the Lynx Zone B: Southern Region.

(4) Notwithstanding subsection (3), there shall be no open season for the trapping and shooting of lynx in

(a) Brunette Island, Fortune Bay; and

(b) Great Southern Harbour.

(7) A person who takes a wolf shall surrender the lower jawbone or complete skull of each wolf taken to the office specified by the Wildlife Division by May 14, 2027.

## PART II BIG GAME

### Black bear – Labrador

6 The open season for the trapping and shooting of black bear in Labrador, excluding Torngat Mountains National Park and Akami- Uapishk ᵁ- KakKasuak -Mealy Mountains National Park Reserve, shall be August 10, 2026 to November 30, 2026 and April 1, 2027 to July 13, 2027.

### Black bear – Newfoundland

7 (1) In this section, "black bear management area" means the black bear management areas described in the Schedule to the Black Bear Hunting and Trapping Order, Newfoundland and Labrador.

(2) The open season for the trapping and shooting of black bear on the Island of Newfoundland in all black bear management areas shall be September 12, 2026 to November 30, 2026 and May 1, 2027 to July 15, 2027.

(3) The open season for the shooting of black bear on the Island of Newfoundland in all black bear management areas by long bow or compound bow and arrow shall be August 29, 2026 to September 11, 2026.

### Caribou – Labrador

8 For the purpose of conservation, there shall be no open season in Labrador for the hunting of caribou.

### Caribou – Newfoundland

9 (1) In this section, "caribou management area" means the caribou management areas described in the Schedule to the Caribou Hunting Order, Newfoundland.

(2) The open season for the shooting of caribou on the Island of Newfoundland in the following caribou management areas shall be:

| 61 | September 12, 2026 to December 6, 2026 |
| 62 | September 12, 2026 to December 6, 2026 |
| 64 | September 12, 2026 to December 6, 2026 |
| 66 to 68 | September 12, 2026 to December 6, 2026 |
| 70 | September 19, 2026 to October 4, 2026 |
| 71A | September 12, 2026 to November 30, 2026 |
| 71B | September 12, 2026 to November 30, 2026 |
| 72 | September 12, 2026 to October 11, 2026 |
| 76 to 79 | September 12, 2026 to December 6, 2026 |

(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), the open season for the shooting of caribou on the Island of Newfoundland by long bow or compound bow and arrow in the caribou management areas listed in subsection (2), except caribou management areas 70, 71A, 71B and 72, shall be August 29, 2026 to September 11, 2026.

### Moose – Labrador

10 (1) In this section, "moose management area" means the moose management areas described in Schedule B of the Moose Hunting Order, Newfoundland and Labrador.

(2) The open season for the shooting of moose in Labrador in all moose management areas shall be September 12, 2026 to March 14, 2027.

(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), the open season for the shooting of moose in Labrador by long bow or compound bow and arrow in all moose management areas shall be August 29, 2026 to September 11, 2026.

### Moose – Newfoundland

11 (1) In this section, "moose management area" means the moose management areas described in Schedule A of the Moose Hunting Order, Newfoundland and Labrador.

(2) The open season for the shooting of moose on the Island of Newfoundland in all moose management areas shall be September 12, 2026 to December 31, 2026.

(3) The open season for the shooting of moose on the Island of Newfoundland by long bow or compound bow and arrow in all moose management areas shall be August 29, 2026 to September 11, 2026.

(4) Notwithstanding anything in this section, there shall be no open season for the shooting of moose in moose management area 43 for moose that are predominately white in colour.

### Polar bears – Labrador

12 The open season for the shooting of polar bear in Labrador in the area from Fish Cove Point in Groswater Bay to Cape Chidley shall be February 1, 2027 to June 30, 2027.

### Small game – Newfoundland

14 (1) In this section, "small game management area" means the small game management areas described in the Schedule to the Small Game Hunting and Snaring Order, Newfoundland and Labrador.

(2) The open season for the shooting of snowshoe hare on the Island of Newfoundland shall be

(a) October 3, 2026 to December 27, 2026 on Great Island;

(b) October 3, 2026 to November 1, 2026 on Little Bay Islands; and

(c) October 3, 2026 to March 14, 2027 on the remainder of the Island of Newfoundland except Bell Island in Conception Bay.

(3) The open season for the shooting of ptarmigan on the Island of Newfoundland in the following small game management areas shall be:

(a) September 19, 2026 to November 15, 2026 in the Burin Area and the Avalon/Swift Current Area;

(b) September 19, 2026 to November 22, 2026 in the Topsails Area; and

(c) September 19, 2026 to December 6, 2026 on the remainder of the Island of Newfoundland and the islands adjacent to the Island of Newfoundland.

(4) The open season for the shooting of ruffed grouse and spruce grouse on the Island of Newfoundland shall be September 19, 2026 to December 27, 2026.

(5) The open season for the shooting of red squirrel on the Island of Newfoundland shall be

(a) October 3, 2026 to December 27, 2026 on Great Island;

(b) October 3, 2026 to November 1, 2026 on Little Bay Islands; and

(c) October 3, 2026 to March 7, 2027 on the remainder of the Island of Newfoundland except Bell Island in Conception Bay.

(6) The open season for the snaring of snowshoe hare on the Island of Newfoundland shall be

(a) October 3, 2026 to December 27, 2026 on Great Island;

(b) October 3, 2026 to November 1, 2026 on Little Bay Islands; and

(c) October 3, 2026 to March 14, 2027 on the remainder of the Island of Newfoundland except Bell Island in Conception Bay, Glover Island in Grand Lake and the following small game management areas:

(i) the Pine Marten Study Area,

(ii) the Main River Study Area,

(iii) the Little Grand Lake Wild Life Reserve, and

(iv) the Little Grand Lake Provisional Ecological Reserve.

(7) The open season for the snaring of ptarmigan on the Island of Newfoundland shall be October 3, 2026 to March 14, 2027 except Bell Island in Conception Bay, Glover Island in Grand Lake and the following small game management areas:

(a) the Pine Marten Study Area;

(b) the Burin Area;

(c) the Main River Study Area;

(d) the Little Grand Lake Wild Life Reserve; and

(e) the Little Grand Lake Provisional Ecological Reserve.

(8) The open season for the snaring of ruffed grouse and spruce grouse on the Island of Newfoundland shall be October 3, 2026 to March 14, 2027 except Bell Island in Conception Bay, Glover Island in Grand Lake and the following small game management areas:

(a) the Pine Marten Study Area;

(b) the Main River Study Area;

(c) the Little Grand Lake Wild Life Reserve; and

(d) the Little Grand Lake Provisional Ecological Reserve.

(9) Notwithstanding anything in this section, there is no open season for the shooting or snaring of small game on

(a) Kelly's Island in Conception Bay; and

(b) Brunette Island in Fortune Bay.

## PART IV COYOTE

### Coyote – Newfoundland and Labrador

15 The open season for the shooting of coyote on the Island of Newfoundland and in Labrador under a coyote shooting licence shall be September 12, 2026 to July 15, 2027.

### Coyote – Labrador

16 (1) In this section, "fur management zone" means the fur management zones described in Schedule B of the Fur Bearing Animals and Coyote Trapping and Shooting Order, Newfoundland and Labrador.

(2) The open season for the trapping and shooting of coyote in Labrador in the following fur management zones shall be:

(a) October 15, 2026 to March 31, 2027 in the Labrador North Fur Zone; and

(b) November 1, 2026 to March 20, 2027 in the Labrador South Fur Zone.

### Coyote – Newfoundland

17 (1) In this section, "fur management area" means the fur management areas described in Schedule A of the Fur Bearing Animals and Coyote Trapping and Shooting Order, Newfoundland and Labrador.

(2) The open season for the trapping and shooting of coyote on the Island of Newfoundland in all fur management areas shall be October 20, 2026 to February 1, 2027.

### Repeal

18 The Open Seasons Hunting and Trapping Order, 2025-2026, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 76/25, is repealed.
