CNLR 1003/96

*Made under the Highway Traffic Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Roads Owned by the Churchill Falls (Labrador) Corporation Limited Order.

### Streets and roads described

2 The Highway Traffic Act shall apply to the following streets and roads of the Community of Churchill Falls that are owned and maintained by the Churchill Falls (Labrador) Corporation Limited:

(a) all the townsite and campsite streets and roads of Churchill Falls; and

(b) the Esker Road from the intersection with the Trans Labrador Highway to Esker.

### Repeal

3 The Order re Roads owned by Churchill Falls (Labrador) Corporation Limited, Newfoundland Regulation 86/95, is repealed.
