NLR 121/18

*Made under the Residential Tenancies Act, 2018*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Residential Tenancies Regulations.

### Persons who may give statement

2 The following persons or classes of persons may provide a statement under subparagraph 25(3)(a)(ii):

(a) a medical practitioner registered and licensed under the Medical Act, 2011;

(a.1) a physician assistant registered and licensed under the Medical Act, 2011;

(b) a licensed practical nurse, registered nurse, registered psychiatric nurse or nurse practitioner licensed under the Nurses Act;

(c) [Rep. by 2024 cN-5.1 s81]

(d) a social worker registered under the Social Workers Act;

(e) a psychologist registered under the Psychologists Act, 2005;

(f) a member of the Royal Newfoundland Constabulary or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police; and

(g) an individual employed by an agency or organization to assist individuals for whom the agency or organization provides accommodation in an emergency or transitional shelter because of violence or abuse.

### Commencement

3 These regulations come into force on January 1, 2019.
