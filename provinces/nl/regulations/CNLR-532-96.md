# Water - Notice of Protected Watershed Area of Bridger’s Cove

CNLR 532/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 910570 99° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 875 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 152° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 74° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 425 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 1° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 272° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 259° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Bridger's Cove Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 248/78, is repealed.
