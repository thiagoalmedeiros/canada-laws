NLR 61/04

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Burin-Placentia West abutted and bounded as follows:

Beginning at a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 660,969 metres and northing 5,252,487 metres;

Then running in a southeasterly direction for a distance of 355 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 661,292 metres and northing 5,252,339 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 721 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 661,063 metres and northing 5,251,655 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 332 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 660,785 metres and northing 5,251,474 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 567 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 660,220 metres and northing 5,251,424 metres;

All coordinates refer Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.

The Notice of Protected Watershed Area of Parker's Cove, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 673/96, is repealed.
