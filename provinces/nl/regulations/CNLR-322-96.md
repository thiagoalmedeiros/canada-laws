CNLR 322/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of New Perlican Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of New Perlican are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 746/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point in the eastern shoreline of Trinity Bay at Jeans Head at the north entrance to the harbour of New Perlican and then running south 88° 30' east 6,600 metres, more or less, to the most northern point of Tim Miller's Pond;

Then running along the eastern shoreline of Tim Miller's Pond to its most southern point;

Then running along the eastern bank of the river to the eastern shoreline of Murphy's First Pond;

Then running along the eastern shoreline to the most southern point in the southwest angle of Murphy's First Pond;

Then running north 87° west 6,800 metres, more or less, to Bacon Cove Head on the eastern shoreline of Trinity Bay;

Then running in a generally northerly direction along the eastern shoreline to Garlep Point;

Then running along the shoreline of the harbour of New Perlican to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
