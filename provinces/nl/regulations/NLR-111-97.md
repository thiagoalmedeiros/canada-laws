NLR 111/97

*Made under the Environment Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Bellevue abutted and bounded as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 22, 290,000 metres east 5,279,000 metres north 184° 00 grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 120° 00 grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 205° 00 grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 253° 00 grid azimuth for a distance of 475 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 302° 00 grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 58° 00 grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 86° 00 grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.
