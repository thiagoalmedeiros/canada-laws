NLR 41/14

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Harbour Main abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Town of Conception Harbour Old Road Drilled Well, having UTM coordinates northing 5,237,333 metres and easting 332,600 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 22 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
