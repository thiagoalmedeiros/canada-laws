CNLR 171/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Fleur de Lys Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Fleur de Lys are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 639/78 and 240/85 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the highwater mark on the shoreline of White Bay, that point being known as Cape Etat and having a military grid reference of 554524;

Then in a generally northeasterly and southeasterly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline of White Bay passing Hard Bay, Flat Bay Cove, Cape Crapaud, Fleur de Lys Harbour and Woody Cove a distance of approximately 24,125 metres to a point known as Paradise Point;

Then south 48° 30' west a distance of 3,200 metres, more or less;

Then north 89° west a distance of 2,400 metres, more or less, to the southern tip of an unnamed pond;

Then north 48° west a distance of 6,600 metres, more or less, to the point of commencement.

All bearings referred to grid north.

All bearings, as with distances given, are subject to approximation.
