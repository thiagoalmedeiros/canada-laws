CNLR 272/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Lewisporte Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Lewisporte are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 711/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning on the shore at Wigwam Point at the western entrance to Burnt Bay;

Then running south 68° 30' west 6,800 metres, more or less;

Then south 10° east 6,000 metres, more or less;

Then east 3,600 metres, more or less;

Then north 30° east 9,600 metres, more or less;

Then west 2,000 metres, more or less, to a point forming the southern entrance to Emily Cove;

Then following the shoreline of Burnt Bay around Lewisporte Harbour to the place of beginning at Wigwam Point.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
