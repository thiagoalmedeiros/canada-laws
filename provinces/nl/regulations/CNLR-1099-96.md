### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the West Brook Ecological Reserve Order.

### Area included

2 The area included in the West Brook Ecological Reserve is as set out in Schedule A.

## Schedule B

### Outline of West Brook Ecological Reserve Management Plan

The area known as West Brook in the vicinity of Springdale is established as an ecological reserve to preserve the Red Pine, the province's rarest indigenous coniferous species, and a portion of the Central Forest Ecoregion for scientific study and educational purposes. To accomplish this, there will be no removal of plants or other materials from this site except under specific permit, and there will be no development within the reserve. Scientific research at this site will be encouraged and educational use will be permitted where it does not conflict with the general objectives of preservation and scientific research.
