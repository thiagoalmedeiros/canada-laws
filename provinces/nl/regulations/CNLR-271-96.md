CNLR 271/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Lawn Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Lawn are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 709/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at the junction point of 2 old highroads leading to Lawn and then running south 22° west 2,700 metres to "East Head" at the eastern entrance to Great Lawn Harbour;

Then running in a generally northerly direction along the eastern shoreline of Great Lawn Harbour to the south side of the bridge spanning Northeast Arm;

Then running along the north shoreline of the harbour to the south side of the bridge spanning Northwest Arm;

Then running along the western shoreline of Great Lawn Harbour in a generally southerly direction to the mouth of a small brook;

Then running along the centre line of the brook in a generally westerly direction 450 metres;

Then running north 9° east 1,500 metres to a point on Lawn Brook;

Then running south 64° east 1,150 metres to a point at the centre of the main dam on East Lawn Brook;

Then running south 24° east 1,350 metres to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
