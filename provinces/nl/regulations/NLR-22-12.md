NLR 22/12

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Feasibility Report Respecting the Annexation of the Local Service District of Pleasantview to the Town of Point Leamington Order.

### Feasibility report

2 A feasibility report shall be prepared to consider the annexation of the Local Service District of Pleasantview to the Town of Point Leamington.

### Appointment

3 Robyn Bursey is appointed to prepare the feasibility report.

### Preparation of report

4 The feasibility report shall be prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Municipalities Act, 1999.
