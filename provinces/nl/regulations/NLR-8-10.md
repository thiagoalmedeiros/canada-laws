NLR 8/10

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Amalgamation

1 The Local Service District of North West Brook-Ivany's Cove and the Local Service District of Queen's Cove are amalgamated with the Local Service District of Random Sound West, in the electoral district of Trinity North.

### Local service district continued

2 The Local Service District of Random Sound West is continued as a local service district.

### Local service district committee continued

3 The Local Service District Committee of Random Sound West is continued as a local service district committee.

### Boundaries

4 The boundaries of the Local Service District of Random Sound West shall be the area comprising the following communities:

(a) Hillview;

(b) Adeytown;

(c) Hatchet Cove;

(d) St. Jones Within;

(e) North West Brook;

(f) Ivany's Cove; and

(g) Queen's Cove.

### Repeal

5 The following orders are repealed:

(a) Local Service District of Queen's Cove, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 380/96;

(b) Local Service District of North West Brook - Ivany's Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 51/99; and

(c) Local Service District of Random Sound West, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 31/09.
