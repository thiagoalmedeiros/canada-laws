CNLR 457/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Stephenville Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Stephenville are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 820/78 and 138/87 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at the mouth of Gadon's Brook and following that brook to a power transmission line and then along that line to the northeastern end of Island Pond;

Then running north 1,800 metres, more or less;

Then running east 3,950 metres, more or less;

Then running south 54° east 6,600 metres, more or less, to the top of a hill northeast of Gull Pond;

Then running south 21° west 2,380 metres, more or less, to the top of a hill south of Gull Pond;

Then running south 45° west 3,800 metres, more or less, to Indian Head;

Then following the coastline of St. George's Bay to the mouth of Gadon's Brook, the place of beginning.

All bearings being referred to UTM grid and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 mapping.
