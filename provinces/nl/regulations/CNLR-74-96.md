CNLR 74/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the community of Brown's Arm is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Brown's Arm.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall comprise the community of Brown's Arm and shall include an area which extends along Route 341 from its intersection at Brown's Arm for a distance of 1.25 kilometres in the direction of the community of Porterville.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 55/90 and 185/90 are repealed.
