# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Granfer’s Pond

CNLR 591/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21, 582000 metres east, 5269000 metres north, 144° 14' for a distance of 1,375 metres to the point of commencement;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 220° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,150 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 137° 14' grid azimuth for a distance of 325 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 262° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 375 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 342° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 44° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 73° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,000 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Granfer's Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Hermitage ? Sandyville, Newfoundland Regulations 56/84 and 15/95, are repealed.
