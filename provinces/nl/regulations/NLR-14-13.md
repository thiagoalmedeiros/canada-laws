NLR 14/13

*Made under the Urban and Rural Planning Act, 2000*

Beginning at point on the shoreline of North Broad Cove in an area known as The Bottom, that point being 430 metres, more or less, northeastwardly of the brook flowing from North Broad Cove Pond, and the northeast corner of the Terra Nova National Park boundary;

Then following the Terra Nova National Park boundary, in a northwesterly direction, a distance of 1665 metres, more or less;

Then due east a distance of 35 metres, more or less, to the most easterly point of the Goose Neck Pond protected water supply boundary;

Then in a generally northeasterly direction along the Goose Neck Pond protected water supply boundary to where the Goose Neck Pond protected water supply boundary crosses the Happy Adventure municipal boundary;

Then north 5° 16' east a distance of 268 metres, more or less, to the most northwesterly point on the Happy Adventure municipal boundary;

Then following the Happy Adventure municipal boundary to a point on the northern shoreline of Newman Sound, that point being the most southwesterly point on the Sandy Cove municipal boundary;

Then following along the sinuosities of the northern shoreline of Newman Sound in a generally westerly direction, via Bottom Cove and North Broad Cove, to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to Grid North and, as with distances given, are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50000 National Topographical Series (NTS) digital mapping; UTM, NAD 83.

The Happy Adventure Municipal Planning Area, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 84/04, is repealed.
