# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of South Side Brook

CNLR 702/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 717000 5199000 00° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 225 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 26° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 125 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 96° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,000 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 119° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 206° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 126° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,150 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 236° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 298° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 316° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,275 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The South Side Brook Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 220/83, is repealed.
