CNLR 205/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Greenspond Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Greenspond are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 658/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

All that area of land situate and being in the District of Bonavista North and known as Greenspond Island together with Ship Island lying adjacent to it and southwardly of Greenspond Island.
