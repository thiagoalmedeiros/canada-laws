NLR 107/04

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of The Straights and White Bay North abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metre radius with its centre being the Town of St. Lunaire - Griquet public water supply drilled well, having UTM co-ordinates northing 5,710,077 metres and easting 605,658 metres.

All co-ordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
