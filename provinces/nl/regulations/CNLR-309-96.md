CNLR 309/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Norris Point Order.

### Town continued

2 The area commonly known as Norris Point is continued as a town.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 754/78 is repealed.
