NLR 42/05

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Conception Bay South abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 700 metres radius with its centre being the Town of Holyrood water supply #3 Main Line Well, having UTM coordinates Northing 5,249,393 metres and Easting 339,747 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 22 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
