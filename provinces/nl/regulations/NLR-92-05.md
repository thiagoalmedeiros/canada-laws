NLR 92/05

*Made under the Municipal Affairs Act*

### Official administrator appointed

1 Mr. Thomas Easton is appointed as official administrator to manage and conduct the affairs of the Town of Indian Bay until a quorum of councillors is elected or appointed.

### Repeal

2 The Official Administrator for the Town of Indian Bay Order, Newfoundland Regulation 17/00, is repealed.
