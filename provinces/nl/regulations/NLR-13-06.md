# Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of George Ricks Pond

NLR 13/06

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Humber Valley abutted and bounded as follows:

Beginning at a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 505 751 metres and northing 5 509 964 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 108 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 05 671 metres and northing 5 509 891 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 150 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 505 667 metres and northing 5 509 741 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 219 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 505 552 metres and northing 5 509 555 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 307 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 505 246 metres and northing 5 509 530 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 543 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 504 710 metres and northing 5 509 445 metres;

Then running in a northerly direction for a distance of 363 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 504 592 metres and northing 5 509 788 metres;

Then running in a easterly direction for a distance of 434 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 505 019 metres and northing 5 509 863 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 509 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 505 375 metres and northing 5 510 227 metres;

Then running in a easterly direction for a distance of 347 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 505 720 metres and northing 5 510 267 metres;

Then running in a southeasterly direction for a distance of 220 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 505 881 metres and northing 5 510 117 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 201 metres, more or less, to the point of commencement.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
