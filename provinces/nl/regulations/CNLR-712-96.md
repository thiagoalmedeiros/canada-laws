# Water - Notice of Protected Watershed Area of Sugarloaf Hill Pond

CNLR 712/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21, 658000 metres east, 5266000 metres north, 85° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 825 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 43° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 84° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 965 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 159° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 628 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 222° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 645 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 261° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 547 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 268° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,255 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Sugarloaf Hill Pond Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 176/78, is repealed.
