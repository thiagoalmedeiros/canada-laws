# Building Near Highways Regulations, 1997

NLR 28/97

*Made under the Works, Services and Transportation Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Building Near Highways Regulations, 1997.

### Definition

2 In these regulations "minister" means the minister appointed under the Executive Council Act to administer the Works, Services and Transportation Act.

### Prohibition

3 A person shall not erect, repair, alter or structurally improve a fence, building or other structure, nor shall he or she plant trees, shrubs or hedges without the prior permission of the minister within

(a) 45 metres from the centre line of the proposed or existing highway described in Schedule A;

(b) 30 metres from the centre line of the proposed or existing highway described in Schedule B;

(c) 20 metres from the centre line of the proposed or existing highway described in Schedule C;

(d) 15 metres from the centre line of the proposed or existing highway described in Schedule D; or

(e) 10 metres from the centre line of the proposed or existing highway described in Schedule E.

### Centre line

8 For the purpose of these regulations, the centre line of the roadway is deemed to be:

(a) the centre line of any 2 lane highway; or

(b) the centre line of the nearest 2 lanes of travel of another highway.

### Penalty

9 A person who commits a breach of these regulations is liable on summary conviction to a fine of a minimum of $250 to a maximum of $5,000 or, in default of payment, to imprisonment for a term of a minimum of 2 days to a maximum of 2 months.

### Repeal

10 The Building Near Highways Regulations, Consolidated Newfoundland Regulation 1028/96, are repealed.
