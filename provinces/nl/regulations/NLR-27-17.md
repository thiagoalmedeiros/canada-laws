# Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of #3 Brian Pumphrey Well Highlands, WS-G-0880, Local Service District of Bay St. George South

NLR 27/17

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of St. George's - Humber abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Local Service District of Bay St. George South water supply #3 Brian Pumphrey Well Highlands, having UTM coordinates northing 5,338,870 metres and easting 358,611 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
