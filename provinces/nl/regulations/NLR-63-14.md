NLR 63/14

*Made under the Electrical Power Control Act, 1994 and the Public Utilities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Biogas Project Exemption Order.

### Definition

2 In this Order, "biogas project" means a biogas or landfill gas generating facility in the province.

### Exemption

3 Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro is exempt from the Electrical Power Control Act, 1994 and the Public Utilities Act for all aspects of its activities respecting the purchase of electrical power and energy from a biogas project under a power purchase agreement with a producer, including the integration of electrical power and energy from a biogas project into Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro's forecasting, system planning, system engineering and supply.
