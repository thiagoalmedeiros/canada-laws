# Local Service District of Hickman's Harbour-Robinson Bight

NLR 24/97

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### District established

1 The area comprising the communities of Hickman's Harbour and Robinson Bight is established as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Hickman's Harbour-Robinson Bight.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall be the area comprising the communities identified in the Newfoundland and Labrador section of the Canada Gazette commonly known as Hickman's Harbour and Robinson Bight.
