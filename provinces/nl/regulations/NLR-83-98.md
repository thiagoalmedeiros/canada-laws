NLR 83/98

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Port au Choix Order, 1998.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Port au Choix are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 The Town of Port au Choix Order, Consolidated Newfoundland Regulation 374/96, is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point, that point being the most westerly point of Point Riche at the highwater mark in the Gulf of St. Lawrence;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline in a generally northerly and easterly direction passing Black Point to Blanche Point;

Then north 71° 30, east 800 metres, more or less, to Laignet Point;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline in a generally northerly, easterly and southerly direction passing Catoche Point, Barbace Cove, Barbace Point to a point on the northern shoreline of Black Arm, that point having UTM co-ordinates north 5,518,950 metres, east 476,075 metres;

Then south 64° 00 east, 1,025 metres, more or less, to a point on the southern shoreline of Back Arm, that point having UTM co-ordinates north 5,518,500 metres, east 477,000 metres;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline in a generally northerly and easterly direction a distance of 5.8 kilometres, more or less, to a point at the mouth of an unnamed river flowing into St. John Bay and having UTM co-ordinates north 5,519,750 metres, east 481,500 metres;

Then south 5° 00 west a distance of 550 metres, more or less, to where the river crosses Highway 73;

Then south 16° 00 west a distance of 5,000 metres, more or less, to a point;

Then south 69° 30 west a distance of 1,900 metres, more or less, to the northeastern extremity of an unnamed pond;

Then along the sinuosities of the southern bank of the pond in a generally westerly direction to the most westerly extremity of the pond;

Then running along the eastern bank of a river which flows out of the unnamed pond and into the Ingornachoix Bay;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline in a generally northerly direction to the most easterly point on the southern shoreline of Gargamelle Cove having UTM co-ordinates north 5,516,200 metres, east 474,450 metres;

Then in an easterly direction to Point Riche, the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to grid north and as with co-ordinates and distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 National Topographic Mapping, Edition 3, Zone 21.
