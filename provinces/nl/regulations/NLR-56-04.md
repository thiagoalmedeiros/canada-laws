# Notice of Wellhead Protected Water Supply Area of the Town of Harbour Main-Chapel Cove-Lakeview, Flynns Hill Well

NLR 56/04

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Harbour Main - Whitbourne abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Town of Harbour Main - Chapel Cove - Lakeview water supply Flynns Hill Well, having UTM co-ordinates northing 5,253,029 metres and easting 337,721 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 22 of the NAD 27 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
