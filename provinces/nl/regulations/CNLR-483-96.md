CNLR 483/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of West St. Modeste Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of West St. Modeste are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 863/78 and 12/79 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point, that point being the highwater mark at Cape Diable;

Then north 78° west a distance of 2,584 metres, more or less, to the summit of an unnamed hill;

Then north 20° east a distance of 3,040 metres, more or less, to a point on the south bank of an unnamed river which flows to the east into Pinware Bay;

Then following the sinuosities of the south shore of the river in a northeasterly direction to a point at the mouth of the river, that point being the highwater mark on the shoreline of Pinware Bay;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline in a general southerly direction running along the Tickle, West St. Modeste and including St. Modeste Island to its point of commencement.

All bearings being referred to grid north.
