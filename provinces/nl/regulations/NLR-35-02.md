NLR 35/02

*Made under the Environment Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Belle vue abutted and bounded as follows:

By a line drawn from Military Grid Reference (Zone 21) 712,000 metres east 5,306,000 metres north 144° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 31° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 125° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 135° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 108° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 900 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 171° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 213° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 319° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,150 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 253° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 297° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 352° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.
