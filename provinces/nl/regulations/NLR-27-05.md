NLR 27/05

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Torngat Mountains abutted and bounded as follows:

Beginning at a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 357 942 metres and northing 6 104 374 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 465 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 358 120 metres and northing 6 104 803 metres;

Then running in a southeasterly direction for a distance of 885 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 358 933 metres and northing 6 104 455 metres;

Then running in a easterly direction for a distance of 1664 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 360 596 metres and northing 6 104 375 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 445 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 360 435 metres and northing 6 103 959 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 1701 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 360 750 metres and northing 6 102 288 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 1957 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 359 793 metres and northing 6 100 580 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 2747 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 360 283 metres and northing 6 097 877 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 1182 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 359 253 metres and northing 6 097 298 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 998 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 358 606 metres and northing 6 096 538 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 1280 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 357 363 metres and northing 6 096 843 metres;

Then running in a northerly direction for a distance of 1765 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 356 821 metres and northing 6 098 522 metres;

Then running in a northerly direction for a distance of 3900 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 358 109 metres and northing 6 102 203 metres;

Then running in a northerly direction for a distance of 2177 metres, more or less, to the point of commencement.

All coordinates refer Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
