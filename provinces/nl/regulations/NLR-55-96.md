### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of New-Wes-Valley Name Change Order.

### Name changed

2 The name of the Town of Badgers Quay - Valleyfield - Pools Island - Wesleyville - Newtown Order is changed to the Town of New-Wes-Valley.
