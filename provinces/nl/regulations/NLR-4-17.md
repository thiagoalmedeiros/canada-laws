NLR 4/17

*Made under the Schools Act, 1997*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Composition of Conseils d'Ecole Order.

### Conseil d'ecole composition

2 (1) There shall be a conseil d'ecole for each French first language school.

(2) Six members shall be elected to each conseil d'ecole.
