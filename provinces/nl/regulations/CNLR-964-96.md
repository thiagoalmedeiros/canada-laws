CNLR 964/96

*Made under the Mineral Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Mineral Act Baie Verte Area Exemption Regulations.

### Area exempted

2 The minerals in, on or under the lands and lands under water described in the Schedule are exempted from the application and operation of the Mineral Act.

### Repeal

3 The Mineral Act (Baie Verte Area) Exemption Regulations, 1982, Newfoundland Regulation 230/82, are repealed.

## Schedule

All that piece or parcel of land situate and being at Baie Verte in the province described as follows:

Beginning at a point being the southeast angle of land described in a licence issued by us to Advocate Mines Limited on September 1, 1966;

Then running east 393.155 metres and then south 28' east 1700.180 metres and then south 88° and 59' west 1733.63 metres and then north 28' west 907.088 metres and then north 52° 50' east 1366.364 metres, more or less, to a point in the southerly boundary of the land described in the previously mentioned licence issued by us to Advocate Mines Limited;

Then running south 89° 39' east 244.922 metres, more or less, to the point of beginning containing an area of 623.641 acres, that is 252.378 hectares.
