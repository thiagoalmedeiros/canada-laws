CNLR 578/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 574000 east, 5496000 north, 155° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,425 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 125° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 147° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 425 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 208° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 375 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 269° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 219° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,125 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 293° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 230° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 325 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 303° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 247° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 850 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 335° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 41° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 975 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 85° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 225 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 22° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 66° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The First Pond Brook Water Supply Area Notice for the Local Service District of Little Bay, Newfoundland Regulation 60/90, is repealed.
