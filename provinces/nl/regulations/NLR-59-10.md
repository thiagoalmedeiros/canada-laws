NLR 59/10

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of St. George's - Stephenville East abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Local Service District of Bay St. George South water supply #2B Lions Club Well McKays, having UTM coordinates northing 5,343,553 metres and easting 363,460 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 27 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
