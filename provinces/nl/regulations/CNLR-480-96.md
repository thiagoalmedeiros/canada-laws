CNLR 480/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the community of Valley Pond is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Valley Pond.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall be the area commonly known as Valley Pond and formerly known as Whales Gulch.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 166/87 is repealed.
