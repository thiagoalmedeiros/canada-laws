NLR 38/00

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of King 's Point Order, 2000.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of King 's Point are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 The Town of King's Point Order, Consolidated Newfoundland Regulation 245/96 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the highwater mark of the eastern shoreline of Southwest Arm at the mouth of an unnamed brook, having NAD 83, UTM co-ordinates north 5,494,584 metres and east 561,422 metres;

Then south 55 ° 57' east, 1,840 metres, more or less, to a point on Highway 391 where it crosses over South Brook;

Then south 64 ° 45' east, 625 metres, more or less, to a high point;

Then south 52 ° 30' west, 3,300 metres, more or less, to a high point;

Then south 39 ° 00' west, 3,950 metres, more or less to a high point;

Then south 63 ° 55' west, 626 metres, more or less, to a point at the intersection of Route 391 with an unnamed road (old Highway);

Then north 80 ° 15' west, 2,452 metres, more or less, to a point on Shoal Pond forest access road;

Then north 9 ° 15' east 800 metres, more or less, to a point on the south eastern boundary of a mining lease to Major General Resources Ltd. # 153 (3407) being registered June 24, 1998, Vol. 1, Fol. 50, at the Department of Mines and Energy;

Then following the boundary of the lease north 3 ° 27' 47" east, 200.09 metres, north 86 ° 33' 57" west, 400 metres, north 40 ° 7' 27" west, 204.14 metres, north 35 ° 37' 29" west, 318.5 metres, NAD 83 and bearings being referred to True North, to a point, having NAD 83, UTM co-ordinates north 5,489,841 metres and east 554,763 m;

Then north 17 ° 15' west, 2,575 metres, more or less to a high point;

Then north 37 ° 30' west, 1,050 metres, more or less, to a high point;

Then north 82 ° 00' west, 1,425 metres, more or less, to a high point;

Then north 17 ° 00' east, 1,500 metres, more or less, to a high point;

Then north 79 ° 30' east, 5,525 metres, more or less, to a point, Stearin Peak;

Then north 77 ° 31' east, 1,468 metres, more or less, to a point on the western shoreline of Southwest Arm at the mouth of Corner Brook where it flows into Corner Brook Cove;

Then in a southerly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline at the high water mark passing Kings Point to the southernmost extremity of Southwest Arm and then in a northerly direction along the western shoreline of Southwest Arm to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred Grid North and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 NTS mapping and with reference to the survey at the Department of Mines and Energy.
