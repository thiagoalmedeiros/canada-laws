# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Herring Neck

CNLR 599/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 000710 227° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 266° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 220° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 275° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 183° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 69° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 475 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 44° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 825 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Herring Neck Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 38/79, is repealed.
