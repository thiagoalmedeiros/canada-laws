NLR 20/11

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Port au Port abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Town of Port au Port West - Aguathuna - Felix Cove water supply #1 Marie Bell Well, having UTM coordinates northing 5,378,856 metres and easting 372,004 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
