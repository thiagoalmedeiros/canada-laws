# Water - Notice of Protected Watershed Area of Purcell’s Harbour

CNLR 676/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21, 662600 metres east, 5498800 metres north, 99° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,464 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 42° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,046 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 104° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,271 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 240° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 821 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 168° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,124 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 271° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,963 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 32° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,593 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 319° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 837 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Purcell's Harbour Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 167/78, is repealed.
