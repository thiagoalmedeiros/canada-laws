NLR 25/00

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### District established

1 The area comprising the community of Norman s Bay is established as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Norman s Bay.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall be the area comprising the community commonly known as Norman s Bay.

### Committee to be elected

3 A local service district committee shall be elected to control and manage the affairs of the local service district in accordance with the Municipalities Act, 1999 and the Local Service District Regulations.
