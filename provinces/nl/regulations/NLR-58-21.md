NLR 58/21

*Made under the Vital Statistics Act, 2009*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Vital Statistics Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations, "Act" means the Vital Statistics Act, 2009.

### Designated agent

3 A person may be a designated agent where

(a) the person is a natural person;

(b) the person is at least 19 years of age;

(c) the person is designated in writing, in the form set by the minister, by the person making the designation; and

(d) the person

(i) has known the person making the designation for at least one year and has signed a statement to that effect in the form set by the minister,

(ii) is a lawyer acting on behalf of the person making the designation, or

(iii) satisfies the registrar general that they are acting on behalf of or employed by a government, charitable or non-profit organization providing social support or social services to the person making the designation and has signed a statement to that effect in the form set by the minister.
