NLR 56/24

*Made under the Public Utilities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro – Town of Tilt Cove Exemption Order.

### Exemption

2 Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro is exempt from section 38 of the Public Utilities Act in respect of the discontinuance of service to the Town of Tilt Cove.
