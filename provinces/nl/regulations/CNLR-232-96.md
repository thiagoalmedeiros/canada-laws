CNLR 232/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Howley Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Howley are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 887/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the eastern shoreline of Grand Lake where the shoreline intersects the power transmission line near the entrance to the main brook;

Then running east 5,175 metres, more or less, to a point on the centre line of the Canadian National Railway;

Then running south 4,425 metres, more or less, to a point on the power transmission line;

Then running west 6,250 metres, more or less, to a point on the eastern shoreline of Grand Lake;

Then running in a generally northerly direction along the sinuosities of the eastern shoreline of Grand Lake to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
