CNLR 553/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 589000 5261000 263° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 172° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 135° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 242° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 221° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 250° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 675 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 295° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 40° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 324° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 43° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 71° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 27° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 78° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 122° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Connaigre Pond - Hutchings Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 87/88, is repealed.
