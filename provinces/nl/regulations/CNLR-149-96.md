CNLR 149/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the communities of Epworth and Great Salmonier is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Epworth - Great Salmonier.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall be the area comprising the communities commonly known as Epworth and Great Salmonier.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 31/86 is repealed.
