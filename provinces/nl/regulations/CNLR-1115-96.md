CNLR 1115/96

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Shops' Closing Regulations.

### Exempt shops

2 (1) For the purpose of paragraph 2(b) of the Shops' Closing Act, the following classes of shops are exempt shops:

(a) art galleries;

(b) automatic laundries;

(c) neighbourhood convenience stores;

(d) bookshops;

(e) newsstands;

(f) motor vehicle service stations;

(g) vehicle rental outlets;

(h) restaurants and food take-outs;

(i) video rental outlets;

(j) pharmacies;

(k) shops where the primary and most substantial business relates to the sale of fresh fruit, flowers, vegetables, plants or gardening supplies;

(l) shops where the primary and most substantial business relates to souvenirs or to crafts;

(m) music shops where the primary and most substantial business relates to the sale of recorded music such as records, compact disks and taped music;

(n) gift stores in hospitals or in an institution or facility which is under the management and control of a hospital board; and

(o) [Rep. by 25/20 s1]

(2) Paragraph (1)(o) shall be repealed on April 11, 2020.

### Repeal

3 The Shops' Closing Regulations, 1992, Newfoundland Regulation 6/93, are repealed.
