NLR 43/10

*Made under the Consumer Protection and Business Practices Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Insurance Contracts Exemption Regulations.

### Insurance contracts exemption

2 Division 2 of Part V of the Act does not apply to a contract as defined in the Insurance Companies Act.
