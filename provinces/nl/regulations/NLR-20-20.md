NLR 20/20

*Made under the Public Health Protection and Promotion Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Communicable Disease Orders Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations, "Act" means the Public Health Protection and Promotion Act.

### Communicable disease orders

3 (1) For the purposes of paragraphs 32(2)(o) and (5)(l) of the Act, a communicable disease order may

(a) prohibit or restrict the person from driving a vehicle; and

(b) require the person to surrender his or her driver's licence to a regional medical officer of health.

(2) A regional medical officer of health shall return a driver's licence surrendered in accordance with a communicable disease order upon termination or suspension of the communicable disease order.
