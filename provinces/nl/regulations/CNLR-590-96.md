CNLR 590/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 508000 5657000 42° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 850 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 23° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 07° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 32° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 357° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,100 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 28° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 103° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 32° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 71° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 180° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 212° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 199° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,900 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 296° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,350 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Grand Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 207/83, is repealed.
