NLR 45/10

*Made under the Pedestrian Trails Liability Protection Act*

### Short title

1 These Regulations may be cited as the Pedestrian Trails Liability Protection Regulations.

### Criteria for designation

2 A trail may be designated as a trail to which the Act applies where

(a) the trail, on Crown land or privately owned land, or both, has been established by a recognized person or group and is marked reasonably clearly as a trail for recreational hiking; and

(b) the trail complies with all standards set by the minister with respect to

(i) ownership,

(ii) insurance,

(iii) signage,

(iv) inspections, and

(v) maintenance.
