NLR 105/04

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Burgeo and La Poile abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 200 metres radius with its centre being the Local Service District of Margaree - Fox Roost water supply Number 1 Well, having UTM co-ordinates northing 5,271,502 metres and easting 345,726 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 27 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
