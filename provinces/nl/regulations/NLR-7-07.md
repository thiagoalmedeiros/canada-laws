# Notice of Wellhead Protected Water Supply Area of the Town of St. Joseph s, St. Josephs Wellfield, WS-G-0695

NLR 7/07

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Placentia & St. Marys abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 150 metres radius with its centre being the Town of St. Josephs water supply Backup Well #1, having UTM coordinates northing 5,220,908 metres and easting 307,927 metres.

All coordinates refer Zone 22 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
