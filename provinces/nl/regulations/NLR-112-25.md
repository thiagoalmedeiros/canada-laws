NLR 112/25

*Made under the Judgment Interest Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Judgment Interest Order, 2026.

### Interest rate

2 Effective January 1, 2026, the pre-judgment and post-judgment interest rate shall be 2%.
