CNLR 881/96

Beginning at a point, that point being where an unnamed brook flows into Kyer Cove;

Then south 72° 30' west a distance of 5,550 metres, more or less, to the summit of Black Hill;

Then north 86° 00' west a distance of 3,900 metres, more or less, to the summit of Birchy Hill;

Then north 76° 30' west a distance of 4,700 metres, more or less, to the summit of an unnamed hill;

Then north 28° 30' west a distance of 2,000 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being the southern bank of Trout River where Trout River flows into Fifty Islands Pond;

Then north 17° 00' east a distance of 2,750 metres, more or less, to the summit of an unnamed hill;

Then north 65° 00' east a distance of 5,550 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being on the southern bank of an unnamed brook where the unnamed brook flows into Effington Harbour;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline of St. Lewis Sound in a generally easterly direction to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to grid north.

Newfoundland Regulation 41/81 is repealed.
