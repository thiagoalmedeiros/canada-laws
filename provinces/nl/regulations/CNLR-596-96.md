# Water - Notice of Protected Watershed Area of Hant’s Harbour

CNLR 596/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 22, 329000 metres east, 5315000 metres north, 125° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 975 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 0° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,768 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 95° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,044 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 131° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,304 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 186° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,255 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 237° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,481 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 258° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 933 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 303° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 853 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Hant's Harbour Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 163/78, is repealed.
