CNLR 333/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Parkers Cove Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Parkers Cove are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 770/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point at the highwater mark in the western shoreline of Placentia Bay at the western entrance to Boat Harbour known as Manny Point;

Then running west 1,600 metres;

Then running north 2,470 metres;

Then running east 2,530 metres to the highwater mark at the mouth of Rattle Brook in the western shoreline of Boat Harbour;

Then running at the highwater mark in a generally southwesterly direction along the western shoreline of Boat Harbour to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
