NLR 71/99

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### District established

1 The area comprising the community of Rock Harbour is established as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Rock Harbour.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall be the area comprising the community of Rock Harbour.

### Committee to be elected

3 A local service district committee shall be elected to control and manage the affairs of the local service district according to the Municipalities Act and the Local Service District Regulations.
