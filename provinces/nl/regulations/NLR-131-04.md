NLR 131/04

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Lake Melville abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Town of North West River water supply Number 2 Well, having UTM co-ordinates northing 5,934,998 metres and easting 689,039 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 20 of the NAD 27 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
