# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Long Pond, Heart’s Delight

CNLR 634/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 930150 168° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,025 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 130° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 156° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 31° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,975 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 323° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 233° 50' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,500 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Long Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Heart's Delight, Newfoundland Regulation 251/78, is repealed.
