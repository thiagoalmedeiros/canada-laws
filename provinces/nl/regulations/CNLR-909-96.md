CNLR 909/96

Beginning at a point, that point being at the innermost penetration of the sea at Keppel Harbour;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline by way of Saunders Point, Port Saunders Harbour, Two Hills Point, to a point, that point being at the mouth of a river which flows into Ingornachoix Bay;

Then following the eastern bank of the river in a generally northerly direction to an unnamed pond;

Then following the southern shoreline of the pond in an easterly direction to its most northeasterly extremity;

Then north 69° 30' east a distance of 6,900 metres, more or less, to the northernmost extremity of Tilt Pond;

Then south 82° east a distance of 700 metres, more or less, to the most western extremity of an unnamed pond;

Then south 6° 30' east a distance of 4,250 metres, more or less, to the mouth of a river which flows into Gilmores Pond;

Then following the southeastern shoreline to the southernmost extremity of Gilmores Pond;

Then south 46° west a distance of 5,350 metres, more or less, to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.

Newfoundland Regulation 258/80 is repealed.
