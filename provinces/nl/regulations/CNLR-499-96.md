# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Anchor Pond, Port Anson

CNLR 499/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21, 585000 metres east, 5490000 metres north, 157° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,650 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 174° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 225 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 98° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 375 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 173° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 150 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 215° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 280° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 257° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 330° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 65° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Anchor Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Port Anson, Newfoundland Regulation 171/ 91, is repealed.
