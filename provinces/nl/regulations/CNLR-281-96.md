CNLR 281/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Meadows Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Meadows are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 739/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the centre line of Route 440 a distance of 275 metres, more or less, to the west of a stream which flows into Gum Cove;

Then north 21° 00' east a distance of 600 metres, more or less, to the summit of an unnamed hill, spot elevation 182.5 metres;

Then north 55° 30' west a distance of 2,650 metres, more or less, to the summit, elevation 183 metres, of an unnamed hill;

Then south 52° 30' west a distance of 1,375 metres, more or less, to the highwater mark on the shoreline of the Humber Arm;

Then in a generally southeasterly direction following the sinuosities of the shoreline of the point of commencement.

All bearings being referred to grid north.
