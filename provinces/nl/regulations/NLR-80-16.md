NLR 80/16

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Feasibility Report Respecting the Local Service District of George's Brook - Milton Order.

### Feasibility report

2 A feasibility report shall be prepared to consider the local governance options for the Local Service District of George's Brook - Milton.

### Appointment

3 LW Consulting is appointed to prepare the feasibility report.

### Preparation of report

4 The feasibility report shall be prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Municipalities Act, 1999.
