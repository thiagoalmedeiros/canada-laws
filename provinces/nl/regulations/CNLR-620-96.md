# Water - Notice of Protected Watershed Area of Lead Cove-Sibley’s Cove

CNLR 620/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 22, 342000 metres east, 5322000 metres north, 98° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 850 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 29° 00' for a distance of 901 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 74° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,046 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 172° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,513 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 134° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,221 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 191° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,835 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 234° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,931 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 322° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,545 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 23° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,609 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 335° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,462 metres to the point of commencement;

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Lead Cove - Sibley's Cove Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 153/78, is repealed.
