# Water - Notice of Protected Watershed Area of North West Pond

CNLR 666/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from the centre of the lighthouse at Northwest Head 46° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 79° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 30° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 293° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 100 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 246° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 195° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 127° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 125 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings are referred to Grid North.

The North West Pond Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 243/78, is repealed.
