# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Long Pond, Summerford

CNLR 636/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 570860, 180° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 161° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 575 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 61° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 117° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 59° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 309° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 297° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 325 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 238° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 375 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Long Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Town of Summerford, Newfoundland Regulation 59/79, is repealed.
