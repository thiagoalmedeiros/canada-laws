CNLR 902/96

Beginning at a point being the intersection of the southern shoreline of Pilley's Island with the eastern face of Flat Rock Tickle Causeway and then running in a generally northerly direction along the sinuosities of the eastern shoreline of Pilley's Island to Randall Point;

Then running in a westerly direction along the sinuosities of the northern shoreline of Pilley's Island to Long Tickle Point;

Then running in a generally southerly direction along the sinuosities of the western shoreline of Pilley's Island to Kelly Head;

Then running in an easterly direction along the southern shoreline of Pilley's Island to the point of beginning.

Newfoundland Regulation 94/80 is repealed.
