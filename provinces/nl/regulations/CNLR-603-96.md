# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Hughes Brook Reservoir

CNLR 603/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 437000 5427000 335° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 125 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 265° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 425 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 283° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 319° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 59° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 92° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 181° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 143° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Hughes Brook Reservoir Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 221/88, is repealed.
