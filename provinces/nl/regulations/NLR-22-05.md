NLR 22/05

*Made under the Urban and Rural Planning Act, 2000*

Beginning at the northwestern side of Gull Pond;

Then south 85 ° east 10,130 metres, more or less, to a high point;

Then north 66 ° east 4,630 metres, more or less;

Then north to the most eastern point of Forest Pond;

Then in a generally westerly direction along the northern shoreline of Forest Pond to the south corner of the eastern boundary of the land of the Persalvic Regional High School;

Then northward along that boundary to the main highroad to Salmon Cove;

Then northward 6,772 metres, more or less, to a hill, known elevation of 308 metres;

Then north 86 ° west 7171 metres, more or less;

Then in a generally southerly direction to the southeastern abutment of a Halfway Bridge;

Then south 82 ° west, 1708 metres, more or less, to a point;

Then south 36 ° west, 8179 metres, more or less, to the northwestern side of Gull Pond, the point of Beginning;

All bearings being referred to Grid North and, as with distances given, are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 Topographic Mapping, UTM zone 22.

The Victoria Municipal Planning Area, 2003, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 93/03, is repealed.
