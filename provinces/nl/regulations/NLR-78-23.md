NLR 78/23

*Made under the Environmental Protection Act*

### Short title

1 This order may be cited as the Trout River Harbour Dredging Project Exemption Order.

### Exemption

2 The dredging of Trout River harbour by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans Canada is exempted from Part X of the Act.

### Expiry

3 This order ceases to have effect on December 31, 2023.
