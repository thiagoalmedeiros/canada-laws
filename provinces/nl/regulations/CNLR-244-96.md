CNLR 244/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of King's Cove Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of King 's Cove are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 582/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point at the mouth of George's Pond Brook on the western shoreline of Rolling Cove and then running south 65° west a distance of 7,000 metres, more or less;

Then running north 3,350 metres, more or less;

Then running north 65° east a distance of 7,300 metres, more or less, to a point in the shoreline of Fishpoint Gulch on the western shoreline of Blackhead Bay;

Then running in a generally southerly direction along the western shoreline of Blackhead Bay via King's Cove to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
