CNLR 1152/96

*Made under the Provincial Court Act, 1991*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Provincial Court Districts Regulations.

### Districts described

2 The province is divided into the following Provincial Court districts for the purpose of the appointment and transfer of a Provincial Court judge or Provincial Court judges:

(a) St. John's;

(b) Holyrood;

(c) Harbour Grace;

(d) Placentia;

(e) Clarenville;

(f) Grand Bank;

(g) Gander;

(h) Grand Falls;

(i) Springdale;

(j) Corner Brook;

(k) Woody Point;

(l) Stephenville;

(m) Port aux Basques;

(n) Happy Valley - Goose Bay;

(o) Wabush.

### Repeal

3 The Provincial Court Districts Regulations, Newfoundland Regulation 147/87, are repealed.
