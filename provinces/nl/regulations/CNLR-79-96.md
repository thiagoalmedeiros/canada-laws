CNLR 79/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Badger Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Badger are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 676/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at the point at the junction of the western bank of Badger Brook with the northern bank of the Exploits River;

Then running along the shore of the Exploits River in a westerly direction 375 metres, more or less, to the mouth of Little Red Indian River;

Then crossing the mouth of that river in a southwesterly direction to the point on the southern side of the mouth of the Little Red Indian River;

Then along the shore of Exploits River in a southwest direction for a distance of 800 metres, more or less;

Then north 8° west 1,225 metres, more or less, to the Canadian National Railway right-of-way;

Then north 45° east 1,100 metres, more or less, to the western bottom of a small cove in the western bank of Badger Brook;

Then running along the western bank of Badger Brook in a southerly direction to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to true meridian and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the National Topographic Mapping 1:50,000.
