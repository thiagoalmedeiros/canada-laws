CNLR 203/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Grand Bank Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Grand Bank are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 656/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at Gunville Point on the southeasterly shoreline of Fortune Bay;

Then running south 48° west a distance of 2,750 metres, more or less, to the highest point on Baggers Hill;

Then running south 79° west a distance of 5,150 metres, more or less, to the highest point on Round Hill;

Then running north 3° 54' east a distance of 1,650 metres, more or less, to the highest point on Boundary Hill;

Then running north 35° 36' west a distance of 700 metres, more or less, to Yellow Rocks on the shoreline of Fortune Bay;

Then along the shoreline in a general northeasterly and easterly direction to the point of commencement.

All bearings are referred to the true meridian.
