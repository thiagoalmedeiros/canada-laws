CNLR 650/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21, 703000 metres east, 5359000 metres north, 122° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,000 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 10° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 3,380 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 94° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,865 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 134° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,851 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 220° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 4,924 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 208° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 4,715 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 240° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 4,812 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 134° 30 grid azimuth for a distance of 1,143 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 256° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,915 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 238° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,207 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 355° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 6,115 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 90° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,816 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 36° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,819 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 69° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,609 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 38° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 3,025 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Middle Brook Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 178/78, is repealed.
