NLR 11/18

*Made under the Urban and Rural Planning Act, 2000*

The Inuit Community of Nain, as referred to in Part 1.1.1 of the Schedule to the Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement Act, the boundaries of which are set out in the Map Atlas and described in Appendix B-1, Part 2 of the Schedule on file with the Crown Lands Administration Division of the Department of Fisheries and Land Resources.

The Nain Municipal Planning Area, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 890/96, is repealed.
