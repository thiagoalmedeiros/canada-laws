NLR 6/13

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This order may be cited as the Order Respecting a Feasibility Report for the Amalgamation of the Local Service District of Virgin Arm-Carter's Cove, the Town of Summerford and the Town of Cottlesville.

### Feasibility report

2 A feasibility report shall be prepared to consider the amalgamation of the Local Service District of Virgin Arm-Carter's Cove, the unincorporated area of Parkview, the Town of Summerford and the Town of Cottlesville.

### Appointment

3 George Whey of Whey Consulting is appointed to prepare the feasibility report.

### Preparation of report

4 The feasibility report shall be prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Municipalities Act, 1999.
