NLR 85/17

*Made under the Municipal Elections Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the General Election Deferral Order for the Town of Little Bay Islands.

### General Election Deferred

2 The 2017 municipal general election for the Town of Little Bay Islands is deferred to no later than September 25, 2018.
