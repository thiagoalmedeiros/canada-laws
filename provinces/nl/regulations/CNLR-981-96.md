CNLR 981/96

*Made under the Young Offenders Act (Canada)*

The Provincial Court of Newfoundland is designated as a Youth Court with effect from April 2, 1984 for the purposes of the Act.

The Order re the Young Offenders Act (Canada), Newfoundland Regulation 72/84, is repealed.
