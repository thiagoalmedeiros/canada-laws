NLR 31/18

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of George's Brook-Milton Election Order.

### First election

2 The first election of a council for the Town of George's Brook-Milton shall take place on May 8, 2018.

### Town council

3 The council which is to have control and management of the Town of George's Brook-Milton shall consist of 7 persons.

### Commencement

4 This Order comes into force on May 8, 2018.
