CNLR 983/96

*Made under the Young Persons Offences Act*

The Pleasantville Youth Centre, the Whitbourne Youth Centre, Youth Corrections group homes and foster homes are facilities of custody within the meaning of the Young Persons Offences Act.

The Designation of Facilities of Custody Order, Newfoundland Regulation 180/86, is repealed.
