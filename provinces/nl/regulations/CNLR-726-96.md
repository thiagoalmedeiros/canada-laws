CNLR 726/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 625000 5198000 20°00' grid azimuth for a distance of 175 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 331° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 274° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 333° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,000 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 87° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 353° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 925 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 100° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 120° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 225° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 211° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 199° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 233° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Waterfall Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 217/86, is repealed.
