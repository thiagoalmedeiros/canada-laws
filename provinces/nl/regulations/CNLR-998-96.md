CNLR 998/96

*Made under the Waste Material Disposal Act*

There are established as Waste Material Disposal Areas the areas named and described in the Schedule. These areas shall include all residential premises, businesses, schools and other structures on the roads and by-roads in the area which may generate waste material.

Waste Material Disposal Orders, Newfoundland Regulations 91/78, 299/78, 359/78, 434/78, 450/78, 453/78, 459/78, 470/78, 488/78, 494/78, 499/78, 502/78, 515/78, 516/78, 556/78, 558/78, 561/78, 563/78, 93/79, 101/79, 107/79, 199/79, 28/80, 262/80, 174/81, 172/82, 197/85, 167/86, 90/87, 75/89, 170/91, 72/92, 214/92, 15/93, 18/93, 21/93, 27/93, 32/93, 35/93, 36/95 and 67/95, are repealed.
