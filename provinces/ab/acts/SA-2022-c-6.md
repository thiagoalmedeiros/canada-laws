# Traffic Safety Amendment Act, 2022

SA 2022, c 6

HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of Alberta, enacts as follows:

### Amends RSA 2000 cT-6

1 The Traffic Safety Act is amended by this Act.
2 Section 1(1) is amended by adding the following after clause (n):
(n.1) “flashing lamp” includes a stationary or rotating flashing lamp, a strobe lamp or a 360 degree warning lamp but does not include a turn signal lamp or a vehicle hazard warning lamp required under the regulations;
3 Section 115 is amended
(a) by renumbering subsection (1) as subsection (1.1) and adding the following before subsection (1.1):

### Prohibited operation of vehicles, etc.

115 (1) For the purposes of this section, “school bus” means a vehicle on which are displayed the words “school bus”.
(b) in subsection (2)(t)
(i) by repealing subclause (iv) and substituting the following:
(iv) is travelling on the same side of the highway as a stopped vehicle with flashing lamps or travelling in the opposite direction as a stopped vehicle with flashing lamps on a highway consisting of only 2 traffic lanes that carry traffic in opposite directions, and
(ii) in subclause (v) by striking out “emergency vehicle or tow truck” and substituting “vehicle”;
(c) by repealing subsection (4) and substituting the following:
(4) Subsection (2)(t) does not apply
(a) where a speed limit is prescribed under section 108(1)(g), or
(b) when the stopped vehicle is a school bus.
4 This Act comes into force on Proclamation.
