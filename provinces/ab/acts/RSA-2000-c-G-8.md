# Government Fees and Charges Review Act

RSA 2000, c G-8

HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of Alberta, enacts as follows:

### Validation of existing fees and charges

1 (1) The purpose of this section is to validate any fee or other charge previously established by a regulation or an order or directive of a Minister or other person or body in the event that it was required to have been established in an Act.
(2) Where, before May 19, 1999, a fee or other charge was established by a regulation or an order or directive of a Minister or other person or body and that fee or other charge was required to be established in an Act, the fee or other charge is deemed, effective from the date the fee or other charge was established by the regulation, order or directive,
(a) to have been established by this Act, and
(b) to have been validly imposed and collected.
