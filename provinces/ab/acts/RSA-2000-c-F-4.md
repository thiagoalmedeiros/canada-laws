RSA 2000, c F-4

HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of Alberta, enacts as follows:

### Family Day

1 In recognition of the importance of the family, the 3rd Monday in February in each year shall be observed as a public holiday and known as “Alberta Family Day”.
