# Electoral Divisions Act

SA 2017, c E-4.3

HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of Alberta, enacts as follows:

### Electoral divisions

1 The electoral divisions for the purposes of the Election Act are the 87 electoral divisions established by this Act.

### Names of electoral divisions

2 The names of the electoral divisions are those set out in the Schedule.

### Description of electoral divisions

3 The boundaries of the electoral divisions are those described in the DVD prepared by the Chief Electoral Officer as directed by a resolution of the Legislative Assembly and tabled as Sessional Paper (624/2017).

### Regulations

4 Notwithstanding section 3, if the boundary between 2 electoral divisions passes through a building used for one or more residences, the Chief Electoral Officer, after consultation with the returning officers for those electoral divisions, may by regulation prescribe the electoral division in which the building is to be located for the purposes of the Election Act .

### Transitional

5 (1) A reference to an electoral division in the Election Act or the Election Finances and Contributions Disclosure Act includes a reference to an electoral division established by this Act as if sections 1 to 4 of this Act were in force in circumstances where, with respect to the next general election, an act or a thing is required, authorized or permitted to be done or exercised under those Acts before a writ is issued for that general election.
(2) Nothing in subsection (1) authorizes any person or organization in any way to circumvent, or attempt to circumvent, an expense limit set out in the Election Finances and Contributions Disclosure Act .

### Repeal

6 The Electoral Divisions Act , SA 2010 cE-4.2, is repealed.

### Coming into force

7 This Act, except section 5, comes into force on the day that a writ is issued under section 40 of the Election Act for the next general election.
(NOTE: The writ issued under section 40 of the Election Act for a general election was issued on March 19, 2019.)
