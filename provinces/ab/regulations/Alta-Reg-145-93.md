# Metis Settlements Election Regulation

Alta Reg 145/93

*Enabling act: Metis Settlements Act*

1 For the purposes of ensuring that the Local Authorities Election Act can be applied to the election of councillors to a settlement council as provided for in section 13(1) of the Metis Settlements Act , the following terms in the Local Authorities Election Act are deemed to have the following meanings:
(a) “council” includes a settlement council;
(b) “elected authority” includes a settlement council;
(c) “local jurisdiction” includes a settlement;
(d) “relevant Minister” means, in the case of a settlement, the member of the Executive Council charged by the Lieutenant Governor in Council with the administration of the Metis Settlements Act ;
(e) “secretary” includes a settlement administrator.
