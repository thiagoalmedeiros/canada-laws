Alta Reg 174/2005

*Enabling act: Marketing of Agricultural Products Act*

### Definitions

1 In this order,

(a) “Commission” means the commission known as the Alberta Canola Producers Commission;

(a.1) “Council” means the Alberta Agricultural Products Marketing Council;

(b) “Federal Act” means the Agricultural Products Marketing Act (Canada).

(c) repealed AR 88/2008 s2.

### Authorization

2 The Council hereby authorizes the Commission to perform all functions and duties and exercise all powers imposed or conferred on the Commission under the Federal Act.
