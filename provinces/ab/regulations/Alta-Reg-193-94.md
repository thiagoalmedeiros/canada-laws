Alta Reg 193/94

*Enabling act: Election Finances and Contributions Disclosure Act*

1 The following corporations, including their subsidiaries, are designated as prohibited corporations for the purpose of the Election Finances and Contributions Disclosure Act :

(a) North West Trust Company;

(b) Gainers Inc.;

(c) Chembiomed Ltd.

2 The Prohibited Corporations Regulation (Alta. Reg. 131/85) is repealed.
