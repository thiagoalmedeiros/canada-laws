# Sound-recording Machine Order

Alta Reg 52/97

*Enabling act: Mechanical Recording of Evidence Act*

### Requirement for recording

1 Evidence given before a court in a civil action or in any action under the Provincial Offences Procedure Act shall be recorded by means of a sound-recording machine.
2 Repealed AR 98/98 s2.

### Repeal

3 The Sound-Recording Machine Order (AR 303/86) is repealed.
