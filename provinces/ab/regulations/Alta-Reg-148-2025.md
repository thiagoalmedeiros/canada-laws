# Election Regulation

Alta Reg 148/2025

*Enabling act: Election Act*

### Maximum costs

1 For the purposes of section 148.1(1) of the Act, the maximum amount of reasonable and proper costs that a judge may direct the Crown in right of Alberta to pay is $20 000.

### Coming into force

2 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of section 6(63)(b) of the Election Statutes Amendment Act, 2025 .
