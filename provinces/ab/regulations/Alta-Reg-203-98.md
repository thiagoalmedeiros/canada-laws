# Forms Regulation (Agricultural Service Board Act)

Alta Reg 203/98

*Enabling act: Agricultural Service Board Act*

### Forms prescribed

1 The forms in the Schedule are the forms prescribed for the purposes of the sections indicated on them.

### Repeal

2 The Forms Regulation (AR 501/81) is repealed.
3 Repealed AR 89/2016 s2.
