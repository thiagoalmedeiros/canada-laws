Alta Reg 136/2026

*Enabling act: Official Time Act*

### Prescribed term

1 The prescribed term by which official time may be known is Alberta Time.

2 to 7 (These sections amend other regulations; the amendments have been incorporated into those regulations.)

### Expiry

8 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on June 30, 2031.
