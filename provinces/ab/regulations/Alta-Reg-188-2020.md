# Local Authorities Capital Financing Regulation

Alta Reg 188/2020

*Enabling act: Local Authorities Capital Financing Act*

### Maximum outstanding loans

1 For the purpose of section 3 of the Act, the total principal amount of all outstanding loans made by the Minister under the Act shall not exceed $18 000 000 000.

### Coming into force

2 This Regulation has effect on the coming into force of section 6 of the Local Authorities Capital Financing Act .
