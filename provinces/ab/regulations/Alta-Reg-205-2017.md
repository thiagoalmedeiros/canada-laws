Alta Reg 205/2017

*Enabling act: Gas Resources Preservation Act*

### Ministerial approval

1 The Minister’s approval under section 6 of the Act is not required in respect of an application for a permit or an amendment of a permit authorizing the removal of gas, unless the gas is a mixture mainly of ethane.

### Repeal

2 The Approval of Short-term Permits Regulation (AR 334/2002) is repealed.
