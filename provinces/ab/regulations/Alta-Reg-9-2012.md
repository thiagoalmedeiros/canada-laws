Alta Reg 9/2012

*Enabling act: Wills and Succession Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation, “ Surrogate Rules ” means the Surrogate Rules (AR 130/95).

### Applications to the Court

2 Every application to the Court under the Wills and Succession Act must be made in accordance with the Surrogate Rules .

### Required financial information

3 A person who receives a request for financial information under section 95(2) of the Act shall provide financial information in accordance with Rule 70.9 of the Surrogate Rules .

### Coming into force

4 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of the Wills and Succession Act .
