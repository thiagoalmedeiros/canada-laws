Alta Reg 235/2026

*Enabling act: Public Works Act*

### Prescribed maximum amount of monetary claim

1 For the purposes of section 14.3(2)(b) of the Act, the maximum amount is $500 000.

### Expiry

2 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on May 1, 2029.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of section 17 of the Red Tape Reduction Statutes Amendment Act, 2026 .
