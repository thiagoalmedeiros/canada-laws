# Alberta Turkey Producers Federal Authorization Order

Alta Reg 154/2000

*Enabling act: Marketing of Agricultural Products Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Order,
(a) “Board” means the board known as the Alberta Turkey Producers;
(b) “Federal Orders” means the Orders, Proclamations and Regulations made under the Canada Act.

### Authorization

2 The Council hereby authorizes the Board to perform all functions and duties and exercise all powers imposed or conferred on the Board under the Canada Act and the Federal Orders.

### Repeal

3 The Alberta - Canada Turkey Marketing Regulations (AR 63/75) are repealed.
4 Repealed AR 209/2002 s2.
