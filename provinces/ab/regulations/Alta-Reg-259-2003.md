Alta Reg 259/2003

*Enabling act: Traffic Safety Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation, “Act” means the Traffic Safety Act .

### Fee for appeals

2 A person who appeals any matter under Part 2, Division 3 of the Act must pay a fee of

(a) $250, if the appellant wishes to appear in person before the Board, or

(b) $125, if the appellant does not wish to appear in person before the Board.

3 Repealed AR 68/2017 s2.
