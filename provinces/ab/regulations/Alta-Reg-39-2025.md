Alta Reg 39/2025

*Enabling act: Government Organization Act*

### Exemption

1 A public post-secondary institution as defined in the Post-secondary Learning Act is not a Provincial agency for the purpose of section 11 of the Government Organization Act.

### Coming into force

2 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of the Provincial Priorities Act.
