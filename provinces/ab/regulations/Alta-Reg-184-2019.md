Alta Reg 184/2019

*Enabling act: Agricultural Operation Practices Act*

### Private insurance

1 For the purpose of section 1.2 of the Act, “private insurance” means a contract of insurance issued by an insurer licensed under the Insurance Act to carry on the business of insurance in Alberta that provides coverage for workers in case of

(a) disability, including bodily injury or sickness, and

(b) accidental death and dismemberment.

2 This Regulation comes into force on January 31, 2020.
