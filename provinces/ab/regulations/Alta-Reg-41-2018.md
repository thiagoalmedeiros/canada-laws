# Conflicts of Interest Part 4.3 Inclusion Order

Alta Reg 41/2018

*Enabling act: Conflicts of Interest Act*

### Definition

1 In this Order, “subsidiaries” means one or more subsidiaries within the meaning of section 2 of the Business Corporations Act .

### Application of Part 4.3

2 Pursuant to section 23.921(1)(c) of the Act, the following are to be treated as public agencies for the purposes of Part 4.3 of the Act:
(a) a hospital operator of a health services sector in at least 3 approved hospitals under the Provincial Health Agencies Act , other than a hospital operator that is a provincial health agency, regional health authority or provincial health corporation under the Provincial Health Agencies Act ;
(b) the subsidiaries of a hospital operator referred to in clause (a).
