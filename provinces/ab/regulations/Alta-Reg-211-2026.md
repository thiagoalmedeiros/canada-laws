Alta Reg 211/2026

*Enabling act: Insurance Act*

1 The Automobile Accident Insurance Benefits Regulation (AR 352/72) is repealed.

2 The Complaint Resolution Regulation (AR 259/2004) is repealed.

3 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of section 2 of the Automobile Insurance Act .
