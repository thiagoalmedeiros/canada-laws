Alta Reg 99/2019

*Enabling act: Education Act*

### Establishment of separate school regions

1 Pursuant to section 123 of the Act, the separate school regions are established.

### Holy Family Roman Catholic Separate School Region

2 (1) The Holy Family Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Fort Vermilion School Division;

(b) Townships 86 to 92 inclusive, in Range 1, West of the 6th Meridian in Peace River School Division and that portion of Peace River School Division lying East of the 6th Meridian;

(c) that portion of High Prairie School Division lying West of Range 13;

(d) that portion of Northern Gateway School Division lying North and West of the Little Smoky River, North and East of the Iosegun River and North of Township 65.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Holy Family Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Grande Prairie Roman Catholic Separate School Region

3 (1) The Grande Prairie Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Peace Wapiti School Division;

(b) Grande Prairie School Division;

(c) that portion of Peace River School Division lying West of the 6th Meridian excluding Townships 86 to 92, in Range 1.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Grande Prairie Roman Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Fort McMurray Roman Catholic Separate School Region

4 (1) The Fort McMurray Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands in Fort McMurray School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Fort McMurray Roman Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Living Waters Roman Catholic Separate School Region

5 (1) The Living Waters Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Grande Yellowhead School Division with the exception of Hinton School District No. 4518;

(b) that portion of Northern Gateway School Division lying South and East of the Little Smoky River, South and West of the Iosegun River and South of Township 65;

(c) that portion of High Prairie School Division lying East of Range 13.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Living Waters Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Evergreen Roman Catholic Separate School Region

6 (1) The Evergreen Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Pembina Hills School Division;

(b) Parkland School Division with the exception of Moon Lake School District No. 4724;

(c) Devon School District No. 4972 in Black Gold School Division;

(d) Hinton School District No. 4518 in Grande Yellowhead School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Evergreen Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Lakeland Roman Catholic Separate School Region

7 (1) The Lakeland Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Northern Lights School Division;

(b) Aspen View School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Lakeland Roman Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Elk Island Roman Catholic Separate School Region

8 (1) The Elk Island Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Elk Island School Division;

(b) Saskatchewan School District No. 2 in Sturgeon School Division;

(c) Stony Creek School District No. 3611 in Black Gold School Division;

(d) those portions of Battle River School Division lying within Camrose County; those portions of Battle River School Division lying within Beaver County; those portions of Battle River School Division lying North of Township 45 within Flagstaff County with the exception of the lands contained in Valley School District No. 1891 and Wheatland School District No. 1636;

(e) that portion of the County of Two Hills contained in Vegreville Catholic Separate School District No. 16 and Norma Village School District No. 4861.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Elk Island Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Edmonton Roman Catholic Separate School Region

9 (1) The Edmonton Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of lands included in Edmonton School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Edmonton Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### St. Thomas Aquinas Roman Catholic Separate School Region

10 (1) The St. Thomas Aquinas Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Black Gold School Division with the exception of Stony Creek School District No. 3611 and the Devon School District No. 4972;

(b) Wetaskiwin School Division;

(c) that portion of Wild Rose School Division lying North of Township 45 and that portion of Wild Rose School Division lying East of the North Saskatchewan River;

(d) Moon Lake School District No. 4724 in Parkland School Division;

(e) Wolf Creek School Division with the exception of Blackfalds School District No. 255, Blindman School District No. 252, Bluebell School District No. 1186, Carritt School District No. 930, Durham School District No. 3907, Eckville School District No. 1459, Saima School District No. 1434, Warren School District No. 2910 and Wolfe Valley School District No. 4010.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of St. Thomas Aquinas Roman Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### East Central Alberta Roman Catholic Separate School Region

11 (1) The East Central Alberta Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Buffalo Trail School Division;

(b) Clearview School Division;

(c) those portions of Battle River School Division lying South of Township 45 within Flagstaff County with the exception of those lands contained in Amity School District No. 1959, Daysland School District No. 1539, Leith School District No. 1565, Poplar School District No. 3003 and Selby School District No. 1545.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act , The Board of Trustees of East Central Alberta Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Red Deer Roman Catholic Separate School Region

12 (1) The Red Deer Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of lands included in the following jurisdictions:

(a) Red Deer School Division;

(b) Chinook’s Edge School Division;

(c) Wild Rose School Division excluding that portion lying North of Township 45 and that portion lying East of the Saskatchewan River;

(d) Blackfalds School District No. 255, Blindman School District No. 252, Bluebell School District No. 1186, Carritt School District No. 930, Durham School District No. 3907, Eckville School District No. 1459, Saima School District No. 1434, Warren School District No. 2910 and Wolfe Valley School District No. 4010 in Wolf Creek School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Red Deer Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Christ the Redeemer Roman Catholic Separate School Region

13 (1) The Christ the Redeemer Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Foothills School Division;

(b) Golden Hills School Division;

(c) Canadian Rockies School Division;

(d) Grasslands School Division;

(e) Prairie Land School Division;

(f) that portion of Prairie Rose School Division lying North of the Red Deer River;

(g) Greig School District No. 3648, Hobert School District No. 1199, Rolling Plains School District No. 1287, Nanton School District No. 730, Round Mound School District No. 1398, Sun Prairie School District No. 1121, Broadway School District No. 1172, Braehead School District No. 4215, Coleraine School District No. 1288, Sunset School District No. 1483 and Muir Head School District No. 2032 included in Livingstone Range School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Christ the Redeemer Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Calgary Roman Catholic Separate School Region

14 (1) The Calgary Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Calgary School Division;

(b) Rocky View School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Calgary Roman Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Holy Spirit Roman Catholic Separate School Region

15 (1) The Holy Spirit Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Lethbridge School Division;

(b) Livingstone Range School Division with the exception of Braehead School District No. 4215, Broadway School District No. 1172, Coleraine School District No. 1288, Greig School District No. 3648, Hobert School District No. 1199, Nanton School District No. 730, Rolling Plains School District No. 1287, Round Mound School District No. 1398, Sun Prairie School District No. 1121, Sunset School District No. 1483 and Muir Head School District No. 2032;

(c) Westwind School Division;

(d) Palliser School Division;

(e) Horizon School Division;

(f) Bow Island School District No. 1883 in Prairie Rose School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Holy Spirit Roman Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Medicine Hat Roman Catholic Separate School Region

16 (1) The Medicine Hat Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Medicine Hat School Division;

(b) that portion of Prairie Rose School Division lying South of the Red Deer River with the exception of Bow Island School District No. 1883.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Medicine Hat Roman Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Greater St. Albert Roman Catholic Separate School Region

17 (1) The Greater St. Albert Roman Catholic Separate School Region shall be comprised of the lands included in the following school jurisdictions:

(a) Sturgeon School Division with the exception of Saskatchewan School District No. 2;

(b) St. Albert School Division.

(2) Pursuant to section 123(3) of the Act, The Board of Trustees of Greater St. Albert Roman Catholic Separate School Division shall be the only separate school board for the separate school region described in subsection (1).

### Repeal

18 The Separate School Regions Establishment Order (AR 165/2018) is repealed.

### Coming into force

19 This Order comes into force on September 1, 2019.
