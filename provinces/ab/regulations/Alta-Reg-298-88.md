# Rules of Youth Court (Provincial Offences) Regulation

Alta Reg 298/88

*Enabling act: Youth Justice Act*

1 The Rules of Youth Court Regulation under the Youth Criminal Justice Act (Canada) approved by the Lieutenant Governor in Council is hereby adopted with respect to the practice and procedure to be followed by youth courts under the Youth Justice Act (Alberta).
