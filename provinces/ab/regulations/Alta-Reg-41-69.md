Alta Reg 41/69

*Enabling act: Legislative Assembly Act*

1 For a certified copy of a Bill or Statute as printed, not exceeding 2 pages in length no charge

2 For a certified copy of a Bill or Statute as printed, exceeding 2 pages in length 50 cents

3 For a certified copy of a Bill of the Legislature, amended to conform with the model in which it received Royal Assent, supplied during a Session of the Legislative Assembly and during 10 cents per 100 a period of 60 days following words to maximum prorogation. charge of $2.00
