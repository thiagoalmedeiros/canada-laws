# Traveller Protection and Destination Development Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Alberta website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/1266.cfm?page=2026_186.cfm&leg_type=Regs&isbncln=9780779864218&display=html) before relying on it.

Alta Reg 186/2026

*Enabling act: Traveller Protection and Destination Development Act*

## Part 1 Definitions, Interpretation and Application

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,

(a) “Act” means the Traveller Protection and Destination Development Act ;

(b) “calendar quarter” means a period of 3 months beginning on the first day of January, April, July or October.

### Tourism experiences

2 For the purposes of section 1(1)(m) of the Act, the following activities and attractions are tourism experiences:

(a) a recreation, sporting or cultural activity or event;

(b) a service or activity offered at a resort or at an all-season resort development as defined in the All-season Resorts Act ;

(c) a guided tour;

(d) an exhibition, fair or combined exhibition and rodeo;

(e) a carnival or amusement park;

(f) a museum or heritage site, other than one owned or operated by the Crown, a municipality or a public body.

### Destination marketing fees

3 (1) The following classes of fees are destination marketing fees for the purposes of the Act:

(a) a fee described as a destination marketing fee;

(b) a fee described as a tourism marketing fee, tourism promotion fee, destination promotion fee, regional marketing fee, visitor marketing fee, destination development fee or regional tourism fee;

(c) a fee described as being for the purposes of marketing, promoting or destination development in relation to a municipality, a provincial park established under the Provincial Parks Act or another geographic area.

(2) The following classes of fees are not destination marketing fees for the purposes of the Act:

(a) a resort fee, amenity fee or facility fee charged for the use of on-site amenities or facilities provided by an operator;

(b) a service charge or gratuity;

(c) a fee charged by an online broker for the use of an online marketplace;

(d) a cleaning fee, damage deposit or pet accommodation fee.

(3) Nothing in subsection (2) prevents the application of section 1(2) of the Act to a fee described using a term referred to in subsection (2).

### Exempt programs

4 For the purposes of section 2(5) of the Act, the Tourism Improvement Fee program established under the Town of Banff Business Licence Bylaw is a prescribed program.

## Part 2 Designation of Entities

### Purposes of destination marketing organizations

5 A designated destination marketing organization may, in respect of the geographic area for which it is designated, undertake activities for any one or more of the following purposes:

(a) to market the tourism assets, attractions, events and opportunities in the geographic area;

(b) to promote and support the development and growth of tourism in the geographic area;

(c) if an accommodation association is designated for the geographic area, to collaborate with that accommodation association;

(d) to assist communities and tourism industry operators in the geographic area to develop and market their products;

(e) to provide visitor services in the geographic area;

(f) to partner with community stakeholders in the geographic area to bid on and secure sports events and business events.

### Requirements for destination marketing organization designation

6 A person applying for designation as a destination marketing organization must meet, and a designated destination marketing organization must continue to meet, each of the following requirements:

(a) incorporation as a society under the Societies Act or registration as a company under Part 9 of the Companies Act , as required by section 3(4) of the Act;

(b) a board of directors, a majority of whose members are

(i) representatives of accommodation providers in the geographic area for which designation is sought or was granted, including, as applicable, representatives of

(A) hotels and motels,

(B) bed and breakfasts and inns,

(C) short-term rental operators, and

(D) providers of other accommodation referred to in section 1(1)(a) of the Act,

and

(ii) representatives of tourism experience providers in that area;

(c) at least one paid staff member;

(d) an annual operating capacity of at least $500 000.

### Application for destination marketing organization designation

7 (1) An application for designation as a destination marketing organization must include

(a) a business plan that meets the requirements of subsection (2), and

(b) the information set out in subsection (3).

(2) The business plan must include

(a) a description of the geographic area for which designation is sought, as required by section 3(5)(a) of the Act,

(b) the applicant’s goals, strategies and performance measures,

(c) the rate for the destination marketing fee to be charged in the geographic area,

(d) information respecting the composition of the applicant’s board of directors, including representation of

(i) the categories of accommodation providers in the geographic area for which designation is sought, as set out in section 6(b)(i), and

(ii) tourism experience providers in that area,

(e) information respecting the applicant’s staffing, and

(f) evidence that the applicant has an annual operating capacity of at least $500 000.

(3) The application must also include

(a) the applicant’s name, address, place of incorporation and incorporation or registration number under the Societies Act or Part 9 of the Companies Act , respectively,

(b) the names and positions of the applicant’s officers and directors,

(c) the beginning and end dates of the applicant’s fiscal year, and

(d) any information required by the Minister to determine whether the designation should be refused under section 6 of the Act.

### Purposes of accommodation associations

8 A designated accommodation association may, in respect of the geographic area for which it is designated, undertake activities for any one or more of the following purposes:

(a) to represent the interests of accommodation providers in the geographic area;

(b) to support the participation of accommodation providers in tourism development in the geographic area;

(c) to collaborate with the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area.

### Application for accommodation association designation

9 (1) An application for designation as an accommodation association must include

(a) a business plan that meets the requirements of subsection (2), and

(b) the information set out in subsection (3).

(2) The business plan must include

(a) identification of the geographic area for which designation is sought, and

(b) the applicant’s goals, strategies and performance measures.

(3) The application must also include

(a) the applicant’s name, address, place of incorporation and incorporation or registration number under the Societies Act or Part 9 of the Companies Act , respectively,

(b) the names and positions of the applicant’s officers and directors,

(c) identification of the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area,

(d) the beginning and end dates of the applicant’s fiscal year, and

(e) any information required by the Minister to determine whether the designation should be refused under section 6 of the Act.

### Duties of designated trustees

10 A designated trustee must

(a) assist operators with the accurate collection of destination marketing fees,

(b) receive destination marketing fees remitted by operators and transfer those fees in accordance with the Act and this Regulation,

(c) conduct compliance audits of operators respecting the collection and remittance of destination marketing fees, and

(d) notify the Minister of any discrepancies or noncompliance identified in the course of an audit conducted under clause (c).

### Requirements for trustee designation

11 (1) A person applying for designation as a trustee must meet, and a designated trustee must continue to meet, each of the following requirements:

(a) registration as a trust corporation under the Loan and Trust Corporations Act , registration as a chartered professional accountant in good standing with the Chartered Professional Accountants of Alberta or registration as a professional corporation under the Chartered Professional Accountants Act , as required by section 5(5) of the Act;

(b) good standing with the regulatory body governing the applicant’s profession or registration;

(c) the support of the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area.

(2) A person applying for designation as a trustee must not be, and a designated trustee must not become, an employee, director or contractor of the destination marketing organization or accommodation association designated for the geographic area.

### Application for trustee designation

12 An application for designation as a trustee must include

(a) evidence that the applicant meets the registration requirement set out in section 11(1)(a),

(b) evidence that the applicant is in good standing with the regulatory body governing the applicant’s profession or registration,

(c) identification of the geographic area for which designation is sought,

(d) identification of the destination marketing organization and, if applicable, the accommodation association designated for the geographic area,

(e) evidence that the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area supports the applicant’s designation as a trustee,

(f) a declaration that the applicant is not an employee, director or contractor of the destination marketing organization or, if applicable, the accommodation association designated for the geographic area, and

(g) any information required by the Minister to determine whether the designation should be refused under section 6 of the Act.

### Municipal control

13 A destination marketing organization, accommodation association or trustee designated under the Act must not be subject to the direction or control of a municipality in respect of the collection, administration, allocation or use of destination marketing fees.

### Suspension or cancellation of destination marketing organization designation

14 (1) If the designation of a destination marketing organization is suspended under section 7 of the Act,

(a) operators in the geographic area may continue to charge and collect destination marketing fees during the suspension,

(b) operators must continue to remit destination marketing fees to the trustee designated for the geographic area, and

(c) the trustee must, in respect of destination marketing fees received by the trustee during the suspension,

(i) continue to transfer to the accommodation association designated for the geographic area, if any, the portion of the fees that would otherwise have been transferred to the accommodation association under section 11(2) of the Act, and

(ii) hold in trust the portion of the fees that would otherwise have been transferred to the destination marketing organization.

(2) If a suspension referred to in subsection (1) is lifted, the trustee must transfer to the destination marketing organization any destination marketing fees held under subsection (1)(c)(ii).

(3) If the designation of a destination marketing organization is cancelled under section 7 of the Act,

(a) operators in the geographic area must cease charging destination marketing fees as of the date the cancellation takes effect,

(b) an operator who has collected a destination marketing fee but has not yet remitted it to the trustee must remit the fee to the trustee within 30 days after the date the cancellation takes effect, and

(c) the trustee must hold any destination marketing fees remaining in trust until the Minister directs the disposition of those fees under section 17.

(4) Despite subsection (3), if an accommodation association is designated for the geographic area at the time the cancellation of the destination marketing organization’s designation takes effect, the Minister may, by order, establish a transitional period of not more than 12 months during which

(a) for the purposes of sections 8 and 9 of the Act, the geographic area is deemed to continue to be a geographic area for which a destination marketing organization is designated,

(b) the trustee must continue to transfer to the accommodation association the portion of the destination marketing fees received that would otherwise have been transferred to it under section 11(2) of the Act, and

(c) the trustee must hold in trust

(i) any destination marketing fees held under subsection (1)(c)(ii) at the time the cancellation takes effect, and

(ii) the portion of fees received during the transitional period that would otherwise have been transferred to the destination marketing organization.

(5) If a replacement destination marketing organization is designated for the geographic area during a transitional period under subsection (4), the trustee must transfer the destination marketing fees held under subsection (4)(c) to the replacement destination marketing organization.

(6) If no replacement destination marketing organization has been designated for the geographic area on the expiry of a transitional period under subsection (4), the trustee must deal with the destination marketing fees held under subsection (4)(c) in accordance with the Minister’s direction under section 17.

### Suspension or cancellation of accommodation association designation

15 (1) If the designation of an accommodation association is suspended under section 7 of the Act, the trustee must, during the suspension, transfer to the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area the portion of destination marketing fees that would otherwise have been transferred to the accommodation association.

(2) If the designation of an accommodation association is cancelled under section 7 of the Act, the trustee must transfer to the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area any destination marketing fees held in trust at the time the cancellation takes effect that would otherwise have been transferred to the accommodation association.

### Suspension or cancellation of trustee designation

16 (1) If the designation of a trustee is suspended or cancelled under section 7 of the Act, including where the designation is suspended under section 7(3) of the Act,

(a) operators must remit destination marketing fees directly to the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area until a replacement trustee is designated or, in the case of a suspension, the suspension is lifted, and

(b) the destination marketing organization must hold destination marketing fees received under clause (a) separate and apart from its own funds until the fees are

(i) transferred to a trustee designated for the geographic area, or

(ii) if no replacement trustee is designated, dealt with in accordance with the Minister’s direction under section 17.

(2) If the designation of a trustee is cancelled under section 7 of the Act and a replacement trustee is designated for the geographic area, the former trustee must transfer to the replacement trustee the destination marketing fees held in the trust account maintained by the former trustee.

(3) If the designation of a trustee is cancelled under section 7 of the Act and no replacement trustee has been designated for the geographic area, the former trustee must deal with the destination marketing fees held in the trust account maintained by the former trustee in accordance with the Minister’s direction under section 17.

### Ministerial direction respecting destination marketing fees that cannot be transferred

17 If destination marketing fees held in trust cannot be transferred under section 14, 15 or 16 because there is no designated entity entitled to receive them, the Minister may direct that the fees be

(a) held in trust by a person specified by the Minister until a replacement entity is designated,

(b) transferred to a replacement entity on designation, or

(c) returned to the operators who remitted the fees.

### Amendment of geographic area of designation

18 (1) A destination marketing organization designated under the Act may submit to the Minister, in the form and manner determined by the Minister, a request to amend the geographic area for which the destination marketing organization is designated.

(2) The request must include any information required by the Minister.

(3) After considering a request under subsection (1), the Minister may

(a) approve the request,

(b) require the destination marketing organization to revise the proposed geographic area, or

(c) refuse the request.

(4) For greater certainty, the Minister may take an action described in subsection (3)(b) or (c) if the proposed amended geographic area overlaps with, or would otherwise affect, a geographic area for which another destination marketing organization is designated.

(5) Before approving a request that would affect the geographic area for which another destination marketing organization is designated, the Minister must give that other destination marketing organization an opportunity to provide any information or representations required by the Minister.

(6) If the Minister approves the request, the Minister must amend each order made under section 3(1) of the Act that designates a destination marketing organization for a geographic area affected by the approval to reflect the geographic area for which that destination marketing organization is designated.

(7) An amendment under subsection (6) takes effect on the date of the amending order or on a later date specified in the order.

(8) On the date an amendment to a geographic area takes effect under subsection (7), an accommodation association or trustee that was designated for that geographic area immediately before the amendment took effect is deemed to be designated for that geographic area as amended.

## Part 3 Destination Marketing Fees

### Rate of destination marketing fee

19 The rate of a destination marketing fee for a geographic area is the rate set out in the business plan of the destination marketing organization designated for that area.

### Purpose and permitted uses of destination marketing fees

20 (1) The purpose of destination marketing fees is to support the growth and competitiveness of the tourism economy in the geographic area in which they are charged through reinvestment in marketing, sales activities, destination development, visitor-related initiatives and initiatives to increase visitation to the geographic area.

(2) For the purposes of sections 1(1)(d) and 13(1) of the Act, destination marketing fees may be used for any of the following:

(a) marketing and promoting the geographic area for which the destination marketing organization or accommodation association is designated;

(b) sales activities that support the growth of tourism in the geographic area;

(c) activities to bid on and secure sports events and business events that support the growth of tourism in the geographic area;

(d) destination development and the enhancement of tourism products, experiences and infrastructure in the geographic area, excluding public infrastructure;

(e) visitor services in the geographic area;

(f) research and market intelligence related to tourism in the geographic area;

(g) partnership initiatives with other destination marketing organizations, accommodation associations, tourism industry operators or tourism entities for the purposes of promoting or developing tourism in the geographic area;

(h) reasonable administrative overhead and operational expenses of the destination marketing organization or accommodation association, including the remuneration of the trustee designated for the geographic area.

### Remittance and transfer of destination marketing fees

21 (1) For the purposes of section 9(1) of the Act, an operator who collects destination marketing fees in a calendar quarter must remit those fees to the trustee designated for the geographic area in which the fees were collected no later than 30 days after the end of that calendar quarter.

(2) For the purposes of section 11(2) of the Act, if an accommodation association is designated for a geographic area, the trustee designated for the geographic area must transfer

(a) not less than 80% of the destination marketing fees remitted in respect of the geographic area to the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area, and

(b) the remainder to the accommodation association designated for the geographic area.

(3) Subject to subsection (2) and with the Minister’s approval, the destination marketing organization and accommodation association designated for a geographic area may agree on the proportions in which destination marketing fees are to be transferred under subsection (2).

(4) An agreement under subsection (3) must be in writing and a copy must be provided to the trustee.

(5) If no agreement under subsection (3) is in effect, the trustee must transfer 80% of the destination marketing fees to the destination marketing organization and 20% of the destination marketing fees to the accommodation association.

(6) Despite subsections (2) to (5), if an operator provides written notice to the Minister and the trustee that the operator elects to have the destination marketing fees collected by the operator transferred entirely to the destination marketing organization designated for the geographic area, the trustee must, on receipt of the notice, transfer to the destination marketing organization all destination marketing fees subsequently received from that operator.

(7) An operator who has provided notice under subsection (6) may revoke the notice by providing written notice of revocation to the Minister and the trustee, in which case subsections (2) to (5) apply to destination marketing fees subsequently received from that operator.

(8) The trustee must make a transfer required under this section in respect of destination marketing fees received in a calendar quarter no later than 30 days after the end of that calendar quarter.

### Trust requirements

22 (1) In addition to the requirements of section 12 of the Act, a designated trustee must

(a) cause each trust account maintained under section 12(2)(a) of the Act to be audited annually by an independent auditor,

(b) provide the destination marketing organization and, if applicable, the accommodation association with quarterly statements respecting

(i) the destination marketing fees received from operators,

(ii) the destination marketing fees held in trust, and

(iii) the destination marketing fees transferred to the destination marketing organization and, if applicable, the accommodation association,

and

(c) if the trustee is designated for more than one geographic area, maintain separate trust records for each geographic area.

(2) The trustee must make the report of the audit conducted under subsection (1)(a) available to the Minister, the destination marketing organization and, if applicable, the accommodation association.

### Records

23 (1) For the purposes of section 10(2) of the Act, the records to be maintained by an operator must include

(a) financial records respecting the purchase price of accommodation and tourism experiences sold by the operator,

(b) copies of all receipts issued to purchasers for accommodation or tourism experiences in respect of which destination marketing fees are charged,

(c) records of destination marketing fees collected from purchasers,

(d) records of destination marketing fees remitted to the trustee, and

(e) any supporting documents related to the records described in clauses (a) to (d).

(2) A trustee must maintain records sufficient to enable the Minister to determine whether the trustee has complied with the Act and this Regulation, including

(a) records of destination marketing fees received from operators,

(b) trust account records, including deposit and withdrawal records,

(c) records of destination marketing fees transferred to a destination marketing organization and, if applicable, an accommodation association,

(d) records related to compliance audits conducted by the trustee under this Regulation, and

(e) any administrative or reporting records related to the trustee’s duties under this Regulation.

(3) A record required under this section must be retained for at least 4 years after the end of the calendar year in which the record was created.

## Part 4 Notification and Reporting

### Notification of changes

24 (1) A destination marketing organization, accommodation association or trustee designated under the Act must notify the Minister in writing within 30 days after any of the following changes:

(a) a change in the legal name, address or legal status of the entity, including an amalgamation, merger or dissolution;

(b) a change in the contact information of the entity;

(c) a change that affects the entity’s ability to meet a requirement for designation under the Act or this Regulation.

(2) A destination marketing organization designated under the Act must notify the Minister in writing within 30 days after a change in the rate of the destination marketing fee charged in the geographic area for which the destination marketing organization is designated.

(3) A destination marketing organization or accommodation association designated under the Act must notify the Minister in writing within 30 days after any of the following changes:

(a) a change in the names or positions of the officers or directors of the entity;

(b) a change in the beginning or end date of the entity’s fiscal year.

(4) A destination marketing organization designated under the Act must notify the Minister in writing within 30 days after a change in the composition of the entity’s board of directors.

(5) A trustee designated under the Act must notify the Minister in writing within 30 days after a change in the principals, directors or managers of the trustee.

(6) If a destination marketing organization notifies the Minister of a change in the rate of the destination marketing fee under subsection (2), the approved business plan of the destination marketing organization is, for the purposes of section 19, deemed to be amended to reflect the new rate, effective on the date of the notification.

(7) For greater certainty, a notification under this section does not constitute a request for or require the Minister’s approval.

### Information required in annual report

25 (1) An annual report submitted under section 22(1) of the Act must contain the following information:

(a) financial information respecting the destination marketing fees received during the fiscal year, including the gross amount received and a summary of the use or disposition of those fees;

(b) the audited financial statements for the fiscal year of the destination marketing organization or accommodation association that submits the annual report;

(c) a summary of expenses paid from destination marketing fees during the fiscal year in support of the purposes of the destination marketing organization or accommodation association under this Regulation;

(d) an overview of operator participation in the destination marketing fee program during the fiscal year;

(e) an overview of any partnerships formed with other destination marketing organizations, accommodation associations or tourism entities during the fiscal year;

(f) if applicable, a summary of any collaboration between the destination marketing organization and the accommodation association and the outcomes of that collaboration;

(g) performance indicators for the geographic area, including but not limited to

(i) accommodation sector performance, including but not limited to occupancy rates and total room nights generated,

(ii) visitation,

(iii) visitor spending,

(iv) the return on investment of initiatives funded by destination marketing fees,

(v) marketing engagement and performance,

(vi) conversion metrics, and

(vii) a summary of material initiatives undertaken during the fiscal year;

(h) a description of the marketing campaigns, events and other activities undertaken during the fiscal year;

(i) an overview of activities undertaken in each area or location within the geographic area.

(2) A destination marketing organization or accommodation association must make its annual report publicly available on its website.

### Annual business plan — destination marketing organization

26 (1) A designated destination marketing organization must, for each of its fiscal years, submit to the Minister, in the form and manner, and at the time, determined by the Minister, a business plan covering that fiscal year and the next 2 fiscal years.

(2) The business plan must include

(a) the destination marketing organization’s goals, strategies and performance measures,

(b) the rate for the destination marketing fee to be charged in the geographic area for which the destination marketing organization is designated,

(c) information respecting the composition of the destination marketing organization’s board of directors, including representation of

(i) the categories of accommodation providers in the geographic area for which the destination marketing organization is designated, as set out in section 6(b)(i), and

(ii) tourism experience providers in that area,

(d) information respecting the destination marketing organization’s staffing and operating capacity,

(e) information respecting the destination marketing organization’s planned use of destination marketing fees during the period covered by the business plan,

(f) an overview of activities to be undertaken in each area or location within the geographic area, and

(g) any other information required by the Minister.

(3) A destination marketing organization must make its annual business plan publicly available on its website.

### Annual business plan — accommodation association

27 (1) A designated accommodation association must, for each of its fiscal years, submit to the Minister, in the form and manner, and at the time, determined by the Minister, a business plan covering that fiscal year and the next 2 fiscal years.

(2) The business plan must include

(a) the accommodation association’s goals, strategies and performance measures,

(b) information respecting the accommodation association’s planned use of destination marketing fees during the period covered by the business plan,

(c) an overview of activities to be undertaken in each area or location within the geographic area for which the accommodation association is designated, and

(d) any other information required by the Minister.

(3) An accommodation association must make its annual business plan publicly available on its website.

### First annual report

28 If a destination marketing organization or accommodation association is designated after the coming into force of the Act, the first annual report required under section 22 of the Act must cover the period beginning on the date of designation and ending on the last day of the entity’s first fiscal year.

## Part 5 Administrative Penalties

### Prescribed contraventions

29 For the purposes of section 20(1) of the Act, an administrative penalty may be imposed in respect of a contravention of any of the following provisions of this Regulation:

(a) section 10, for failing to perform the duties of a designated trustee;

(b) section 21(2), for failing to transfer destination marketing fees in the proportions required under that subsection;

(c) section 22, for failing to comply with supplementary trust requirements;

(d) section 23(1) or (2), for failing to maintain records as required;

(e) section 23(3), for failing to retain records for the required period;

(f) section 24, other than section 24(1)(b), in respect of a contravention by a trustee, for failing to notify the Minister of a change within the required period.

### Notice of administrative penalty

30 For the purposes of section 20(2)(b) of the Act, a notice of administrative penalty must contain the following information:

(a) the date of the notice;

(b) the name of the person on whom the administrative penalty is imposed;

(c) identification of the provision of the Act or this Regulation that was contravened;

(d) a description of the contravention;

(e) the amount of the administrative penalty;

(f) the period within which the administrative penalty must be paid;

(g) a statement that the person may appeal the decision to the Court of King’s Bench under section 14 of the Act.

### Amount of administrative penalty

31 (1) The amount of an administrative penalty must not exceed $100 000.

(2) In determining the amount of an administrative penalty, the Minister must consider the following:

(a) the seriousness of the contravention;

(b) the degree of wilfulness or negligence involved in the contravention;

(c) any history of non-compliance with the Act or this Regulation by the person on whom the administrative penalty is imposed;

(d) any mitigating factors relating to the contravention;

(e) any economic benefit obtained as a result of the contravention.

### Time for payment

32 A person on whom an administrative penalty is imposed must pay the penalty within 30 days after the date on which the notice of administrative penalty is served or within the period stated in the notice, whichever is later.

### Service of notices

33 (1) A notice of administrative penalty may be served on a person

(a) by personal service,

(b) by ordinary mail sent to

(i) the last address the person provided to the Minister under the Act or this Regulation,

(ii) the address shown for the person on the corporate registries system maintained under the Societies Act or the Companies Act , or

(iii) the last address of the person who is to be served, as shown in any corporate registry maintained under an Alberta enactment,

or

(c) by registered mail sent to an address described in clause (b).

(2) A notice served by ordinary mail is deemed to have been received by the person 7 days after the date on which the notice is mailed.

(3) For the purposes of section 14(1) of the Act, a person on whom an administrative penalty is imposed is notified in writing of the decision on

(a) the date the notice is served personally under subsection (1)(a),

(b) the date the notice is deemed to have been received under subsection (2), or

(c) the date the notice is delivered by registered mail under subsection (1)(c).

## Part 6 Coming into Force

### Coming into force

34 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of section 23 of the Traveller Protection and Destination Development Act .
