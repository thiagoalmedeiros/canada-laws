# Preventative Health Testing Services Benefits Regulation

Alta Reg 194/2026

*Enabling act: Alberta Health Care Insurance Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,

(a) “cancer” means a malignant neoplastic disease or disorder characterized by uncontrolled cellular proliferation, including a malignant neoplastic disease or disorder that has the capacity for local tissue invasion or metastasis;

(b) “clinical finding of cancer” means a diagnosis of cancer established by a regulated practitioner within their scope of practice who relied on, in whole or in part, a diagnostic testing result identified by a regulated practitioner as the result of a preventative health testing service to establish the diagnosis;

(c) “regulated member” means a regulated member as defined in the Health Professions Act ;

(d) “regulated practitioner” means a practitioner who is a regulated member;

(e) “Schedule of Preventative Health Testing Services” means the Schedule of Preventative Health Testing Services referred to in section 3.

### Eligibility

2 (1) In this section,

(a) “Alberta EHR” means the Alberta EHR as defined in the Health Information Act ;

(b) “college” means a college as defined in the Health Professions Act ;

(c) “guardian” means a guardian as determined in accordance with the Family Law Act or Child, Youth and Family Enhancement Act .

(2) Benefits are payable in accordance with the regulations under Part 1 of the Act for preventative health testing services provided to a resident.

(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), benefits are not payable for preventative health testing services if a declaration under section 25 of the Health Insurance Premiums Act is in effect in respect of the resident who receives the services.

(4) The Minister may only pay a claim for benefits to a resident in respect of a preventative health testing service if

(a) the preventative health testing service was performed in Alberta,

(b) the preventative health testing service was performed by a regulated practitioner who, at the time of providing the preventative health testing service, was authorized by the regulated practitioner’s college to provide the service,

(c) the preventative health testing service was performed on or after the date the service is specified as a preventative health testing service in the Schedule of Preventative Health Testing Services,

(d) the diagnostic testing results of the preventative health testing service were made accessible via the Alberta EHR,

(e) the preventative health testing service was provided to a resident who is

(i) 18 years of age or older, or

(ii) under 18 years of age with the consent of the guardian of the resident,

and

(f) a clinical finding of cancer is established in relation to the resident and that diagnosis was not previously established by a regulated practitioner in relation to that resident.

### Benefits payable

3 (1) The following are set out in the Schedule of Preventative Health Testing Services made by order of the Minister under section 6.1 of the Act:

(a) the goods and services or classes of goods and services specified as preventative health testing services;

(b) the rates of benefits payable in respect of those preventative health testing services.

(2) The Minister must make the Schedule of Preventative Health Testing Services publicly available on the website of the Minister’s department.

(3) The benefits payable in respect of a preventative health testing service provided to a resident are limited to the lesser of

(a) the amount claimed by the resident, and

(b) the applicable rate of benefits set out in the Schedule of Preventative Health Testing Services for that preventative health testing service.

(4) The rate of benefits in respect of a preventative health testing service set out in the Schedule of Preventative Health Testing Services includes amounts for the following:

(a) performing the preventative health testing service;

(b) administrative activities that are directly related to the provision of the preventative health testing service;

(c) recording and maintaining information respecting the preventative health testing service provided, unless the recording or maintenance of the information is for the purposes of a third party;

(d) discussion or correspondence with a regulated member regarding treatment or a service to be provided to a resident directly related to managing the resident’s care after the preventative health testing service is provided.

### Information re claim for benefits for preventative health testing service

4 (1) In this section, “testing facility” means a place or premises in Alberta at which a preventative health testing service is provided to an individual.

(2) For the purposes of submitting a claim for benefits in respect of a preventative health testing service, a resident must provide the Minister with the following information:

(a) the resident’s

(i) name,

(ii) date of birth,

(iii) valid personal health number,

(iv) phone number,

(v) mailing address, and

(vi) email address, if any;

(b) the type of preventative health testing service that was provided to the resident;

(c) the date on which the preventative health testing service was provided to the resident;

(d) the name and address of the testing facility in which the preventative health testing service was provided;

(e) proof of payment of the amount paid by the resident for the preventative health testing service and the person to whom the amount was paid;

(f) the diagnostic testing results identified by a regulated practitioner as a result of the preventative health testing service;

(g) if the resident’s insurer paid all or a portion of the cost of the preventative health testing service, the name of the insurer and the amount paid by the insurer;

(h) confirmation of a clinical finding of cancer in relation to the resident;

(i) an attestation by the resident that the clinical finding of cancer is a diagnosis that was not previously established by a regulated practitioner in relation to that resident.

### Expiry

5 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on May 31, 2031.

### Coming into force

6 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of section 1(9) of the Health Statutes Amendment Act, 2026 .
