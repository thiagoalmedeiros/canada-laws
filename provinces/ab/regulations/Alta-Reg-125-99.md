# Exemption Regulation (Alberta Personal Property Bill of Rights)

Alta Reg 125/99

*Enabling act: Alberta Personal Property Bill of Rights*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation, “the Act” means the Alberta Personal Property Bill of Rights .

### Exempt Acts

2 Section 2 of the Act does not apply in respect of the following provincial Acts:
(a) the ATB Financial Act ;
(a.1) the Emissions Management and Climate Resilience Act ;
(b) the Environmental Protection and Enhancement Act ;
(c) the Financial Administration Act ;
(c.1) the Gaming, Liquor and Cannabis Act ;
(c.2) the Horse Racing Alberta Act ;
(d) the Meat Inspection Act ;
(e) the Public Lands Act ;
(e.1) the Unclaimed Personal Property and Vested Property Act ;
(f) repealed AR 33/2009 s2;
(f.1) the Victims Restitution and Compensation Payment Act ;
(g) the Water Act .

(a) the taking of specimens of a pest or nuisance or of any matter or thing that contains or is suspected of containing a pest or nuisance under section 17(2) of the Agricultural Pests Act ;

### Exempt activities and things

4 Section 2 of the Act does not apply in respect of the following:
(a) where title to personal property is acquired under, pursuant to or as a result of
(i) any proceedings taken respecting a certificate registered or filed in the court under the Alberta Corporate Tax Act , the Alberta Personal Income Tax Act , the Fuel Tax Act , the Tobacco Tax Act or the Tourism Levy Act ;
(i.1) any proceedings taken respecting a certificate issued under section 93 of the Hospitals Act , RSA 2000 cH-12;
(ii) any proceedings taken to enforce purchase-money security agreements under the Law of Property Act ;
(iii) repealed AR 33/2009 s4;
(b) an order of the Minister to take possession and control of the assets of a registered corporation under the Loan and Trust Corporations Act ;
(b.1) any act of an administrator, a provisional liquidator or the Minister under the Loan and Trust Corporations Act ;
(c) an order of the Minister or the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Corporation placing a credit union under supervision or an order of that Corporation placing a credit union under administration under the Credit Union Act ;
(c.1) any act of an administrator, a provisional liquidator, a liquidator, the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Corporation or the Minister under the Credit Union Act ;
(d) any act of an administrator, a provisional liquidator, liquidator, the Superintendent of Insurance or the Minister under the Insurance Act ;
(e) money payable to the President of Treasury Board and Minister of Finance pursuant to a written notice issued under the Alberta Corporate Tax Act , the Alberta Personal Income Tax Act , the Crown’s Right of Recovery Act , the Fuel Tax Act , the Investing in a Diversified Alberta Economy Act , the Tobacco Tax Act or the Tourism Levy Act;
(f) the seizure of money to enforce the collection of any grant paid by the Government under the Government Organization Act in the event of a default by the grantee;
(g) seizure by a liquidator under the Securities Act ;
(h) realization of security in the event of a borrower’s default on a loan that the Credit Union Deposit Guarantee Corporation acquired from a credit union;
(i) the acquisition by the Crown of ownership of a stray under regulations made under section 33(2)(b) and (c) of the Livestock Industry Diversification Act .

### Exempt regulations

5 Section 2 of the Act does not apply in respect of
(a) the Gaming, Liquor and Cannabis Regulation (AR 143/96);
(a.1) the Meat Inspection Regulation (AR 42/2003);
(a.2) the Public Lands Administration Regulation (AR 187/2011);
(b) the Timber Management Regulation (AR 60/73);
(c) the Vehicle Seizure and Removal Regulation (AR 251/2006).

### Exempt matters under regulations

6 Section 2 of the Act does not apply in respect of the following matters:
(a) orders issued under the authority of sections 8 and 11 of the Communicable Diseases Regulation (AR 238/85);
(b) the capture, destruction or other disposal of an animal under section 18 of the Provincial Parks (General) Regulation (AR 102/85).
