# Landfill Disposal Ban (Ministerial) Regulation

Alta Reg 182/2026

*Enabling act: Environmental Protection and Enhancement Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,
(a) “landfill” means a landfill as defined in the Waste Control Regulation (AR 192/96);
(b) “solar panel” means a photovoltaic module that
(i) is designed to convert sunlight into electricity, and
(ii) as designed and manufactured, has a surface area of 1 m² or more,
and includes a part or component of the module.

### Prohibition

2 No person shall dispose of or permit the disposal of a solar panel in a landfill.

### Expiry

3 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on June 30, 2031.

### Coming into force

4 This Regulation comes into force on October 1, 2026.
