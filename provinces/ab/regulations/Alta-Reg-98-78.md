# Revolving Fund Designation Regulation

Alta Reg 98/78

*Enabling act: Financial Administration Act, 1977*

1 In this regulation, “Act” means The Financial Administration Act, 1977 .
2 The Funds listed in the Schedule are designated as revolving funds under the Act.
3 Repealed AR 264/78 s2.

## Schedule

1 The Land Purchase Fund established under section 1 of The Government Land Purchases Act .
2 The Gas Alberta Operating Fund established under section 34 of The Rural Gas Act.
3 The Seed Grain and Livestock Fund established under section 11 of The Department of Agriculture Act.
