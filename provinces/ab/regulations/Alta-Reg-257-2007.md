# Gas Utilities Designation Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Alberta website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/1266.cfm?page=2007_257.cfm&leg_type=Regs&isbncln=9780779863655&display=html) before relying on it.

Alta Reg 257/2007

*Enabling act: Gas Utilities Act*

### Gas utility pipelines

1 The following gas utilities are designated for purposes of section 1(g.1) of the Gas Utilities Act :

(a) ATCO Gas and Pipelines Ltd.;

(b) repealed AR 185/2009;

(c) Apex Utilities Inc.

### Application of ss26 and 27

2 Sections 26 and 27 of the Gas Utilities Act apply to the following owners of gas utilities:

(a) Apex Utilities Inc.;

(b), (b.1) repealed AR 119/2021 s3;

(c) ATCO Gas and Pipelines Ltd.;

(d) Canadian Utilities Limited;

(e) CU Inc.;

(f) TriSummit Utilities Inc.;

(g) TriSummit Utility Group Inc.;

(h) TriSummit Utility Holdings Inc.

### Repeal

3 The Designation Regulation (AR 237/2005) is repealed.

### Expiry

4 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on July 31, 2031.

### Coming into force

5 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of the Alberta Utilities Commission Act .
