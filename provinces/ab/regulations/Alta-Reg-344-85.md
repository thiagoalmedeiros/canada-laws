Alta Reg 344/85

*Enabling act: Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act*

1 The following are, or are declared to be, reciprocating jurisdictions for the purposes of the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act :

The Province of British Columbia

The Province of Manitoba

The Province of Newfoundland

The Province of New Brunswick

The Province of Nova Scotia

The Province of Ontario

The Province of Prince Edward Island

The Province of Saskatchewan

The Northwest Territories

The Yukon Territory

Nunavut

The Commonwealth of Australia

The State of Washington, United States of America

The State of Idaho, United States of America

The State of Montana, United States of America

The State of Arizona, United States of America

2 Alberta Regulation 579/57 is repealed.
