# Adult Adoption Regulation

> **Unofficial version.** Text obtained from the official Alberta website via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the [official source](https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/1266.cfm?page=1998_246.cfm&leg_type=Regs&isbncln=9780779864980&display=html) before relying on it.

Alta Reg 246/98

*Enabling act: Adult Adoption Act*

### Forms

1 The forms set out in the Schedule are prescribed as the forms for the purposes of the Adult Adoption Act.

### Appearance by potential adoptee

2 Where the person to be adopted indicates in Form 3 a wish to appear in person before the judge considering the adoption application, the applicant must

(a) serve the person to be adopted with a copy of the filed application and supporting documents personally or by double registered mail at least 10 days before the date of the hearing, and

(b) file an affidavit of service with the court clerk of the Court of King’s Bench at least 7 days before the date of the hearing.

### Repeal

3 The Adult Adoption Regulation (AR 359/94) is repealed.

### Expiry

4 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on November 30, 2036.
