Alta Reg 43/2019

*Enabling act: Supporting Alberta’s Local Food Sector Act*

### Definition of process

1 For the purposes of the Act, “process” means altering an agricultural product for the purpose of making a product for human consumption or livestock feed.

### Complaints

2 A person may make a complaint under section 10 of the Act in writing in a form approved by the Minister.
