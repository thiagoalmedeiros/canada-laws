Alta Reg 255/80

*Enabling act: Court of Justice Act*

1 The authorized seal to be used by the Alberta Court of Justice as occasion requires shall be in accordance with the facsimile on the attached Schedule.

2 The Provincial Court of Alberta Seal Regulation (Alta. Reg. 143/80) is repealed.

3 This Regulation comes into force September 1, 1980.
