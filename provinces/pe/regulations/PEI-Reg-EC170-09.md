PEI Reg EC170/09

*Made under the Wildlife Conservation Act*

Pursuant to section 28 of the Wildlife Conservation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. W-4.1, Council made

the following regulations:

### Wildlife Conservation Fund

1.(1) The Wildlife Conservation Fund is continued pursuant to the Wildlife Conservation Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. W-4.1.

### Fees

(2) Subject to subsection (2.1), every person who obtains a license or a permit for angling,

hunting or trapping pursuant to the Wildlife Conservation Act or any regulations made or

continued under the Act, shall pay a Wildlife Conservation Fund fee

(a) in the amount of $13.00 (HST not included) where the person is 65 years of age or

older;

(b) in the amount of $20.00 (HST not included) where the person is between 16 and 65

years of age; or

(c) in the amount of $5.00 (HST not included) for a Family Wildlife Conservation Fund

license where the person purchases a family angling license.

### Exemption

(2.1) The following persons who obtain a license or permit pursuant to the Wildlife Conservation

Act or its regulations are exempt from the requirement to pay a fee specified under subsection

(2) in respect of that license or permit:

(a) a person who is exempt from the requirement to pay a fee for an angling license

pursuant to clause 1(d) or (e) of the Schedule to the Angling Regulations (EC532/19);

(b) a person who is exempt from the requirement to pay a fee for a hunting license under

clause 1(c) or (d) of the License and Permit Fees Regulations.

### Fees paid to Fund

(3) Wildlife Conservation Fund fees collected pursuant to subsection (2) shall be paid into the

Wildlife Conservation Fund.

### Wildlife/Family Wildlife Conservation Fund license

(4) Where a person has paid the Wildlife Conservation Fund fee pursuant to subsection (2), or is

exempt from that requirement pursuant to subsection (2.1), the Department shall issue

(a) a Wildlife Conservation Fund license valid for the person named on the license; or

(b) a Family Wildlife Conservation Fund license valid for the person named on the

license and the person’s spouse and dependents.

### Expiry of license

(5) Every Wildlife Conservation Fund license expires on March 31 of the year immediately

following the issue of the license.

### Idem

(6) Every Family Wildlife Conservation Fund license expires five days after the date of the issue

of the license.

### Prohibition

(7) No person shall hunt or trap without being in possession of a valid Wildlife Conservation

Fund license.

### Idem

(8) No person shall angle without being in possession of a valid Wildlife Conservation Fund

license or a valid Family Wildlife Conservation Fund license.

### Multiple licenses

(9) Where a person holds more than one license pursuant to the Act or any regulations made or

continued pursuant to the Act, the person is required to hold only one valid Wildlife

Conservation Fund license.

### Exception

(10) These regulations do not apply to aboriginal persons or persons under the age of sixteen

years.

### Revocation

2. The Wildlife Conservation Act Wildlife Conservation Fund Regulations (EC211/99) are

revoked.
