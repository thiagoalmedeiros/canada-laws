PEI Reg EC616/19

*Made under the Coroners Act*

Pursuant to section 56 of the Coroners Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-25.1, Council made the following

regulations:

### Qualifications for field investigator

1. In order to be considered for appointment as a field investigator under the Coroners Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-25.1, a person must have successfully completed

(a) a nursing education program;

(b) a medical degree program; or

(c) training in emergency medical services.
