PEI Reg EC219/96

*Made under the Prince Edward Island Lands Protection Act*

Pursuant to clause 17(1)(c) of the Prince Edward Island Lands Protection Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. L-5, Council made the following regulations:

### Act, defined

1.(1) In these regulations, “Act” means the Prince Edward Island Lands Protection Act R.S.P.E.I.

1988, Cap. L-5.

### Forms

(2) The following forms are prescribed:

(a) Form 1 - application by or on behalf of a non-resident pursuant to section 4 of

the Act;

(b) Form 2 - application by or on behalf of a corporation pursuant to subsection 5(1) of

the Act;

(b.1) Form 2A - application by or on behalf of a corporation for an amended permit

pursuant to subsection 5(2) of the Act;

(b.2) Form 2B – application by or on behalf of a non-profit corporation pursuant to

subsection 5(1) of the Act;

(c) to (g) revoked by EC647/09;

(h) Form 6A - aggregate land holding declaration for a person pursuant to section 10 of

the Act;

(i) Form 6B - aggregate land holding declaration for a corporation pursuant to section 10

of the Act;

(j) to (m) - revoked by EC191/22

(n) Form 8A - application by or on behalf of a non-resident or corporation pursuant to

clause 5.3(1)(b) of the Act;

(o) Form 8B - statement for reporting land leased in pursuant to subsection 5.3(2) of

the Act;

(p) Form 9 - application by or on behalf of a cooperative venture pursuant to

subsection 1.1(3) of the Prince Edward Island Lands Protection Act Exemption

Regulations;

(q) Form 10A - aggregate land holding declaration for a person pursuant to

subsection 11(2) of the Act;

(r) Form 10B - aggregate land holding declaration for a corporation pursuant to

subsection 11(2) of the Act.
