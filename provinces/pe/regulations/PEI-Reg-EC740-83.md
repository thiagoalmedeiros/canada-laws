PEI Reg EC740/83

*Made under the Limited Partnerships Act*

Under authority of section 33 of the Limited Partnerships Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. L-13, Council

made the following regulations:

### Declaration

1.(1) to (3) Revoked by EC336/06.

### Power of attorney

(4) A power of attorney shall be in Form 4 as set out in the Schedule.

### Fees

2. The following fees are prescribed:

(a) for filing a declaration under subsection 3(1) or 24(1) of the Act.......................$200.00

(b) for filing a new declaration ......................................................................................200.00

(c) for filing a declaration of change under subsection 18(1) or section 24(7) ..........100.00

(d) for a certificate of good standing…..........……………….……………….. ...........50.00
