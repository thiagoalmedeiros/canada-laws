PEI Reg EC394/04

*Made under the Certified Fisheries Organizations Support Act*

Pursuant to section 10 of the Certified Fisheries Organizations Support Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-

2.1, Council made the following regulations:

### Annual Support Fee

1. The prescribed annual support fee payable, under subsection 8(1) of the Act, to a certified

fisheries organization is $500.

### Additional certification requirements

2. In addition to the eligibility criteria or requirements for certification required under

subsection 7(3) of the Act, in order to be eligible for certification, a fisheries organization

shall satisfy the Minister that the fisheries organization

(a) has membership in each of the three counties in the province; and

(b) has, as members at the time the fisheries organization applies for certification, at

least 20% of the total number of persons who are fishers.

### Issuance of receipts

3. A certified fisheries organization shall issue any necessary receipts to fishers for moneys

received under the Act or these regulations.
