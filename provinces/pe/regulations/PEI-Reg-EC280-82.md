PEI Reg EC280/82

*Made under the Consumer Protection Act*

Pursuant to section 28 of the Consumer Protection Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-19, Council made the

following regulation:

### Exemption

1. The Consumer Protection Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-19 does not apply to credit unions and

the Credit Union League as defined in the Credit Unions Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-29.
