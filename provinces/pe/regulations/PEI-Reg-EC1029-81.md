PEI Reg EC1029/81

*Made under the Highway Traffic Act*

Pursuant to section 299 of the Highway Traffic Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-5, Council made the

following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations

(a) “waste” means waste ordinarily accepted for garbage disposal by a municipality and

includes ashes, garbage, tree limbs, leaves, refuse and domestic waste;

(b) “designated area” means the area within the boundaries of the following

municipalities and communities:

Charlottetown Crossroads Parkdale

North River Sherwood Winsloe

Bunbury Union Southport

Cornwall Elliot River Miltonvale Park

Hillsborough Park East Royalty West Royalty

### Load covering

2.(1) Subject to subsection (2), where a motor vehicle, with or without a trailer attached, is

carrying within the designated area a load that is waste, that portion of the load that is not

enclosed by the vehicle or load container shall be covered by a covering that is made of

tarpaulin, canvas, netting or other material in such manner as to confine the load within the

vehicle or load container.

### Exceptions

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply where the motor vehicle is being operated

(a) in the course of collecting waste; or

(b) within the limits of a highway construction contract.

### Offence

(3) Any person who operates a motor vehicle on a highway in contravention of subsection (1) is

guilty of an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not less than fifty

dollars.
