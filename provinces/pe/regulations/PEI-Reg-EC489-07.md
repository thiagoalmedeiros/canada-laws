PEI Reg EC489/07

*Made under the Coroners Act*

Pursuant to section 56 of the Coroners Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-25.1, Council made the

following regulations:

### Act

1. In these regulations, “Act” means the Coroners Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-25.1.

### The remuneration payable to a coroner

2.(a) for performing his or her duties under the Act,

(i) between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 12 midnight, is $100 per hour, and

(ii) between the hours of 12 midnight and 8:00 a.m., is $150 per hour;

(b) for arranging an autopsy and interpreting the results, is $150;

(c) for preparing and submitting any report required under the Act, is $50.

### Remuneration payable to a pathologist

3. The remuneration payable to a pathologist

(a) for preparing and reporting the results of a post-mortem examination is $436; and

(b) for preparing and reporting the results of a forensic post-mortem examination is

$555.

### Remuneration for witness

4. The remuneration payable to a witness summoned by a coroner to give evidence at an

inquest is,

(a) in the case of a witness other than one referred to in clause (b) or (c), $25 per

half day;

(b) in the case of a witness who is a pathologist or medical practitioner, $150 per half

day; and

(c) in the case of a witness who is a professional person, other than a person mentioned

in clause (b), $100 per half day.

### Travel allowance for witness

5. The travel allowance payable to a witness who attends an inquest is,

(a) where the witness resides within the city or town in which the inquest is held, $3 per

day;

(b) where the witness resides outside the city or town where the inquest is held, 24 cents

per kilometre each way between his or her residence and the place of the inquest; and

(c) where the witness resides outside the Province of Prince Edward Island and is

required to attend an inquest in Prince Edward Island, the minimum return airfare

plus reasonable living expenses.

### Remuneration payable to an interpreter

6. The remuneration payable to an interpreter at an inquest is $50 per half day.

### Remuneration and allowances payable to jurors

7. The remuneration and allowances payable to jurors at an inquest shall be the same as those

paid to jurors under the Jury Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. J-5.1.

### Fees

8. The Minister may authorize any further fees and allowances that the Minister considers

reasonable respecting services required and provided in the administration of the Act.

### Warrant to take possession of body, Form

9.(1) A warrant to take possession of a body pursuant to clause 6(1)(a) of the Act shall be in

Form 1 of the Schedule.

### Warrant re post-mortem examination, Form

(2) A warrant pursuant to subsection 10(1) of the Act requiring a post-mortem examination shall

be in Form 2 of the Schedule.

### Report of post-mortem, Form

(3) A report of a post-mortem examination pursuant to subsection 10(3) of the Act shall be in

Form 3 of the Schedule.

### Warrant to disinter body, Form

(4) An order to disinter a body pursuant to section 11 of the Act shall be in Form 4 of the

Schedule.

### Certificate for release of a body, Form

(5) A certificate of a coroner releasing a body pursuant to subsection 14(2) of the Act shall be in

Form 5 of the Schedule.

### Idem

(6) A certificate of a coroner releasing a body pursuant to subsection 14(4) of the Act shall be in

Form 6 of the Schedule.

### Report, Form

(7) A report of a coroner pursuant to clause 15(1)(a) of the Act shall be in Form 7 of the

Schedule.

### Order directing inquest, Form

(8) An order directing an inquest pursuant to section 19 of the Act shall be in Form 8 of the

Schedule.

### Summons, Form

(9) A summons to an inquest witness pursuant to subsection 38(1) of the Act shall be in Form 9

of the Schedule.

### Order to witness, Form

(10) An order to a witness pursuant to subsection 38(3) of the Act who is confined to a place

mentioned in clause 5(1)(h) of the Act shall be in Form 10 of the Schedule.

### Jury report, Form

(11) A jury report prepared pursuant to section 49 of the Act shall be in Form 11 of the Schedule.
