PEI Reg EC456/95

*Made under the Direct Sellers Act*

Pursuant to section 15 of the Direct Sellers Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. D-11, Council made the

following regulations:

### Fee

1. The fee for

(a) a vendor’s license is $200;

(b) a salesperson’s license is $50.

### Term

2. A license issued under the Act is valid for a two-year term unless earlier cancelled.
