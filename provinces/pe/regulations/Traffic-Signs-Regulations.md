# Traffic Signs Regulations

*Made under the Highway Traffic Act*

Pursuant to sections 242 and 243 of the Highway Traffic Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-5, the Minister

of Transportation, Infrastructure and Energy made the following regulations:

### Stop sign

1.(1) A stop sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 1 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a stop sign is erected at or within 15 metres of the intersection of two or more

highways and facing the traffic on one highway, the driver of a vehicle on that last mentioned

highway shall stop the vehicle before entering as near as practicable to the driving surface of

the other highway and shall yield the right-of-way to traffic on the other highway, but if there

is a crosswalk at or in the vicinity of the sign, the driver of the vehicle facing such sign shall

stop the vehicle before entering the crosswalk and keep the vehicle stopped until the

crosswalk is clear of pedestrians and shall yield the right-of-way to traffic on the other

highway.

### Flagperson sign

2.(1) A flagperson sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 2 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a flagperson sign is held or displayed facing traffic on a highway, the driver of a

vehicle on that highway shall stop the vehicle before reaching the sign, and keep the vehicle

stopped until the sign is removed, and if the sign is held or displayed at or near a crosswalk

the driver shall stop the vehicle before entering the crosswalk and keep the vehicle stopped

until the crosswalk is clear of pedestrians and the sign is removed. (Jan.19/84)

### Yield sign

3.(1) A yield sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 3 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a yield sign is erected at or within 15 metres of the intersection of two or more

highways and facing the traffic on one highway, the driver of a vehicle on that last mentioned

highway shall reduce speed and shall yield the right-of-way to traffic on the other highway.

(Jan.19/84)

### Crosswalk sign

4.(1) A crosswalk sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 4 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Each sign of the form prescribed by subsection (1) may be varied by the insertion on the sign

of such words, figures, or colors as may be prescribed by the Minister or the traffic authority

for the place where the sign is erected.

### Idem

(3) Where a crosswalk sign is erected at or within 15 metres of a crosswalk and facing the traffic

on a highway and a flagperson stop sign is held or displayed facing traffic on the highway,

the driver shall obey the flagperson stop sign, and, if there is no flagperson stop sign and a

pedestrian is in the crosswalk or about to enter the crosswalk, the driver shall stop the vehicle

before entering the crosswalk and keep the vehicle stopped until the crosswalk is clear of

pedestrians, (Jan.19/84)

### Maximum speed sign

5.(1) Subject to section 5.1, a maximum speed sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 5 of Schedule

A, and having any two digit number ending in zero.

### Idem

(2) Subject to subsection (4), where a maximum speed sign is erected facing the traffic on a

highway the number shown on the face of the sign shall be the maximum speed in kilometers

per hour permitted on that section of highway between that maximum speed sign and the next

maximum speed sign facing the same traffic on that highway.

### Sign varied by Minister

(3) Each sign of the form prescribed by subsection (1) may be varied by the insertion on the sign

of such words, figures, or colors as may be prescribed by the Minister or the traffic authority

for the place where the sign is erected.

### Zone end

(4) The maximum speed on a section of highway as established by a maximum speed sign may

be terminated by a sign indicating the end of that zone, and beyond the sign indicating the

end of that zone the maximum speed shall be the same as that permitted immediately before

entering that zone. (Jan.19/84; May 18/02)

### “Variable speed sign”

5.1.(1) ”Variable speed sign” means an electronic sign that

(a) uses an illuminated screen to display the maximum speed limit in kilometres per

hour; and

(b) is capable of being varied to indicate a maximum speed for a highway or part of a

highway as any two-digit number ending in zero.

### Use of variable speed sign

(2) Where permitted by the Minister, a variable speed sign may be used on a highway or any part

of a highway.

### Effect of variable speed sign

(3) Where use of a variable speed sign is permitted pursuant to subsection (2), the maximum

speed on the highway or part of the highway shall be the number displayed on the variable

speed sign.

### Depiction

(4) A variable speed sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 5.1 of Schedule A. (May 18/02)

### No right turn sign

6.(1) A no right turn sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 6 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a no right turn sign is erected at or within 15 metres of an intersection of two or more

highways and facing the traffic on one highway, the driver of a vehicle on that last mentioned

highway shall not turn right at the intersection. (Jan.19/84)

### No left turn sign

7.(1) A no left turn sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 7 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a no left turn sign is erected at or within 15 metres of an intersection of two or more

highways and facing the traffic on one highway, the driver of a vehicle on the last mentioned

highway shall not turn left at the intersection. (Jan.19/84)

### No U turn sign

8.(1) A no U turn sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 8 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a no U turn sign is erected facing the traffic on a highway, the driver of a vehicle shall

not execute a U turn at or near that location. (Jan.19/84)

### No right turn on red traffic signal sign

9.(1) A no right turn on red traffic signal sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 9 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a no right turn on a red traffic signal sign is erected at or within 15 metres of an

intersection of two or more highways and facing the traffic on one highway, the driver of a

vehicle on the last mentioned highway shall not turn right at the intersection while the red

signal facing the traffic on that highway is shown. (Jan.19/84)

### Turn left sign

10.(1) A turn left sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 10 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a turn left sign is erected at or within 15 metres of an intersection of two or more

highways and facing the traffic on one highway the driver of a vehicle on that last mentioned

highway shall turn left at the intersection. (Jan.19/84)

### Turn right sign

11.(1) A turn right sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 11 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a turn right sign is erected at or within 15 metres of an intersection of two or more

highways and facing the traffic on one highway, the driver of a vehicle on that last mentioned

highway shall turn right at the intersection. (Jan.19/84)

### No turns sign

12.(1) A no turns sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 12 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a no turns sign is erected at or within 15 metres of an intersection of two or more

highways and facing the traffic on one highway, the driver of a vehicle on that last mentioned

highway shall proceed straight ahead at the intersection. (Jan.19/84)

### One way sign

13.(1) A one way sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 13 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a one way sign is erected at or within 15 metres of an intersection of two or more

highways and facing the traffic on one highway, the driver of a vehicle on that last mentioned

highway, if entering the other highway shall turn as required to proceed in the direction

indicated by the arrow on the one way sign. (Jan.19/84)

### Do not enter sign

14.(1) A do not enter sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 14 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a do not enter sign is erected on a highway at the intersection of two or more

highways, the driver of a vehicle shall not proceed onto the first mentioned highway in the

direction facing the sign.

### Idem

(3) A separate sign with the words “DO NOT ENTER” shall be attached to this sign. (Jan.19/84)

### Keep right sign

15.(1) A keep right sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 15 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a keep right sign is erected at an obstruction within the highway and facing the traffic

on the highway, the driver of a vehicle shall drive to the right of the obstruction. (Jan.19/84)

### Do not pass sign

16.(1) A do not pass sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 16 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a do not pass sign is erected facing the traffic on a highway, the driver of a vehicle

shall not overtake or pass another vehicle proceeding in the same direction except that, when

the other vehicle is in another lane marked as a separate turn lane preceding an intersection,

the driver may pass the other vehicle by staying in the lane marked as the through lane.

(Jan.19/84)

### Passing permitted sign

17.(1) A passing permitted sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 17 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a passing permitted sign is erected facing the traffic on a highway, it shall indicate the

end of the no-passing zone created by a do not pass sign, and the driver of a vehicle may

proceed to pass other vehicles provided that all other conditions for passing as required by the

Highway Traffic Act and regulations are met. (Jan.19/84)

### Keep right except to pass sign

18.(1) A keep right except to pass sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 18 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a keep right except to pass sign is erected facing the traffic on a highway, the driver of

a vehicle shall drive in the lane closest to the right side of the roadway except when

overtaking another vehicle or where the lane closest to the right side of the roadway is

marked as a right turn lane preceding an intersection. (Jan.19/84)

### No parking sign

19.(1) A no parking sign

(a) shall be as depicted in Diagram 19 of Schedule A; or

(b) shall bear the words “NO PARKING” and where a sign referred to in this clause is

used, the part of the highway to which the sign applies shall be indicated by painted

lines, words, or markers on the highway.

### Sign varied, when

(2) Each sign of the form prescribed by subsection (1) may be varied by the insertion on the sign

of such symbols, words, figures, or letters as may be required to indicate the times of day and

the days of the week for which the sign is to be effective, or to indicate the types of vehicles

which are not affected.

### Sign beside highway

(3) Where a no parking sign is erected beside a highway, the driver of a vehicle shall not park the

vehicle in the area to which the sign applies unless the parking of that type of vehicle is

permitted by the sign.

### When parking permitted

(4) Where a no parking sign indicating the times of the day and the days of the week for which it

is to be effective as prescribed in subsection (2) is erected beside a highway, the driver of a

vehicle shall not park the vehicle in the area to which the sign applies during the times

indicated on the sign unless the parking of that type of vehicle is permitted by the sign.

(Jan.19/84)

### Parking control sign

20.(1) A parking control sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 20 of Schedule A.

### Sign varied

(2) Each sign of the form prescribed in subsection (1) may be varied by the insertion on the sign

of such symbols, words, figures, or letters as may be required to indicate the duration of the

parking, the time of day and the days of the week for which the sign is to be effective.

### Idem

(3) Where a parking control sign is erected beside a highway, the driver of a vehicle may park

the vehicle in the area to which the sign applies

(a) during the time of the day and the days of the week indicated by the sign for a length

of time not exceeding the duration indicated on the sign; and

(b) during the time of the day and the days of the week not indicated on the sign

provided that such parking is not in violation of any other section of the Act or

regulations. (Jan.19/84)

### No stopping sign

21.(1) A no stopping sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 21 of Schedule A.

### Sign varied

(2) Each sign of the form prescribed by subsection (1) may be varied by the insertion on the sign

of such symbols, words, figures, or letters as may be required to indicate the times of the day

and the days of the week for which the sign is to be effective, or to indicate the types of

vehicles that are not affected.

### When parking permitted

(3) Where a no stopping sign is erected beside a highway, the driver of a vehicle shall not stop

the vehicle in the area to which the sign applies unless that type of vehicle is permitted by the

sign.

### Idem

(4) Where a no stopping sign indicating the times of the day and the days of the week for which

it is to be effective as prescribed in subsection (2) is erected beside a highway, the driver of a

vehicle shall not stop the vehicle in the area to which the sign applies during the times

indicated on the sign unless the stopping of that type of vehicle is permitted by the sign.

(Jan.19/84)

### Truck prohibition sign

22.(1) A truck prohibition sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 22 of Schedule A.

### Idem

(2) Where a truck prohibition sign is erected on a highway at the intersection of two or more

highways, the driver of a vehicle or combination of vehicles designed for the transportation of

property rather than passengers which has either two axles and six or more tires, or has three

or more axles, shall not proceed onto the first mentioned highway unless the vehicle is

actively involved in performing a service along the highway having the truck prohibition

sign, and no alternative route is available.

### Effective periods

(3) Each truck prohibition sign in the form prescribed by subsection (1) may be varied by the

insertion on the sign of such symbols, words, figures or letters as may be required to indicate

the times of day, the days of the week or any other limited period of time for which the sign is

to be effective.

### City bylaw to govern use of sign within city

(4) Where a truck prohibition sign in the form prescribed by subsection (1) is placed or erected

within the City of Charlottetown, its use shall be governed by the provisions of the City of

Charlottetown Act, 28 Eliz. II (1979) c.22 and the bylaws enacted thereunder.

### Maximum weight sign

23.(1) A maximum weight sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 23 of Schedule A and having any

one or two digit number.

### Idem

(2) Where a maximum weight sign is erected facing the traffic on a highway, the driver of a

vehicle or combination of vehicles which exceeds the weight shown on the sign shall stop the

vehicle and not proceed on that highway until the vehicle weight has been reduced to that

shown on the sign or shall proceed on an alternative route. (Jan.19/84)

### Weigh scale sign

24.(1) A weigh scale sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 24 of Schedule A.

### Sign varied

(2) Each sign of the form prescribed by subsection (1) may be varied by the use on the sign of

letters and numbers to indicate the approximate distance from the sign to the scale site.

### Weighing requirement

(3) Where a weigh scale sign is erected facing the traffic on a highway, the driver of a vehicle or

combination of vehicles designed for the transportation of property rather than passengers

which has either two axles and six or more tires, or has three or more axles, shall proceed to

the scale site and stop and cause the vehicle to be weighed.

### Electric vehicle recharge sign

24.1.(1) An electric vehicle charging station sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 25 of Schedule A

and be designated by

(a) a sign bearing a symbol or words approved by the Minister; or

(b) a painted area that may bear a symbol or words approved by the Minister.

### Sign varied

(2) Each sign of the form prescribed in subsection (1) may be varied by the insertion on the sign

of symbols, words, figures or letters that may be required to indicate the times of day and

days of the week for which the sign is to be effective.

### Electric vehicle parking space

24.2.(1) An electric vehicle parking space sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 26 of Schedule A and

be designated by

(a) a sign bearing a symbol or words approved by the Minister; or

(b) a painted area that may bear a symbol or words approved by the Minister.

### Sign varied

(2) Each sign of the form prescribed in subsection (1) may be varied by the insertion on the sign

of symbols, words, figures or letters that may be required to indicate the duration of the

parking, the times of day and days of the week for which the sign is to be effective.

### Yield to transit bus sign

24.3.(1) A yield to transit bus sign shall be as depicted in Diagram 27 of Schedule A.

### Sign varied

(2) Each sign of the form prescribed in subsection (1) may be varied by the insertion on the sign

of symbols, words, figures or letters that may be required to indicate the times of day and

days of the week for which the sign is to be effective.

### Dimensions

25. The dimensions shown in each Diagram of Schedule A are the minimum dimensions of the

sign and a larger sign may be used with each dimension being increased in the same

proportion except that larger no parking signs, parking control signs, no stopping signs, and

weigh scale signs may be used without each dimension being increased in the same

proportion. (Jan.19/84)

### Information

26. The words listed in Column 2 of Schedule B may be attached as a separate sign to the sign as

listed in Column 1. (Jan.19/84)

### Offence and penalty

27. Every person who fails to comply with these regulations is guilty of an offence and liable on

summary conviction to a fine of not less than $100 and not more than $500.
