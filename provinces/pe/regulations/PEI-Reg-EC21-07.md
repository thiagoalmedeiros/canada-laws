PEI Reg EC21/07

*Made under the Real Property Transfer Tax Act*

Pursuant to section 5.1 of the Real Property Transfer Tax Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-5.1, Council

made the following regulations:

### “Act”, defined

1. In these regulations, “Act” means the Real Property Transfer Tax Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. R-5.1.

### Declaration, form

2. The declaration referred to in subsection 5(2) of the Act shall be in a form approved by the

Minister and shall be filed upon the registration of the deed with the Registrar.

### Prescribed maximum dollar amount

3. Revoked by EC428/16.
