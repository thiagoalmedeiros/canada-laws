PEI Reg EC446/12

*Made under the Court Fees Act*

Pursuant to section 4 of the Court Fees Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-27.001, Council made the

following regulations:

### Fees payable

1. The fees payable in the Court of Appeal and Supreme Court are as set out in Schedule 1.

### Idem

2. The fees payable for criminal court matters in the Court of Appeal, Supreme Court and

Provincial Court are as set out in Schedule 2.

### Deposit

3. The deposit for a jury trial is as set out in Schedule 3.

### Fees

4. The fees for the services performed by a sheriff are as set out in Schedule 4.

### Fees and allowances

5. The fees and allowances payable to Crown witnesses and jurors are as set out in Schedule 5.

## SCHEDULE 2

CRIMINAL COURT MATTERS

1. The following fees are payable in the Court of Appeal, Supreme Court and Provincial Court:

MATTER FEE

1. For a criminal conviction search $ 20

2. For each certificate of conviction 20

3. Revoked by EC224/22

4. For the making of a copy of a recorded proceeding 20

## SCHEDULE 3

JURY TRIAL

1. Subject to the provisions of the Jury Act, in the Supreme Court, the party filing a jury notice

shall deposit a fee of not less than $2,000 with the Registrar at least 30 days prior to the trial date.

## SCHEDULE 4

SHERIFF’S FEES

1. (1) The following fees and costs are payable for services performed by a sheriff:

SERVICE FEE

1. For up to three attempts, whether or not successful, to serve $125 plus $10 notary fee and

a claim, third party claim, summons to witness, notice of mileage at the rates referred to in

examination or notice of garnishment, issued out of another subsection (2)

jurisdiction, for each person to be served at the same address

2. The execution of an enforcement order under Rule 60 or 74 $100 plus 10% on the amount

of the Rules of Court, or a seizure under a written realized, mileage at the rates

authorization to act as agent referred to in subsection (2), and

all reasonable expenses and

disbursements necessarily incurred

including, without limitation, fees

for towing, appraisals and storage

3. Enforcing an eviction order $125 plus mileage at the rates

referred to in subsection (2)

4. Booking fee for a mortgage sale or tax sale 100

5. Conducting a mortgage sale or tax sale 150

6. Conducting a judgment debtor exam 75

(2) Travel costs pertaining to services performed by a sheriff shall be charged at the same rates as

those specified from time to time by the Treasury Board.

(3) The hourly rate charged for each sheriff performing services, in addition to the fees set out in

subsection (1), shall be $50.
