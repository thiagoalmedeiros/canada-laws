# Ukrainian Famine and Genocide (Holodomor) Memorial Day Act

RSPEI 1988, c U-.01

(Assented to November 24, 2023)

### Preamble

WHEREAS the Holodomor was a famine in Soviet Ukraine in 1932 and 1933 that killed millions of

Ukrainians;

AND WHEREAS the term Holodomor emphasizes the Ukrainian famine’s intentional aspects

including the Soviet regime’s rejection of outside aid, confiscation of household food and restriction of

population movement;

AND WHEREAS the Ukrainian famine is recognized by 16 countries, including Canada, as a genocide;

AND WHEREAS Prince Edward Island must join other provinces in recognizing the Ukrainian famine

as a genocide;

THEREFORE BE IT ENACTED by the Lieutenant Governor and the Legislative Assembly of the

Province of Prince Edward Island as follows:

### Ukrainian Famine and Genocide (Holodomor) Memorial Day

1.(1) Throughout the Province of Prince Edward Island, the fourth Saturday in November each year

shall be known as “Ukrainian Famine and Genocide (Holodomor) Memorial Day”.

### Not a holiday

(2) For greater certainty, Ukrainian Famine and Genocide (Holodomor) Memorial Day is not a

holiday under any enactment.
