# White Cane Act

RSPEI 1988, c W-4

### Definitions

1. In this Act

(a) “blind person” means a person who

(i) is registered as blind with the Canadian National Institute for the Blind, a

corporation incorporated under the Canada Corporations Act R.S.C. 1970,

Chap. C-32,

(ii) having been in receipt of a pension on account of blindness under a statute of

Canada or this province, is in receipt of an old age pension, or

(iii) is certified as blind by the Chief Health Officer for the province;

(b) “white cane” means a cane or walking stick the major portion of which is white.

### Application of Act

2.(1) This Act applies only in respect of persons who are resident in the province.

### Prosecution, proof of residence

(2) In any prosecution under this Act it shall be presumed, until the contrary is proved, that the

person charged is resident in the province.

### Use of white cane

3. No person not being a blind person shall carry or use a white cane in any public thoroughfare,

public conveyance or public place.

### Offence & penalty

4. Any person who violates this Act is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to

a fine of $25.
