# Vaping Products Taxation Agreement Act

RSPEI 1988, c V-0.1

### Definitions

1. In this Act,

(a) “Minister” means the Minister of Finance and Affordability;

(b) “vaping products” means vaping products as specified in a vaping products taxation

agreement;

(c) “vaping products taxation agreement” means an agreement implementing a co-

ordinated framework for the taxation of vaping products among the Government of

Canada and the governments of participating provinces of Canada, together with any

amendments made pursuant to this Act.

### Administration

2. The Minister has the general supervision and management of this Act.

### Vaping products taxation agreement

3.(1) The Minister, on behalf of the Government and with the approval of the Lieutenant Governor

in Council, may enter into a vaping products taxation agreement.

### Payments

(2) The Minister may make payments from the Operating Fund in accordance with a vaping

products taxation agreement.

### Amendments

(3) The Minister, on behalf of the Government and with the approval of the Lieutenant Governor

in Council, may from time to time enter into an agreement with the Minister of Finance and

Affordability for Canada on behalf of the Government of Canada to amend a vaping products

taxation agreement.

### Regulations

4. The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations

(a) defining any word or expression used but not defined in this Act;

(b) respecting any matter or thing the Lieutenant Governor in Council considers

necessary or advisable to effectively carry out the intent and purpose of this Act.
