RSPEI 1988, c I-10.01

BE IT ENACTED by the Lieutenant Governor and the Legislative Assembly of the Province of

Prince Edward Island as follows:

### Islander Day

1. Throughout the Province of Prince Edward Island, the third Monday in February in each year

shall be a public holiday, and shall be kept and observed as such under the name of “Islander

Day”.
