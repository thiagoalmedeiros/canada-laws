# Institute of Man and Resources Act

RSPEI 1988, c I-3

### Definitions

1. In this Act

(a) “corporation” means the members of the Institute of Man and Resources;

(b) “Institute” means the Institute of Man and Resources.

### The Institute of Man and Resources, Incorporated

2. The Honourable Alexander B. Campbell, the Honourable John H. Maloney, Melvin J.

McQuaid, Andrew B. Wells, Thomas R. Connor and David M. Catmur and such persons as

shall become members of the Corporation hereby incorporated are hereby constituted a body

corporate under the name “The Institute of Man and Resources”, and by that name shall have

succession and a common seal for the objects and purposes and with the powers hereinafter

set forth.

### Objects and purposes

3. The objects and purposes of the corporation are for the carrying on in Prince Edward Island

without pecuniary gain to its members objects of a scientific and educational character and

without restricting the generality of the foregoing, particularly for the following objects:

(a) the mobilization of talent and resources for the analysis, invention, adaption and

application of appropriate energy, food and crop production and living and shelter

systems which are socially desirable and ecologically sustainable, and the assessment

of probable impacts of such systems;

(b) the analysis, adaption, testing and implementation of appropriate energy systems

including solar, wind, water, wood, methane and other sources, with a view to

improving knowledge of such systems, their social, economic and environmental

costs and benefits, and further with a view to providing a better basis for public

policy and programs and for institutional and private decisions in the field of

renewable energy use;

(c) the analysis, adaption, testing and demonstration of environmentally appropriate

agricultural, aquacultural, horticultural, silvacultural and animal husbandry systems

which reduce dependence on non-renewable resources, inorganic fertilizers and other

agro-chemicals and on inappropriate equipment;

(d) the testing, demonstration and development of environmentally sustainable

production technologies and management systems, having in view a variety of

physical and social environments;

(e) the provision of support, technical guidance, advice and management assistance to

individuals, groups, communities and governments seeking to resolve their energy

and food problems;

(f) the acquisition and dissemination of information relating to appropriate energy and

food production systems;

(g) the identification of potential benefits of the local manufacture of components of

these alternative systems and, where appropriate, the promotion in Prince Edward

Island of the necessary programs pursuant thereto;

(h) the examination of existing practices in the production of food and other naturally

renewable products and in associated marketing, storage and distributive systems

with particular reference to the reduction of waste and the conversion of “waste” to

by-product, to assessments of values and attitudes relating to rural homes and

communities, to the study of nutritional aspects and to evaluation of the impacts on

employment in rural and semi-rural areas of alternative public policies;

(i) the design and implementation of programs and processes, pursuant to the above,

beneficial to the long-term economy and environment of Prince Edward Island and

the Atlantic Provinces, to the eastern seaboard region of North America, and to other

areas of the world;

(j) in furtherance of the above, the fostering, adaption, testing and evaluation of new

inventions and processes for the better use of renewable resources, and the provision

and maintenance of laboratories, simulation facilities, library and instructional

facilities, and staffs and pilot projects related thereto.

### Powers, general

4.(1) In furtherance of its objects and purposes the corporation has the same general powers as

conferred upon companies incorporated under the Companies Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-14,

except where such powers are inconsistent with this Act.

### Powers, specific

(2) In addition to the powers conferred upon the corporation pursuant to subsection (1) the

corporation also has the following powers:

(a) to acquire, accept, solicit or receive by purchase, lease, contract, donation, legacy,

gift, grant, bequest or otherwise, any kind of real or personal property; and to enter

into and to carry out agreements, contracts and undertakings incidental thereto; and

(b) to employ and pay such assistance, clerks, agents, representatives and employees and

to procure, equip, and maintain such offices or other facilities, and to incur such

reasonable expenses as may be necessary.

### Head office

5. The head office and chief place of business of the corporation shall be at Charlottetown in

Queens County in the Province of Prince Edward Island.

### Membership of corporation

6. The corporation shall consist of the persons named in section 2 who are members in good

standing of the corporation, together with all other persons who, with the approval of the

board of directors, become members of the corporation.

### Management of corporation: board of directors

7. The corporation shall be managed by a board of not less than five nor more than nine

directors, a majority in number of whom shall constitute a quorum, who shall be elected at

the annual meeting of the members to be held on such day as is fixed by the bylaws of the

corporation.

### Limitation of liability

8. The members of the corporation shall not, as such, be held responsible for any act, default, or

liability whatsoever of the corporation under any engagement, claim, payment, loss, injury,

damage, transaction, matter or thing in relation to or connection with the corporation.

### Regulations and bylaws

9. The corporation may make such regulations and bylaws as are necessary for the internal

organization, operation and conduct of the affairs of the corporation.

### One vote per member

10. At all meetings of the members, each member shall be entitled to one vote.

### Application of Companies Act

11. The Companies Act applies to and shall be read as a part of this Act insofar as it is not

inconsistent therewith.

### Distribution of surplus on dissolution

12. In the event of the dissolution of the corporation then all of the surplus assets of the

corporation shall be disposed of to another recognized charitable organization in the Province

of Prince Edward Island or elsewhere in Canada having similar objects and purposes as the

Institute.
