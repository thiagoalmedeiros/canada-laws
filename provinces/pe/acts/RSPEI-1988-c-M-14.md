# Museum Act

RSPEI 1988, c M-14

### Definitions

1. In this Act

(a) “acquire” means to acquire by purchase, gift, devise, bequest or otherwise;

(b) “Board” means the Board of Governors of the Museum;

(c) “Foundation” means the Prince Edward Island Heritage Foundation;

(d) “Minister” means the Minister of Fisheries, Rural Development and Tourism;

(e) “Museum” means the Prince Edward Island Museum and Heritage Foundation.

### Administration

2. The Minister is responsible for the administration of this Act.

### Museum

3.(1) A museum is established to be known as the Prince Edward Island Museum and Heritage

Foundation.

### Status

(2) The Museum is a body corporate and shall be administered and conducted in accordance with

this Act.

### Membership

(3) The membership of the Museum, subject to clause 12(d), shall be composed of all persons

who have paid the prescribed membership fee.

### Purpose

4. The purpose of the Museum shall be to study, collect, preserve, interpret, and protect the

human and natural heritage of Prince Edward Island for the use, benefit and enjoyment of the

people of the province.

### Board of Governors

5.(1) The work of the Museum shall be under the direction and control of a Board of Governors

consisting of not fewer than six and not more than eight members appointed by the

Lieutenant Governor in Council, one of whom shall be designated as the chairperson.

### Geographical and cultural representation on Board

(2) The Lieutenant Governor in Council shall give consideration to the geographical and cultural

representation on the Board in making appointments to the Board.

### Transitional

(3) The members of the Board holding office immediately prior to the coming in force of

subsection (1) shall continue to hold office until their terms expire.

### Term of office

(4) Subject to subsection (3), the term of office of a member of the Board is three years.

### Minister is member

(5) The Minister

(a) is, by virtue of his or her office, a member of the Board in addition to the members

appointed under subsection (1); and

(b) may delegate his or her role as a member of the Board to an officer in the public

service of the province.

### Vice-chairperson, secretary-treasurer

(6) The Board shall elect a vice-chairperson and a secretary-treasurer from among its members.

### Re-appointment

(7) A Board member may be re-appointed to one additional consecutive term.

### Duties of Board member or officer

(8) A member of the Board or officer of the Museum shall, in exercising his or her powers and

discharging his or her duties

(a) act honestly and in good faith with a view to the best interests of the Museum;

(b) exercise the care, diligence and skill that a reasonably prudent person would exercise

in comparable circumstances; and

(c) comply with this Act and the bylaws of the Museum.

### Quorum

(9) A majority of the members of the board constitutes a quorum of the Board.

### Members

6. Repealed by 1994,c.42,s.2.

### Remuneration of Board members

7. Repealed by 2004,c.9,s.2. 1983,c.34,s.7; 2004,c.9,s.2.

### Powers of the Board

8. The Board may

(a) operate museums, branches, or other facilities;

(b) administer grants to museums, organizations or individuals;

(c) cooperate with and assist other bodies engaging in museum activities;

(d) acquire specimens, articles or materials related to human activities in Prince Edward

Island and classify, store, study, and exhibit them in any manner that it considers

desirable;

(e) acquire specimens, articles or materials related to natural or applied science and

classify, store, study, and exhibit them in any manner that it considers desirable;

(f) engage in activities to advance the general knowledge of human history and of the

natural and applied sciences;

(g) enter into agreements to advance the purposes of this Act;

(h) acquire, hold, preserve, maintain, reconstruct, restore, and manage property of

historical, architectural, and cultural interest;

(i) support and contribute to the acquisition, holding, preservation, maintenance,

reconstruction, restoration and management of property of historical, architectural,

and cultural interest by municipalities and by associations whose objects are similar

to those of the Museum;

(j) identify, mark, acquire, preserve and develop sites of significance to the human

history and the natural history of the province;

(k) publish or cause to be published such information relating to its collection and

activities as the Board considers advisable;

(l) perform such other functions as the Lieutenant Governor in Council may require.

### Civil Service Act applies

8.1. The Civil Service Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-8 applies to the Executive Director and

employees of the Museum.

### Chief Executive Officer

9.(1) The Lieutenant Governor in Council, after consultation with the Board, shall appoint a

Executive Director who shall be responsible for the affairs of the Board and shall report

thereon to the Board.

### Remuneration

(2) The Executive Director shall be paid out of the funds of the Museum such remuneration as

the Lieutenant Governor in Council may determine on the recommendation of the Public

Service Commission.

### Agreements with branch museums

10. The Board may enter into agreements with community corporations engaged in the operation

of branch museums.

### Restrictive covenants

11.(1) Any person owning land in the province who wishes to impose limitations or restrictions on

the use to which his land and structures thereon may be put, by himself, his heirs, successors

or assigns, or any of them may make application, on such form as the Museum may provide,

to the Museum for the purpose of entering an agreement with the Museum respecting such

limitation or restriction on the use of his land.

### Review of applications

(2) After receipt of an application under section (1), the Museum, or such committee thereof as

may be appointed by the Board, shall review the application and on the basis of such criteria

as shall be established by bylaw shall accept or reject the application.

### Agreements

(3) The Museum may enter into agreements with persons who make application under

subsection (1); such agreements shall be in the form of covenants or easements and shall be

worded in such manner as shall be prescribed by the Board.

### Registry

(4) An agreement referred to in subsection (3) shall after execution thereof be registered in the

registry of deeds for the county in which the land is located, and the Registrar of Deeds is

authorized and directed to register such agreements as an encumbrance against such land.

### Effect of agreement

(5) Where an agreement is registered against real property under this subsection, such agreement

shall run with the real property and the Museum may enforce such agreement, whether

positive or negative in nature, against the owner or any subsequent owners of the real

property and the Museum may enforce such agreement even where it owns no other land

which would be accommodated or benefited by such agreement.

### Assignment

(6) Any agreement entered into by the Museum under this section may be assigned to any body

corporate having objects similar to the Museum and such agreement shall continue to run

with the real property, and the assignee may enforce the agreement as if it were the Museum

and it owned no other land which would be accommodated or benefited by such an

agreement.

### Cancellation

(7) The Museum may, with or without the consent of the owners of a parcel of land against

which an agreement has been registered, cancel an agreement entered into under this section,

but such cancellation may be made only under such circumstances as shall be prescribed by

bylaw; where an agreement has been cancelled notice thereof shall be given to the Registrar

of Deeds for the county in which the land is located and the registration of the agreement

shall be marked as cancelled by the Registrar upon receipt thereof, together with the date and

time thereof.

### Bylaws

12. The Board may, with the approval of the Lieutenant Governor in Council, make bylaws

generally for the conduct of its business and for the control and direction of the work of the

Museum and in particular

(a) to regulate the use of Museum buildings and their contents;

(b) to protect Museum buildings and the fittings, furniture, and contents thereof from

injury, destruction or mischief;

(c) to enable the officers and servants of the Board to exclude from the said buildings

persons violating against the bylaws;

(d) to establish the conditions of membership of the Museum;

(e) to establish procedures for recommending directors to the Lieutenant Governor in

Council;

(f) to establish such honorary offices as it considers desirable.

### Reports

13.(1) The Board shall make an annual report to the Minister and such other reports as the Minister

requests.

### Report to be tabled

(2) The Minister shall lay a copy of the annual report before the Legislative Assembly within

fifteen days after it is submitted to him or, if the Legislative Assembly is not then sitting,

within fifteen days of the opening of the next session of the Legislative Assembly.

### Exemption from provincial property tax

14.(1) The real property of the Museum is exempt from provincial real property tax under section 3

of the Real Property Tax Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-5.

### Exemption from sales tax

(2) The personal property of the Museum is exempt from tax under the Revenue Tax Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-14.

### Transitional provisions

15.(1) Agreements entered into by the Foundation are assigned to the Museum.

### Members

(2) The paid up members of the Foundation shall be members in good standing of the Museum.

### Transfer of assets

(3) The assets and liabilities of the Foundation are transferred to and become the assets and

liabilities of the Museum.

### Reference in instruments

(4) In any instrument or enactment a reference to the Foundation shall be construed as a

reference to the Museum.
