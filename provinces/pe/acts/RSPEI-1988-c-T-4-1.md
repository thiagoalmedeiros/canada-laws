RSPEI 1988, c T-4.1

## PART 1 - INTERPRETATION

### Definitions

1. In this Act

(a) “active transportation pathway” means a trail that provides a means of

transportation and recreation for multiple types of users, including

(i) pedestrians,

(ii) bicyclists,

(iii) persons in wheelchairs,

(iv) power-assisted bicycle users, and

(v) motorized mobility aid users;

(b) “Confederation Trail” means the greenway designated by the Lieutenant Governor

in Council as the Confederation Trail pursuant to subsection 4(1);

(c) “greenway” means a type of trail reserved for recreational use and enjoyment;

(d) “Minister” means the Minister of Transportation, Infrastructure and Energy;

(e) “motorized mobility aid” means a device that is specifically manufactured or

modified for operation by a person with a mobility impairment and that

(i) has a maximum speed capability of not more than 15 kilometres per hour,

(ii) has a maximum width of not more than 81.2 centimetres, and

(iii) has a maximum mass of not more than 226 kilograms,

and includes a motorized electric wheelchair, medical scooter or a device approved

by the Registrar;

(f) “plan” means the strategic management plan established by the Minister pursuant to

subsection 6.1(1);

(g) “power-assisted bicycle” means a power-assisted bicycle as defined in clause

1(1)(d) of the Highway Traffic Act Power-Assisted Bicycles Regulations (EC557/21);

(h) “Registrar” means the Registrar of Motor Vehicles referred to in section 4 of the

Highway Traffic Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-5;

(i) “trail” includes

(i) the Confederation Trail,

(ii) an active transportation pathway,

(iii) a greenway, and

(iv) any other trail designated by the Lieutenant Governor in Council pursuant to

subsection 4(2);

(j) “trail crossing” means a portion of the Confederation Trail designated by the

Minister pursuant to subsection 4.2(1) that connects the Confederation Trail with

other trails subject to the restrictions and conditions specified in the designation and

this Act;

(k) “trail manager” means the trail manager appointed by the Minister pursuant to

subsection 5(2);

(l) “trails officer” means a trails officer designated by the Minister pursuant to

subsection 5.1(1).

### Purpose

2. The purposes of this Act are

(a) to provide for the establishment and operation of the Confederation Trail on Crown

land for recreational use and enjoyment;

(b) to provide for the establishment and operation of other trails for recreational use and

enjoyment;

(c) to increase awareness about and encourage the public use of the Confederation Trail

and other trails;

(d) to improve trail connectivity within and among communities and between key

destinations across the province;

(e) to protect the Confederation Trail and other trails as sustainable public resources; and

(f) to recognize the contributions the Confederation Trail and other trails make to the

health and well-being and quality of life of the citizens of the province.

### Best Practices

The Minister may establish best practices to further the purposes of this Act.

### Acquisition or setting aside of land

3. Subject to the provisions of the Public Works Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. P-34, the Minister

may acquire or set aside Crown land for the purpose of establishing the Confederation Trail

and other trails.

## PART 2 - CONFEDERATION TRAIL AND OTHER TRAILS

### Confederation Trail, designation

4. The Lieutenant Governor in Council, upon the recommendation of the Minister, may

designate a trail over Crown land to be known as “Confederation Trail”.

### Designation of other trails

The Lieutenant Governor in Council, upon the recommendation of the Minister, may

designate a trail over Crown land or, with the written consent of the landowner, over privately

owned land.

### Duties of Minister upon designation

On and after the coming into force of this subsection, where the Lieutenant Governor in

Council designates a trail pursuant to subsection (1) or (2), the Minister shall

(a) publish in the Gazette and in a newspaper circulating in the county or counties where

the Confederation Trail or other trail, as the case may be, is located, a notice

containing a survey plan and general description of the land affected sufficient to

identify the land to the public;

(b) register a copy of the survey plan and general description of the land in the office of

the Registrar of Deeds for the area where the Confederation Trail or other trail, as the

case may be, is located;

(c) give notice to the owner of any privately owned land who has given consent pursuant

to subsection (2), by serving upon the owner or by mailing by registered mail to the

owner at the owner’s last known address, a notice containing a survey plan and

general description of the trail; and

(d) post signs at the usual points of access to a trail, indicating that the land is the

Confederation Trail or other trail, as the case may be.

### Proof of sign

Evidence that a sign has been posted is proof, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, that

the sign has been posted pursuant to subsection (3).

### Proof of designation

A general description and survey plan of the Confederation Trail or other trail certified by the

Minister or a Registrar of Deeds shall be received as proof that the land is designated as the

Confederation Trail or other trail, as the case may be, without proof of signature.

### Consent to be filed

4.1. Where the owner of privately owned land gives written consent to the designation of a trail

over that land, the written consent shall be filed in the office of the Registrar of Deeds for the

area where the land is located.

### No compensation

Where consent is given pursuant to subsection (1), no compensation is payable unless

expressly stated otherwise in the consent. 2021,c.47,s.7.

## PART 3 - DESIGNATION OF TRAIL CROSSINGS

### Designation

4.2. On and after the coming into force of this section, the Minister may, by notice published in

the Gazette, designate a portion of the Confederation Trail as a trail crossing for the purposes

of this Act and the regulations.

### Period of designation

The Minister may specify that a designation of a trail crossing is for a definite period of time,

including a term in perpetuity.

### Amend, vary, cancel

The Minister may at any time amend, vary or cancel a designation of a trail crossing.

### Restrictions, conditions

The Minister may specify restrictions and conditions in a designation of a trail crossing.

### Distance between crossings

Where a trail crossing is designated pursuant to subsection (1), the trail crossing shall not be

within 15 kilometres of any other new or existing trail crossing.

### Posting of signs

The Minister shall post signs at the usual points of access to a trail crossing indicating that the

land is a trail crossing.

### Publication of designation

The Minister shall ensure that a notice of a designation of a trail crossing and of an

amendment, a variation or cancellation of a designation of a trail crossing is published in not

less than two consecutive issues of the Gazette within 21 days after the designation,

amendment, variation or cancellation is made.

### Publication in newspaper

Notwithstanding subsection (7), where

(a) a designation of a trail crossing or an amendment, a variation or cancellation of a

designation of a trail crossing is for a period of less than 30 days; or

(b) a cancellation of a designation of a trail crossing takes place less than 30 days after

the designation is made,

the Minister shall ensure that a notice of the designation of a trail crossing or of the

amendment, variation or cancellation of the designation of a trail crossing, as the case may

be, is published in at least two daily or weekly newspapers in circulation in the province

within 14 days after the designation, amendment, variation or cancellation is made.

## PART 4 - RESPONSIBILITIES OF MINISTER

### Minister, responsibilities

5. The Minister is responsible for the general supervision, construction, administration,

operation and maintenance of the Confederation Trail and other trails.

### Trail manager

Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Minister may appoint as the Minister’s agent a trail

manager to manage the Confederation Trail and other trails in accordance with the

management principles set out in the plan established under subsection 6.1(1).

### Closing trail

The Minister may, by notice published in accordance with subsection (4), close the

Confederation Trail or other trail, or a portion of the Confederation Trail or other trail, as the

case may be, where the Minister believes it is in the public interest to do so.

### Notice of closure

Where the Minister closes the Confederation Trail or other trail or a portion of the

Confederation Trail or other trail pursuant to subsection (3), the Minister shall ensure that a

notice of closure is published in at least two daily or weekly newspapers in circulation in the

province within 14 days after the closure is made. 1994,c.62,s.5; 2021,c.47,s.9.

### Trails officers

5.1. The Minister may designate persons, by notice published in the Gazette, as trails officers.

### Function

The function of a trails officer is to enforce such requirements of this Act and the regulations

as the Minister may authorize.

### Identification

An identification card purporting to be signed by the Minister is proof that the individual

named therein is a trails officer.

### Trails officers

The following persons are trails officers by virtue of their office:

(a) an auxiliary peace officer designated under the Off-Highway Vehicle Act R.S.P.E.I.

1988, Cap. O-3;

(b) a conservation officer appointed under the Wildlife Conservation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. W-4.1.

### Powers

A trails officer has no power of search or arrest.

### Tickets

For greater certainty, a trails officer may issue tickets under the Summary Proceedings Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. S-9 in respect of a contravention of this Act or the regulations.

### Right of entry

A trails officer may, at any reasonable time and without a warrant, enter any land adjoining

the Confederation Trail or other trail for the purposes of enforcing this Act or the regulations.

### Obstruction

No person shall obstruct, impede or assault a trails officer, or any person assisting a trails

officer, while the trails officer is exercising his or her authority or performing a duty under

this Act or the regulations, and no person shall aid or assist any person in obstructing,

impeding or assaulting a trails officer.

### Liability

The Minister, the trail manager, a trails officer or other person employed or engaged in the

administration or enforcement of this Act or the regulations, is not liable for any loss or

damage suffered by any person as a result of any act done in good faith or omitted to be done

in the course of the administration or enforcement of this Act or the regulations.

## PART 5 - AGREEMENTS, STRATEGIC MANAGEMENT PLAN

### Agreements

6. The Minister may enter into agreements with

(a) the Government of Canada;

(b) a province;

(c) a municipality;

(d) an agency of the Government of Canada, a province or a municipality;

(e) a person;

(f) an organization, whether incorporated or not; or

(g) any combination thereof,

for any purpose coming within the provisions of this Act or the regulations.

### Agreement with owner

Without restricting the generality of subsection (1), the Minister may enter into agreements

with the owners of privately owned land adjacent to the Confederation Trail or other trails to

manage or preserve that land so that it complements the Confederation Trail and other trails.

### Strategic management plan

6.1. The Minister shall establish and maintain a strategic management plan

(a) that sets out strategic directions and targets for the establishment, management,

promotion and use of the Confederation Trail and other trails in the province; and

(b) that is guided by the vision of a province that has a world-class system of diversified

trails that are planned and used in an environmentally responsible manner, and that

enhance the health and well-being of all citizens of the province.

### Periodic review - progress reports

Where the Minister establishes a strategic management plan pursuant to subsection (1), the

Minister

(a) shall conduct a review of the plan; and

(b) shall prepare reports respecting the progress made in implementing the plan,

at such times as the Minister considers necessary or advisable.

### Requirement for initial report

The Minister shall prepare an initial report pursuant to clause (2)(b) not later than 24 months

after the date this section comes into force.

### Consultation

6.2. In the course of reviewing the plan or establishing best practices to further the purposes of

this Act, the Minister shall

(a) consult with any person and entities the Minister considers appropriate;

(b) consider any government policies and programs that affect or relate to the

Confederation Trail or other trails in the province,

(i) that the Minister believes are appropriate to consider, or

(ii) that the Minister has been advised by another Minister are relevant.

### Legislation and policies

The Minister shall ensure that the plan and any established best practices are not inconsistent

with provincial legislation or Government policies that affect or relate to the Confederation

Trail and other trails. 2021,c.47,s.11.

### Public access to information

7. The Minister shall publish the following on a Government website:

(a) the location of the Confederation Trail and the name, if any, and location of every

other trail designated by the Lieutenant Governor in Council under section 4;

(b) the location of trail crossings designated by the Minister under section 4.2;

(c) the location of active transportation pathways;

(d) the plan established under subsection 6.1(1);

(e) progress reports required under subsections 6.1(2) and (3);

(f) best practices, if any are established under subsection 2(2).

## PART 6 - GENERAL

### Posting of signs

8. For the purposes of identifying the location of the Confederation Trail and other trails, and to

administer, control and operate the Confederation Trail and other trails, the Minister

(a) shall post, erect and maintain signs on the Confederation Trail and other trails; or

(b) may remove signs posted or erected on the Confederation Trail or other trails.

### In accordance with the regulations

The signs referred to in subsection (1) shall be in accordance with, and shall be posted,

erected, maintained and removed in accordance with, the regulations.

### Permitted uses

The Minister may post or erect signs specifying the recreational uses or activities permitted

on the Confederation Trail or other trails.

### Clearly visible

The Minister shall ensure that signs posted or erected pursuant to this section are clearly

visible in daylight and under normal atmospheric conditions from the approach to each usual

point of access to the Confederation Trail or other trail.

### Proof of posting

Evidence that a sign has been posted is proof, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, that

the sign has been posted pursuant to this section.

### Prohibition - signs

No person shall tear down, remove, damage, cover, deface or alter a sign posted or erected

pursuant to this section unless doing so under the authority of subsection (1).

### Prohibition - travel

No person shall travel on the Confederation Trail or any other trail in contravention of a sign

posted or erected pursuant to this section.

### Lawful user is not a trespasser

9. A person making lawful use of the Confederation Trail or other trail for recreational purposes

pursuant to this Act and the regulations is not, and is deemed not to be, trespassing upon the

land to the extent of that use.

### Assumption of risk

A person referred to in subsection (1) is deemed to have willingly assumed all risks that may

be encountered on the land when using the Confederation Trail or other trail, whether or not

the person is on the Confederation Trail or other trail.

### Duty not to create danger

The duty of care of the Minister in respect of a person referred to in subsection (1) is limited

to the duty not to create a danger specified in subsection 4(1) of the Occupiers’ Liability Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. O-2.

### No liability in respect of defect in construction etc.

No claim for damages may be made by a person referred to in subsection (1) in respect of

damage to property or bodily injury resulting from a defect in construction, improvement or

maintenance of the Confederation Trail or other trail.

### Arrest and detention

10. A peace officer may arrest a person for an offence under this Act or the regulations or for an

offence committed on the Confederation Trail or other trail under any other enactment and

detain that person in custody after the arrest if, on reasonable and probable grounds, the peace

officer believes that the arrest and detention is necessary to

(a) prevent the continuation or repetition of the offence; or

(b) establish the identity of the person.

### Arrest without warrant

A peace officer making an arrest without warrant shall, with reasonable diligence, take the

person arrested before a provincial court judge to be dealt with according to law.

### Search and seizure

11. A peace officer may search, without warrant, a vehicle, vessel or other receptacle when the

peace officer has reason to believe that it contains anything or is being used in connection

with the commission of an offence pursuant to this Act or the regulations or any other

enactment, if the offence is committed on the Confederation Trail or other trail, and the peace

officer may seize the vehicle, vessel or receptacle.

### Issue of order of prohibition

12. Where the Minister or a peace officer has reasonable and probable grounds to believe that a

person has violated or is about to violate a provision of this Act or the regulations, or that the

entry upon or remaining within the Confederation Trail or other trail by a person may be

detrimental to the safety of other trail users or their enjoyment of the Confederation Trail or

other trail and its facilities, the Minister, or any person authorized to act on behalf of the

Minister, may, without notice or hearing, issue an order in writing prohibiting that person

from entering upon or being within the Confederation Trail or other trail specified in the

order for a period specified therein.

### Duty to observe order

Every person having knowledge of an order made pursuant to subsection (1) shall observe

that order and, in the event the person is within the Confederation Trail or other trail when the

order is made, that person shall immediately leave.

## PART 7 - PROHIBITIONS

### Prohibition

13. No person shall, while on the Confederation Trail or other trails,

(a) be impaired by alcohol or drugs;

(b) act in a noisy or disorderly manner;

(c) create a disturbance;

(d) pursue a course of conduct that is detrimental to the safety or enjoyment of other

users of the Confederation Trail or other trails;

(e) wilfully destroy property or other natural resources found on or adjacent to the

Confederation Trail or other trails;

(f) dump or deposit garbage or other unwanted material; or

(g) engage in any other activity prohibited by notice or the regulations.

### Prohibition - closure

No person shall travel on the Confederation Trail or other trails or on any portion of them that

is closed.

### Removal of signs

Repealed by 2021,c.47,s.17.

### Offence and penalty

Every person who fails to comply with or contravenes any provision of this Act or the

regulations is guilty of an offence and is liable, on summary conviction, if no specific penalty

is provided for the offence in this Act or the regulations, to a fine of not less than $200 and

not more than $2,000.

### Regulations

14. The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations

(a) providing a detailed set of rules to allow orderly recreational use of the Confederation

Trail and other trails;

(a.1) respecting the recreational uses or activities permitted on the Confederation Trail and

other trails;

(a.2) respecting the uses or activities prohibited on the Confederation Trail and other trails;

(b) providing standards for the design, construction, length, width, surface type,

maintenance, operation and administration of the Confederation Trail and other trails;

(c) providing, and requiring compliance with, safety, health and environmental standards

with respect to the Confederation Trail and other trails;

(d) prescribing fees or a schedule of fees which may be charged to users of the

Confederation Trail and other trails;

(e) providing for the collection of fees;

(e.1) prescribing the fine or range of fines that a person is liable to, on summary

conviction, where the person is found guilty of an offence for contravening a

provision of the regulations;

(f) respecting the management or preservation of an area adjacent to the Confederation

Trail and other trails;

(g) prohibiting the use of certain types of vehicles on the Confederation Trail and other

trails;

(g.1) exempting certain types of vehicles from the application of this Act;

(h) prescribing signs

(i) to indicate the location of the Confederation Trail and other trails,

(ii) prohibiting by means of symbols certain uses or activities on the

Confederation Trail and other trails;

(h.1) respecting the standards to be met for signs to be posted, erected and maintained on,

or removed from, the Confederation Trail and other trails;

(i) providing for the enforcement of this Act and any rules and regulations made with

respect to the Confederation Trail and other trails;

(j) defining any term used but not defined in this Act; and

(k) respecting any matter necessary or advisable to carry out effectively the intent and

purposes of this Act.
