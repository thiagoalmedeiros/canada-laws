M.R. 109/2026

The Elections Act, C.C.S.M. c. E30

### Fees, expenses and rentals

1 The fees, expenses and rentals to be paid for the purpose of The Elections Act are set out in the Schedule.

### Inflation adjustment

2 On January 1 in 2028 and every year after that, the fees, expenses and rentals set out in the Schedule must be increased by the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for Winnipeg for the 12-month period ending in November of the preceding year, rounded up to the nearest dollar.

### Repeal

3 The Election Fees, Expenses and Rentals Regulation, Manitoba Regulation 119/2022, is repealed.

## SCHEDULE

(Section 1)

### Fees for returning officers and assistant returning officers

1 For an activity listed in the first column of the following table,

(a) a returning officer is to be paid the amount listed opposite in the second column;

(b) an assistant returning officer is to be paid the amount listed opposite in the third column; and

(c) a part-time assistant returning officer is to be paid the amount listed opposite in the fourth column.

| Activity | Returning officer | Assistant returning officer | Part-time assistant returning officer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Services provided for election preparation before registration starts 1 | up to $6,000 per electoral division |  |  |
| Services provided for office opening 2 | up to $1,000 per week | up to $850 per week | up to $850 per week |
| Services provided from the start of registration until the writ is issued 2 | up to $1,000 per week | up to $850 per week | up to $850 per week |
| Services provided during the election period 2 | $12,750 3 4 | $11,750 3 4 | up to $11,750 |
| Services provided after election day | $3,000 | $2,500 | up to $2,500 |
| Attendance at a judicial recount or appeal of a decision on a recount (per day) | $250, plus expenses 5 | $250, plus expenses 5 | $250, plus expenses 5 |
| Completing a course on electoral procedure conducted by the chief electoral officer (per day) | $250, plus expenses 5 | $250, plus expenses 5 | $250, plus expenses 5 |

Notes:

1 $6,000 is the maximum amount payable to both the returning officer and the assistant returning officer for election preparation. The actual amount payable to each officer for this activity is to be determined on the basis of services provided by the individual officer.

2 If an election is not held in an electoral division, the amounts in this row are to be prorated based on the number of days the election official worked.

3 An additional allowance of $2,000 is to be paid to returning officers of the electoral divisions of Flin Flon, Keewatinook and Thompson and an additional allowance of $1,500 is to be paid to the assistant returning officers of those electoral divisions. These amounts are not subject to being prorated under note 2. The returning officers and assistant returning officers of these electoral divisions are not eligible for the additional allowances under note 4.

4 An additional allowance of $1,000 is to be paid to the returning officers of the electoral divisions of Dauphin, Riding Mountain, Swan River and The Pas-Kameesak and an additional allowance of $750 is to be paid to the assistant returning officers of these electoral divisions. These additional allowances are also payable to the returning officer and assistant returning officer of any other electoral division with 10 or more advance voting places. These amounts are not subject to being prorated under note 2.

5 Expenses are actual travel and accommodation expenses incurred, based on receipts presented by the election official to the chief electoral officer.

### Fees for voting officials

2(1) The following voting officials at regular and advance voting locations are to be paid the minimum wage plus the following additional amounts per hour:

(a) $6 per hour for a senior voting officer;

(b) $4 per hour for a voting officer;

(c) $2 per hour for an information officer or an interpreter.

2(2) Voting officials are to be paid for 14 hours on election day, 8 hours on an advance voting day held on a Sunday and 14 hours for an advance voting day held on any other day.

2(3) Voting officials responsible for transporting ballots are to be paid for the additional time in transporting the ballots.

2(4) A returning officer or assistant returning officer who acts as a voting official is not entitled to receive payment under this section.

### Fees for registration agents

3 A registration agent is to be paid the minimum wage plus an additional $2 per hour during registration.

### Fees for administrative and clerical staff

4 Administrative or clerical staff required at a returning office are to be paid the minimum wage plus an additional amount of up to $8 per hour, depending on job requirements, qualifications and experience.

### Training fees

5 A voting official who completes a course on electoral procedure is to be paid a fee between $25 and $150. A minimum fee of $25 is payable for a course of up to one hour in duration and a maximum fee of $150 is payable for a course that is five hours or longer in duration with the exact amount payable to be determined by the chief electoral officer based on the duration of the course, the voting official's position and the course delivery format (in-person or online).

### Fees do not include vacation pay

6 The amounts in sections 1 to 5 do not include any amount payable as a vacation allowance under The Employment Standards Code.

### Travel expenses for election officials

7 With the approval of the chief electoral officer, election officials are entitled to be paid the following for travel expenses:

(a) for travel by road — expenses based on the current per/km rates payable to government employees;

(b) for travel by air, rail or water — expenses for the actual cost incurred, based on receipts presented by the election official to the chief electoral officer.

### Voting place rental

8 Unless a higher amount is approved by the chief electoral officer, the rent payable for a voting place is $300 per day for the first voting station and an additional $50 per day for each additional voting station in the same building.
