M.R. 33/2012

The Poverty Reduction Strategy Act, C.C.S.M. c. P94.7

### Poverty reduction and social inclusion indicators

1 The indicators set out in the Schedule are prescribed as indicators of poverty reduction and social inclusion under The Poverty Reduction Strategy Act.

## SCHEDULE

(Section 1)

### INDICATORS OF POVERTY REDUCTION AND SOCIAL INCLUSION

| INDICATOR | SOURCE OF INFORMATION |
| --- | --- |
| Child mortality rate | Manitoba Department of Health, Seniors and Long-Term Care |
| Core housing need | Statistics Canada, Census of Population |
| Employment rate | Statistics Canada, Labour Force Survey |
| Food insecurity | Statistics Canada, Canadian Income Survey |
| High school graduation rate | Manitoba Department of Education and Early Childhood Learning |
| Infant mortality rate | Manitoba Department of Health, Seniors and Long-Term Care |
| Poverty rate based on market basket measure | Statistics Canada, Canadian Income Survey |
| Youth not in employment, education or training rate | Statistics Canada, Labour Force Survey |
