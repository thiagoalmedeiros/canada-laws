M.R. 401/88 R

The Public Works Act, C.C.S.M. c. P300

1 The acute angle from the horizontal for the purposes of the definition of "height line" in subsection 7(3) of the Act is prescribed as 11 degrees 51 minutes and 10 seconds (11° 51'10").
