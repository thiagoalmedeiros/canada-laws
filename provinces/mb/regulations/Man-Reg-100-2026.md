M.R. 100/2026

The Municipal Act, C.C.S.M. c. M225

### Definitions

1 The following definitions apply in this regulation.

"Act" means The Municipal Act. (« Loi »)

"borrowing threshold" means the dollar amount that is 7% of a municipality's most recent total municipal assessment. (« plafond d'emprunt »)

"total municipal assessment" has the same meaning as in The Municipal Assessment Act. (« évaluation municipale totale »)

### Municipal Board approval

2 For the purpose of section 176 of the Act, only the following borrowings of a municipality require the approval of The Municipal Board:

(a) any borrowing in an amount that exceeds the borrowing threshold;

(b) any series of borrowings in an aggregate amount that exceeds the borrowing threshold;

(c) any borrowing or series of borrowings that, together with the outstanding borrowings of the municipality, would exceed the borrowing threshold.

### Transitional

3 Any borrowing under a borrowing by-law that has been given first reading by a municipality before the coming into force of this regulation must be approved by The Municipal Board under section 176 of the Act.

### Coming into force

4 This regulation comes into force on the same day that subsections 26(2) and (10) of The Red Tape Reduction and Government Efficiency Act, 2018, S.M. 2018, c. 29, come into force.
