M.R. 61/2001

The Protection for Persons in Care Act, C.C.S.M. c. P144

### Designation

1 The Selkirk Mental Health Centre is designated as a health facility for the purpose of The Protection for Persons in Care Act.

### Coming into force

2 This regulation comes into force on the day The Protection for Persons in Care Act, S.M. 2000, c. 12, comes into force.
