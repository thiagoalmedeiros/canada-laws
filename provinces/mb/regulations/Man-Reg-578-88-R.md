M.R. 578/88 R

The Public Schools Act, C.C.S.M. c. P250

### Establishment

1 In accordance with section 14 of The Public Schools Act, there is hereby established a northern school division, to be called The Frontier School Division No. 48, with the boundaries outlined by the solid black line in the Schedule.
