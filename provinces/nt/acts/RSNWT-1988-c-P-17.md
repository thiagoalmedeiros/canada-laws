RSNWT 1988, c P-17

## ACT

### Definition of "public service"

1. In this Act, "public service" means the public

service of the Territories and includes the employees

of any board, commission or other body whose wages

or salary is paid out of the Consolidated Revenue

Fund.

### Attachment of wages or salary

2. A person who obtains a judgment or order for the

payment or recovery of money against a person

employed in the public service may, for the purpose of

enforcing the judgment or order, attach the wages or

salary of the employee.

### Application of Rules of the Supreme Court

3. Except as otherwise provided by this Act, the

provisions respecting garnishment contained in the

Rules of the Supreme Court apply to the attachment of

wages or salary under this Act.

### Service of summons

4. A garnishee summons in respect of a judgment

debtor who is employed in the public service shall be

served on the Comptroller General.

5. (1) Repealed, S.N.W.T. 2006,c.18,s.3(3).

### Payment of wages and salary

(2) The Comptroller General is not compelled to

pay any wages or salary otherwise than in accordance

with any Act, regulation or rule relating to the payment

of any class of employees in the public service.

S.N.W.T. 2006,c.18.s.3(3).

### Exemption from attachment

6. The wages or salary of an employee of the public

service are exempt from attachment under this Act to

the extent of that portion of the wages or salary that is

exempt from attachment by garnishment under section

7 of the Exemptions Act , including any amounts

required to be deducted by the Comptroller General by

or under an Act of Canada or an Act of the Territories.
