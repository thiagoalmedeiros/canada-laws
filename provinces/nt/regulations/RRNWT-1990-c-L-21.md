RRNWT 1990, c L-21

*Made under the Liquor Act*

1. These regulations apply to the community known

as Dettah.

2. No person shall at any time have in his or her

possession in, or in any month take into Dettah a

quantity of liquor that is in excess of

(a) twelve 355 m R containers of beer and one

750 m R container of spirits; or

(b) 4,500 m R of wine in sealed containers.

3. Every person who contravenes section 2 is guilty of

an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine

not exceeding $500 or to imprisonment for a term not

exceeding six months or to both.
