NWT Reg 125-2016

*Made under the Land Titles Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

every enabling power, makes the Land Titles Office

1. The land titles office must be kept open to the

public from 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. every day except

(a) Saturday;

(a.1) Sunday;

(b) a holiday; and

(c) any day on which the Registrar, and other

persons employed in the public service,

are on mandatory leave.

2. The Land Titles Office Regulations , established by

regulation numbered R-061-93, are repealed.
