NWT Reg 104-2019

*Made under the Protected Areas Act*

## ORDER

The Minister, under section 40 of the Protected

Areas Act and every enabling power, makes the

1. Persons appointed as officers under the Wildlife Act

are, by virtue of their office, designated as officers for

the purposes of the Act.
