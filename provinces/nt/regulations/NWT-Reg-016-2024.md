## ORDER

The Minister, under section 3 of the

Act and every enabling power, makes the Designated

1. The trades set out in the Schedule are designated

trades appropriate for apprenticeship or for the issuing

of certificates of qualification.

2. The Designated Trades Order , established by

### regulation numbered R-055-2012, is repealed by this

### order.
