NWT Reg 065-2001

*Made under the Garage Keepers Lien Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

Minister, under section 9 of the Garage Keepers Lien

Act and every enabling power, makes the Garage

1. The prescribed form of the warrant referred to in

paragraph 4(2)(a) of the Garage Keepers Lien Act is set

out in the Schedule.

2. The Garage Keepers Lien Forms Regulations ,

established by regulation numbered R-045-91, are

repealed.

3. These regulations come into force May 7, 2001.
