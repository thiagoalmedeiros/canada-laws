NWT Reg 052-2021

*Made under the Public Service Act*

The Minister, under subsection 35(1.1) of the

1. The following days are fixed as holidays for all the

public service:

(a) Boxing Day, December 26;

(b) the first Monday in August;

(c) National Day for Truth and

Reconciliation, which is observed on

September 30.

2. The Public Service Holidays Order , established

### by regulation numbered R-025-2000, is repealed.
