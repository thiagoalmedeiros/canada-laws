*Made under the Territorial Parks Act*

The Minister, under subsection 5(4) of the

every enabling power, makes the Wayside Parks

1. The Wayside Parks set out in items 5, 9 to 16, 19

and 21 to 23 of the Schedule to the Wayside Parks

Order , established by regulation numbered R-102-95,

are continued as Wayside Parks under the names set

out, and consisting of the lands described, in items 1

to 13 of the Schedule.

2. Repealed, R-080-2011,s.2.

3. Repealed, R-080-2011,s.2.
