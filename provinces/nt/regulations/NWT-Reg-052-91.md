NWT Reg 052-91

*Made under the Miners Lien Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

R.S.N.W.T. 1988, c. M-12 and every enabling power,

1. The prescribed forms for a claim of lien referred to

in subsection 3(1) of the Miners Lien Act are set out in

Forms 1 and 2 of the Schedule.

2. The prescribed form of a claim of lien where there is

more than one claimant, referred to in subsection 3(1) of

the Miners Lien Act , is set out in Form 3 of the

Schedule.

3. The prescribed form of an affidavit referred to in

subsections 3(2) and 4(2) of the Miners Lien Act is set

out in Form 4 of the Schedule.

4. The prescribed form of a summons referred to in

subsection 11(1) of the Miners Lien Act is set out in

Form 5 of the Schedule.
