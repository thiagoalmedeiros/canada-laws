NWT Reg 050-2011

*Made under the Cities, Towns and Villages Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS the council of the Town of Norman

Wells has requested the variation of the number of

elected councillors on the council;

The Minister, under section 14 of the Cities, Towns

and Villages Act and every enabling power, orders as

follows:

1. The council of the Town of Norman Wells is

composed of a mayor and six elected councillors.
