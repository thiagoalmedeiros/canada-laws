*Made under the Mental Health Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

and every enabling power, makes the Designation of

1. (1) Subject to subsection (2), the following are

(a) Hay River Health Centre;

(b) Inuvik Regional Hospital;

(c) Stanton Territorial Hospital.

and ending on August 31, 2022.

2. The Hospitals Designation Order ,

R.R.N.W.T. 1990, c.M-6, is repealed.

3. These regulations come into force on the day on
