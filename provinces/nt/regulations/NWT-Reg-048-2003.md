NWT Reg 048-2003

*Made under the Hospital Insurance and Health and Social Services Administration Act*

The Minister, under section 10 of the Hospital

Administration Act and every enabling power, makes

the Hay River Health and Social Services Authority

Order .

1. The Hay River Health and Social Services

Authority, a Board of Management, is composed of a

chairperson and six members.

2. Repealed, R-111-2005, s.3.
