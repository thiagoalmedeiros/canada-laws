NWT Reg 032-96

*Made under the Jury Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

1. A fee of $80 shall be paid to a juror for each day

the juror serves,

(a) in a civil action, by the party who made

the application for trial of an action before

a jury under subsection 2(1) of the Jury

Act ; or

(b) in a criminal action, by the Supreme

Court.

2. Repealed, R-063-2010,s.3.
