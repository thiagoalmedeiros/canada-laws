RRNWT 1990, c A-6

*Made under the All-terrain Vehicles Act*

1. The fees for certificates of registration, documents

and services respecting special all-terrain vehicles are

set out in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

1. Certificate of registration

(a) for persons under 60 years of

age ......................... $36

(b) for persons 60 years of age or

older ........................ $18

2. Transfer of a licence plate to another special

all-terrain vehicle......................... $25

3. Replacement of a lost, stolen, destroyed or

illegible certificate of registration............ $21

4. Replacement of a lost, stolen, destroyed or

illegible licence plate...................... $11
