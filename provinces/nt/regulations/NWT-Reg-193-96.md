NWT Reg 193-96

*Made under the Lotteries Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

enabling power, makes the Yellowknife Delegation

1. The authority to regulate and licence lottery

schemes within the City of Yellowknife is hereby

delegated to the City of Yellowknife.

2. The Lotteries Regulations, R.R.N.W.T. 1990,

c.L-49, do not apply to the regulation and licensing of

a lottery scheme within the City of Yellowknife.

3. These regulations come into force on April 1, 1997.
