NWT Reg 096-2025

*Made under the Hamlets Act*

### Preamble

Whereas the Minister ordered the Hamlet of

Enterprise be placed under administration by the

Hamlet of Enterprise Administration Order , established

by regulation numbered R-033-2025;

And whereas the municipal administrator for the

Hamlet of Enterprise has requested the variation of the

number of councillors on the council;

and every enabling power, orders as follows:

1. The council of the Hamlet of Enterprise is

composed of a mayor and four elected councillors.

2. The Enterprise Council Variation Order ,

### established by regulation numbered R-115-2009, is

repealed.
