NWT Reg 060-2007

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

Board, under paragraph 107(g) of the Financial

Administration Act and every enabling power, makes

the Assignment of Government Debt Exemption

1. The Assignment of Government Debt Regulations

do not apply to any assignment of debt owed by the

Government pursuant to a concession agreement made

under the Deh Cho Bridge Act .
