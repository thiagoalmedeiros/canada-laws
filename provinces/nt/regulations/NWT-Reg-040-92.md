NWT Reg 040-92

*Made under the Cities, Towns and Villages Act*

The Minister, at the request of the council of the

Village of Norman Wells, upon confirming that the

total assessed value of all assessable land in the

municipality exceeds $50,000,000 and upon giving

notice in accordance with subsection 5(4), under

every enabling power, orders as follows:

1. The Village of Norman Wells is declared to be a

town and shall be known as the Town of Norman

Wells.
