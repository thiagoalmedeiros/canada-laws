NWT Reg 087-2015

*Made under the Health Information Act*

The Minister, under section 68 of the Health

Information Act and every enabling power, makes the

1. The Aurora College Research Ethics Committee is

designated as a research ethics committee for the

purposes of the Act.

2. This order comes into force on the day on which

the Health Information Act , S.N.W.T. 2014, c.2, comes

into force.
