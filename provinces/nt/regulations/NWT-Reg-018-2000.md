NWT Reg 018-2000

*Made under the Hamlets Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS the council of the Hamlet of Fort Liard

has requested the variation of the number of elected

councillors on the council;

The Minister, under subsection 12(2) of the Hamlets

Act and every enabling power, orders as follows:

1. The council of the Hamlet of Fort Liard is

composed of a mayor and six elected councillors.
