## ORDER

The Commissioner in Executive Council, under

section 5 of the Health and Social Services Professions

Act and every enabling power, makes the Designated

1. The professions set out in the Schedule are

designated as professions to which the Health and

Social Services Professions Act applies.

2. The Designated Professions Order , established by

### regulation numbered R-017-2022, is repealed.
