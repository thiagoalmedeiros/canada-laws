NWT Reg 140-98

*Made under the Family Law Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

S.N.W.T. 1997, c.18, and every enabling power, makes

1. The prescribed form of the election referred to in

subsection 37(7) of the Family Law Act is set out in the

Schedule.

2. These regulations come into force on the day on

which the Family Law Act , S.N.W.T. 1997, c.18, comes

into force.
