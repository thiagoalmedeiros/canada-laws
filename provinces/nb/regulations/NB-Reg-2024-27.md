NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 2024-27

under the Local Governance Commission Act (O.C. 2024-117)

Filed May 9, 2024

Under section 84 of the Local Governance Commission Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

### Citation

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Regions of the Tribunal Regulation – Local Governance Commission Act.

### Definitions

2 The following definitions apply in this Regulation.

“Act” means the Local Governance Commission Act. (Loi)

“Regulation 2022-46” means New Brunswick Regulation 2022-46 under the Regional Service Delivery Act. (Règlement 2022-46)

### Establishment of regions of the Tribunal

3 For the purposes of the definition “region” in section 55 of the Act, the following regions of the Tribunal are established:

(a) Region A, consisting of regions 1 and 2 described in Schedule A of Regulation 2022-46;

(b) Region B, consisting of regions 3, 4 and 5 described in Schedule A of Regulation 2022-46;

(c) Region C, consisting of regions 6 and 7 described in Schedule A of Regulation 2022-46;

(d) Region D, consisting of regions 8, 9 and 10 described in Schedule A of Regulation 2022-46; and

(e) Region E, consisting of regions 11 and 12 described in Schedule A of Regulation 2022-46.

### Commencement

4 This Regulation comes into force on May 15, 2024.
