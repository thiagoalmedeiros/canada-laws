NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 82-190

under the Fisheries Bargaining Act (O.C. 82-834)

Filed September 30, 1982

Under section 117 of the Fisheries Bargaining Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Species of Fish Regulation - Fisheries Bargaining Act.
