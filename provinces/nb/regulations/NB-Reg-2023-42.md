NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 2023-42

under the Electoral Boundaries and Representation Act (O.C. 2023-162)

Filed June 29, 2023

Under section 20 of the Electoral Boundaries and Representation Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

### Citation

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Electoral Districts Boundaries Regulation – Electoral Boundaries and Representation Act.

### Definition of “Act”

2 In this Regulation, “Act” means the Electoral Boundaries and Representation Act.
