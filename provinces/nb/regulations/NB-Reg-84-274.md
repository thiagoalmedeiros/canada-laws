NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 84-274

under the Foreign Resident Corporations Act (O.C. 84-946)

Filed November 2, 1984

Under section 21 of the Foreign Resident Corporations Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the General Regulation - Foreign Resident Corporations Act.

2 In this Regulation, “Act” means the Foreign Resident Corporations Act.

3 A fee of two hundred dollars shall be paid by a foreign corporation on the making of an application to operate within New Brunswick as a foreign resident corporation pursuant to the Act.

4 A fee of one hundred dollars shall be paid on filing changes pursuant to section 5 of the Act.

5 A foreign resident corporation shall pay an annual fee of two hundred dollars on the anniversary date of the issuing of a certificate by the Minister pursuant to subsection 4(1) of the Act.

6 A fee of two hundred and fifty dollars shall be paid on filing documents pursuant to sections 7 and 11 of the Act.

7(1) For the purposes of paragraphs 3(1)(f) and 11(a) of the Act, a member of a diplomatic mission signing a certificate shall be

(a) a head of the diplomatic mission, and

(b) duly accredited to Canada.

7(2) For the purposes of paragraphs 3(1)(f) and 11(a) of the Act, a member of a consular post signing a certificate shall be

(a) a head of the consular post, other than an honourary consular officer, and

(b) duly accredited to Canada.
