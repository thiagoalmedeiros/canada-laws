NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 92-74

under the Fish and Wildlife Act (O.C. 92-416)

Filed May 20, 1992

Under section 118 of the Fish and Wildlife Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Exotic Wildlife Regulation - Fish and Wildlife Act.

2 The species and subspecies of exotic wildlife listed in Schedule A are excluded from the operation of paragraphs 38.1(1)(a) and (b) of the Fish and Wildlife Act.

3 This Regulation comes into force on June 15, 1992.
