NS Reg 125/2001

*Made under Section 14 of the Emergency "911" Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Emergency "911" Act Definitions Regulations .

### Definitions for purposes of Act

2 For the purposes of the Emergency "911" Act , local telecommunication services includes wireless telecommunication services provided by a company that is registered to conduct business in the Province, whose wireless telecommunication services include access to the "911" emergency telephone system.
