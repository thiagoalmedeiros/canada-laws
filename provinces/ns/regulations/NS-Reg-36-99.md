NS Reg 36/99

*Made under Section 520 of the Municipal Government Act*

N.S. Reg. 36/99 ("commitment") For the purposes of subsection 88(4) of the Municipal Government Act , "commitment" means a commitment with respect to the possession, use or control of physical or intellectual property.

N.S. Reg. 7/2004 ("prohibit") In Section 198, subsection 220(3), clause 220(5)(n), Section 250 and Section 252 of the Municipal Government Act , "prohibit" includes prohibit in part, limit, limit in number, control or regulate.
