NS Reg 22/2005

*Made under Section 7 of the Crop and Livestock Insurance Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Designation of Insurable Crops and Livestock

Regulations .

### Definition of crops and livestock

2 (1) The crops designated in these regulations are as defined in the applicable plan for the

crop.

(2) The livestock designated in these regulations are as defined in the applicable plan for

the livestock.

### Designated crops

3 The following crops are designated as insurable crops under the Crop and Livestock

Insurance Act :

(a) corn;

(b) forage;

(ba) grapes;

(c) highbush blueberries;

(d) lowbush blueberries;

(da) maple syrup;

(e) peas and beans;

(f) potatoes;

(g) raspberries;

(h) soybeans;

(i) spring grain;

(j) strawberries;

(k) tree fruit;

(l) vegetables;

(m) winter grain.

### Designated livestock

4 The following livestock are designated as insurable livestock under the Crop and Livestock

Insurance Act :

(a) cattle;

(b) poultry.
