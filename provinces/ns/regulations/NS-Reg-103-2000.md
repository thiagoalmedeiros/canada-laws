NS Reg 103/2000

*Made under Section 6 of the Volunteer Services Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Definition of Sundries Regulations .

2 For the purposes of the Act, "sundries" includes the cellular telephones donated by MTT Mobility Incorporated for use in the Victims First Emergency Cellular Telephone Service.
