NS Reg 41/66

*Made under Section 302 of the Motor Vehicle Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of

Highways dated the 11th day of August, 1966 and pursuant to Section 245

[Section 302] of the Motor Vehicle Act, is pleased to order and determine:

1 That when a person has obtained registration of one classification, he may be

credited with the fee paid in that classification when the same person wishes to

register the same vehicle in another classification as follows

(a) farm tractors: Class I to Class II;

(b) farm trucks to full commercial registration;

(c) service trucks to full commercial registration;

(d) ambulances and hearses to full commercial registration or passenger

registration.

2 That such credit shall only be allowed where the registration of the vehicle in the

new classification occurs within the same registration year.
