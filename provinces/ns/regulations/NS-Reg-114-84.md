NS Reg 114/84

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 25th day of May, A.D., 1984, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Ragged

Harbour Beach, in the County of Queens, as shown outlined in red on the copy of Crown Land

Records Office Plan No. E-13-49 marked Schedule “B” and described in Schedule “A”, both

Schedules attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Ragged Harbour

in the vicinity of East Berlin, County of Queens, Province of Nova Scotia and being shown on

Plan No. E-13-49 signed by David L. Crooker, N.S.L.S. on the 18th day of May 1984, said plan

being on file in the Crown Land Records Office of the Department of Lands and Forests,

Halifax, Nova Scotia, said area of land being more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at Survey Marker No. Q2735 situate on the mean high watermark of Ragged

Harbour, said survey marker being (by grid bearings referenced to the Nova Scotia 3° Modified

Transverse Mercator Projection, Zone 5, Central Meridian 64° 30' Longitude West)

S 68° 21' 23" E, 10,429.83 feet from Nova Scotia Co-ordinate Monument No. 21115 having

co-ordinate values of North 16, 024, 598.43 feet and East 18, 019, 926.23 feet based on a 1979

redefined adjustment;

From thence N 12° 41' 45" W, 168.90 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2734;

Thence S 72° 27' 09" W, 296.29 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2733;

Thence S 40° 05' 04" W, 1030.34 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2732;

Thence S 22° 41' 56" W, 1032.39 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2731;

Thence S 03° 47' 16" E, 432.95 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2730, said survey marker being

N 18° 16' 54" E, 4634.49 feet from Nova Scotia Co-ordinate Monument No. 16526 having

co-ordinate values of North 16, 014, 253.70 feet and East 18, 026, 814.07 feet based on a 1979

adjustment;

Thence N 86° 12' 44" E, 20 feet more or less to the mean high watermark of Ragged Harbour;

Thence in a northeasterly direction following the sinuosities of the mean high watermark of

Ragged Harbour, 2700 feet more or less to the place of beginning, containing 8 acres more or

less.

Excepting and reserving from the above described area the existing access road which runs in

a northerly direction across the beach, the said access road being approximately 20 feet in width

and shown on the aforementioned Plan No. E-13-49.
