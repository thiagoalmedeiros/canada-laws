NS Reg 142/2023

*Made under clause 8A(1)(c) of the Environment Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Packaging, Paper Products and Packaging-Like

Products Material Management Standard Adoption Regulations .

### Adopted standard

2 The Packaging, Paper Products and Packaging-Like Products Material Management

Standard developed by the Department of Environment and Climate Change, as

amended or replaced from time to time, is adopted by the Minister of Environment and

Climate Change under clause 8A(1)(c) of the Environment Act as a standard setting out

the minimum material management requirements for the prescribed designated materials

in the Extended Producer Responsibility for Packaging, Paper Products and

Packaging-Like Products Regulations.

### Public availability

3 The standard adopted by these regulations must be available to the public, clearly

identifying amendments and effective dates, as follows:

(a) on the Department of Environment and Climate Change’s website; and

(b) at the offices of the Department of Environment and Climate Change,

which, as of the date these regulations are in force, is at 1903 Barrington

Street, Suite 2085, Halifax, Nova Scotia.
