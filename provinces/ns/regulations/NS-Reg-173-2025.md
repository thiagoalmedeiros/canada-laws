NS Reg 173/2025

*Made under Section 236A of the Halifax Regional Municipality Charter*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Development Approval Cost Freeze Expiration

Date Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations, “Charter” means the Halifax Regional Municipality Charter .

### Prescribed expiration date for Section 236A of Charter

3 The prescribed date for the expiration of Section 236A of the Charter is November 9,

2026.
