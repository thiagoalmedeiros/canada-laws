NS Reg 20/2017

*Made under Section 23 of the Public Archives Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Advisory Board Composition Regulations .

### Board composition

2 The Board must consist of up to 8 members, as follows:

(a) up to 4 members appointed from among individuals recommended by archival,

heritage, information management or family history organizations in the

Province; and

(b) up to 4 members appointed from the public at large, including the academic,

library, cultural and general research communities.

### Term and reappointment of Board members

3 (1) The term of office of each member of the Board is 3 years and, subject to

subsection (2), a Board member may be reappointed.

(2) A Board member who has served 2 consecutive terms is not eligible for

reappointment until 2 years after the date their most recent term ended.
