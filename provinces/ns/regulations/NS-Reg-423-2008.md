NS Reg 423/2008

*Made under Section 6 of the Gunshot Wounds Mandatory Reporting Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Gunshot Wounds Mandatory Reporting Regulations .

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means the Gunshot Wounds Mandatory Reporting Act .

### Prescribed persons for making disclosure under Section 3 of Act

3 The persons responsible for disclosing the information required by Section 3 of the Act on

behalf of a hospital, facility or service that has treated an individual for a gunshot wound

are prescribed as the persons designated for that purpose by the hospital, facility or service.
