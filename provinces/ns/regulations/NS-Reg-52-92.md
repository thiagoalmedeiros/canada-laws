NS Reg 52/92

*Made under Section 26 of the Partnerships and Business Names Registration Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the "Partnerships and Business Names

Registration Act Regulations".

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations

(a) "Act" means the Partnerships and Business Names Registration Act;

(b) "declaration" means a declaration required to be filed under Section 5 of

the Act.

### Declaration where partner not a corporation

3 A declaration filed under Section 5 of the Act by one or more individuals where

the partner is not a corporation shall contain

(a) the full first and surname of each individual;

(b) the full residential address of each individual including the postal code;

(c) the full business address of the business in the Province including the

postal code;

(d) the full mailing address of each individual and the business including

post office box references where applicable;

(e) the name of the business;

(f) the nature of the business to be carried on; and

(g) the date the business commenced.

### Declaration where partner is a corporation

4 A declaration filed under Section 5 of the Act where a partner is a corporation

shall

(a) be made under oath by an officer or director of the corporation and state

the position held by the officer or director in the corporation; and

(b) contain

(i) the full name of the corporation,

(ii) the full business address of the partnership in the Province

including postal code,

(iii) the registered office address of the corporation,

(iv) the nature of the business to be carried on,

(v) the date business commenced, and

(vi) the name under which the business is to be carried on.
