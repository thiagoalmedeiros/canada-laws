NS Reg 136/73

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 1st day of February, A.D., 1973, pursuant to the provisions of Section 2 of

Chapter 244 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased

to

(a) constitute all that area of land including land covered with water owned by Her

Majesty in right of the Province of Nova Scotia and situate at Shinimicas

Bridge, in the County of Cumberland, Province of Nova Scotia, to be a

Provincial Park, said area of land being included within the boundaries of the

lands described in Schedule “A” attached to the report and recommendation,

and shown outlined on the Plan of Survey marked Schedule “B” also attached

to the report and recommendation;

(b) declare the name by which the park shall be known as “Shinimicas Provincial

Park”.

## Schedule “A”

### Shinimicas Provincial Park

All that certain lot, piece or parcel of land situate, lying or being at Shinimicas Bridge, in the

County of Cumberland, Province of Nova Scotia, as shown on Plan E-5-27 filed in the Crown Land Records Office, Department of Lands and Forests, Halifax and being more

particularly described as follows:

Beginning at Crown Survey Post No. C1017 situated on the southern boundary of public

highway No. 6 leading from Pugwash to Amherst, and being westerly, a distance of forty-six

feet more or less from a point “A” shown on said plan E-5-27, said point “A” being that point

where the southern boundary of said highway No. 6 intersects the westerly edge of Shinimicas

River so-called;

From thence easterly following the southern boundary of said highway No. 6 forty-six feet,

more or less, to the aforementioned point “A”;

Thence in a southerly direction following the several courses of the western edge of Shinimicas

River to Crown Survey Post No. C1018, situate (by astronomic bearings) south five degrees

twenty-six minutes twenty-one seconds west, one thousand one hundred two decimal

twenty-four feet from aforesaid post No. C1017;

Thence north fifty-six degrees twelve minutes twenty-four seconds west, one hundred

ninety-two decimal zero five feet to Crown Survey Post No. C1019;

Thence south eighty-seven degrees forty-eight minutes thirty-three seconds west, two hundred

seventy-eight decimal forty feet to Crown Survey Post No. C1020;

Thence north twenty-eight degrees ten minutes three seconds west, three hundred ninety-two

decimal sixteen feet to Crown Survey Post No. C1021 situated near the eastern edge of a small

brook flowing in a southerly direction;

Thence north three degrees forty-five minutes forty-eight seconds east to a point marked “B”

on said Plan E-5-27, said point “B” being situate on the eastern edge of aforesaid brook;

Thence in a northerly direction following the eastern edge of said brook to a point marked “C”

on said plan, said point “C” being situate on the southern boundary of the aforesaid public

highway No, 6 leading from Pugwash to Amherst;

Thence easterly following the southern boundary of said highway twenty feet more or less to

Crown Survey Post No. C1022 situated north three degrees forty-five minutes forty-eight

seconds east, nine hundred eleven decimal ninety-two feet from aforementioned Crown Survey

Post No. C1021, and being north sixty-nine degrees seven minutes twenty-two seconds west,

seven hundred fourteen decimal thirty-two feet from the place of beginning;

Thence easterly following the said southern boundary of public highway No. 6 to the place of

beginning, the said lot containing nineteen acres more or less.
