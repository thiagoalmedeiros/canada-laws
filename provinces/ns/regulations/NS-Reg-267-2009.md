NS Reg 267/2009

*Made under Section 10 of the Retail Business Designated Day Closing Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Retail Business Designated Day Closing

Regulations .

### Definitions

2 (1) In these regulations, “Act” means the Retail Business Designated Day Closing Act .

(2) In the Act, “Canada Day” means July 1.

### Prescribed goods and services

3 Selling liquor or offering liquor for sale by a retail business under the authority of a

permanent license issued under the Liquor Licensing Regulations made under the Liquor

Control Act are prescribed as goods and services for the purposes of an exemption from

subsection 3(1) of the Act under clause 3(3)(q) of the Act.
