NS Reg 64/2023

*Made under Section 369 of the Municipal Government Act*

### Order M10866

### Nova Scotia Utility and Review Board

### In the matter of the Municipal Government Act

- and -

In the matter of an application by the Town of Westville to confirm the number of

councillors

Before : Julia E. Clark, LL. B., Member

### Order

The Town of Westville made an application under s. 369 of the Municipal Government Act and

the Board issued its written Decision on March 31, 2023.

The Board approves the application and orders that the number of councillors for the Town is

confirmed at four (4), to be elected at-large.

Dated at Halifax, Nova Scotia, this 31st day of March, 2023.

sgd. Bruce A. Kiley Chief Clerk of the Board
