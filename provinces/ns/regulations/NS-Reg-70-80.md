NS Reg 70/80

*Made under subsection 184(4) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

Pursuant to Section 166(2) of Chapter 191 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia,

1967, [subsection 184(4), R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 293], the Motor Vehicle Act, I hereby

approve of the display on the lower right inside corner of the windshield of a motor

vehicle of an identification permit in the form of a sticker issued under the regulations

respecting Identification Permits for Vehicles Transporting Handicapped Persons.

Dated and made at Halifax, in the County of Halifax, Province of Nova Scotia, this

10th day of April, 1980.

Signed: Thomas J. McInnis Minister of Transportation
