NS Reg 68/2020

*Made under Section 179 of the Assessment Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Small-Scale Residential Tourist Accommodation

Establishments Regulations .

Number of rooms prescribed for definition of “small-scale residential tourist

accommodation establishment”

2 For the purpose of defining a small-scale residential tourist accommodation

establishment pursuant to Section 47A of the Assessment Act , the number of rooms in a

small-scale residential tourist accommodation establishment that may be rented to the

travelling or vacationing public to provide overnight sleeping accommodation is 4 or

fewer.
