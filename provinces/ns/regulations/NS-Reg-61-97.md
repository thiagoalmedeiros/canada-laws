NS Reg 61/97

*Made under subsection 302(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Prorated Registration Fees for Inter-jurisdictional

Commercial Trucks Regulations .

### Section 1 added: O.I.C. 2001-149, N.S. Reg. 36/2001.

1A The unused portion of any registration fee for a vehicle registered under the International

Registration Plan may be applied against a liability for fees incurred as a result of additions

to vehicle fleets or replacement of vehicles within the vehicle fleets during a registration

year, but may not be carried forward to subsequent registration years.

### Original Section 1 renumbered 1A and amended: O.I.C. 2001-149, N.S. Reg. 36/2001.

2 Fees are to be paid as set out in the regulations respecting documents and services fees for

the following services:

(a) issue, re-issue or renewal of a prorated registration permit or cab card;

### Clause 2(a) amended: O.I.C. 2005-92, N.S. Reg. 74/2005.

(b) replacement International Registration Plan number plates;

### Clause 2(b) amended: O.I.C. 2005-92, N.S. Reg. 74/2005.

(c) duplicate permit (cab card);

(d) certificate of registration; and

(e) temporary breakdown permit.

### Clause 2(e) added: O.I.C. 2005-92, N.S. Reg. 74/2005.

### Section 2 amended: O.I.C. 2001-149, N.S. Reg. 36/2001; O.I.C. 2005-92, N.S. Reg. 74/2005.

3 The registration fee for a vehicle registered under the International Registration Plan is set

out in Table A opposite the weight range within which the vehicle falls.

### Section 3 amended: O.I.C. 2001-149, N.S. Reg. 36/2001.

4 The registration fee for a vehicle added to a vehicle fleet during the registration year for

that vehicle fleet is 1/12 of the annual fee set out in Table A opposite the weight range

within which the vehicle falls, multiplied by the number of months remaining in the

registration period, plus 10% of the annual fee.
