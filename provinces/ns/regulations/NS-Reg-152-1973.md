NS Reg 152/1973

*Made under subsection 253(1) of the Municipal Government Act*

1 In this order

(a) “Commission” means the Lunenburg County District Planning

Commission;

(b) “Minister” means the Minister of Municipal Affairs;

(c) “Participating Municipality” means the Municipality of the District of

Chester, the Town of Lunenburg, or the Town of Mahone Bay;

(d) “District” means the geographical area made up of the geographical

areas of the participating municipalities.

2 The Commission shall be called the “District Planning Commission of Lunenburg

County”.

3 The area with respect to which the commission shall exercise its powers is the

geographical areas of the Municipality of the District of Chester, the Town of

Lunenburg and the Town of Mahone Bay.

4 The municipality and towns to be represented on the commission are the

Municipality of the District of Chester, the Town of Lunenburg and the Town of

Mahone Bay.

5 The commission shall consist of 9 members as follows:

• three (3) members to be appointed by the Municipality of the District

of Chester

• three (3) members to be appointed by the Town of Lunenburg

• three (3) members to be appointed by the Town of Mahone Bay.

6 (1) One member appointed by each participating municipality shall be a

member of council and shall be appointed annually at the annual meeting of

the participating municipality.

(2) The other 2 members appointed by each participating municipality shall be

resident in the municipality which appointed them and shall hold office for 3

years from the date of their appointment excepting the initial appointments

one of which shall be for a 3-year term and the other for a 2-year term.

(3) Members shall hold office subject to Section 23 of the Planning Act .

(4) In the event of death or resignation of a member, the Council shall appoint

another person to fill the unexpired term.

7 Until further Order, the Minister shall not appoint any member or members to the

Commission.

8 [repealed]

9 (1) The Commission shall elect a Chairman and a Vice-Chairman from among

its members and a Secretary-Treasurer, who does not have to be a member

of the Commission, and they shall hold office until their successors are

elected. The Chairman and Vice-Chairman shall not be appointed of the

same municipality.

(2) There shall be an Executive Committee comprising the Chairman, the Vice

Chairman and one other member so that all 3 participating municipalities are

represented.

(3) The Commission shall elect these officers at its 1st meeting after the making

of this Order, and, thereafter, shall elect officers at its annual meeting.

(4) A simple majority shall constitute a quorum.

(5) (i) That decisions on all matters other than salary and budget be

made by majority vote of all members (individuals) present,

(ii) that decisions on all matters of salary and budget be made by a

majority vote whereby there must be agreement of the majority

of the units that contain greater than 51% of the combined

uniform assessment of the participating municipalities, with

each unit having 1 vote, and all participating municipal units

shall be represented at the meeting. [ sic ]

(6) Decisions of the Commission shall be forwarded to the Councils of the

participating municipalities, and each Council may require the Commission

to reconsider any or all aspects of any policy or decision which is offensive

to that Council.

10 There shall be quarterly meetings of the Commission in each calendar year and

such other meetings as may be called from time to time by the Chairman or by

any 2 members of the Commission.

10A (1) The Commission shall appoint an arbitrator at its annual meeting.

(2) If the council of any one of the participating municipalities is dissatisfied

with the amount of the estimate referred to in subsection 23(1) of the Planning Act , then, within 60 days after the estimate has been delivered to

the clerk of the participating municipality, it shall require the arbitrator

referred to in subsection (1) to determine and report the amount which the

municipality shall provide for the commission and the arbitrator shall make

the report within 10 days of receiving the request.

11 The Minister reserves the right to make such other Orders as may be required

under the authority of the Planning Act .
