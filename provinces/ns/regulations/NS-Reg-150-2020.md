NS Reg 150/2020

*Made under clauses 8(a) & (d) of the Provincial Parks Act*

### O.I.C. 2020-272

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

 Forestry dated September 10, 2020, and pursuant to clauses 8(a) and (d) of Chapter 367 of the

 Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased, effective October

 13, 2020, to

(a) designate approximately 722.3 hectares (1784.81 acres) of Crown lands shown outlined

in bold and shaded dark grey on the plan marked Schedule “A” attached to and forming

part of the report and recommendation as a provincial park, and declare that it be known

as Barra Forest and MacNeils Vale Provincial Park; and

(b) authorize the Minister of Lands and Forestry to execute such documents as may be

necessary to achieve the purposes of this order.
