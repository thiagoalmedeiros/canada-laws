NS Reg 70/2007

*Made under Section 23 of the Public Archives Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Public Archives Fees Regulations .

### Fees for copies of registrations

2 The fees for copies of birth, marriage and death registrations held by the Public Archives

of Nova Scotia are as set out in the following table:

| Document | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Digital copy delivered electronically | $11.13 |
| Certified paper copy delivered by mail | $22.39 |
