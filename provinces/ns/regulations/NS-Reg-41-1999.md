NS Reg 41/1999

*Made under Section 18 of the Communications and Information Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Royal Gazette Part I Fees Regulations .

### Fees

2 The fees for the Royal Gazette, Part I, are as follows:

(a) single issue ................................................................................. $0.36 per page

(b) advertising:

(i) citation notices, 5 insertions ........................................................... $26.22

(ii) citation notices, ancillary probate, 3 insertions ............................... $26.22

(c) estate notices:

(i) 1 month (under $800.00 value) ...................................................... $26.22

(ii) 3 issues (ancillary) ......................................................................... $26.22

(iii) 6 months ....................................................................................... $59.78

(d) all other notices, including corrections to previously published notices, for maximum number of insertions required by statute ........................... $26.22

(e) [repealed]

### Minimum charge

3 The fee prescribed in clause 2(a) is subject to a $5.83 minimum charge.
