NS Reg 147/77

*Made under Section 113 of the Wildlife Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and Forests dated the 8th day of November, A. D., 1977, pursuant to Section 161(6) of Chapter 163 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Lands and Forests Act , Part III-Game, is pleased to:

(a) declare that the area described in Schedule "A" and shown outlined in red on Schedule "B", both Schedules attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation, be a wildlife management area, to be known as the Abercrombie Wildlife Management Area;

(b) make regulations in the form set out in Schedule "C" attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation, for the control of the wildlife management area and the management of the wildlife therein.

Schedule "C"

 Regulations for the Management of the Abercrombie Wildlife Management Area

 made pursuant to Section 161 of the Lands and Forests Act .

1 In these regulations,

(a) "Department" means the Department of Lands and Forests;

(b) "firearm" means any barrelled weapon or bow from which any shot, bullet or missile can be discharged and includes anything that can readily be adapted for use as a firearm;

(c) "Minister" means the Minister of Natural Resources;

(d) "vehicle" means every device in, upon or by which any person or property is or may be transported and includes a boat, canoe or other water conveyance or any snowmobile;

(e) "Wildlife Management Area" means the Abercrombie Wildlife Management Area and includes the double-crested cormorant colony located between Abercrombie Point and the causeway leading to the town of Pictou.

2 (1) Except as authorized under these regulations, it shall be an offence for any person to possess or discharge any firearm within the boundaries of the Wildlife Management Area.

(2) Except as authorized under these regulations, it shall be an offence for any person to enter the Wildlife Management Area by means of a vehicle.

(3) Subsection (2) does not apply to visitors who enter the Wildlife Management Area for the purpose of viewing wildlife, forest practices and picnicing in such areas as are designated by the Department and those employees of Scott Paper Company who are housed within or working in the Wildlife Management Area by virtue of their employment.

(4) Except as authorized by the Minister, it shall be an offence to kill, take, hunt, trap or chase, pursue, worry, follow after or on the trail of or search for any wild mammals or birds within the Wildlife Management Area.

(5) Except as authorized by the Minister, it shall be an offence for any person to be in possession of any trap, snare, poison, wild mammal or bird or the carcass or any part of any wild mammal or bird within the boundaries of the Wildlife Management Area.

3 (1) Notwithstanding Section (2) [2] of these regulations, those persons employed by the Department when in performance of their Departmental duties, may

(a) possess and discharge a firearm within the boundaries of the Wildlife Management Area;

(b) enter the Wildlife Management Area by means of a vehicle;

(c) kill, take, hunt, trap, or chase, pursue, worry, follow after or on the trail of or search for wild mammals or birds within the Wildlife Management Area;

(d) be in possession of any trap, snare, poison, wild mammals or birds or the carcass or any part of any wild mammal or bird within the boundaries of the Wildlife Management Area.

4 (1) It shall be an offence for any person to permit a dog or other animal owned by them, in their care or brought by them within the Wildlife Management Area to run at large.

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1) a dog or other animal is deemed to be at large unless such dog or other animal is on a leash which does not exceed six feet in length.

5 (1) For the purpose of these regulations, the employees of Scott Paper Company, who by virtue of their employment are housed in the Survey House, so called, within the Wildlife Management Area, may possess at that location firearms, traps and snares for the purpose of storage.

(2) The firearms, traps and snares mentioned in subsection (1), while being transported to and from or being stored at the Survey House, so called, are to be made unoperable during these periods.

6 These regulations shall come into force on the 15th day of November, A.D., 1977.
