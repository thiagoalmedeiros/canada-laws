NS Reg 18/2018

*Made under Section 72 of the Marine Renewable-energy Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Marine Renewable-energy Fees Regulations .

### Definition

2 In these regulations,

“Act” means the Marine Renewable-energy Act .

### Application fees prescribed

3 (1) The fee for an application for a licence, permit, approval or consent under the Act

is as set out in the following table:

| Type of Application | Fee |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Licence | $2500.00 |  |

| ∙ | under clause 35(1)(b) of the Act, if issued at the same time as the
