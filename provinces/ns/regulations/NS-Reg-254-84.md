NS Reg 254/84

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 4th day of October, A.D., 1984, pursuant to Section 2 of Chapter 244 of the

Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to:

(a) constitute all that area of land situate at Long Lake and Spruce Hill Lake, in the

County of Halifax, owned by Her Majesty the Queen in Right of the Province

of Nova Scotia, to be a Provincial Park, the said area of land being described in

Schedule “A” and shown outlined in red on Department of Lands and Forests

Plan No. P-122/84 marked Schedule “B”, both Schedules attached to and

forming part of the report and recommendation; and

(b) declare that the park shall be known by the name of “Long Lake Provincial

Park”.

### Order approved by O.I.C. 93-364

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated the 12th day of March, A.D., 1993, pursuant to Section 8(b) of Chapter 367 of

the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to approve the

reduction in the size of Long Lake Provincial Park, in the County of Halifax, so constituted by

Order in Council 84-1189, dated the 9th day of October, A.D., 1984, by approving the

withdrawal from the park of approximately 3.046 hectares of Crown land shown outlined in red

on the plans marked Schedule “A” through “D”, attached to and forming part of the report and

recommendation, for the purpose of construction of a ramp connecting Trunk #3 to Northwest

Arm Drive and to facilitate the upgrading of the Old Sambro Road, and to authorize the

Minister of Natural Resources to execute such documents as may be necessary to achieve the

purposes of this Order.
