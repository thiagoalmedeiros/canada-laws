NS Reg 93/2008

*Made under subsection 304(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Extension of Certificates, Licenses and Permits

Regulations .

### Registrar may order extension

2 The Registrar may order the extension of any certificate, license or permit issued under the Motor Vehicle Act and its regulations for a specified period, if the Registrar considers it

necessary as a result of any of the following circumstances:

(a) a natural disaster;

(b) a system outage;

(c) an act of God;

(d) any temporary situation that the Registrar believes requires the extension of the

certificate, license or permit for the purpose of carrying out the true intent,

purpose and object of the Act.
