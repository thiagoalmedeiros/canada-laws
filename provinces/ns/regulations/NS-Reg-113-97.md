NS Reg 113/97

*Made under Section 29 of the Apprenticeship and Trades Qualifications Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Trade Names Equivalency Regulations .

2 For the purposes of the trade regulations made pursuant to Section 41 of Chapter 17 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Apprenticeship and Trades Qualifications Act ,

(a) "Bricklaying Trade" shall be read as Bricklayer Trade;

(b) "Cooking Trade" shall be read as Cook Trade;

(c) "Electrical Construction Trade" shall be read as Construction Electrician Trade;

(d) "Farm Equipment Repair Trade" shall be read as Farm Equipment Mechanic Trade;

(e) "Industrial Electrical Trade" shall be read as Industrial Electrician Trade;

(f) "Industrial Instrumentation Trade" shall be read as Industrial Instrument Mechanic Trade;

(g) "Lineman Trade" shall be read as Powerline Technician Trade;

(h) "Metal Fabricator Trade" shall be read as Metal Fabricator (Fitter) Trade; Clause 2(h) amended: O.I.C. 2001-322, N.S. Reg. 86/2001.

(i) "Motor Vehicle Repair (Body) Trade" shall be read as Motor Vehicle Body Repairer (Metal and Paint) Trade;

(j) "Motor Vehicle Repair (Bus & Transport) Trade" shall be read as Truck & Transport Mechanic Trade;

(k) "Motor Vehicle Repair (Mechanical) Trade" shall be read as Automotive Service Technician Trade;

(l) "Motor Vehicle Repair (Heavy Duty) Trade" shall be read as Heavy Duty Equipment Technician Trade; Clause 2(l) amended: O.I.C. 2001-322, N.S. Reg. 86/2001.

(la) "Oil Burner Mechanic Trade" shall be read as Oil Burner Installer and Maintenance Mechanic Trade; Clause 2(la) added: O.I.C. 2000-295, N.S. Reg. 106/2000.

(m) "Painting and Decorating Trade" shall be read as Painter and Decorator Trade;

(n) "Plumbing Trade" shall be read as Plumber Trade;

(o) "Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Trade" shall be read as Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Mechanic Trade;

(p) "Sheet Metal Trade" shall be read as Sheet Metal Worker Trade;

(q) "Sprinkler - Fitter Trade" shall be read as Sprinkler System Installer Trade;

(r) "Steamfitting Trade" shall be read as Steamfitter/Pipefitter Trade;

(s) "Welding Trade (C, B, and A levels)" shall be read as Welder (C, B, and A levels) Trade,

wherever it appears in the trade regulations.
