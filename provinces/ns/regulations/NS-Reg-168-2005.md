NS Reg 168/2005

*Made under Section 12A of the Income Tax Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Sport and Recreation Activity Designation Regulations .

### Definition

2 In these regulations, "Act" means the Income Tax Act .

### Designation

3 For the purposes of the deduction provided for in Section 12A of the Act, a designated sport or recreation activity means a sport, physical recreation or physical activity program or organization that is registered with the Nova Scotia Department of Health and Wellness before December 31 of the applicable taxation year. [Note: the reference to the Office of Health Promotion has been updated in accordance with Order in Council 2011-15 under the Public Service Act , R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 376, effective January 11, 2011.]
