NS Reg 305/90

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 30th day of October, A.D., 1990, pursuant to Section 8 of Chapter 367 of the

Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to:

(a) designate all that area of land situate at North Wallace Point, in the County of

Cumberland, owned by Her Majesty the Queen in right of the Province of

Nova Scotia, to be a Provincial Park, the said area of land being described in

Schedule “A” and shown outlined in bold line on the copy of Provincial

Crown Lands Record Centre Plan No. E-15-28 marked Schedule “B”, both

Schedules attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation; and

(b) declare that the Park shall be known by the name of “Fox Harbour Provincial

Park”.

## Schedule “A”

All and singular those certain lots, pieces, or parcels of land situate, lying, and being at North

Wallace Point, in the County of Cumberland, Province of Nova Scotia and shown outlined in

red as Parcel A and Parcel B on a compiled plan showing Fox Harbour Provincial Park, North

Wallace Point, Cumberland County, Nova Scotia, and filed in the Crown Land Records Centre,

Dartmouth, Nova Scotia (C.L.R. No. E-15-28) and being more particularly described as

follows:

### Parcel A

Beginning at SM C2343 situate on the northern boundary of the main public highway leading

from Fox Harbour to North Wallace and the eastern boundary of lands now or formerly of

Edward Greer;

Thence along the eastern boundary of lands now or formerly of Edward Greer 06° 44' 25", a

distance of 632.25 feet to SM C2344;

Thence continuing along the eastern boundary of lands now or formerly of Edward Greer, 06° 44' 25", a distance of 10 feet to the ordinary high was [water] mark of Fox Harbour;

Thence easterly along the ordinary high water mark of Fox Harbour, a distance of 6700 feet

more or less to a point. Said point being on the northern boundary of lands now or formerly of

David Elliott;

Thence along the northern boundary of lands now or formerly of David Elliott, 271° 58' 55", a

distance of 21 feet to SM C2345;

Thence continuing along the northern boundary of lands now or formerly of David Elliott,

271° 58' 55", a distance of 702.87 feet to SM C2346. Said survey marker being on the eastern

boundary of lands now or formerly of Gerald Thompson;

Thence along the eastern boundary of lands now or formerly of Gerald Thompson, 08° 30' 49",

a distance of 416.23 feet to SM C2347;

Thence along the northern boundary of lands now or formerly of Gerald Thompson, 269° 52' 54", a distance of 730.79 feet to SM C2348. Said survey marker being on the northern

boundary of lands now or formerly of Gerald Thompson and the eastern boundary of the

aforementioned main public highway leading from Fox Harbour to North Wallace;

Thence along a curve to the left having a radius of 527.49 feet, an arc distance of 747.88 feet to

mentioned SM C2348;

Thence 280° 02' 56", a distance of 751.79 feet to SM C2355;

Beginning at SM C2349 situate on the western boundary of the main public highway leading

Parcel A and Parcel B have a total combined area of 75.3 acres more or less.
