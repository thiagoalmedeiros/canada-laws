NS Reg 127/74

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 26th day of November, A.D., 1974, pursuant to the provisions of Section 2 of

Chapter 244 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased

to:

(a) constitute all that area of land including land covered with water owned by Her

Majesty in right of the Province of Nova Scotia, and situate at or near

Northport, in the County of Cumberland, Province of Nova Scotia, to be a

Provincial Park, said area of land being included within the boundaries of the

lands described in Schedule “A” and shown outlined on the Plan of Survey

marked Schedule “B”, both Schedules attached to and forming part of the

report and recommendation, and

(b) declare the name by which the park shall be known as “Northport Beach

Provincial Park”.

## Schedule “A”

### Northport Beach Provincial Park

All that certain lot, piece or parcel of land situate, lying and being at or near Northport, County

of Cumberland, Province of Nova Scotia, as the same is shown on Plan E-5-25, recorded in the

Crown Land Registry office, Department of Lands and Forests, Halifax, and being more

particularly described as follows:

Beginning at a Crown Land Iron Post (C.L.I.P.) marked 1249 set on the northern boundary of

Highway No. 366 and the most westerly corner of lands now or formerly owned by Hibbert

Oulton;

Thence north seventy-nine degrees eleven minutes thirty seconds west (N 79° 11' 30" W) along

the northern boundary of said Highway No. 366 distant one hundred sixteen decimal seven

(116.7) feet to a C.L.I.P. marked 1253 set on the eastern boundary of lands now or formerly

owned by Hibbert Oulton;

Thence north twenty-seven degrees thirty seconds east (N 27° 00' 30" E) along the eastern

boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by Hibbert Oulton distant six hundred twenty

decimal two (620.2) feet to a C.L.I.P. marked 1252;

Thence north forty-seven degrees forty-five minutes thirty seconds west (N 47° 45' 30" W)

along the northern boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by Hibbert Oulton distant

five hundred thirteen decimal four (513.4) feet to a C.L.I.P.;

Thence north twenty-nine degrees twenty minutes thirty seconds east (N 29° 20' 30" E) along

the eastern boundary of lands now or formerly owned by Donald Brander distant three hundred

fifty-four decimal four (354.4) feet to a stake;

Thence north twenty-nine degrees forty-one minutes thirty seconds east (N 29° 41' 30" E) along

the eastern boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by Donald Brander distant three

hundred forty-five decimal seven (345.7) feet to a C.L.I.P.;

Thence north twenty-seven degrees fifty minutes thirty seconds east (N 27° 50' 30" E) along the

eastern boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by Donald Brander distant sixty-nine

decimal zero (69.0) feet to a C.L.I.P.;

Thence north forty-six degrees nine minutes thirty seconds west (N 46° 09' 30" W) along the

eastern boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by Donald Brander distant four hundred

Thence north forty-six degrees thirty seconds west (N 46° 00' 30" W) along the eastern

boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by Donald Brander distant two hundred twelve

Thence south twenty-six degrees twenty-nine minutes thirty seconds west (S 26° 29' 30" W)

Campbell distant three hundred decimal zero (300.0) feet to a C.L.I.P. set on the northeastern

boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by Hibbert Oulton;

Thence north forty-seven degrees thirty-five minutes thirty seconds west (N 47° 35' 30" W)

along the northeastern boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by Hibbert Oulton distant

Thence south eighty-five degrees thirty-eight minutes thirty seconds west (S 85° 38' 30" W)

Thence south twenty-six degrees four minutes thirty seconds west (S 26° 04' 30" W) along the

The above described lands containing an area of twenty decimal four (20.4) acres more or less.
