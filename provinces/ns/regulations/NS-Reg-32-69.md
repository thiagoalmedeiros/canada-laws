NS Reg 32/69

*Made under Section 7 of the Imitation Dairy Products Act*

1 The following products and classes of products are designated as imitation dairy products:

(a) milk and products of milk in which part or all of the fat is replaced with a fat

or oil other than that of milk;

(b) synthetic milk;

(c) synthetic ice cream.

2 (1) The following imitation dairy products and classes of imitation dairy products may

be manufactured within the Province provided the person who manufactures them is

the holder of a license issued under these regulations:

(a) Coffee-Prime

(b) Farmers Fifty-Fifty

(c) Twenty-Eighty

(d) Farmers Soft Spread

(e) Mutter

(f) Maritime Brand 4-2-1 Spread

3 Every application to manufacture an imitation dairy product shall be in Form “A” of the

Schedule hereto or to like effect.

4 The Minister, if satisfied that the premises upon which it is proposed to manufacture an

imitation dairy product are of suitable construction and that they are equipped with

adequate sanitary facilities and equipment, and which construction, facilities and

equipment shall be the minimum standard required by regulations made under the Public

Health Act for pasteurization plants, will issue a license in Form “B” of the Schedule

hereto, or to like effect.

[Note: Effective May 19, 1977, the name of the Public Health Act is changed to the Health Act by S.N.S. 1977,

c. 2, s. 11.]

[Note: Effective November 1, 2005, the referenced regulations are continued under the Health Protection Act ,

S.N.S. 2004, c. 4, which repeals Part I of the Health Act .]

5 The fee for a license to manufacture an imitation dairy product or products is $5.00 and

shall accompany the application.

6 A license expires the 31st day of December of the year for which it is issued.

7 Licenses are not transferable.

8 The Minister may

(a) vary or amend a license;

(b) suspend, or cancel a license for any violation of the Act or regulations.

### Advertising & labelling

9 No person shall make an untrue claim by word or design in respect of an imitation dairy

product in an advertisement or on the label of a container.

10 (1) No advertisement respecting an imitation dairy product and no label on a container

of an imitation dairy product shall

(a) state or imply that the imitation dairy product has a relation to any dairy

product;

(b) use a dairy term or expression; or

(c) depict a dairy scene.

(2) Every container of an imitation dairy product shall have legibly displayed thereon the

name of the manufacturer and a list of the ingredients.

(3) No person shall display for sale at retail an imitation dairy product in a manner that

implies the imitation dairy product has a relation to any dairy product.

### Records

11 (1) Every manufacturer of an imitation dairy product shall keep records showing

(a) the ingredients and the amounts thereof used in the manufacture of the

imitation dairy product;

(b) the quantities of the imitation dairy product that he has manufactured, and sold.

(2) Every manufacturer shall maintain the records made under subsection (1) of Section

11 for a period of not less than twelve months.

[Note: Effective February 24, 2006, the references to the Department of Agriculture and Marketing and

Minister of Agriculture and Marketing in Forms “A” and “B” should be read as references to the

Department of Agriculture and Minister of Agriculture in accordance with Order in Council 2006-121

under the Public Service Act , R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 376.]
