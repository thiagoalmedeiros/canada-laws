NS Reg 117/2014

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated July 23, 2014, and pursuant to Section 8 of Chapter 367 of the Revised

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased, effective on and after

August 12, 2014, to

(a) designate as a provincial park the lands shown outlined in bold on the plan marked

Schedule “A” attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation and

declare that it be known as Bell Provincial Park; and

(b) [Clause (b) not regulations within the meaning of the Regulations Act .]
