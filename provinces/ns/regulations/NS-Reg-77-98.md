*Made under Section 46 of the Agriculture and Marketing Act*

### Commodity Group/Levies

Whereas Part F, Section 46 of Chapter 6 of the Revised Statues of Nova Scotia, 1989, Agriculture and Marketing Act , as amended provides that the Minister by Order may designate

a body of a specific agricultural product as a commodity group and approve levy amounts.

For greater certainty, the Minister of Agriculture and Marketing confirms the levies for
