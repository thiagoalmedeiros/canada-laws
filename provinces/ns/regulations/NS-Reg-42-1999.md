NS Reg 42/1999

*Made under Section 21 of the Regulations Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Regulations under the Regulations Act .

### Title required

2 All regulations submitted to the Registrar for filing shall contain a title indicating the

subject of those regulations.

### Certificate of filing

3 A certificate of filing required by subsection 3(1) of the Act shall be in the form

prescribed in Schedule “B”.

### Failure to comply

4 (1) If a regulation fails to comply in one or more respects with the requirements of

these regulations, the Registrar may file the regulation if the Registrar is of the

opinion that there is substantial compliance with these regulations.

(2) A regulation is not invalid for the reason only that it fails to comply in one or more

respects with the requirements of these regulations.

### Fees

5 The fees to be charged by the Registrar for supplying copies of regulations are as set out

in Schedule “A”.

(b) for an electronic copy

(i) [repealed]

(ii) by e-mail ..................................................................... $0.27 per page

(3) In this Section, “page” means a page of text as formatted by the Office of the

documents provided.
