NS Reg 92/96

*Made under Section 79 of the Motor Vehicle Act*

### Order in Council 96-315:

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of

Business and Consumer Services dated April 17, 1996, pursuant to Section 79 of

Chapter 293 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Motor Vehicle Act, is

pleased to determine that on, from and after May 1, 1996, every driver's licence shall

expire in the year endorsed on the license, effective on the anniversary of the birth day

of the driver.
