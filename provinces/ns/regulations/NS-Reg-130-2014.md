NS Reg 130/2014

*Made under Section 5 of the Electricity Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Renewable Electricity Retail Sale Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations, “Act” means the Electricity Act .

### Prescribed date for retail sales and retail purchase of renewable electricity

3 The prescribed date for retail sales and retail purchase of renewable electricity under

clauses 3C(1)(a) and (b) of the Act is October 18, 2022.
