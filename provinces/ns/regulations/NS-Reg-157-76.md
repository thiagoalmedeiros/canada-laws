NS Reg 157/76

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 7th day of April, A.D., 1976, pursuant to the provisions of Section 2 of

Chapter 244 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased

to

(a) constitute all that area of land including land covered with water owned by Her

Majesty in the right of the Province of Nova Scotia and situate at or near Sand

Hills Beach, in the County of Shelburne, Province of Nova Scotia, to be a

Provincial Park, said area of land being included within the boundaries of the

lands described in Schedule “A” attached to and forming part of the report and

recommendation, and shown outlined on the Plan of Survey marked Schedule

“B” also attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation;

(b) declare the name by which the park shall be known as “Sand Hills Beach

Provincial Park”.

## Schedule “B” replaced: O.I.C. 88-406, N.S. Reg. 91/88.

### Order approved by O.I.C. 89-1028

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 1st day of August, A.D., 1989, pursuant to Section 16(1)(a) of Chapter 5 of

the Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1987, the Crown Lands Act , and Section 8(c) of Chapter 18 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1988, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to

(a) approve the alteration of the boundary of lands situate at or near Villagedale, in

the County of Shelburne, constituted as Sand Hills Beach Provincial Park by

Order in Council 76-558 dated the 11th day of May, A.D., 1976, and amended

by Order in Council 88-406 dated the 20th day of April, A.D., 1988, by

terminating the status of 27,000 square feet, more or less, of Sand Hills Beach

Provincial Park shown shaded in yellow on the plan marked Schedule “A”

attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation;

(b) approve Petition No. 33419 for the conveyance of a lot of Crown land

containing 55,500 square feet, more or less, situate at or near Villagedale, in

the County of Shelburne, as shown outlined in green on the plan marked

Schedule “B” attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation

to David Wilson and E. Lois Wilson, his wife, as joint tenants and not as

tenants in common, of Brass Hill, in the County of Shelburne, and to order that

a quit claim deed be executed by the Minister of Lands and Forests on behalf

of Her Majesty the Queen in right of the Province of Nova Scotia, to convey

the said lot of land containing 55,500 square feet, more or less, subject to rights

of way to adjacent cottage lot owners, to David Wilson and E. Lois Wilson, at

a cost of Sixteen Thousand Six Hundred and Fifty Dollars ($16,650.00), based

on the appraised value of $0.30 per square foot, with the exact cost to be

determined by a legal survey, plus all costs as defined by the Minister of Lands

and Forests;

(c) approve Petition No. 33430 for the conveyance of a lot of Crown land

containing 47,200 square feet, more or less, situate at or near Villagedale, in

the County of Shelburne, as shown outlined in red on the plan marked

Schedule “B” attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation

to Patsy Ford of Yarmouth, in the County of Yarmouth, and to order that a quit

claim deed be executed by the Minister of Lands and Forests on behalf of Her

Majesty the Queen in right of the Province of Nova Scotia, to convey the said

lot of land containing 47,200 square feet, more or less, subject to rights of way

to adjacent cottage lot owners, to Patsy Ford at a cost of Fourteen Thousand

One Hundred and Sixty Dollars ($14,160.00), based on the appraised value of

$0.30 per square foot, with the exact cost to be determined by a legal survey,

plus all costs as defined by the Minister of Lands and Forests;

(d) authorize the Minister of Lands and Forests to execute such other documents

as may be necessary to achieve the purposes of this order.
