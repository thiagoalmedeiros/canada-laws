### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Fatality Investigations Regulations .

2 In these regulations,

“Nova Scotia Medical Examiner Service” means the organization through which

the Chief Medical Examiner coordinates the services provided for in the Act and

any other enactments under which a role is assigned to the Chief Medical

Examiner or the delegates of the Chief Medical Examiner.
