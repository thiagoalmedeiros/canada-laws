NS Reg 85/98

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated October 26, 1998, pursuant to subsection 5(1) of Chapter 32 of the Revised

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Beaches Act , and to clause 19(f) of Chapter 235 of the said

Revised Statutes, the Interpretation Act , is pleased to:

(a) rescind Order in Council 93-679, dated August 26, 1993, with respect to the

designation of a beach; and

(b) determine as a beach an area of land lying landward of the mean high

watermark at Johnstons Pond, Little Port l’Hebert, Shelburne County, as

described in Schedules “A” and “B”, and contained within the area shown

outlined in bold line and marked on Schedule “C”, all such Schedules attached

to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain lot, piece, parcel or tract of land situate, lying and being at Johnstons Pond in

the County of Shelburne, Province of Nova Scotia, and comprising Parcel “A” and Parcel “C”

on a Plan of Survey having C.L.R. No. E-15-41B (Field Plot No. P-035/93B) signed on the

12th day of March 1993 and revised June 4, 1996 by Gerald A. Pottier, Nova Scotia Land

Surveyor, and filed in the Department of Natural Resources office, Halifax, Nova Scotia; the

said parcel of land being more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at a Survey Marker Numbered S848 situated on the easterly edge of an abandoned

old former highway leading from East Sable River to Port L’Hebert across the beach, and being

(By Grid Bearing referenced to the Nova Scotia 3° Modified Transverse Mercator Projection,

Zone 5, Central Meridian 64° 30' West Longitude) N 04° 58' 24" W, a distance of 1,439.157

metres from Nova Scotia Coordinate Monument No. 24759 having coordinates of north 4, 847, 044.498 metres and east 5, 463, 921.896 metres;

Thence across the abandoned old former highway, previously mentioned, and land of Robert

Freeman N 62° 50' 50" W, a distance of 36.987 metres to a Survey Marker Numbered S849;

Thence across a portion of the waters of Johnstons Pond, lands of Marilyn Scher N 55° 48' 54"

W, a distance of 152.489 metres to a Survey Marker Numbered S850;

Thence across lands of Marilyn Scher, waters of Johnstons Pond and lands of Lew-Mel

Holding Corporation N 83° 09' 32" W, a distance of 426.855 metres to a Survey Marker

Numbered S851;

Thence across lands of Lew-Mel Holding Corporation, waters of Johnstons Pond, and again

lands of Lew-Mel Holding Corporation N 43° 38' 22" E, a distance of 313.808 metres to a

Survey Marker Numbered S852;

Thence across lands of Lew-Mel Holding Corporation and waters of Johnstons Pond N 43° 38'

22" E, a distance of 344.161 metres to an unmonumented point in the water;

Thence across waters of Johnstons Pond; and lands of Marilyn Scher S 86° 32' 28" E, a

distance of 195.078 metres to a Survey Marker Numbered S853;

Thence across lands of Marilyn Scher S 86° 32' 28" E, a distance of 100.255 metres to a Survey

Marker Numbered S880;

Thence across lands of Marilyn Scher S 45° 03' 16" E, a distance of 89.188 metres to a Survey

Marker Numbered S854;

Thence across lands of Marilyn Scher N 56° 55' 34" E, a distance of 87.261 metres to a Survey

Marker Numbered S979;

Thence across lands of Marilyn Scher S 72° 37' 46" E, a distance of 16.850 metres to a Survey

Marker Numbered S980 situated on the northwestern boundary of the East Sable Road, said

Survey Marker being S 61° 01' 20" W, a distance of 3077.297 metres from Nova Scotia

Coordinate Monument Numbered 24755 having coordinates of North 4, 850, 560.435 metres

and East 5, 466, 807.504 metres;

Thence across the East Sable Road S 72° 37' 46" E a distance of 20.282 metres to a Survey

Marker Numbered S981 situated on the southeastern boundary of the East Sable Road;

Thence across lands of Marilyn Scher S 72° 37' 46" E a distance of 63.093 metres to a Survey

Marker Numbered S982;

Thence across lands of Marilyn Scher S 41° 16' 00" W a distance of 62.939 metres to a Survey

Marker Numbered S983;

Thence across lands of Marilyn Scher; waters of the Atlantic Ocean and other lands of Marilyn

Scher S 34° 28' 11" W a distance of 629.700 metres to a Survey Marker Numbered S848 and

the place of beginning :

Saving and excepting therefrom any land lying below the mean high watermark.

Containing an area of 31.8 hectares.
