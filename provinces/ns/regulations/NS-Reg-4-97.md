# Lake George Provincial Park

NS Reg 4/97

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated December 3, 1996, pursuant to Section 8 of Chapter 367 of the Revised

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to

(a) designate all that area of land situate at Lake George, Kings County, owned by

Her Majesty the Queen in right of the Province of Nova Scotia, to be a

Provincial Park, the said area of land being described in Schedule “A” and

shown outlined in bold line on a copy of Provincial Crown Lands Record

Centre Plan No. E-15-53 marked Schedule “B”, both Schedules attached to

and forming part of the report and recommendation; and

(b) declare that the Park shall be known by the name of “Lake George Provincial

Park”.

## Schedule “A”

All those certain lots, pieces, or parcels of land situate, lying, and being at Lake George in the

County of Kings, Province of Nova Scotia, shown as Parcel A and B on a compiled plan, Lake

George Provincial Park (Field Plot P-067/96-2) and filed in the Provincial Crown Lands

Records Centre, Halifax, under C.L.R. No. E-15-53, the said parcels being more particularly

described as follows:
