NS Reg 62/77

*Made under subsection 302(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Registration Fees for Campers Regulations .

### Section 1 added: O.I.C. 2003-263, N.S. Reg. 117/2003.

### Definition of “camper”

1A In these regulations “camper” means a motor vehicle designed or reconstructed, equipped

and used or intended to be used primarily for sleeping, eating and living quarters, and

includes a motorized home or a bus converted for such purposes.

### Original Section 1 renumbered 1A and heading added: O.I.C. 2003-263, N.S. Reg. 117/2003.

### Classification and annual registration fees

2 There is a class of vehicle designated and known as a camper.

3 (1) In this Section,

(a) “Class 1 Camper” means a camper having a weight of up to and including

1500 kilograms;

(b) “Class 2 Camper” means a camper having a weight of between 1501 and 3200

kilograms, inclusive;

(c) “Class 3 Camper” means a camper having a weight of between 3201 and 4100

kilograms, inclusive;

(d) “Class 4 Camper” means a camper having a weight of between 4101 and 5000

kilograms, inclusive;

(e) “Class 5 Camper” means a camper having a weight of 5001 kg or more.

### Clause 3(1)(e) amended: O.I.C. 2003-263, N.S. Reg. 117/2003.

(2) Class 1, 2, 3 and 4 Campers shall be registered under the staggered system of registration.

(3) The registration fees for campers are as follows:

| Class 1 Camper | 0–1500 kg | $88.45 per year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Class 2 Camper | 1501–3200 kg | $110.85 per year |
| Class 3 Camper | 3201–4100 kg | $138.25 per year |
| Class 4 Camper | 4101–5000 kg | $166.95 per year |

### Subsection 3(3) replaced: O.I.C. 2015-96, N.S. Reg. 132/2015.

(4) The registration fee for a Class 5 Camper shall be the amount payable pursuant to the Registration Fees for Commercial Motor Vehicles Regulations under the Motor

Vehicle Act for a commercial motor vehicle of the same weight.

### Section 3 replaced: O.I.C. 92-17, N.S. Reg. 6/92; amended: O.I.C. 2003-263, N.S. Reg. 117/2003.

### Section 2 and 3 heading added: O.I.C. 2003-263, N.S. Reg. 117/2003.

### Partial-year registration of Class 5 Camper

4 (1) A Class 5 Camper may be registered for a partial year upon payment to the

Department of

(a) a fee of 1/12 of the annual registration fee multiplied by the number of months

for which the camper is to be registered; and

(b) a short-term registration administration fee in the amount prescribed in the

regulations respecting documents and services fees under the Motor Vehicle

Act .

(2) Partial-year registration of a Class 5 Camper pursuant to subsection (1) must be for a

period of not less than 3 months and not more than 12 months.

### Section 4 added: O.I.C. 2003-263, N.S. Reg. 117/2003.
