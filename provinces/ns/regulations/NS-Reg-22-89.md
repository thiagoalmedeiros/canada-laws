NS Reg 22/89

*Made under Section 12 of the Industrial Loan Act*

1 For purposes of clause (c) of Section 2 and pursuant to subsection (1) of Section 12 of Chapter 224 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Industrial Loan Act, every industry, business, trade or undertaking in the Province is hereby designated as an industry to which the Act shall apply.
