NS Reg 171/2026

*Made under Section 23 of the Housing in the Halifax Regional Municipality Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Order Criteria and Factors Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

“Act” means the Housing in the Halifax Regional Municipality Act ;

“Board” means the Nova Scotia Regulatory and Appeals Board;

“order” means an order that the Minister may issue to the Municipality or a utility

owned by the Municipality under Section 21A of the Act.

### Criteria and factors for issuing orders

3 Before issuing an order, the Minister must consider all of the following:

(a) relevant and available data and information respecting water resources and

wastewater and stormwater capacity;

(b) public health and safety;

(c) existing municipal and utility capital planning, and the condition of assets

and infrastructure;

(d) the potential impact to provincial, municipal and utility budgets, finances,

and human resources;

(e) the potential impact to rates, fees and charges;

(f) any existing or potential funding sources or funding agreements;

(g) any potential compliance issues with respect to federal or provincial

legislation or Board orders and decisions;

(h) any relevant information provided by the Municipality or the utility owned

by the Municipality;

(i) whether discussions with the Municipality and the utility owned by the

Municipality to identify mutually beneficial solutions have occurred.
