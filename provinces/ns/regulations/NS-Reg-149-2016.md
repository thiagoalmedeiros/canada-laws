NS Reg 149/2016

*Made under subsection 49A(14A) of the Income Tax Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Maximum Amount of Capital Investment Tax

Credits for Approved Projects Regulations .

### Maximum amount of capital investment tax credits for approved project

2 The amount that may not be exceeded under subsection 49A(7A) of the Act, as the

aggregate amount of tax credits claimed by a corporation in respect of an approved

project, is $100 million.
