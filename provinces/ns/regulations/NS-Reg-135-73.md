NS Reg 135/73

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Acting Minister of Lands

and Forests dated the 7th day of June, A.D., 1973, pursuant to Section 2 of Chapter 244 of the

Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to

(a) constitute all that area of land including land covered with water, owned by Her

Majesty in the right of the Province of Nova Scotia and situate at or near Risser’s

Beach in the registration district of the district of Lunenburg, County of Lunenburg,

Province of Nova Scotia, to be a Provincial Park, said area of land being more

particularly described in Schedule “A” and shown outlined on the Plan of Survey

marked Schedule “B”, both Schedules “A” and “B” being attached to and forming

part of the report and recommendation;

(b) declare the name by which the Park shall be known as “Rissers Beach Provincial

Park”.

### Clause (b) amended: O.I.C. 89-960, N.S. Reg. 166/89.
