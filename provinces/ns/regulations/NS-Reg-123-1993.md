NS Reg 123/1993

*Made under Section 10 of the Mineral Resources Act*

### Order in Council 93-741

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated the 15th day of July, 1993, pursuant to clauses (a) and (b) of subsection (1) of

Section 8A of Chapter 18 of the Acts of Nova Scotia 1990, the Mineral Resources Act , is

pleased to

(a) designate as a geothermal resource area that area of Pictou County, Nova

Scotia, more particularly described in Schedules “A” and “B” attached to

and forming part of the report and recommendation, and which area shall be

referred to as the Pictou County Geothermal Resource Area;

(b) determine that the designation of the Pictou County Geothermal Resource

Area shall be effective on, from and after the 3rd day of September, 1993;

(c) direct that a copy of this Order be published in the Royal Gazette and two

other local newspapers which are circulated in the Pictou County area of

Nova Scotia; and

(d) determine that the Mineral Resources Act shall apply to the Pictou County

Geothermal Resource Area, saving, excepting those portions of the Mineral

Resources Act , more particularly described in Schedule “C” attached to and

forming part of the said report and recommendation.
