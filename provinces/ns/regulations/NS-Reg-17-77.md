NS Reg 17/77

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 7th day of January, A.D., 1977, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased

to designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high water mark of Beatty

Marsh Beach, situate at Malagash Point, in the County of Cumberland, Province of Nova

Scotia, as shown outlined in red on the copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-10-71,

marked Schedule “B” and described in Schedule “A”, both attached to and forming part of the

report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain area of land lying landward from the mean high water mark of Beatty Marsh

Beach, situate at Malagash Point, in the County of Cumberland, Province of Nova Scotia as

shown outlined in red on the copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-10-71, attached

hereto and being more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at iron survey post # 1 as shown on said Plan No. E-10-71 the said post being situate

nineteen feet more or less from the mean high water line of the waters of Northumberland Strait

and having coordinates (N 16, 645, 195.45, E 1, 299, 416.17) based on the Nova Scotia Three

Degree Transverse Mercator Projection, Zone 5, Central Meridian sixty-four degrees thirty

minutes west longitude;

From thence (by grid bearings) south twenty-three degrees fifty-nine minutes nineteen seconds

east thirty-one feet to a point;

Thence as shown on said plan following a line which runs parallel to and fifty feet inland from

the mean highwater line of the waters of Northumberland Strait or the edge of a Marshland,

so-called, easterly, southerly, northerly, easterly and northerly to iron survey post # 4 as shown

on said plan, said post # 4 having coordinates (N 16, 648, 085.53, E 1, 304, 767.28);

Thence north forty degrees forty-nine minutes ten seconds west two hundred eighteen decimal

seventeen feet to iron survey post # 3 having coordinates (N 16, 648, 250.64, E 1, 304, 624.67)

and being situated near the mean highwater line of the waters of Northumberland Strait;

Thence as shown on said plan north forty degrees forty-nine minutes ten seconds west five

hundred feet to a point “A” being a calculated point situated in the waters of Northumberland

Strait and having coordinates (N 16, 648, 629.03, E 1, 304, 297.83);

Thence as shown on said plan south fifty-nine degrees thirty-nine minutes eighteen seconds

west five thousand eight hundred ninety-two decimal nineteen feet to point “B” being a

calculated point in the waters of Northumberland Strait and having coordinates (N 16, 645, 652.26, E 1, 299, 212.89);

Thence as shown on said plan south twenty-three degrees fifty-nine minutes nineteen seconds

east five hundred feet to the place of beginning.

Excepting and reserving from the above described area of land all the portion thereof lying

seaward from the mean high watermark.

[Note: the map referred to as Schedule “B” was not filed with the Office of the Registrar of Regulations.]
