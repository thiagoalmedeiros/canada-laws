NS Reg 190/2009

*Made under subsection 2(1A) of the Costs and Fees Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Fee Waivers for Victims Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

(a) “Act” means the Costs and Fees Act ;

(b) “restitution order” means an order made under section 732.1, section 738,

section 739 or section 742.3 of the Criminal Code (Canada) ordering an

amount to be paid as restitution to a person;

(c) “victim” means victim as defined in the Victims’ Rights and Services Act .

### Fee to be waived

3 A person responsible for collecting a fee under the Act must waive the fee payable by a

victim for any of the following:

(a) filing a restitution order;

(b) entering as a judgment the amount ordered to be paid under a restitution order;

(c) enforcing a restitution order.
