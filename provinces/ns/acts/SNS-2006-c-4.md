SNS 2006, c 4

Police and Peace Officers'

Memorial Day Act

An Act Respecting a Memorial Day

to Honour Police and Peace Officers

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Police and Peace Officers' Memorial Day Act*.

### Police and Peace Officers' Memorial Day

2 Throughout the Province, in each and every year, the third Sunday in October shall be kept and observed under the name of Police and Peace Officers' Memorial Day.

### Proclamation

3 This Act comes into force on such day as the Governor in Council orders and declares by proclamation.
