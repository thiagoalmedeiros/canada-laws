SNS 2017, c 3

Pregnancy and Infant Loss

Awareness Act

An Act Respecting Pregnancy

and Infant Loss Awareness

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Pregnancy and Infant Loss Awareness Act*.

### Pregnancy and Infant Loss Awareness Day

2 Throughout the Province, in each and every year, October 15th shall be kept and observed under the name of Pregnancy and Infant Loss Awareness Day.
