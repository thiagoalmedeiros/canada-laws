SNS 2005, c 1

Alexander Graham Bell Day Act

An Act to Establish

a Day to Recognize

Alexander Graham Bell

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Alexander Graham Bell Day Act*.

### Alexander Graham Bell Day

2 Throughout the Province, in each and every year, March 7th shall be kept and observed under the name of Alexander Graham Bell Day.
