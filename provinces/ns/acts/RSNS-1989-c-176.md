RSNS 1989, c 176

Floral Emblem Act

An Act Respecting the

Floral Emblem of Nova Scotia

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Floral Emblem Act*.

### Floral emblem

2 The trailing Arbutus (Epigaea repens Linn), commonly known as the Mayflower, is declared to be, and from time immemorial to have been, the floral emblem of the Province.
