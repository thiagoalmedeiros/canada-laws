RSNS 1989, c 20

Arbor Day Act

An Act to Provide for

the Designation of Arbor Day

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Arbor Day Act*.

### Designation of Arbor Day

2 The Governor in Council may designate one day each year to be kept and observed throughout the Province under the name of Arbor Day.
