SNS 2026, c 13

Down Syndrome Day Act

An Act to Establish Down Syndrome Day

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Down Syndrome Day Act*.

### Down Syndrome Day

2 Throughout the Province, in each and every year, March 21st shall be kept and observed under the name of Down Syndrome Day.
