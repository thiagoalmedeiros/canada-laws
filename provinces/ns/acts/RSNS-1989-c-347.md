RSNS 1989, c 347

Pledging of Service Emblems Act

An Act Respecting

Pledging of Certain Articles

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Pledging of Service Emblems Act*.

### Prohibited activity

2 No person shall take in pawn from any other person any naval or military medal, badge, decoration or order.

### Penalty

3 Any person violating this Act shall be liable to a penalty of not more than fifty dollars nor less than twenty dollars.
