SNS 2026, c 16, Sch B

Peggy's Cove Commission Dissolution Act

An Act to Dissolve

the Peggy's Cove Commission

NOTE - *Some provisions of this Act are not proclaimed in force. See note following Section 11.*

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Peggy's Cove Commission Dissolution Act*.

### Interpretation

2 In this Act,

"Commission" means the Peggy's Cove Commission established by the *Peggy's Cove Commission Act*;

"Crown" means the Crown in right of the Province;

"Minister" means the Minister of Growth, Development and Defence Industries.

### Commission dissolved

3 The Commission is dissolved.

### Appointments revoked

4 (1) All appointments of persons as members of the Commission are revoked.

(2) All contracts, agreements or orders relating to the remuneration, allowances or expenses to be paid to the members of the Commission are void.

### By-laws repealed

5 (1) All by-laws made by the Commission are repealed.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), a permit or other instrument issued under the by-laws made by the Commission continues in force and effect and is deemed to have been issued under the *Halifax Regional Municipality Charter* and the Charter applies, with necessary changes, to such permit or instrument.

### Members indemnified

6 The Crown shall indemnify and hold harmless a person who was a member of the Commission immediately before the dissolution of the Commission, and the heirs, executors, estate and effects of the person, with respect to all costs, charges and expenses that the person incurs in relation to any action, application or other proceeding brought or prosecuted against the person in connection with the duties of the person as a member of the Commission, except costs, charges and expenses that are occasioned by the person's own wilful neglect or wilful default.

### No action lies

7 No action, application or other proceeding lies or may be instituted against the Commission, the Minister or the Crown before any court or administrative body in the Province as a result of

(a) the dissolution of the Commission; or

(b) the revocation of the appointments of the members of the Commission.
