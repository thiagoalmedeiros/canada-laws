RSNS 1989, c 422

Senior Citizens' Week Act

An Act Respecting

Senior Citizens' Week

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Senior Citizens' Week Act*.

### Senior Citizens Week

2 The Governor in Council shall designate, for each year, a week to be kept and observed throughout the Province under the name of Senior Citizens' Week.
