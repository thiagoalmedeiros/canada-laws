SNS 2006, c 20

Provincial Fish Act

An Act to Declare

the Brook Trout to be the

Provincial Fish of Nova Scotia

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Provincial Fish Act*.

### Provincial Fish

2 The brook trout (*Salvelinus fontinalis*) is declared to be the Provincial Fish of the Province.

### Proclamation

3 This Act comes into force on such day as the Governor in Council orders and declares by proclamation.
