# STOPPING OF VEHICLES ON PARTS OF THE KING’S HIGHWAY

R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 622

1 (1) No person shall stop a vehicle on a part of the King’s Highway described in a Schedule to Appendix A.

(2) No person shall stop a vehicle on a part of the King’s Highway described in Column 1 of a Schedule to Appendix B between the limits set out in Column 2 during the period set out in Column 3 for a longer period of time than that set out in Column 4.

2 Where a highway is referred to in a Schedule by number or name, the reference is to that part of the King’s Highway known by it.
