O. Reg. 549/21

### Application

1 This Order applies to the lands in the Town of Oakville, in the Regional Municipality of Halton, in the Province of Ontario, described as part of Lots 3, 4 and 5, Concession 3 (Trafalgar), South of Dundas Street, designated as Part 1 on Reference Plan 20R-10010, and further identified by Property Identification Number 24792-0047 (LT) registered in the Land Registry Office for the Land Titles Division of Halton (No. 20).

### Zoning requirements

2 Despite the requirements set out in the Town of Oakville Zoning By-law No. 2014-014, the zoning requirements for the lands described in section 1 are as follows:

1. The maximum gross floor area of the existing building is 29,200 square metres.

2. The maximum number of parking spaces is 689 spaces.

### Deemed by-law

3 This Order is deemed for all purposes, except the purposes of section 24 of the Act, to be and to always have been a by-law passed by the council of the Town of Oakville.

4 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
