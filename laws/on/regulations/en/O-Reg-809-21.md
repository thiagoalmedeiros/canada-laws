O. Reg. 809/21

### Designated broadband projects

1 Every broadband project where funding, in full or in part, has been provided through the Ministry of Infrastructure for the purposes of deploying broadband and high-speed internet infrastructure in Ontario is a designated broadband project for the purposes of the Act.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
