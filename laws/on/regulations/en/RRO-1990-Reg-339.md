R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 339

1 In this Regulation,

“highway” has the same meaning as in the Highway Traffic Act;

“road authority” means any person or persons having jurisdiction and control over a highway.

2 Where any substance used on a highway by the Crown as represented by the Minister of Transportation or any road authority or any agent or employee of any of them for the purpose of keeping the highway safe for traffic under conditions of snow or ice or both is a contaminant, it is classified and is exempt from the Act and the regulations.
