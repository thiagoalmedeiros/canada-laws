O. Reg. 454/09

### Prescribed temporary indexing factor

1 (1) For the purposes of subsections 51 (5) and 52 (1.3) of the Act, a temporary indexing factor of 0.5 per cent shall be applied for 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015 and 2016.

(2) For the purposes of subsections 51 (5) and 52 (1.3) of the Act, a temporary indexing factor of 1 per cent shall be applied for 2017.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
