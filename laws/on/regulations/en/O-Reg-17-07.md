O. Reg. 17/07

### Prohibition

1 No person shall transfer a container to Brewers Retail Inc., the Liquor Control Board of Ontario or an agent of Brewers Retail Inc. or the Liquor Control Board of Ontario for the purpose of receiving or attempting to receive a payment as a refund of a deposit unless a deposit on the container was collected by an operator of a retail store within the meaning of the Liquor Licence and Control Act, 2019.
