O. Reg. 626/00

### Jurisdiction

1 (1) The maximum amount of a claim in the Small Claims Court is $50,000.

(2) The maximum amount of a claim over which a deputy judge may preside is $50,000.

### Appeal limit

2 (1) For the purposes of clause 31 (a) of the Act, the prescribed amount is $5,000.

(2) For the purposes of clause 31 (b) of the Act, the prescribed amount is $5,000.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
