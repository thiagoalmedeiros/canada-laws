O. Reg. 384/25

### Payment of holdback

1 (1) In this section,

“transition day” means the day section 26 of Schedule 4 to the Building Ontario For You Act (Budget Measures), 2024 comes into force.

(2) If, before the transition day, the payer was permitted to make payment of a holdback on an annual basis or on a phased basis under section 26.1 or 26.2 of the Act, as it read before its repeal, that section continues to apply with respect to the payment of the holdback until the day specified in paragraph 1 of subsection 87.4 (4) of the Act.

(3) Subsection (2) applies with necessary modifications with respect to the project agreements prescribed under section 2.

(4) For the purposes of subsections 87.4 (4) and (5) of the Act, section 26 of the Act, as it read before the transition day, applies without reference to section 27.1 of the Act.

### Prescribed project agreements

2 The project agreements listed in Schedule 1 are prescribed for the purposes of subsection 87.4 (5) of the Act.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
