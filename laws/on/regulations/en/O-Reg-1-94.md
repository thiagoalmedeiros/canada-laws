O. Reg. 1/94

1 The delegation of authority under section 51 of the Act set out in Ontario Regulation 5 1 6/89 is hereby withdrawn in respect of all applications for approval regarding land described in section 2.

2 This Order applies to the land in the City of Timmins in the District of Cochrane being part of Lot 5 in Concession 11 designated as lots 6, 7 and 8 on Plan M-197-C registered in the Land Registry Office for the Land Titles Division of Cochrane (No. 6). O. Reg. 1 /94, s. 2.
