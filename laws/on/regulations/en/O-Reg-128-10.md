O. Reg. 128/10

### Dissolution of “Art Gallery of Ontario Crown Foundation”

1 (1) The crown foundation established by order of the Lieutenant Governor in Council dated December 4, 1996 under section 2 of the Act and known as the “Art Gallery of Ontario Crown Foundation” is hereby dissolved.

(2) Upon the dissolution of the “Art Gallery of Ontario Crown Foundation” under subsection (1), the assets of the foundation become the assets of the Art Gallery of Ontario.

### Dissolution of “Ontario Foundation for the Arts”
