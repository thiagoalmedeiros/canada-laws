### Wild rice harvesting areas

1 (1) Ontario is divided into the wild rice harvesting areas shown on the plans filed in the office of the Surveyor General under the numbers in Column 1 of the following Table on the dates shown opposite in Column 2 of the Table.

(2) Each wild rice harvesting area shown on the plans listed in Column 1 of the following Table is situate in whole or in part in the geographic area, as described in Ontario Regulation 180/03 (Division of Ontario into Geographic Areas) made under the Territorial Division Act, 2002, listed opposite in Column 3 of the Table.

### TABLE wild rice harvesting areas

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan number | Date of Plan | General Location |
| 294 | March 30, 2009 | Kenora |
| 295 | March 30, 2009 | Rainy River |
| 296 | March 30, 2009 | Thunder Bay |
| 297 | March 30, 2009 | Kenora, Thunder Bay |

### Application for licence

2 (1) An application for a licence shall be in the form that the Deputy Minister approves.

(2) The fee for a licence is $1.

### Form of licence

3 (1) A licence shall be in the form that the Deputy Minister approves.

(2) A licence expires on October 31 next after the date of issue.

(3) A licence is not valid unless it is signed by the licensee.

### Transferability of licence

4 A licence shall not be transferred without the approval of the Deputy Minister.

5 Omitted (revokes other Regulations).

6 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
