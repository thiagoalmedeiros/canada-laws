O. Reg. 374/04

### Exemption

1 (1) No tax is payable under the Act on the acquisition of land by a municipality pursuant to a transfer order under section 2 of the Municipal Water and Sewage Transfer Act, 1997.

(2) In subsection (1),

“municipality” has the same meaning as in the Municipal Water and Sewage Transfer Act, 1997.

2 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
