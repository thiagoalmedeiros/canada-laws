# DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY OF MINISTER TO APPROVE CONDOMINIUM DESCRIPTIONS

O. Reg. 145/95

1 (1) All authority of the Minister to approve condominium descriptions under section 50 of the Condominium Act is hereby delegated to the council of each of the municipalities listed in Schedule 1 in respect of all of the land situate in the municipality.

(2) The delegation is subject to the following conditions:

1. Revoked: O. Reg. 434/96, s. 1 (1).

2. If any of the authority delegated to a council is in turn delegated by a council to a committee of council or an appointed officer under subsection 5 (1) of the Act, the council shall forward to the Minister a certified copy of the delegating by-law within 15 days of its passing.

(3) The delegation does not apply to an application made before March 28, 1995.

2 The delegation of authority set out in this Regulation is not terminated by reason only that a condition in subsection 1 (2) is not complied with.

3 Omitted (revokes other Regulations).

4 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).

## Schedule 1

1.-3. Revoked: O. Reg. 434/96, s. 2.

4. The Municipality of Metropolitan Toronto.

5. The Town of Orangeville.
