O. Reg. 426/11

### Application

1 An application under the Act shall be in the form that the Director specifies.

### Certificate of correction

2 A certificate of correction of an examiner that is required by subsection 12 (3) of Regulation 60 of the Revised Regulations of Ontario, 1990 (General) made under the Act shall be in the form that the Director specifies.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
