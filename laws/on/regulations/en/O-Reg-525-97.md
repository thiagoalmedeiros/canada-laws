O. Reg. 525/97

### Exemption from approval

1 (1) All amendments to the official plan of a municipality listed in the Schedule that were commenced on or after the corresponding date set out in the Schedule are exempt from the approval of the Minister.

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1), an official plan amendment is deemed to be commenced on the day that it is adopted by the council of the municipality.

(3) The exemption is subject to the following conditions:

1. The municipality shall provide a copy of the proposed official plan amendment to the Minister during consultation under clause 17 (15) (a) of the Act.

2. The municipality shall provide a copy of a sworn declaration made under subsection 17 (28) of the Act to the Minister within 15 days of it being sworn.

3. A copy of the record compiled under subsection 17 (29) or 22 (9) of the Act shall be forwarded to the Minister at the same time it is forwarded to the Tribunal.

4. A copy of the adopted plan shall be forwarded to the Minister at the same time that notice is given under clause 17 (23) (a) of the Act.

(4) The exemption is not terminated because a municipality does not meet a condition set out in subsection (3).

2 Revoked: O. Reg. 560/24, s. 2.

### Removal of exemption

3 (1) The exemption under subsection 1 (1) is removed in respect of proposed Amendment No. 668 to the Official Plan of the City of Toronto and proposed Amendment No. 680 to the Official Plan of the City of Toronto.

(2) The exemption under subsection 1 (1) is removed in respect of the proposed amendment to the Official Plan of the Town of Caledon that has the file number POPA 2026-0006.
