### Deemed zoning by-law — Town of Oakville

1 Sections 1, 14 to 17, 20, 23, 24, 28, 31, 32, 39 to 41, 43 to 47, 50 to 52, 55 to 57, 59, 60, 63, 66, 69, 71, 73 to 75, 80 to 82 and 85 to 91 and Schedules 1 to 3, 6, 9, 10, 16, 17, 20, 21, 28 to 30, 32 to 35, 39 to 41, 44 to 46, 48, 49, 52, 55, 58 and 62 to 64, as they read immediately before the day section 1 of Ontario Regulation 214/26 made under the Act came into force, are deemed to be and to always have been a by-law passed by the council of the Town of Oakville.

### Deemed zoning by-law — Town of Halton Hills

3 Sections 1 and 86, as they read immediately before the day section 1 of Ontario Regulation 214/26 made under the Act came into force, are deemed to be and to always have been a by-law passed by the council of the Town of Halton Hills.

### Deemed zoning by-law — City of Mississauga

4 Sections 1 and 86, as they read immediately before the day section 1 of Ontario Regulation 214/26 made under the Act came into force, are deemed to be and to always have been a by-law passed by the council of the City of Mississauga.
