O. Reg. 308/98

1 Despite clauses 6 (2) (a) and (b) of the Act,

(a) the number of individual members of the Corporation elected to the Council by the members of the Corporation shall be nine; and

(b) the number of persons appointed to the Council by the Lieutenant Governor in Council shall be four.
