O. Reg. 273/24

### Prescribed revenue horizon

1 The number of years specified for the purposes of subsection 36.0.1 (2) of the Act is,

(a) 40, with respect to consumers who are forecasted to use less than 50,000 cubic metres of gas annually; and

(b) 20, with respect to any other consumers.

2 Omitted (provides for amendments to this Regulation).

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
