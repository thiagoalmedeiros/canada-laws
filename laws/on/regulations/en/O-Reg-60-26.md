O. Reg. 60/26

### Protected Species in Ontario List

1 (1) The species set out in Columns 3 and 4 of Table 1 according to their common and scientific names and belonging to the species grouping referred to in Column 2 opposite the listed species are classified by COSSARO in the manner set out in Column 5.

(2) The following geographic limitations apply in respect of the following classifications:

1. The classification set out in Table 1 in respect of the Kentucky Coffee-tree applies only in the following geographic areas:

i. The County of Elgin.

ii. The County of Essex.

iii. The County of Lambton.

iv. The County of Middlesex.

v. The County of Norfolk.

vi. The County of Oxford.

vii. The Municipality of Chatham-Kent.
