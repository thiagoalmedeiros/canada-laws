# FEES FOR CENTRAL BARGAINING FOR THE 2016-2017 SCHOOL BOARD FISCAL YEAR

O. Reg. 222/16

### Application

1 This Regulation sets out the employer bargaining agency fees to be paid by school authorities and district school boards for the 2016-2017 school board fiscal year for the activities of trustees’ associations under the Act.

### School authorities

2 (1) Every school authority shall pay a fee of $1,000 to the Ontario Public School Boards’ Association for the 2016-2017 school board fiscal year.

(2) Despite subsection (1), the Ottawa Children’s Treatment Centre School Authority is not required to pay fees for the 2016-2017 school board fiscal year.

### District school boards

3 Every district school board shall pay a fee to the applicable trustees’ association for the 2016-2017 school board fiscal year, as follows:

1. An English-language public district school board shall pay $43,316 to the Ontario Public School Boards’ Association.

2. An English-language separate district school board shall pay $43,017 to the Ontario Catholic School Trustees’ Association.

3. A French-language public district school board shall pay $233,628 to l’Association des conseils scolaires des écoles publiques de l’Ontario.

4. A French-language separate district school board shall pay $129,854 to l’Association franco-ontarienne des conseils scolaires catholiques.

### Consequences of non-payment

4 (1) Every school authority and district school board shall pay the fee required under section 2 or 3 on or before October 15, 2016.

(2) If a school authority or district school board does not pay the required fee on or before October 15, 2016, the affected trustees’ association shall notify every board it represents of this fact.

(3) If a school authority or district school board does not pay the required fee on or before November 15, 2016, the affected trustees’ association shall notify every board it represents, and the Crown, of this fact, and the school authority or district school board forfeits its entitlement to participate in a vote described in subsection 21 (4) of the Act starting on the day the notice is given.

(4) If a school authority or district school board pays all fees in respect of which its entitlement was forfeited under this regulation and under predecessors and successors of this section in regulations made under subsection 21 (10) of the Act, the trustees’ association shall notify every board it represents, and the Crown, of this fact within 14 days after receiving the payment, and the entitlement is reinstated on the earlier of,

(a) the day the trustees’ association gives the notice; and

(b) 14 days after the trustees’ association receives payment of the fees in full.

5 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
