### Deemed zoning by-law, O. Reg. 484/71

1 (1) Ontario Regulation 484/71, as it read immediately before this Regulation comes into force, shall be deemed to be and to always have been a by-law passed under section 34 of the Act by the North Almaguin Planning Board.

(2) The deemed by-law shall be referred to as by-law number 2011-01 of the North Almaguin Planning Board.
