O. Reg. 37/25

### Additional object of the Corporation

1 For the purposes of paragraph 10 of section 40 of the Act, the following is prescribed as an additional object of the Corporation:

1. To assist the Ministry in fulfilling Ontario’s obligations to report to the federal government in relation to trades and apprenticeship.

### Prescribed matter, collection and use of personal information

2 For the purposes of paragraph 4 of subsection 62 (2) of the Act, the following is prescribed as a matter for which the Corporation may collect and use personal information:

1. Assisting the Ministry in fulfilling Ontario’s obligations to report to the federal government in relation to trades and apprenticeship.

3 Omitted (provides for coming into force of provisions of this Regulation).
