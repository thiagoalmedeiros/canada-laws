R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 623

1 The intersections on the King’s Highway that are described in paragraph 1 of each Schedule are designated as intersections at which stop signs shall be erected to face traffic travelling in the direction referred to in paragraph 2 of each Schedule.

2 Where a highway is referred to in a Schedule by a number or name, the reference is to that part of the King’s Highway that is known thereby.

3 (1) Every intersection of a controlled-access King’s Highway exit ramp and a highway that is not otherwise controlled by a traffic light or yield sign is designated as an intersection at which a stop sign shall be erected.

(2) A stop sign at an intersection described in subsection (1) shall face the traffic on the exit ramp.
