O. Reg. 33/98

1 The prescribed amount for a taxation year for the purposes of subsection 320 (2) of the Act is 0.5 per cent times the assessment made under the Assessment Act in the year for the land used for the purposes of the bridge or tunnel.

2 Despite section 1, the maximum prescribed amount payable under that section with respect to a bridge or tunnel in a taxation year is $40,000.

3 The following bridges are prescribed for the purposes of subsection 320 (3) of the Act:

1. The Ambassador bridge having an Ontario portion located in the City of Windsor.

2. The Fort Frances-International Falls bridge having an Ontario portion located in the Town of Fort Frances.
