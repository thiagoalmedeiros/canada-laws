R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 973

1 Subject to any terms and conditions that are consistent with the Act and the regulations,

(a) a district manager or his or her delegate may issue, in respect of public lands in the administrative district administered by the manager, a land use permit permitting the holder to occupy the public lands described in the permit for the purposes stated in it;

(b) the Manager, Land Management Section, may issue, in respect of public lands situated in two or more administrative districts, a land use permit permitting the holder to occupy the public lands described in the permit for the purposes stated in it.

2 A land use permit shall be in the form furnished by the Ministry.
