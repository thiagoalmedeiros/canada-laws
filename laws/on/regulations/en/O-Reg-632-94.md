O. Reg. 632/94

### Public bodies

1 The following are public bodies for the purposes of the Act:

1. The Crown in right of Canada.

2. Export Development Canada, a corporation established by section 3 of the Export Development Act (Canada).

3. The Independent Electricity System Operator.

4. The Law Society of Ontario.

5. The Chief Executive Officer of the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario in the Chief Executive Officer’s capacity as administrator of the Pension Benefits Guarantee Fund.

6. The Workplace Safety and Insurance Board.

7. Ontario Power Generation.

2.-4 Revoked: O. Reg. 166/19, s. 1.
