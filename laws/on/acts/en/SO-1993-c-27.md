S.O. 1993, c. 27

###

1 The Acts in the Revised Statutes of Ontario, 1990, as printed by the Queen’s Printer, and the public and private Acts in Volume 2 of the Statutes of Ontario, 1991 shall have effect as law in the form in which they were printed.

### Schedule A, confirmed

2 The Acts mentioned in Schedule A to the Revised Statutes of Ontario, 1990, as printed by the Queen’s Printer, are repealed to the extent mentioned in the third column of that Schedule.

3-10 Repealed: 2018, c. 17, Sched. 8, s. 21.
