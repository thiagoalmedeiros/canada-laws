# Broadband is an Essential Service Act

RSPEI 1988, c B-5.01

## PART 1 - INTERPRETATION

### Definitions

1. In this Act

(a) “high-speed internet” means internet service with download speeds of 50 megabits

per second or faster and upload speeds of 10 megabits per second or faster;

(b) “Minister” means the Minister of Economic Development, Trade and Artificial

Intelligence or such other member of the Executive Council to whom the

administration of this Act may be assigned by the Executive Council;

(c) “strategy” means the Broadband Connectivity Strategy developed under section 3.

### Purpose

2. The purpose of this Act is to guide policies and investments towards a goal of universal

access to affordable and reliable high-speed internet, particularly for rural areas of the

province, given that high-speed internet constitutes an essential service.

## PART 2 – BROADBAND CONNECTIVITY STRATEGY

### Strategy

3.(1) The Minister shall develop a Broadband Connectivity Strategy that aims to deliver high-

speed internet connectivity to 95 per cent of residents of Prince Edward Island by June 30,

2023, and to all residents by 2025.

### Elements of strategy

(2) Without limiting the generality of subsection (1), the strategy shall

(a) identify the number of residents who lack access to high-speed internet and where,

generally, they are situated within the province;

(b) identify actions to be taken to achieve the strategy’s targets;

(c) describe the roles of any partners in implementing the strategy; and

(d) set out timelines for taking specified actions as well as performance measures against

which the progress of the strategy may be measured.

### Updated strategy

(3) The Minister shall ensure that the strategy is updated at least once every two years after its

initial development under subsection (1).

### Progress report

(4) Any updated strategy shall also include a progress report that summarizes the measures taken

since the publication of the previous version of the strategy and an evaluation of the progress

of the strategy against the performance measures set out in the previous version of the

strategy.

### Consultation

(5) In developing or updating the strategy, the Minister shall consult with representatives of the

federal government, municipal governments, Indigenous communities and the general public.

### Publication

(6) The Minister shall ensure that the strategy developed under subsection (1) and any updated

strategy are published on a website of the Government.

### Tabling

(7) The Minister shall table the strategy developed under subsection (1) and any updated strategy

before the Legislative Assembly as soon as reasonably possible after they are developed or

updated.
