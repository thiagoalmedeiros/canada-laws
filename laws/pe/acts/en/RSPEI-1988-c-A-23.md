# Auctioneers Act

RSPEI 1988, c A-23

### Definition

1. In this Act “non-resident” means a person who has not resided in Prince Edward Island for

at least one year immediately prior to the date of his application for a license under this Act.

### License to conduct auction sales

2. Every auctioneer shall, before he conducts any auction sale of real or personal property, or

carries on business as an auctioneer in Prince Edward Island, obtain from the Department of

Justice and Public Safety a license for that purpose, in such form as the Minister of Justice

and Public Safety and Attorney General may determine.

### Expiration of licenses

3. Every license issued under this Act expires two years after the issuing thereof.

### Offences and penalties

4. Every person who sells real or personal property by auction, without having first obtained a

license under this Act, or contrary to the provisions of the license, is liable to a fine of not less

than $100 and not more than $500.

### Record of persons licensed

5. There shall be kept in the Department of Justice and Public Safety a record of all persons

licensed under this Act.

### Regulations

6. The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations respecting licenses under this Act

and, without prejudice to the generality thereof, the regulations may

(a) provide for the refusal, suspension and cancellation of licenses;

(b) require applicants for licenses to produce evidence of qualifications and to be

familiar with the law of sale by auction and, in particular, the provisions of section 58

of the Sale of Goods Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. S-1;

(c) exempt persons from the requirement to obtain a license;

(d) prescribe fees for licenses.

## SCHEDULE

Repealed by 1994,c.48,s.2
