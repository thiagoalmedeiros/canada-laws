# Provincial Health Number Act

RSPEI 1988, c P-27.01

### Definitions

1. In this Act

(a) “health and community service” means a service, thing, subsidy or other benefit

that is funded in whole or in part, directly or indirectly, by the province and that is

related to health, social welfare, income security, vital statistics or such other service

as may be prescribed;

(b) “health card” means a health card provided to a person who has a health number;

(c) “health number” means the number assigned under the authority of the Minister or

the Minister of Social Development and Seniors to a person entitled to receive health

and community services that uniquely identifies that person for the purpose of

receiving such services;

(d) “Minister” means the Minister of Health and Wellness.

### Confidentiality of health numbers and cards

2.(1) Except as authorized by this section, no person shall

(a) require the production of another person’s health card;

(b) collect or use another person’s health number.

### Exception, health and community services

(2) A person may require the production of another person’s health card and collect or use

another person’s health number for purposes related to the provision and administration, and

such planning and research as may be authorized by the Minister, of health and community

services.

### Exception, prescribed persons

(3) A person prescribed in the regulations may collect or use health numbers for purposes related

to the administration or planning of health and community services or for research or

epidemiological studies.

### Exception, professional governing bodies

(4) The statutory regulatory body which governs a profession whose members provide health and

community services may collect or use health numbers for purposes related to its functions.

### Offence

3. Every person who contravenes subsection 2(1) is guilty of an offence and is liable on

summary conviction

(a) in the case of an individual, to a fine of not more than $5,000 or to imprisonment for

a term not exceeding six months;

(b) in the case of a corporation, to a fine of not more than $25,000.

### Regulations

4. The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations.
