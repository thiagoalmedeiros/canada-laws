RSPEI 1988, c E-12

### Composition of Executive Council

1. There shall be an Executive Council of the province consisting of the Premier, who may be

President of the Executive Council, and not less than seven or more than eleven other persons

appointed by the Lieutenant Governor on the advice of the Premier.

### Appointment of Executive Council

2. Members of the Executive Council shall be appointed by the Lieutenant Governor under the

Great Seal of the province and shall hold office during pleasure.

### Functions of Executive Council

3. The Executive Council is responsible for the executive government of the province and the

several departments thereof as set out in the Public Departments Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. P-

29.

### Remuneration

4. Each member of the Executive Council shall be paid remuneration determined in accordance

with section 52 or 53 of the Legislative Assembly Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. L-7.1.

### Clerk of Executive Council

5.(1) The Lieutenant Governor in Council shall appoint a Clerk of the Executive Council who shall

be of deputy minister rank and shall hold office during pleasure.

### Duties

(2) The duties of the Clerk of the Executive Council are to provide administrative services to,

and preserve all documents and records of, the Executive Council.

### Clerk Assistant

(3) The Lieutenant Governor in Council may appoint a Clerk Assistant of the Executive Council

to perform the duties of the Clerk in his absence.

### Staff

6. There may be appointed pursuant to the Civil Service Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-8 such staff

as may be required for the proper discharge of the functions of the Executive Council.

### Great Seal

7.(1) There shall continue to be a Great Seal of the province.

### Variation

(2) The Lieutenant Governor in Council may vary or change the Great Seal of the province.

### Custody

(3) The Clerk of the Executive Council shall be the custodian of the Great Seal of the province

and shall cause it to be affixed to documents as required by law or as instructed by the

President of the Executive Council.

### Treasury Board

8.(1) There shall be a committee of the Executive Council called the Treasury Board which shall

advise on budget preparation, financial analysis, government organization, operating policy

and procedures, financial administration, collective bargaining, staff relations, human

resource development, and classification.

### Composition

(2) The Board shall consist of the Minister of Finance and Affordability and not more than four

other members of the Executive Council appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in Council.

### Idem

(2.1) Repealed by 2020,c.89,s.60(3).

### Chairperson

(3) The Minister of Finance and Affordability shall be the chairperson of the Board.

### Secretary

(4) Repealed by 2012,c.15,s.17.

### Procedure

(5) The Board may determine its own procedure.

### Establish committees

9. In addition to the Treasury Board, the Executive Council may establish other committees of

the Executive Council that it considers necessary for the discharge of its functions.

### Remuneration

9.1. Repealed by 2020,c.89,s.60(5).

### Agreements with other authorities

10. The Lieutenant Governor in Council may authorize a Minister of the Crown, for and on

behalf of the government, or an agency of the government, to enter into an agreement with

(a) the Government of Canada, or a minister or agency of the Government of Canada;

(b) the government of another province of Canada, or a minister or agency of the

government of another province of Canada;

(c) a municipality, education authority or other local authority; or

(d) any person or group of persons,

for the benefit or purposes of the residents of the province or any part thereof.
