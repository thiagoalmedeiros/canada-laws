RSPEI 1988, c C-34

### Preamble

WHEREAS cyberbullying consists of electronic communication that, directly or indirectly, causes or

is likely to cause harm to another individual’s physical or mental health and wellbeing;

AND WHEREAS cyberbullying can include intimidation, threats and harassment and can have

significant and lifelong negative effects on children and adults;

AND WHEREAS Prince Edward Island has seen an increase in cases of cyberbullying;

AND WHEREAS Cyberbullying Awareness Day will increase awareness of the problem of

cyberbullying and help lead to its prevention;

AND WHEREAS the day will help children and adults to protect themselves from cyberbullying by

encouraging discussion in schools and workplaces;

THEREFORE BE IT ENACTED by the Lieutenant Governor and the Legislative Assembly of the

Province of Prince Edward Island as follows:

### Cyberbullying Awareness Day

1.(1) Throughout the Province of Prince Edward Island, April 25th each year shall be known as

“Cyberbullying Awareness Day”.

### Not a holiday

(2) For greater certainty, Cyberbullying Awareness Day is not a holiday under any enactment.
