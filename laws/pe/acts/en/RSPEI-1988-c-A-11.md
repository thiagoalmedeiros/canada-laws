# Ancient Burial Grounds Act

RSPEI 1988, c A-11

1. Burial grounds vested in Minister of Justice and Public Safety and Attorney General

All burial grounds within the province in which the freehold is not vested in a corporation or

one or more trustees for cemetery purposes and which are no longer actually used for burial

purposes, are declared to be vested in the Minister of Justice and Public Safety and Attorney

General of the province and the Minister’s successors in office as a corporation sole.

1974, Cap.A-9,s.1;

2. Legal action by the Minister of Justice and Public Safety and Attorney General

The Minister of Justice and Public Safety and Attorney General, for the purpose of protecting

the burial grounds, has the power at all times to take and bring in the Minister’s name as

Minister of Justice and Public Safety and Attorney General all proceedings, actions and suits

as may be necessary to protect the burial grounds from being trespassed upon or defaced or

damaged. R.S.P.E.I. 1974,Cap.A-9,s.2;

### Rights of ordinary plaintiff

3. In any suits or proceedings under this Act, the Minister of Justice and Public Safety and

Attorney General has the same rights as an ordinary plaintiff. R.S.P.E.I. 1974, Cap.A-9,s.3;

### Permit to visit

4. The Minister of Justice and Public Safety and Attorney General has the right to grant permits

to persons, in good faith, desirous of visiting or entering the burial grounds surrounded by

privately owned lands and the permit is sufficient answer to any claim or suit threatened or

brought against any persons using a permit. R.S.P.E.I. 1974, Cap.A-9,s.4;

### Idem

5. A permit may be granted under section 4 whether or not the cemetery is vested in a

corporation or in trustees. R.S.P.E.I. 1974, Cap. A-9, s.5.
