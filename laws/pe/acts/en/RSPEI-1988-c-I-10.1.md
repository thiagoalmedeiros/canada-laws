# Island Investment Development Act

RSPEI 1988, c I-10.1

### Definitions

1. In this Act

(a) “board” means the board of directors of the Corporation;

(b) “Corporation” means Island Investment Development Inc. established under

section 2;

(c) “Executive Director” means the Executive Director of the Corporation appointed

under section 6;

(d) “Minister” means the Minister of Economic Development, Innovation and Trade.

### Corporation

2. There is established a Crown corporation to be known as Island Investment Development Inc.

consisting of the persons referred to in section 3.

### Board

3.(1) The affairs of the Corporation shall be conducted by a board of directors consisting of:

(a) the Deputy Minister of Economic Development, Trade and Artificial Intelligence;

(b) the Deputy Minister of Finance and Affordability;

(c) the Executive Director of Island Investment Development Inc.;

(d) four representatives of the private sector, appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in

Council, broadly representing the Prince Edward Island business community.

### Lieutenant Governor in Council appoints

(1.01) The Lieutenant Governor in Council shall

(a) appoint the Deputy Minister of Finance and Affordability, the Deputy Minister of

Economic Development, Trade and Artificial Intelligence, and the Executive Director

of Island Investment Development Inc. as members of the board of directors;

(b) appoint the four private sector members of the board of directors; and

(c) designate one of the members of the board as the chairperson of the board.

### Not eligible for appointment

(1.1) The following persons are not eligible for appointment to the board:

(a) a member of the Legislative Assembly of Prince Edward Island or Parliament of

Canada;

(b) a judge of the provincial court, Supreme Court or Court of Appeal.

### Transitional

(1.2) The appointment to the board of a person who is a member of the board on the date this

subsection comes into force and is not eligible for appointment under subsection (1.1) shall

terminate 30 days after the date this subsection comes into force or on the appointment of the

person’s successor pursuant to subsection (1), whichever occurs sooner.

### Term of office

(2) A member of the board appointed under subsection (1) holds office for such term as may be

determined by the Lieutenant Governor in Council.

### Vacancies

(3) In the event of a vacancy among the members of the board appointed under subsection (1),

the Lieutenant Governor in Council may appoint a replacement for the unexpired portion of

the term of the person replaced.

### Remuneration

(4) The members of the board shall be paid such remuneration and expenses as the Lieutenant

Governor in Council may determine.

### Bylaws

(5) The board shall determine its own procedure and the internal organization and conduct of the

operations of the Corporation.

### Functions

(6) Subject to directions as to policy issued by the Lieutenant Governor in Council, the board is

responsible for the administration of this Act and the general management and operations of

the Corporation.

*Name changed from Enterprise P.E.I. Act Stats. P.E.I. 1990, c.13 by 1993, c.6, s.19.

### Objects

4. The objects of the Corporation are

(a) to raise capital in foreign and domestic markets;

(b) to provide investment opportunities in projects of significant economic benefit to the

province;

(c) to promote the province as a destination for offshore investment;

(d) to attract entrepreneurial expertise to the province.

### Powers

5. The Corporation may

(a) hold the common shares of the Prince Edward Island Government Economic

Development Fund Inc. a corporation that carries on business under the name of the

“Island Fund”;

(b) assist in the establishment, expansion or reorganization of any enterprise or industry;

(c) make loans to or invest in venture capital projects;

(d) purchase or otherwise acquire, hold, dispose of and deal with any real or personal

property as if the Corporation were an individual;

(e) acquire, hold, deal with and dispose of shares, stocks, bonds, debentures and other

securities as if the Corporation were an individual;

(f) guarantee the contracts or obligations of any person;

(g) engage the services of professional or technical personnel;

(h) enter into contracts with any person or any agency of the Crown in right of Canada or

of any province;

(i) delegate the performance of any function to any officer or employee of the

Corporation on such conditions as it may determine;

(j) do all such other things as it considers incidental or conducive to the attainment of

the objects of the Corporation.

### Executive Director

6.(1) The Lieutenant Governor in Council shall appoint an Executive Director of the Corporation

who shall co-ordinate its activities and be responsible for the day to day administration of the

affairs of the Corporation.

### Remuneration

(2) The Executive Director shall be paid such salary or other compensation as may be determined

by the Board and approved by the Lieutenant Governor in Council.

### Staff

7.(1) The Corporation may appoint such employees as it, with the approval of the Minister,

considers necessary and may determine their remuneration and terms of employment.

### Application of Civil Service Act

(2) The Civil Service Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-8 does not apply to the appointment or

employment of any person as an employee of the Corporation.

### Auditor

8. The Corporation shall appoint an independent auditor who shall conduct an annual audit of

the accounts and financial transactions of the Corporation and submit a report thereon to the

board.

### Conflict of interest

9.(1) Subject to subsection 3(4), no member of the board shall derive any profit or financial

advantage from his position as a member and where a member of the board has any pecuniary

interest in or is affected by any matter before the board, he shall declare his interest therein

before the matter is considered by the board and refrain from voting on that matter.

### Contracts

(2) Subject to subsection (1), no member of the board is disqualified from office by contracting

with the Corporation.

### Liability

10. The members of the board or of any of the subsidiaries of the Corporation, the Executive

Director, or any person acting under their instructions or under the authority of this Act are

not personally liable for any loss or damage suffered by any person by reason of any act done

by any of them in good faith in the exercise or purported exercise of the powers conferred by

or under this Act.

### Confidentiality of information

11. The members of the board, the Executive Director and the employees of the Corporation shall

preserve the confidentiality of information received in respect of the business of any client of

the Corporation in the course of their duties in carrying out the objects of the Corporation and

shall not disclose that information to any person except under the specific or general direction

of the board.

### Regulations

12. The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations.

### Amendment

13. Consequential amendment.

### Transitional provisions

14.(1) In this section “existing corporation” means Enterprise P.E.I. Inc. a corporation incorporated

under the Companies Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-14 on January 30, 1990.

### Idem

(2) On the date this Act comes into force

(a) the existing corporation is dissolved and the Corporation becomes the successor to

the existing corporation;

(b) the Corporation assumes responsibility for the management and administration of the

programs of the existing corporation;

(c) the assets and liabilities of the existing corporation are transferred to and become the

assets and liabilities of the Corporation;

(d) the real and personal property of the existing corporation is transferred and becomes

the real and personal property of the Corporation;

(e) all contracts and agreements entered into by the existing corporation are assumed by

the Corporation and may be enforced by and against the Corporation as if it had been

a party thereto instead of the existing corporation;

(f) all legal proceedings and rights of action by or against the existing corporation may

be brought or continued by or against the Corporation;

(g) the policies and bylaws made by the existing corporation shall continue in force as if

made by the Corporation pursuant to the powers conferred by this Act; and

(h) references in any enactment or document to the existing corporation shall be

construed as references to the Corporation.
