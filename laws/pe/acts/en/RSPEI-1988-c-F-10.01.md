RSPEI 1988, c F-10.01

BE IT ENACTED by the Lieutenant Governor and the Legislative Assembly of the Province of Prince

Edward Island as follows:

### Purpose

1. The purpose of this Act is to promote financial education and awareness in schools, workplaces

and communities throughout the province.

### Financial Literacy Week

2. The third full week of November in each year shall be recognized as Financial Literacy Week.

### Support of Minister

3. In support of Financial Literacy Week, the Minister designated by the Lieutenant Governor in

Council to administer this Act may do any or all of the following:

(a) share information and tools to help residents strengthen their financial knowledge and

skills;

(b) hold or support activities recognizing Financial Literacy Week;

(c) promote Financial Literacy Week in schools, workplaces and communities.
