RSPEI 1988, c C-10.1

### Armorial Bearings

1.(1) The Armorial Bearings of Prince Edward Island consist of the complete armorial

achievement for the Province of Prince Edward Island, being the Armorial Bearings granted

by Royal Warrant of His Late Majesty, King Edward VII, dated 30 May 1905, as augmented

by Vice-regal Warrant of Her Excellency the Right Honourable Adrienne Clarkson, Governor

General of Canada, dated 26 April 2002, and is described as follows:

(a) ARMS: Argent on an island Vert, to the sinister an oak tree fructed, to the dexter

thereof three oak saplings sprouting all proper, on a chief Gules a lion passant

guardant Or;

(b) HELMET: a helmet mantled Gules doubled Argent within a wreath of these colours;

(c) CREST: on a grassy mount, a blue jay (Cyanocitta cristata) reguardant crowned with

the Royal Crown and bearing in its beak the leaf of a red oak (Quercus rubra L.)

fructed proper;

(d) SUPPORTERS: Two foxes (Vulpes fulva) Sable embellished Argent, that to the

dexter gorged with a collar of potato blossoms proper, that to the sinister gorged with

a length of fishing net Argent;

(e) COMPARTMENT: a mount Vert set with a Mi’kmaq star Azure between lady’s

slippers (Cypripedium acaule), roses, thistles, shamrocks and lilies proper;

(f) MOTTO: Beneath the compartment, a scroll inscribed with the Motto, PARVA SUB

INGENTI.

### Official drawing

(2) The official drawing of the Armorial Bearings is set out in Schedule A.

### Shield of Arms

2.(1) The Shield of Arms of Prince Edward Island, granted by Royal Warrant of His Late Majesty,

King Edward VII, dated 30 May 1905, is described as follows:

Argent on an island Vert, to the sinister an oak tree fructed, to the dexter thereof three oak

saplings sprouting all proper, on a chief Gules a lion passant guardant Or.

### Official drawing

(2) The official drawing of the Shield of Arms is set out in Schedule B.

### Unauthorized use prohibited

3.(1) Subject to subsection (2), no person shall assume, display, use or reproduce

(a) the Armorial Bearings of this province or any part thereof;

(b) any design in imitation of the Armorial Bearings of this province or any part thereof;

or

(c) any design which resembles the Armorial Bearings of this province or any part

thereof,

unless that assumption, display, usage or reproduction is authorized by the regulations or by

written permission of the Clerk of the Executive Council granted in accordance with the

regulations.

### Accustomed usage

(2) Nothing herein shall affect the accustomed use of the Armorial Bearings of this province by

the Lieutenant Governor, a member of the Executive Council, a member of the Legislative

Assembly, a judge of the Supreme Court, a judge of the Provincial Court, or by duly

authorized officers of the provincial government in accordance with the regulations.

### Shield of Arms

(3) A reference in this section to the Armorial Bearings of this province includes the Shield of

Arms of this province.

### Offence and penalty

4. Any person who violates any provision of this Act is guilty of an offence and liable on

summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $50 for every day during which the offence

continues.

### Administration of Act

5. The President of the Executive Council shall have responsibility for the administration of this

Act.

### Regulations

6. The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations

(a) amending this Act for the purpose of adding the official drawings of the Armorial

Bearings and the Shield of Arms of this province;

(b) respecting the authorization of the assumption, display, usage or reproduction of the

Armorial Bearings and the Shield of Arms of this province; and

(c) respecting any other matter that is considered necessary or advisable for the more

effective carrying out of the purposes and provisions of this Act.
