PEI Reg EC90/03

*Made under the Farm Registration And Farm Organizations Funding Act*

Pursuant to subsection 10(2) of the Farm Registration and Farm Organizations Funding Act Stats.

P.E.I. 2002, c.10, Council made the following regulations:

### Act

1. In these regulations “Act” means the Farm Registration and Farm Organizations Funding

Act Stats. P.E.I. 2002, c.10.

### Fees

2. The registration fees payable each year by a farm business for registration under section 6 of

the Act are as follows:

(a) where a farm business has an annual gross income for the immediately preceding

taxation year of $0.00 to $99,999, the registration fee is .........................................$150;

(b) where a farm business has an annual gross income for the immediately preceding

taxation year of $100,000 to $250,000, the registration fee is.................................$250;

(c) where a farm business has an annual gross income for the immediately preceding

taxation year in excess of $250,000, the registration fee is .....................................$375.
