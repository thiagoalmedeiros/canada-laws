PEI Reg EC223/05

*Made under the Co-Operative Associations Act*

Pursuant to section 63 of the Co-operative Associations Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-23, Council

made the following regulations:

### Fees

1. The following fees are prescribed for services under the Act:

(a) incorporation of an association ................................ ..................................................$250

(b) application for restoration of an association under section 44.1 ..............................$250

(c) filing general statement under section 41................ ....................................................$30
