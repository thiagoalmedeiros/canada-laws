PEI Reg EC476/95

*Made under the Private Investigators And Security Guards Act*

Pursuant to section 24 of the Private Investigators and Security Guards Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. P-20 Council made the following regulations:

### Fees

1. The following fees are prescribed:

(a) upon application for a license to engage in the business of providing private

investigators for hire or renewal thereof ....................................................................$300

(b) upon application for a license to act as a private investigator or renewal thereof...$100

(c) upon application for a license to engage in the business of providing security guards

for hire or renewal thereof ...........................................................................................$300

(d) upon application for a license to act as a security guard or renewal thereof .............$40
