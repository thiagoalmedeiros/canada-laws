PEI Reg EC395/87

*Made under the Interprovincial Subpoena Act*

Pursuant to section 9 of the Interprovincial Subpoena Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. I-9, Council made the

following regulations:

### Witness fees

1. For the purposes of the Interprovincial Subpoena Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. I-9 the following

witness fees are prescribed:

(a) $100 per day for an ordinary witness;

(b) $300 per day for an expert or professional witness.

### Travelling and living expenses

2. For the purposes of the said Act the following travelling and living expenses are prescribed:

Actual, reasonable travelling expenses incurred in relation to attendance in court together

with actual, reasonable living expenses, including accommodation, meals, local transport and

sundry expenses.
