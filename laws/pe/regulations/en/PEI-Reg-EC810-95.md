PEI Reg EC810/95

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

Pursuant to section 42 of the Employment Standards Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. E-6.2, Council made

the following order:

### Reciprocating province- Nova Scotia

1.(1) The Province of Nova Scotia is declared to be a reciprocating province for the purpose of the

enforcement of orders made under the Labour Standards Code of that province.

### Authority

(2) The Director of Labour Standards of the Province of Nova Scotia is designated as the

authority within that province for the purposes of clause 42(1)(b) of the Employment

Standards Act.

### Reciprocating province - Ontario

2.(1) The Province of Ontario is declared to be a reciprocating province for the purpose of the

enforcement of orders made under the Labour Standards Code of that province.

### Authority

(2) The Director of Employment Practices of the Province of Ontario is designated as the

authority within that province for the purposes of clause 42(1)(b) of the Employment

Standards Act.

### Reciprocating province - British Columbia

3.(1) The Province of British Columbia is declared to be a reciprocating province for the purpose

of the enforcement of orders made under the Labour Standards Code of that province.

### Authority

(2) The Director of Labour Standards of the Province of British Columbia is designated as the

authority within that province for the purposes of clause 42(1)(b) of the Employment

Standards Act.

### Reciprocating province - New Brunswick

4.(1) The Province of New Brunswick is declared to be a reciprocating province for the purpose of

### Authority

### Reciprocating territory - Yukon
