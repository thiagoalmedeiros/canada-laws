PEI Reg EC546/15

*Made under the Environment Tax Act*

Pursuant to section 3 of the Environment Tax Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. E-8.3, Council made the

following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations,

(a) “Act” means the Environment Tax Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. E-8.3;

(b) “eligible diplomat” means a diplomat who, pursuant to an international agreement, is

not subject to Canadian taxes during his or her service in Canada.

### Rebate of tax – use outside province

2. Where a motor vehicle registered or required to be registered under the Highway Traffic Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-5,

(a) is sold within the province; and

(b) is taken out of the province within 30 days after the date of the sale,

the Minister, on application in the form approved by the Minister and receipt of satisfactory

evidence that the motor vehicle is to be used outside the province, may refund to the

purchaser the tax paid under the Act at the time of purchase.

### Rebate of tax – eligible diplomat, etc.

3. Where a purchaser is an eligible diplomat or the spouse or a family member of an eligible

diplomat, the Minister, on application in the form approved by the Minister and receipt of

satisfactory evidence of the purchaser’s status, may refund to the purchaser the tax paid under

the Act at the time of purchase.
