PEI Reg EC476/97

*Made under the Roads Act*

Pursuant to section 54 of the Roads Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-15, the Minister of Transportation

and Public Works made the following regulations:

### Public utility easement fees

1.(1) A public utility, except a water or sewerage public utility, shall pay to the Provincial

Treasurer in respect of the grant of a public utility easement pursuant to section 4.1 of the Act

an annual fee calculated as follows:

(a) $75 per kilometre where the aerial lines or cables extend along highways designated

as arterial highways;

(b) $37.50 per kilometre where the aerial lines or cables extend along highways

designated as collector or local highways or along seasonal roads or subdivision

streets.

### Application

(2) The fees set out in subsection (1) apply to existing and future installations.
