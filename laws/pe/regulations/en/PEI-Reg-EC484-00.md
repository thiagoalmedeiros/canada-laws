PEI Reg EC484/00

*Made under the Forest Management Act*

Pursuant to section 25 of the Forest Management Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. F-14, Council made the

following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations

(a) “Provincial Forest”, as defined in the Act, means any land designated as a

Provincial Forest in Schedule 1;

(b) “Satellite Provincial Forest” means any land designated as a Satellite Provincial

Forest in Schedule 2.

### Conditions re designated Forests

2. Lands designated as Provincial Forests or Satellite Provincial Forests that are both

(a) owned by the Government; and

(b) have been assigned for the purpose of management to the Department

shall be managed in accordance with Crown Forest Land Management Plans.

### Additions to Provincial Forests and Satellite Provincial Forests

3. After these regulations come into force, land acquired by the Government of Prince Edward

Island and assigned to the Department shall

(a) become part of the Provincial Forest or Satellite Provincial Forest to which the

acquisition document designates it; and

(b) be governed by the provisions of section 2.
