PEI Reg EC856/72

*Made under the Highway Traffic Act*

Made by the Lieutenant Governor in Council under the Highway Traffic Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. H-5.

### Definitions

1. In these regulations

(a) “mobile home” means a dwelling for year round living, towable on its own chassis,

for use without a permanent foundation and for connection to utilities;

(b) “motor vehicle” means a motor vehicle as defined in the Highway Traffic Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-5;

(c) “recreational vehicle” means a portable structure intended as a temporary

accommodation for travel, recreational or vacational use, including travel trailers,

motorized homes, slide-in campers, chassis mounted campers and tent trailers.

### Inspection agency

2. The Canadian Standards Association is designed as the inspection agency for CSA Standards

adopted under these regulations.

### Standards for mobile homes and recreational vehicles

3. No person shall have for sale, sell, offer for sale or use a mobile home or recreational vehicle

in the province unless the mobile home or recreational vehicle meets:

(a) the manufacturing standards of the American National Standards Institute (ANSI),

Recreational Vehicle Industry Association (RVIA), National Fire Protection

Association (NFPA), including any amendments to such standards; or

(b) the following CSA standards, including any amendments to them:

(i) CSA Z240 MH Series 92 (R2005), entitled “Mobile homes”,

(ii) CSA Z240 RV Series-14, entitled “Recreational vehicles”.

### Standards for motor vehicles

4. No person shall have for sale, sell, offer for sale or use a motor vehicle in the province unless

the motor vehicle meets the:

(a) CSA Standard CSA D106.1-1977 entitled “Vehicle Lighting Equipment (North

American Practice)”;

(b) ANSI Z26.1-1996 Safety Standard entitled “Safety Glazing Materials for Glazing

Motor Vehicles and Motor Vehicle Equipment Operating on Land Highways”, for

each piece of glazing material on the motor vehicle.
