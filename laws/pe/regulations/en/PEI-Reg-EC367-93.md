PEI Reg EC367/93

*Made under the Highway Traffic Act*

Pursuant to section 312 of the Highway Traffic Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-5, Council made the

following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations

(a) “authorized configuration” means for commercial vehicles the specifications and

dimensions set out in Tables 2 to 16 of the Vehicle Weights Regulations

under the Roads Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-15;

(b) “commercial vehicle” has the same meaning as set out in clause 1(b.2) of the

Highway Traffic Act;

(c) “non-complying vehicle configuration” means a configuration that is not an

authorized vehicle configuration.

### Compliance with authorized configuration

2. Subject to sections 3, 4 and 5, all commercial vehicles operating and registered on Prince

Edward Island must comply with one of the authorized vehicle configurations.

### Exception, special permit

3. The owner of a commercial vehicle that has a non-complying vehicle configuration must

apply to the Registrar for a special permit to operate the vehicle on the highway.

### Refusal of registration etc.

4. The Registrar shall have the authority to refuse to register or allow the operation of any

commercial vehicle that has a non-complying vehicle configuration.

### Details of permit

5. A special permit shall show the owner’s name, vehicle identification number, plate number,

permit expiry date, fee and any other conditions as determined by the Registrar.

### Offence

6.(1) It is an offence for any person to operate or, being the owner, to permit another person to

operate or register a commercial vehicle, that has a non-complying vehicle configuration.

### Penalty

(2) Any person guilty of an offence under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a

fine not exceeding $500.
