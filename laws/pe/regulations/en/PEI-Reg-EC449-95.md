PEI Reg EC449/95

*Made under the Auctioneers Act*

Pursuant to section 6 of the Auctioneers Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. A-23, Council made the following

regulations:

### Fee

1. The fee for an auctioneer’s license is $300.

### Term

2. A license issued under the Act is valid for a two year term unless earlier cancelled.
