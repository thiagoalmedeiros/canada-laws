PEI Reg EC519/09

*Made under the Environmental Protection Act*

Pursuant to section 25 of the Environmental Protection Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. E-9, Council made

the following regulations:

### Fees

1. A fee of $50 per lot is payable for the inspection of a lot by an environment officer, for the

purposes of

(a) determining the drinking quality of the water on the lot;

(b) identifying sensitive environmental issues on the lot;

(c) establishing the necessary buffer zone requirements for the lot;

(d) conducting an environmental site inspection of the lot;

(e) reviewing an application for the subdivision of the lot into more than two parcels of

land or an application for the subdivision of the lot where known environmentally

sensitive features exist, such as a watercourse, a wetland or a sand dune.

### Schedule

2. An application for a property inspection is set out in the Schedule to these regulations.
