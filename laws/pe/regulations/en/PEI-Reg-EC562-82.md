PEI Reg EC562/82

*Made under the Public Health Act*

Pursuant to clause 23(e) of the Public Health Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. P-30 Council made the

following regulations:

### Permit required, exception

1. Except where a disinterment of human remains is ordered by a coroner, the Supreme Court or

the Attorney General, no person shall disinter human remains unless he first obtains a

disinterment permit.

### Application for permit

2. An application for a permit shall be made to the Chief Health Officer and shall provide such

information as he may require.

### Issue

3. The Chief Health Officer may issue a permit.

### Directions of Chief Health Officer

4. Where a permit is issued under section 3 or where a disinterment of human remains is

otherwise ordered under the authority of an enactment, the Chief Health Officer shall give

such directions as he may determine respecting the disinterment, transportation, storage and

reinterment of the human remains.

### Requirements of Vital Statistics Act

5. Nothing in these regulations affects the requirements for a burial permit under the Vital

Statistics Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. V-4.
