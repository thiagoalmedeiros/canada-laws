PEI Reg EC643/13

*Made under the Narcotics Safety And Awareness Act*

Pursuant to section 18 of the Narcotics Safety and Awareness Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. N-.01,

Council made the following regulations:

### Definition

1. In these regulations, “Act” means the Narcotics Safety and Awareness Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. N-.01.

### Monitored drug

2. In addition to the controlled substances listed in the Schedules to the Controlled Drugs and

Substances Act (Canada), a drug product that contains any of the following substances is a

monitored drug for the purposes of the Act:

(a) Tramadol;

(b) Tapentadol;

(c) any opioid not listed in Schedule I of the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act

(Canada).
