PEI Reg EC225/65

*Made under the Highway Traffic Act*

Made by the Lieutenant Governor in Council under the Highway Traffic Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. H-5.

### Urban districts

1. That section of a highway wherein a maximum speed limit of fifty kilometers per hour is

indicated by the erection or existence of a sign at each end of such section of the highway and

facing the stream of traffic entering such section of the highway, is designated as an urban

district.

### Business districts

2. That section of a highway wherein a maximum speed limit of sixty kilometers per hour is

indicated by the erection or existence of a sign at each end of the section of the highway and

facing the stream of traffic entering the section of the highway is designated as a business

district.
