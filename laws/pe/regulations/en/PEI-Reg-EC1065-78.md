### Fees

1. The following fees and rental rates are prescribed:

Section Fee

5(3) Inspection of records $5.00

19(1) Issue of exploration license $5.00 per claim

28(1)(b) Renewal of exploration license $5.00 per claim

33(1) Issue of development license $1.00 per acre

33(2) Renewal of development license $1.00 per acre

35(1)(b)

& 39(2) Lease rental per year $1.00 per acre
