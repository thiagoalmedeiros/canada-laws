PEI Reg EC506/99

*Made under the Farm Practices Act*

Pursuant to section 17 of the Farm Practices Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. F-4.1, Council made the

following regulations:

### Farm organizations

1. The following organizations are designated farm organizations pursuant to clause 1.1(f) of the

Act for the purposes of the Act and these regulations:

FARM ORGANIZATIONS

(a) Chicken Farmers of Prince Edward Island;

(b) Dairy Farmers of Prince Edward Island;

(c) Egg Farmers of PEI;

(d) Federated Women’s Institute of Prince Edward Island;

(e) National Farmers Union, Atlantic Region, PEI District;

(f) P.E.I. Soil & Crop Improvement Association;

(g) P.E.I. Women in Agriculture Inc.;

(h) PEI Wild Blueberry Growers’ Association Inc.;

(i) Prince Edward Island 4-H Council;

(j) Prince Edward Island Beekeepers’ Association Inc.;

(k) Prince Edward Island Cattle Producers;

(l) Prince Edward Island Certified Organic Producers Co-Operative Ltd.;

(m) Prince Edward Island Federation of Agriculture;

(n) Prince Edward Island Hog Commodity Marketing Board;

(o) Prince Edward Island Horticultural Association Inc.;

(p) Prince Edward Island Potato Board;

(q) Prince Edward Island Sheep Breeders’ Association;

(r) Prince Edward Island Turkey Marketing Commission.
