PEI Reg EC530/24

*Made under the Stretcher Transportation Act*

Pursuant to section 16 of the Stretcher Transportation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. S-8.11, Council made

the following regulations:

### Safety of vehicle

1.(1) A stretcher transportation vehicle shall be

(a) registered and inspected in accordance with the Highway Traffic Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. H-5, as evidenced by the motor vehicle registration and the inspection certificate

carried in the vehicle;

(b) maintained in good mechanical condition and repair; and

(c) cleaned and disinfected, regularly and as needed.

### Design requirements

(2) The vehicle design requirements for a stretcher transportation vehicle are set out in Schedule

A.

### Vehicle equipment

2.(1) The following vehicle equipment shall be carried in a stretcher transportation vehicle:

(a) a two-way radio or a mobile phone;

(b) at least two battery-operated flashlights;

(c) a properly maintained dry chemical fire extinguisher of a type approved by the Fire

Marshal appointed pursuant to the Fire Prevention Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. F-11.

### Other equipment and supplies

(2) The equipment and supplies set out in Schedule B shall be carried in a stretcher transportation

vehicle.

### Prevention of uncontrolled movement

(3) The equipment and supplies carried in a stretcher transportation vehicle shall be properly stored

and secured to prevent uncontrolled movement.

### Safety of equipment

(4) The equipment carried in a stretcher transportation vehicle shall be maintained and calibrated

in accordance with any recommendations of the manufacturer.

### Insurance

3. An applicant for a licence or an operator, as the case may be, shall hold

(a) public liability insurance with coverage of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence for

the stretcher transportation operation; and

(b) third-party motor vehicle liability insurance with coverage of not less than $5,000,000

per occurrence for each stretcher transportation vehicle used or intended to be used to

provide stretcher transportation services.

### Staffing

4. A stretcher transportation vehicle shall be staffed by not less than two attendants who are

certified in standard first aid and cardiopulmonary resuscitation.

### Incident reports

5. An operator shall make a report to the Minister, in the manner and within the time required by

the Minister, of any occurrence during the provision of stretcher transportation services that

(a) causes personal injury to any person; or

(b) is required to be reported to a peace officer under the Highway Traffic Act.

## SCHEDULE A

STRETCHER TRANSPORTATION VEHICLE DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

1. A stretcher transportation vehicle shall provide for entry by rear doors or a curbside door to

permit loading of a stretcher.

2. A stretcher transportation vehicle shall accommodate

(a) at least one stretcher;

(b) at least one seated attendant who can observe the person on the stretcher; and

(c) communication between attendants in the driver section and stretcher section.

3. (1) A stretcher transportation vehicle shall be equipped with floor-mounted or wall-mounted

assemblies to secure at least one wheeled, adjustable-height stretcher.

(2) The assemblies shall be reinforced to transmit stresses to the main body of the vehicle.

(3) The assemblies shall be positioned to allow space at the head of the stretcher for an attendant

to provide assistance to the person on the stretcher.

4. A stretcher transportation vehicle shall be equipped with

(a) at least one attendant seat with a headrest in the stretcher section;

(b) a restraint belt for each person in the vehicle; and

(c) secure storage for all required supplies, equipment, and personal items belonging to

the person on the stretcher.

5. The interior surfaces of a stretcher transportation vehicle shall be easily cleaned and free from

sharp edges.

6. A stretcher transportation vehicle shall be insulated and have a system of heating and cooling

or ventilation that keeps the stretcher section approximately at room temperature.

7. A stretcher transportation vehicle shall be equipped with the following lighting:

(a) a map light in the driver section;

(b) at least two dimmable interior lights, located on each side of the stretcher section;

(c) lighting to illuminate the rear entry, if any, when the doors are opened or when the

transmission is in reverse gear;

(d) lighting to illuminate the curbside entry, if any, when the door is opened.

4. Pillowcases 2 x stretcher capacity

6. Bedpan, with toilet paper 1
