PEI Reg EC191/87

*Made under the Housing Corporation Act*

Pursuant to section 38 of the Housing Corporation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-11, Council approved

the following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations

(a) “applicant” means a person who is making application for a second mortgage loan

under these regulations;

(b) “approved applicant” means an applicant whose application for a second mortgage

loan under these regulations has received final approval from the Corporation;

(c) revoked by EC639/93;

(d) “conditional approval” means a conditional approval by the Corporation of a second

mortgage loan to an applicant;

(e) “Corporation” means the Prince Edward Island Housing Corporation;

(f) “final approval” means a final approval by the Corporation of a second mortgage

loan to an applicant whose application has been conditionally approved and the

applicant has been successful in obtaining first mortgage financing;

(g) “fixed debt” means the annual amount of regularly scheduled loan payments,

excluding mortgage loan payments;

(h) “gross debt service ratio” means the ratio of shelter costs to gross household

income;

(i) “gross household income” means the annual income in whatever form received by

the applicant and spouse, and includes salaries, commissions, fees and pensions, but

in the case of self-employed persons, the establishment of such income shall be at the

discretion of the Corporation;

(j) “housing unit” means housing accommodation, in whatever form, intended for

year-round occupancy and providing therein living, sleeping, eating and food

preparation facilities for a family;

(k) “progress payment” means the disbursement of loan funds to an approved applicant

based on the value of in-place work as determined by the Corporation;

(l) “shelter costs” means the annual payments of principal and interest on the first and

second mortgages, annual property tax payments, and the estimated annual payments

for heating the housing unit;

(m) “total debt service ratio” means the ratio of shelter costs plus fixed debt to gross

household income.

### Eligibility

2. To be eligible to make application for a second mortgage loan under these regulations, a

person must have applied for a first mortgage loan from a private lender.

### Application

3. An applicant shall submit a completed application to the Corporation in such form as

prescribed by the Corporation.

### Maximum loan

4. The maximum second mortgage loan available to an applicant under these regulations is

$15,000.

### Requirements for conditional approval

5. To be eligible for a conditional approval under these regulations, an applicant must show to

the satisfaction of the Corporation

(a) revoked by EC5/89;

(b) that the sale price or construction cost of the housing unit which he intends to buy or

build does not exceed $86,000. (In the case of a newly constructed housing unit, the

construction cost established by the first mortgage lender shall be taken);

(c) that he is making an equity contribution toward the purchase or construction of the

housing unit which amounts to at least 10% of the purchase price or construction

cost;

(d) that he has established a favourable credit rating;

(e) that his gross debt service ratio will be acceptable to the Corporation;

(f) that his total debt service ratio will be 40% or less.

### Eligible units

6. Any housing unit, including a mobile home, is eligible for a second mortgage loan under

these regulations provided that a real property mortgage can be registered against the

property.

### Evidence

7. An application under these regulations must be supported by evidence of eligibility, as

required under section 5, in such form as the Corporation may require.

### Conditional approval

8. Where, in the opinion of the Corporation, the applicant has provided satisfactory evidence of

eligibility, the Corporation shall issue the applicant a conditional approval for a second

mortgage loan.

### Evidence of first mortgage

9. Where an applicant whose application has been given conditional approval provides evidence

satisfactory to the Corporation of his first mortgage loan approval by a private lender, the

Corporation shall issue the applicant a final approval for a second mortgage loan.

### Cancellation

10. If, after a period of 60 days, an applicant whose application has been given conditional

approval fails to provide the Corporation with satisfactory evidence of first mortgage loan

approval by a private lender, the Corporation shall cancel the conditional approval.

### New application

11. Where a conditional approval has been cancelled under section 10, the applicant may make a

new application for a second mortgage loan under these regulations.

### Progress payment

12.(1) Where the second mortgage loan funds are being used for new construction, the funds may be

advanced on a progress payment basis.

### Amount of payments

(2) The amount of the progress payment shall be based on the value of completed, in-place work

as determined by the Corporation, and by such other evidence of an approved applicant’s

expenditures as may be requested by the Corporation.

### Security

13. The mortgage loan by the Corporation shall be secured by a registered second mortgage upon

the housing unit, held in favour of the Corporation.

### Amortization period

14. The amortization period of the second mortgage shall not exceed 15 years.

### Interest rate

15. The interest rate on second mortgage loans issued under these regulations shall be the

greater of

(a) 1% above the interest rate on the first mortgage loan; or

(b) 1% above the Corporations’s cost of borrowing at the time the loan is issued.

### Conditions

16. The mortgage securing the second mortgage loan shall be non-transferable and

non-assumable.

### Fire insurance

17. The Corporation shall require that the approved applicant has fire insurance on the housing

unit on which the second mortgage was secured, adequate to cover the outstanding balance of

the second mortgage loan, and made payable to the Corporation in the event of loss.

### Default

18.(1) In the event of default on the second mortgage by the approved applicant, where a loan

becomes due and payable, the Corporation may take whatever steps it considers advisable to

ensure repayment by the approved applicant of the outstanding balance to the Corporation.

### Application of payment

(2) The Corporation shall first apply all payments from or on behalf of the approved applicant in

payment of legal costs, if any, before applying the payment to the balance outstanding on the

defaulted second mortgage loan.

### False statements

19. In the event that an applicant makes any statement in the application for a second mortgage

loan that is false in any material respect, all second mortgage loan funds advanced to the

applicant by the Corporation shall immediately become due and payable, and the Corporation

shall take any action that it considers proper in the circumstances to secure payment.
