PEI Reg EC246/05

*Made under the Insurance Act*

Pursuant to sections 83 and 84 of the Insurance Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. I-4, Council made the

following regulations:

### Fees

1. The following fees are prescribed:

(a) license for an insurer, for a one-year term .............. ..................................................$900

(b) certificate of authority for an agent, for a two-year term ..........................................$200

(c) full or probationary license for an adjuster, for a two-year term..............................$200

(d) transfer of a license of an agent or adjuster............. ...................................................$ 50

(e) insurance exam .......................................................... ...................................................$ 50

(f) letter of good standing .............................................. ...................................................$ 25

(g) copy of a certificate of authority .............................. ...................................................$ 25

### Revocation

2. The Insurance Act Fees Regulations (EC42/97) and the License Fees for Insurers Regulations

(EC257/75) are revoked.
