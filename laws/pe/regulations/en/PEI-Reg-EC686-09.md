PEI Reg EC686/09

*Made under the Recreation Development Act*

Pursuant to section 15 of the Recreation Development Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-8, Council made

the following regulations:

### Conservation officer may enforce Act and regulations

1. A conservation officer appointed under the Wildlife Conservation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. W-4.1 has the power and authority to enforce the Recreation Development Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-8 and the regulations made under the Recreation Development Act.
