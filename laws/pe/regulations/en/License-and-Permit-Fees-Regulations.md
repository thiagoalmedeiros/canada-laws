*Made under the Wildlife Conservation Act*

Pursuant to section 28 of the Wildlife Conservation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. W-4.1, Council made

the following regulations:

### Fees

1. LICENSE AND PERMIT FEES

(a) Resident Game Hunting License (18 years old and older) ………………………...... $5.00

(b) Complimentary Resident Game Hunting License (60 years old and older) ….... No Charge

(c) Complimentary Hunting License (Canadian Armed Forces (CAF) member) … No Charge

(d) Complimentary Hunting License (Veteran) …………………………………… No Charge

(e) Youth Hunting License (12-17 years old) ……………………………………... No Charge

(f) Non-resident Hunting License (18 years old and older) ……………………………...75.00

(g) Non-resident 3-day Hunting License (18 years old and older) ……………………….60.00

(h) Resident Furbearer Hunting License …………………………………………………. 5.00

(i) Resident Fur Dealer License ...…………………………...…….………………….... 25.00

(j) Non-resident Fur Dealer License ...………………………..………………………... 175.00

### Revocation

2. The Wildlife Conservation Act License and Permit Fees Regulations (EC684/06) are revoked.
