PEI Reg EC802/17

*Made under the Real Property Tax Act*

Pursuant to section 43 of the Real Property Tax Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-5, Council made the

following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations

(a) “Act” means the Real Property Tax Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-5;

(b) “tax credit factor” means the tax credit factor for a specified municipality in a

specified year.

### Tax credit factor

2.(1) The tax credit factor for a municipality for the purposes of the calculation of the municipal

tax credit for that municipality under section 4.1 of the Act is that specified in the table in the

Schedule to these regulations.

### Schedule adopted

(2) The Schedule to these regulations is hereby adopted and forms part of these regulations.
