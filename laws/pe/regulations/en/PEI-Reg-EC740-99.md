PEI Reg EC740/99

*Made under the Workers Compensation Act*

Pursuant to section 80 of the Workers Compensation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. W-7.1, Council

approved the following regulations made by the Workers Compensation Board of Prince

Edward Island:

### Spousal benefits

1. Where, pursuant to subsection 38(1) of the Workers Compensation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. W-7.1 or its predecessor Act, a surviving spouse in receipt of spousal benefits lost the

right to the spousal benefits upon re-marriage prior to April 17, 1985, the surviving spouse

shall have the benefits reinstated, without interest, from April 17, 1985 to January 1, 1998, by

way of a lump sum payment reflecting the amounts payable during that period.
