PEI Reg EC210/12

*Made under the Roads Act*

Pursuant to section 46 of the Roads Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-15, the Minister made the

following regulations:

### Fees

1. The fees prescribed for the issuance of a permit in respect of an overweight vehicle are set out

in the table below:

## OVERWEIGHT VEHICLE PERMITS

### Vehicle’s number of axles Gross weight of vehicle Fee

3 axles up to 30,500 kg $50

4 axles 30,501 kg up to 43,000 kg 60

5 to 7 axles 43,001 kg up to 56,000 kg 80

6 to 8 axles 56,001 kg up to 64,000 kg 120

7 to 9 axles 64,001 kg up to 72,000 kg 200

10 or more axles over 72,000 kg 350
