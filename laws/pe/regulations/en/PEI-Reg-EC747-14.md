PEI Reg EC747/14

*Made under the Prince Edward Island Lands Protection Act*

Pursuant to clause 17(1)(a) of the Prince Edward Island Lands Protection Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. L-5 Council made the following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations,

(a) “Act” means the Prince Edward Island Lands Protection Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988,

Cap. L-5;

(b) “shore frontage” means, with respect to a parcel of land, the side or sides of the

parcel of land that directly abut the waters of Northumberland Strait, the Gulf of St.

Lawrence, a bay listed in the Schedule to these regulations or any body of water that

is connected to tidal waters and has a tidal flow.

### Location of measurement

2. In measuring shore frontage for the purposes of clause 4(b) or 5(1)(b) of the Act, the person

measuring shall do so along a line that follows the general trend of the shore frontage and that

runs as close as practicable to

(a) the top of the scarp, in the case of land that ends in a cliff or steep embankment; and

(b) the beginning of predominately land-based vegetation, in the case of low-relief dune

environments.
