PEI Reg EC646/26

*Made under the Charitable Lotteries Act*

Pursuant to section 26 of the Charitable Lotteries Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-3.2, Council made the

following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations,

(a) “Act” means the Charitable Lotteries Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-3.2;

(b) “bingo” means a lottery scheme in which

(i) one or more bingo cards or papers are sold to a number of players and a caller

randomly calls numbers that may appear on the bingo cards or papers,

(ii) players attempt to complete a predetermined pattern on the bingo cards or

papers using the numbers called, and

(iii) a prize or prizes are awarded or disposed of to the players who declare and are

verified as having correctly completed the pattern, according to the approved

rules of play;

(c) “casino night” means a lottery scheme in which a prize or prizes may be awarded or

disposed of to a winning player of a game, including blackjack, wheel of fortune or

another game authorized by the Registrar;

(d) “raffle” means a lottery scheme in which tickets are sold in the province for a chance

to win a prize in a single draw or multiple draws, and includes

(i) cash or prize draws,

(ii) 50/50 draws,

(iii) Gold Rush draws, and

(iv) Chase the Ace or other card-style raffles.

### Registrar’s authority

2. For the purposes of section 3 of the Act, the Order in Council authorizing the Registrar to issue

licences and impose terms and conditions on those licences is Order in Council (EC647/26).

### Types of licences

3. The Registrar may issue a licence under the Act for the following types of lottery scheme for

which a prize or prizes may be awarded or disposed of:

(a) a bingo;

(b) a casino night;

(c) a raffle;

(d) a game of chance, including games of mixed chance and skill.

### Terms and conditions apply

4.(1) All licences issued to conduct and manage a lottery scheme are subject to the terms and

conditions set out in this section.

### Terms and conditions

(2) A licence issued to conduct and manage a lottery scheme is subject to the following terms and

conditions:

(a) the licensee shall not engage in or permit any person with whom the licensee contracts

to engage in advertising in the province that

(i) implies the lottery scheme promotes or is required for social acceptance,

personal or financial success or the resolution of any economic, social or

personal problems,

(ii) contains one or more endorsements by well-known persons that suggest

playing a lottery scheme contributed to their success, or

(iii) compares playing the lottery scheme to other forms of gaming in the province;

(b) the licensee shall ensure that any advertisement of the lottery scheme in the province

shall state the total cash value of the prizes to be awarded;

(c) at the request of the Registrar, the licensee shall provide the Registrar with samples of

any advertising and promotional materials to be used in the province in connection

with the lottery scheme;

(d) the licensee shall not use advertising or promotional materials that, in the opinion of

the Registrar, are inconsistent with the Act or these regulations;

(e) the licensee shall not advertise the lottery scheme or sell lottery tickets in the province

outside any time period specified by the Registrar on the licence;

(f) the licensee shall maintain a separate account at a deposit-taking financial institution

within the province for the purposes of depositing and administering all proceeds

received from the lottery scheme that originated within the province, if required by the

Registrar.

### Specific terms and conditions - bingo

(3) A licence issued to conduct and operate a lottery scheme that is a bingo is subject to the

additional terms and conditions set out in Schedule A to these regulations.

### Specific terms and conditions – casino night

(4) A licence issued to conduct and operate a lottery scheme that is a casino night is subject to the

additional terms and conditions set out in Schedule B to these regulations.

### Specific terms and conditions - raffle

(5) A licence issued to conduct and operate a lottery scheme that is a raffle is subject to the

additional terms and conditions set out in Schedule C to these regulations.

### Specific terms and conditions – other lottery scheme

(6) A licence issued to conduct and operate a lottery scheme described in paragraph 207(1)(f) of

the Criminal Code (Canada) is subject to the additional terms and conditions set out in Schedule

D to these regulations.

### Fees

5. The fees prescribed for issuance of a licence are set out in Schedule E to these regulations.

### Schedules

6. The Schedules to these regulations are hereby adopted and form part of these regulations.

## SCHEDULE A – BINGOS

1. In this Schedule,

(a) “bingo card” or “bingo paper” means a card, sheet or booklet of sheets consisting of

various rows and columns of numbers;

(b) “bingo event” means a single gathering or session at which a series of successive bingo

games are played within the time indicated on the licence;

(c) “progressive bingo games” means two or more bingo games played in succession on

the same bingo card or sheet of bingo paper;

(d) “personal bingo verifier” means a device used by a player to keep track of and verify

numbers called by the caller at a bingo event.

2. A licence issued under the Act to conduct and manage a lottery scheme that is a bingo is subject

to the following terms and conditions:

(a) the total prizes offered or paid at any bingo event shall not exceed $5,500;

(b) bingo cards or bingo papers shall not be given as a bonus or prize;

(c) accumulating or build-up jackpots shall not exceed $4,000 and each incremental

increase shall not exceed $200;

(d) the total guaranteed prizes, excluding accumulating or build-up jackpots, at any bingo

event shall not exceed $3,500;

(e) the total value of all prizes plus operating expenses provided in any calendar month

under the licence shall not exceed

(i) 85 per cent of the gross receipts for that month in the case of a bingo whose

yearly projected aggregate value of prizes awarded exceeds $100,000, and

(ii) 90 per cent of the gross receipts for that month in the case of a bingo whose

yearly projected aggregate value of prizes awarded does not exceed $100,000;

(f) the licensee shall report to the Registrar, in the form approved by the Registrar, a report

each month, or at other times as specified by the Registrar, that sets out the licensee’s

planned expenditure of the revenues generated under the licence;

(g) where a licensee engages a third-party operator to manage and conduct a bingo on its

behalf,

(i) at least 15 per cent of the gross receipts in any calendar month shall be

forwarded by the operator to the licensee, and

(ii) the operator is not entitled to charge for services rendered on the basis of a

percentage of gross receipts or net profits;

(h) the licensee shall not

(i) sell or otherwise provide bingo cards or bingo papers to another licensee, or

(ii) cut or alter bingo paper sheets or booklets in any way;

(i) the licensee shall ensure that secure storage is provided for bingo supplies in any

premises used by the licensee for bingo operations;

(j) during play, players shall mark bingo cards or bingo papers in a manner which

(i) allows reading of numbers, and

(ii) eliminates re-use of the bingo paper;

(k) players shall not use bingo chips or other similar items to mark bingo cards or bingo

papers;

(l) a caller at a bingo shall not participate as a player;

(m) no person involved in the conduct or management of a bingo event shall participate as

a player, unless specified in the licensee’s rules of play;

(n) when a player has declared “bingo”, the following rules apply in the sequence they are

listed:

(i) no further numbers shall be called pending confirmation of the player’s bingo

card, cards or papers,

(ii) the bingo machine shall be left on until the bingo has been verified,

(iii) the checker shall take the player’s card and

(A) place the card in front of another player or in another manner that is

visible to all players, and

(B) the checker shall call back the numbers to the caller and receive

verification from the caller of the bingo,

(iv) once all declared bingos have been checked and verified, the caller shall ask

clearly if there are any more bingos and indicate that it is the last call,

(v) if no further bingos are declared,

(A) the caller shall state clearly to the players that the game is closed, or

(B) in a series of progressive bingo games, the caller shall continue calling

numbers until the final progressive bingo game has been completed;

(o) no rule of play shall specify that a valid bingo shall include the last number called; and

(p) the licensee shall award any jackpot in existence during the final bingo event if the

licensee plans to cease the operation of the bingo.

3. A licensee may permit a player to use a personal bingo verifier at a bingo event where

(a) the licensee has obtained approval from the Registrar for the use of personal bingo

verifiers at the bingo event;

(b) the player obtains the personal bingo verifier from the licensee;

(c) the player uses the personal bingo verifier to assist the player in tracking and verifying

the numbers called by the caller at the bingo event; and

(d) the player does not use or attempt to use the personal bingo verifier to replace the use

of bingo paper and numbers dabbed by the player in the conventional manner.

4. A licensee may use radio or television in conjunction with a live bingo event where

(a) the licensee has obtained approval to do so from the Registrar; and

(b) the use of radio and television is conducted in accordance with the purposes approved

in the licensee’s application.

## SCHEDULE B – CASINO NIGHTS

1. In this Schedule, “casino night event” means a single gathering or session at which a series of

successive games are played within the time indicated on the licence.

2. A licence issued under the Act to conduct and manage a lottery scheme that is a casino night is

subject to the following terms and conditions:

(a) no cash prizes shall be offered;

(b) all games shall be played with scrip, chips, tokens or play money;

(c) a casino night event shall not exceed a period of 12 consecutive hours, and shall

conclude by 2:00 a.m.

## SCHEDULE C – RAFFLES

1. (1) In this Schedule, “electronic raffle system” means electronic gaming equipment used by a

licensee to conduct and manage a lottery scheme that is a raffle, and includes

(a) electronic services and platforms provided by a supplier;

(b) a raffle sales unit device that is used as a point of sale for raffle tickets; and

(c) a random number generator device or system that is used to randomly generate results

for the raffle.

(2) For greater certainty, the use of an electronic raffle system by a licensee does not include

(a) live streaming a raffle using a website available to the public and approved by the

Registrar;

(b) using an online banking service to send or receive an electronic fund transfer; or

(c) using a website approved by the Registrar for the sale of raffle tickets, where the

website is used to accept payment and the ticket is generated manually and provided

to the purchaser, whether an e-mail confirmation is sent to the purchaser or not.

2. A licence issued under the Act to conduct and manage a lottery scheme that is a raffle is subject

to the following terms and conditions:

(a) where the raffle is a “Chase the Ace” raffle,

(i) the licensee shall submit to the Registrar, on a form approved by the Registrar,

a report each week, or other period specified by the Registrar, that includes

information respecting

(A) the beginning jackpot amount,

(B) the total ticket sales collected,

(C) the amount added to the jackpot for each reporting period,

(D) the prize amount paid to the winner of the raffle,

(E) the card drawn for each reporting period,

(F) the number of cards remaining after a card has been drawn, and

(G) where there is a winner, the winner’s name and ticket number, and

(ii) any rules of play established by the licensee for the raffle shall comply with

the following:

(A) tickets shall only be sold to individuals who are physically present in

the province at the point of sale,

(B) where tickets are sold throughout the week prior to the draw, or the

winner is not required to be present at the draw to win, the tickets shall

contain the player’s name and contact information and a draw process

shall be specified in the rules of play,

(C) where the target card is a joker, another card shall be removed from

the deck prior to the start of the draw,

(D) the licensee may seed a jackpot up to a maximum of $5,000.00, and

(E) a minimum of 50 per cent of the gross ticket sales for each draw shall

go to the licensee;

(b) where the raffle is a “Goldrush draw” raffle,

(i) the licensee shall submit to the Registrar, on a form provided by the Registrar,

a report each week, or other period specified by the Registrar, that includes

information respecting

(A) the beginning draw amount,

(B) the total ticket sales collected,

(C) the total pot amount,

(D) the total prize amount paid to the winning player,

(E) the winning number,

(F) the new pot amount, and

(G) the winning player’s name and ticket number, and

(ii) any rules of play established by the licensee for the Goldrush draw shall

comply with the following:

(A) to participate in weekly draws, a player shall register with and have a

number assigned by the licensee,

(B) the licensee shall place the player’s number in the draws either

manually or by electronic means,

(C) a player may pay each week or in advance at a location determined by

the licensee, which may include a selling location or purchasing and

payment online by means of a platform approved by the Registrar,

(D) a player shall pay prior to a draw to claim a prize from that draw,

(E) where a player’s number is drawn and the player has not paid for that

draw, the jackpot rolls over to the next draw,

(F) where a player indicates to the licensee, in writing, that the player does

not wish to retain the player’s number, the licensee shall remove the

number from the draw,

(G) where a player’s number is drawn and the player has previously

requested that the number be removed, the licensee shall draw a

subsequent number;

(c) where a licensee uses an electronic raffle system,

(i) the licensee shall ensure the electronic raffle system

(A) has been certified by an independent laboratory approved by the

Registrar, or a charitable lotteries regulatory authority of another

province using recognized standards, including but not limited to

(I) Gaming Laboratories International Standards (GLI-31)

Electronic Raffle Systems, or

(II) technical standards established by a charitable lotteries

regulatory authority of another province, if the Registrar is

satisfied that the technical standards substantially meet the

requirements referred to in subparagraph (I),

(B) is up to date, in good working order and has not been compromised or

altered in a way that would affect the integrity of the draw,

(C) has safety monitoring tools to control access and network security,

(D) ensures availability, by having processes to save and restore

applications and data, a disaster recovery plan, data redundancy and

incident management procedures,

(E) protects processing integrity by collecting and storing the entirety of

the data, recording all valid tickets in the draw, using audit journals to

document and track the activity and precisely recording and noting the

results of the draw,

(F) is the subject of a lifetime software development process, and

(G) uses a server situated in Canada,

(ii) during the conduct and management of the raffle, the licensee shall

(A) store the electronic raffle system securely and protect access to it at all

times,

(B) monitor the electronic raffle system, detect errors and intervene during

any unusual or suspicious activity,

(C) resolve any technical difficulties that occur during the raffle and may

affect its integrity,

(D) inform the Registrar of any incident that might affect the safety or

integrity of the raffle, and of the measures taken to correct it, and

(E) where changes are made to any critical component of the electronic

raffle system, ensure that the changes are certified in accordance with

paragraph (i)(A) and provide the Registrar with satisfactory evidence

of the certification,

(iii) where an electronic raffle system is used for the sale of tickets, the licensee

shall ensure that the system shall

(A) limit the period during which tickets may be purchased,

(B) have a means of ensuring that a player is located in the province and

is at least 19 years of age,

(C) allow for secure payments,

(D) have a means of ensuring that players agree to the privacy policies and

the rules of play and operation,

(E) protect players’ personal information in accordance with applicable

laws, and

(F) enable a ticket to be cancelled or reissued after it is sold,

(iv) where a random number generator is used to choose a winner, the licensee

shall ensure that the random number generator

(A) uses a proven and reliable algorithm to generate unpredictable random

numbers that are statistically independent and have the same odds of

being generated within the same series, and

(B) passes recognized statistical tests to show, with a high degree of

confidence, that the results produced by the random number generator

meet the conditions of randomness; and

(d) where a licensee uses any electronic or technologically facilitated components that are

not an electronic raffle system to conduct and manage a raffle, the licensee shall use

the components in accordance with the purposes approved by the Registrar in the

licence application.

## SCHEDULE D – INTERPROVINCIAL LOTTERIES

1. A licence issued pursuant to paragraph 207(1)(f) of the Criminal Code (Canada) is subject to

the following terms and conditions:

(a) the licensee shall make prizes available to each winning player or purchaser in the

province without imposing additional costs, fees or other expenses on the player or

purchaser;

(b) the licensee shall not offer a prize in the lottery scheme that has a legal, financial or

contractual term or restriction that limits the ability of a prize winner in the province

to enjoy, use or transfer the prize;

(c) the licensee shall submit to the Registrar, in the form approved by the Registrar, a

report each month or other period specified by the Registrar that includes information

respecting

(i) the prizes,

(ii) the draw dates,

(iii) the total ticket sales collected, including total ticket sales in the province,

(iv) the total prize amounts paid to the winning players,

(v) the winning numbers, and

(vi) the winning payers’ names and ticket numbers.
