PEI Reg EC846/78

*Made under the Reciprocal Enforcement Of Judgments Act*

Made by the Lieutenant Governor in Council under the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act

R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. R-6

### Reciprocating states

1. The provinces of

Alberta British Columbia

Manitoba New Brunswick

Newfoundland & Labrador Nova Scotia

Ontario Saskatchewan

are reciprocating states for the purposes of the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act.

### Idem

2. The territories known as the Yukon Territory and the Northwest Territories are declared to be

reciprocating states for the purposes of the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act.

### Idem

3. The state of Washington in the United States of America is declared to be a reciprocating

state for the purposes of the said Act.

### Idem

4. The territory of Canada to be known as Nunavut is declared to be a reciprocating state for the

purposes of the said Act.
