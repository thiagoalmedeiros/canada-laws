PEI Reg EC89/88

*Made under the Highway Traffic Act*

Pursuant to section 312 of the Highway Traffic Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-5, Council made the

following regulations:

### Driver Rehabilitation Program

1. Every person whose driver’s license is cancelled under section 261 of the Highway Traffic

Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. H-5 shall attend a Driver Rehabilitation Program as prescribed by

these regulations.

### Content of program

2. The Driver Rehabilitation Program

(a) shall be conducted at locations in Charlottetown, Summerside and Montague and

such other locations as the Minister may direct by an employee of the Highway

Safety Division appointed by the Director;

(b) shall consist of

(i) in the case of a first time offender, up to three hours of interviews, lectures,

films and discussions or, in lieu thereof, a personal interview,

(ii) in the case of a second time offender, up to eight hours of interviews,

lectures, films, discussions, demonstration projects and written assignments;

(c) may include such other elements for the rehabilitation of the offender as the Registrar

may direct.

### Record

3.(1) The program conductor shall create and maintain a record in respect of each person attending

a program.

### Recommendation

(2) The program conductor shall make a recommendation to the Registrar as to whether the

history of the use of alcohol by the person attending the program should preclude the issue to

that person of a driver’s license.

### Copies

(3) Upon request, a copy of the program record and the recommendation under subsection (2)

shall be made available to a person attending a program.

### Other conditions

4. The completion of a program does not affect other conditions of reinstatement of driving

privileges.
