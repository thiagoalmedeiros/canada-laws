PEI Reg EC705/04

*Made under the Wildlife Conservation Act*

Pursuant to section 28 of the Wildlife Conservation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. W-4.1, Council made

the following regulations:

### Definitions

1. In these regulations

(a) “Act” means the Wildlife Conservation Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. W-4.1;

(b) “license” means a license issued under section 2.

### Snowshoe hare snaring license

2.(1) The Minister may issue a snowshoe hare snaring license to an applicant who

(a) makes an application on a form approved by the Minister;

(b) is at least 12 years of age;

(c) pays the prescribed fee set out in subsection (6); and

(d) is not precluded from making an application under subsection (2).

### License suspended

(2) A person whose current license is suspended under subsection 31(2) of the Act may not make

an application for a new license under subsection (1) until the suspension has expired.

### Duration

(3) A license expires on March 31 following the date the license was issued.

### Authorization to snare

(4) A license authorizes the holder, during the term of the license, to snare snowshoe hares,

subject to the Act and these regulations.

### Prohibition

(5) No person shall snare or attempt to snare snowshoe hare unless the person holds a snowshoe

hare snaring license.

### Prescribed fee

(6) The prescribed fee payable for a license application is $5.00 (HST not included).

### Wire gauge

3.(1) No person shall set a snare for snowshoe hare unless the snare is made of a single strand of

copper or brass wire, commonly known as rabbit wire, that is of 20 gauge or 0.812 mm

(0.032 inches) or smaller.

### Wire loop

(2) No person shall set a snare for snowshoe hare with a wire loop greater than 10 cm (4 inches)

in diameter.

### Open season, limits, possession

4. The open season, daily limits and possession limits in respect of the snaring of snowshoe hare

are as follows:

Open Season Daily Limit Possession Limit

November 1 - February 28 no limit no limit
