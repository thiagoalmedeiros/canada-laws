PEI Reg EC562/92

*Made under the Companies Act*

Pursuant to section 81 of the Companies Act R.S.P.E.I. 1988, Cap. C-14, Council made the following

regulations:

### Fees

1. The following fees are prescribed:

(1) On applications for Letters Patent incorporating a company with share $250

capital (Part I companies)

(2) On applications for Letters Patent incorporating a company without $125

share capital (Part II companies)

(3) On applications for Supplementary Letters Patent $250

(4) On applications for a change of company name $250

(5) On amalgamations by Letters Patent of two or more companies $250

(6) On applications for revival of companies $250

(7) On applications for continuance of companies $250

(8) On applications for discontinuance of companies $250

(9) On applications for surrender of charter $125

(10) Name Searches

(a) for a level 1 search of corporate and business names in the

$40

Atlantic Provinces plus Federal corporations and trademarks,

using the Newly Upgraded Automated Name Search System

(b) for a level 2 search of corporate and business names throughout

$50

Canada plus trademarks, using the Newly Upgraded Automated

Name Search System

(11) For a certified copy of Letters Patent, Supplementary Letters Patent, $30

Certificate of Name Change, Certificate of Dissolution, Certificate of

Revival, Certificate of Continuance, Certificate of Discontinuance, or

Certificate of Good Standing

(12) For a copy of Letters Patent, Supplementary Letters Patent, Certificate of

Name Change, Certificate of Dissolution, Certificate of Revival,

$10

Certificate of Continuance or Certificate of Discontinuance

(13) For registration of the name of a company incorporated in any province $250

of Canada

(14) On filing an annual return by companies with share capital (Part I $30

companies)

(15) On filing an annual return by corporations without share capital (Part II $20

corporations)
