SS 1993, c C-51.01

An Act respecting Culture and Recreation

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Culture and Recreation Act, 1993.

### Interpretation

2 In this Act, “minister” means the member of the Executive Council to whom

for the time being the administration of this Act is assigned.

### Responsibilities of minister

3 The minister may:

(a) develop, initiate, conduct and encourage policies, programs and activities

that provide for the orderly cultural, physical and social development of

Saskatchewan or of any class of persons within Saskatchewan;

(b) conduct studies and surveys, collect information and compile statistics

with respect to:

(i) cultural, physical and social activities;

(ii) the facilities where those activities are held; and

(iii) the measures being taken to organize and promote those activities

and to provide facilities for the holding of any or all of those activities;

(c) promote the provision of additional facilities for the holding of physical,

cultural and social activities;

(d) disseminate information with respect to cultural, physical and social

development;

(e) plan, develop, construct, operate and maintain any facility for the

promotion of cultural, physical and social development;

(f) acquire artifacts or other items of historic or prehistoric interest;

(g) perform any other responsibilities and functions that the Lieutenant

Governor in Council may prescribe in the regulations.
