### Ecological Reserves Development Fund

6 The Lieutenant Governor in Council may authorize the minister to establish

a special fund, to be known as the Ecological Reserves Development Fund, from

moneys donated to the province for the purchase of land considered by the minister

to be suitable for an ecological reserve.
