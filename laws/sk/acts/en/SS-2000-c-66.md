SS 2000, c 66

An Act respecting a By-election in the Constituency of Wood River

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Wood River Constituency By-election Act.

### Delwood F. Huyghebaert deemed elected

2 Notwithstanding any other Act, with respect to the by-election held in the

constituency of Wood River on June 26, 2000 for the purpose of electing a member

of the Legislative Assembly, Delwood F. Huyghebaert is deemed to have been duly

elected at that by-election.

### Duties respecting election to be performed

3 All things and proceedings that are required by The Election Act, 1996, The

Controverted Elections Act or any other Act to be done or taken with respect to the

by-election described in section 2 are to be done or taken in the same manner that

they would have been done or taken, and when done or taken have the same effect

that they would have had, as if this Act had not been passed.

### Powers and privileges preserved

4 All things and proceedings that, pursuant to The Election Act, 1996, The

Controverted Elections Act or any other Act, may be done or taken with respect to

the by-election described in section 2 may be done or taken in the same manner

that they might have been done or taken, and when done or taken have the same

effect that they would have had, as if this Act had not been passed.

### Consequences if Delwood F. Huyghebaert determined not to be duly elected

5 If, after all things or proceedings mentioned in section 3 have been done or taken

or the time within which all things or proceedings mentioned in section 4 may be done

or taken has expired, it appears that Delwood F. Huyghebaert was not duly elected:

(a) section 2 is repealed; and

(b) Delwood F. Huyghebaert ceases to be a member of the Legislative

Assembly.

### Coming into force

6 This Act comes into force on assent.
