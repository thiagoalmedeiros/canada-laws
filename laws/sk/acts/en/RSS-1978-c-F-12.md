RSS 1978, c F-12

An Act respecting The Federal Court of Canada

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Federal Courts Act.

### Jurisdiction of court

2 The Federal Court of Canada has jurisdiction, in accordance with the Federal

Court Act (Canada), in cases of:

(a) controversies between Canada and Saskatchewan;

(b) controversies between any other province or territory of Canada, that

may have passed an Act or ordinance similar to this Act, and Saskatchewan.
