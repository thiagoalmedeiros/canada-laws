SS 2007, c R-23.01

An Act respecting the Royal Saskatchewan Museum

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Royal Saskatchewan Museum Act.

### Interpretation

2 In this Act:

(a) “Aboriginal sacred and culturally sensitive object” means any

object that:

(i) has been used in an Aboriginal ceremony;

(ii) reflects the spiritual power of an Aboriginal person;

(iii) was left as an offering in an Aboriginal ceremony or practice; or

(iv) has ongoing historical or cultural importance to an Aboriginal

community;

(b) “department” means the department over which the minister presides;

(c) “director” means the Director of the Royal Saskatchewan Museum

designated pursuant to subsection 3(2);

(d) “minister” means the member of Executive Council to whom for the time

being the administration of this Act is assigned;

(e) “museum” means the Royal Saskatchewan Museum continued pursuant

to subsection 3(1).

### Continuation of branch, director

3(1) The branch of the department called the Royal Saskatchewan Museum is

continued.

(2) The minister shall designate an employee of the department as the Director

of the Royal Saskatchewan Museum.

(3) The staff of the museum is to consist of the director and any other employees

that the minister considers necessary to carry out the mandate and activities of

the museum.

### Museum mandate and powers

4(1) Subject to the direction of the minister, the museum is responsible for

undertaking any measures that the museum considers advisable to increase

interest in, knowledge and understanding of, and appreciation and respect for

Saskatchewan’s natural history and Aboriginal cultures.

(2) For the purposes set out in subsection (1), the museum may do all or any of

the following:

(a) establish, study, document, preserve and exhibit collections of objects of

natural and cultural origin;

(b) generate and disseminate new knowledge;

(c) inform the public and the academic and scientific communities of its

discoveries and its understanding of Saskatchewan’s natural history and

Aboriginal cultures throughout Canada and the world through education,

exhibition and communication;

(d) use the museum’s collections and expertise to promote social and scientific

change and understanding;

(e) support the activities and programs of persons, institutions and

organizations with a purpose similar to its own by providing expert advice

and professional and technical services;

(f) perform any other functions that the minister considers advisable to carry

out the mandate of the museum.

### Power to charge fees, accept funds etc.

4.1 The museum may:

(a) Not yet in force.

(b) accept grants, payments, subscriptions, donations, gifts and bequests,

whether of real or personal property from any person, including funds

appropriated by the Legislature or by the Parliament of Canada, for any

purpose related to the fulfilment of the museum’s mandate and purposes; and

(c) subject to the terms of any trust imposed by the testator or donor of real

or personal property, sell, lease, convey or otherwise dispose of and convert

into money any real or personal property given or bequeathed for any purpose

related to the fulfilment of the museum’s mandate and purposes.

### Role of director

5(1) The director is responsible for implementing the mandate of the museum.

(2) Subject to The Heritage Property Act and any regulations made pursuant to

that Act, the director may do all or any of the following:

(a) collect specimens, artifacts, objects, other material evidence, and

information, originating primarily but not exclusively within Saskatchewan,

that illustrate and increase an understanding of Saskatchewan’s natural

history and Aboriginal cultures;

(b) develop and oversee the scientific and cultural research of the museum;

(c) maintain the museum’s collections by preservation, conservation and

restoration and by establishment of records and documentation;

(d) assume stewardship responsibility for the protection and preservation

of the palaeontological and archaeological objects under the ownership of the

Crown in right of Saskatchewan;

(e) exchange, give away, destroy or otherwise dispose of specimens, artifacts,

objects and other museum material within the museum’s collections:

(i) after considering the scientific and cultural significance of the objects

and the public interest in retaining the objects; and

(ii) in accordance with ethical and other standards adopted by the

museum;

(f) exchange with, lend to or borrow from any other person, institution

or organization specimens, artifacts, objects, exhibits, museum material,

knowledge or information.

### Aboriginal sacred and culturally sensitive objects

6(1) Subject to the direction of the minister and any regulations made pursuant

to this Act, and after consultation with Aboriginal peoples determined appropriate

by the minister, the museum is responsible for developing a policy to address the

concerns of Aboriginal peoples about the access to and the care, use and repatriation

of sacred and culturally sensitive objects originating with their cultures in the

museum collections.

(2) In administering its collections and in developing the policy mentioned in

subsection (1), the museum shall consider the following principles:

(a) Aboriginal peoples have a connection with Aboriginal sacred and culturally

sensitive objects, regardless of where those objects are held;

(b) Aboriginal sacred and culturally sensitive objects in the museum

collections are vital to the maintenance of traditional ways;

(c) the deeper meanings associated with Aboriginal sacred and culturally

sensitive objects are known only to Aboriginal members of their cultures of

origin.

### Regulations

7 The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations:

(a) defining, enlarging or restricting the meaning of any word or expression

used in this Act but not defined in this Act;

(b) respecting the policy mentioned in subsection 6(1);

(c) respecting any other matter or thing that the Lieutenant Governor in

Council considers necessary to carry out the intent of this Act.

### Coming into force

8 This Act comes into force on assent.
