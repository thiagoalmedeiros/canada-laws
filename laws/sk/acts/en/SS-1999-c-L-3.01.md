SS 1999, c L-3.01

An An Act to facilitate the Modernization of Land Information Services

in Saskatchewan and to amend The Land Titles Act

and The Land Surveys Act

## PART I

### Short Title

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Land Information Services Facilitation Act.

## PART II

### Amendments to The Land Titles Act

### RSS 1978, c L-5 amended

2 The Land Titles Act is amended in the manner set forth in this Part.

### Section 2 amended

3 Subsection 2(1) is amended:

(a) by adding the following clause after clause (a.1):

“(a.2) “corporation” means the Crown corporation created by the

Lieutenant Governor in Council pursuant to The Crown Corporations

Act, 1993 as the Crown corporation through which the minister shall

administer this Act and The Land Surveys Act”; and

(b) by adding the following clause after clause (m.1):

“(m.2) “minister” means the member of the Executive Council to whom

for the time being the administration of this Act is assigned”.

### New section 2.1

4 The following section is added after section 2:

“Administration of Act

2.1(1) The corporation is responsible to the minister for the performance of the

duties imposed on the corporation, and in the exercise of the powers conferred

on the corporation, for the purpose of administering this Act.

(2) All revenues derived from fees, levies, rates and other charges imposed

or collected pursuant to this Act are to be paid to and are the property of the

corporation, unless the Lieutenant Governor in Council directs that all or any

of the revenues be paid to the general revenue fund”.

### Section 9 amended

5 Section 9 is amended by striking out “or by the Minister of Justice” and

substituting “, the minister or the corporation”.

### Section 17 repealed

6 Section 17 is repealed.

### New section 17.1

7 The following section is added before section 18:

“Superannuation

17.1 The Public Service Superannuation Act and The Public Employees

Pension Plan Act apply, with any necessary modification, to the persons

employed by the corporation for the purpose of administering this Act”.

### Section 29 amended

8 Subsection 29(3) is amended by striking out “Minister of Justice” and

substituting “minister”.

### Section 33 amended

substituting “minister”.

## PART III

### Amendments to The Land Surveys Act

### RSS 1978, c L-4 amended

21 The Land Surveys Act is amended in the manner set forth in this Part.

### Section 2 amended

22 The following clause is added after clause 2(b):

“(b.1) ‘corporation’ means the Crown corporation created by the Lieutenant

Governor in Council pursuant to The Crown Corporations Act, 1993 as the

Crown corporation through which the minister shall administer this Act and

The Land Titles Act”.

### New section 3

23 Section 3 is repealed and the following substituted:

“Administration of Act

3 The corporation is responsible to the minister for the performance of the

duties imposed on the corporation, and in the exercise of the powers conferred

on the corporation, for the purpose of administering this Act”.

### New sections 4 and 4.01

24 Section 4 is repealed and the following substituted:

“Controller of Surveys

4 There may be appointed an officer to be known as the Controller of Surveys,

who shall be a Saskatchewan land surveyor and who, under the direction of

the minister and the corporation, shall administer the provisions of this Part

and the regulations made pursuant to this Part.

“Superannuation

4.01 The Public Service Superannuation Act and The Public Employees

Pension Plan Act apply, with any necessary modification, to the persons

employed by the corporation for the purpose of administering this Act”.

### Section 4.1 amended

25 Section 4.1 is amended in the portion preceding clause (a) by striking

out “minister” and substituting “corporation”.

### Section 5 amended

26 The following clause is added after clause 5(f):

“(f.1) respecting any matter necessarily incidental to the carrying out of

the corporation’s powers and the performance of its duties for the purpose of

administering this Act”.

### Section 43 amended

27 Subsection 43(1) is amended by striking out “director of the Central Survey

and Mapping Agency” and substituting “Controller of Surveys”.

### New section 48

28 Section 48 is repealed and the following substituted:

“Minister’s order approving plan

48(1) On a report of the Master of Titles, the minister may, by order:

(a) approve the plan;

(b) direct the Master of Titles to cause the order and plan to be registered

in the proper land titles office; and

(c) confirm the apportionment of costs made by the surveyor or, in

view of special circumstances peculiar to any particular case, confirm

the apportionment with any modification or alteration that the minister

deems advisable.

(2) An order of the minister pursuant to subsection (1) is final”.

### Section 97 amended

29 Subsection 97(2) is amended by striking out “, Controller of Surveys or

director of the Central Survey and Mapping Agency” and substituting “or the

Controller of Surveys”.

### Section 98 amended

30 Subsection 98(5) is amended by striking out “or the director of the Central

Survey and Mapping Agency” and substituting “or the Controller of Surveys”.

### Schedule

31 The provisions listed in the Schedule to this Act are amended by

striking out “Minister of Justice” wherever it appears and in each case

substituting “minister”.
