SS 2005, c P-29.1

An Act to raise awareness of Prostate Cancer in Saskatchewan

### Preamble

### Preamble

WHEREAS prostate cancer is the most commonly diagnosed cancer in men and

the third most common cause of cancer death in men;

WHEREAS a Canadian man has a 1 in 7 chance of developing prostate cancer in

his lifetime;

WHEREAS an estimated 20,500 men will be diagnosed with prostate cancer in

Canada in 2005 and an estimated 4,300 will die from the disease;

WHEREAS in Saskatchewan, an estimated 840 men will be diagnosed with prostate

cancer in 2005 and an estimated 250 men will die of the disease;

WHEREAS early detection and advances in treatment can save more lives each

year;

WHEREAS the Canadian Cancer Society in Saskatchewan has declared September

to be Prostate Cancer Awareness Month.

THEREFORE HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative

Assembly of Saskatchewan, enacts as follows:

### Short title

1 The Prostate Cancer Awareness Month Act.

### Prostate Cancer Awareness Month

2 The month of September of each year is designated as Prostate Cancer Awareness

Month in Saskatchewan.

### Coming into force

3 This Act comes into force on assent.
