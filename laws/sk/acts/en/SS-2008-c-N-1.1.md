SS 2008, c N-1.1

An Act to recognize National Peacekeepers

Day in Saskatchewan

### Preamble

### Preamble

WHEREAS Saskatchewan has been a loyal supporter of the United Nations

peacekeeping force;

WHEREAS Saskatchewan men and women have served on peacekeeping missions

around the world;

WHEREAS Saskatchewan peacekeepers died in the pursuit of peace and did not

return from their tour of duty; and

WHEREAS the largest number of Canadian peacekeepers lost their lives

participating in a peacekeeping operation while flying over Syria on August 9, 1974.

THEREFORE HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative

Assembly of Saskatchewan, enacts as follows:

### Short title

1 The National Peacekeepers Recognition Day Act.

### National Peacekeepers Awareness Day

2 August 9 each year is designated as National Peacekeepers Day in Saskatchewan.

### Coming into force

3 This Act comes into force on assent.
