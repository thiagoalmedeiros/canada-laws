SS 2006, c R-4.4

An Act to recognize Telemiracle in Saskatchewan

### Preamble

### Preamble

WHEREAS Telemiracle was founded in 1977 by the Kinsmen Foundation;

WHEREAS each year, over the last 30 years, Telemiracle has supported hundreds

of funding applications per year for medical travel, special needs equipment,

handi-vans, group homes, community organizations and institutions that provide

service to a wide range of Saskatchewan residents;

WHEREAS over 1,000 Kinsmen and Kinettes from across Saskatchewan volunteer

in Telemiracle along with television production, performers and other volunteers;

WHEREAS Telemiracle is a Saskatchewan fundraising phenomenon that has raised

over 68 million dollars in the last 30 years as a result of the extraordinary support

of the people of Saskatchewan.

THEREFORE HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative

Assembly of Saskatchewan, enacts as follows:

### Short title

1 The Recognition of Telemiracle Week Act.

### Recognition of Telemiracle Week

2 The week immediately before the Telemiracle television fundraiser in each year

is declared to be Telemiracle week in Saskatchewan.

### Coming into force

3 This Act comes into force on assent.
