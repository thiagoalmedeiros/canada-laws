SS 1988-89, c V-6.1

An Act to declare a Day of Mourning for Workers Killed

or Injured in the Course of their Employment

### Preamble

### Preamble

WHEREAS every year thousands of Saskatchewan working men and women become

victims of workplace injuries causing death or disability; and

WHEREAS the Legislative Assembly of Saskatchewan hereby recognizes the tragic

sacrifice made by those workers who are victims of workplace injuries; and

WHEREAS it is appropriate that a solemn day of mourning be declared in honour of

those who have been the victims of workplace injuries in Saskatchewan;

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Victims of Workplace Injuries Day of Mourning Act.

### April 28 Day of Mourning

2 April 28 of each year is hereby declared to be a day of mourning in Saskatchewan

for workers killed or injured in the course of their employment.
