RSS 1978, c S-2

An Act respecting the Sale or Lease of Certain Lands

belonging to the Crown

### Application of Act

1 This Act applies to the lands, being the property of the Crown set out in the

schedule and to any other lands belonging to the Crown which may from time to

time be added to the schedule by the Lieutenant Governor in Council.

### Sale of lands subject to conditions

2 Notwithstanding anything in The Land Titles Act, 2000, the Lieutenant Governor

in Council shall have power, in connection with the transfer or lease of any of the

lands set out in the schedule or any portion thereof, to provide that the transferee

or lessee of the same and the transferee’s or lessee’s assigns shall hold the same

subject to the conditions, restrictions and limitations, and subject to the performance

by the transferee or lessee and the transferee’s or lessee’s assigns of such covenants,

as may be provided by order in council.

### Effect of registration of order in council

3 Upon the granting of a transfer or lease an order in council shall be passed

setting out the conditions, restrictions, limitations and covenants that attach

thereto and an interest based on the order in council, accompanied by a copy of the

order in council, must be registered in the Land Titles Registry, and the conditions,

restrictions, limitations and covenants set out in the order in council shall attach

to the lands and shall be binding upon the transferee or lessee, the transferee’s or

lessee’s heirs and assigns, and all persons acquiring any interest in the land by way

of encumbrance, lien or otherwise shall hold the interest subject to those conditions,

restrictions, limitations and covenants.

### Taxes

4 The said lands shall, while the title thereto remains in the Crown, be subject to

such taxes as may be agreed upon by the Lieutenant Governor in Council with the

municipality in which such land is situated.

### Application of The Land Titles Act, 2000

5 Save as herein provided, the said lands shall be and remain subject to the

provisions of The Land Titles Act, 2000.
