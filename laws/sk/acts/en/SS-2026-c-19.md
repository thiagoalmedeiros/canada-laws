SS 2026, c 19

An Act recognizing the Heritages of Saskatchewan People

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Heritage Recognition (From Many Peoples,

Strength) Act.

### Definitions

2 In this Act:

“designated” means designated in the regulations; (« désigné »)

“heritage day” means a heritage day designated pursuant to this Act;

(« journée du patrimoine »)

“heritage month” means a heritage month designated pursuant to this Act;

(« mois du patrimoine »)

“heritage week” means a heritage week designated pursuant to this Act;

(« semaine du patrimoine »)

“minister” means the member of the Executive Council to whom for the time

being the administration of this Act is assigned; (« ministre »)

“prescribed” means prescribed in the regulations. (« réglementaire »)

### Purpose

3 The purpose of this Act is to recognize the strength Saskatchewan derives from

the heritages of its many peoples.

### Act does not apply

4 This Act does not apply to a day, week or month recognized, declared, observed

or proclaimed pursuant to another enactment.

### Heritage days, weeks and months

5(1) The Lieutenant Governor in Council may designate the following:

(a) a day as a heritage day;

(b) a week as a heritage week;

(c) a month as a heritage month.

(2) Unless otherwise prescribed by the Lieutenant Governor in Council, a heritage

day, heritage week or heritage month occurs on an annual basis until the designation

is repealed by regulation.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), the Lieutenant Governor in Council may

prescribe any rule or other measure for determining the timing or date of a heritage

day, heritage week or heritage month.

### Regulations

6 The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations:

(a) defining, enlarging or restricting the meaning of any word or expression

used in this Act but not defined in this Act;

(b) for the purposes of subsection 5(1), designating heritage days, heritage

weeks and heritage months;

(c) for the purposes of subsection 5(3), prescribing any rule or other measure

for determining the timing or date of a heritage day, heritage week or

heritage month;

(d) prescribing any matter or thing required or authorized by this Act to be

prescribed in the regulations;

(e) respecting any other matter or thing the Lieutenant Governor in Council

considers necessary to carry out the intent of this Act.

### Coming into force

7 This Act comes into force by order of the Lieutenant Governor in Council.
