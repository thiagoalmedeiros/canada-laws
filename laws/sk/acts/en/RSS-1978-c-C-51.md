RSS 1978, c C-51

An Act respecting Payment of Costs by and to the Crown in Certain

Actions and other Civil Proceedings

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Crown Suits (Costs) Act.

### Order for payment of costs by or to the Crown

2 In an action or other civil proceeding commenced before any court in Saskatchewan

by the Crown or by the Attorney General or any person on behalf of the Crown, or

in any other action or civil proceeding to which the Crown or the Attorney General

or any person on behalf of the Crown is a party:

(a) the costs of and incidental to the action or proceeding shall be in the

discretion of the court, to be exercised in the same manner and on the

same principles as between party and party; and

(b) the court may order payment of costs by or to the Crown in accordance

with the principles mentioned in clause (a).

### Recovery of costs by Her Majesty

3 When costs are awarded to the Crown, the same remedies as are authorized for

enforcing payment of costs as between subject and subject shall be available for

the recovery thereof.

### Recovery of costs from Her Majesty

4(1) When costs are awarded to a party against the Crown, that party shall obtain

from the taxing officer and forward to the Minister of Finance a certificate of taxation

stating the total amount of the costs as taxed.

(2) On receipt of the certificate mentioned in subsection (1), the Minister of

Finance shall pay out of the general revenue fund to the party mentioned in that

subsection the amount of the taxed costs.

### Application of Act

5 This Act does not apply to proceedings against the Crown under The Proceedings

Against the Crown Act, 2019.
