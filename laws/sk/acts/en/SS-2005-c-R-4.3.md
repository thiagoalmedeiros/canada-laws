SS 2005, c R-4.3

An Act to recognize the contributions of John George Diefenbaker

### Preamble

### Preamble

WHEREAS John George Diefenbaker served as the Prime Minister of Canada

from 1957 to 1963;

WHEREAS John George Diefenbaker’s family homesteaded in Saskatchewan and

he made Saskatchewan his home;

WHEREAS John George Diefenbaker was instrumental in passing the Agricultural

Stabilization Act (Canada) in 1958;

WHEREAS the First Canadian Bill of Rights was introduced in Parliament by John

George Diefenbaker and subsequently passed in 1960;

WHEREAS in 1960, during John George Diefenbaker’s Administration, the Canada

Election Act was amended to enfranchise First Nations people; and

WHEREAS September 18 marks the anniversary of the birth date of John George

THEREFORE HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative

Assembly of Saskatchewan, enacts as follows:

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as The Recognition of John George Diefenbaker Day Act.

### Recognition of John George Diefenbaker Day

2 September 18 of each year is hereby declared to be “John George Diefenbaker

Day” in Saskatchewan.

### Coming into force

3 This Act comes into force on assent.
