RRS c P-19 Reg 1

*Made under The Power Corporation Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Power Corporation Regulations.

### Range of nominal design voltages

2 The range of nominal design voltages within which a distribution of electrical

energy constitutes a “distribution” within the meaning of clause 1.1(c.1) of The Power

Corporation Act is from 0 kilovolts up to, but not including, 72 kilovolts.

### Annual payments

2.1(1) In this section:

“Act” means The Power Corporation Act;

“corporation’s gross revenues” means the corporation’s gross revenues

from all accounts of customers in Saskatchewan for the supply of energy, not

including exports.

(2) For the purposes of section 44.1 of the Act, the corporation shall, in each

financial year, pay an amount equal to 1.0% of the corporation’s gross revenues to

the Minister of Finance for deposit into the General Revenue Fund.

(3) The payment mentioned in subsection (2) must be paid at the time and in the

manner agreed to by the Minister of Finance and the corporation.

### Moving poles or structures at the request of a municipality

2.5(1) In this section, “poles or structures” means poles, structures, wires,

conduits, pipes

or other parts of the corporation’s electrical system.

(2) If a municipality proposes to pave, widen or otherwise alter any highway, road,

street, lane or other public place on or under which the corporation has placed

its poles or structures with the result that it is necessary that the corporation

relocate those poles or structures, the municipality shall request in writing that

the corporation relocate the poles or structures.

(3) If a municipality requests that poles or structures be relocated pursuant to

subsection (2), the corporation may notify the municipality of the corporation’s costs

in writing and may require the municipality to reimburse the corporation for all

or part of the corporation’s costs incurred in connection with the relocation, as a

condition of undertaking the relocation.

(4) Within a reasonable time after receiving a request from a municipality pursuant

to subsection (2), the corporation shall relocate the poles or structures.

### Coming into force

3(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day on

which section 8 of The Power Corporation Amendment Act, 2004 comes into force.

(2) If section 8 of The Power Corporation Amendment Act, 2004 comes into force

before these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations, these regulations

come into force on the day on which they are filed with the Registrar of Regulations.
