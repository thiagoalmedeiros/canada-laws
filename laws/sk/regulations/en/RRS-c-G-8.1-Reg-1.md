RRS c G-8.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Growth and Financial Security Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Growth and Financial Security Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Growth and Financial Security Act;

(b) “fiscal year” means the period commencing on April 1 in one year and

ending on March 31 in the following year.

### Determination to be based on fiscal year

3(1) For the purposes of subsection 33(2) of the Act, Treasury Board shall determine

for each fiscal year whether the public service is growing or diminishing as a

percentage of the population of Saskatchewan.

(2) The determination mentioned in subsection (1) must be made within 90 days

after the end of each fiscal year.

(3) In making the determination mentioned in subsection (1) for a fiscal year,

Treasury Board shall:

(a) determine the size of the public service for that fiscal year in accordance

with section 4; and

(b) use the determination of the population of Saskatchewan for that fiscal

year mentioned in section 5.

### Method of determining size of public service

4(1) The size of the public service for a fiscal year is to be calculated in accordance

with this section.

(2) For each employee in the public service, the employee’s employment in the

fiscal year is to be converted to a full-time equivalency calculated in accordance

with the following formula:

P

FTE =

H

where:

FTE is the full-time equivalency for the employee’s employment in the fiscal

year;

P is the total number of hours respecting employment for which the employee

was paid in the fiscal year, as determined by the Public Service Commission

and as approved by Treasury Board; and

H is the total number of hours respecting employment that the employee

would normally be paid for if the employee worked full time in the fiscal year,

as determined by the Public Service Commission and as approved by Treasury

Board.

(3) The size of the public service in the fiscal year is the sum of the full-time

equivalencies for all employees in the public service calculated in accordance with

subsection (2).

### Method of determining population of Saskatchewan

5 The population of Saskatchewan for a fiscal year is the population as determined

by the Chief Statistician of Canada on the basis of Statistics Canada’s official

estimate of the population of Saskatchewan on July 1 of that fiscal year.

### Size of the public service as a percentage of the population

6 The size of the public service as a percentage of the population of Saskatchewan

for a fiscal year is to be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

where:

S is the size of the public service as a percentage of the population of

Saskatchewan for the fiscal year;

SFTE is the size of the public service as determined in accordance with section 4

for the fiscal year; and

POP is the population of Saskatchewan as determined in accordance with

section 5 for the fiscal year.

### Coming into force

7 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
