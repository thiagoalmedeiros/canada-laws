RRS c C-50.102 Reg 1

*Made under The Crown Corporations Public Ownership Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Crown Corporations Public Ownership

Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Crown Corporations Public Ownership Act.

### Definition of Crown corporation - section 2 of the Act

3 For the purposes of subclause 2(b)(xiii) of the Act, Lotteries and Gaming

Corporation Saskatchewan is added as a Crown corporation.

### Coming into force

4 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with

the Registrar of Regulations.
