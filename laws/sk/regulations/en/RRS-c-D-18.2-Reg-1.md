RRS c D-18.2 Reg 1

*Made under The Department of Justice Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Community Service Order Program

Regulations.

### Agreements authorized

2 For the purposes of section 12 of The Department of Justice Act, the minister is

authorized to enter into agreements for the purpose of implementing and facilitating

a Community Service Order Program by which community service work that is

ordered by a court to be performed as a term of a probation order may be carried out.
