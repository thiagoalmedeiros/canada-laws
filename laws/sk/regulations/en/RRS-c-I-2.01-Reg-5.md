RRS c I-2.01 Reg 5

*Made under The Income Tax Act, 2000*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Rental Housing Rebate Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 The deemed profit margin mentioned in the description PM in clause 64.2(10)(b) of The Income

Tax Act, 2000 is 30%.

### Coming into force

3 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the Registrar of

Regulations.
