# The Multi-lateral Oil Well Program Regulations

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Office of the King's Printer, Saskatchewan. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Saskatchewan. Text obtained from the [official source](https://publications.saskatchewan.ca/api/v1/products/123648/formats/143925/download) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

RRS c C-50.2 Reg 37

*Made under The Crown Minerals Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Multi-lateral Oil Well Program Regulations.

### Definitions

2(1) In these regulations:

“applicant” means:

(a) a person who is a licensee, as defined in section 2 of The Oil and Gas

Conservation Act, of a multi-lateral oil well; or

(b) any other person approved by the minister;

“appraisal multi-lateral oil well” means a multi-lateral oil well approved by

the minister in accordance with section 5 as an appraisal multi-lateral oil well;

“approved” means approved by the minister for the purposes of these

regulations;

“kick-off point” means the point at which the drilling string has been pulled

back on a horizontal well to begin the drilling of a subsequent horizontal

wellbore;

“lateral” means a horizontal section with a lateral length greater than 200

metres;

“lateral length” means the lesser of:

(a) the length from the kick-off point to the termination point of the

lateral; and

(b) the length from the initial point of penetration into the productive

zone to the termination point of the lateral;

“multi-lateral oil well” means a horizontal oil well:

(a) that has more than 2 laterals; and

(b) that is not part of an EOR project;

“program” means the Multi-lateral Oil Well Program established pursuant

to section 3.

(2) For the purposes of these regulations, unless otherwise defined in these

regulations, the definitions in sections 2 and 12 of The Crown Oil and Gas Royalty

Regulations, 2012 and sections 2, 6 and 11 of The Freehold Oil and Gas Production

Tax Regulations, 2012 apply, with any necessary modification.

### Program established

3(1) The Multi-lateral Oil Well Program is established.

(2) The minister shall administer the program in accordance with these regulations.

### Application for multi-lateral oil well incentive

4(1) An applicant may apply for an incentive volume pursuant to the program by

submitting an application to the minister that is in a form acceptable to the minister

and that contains any information that the minister may require.

(2) A multi-lateral oil well with respect to which an application is being made

must have a finished drilling date on or after April 1, 2024 and before April 1, 2028.

(3) On receipt of an application made pursuant to subsection (1), the minister:

(a) may approve the application if the minister is satisfied that the applicant

meets the requirements of these regulations and that it is in the public interest

to do so; or

(b) may refuse the application.

(4) If the minister makes a decision pursuant to subsection (3), the minister shall

notify the applicant of the minister’s decision and, in the case of a decision pursuant

to clause (3)(b), provide written reasons for the decision.

(5) The minister may impose any terms and conditions on an approval pursuant

to clause (3)(a) that the minister considers reasonable.

(6) The minister may waive the requirement for an application.

### Application for appraisal multi-lateral oil well

5(1) An applicant may apply for an appraisal multi-lateral oil well approval and

related incentive volume pursuant to the program by submitting an application

to the minister that is in a form acceptable to the minister and that contains any

information that the minister may require.

(2) On receipt of an application pursuant to subsection (1), the minister:

(a) may approve the application if the minister is satisfied that the applicant

meets the requirements of these regulations and that it is in the public interest

to do so; or

(b) may refuse the application.

(3) If the minister makes a decision pursuant to subsection (2), the minister shall

notify the applicant of the minister’s decision and, in the case of a decision pursuant

to clause (2)(b), provide written reasons for the decision.

(4) The minister may impose any terms and conditions on an approval pursuant

to clause (2)(a) that the minister considers reasonable.

(5) An applicant for an appraisal multi-lateral oil well must provide to the minister:

(a) the location of the multi-lateral oil well that is to be considered as the

appraisal multi-lateral oil well; and

(b) the locations of at least 10 multi-lateral oil wells that are to be drilled in

addition to the appraisal multi-lateral oil well.

(6) For the purposes of subsection (5), the multi-lateral oil wells are to be located

on contiguous tracts of mineral lands in the same zone.

(7) An applicant must not include more than 1 appraisal multi-lateral well in an

application.

(8) The appraisal multi-lateral oil well must be the first multi-lateral oil well

drilled within the mineral lands included in the application.

(9) The applicant must not start drilling the appraisal multi-lateral oil well that

is the subject of the application mentioned in subsection (1) until the minister

approves that application.

(10) An appraisal multi-lateral oil well must have a finished drilling date on or

after April 1, 2024 and before April 1, 2028.

(11) For the purposes of subsection (5), the multi-lateral oil wells that are not

considered to be appraisal multi-lateral oil wells are to have a finished drilling date

within 48 months after the finished drilling date of the appraisal multi-lateral oil

well.

(12) If an applicant fails to comply with the conditions of the approval, the

minister may revoke the multi-lateral oil well incentive assigned to the appraisal

multi-lateral oil well.

(13) Notwithstanding subsections (1) to (12), the minister may approve any

multi-lateral oil well as an appraisal multi-lateral oil well.

### Approval of multi-lateral oil well incentive

6(1) If the minister approves an application pursuant to section 4 or 5, the minister

shall, with respect to a multi-lateral oil well or an appraisal multi-lateral oil well

drilled on or after April 1, 2024 and before April 1, 2028, provide a successful

applicant one multi-lateral oil well incentive.

(2) The multi-lateral oil well incentive is to be based on the configuration of the

subject oil well as of the date on which the oil well is placed on production, as follows:

(a) in the case of a multi-lateral oil well, according to Table 1 of the Appendix;

(b) in the case of an appraisal multi-lateral oil well, according to Table 2 of

the Appendix.

### Calculation of royalties and production tax

7(1) With respect to oil produced from a multi-lateral oil well or appraisal

multi-lateral oil well allocated to any Crown lands for which the minister has

approved a multi-lateral oil well incentive pursuant to section 6:

(a) the royalties are to be determined and paid in accordance with The Crown

Oil and Gas Royalty Regulations, 2012;

(b) the oil is deemed to be fourth tier oil; and

(c) the Crown royalty rate is the lesser of the fourth tier oil Crown royalty

rate calculated pursuant to clause 10(a) of The Crown Oil and Gas Royalty

Regulations, 2012 and 2.5%, with respect to the portion of oil produced from

or allocated to Crown lands that is included in the incentive volume approved

under the program and that is not incremental waterflood oil.

(2) With respect to oil produced from a multi-lateral oil well or appraisal

multi-lateral oil well allocated to any freehold lands for which the minister has

approved a multi-lateral oil well incentive pursuant to section 6:

(a) the taxes are to be determined and paid in accordance with The Freehold

Oil and Gas Production Tax Regulations, 2012;

(b) the oil is deemed to be fourth tier oil; and

(c) the tax rate is the lesser of the fourth tier oil tax rate calculated pursuant

to clause 9(a) of The Freehold Oil and Gas Production Tax Regulations, 2012

and the amount, if any, by which 2.5% exceeds the PTF, with respect to the

portion of oil produced from or allocated to freehold lands that is included in

the incentive volume approved under the program and that is not incremental

waterflood oil.

### Reduction of multi-lateral oil well incentive volume

8 If a multi-lateral oil well or appraisal multi-lateral oil well becomes part of an

EOR project, the volume of oil that is applicable to the multi-lateral oil well for the

purposes of section 6, and any other volume of oil that has already been assigned to

the well as an incentive, are to be reduced by the minister in the same proportion

as the total investment, as the case may be, within the meaning of:

(a) clauses 30(2)(j) and (3)(f) of The Crown Oil and Gas Royalty

Regulations, 2012 related to the drilling of the multi-lateral oil well or appraisal

multi-lateral oil well that is included in calculating the royalty rate pursuant

to clause 32(a) of those regulations; and

(b) clauses 29(2)(j) and (3)(f) of The Freehold Production Tax Regulations, 2012

related to the drilling of the multi-lateral oil well or appraisal multi-lateral oil

well that is included in calculating the freehold production tax rate pursuant

to clause 31(a) of those regulations.

### Publication of policies

9 If the minister establishes policies for the purposes of these regulations, the

minister shall cause those policies to be made public in any manner that the minister

considers appropriate, including by publishing them on the ministry’s website.

### Coming into force

10 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations, but are retroactive and are deemed to have been in force

on and from April 1, 2024.
