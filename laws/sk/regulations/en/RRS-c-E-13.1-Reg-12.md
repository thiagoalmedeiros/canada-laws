RRS c E-13.1 Reg 12

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Executive Government Administration

Exemption Regulations.

### No annual return required

2 Section 12 of The Executive Government Administration Act does not apply to:

(a) the minister responsible for the administration of The Provincial

Secretary’s Act in connection with work performed by the minister pursuant

to that Act; or

(b) the President of the Executive Council in connection with work performed

by the Executive Council.

### Approval for certain contracts not required

3 Subsection 18(2) of The Executive Government Administration Act does not apply

with respect to agreements for the provision of goods and services that are routinely

required for the normal operation of a ministry.

### RRS c G-5.1 Reg 18 repealed

4 The Government Organization Exemption Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
