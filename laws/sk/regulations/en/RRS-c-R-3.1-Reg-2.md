RRS c R-3.1 Reg 2

*Made under The Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act, 1996*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments

Regulations, 1999.

### Reciprocating states

2 The Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act, 1996 applies to the following

jurisdictions:

(a) British Columbia;

(b) Alberta;

(c) Manitoba;

(d) Ontario;

(e) Nova Scotia;

(f) New Brunswick;

(g) Prince Edward Island;

(h) Newfoundland;

(i) Yukon Territory;

(j) Northwest Territories;

(k) Nunavut.

### R.R.S. c.R-3.1 Reg 1 repealed

3 The Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Regulations, 1997 are repealed.
