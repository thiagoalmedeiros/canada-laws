RRS c E-13.1 Reg 9

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Energy and Resources

Regulations, 2018.

### Ministry established

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry of Energy

and Resources is established.

### Objects and purposes

3 The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Energy and Resources are to provide

the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities and functions of

the Minister of Energy and Resources may be exercised and carried out in relation

to the following:

(a) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan for the economic and orderly development

of minerals and mineral resources of Saskatchewan;

(b) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan related to:

(i) the exploration for, development of and management of non-renewable

resources; and

(ii) the development and management of energy;

(c) to work together with businesses, communities, governments and

ministries to support and encourage the growth and development of the

Saskatchewan forestry industry;

(d) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan:

(i) to enhance the competitiveness of the Saskatchewan forest industry;

(ii) to increase investment in the Saskatchewan forest industry; and

(iii) to support and encourage value-added production in the

Saskatchewan forest industry.

### Coming into force

4 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
