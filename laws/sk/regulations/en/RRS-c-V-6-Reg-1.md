RRS c V-6 Reg 1

*Made under The Veterinary Services Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Veterinary Services Grant Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “animal hospital or clinic” means any building, in a district, that has

been constructed or renovated to provide for the surgical or medical treatment

of animals together with any office and dispensary accommodation attached

to that building;

(b) “fiscal year” means the period commencing on April 1 in one year and

ending on March 31 in the following year.

### Annual grants

3(1) Where the minister is satisfied that satisfactory veterinary service is being

provided in a district, he may make a grant to the board of the district in each fiscal

year in an amount not exceeding:

(a) in the case of a district consisting of:

(i) at least nine townships that are served by one veterinarian; or

(ii) at least nine townships but fewer than 27 townships that are served

by more than one veterinarian;

the amounts determined in accordance with Table 1;

(b) in the case of a district consisting of at least 27 townships that are served

by more than one veterinarian, the amounts determined in accordance with

## Table 2.

(2) Where a district is only in operation for a portion of the fiscal year to which

a grant payable pursuant to subsection (1) relates, the minister shall prorate the

amount of the grant.

### Disposition of unexpended grant funds

4(1) Where a district ceases operating before the end of the fiscal year, the

secretary of the board for the district shall furnish the minister with a statement

of unexpended funds.

(2) The disposition of unexpended funds is subject to the directions of the minister,

and, if no submission is received by him to disorganize the district, he may direct,

after all debts have been paid, that the balance of funds remain in trust until

the district again becomes operative or that the balance of funds be refunded

to the parties that would have been entitled to it on the making of an order for

disorganization.

5 Repealed. 20 Jly 84 SR 83/84 s3.

7 Repealed. 20 Jly 84 SR 83/84 s3.

### Sales, etc., of animal hospital or clinic

9 The sale, or change in the use, of an animal hospital or clinic for which a grant

has been provided pursuant to these regulations or any former grant regulations

under The Veterinary Services Act is subject to the approval of the minister.
