RRS c E-13.1 Reg 30

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

## PART 1

### Preliminary Matters

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Apprenticeship Training Allowance

Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“apprentice” means an apprentice as defined in The Apprenticeship and

Trade Certification Act, 2019;

“apprenticeship training allowance” means a living away from home

allowance, a living allowance, or both, paid to apprentices in accordance with

these regulations;

“apprenticeship training program” means a technical training program

pursuant to The Apprenticeship and Trade Certification Act, 2019 or the

regulations pursuant to that Act;

“approved”means approved by the minister;

“commission” means the Saskatchewan Apprenticeship and Trade

Certification Commission continued pursuant to The Apprenticeship and Trade

Certification Act, 2019;

“Crown” means the Crown in right of Saskatchewan;

“dependent child” means a person who:

(a) is the child of the apprentice or the apprentice’s spouse;

(b) resides with the apprentice or the apprentice’s spouse; and

(c) has a gross income of not more than $280 per week;

“dependent spouse” means a person who:

(a) either:

(i) is the legal spouse of the apprentice; or

(ii) has cohabited with the apprentice:

(A) continuously for a period of at least 1 year and has

represented themselves as the apprentice’s spouse; or

(B) in a relationship of some permanence, if that person and

the apprentice are the parents of a child; and

(b) has a gross income of not more than $280 per week;

“EI client” means an EI Client as defined in the Canada-Saskatchewan

Labour Market Development Agreement entered into on or about

February 6, 1988 by the Government of Canada and the Government of

Saskatchewan, as amended from time to time;

“minister” means the member of the Executive Council to whom for the time

being the administration of these regulations is assigned;

“ministry” means the ministry over which the minister presides;

“record” includes any information that is recorded or stored in any medium

or by means of any device, including a computer and its hard drive or any

electronic media;

“social insurance number” means a social insurance number assigned to

a person pursuant to the authority of an Act of the Parliament of Canada.

## PART 2

### Apprenticeship Training Allowance

## DIVISION 1

### Eligibility

### Living away from home allowance

3 To be eligible to receive a living away from home allowance, a person must:

(a) be an apprentice;

(b) have a valid social insurance number;

(c) be legally entitled to work in Saskatchewan;

(d) be enrolled in an approved apprenticeship training program;

(e) be eligible to receive employment insurance pursuant to the Employment

Insurance Act (Canada) or be an EI client; and

(f) maintain a second residence that is located 50 kilometres or more from

the apprentice’s primary residence.

### Living allowance

4 To be eligible to receive a living allowance, a person must:

(a) be an apprentice;

(b) have a valid social insurance number;

(c) be legally entitled to work in Saskatchewan;

(d) be enrolled in an approved apprenticeship training program; and

(e) have received employment insurance pursuant to the Employment

Insurance Act (Canada) within the previous 60 months or, at the date of

application, be ineligible to receive employment insurance.

### Out-of-province programs

5 For the purposes of these regulations, the minister may approve an apprenticeship

training program that is offered outside Saskatchewan if an equivalent program is

not available in Saskatchewan.

### Ineligibility re allowance

6 The minister may deny an apprenticeship training allowance to a person who

has an outstanding overpayment for an apprenticeship training allowance.

## DIVISION 2

### Application

### Application

7 An apprentice may apply to the minister for an apprenticeship training allowance

if the apprentice:

(a) meets the eligibility requirements set out in section 3 or 4, or both, as

the case may be; and

(b) submits the following to the minister within the period specified by the

minister:

(i) an application in the form and manner specified by the minister;

(ii) any authorization or acknowledgement that the minister may

require;

(iii) any release for information or records that the minister may require;

(iv) in the case of an apprentice applying for a living away from home

allowance, the address of the second residence mentioned in clause 3(f);

(v) any other information or record that the minister may reasonably

require.

## DIVISION 3

### Payments

### Payment

8(1) On receipt of an application pursuant to section 7 and subject to sections 9

and 10, the minister may authorize the payment of an apprenticeship training

allowance to an apprentice if the apprentice meets the requirements set out in

section 3 or 4, or both, as the case may be.

(2) The total of the periods for which an apprenticeship training allowance may

be paid is not to exceed 36 months during the lifetime of the person to whom the

apprenticeship training allowance is being paid.

(3) An apprenticeship training allowance is to be paid every 2 weeks.

### Living away from home allowance re amount

9 The amount of a living away from home allowance to be paid to an eligible

apprentice is:

(a) $250 per week in the case of an apprentice whose principal residence or

whose secondary residence for the purpose of attending an apprenticeship

training program is located in the Northern Saskatchewan Administration

District; and

(b) $200 per week in any other case.

### Living allowance re amount

10 The amount of a living allowance to be paid to an eligible apprentice is:

(a) $196 per week if the apprentice has no dependants;

(b) $232 per week if the apprentice has 1 dependant (dependent child or

dependent spouse);

(c) $246 per week if the apprentice has 2 dependants (dependent children or

dependent spouse);

(d) $259 per week if the apprentice has 3 dependants (dependent children

or dependent spouse); and

(e) $347 per week if the apprentice has 4 or more dependants (dependent

children or dependent spouse).

### Change in circumstances

11(1) No person who is receiving an apprenticeship training allowance shall fail to

immediately notify the minister of any change in circumstances that might affect:

(a) the person’s continued eligibility to receive an apprenticeship training

allowance; or

(b) the amount of the person’s apprenticeship training allowance.

(2) The minister, on being notified that there has been a change in a person’s

circumstances, may vary the amount of or cancel the person’s apprenticeship

training allowance.

(3) If a person fails to comply with subsection (1), the minister may:

(a) vary the amount of or cancel the person’s apprenticeship training

allowance; and

(b) declare any apprenticeship training allowance, or any portion of an

apprenticeship training allowance, paid after the date of the change in

circumstances to be an overpayment.

## PART 3

### Administrative Matters

### Agreements with commission

12(1) The minister may enter into an agreement with the commission to have the

commission or its employees do all or any of the following:

(a) receive applications for apprenticeship training allowances;

(b) verify the information that is provided on applications for apprenticeship

training allowances;

(c) hold funds to be paid as apprenticeship training allowances in trust for

the Crown;

(d) pay apprenticeship training allowances on behalf of the Crown from the

funds mentioned in clause (c);

(e) report to the minister within the times and in the manner required by the

minister respecting apprenticeship training allowances paid by the commission

on behalf of the Crown pursuant to clause (d).

(2) In an agreement mentioned in subsection (1), the minister may delegate to the

commission or its employees the exercise of the minister’s discretion with respect to

applications for apprenticeship training allowances, on a case-by-case basis, within

the parameters set out in these regulations respecting:

(a) the criteria to be applied when assessing applications for apprenticeship

training allowances; and

(b) the amount and duration of apprenticeship training allowances.

### Limits on funding

13 The minister may limit funding in any area or region of Saskatchewan.

### Personal financial assistance limit

14 The minister has the right to limit the total amount of financial assistance paid

to a person pursuant to these regulations.

### Prohibitions

15(1) No person shall make a representation on an application for an apprenticeship

training allowance that the person knows to be false or misleading.

(2) No person shall make a declaration or acknowledgement on an application for

an apprenticeship training allowance that the person knows is false or misleading

due to the non-disclosure of any fact.

(3) No person shall, without reasonable excuse, fail to attend, fail to carry out or

fail to complete an apprenticeship training program for which the person receives

an apprenticeship training allowance.

### Audit

16 A person receiving an apprenticeship training allowance shall provide, at the

minister’s request, any information or record that the minister reasonably requires

respecting the person’s application and eligibility for an apprenticeship training

allowance.

### Overpayment

17(1) If a person receiving an apprenticeship training allowance contravenes any

provision of these regulations, the minister may declare all or any part of a payment

received by the person pursuant to these regulations to be an overpayment.

(2) If a person fails to complete an apprenticeship training program for which

an apprenticeship training allowance is paid, the minister may declare that all or

any part of a payment received by the person pursuant to these regulations for the

week in which the person ceased to continue in the program and for any subsequent

weeks to be overpayments.

(3) An overpayment made to a person receiving an apprenticeship training

allowance is a debt due and owing to the Crown and may be recovered from the

person in any manner authorized by The Financial Administration Act, 1993 or in

any other manner authorized by law.

## PART 4

### Repeal, Transitional and Coming into Force

### RRS c G-5.1 Reg 85 repealed

18 The Skills Training Benefit Regulations are repealed.

### Transitional

19(1) In this section:

“former regulations” means The Skills Training Benefit Regulations as those

regulations existed on the day before the coming into force of these regulations;

“living allowance” means, with any necessary modification, a living allowance

within the meaning of Part III.1 of the former regulations;

“living away from home allowance” means, with any necessary modification,

a living away from home allowance within the meaning of Part III.1 of the

former regulations.

(2) Notwithstanding the repeal of the former regulations, the former regulations

continue to apply to every living away from home allowance or living allowance

that was approved pursuant to the former regulations but not paid out in full by

the minister before the coming into force of these regulations.

(3) Every application for a living away from home allowance or a living allowance

made pursuant to the former regulations but not considered by the minister before

the coming into force of these regulations is continued and shall be dealt with

pursuant to these regulations as if the application were made pursuant to these

regulations.

(4) Every agreement that was entered into pursuant to the former regulations

and that was in force immediately before the coming into force of these regulations

continues in force and shall be dealt with pursuant to these regulations as if the

agreement were entered into pursuant to these regulations.

### Coming into force

20 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
