RRS c A-28.001 Reg 1

*Made under The Arts Board Act, 1997*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Arts Board Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Arts Board Act, 1997;

(b) “fiscal year” means the period commencing on April 1 in one year and

ending on March 31 in the following year.

### Nominating committee

3(1) For the purpose of subsections 14(2) and (3) of the Act, the board of directors

shall establish a nominating committee, to be composed of the following members:

(a) two persons from the arts community, appointed by the arts community;

(b) one member of the board of directors, appointed by the board of directors;

(c) one person appointed by the minister, who shall not be a member of the

board of directors.

(2) The nominating committee shall:

(a) solicit and receive nominations from the arts community for appointment

to the board of directors; and

(b) within 30 days after requested to do so by the minister, provide the minister

with a list of the nominees.

### Equity investments

4(1) For the purposes of subsection 24(2) of the Act, subject to subsections (2)

and (3), the Arts Board may invest in the following classes of equity investments

where the Arts Board acquires an ownership interest in the personal property

resulting from the arts project or undertaking:

(a) arts projects or undertakings carried out by an individual who has resided

in Saskatchewan for the 12 months preceding the application to the Arts Board

for funding;

(b) arts projects or undertakings carried out by a body corporate that has

its head office in Saskatchewan and is incorporated or continued pursuant to

one of the following Acts:

(i) The Business Corporations Act;

(ii) The Non-profit Corporations Act, 1995;

(iii) The Co-operatives Act, 1989.

(2) The Arts Board shall not make any equity investment for the purpose of retiring

the debt of the applicant.

(3) The maximum amount that the Arts Board invests in equity investments

in any fiscal year shall not exceed 10% of the Art Board’s appropriation from the

Legislature for that fiscal year.
