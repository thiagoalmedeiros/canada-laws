RRS c E-13.1 Reg 22

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Highways Regulations, 2020.

### Ministry continued

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry of Highways

and Infrastructure is continued as the Ministry of Highways.

### Objects and purposes

3 The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Highways are the following:

(a) to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities

and functions of the Minister of Highways may be exercised and carried out;

(b) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to transportation services and

facilities;

(c) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan relating to:

(i) water, sewage and other infrastructure and facilities of municipalities;

and

(ii) the infrastructure program of the Government of Canada;

(d) to collect, assimilate and publish information concerning matters relating

to transportation;

(e) to provide information to municipalities respecting the planning, design,

construction, improvement and maintenance of public highways;

(f) to coordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan relating to roads, bridges, ferries and general

transportation in or affecting municipalities.

### RRS c G-5.1 Reg 140 repealed

4 The Ministry of Highways and Infrastructure Regulations, 2007 are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
