RRS c P-1.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Parks Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Historic Sites Regulations.

### Constitution of historic sites

2 The lands described in the Appendix are hereby constituted historic sites to be

known by the names set out in the Appendix.

## Appendix

BUFFALO RUBBING STONE HISTORIC SITE

Parcel “A” in the north-east quarter of Section 8, in Township 29, in Range 26, west

of the Third Meridian, as shown on Plan No. 63 S 15284 in the Land Titles Office

for the Saskatoon Land Registration District.

CHIMNEY COULEE HISTORIC SITE

Parcel “A” in the south-east quarter of Section 20, in Township 7, in Range 21, west

of the Third Meridian, as shown on Plan No. 77 SC 08629 in the Land Titles Office

for the Swift Current Land Registration District.

FORT PELLY #1 HISTORIC SITE

Parcel “C”, being part of the Hudson’s Bay Company Reserve of Fort Pelly, as shown

on Plan No. 73 Y 08155 in the Land Titles Office for the Yorkton Land Registration

District excluding 0.03 hectares taken as roadway as shown on Plan No. 84 Y 00293

in that office;

All that portion of the north-east quarter of Section 25, in Township 32, in

Range 33, west of the First Meridian, lying to the north of the Hudson’s Bay Company

Reserve and containing 11.60 acres, more or less, according to a Plan of Survey of

said Township, dated at Ottawa on the 7th day of August, A.D. 1913, including

the undeveloped road allowance lying to the west of this area and excluding 0.45

hectares taken for roadway as shown on Plan No. 84 Y 00293 in that office; and

Parcel “A”, being part of Hudson’s Bay Company Reserve of Fort Pelly, as shown on

a Plan No. B.U. 1771 in the Land Titles Office for the Yorkton Land Registration

District and containing 2.44 acres, more or less, excluding 0.02 hectares taken as

roadway as shown on Plan No. 84 Y 00293 in that office.

FORT QU’APPELLE - TOUCHWOOD HILLS TRAIL HISTORIC SITE

Parcel “A” in the north-east quarter of Section 2, in Township 22, in Range 14, west

of the Second Meridian as shown on Plan No. 64 R 22337 in the Land Titles Office

for the Regina Land Registration District.

HAMONA HISTORIC SITE

Parcel “C” in Section 10, in Township 18, in Range 31, west of the First Meridian,

as shown on Plan No. B.M. 972 in the Land Titles Office for the Regina Land

Registration District.

HOLY TRINITY ANGLICAN CHURCH,

STANLEY MISSION HISTORIC SITE

LA ROCHE PERCÉE HISTORIC SITE

Parcel “Q” in Section 30, in Township 1, in Range 6, west of the Second Meridian,

SOUTH BRANCH HOUSE HISTORIC SITE

Parcel “A” in a portion of River Lots 12 and 13, in Section 2, in Township 45, in

Range 1, west of the Third Meridian, as shown on Plan No. 78 PA 21251 in the Land

Titles Office for the Prince Albert Land Registration District.
