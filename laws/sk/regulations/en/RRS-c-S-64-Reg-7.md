RRS c S-64 Reg 7

*Made under The Superannuation (Supplementary Provisions) Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The District Health Boards Designation

Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Superannuation (Supplementary Provisions) Act;

(b) “district health board” means a district health board established or

deemed to have been established pursuant to The Health Districts Act.

### Designation

3 Each district health board is designated as an institution for the purposes of

section 16 of the Act.
