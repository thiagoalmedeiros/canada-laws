# The Municipal Water Treatment Filter Membranes (Education and Health Tax) Exemption Regulations

RRS c F-13.4 Reg 19

*Made under The Financial Administration Act, 1993*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Municipal Water Treatment Filter

Membranes (Education and Health Tax) Exemption Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “eligible water treatment facility” means a water treatment facility

owned by a municipality that is used to treat drinking water and that uses

eligible filter membranes for the purpose of water treatment;

(b) “eligible filter membranes” means the filter membranes used in

mechanical water treatment technologies, including electrodialysis reversal,

reverse osmosis and nano filtration;

(c) “tax” means the tax payable pursuant to The Education and Health Tax

Act.

### Exemption

3(1) Every municipality that purchases eligible filter membranes for use in an

eligible water treatment facility is exempt from paying the tax on eligible filter

membranes purchased on or after May 1, 1999.

(2) Where, notwithstanding subsection (1), a municipality purchases eligible

filter membranes and pays the tax on those eligible filter membranes on or after

May 1, 1999, a remission of the tax that is equivalent to the exemption set out in

subsection (1) is granted to the municipality.

### Application

4 A municipality that wishes to claim a remission of tax pursuant to

subsection 3(2) shall apply to the minister or the minister’s designate at the time

and in the manner specified by the minister or the minister’s designate.
