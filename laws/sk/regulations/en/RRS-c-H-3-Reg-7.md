# The Controlled Access Highways Regulations

RRS c H-3 Reg 7

*Made under The Highways and Transportation Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Controlled Access Highways Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Highways and Transportation Act;

(b) “control line” means a control line established pursuant to section 5;

(c) “controlled area” means the area:

(i) bounded by a control line or a special control line; or

(ii) located between a control line or a special control line and the

provincial highway mentioned in connection with:

(A) the control line in Part II of the Appendix; or

(B) the special control line in Part III of the Appendix;

as the case may be;

(c.1) “permit” means a permit issued by the minister pursuant to

section 8;

(d) “special control line” means a special control line established

pursuant to section 6.

### Controlled access highways

3 The lands described in Part I of the Appendix which include:

(a) provincial highways and parts of provincial highways; and

(b) land over which the minister proposes to construct a highway and in

respect of which there is a plan in the department indicating the route of the

proposed highway;

are designated as controlled access highways pursuant to clause 2(b) of the Act.

### Special controlled area

4 Every part of a controlled access highway listed in Part I of the Appendix that

lies within a town or a village is designated as a special controlled area pursuant

to section 79 of the Act.

### Control lines

5 The lines described in Part II of the Appendix are established as control lines

pursuant to section 78 of the Act.

### Special control lines

6 The lines described in Part III of the Appendix are established as special control

lines pursuant to section 80 of the Act.

### Prohibitions

7 No person shall:

(a) erect or place or cause to be erected or placed a structure of any kind

including:

(i) an embankment; or

(ii) a lighting or reflecting device;

(b) make or cause to be made any addition or alteration to a structure

described in clause (a);

on land lying within a controlled area without first obtaining a permit.

### Permits

8(1) Subject to section 9, a person may apply to the minister for a permit authorizing

the erection or placement of a structure described in clause 7(a) on land lying within

a controlled area.

(2) Every person who applies for a permit pursuant to subsection (1) shall submit

with the application for a permit the plans, including specifications, of the proposed

structure along with any other information that the minister may require.

(3) Where the minister considers it appropriate the minister may:

(a) issue a permit to the applicant authorizing the erection or placement of

a structure on land lying within a controlled access area; and

(b) impose any terms and conditions on the permit that the minister considers

necessary.

(4) Subject to the Act or any other regulations made pursuant to the Act:

(a) the permit expires on the date specified on the permit; and

(b) the holder of the permit shall comply with any terms and conditions

specified on the permit.

### Public improvements

9 No permit is to be issued for any building, structure or gas, oil or sewer and

water pipe which will encroach on or interfere with a public improvement that:

(a) the minister proposes to construct within a controlled area; or

(b) in the opinion of the minister, is likely to be constructed within a controlled

area.

### Land within city boundaries

10 These regulations do not apply to any land that is within the boundaries of a city.

### Repeal

11 Saskatchewan Regulations 120/71, 122/71, 156/71 and 157/71 are repealed.
