RRS c C-16.002 Reg 1

*Made under The Common Business Identifiers Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Common Business Identifiers Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Common Business Identifiers Act;

(b) “business number” means a number assigned to a business by the

Canada Customs and Revenue Agency for federal programs;

(c) “program account number” means a number, consisting of the business

number and an alphanumeric identifier, assigned to a business entity, that

is used by the minister in administering a program pursuant to a designated

enactment.

### Designated business information

3 For the purposes of subclause 2(b)(xiii) of the Act, the following information,

as applicable in relation to a business entity’s legal structure and associated filing

requirements pursuant to a designated enactment, is business information:

(a) the date of its registration;

(b) its registered address;

(c) the mailing addresses of its directors;

(d) the name of its sole proprietor;

(e) the telephone numbers of the partners mentioned in subclause 2(b)(xii)

of the Act;

(f) the name and telephone number of its authorized representative;

(g) a description of the business it carries on;

(h) the jurisdiction pursuant to whose law it is registered and its entity

number or similar identifying number in that jurisdiction;

(i) its entity number in Saskatchewan if it is registered in a jurisdiction other

than Saskatchewan;

(j) notice that it:

(i) is continued in Saskatchewan pursuant to the laws of another

jurisdiction;

(ii) is amalgamated or was part of an amalgamation;

(iii) has a receiver or receiver-manager appointed;

(iv) has a liquidator appointed;

(k) if it has changed its name:

(i) its new name; and

(ii) the date of its name change.

### Designated enactments

4 For the purposes of clause 2(e) of the Act, the designated enactments are set out

in Table 1 of the Appendix.

### Adoption of system of common business identifiers

5 The numbering system used by the Minister of National Revenue for the purposes

of the definition of “business number” in the Income Tax Act (Canada) is adopted as

the system of common business identifiers for identifying business entities for the

purposes of section 4 of the Act.

### Collection and disclosure of information

6(1) For the purpose of assigning a common business identifier to a business entity,

the minister may obtain from the business entity and disclose to the Minister of

National Revenue the business entity’s business information in accordance with an

agreement entered into pursuant to subsection 4(2) of the Act.

(2) The authority to disclose information pursuant to subsection (1) applies

notwithstanding any provision of any enactment restricting or prohibiting the

disclosure of information.

### Prescribed date

7 The prescribed date for the purposes of subsection 6(2) of the Act is

October 19, 2013.

### Filing information, documents or forms requiring signatures

8(1) Any forms that business entities are required to file pursuant to these

regulations that are required to be signed may be filed without signatures if:

(a) the minister has established a procedure satisfactory to the minister to

ensure the forms accurately represent the information contained on a signed

version of the form; or

(b) in the circumstances the minister considers it appropriate to accept the

filing without signature.

(2) Any information, forms or documents that are required to be signed by a

person for filing with the minister pursuant to these regulations may be filed with

the minister if signed:

(a) by electronic signature in a manner approved by the minister; or

(b) by electronically transmitted or reproduced signature.

### Coming into force

9(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on October 19, 2013.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after

October 19, 2013, these regulations come into force on the day on which they are

filed with the Registrar of Regulations.
