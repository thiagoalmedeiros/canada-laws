RRS c S-64 Reg 11

*Made under The Superannuation (Supplementary Provisions) Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Saskatchewan Trade and Export

Partnership Inc. Designation Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Superannuation (Supplementary

Provisions) Act.

### Designation

3 Saskatchewan Trade and Export Partnership Inc. is designated as an institution

for the purposes of subclause 2(e)(iii) of the Act.
