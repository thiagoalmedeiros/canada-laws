RRS c I-2 Reg 3

*Made under The Income Tax Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Assignment of Income Tax Refunds

Regulations, 1984.

### Form of notice

2(1) The form and wording of the notice required under subsection 24(3) of The

Income Tax Act is required to be in accordance with the form prescribed in the

## Appendix.

(2) The form mentioned in subsection (1) is required to be at least:

(a) 44 centimetres in length; and

(b) 29 centimetres in width.

### Repeal

3 Saskatchewan Regulations 280/80 are repealed.

## Appendix

NOTICE

Tax Discounting Practices

Section 24 of The Income Tax Act (Saskatchewan) requires tax discounters to pay at

least 85% of the income tax refund in consideration for the assignment of that refund.

If, for example, the refund is $100.00, at least $85.00 must be paid to the taxpayer.

It is an offence to pay less than this amount.

Any questions or complaints about compliance with this requirement should be
