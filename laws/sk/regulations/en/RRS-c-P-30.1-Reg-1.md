RRS c P-30.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Provincial Court Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Provincial Court Superannuation

Supplementary Allowance Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Provincial Court Act;

(a.1) “annuity” means an annuity with respect to services as a provincial

magistrate or as a judge of the magistrates’ courts;

(b) “pension” means a pension in respect of service as a judge;

(c) “service” means service as a provincial magistrate, as a judge of the

magistrates’ courts and as a judge.

### Supplementary allowance effective January 1, 1979

3 Where an annuity or a pension:

(a) commenced on or before January 1, 1979, with payment to:

(i) a superannuated judge; or

(ii) the spouse, child or children of a deceased judge or a deceased

superannuated judge;

and was being paid on January 1, 1979; or

(b) is being paid to the spouse, child or children of a deceased superannuated

judge who died after January 1, 1979;

a supplementary allowance is to be paid to the recipient of the annuity or pension,

in addition to any supplementary allowance currently being paid, calculated from

January 1, 1979, at the rate of:

(c) $14 per year, where the recipient is a superannuated judge;

(d) $7 per year, where the recipient is the spouse of a deceased superannuated

judge;

for each full year, and a prorated amount of that rate for a portion of a year, of

service of the judge, to a maximum of 23 1/3 years.

judge;

for each full year, and a prorated amount of that rate for a portion of a year, of

### Supplementary allowance effective January 1, 1984

5.3(1) Where an annuity or a pension:

(a) commenced on or before January 1, 1984 with payment to:

(i) a superannuated judge; or

(ii) the spouse, child or children of a deceased judge or a deceased

superannuated judge;

and was being paid on January 1, 1984; or

(b) is being paid to the spouse, child or children of a deceased judge who

superannuated on or before January 1, 1984 and who died after that day;

a supplementary allowance is to be paid to the recipient of the annuity or pension,

in addition to any supplementary allowance currently being paid, calculated from

January 1, 1984, at the rate of:

(c) $19.50 per year, where the recipient is a superannuated judge;

(d) $9.75 per year, where the recipient is the spouse of a deceased judge or a

deceased superannuated judge;

(e) subject to subsection (2), $2.93 per year, where the recipient is the child

of a deceased judge or a deceased superannuated judge;

for each full year, and a prorated amount of that rate for a portion of a year of service

of the judge, to a maximum of 23 1/3 years.

(2) The maximum total amount of all increases paid to all children of a deceased

judge or a deceased superannuated judge in accordance with clause (1)(e) is $11.72

per year and is to be divided equally among them.

### Supplementary allowance effective January 1, 1985

5.4(1) In this section, “annuity or pension” includes any supplementary

allowances payable pursuant to sections 3 to 5.3 or section 46 of the Act or described

in section 52 of the Act.

(2) On and from January 1, 1985, an annuity or pension that:

(a) commenced on or before January 1, 1985 with payment to:

(i) a superannuated judge; or

(ii) the spouse, child or children of a deceased judge or a deceased

superannuated judge;

and was being paid on January 1, 1985; or

(b) is being paid to the spouse, child or children of a deceased judge who

superannuated on or before January 1, 1985 and who died after that day;

is to be increased by 2.37%.

(3) On and from January 1, 1985, an annuity or pension that is being paid to:

(a) a superannuated judge; or

(b) the spouse, child or children of a deceased judge or deceased superannuated

judge;

is, in addition to any increase that may be payable pursuant to subsection (2), to

be increased by:

(c) 0.4% if the allowance commenced in 1983;

(d) 0.8% if the allowance commenced in 1982;

(e) 1.2% if the allowance commenced in 1981;

(f) 1.6% if the allowance commenced in 1980;

(g) 2% if the allowance commenced in 1979;

(h) 2.4% if the allowance commenced in 1978;

(i) 2.8% if the allowance commenced in 1977;

(j) 3.2% if the allowance commenced in 1976;

(k) 3.6% if the allowance commenced in 1975;

(l) 4% if the allowance commenced in 1974 or any earlier year.

### Supplementary allowance to children

6 Where there is no spouse in receipt of an annuity or pension and an annuity or

pension is being paid to a child or children, the child or children are entitled to be

paid a supplementary allowance at the rate provided for a spouse, for the period for

which he is or they are entitled to receive the annuity or pension and on the same

terms and conditions that apply to the payment of that annuity or pension.

### Provisions re payment

7 The provisions of The Provincial Court Act that apply to the payment of annuities

or pensions or other allowances apply to the payment of a supplementary allowance

as if it were an annuity or pension payable under the Act.
