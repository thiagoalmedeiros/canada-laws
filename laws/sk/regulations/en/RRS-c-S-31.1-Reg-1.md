RRS c S-31.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Saskatchewan Natural Resources Transfer Agreement*

(Treaty Land Entitlement) Act

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Saskatchewan Natural Resources Transfer

Agreement (Treaty Land Entitlement) Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Saskatchewan Natural Resources Transfer

Agreement (Treaty Land Entitlement) Act.

### Agreements confirmed

3 Pursuant to clause 2(2)(d) of the Act, the following agreements in English and

French are confirmed and are to take effect according to their terms:

(a) the Muskoday Natural Resources Transfer Agreement Amendment

Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and the

Government of Saskatchewan, dated March 10, 2008;

(b) the Sturgeon Lake Natural Resources Transfer Agreement Amendment

Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and the

Government of Saskatchewan, dated March 10, 2008;

(c) the George Gordon Natural Resources Transfer Agreement Amendment

Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and the

Government of Saskatchewan, dated November 28, 2008;

(d) the Pasqua Natural Resources Transfer Agreement Amendment

Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and the

Government of Saskatchewan, dated November 28, 2008;

(e) the Carry the Kettle Natural Resources Transfer Agreement Amendment

Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and the

Government of Saskatchewan, dated April 29, 2015;

(f) the Cowessess Natural Resources Transfer Agreement Amendment

Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and the

Government of Saskatchewan, dated April 29, 2015;

(g) the Kawacatoose Natural Resources Transfer Agreement Amendment

Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and the

Government of Saskatchewan, dated April 29, 2015;

(h) the Ahtahkakoop Natural Resources Transfer Agreement Amendment

Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and the

Government of Saskatchewan, dated March 9, 2022;

(i) the Mistawasis Nêhiyawak Natural Resources Transfer Agreement

Amendment Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and

the Government of Saskatchewan, dated May 27, 2022;

(j) the Zagimē Anishinabēk Natural Resources Transfer Agreement

Amendment Agreement entered into between the Government of Canada and

the Government of Saskatchewan, dated June 20, 2022.

### Coming into force

4(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day on which

The Saskatchewan Natural Resources Transfer Agreement (Treaty Land Entitlement)

Amendment Act, 2001 comes into force.

(2) If The Saskatchewan Natural Resources Transfer Agreement (Treaty Land

Entitlement) Amendment Act, 2001 comes into force before the day on which these

regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations, these regulations come into

force on the day on which they are filed with the Registrar of Regulations.
