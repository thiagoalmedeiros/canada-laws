# The Dog Training Regulations, 1982

RRS c W-13.1 Reg 11

*Made under The Wildlife Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Dog Training Regulations, 1982.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “assistant dog trainer” means any person who assists a dog trainer, in

any manner, in either training or handling bird dogs on the licenced training

area of a dog trainer;

(b) “bird dog” means a dog trained or capable of being trained to hunt birds

and includes pointers, setters, flushing dogs and retrievers;

(c) “dog trainer” means any person who trains bird dogs;

(c.1) “game bird” means migratory game bird and upland game bird;

(c.2) “migratory game bird” includes any bird protected by the Migratory

Birds Convention Act (Canada), as amended from time to time, or the

regulations made under that Act;

(d) “resident of Saskatchewan” means a person who:

(i) has his principal residence in Saskatchewan, is a Canadian citizen

or has resided in Canada for the last year and has resided in the province

for the three months preceding the date of his application for a licence; or

(ii) is a member of a regular force of the Canadian Armed Forces or of

the Royal Canadian Mounted Police and is stationed and residing in the

province;

(e) “train” means any activity related to the supervision or teaching of a bird

dog to hunt and includes allowing any bird dog to run free and untethered in

the field.

(f) “upland game bird” includes the following birds and the birds of all

species of the following families:

(i) tetraonidae, commonly known as grouse, including ruffed grouse,

spruce grouse, prairie chickens, sharp-tailed grouse, sage grouse and

ptarmigans;

(ii) phasianidae, commonly known as pheasants and partridges,

including ring-necked pheasants and European grey or Hungarian

partridges.

### Application

3 These regulations do not apply to a resident of Saskatchewan who trains not

more than four bird dogs for his personal use.

### Licence required

4 No person shall train or engage in the business of training bird dogs unless he

holds a Dog Training Licence or is an assistant dog trainer registered on a Dog

Training Licence.

### Application for licence

5(1) An application for a Dog Training Licence is to be made to the minister in

Form A and is required to:

(a) contain the legal description of the area in which the applicant proposes

to train dogs;

(b) state the name of each assistant dog trainer;

(c) Repealed. 17 Jne 88 SR 47/88 s4.

(d) indicate that the applicant has obtained the permission of all owners of

land within the area described in clause (a) to train dogs during the effective

period of the Dog Training Licence;

(e) state the number of bird dogs that the applicant will have in his possession

or control during the effective period of the Dog Training Licence in the training

area; and

(f) state the period for which the Dog Training Licence is required.

(2) The fee for each Dog Training Licence is:

(a) $20, where the applicant is a resident of Saskatchewan;

(b) $100, where the applicant is not a resident of Saskatchewan;

and, in addition, $10 for each bird dog described in clause (1)(e).

### Issuance of licence

6 Any wildlife officer may issue a Dog Training Licence to an applicant and may

limit the training area and the number of dogs that the applicant may have in the

training area at any one time.

### Activities limited to training area

7 No dog trainer or assistant dog trainer shall train or engage in the business of

training bird dogs except in the training area permitted in the Dog Training Licence.

### Certain bird dogs deemed to be undergoing training

8(1) Any bird dog over six months of age in the possession or control of a dog trainer

or his assistant is presumed to be undergoing training by the dog trainer.

(2) Every dog trainer shall, upon the request of a wildlife officer, provide proof of

the age of any dog.

### Limitation on number of bird dogs

9 No dog trainer or assistant dog trainer shall have collectively in his control or

possession more bird dogs than are allowed in the Dog Training Licence.

### Prohibitions, etc.

10(1) No person shall:

(a) carry or have in his possession in the field or fire or discharge or cause to

be fired or discharged while training a bird dog any firearm or live ammunition,

other than a training firearm using blank cartridges, except during an open

game bird season and under a game bird licence;

(b) train bird dogs in a place frequented or likely to be frequented by game

birds between April 1 and July 15 in any year; or

(c) train bird dogs on any land without the permission of the owner or occupant

of the land.

(2) No person shall conduct any field trial or open competition for dogs used in

hunting or retrieving game birds unless permitted, in writing, by the director and

subject to any conditions with respect to conducting the trial or competition that

the director may impose.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), no person shall conduct any field trial or open

competition for dogs used in hunting or retrieving game birds at which any game

bird is used or shot unless:

(a) the trial or competition is conducted during open season for hunting that

bird;

(b) the person holds a valid licence to hunt that bird; and

(c) the person secures the permission, in writing, of the director to do so and

complies with any conditions with respect to conducting the trial or competition

that the director may impose.

(4) A person may, at any time, conduct a field trial for dogs used in hunting or

retrieving game birds at which domestically raised game birds, other than migratory

game birds, are used or shot where:

(a) the trial is conducted by a club, the operator of which is registered or

incorporated under The Non-profit Corporations Act;

(b) the domestically raised game birds to be used or shot are purchased from

a commercial game bird raiser and are banded prior to the field trial by the

person conducting the trial;

(c) the trial is conducted on land in respect of which the owner or occupant

has given his consent to the use of the land for such purpose; and

(d) the person conducting the trial has secured the permission of the director

and complies with any conditions with respect to conducting the field trial that

the director may impose.

(5) Nothing in this section relieves a person from complying with any bylaw of a

rural, urban or northern municipality prohibiting or governing the use of firearms

in the municipality.

### Compliance with federal regulations

11 No dog trainer or assistant dog trainer shall bring into the province or have

in his possession within the province any bird dog that does not comply with the

Animal Disease and Protection Regulations (Canada), as amended from time to time.

12 Repealed. 17 Jne 88 SR 47/88 s6.

### Furnishing information

13 The holder of a Dog Training Licence shall furnish the director with any

information that he may request regarding bird populations and dog training.

### Training area

14(1) A dog trainer who does not train bird dogs in his registered training area for

two successive years loses any priority to the training area.

(2) Dog trainers have no legal right or claim to a training area and may not transfer

the area to another trainer.

### Sask. Reg. 93/80 repealed

15 Saskatchewan Regulations 93/80 are repealed.
