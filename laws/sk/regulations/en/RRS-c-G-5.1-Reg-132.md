RRS c G-5.1 Reg 132

*Made under The Government Organization Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Education Regulations, 2007.

### Ministry continued

2 The department of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Department of

Learning is continued as the Ministry of Education.

### Objects and purposes

3(1) The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Education are the following:

(a) to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities

and functions of the Minister of Education may be exercised and carried out;

(b) to support the growth and development of children and youths;

(c) to co-ordinate, develop, implement, promote and enforce policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan related to kindergarten,

elementary, middle and secondary education;

(d) to co-ordinate, develop, implement, promote and enforce policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan related to kindergarten,

elementary, middle and secondary French language education;

(e) to co-ordinate, develop, implement, promote and enforce policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan related to developing curriculum

for early childhood, kindergarten, elementary, middle and secondary education,

professional development of early childhood, kindergarten, elementary, middle

and secondary educators, e-learning and distance education;

(f) to co-ordinate, develop, implement, promote and enforce policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan related to early childhood

development, early childhood intervention, early learning and child care;

(g) to co-ordinate, develop, implement and promote policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan related to libraries and library services;

(h) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan to contribute to the enhancement of literacy

in Saskatchewan, including policies and programs respecting workplace-based

literacy, early literacy and family literacy;

(i) to identify obstacles to literacy and co-ordinate, develop, promote and

implement policies and programs to remove or reduce obstacles to literacy in

Saskatchewan;

(j) to assess policy decisions of the Government of Saskatchewan for

their impact on literacy in Saskatchewan and make recommendations for

modifications to those policies as they affect literacy;

(k) to promote understanding of the importance of literacy and to disseminate

information respecting literacy;

(l) to work together with educational institutions, other ministries, agencies,

governments and other organizations in carrying out any of the objects and

purposes set out in clauses (h) to (k).

(2) Repealed. 9 Feb 2018 SR 11/2018 s2.

### Coming into force

4 These regulations come into force on November 21, 2007.
