RRS c H-3.1 Reg 15

*Made under The Highway Traffic Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The School Bus Safety Lights Definition

Regulations.

### Safety lights

2 For the purposes of section 43 of The Highway Traffic Act, “safety lights” means

red flashing stop lamps within the meaning of section 90 of The Vehicle Equipment

Regulations, 1987.
