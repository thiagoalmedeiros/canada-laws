RRS c C-50.11 Reg 1

*Made under The Crown Employment Contracts Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Crown Employment Contracts Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Crown Employment Contracts Act.

### Application form

3 Form A in the Appendix to these regulations is prescribed as the form for the

purposes of clause 4(4)(a) of the Act.

### Fee

4 Every person who applies for access to Crown employment contracts under the

Act shall pay a fee equal to:

(a) $5 for every Crown employment contract applied for, to a maximum of $50

for each application; and

(b) if the person wishes to receive a copy of any Crown employment

contract, $0.25 for each page of the contract.
