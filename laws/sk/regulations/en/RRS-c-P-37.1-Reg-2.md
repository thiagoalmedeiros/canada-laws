RRS c P-37.1 Reg 2

*Made under The Public Health Act, 1994*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Public Health Forms Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Public Health Act, 1994.

### Forms prescribed

3(1) The forms set out in the Appendix are the forms prescribed for the purposes

set out in this section.

(2) Form 1 is prescribed for the purposes of subsection 29(3) of the Act.

(3) Form 2 is prescribed for the purposes of subsection 29(5) of the Act.

(4) Form 3 is prescribed for the purposes of subsection 40(2) of the Act.
