RRS c E-7.22 Reg 1

*Made under The Electronic Information and Documents Act, 2000*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Electronic Information and Documents

Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Electronic Information and Documents

Act, 2000.

### Non-application of Part II of Act

3 For the purposes of clause 4(1)(f) of the Act, Part II of the Act does not apply to

the provisions set out in Table 1 of the Appendix.

3.1 Repealed. 14 Oct 2022 SR 77/2022 s2.

### Coming into force

4 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
