RRS c A-34 Reg 2

*Made under The Auctioneers Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Auctioneers Regulations.

### Licence fees

2 The fee for a licence as:

(a) an auctioneer is $250;

(b) an auction sales company is:

(i) $750;

(ii) effective January 1, 2018, $1,000.

### Exemption

3 Any person or company dealing only in livestock pursuant to a licence issued

pursuant to The Livestock Dealer Regulations, 1995, is exempted from The

Auctioneers Act.

### R.R.S. c.A-34 Reg 1 repealed

4 The Auctioneers Licence Fees Regulations are repealed.
