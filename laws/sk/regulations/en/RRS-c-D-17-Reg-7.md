RRS c D-17 Reg 7

*Made under The Department of Health Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Community Therapy Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations:

(a) “Act” means The Department of Health Act;

(b) “community therapist” means a person who holds a valid licence to

practise pursuant to The Physical Therapists Act, 1984 or who is registered to

practise pursuant to The Registered Occupational Therapists Act and whose

registration is not under suspension;

(b.1) “district health board” means a district health board established

pursuant to The Health Districts Act;

(c) “fee” means a fee required to be paid pursuant to these regulations;

(d) “minister” means the member of the Executive Council to whom for the

time being the administration of the Act is assigned;

(e) “non-resident” means a person who is not a resident;

(f) “resident” means a person who resides in Saskatchewan and

whose name appears on a valid Saskatchewan Health Services Card

issued for the purposes of The Saskatchewan Hospitalization Act or

The Saskatchewan Medical Care Insurance Act;

(g) “services” means services provided pursuant to section 10 of the Act

that a community therapist is authorized to provide, and includes:

(i) assessing a person’s need for treatment;

(ii) prescribing the required treatment;

(iii) providing educational programs to the public;

(iv) consulting with and providing reports to others who are associated

with a person’s medical care;

(h) “treatment” means the treatment designed to maintain or restore a

physical function that has been diminished by injury or disease.

### Agreements

2.1(1) The minister may enter into agreements with district health boards for the

provision of services pursuant to these regulations.

(2) Where the minister has entered into an agreement mentioned in sub-

section (1), the district health board shall provide services in accordance with these

regulations.

### Services to residents without charge

3 A resident is eligible to receive services without charge if:

(a) Repealed. 7 Apr 95 SR 21/95 s5.

(b) in the opinion of a community therapist, the services are required by the

resident; and

(c) either:

(i) the minister is satisfied that the resident is not entitled to receive

those services without charge pursuant to:

(A) any program or plan established or administered by any agency

or department of the Government of Canada or the Government of

Saskatchewan, other than pursuant to these regulations; or

(B) a policy of insurance; or

(ii) the resident is entitled to receive those services pursuant to any

program or plan established or administered by any agency or department

of the Government of Canada or the Government of Saskatchewan, other

than pursuant to these regulations, or pursuant to a policy of insurance

and that agency, department or insurer has agreed in writing that it will

pay either the minister or the district health board that provided the

services for the cost of providing those services.

### Charges for services to residents

4 If services are provided to a resident who is not entitled to receive the services

without charge pursuant to section 3, the resident shall pay to the district health

board that provided the services a fee of $35 for each visit to the community therapist.

### Fees for services to non-residents

5 A non-resident who receives services shall pay to the district health board that

provided the services a fee of $35 for each out-patient visit.

### Fees for reports

6 If any person requests a report respecting the treatment and progress of a person

who has received services from a community therapist for a purpose other than the

ongoing treatment of that person, the person requesting the report shall pay to the

district health board that provided the services a fee of $150 for the report.

### No fee for certain examinations

7 Notwithstanding any other provision of these regulations, no person is required

to pay any fee for an examination by a community therapist to determine that

person’s eligibility for services.
