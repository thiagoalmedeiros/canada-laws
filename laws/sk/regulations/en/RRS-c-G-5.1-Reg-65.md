RRS c G-5.1 Reg 65

*Made under The Government Organization Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Extraordinary Assistance (HIV-infected

Persons) Regulations.

### Agreement to provide financial assistance

2 The Minister of Health is authorized to enter into and execute agreements for

the provision of financial assistance to HIV-infected persons, their spouses and

dependants that are substantially in the form set out in Part I or Part II of the

Appendix to these regulations.

### Coming into force

3 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
