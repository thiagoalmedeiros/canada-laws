RRS c P-30.2 Reg 2

*Made under The Provincial Emblems and Honours Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Queen Elizabeth II Platinum Jubilee

Medal (Saskatchewan) Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Provincial Emblems and Honours Act;

“medal” means the Queen Elizabeth II Platinum Jubilee Medal (Saskatchewan)

established pursuant to section 23.3 of the Act.

### Criteria

3 To be eligible to be awarded the medal, an individual, in addition to meeting the

criteria set out in the Act, must have been alive on February 6, 2022, being the 70th

anniversary of Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II’s accession to the throne of Canada.

### Nominations

4(1) The following persons and organizations are designated for the purposes of

subsection 27.2(1.2) of the Act as persons and organizations that may nominate

individuals to receive the medal in accordance with the Act and these regulations:

(a) the Lieutenant Governor;

(b) a member of the Executive Council;

(c) a member of the Legislative Assembly, including the Speaker;

(d) the Chief of Protocol;

(e) the deputy minister or other head of a government institution;

(f) Federation of Sovereign Indigenous Nations;

(g) Métis Nation – Saskatchewan;

(h) municipalities;

(i) Canadian Armed Forces;

(j) Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

(k) an organization that is designated pursuant to The Saskatchewan

Protective Services Medal Regulations for the purposes of subsection 27.2(1.1)

of the Act;

(l) non-governmental organizations.

(2) For the purposes of clause (1)(e), “government institution” and “head” have the

meanings given to them in The Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act.

### Coming into force

5(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day on

which The Provincial Emblems and Honours Amendment Act, 2023 comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day

on which The Provincial Emblems and Honours Amendment Act, 2023 comes into

force, these regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
