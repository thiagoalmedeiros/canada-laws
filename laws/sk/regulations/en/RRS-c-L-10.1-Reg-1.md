RRS c L-10.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Legal Profession Act, 1990*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Legal Profession Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Legal Profession Act, 1990.

### Application of certain provisions of Act to limited licensees

2.1(1) For the purposes of subsection 2(2.2) of the Act, in the provisions set out in

the Appendix, a reference to “member” includes a reference to a “limited licensee”,

and those provisions apply, with any necessary modification, to limited licensees

who are entitled to engage in the limited practice of law in Saskatchewan.

(2) For the purposes of this section and the Appendix:

(a) references to “disbarred”, “disbarment” or other grammatical forms of

those words that are contained in the provisions mentioned in the Appendix

are to be read as “removed”, “removal” or other applicable grammatical forms

of those words;

(b) references to “roll” that are contained in the provisions mentioned in the

Appendix are to be read as “register of limited licensees”.

### Exclusion for the purposes of subsection 11.1(4) of the Act

3 For the purposes of subsection 11.1(4) of the Act, The Saskatchewan Legal

Aid Commission is excluded from the definition of “government institution” in

subsection 11.1(1) of the Act.

### Coming into force

4(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day on

which section 1 of The Legal Profession Amendment Act, 2017 comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the

day on which section 1 of The Legal Profession Amendment Act, 2017 comes into

force, these regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with

the Registrar of Regulations.
