RRS c L-9.001 Reg 1

*Made under The Leafcutting Beekeepers Registration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Leafcutting Beekeepers Registration

Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Leafcutting Beekeepers Registration Act.

### Time periods for applications

3(1) Any application for a certificate of registration pursuant to subsection 3(2) of

the Act must be made not more than 30 days after the day on which any bees are,

or beekeeping equipment is, acquired from within Saskatchewan.

(2) Any application for a certificate of registration pursuant to subsection 3(3) of

the Act must be made not more than 60 days after the first day on which:

(a) subsection 3(3); and

(b) clauses 11 (b) and (d);

of the Act are in force.

(3) Any application for a certificate of registration pursuant to section 4 of the Act

must be made at least 30 days before the day on which any bees are, or beekeeping

equipment is, brought into Saskatchewan.
