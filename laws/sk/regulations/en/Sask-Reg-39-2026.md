Sask Reg 39/2026

*Made under The King’s Printer Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The King’s Printer (Fees) Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The King’s Printer Act.

### Tariff of charges

3 In accordance with clause 8(1)(a) of the Act, the tariff of charges for publishing

notices, advertisements and documents in the Gazette is set out in the Appendix.

### Designation of persons required to pay charges

4 A person, partnership or association on whose behalf a notice is required by an

Act to be published in the Gazette is designated for the purposes of section 8 of the

Act as a person, partnership or association that is required to pay the applicable

charge set out in the tariff of charges for publishing that notice in the Gazette.

### Subscription price

5 In accordance with clause 8(1)(b) of the Act, the subscription price for the Gazette

is $125 per year.

### RRS c Q-3 Reg 2 repealed

6 The Queen’s Printer’s Fees Regulations, 2004 are repealed.

### Coming into force

7 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
