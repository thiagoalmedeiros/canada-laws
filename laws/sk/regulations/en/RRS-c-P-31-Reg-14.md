RRS c P-31 Reg 14

*Made under The Provincial Lands Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Saskatchewan Wetland Conservation

Corporation Land Regulations, 1993.

### Disposition authorized

2 The Minister of Agriculture and Food is authorized to sell the lands described

in the Appendix to these regulations to Saskatchewan Wetland Conservation

Corporation for $1 and other valuable consideration, on those terms and conditions

that the Minister of Agriculture and Food considers to be in the public interest.

NOTICE OF EXEMPTION FROM PUBLICATION:

Re: The Saskatchewan Wetland Conservation Corporation Land Regulations, 1993

Pursuant to the authority vested in me by subsection 7(2) of The Regulations

Act, 1989, the Appendix to The Saskatchewan Wetland Conservation Corporation Land

Regulations, 1993 is exempt from publication in The Saskatchewan Gazette.

The Appendix to The Saskatchewan Wetland Conservation Corporation Land

Regulations, 1993 is on file at the office of the Registrar of Regulations, Legislative

Building, Regina, Saskatchewan, and may be inspected between the hours of 8:30 a.m.

and 4:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, not including statutory holidays.

Dated at Regina, Saskatchewan this 14th day of September, 1993.

Sandra Morgan

Registrar of Regulations
