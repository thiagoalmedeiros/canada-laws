RRS c P-34 Reg 6

*Made under The Provincial Parks, Protected Areas*

Recreation Sites and Antiquities Act

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Mission Ridge Ski Development Inc.

Development Agreement and Lease Regulations.

### Agreement and Lease

2 The Minister of Parks and Renewable Resources is hereby authorized:

(a) to enter into a Development Agreement and Lease granting rights to

Mission Ridge Ski Development Inc. in accordance with the provisions set out

substantially in the form of the Development Agreement and Lease contained

in the Appendix; and

(b) to enter into and execute an agreement and lease with Mission Ridge Ski

Development Inc. substantially in the form of the Development Agreement and

Lease contained in the Appendix.

NOTE: Pursuant to subsection 4(5) of The Regulations Act, publication of the

agreement referred to in these regulations has been dispensed with. A copy of the

agreement is on file in the office of the Registrar of the Regulations.
