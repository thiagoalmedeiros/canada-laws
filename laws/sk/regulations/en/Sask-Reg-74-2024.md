Sask Reg 74/2024

*Made under The Post-Secondary Education and Skills Training Act, 2022*

## PART 1

### Preliminary Matters

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Training Programs Regulations, 2024.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“adult basic education transcript” means an official record issued by the

Government of Saskatchewan of an individual’s marks for courses taken in

an adult basic education program;

“Canadian adult education credential diploma” means the credential

recognized:

(a) in Canada as the indicator of an adult’s level of educational

achievement; and

(b) in Saskatchewan as equivalent to a high school diploma;

“Canadian adult education credential transcript” means an official record

issued by the Government of Saskatchewan of an individual’s scores for tests

written towards obtaining a Canadian adult education credential diploma;

“former regulations” means The Training Programs Regulations, 2020 as

they existed on the day before the coming into force of these regulations;

“person” includes a partnership, association or other unincorporated body

of individuals;

“record” means a record of information in any form and includes information

that is written, photographed, recorded or stored in any manner, but does not

include computer programs or other mechanisms that produce records.

## PART 2

### Adult Basic Education

### Adult basic education programs

3(1) The adult basic education programs mentioned in the former regulations are

continued.

(2) The purpose of an adult basic education program is to assist adults in furthering

their education at the primary or secondary education level.

### Application and courses

4(1) Any individual who wishes to participate in an adult basic education program

must:

(a) be 18 years of age or older;

(b) have been out of school for at least 1 year; and

(c) apply to participate in an adult basic education program offered by an

organization that is approved by the minister.

(2) An adult basic education program:

(a) must include credit courses; and

(b) may include non-credit courses.

(3) An adult basic education credit course must follow curricula approved by the

minister.

### Adult basic education transcripts

5(1) Any person who wishes to obtain an adult basic education transcript shall:

(a) apply to the minister in a form acceptable to the minister; and

(b) pay the applicable fee as determined by the minister.

(2) On receipt of an application pursuant to subsection (1) and if the minister is

satisfied that the applicant has complied with this Part, the minister shall provide

to the applicant the adult basic education transcript that was applied for.

## PART 3

### Canadian Adult Education Credential

### Canadian adult education credential

6 The Canadian adult education credential is established to assist individuals in

obtaining a high school equivalency credential.

### Application and approval to write

7(1) Any individual who meets the requirements of subsection (2) and wishes to

write or rewrite one or more tests of the Canadian adult education credential shall

apply to the minister in a form acceptable to the minister.

(2) To be eligible to apply to write or rewrite one or more tests of the Canadian

adult education credential, an individual must:

(a) be:

(i) 18 years of age or older; and

(ii) a Saskatchewan resident; or

(b) have obtained the written approval of the minister.

(3) On receipt of an application pursuant to subsection (1) and if the minister is

satisfied that the applicant has complied with this Part, the minister shall allow the

applicant to write or rewrite the tests of the Canadian adult education credential

that were applied for.

### Canadian adult education credential transcripts and diplomas

8(1) Any individual who wishes to obtain a duplicate of a Canadian adult education

credential transcript or Canadian adult education credential diploma shall:

(a) apply to the minister in a form acceptable to the minister; and

(b) pay the applicable fee as determined by the minister.

(2) On receipt of an application pursuant to subsection (1) and if the minister is

satisfied that the applicant has complied with this Part, the minister shall provide

to the applicant the duplicate of the Canadian adult education credential transcript

or the Canadian adult education credential diploma that was applied for.

## PART 4

### General Educational Development Program

### General educational development program

9(1) In this section:

“general educational development diploma” means a general educational

development diploma as defined in the former regulations;

“general educational development transcript” means a general

educational development transcript as defined in the former regulations.

(2) The general educational development program mentioned in the former

regulations was discontinued as of May 3, 2024.

(3) Any individual who wishes to obtain a duplicate of a general educational

development diploma or a general educational development transcript shall:

(a) apply to the minister in a form acceptable to the minister; and

(b) pay the applicable fee as determined by the minister.

(4) On receipt of an application pursuant to subsection (1) and if the minister is

satisfied that the applicant complied with this section, the minister shall provide

to the applicant the duplicate of the general educational development diploma or

the general educational development transcript that was applied for.

(5) Notwithstanding the discontinuation of the general educational development

program, if an individual passed one or more tests of the general educational

development program but did not obtain a general educational development diploma

by May 3, 2024, the individual may apply to the minister, in the form and manner

and within the period required by the minister, to have those tests recognized as

tests passed towards obtaining a Canadian adult education credential diploma.

## PART 5

### Repeal and Coming into Force

### RRS c P-16.11 Reg 1 repealed

10 The Training Programs Regulations, 2020 are repealed.

### Coming into force

11 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed

with the Registrar of Regulations.
