Sask Reg 53/2024

*Made under The Plant Health Act*

## PART 1

### Preliminary Matters

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Plant Health Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Plant Health Act;

“cull potatoes” means potatoes:

(a) that have been harvested and left outdoors; and

(b) that are unmarketable or have been discarded for other reasons;

“infested” means the presence of, or a condition that shows signs of, a pest;

“potatoes” means potatoes grown for commercial sale;

“seed potatoes” means seed potatoes that:

(a) have been inspected in the field by an inspector of the Canadian

Food Inspection Agency and are of the Foundation class or any higher

class of seed potatoes as defined in the Seeds Regulations (Canada), CRC,

c 1400; or

(b) have been certified by a recognized certification agency in the United

States and are of a certification class substantially the same as the

Foundation class or any higher class of seed potatoes as defined in the

Seeds Regulations (Canada), CRC, c 1400.

## PART 3

### Pest Control Officer Orders

### Orders re control, removal and destruction of plants and materials from land or other premises

4(1) In this Part, “infested land or premises” means:

(a) land or premises; or

(b) if only a part of land or premises is subject to an order pursuant to the

Act, that part of the land or premises.

(2) If a pest control officer has reasonable grounds to believe that a pest is present

at or on any infested land or premises, the pest control officer may make an order to:

(a) restrict access to of the infested land or premises or part of the infested

land or premises; and

(b) restrict access to or movement of any of the following at or on the infested

land or premises:

(i) a plant or plant part or an animal or animal product;

(ii) equipment;

(iii) waste material;

(iv) a conveyance;

(v) other thing.

(3) An order made pursuant to subsection (2) may include any terms and conditions

that the pest control officer considers necessary to minimize the risk of the pest

spreading and may:

(a) specify the pest or the suspected pest;

(b) name the owner or occupant responsible for a plant or plant part, animal

or animal product, equipment, waste material, conveyance or other thing;

(c) describe the location or boundaries of the infested land or premises;

(d) describe a plant or plant part, animal or animal product, equipment,

waste material, conveyance or other thing and its location at or on the infested

land or premises;

(e) require the owner or occupant responsible for a plant or plant part, animal

or animal product, equipment, waste material, conveyance or other thing to

present it for examination and testing;

(f) require the application or installation of an identifying marker, signage

or temporary barrier at or on the infested land or premises;

(g) impose restrictions on:

(i) the movement of a plant or plant part, animal or animal product,

equipment, waste material, conveyance or other thing at or on the infested

land or premises; or

(ii) entering or leaving the infested land or premises;

(h) specify any precautionary or biosecurity measures that must be observed

when entering or leaving the infested land or premises;

(i) require tests to be conducted to identify or confirm the presence of the pest;

(j) require the owner or occupant responsible for the infested land or premises,

conveyance or other thing that is or may be contaminated with a pest to clean

and disinfect the infested land or premises, conveyance or other thing, including

requiring that specific methods be used and certain conditions observed;

(k) inspect the infested land or premises for the purpose of ensuring

compliance with the order;

(l) order disposal or destruction of cull potatoes in accordance with section 10

if the potatoes have or are believed on reasonable grounds to be infested;

(m) allow the movement of a plant or plant part, animal or animal product,

equipment, waste material, conveyance or other thing from the infested land

or premises to another location;

(n) require the owner or occupant responsible for the infested land or premises

to inform the pest control officer of any animals that become sick or have died

at or on the infested land or premises while the order is in effect;

(o) require that records be kept and information reported with respect to

complying with that order;

(p) state the time within which any of the things mentioned in an order must

be done;

(q) fix the duration of the order; and

(r) impose any other terms or conditions that the pest control officer considers

necessary.

(4) If the owner or occupant responsible for the infested land or premises, plant

or plant part, animal or animal product, equipment, waste material, conveyance

or other thing that is subject to the order fails to comply with the order, the pest

control officer may carry out the order at the expense of that owner or occupant

pursuant to section 26 of the Act.

(5) A pest control officer must provide in writing to the person mentioned in

subsection (4) the pest control officer’s determination of the expenses incurred

pursuant to that subsection.

(6) Unless a person has the written consent of a pest control officer, no person

other than a pest control officer shall remove, alter, deface, conceal, damage, destroy,

move or render illegible:

(a) an order or copy of an order that has been posted; or

(b) any identifying marker, signage or temporary barrier applied or installed

as authorized pursuant to this section.

### Prohibition of access, control, removal and movement of infested land or premises

5(1) No person shall move a plant or plant part, animal or animal product,

equipment, waste material, conveyance, or other thing from infested land or premises

to another location:

(a) unless done in accordance with an order made pursuant to section 4; and

(b) without notifying the pest control officer at least 24 hours before moving

a plant or plant part, animal or animal product, equipment, waste material,

conveyance or other thing from the infested land or premises.

(2) A person who moves a plant or plant part, animal or animal product, equipment,

waste material, conveyance or other thing from infested land or premises to another

location must:

(a) carry a copy of the order; and

(b) provide a copy of the order to any person who takes custody or care and

control of the plant or plant part, animal or animal product, equipment, waste

material, conveyance or other thing.

(3) A pest control officer may require that a plant or plant part, animal or animal

product, equipment, waste material, conveyance, or other thing that has been

removed by a person responsible for land or other premises from infested land or

premises be returned to the infested land or premises.

(4) If, pursuant to subsection (3), the owner or occupant responsible for infested

land or premises is required by a pest control officer to return a plant or plant part,

animal or animal product, equipment, waste material, conveyance, or other thing

removed from the land or premises and the owner or occupant responsible refuses

to comply with the order of the pest control officer, the pest control officer may

return or cause to be returned the plant or plant part, animal or animal product,

equipment, waste material, conveyance or other thing at the expense of that owner

or occupant pursuant to section 26 of the Act.

## PART 4

### Potato Pests

### Seed potato

6 Every owner or occupant shall only use seed potatoes in a potato planting.

### Requirement to plant on land free of bacterial ring rot and potato wart

7(1) No owner or occupant shall plant seed potatoes, or any other potato, on land

that was infested with bacterial ring rot within the previous 2 growing seasons.

(2) No owner or occupant shall plant seed potatoes, or any other potato, on land that:

(a) was infested with potato wart within the previous 5 growing seasons;

(b) has potato wart spore counts in the soil that are higher than 5 spores per

gram of soil; and

(c) has a positive potato wart bioassay.

### Disposal or destruction of cull potatoes

8 Every owner or occupant shall ensure that all cull potatoes on the owner’s or

occupant’s land are disposed of or destroyed on or before June 15 of each year.

### Records

9(1) Every owner or occupant shall:

(a) maintain accurate records of all potato plantings, including:

(i) the date and location of all potato plantings; and

(ii) the weight and class of certified seed potato used;

(b) maintain accurate records of all cull potatoes, including the location of

cull potatoes that have been disposed of or destroyed; and

(c) retain a copy of each bill of sale for the seed potato used in each potato

planting.

(2) Every owner or occupant shall retain the records mentioned in clauses(1)(a), (b)

and (c) for at least 3 years after the expiration of the year in which the records were

created.

### Requirement for disposal or destruction

10(1) A pest control officer may issue an order to an owner or occupant pursuant

to section 24 of the Act for the disposal or destruction of:

(a) all potatoes that have tested positive for bacterial ring rot, potato wart

or late blight; and

(b) all potatoes that have been or may have been in contact with those infested

potatoes.

(2) Every order issued pursuant to subsection (1) that does not require the potatoes

to be destroyed must require that the potatoes be disposed of and treated in a manner

ordered by the pest control officer to prevent the growth of potato sprouts.

(3) A pest control officer may issue an order pursuant to section 24 of the Act to

an owner or occupant for the disposal or destruction of any cull potatoes found on

the owner’s or occupant’s land.

### Requirements for cleanup

11 All storage locations used by an owner or occupant for loading, processing or

transporting, and all field equipment, packaging equipment and other material, shall

be cleaned and disinfected in a manner ordered by a pest control officer pursuant

to section 24 of the Act if the locations, equipment and material have been used for

the storage or production of potatoes that:

(a) are infested with bacterial ring rot, potato wart or late blight; or

(b) have been or may have been in contact with those infested potatoes.

## PART 5

### General Provisions

### Forms

12(1) For the purposes of subsections 21(1) and (2) of the Act, Form A of Part 2 of

the Appendix is prescribed as the form of agreement.

(2) For the purposes of section 24 of the Act, Form B of Part 2 of the Appendix is

## Appendix

## PART 1

[Section 3]

### Animals, Insects and Diseases Declared to be Pests

“Bacterial ring rot of potato” – Bacterial potato disease caused by Clavibacter

michiganensis ssp. sepedonicus also known as Corynebacterium sepedonicum, which

affects potato plants and tubers

“Brown rat” or “Norway rat” – Rattus norvegicus and roof rat, black rat, or ship rat,

Rattus rattus

“Feral pigs” – free-living members of the family Suidae

“Late blight” – potato disease caused by the fungus-like organism Phytophthora

infestans, which affects potato plants and tubers

“Potato wart” – Potato disease caused by the soil-borne fungus, Synchytrium

endobioticum, which attacks the growing points on the potato plants
