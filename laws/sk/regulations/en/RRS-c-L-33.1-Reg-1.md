RRS c L-33.1 Reg 1

*Made under The Local Improvements Act, 1993*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Local Improvements Forms Regulations.

### Interpretation

2 In these regulations, “Act” means The Local Improvements Act, 1993.

### Forms prescribed

3 The forms set out in the Appendix to these regulations are prescribed as forms

for the purposes of the Act.

### Form and manner of notice re section 15

4(1) For the purposes of publishing a notice of the proposed local improvement

pursuant to section 15 of the Act, the council shall publish the notice in Form A

once each week for 2 successive weeks, at least 6 days apart.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), the council shall publish the notice in at

least 1 newspaper or other printed publication circulating widely in the municipality,

unless:

(a) in the opinion of the council, it is not feasible or practicable to do so; and

(b) all or part of the contents of the notice are:

(i) distributed by mail;

(ii) published on a website or publicly distributed by other electronic

means; or

(iii) distributed by any other means of publishing or otherwise providing

notice as long as notice is given within the same time frame and with the

same frequency required by subsection (1).

(3) For the purposes of subsection 15(3), the council shall send the notice of the

proposed local improvement in Form B to the owner of every lot that will be liable

to be specially assessed with respect to the proposed local improvement.

(4) Council shall provide the report mentioned in section 6 of the Act for proposed

undertaking of a local improvement by any of the following means:

(a) publication in a newspaper;

(b) publication on the municipality’s website;

(c) by mail or email;

(d) distribution by any other electronic means

5 Repealed. 8 Dec 2023 SR 122/2023 s4.

### Subsection 33(2) form

6 Form C is the form to be served by the council pursuant to subsection 33(2) of

the Act.

### Subsection 35(1) form

7 Form D is the form to be given by the board of revision pursuant to subsection 35(1)

of the Act.

### Coming into force

8(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations come into force on the day on which

section 60 of The Local Improvements Act, 1993 comes into force.

(2) If these regulations are filed with the Registrar of Regulations after the day

on which section 60 of The Local Improvements Act, 1993 comes into force, these

regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the Registrar

of Regulations.
