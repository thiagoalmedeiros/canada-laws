RRS c E-13.1 Reg 10

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Immigration and Career

Training Regulations.

### Ministry continued

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry of

Immigration and Careers Training is continued as the Ministry of Immigration

and Career Training.

### Objects and purposes

3 The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Immigration and Career Training

are to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities and

functions of the Minister of Immigration and Career Training may be exercised and

carried out in relation to the following:

(a) to co-ordinate, develop, implement, promote and enforce policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan related to:

(i) training and career-related services;

(ii) enhancing learning through the use of technology; and

(iii) research and development at post-secondary educational institutions;

(b) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan related to promoting the economic

independence and self-reliance of individuals with the following objectives:

(i) increasing participation in the labour market;

(ii) helping individuals match their employable skills to the needs of

employers and the labour market;

(c) to co-ordinate, develop, implement, promote and enforce policies and

programs of the Government of Saskatchewan related to support for employers

in meeting their labour market needs;

(d) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs of

the Government of Saskatchewan related to career and employment training

and services;

(e) to attract immigrants for the following purposes:

(i) to contribute to Saskatchewan’s economy;

(ii) to meet labour market needs for employers;

(iii) to establish businesses in Saskatchewan;

(iv) to join their family members who are already resident in

Saskatchewan or to join Saskatchewan communities whose members

have a similar culture or origin;

(f) to assist immigrants and refugees in settling and integrating in

Saskatchewan communities and workplaces;

(g) to facilitate the acceptance and reception of immigrants and refugees in

Saskatchewan communities and workplaces.

### RRS c E-13.1 Reg 7 repealed

4 The Ministry of Immigration and Careers Training Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
