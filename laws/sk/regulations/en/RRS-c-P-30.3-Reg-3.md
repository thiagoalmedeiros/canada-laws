RRS c P-30.3 Reg 3

*Made under The Provincial Health Authority Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Surgical Registry Regulations, 2024.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations:

“Act” means The Provincial Health Authority Act;

“emergency surgical procedure” means a surgical procedure of an

unexpected or unplanned nature that must be performed immediately on a

patient as a result of a condition that is life threatening, limb threatening or

organ threatening;

“health services number” means a unique number assigned to an individual

who:

(a) is or was registered as a beneficiary to receive insured services within

the meaning of The Saskatchewan Medical Care Insurance Act;

(b) pursuant to the legislation of another province or territory of Canada,

is or was entitled to receive services similar to the insured services

mentioned in clause (a); or

(c) is or was entitled to receive services similar to the insured services

mentioned in clause (a) that are paid for by the Government of Canada;

“operator” means the provincial health authority, a health care organization

or a surgical services entity, as the case may be, that operates a surgical facility;

“patient” means a person who is the subject of a surgical procedure;

“planned surgical procedure” means a surgical procedure other than an

emergency surgical procedure;

“Saskatchewan Provincial Diagnosis Code List” means the Saskatchewan

Provincial Diagnosis Code List published by the ministry, as amended from

time to time;

“Saskatchewan Surgical Registry Policies and Procedures manual”

means the Saskatchewan Surgical Registry Policies and Procedures manual

published by the ministry, as amended from time to time;

“Saskatchewan Surgical Registry Procedure List” means the

Saskatchewan Surgical Registry Procedure List published by the ministry,

as amended from time to time;

“surgical facility” means a facility at which a surgical procedure is provided;

“surgical procedure” means a surgical procedure listed in the Saskatchewan

Surgical Registry Procedure List that is performed on a patient;

“surgical services entity” means a person or entity, other than a health

care organization, that receives funding from the provincial health authority

to provide a surgical procedure.

### Application of regulations

3(1) Subject to subsection (2), these regulations apply to all surgical procedures

performed at a surgical facility that is operated by:

(a) the provincial health authority;

(b) a health care organization; or

(c) a surgical services entity.

(2) These regulations do not apply to a surgical procedure that is not funded by

the provincial health authority.

### Submission of information generally

4 All information required to be submitted to the surgical registry pursuant to

these regulations must be submitted in a format acceptable to the minister and

in accordance with the Saskatchewan Surgical Registry Policies and Procedures

manual.

### Information before planned surgical procedure

5(1) If an operator receives information respecting a planned surgical procedure

that is to be performed at a surgical facility that it operates, the operator shall

submit the following information to the surgical registry with respect to the planned

surgical procedure:

(a) the surgical procedure to be performed, as that procedure is listed in the

Saskatchewan Surgical Registry Procedure List;

(b) whether the surgical procedure will be performed on an in-patient or

out-patient basis;

(c) the name of the surgical facility at which the surgical procedure is to be

performed;

(d) the name of:

(i) the patient’s family physician; and

(ii) the physician, surgeon or dentist who is to perform the surgical

procedure;

(e) the date on which the patient was referred to the physician, surgeon or

dentist for the surgical procedure and the date of the patient’s initial visit with

that physician, surgeon or dentist;

(f) the assessment by the physician, surgeon or dentist of the patient’s

diagnosis, using the Saskatchewan Provincial Diagnosis Code List;

(g) the name, address, telephone number, date of birth, sex and health services

number of the patient;

(h) a list of any dates on which the patient is not available for surgery and

the reason for the unavailability;

(i) all additional surgical data that is required to be submitted pursuant to

the Saskatchewan Surgical Registry Policies and Procedures manual.

(2) The operator must submit the information mentioned in subsection (1) to the

surgical registry within 7 days after receiving the information and, in any event,

before the planned surgical procedure takes place.

(3) If the operator receives any change to the information submitted to the surgical

registry pursuant to subsection (2) with respect to a planned surgical procedure,

the operator must, within 5 days after receiving the change of information, submit

the change of information to the surgical registry.

### Clinical priority

6 The clinical priority of a planned surgical procedure to be performed in a surgical

facility shall be based on the information submitted by the operator to the surgical

registry pursuant to clause 5(1)(f).

### Information after planned surgical procedure

7 Within 7 days after a planned surgical procedure is performed, the operator of

the surgical facility at which the planned surgical procedure was performed must

submit the following information to the surgical registry:

(a) the surgical procedure that was performed, as that procedure is listed in

the Saskatchewan Surgical Registry Procedure List;

(b) whether the surgical procedure was performed on an in-patient or

out-patient basis;

(c) the name of the surgical facility at which the surgical procedure was

performed;

(d) the name of the physician, surgeon or dentist who performed the surgical

procedure;

(e) the date on which the surgical procedure was performed;

(f) the type of anaesthesia that was used;

(g) the date on which the patient was discharged from the surgical facility

and the circumstances of discharge.

### Information after emergency surgical procedure

8 Within 7 days after the day on which an emergency surgical procedure is

performed, the operator of the surgical facility at which the emergency surgical

procedure was performed must submit to the surgical registry the information

mentioned in clause 5(1)(h) and section 7 with respect to the emergency surgical

procedure.

### Information sharing

9(1) For the purposes of subsection 2-11(4) of the Act, a physician, surgeon or

dentist who provides health services to a patient is prescribed as a person to whom

the minister may disclose information from the surgical registry with respect to

that patient.

(2) The minister may, pursuant to subsection 2-11(4) of the Act, disclose information

from the surgical registry with respect to a patient only to the following:

(a) the provincial health authority;

(b) a health care organization;

(c) a surgical services entity.

(3) Information from the surgical registry that is being disclosed pursuant to

subsection 2-11(4) of the Act may be provided electronically or in writing.

### RRS c R-8.2 Reg 2 repealed

10 The Surgical Registry Regulations are repealed.

### Coming into force

11 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
