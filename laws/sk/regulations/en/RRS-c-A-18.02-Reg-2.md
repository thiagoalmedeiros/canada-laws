RRS c A-18.02 Reg 2

*Made under The All Terrain Vehicles Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Operation of All Terrain Vehicles on Crown

Land Prohibition Regulations.

### Operation on described lands prohibited

2 No person shall operate an all terrain vehicle on Crown land described in

Table 1 of the Appendix to these regulations.

## Appendix

## TABLE 1

[Section 2]

Item Description of Crown Land

1 All those lands in Township 21, in Range 22, west of the Second Meridian, described

as follows:

(a) the north-west quarter of Section 13;

(b) the south-east quarter of Section 13;

(c) the south-west quarter of Section 13;

(d) the north-east quarter of Section 14; and

(e) the north-west quarter of Section 14.
