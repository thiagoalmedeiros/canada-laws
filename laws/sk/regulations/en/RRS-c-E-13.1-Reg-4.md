RRS c E-13.1 Reg 4

*Made under The Executive Government Administration Act*

### Title

1 These regulations may be cited as The Ministry of Parks, Culture and Sport

Regulations, 2016 (No. 2).

### Ministry continued

2 The ministry of the Government of Saskatchewan called the Ministry of

Parks, Culture and Sport is continued.

### Objects and purposes

3(1) The objects and purposes of the Ministry of Parks, Culture and Sport are

to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities and

functions of the Minister of Parks, Culture and Sport may be exercised and

carried out in relation to the following:

(a) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to:

(i) conserving, preserving, managing, protecting and developing

Saskatchewan’s heritage; and

(ii) conserving, preserving, managing, protecting and developing

areas of prehistoric, environmental, recreational or archaeological

interest or significance;

(b) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan that support a vibrant and sustainable

arts community in Saskatchewan that is recognized and valued throughout

Saskatchewan and at the national and international level;

(c) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan that relate to the recognition and

appreciation of the diverse cultures and backgrounds of Saskatchewan

people and that preserve and enhance Saskatchewan’s multicultural

composition;

(d) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to urban parks other than

Wascana Centre;

(e) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to the Royal Saskatchewan

Museum;

(f) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to sport and recreation;

(g) to co-ordinate, develop, promote and implement policies and programs

of the Government of Saskatchewan relating to the cultural, physical and

social development of youth.

(2) In addition to the objects and purposes mentioned in subsection (1), the objects

and purposes of the Ministry of Parks, Culture and Sport are the following:

(a) to provide the structure wherein and whereby the powers, responsibilities

and functions of the Minister Responsible for the Status of Women may be

exercised and carried out;

(b) to monitor programs, services and initiatives of the Government of

Saskatchewan to determine their impact on women and on families and to

recommend modifications to those programs, services and initiatives.

### R.R.S. c.E-13.1 Reg 2 repealed

4 The Ministry of Parks, Culture and Sport Regulations, 2016 are repealed.

### Coming into force

5 These regulations come into force on the day on which they are filed with the

Registrar of Regulations.
