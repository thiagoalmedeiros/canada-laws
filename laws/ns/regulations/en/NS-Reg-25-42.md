NS Reg 25/42

*Made under Section 10 of the Motor Vehicle Act*

1 Vehicles originally designed as passenger vehicles may be registered as

commercial vehicles only when converted in the case of each of the following

types in the manner hereinafter set forth:

(a) Sedan: All seats and upholstery to the rear of the driver's seat shall

be removed; the rear side doors shall be permanently fastened, and a

door or doors shall be fitted to the rear of the body;

(b) Coach: All seats and upholstery to the rear of the driver's seat shall

be removed and a door or doors shall be fitted to the rear of the body;

(c) Coupe: The rear compartment cover shall be removed and a body of

solid construction shall be built permanently into the rear

compartment, and such body shall extend at least two feet to the rear

of the original body, to the full width of compartment opening, with

sides eighteen inches high.
