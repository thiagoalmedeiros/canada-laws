NS Reg 188/2006

*Made under subsection 3(2) and Section 8 of the Retail Business Uniform Closing Day Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Retail Business Uniform Closing Day Regulations .

### Definitions

1A (1) In these regulations, “Act” means the Retail Business Uniform Closing Day Act .

(2) In the Act, “Canada Day” means July 1.

### Exemptions from Section 3 of the Act

2 All goods and services sold, offered for sale or purchased by retail are prescribed as goods

and services to which Section 3 of the Act does not apply.
