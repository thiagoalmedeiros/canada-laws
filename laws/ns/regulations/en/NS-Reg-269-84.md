NS Reg 269/84

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 9th day of October, A.D., 1984, pursuant to Section 2 of Chapter 244 of the

Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to

(a) constitute all that area of land situate at Point Edward, in the County of Cape Breton,

owned by Her Majesty the Queen in right of the Province of Nova Scotia, to be a

Provincial Park, the said area of land being described in Schedule “A” and shown

outlined in red on the copy of Crown Lands Records Plan No. P-097/84 marked

Schedule “B”, both Schedules attached to and forming part of the report and

recommendation; and

(b) declare that the Park shall be known by the name “Petersfield Provincial Park”.
