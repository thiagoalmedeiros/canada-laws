NS Reg 41/2024

*Made under clauses 8(a) and (d) of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources and Renewables dated December 20, 2023, and pursuant to clauses 8(a) and (d) of

Chapter 367 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased,

effective on and after February 13, 2024, to

(a) designate approximately 71 hectares (176 acres) of Crown lands in Annapolis County,

currently identified as PIDs 05017769 and 05017900 shown outlined in bold and shaded

dark grey on the plan marked Schedule “A” attached to and forming part of the report

and recommendation and declare that it be known as North Mountain Provincial Park;

and

(b) authorize the Minister of Natural Resources and Renewables to execute such documents

as may be necessary to achieve the purposes of this Order.
