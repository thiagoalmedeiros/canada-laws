*Made under subsection 9(1A) of the Rental Property Conversion Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Rental Property Conversion Statutory Declaration

## Form of statutory declaration

## Form A

### Statutory Declaration

(Clause 4(1)(a) of the Rental Property Conversion Act )

I, ______________________________, am the ______________________________ (insert title) of

the following property/properties:

_______________________________________________________________________________________ _______________________________________________________________________________________ _______________________________________________________________________________________ _______________________________________________________________________________________

and I hereby give notice that I propose to convert the property/properties listed above to a use

other than rental property.

The date of the proposed conversion is ____________________, 20___.

Three months’ written notice as required by clause 4(1)(b) of the Rental Property Conversion

Act has been delivered to each tenant occupying the rental property/properties, and a copy of the

form of notice delivered is attached to this declaration as Schedule A.
