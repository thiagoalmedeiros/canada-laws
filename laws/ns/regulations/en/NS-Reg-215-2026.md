NS Reg 215/2026

*Made under Section 20 of the Agricultural Weed Control Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Noxious Weeds Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

“Act” means the Agricultural Weed Control Act .

### Noxious weeds

3 The following plants and their seeds are designated by the Minister as noxious weeds for

the corresponding designated area of the Province where the weed may negatively

impact agricultural activities:

| Noxious Weed | Area Designation Applies |
| --- | --- |
| Common milkweed ( Asclepias syriaca L.) | Province-wide |
| Field bindweed ( Convolvulus arvensis L.) | Province-wide |
| Leafy spurge ( Euphorbia esula L.) | Province-wide |
| Marsh hedge nettle ( Stachys palustris L.) | Province-wide |
| Tansy ragwort ( Senecio jacobaea L.) | Counties of Kings, Annapolis, Digby,

Yarmouth, Shelburne, Queens, and

Lunenburg, and including Halifax County

and areas near the Halifax/

Colchester/Hants County lines or near

rivers and streams flowing into Halifax

and Hants Counties |

| Velvetleaf ( Abutilon theophrasti Medic.) | Province-wide |
| White cockle ( Lychnis alba Mill.) | Province-wide |
| Wild Chervil ( Anthriscus sylvestris ) | Province-wide |
| Yellow nut sedge ( Cyperus esculentus L.) | Province-wide |

### Notifying inspector

4 A responsible person must notify an inspector as soon as they become aware that a

noxious weed is present on their land within a designated area.

### Treatment of noxious weeds

5 (1) All of the following are designated as treatment methods for preventing the growth

or spread of noxious weeds:

(a) covering the noxious weeds with a deep layer of mulch or other substances

that prevent the growth of the noxious weeds or ripening of seeds;

(b) cultivating the soil in which the noxious weeds are growing and cutting

roots or stalks before seeds ripen and become viable;

(c) treating the noxious weeds with chemicals that destroy the noxious weeds

or prevent their growth or the ripening of their seeds;

(d) solarization of the noxious weeds by placing them in a black bag in direct

sunlight for 3 to 4 weeks;

(e) composting the noxious weeds at a high temperature.

(2) Unless otherwise ordered by an inspector, a responsible person must use 1 or more

of the methods designated in subsection (1), at the responsible person’s cost, at

such times as are necessary to prevent the growth or spread of noxious weeds.

### Treatment of contaminated material

6 (1) All of the following are designated as treatment methods for contaminated

material:

(a) removing the contaminated material to locations where noxious weeds are

unable to spread or germinate or, if germination were to take place, where

noxious weeds are unable to grow to maturity;

(b) composting;

(c) grinding;

(d) crushing;

(e) burning;

(f) burying;

(g) chemical treatment.

(2) Unless otherwise ordered by an inspector, a person must use 1 or more of the

methods designated in subsection (1), to treat contaminated material.

### Interfering with signs installed

7 A person must not remove, deface, or obscure any signs installed by the Minister, the

Chief Inspector or an inspector without authorization from the Minister, the Chief

Inspector or an inspector.

## Form of inspector’s orders

8 The following forms attached to these regulations are prescribed as the forms to be used

for an order issued by an inspector under the Act:

(a) Form 1—Order to Destroy Noxious Weeds under the Agricultural Weed

Control Act , for an order under subsection 11(2) or Section 12 of the Act;

(b) Form 2—Order to Prohibit Sowing of a Crop under the Agricultural Weed

Control Act , for an order under Section 13 of the Act;

(c) Form 3—Order to Destroy or Treat Contaminated Material under the Agricultural Weed Control Act , for an order under subsection 14(2) of the

Act;

(d) Form 4—Order to Prohibit the Movement of Contaminated Material under

the Agricultural Weed Control Act , for an order under subsection 14(3) of

the Act.

### Person served with order not owner

9 If the person served with an order under Section 11, 12, 13 or 14 of the Act is not the

owner, a copy of the order must be delivered to the owner.
