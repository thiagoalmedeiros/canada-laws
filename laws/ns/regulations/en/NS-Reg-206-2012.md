NS Reg 206/2012

*Made under Section 10 of the Snow Sport Helmet Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Snow Sport Helmet Regulations .

### Helmet standards

2 A helmet required by subsection 5(1) of the Act must meet the standards set out in the

latest edition of the standards for helmets for recreational skiing and snowboarding of 1 or

more of the following certification agencies:

(a) American Society for Testing and Materials;

(b) Central European Committee for Standardization;

(c) Canadian Standards Association;

(d) Snell Memorial Foundation.

### Notification signs

3 (1) A notification sign required by subsection 6(1) of the Act must be in the form set out

in Appendix A and must measure 46 cm in width and 61 cm in height.

(2) A notification sign must be displayed at a point of purchase in accordance with all of

the following conditions:

(a) it must be displayed within 1 m of each cash register where lift tickets are for

sale;

(b) it must be readily legible and unobstructed;

(c) it must be clearly visible to the customer at the point of purchase.

(3) A notification sign must be displayed at a lift entrance in accordance with all of the

following conditions:

(a) it must be displayed at the entrance to all ski lifts;

(b) it must be clearly visible to persons before loading the ski lift;

(c) it must be readily legible and unobstructed.

(4) Notification signs must be obtained from the Department of Health and Wellness.
