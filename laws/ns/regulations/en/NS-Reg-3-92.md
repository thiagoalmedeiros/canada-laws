NS Reg 3/92

*Made under subsection 302(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

1 In these regulations,

(a) “Passenger Class 1” means a passenger motor vehicle or stationwagon having

a weight up to and including 1000 kilograms;

(b) “Passenger Class 2” means a passenger motor vehicle or stationwagon having

a weight between 1001 and 1500 kilograms, inclusive;

(c) “Passenger Class 3” means a passenger motor vehicle or stationwagon having

a weight between 1501 and 3200 kilograms, inclusive.

2 The registration fees for Passenger Classes 1, 2, and 3 are as follows:

Class 1 Vehicle (0–1000 kg) ...................................................... $71.65 per year

Class 2 Vehicle (1001–1500 kg) ................................................ $88.45 per year

Class 3 Vehicle (1501–3200 kg) .............................................. $110.85 per year

### Section 2 replaced: O.I.C. 2015-96, N.S. Reg. 135/2015.
