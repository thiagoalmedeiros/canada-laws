NS Reg 112/93

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated the 6th day of August, A.D., 1993, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 32 of

the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Beaches Act , is pleased to designate as a beach

an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Inverness, in the County of

Inverness, as described in Schedule “A” and shown outlined in bold line on the copy of

Provincial Crown Lands Record Centre Plan No. E-15-35 marked Schedule “B”, both

Schedules attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All those certain lots, pieces or parcels of land situate, lying and being at Inverness, in the

County of Inverness, Province of Nova Scotia shown as lot No. 1 and lot No. 2 on a Plan of

Survey (Field Plot No. P-118/92) signed on the 2nd day of October A.D., 1992 by Athol C.

Grant, Nova Scotia Land Surveyor and filed in the Provincial Crown Lands Record Centre,

Dartmouth, as plan No. E-15-35, the said two lots of land being more particularly described as

follows:

### Lot

1

Beginning at Survey Marker IN4122 situate (by grid azimuths referenced to the Nova Scotia 3°

Modified Transverse Mercator Projection, Zone 4, Central Meridian 61° 30' West Longitude)

24° 14' 28", a distance of 968.944 metres from Nova Scotia Coordinate Monument (NSCM)

No. 14148 having coordinate values of 5 120 340.208 metres in northings and 4 513 648.003

metres in eastings;

Thence (from the place of beginning) 328° 56' 42", a distance of 67.118 metres to Survey

Marker IN4123;

Thence 49° 21' 12", a distance of 1494.510 meters to Survey Marker IN4124;

Thence 36° 26' 34", a distance of 544.960 metres to Survey Marker IN4125;

Thence 45° 41' 20", a distance of 201.029 metres to Survey Marker IN4126;

Thence 89° 50' 25", a distance of 205.960 metres to Survey Marker IN4127;

Thence 40° 52' 42", a distance of 37 metres more or less to mean high watermark of Broad

Cove River;

Thence westerly, northerly, southerly and easterly following the sinuosities of the mean high

watermark of Broad Cove River, the Gulf of St. Lawrence and Inverness Harbour a distance of

4311 metres more or less to a point;

Thence 328° 56' 42", a distance of 2 metres more or less to the place of beginning.

Above lot of land containing an area of 23 hectares, more or less.

### Lot

2

Beginning at Survey Marker IN4130 situate (by grid azimuths referenced to the Nova Scotia 3º

Modified Transverse Mercator Projection, Zone 4, Central Meridian 61° 30' West Longitude)

334° 36' 05", a distance of 1346.393 metres from Nova Scotia Coordinate Monument (NSCM)

No. 23866 having coordinate values of 5 122 156.397 metres in northings and 4 516 670.096

metres in eastings;

Thence (from the place of beginning) 304° 18' 47", a distance of 25 metres more or less to the

mean high watermark of the Gulf of St. Lawrence;

Thence southerly and easterly following the sinuosities of mean high watermark of the Gulf of

St. Lawrence and Broad Cove River a distance of 645 metres more or less to a point;

Thence 40° 52' 42", a distance of 182 metres more or less to Survey Marker IN4128;

Thence 25° 39' 43", a distance of 366.073 metres to Survey Marker IN4129;

Thence 304° 18 '47", a distance of 49.567 metres to the place of beginning;

Above lot of land containing an area of 6.4 hectares more or less.
