NS Reg 127/80

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 18th day of July, A.D., 1980, and pursuant to the Provincial Parks Act and the Interpretation Act , is pleased to amend Order in Council 74-1373 dated the 19th day of

December, A.D., 1974, by deleting therefrom Schedules “A” and “B” approved by that Order in

Council and substituting therefor the new Schedules “A” and “B” attached to and forming part

of the report and recommendation, thereby redefining the boundaries of Coldbrook Provincial

Park.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain lot, piece or parcel of land situate, lying and being at Coldbrook, County of

Kings, Province of Nova Scotia as shown outlined in red on Plan E-13-1, recorded in the

Crown Land Registry Office, Department of Lands and Forests, Halifax and being more

particularly described as follows:

Beginning at a point on the northern boundary of Highway No. 1, marking the southeastern

corner of lands now or formerly of Scotia Gold Co-operative Limited;

From thence (by magnetic bearings of the year 1960) N 07° 05' E along the eastern boundary

of the aforesaid lands of Scotia Gold Co-operative Limited 659', more or less, to a point

marking the southwestern corner of lands owned by Valley Fertilizers Limited;

Thence N 81° 10' E along the southern boundary of the aforesaid lands of Valley Fertilizers

Limited 418', more or less, to a point marking the southeastern corner of lands of Valley

Fertilizers Limited and situate on the western boundary of land now or formerly of C. Meister;

Thence S 08° 30' W along a wire fence marking the western boundary of the aforesaid lands of

C. Meister 862', more or less, to a point marking the southwestern corner of lands of C. Meister

and situate on the northern boundary of the aforesaid Highway No. 1;

Thence N 69° 55' W along the northern boundary of Highway No. 1 a distance of 391', more or

less, to the place of beginning.

The above described lot containing 6.8 acres, more or less.
