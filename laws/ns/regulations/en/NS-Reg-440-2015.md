NS Reg 440/2015

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated December 3, 2015, and pursuant to Section 8 of Chapter 367 of the Revised

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased, effective on and after

December 29, 2015, to:

(a) designate as a provincial park approximately 879.1 hectares (2,172.26 acres) of

Crown lands shown outlined in bold on the plan marked Schedule “A” attached to

and forming part of the report and recommendation and declare that it be known as

Lake Charlotte Provincial Park; and

(b) authorize the Minister of Natural Resources to execute such documents as may be

necessary to achieve the purposes of this Order.
