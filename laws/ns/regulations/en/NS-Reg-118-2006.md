NS Reg 118/2006

*Made under Section 18 of the Summary Proceedings Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Investigative Warrant Enactment Regulations .

### Enactments added to Schedule B to the Act

2 Schedule B to the Summary Proceedings Act is amended by adding all of the following

enactments:

Apprenticeship and Trades Qualifications Act

Fire Safety Act

Labour Standards Code

Private Investigators and Private Guards Act

Remembrance Day Act

Revenue Act

Sales Tax Act

Technical Safety Act

### Section 2 replaced: O.I.C. 2010-212, N.S. Reg. 77/2010.

### Enactments deleted from Schedule B to Act

3 Schedule B to the Summary Proceedings Act is amended by deleting the following

enactments:

Fire Prevention Act

Retail Business Uniform Closing Day Act

### Section 3 added: O.I.C. 2010-212, N.S. Reg. 77/2010.
