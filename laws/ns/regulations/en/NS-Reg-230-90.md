NS Reg 230/90

*Made under subsection 17(2) of the Summary Proceedings Act*

1 The form of information set out in Schedule 1 to these regulations is prescribed as the form of an information for the purposes of subsection (1) of Section 2B of the Summary Proceedings Act.

2 The form of search warrant set out in Schedule 2 to these regulations, varied to suit the case, is prescribed as the form of a search warrant to be issued pursuant to Section 2B of the Summary Proceedings Act.

3 The form of report to a justice set out in Schedule 3 to these regulations, varied to suit the case, is prescribed as the form of a report to justice pursuant to Section 2E of the Summary Proceedings Act.

## Schedule 1

 [Summary Proceedings Act, Section 2B(1)]

the (dwelling-house, etc.) of C.D., ______________ in the County of ___________.
