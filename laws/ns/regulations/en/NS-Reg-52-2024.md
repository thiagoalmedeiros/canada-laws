NS Reg 52/2024

*Made under subsection 97(1) of the Education Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Excluded Class of Municipality Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means the Education Act .

### Excluded class of municipality

3 A municipality that does not have a current and valid service exchange agreement with

the Province is excluded from the first right of offer in subsection 77(4) of the Act.
