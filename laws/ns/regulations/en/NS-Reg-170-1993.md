NS Reg 170/1993

*Made under Section 6(1) of the Mineral Resources Act*

### Order in Council 93-913

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated the 9th day of November, 1993, pursuant to Section 5 of Chapter 18 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia 1990, the Mineral Resources Act , is pleased to make regulations

declaring certain limestone deposits described in Schedule “A” attached to and forming part of

the report and recommendation to be a mineral within the meaning of Section [clause] 5(1)(b)

of the Mineral Resources Act .

## Schedule “A”

1 Limestone deposits occurring in, upon and under those Crown lands purchased pursuant

to document 2067 dated May 23, 1974, comprising 317 acres, more or less, in the area of

Marble Mountain, Inverness County, and falling within the bounds of the following

claims, is hereby declared to be a mineral:

| Claims | Tract | Claim Reference Map |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HJKOPQ | 70 | 11 F 14 A |
| DEFM | 71 | 11 F 14 A |
| BCDEFGLM | 75 | 11 F 14 A |
| H | 76 | 11 F 14 A |

2 A map depicting the area subject to the declaration in Section 1 is attached as Appendix 1

to these regulations.

3 These regulations come into force on, from and after the 1st day of December, 1993.
