NS Reg 40/68

*Made under subsection 25(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

### Order in Council 68-907

All Department of National Defence vehicles shall be exempt from registration,

provided that the Registrar of Motor Vehicles is provided with an identification list of

all Department of National Defence vehicles operated in Canada, together with a list of

all such vehicles operated in this Province.
