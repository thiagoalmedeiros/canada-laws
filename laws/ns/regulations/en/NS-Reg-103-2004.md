NS Reg 103/2004

*Made under Section 25 of the Off-highway Vehicles Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Off-highway Vehicles Fees Regulations .

### Prescribed fees

2 The fees payable to the Registrar for off-highway vehicles are as follows:

(a) certificate of registration ..................................................................... $39.80

(b) dealer’s license ................................................................................... $33.10

(c) dealer’s plate ...................................................................................... $19.85

(d) dealer’s license renewal ...................................................................... $33.10

(e) registration permit .............................................................................. $13.20

(f) registration permit renewal ................................................................. $13.20

(g) transfer of ownership .......................................................................... $13.20

(h) duplicate of replacement certificate of registration, permit or identification number ......................................................................... $13.20

(i) assigned special identification number .................................................. $2.30

(j) search of department records .............................................................. $13.20

(k) Off-highway Vehicle Infrastructure Fund ........................................... $40.00

### Trail permit fees

3 (1) In this Section,

(a) “Act” means the Off-highway Vehicles Act ;

(b) “designated trail” means a designated trail as defined in clause 2(b) of the Off-highway Vehicles Designated Trails and Trail Permits Regulations made under the Act;

(c) “snow vehicle” means a snow vehicle as defined in clause 2(2)(e) of the Off-highway Vehicles Safety and Training Regulations made under the Act.

(2) The fees as set out in the following table are prescribed as payable to the

Snowmobilers Association of Nova Scotia by operators of snow vehicles for the

use of designated trail permits issued under the Off-highway Vehicles Designated

Trails and Trail Permit Regulations :

| Type of Trail Permit | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Full season if purchased before December 15 | $175.43 |
| Full Season | $263.15 |
| Full season for 3rd or subsequent snow vehicle

registered to person(s) at the same residence | $131.57 |

| Full season—classic (snow vehicle aged 15–29 years

on December 1) | $131.57 |

| Full season—vintage (snow vehicle aged 30 years or

older on December 1) | $43.85 |

| Dealer Tag | $175.43 |
| 1-day Pass | $43.85 |
| 3-day Pass | $78.94 |
| 7-day Pass | $131.57 |

(3) Trail permits for snow vehicles aged 15 years or older must be purchased before

December 15th of the current fee year.
