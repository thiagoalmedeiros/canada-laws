NS Reg 42/84

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 9th day of March, A.D., 1983, and pursuant to Section 2 of Chapter 244 of the

Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to:

(a) constitute all that area of land situate at Wentzell’s Lake, in the County of

Lunenburg, owned by Her Majesty the Queen in right of the Province of Nova

Scotia, to be a Provincial Park, the said area of land being described in

Schedule “A” and shown outlined in red on the copy of Crown Land Records

Plan No. E-13-43 marked Schedule “B”, both Schedules attached to and

forming part of the report and recommendation; and

(b) declare that the park shall be known by the name of “Wentzells Lake

Provincial Park”.

## Schedule “A”

All those certain lots, pieces or parcels of land situate, lying and being at Wentzells Lake, in the

County of Lunenburg, Province of Nova Scotia, shown as Lot No. 1 and Lot No. 2 on Crown

Land Records Plan No. E-13-43 filed in the Crown Land Records Office, Department of Lands

and Forests, Halifax the said parcels of land being more particularly described as follows:

### First Lot

All that certain lot, piece or parcel of land situate, lying and being at Wentzells Lake, in the

County of Lunenburg, and identified as Lot No. 1 on Plan No. E-13-43 filed in the Crown Land

Records Office, Department of Lands and Forests Office, Halifax, the said parcel of land being

more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at a Survey Marker situate on the northern boundary of the public highway leading

from Wentzells Lake to Branch La Have and situate (by magnetic bearings 1972),

N 72° 54' 00" W, a distance of 142.18 feet from the northeastern corner of Lot No. 2;

Thence westerly following the northern boundary of the said public highway 1550 feet, more or

less, to the ordinary high water mark of the eastern shore of La Have River;

Thence northerly and easterly following the various courses of the ordinary high water mark of

the eastern shore of the aforesaid river and the southern shore of Wentzells Lake, 3800 feet

more or less to a point;

Thence S 12° 35' 40" W, a distance of 35 feet, more or less, to a Survey Marker;

Thence continuing S 12° 35' 40" W, 451.09 feet to a Survey Marker;

Thence S 2° 45' 50" E, 183.06 feet to a Survey Marker;

Thence S 55° 07' 03" W, 290.60 feet to a Survey Marker;

Thence S 9° 37' 10" E, 110.57 feet to the place of beginning.

The above described parcel of land contains 54.08 acres, more or less.

### Second Lot

All that certain lot, piece or parcel of land situate, lying and being at Wentzells Lake, in the

County of Lunenburg, and identified as Lot No. 2 on Plan No. E-13-43 filed in the Crown Land

Records Office, Department of Lands and Forests Office, Halifax, the said parcel of land being

more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at a Survey Marker situate on the southern boundary of the public highway leading

from Wentzells Lake to Branch La Have and situate (by magnetic bearings 1972),

S 72° 54' 00" E, a distance of 142.18 feet from the point of beginning of Lot No. 1;

Thence S 10° 37' 30" E, 169.97 feet to a Survey Marker;

Thence S 79° 22' 30" W, 512.17 feet to a Survey Marker;

Thence N 10° 37' 30" W, 166.68 feet to a Survey Marker situate on the southern boundary of

the aforesaid public highway;

Thence northeasterly along the southern boundary of the said public highway 513 feet, more or

less, to the place of beginning, said place of beginning being N 79° 00' E, 512.37 feet from the

last described Survey Marker.

The above described parcel of land contains 1.98 acres, more or less.
