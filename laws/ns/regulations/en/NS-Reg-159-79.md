NS Reg 159/79

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 19th day of September, A.D., 1979, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of

the Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Lower

Half Island Cove Beach, in the County of Guysborough, as shown outlined in red on the copy

of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-11-29 marked Schedule “B” and described in

Schedule “A”, both attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain parcel, piece or lot of land situate, lying and being landward from the mean high

watermark of Lower Half Island Cove Beach, County of Guysborough, Province of Nova

Scotia and being more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at Crown Land Iron Post (IP) Number 4, the said iron post being S 77° 49' 00" E,

distant 3110.00 feet more or less from Crown Land Post Number 6804;

From thence by astronomic bearings, N 20° 20' 00" W, distant 145.42 feet more or less to Iron

Post Number 3 set on the mean high watermark of Lower Half Island Cove Beach;

Thence in generally a northwesterly direction along the many courses of the said mean high

watermark to Iron Post Number 1 being N 56° 58' 46" W, distant 1638.15 feet more or less

from Iron Post Number 3;

Thence S 61° 46' 00" W, distant 145.42 feet more or less to Iron Post Number 2;

Thence S 61° 46' 00" W, distant 104.58 feet to a point;

Thence S 32° 29' 52" E, distant 506.07 feet more or less to Iron Post Number 7;

Thence S 65° 34' 21" E, distant 775.02 feet more or less to Iron Post Number 6;

Thence S 66° 17' 22" E, distant 294.57 feet more or less to Iron Post Number 5;

Thence S 71° 44' 00" E, distant 456.43 feet to a point;

Thence N 20° 20' 00" W, distant 104.58 feet more or less to the place of beginning.
