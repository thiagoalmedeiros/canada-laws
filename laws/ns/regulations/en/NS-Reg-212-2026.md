NS Reg 212/2026

*Made under Section 15 of the Body Armour Control Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Body Armour Temporary Permits Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

“Act” means the Body Armour Control Act ;

“Executive Director” means the Executive Director of the Public Safety and

Security Division of the Department of Justice;

“permit” means a temporary permit issued by the Executive Director under Section

5.

### Delegation of Executive Director powers or duties

3 The Executive Director may delegate, in writing, any of their powers or duties under the

regulations to a person employed in the civil service of the Province.

### Individuals issued permits are prescribed persons

4 An individual who is issued a permit under these regulations is prescribed as a person

who is authorized to possess body armour under clause 4(2)(e) of the Act, in accordance

with the system of permits set out in these regulations.

### Criteria for issuing permit

5 The Executive Director may issue a permit to a person who meets all of the following:

(a) they are at least 19 years old;

(b) they are a resident of the Province;

(c) they plan to engage in journalism or other media production for

humanitarian purposes outside of the Province;

(d) they require body armour to carry out the activities described in clause (c)

safely;

(e) they plan to leave the Province within 72 hours of the start of the term

specified on the permit.

### Term of permit

6 (1) A permit is in effect for the term specified by the Executive Director on the permit.

(2) The Executive Director may amend the term of a permit at any time.

### Conditions on permit

7 (1) The Executive Director may impose any condition on a permit that the Executive

Director considers advisable for purposes of public safety.

(2) The Executive Director may amend a permit’s conditions at any time.

## Form of permit

8 (1) The Executive Director may prescribe the form of a permit.

(2) A permit must include all of the following:

(a) the name of the permit holder;

(b) the term of the permit;

(c) any conditions on the permit imposed by the Executive Director under

Section 7.

### Permit applications

9 (1) An application for a permit must be in the form prescribed by the Executive

Director and contain all the following:

(a) all of the following information about the applicant:

(i) full legal name,

(ii) date of birth,

(iii) civic address,

(iv) mailing address,

(v) telephone number,

(vi) e-mail address;

(b) the date that the applicant plans to leave the Province;

(c) their written consent to a criminal background check;

(d) any information or documents that the Executive Director considers

necessary to assess whether the applicant satisfies all applicable

requirements prescribed by the Act and regulations.

(2) In addition to a completed application form, an applicant must submit all of the

following together with their application:

(a) a copy of their Government-issued photo identification satisfactory to the

Executive Director;

(b) any application fee set out in Section 13;

(c) proof, in a form acceptable to the Executive Director, that the applicant

plans to leave the Province on the date specified in the application.

### Permitted possession of body armour

10 An individual issued a permit may possess body armour for the term and under the

conditions set out in their permit.

### Refusal to issue a permit

11 (1) The Executive Director may not issue a permit unless the Executive Director is

satisfied that the person identified on the permit meets all applicable requirements

under the Act and its regulations and that they

(a) plan to leave the Province within 72 hours of the start of the term specified

on the permit; and

(b) will possess the body armour safely and responsibly.

(2) The Executive Director must notify an applicant who is refused a permit in writing

of the Executive Director’s decision.

### Revocation of a permit

12 (1) The Executive Director may revoke a permit in any of the following

circumstances:

(a) the Executive Director determines that the application contains a false

statement or false information;

(b) the permit holder does not leave the Province within 72 hours of the start of

the term specified on the permit;

(c) the Executive Director is not satisfied that the permit holder will possess the

body armour safely and responsibly;

(d) any additional good and sufficient reason.

(2) A permit holder whose permit is revoked must immediately return it to the

Executive Director.

### Fees

13 No fee is prescribed for a permit.

### Notice to Executive Director

14 (1) A permit holder must notify the Executive Director within 24 hours of becoming

aware that they will not leave the Province by the date specified in their

application.

(2) A permit holder must notify the Executive Director and surrender the permit to the

Executive Director within 24 hours of becoming aware of any of the following:

(a) the permit is lost or stolen;

(b) any body armour in the permit holder’s possession is lost or stolen.

### Permit kept with body armour and presented to peace officer

15 A permit holder must keep the permit with any possessed body armour and must present

the permit to a peace officer or other authority when requested to do so.

### Return to Province

16 A permit holder must do 1 of the following within 24 hours of re-entering the Province:

(a) report to the Executive Director about the disposition of their body armour;

(b) surrender their body armour as directed by the Executive Director.

### Surrender of permit on request

17 A permit holder must surrender their permit to the Executive Director when requested by

the Executive Director.
