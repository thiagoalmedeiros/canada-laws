NS Reg 415/2008

*Made under Section 14 of the Special Places Protection Act*

I, Mark Parent, Minister of Environment for the Province of Nova Scotia, pursuant to

Section 14 of Chapter 438 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Special Places

Protection Act , hereby designate the area of land described in Schedule A and shown on the

map in Schedule B attached to and forming part of this order, as an ecological site, to be

known as Sixth & Coades Lakes Nature Reserve.

This designation is effective on and after the date it is published in the Royal Gazette.

Dated and made at Halifax Regional Municipality, Province of Nova Scotia, 2 September,

2008.

Sgd.: Mark Parent

Honourable Mark Parent

Minister of Environment

## Schedule A

### Description of Sixth & Coades Lakes Nature Reserve

All those certain tracts of land shown outlined in bold line as identified on Compiled Plan

Showing Block 11A & 11B Crown Land, filed in the Department of Natural Resources

Office at Halifax, under Field Plot P-038/08, said tracts of land located at Coades Lake,

Queens County, Province of Nova Scotia.

Containing a total area of 128.9 hectares, more or less.

Excepting the 66-foot wide road on Block 11 A to be retained by the Department of Natural

Resources. Area of said road to be excepted is 1 hectare more or less.

Being and intended to be all those lands registered as:

Parcel Description: BLOCK 11A at Coades Lake

Municipality/County: Queens County

Designation of Parcel on Plan: BLOCK 11A

Mersey Paper Company Ltd. to Her Majesty the Queen in right of the

Province of Nova Scotia

Subject to flowage rights in favour of Nova Scotia Power Inc. and filed in

the Registry of Deeds for Queens County in Liverpool in Book 359, Page

Parcel Description: BLOCK 11B at Coades Lake

Municipality/County: Queens County

Designation of Parcel on Plan: BLOCK 11B

Title of Plan: BLOCK 11A & 11B Land under conveyance from Bowater

### Municipal Government Act compliance statement

Blocks 11A and 11B are exempt from the requirement for subdivision

approval under the Municipal Government Act pursuant to s. 268(2)(b)

because the parcel results from an expropriation.
