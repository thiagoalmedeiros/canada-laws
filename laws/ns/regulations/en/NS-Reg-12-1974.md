NS Reg 12/1974

*Made under Section 25 of the Consumer Reporting Act*

1 The Act shall not apply to

(a) a consumer engaging in a transaction, other than relating to employment, in the

course of carrying on a business, trade or profession; or

(b) a police officer conducting an investigation in the ordinary course of his duty.

### Registration

2 An application for registration as a consumer reporting agency or renewal thereof shall be

in a form as the Director may from time to time specify.

3 Fees payable to the Director are as follows:

| (a) | upon application for registration or reinstatement of registration as a consumer reporting agency | $133.35 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (b) | for each branch office | $30.90 |
| (c) | upon application for renewal of license | $33.00 |
| (d) | upon letter of application for a duplicate certificate of registration when the original has been lost, stolen or destroyed | $30.90 |

### Terms and conditions of registration

4 Every registration expires on the 31st day of March in each year.

5 (1) No person shall be registered as a consumer reporting agency unless the applicant

(a) if an individual, is of the full age of nineteen years or, if a partnership, if each

member of the partnership is of the full age of nineteen years and further if

each individual or member of the partnership is a Canadian citizen or has been

lawfully admitted to Canada and is ordinarily resident in Canada;

(b) if a corporation, is incorporated in Canada and registered to do business in

Nova Scotia.

(2) No person shall be registered as a consumer reporting agency unless the applicant

(a) if an individual, has at least two years of actual related experience in all phases

of consumer reporting;

(b) if a partnership, if each member of the partnership has at least two years of

actual related experience in all phases of consumer reporting;

(c) if a corporation, if the principle officers of the corporation and the managers of

the head office and of each branch thereof, have at least two years of actual

related experience in all phases of consumer reporting.

6 Every applicant for registration shall state in the application an address for service in Nova

Scotia and notification of any change shall be made to the Director forthwith.

7 Every person registered as a consumer reporting agency shall operate from a fixed place of

business in Nova Scotia that shall be open to the public during normal business hours.

8 No registrant shall operate a branch office in Nova Scotia unless such branch office has

been authorized by the registration.

9 Where the registration of a consumer reporting agency is revoked or suspended or where

the registrant has voluntarily gone out of business, he shall immediately return his

certificate of registration to the Registrar by registered mail.

10 For the purposes of compliance with subsection (c) of Section 15 [of the Act], the

disclosure of any change in the ownership of shares will only be required where the

ownership of twenty-five percent or more of the voting shares is changed.

11 An applicant for registration or for renewal of registration or [of a] registered consumer

reporting agency shall furnish the Director with information or material concerning its

officers, organization and operating practices and procedures and such information will be

furnished within a specified time limit and may require verification by affidavit if so

requested by the Director.

### Additional forms

12 A voluntary cancellation of registration under subsection (1) of Section 7 of the Act shall

be in a form as the Director may from time to time specify.

### Definitions

13 The definition of terms as set out in subsection (1) of Section 2 of the Act shall apply to

these terms where same are used in these regulations.
