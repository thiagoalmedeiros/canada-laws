NS Reg 214/2015

*Made under clause 8A(1)(c) and Sections 25 and 66 of the Environment Act*

I, Randy Delorey, Minister of Environment for the Province of Nova Scotia, pursuant to clause

8A(1)(c) and Sections 25 and 66 of Chapter 1 of the Acts of 1994-95, the Environment Act ,

hereby establish standards that apply to watercourse alterations activities for which notification

is required under the Activity [Activities] Designation Regulations by adopting the following

standard published by the Department of Environment on June 1, 2015:

Nova Scotia Watercourse Alterations Standard, v.2

This version replaces all other versions of the Standard previously established and adopted. The

standard is available to the public at https://novascotia.ca/nse/watercourse-alteration/

or at the Department of Environment, 1903 Barrington Street, Suite 2085, Halifax, Nova Scotia.
