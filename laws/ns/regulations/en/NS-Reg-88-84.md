NS Reg 88/84

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 26th day of March, A.D., 1984, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Carters

Beach, in the County of Queens, as shown outlined in red on the copy of Crown Land Records

Office Plan No. E-13-48 marked Schedule “B” and described in Schedule “A”, both Schedules

attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

All that certain area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Carters and

Wobamkek Beaches situate near Southwest Port Mouton, in the County of Queens, Province of

Nova Scotia, as shown on Plan No. C.L.R. E-13-48 filed in the Crown Land Records Office,

Department of Lands and Forests, Halifax, Nova Scotia, a copy of which is attached hereto and

being more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at Rock Post No. G2577 situate at or near the mean high watermark at the southeast

end of Wobamkek Beach, and situate (by grid bearings referred to the Nova Scotia 3° Modified

Transverse Mercator Projection, zone 5, central meridian 64° 30' West longitude) N 81° 26' 00"

W, a distance of 606.98 feet from Nova Scotia Co-Ordinate Monument No. 24750 having

co-ordinate values (Northing 15 953 901.44 feet Easting 17 963 364.39 feet);

From thence S 00° 44' 53" W, 408.06 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2578;

Thence N 49° 43' 45" W, a distance of 855.69 feet to Rock Post No. Q2579;

Thence S 85° 13' 54" W, a distance of 1421.98 feet to Rock Post No. Q2580;

Thence S 73° 48' 17" W, a distance of 835.82 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2581;

Thence N 16° 27' 57" W, a distance of 1279.55 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2582;

Thence N 51° 24' 21" W, a distance of 765.80 feet to Survey Marker No. Q2583;

Thence N 22° 17' 18" E, a distance of 231.95 feet to Rock Post No. Q2584 situate at or near the

mean high watermark at the northwest end of Carters Beach, said point being N 65° 27' 13" W,

a distance of 4123.86 feet from the place of beginning;

Thence southeasterly along the mean high watermark of Carters Beach and crossing Carters

River and along the mean high water mark of Wabamkek Beach 4600 feet, more or less, to the

place of beginning.

Containing 65 acres more or less.
