NS Reg 208/2006

*Made under subsection 302(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Owner’s Permit and License Fees for Foreign

Government Employees and Vehicles Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

(a) “Act” means the Motor Vehicle Act ;

(b) “foreign applicant” means an individual applying to the Registrar of Motor

Vehicles for a driver’s license or owner’s permit and who meets all of the

following criteria:

(i) the individual is a national of a foreign country,

(ii) the individual is a resident,

(iii) the individual is an employee of a foreign country and is posted to the

Province for a specified time period to perform diplomatic, military or

other official functions on behalf of their country, or is a dependent of

such an employee.

### Owner’s permit fees for foreign applicants

3 There is no fee for an owner’s permit for a motor vehicle owned by a foreign applicant.

### Owner’s permit fees for foreign vehicles

4 There is no fee for an owner’s permit for a motor vehicle owned by a foreign government.

### Driver’s license fee for foreign applicant

5 There is no fee for a driver’s license for a foreign applicant.

### Fee for recording ownership

6 There is no fee for recording a foreign applicant or foreign government as the owner of a

motor vehicle.
