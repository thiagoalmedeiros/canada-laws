NS Reg 22/77

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 7th day of January, A.D., 1977, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of

Framboise Beach, in the County of Richmond, Province of Nova Scotia, as shown outlined in

red on the copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-10-66, marked Schedule “B” and

described in Schedule “A”, both attached to and forming part of the report and

recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Framboise

Beach, in the County of Richmond, Province of Nova Scotia as shown outlined in red on the

copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-10-66, attached hereto and being more

particularly described as follows:

Beginning at Crown Land iron post #R3387 situated near Wingham Point;

From thence (by astronomic bearings referenced to Meridian 61° 30' West) north fifty-one

degrees eight minutes thirty-four seconds west nine hundred fifty-six decimal seventy-six feet to

Crown Land iron post #R3388;

Thence north sixty-seven degrees twenty minutes twenty-nine seconds west six hundred twelve

decimal forty-two feet to Crown Land iron post #R3389;

Thence north eighty-two degrees fifty-two minutes fifty-four seconds west one thousand

sixty-nine decimal seventy-eight feet to Crown Land iron post #R3390;

Thence south eighty-eight degrees forty minutes fifty-six seconds west seven hundred

sixty-seven decimal nineteen feet to Crown Land iron post #R3391;

Thence south eighty degrees twenty-six minutes fifty-one seconds west one thousand

twenty-one decimal twenty-seven feet to Crown Land iron post #R3392;

Thence south seventy-seven degrees fifty-three minutes forty-one seconds west eight hundred

fifty-five decimal zero feet to Crown Land iron post #R3393;

Thence south sixty-one degrees ten minutes twenty-one seconds west eight hundred forty-four

decimal ninety-seven feet to Crown Land iron post #R3394;

Thence south fifty-nine degrees forty-one minutes twenty-six seconds west one thousand

eighteen decimal sixteen feet to Crown Land iron post #R3395;

Thence south fifty-two degrees four minutes eleven seconds west six hundred ten decimal

thirty-one feet to Crown Land iron post #R3396;

Thence south fifty-two degrees ten minutes thirty-five seconds west one thousand one hundred

five decimal sixty-nine feet to Crown Land iron post #R3397;

Thence south forty-three degrees three minutes three seconds west two thousand one hundred

fifty-six decimal sixty-five feet to Crown Land iron post #R3398;

Thence south two degrees twelve minutes fifty-eight seconds west five hundred eighty-six

decimal seventy feet to Crown Land iron post #R3399;

Thence south thirty-one degrees forty-four minutes thirty-eight seconds west seven hundred

thirteen decimal thirteen feet to Crown Land iron post #R3400;

Thence south twenty-six degrees twenty minutes thirty-five seconds east three hundred

eighty-nine decimal sixty-four feet to Crown Land iron post #R3401;

Thence north sixty-five degrees three minutes sixteen seconds east ten thousand six hundred

forty-six decimal eighteen feet crossing the waters of Framboise Cove to the place of beginning;

Excepting and reserving from the above described area of land all the portion thereof lying

seaward from the mean high watermark.
