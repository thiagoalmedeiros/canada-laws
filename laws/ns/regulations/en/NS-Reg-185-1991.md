NS Reg 185/1991

*Made under subsection 6A(4) of the Provincial Court Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Regulations Respecting Part-time Judges of the

Provincial Court .

2 In these regulations,

“Desmond Inquiry” means the fatality inquiry ordered on February 14, 2018,

pursuant to subsection 27(1) of the Fatality Investigations Act regarding the deaths

of Aaliyah, Shanna, Brenda and Lionel Desmond;

“part-time judge” means a judge who is retired and is appointed in office as a

judge by the Chief Judge pursuant to Section 6A of the Act.

3 (1) A part-time judge may, subject to the annual approval of the Chief Judge, continue

in office as a part-time judge until the part-time judge attains the age of 75 years.

(2) Despite subsection (1), the part-time judge appointed pursuant to subsection 27(3)

of the Fatality Investigations Act for the purpose of the Desmond Inquiry may

continue in office up to and including June 30, 2023, for the purposes of

completing the inquiry.

4 [repealed]

5 A part-time judge shall have the jurisdiction, power and authority of a judge and shall

exercise that jurisdiction, power and authority at the request of the Chief Judge.

6 A part-time judge shall be remunerated according to the per diem rate from time to time

determined by the tribunal appointed pursuant to Section 21A of the Act.

7 The services of part-time judge provided pursuant to these regulations shall not be

credited as service for purposes of

(a) the Public Service Superannuation Act ;

(b)the Public Service Award; or

(c)the pension benefits provided by Section 23 of the Act.

8 These regulations do not apply to a retired judge who has been reinstated as a practising

member of the Nova Scotia Barristers' Society.
