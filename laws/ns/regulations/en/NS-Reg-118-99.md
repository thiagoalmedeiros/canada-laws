NS Reg 118/99

*Made under Section 11 of the Intercountry Adoption Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Intercountry Adoption Regulations .

### Convention

2 A child adopted in accordance with the Convention has a right to access to information in accordance with the provisions of the Adoption Information Act and the regulations made pursuant to that Act.

### Forms

3 Any forms required for the purpose of implementing the Intercountry Adoption Act and these regulations may be prescribed by the Director, Child Welfare and Residential Services, Department of Community Services.
