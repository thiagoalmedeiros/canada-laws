NS Reg 332/2015

*Made under Section 30 of the Community Economic Development Fund Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Community Economic Development Fund

Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations, “Act” means the Community Economic Development Fund Act .

### Maximum economic development incentive by Department

3 The consent of the Treasury and Policy Board is required for any decision of the

Department approving any 1 economic development incentive that is more than $3

million.

### Criteria for approving economic development incentives

4 In considering applications for economic development incentives, the Department may

only approve economic development incentives to organizations if all of the following

are met:

(a) the purpose of the organization is consistent with the objects and purposes

of the Fund;

(b) the profits, if any, of the organization are used for the benefit of the public

and not for private shareholders.

### Applications for economic development incentives

5 (1) An application for an economic development incentive must be in writing in a

form required by the Department and must include any information the

Department requires to satisfactorily assess the application.

(2) [repealed]

(3) Subject to the Act and these regulations, the Department may approve or reject an

application for an economic development incentive in any manner it considers

appropriate.

### Amendments to economic development incentives

6 (1) A request for an amendment to an economic development incentive must be in

writing in a form required by the Department and must include any information the

Department requires to satisfactorily assess the request.

(2) [repealed]

(3) Subject to the Act and these regulations, the Department may approve or reject a

request for an amendment to an economic development incentive in any manner it

considers appropriate.
