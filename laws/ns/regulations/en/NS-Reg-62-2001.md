NS Reg 62/2001

*Made under clause 9(ae) of the Dairy Industry Act*

The Natural Products Marketing Council , pursuant to clause 9(ae) of Chapter 24 of the Acts of 2000, the Dairy Industry Act , effective on April 1, 2001:

Hereby orders that all information in the possession of [the] Natural Products Marketing Council which is necessary for the proper administration of the milk quota system, the price calculations and producer payments for raw milk be transferred to [the] Dairy Farmers of Nova Scotia.
