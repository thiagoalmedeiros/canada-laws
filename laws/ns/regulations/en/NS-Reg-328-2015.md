NS Reg 328/2015

*Made under clauses 51(1)(fd) and (fe) of the Vital Statistics Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Change of Sex Indicator Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

“Act” means the Vital Statistics Act ;

“physician” means medical practitioner as defined in the Medical Act or the

equivalent under the legislation of a jurisdiction other than the Province;

“psychologist” means registered psychologist or candidate as defined in the Psychologists Act or the equivalent under the legislation of a jurisdiction other than

the Province;

### Professions prescribed for statement in support of application

3 (1) [repealed]

(2) The following are prescribed as professions whose members are authorized to

provide a written statement required by clause 25(2)(c) of the Act to support an

application by a person who is under the age of 16 to change the sex shown on the

person’s birth registration:

(a) physician;

(b) psychologist.
