NS Reg 37/75

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 20th day of January, A.D., 1975, pursuant to the provisions of Section 2 of

Chapter 244 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased

to:

(a) constitute all that area of land including land covered with water, owned by Her

Majesty in right of the Province of Nova Scotia, and situate at or near South West

Margaree, in the County of Inverness, Province of Nova Scotia, to be a Provincial

Park, said area of land being included within the boundaries of the lands described in

Schedule “A” attached to the report and recommendation, and shown outlined on the

Plan of Survey marked Schedule “B”, also attached to the report and

recommendation; and

(b) declare the name by which the park shall be known as “South West Margaree

Provincial Park”.

## Schedule “A”

### South West Margaree Provincial Park

All that certain lot, piece or parcel of land situate, lying and being at or near South West

Margaree, County of Inverness, Province of Nova Scotia as is shown on Plan E-5-42, recorded

in the Crown Land Registry office, Department of Lands and Forests, Halifax, being more

particularly described as follows:

Beginning at a Crown Land Iron Post (C.L.I.P.) set on the intersection of the southwestern

boundary of Highway No. 19 and the northwestern boundary of the road to farms;

Thence south fifty-one degrees forty-four minutes west (S 51° 44' W) along the northwestern

boundary of the said road to farms distant two hundred thirty-one decimal eighty-six (231.86)

feet to a C.L.I.P.;

Thence south thirty-seven degrees twenty-seven minutes west (S 37° 27' W) along the

northwestern boundary of the said road to farms distant four hundred forty-two decimal forty

(442.40) feet to a C.L.I.P.;

Thence southwesterly along the northwestern boundary of said road to farms to a C.L.I.P.

marked I-1864 set on the most eastern corner of lands now or formerly owned by John G.

MacLeod, said C.L.I.P. marked I-1864 being south sixteen degrees nine minutes forty-six

seconds west (S 16° 09' 46" W), distant four hundred nineteen decimal ninety-five (419.95) feet

from the immediate above mentioned C.L.I.P.;

Thence north fifty-two degrees twenty-two minutes fifty seconds west (N 52° 22' 50" W) along

the eastern boundary of said lands now or formerly owned by John G. MacLeod, distant two

hundred forty-four decimal zero (244.0) feet to a C.L.I.P. marked I-1865;

Thence north thirty-nine degrees thirty-seven minutes fifty seconds west (N 39° 37' 50" W)

two hundred thirty-eight decimal sixty-three (238.63) feet to a C.L.I.P. marked I-1866;

Thence south fifty-seven degrees fifty minutes eighteen seconds west (S 57° 50' 18" W) along
