NS Reg 128/96

*Made under Section 72 of the Personal Property Security Act*

| 1 | For filing and registering any document under Section 3 | $50.00 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | For filing and recording court order | $50.00 |
| 3 | For filing and registering other document | $50.00 |
| 4 | For filing certificate under Section 10 | $50.00 |
| 5 | For certificate of status | $25.00 |
| 6 | For each encumbrance in excess of five | $0.00 |
| 7 | For providing certified copy | $20.00 |
| 8 | For providing a photocopy of any document | $20.00 |
| [Note: the Sectional references in items 1 and 4 are to the Corporations Securities Registration Act , R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 102, under which these regulations were originally made and which was repealed by the Personal Property Security Act effective November 3, 1997.] |  |  |
