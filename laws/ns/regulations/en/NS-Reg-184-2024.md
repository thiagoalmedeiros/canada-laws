NS Reg 184/2024

*Made under subsection 80(1) of the Income Tax Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Personal Income Tax Indexation Regulations .

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means the Income Tax Act .

### Consumer price index amount

3 (1) For the purposes of clause 22A(2)(b) of the Act, the consumer price index amount

is 1 of the following:

(a) if approved by the Governor in Council, the amount determined by the

following formula:

A ÷ B - 1

in which

A= the consumer price index for the 12-month period ending August 31

before the taxation year

B = the consumer price index for the 12-month period preceding the

period described in “A”;

(b) an amount determined by the Governor in Council.

(2) If the formula in clause (1)(a) results in a negative number, the result is deemed to

be nil.

(3) The consumer price index for any 12-month period is determined by calculating

the sum of the all-items consumer price indices, not seasonally adjusted, for each

month in the period, dividing the sum by 12, and rounding the result to the nearest

one-thousandth, or if equidistant, rounding to the higher one-thousandth.
