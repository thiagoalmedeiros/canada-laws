NS Reg 144/2000

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated July 13, 2000, and pursuant to Chapter 367 of the Revised Statutes of Nova

Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased, with respect to the designation of provincial

parks, to

(a) designate land at Amherst Shore, Cumberland County, owned by Her Majesty

in right of the Province of Nova Scotia, to be a provincial park, the said land

being described in Schedule “A” and shown outlined in bold line on a copy of

Provincial Crown Lands Record Centre Plan No. E-15-23 marked Schedule

“B”, both schedules attached to and forming part of the report and

recommendation;

(b) declare that the provincial park is to be known by the name “Amherst Shore

Provincial Park”; and

(c) authorize the Minister of Natural Resources to execute such documents as may

be necessary to achieve the purposes of this Order.
