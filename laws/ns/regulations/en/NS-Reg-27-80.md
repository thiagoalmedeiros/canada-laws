NS Reg 27/80

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 12th day of February, A.D., 1980, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high water mark of Sand

River Beach, in the County of Cumberland, as shown outlined in red on the copy of Crown

Land Records Office Plan No. E-11-18 marked Schedule “B” and described in Schedule “A”,

both attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain lot, piece or parcel of land situate lying and being “Lands Lying Landward From

The Mean High Water Mark Of Sand River Beach” as shown on a plan of survey number

P-167/78 prepared by Province of Nova Scotia Department of Lands and Forests, Surveys

Division, dated November 22, 1978 being in the Community of Sand River, County of

Cumberland, Province of Nova Scotia being more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at Iron Post #1, situate on the mean high water mark of Chignecto Bay, said Iron

Post #1 being in a southwesterly direction 1,629.70 feet (on a clockwise angle of 140° 35' 31"

referenced to the centre of the Sand River Bridge and on a clockwise angle of 110° 57' 32"

referenced to Crown Post No. C-30) from Hub “A”, said Hub “A” being 1,416.18 feet from the

centre of the Sand River Bridge and 1,145.44 feet from Crown Post No. C-30, as shown on the

above mentioned plan of survey;

Thence from the place of beginning in a northeasterly and southwesterly direction following

the various courses of the mean high water mark of Chignecto Bay and Sand River 3200 feet

more or less, to a point;

Thence westerly 47 feet more or less to Iron Post #2, said Iron Post #2 being on a clockwise

angle at said Hub “A” referenced to the centre of the Sand River Bridge 82° 33' 06", 243.94 feet

from Hub “A”;

Thence continuing westerly 33 feet more or less to a point;

Thence northerly and southwesterly parallel to an equal distant [distance] from the mean high

water mark of Sand River and Chignecto Bay 1600 feet more or less to a point;

Thence northerly 80 feet more or less to the place of beginning.
