# Contaminated Sites Protocols Adoption Regulations

NS Reg 17/2017

*Made under clause 8A(1)(c) and Section 90 of the Environment Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Contaminated Sites Protocols Adoption Regulations .

### Adopted protocols

2 The following protocols developed by the Department of Environment, as amended, are

adopted by the Minister of Environment under clause 8A(1)(c) of the Environment Act as

standards for the remediation of contaminated sites:

| Protocol | Date adopted |
| --- | --- |
| Notification of Contamination Protocol | July 6, 2013 |
| Environmental Site Assessment for Limited Remediation

Protocol | July 6, 2013 |

| Phase 1 Environmental Site Assessment Protocol | July 6, 2013 |
| Phase 2 Environmental Site Assessment Protocol | July 6, 2013 |
| Remediation Levels Protocol | July 6, 2013 |
| Remedial Action Plan Protocol | July 6, 2013 |
| Confirmation of Remediation Protocol | July 6, 2013 |

### Public availability

3 The protocols adopted by these regulations must be available to the public, clearly

identifying amendments and effective dates, as follows:

(a) on the Department of Environment’s website (currently found at

https://novascotia.ca/nse/contaminatedsites/protocols.asp); and

(b) at the offices of the Department of Environment (currently at 1903 Barrington

Street, Suite 2085, Halifax, Nova Scotia).
