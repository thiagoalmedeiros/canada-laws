NS Reg 167/76

*Made under subsection 3(4) of the Natural Products Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Agriculture and Marketing dated the 28th day of July, 1976, and pursuant to subsection (3) of Section 2 of Chapter 206 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Natural Products Marketing Act, as amended, is pleased to determine that three members of the Nova Scotia Marketing Board shall constitute a quorum.

[Note: The name of the Nova Scotia Marketing Board was changed to the Natural Products Marketing Council in amendments to the Act made by S.N.S. 1982, c. 41.]
