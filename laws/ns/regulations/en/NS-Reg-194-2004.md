NS Reg 194/2004

*Made under subsection 36(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

1 Pursuant to subsection 36(1) of Chapter 293 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Motor Vehicle Act , Nova Scotia number plates issued for the registration year 2004/2005 and subsequent years, in the case of commercial vehicles registered under the International Registration Plan, shall have the following appearance:

(a) the words "NOVA SCOTIA" shall be at the top of the number plate;

(b) the word "APPORTIONED" shall appear at the bottom of the number plate;

(c) the background colour of the number plate shall be yellow and all other letters and numbers shall be red; and

(d) be otherwise in accordance generally with the number plate depiction shown on Schedule "A" attached hereto.

2 The Ministerial Order made under subsection 36(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act , dated February 22, 1993, is hereby repealed.
