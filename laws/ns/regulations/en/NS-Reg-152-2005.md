NS Reg 152/2005

*Made under clause 4(1)(a) of the Universities Accountability and Sustainability Act*

I, Jamie Muir, Minister of Education, pursuant to clause 4(a) of Chapter 13 of the Acts of 1994, the Universities Assistance Act , hereby designate the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design as a university for all the purposes of the Universities Assistance Act , effective on and after the date of this Order.
