NS Reg 137/2014

*Made under Section 8 of the Mi’kmaq Education Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Education dated

February 28, 2012, and pursuant to Section 8 of Chapter 17 of the Acts of 1998, the Mi’kmaq

Education Act , is pleased to make an order adding the Paq’tnkek First Nation to the schedule of

the Mi’kmaq Education Act , effective on and after April 1, 2012.

### O.I.C. 2014-354, N.S. Reg. 129/2014

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Education and

Early Childhood Development dated July 29, 2014, and pursuant to Section 8 of Chapter 17 of

the Acts of 1998, the Mi’kmaq Education Act , is pleased to make an order adding the Glooscap

First Nation to the schedule of the Mi’kmaq Education Act , effective on and after April 1, 2015.
