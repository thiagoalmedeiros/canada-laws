NS Reg 98/2018

*Made under Section 38 of the Motor Vehicle Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Mi’kmaq Number Plates Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

“Mi’kmaq number plate” means a special number plate as described in Section 3;

“Mi’kmaw Youth Fund” means the Mi’kmaw Youth Fund established as a special

purpose fund under Section 10 of the Finance Act .

### Description of plate

3 On application to the Registrar of Motor Vehicles in the form approved by the Registrar,

an eligible person may be issued a special number plate that

(a) is 15.24 cm in width by 30.48 cm in length;

(b) shows a Mi’kmaq petroglyph, a round symbol with 8 stars in it, on the top left

followed by blue letters and numerals on a silver white field; and

(c) bears the words “NOVA SCOTIA” at the top and the words “MI’KMA’KI

LAND OF THE MI’KMAQ” on the bottom centre, and is otherwise in

general accordance with the number plate depicted in Schedule A.

### Eligibility for initial Mi’kmaq number plate

4 A person who has made a contribution of $24 to the Mi’kmaw Youth Fund is eligible to

apply for a Mi’kmaq number plate.

### Maintaining Mi’kmaq number plate

5 A person who has been issued a Mi’kmaq number plate may retain the plate if a $24

donation is made to the Mi’kmaw Youth Fund each time the permit for the motor vehicle

the plate is issued for is renewed.

### Vehicle weight restriction

6 A Mi’kmaq number plate may be issued only for a vehicle that is a passenger motor

vehicle or a commercial motor vehicle and has a registered weight of 5000 kg or less.
