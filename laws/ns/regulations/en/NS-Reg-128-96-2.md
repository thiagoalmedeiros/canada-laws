NS Reg 128/96

*Made under Section 12 of the Corporations Registration Act*

1 Fees to be paid to the Registrar for initial registration of a domestic, foreign, or Dominion

corporation under subsection 12(1) shall be the pro-rated portion of the fees incurred from

the month of registration until the next anniversary month of incorporation based on the

annual fees referred to herein.
