NS Reg 42/78

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 2nd day of February, A.D., 1978, and pursuant to the Beaches Preservation

and Protection Act , Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, Chapter 6, Section 5(1) is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of

Livingston Cove Beach, in the County of Antigonish, Province of Nova Scotia, as shown

outlined in red on the copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-7-90, marked Schedule

“B”, and described in Schedule “A”, both attached to and forming part of the report and

recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

Beginning at iron post No. 2 as shown on Plan E-7-90 on file in the Crown Land Records

Office, Department of Lands and Forests, Halifax, said iron post being placed approximately

one hundred feet inland from the mean high waterline of Northumberland Strait and

approximately on the line of vegetation, and being on a grid bearing of north seven degrees

forty-two minutes twenty-nine seconds east five thousand four hundred seven decimal

sixty-seven feet from Nova Scotia Control Monument 11446 having grid co-ordinates

(N 16, 665, 290.77, E 876, 617.63) based on the Nova Scotia 3° Transverse Mercator

Projection, Zone 4, Central Meridian 61° 30' West Longitude;

From thence (by grid bearings) north sixty-seven degrees fifty minutes thirty-four seconds west

one hundred feet, more or less, to a point on the mean high watermark of Northumberland

Strait as shown on said Plan E-7-90;

Thence generally in a southerly direction along the mean high watermark of the

Northumberland Strait, five thousand seven hundred sixty feet, more or less, to a point as

shown on said Plan E-7-90;

Thence south sixty-seven degrees fifty-minutes thirty-four seconds east one hundred feet, more

or less, to a point as shown on said Plan E-7-90;

Thence northerly along the line of vegetation to the place of beginning.
