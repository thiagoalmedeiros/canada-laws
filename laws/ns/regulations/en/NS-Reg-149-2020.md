NS Reg 149/2020

*Made under clauses 8(a) and (d) of the Provincial Parks Act*

### O.I.C. 2020-271

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

 Forestry dated September 10, 2020, and pursuant to clauses 8(a) and (d) of Chapter 367 of the

 Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased, effective on and

 after October 13, 2020, to

(a) designate approximately 3212 hectares (7937 acres) of Crown lands shown

outlined in bold and shaded dark grey on the plans marked as Schedules “A” to

“K”, attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation, and declare

that it be known as St. Marys River Provincial Park; and

(b) authorize the Minister of Lands and Forestry to execute such documents as may

be necessary to achieve the purposes of this order.
