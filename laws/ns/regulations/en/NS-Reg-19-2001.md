NS Reg 19/2001

*Made under Section 8 of the Sydney Steel Corporation Sale Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Finance dated February 20, 2001, and pursuant to subsection 2(d) [Section 8] of Chapter 33 of the Statutes of Nova Scotia, 2000, the Sydney Steel Corporation Sale Act , is pleased to designate February 28, 2001, as the Effective Date for the purposes of the Sydney Steel Corporation Sale Act .
