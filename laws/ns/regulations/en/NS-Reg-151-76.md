NS Reg 151/76

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 1st day of October, A.D., 1976, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Colindale

Beach, in the County of Inverness, Province of Nova Scotia, as shown outlined in red on the

copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-7-76, marked Schedule “B” and described in

Schedule “A”, both attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Colindale Beach,

in the County of Inverness, Province of Nova Scotia as shown outlined in red on the copy of

Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-7-76, attached hereto and being more particularly

described as follows:

Beginning at an iron post # W11 situated on the southwest end of the sand beach and

approximately forty feet south of the mean high waterline of Northumberland Strait;

From thence (by astronomic bearings) south eighty-seven degrees forty-two minutes

twenty-seven seconds east a distance of one thousand three hundred fifty-eight decimal

sixty-one feet to an iron post # W9;

Thence north six degrees twenty-eight minutes five seconds east a distance of seven hundred

seven decimal seventy-one feet to an iron post # W10 being approximately fifteen feet south

from the mean high waterline of Northumberland Strait;

Thence north twenty-four degrees thirty minutes west a distance of two thousand one hundred

sixty feet to a point situate in the waters of Northumberland Strait;

Thence south sixty-six degrees twenty-five minutes forty-six seconds west a distance of one

thousand five hundred seventy-seven decimal twelve feet to a point situate in the waters of

Northumberland Strait;

Thence south twenty-four degrees thirty minutes east a distance of two thousand one hundred

eighty feet to the place of beginning.

Excepting and reserving from the above described area of land all the portion thereof lying

seaward from the mean high watermark.
