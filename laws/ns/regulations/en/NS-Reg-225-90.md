NS Reg 225/90

*Made under Section 10 of the Motor Vehicle Act*

1 In these regulations, "ambulance" means a vehicle which has passed the

Ambulance Vehicle Inspection Report administered by the Health Services and

Insurance Commission.

2 There is a class of vehicle designated and known as an ambulance.

3 Where all requirements of the Act and any regulations thereunder for vehicle

registration are complied with in respect of a vehicle and the Registrar is

satisfied that the vehicle is an ambulance, the vehicle may be registered as an

ambulance, and the motor vehicle permit assigned to the vehicle may identify

the vehicle as ambulance.
