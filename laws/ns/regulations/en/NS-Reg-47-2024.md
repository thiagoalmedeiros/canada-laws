NS Reg 47/2024

*Made under clauses 8(a) and (d) of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources and Renewables dated January 12, 2023 [2024], and pursuant to clauses 8(a) and (d)

of Chapter 367 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is

pleased, effective on and after February 13, 2024, to

(a) designate approximately 20 hectares (50 acres) of Crown lands in Pictou County,

currently identified as PIDs 00884064 and 00884049 shown outlined in bold and shaded

dark grey on the plan marked Schedule “A” attached to and forming part of the report

and recommendation and declare that it be known as Merigomish Harbour Provincial

Park; and

(b) authorize the Minister of Natural Resources and Renewables to execute such documents

as may be necessary to achieve the purposes of this Order.
