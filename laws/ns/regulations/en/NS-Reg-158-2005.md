NS Reg 158/2005

*Made under Section 5 of the Volunteer Protection Act*

### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Designation of Non-profit Organizations

Regulations .

### Designated non-profit organizations

2 The following bodies are designated as non-profit organizations for the purposes of the Volunteer Protection Act :

(a) the Canadian Mental Health Association, Nova Scotia Division;

(b) the Nova Scotia Voluntary Planning Board;

(c) the Progressive Conservative Association of Nova Scotia, a recognized party

registered by the Chief Electoral Officer under the Elections Act , and every

electoral district association of the Progressive Conservative Association of

Nova Scotia that is registered under the Elections Act ;

### Clause 2(c) added: O.I.C. 2009-172, N.S. Reg. 187/2009.

(d) the Nova Scotia Liberal Party, a recognized party registered by the Chief

Electoral Officer under the Elections Act , and every electoral district

association of the Liberal Association of Nova Scotia that is registered under

the Elections Act ;

### Clause 2(d) added: O.I.C. 2009-172, N.S. Reg. 187/2009.

(e) the Nova Scotia New Democratic Party, a recognized party registered by the

Chief Electoral Officer under the Elections Act , and every electoral district

association of the Nova Scotia New Democratic Party that is registered under

the Elections Act ;

### Clause 2(e) added: O.I.C. 2009-172, N.S. Reg. 187/2009.

(f) the Green Party of Nova Scotia, a recognized party registered by the Chief

Electoral Officer under the Elections Act , and every electoral district

association of the Green Party Association of Nova Scotia that is registered

under the Elections Act .

### Clause 2(f) added: O.I.C. 2009-172, N.S. Reg. 187/2009.

### Section 2 replaced: O.I.C. 2006-528, N.S. Reg. 222/2006.

### Deemed revocation of designation

3 The designation of a recognized party and each of its electoral district associations under

Section 2 is deemed to be revoked effective the date that the party is deregistered by the

Chief Electoral Officer under the Elections Act .

### Section 3 added: O.I.C. 2009-172, N.S. Reg. 187/2009.
