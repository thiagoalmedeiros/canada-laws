NS Reg 34/1997

*Made under Section 13 of the Sales Tax Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Health Services Tax Suspension Regulations .

2 (1) Part II of the Revenue Act does not apply to the purchase of tangible personal

property on or after April 1, 1997.

(2) For greater certainty, nothing in this Section affects the operation of Section 12 of

the Sales Tax Act or clause 31C(1)(b) of the Revenue Act .

3 [repealed]

4 [repealed]

5 These regulations have effect on and after April 1, 1997.
