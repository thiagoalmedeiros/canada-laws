NS Reg 86/80

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 7th day of May, A.D., 1980, pursuant to Section 2 of Chapter 244 of the

Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased to:

(a) constitute all that area of land situate near Waterside, in the County of Pictou, owned

by Her Majesty in the right of the Province of Nova Scotia, to be a Provincial Park,

the said area of land being described in Schedule “A” and shown outlined in red on

the copy of Crown Land Records Plan No. E-9-60 marked Schedule “B”, excepting

the road shown shaded in green on Schedule “B”, both Schedules attached to and

forming part of the report and recommendation; and

(b) declare that the park shall be known by the name of “Waterside Beach Provincial

Park”.

### Order approved by O.I.C. 94-399

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated the 15th day of April, A.D., 1994, pursuant to Section 8(b) of Chapter 367 of

the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , and to Section 16(1)(a) of

Chapter 114 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Crown Lands Act , is pleased to:

(a) decrease the size of the Waterside Beach Provincial Park, in the County of Pictou, so

constituted by Order in Council 80-666, dated the 20th day of May, A.D., 1980, by

withdrawing from the park of a parcel of Crown land containing approximately

0.5374 acres and shown outlined in bold line on the plan marked Schedule “A”,

attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation; and

(b) approve the issuance of a deed by the Minister of Natural Resources to convey the

said parcel of Crown land containing approximately 0.5374 acres and shown

outlined in bold line on the said Schedule “A” to the Waterside Cemetery, a body

corporate registered pursuant to the Cemetery Companies Act and with its head

office at Waterside, in the County of Pictou, for the nominal consideration of One

Dollar ($1.00) plus all costs as defined by the Minister of Natural Resources, and to

authorize the Minister of Natural Resources to execute such documents as may be

necessary to achieve the purposes of this Order.
