NS Reg 112/87

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 21st day of May, A.D., 1987, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

determine as a beach an area of land situate at Tor Bay, in the County of Guysborough, lying

landward from the mean high water mark and immediately adjacent thereto, the said land being

shown outlined in bold line on the copy of Crown Lands Records Centre Plan No. E-13-41

marked Schedule “B” and described in Schedule “A”, both Schedules attached to and forming

part of the report and recommendation.
