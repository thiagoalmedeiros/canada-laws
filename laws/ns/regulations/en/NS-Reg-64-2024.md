NS Reg 64/2024

*Made under Section 15 of the Housing Supply and Services Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Municipal Public Housing Contributions

Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

“Act” means the Housing Supply and Services Act ;

“public housing” means housing owned by the Province in which the residents are

provided with financial assistance by the Province to obtain or maintain housing.

### Calculation of annual public housing contribution

3 A municipality’s annual municipal public housing contribution under subsection 11(3) of

the Act is equal to [the] total of the municipality’s percentage contribution for all public

housing properties located in the municipality and for which the municipality was

required to contribute on January 1, 2024.

### Municipalities excluded from municipal public housing contributions

4 A municipality is excluded from the requirement to pay a municipal contribution amount

towards public housing under subsection 11(4) of the Act if the municipality has signed

the service exchange agreement with the Province that is effective April 1, 2024.
