NS Reg 414/2008

*Made under Section 14 of the Special Places Protection Act*

I, Mark Parent, Minister of Environment for the Province of Nova Scotia, pursuant to

Section 14 of Chapter 438 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Special Places

Protection Act , hereby designate the area of land described in Schedule A and shown on the

map in Schedule B, attached to and forming part of this order, as an ecological site, to be

known as West Branch Medway River Nature Reserve.

This designation is effective on and after the date it is published in the Royal Gazette.

Dated and made at Halifax Regional Municipality, Province of Nova Scotia, 2 Sept., 2008.

Sgd.: Mark Parent

Honourable Mark Parent

Minister of Environment

## Schedule A

### Description of West Branch Medway River Nature Reserve

All that certain tract of land shown outlined in bold line as identified on Compiled Plan

Showing Block 22 Crown Land, filed in the Department of Natural Resources Office at

Halifax, under Field Plot P-034/08, said tract of land located at Medway Lake, Annapolis

County, Province of Nova Scotia.

Containing a total area of 79 hectares, more or less.

Being and intended to be all those lands registered as:

Parcel Description: BLOCK 22 at Medway Lake

Municipality/County: Annapolis County

Designation of Parcel on Plan: BLOCK 22

Title of Plan: BLOCK 22 Proposed conveyance from Bowater Mersey Paper

Company Ltd. to Her Majesty the Queen in right of the Province of Nova

Scotia

Registration District: Annapolis County

Registration Reference of Plan: 87375169

Nova Scotia Department of Natural Resources Field Plot P-097/06

### Municipal Government Act compliance statement

The parcel is exempt from the requirement for subdivision approval under

the Municipal Government Act pursuant to s. 268(2)(b) because the parcel

results from an expropriation.
