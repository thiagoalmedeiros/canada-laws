NS Reg 41/1999

*Made under Section 18 of the Communications and Information Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Royal Gazette Part II Fees Regulations .

### Fee for single issue

2 The fee for a single issue of the Royal Gazette Part II is $0.36 per page.

### Minimum charge

3 The fee prescribed in Section 2 is subject to a $5.83 minimum charge.
