NS Reg 179/2024

*Made under Section 251A of the Halifax Regional Municipality Charter*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Planning Appeals Non-Substantive Matters

Regulations .

### Non-substantive matters prescribed

2 For the purposes of Section 251A of the Halifax Regional Municipality Charter , each of

the following is prescribed as a non-substantive matter in respect of a residential

development:

(a) colour;

(b) the material to be used for cladding;

(c) fenestration;

(d) landscaping.
