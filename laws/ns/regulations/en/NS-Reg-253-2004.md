*Made under Section 28 of the Ombudsman Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Designation of Agencies Regulations .

2 In these regulations, “Act” means the Ombudsman Act .

of the Act.

9 The Nova Scotia Community College established under the Community Colleges Act is

designated as an agency for the purpose of clause 2(a) of the Act.
