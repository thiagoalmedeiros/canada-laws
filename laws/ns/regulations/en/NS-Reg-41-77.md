NS Reg 41/77

*Made under subsection 117(4) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

Pursuant to Section 106(4) [subsection 117(4)] of the Motor Vehicle Act, I hereby

prescribe that the sign, emblem or device to be exhibited on a farm tractor, a vehicle

drawn by a farm tractor or a motor vehicle other than a motor-driven cycle and not

capable of maintaining a rate of speed in excess of 25 miles per hour when driven on a

highway shall be as defined, described and mounted in accordance with and in the

manner provided by C.S.A. Standard D198-1977.

Made at Halifax this 31st day of May, 1977 (Signed) J. Fraser Mooney
