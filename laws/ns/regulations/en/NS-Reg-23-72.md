NS Reg 23/72

*Made under Section 74 of the Health Protection Act*

1 Except as provided in Section 2, no person shall sell turtles of any kind or genera.

2 Section 1 shall not apply to the sale of turtles to zoos, universities, or other post second ary educational institutions or to such other persons as the Deputy Minister of Health and Wellness may authorize. [Note: The reference to the Deputy Minister has been updated in accordance with Order in Council 2011-15 under the Public Service Act , R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 376, effective January 11, 2011.]
