NS Reg 189/2014

*Made under clauses 46(3)(b) and (c) of the Agriculture and Marketing Act*

I certify that the Wild Blueberry Producers Association of Nova Scotia, a commodity group

designated under the Agriculture and Marketing Act by Order dated December 14, 1987,

pursuant to clauses 46(3)(b) and (c) of Chapter 6 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989,

the Agriculture and Marketing Act , at a meeting held on March 22, 2014, carried a motion to

(a) revoke the levy set by the commodity group that was effective June 21,

2006;

(b) require any person engaged in the production of wild blueberries to pay a

levy of $0.01 per pound of wild blueberries.

(c) require any person engaged in the marketing of wild blueberries to deduct,

from any amount payable by that person to any person engaged in the

production of wild blueberries, any amount payable by the latter person to

the commodity group by way of levy or charge fixed pursuant to clause (b)

and to remit all amounts so deducted to the commodity group,

effective on and after the date of approval by the Minister of Agriculture.

Signed at , Nova Scotia, on [ sic ]

Wild Blueberry Producers Association of Nova

Scotia

per: sgd.: Peter J. Rideout

Peter Rideout

Executive Director

I, Keith Colwell, Minister of Agriculture for the Province of Nova Scotia, hereby approve the

above levy set by the Wild Blueberry Producers Association of Nova Scotia.

Dated: Dec. 3/14

Sgd.: Keith Colwell

Honourable Keith Colwell

Minister of Agriculture
