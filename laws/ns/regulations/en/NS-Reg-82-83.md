NS Reg 82/83

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 29th day of April, A.D., 1983, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high water mark of St.

Catherines River Beach, in the County of Queens, as shown outlined in red on the copy of

Crown Lands Records Office Plan No. E-8-63 B marked Schedule “B” and described in

Schedule “A”, both Schedules attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

River Beach, situate at South West Mouton, in the County of Queens, Province of Nova Scotia,
