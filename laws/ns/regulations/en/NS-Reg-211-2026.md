NS Reg 211/2026

*Made under Section 21 of the Agricultural Weed Control Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Agricultural Weed Control Regulations .

### Definitions

2 (1) In these regulations,

“Act” means the Agricultural Weed Control Act .

(2) In the Act and its regulations,

“inspector” means a person appointed as an inspector by the Minister.

### Moving contaminated material

3 A person must not move or transport any contaminated material unless the contaminated

material has been treated with treatment methods designated by the Minister in the Noxious Weeds Regulations made under the Act.
