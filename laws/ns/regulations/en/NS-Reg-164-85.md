NS Reg 164/85

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 31st day of July, A.D., 1985, pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land situate at Port Morien, in the County of Cape Breton, lying

landward of the mean high watermark and immediately adjacent thereto, the said land being

shown outlined in red on the copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-15-6 marked

Schedule “B” and described in Schedule “A”, both Schedules attached to and forming part of

the report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain area of land lying to the landward and seaward from the mean high watermark

of Port Morien Beach, Cape Breton County, Nova Scotia, as shown on Field Plot No. P-066/85

(C.L.R. No. E-15-6) signed on the 16th day of January, 1985 by Wayne D. Hardy, Nova Scotia

Land Surveyor and filed in the Department of Lands and Forests Office, Halifax and being

more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at Survey Marker Number C.B. 3407 being by Grid Azimuth (based on the Nova

Scotia 3° Transverse Mercator Projection, Zone 4, Central Meridian at 61° 30' west longitude)

210° 26' 51" at a distance of 1791.97 feet from Nova Scotia Co-ordinate Monument Number

1861 and also being 305° 39' 07" at a distance of 45 feet more or less from the mean high

watermark on the northern shore of Morien Bay;

Thence 305° 39' 07", a distance of 21 feet more or less to the southern boundary of the public

highway leading from Glace Bay to Catalone Road;

Thence southwesterly along the southern boundary of the said road, a distance of 700 feet more

or less to a point on the mean high watermark on the northern shore of the inner bay of Morien

Bay;

Thence southwesterly along the mean high watermark on the northern shore of the said inner

bay, a distance of 670 feet more or less to a point, said point being southeasterly at a distance of

4 feet more or less from Survey Marker Number C.B. 3408;

Thence continuing southwesterly along the mean high watermark on the northern shore of the

said inner bay, a distance of 5300 feet more or less to a point, said point being 135° 02' 52" at

distance of 5 feet more or less from Survey Marker Number C.B. 3409;

Thence continuing 135° 02' 52", crossing the inner bay of Morien Bay a distance of 6460 feet

more or less to a point situate on the mean high watermark on the southern shore of the said

bay, said point being 315° 02' 52" a distance of 2 feet more or less from Survey Marker C.B.

3410;

Thence northeasterly along the mean high watermark along the southern shore of the said inner

bay a distance of 8200 feet more or less to a point;

Thence 40° 45' 10", a distance of 135 feet more or less to Survey Marker Number C.B. 3406;

Thence 305° 39' 07", a distance of 21 feet more or less to a point on the mean high watermark

on the southern shore of Morien Bay;

Thence continuing 305° 39' 07", crossing Morien Bay a distance of 6530 feet more or less to a

point on the mean high watermark on the northern shore of said Morien Bay;

Thence continuing 305° 39' 07", a distance of 45 feet more or less to Survey Marker Number

C.B. 3407 and the place of beginning.
