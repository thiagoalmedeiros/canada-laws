NS Reg 82/76

*Made under subsection 25(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

Pursuant to Section 25 of the Motor Vehicle Act, the following types of vehicles are exempt

from registration provided that the vehicles:

(a) are used exclusively for Department of Transportation and Public Works

purposes;

(b) are painted Department of Transportation and Public Works yellow;

(c) display a Department of Transportation and Public Works machine

identification number:

Asphalt plants Motor graders

Asphalt spreaders Mowers

Bulldozers Mulchers

Bush chippers Pull graders

Chip spreaders Rollers

Compressors Rubber-tired loaders

Concrete mixers Scales

Conveyors Sweepers

Crushers Tank car heaters

Utility tractors Tar kettles

Hydro-seeders Welders

Industrial tractors Other similar units being used

Mixalls for maintenance purposes
