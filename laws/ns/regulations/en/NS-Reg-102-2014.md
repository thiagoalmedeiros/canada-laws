NS Reg 102/2014

*Made under Section 50 of the Technical Safety Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Technical Safety Standards Regulations .

### Edition of standard adopted

2 The edition listed by date in these regulations is the edition of the standard adopted by

these regulations.

### Definitions

3 In these regulations,

“API” means the American Petroleum Institute;

“ASME” means the American Society of Mechanical Engineers;

“ASHRAE” means the American Society of Heating, Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning Engineers;

“CSA” means the Canadian Standards Association;

“NBBI” means the National Board of Boiler and Pressure Vessel Inspectors.
