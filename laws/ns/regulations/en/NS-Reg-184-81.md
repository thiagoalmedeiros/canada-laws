NS Reg 184/81

*Made under subsections 25(2) and 304(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

1 (1) The Canadian Agreement on Vehicle Registration, as executed by the

Council of Ministers of Transportation and Highway Safety at Toronto,

Ontario on October 2, 1980 is adopted as a mileage prorated vehicle

registration plan for inter-provincial trucking, effective from April 1, 1982.

(2) The mileage prorated vehicle registration plan referred to in subsection (1)

shall be administered in accordance with the Canadian Agreement on

Vehicle Registration Procedural Manual and amendments thereto.
