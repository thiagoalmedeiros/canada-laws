NS Reg 160/1990

*Made under Section 10 of the Trade Union Act*

1 In determining the composition of a “sector” as defined in clause (h) of Section 92 of the

Act, consideration shall be given to work characteristics which shall include the type of

(a) processes;

(b) materials;

(c) techniques;

(d) skills;

(e) trades; and

(f) end use

involved in the construction.
