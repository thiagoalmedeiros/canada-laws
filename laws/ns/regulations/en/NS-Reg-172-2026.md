NS Reg 172/2026

*Made under Section 277B of the Halifax Regional Municipality Charter*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Order Criteria and Factors Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

“Act” means the Halifax Regional Municipality Charter ;

“order” means an order that the Minister may issue under Section 223B of the Act

to extend or alter an urban service area;

“urban service area” means an urban service area as defined in Section 223B of the

Act.

### Criteria and factors for issuing orders

3 (1) Before issuing an order, the Minister must consider whether the extension or

alteration of the urban service area is related to any of the following:

(a) materially increasing housing supply;

(b) supporting major employment growth;

(c) removing barriers to significant economic development opportunities;

(d) advancing provincial population growth objectives.

(2) The Minister must not issue an order unless it is proposed by 1 of the following:

(a) the Municipality;

(b) a department of the provincial government;

(c) a Crown agency;

(d) another provincial authority.

(3) In the order, the Minister must state that extending or altering the urban service

area does not include a requirement that the Municipality service the area or

require or guarantee infrastructure servicing in the area.
