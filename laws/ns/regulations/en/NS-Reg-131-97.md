NS Reg 131/97

*Made under clauses 44(1)(b), (e) & (f) of the Real Estate Trading Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Real Estate Trading Commission Regulations .

2 For greater certainty, "majority vote" means a vote of the majority of voting members of the Commission present at a meeting.

3 (1) The Commission is vested with the power to sue and be sued, to contract and

be contracted with in its name, to have a common seal and to alter or change

it at pleasure, to have perpetual succession, to acquire and hold property or

movables and to alienate the same at pleasure.

(2) Members of the Commission when acting within the powers and duties

assigned to them under the Act and the regulations are exempt from personal

liability for its debts, obligations or acts.

4 The Commission shall forward to the Superintendent a copy of each annual

financial report filed in respect of the trust accounts of licensed brokers for the first

year of the operation of the Commission.
