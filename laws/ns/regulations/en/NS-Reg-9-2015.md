NS Reg 9/2015

*Made under Section 2 of the February Holiday Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Labour and

Advanced Education dated December 17, 2014, and pursuant to Section 2 of Chapter 35 of the

Acts of 2013, the February Holiday Ac t, is pleased to determine “Nova Scotia Heritage Day” as

the name of the holiday to be kept and observed throughout the Province on the third Monday

in February in 2015 and in each subsequent year.
