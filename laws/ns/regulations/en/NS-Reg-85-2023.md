NS Reg 85/2023

*Made under Section 369 of the Municipal Government Act*

### Order M10868

### Nova Scotia Utility and Review Board

### In the matter of the Municipal Government Act

- and -

In the matter of an application by the Town of Wolfville to confirm the number of

councillors

Before : Jennifer L. Nicholson, CPA, CA, Panel Chair

### Order

The Town of Wolfville made an application under s. 369 of the Municipal Government Act and

the Board issued its written Decision on May 12, 2023.

The Board approves the application and orders that the number of councillors for the Town is

confirmed at six (6), to be elected at-large.

Dated at Halifax, Nova Scotia, this 12th day of May, 2023.

sgd. Bruce A. Kiley Chief Clerk of the Board
