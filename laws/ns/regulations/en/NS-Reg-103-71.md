NS Reg 103/71

*Made under subsection 302(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act*

### Order in Council 71-360

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Highways dated the 5th day of March, 1971, pursuant to Section 270(1) of the Motor Vehicle Act, Chapter 191, R.S.N.S. 1967 [Section 302(1), R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 293] is pleased to determine as follows:

Vehicles owned and operated by members of the Canadian Armed Forces on detached duty in jurisdictions outside Canada may be registered upon payment of a nominal registration fee as set out in the regulations respecting documents and services fees subject to the conditions that: Amended: O.I.C. 2005-81, N.S. Reg. 24/2005. (a) the application for registration be accompanied by a statement from the commanding officer of the member making an application that such member is serving on detached duty in a jurisdiction outside Canada, and (b) the commanding officer of the member having a vehicle registered under this provision advises that Registrar of Motor Vehicles when such member has been returned to duty in this Province.
