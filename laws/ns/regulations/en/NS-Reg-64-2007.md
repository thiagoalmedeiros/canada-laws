NS Reg 64/2007

*Made under Section 16 of the Conservation Easements Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Archaeological Sites Regulations .

### Definitions

2 In these regulations,

(a) “Act” means the Conservation Easements Act ;

(b) “recognized archaeological site” means land that has been documented and

recognized as part of the Maritime Archaeological Resource Inventory as

maintained by the Department of Tourism, Culture and Heritage.

### Conservation easement for recognized archaeological site

3 A conservation easement may be granted under the Act for the purpose of protecting,

restoring or enhancing land that is a recognized archaeological site.
