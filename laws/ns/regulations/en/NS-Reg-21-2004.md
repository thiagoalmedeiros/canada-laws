### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Assignment of Authority Regulations .

### Appointments of notaries public and commissioners

2 For the purpose of Sections 2, 6 and 7 of the Notaries and Commissioners Act , the Minister of Justice is assigned the authority to approve appointments of notaries public and commissioners for the Province.
