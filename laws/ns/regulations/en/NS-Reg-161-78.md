NS Reg 161/78

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 18th day of July, A.D., 1978, and pursuant to Section 5(1) of Chapter 6 of the

Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , is pleased to

designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Cooks

Cove Beach, in the County of Guysborough, Province of Nova Scotia, as shown outlined in red

on the copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-9-57 marked Schedule “B” and

described in Schedule “A”, both schedules attached to and forming part of the report and

recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Cooks Cove

Beach, in the County of Guysborough, Province of Nova Scotia, as shown outlined in red on

Plan of Survey number E-9-57 prepared by Province of Nova Scotia, Department of Lands and

Forests, Surveys Division, dated June 13, 1978 and being more particularly described as

follows:

### Lot A

Beginning at a point, said point being theoretical point “A” as shown on said plan, said point

also being situate on the mean high watermark of – Cooks Cove Beach;

Thence (by astronomic bearings) S 49° 46' 52" E, 65' more or less to an iron post;

Thence continuing S 49° 46' 52" E, 142' more or less to a point on the mean high watermark of

a pond, said point being theoretical point “B”;

Thence northeasterly along the mean high watermark of said pond to the mean high watermark

of Cooks Cove Beach;

Thence northwesterly along the mean high watermark of Cooks Cove Beach to the point of

beginning.

### Lot B

Beginning at a point, said point being theoretical point “C” as shown on said plan, said point

also being situate on the mean high watermark of Cooks Cove Beach;

Thence (by astronomic hearings) S 15° 22' 52" W, 20' more or less to an iron post, said post

being N 87° 52' 52" E, 927.13' from the iron post as described in Lot A;

Thence continuing S 15° 22' 52" W, 45.5' more or less to a point on the mean high watermark

of a pond, said point being theoretical point “D”;

Thence westerly along the mean high watermark of said pond to the mean highwater mark of

Cooks Cove Beach;

Thence easterly along the mean high watermark of Cooks Cove Beach to the point of

beginning.
