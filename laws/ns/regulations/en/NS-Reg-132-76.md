NS Reg 132/76

*Made under subsection 5(1) of the Beaches Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Lands and

Forests dated the 7th day of May, A.D., 1976, pursuant to subsection (1) of Section 5 of the Beaches Preservation and Protection Act , Chapter 6 of the Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1975, is

pleased to designate as a beach an area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark

of Mahoney’s Beach, in the County of Antigonish, Province of Nova Scotia, as shown on the

copy of Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-5-58 and described in Schedule “A”, both

attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation.

## Schedule “A”

All that certain area of land lying landward from the mean high watermark of Mahoney’s

Beach, in the County of Antigonish, Province of Nova Scotia as shown on Plan No. E-5-58

filed in the Crown Land Records Office, and being more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at an iron pipe placed on the east side of the road leading to Mahoney’s Beach and

designated as Point “A” on said Plan E-5-38;

From thence (by astronomic bearings referenced to meridian 61° 30' west longitude) south

eleven degrees twenty-five minutes west, a distance of two hundred nineteen decimal seven feet

to an iron pipe situate on or near the mean high watermark of Mahoney’s Beach, said iron pipe

designated as Point “B” on Plan E-5-58;

Thence southerly, easterly and northerly following the various courses of the mean high

watermark of Mahoney’s Beach to a point situate south forty-five degrees forty-eight minutes

east, a distance of one hundred fifty feet more or less from place of beginning, the said point

being designated as Point “C” on said Plan E-3-58;

Thence north forty-five degrees forty-eight minutes west, one hundred fifty feet more or less to

the place of beginning.

[Note: The map referred to as Crown Land Records Office Plan No. E-5-58 was not filed with the Office of

the Registrar of Regulations.]
