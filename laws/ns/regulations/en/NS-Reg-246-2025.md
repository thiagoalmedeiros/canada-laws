NS Reg 246/2025

*Made under clauses 8(a) and (d) of the Provincial Parks Act*

### O.I.C. 2025-318

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Minister of Natural

Resources dated September 15, 2025, and pursuant to clauses 8(a) and (d) of Chapter 367 of the

Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1989, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased, effective on and

after November 4, 2025, to

(a) designate approximately 39 hectares (96.4 acres) of Crown lands in Guysborough

County, currently identified as PIDs 35182575, 35079615, 35078203, 35052281,

35239268 and 35239276, shown outlined in bold and shaded dark grey on the plan

marked Schedule “A” attached to and forming part of the report and recommendation

and declare that it be known as Port Shoreham Beach Provincial Park; and

(b) authorize the Minister of Natural Resources to execute such documents as may be

necessary to achieve the purposes of this Order.
