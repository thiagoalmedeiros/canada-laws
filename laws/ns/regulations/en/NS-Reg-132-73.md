NS Reg 132/73

*Made under Section 8 of the Provincial Parks Act*

The Governor in Council on the report and recommendation of the Acting Minister of Lands

and Forests dated the 31st day of May, A.D., 1973, pursuant to the provisions of Section 2 of

Chapter 244 of the Revised Statutes of Nova Scotia, 1967, the Provincial Parks Act , is pleased

to:

(a) constitute all that area of land including land covered with water owned by Her

Majesty in right of the Province of Nova Scotia, and situate at or near the

Village of Middle Musquodoboit, in the County of Halifax, Province of Nova

Scotia, to be a provincial park, said area of land being included within the

boundaries of the lands described in Schedule “A” attached to the report and

recommendation, and shown outlined on the Plan of Survey marked Schedule

“B”, also attached to the report and recommendation; and

(b) declare the name by which the park shall be known as “Musquodoboit Valley

Provincial Park”.

## Schedule “A”

### Musquodoboit Valley Provincial Park

All that certain lot, piece or parcel of land situate, lying and being near the Village of Middle

Musquodoboit, County of Halifax, Province of Nova Scotia as shown on Plan #E-5-28 on file

at the Crown Land Records Office, Department of Lands and Forests, Halifax and being more

particularly described as follows:

Beginning at an Iron Pin No. H4639, situate where the easterly boundary of lands appearing to

be now or formerly owned by P. L. Fisk intersects the southern boundary of the public highway

No. 224 leading from Middle Musquodoboit to Upper Musquodoboit;

From thence (by astronomic bearings) S 36° 54' 12" E, 88.7 feet to Iron Pin No. H4640 situate

on the northern boundary of the C.N.R. right-of-way;

Thence crossing the said C.N.R. right-of-way S 36° 54' 12" E, 110.65 feet to Iron Pin No.

H4641 situate on the south boundary of the aforementioned C.N.R. right-of-way;

Thence S 36° 54' 12" E, 36.3 feet to Iron Pin No. H4641A situate on the east bank of a brook

flowing into the Musquodoboit River;

Thence in a southerly and southwesterly direction following the east bank of the said brook to

Iron Pin No. H4642 situate on the northern bank of the Musquodoboit River, said Iron Pin

bearing S 01° 08' 18" W, 231.9 feet from the last mentioned Iron Pin No. H4641A;

Thence crossing the Musquodoboit River to Iron Pin No. H4643 situate on the south bank of

Musquodoboit River and bearing S 71° 18' 24" W, 394.0 feet from the last mentioned Iron Pin

No. H4642;

Thence S 36° 14' 00" E, 2689.9 feet to Iron Pin No. H4644 situate on the northern edge of

MacKeen Lake;

Thence following the edge of the said MacKeen Lake in a northerly, easterly and southerly

direction to Iron Pin No. H4645, said Iron Pin bearing N 56° 14' 18" E, 497.0 feet from the last

mentioned Iron Pin No. H4644;

Thence N 56° 00' 42" E, 740.5 feet to Iron Pin No. H4646;

Thence N 35° 52' 42" W, 939.7 feet following an old fence to Iron Pin No. H4647, situate on

the south bank of the Musquodoboit River before mentioned;

Thence crossing the said Musquodoboit River to Iron Pin No. H4647;

Thence following the aforementioned old fence N 30° 05' 145.9 feet to Iron Pin No. H4650;

Thence continuing along the said fence N 18° 27' 30" W, 802.2 feet to Iron Pin No. H4651

situate on the south boundary of the before mentioned C.N.R. right-of-way;

Thence N 77° 7' 6" W, 114.5 feet to Iron Pin No. H4652 situate on the north boundary of the

C.N.R. right-of-way;

Thence N 29° 40' 24" W, 133.3 feet to Iron Pin No. H4653 situate on the southern boundary of

Highway No. 224 leading from Middle Musquodoboit to Upper Musquodoboit before

mentioned and is being N 58° 18' 12" E, 945.85' feet from the place of beginning;

Thence following the south boundary of the said highway No. 224 in a southwesterly direction

to the place of beginning.

Saving and excepting that portion of the C.N.R. right-of-way which passes through the lands

herein described. The said lot containing 75 acres more or less.
