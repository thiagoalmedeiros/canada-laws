### Citation

1 These regulations may be cited as the Standard Expenditure per Dwelling Unit

(2008–2009) Regulations .

### Standard expenditure per dwelling unit

municipality under Section 11 of the Municipal Grants Act for the 2008–2009 fiscal year

is as set out in the following table:

| Class of Municipality | Standard Expenditure Per Dwelling Unit |
| --- | --- |
| I | 1179 |
| II | 492 |
