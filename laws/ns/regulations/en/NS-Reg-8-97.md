NS Reg 8/97

*Made under clause 44(1)(c) of the Real Estate Trading Act*

1 These regulations may be cited as the Real Estate Recovery Fund Regulations.

2 For the purpose of clause 44(1)(c) of the Act, the minimum amount of the Real

Estate Recovery Fund shall be $300,000.00.
