SNS 2002, c 11

Provincial Fossil Act

An Act to Declare Hylonomus lyelli

the Provincial Fossil of Nova Scotia

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Provincial Fossil Act*.

### Hylonomus lyelli is Provincial Fossil

2 The reptile fossil *Hylonomus lyelli* is declared to be the Provincial Fossil of the Province.
