RSNS 1989, c 324

Official Tree Act

An Act Respecting

an Official Tree of Nova Scotia

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Official Tree Act*.

### Arboreal emblem

2 The red spruce (*Picea rubens*) is declared to be, and from time immemorial to have been, the arboreal emblem of the Province.
