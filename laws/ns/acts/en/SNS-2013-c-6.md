SNS 2013, c 6

Mariners' Day Act

An Act Respecting

a Memorial Day for Mariners

### Preamble

WHEREAS Nova Scotia is a province with strong and long-standing connections to the sea;

AND WHEREAS the House of Assembly believes that there should be a day set aside on which to commemorate, each and every year, the many Nova Scotian mariners, men and women, who have lost their lives at sea:

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Mariners' Day Act*.

### Mariners’ Day

2 Throughout the Province, in each and every year, the second Sunday in August shall be kept and observed under the name of Mariners' Day.
