SNS 1994, c 6

Education Amendments (1994) Act

An Act to Implement an Agreement

Between the Minister of Education and

the Nova Scotia Teachers' Union and

Amend Chapter 136 of the Revised

Statutes, 1989, the Education Act, and

Chapter 461 of the Revised Statutes, 1989,

the Teachers' Pension Act

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Education Amendments (1994) Act*.

### Interpretation

2 (1) In this Act,

(a) "Amending Agreement" means the agreement dated June 5, 1994, between the Minister of Education and the Nova Scotia Teachers' Union;

(b) "professional agreement" means a professional agreement pursuant to the *Teachers' Collective Bargaining Act*.

(2) Notwithstanding the *Education Act*, the *Teachers' Collective Bargaining Act* or any professional agreement, every professional agreement is amended to comply with the Amending Agreement.

### Education Act amended

3 to 6 *amendments*

### Teachers' Pension Act amended

7 *amendment*

### Effective date of certain Sections

8 Sections 2 and 6 have effect on and after June 5, 1994.
