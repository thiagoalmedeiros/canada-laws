SNS 2013, c 35

February Holiday Act

An Act to Establish a Holiday in February

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *February Holiday Act*.

### Third Monday in February

2 The third Monday in February in 2015 and in each subsequent year shall be kept and observed throughout the Province under such name as the Governor in Council determines.
