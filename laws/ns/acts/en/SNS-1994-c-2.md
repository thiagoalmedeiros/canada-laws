SNS 1994, c 2

All Saints Springhill Hospital Gifts Act

An Act to Substitute All Saints

Community Health Care Foundation for

All Saints Springhill Hospital and

All Saints Springhill Hospital Corporation as

the Beneficiary of Certain Gifts

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *All Saints Springhill Hospital Gifts Act*.

### Interpretation

2 In this Act,

(a) "Foundation" means All Saints Community Health Care

Foundation, incorporated pursuant to the *Societies Act*; and

(b) "Hospital" means All Saints Springhill Hospital Corporation,

incorporated by Chapter 196 of the Acts of 1893 and includes All

Saints Springhill Hospital.

### Foundation substituted as beneficiary of gift

3 (1) As regards any subsequent transaction, matter or thing, the

Foundation is hereby substituted for the Hospital as the beneficiary of any

gift, including any devise, bequest, endowment or trust of which the

Hospital would, but for this Section, be the beneficiary and every

document, including every will, trust instrument, deed, life insurance

policy, endowment or indenture that refers to the Hospital is amended to

the extent necessary to give effect to such substitution.

### Deemed repeal of subsection (1)

(2) If the objects of the Foundation are changed from the objects as now

set out in its Memorandum of Association on the coming into force of

this Act, subsection (1) is deemed to have been repealed.
