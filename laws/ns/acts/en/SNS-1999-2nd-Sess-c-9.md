SNS 1999 (2nd Sess), c 9

Provincial Mineral Act

An Act to Declare Stilbite

the Provincial Mineral of Nova Scotia

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Provincial Mineral Act*.

### Provincial Mineral

2 Stilbite is declared to be the Provincial Mineral of the Province.
