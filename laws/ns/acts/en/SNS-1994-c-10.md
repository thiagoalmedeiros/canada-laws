SNS 1994, c 10

Provincial Bird Act

An Act to Declare the Osprey to be

the Bird Emblem of Nova Scotia

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the *Provincial Bird Act*.

### Bird emblem of the Province

2 The osprey (*Pandion haliaetus*) is declared to be the bird emblem of the Province.
