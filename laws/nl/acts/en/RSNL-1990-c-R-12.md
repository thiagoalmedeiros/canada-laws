RSNL 1990, c R-12

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the Remembrance Day Act.

### Remembrance Day

2 (1) November 11 in every year shall be a day of remembrance under the name "Remembrance Day".

(2) Remembrance Day shall be observed as a holiday where that observance is specifically provided for by law.

(3) Where it is provided by law that Remembrance Day shall be observed as a holiday, a day shall not be substituted for Remembrance Day for the holding of that holiday.

### Poppy

3 The Poppy, as recognized by the Royal Canadian Legion, shall be the official symbol of Remembrance Day.
