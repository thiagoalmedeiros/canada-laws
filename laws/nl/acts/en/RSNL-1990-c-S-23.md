RSNL 1990, c S-23

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the Standard Time Act.

### Standard time

2 (1) Time in the province shall be reckoned as 3 1/2 hours later than Greenwich mean solar time.

(2) Time reckoned in accordance with subsection (1) shall be known as "Newfoundland Standard Time".

### Daylight time

3 (1) During the period in each year that the Lieutenant-Governor in Council may by regulation establish, time in the province shall be reckoned as 2 1/2 hours later than Greenwich mean solar time.

(1.1) Until changed under subsection (1), during the period in each year between 2 a.m. of the second Sunday in March and 2 a.m. of the first Sunday in November time in the province shall be reckoned as 2 1/2 hours later than Greenwich mean solar time.

(2) Time reckoned in accordance with subsection (1) shall be known as "Newfoundland Daylight Time".

### Regulations

4 Notwithstanding sections 2 and 3, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council may make regulations

(a) varying the reckoning of Newfoundland Standard Time and Newfoundland Daylight Time;

(b) prescribing the application of Newfoundland Standard Time and Newfoundland Daylight Time to the whole province or to a part or portion of the province;

(c) prescribing, where necessary, the application of a time zone other than Newfoundland Standard Time to a part or portion of the province; and

(d) generally, to give effect to the purpose of this Act.

### Expressions relating to time

5 Where

(a) an expression of time reckoned or to be reckoned in the province occurs in

(i) an Act, proclamation, order of the Lieutenant-Governor in Council, regulation, order, rule, rule or order of a court, by-law, resolution or other enactment, or

(ii) an agreement, contract, deed, lease or other instrument previously or later enacted, made or executed;

(b) an hour or other period of time is stated either orally or in writing; or

(c) a question as to time arises,

the time referred to or intended shall, unless it is otherwise specifically expressed, be held to be time reckoned as prescribed by this Act.
