SNL 2021, c R-12.1

Be it enacted by the Lieutenant-Governor and House of Assembly in Legislative Session convened, as follows:

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the Renaming of Red Indian Lake Act.

### Change of name

2 (1) Notwithstanding the Geographical Names Board Act, the name "Red Indian Lake" is changed to "Beothuk Lake".

(2) An A ct, or a regulation or an order made under an Act, that contains a reference to "Red Indian Lake" is amended to refer to "Beothuk Lake".

(3) The validity of any proceedings, agreements, notices or documents that contain a reference to "Red Indian Lake" shall not be affected by the change of name.
