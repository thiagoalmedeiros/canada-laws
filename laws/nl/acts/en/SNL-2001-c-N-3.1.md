SNL 2001, c N-3.1

Be it enacted by the Lieutenant-Governor and House of Assembly in Legislative Session convened, as follows:

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the Newfoundland and Labrador Act.

### Reference to Newfoundland

2 An Act that contains a reference to " Newfoundland " is amended to refer to " Newfoundland and Labrador ".

### Exclusions

3 Section 2 does not apply to

(a) the Royal Newfoundland Constabulary;

(b) an Act relating to the Memorial University of Newfoundland, its pension plan or the foundation; or

(c) an Act incorporating a professional or occupational group that includes the name " Newfoundland " in its title.

### Commencement

4 This Act comes into force on the day the Constitution Amendment, 2001 (Newfoundland and Labrador) comes into force. (In force - Dec. 6/01)
