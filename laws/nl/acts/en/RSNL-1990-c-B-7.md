RSNL 1990, c B-7

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the Bravery Award Act.

### Definition

2 In this Act "award" means the Newfoundland and Labrador Bravery Award that is described in the Schedule to this Act.

### Award presented

3 The government of the province may present an award to a citizen of the province whose life and safety have been risked while giving assistance to another person.

### Review panel

4 (1) The Lieutenant-Governor in Council may appoint a review panel for the purpose of accepting and considering nominations of persons eligible to receive the award.

(2) The review panel shall recommend to the Lieutenant-Governor in Council the names of those persons who are eligible to receive the award.

### Regulations

5 The Lieutenant-Governor in Council may make regulations to give effect to the purpose of this Act.

## Schedule

The award is 5.72 centimetres square and is inscribed and decorated on

(a) the obverse side with a circular inner rim enclosing a stylized drama at sea symbolic of the courageous heritage of Newfoundland and Labrador; and

(b) the reverse side with the words

" Newfoundland and Labrador FOR BRAVERY".

The award is minted in bronze with a gold plated mat finish and suspended from a blue grogram ribbon.
