RSNL 1990, c C-40

### Short title

1 This Act may be cited as the Crown Corporations Local Taxation Act.

### Definitions

2 In this Act

(a) "Crown corporation" means a corporation in which not less than 90% of all of the issued common shares are owned by the Crown; and

(b) "local taxation" means taxation imposed under an Act or law of the province for the benefit of a city, a municipality, local schools, or a municipal area.

### Exemption from local taxation

3 Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in another Act, while a corporation is a Crown corporation

(a) it shall, unless the Lieutenant-Governor in Council otherwise directs, be exempt, as to the corporation and as to its property, from local taxation; and

(b) a claim, demand, action, suit or a proceeding in law shall not be made or taken against it for the recovery of local taxation imposed on it in respect of a period before it became a Crown corporation.

### Order to pay taxes

4 (1) The Lieutenant-Governor in Council may by order direct that this Act no longer applies to a Crown corporation and where an order is made under this section, the Crown corporation to which the order applies shall pay all money payable as a result of the order.

(2) An order made under subsection (1) has effect from the date of the order or from the date that may be stated in the order.
