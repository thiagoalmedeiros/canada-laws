# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Winterhouse Pond

CNLR 733/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 472000 5617000 70° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,500 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 82° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 176° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 240° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,125 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 282° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,025 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 360° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 575 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Winterhouse Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 294/85, is repealed.
