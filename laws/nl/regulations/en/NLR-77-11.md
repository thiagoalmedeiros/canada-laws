NLR 77/11

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Terra Nova abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Local Service District of Bunyans Cove water supply #2 Well, having UTM coordinates northing 5,365,410 metres and easting 722,408 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
