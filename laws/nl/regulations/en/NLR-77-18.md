NLR 77/18

*Made under the Wild Life Regulations and the Wild Life Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Wolf Hunting Order, Newfoundland and Labrador.

77/18 s1

### Definition

2 In this Order, "licence" means a licence issued under section 15 of the Wild Life Regulations.

77/18 s2

### Devices permitted

3 A person shall only shoot a wolf under a licence using

(a) a centre-fire rifle not greater than .225 calibre; or

(b) a shotgun using shot size 2 or larger.

77/18 s3; 77/25 s1

### Prohibition

3.1 Notwithstanding paragraph 3(a), a person shall not carry, transport or possess a firearm referred to in paragraph 3(a) in any area on the Island of Newfoundland frequented by wild life during any part of the open season within the period September 1 to December 31, inclusive, each year.

57/22 s1; 63/23 s1

### Bag limit

4 The bag limit shall be one wolf per licence.

77/18 s4
