CNLR 370/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Petty Harbour - Maddox Cove Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Petty Harbour - Maddox Cove are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 759/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point at the highwater mark in the northern shoreline of Motion Bay locally known as Tinker's Point and then running north a distance of 1,375 metres, more or less;

Then due west a distance of 2,375 metres, more or less, to a point;

Then south 5° west a distance of 1,775 metres, more or less;

Then south 51° east a distance of 950 metres, more or less;

Then south 77° east a distance of 1,375 metres, more or less, to a point at the highwater mark in the Atlantic shoreline of an inlet at Horseshoe Cliff;

Then running in a generally northerly and easterly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline passing Petty Harbour, Maddox Cove to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 National Topographic Mapping, 1983 edition.
