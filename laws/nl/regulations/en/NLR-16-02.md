NLR 16/02

*Made under the Environment Act*

This area includes all lands in the provincial electoral district of St. Georges - Stephenville East abutted and bounded as follows:

That is to say, by a circle of 600 metres diameter whose centre is the Local Service District of Barachois Brook water supply well located at UTM position 5,367,550 north, 392,950 east. Zone 21, NAD 27.
