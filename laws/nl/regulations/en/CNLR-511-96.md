CNLR 511/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 420720, 210° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 262° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 233° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,725 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 214° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 237° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,100 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 121° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 74° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 725 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 135° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,675 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 49° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,375 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 3° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 43° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,175 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 3° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Beverly Lake Water Supply Area Notice for Labrador City, Newfoundland Regulation 53/ 79, is repealed.
