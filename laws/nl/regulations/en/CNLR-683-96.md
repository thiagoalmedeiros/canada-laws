CNLR 683/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 22, 320000 metres east, 5274000 metres north, 72° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 225 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 79° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,899 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 27° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,851 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 81° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,658 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 122° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 740 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 215° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 4,297 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 245° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,446 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 353° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,720 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Rocky Pond Watershed Area Notice for the Community of Spaniards Bay, Newfoundland Regulation 228/ 78, is repealed.
