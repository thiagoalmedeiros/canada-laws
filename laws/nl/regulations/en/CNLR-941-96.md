CNLR 941/96

Beginning at a point being the southwestern extremity of West Arm;

Then south 55° west a distance of 1,000 metres, more or less, to the most easterly extremity of Long Arm;

Then following the shoreline in a counter-clockwise direction around Triton Island to the point of beginning.

All offshore islands excepting Pretty Island, Big Island, Grassy Island and Great Tinker Island are included.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.

Newfoundland Regulation 1133/78 is repealed.
