CNLR 843/96

Beginning at a point, that point being the horizontal control monument on Burnt Ridge;

Then north 15° 00' west a distance of 2,750 metres, more or less, to a point on the centre line of Highway 238;

Then north 77° 00' east a distance of 3,800 metres, more or less, to a point on the highwater mark on the western side of Trinity Bay;

Then in a generally southerly direction along the shoreline at highwater mark, passing through Elliston Cove, Elliston Point, Flower's Point, to a point at the highwater mark at the mouth of an unnamed river flowing into Trinity Bay, that point being on the Little Catalina Municipal Planning Area boundary;

Then north 34° 00' west a distance of 3,600 metres, more or less, along that boundary;

Then south 49° 00' west a distance of 3,050 metres, more or less, to a point 150 metres east of the centre line of Highway 230 and on a line perpendicular to the centre line of Highway 230;

Then in a northerly direction parallel to and 150 metres east of the centre line of Highway 230 to a point 150 metres north of and on a line perpendicular to the centre line of Highway 238;

Then in a northeasterly direction parallel to and 150 metres from the centre line of Highway 238 to a point on the boundary of the Bonavista watershed;

Then in a northeasterly direction along the watershed boundary to a point, that point being the horizontal control monument on Burnt Ridge, the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 mapping, 1972 edition.

Newfoundland Regulation 35/84 is repealed.
