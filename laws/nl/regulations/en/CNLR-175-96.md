CNLR 175/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Fox Cove - Mortier Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Fox Cove - Mortier are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 649/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a coastal point west-northwestwardly from Western Head, that point being at UTM grid co-ordinate 643 470 metres east, 5 214 160 metres north;

Then running north 78° 30' west 1,950 metres, more or less;

Then running north 32° 30' west 550 metres, more or less;

Then running north 2° west 2,000 metres, more or less;

Then running north 3° 30' east 3,800 metres, more or less;

Then running north 57° 30' east 2,600 metres, more or less;

Then running south 44° east 2,400 metres, more or less, to Blow Me Down;

Then following the coastline by way of Tides Cove Point, Eastern Head and Little Mortier Bay to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to UTM grid and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 Map 1 M/3, edition 3, 1988.
