NLR 1/09

*Made under the Labour Relations Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Labour Management Arbitration Committee Regulations.

### Organizations prescribed

2 The employer and labour organizations from which the arbitration committee shall be constituted are:

(a) the Newfoundland and Labrador Employers' Council; and

(b) the Newfoundland and Labrador Federation of Labour.
