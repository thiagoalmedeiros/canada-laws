# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Little Pond, Benton

CNLR 625/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 190900 253° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of approximately 200 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 203° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 270° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 875 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 36° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 71° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 123° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Little Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Benton, Newfoundland Regulation 56/ 79, is repealed.
