CNLR 587/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 295000 5392000 152° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 575 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 191° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 267° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 210° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 325 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 179° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 280° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 257° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,775 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 308° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 87° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 54° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 975 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 65° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 87° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 76° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,525 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 113° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Goose Neck Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 220/88, is repealed.
