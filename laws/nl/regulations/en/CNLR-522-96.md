CNLR 522/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21, 640000 metres east, 5222000 metres north, 127° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 76° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 885 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 198° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 840 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 135° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 191° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,220 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 163° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 675 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 200° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,591 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 280° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,220 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 328° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,610 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 38° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 3,315 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 70° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 740 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 22° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 690 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Big Pond Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 182/78, is repealed.
