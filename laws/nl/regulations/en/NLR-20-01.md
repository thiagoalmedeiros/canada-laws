NLR 20/01

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Fox Harbour Order, 2001.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Fox Harbour are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 The Town of Fox Harbour Order, Consolidated Newfoundland Regulation 157/96, is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at Kelly Point at the south entrance to Fox Harbour, that point having UTM coordinates north 5,244,749 metres and east 279,014 metres, and following the high water shoreline of Placentia Sound via Samson Point, Conner Cove Point, Powell Point, White Point to the mouth of the river at the eastern edge of the sound;

Then running along the northern bank of the river to Highway 102;

Then running north 35 ° east 3,750 metres, more or less;

Then running north 87 ° west 6,450 metres, more or less, to the eastern shoreline of Placentia Bay at the north entrance to Maurice Poole Cove;

Then following the high water shoreline via Maurice Poole Cove, Isaac Heads, Isaac Point, Bottom Barasway, Maker Point and Fox Harbour to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to Grid North and, as with distances given, are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 digital, NAD 83, Zone 22, National Topographic Sheet.
