CNLR 817/96

Beginning at a point on the eastern shoreline of Birchy Bay Brook where it empties into the most southern inlet of Birchy Bay;

Then following Birchy Bay Brook in a generally southerly direction to an unnamed lake;

Then following the eastern shoreline of the unnamed lake to its most southern point;

Then south 64° east a distance of 3,550 metres, more or less, to a point on the western shoreline of a second unnamed lake;

Then following the western shoreline of the unnamed lake in a generally northerly direction to its most northern point;

Then north 55° east a distance of 3,400 metres, more or less;

Then north 5° 30' east a distance of 13,500 metres, more or less;

Then due west for a distance of 450 metres, more or less, to a point on the eastern shoreline of "The Reach";

Then in a generally southwesterly direction along the eastern shoreline of The Reach to Birchy Bay Point;

Then continuing in a generally southwesterly direction along the eastern shoreline of Birchy Bay to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.

Newfoundland Regulation 936/78 is repealed.
