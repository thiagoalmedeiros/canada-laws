NLR 94/04

*Made under the Labour Standards Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Labour Standards Reciprocating Jurisdictions Regulations.

### Reciprocating jurisdictions

2 The provinces and territories of Canada listed in the Schedule are reciprocating jurisdictions for the purpose of section 80 of the Act and the authorities listed in conjunction with those jurisdictions are designated as the authorities who may make an application under section 80.

## Schedule

Yukon Director of Employment Standards Labour Services (J-8), Department of Justice

North West Territories Executive Assistant Labour Standards Board

Nunavut Executive Assistant Labour Standards Board

British Columbia Director Employment Standards

Alberta Director of Employment Standards Department of Human Resources and Employment

Saskatchewan Director Labour Standards Branch Department of Labour

Manitoba Director Employment Standards Division

Ontario Director of Employment Standards Employment Practices Branch, Department of Labour

Quebec Commission des normes du travail

New Brunswick Director of Employment Standards Department of Training and Employment Development

Prince Edward Island Inspector of Labour Standards Department of Community & Cultural Affairs

Nova Scotia Director of Labour Standards Department of Environment and Labour
