CNLR 326/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Northern Arm Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Northern Arm are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 750/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point at the mouth of an unnamed brook on the northern shoreline of the Bay of Exploits, that point being located southwestwardly a distance of 2,000 metres, more or less, from Phillip's Head;

Then running west a distance of 5,050 metres, more or less;

Then running south 21° west a distance of 8,600 metres, more or less, to the northern bank of Northern Arm Brook;

Then running in a generally easterly direction along the northern bank to the mouth of Northern Arm Brook;

Then running in a generally northwesterly and northeasterly direction along the shoreline of Northern Arm to High Point;

Then continuing in a generally northeasterly direction along the northern shoreline of Bay of Exploits to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
