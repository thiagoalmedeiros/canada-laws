### Short title

1 This order may be cited as the Vacated Communities of Great Harbour Deep, Petites and Big Brook Order.

### Great Harbour Deep

2 Great Harbour Deep is a vacated community effective November 1, 2002.

### Petites

3 Petites is a vacated community effective November 1, 2003.

### Big Brook

4 Big Brook is a vacated community effective October 15, 2004.
