# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Long Pond, Whiteway

CNLR 637/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 315000 5290000 185° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 137° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,925 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 162° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 575 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 218° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,875 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 313° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 348° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 35° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,100 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 315° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 24° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,075 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 80° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,000 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Long Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Whiteway, Newfoundland Regulation 196/ 89, is repealed.
