CNLR 727/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 515000 5675000 53° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 625 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 308° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 51° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 575 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 12° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 348° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 76° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 51° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 100° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 167° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 184° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 238° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 291° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,475 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 225° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 375 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 258° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 775 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Well Cove Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Anchor Point, Newfoundland Regulation 184/ 94, is repealed.
