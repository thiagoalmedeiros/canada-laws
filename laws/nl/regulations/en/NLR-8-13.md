NLR 8/13

*Made under the Regional Service Boards Act, 2012*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Eastern Regional Service Board Regulations, 2013.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, "board" means the Eastern Regional Service Board.

### Prescribed services

3 (1) The board has the power to construct, acquire, maintain and operate a waste management system within the Eastern Region.

(2) The board has the power to provide operational oversight of water and waste water systems owned by municipal authorities within the Eastern Region which have been identified through the Community Sustainability Partnership Initiative.

(3) The board has the power to provide fire protection services within the Eastern region subject to the terms and conditions that the minister may determine.

### Recovery of debt

4 An amount owing under section 24 of the Act is a debt due to the board and the board may recover it by civil action in a court.

### Withdrawal of service

5 The board may stop providing a service to a municipal authority, user or other person where the municipal authority, user or other person fails to comply with a policy of the board respecting the service.

### Repeal

6 The Eastern Regional Service Board Regulations, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 92/11, are repealed.
