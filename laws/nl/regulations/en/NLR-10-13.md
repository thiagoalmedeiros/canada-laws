NLR 10/13

*Made under the Regional Service Boards Act, 2012*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Western Regional Service Board Order.

### Creation of region

2 The city, towns, local service districts and unincorporated areas in the western portion of the Island of Newfoundland situated south and west of a line drawn from Bellburns to Jackson's Arm to The Beaches to Ramea, as well as the local service district of Grey River, are constituted as a region with the name of the Western Region.

### Establishment of board

3 The Western Regional Service Board is established for the region with the purpose of providing regional services in accordance with the Act.
