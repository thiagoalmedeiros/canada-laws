CNLR 684/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 22, 333000 metres east, 5298000 metres north, 282° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 220° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 966 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 231° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,336 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 306° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,740 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 258° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,660 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 289° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 885 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 305° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 33° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,225 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 63° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,110 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 11° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,080 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 83° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 124° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,207 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Rocky Pond Watershed Area Notice for the Community of Victoria, Newfoundland Regulation 165/ 78, is repealed.
