### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Fischell's Brook Gypsum Quarry Access Road Exemption Order.

### Exemption

2 The construction of the Fischell's Brook Gypsum Quarry Access Road is exempted from the application of the Environmental Assessment Act.
