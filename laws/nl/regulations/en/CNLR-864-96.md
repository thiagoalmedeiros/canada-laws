CNLR 864/96

Beginning at a point, that point being located at the centre point of the bridge on Route 440 over Hughes Brook;

Then in a generally northeasterly direction along the centre line of Hughes Brook 1,825 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being where an unnamed brook flows into Hughes Brook;

Then in a generally southeasterly direction along the southern bank of the unnamed brook a distance of 775 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being on the southernmost abutment of a bridge spanning the brook;

Then south 8° east a distance of 1,850 metres, more or less, to a point on the planning area boundary of the City of Corner Brook;

Then along the planning area boundary of Corner Brook north 80° 30' west a distance of 2,800 metres, more or less, to a point on the centre line of Hughes Brook;

Then in a generally northeasterly direction along the centre line of Hughes Brook a distance of 800 metres, more or less, to the point of beginning.

All bearings as with distances given are subject to approximation.

All bearings refer to grid north.

Newfoundland Regulation 207/87 is repealed.
