NLR 17/04

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Cartwright-LAnse au Clair abutted and bounded as follows:

Beginning at a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 556,465 metres and northing 5,846,696 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 1,975 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 554,517 metres and northing 5,847,022 metres;

Then running in a northerly direction for a distance of 703 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 554,755 metres and northing 5,847,683 metres;

Then running in a easterly direction for a distance of 1121 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 555,876 metres and northing 5,847,687 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 1,171 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 556,913 metres and northing 5,848,230 metres;

Then running in a easterly direction for a distance of 1,244 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 558,122 metres and northing 5,847,939 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 1,300 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 558,460 metres and northing 5,846,684 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 415 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 558,380 metres and northing 5,846,277 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 928 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 557,717 metres and northing 5,845,628 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 1,589 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 556 185 metres and northing 5,845,206 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 561 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 555,625 metres and northing 5,845,234 metres;

Then running in a northwesterly direction for a distance of 737 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 554,967 metres and northing 5,845,565 metres;

Then running in a northerly direction for a distance of 423 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 554,843 metres and northing 5,845,969 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 1,777 metres, more or less, to the point of commencement.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 27 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.

The Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of John Martins Pond, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 79/98, is repealed.
