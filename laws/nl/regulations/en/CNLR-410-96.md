CNLR 410/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Sandy Cove Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Sandy Cove are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 834/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point located on the easternmost tip of Sandy Cove Point on the northern shore of Newman Sound, Bonavista Bay;

Then running in a generally westerly direction along the shore 325 metres;

Then running north 17° west 1,450 metres to the southern limit of the road from Sandy Cove to Eastport;

Then running due east along the southern limit of the road 150 metres;

Then running due north 150 metres;

Then running north 65° east 3,550 metres;

Then running south 71° east 3,300 metres, more or less, to the southwest shoreline of Scotts Tickle;

Then running in a generally southwesterly direction along the northern shoreline of Newman Sound to the point of commencement.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
