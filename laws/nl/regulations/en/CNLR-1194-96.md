CNLR 1194/96

*Made under the Lands Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Blueberry Management Units Designation Order.

### Units designated

2 The Lieutenant-Governor in Council designates those areas on file with the Minister of Forest Resources and Agrifoods as Blueberry Management Units.

### Use of designated units

3 The lands designated under section 2 shall be set apart for the cultivation or harvesting of blueberries subject to the terms and conditions that the Minister of Forest Resources and Agrifoods may prescribe.
