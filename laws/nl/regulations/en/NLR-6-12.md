### Official administrator appointed

1 Ed Goodyear is appointed as official administrator to manage and conduct the affairs of the Town of Salvage until the Lieutenant-Governor in Council revokes the appointment to allow for the election of a new council under section 261 of the Municipalities Act, 1999.
