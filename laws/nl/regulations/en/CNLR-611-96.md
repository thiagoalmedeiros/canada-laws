CNLR 611/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 564000 5621000 45° 00' for a distance of 520 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 299° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 430 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 332° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 14° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 63° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 880 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 148° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 204° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 211° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 880 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Island Cove Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Town of Englee, Newfoundland Regulation 194/90, is repealed.
