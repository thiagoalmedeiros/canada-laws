NLR 66/98

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Middle Arm, Green Bay Name Change Order.

### Name changed

2 The name of the Town of Middle Arm, Green Bay is changed to the Town of Middle Arm.

### CNR 283/96 Amdt.

3 The Town of Middle Arm, Green Bay Order is amended by deleting the phrase "Middle Arm, Green Bay " wherever it appears and substituting the phrase "Middle Arm".
