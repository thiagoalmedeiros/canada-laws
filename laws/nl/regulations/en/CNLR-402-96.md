CNLR 402/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of St. Bride's Order.

### Town continued

2 The area commonly known as St. Bride's is continued as a town.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 826/78 is repealed.
