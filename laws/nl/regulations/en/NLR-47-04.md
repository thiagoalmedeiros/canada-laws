# Notice of Wellhead Protected Water Supply Area of the Town of St. Marys, Town of St. Marys Wellfield

NLR 47/04

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Placentia & St. Marys abutted and bounded as follows:

Beginning at a point having scaled UTM co-ordinates of northing 5,198,280 metres and easting 304,646 metres;

Then running in a general northeasterly direction to a point having scaled UTM co-ordinates of northing 5,198,520 metres and easting 304,967 metres;

Then running in a general southeasterly direction to a point having scaled UTM co-ordinates of northing 5,197,930 metres and easting 305,406 metres;

Then running in a general southerly direction to a point having scaled UTM co-ordinates of northing 5,197,267 metres and easting 305,532 metres;

Then running in a general westerly direction to a point having scaled UTM co-ordinates of northing 5,197,196 metres easting 305,137 metres;

Then running in a general northerly direction to a point having scaled UTM co-ordinates of northing 5,197,767 metres and easting 305,032 metres; and

Then running in a general northwesterly direction to the point of commencement.

All points refer to Zone 22 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
