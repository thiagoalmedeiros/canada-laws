CNLR 169/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the community of Freshwater is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Freshwater.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 177/84 and 124/85 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the highwater mark of Conception Bay North, that point being on the northeastern extremity of the Carbonear municipal boundary;

Then in a westerly direction along that boundary to a point, that point being the centre line of a road locally known as Lily Pond Road;

Then in a northwesterly direction a distance of 1,200 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being the intersection of the eastern right-of-way of Route 70 with the Carbonear - Victoria municipal planning area boundary;

Then in a northeasterly direction along that boundary to a point, that point being the highwater mark at the easternmost extremity of Salmon Cove Head;

Then in a southwesterly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline at highwater mark passing Folly Rocks, Clowns Cove, Freshwater Cove to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to grid north.
