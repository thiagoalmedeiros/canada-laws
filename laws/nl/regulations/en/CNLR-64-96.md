CNLR 64/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the community of Black Duck is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Black Duck.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 175/84 and 203/87 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point, that point being located on the northernmost shoreline of Mistaken Pond and having a military grid reference of 989839;

Then south 36° east a distance of 6,000 metres, more or less;

Then due west a distance of 6,200 metres, more or less, to a point on the western limit of the right-of-way of Route 460;

Then due north a distance of 4,900 metres, more or less;

Then due east a distance of 2,700 metres, more or less, to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation.
