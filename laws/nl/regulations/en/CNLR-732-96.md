CNLR 732/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 22, 300000 metres east, 5394000 metres north, 104° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,225 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 3° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 325 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 69° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 47° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 675 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 86° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 145° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 75° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 375 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 112° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 197° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 272° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 222° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 281° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 227° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 275° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 850 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Wild Cove Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Town of Salvage, Newfoundland Regulation 173/91, is repealed.
