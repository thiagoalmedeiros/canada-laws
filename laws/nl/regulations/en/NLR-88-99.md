NLR 88/99

*Made under the Jury Act, 1991*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Prospective Jurors Alternate Sources Regulations.

### Alternative sources

2 For the purpose of the enumeration of prospective jurors' lists compiled under section 11 of the Jury Act, 1991, the sheriff may use one or more of the following alternative sources;

(a) motor registration division – licensed drivers, management information system database;

(b) a membership list of an association francophone;

(c) the telephone directory; or

(d) another source considered appropriate for the purpose by the sheriff.
