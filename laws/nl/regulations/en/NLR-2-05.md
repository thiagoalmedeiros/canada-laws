NLR 2/05

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### District established

1 The area comprising the communities of Biscay Bay in the Electoral District of Ferryland is established as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Biscay Bay.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall be the area commonly known as Biscay Bay.

### Committee to be elected

3 A local service district committee shall be elected to control and manage the affairs of the local service district according to the Municipalities Act, 1999 and the Local Service District Regulations.
