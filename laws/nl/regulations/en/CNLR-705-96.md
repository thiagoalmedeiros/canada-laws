CNLR 705/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 284000 5424000 346° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,850 metres from the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 45° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 74° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 60° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 107° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 178° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,100 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 255° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 206° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 255° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,100 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Southwest Feeder Water Supply Area Notice for the Town of Trinity, Newfoundland Regulation 75/81, is repealed.
