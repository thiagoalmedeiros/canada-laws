CNLR 412/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Seal Cove Order.

### Town continued

2 The area commonly known as Seal Cove, Fortune Bay, is continued as a town.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 835/78 is repealed.
