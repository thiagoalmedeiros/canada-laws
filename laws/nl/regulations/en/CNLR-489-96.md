CNLR 489/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the communities of West Bay and West Bay Centre is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of West Bay.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall comprise the area which includes all householders residing upon the Port au Port Peninsula along Route 463 between the southernmost boundary of Lourdes and Rioux' Brook and all streets and roads between.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 99/85 and 149/85 are repealed.
