NLR 3/06

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Port au Port, abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 500 metres radius with its centre being the Town of Port au Port water supply Number 1 Well, having UTM co ordinates northing 5,379,661 metres and easting 374,588 metres.
