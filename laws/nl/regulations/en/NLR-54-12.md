NLR 54/12

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Local service district established

1 The area comprising the communities of Southport, Gooseberry Cove, Butter Cove, Little Hearts Ease, Caplin Cove and Long Beach in the Electoral District of Trinity North is established as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Caplin Cove-Southport.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the local service district shall be the area comprising of each of the communities referred to in section 1.

### Local service district committee

3 The local service district committee established by Newfoundland and Labrador Regulations 88/04 is continued as the local service district committee to control and manage the affairs of the local service district according to the Municipalities Act, 1999 and the Local Service District Regulations.

### NLR 88/04 Rep.

4 The Local Service District of Caplin Cove-Southport, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 88/04, is repealed.
