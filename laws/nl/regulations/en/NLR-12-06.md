# Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Lookout Brook

Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.assembly.nl.ca/Legislation/sr/regulations/rc060012.htm) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

NLR 12/06

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Strait of Belle Isle abutted and bounded as follows:

Beginning at a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 605 476 metres and northing 5 711 987 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 407 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 605 229 metres and northing 5 711 663 metres;

Then running in a southeasterly direction for a distance of 326 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 605 489 metres and northing 5 711 467 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 97 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 605 445 metres and northing 5 711 381 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 592 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 604 855 metres and northing 5 711 428 metres;

Then running in a northerly direction for a distance of 465 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 604 792 metres and northing 5 711 889 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 271 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 604 983 metres and northing 5 712 081 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 313 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 605 268 metres and northing 5 712 210 metres;

Then running in a southeasterly direction for a distance of 305 metres, more or less, to the point of commencement.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
