CNLR 257/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Lushes Bight - Beaumont - Beaumont North Order.

### Town continued

2 The area commonly known as Lushes Bight - Beaumont - Beaumont North is continued as a town.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 725/78 is repealed.
