NLR 74/20

*Made under the Provincial Parks Regulations and the Provincial Parks Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Hunting in a Waterway Provincial Park Permission Order.

### Hunting permitted

2 Hunting is permitted within a waterway provincial park in accordance with permits and licences issued under the Wild Life Act or the Migratory Birds Convention Act, 1994 (Canada) or regulations made under these Acts.
