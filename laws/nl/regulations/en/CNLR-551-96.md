CNLR 551/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 580000 5273000 56° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 30° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 725 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 06° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 45° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 139° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 875 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 79° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 139° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 204° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 220° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 193° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 260° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 197° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 301° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 246° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 475 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 316° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 700 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Cluetts - Bottom Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 9/84, is repealed.
