# Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Gurges Pond, WS-S-0881

NLR 28/12

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Bay of Islands abutted and bounded as follows:

Beginning at a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 414,447 metres and northing 5,432,209 metres;

Then running in a southeasterly direction for a distance of 223 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 414,632 metres and northing 5,432,084 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 134 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 414,623 metres and northing 5,431,951 metres;

Then running in a southeasterly direction for a distance of 243 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 414,805 metres and northing 5,431,789 metres;

Then running in a southerly direction for a distance of 557 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 414,723 metres and northing 5,431,239 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 153 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 414,578 metres and northing 5,431,187 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 158 metres, more or less, to the point of commencement.

All coordinates refer Zone 21N of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
