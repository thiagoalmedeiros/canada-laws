NLR 5/00

*Made under the Criminal Code*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Victim Surcharge Order.

### Payment of surcharge

2 Where a court does not impose a fine for an offence, a victim surcharge arising under the Criminal Code shall be paid within 30 days of the date of conviction or discharge of the offence.
