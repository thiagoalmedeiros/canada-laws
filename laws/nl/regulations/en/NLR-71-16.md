NLR 71/16

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Feasibility Report Respecting the Disincorporation of the Town of Duntara Order.

### Feasibility report

2 A feasibility report shall be prepared to consider the disincorporation of the Town of Duntara.

### Appointment

3 Lori Evoy is appointed to prepare the feasibility report.

### Preparation of report

4 The feasibility report shall be prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Municipalities Act, 1999.
