NLR 15/16

*Made under the Evacuated Communities Act*

### Short title

1 This order may be cited as the Vacated Community of Grand Bruit Confirmation Order.

### Vacated community confirmed

2 The community of Grand Bruit is confirmed as a vacated community as of the date of publication of the Vacated Community of Grand Bruit Order, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 52/10, in the Gazette and, notwithstanding the repeal of that order, continues to be a vacated community for the purposes of the Act.
