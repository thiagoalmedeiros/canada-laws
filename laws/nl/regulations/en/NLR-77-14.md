## PLANNING AREA

Beginning at a point on the Town of Lawn municipal boundary, that point being the junction point of 2 old highroads leading to the Town of Lawn, having coordinates north 5,199,417 metres and east 612,041 metres, more or less;

Then south 19° 16" west a distance of 2,646 metres, more or less, along the Town of Lawn municipal boundary to a point on the high water mark of the coastline called East Head, located at the eastern entrance to Great Lawn Harbour;

Then in a generally northerly direction along the eastern shoreline of Great Lawn Harbour to the south side of the bridge spanning Northeast Arm to a point having coordinates north 5,200,114 metres and east 611,226 metres, more or less;

Then along the north shoreline of the harbour to the south side of the bridge spanning Northwest Arm;

Then along the western shoreline of Great Lawn Harbour in a generally southerly direction on the shoreline of Great Lawn Harbour to a point 1,120 metres, more or less, south of the mouth of a small brook leading from Bakeapple Pond;

Then north 66° 0' west a distance of 1,518 metres, more or less, to a point on the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then north 51° 03' west a distance of 1,470 metres, more or less, along the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then north 42° 20' west a distance of 1,710 metres, more or less, to a point on the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then north 38° 41' west a distance of 5,123 metres, more or less, to a point on the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then south 53° 10' west a distance of 1,515 metres, more or less, along the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then north 22° 39' west a distance of 1,937 metres, more or less, to a point on the Grand Bank Municipal Planning Area boundary;

Then north 42° 54' west a distance of 6,541 metres, more or less, along the Grand Bank Municipal Planning Area boundary;

Then south 38° 45' east a distance of 116 metres, more or less, to a point on the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then south 33° 28' east a distance of 659 metres, more or less, along the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then south 15° 51' west a distance of 805 metres, more or less, along the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then south 37° 57' east a distance of 1,784 metres, more or less, along the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then south 33° 53' east a distance of 1,981 metres, more or less, to a point on the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary;

Then south 12° 09' east a distance of 91 metres, more or less, to where the Brazil Pond Protected Water Supply Area boundary meets the centre of Newfoundland Power transmission line 305L, Crown Lease number 66474;

Then in a generally southeast direction, a distance of 4,520 metres, more or less, along the centre of Newfoundland Power transmission line 305L, Crown Lease number 66474;

Then south 0° 23' east a distance of 1,153 metres, more or less, to the most northeasterly point on the Town of Lawn municipal boundary;

Then following the Town of Lawn municipal boundary in a generally southeasterly direction to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to Grid North and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 National Topographical Series (NTS) digital mapping; UTM, NAD 83.
