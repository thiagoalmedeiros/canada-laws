CNLR 700/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 820320 46° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of approximately 450 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 9° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 77° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 3,150 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 94° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 81° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 127° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,125 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 80° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 113° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 240° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 4,150 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 257° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 3,500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 239° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 287° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 3,250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 299° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 282° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,675 metres;

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The South East River Water Supply Area Notice for the Town of Placentia, Newfoundland Regulation 109/78, is repealed.
