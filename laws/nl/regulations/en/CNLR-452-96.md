CNLR 452/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of South River Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of South River are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 817/78 and 171/84 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point, that point being the eastern bank of Goulds Brook where the brook is crossed by the Hodgewater Line;

Then in a southerly direction along the eastern bank of the brook to a point on a line perpendicular to and 100 metres, more or less, from the centre line of the Hodgewater Line;

Then in a northeasterly direction along a line parallel to and 100 metres, more or less, from the centre line of Hodgewater Line to a point on the centre line of the Canadian National Railway right-of-way;

Then in a northeasterly direction along the centre line of the right-of-way to a point, that point being where the right-of-way is crossed over by a power transmission line;

Then in a northeasterly direction a distance of 914.4 metres, more or less, to a point on the centre line of the Roaches Line, that point being 100 metres, more or less, southeast of the intersection of Cupids Crossing Road and the Roaches Line;

Then in a northwesterly direction along a line parallel to and 100 metres, more or less, from the centre line of Cupids Crossing Road - Carroll's Road to a point on the centre line of the Canadian National Railway right-of-way;

Then in a northeasterly direction along the centre line of the right-of-way a distance of 213 metres, more or less, to a point along the line of demarcation of the property at the southeast corner of Carroll's Road and the old Conception Bay Highway intersection;

Then along the line in a northwesterly direction a distance of 152 metres, more or less, to a point on the centre line of Carroll's Road;

Then along the centre line of Carroll's Road in a northeastern direction crossing over the old Conception Bay Highway and then continuing along the centre line of a woods road to a point, that point being north 38° east and 3,600 metres, more or less, from the last given point;

Then in a northwesterly direction along the woods road a distance of 548.6 metres, more or less, to a point where the woods road ties into Salmon Cove Road, that point being north 52° west and 503 metres, more or less, from the last given point;

Then north 45° west a distance of 97.5 metres, more or less, to a point on the highwater mark on the southeastern shore of Bay de Grave;

Then in a generally southwesterly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline at highwater mark to a point, that point being the eastern bank of South River at the mouth of the river on the southeastern side of Bay de Grave;

Then in a southwesterly direction along the eastern bank of South River to a point on the eastern bank and at the mouth of Goulds Brook;

Then in a southeasterly direction along the eastern bank of Goulds Brook to a point where it is crossed by Hodgewater Line, the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:12,000 Crown Lands and Surveys Division Mapping, 1971 edition.
