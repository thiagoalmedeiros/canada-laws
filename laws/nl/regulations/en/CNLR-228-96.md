CNLR 228/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Heart's Content Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Heart 's Content are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 883/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point, that point being the most western extremity of Bacon Cove Head;

Then in a generally easterly direction a distance of 6,775 metres, more or less, to the southern extremity of Murphy's First Pond;

Then in a generally southerly direction a distance of 7,650 metres, more or less, to the southeastern abutment of Halfway Bridge;

Then in a generally westerly direction a distance of 7,050 metres, more or less, to the most northern extremity of Hanging Hill Pond;

Then in a generally northwesterly direction a distance of 5,975 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being where a small brook flowing from Freshwater Pond meets the sea;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline in a generally northerly direction to the point of commencement.
