# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Davies Pond

CNLR 563/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 510090, 111° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 75 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 183° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 112° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 825 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 48° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 315° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 292° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 725 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Davies Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 152/79, is repealed.
