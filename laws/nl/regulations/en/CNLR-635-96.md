CNLR 635/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21 710000 metres east, 5514000 metres north 255° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,825 to the commencement point.

Then from the above point by a line drawn 175° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,000 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 263° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 157° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,575 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 212° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 262° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 175 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 280° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 322° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,225 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 287° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 175 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 356° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 303° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 76° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 48° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 94° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 375 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Long Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Town of Joe Batt's Arm - Barr'd Island - Shoal Bay, Newfoundland Regulation 174/91, is repealed.
