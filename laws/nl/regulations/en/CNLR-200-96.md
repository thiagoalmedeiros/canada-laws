CNLR 200/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Gaultois Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Gaultois are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 652/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point at the tip of land referred to as Fox Burrow Point located on the southern shore of Long Island;

Then running north 20° west 1,700 metres;

Then running north 23° 30' east 1,825 metres;

Then running south 46° east 1,500 metres;

Then running due south 600 metres, more or less, to The Bottom of Gaultois Harbour;

Then running in a generally southwesterly direction along the shoreline of Hermitage Bay to Deepwater Point;

Then running in a westerly direction along the shoreline to the point of commencement.

All bearings are referred to the true meridian.
