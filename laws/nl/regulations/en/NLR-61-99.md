This area includes all lands in the provincial Electoral District of Terra Nova abutted and bounded as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from 3 ° traverse mercator, zone 1, NAD 27, bench mark 838021, 816,270.13 metres east, 17,684,474.78 metres north, 16 ° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 84 metres to the commencement point.
