CNLR 1173/96

*Made under the Newfoundland and Labrador Chicken Marketing Scheme and Natural Products Marketing Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Chicken Farmers of Newfoundland and Labrador Licensing Regulations.

### Prohibition

2 A person shall not engage in the marketing of chicken in the province unless he or she is the holder of a valid licence issued by the board and pays to the board annually the fee prescribed by the minister.

### Monthly report

3 A licensee shall report on a monthly basis to the board the total number of kilograms of chicken which was marketed by him or her in the previous month.

### Expiry

4 A licence shall expire on December 31 of the year for which it was issued.

### Compliance required

5 The licensee shall comply with all orders and regulations of the board.

### Refusal of licence

6 The board may refuse to issue or renew a licence or suspend or revoke a licence where the applicant or licensee has failed to observe, perform or carry out a condition of the licence.

### Repeal

7 The Newfoundland Chicken Marketing Board Licensing Regulations, 1981, Newfoundland Regulation 135/81, are repealed.
