CNLR 819/96

Beginning at a point, that point being on the highwater mark on the northwesterly extremity of Danson Head in Bonavista Bay;

Then south 41° 30' east a distance of 7,825 metres, more or less, to a point;

Then north 49° 00' east a distance of 2,475 metres, more or less, to the centre line of Highway 230;

Then in a northeasterly direction perpendicular to the centre line of Highway 230 a distance of 150 metres;

Then in a northerly direction parallel to and 150 metres from the centre line of Highway 230 to a point 150 metres north of and on a line perpendicular to the centre line of Highway 238;

Then in a northeasterly direction parallel to and 150 metres from the centre line of Highway 238 to a point on the boundary of the Bonavista watershed;

Then in a northeasterly direction along the watershed boundary to a point, that point being a horizontal control monument on Burnt Ridge;

Then north 15° 00' west a distance of 2,750 metres, more or less, to a point on the centre line of Highway 238;

Then north 77° 00' east a distance of 3,800 metres, more or less, to the point on the highwater mark on the western side of Trinity Bay;

Then in a northerly direction along the shoreline at the highwater mark, passing Cape L'Argent, Spillars Cove, Lance Cove, to the most northerly point on the Bonavista Peninsula;

Then in a southerly direction along the shoreline at the highwater mark, passing through Red Cove, Bonavista Harbour, Danson Cove, to Danson Head the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 mapping, 1972 edition.

Newfoundland Regulation 144/86 is repealed.
