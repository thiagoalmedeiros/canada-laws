CNLR 254/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Little Bay Islands Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Little Bay Islands are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 723/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

All that area being the island of Little Bay Island together with Harbour Island, Boatswain Tickle Island, Goat Island, Black Island, Macks Island and the several small islands adjacent thereto.
