CNLR 343/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Pool's Cove Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Pool 's Cove are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 779/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at the highway between Salmon River and Turnip Cove and running along the stream from Jim Grandy's Pond to Dicks Beach;

Then following the coastline of North Bay by way of Turnip Cove and Pool's Cove to the mouth of a stream from Big Mesh Pond;

Then following the stream to Big Mesh Pond and then running northwardly to the place of beginning.
