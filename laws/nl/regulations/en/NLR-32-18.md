# Local Service District of George's Brook-Milton Disestablishment Order

NLR 32/18

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Local Service District of George's Brook-Milton Disestablishment Order.

### Local service district disestablished

2 The Local Service District of George's Brook-Milton in the Provincial Electoral District of Bonavista is disestablished.

### Repeal

3 The Local Service District of George's Brook-Milton, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 24/05, is repealed.

### Commencement

4 This Order comes into force on May 8, 2018.
