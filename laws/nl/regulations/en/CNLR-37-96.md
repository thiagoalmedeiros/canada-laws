CNLR 37/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Appleton Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Appleton are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 672/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the centre line of the western face of the east abutment of the highway bridge spanning the Gander River between Glenwood and Appleton;

Then running in a generally northerly direction along the east shoreline of the Gander River 650 metres, more or less;

Then south 83° 00' east 1,450 metres, more or less;

Then south 6° 00' west 4,700 metres, more or less, to the northern bank of an unknown river;

Then in a general westerly direction along the northern bank of the river to the eastern shoreline of The Outflow - Gander River;

Then in a northerly direction along the eastern shoreline of The Outflow - Gander River to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 National Topographic Mapping 2E/2 and 2D/15, edition 3.
