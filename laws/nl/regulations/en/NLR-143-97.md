# Local Service District of Cavendish

NLR 143/97

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### District continued

1 The area comprising the community of Cavendish is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Cavendish.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Consolidated Newfoundland Regulation 114/96 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point in the eastern shoreline of Trinity Bay at the northern entrance to Whiteway Bay, that point being the westernmost extremity of Witless Point and having Military Grid Reference 120872 on NTS 1N12;

Then running south 61° east 1,950 metres, more or less, to a point having MTM co-ordinates north 5,284,955 metres and east 268,575 metres as derived from Provincial Topographic Mapping Sheet 1N11-360, scale 1:2500;

Then in an easterly direction 340 metres, more or less, along a line parallel to and 30 metres from the northern right-of-way of Pump House Road to the centre line of a hydro line;

Then in a generally northerly direction along the hydro line a distance of 3,750 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being 213 metres, more or less, due east of horizontal control monument No. 83250;

Then due west to a point on a high water mark on the eastern shoreline of Trinity Bay;

Then in a generally southerly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline passing through Cavendish Bay to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to grid north.
