NLR 74/23

*Made under the Lands Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Ragged Beach Land Reserve Order.

### Purpose

2 The Ragged Beach Land Reserve is established for the purpose of protecting the Witless Bay Ecological Reserve from the potential impacts of future development.

### Area included

3 The areas included in the Ragged Beach Land Reserve are set out in the Schedule.

### Term

4 The Ragged Beach Land Reserve is established for a 10 year period.

## Schedule
