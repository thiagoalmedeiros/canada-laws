NLR 11/24

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Local service district established

1 The area comprising the communities of Swift Current and Black River is established as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Swift Current-Black River.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the local service district shall be the area comprising the communities of Swift Current and Black River.

### Local service district committee

3 The local service district committee established by Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 58.1/88 is continued as the local service district committee to control and manage the affairs of the local service district according to the Municipalities Act, 1999 and the Local Service District Regulations.

### CNLR 437/96 Rep.

4 The Local Service District of Swift Current, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 437/96, is repealed.
