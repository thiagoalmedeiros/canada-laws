CNLR 663/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 5278000 324300 309° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 340 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 209° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 160 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 238° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 208° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 430 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 248° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 415 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 42° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 59° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 190 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 78° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 120 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 67° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 158° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 220 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The North Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 3/87, is repealed.
