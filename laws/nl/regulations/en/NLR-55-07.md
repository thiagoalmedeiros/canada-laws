NLR 55/07

*Made under the Jury Act, 1991*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Judicial Centre of Happy Valley-Goose Bay Jury List Boundary Variation Order.

### Boundary varied

2 For the purpose of compiling a jury list for the judicial centre of Happy Valley-Goose Bay, the jury list shall be compiled, in addition to those eligible persons living within 25 kilometres of the judicial centre of Happy Valley-Goose Bay, so as to include all those eligible persons living in Sheshatshui and North West River who live outside the 25 kilometre radius of the judicial centre.
