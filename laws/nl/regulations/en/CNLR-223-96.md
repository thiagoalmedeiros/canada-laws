CNLR 223/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Harbour Breton Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Harbour Breton are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 878/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at the mouth of Mile Pound Brook and then following the shoreline of Deadman's Cove, Bill's Cove, Red Head, Woody Point, Western Head in a general easterly direction;

Then continuing along the shoreline of Western Head, the Friar and Rocky Point in a general northerly direction;

Then continuing along the shoreline to Coady's Point in a general westerly direction;

Then following the shoreline of Harbour Breton to the mouth of South West Corner Brook;

Then continuing along the shoreline to Sandy Point and to the mouth of Spencer's Brook;

Then following the shoreline of the Barasway to North East Bottom Brook and then to Tickle Point;

Then following the northern shoreline of Harbour Breton to Keeping's Point and Hunt's Point;

Then following the shoreline to Devil's Dancing Hole at Poole 's Cove;

Then running north 89° west 2,900 metres, more or less;

Then running south 33° west 3,500 metres, more or less, to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
