CNLR 50/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Brighton Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Brighton are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 313/85 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the northern limit of the causeway crossing Brighton Tickle where the causeway intersects the shoreline of Brighton Tickle Island at the highwater mark;

Then in a general northwesterly and northeasterly direction along the shoreline of Brighton Tickle Island to a point where the shoreline intersects the southern limit of the causeway crossing Cobbler Tickle;

Then in a generally westerly direction along the southern limit of the aforementioned causeway to a point on the shoreline of Cobble Island where the causeway intersects the shoreline at the highwater mark;

Then in a generally westerly, northeasterly and southerly direction along the shoreline of Cobbler Island to a point where the shoreline intersects the northern limit of the causeway crossing Cobbler Tickle;

Then along the northern limit of the aforementioned causeway in a generally easterly direction to a point on the highwater mark on the shoreline of Brighton Tickle Island;

Then in a generally northeasterly, southeasterly and southwesterly direction along the shoreline of the aforementioned Brighton Tickle Island to a point on the southern limit of the aforementioned causeway crossing Brighton Tickle where the causeway intersects the shoreline;

Then in a southeasterly direction along the southern limit of the aforementioned causeway to a point where the causeway intersects the shoreline;

Then in a southwesterly direction along the shoreline a distance of approximately 200 metres to a point, that point having a grid reference of 983881;

Then south 25° west a distance of 1.55 kilometres, more or less, to the northern tip of an unnamed pond;

Then due west a distance of 350 metres, more or less, to the eastern limit of the right-of-way of the highway to Brighton;

Then in a northerly direction along the eastern limit of the aforementioned right-of-way a distance of 400 metres, more or less;

Then due west a distance of 450 metres, more or less, to a point on the highwater mark of the eastern shoreline of Pilley's Tickle;

Then in a generally northeasterly and southeasterly direction along the sinuosities of the aforementioned shoreline passing Hynes Cove and Budgells Cove a distance of approximately 2.55 kilometres to a point on the western limit of the causeway crossing Brighton Tickle;

Then in a northeasterly direction along the western limit of the aforementioned causeway a distance of approximately 150 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings herein refer to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 National Topographic Mapping.
