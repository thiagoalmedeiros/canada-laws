NLR 27/99

*Made under the Provincial Parks Regulations and the Provincial Parks Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Road Crossings Designation Order.

### Road crossings designated

2 All road crossings within the Newfoundland and Labrador TRailway Provincial Park in existence immediately prior to the proclamation of that park which are under the jurisdiction of the province, cities, towns and local service districts are designated for motor vehicle use.
