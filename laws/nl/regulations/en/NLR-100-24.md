NLR 100/24

*Made under the Electrical Power Control Act, 1994 and the Public Utilities Act (OC 2024-217)*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Come by Chance Capacitor Bank Assets Exemption Order.

### Definition

2 In this Order, "Come by Chance capacitor bank assets" means the 38.35 MVAR capacitor banks and related equipment located at the Come by Chance terminal station. 100/24 s2 Back to Top Exemption

### Exemption

3 Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro is exempt from the Electrical Power Control Act, 1994 and the Public Utilities Act for all aspects of its activities pertaining to the Come by Chance capacitor bank assets.

### Commencement

4 This Order comes into force on January 1, 2025.
