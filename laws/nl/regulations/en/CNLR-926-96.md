CNLR 926/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the St. John's Urban Region, Regional Development Order.

### Application of Order

2 This Order applies generally throughout the St. John's Urban Region, Regional Planning Area declared by the minister and described or referred to in the St. John's Urban Region, Regional Plan approved by the minister.

### Prohibition

3 All public authorities and all other persons, corporations, partnerships, associations or other organizations are prohibited from taking any action or undertaking any development that conflicts with or is inconsistent with the approved regional plan for the St. John's Urban Region, Regional Planning Area.

### Repeal

4 Newfoundland Regulation 914/78 is repealed.
