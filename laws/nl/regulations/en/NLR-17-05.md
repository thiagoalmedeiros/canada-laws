This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Port au Port abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with it = s centre being the Local Service District of Sheaves Cove water supply Drilled Well, having UTM coordinates Northing 5,376,795 metres and Easting 349,106 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
