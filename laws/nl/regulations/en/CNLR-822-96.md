Then south 54° 19' east 136.86 metres;

Then following Buchans River in a generally southeasterly direction to a point west across from the extreme western end of an island, that point being the mouth of a stream which flows from a southwesterly distant lake, namely, "Lake 2";

Then following the north bank of that stream to a point in the southwestern limit of the Badger to Buchans highway (30.48 metres wide);

Then north 56° 23' west 39.39 metres;

Then south 17° 23' west 76.20 metres;

Then south 44° 54' west 207.45 metres;

Then north 85° 51' west 454.15 metres;

Then south 69° 54' west 405.38 metres;

Then north 20° 6' west 352.22 metres to the point of commencement and containing in all an area of 131.3369 hectares, all bearings being true.
