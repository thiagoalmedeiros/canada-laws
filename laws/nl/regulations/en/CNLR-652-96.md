CNLR 652/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 630000 5437000 145° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 191° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 251° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,025 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 179° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 239° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,575 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 258° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 304° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 825 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 48° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 69° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 60° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 47° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,100 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 85° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,175 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Mill Lake Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 200/87, is repealed.
