NLR 64/21

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Burlington Order, 2021.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Burlington are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 The Town of Burlington Order, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 52/96, is repealed.
