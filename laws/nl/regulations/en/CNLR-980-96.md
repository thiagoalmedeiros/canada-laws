CNLR 980/96

*Made under the Young Offenders Act (Canada)*

For the purposes of the Act

(a) social workers employed by the government of the province and registered social workers employed by a regional health and community services board constituted under section 4 of the Health and Community Services Act and the Grenfell Regional Health Services Board are designated as youth workers; and

(b) group homes for young offenders throughout the province are designated as "open custody facilities".

The Order re: Young Offenders Act (Canada) No. 2, Newfoundland Regulation 135/84, is repealed.
