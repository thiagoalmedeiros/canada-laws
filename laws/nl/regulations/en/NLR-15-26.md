NLR 15/26

*Made under the Forestry Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Forest Fire Season Order, 2026.

### Forest fire season

2 The forest fire season for 2026 shall be

(a) between the hours of 12:01 a.m. on May 1, 2026 and midnight on September 30, 2026 on the island portion of the province; and

(b) between the hours of 12:01 a.m. on May 15, 2026 and midnight on September 30, 2026 on the Labrador portion of the province.
