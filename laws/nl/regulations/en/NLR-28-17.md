NLR 28/17

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of St. Barbe - L'Anse aux Meadows abutted and bounded as follows:

Beginning at a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 472,449 metres and northing 5,596,326 metres;

Then running in an easterly direction for a distance of 451 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 472,896 metres and northing 5,596,265 metres;

Then running in a southeasterly direction for a distance of 404 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 473,149 metres and northing 5,595,950 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 205 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 473,034 metres and northing 5,595,780 metres;

Then running in a southwesterly direction for a distance of 442 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 472,633 metres and northing 5,595,595 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 304 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 472,344 metres and northing 5,595,688 metres;

Then running in a westerly direction for a distance of 362 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 471,982 metres and northing 5,595,698 metres;

Then running in a northwesterly direction for a distance of 226 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 471,886 metres and northing 5,595,903 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 284 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 472,026 metres and northing 5,596,150 metres;

Then running in a northeasterly direction for a distance of 257 metres, more or less, to a point having scaled UTM coordinates of easting 472,228 metres and northing 5,596,309 metres;

Then running in an easterly direction for a distance of 222 metres, more or less, to the point of commencement.

All coordinates refer Zone 21 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
