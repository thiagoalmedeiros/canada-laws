CNLR 784/96

*Made under the Penitentiary Act (Canada)*

The officer holding the position of Assistant Superintendent of Her Majesty's Penitentiary, St. John's, is designated as an officer authorized to approve the transfer of prisoners from Her Majesty's Penitentiary in the province to any penitentiary in accordance with the Act.

The Order: re Authorization to Approve the Transfer of Prisoners, Newfoundland Regulation 144/91, is repealed.
