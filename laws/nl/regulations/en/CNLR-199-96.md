CNLR 199/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Garnish Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Garnish are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 651/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a coastal point in "The Ridge" at Frenchman's Cove, that point being at UTM grid co?ordinate 621 910 metres east, 5 230 430 metres north;

Then following the coastline by way of Deepwater Point, Doughball Head and Doughball Cove to a point north 54° 30' west of Garnish Tolt;

Then running south 54° 30' east 6,350 metres, more or less, to Garnish Tolt;

Then running south 49° 30' west 4,900 metres, more or less;

Then running north 70° 30' west 1,800 metres, more or less;

Then running south 21° west 3,170 metres, more or less;

Then running north 25° 30' west 6,200 metres, more or less, to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to UTM grid and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 Map 1 M/3, edition 3, 1988.
