NLR 36/19

*Made under the Wild Life Regulations and the Wild Life Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Salmon Angling and Tag Use Order, 2019.

### Tags - class 2 rivers

2 Salmon retained by angling on rivers designated as Class 2 rivers under the Newfoundland and Labrador Fishery Regulations (Canada) shall be tagged with only red tag number one.

### Tags – class 4 and 6 rivers and unclassified rivers

3 Salmon retained by angling on rivers designated as Class 4 or 6 and on unclassified rivers under the Newfoundland and Labrador Fishery Regulations (Canada) shall be tagged with only

(a) red tag number one; or

(b) green tag number 2.

### Conditions on tag use

4 When a person affixes a tag referred to in this Order to a salmon, the person shall immediately cut out and remove the month and day of capture on the tag.

### Repeal

5 The Salmon Angling and Tag Use Order, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 44/18, is repealed.
