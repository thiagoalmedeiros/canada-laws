CNLR 252/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Little Bay Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Little Bay are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 722/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point in the northwest shoreline of Little Bay, that point having reference 625 metres, more or less, northeast of the community church and then running north 23 o west 202 metres, more or less;

Then running south 66 o west 1400 metres, more or less;

Then running south 25 o east 525 metres, more or less;

Then running south 5 o 30 east 175 metres, more or less;

Then running south 25 o 30 west 250 metres, more or less;

Then running south 55 o 30 west 880 metres, more or less;

Then running north 55 o east 550 metres, more or less;

Then running north 4 o west 400 metres, more or less;

Then running north 50 o east 457 metres, more or less;

Then running north 29 o 10 west 188 metres, more or less, to a point in the northeast shoreline of Little Bay;

Then running in a generally southwesterly and northeasterly direction along the shoreline of Little Bay to the point of beginning.

All bearings refer to grid north.
