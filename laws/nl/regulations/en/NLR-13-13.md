NLR 13/13

*Made under the Urban and Rural Planning Act, 2000*

All that area within the boundary of the Town of Fogo Island as defined or redefined by an Order-in-Council and published in the Gazette and all islands within 800 metres of the shoreline of the Town of Fogo Island.

The following are repealed:

(a) the Fogo Municipal Planning Area, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 847/96; and

(b) the Joe Batts Arm - Barrd Islands - Shoal Bay Municipal Planning Area, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 869/96.
