CNLR 741/96

*Made under the City of St. John's Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Northeast Avalon Regional Services Designation Order.

### Regional firefighting services

2 Regional firefighting is designated as a service owned, managed and operated by the City of St. John's in the following municipalities:

City of Mount Pearl

City of St. John's

Town of Logy Bay-Middle Cove-Outer Cove

Town of Paradise-St. Thomas

Town of Petty Harbour-Maddox Cove

Town of Portugal Cove-St. Philip's-Hogan's Pond.

### Water supply services

3 Water supply is designated as a service to be supplied by the City of St. John's to the following municipalities:

City of Mount Pearl

City of St. John's

Town of Conception Bay South

Town of Paradise-St. Thomas.

### Repeal

4 The Northeast Avalon Regional Services Designation Order, 1992, Newfoundland Regulation 60/92, is repealed.
