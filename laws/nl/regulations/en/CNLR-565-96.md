CNLR 565/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 354000 5211000 0 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 200 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 26° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 87° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 181° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 150 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 120° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 198° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 257° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 321° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Deep Cove Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 1/90, is repealed.
