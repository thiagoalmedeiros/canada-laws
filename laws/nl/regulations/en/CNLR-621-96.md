# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Lilly Pond

CNLR 621/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 780440 21° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of approximately 200 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 98° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 850 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 148° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 221° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,050 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 344° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,350 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Lilly Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Milton, Newfoundland Regulation 256/ 78, is repealed.
