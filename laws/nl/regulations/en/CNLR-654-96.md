CNLR 654/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference, 210810 322° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 900 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 313° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 346° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 84° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 45° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,100 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 13° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 64° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 24° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 71° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,350 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 124° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 900 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 206° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 198° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 135° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 214° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 675 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 258° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 220° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,075 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Miller's Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Town of Trepassey, Newfoundland Regulation 38/80, is repealed.
