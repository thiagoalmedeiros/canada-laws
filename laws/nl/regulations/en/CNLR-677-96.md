# Water - Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Pynn’s Pond, Summerside

CNLR 677/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 429000 5427000 31° 00' for a distance of 460 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 320° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 920 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 49° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,640 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 16° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,040 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 75° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,000 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 162° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 810 metres to the point of commencement.

Then from the above point by a line drawn 193° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,800 metres to the point of commencement.

Then from the above point by a line drawn 208° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres to the point of commencement.

Then from the above point by a line drawn 300° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Pynn's Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Summerside, Newfoundland Regulation 36/ 91, is repealed.
