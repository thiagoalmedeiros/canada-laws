NLR 22/21

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of North River Order, 2021.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of North River are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 The Town of North River Order, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 310/96, is repealed.

## SCHEDULE

Beginning at a point on the center line of the Conception Bay Highway having coordinates east 283,478.581 metres and north 5,267,928.340 metres, more or less;

Then running in a northwesterly direction along the center line of the Conception Bay Highway to a point having coordinates east 283,214.052 metres and north 5,268,341.356 metres, more or less;

Then running in an easterly direction along a line parallel to and 50 meters from the center line of that road to a point being 284,235.966 metres and north 5,269,369.860 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a northwesterly direction to a point having coordinates east 284,081.738 metres and north 5,269,577.334 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a westerly direction to a point situated on Bay Roberts Municipal Boundary having coordinates east 283,274.256 metres and north 5,269,076.551 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a southerly direction to a point situated on Bay Roberts Municipal Boundary having coordinates east 283,266.000 metres and north 5,268,996.000 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a westerly direction to a point situated on Bay Roberts Municipal Boundary having coordinates east 282,454.491 metres and north 5,268,994.964 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a southerly direction to a point having coordinates east 282,454.244 metres and 5,268,669.358 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a southwesterly direction to a point on a high water mark of Mussel Bed Pond having coordinates east 281,621.802 metres and north 5,268,188.137 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a southwesterly direction to a point situated on Bay Roberts Municipal Boundary having coordinates east 280,526.872 metres and north 5,267,789.600 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a southwesterly direction to a point situated on Bay Roberts Planning Area Boundary having coordinates east 278,799.276 metres and north 5,266,511.633 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a southwesterly direction to a point on the centerline of an unnamed track having coordinates east 278,392.028 metres and north 5,265,174.427 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a southeasterly direction to a point at the intersection of the unnamed track and Snow's Pond Road having coordinates east 278,509.020 metres and north 5,264,958.207 metres, more or less;

Then heading in an easterly direction to a point having coordinates east 279,074.146 metres and north 5,264,954.026 metres, more or less;

Then heading in a northeasterly direction to a point on the centerline of the Veterans Memorial Highway having coordinates east 279,346.842 metres and north 5,265,136.233 metres, more or less;

Then running in a northeasterly direction along the sinuosities of an unnamed stream to a point on a high water mark of North River having coordinates east 279,880.054 and north 5,265,340.462 metres, more or less;

Then running in a northeasterly direction along the sinuosities of North River to a point on the south side of the Newfoundland T'railway bridge having coordinates east 283,414.567 metres and north 5,267,930.162 metres, more or less;

Then running in a general northeasterly direction along the sinuosities of North River back to the point of beginning.

All coordinates being scaled from 55cm Orthorectified Imagery and being referenced to North America Datum 1983, (NAD1983) using the Modified Transverse Mercator (MTM) Zone 1 Projection.
