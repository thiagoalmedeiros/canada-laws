CNLR 411/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Savage Cove - Sandy Cove Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Savage Cove - Sandy Cove are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 214/94 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the eastern shoreline of Strait of Belle Isle southwest of Cooper Island, that point bearing north 45° west from horizontal control monument 44000, that monument having UTM co-ordinates north 5 685 950, east 519 150;

Then south 45° east a distance of 2,100 metres;

Then north 45° east a distance of 4,000 metres;

Then north 45° west to a point on the eastern shoreline of the Strait of Belle Isle;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline in a generally southwesterly direction to the point of commencement.

All bearings herein refer to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 National Topographic Mapping, edition 2.
