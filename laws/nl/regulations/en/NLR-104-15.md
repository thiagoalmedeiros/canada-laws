NLR 104/15

*Made under the Municipal Affairs Act*

### Official administrator appointed

1 Mr. Daniel Hynes is appointed as official administrator to manage and conduct the affairs of the Town of Seal Cove, Fortune Bay until a quorum of councillors is elected or appointed.

### NLR 28/06 Rep.

2 The Official Administrator for the Town of Seal Cove, 2006, Newfoundland and Labrador 28/06, is repealed.
