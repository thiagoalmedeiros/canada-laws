NLR 55/05

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### District established

1 The area commonly known as Deer Park/Vineland Road is established as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Deer Park/Vineland Road.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service are the areas encompassing all the building lots fronting onto or having direct access off Vineland Road, Deer Park Road, Pioneer Line, Marshland Trails, Beaver Pond Road, Loon Lane, Country Lane, Hickman's Lane and Dawe's Pond Road.

### Committee to be elected

3 A Local Service District Committee comprised of householders of the area encompassed by the local service district shall be elected to control and manage the affairs of the local service district in accordance with the Municipalities Act, 1999 and the Local Service District Regulations.
