CNLR 624/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 22, 302000 metres east, 5443000 metres north, 44° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 885 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 329° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 3,800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 40° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 61° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 5,000 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 95° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 127° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,925 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 224° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,050 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 240° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,950 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 246° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,850 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 234° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 220° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,775 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 270° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 700 to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Little North West Pond Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 236/78, is repealed.
