CNLR 466/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Trout River Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Trout River are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 853/78 and 29/80 are repealed.

## Schedule

Starting at a point on the Gulf of St. Lawrence, more specifically having UTM references north 5 480 800, east 417 050;

Then travelling south 12° east a distance of 500 metres to the summit of an unnamed peak;

Then south 5° east a distance of 1,250 metres to the summit of an unnamed hill;

Then north 81° east a distance of 2,800 metres to the summit of a small hill northeast of Lower Trout River Pond;

Then north 20° west a distance of 1,750 metres to the summit of a small hill adjacent to Highway 431;

Then continuing on the same bearing a distance of 1,900 metres to a point on the shoreline of the Gulf of St. Lawrence;

Then following the shoreline westwards through Trout River Bay to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to grid north.
