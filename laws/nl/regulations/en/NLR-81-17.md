# Town of Duntara Disincorporation Order

NLR 81/17

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Duntara Disincorporation Order.

### Town disincorporated

2 The Town of Duntara is disincorporated.

### Repeal

3 The Town of Duntara Order, 2001, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 68/01, is repealed.

### Effective date

4 This Order shall come into force on the date of publication.
