NLR 54/26

*Made under the Income Tax Act, 2000*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Seniors' Affordability Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations

(a) "Act" means the Income Tax Act, 2000;

(b) "adjusted income" means the total of the individual’s income for the taxation year calculated as if in computing that income no amounts were

(i) included

(A) under paragraph 56(1)(q.1) of the federal Act or subsection 56(6) of the federal Act,

(B) in respect of any gain from a disposition of property to which section 79 of the federal Act applies,

(C) in respect of a gain described in subsection 40(3.21) of the federal Act, or

(ii) deductible under paragraph 60(y) or (z) of the federal Act;

(c) "eligible individual" means an individual who

(i) is 65 years of age or older on December 31, 2025,

(ii) filed a return of income for the 2025 taxation year as a resident of the province,

(iii) filed the return of income referred to in subparagraph (ii) on or before December 31, 2026, and

(iv) has an adjusted income equal to or less than $100,000 for the 2025 taxation year;

(d) "minister" means the minister appointed under the Executive Council Act to administer the Act; and

(e) "return of income" means in respect of an individual who is resident in the province at the end of the taxation year, the individual's return of income, other than a return of income under subsection 70(2) or 104(23) of the federal Act, paragraph 128(2)(e) of the federal Act or subsection 150(4) of the federal Act, that is required to be filed for the taxation year or that would be required to be filed if the individual had tax payable under that Act for the taxation year.

### Overpayment and refund

3 (1) An eligible individual is considered to have made an overpayment of tax in relation to the 2025 taxation year in the amount of

(a) $500 where the eligible individual's adjusted income is equal to or less than $75,000 for the 2025 taxation year; or

(b) an amount calculated in accordance with the following formula where the eligible individual's adjusted income is more than $75,000 but less than or equal to $100,000:

$500 – [(adjusted income - $75,000) x 1.6%]

(2) The minister shall refund the amount of the overpayment referred to in subsection (1) to the eligible individual.

(3) Notwithstanding subsections (1) and (2), the minister shall not make a payment under this section after December 31, 2027.

### Application of subsection 38(6) of the Act

4 Subsection 38(6) of the Act shall apply to an amount considered to be an overpayment of tax under these regulations.
