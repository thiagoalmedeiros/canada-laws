NLR 37/22

*Made under the Judicature Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Composition of the Supreme Court Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, “Act” means the Judicature Act.

### Composition of court

3 In addition to the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court and the Associate Chief Justice of the Supreme Court referred to in subsection 21(1) of the Act, the court shall consist of 24 other judges.
