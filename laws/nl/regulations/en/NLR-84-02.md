NLR 84/02

*Made under the Wilderness and Ecological Reserves Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Little Grand Lake Provisional Ecological Reserve Regulations.

### Definitions

2 In these regulations

(a) "managing agency" means the Parks and Natural Areas Division of the Department of Tourism, Culture and Recreation;

(b) "motorized boat" means a watercraft equipped with a mechanical means of propulsion and excludes a personal watercraft;

(c) "Newfoundland Marten" means the Newfoundland population of the Martes americana atrata;

(d) "personal watercraft" means a jet-ski, sea-doo, wave-runner and similar motorized watercraft; and

(e) "reserve" means the Little Grand Lake Provisional Ecological Reserve.

### Scientific research

3 (1) Scientific research within the reserve shall require a permit and those permits may be obtained from the managing agency on submission of a written request fully outlining and detailing the research project.

(2) Scientific research permits issued under subsection (1) may be subject to the terms and conditions the managing agency may determine.

### Permitted activities

4 Notwithstanding the prohibitions in section 24 of the Wilderness and Ecological Reserves Act, a person may do the following within the reserve:

(a) fish;

(b) hunt or net an animal other than the Newfoundland Marten;

(c) use a motorized boat or snowmobile but not a personal watercraft; and

(d) land aircraft.
