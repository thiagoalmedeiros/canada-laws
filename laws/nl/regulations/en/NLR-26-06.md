NLR 26/06

*Made under the Urban and Rural Planning Act, 2000*

The Labrador Inuit Settlement Area, as referred to in Part 4.2 of the Schedule to the Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement Act, the boundaries of which are set out in the Map Atlas and described in Appendix A-3, Part I of the Schedule on file with the Crown Lands Division of the Department of Environment and Conservation, is established as a regional planning area.
