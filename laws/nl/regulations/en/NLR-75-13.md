NLR 75/13

*Made under the Regional Service Boards Act, 2012*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Discovery Regional Service Board Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations, "board" means the Discovery Regional Service Board.

### Prescribed services

3 The board has the power to construct, acquire, maintain and operate a waste management system within the Discovery Region.

### Recovery of debt

4 An amount owing under section 24 of the Act is a debt due to the board and the board may recover it by civil action in a court.

### Withdrawal of service

5 The board may stop providing a service to a municipal authority, user or other person where the municipal authority, user or other person fails to comply with a policy of the board respecting the service.
