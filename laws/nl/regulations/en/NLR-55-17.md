NLR 55/17

*Made under the Tobacco and Vapour Products Control Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Tobacco and Vapour Products Control Regulations.

### Definition

2 In these regulations "Act" means the Tobacco and Vapour Products Control Act.

### Signs

3 (1) For the purpose of paragraph 4.5(a) of the Act a sign that lists the types of tobacco, vapour products or non-tobacco shisha for sale and their prices may be displayed in accordance with subsection (2) in a place or premises in which tobacco, vapour products or non-tobacco shisha is sold or offered for sale if the sign complies with the following requirements:

(a) the size of the sign shall not exceed 216 millimetres by 279 millimetres;

(b) the sign shall be white except for its text, which shall be black;

(c) the text on the sign shall not identify or reflect a brand of tobacco, vapour products or non-tobacco shisha or any element of a brand;

(d) the text on the sign shall use only the following words, or an abbreviation or an equivalent in another language, to list the types of tobacco, vapour products or non-tobacco shisha for sale

(i) cigarettes,

(ii) cigarillos,

(iii) cigars,

(iv) smokeless tobacco,

(v) loose tobacco,

(vi) vapour products,

(vii) non-tobacco shisha,

(viii) electronic cigarette, and

(ix) e-substance;

(e) the text on the sign shall include the price range for the types of tobacco, vapour products or non-tobacco shisha for sale; and

(f) the sign shall not include color, graphics, pictures, logos or other illustration.

(2) In a place or premises in which tobacco, vapour products or non-tobacco shisha is sold or offered for sale a sign that meets the requirements of subsection (1) may be displayed however

(a) no more than 3 signs in total may be displayed in the vendor's place or premises and of the 3 only one sign may be displayed at any till; and

(b) the text of the sign shall not be visible from outside the vendor's place or premises.

### Flavoured tobacco exemptions

4 The following types and flavours of tobacco are exempted from the definition of "flavoured tobacco" in the Act:

(a) a cigar that

(i) has a retail price of $4 or more,

(ii) weighs 5 grams or more, and

(iii) has a wine, port, rum or whisky flavour; and

(b) pipe tobacco that has a wine, port, rum or whisky flavour.

### Repeal

5 The Tobacco Control Regulations, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 119/09, are repealed.

### Commencement

6 These regulations come into force on July 1, 2017.
