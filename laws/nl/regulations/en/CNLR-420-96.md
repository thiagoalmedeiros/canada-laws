CNLR 420/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the community of Searston is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Searston.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall be the area commonly known as Searston.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 70/90 is repealed.
