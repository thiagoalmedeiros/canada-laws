CNLR 506/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 566000 5535000 6° 00' for a distance of 775 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 358° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 55° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,425 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 88° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 450 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 163° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 239° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 201° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 272° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 975 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Bear Pond Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Ming's Bight, Newfoundland Regulation 28/92, is repealed.
