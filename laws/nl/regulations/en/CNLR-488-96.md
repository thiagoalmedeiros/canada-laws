CNLR 488/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Woody Point, Bonne Bay Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Woody Point, Bonne Bay are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 868/78 and 30/80 are repealed.

## Schedule

Commencing at a point on the western shoreline of Bonne Bay north of Curzon, that point having UTM co-ordinates north 5 485 009 metres and east 433 377 metres;

Then due west a distance of 381 metres to a point having UTM co-ordinates north 5 485 009 metres and east 432 995 metres;

Then due south a distance of 1,280 metres to a point on the bank of an unnamed brook having UTM co-ordinates north 5 483 729 metres and east 432 988 metres;

Then crossing the brook south 31° west a distance of 2,237 metres to a point on the northern limit of Route 431 having UTM co-ordinates north 5 481 829 metres and east 431 805 metres;

Then south 20° east a distance of 624 metres to a point having UTM co-ordinates north 5 481 274 metres and east 432 091 metres;

Then running south 74° east a distance of 791 metres to a point on the low water mark of the shoreline of South Arm, Bonne Bay having UTM co-ordinates north 5 481 061 metres and east 432 853 metres;

Then following the sinuosities of the shoreline through Hell Cove, Paynes Point, Woody Point and Crolly Cove to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to grid north.
