# L'Anse aux Meadows Public Works Development Area Order

CNLR 1030/96

*Made under the Works, Services and Transportation Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the L'Anse aux Meadows Public Works Development Area Order.

### Area defined

2 The area described in the Schedule is declared to be a public works development area to be known as the L'Anse aux Meadows Public Works Development Area.

### Repeal

3 The L'Anse Aux Meadows Public Works Development Area Order, 1982, Newfoundland Regulation 40/82, is repealed.

## Schedule

All that piece or parcel of land situate and being at Beak Point and being north of a line beginning at a point in the western shoreline of Medee Bay and running on a bearing of south 47° 59' west to the eastern shoreline of Epaves Bay, that point of beginning having reference from a point in the boundary of the survey in Medee Bay of the above described area, that point having co-ordinates of north latitude 18,760,937'.45 and east longitude 1,106,026'.19;

Then running north 47° 30' west 228.6 metres;

Then south 47° 59' west to the principal point of beginning.
