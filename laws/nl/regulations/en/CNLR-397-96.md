CNLR 397/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Ramea Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Ramea are the northwest island of the Ramea Islands which contains Ship Cove, Muddy Hole, Hatcher's Point and the Beach.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 788/78 is repealed.
