NLR 86/25

*Made under the Electrical Power Control Act, 1994 and the Public Utilities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro - Exploits Generation Assets Exemption Order.

### Definition

2 In this Order, "Exploits generation assets" means

(a) the hydroelectric generation station and related facilities located at Bishop's Falls;

(b) the hydroelectric generation station and related facilities located at Buchans;

(c) the hydroelectric generation station and related facilities located at Grand Falls- Windsor;

(d) the hydroelectric generation station and related facilities located at Star Lake;

(e) the dams, dykes, control structures and other works required for the generation of power at the sites referred to in paragraphs (a), (b), (c) and (d);

(f) the transmission facilities necessary to deliver power generated at the sites referred to in paragraphs (a), (b), (c) and (d) to a suitable point of interconnection with existing transmission facilities; and

(g) any other facilities and equipment necessary for the facilities, works or equipment referred to in paragraphs (a) to (f).

### Exemption

3 Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro is exempt from the Electrical Power Control Act, 1994 and the Public Utilities Act for all aspects of its activities pertaining to the Exploits generation assets.

### Repeal

4 The Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro-Exploits Generation Exemption Order, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 109/13, is repealed.
