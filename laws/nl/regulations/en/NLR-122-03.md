# Notice of Wellhead Protected Water Supply Area for the Town of Wabana, Middleton Avenue Well

NLR 122/03

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Conception Bay East and Bell Island abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Town of Wabana water supply Middleton Avenue Well, having UTM coordinates of northing 5,277,103 metres and easting 351,391 metres.

All bearings refer to Zone 22 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
