CNLR 513/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21, 573000 metres east, 5266000 metres north, 140° 30' for a distance of 925 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 131° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 725 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 207° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 200° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 400 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 175° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 231° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,175 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 260° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 725 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 15° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 56° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 775 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 319° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 41° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 17° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 358° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 70° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 350 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Big Black Duck Pond Protected Water Supply Area Notice for the Community of Seal Cove, Fortune Bay, Newfoundland Regulation 12/95, is repealed.
