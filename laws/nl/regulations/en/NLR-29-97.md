NLR 29/97

*Made under the Judicature Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Interest Rate on Consolidated Trust Funds Order, 1997.

### Interest rate applicable

2 The interest rate payable on the consolidated trust funds kept by the Registrar of the Supreme Court under subsection 71(1) of the Judicature Act shall be at the rate of 2% below the average monthly prime lending rate of the Bank of Montreal.

### Repeal

3 The Interest Rate on Consolidated Trust Funds Order, Consolidated Newfoundland Regulation 743/96, is repealed.

### Commencement

4 This Order shall come into force on April 1, 1997.
