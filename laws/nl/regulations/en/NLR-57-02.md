NLR 57/02

*Made under the Endangered Species Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Endangered Species List Regulations.

### Endangered species

2 The species, subspecies or populations of species listed in Schedule A are designated as endangered.

### Threatened species

3 The species, subspecies or populations of species listed in Schedule B are designated as threatened.

### Vulnerable species

4 The species, subspecies or populations of species listed in Schedule C are designated as vulnerable.

### Extinct species

5 The species, subspecies or populations of species listed in Schedule D are designated as extinct.
