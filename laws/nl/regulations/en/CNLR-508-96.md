CNLR 508/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference zone 21, 680000 metres east, 5488875 metres north, 267° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,784 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 321° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 869 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 49° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 772 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 126° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 805 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 224° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,014 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Beaverton Pond, Port Albert Watershed Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 157/78, is repealed.
