NLR 2/07

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Carbonear – Harbour Grace abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Town of Harbour Grace water supply Southside Well, having UTM coordinates northing 5,283,164 metres and easting 334,140 metres.

All coordinates refer Zone 22 of the NAD 83 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
