CNLR 93/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Buchans Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Buchans are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 700/78 and 188/92 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the western shoreline of Buchans Brook, that point being on the western shoreline of Middle Branch River where that river runs into Buchans Brook;

Then in a westerly direction along the western shoreline of Middle Branch River to and along the western shoreline of Duhart's Pond and continuing along the western shoreline of Middle Branch River to a point, that point being where the river is crossed by a hydro transmission line;

Then in a northerly direction along the transmission (centre) line via its northern branch a distance of 3,650 metres, more or less, to a point on the western shoreline of Rothermere Pond;

Then in a northerly direction along the western shoreline of Rothermere Pond to the northwest extremity of that pond;

Then north 62° 30' east 1,225 metres, more or less, to summit of a hill, locally known as Laycocks Lookout;

Then north 87° 30' east 640 metres, more or less, to a point on the eastern end of a dam on the southern shoreline of an unnamed pond which feeds into Buchans Brook;

Then in an easterly direction via the dam to the western shoreline of Buchans Brook;

Then in a southerly direction along the western shoreline of Buchans Brook to a point, that point being the point of beginning.

All bearings herein refer to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:2500 scale mapping and the 1:50,000 National Topographic Mapping.
