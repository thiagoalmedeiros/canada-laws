NLR 34/96

*Made under the Urban and Rural Planning Act*

Beginning at Indian Head and running north 45° east 3,800 metres, more or less, to the top of a hill south of Gull Pond;

Then running north 21° east 2,380 metres, more or less, to the top of a hill northeast of Gull Pond;

Then running north 43° east 1,700 metres, more or less, to the top of a hill;

Then running south 89° east 4,550 metres, more or less, to the intersection of Rushy Brook with a stream from Long Gull Pond;

Then following that stream northwardly to a point situated south 39° west 4,500 metres, more or less, from the northeastern end of Mistaken Pond;

Then running north 39° east 4,500 metres, more or less, to the northeastern end of Mistaken Pond;

Then running south 35° 30' east 8,000 metres, more or less, to the northeastern end of a pond;

Then running south 30° 30' east 4,000 metres, more or less, to the northern bank of St. George's River;

Then along an exclusive line parallel to and 400 metres from the Trans Canada Highway, running southwesterly to the northern bank of Little Barachois Brook;

Then following that northern bank westwardly to the mouth of Little Barachois Brook;

Then following the coastline of St. George's Bay by way of Rothesay Bay to Indian Head, the place of beginning.

All bearings refer to UTM grid and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:50,000 mapping.

The Stephenville Crossing Municipal Planning Area, 1988, Newfoundland Regulation 155/88, is repealed.
