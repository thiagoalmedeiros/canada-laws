NLR 56/14

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Feasibility Report Respecting the Amalgamation of the Town of Glovertown and the Town of Traytown Order.

### Feasibility Report

2 A feasibility report shall be prepared to consider the amalgamation of the Town of Glovertown and the Town of Traytown.

### Appointment

3 George Whey of Whey Consulting is appointed to prepare the feasibility report.

### Preparation of report

4 The feasibility report shall be prepared in accordance with the provisions of the Municipalities Act, 1999.
