NLR 52/26

*Made under the Towns and Local Service Districts Act and the Municipal Elections Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Mainland-Three Rock Cove Election Order.

### Wards

2 The Town of Mainland-Three Rock Cove shall be divided into 2 wards as follows:

(a) the Mainland Ward; and

(b) the Three Rock Cove Ward.

### Election

3 The first election for the Town of Mainland-Three Rock Cove shall be held on December 1, 2026.

### Council

4 The town council shall consist of 7 councillors, as follows:

(a) 5 councillors to be elected at large;

(b) one councillor to be elected to represent the Mainland Ward; and

(c) one councillor to be elected to represent the Three Rock Cove Ward.

### Returning officer

5 Kimberly Perry is appointed as the returning officer for the purpose of conducting the election referred to in section 3.

### Alternate returning officer

6 Peggy Sheppard is appointed as the alternate returning officer.
