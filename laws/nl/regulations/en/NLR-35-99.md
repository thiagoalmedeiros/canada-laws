NLR 35/99

*Made under the Environment Act*

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from Military Grid Reference, Zone 21, 602,000 metres east 5,694,000 metres north 10 ° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres from the commencement point.

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 298 ° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres.

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 14 ° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres.

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 60 ° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 225 metres.

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 81 ° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 425 metres.

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 170 ° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres.

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 264 ° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 525 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.
