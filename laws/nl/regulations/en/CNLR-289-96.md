CNLR 289/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the community of Mainland is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Mainland.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district shall be the area commonly known as Mainland.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 185/81 is repealed.
