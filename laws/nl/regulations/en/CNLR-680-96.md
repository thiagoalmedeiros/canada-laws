CNLR 680/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 405000 6003000 348° 00' for a distance of 600 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 250° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,360 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 246° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,920 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 257° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,040 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 354° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,060 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 10° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,000 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 52° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 86° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,200 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 98° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 800 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 83° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,180 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 91° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,920 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 190° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,820 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Rigolet Pond Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 137/91, is repealed.
