CNLR 454/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Spaniard's Bay Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Spaniard 's Bay are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 168/91 and 82/92 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point, that point being the bridge spanning Shearstown Brook on the Conception Bay North Highway;

Then along the northern shoreline of the brook to a point having co-ordinates north 5 272 680 metres and east 282 490 metres and being some 70 metres, more or less, northeast of an unnamed brook flowing into Shearstown Brook;

Then due west to a point on the westernmost extremity of Goose Pond, that point having co-ordinates north 5 272 680 metres and east 281 416 metres;

Then south 89° 00' west a distance of 772 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being the intersection of the centre line of an unnamed road and a power transmission line;

Then south 50° 15' west a distance of 1,240 metres, more or less, to a high point, spot elevation 182.5 metres;

Then south 27° 30' west a distance of 1,180 metres, more or less, to a high point, spot elevation 157.0 metres;

Then south 65° 00' west a distance of 5,125 metres, more or less, to the northeastern extremity of an unnamed pond due south of Grassy Gullies Pond;

Then north 38° 00' east a distance of 2,100 metres, more or less, to horizontal control point 92010;

Then north 1° 00' east a distance of 800 metres, more or less, to the intersection of New Harbour Road and an unnamed road running southwesterly along Brazils Pond;

Then north 12° 00' west a distance of 1,860 metres, more or less, to horizontal control point ROBERT (38017);

Then north 54° 30' west a distance of 1,350 metres, more or less, to a high point, that point being on the catchment area of Kelly's Pond;

Then in a northeasterly direction along the catchment area to a high point, that point being north 29° 30' east and 5,900 metres from the last given point;

Then north 77° 00' east a distance of 2,725 metres, more or less, to a point on the centre line of Glover Road, that point being due south of the westernmost extremity of Spider Pond;

Then south 81° 30' east a distance of 3,880 metres, more or less, to the westernmost extremity of Muddy Hole Pond;

Then south 63° 30' east a distance of 670 metres, more or less, to the easternmost extremity of First Gully Pond;

Then south 32° 30' east a distance of 830 metres, more or less, to a high point, spot elevation of 177.4 metres;

Then south 36° 00' east a distance of 838 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being the southernmost extremity of the pond which empties into Bread and Cheese Cove Pond;

Then south 51° 00' east a distance of 1,036 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being a horizontal control point M-120 252, spot elevation 141.8 metres;

Then south 51° 00' east a distance of 562 metres, more or less, to a point on the highwater mark of the northern shoreline of Conception Bay;

Then in a southerly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline at highwater mark passing North Cove, Green Head, Woody Point to the sandy outlet of Arnies Pond and along the northern shoreline of the pond to the point of beginning.

All bearings herein refer to grid north, central meridian 53° west longitude, and as with distances and/or co-ordinates given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:2500, 1984, 1:5000, 1984 and 1:12,000, 1971, Crown Lands and Surveys Division Mapping and the 1:50,000 National Topographic Mapping.
