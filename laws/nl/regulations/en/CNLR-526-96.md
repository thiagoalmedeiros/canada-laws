CNLR 526/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 361000 5280000 135° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 340 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 81° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 580 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 121° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 570 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 175° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 660 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 222° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 900 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 242° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 490 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 305° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 348° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 440 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 22° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 58° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 290 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 356° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 370 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 25° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Blast Hole Ponds Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 221/83, is repealed.
