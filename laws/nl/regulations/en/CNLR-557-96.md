CNLR 557/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 505000 5511000 133° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 325 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 185° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 185° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 130° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 228° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 475 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 258° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 425 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 348° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 05° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 900 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 83° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 118° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Country Cove Water Supply Area Notice, Newfoundland Regulation 32/87, is repealed.
