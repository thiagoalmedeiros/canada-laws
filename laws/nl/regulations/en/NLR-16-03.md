NLR 16/03

*Made under the Historic Resources Act*

### Short title

1 These regulations may be cited as the Registered Heritage District Regulations.

### Application for registration

2 A person, association of persons, whether incorporated or unincorporated, and a municipality may apply to the foundation for the designation of an area containing a number of buildings or other structures that the applicant considers to be of architectural or historical significance to the province as a registered heritage district.

### Description

3 An application under section 2 shall include a description of the area to be included in the registered heritage district.

### Resolution of council

4 Where an application under section 2 relates to an area located within a municipality, the application shall be accompanied by a copy of a resolution of the council of the municipality in support of the proposed designation.

### Notice to property owners

5 The foundation shall inform the owners of property located in the area of the application by

(a) holding a public meeting for the purpose of informing them of the application; and

(b) publishing a notice respecting the application in a newspaper circulating in the area comprising the proposed district or by another appropriate means.

### Additional information

6 The foundation may require an applicant to provide additional information respecting the application that it considers necessary.

### Designation of district

7 The foundation may designate an area described in an application to be a registered heritage district where the foundation is of the opinion the area contains buildings or other structures that are of architectural or historical significance to the province.

### Publication of notice of designation

8 The foundation shall provide public notice of the designation by publication of a notice in a newspaper circulating in the area of the designation or by another appropriate means.
