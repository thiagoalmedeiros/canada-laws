# Order - Local Service District of Green’s Harbour

CNLR 195/96

### District continued

1 The area comprising the community of Green's Harbour is continued as a local service district by the name of the Local Service District of Green's Harbour.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries for the local service district are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulations 214/83 and 38/85 are repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point on the highwater mark on the northeastern shoreline of Green's Harbour, that point being on the southern boundary of Backside Pond Provincial Park;

Then in a counterclockwise direction along that boundary to a point, that point being on the southern boundary of the Community of Whiteway;

Then in an easterly direction along that boundary to a point, that point being on a line perpendicular to and 152 metres eastward of the centre line of Route 4;

Then in a southerly direction along a line parallel to and 152 metres from the centre line of Route 4 to a point, that point being the centre line of a transmission line right-of-way;

Then south 1° 30' west along the centre line of the right-of-way and continuing to a point, that point being the northeastern tip of Little Coopers Pond;

Then in a southwesterly direction along the sinuosities of the western shoreline of the pond to a point, that point being due west of the southern tip of a small island in the southern portion of the pond;

Then due west to a point, that point being on a line perpendicular to and 152 metres west of the centre line of Route 4;

Then in a northerly direction along a line parallel to and 152 metres from the centre line of Route 4 to a point, that point being the centre line of a woods road which leads into Hopeall;

Then due west a distance of 671 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being horizontal control monument No. 83200;

Then north 19° 30' west 1,343 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being horizontal control monument No. 83210;

Then due west a distance of 320 metres, more or less, to a point, that point being the highwater mark on the northeast coast of Hopeall Bay;

Then in a generally northerly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline at highwater mark passing through Green's Harbour to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to grid north and as with distances given are subject to approximation as delineated on the 1:12,000 Crown Lands and Surveys Division Mapping, 1971 edition.
