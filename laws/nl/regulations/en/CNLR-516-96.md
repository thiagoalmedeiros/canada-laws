CNLR 516/96

This area includes all lands described as follows:

That is to say by a line drawn from military grid reference 671000 5281000 129° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres to the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 117° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 196° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 142° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 211° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,100 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 290° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 900 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 30° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 58° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 500 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 07° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 304° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 43° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 750 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 92° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 600 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn 41° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 250 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Big Brook Water Supply Area Notice for the Town of Terrenceville, Newfoundland Regulation 195/94, is repealed.
