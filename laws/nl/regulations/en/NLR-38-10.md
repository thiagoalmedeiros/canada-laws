NLR 38/10

*Made under the Municipalities Act, 1999*

### Local service district established

1 The areas commonly known as Lethbridge, Morley's Siding, Brooklyn, Charleston, Portland, Winter Brook and Sweet Bay, in the electoral district of Bonavista North are established as the Local Service District of Lethbridge, Morley's Siding, Brooklyn, Charleston, Jamestown, Portland, Winter Brook and Sweet Bay.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the local service district shall be the boundaries of the areas of Lethbridge, Morely's Siding, Brooklyn, Charleston, Jamestown, Portland, Winter Brook and Sweet Bay.

### Local service district committee

3 A local service district committee comprised of householders of the area encompassed by the local service district shall be elected to control and manage the affairs of the local service district in accordance with the Municipalities Act, 1999 and the Local Service District Regulations.
