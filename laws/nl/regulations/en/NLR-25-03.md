# Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Rocky Pond

NLR 25/03

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Bonavista North abutted and bounded as follows:

By a line drawn from Military Grid Reference NAD 27, Zone 22, 289,700 metres east 5,476,000 metres north 291° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,150 metres from the commencement point;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 195° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 550 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 252° 15' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,300 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 237° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,650 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 190° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,375 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 225° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,700 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 323° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,650 metres

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 1° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 4,250 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 74° 45' grid azimuth for a distance of 2,275 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 101° 00' grid azimuth for a distance of 4,075 metres;

Then from the above point by a line drawn at 182° 30' grid azimuth for a distance of 1,450 metres to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to Grid North.

The Notice of Protected Water Supply Area of Rocky Pond, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 681/96, is repealed.
