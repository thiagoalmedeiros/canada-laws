CNLR 270/96

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of LaScie Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of LaScie are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 708/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at the most northern point in the shoreline of Hill Graplin Head and then running south 6,400 metres, more or less;

Then running north 65° east 2,750 metres, more or less;

Then running south 25° east 1,525 metres, more or less;

Then running north 65° east 2,750 metres, more or less;

Then running north 25° west 1,525 metres, more or less;

Then running along the district line north 65° east 1,525 metres, more or less;

Then running north 4,725 metres, more or less, to a point in the shoreline at the extreme eastern entrance to Reddits Cove;

Then running in a generally southwesterly direction along the sinuosities of the shoreline via Reddit's Cove and Reddit's Point to Sleepy Point;

Then running along the shorelines of the harbour of LaScie and continuing in a generally northwesterly direction via White Cove to the point of beginning.

All bearings being referred to the true meridian.
