NLR 9/26

*Made under the Towns and Local Service Districts Act and the Municipal Elections Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Special Election for the Town of Victoria Order.

### Special election

2 A special election for a 7-member town council shall be held in the Town of Victoria on May 14, 2026.

### Returning officer

3 Heather Elliott is appointed as the returning officer for the purpose of conducting the election referred to in section 2.

### Alternate returning officer

4 Jacob Kimball is appointed as the alternate returning officer.
