# Roddickton-Bide Arm Municipal Planning Area

NLR 86/17

*Made under the Urban and Rural Planning Act, 2000*

The areas of land described in Schedules A and B together are defined as the Roddickton-Bide Arm Municipal Planning Area.

The Roddickton Municipal Planning Area, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 921/96, is repealed.

## Schedule A

Beginning at a point, that point being the intersection of the eastern bank of Northeast Brook and the northern side of a bridge on Route 433 where Route 433 passes over Northeast Brook;

Then running east a distance of 2,400 metres, more or less, closest to map coordinates 5,643,039 metres north and 299,273 metres east;

Then running southeast a distance of 6,610 metres, more or less, closest to map coordinates 5,636,645 metres north and 300,950 metres east;

Then running southwest a distance of 2,045 metres, more or less, closest to map coordinates 5,634,954 metres north and 299,800 metres east;

Then running west a distance of 900 metres, more or less, closest to map coordinates 5,634,954 metres north and 298,900 metres east;

Then running southwest a distance of 2,250 metres, more or less, closest to map coordinates 5,634,301 metres north and 296,765 metres east;

Then running west a distance of 1,352 metres, more or less, to the high water mark on the shoreline of Chimney Bay closest to map coordinates 5,634,300 metres north and 295,413 metres east;

Then in a generally northerly direction along the eastern shoreline of Chimney Bay, passing Chaine Point, Dowers Harbour, Scott Point, Roddickton, Adamson Point and Muddy Hole to a point where Northeast Brook flows into Northeast Arm and then continuing along the eastern bank of Northeast Brook to the point of beginning.

All coordinate values were scaled from best available mapping and are referenced to grid north of the North American Datum 1983 (NAD83) using the Modified Transverse Mercator coordinate system (MTM), specifically zone 2 of the Newfoundland mapping system.
