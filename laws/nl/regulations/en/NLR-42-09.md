NLR 42/09

*Made under the Water Resources Act*

This area includes all lands in the Provincial Electoral District of Terra Nova abutted and bounded as follows:

By a circle of 100 metres radius with its centre being the Local Service District of Cannings Cove Water Supply #3 Glenda Penney Well, having UTM coordinates northing 5,369,097 metres and easting 289,357 metres.

All coordinates refer to Zone 22 of the NAD 27 Universal Transverse Mercator Projection.
