CNLR 105/96

*Made under the Municipalities Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Town of Cormack Order.

### Boundaries

2 The boundaries of the Town of Cormack are as set out in the Schedule.

### Repeal

3 Newfoundland Regulation 622/78 is repealed.

## Schedule

Beginning at a point where the Bonne Bay road intersects the Cormack - Deer Lake Highway and then following the centre line of the Cormack - Deer Lake Highway in a southerly direction for a distance of 1,050 metres, more or less;

Then in the direction north 48° east for a distance of 22,400 metres, more or less;

Then in the direction north 58° west for a distance of 7,550 metres, more or less;

Then in the direction south 33° 30' west for a distance of 13,100 metres, more or less;

Then in the direction south 53° 13' west for a distance of 5,975 metres, more or less, to an intersection with the Bonne Bay road;

Then in a southerly direction following the centre line of the Bonne Bay road to the point of commencement.

All bearings refer to grid north.
