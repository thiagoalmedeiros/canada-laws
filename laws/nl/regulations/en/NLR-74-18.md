NLR 74/18

*Made under the Wild Life Regulations and the Wild Life Act*

### Short title

1 This Order may be cited as the Polar Bear Hunting Order, Labrador.

### Shooting only

2 Polar bears shall only be taken by shooting.

### Bears in den

3 Polar bears shall not be taken in a den and active dens shall not be disturbed in any way.

### Bears with transmitters, etc.

4 Polar bears equipped with radio transmitters or collars shall not be taken.

### Bag limit

5 The bag limit shall be one polar bear of either sex per licence except that female polar bears accompanied by cubs, young of the year, shall not be taken.

### Repeal

6 The Open Season Big Game Polar Bear Hunting Order, Labrador, Newfoundland and Labrador Regulation 26/07 is repealed.
