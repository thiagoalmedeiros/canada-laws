# Remission Order for Certain Fees Under the Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees Regulations (April 1, 2020 to March 31, 2023)

SI/2024-5 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-01-30

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2024-02-14

P.C. 2024-76 2024-01-30

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2024-5/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the collection of certain amounts is unreasonable, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_81000-2-5385_hq_1704216660] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-2-5385_hq_1704217172], remits the amount of the adjustment of the fees set out in column 2 of subparagraphs 1(a)(i) and (ii), (b)(i) and (ii), paragraphs 2(a) and (b) and items 3 to 6 of the schedule to the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees Regulations](SOR-2012-253.md)[^fn_81000-2-5385_hq_1704229880], by operation of sections 4 to 10 of those Regulations during the period beginning on April 1, 2020 and ending on March 31, 2023.
