# Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Pursuant to Subsection 23(1) of the Act

SI/2017-17 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-02-24

Registration: 2017-03-08

P.C. 2017-162 2017-02-24

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, acknowledges receipt, on the making of this Order, of assessments done pursuant to subsection 23(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-2729_E_hq_17258] by the Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada with respect to the status of the species set out in the annexed schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## Endangered Species

**Birds**

- Chat virens subspecies, Yellow-breasted (Icteria virens virens) — Paruline polyglotte de la sous-espèce virens

- Warbler, Cerulean (Setophaga cerulea) — Paruline azurée

## Threatened Species

**Birds**

- Bobolink (Dolichonyx oryzivorus) — Goglu des prés

- Meadowlark, Eastern (Sturnella magna) — Sturnelle des prés

- Swallow, Bank (Riparia riparia) — Hirondelle de rivage

- Swallow, Barn (Hirundo rustica) — Hirondelle rustique

- Thrush, Wood (Hylocichla mustelina) — Grive des bois

## Special Concern

**Birds**

- Grebe, Western (Aechmophorus occidentalis) — Grèbe élégant

- Sparrow pratensis subspecies, Grasshopper (Ammodramus savannarum pratensis) — Bruant sauterelle de la sous-espèce de l’Est

- Wood-pewee, Eastern (Contopus virens) — Pioui de l’Est

## Not at Risk

**Birds**

- Warbler, Hooded (Setophaga citrina) — Paruline à capuchon
