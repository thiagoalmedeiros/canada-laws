# Annual Statement (Banks and Bank Holding Companies) Regulations

SOR/2010-230 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-02-06

Enabling authority: [BANK ACT](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsections 308(2)[^fn_1a], 840(2)[^fn_1b] and 978(1)[^fn_1c] of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md)[^fn_1d], hereby makes the annexed [Annual Statement (Banks and Bank Holding Companies) Regulations](SOR-2010-230.md).

## Contents of Annual Statement

**1** The prescribed statements referred to in subsections 308(2) and 840(2) of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md) are

- **(a)** a balance sheet as at the end of the financial year;

- **(b)** a statement of income for the financial year;

- **(c)** a statement of cash flows for the financial year;

- **(d)** a statement of changes in shareholders’ equity for the financial year; and

- **(e)** a statement of comprehensive income for the financial year.

<sub>History: SOR/2014-273, s. 14(F); SOR/2015-29, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on June 1, 2011.
