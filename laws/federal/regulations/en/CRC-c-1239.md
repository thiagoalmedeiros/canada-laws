# Oaths of Allegiance and Office Order (N.W.T.)

*Order prescribing oaths of allegiance and office*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Oaths of Allegiance and Office Order (N.W.T.)](CRC-c-1239.md).

<sub>History: 2014, c. 2, s. 59</sub>

## Oath of Allegiance

**2** The oath of allegiance prescribed by the Oaths of Allegiance Act is the oath of allegiance to be taken and subscribed by the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories.

<sub>History: 2014, c. 2, s. 60</sub>

## Oath of Office

**3** The oath of office to be taken and subscribed by the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories is in the form set out in Schedule I.

**4** \[Repealed, 2014, c. 2, s. 61\]

## Swearing

**5** The oath of allegiance and oath of office of the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories shall be sworn and subscribed before the Clerk or Assistant Clerk of the Privy Council.

**6** \[Repealed, 2014, c. 2, s. 62\]

## SCHEDULE I — Oath of Office for Commissioner of the Northwest Territories

*(Section 3)*

I,  …  do solemnly and sincerely promise and swear that I will duly and faithfully and to the best of my skill and knowledge execute the powers and trust reposed in me as the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories. So help me God.
