# IAC Limited Shareholders Order

*Order Dispensing with the Submission of a Proposed Amalgamation Agreement to the Shareholders of Iac Limited*

SOR/81-798 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [AN ACT TO INCORPORATE CONTINENTAL BANK OF CANADA](../../acts/en/C-38.5.md)

Registration: 1981-10-09

P.C. 1981-2727 1981-10-08

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-81-798/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 10(3)[^footnote1_e] of An Act to Incorporate Continental Bank of Canada, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order dispensing with the submission of a proposed amalgamation agreement to the shareholders of IAC Limited](SOR-81-798.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [IAC Limited Shareholders Order](SOR-81-798.md).

## Dispensation

**2** Notwithstanding any provision of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md), the proposed amalgamation agreement between Continental Bank of Canada and IAC Limited need not be submitted to the shareholders of IAC Limited.
