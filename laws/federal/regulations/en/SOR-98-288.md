# Order Designating the Province of Manitoba for the Purposes of the Definition “applicable guidelines” in subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act

SOR/98-288 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [DIVORCE ACT](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)

Registration: 1998-05-14

P.C. 1998-835 1998-05-14

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-288/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Province of Manitoba has established comprehensive guidelines for the determination of child support that deal with the matters referred to in section 26.1[^fna_e] of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)[^fnb_e];

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 2(5)[^fnc_e] of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)[^fnb_e], hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating the Province of Manitoba for the Purposes of the Definition “applicable guidelines” in subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act](SOR-98-288.md).

## Designation

**1** The Province of Manitoba is hereby designated for the purposes of the definition ***applicable guidelines*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md).

## Guidelines

**2** For the purposes of subsection 2(5) of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md), the law that constitutes the comprehensive guidelines for the Province of Manitoba is the Child Support Guidelines Regulation, Man. Reg. 58/98.

## Coming into Force

**3** This Order comes into force on June 1, 1998.
