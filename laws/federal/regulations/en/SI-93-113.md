# Order Designating the Minister of Supply and Services as Minister for Purposes of the Act

SI/93-113 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [TRANSLATION BUREAU ACT](../../acts/en/T-16.md)

Registration: 1993-07-14

P.C. 1993-1459 1993-06-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-113/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the [Translation Bureau Act](../../acts/en/T-16.md), is pleased hereby to designate the Minister of Supply and Services, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, to act as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
