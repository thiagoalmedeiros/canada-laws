# Order Designating the Minister of Canadian Heritage for Purposes of Section 110 of the Act

SI/2005-125 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2005-12-14

P.C. 2005-2047 2005-11-21

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2005-125/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 110(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby designates the Minister of Canadian Heritage for the purposes of section 110 of that Act, effective December 31, 2005.

[^footnotea_e]: S.C. 2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
