# Billiard Cloth Remission Order, 1996

*Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Billiard Cloth for the Manufacture or Repair of Billiard Tables*

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)[^fn_SOR-96-201_e_hq_6200], is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duties on billiard cloth for the manufacture or repair of billiard tables](SOR-96-201.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Billiard Cloth Remission Order, 1996](SOR-96-201.md).

## Remission

**2** Subject to section 3, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) on billiard cloth, of tariff item Nos. 5111.19.00 or 5112.19.10, for the manufacture or repair of billiard tables.

## Conditions

**3** The remission is granted pursuant to section 2 on the condition that:

- **(a)** the billiard cloth is imported into Canada during the period commencing on July 1, 1995 and ending on December 31, 1997; and

- **(b)** a claim for remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue within three years of the date of importation of the goods for which remission is claimed.
