# Exemption from Restrictions on Investments (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations

SOR/2001-386 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md)

Registration: 2001-10-04

P.C. 2001-1757 2001-10-04

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraphs 459(a)[^fn_SOR-2001-386_e_hq_6013] and (b)[^fn_SOR-2001-386_e_hq_6013] of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md)[^fn_SOR-2001-386_e_hq_6015], hereby makes the annexed [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations](SOR-2001-386.md).

## Exemption from Restrictions on Investments

### Prescribed circumstances

**1** For the purposes of subparagraph 453(3)(f)(ii) of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md), subsections 453(4) to (6) of the Act do not apply in determining whether a company would be permitted to acquire a substantial investment in an entity under subsection 451(2), paragraph 451(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 451(4) or 453(1) or (2) of the Act.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^fn_SOR-2001-386_e_hq_6016] These Regulations come into force on the day on which paragraphs 459(a) and (b) of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md), as enacted by section 550 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../../acts/en/F-11.1.md), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
