# Prescribed Province Pension Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing the Province of Quebec as a Province Providing a Comprehensive Pension Plan*

C.R.C., c. 391 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CANADA PENSION PLAN](../../acts/en/C-8.md)

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._391/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Prescribed Province Pension Regulations](CRC-c-391.md).

## General

**2** The Province of Quebec is hereby prescribed, for the purposes of the [Canada Pension Plan](../../acts/en/C-8.md), as a province the government of which has, before the 30th day after April 3, 1965, signified the intention of such province to provide for the establishment and operation in that province, in lieu of the operation therein of the [Canada Pension Plan](../../acts/en/C-8.md), of a plan of old age pensions and supplementary benefits providing for the making of contributions thereunder, commencing with the year 1966 and providing for the payment of benefits thereunder comparable to those provided by the [Canada Pension Plan](../../acts/en/C-8.md).
