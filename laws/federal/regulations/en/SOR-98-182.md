# Ontario Hydro Nuclear Facilities Exclusion Regulations (Use of Tobacco)

SOR/98-182 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [NON-SMOKERS’ HEALTH ACT](../../acts/en/N-23.6.md)

Registration: 1998-03-19

P.C. 1998-410 1998-03-19

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 8.2[^fn_SOR-98-182_e_hq_5567] of the [Non-smokers’ Health Act](../../acts/en/N-23.6.md)[^fn_SOR-98-182_e_hq_5568], hereby makes the annexed [Ontario Hydro Nuclear Facilities Exclusion Regulations (Use of Tobacco)](SOR-98-182.md).

## Interpretation

**1** The definitions in this section apply in these Regulations.

***Act*** means the Smoking in the Workplace Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. S.13. (***Loi***)

***employment*** means employment with Ontario Hydro or a person referred to in paragraph (b) of the definition ***nuclear facility***. (***emploi***)

***nuclear facility*** means a nuclear facility in Ontario that is subject to the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](../../acts/en/N-28.3.md) or any regulations made under that Act and that is

- **(a)** owned and operated by Ontario Hydro; or

- **(b)** owned or operated by a person other than Ontario Hydro if, on the day on which these Regulations come into force or on a day after that day, it is owned and operated by Ontario Hydro. (***installation nucléaire***)

<sub>History: SOR/2002-208, s. 44</sub>

## Exclusion

**2** Employment on or in connection with a nuclear facility is hereby excluded from the application of the [Non-smokers’ Health Act](../../acts/en/N-23.6.md), except sections 8.1 and 8.2.

## Application of Ontario Act

**3** Subject to section 4, the Act, as amended from time to time, applies, to the extent that it is relevant, in relation to employment on or in connection with a nuclear facility.

**4** For the purposes of section 3, the reference to “another Act or a regulation” in subsection 11(1) of the Act shall be read as a reference to “another Act or a regulation including the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](../../acts/en/N-28.3.md), and any regulations made pursuant to that Act”.

<sub>History: SOR/2002-208, s. 45</sub>

## Coming into Force

**5** These Regulations come into force on April 1, 1998.
