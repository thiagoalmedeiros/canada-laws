# Remission Order for Certain Fees Under the Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees Regulations (March 31, 2026 to April 29, 2026)

SI/2026-28 · Consolidated: 2026-06-14 · Last amended: 2026-06-04

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2026-06-17

P.C. 2026-555 2026-06-04

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2026-28/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the collection of certain amounts is unreasonable, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_81000-2-6180_hq_1776193787] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-2-6180_hq_1776193818], remits the amount of the difference between the fee to be paid for a service set out in any of items 1 to 3 and 5 of the schedule to the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees Regulations](SOR-2012-253.md)[^fn_81000-2-6180_hq_1776193863], as adjusted on March 31, 2026 in accordance with section 17[^fn_81000-2-6180_hq_1776193919] of the [Service Fees Act](../../acts/en/S-8.4.md)[^fn_81000-2-6180_hq_1776193949], and the fee actually paid in respect of that service if an application form indicating the fee in effect for that service until March 30, 2026 was received by mail during the period beginning on March 31, 2026 and ending on April 29, 2026.
