# Order Designating the Minister of Communications as Minister for Purposes of Section 47 of the Act

SI/93-232 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 1993-12-15

P.C. 1993-1985 1993-12-02

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-232/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 47[^fn_SI-93-232_e_hq_6546] of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md), is pleased hereby to designate the Minister of Communications, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of section 47 of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md).
