# Order Authorizing the Minister of Transport to Prescribe Fees (Ship/Shore Communications Services)

SI/94-56 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1994-05-04

P.C. 1994-641 1994-04-21

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-94-56/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)[^fn_SI_94-56_EN_hq_20089] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Transport to prescribe, by order, the charges to be paid by users of ship/shore communications services provided by Coast Guard Radio Stations.
