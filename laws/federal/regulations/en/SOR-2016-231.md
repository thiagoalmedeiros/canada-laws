# Cannabis Exemption (Food and Drugs Act) Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to paragraph 30(1)(j) of the [Food and Drugs Act](../../acts/en/F-27.md)[^fn_a], makes the annexed [Cannabis Exemption (Food and Drugs Act) Regulations](SOR-2016-231.md).

## Definitions

### Exemption

**1** *Cannabis*, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Cannabis Act](../../acts/en/C-24.5.md) and in relation to which an activity is conducted in accordance with the [Cannabis Act](../../acts/en/C-24.5.md), is exempt from the application of the [Food and Drugs Act](../../acts/en/F-27.md), unless the cannabis

- **(a)** is manufactured, sold or represented for use in the diagnosis, treatment, mitigation or prevention of a disease, disorder or abnormal physical state, or its symptoms, or in restoring or correcting organic functions, in human beings or animals;

- **(b)** is represented for use in modifying organic functions in human beings or animals;

- **(c)** is manufactured, sold or represented for use in disinfection in premises in which food is manufactured, prepared or kept;

- **(d)** is an *active pharmaceutical ingredient* as defined in subsection C.01A.001(1) of the [Food and Drug Regulations](CRC-c-870.md); or

- **(e)** \[Repealed, SOR/2025-43, s. 75\]

- **(f)** is sold to be used for the purpose of a *clinical trial*, as defined in section C.05.001 of the [Food and Drug Regulations](CRC-c-870.md), or an *experimental study*, as defined in section C.08.013 of those Regulations.

<sub>History: SOR/2018-144, s. 373; SOR/2025-43, s. 75</sub>

### Cannabis for medical purposes

**2** Despite paragraph 1(a), cannabis that is manufactured or sold in accordance with the [Cannabis Regulations](SOR-2018-144.md) is exempt from the application of the [Food and Drugs Act](../../acts/en/F-27.md) from the time it is manufactured or sold by any of the following persons, unless it is represented for a use referred to in that paragraph:

- **(a)** a person to which a licence, other than a cannabis drug licence, was issued under subsection 62(1) of the [Cannabis Act](../../acts/en/C-24.5.md); and

- **(b)** a *designated person* as defined in Part 14 of the [Cannabis Regulations](SOR-2018-144.md).

<sub>History: SOR/2018-144, s. 373</sub>

### Non-therapeutic research on cannabis

**3** Despite paragraph 1(f), cannabis is exempt from the application of the [Food and Drugs Act](../../acts/en/F-27.md) if it is sold to be used for the purpose of *non-therapeutic research on cannabis*, as defined in subsection 1(2) of the [Cannabis Regulations](SOR-2018-144.md), that is conducted in accordance with those Regulations.

<sub>History: SOR/2022-251, s. 25</sub>
