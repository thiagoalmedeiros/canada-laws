# Order Designating the Minister of Health to Be the Minister for the Purposes of the Physical Activity and Sport Act in Respect of Physical Activity and the Minister of State (Sport) to Be the Minister for the Purposes of that Act in Respect of Sport

SI/2025-83 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-05-13

Enabling authority: [PHYSICAL ACTIVITY AND SPORT ACT](../../acts/en/P-13.4.md)

Registration: 2025-06-04

P.C. 2025-494 2025-05-13

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 2 of the [Physical Activity and Sport Act](../../acts/en/P-13.4.md)[^fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-427 of March 14, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2911_hq_1746976651];

**(b)** designates the Minister of Health, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of the [Physical Activity and Sport Act](../../acts/en/P-13.4.md)[^fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484] in respect of physical activity; and

**(c)** designates the Minister of State (Sport), a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of the [Physical Activity and Sport Act](../../acts/en/P-13.4.md)[^fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484] in respect of sport.
