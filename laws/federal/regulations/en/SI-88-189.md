# Minister of National Revenue Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/88-189 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1988-10-12

P.C. 1988-2271 1988-09-29

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-88-189/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the cost of providing copies of documents containing information to be communicated or made available to the public under subsection 149.1(15) of the [Income Tax Act](../../acts/en/I-3.3.md) should be borne by the persons who receive that service;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of National Revenue to prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by a person for copies of documents containing information to be communicated or made available under subsection 149.1(15) of the [Income Tax Act](../../acts/en/I-3.3.md).
