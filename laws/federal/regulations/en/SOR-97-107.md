# General Export Permit No. 12 — United States Origin Goods

SOR/97-107 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](../../acts/en/E-19.md)

Registration: 1997-01-29

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-97-107/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to subsections 7(1.1)[^footnotea_e] and 10(1)[^footnoteb_e] of the [Export and Import Permits Act](../../acts/en/E-19.md), hereby issues the annexed [General Export Permit No. 12 — United States Origin Goods](SOR-97-107.md).

Ottawa, January 24, 1997

LLOYD AXWORTHY

*Minister of Foreign Affairs*

## General

**1** Subject to section 2, any person may, under the authority of this Permit, export from Canada any goods of United States origin as described in item 5400 of Group 5 of the Schedule to the [Export Control List](SOR-89-202.md).

**2** This Permit does not authorize the exportation of goods described in section 1 to any country listed in the [Area Control List](SOR-81-543.md) or to any of the following countries:

- **(a)** Cuba;

- **(b)** Democratic People’s Republic of Korea;

- **(c)** Iran; and

- **(d)** Syria.

<sub>History: SOR/99-203, s. 1; SOR/2005-223, s. 1</sub>

**3** Where any goods exported under the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the prescribed form under the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md), the statement “GEP-12” or “LGE-12” shall be inserted in the appropriate field of the report.

## Repeal

**4** General Export Permit No. Ex. 12 — United States Origin Goods[^footnote1_e] is cancelled.

## Coming into Force

**5** This Permit comes into force on January 29, 1997.
