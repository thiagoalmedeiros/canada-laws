# Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees or Charges Order

SI/87-93 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1987-05-13

P.C. 1987-844 1987-04-30

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-87-93/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fees or charges to be paid by private sector firms using data from the Department of Consumer and Corporate Affairs for sale to the public.
