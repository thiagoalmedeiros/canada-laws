# Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons to be the Minister for the purposes of the Act

SI/2017-64 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-10-06

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL SECURITY AND INTELLIGENCE COMMITTEE OF PARLIAMENTARIANS ACT](../../acts/en/N-16.6.md)

Registration: 2017-10-18

P.C. 2017-1237 2017-10-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2017-64/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [National Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians Act](../../acts/en/N-16.6.md), designates the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act, effective on the day on which the [National Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians Act](../../acts/en/N-16.6.md), chapter 15 of the Statutes of Canada, 2017, comes into force.
