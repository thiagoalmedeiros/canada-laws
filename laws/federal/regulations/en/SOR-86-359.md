# Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations

*Regulations Delegating Powers Conferred by Section 163 of the Customs Act Upon the Minister of National Revenue*

SOR/86-359 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS ACT](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)

Registration: 1986-03-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-86-359/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 163(2) of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md), hereby revokes the [Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations](SOR-86-359.md) made on the 12th of October, 1984[^footnotea] and, in substitution therefor, makes the annexed [Regulations delegating powers conferred by section 163 of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md) upon the Minister of National Revenue](SOR-86-359.md).

Ottawa, March 25, 1986

ELMER MacKAY

*Minister of National Revenue*

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations](SOR-86-359.md).

## General

**2** The officers named in the schedule may exercise the powers conferred upon the Minister of National Revenue by section 163 of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md).
