# Maximum Amount for Agreements with Governments Order

SOR/99-15 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [NUNAVUT ACT](../../acts/en/N-28.6.md)

Registration: 1998-12-16

P.C. 1998-2267 1998-12-16

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-99-15/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to paragraph 73(2.1)(b)[^fn_SOR-99-15_e_hq_5590] of the [Nunavut Act](../../acts/en/N-28.6.md)[^fn_SOR-99-15_e_hq_5591], hereby makes the annexed [Maximum Amount for Agreements with Governments Order](SOR-99-15.md).

## Maximum Amount

**1** For the purposes of paragraph 73(2.1)(b) of the [Nunavut Act](../../acts/en/N-28.6.md), an amount of $15,000,000 is hereby prescribed for agreements with governments.

## Coming into Force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
