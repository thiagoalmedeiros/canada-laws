# Order Designating the National Capital Region as the Place in Canada at which the Principal Office of the National Round Table on the Environment and the Economy Shall be Located

SI/94-61 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL ROUND TABLE ON THE ENVIRONMENT AND THE ECONOMY ACT](../../acts/en/N-16.4.md)

Registration: 1994-05-18

P.C. 1994-695 1994-04-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-94-61/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 14 of the [National Round Table on the Environment and the Economy Act](../../acts/en/N-16.4.md)[^fn_SI-94-61_e_hq_6559], is pleased hereby to designate the National Capital Region as the place in Canada at which the principal office of the National Round Table on the Environment and the Economy shall be located.
