# Pacific Hake Exemption Notice

*Notice Excepting Certain Pacific Hake from the Operation of Section 29 of the Fisheries Act*

SOR/86-750 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FISHERIES ACT](../../acts/en/F-14.md)

Registration: 1986-07-07

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-86-750/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to section 29 of the [Fisheries Act](../../acts/en/F-14.md), hereby makes the annexed Notice excepting certain Pacific hake from operation of section 29 of the [Fisheries Act](../../acts/en/F-14.md).

Ottawa, July 3, 1986

TOM SIDDON

*Minister of Fisheries and Oceans*

## Short Title

**1** This Notice may be cited as the [Pacific Hake Exemption Notice](SOR-86-750.md).

## Exemption

**2** Pacific hake caught in those waters described in the [Pacific Fishery Management Area Regulations](SOR-82-215.md) as Areas 121, 123, 124, 125 and 126 are hereby excepted from the operation of section 29 of the [Fisheries Act](../../acts/en/F-14.md).
