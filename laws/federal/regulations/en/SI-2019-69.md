# Order Designating the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [Intelligence Commissioner Act](../../acts/en/I-14.85.md)[^fn_81000-3-1792_hq_24649], designates the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act.
