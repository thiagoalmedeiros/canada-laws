# Regulations Specifying Territories and Indicating International Registers

SOR/2017-170 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-01-01

Enabling authority: [COASTING TRADE ACT](../../acts/en/C-33.3.md)

Registration: 2017-09-01

P.C. 2017-1119 2017-08-31

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-170/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraphs 7(a)[^fn_2-197e_hq_17211] and (b)[^fn_2-197e_hq_17211] of the [Coasting Trade Act](../../acts/en/C-33.3.md)[^fn_2-197e_hq_12052], makes the annexed [Regulations Specifying Territories and Indicating International Registers](SOR-2017-170.md).

## Territories in Schedule 1

**1** The territories set out in Schedule 1 are not referred to in the definition *territory of the European Union* in subsection 2(1) of the [Coasting Trade Act](../../acts/en/C-33.3.md).

## International registers

**2** The registers set out in Schedule 2 are international registers for the purpose of paragraph 3(2.2)(c) of the [Coasting Trade Act](../../acts/en/C-33.3.md).

<sub>History: SOR/2020-239, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into force

**3**[^fn_Ind8E91_hq_20676] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 94 of the [Canada–European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement Implementation Act](../../acts/en/C-4.8.md), chapter 6 of the Statutes of Canada, 2017, comes into force but, if they are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
