# Order Repealing Order in Council P.C. 1996-324 and Designating the Minister of Industry as the Minister for the purposes of certain sections of the Act

SI/2017-56 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-06-17

Enabling authority: [TRADEMARKS ACT](../../acts/en/T-13.md)

Registration: 2017-10-18

P.C. 2017-1201 2017-09-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2017-56/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 11.11(2)[^fn_81000-3-1429-E-GC2_hq_20289] of the [Trade-marks Act](../../acts/en/T-13.md)[^fn_81000-3-1429-E-GC2_hq_20290],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 1996-324 of March 12, 1996[^fn_81000-3-1429-E-GC2_hq_20291]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of sections 11.11 to 11.24[^fn_81000-3-1429-E-GC2_hq_20292] of that Act.
