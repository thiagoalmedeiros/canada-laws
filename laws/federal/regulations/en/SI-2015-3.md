# Order Designating the Minister of Transport as the appropriate Minister for The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited for purposes of the Act

SI/2015-3 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-01-26

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2015-02-11

P.C. 2015-32 2015-01-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-3/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15110] and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in subsection 83(1) of that Act,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2000-409 of March 23, 2000[^fn_81000-3-968-E_hq_15111], that repeal taking effect on the day of the amalgamation referred to in paragraph (b);

**(b)** designates the Minister of Transport as the appropriate Minister for The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited, an amalgamated corporation resulting from the amalgamation of The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited with the St. Mary’s River Bridge Company, that designation taking effect on the day of that amalgamation and ceasing to have effect on the day before the day on which the designation in paragraph (c) takes effect; and

**(c)** designates the Minister of Transport as the appropriate Minister for The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited, an amalgamated corporation resulting from the amalgamation of The Federal Bridge Corporation Limited — an amalgamated corporation resulting from the amalgamation referred to in paragraph (b) — with the Blue Water Bridge Authority, that designation taking effect on the day of that amalgamation with the Blue Water Bridge Authority.
