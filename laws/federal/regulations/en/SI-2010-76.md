# Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Pursuant to Subsection 23(1) of the Act

SI/2010-76 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2010-09-30

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2010-10-13

P.C. 2010-1177 2010-09-30

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2010-76/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, hereby acknowledges receipt, on the making of this Order, of the assessments conducted under subsection 23(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_612991-E_hq_8959] by the Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada with respect to the species set out in the annexed schedule.

## SCHEDULE

## Extirpated Species

**Fish**

- Bass, Striped (Morone saxatilis) St. Lawrence Estuary population — Bar rayé population de l’estuaire du Saint-Laurent

## Endangered Species

**Fish**

- Chubsucker, Lake (Erimyzon sucetta) — Sucet de lac

- Shark, White (Carcharodon carcharias) Atlantic population — Grand requin blanc population de l’Atlantique

**Molluscs**

- Abalone, Northern (Haliotis kamtschatkana) — Ormeau nordique

- Physa, Lake Winnipeg (Physa sp) — Physe du lac Winnipeg

## Threatened Species

**Mammals**

- Whale, Killer (Orcinus orca) Northeast Pacific offshore population — Épaulard population océanique du Pacifique Nord-Est

**Fish**

- Bocaccio (Sebastes paucispinis) — Bocaccio

- Rockfish, Canary (Sebastes pinniger) — Sébaste canari

## Species of Special Concern

**Mammals**

- Whale, Sowerby’s Beaked (Mesoplodon bidens) — Baleine à bec de Sowerby

**Fish**

- Buffalo, Bigmouth (Ictiobus cyprinellus) Saskatchewan River and Nelson River populations — Buffalo à grande bouche populations des rivières Saskatchewan et Nelson

- Rockfish, Yelloweye (Sebastes ruberrimus) Pacific Ocean inside waters population — Sébaste aux yeux jaunes population des eaux intérieurs de l’océan Pacifique

- Rockfish, Yelloweye (Sebastes ruberrimus) Pacific Ocean outside waters population — Sébaste aux yeux jaunes population des eaux extérieures de l’océan Pacifique
