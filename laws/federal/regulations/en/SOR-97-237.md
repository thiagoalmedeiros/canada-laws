# Order Designating the Province of Quebec for the Purposes of the Definition “applicable guidelines” in Subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act

SOR/97-237 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [DIVORCE ACT](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)

Registration: 1997-04-22

P.C. 1997-644 1997-04-22

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Province of Quebec, pursuant to subsection 2(5)[^fn_SOR-97-237_e_hq_5721] of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)[^fn_SOR-97-237_e_hq_5722], has established comprehensive guidelines for the determination of child support that deal with the matters referred to in section 26.1[^fn_SOR-97-237_e_hq_5723] of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)[^fn_SOR-97-237_e_hq_5722];

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 2(5)[^fn_SOR-97-237_e_hq_5721] of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)[^fn_SOR-97-237_e_hq_5722], hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating the Province of Quebec for the Purposes of the Definition “applicable guidelines” in Subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act](SOR-97-237.md).

## Designation

**1** The Province of Quebec is hereby designated for the purposes of the definition ***applicable guidelines*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md).

## Guidelines

**2** For the purposes of subsection 2(5) of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md), the following legislative texts are the laws that constitute the comprehensive guidelines for the Province of Quebec:

- **(a)** An Act to amend the Civil Code of Québec and the Code of Civil Procedure as regards the determination of child support payments, S.Q. 1996, c. 68;

- **(b)** the Regulation respecting the determination of child support payments, made by Order 484-97 of April 9, 1997;

- **(c)** Title Three of Book Two of the Civil Code of Quebec, S.Q. 1991, c. 64; and

- **(d)** Chapter VI.1 of Title IV of Book V of the Code of Civil Procedure, R.S.Q., c. C-25.

## Coming into Force

**3** This Order comes into force on May 1, 1997.
