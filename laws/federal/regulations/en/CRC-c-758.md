# Dipropylacetic Acid (also known as 2-Propylpentanoic Acid) Remission Order

*Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties nn Dipropylacetic Acid also known as 2-Propylpentanoic Acid or its Sodium Salt*

C.R.C., c. 758 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._758/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Dipropylacetic Acid (also known as 2-Propylpentanoic Acid) Remission Order](CRC-c-758.md).

## Remission

**2** Remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) on dipropylacetic acid, also known as 2-propylpentanoic acid, or its sodium salt, whether or not with admixture, imported on or after January 1, 1976, for use in the treatment of epilepsy.
