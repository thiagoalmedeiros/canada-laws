# Critical Habitat of the Paxton Lake Benthic Threespine Stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) Order

SOR/2020-25 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2020-02-17

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2020-02-17

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-25/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Paxton Lake Benthic Threespine Stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_a];

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2)[^fn_b] of that Act;

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is of the opinion that the annexed Order would affect an area in respect of which a wildlife management board is authorized by a land claims agreement to perform functions in respect of wildlife species and, pursuant to subsection 58(8) of that Act, has consulted the wildlife management board in question with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_a], makes the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Paxton Lake Benthic Threespine Stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) Order](SOR-2020-25.md).

Ottawa, February 13, 2020

Bernadette Jordan

Minister of Fisheries and Oceans

## Application

**1** Subsection 58(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md) applies to the critical habitat of the Paxton Lake Benthic Threespine Stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
