# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities to Be the Minister for the Purposes of the Build Canada Homes Act

SI/2026-40 · Consolidated: 2026-07-21 · Last amended: 2026-07-07

Enabling authority: [BUILD CANADA HOMES ACT](../../acts/en/B-9.87.md)

Registration: 2026-07-29

P.C. 2026-696 2026-07-07

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2026-40/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the [Build Canada Homes Act](../../acts/en/B-9.87.md)[^fn_81000-3-3084_hq_1779887041], designates the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
