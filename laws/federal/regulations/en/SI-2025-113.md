# Fee to be Paid by Foreign Nationals to Participate in an International Youth Exchange Program in Canada Remission Order

SI/2025-113 · Consolidated: 2025-12-10 · Last amended: 2025-12-05

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2025-12-17

P.C. 2025-895 2025-12-05

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-113/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the collection of certain amounts is unreasonable, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Foreign Affairs, under subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_81000-2-5824_hq_1743514577] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-2-5824_hq_1743514586], remits the difference between the fee to be paid under section 2[^fn_81000-2-5824_hq_1743514649] of the [Order Prescribing the Fee to be Paid by Foreign Nationals to Participate in an International Youth Exchange Program in Canada](SOR-2000-418.md)[^fn_81000-2-5824_hq_1743514670] as adjusted in accordance with section 17[^fn_81000-2-5824_hq_1743514686] of the [Service Fees Act](../../acts/en/S-8.4.md)[^fn_81000-2-5824_hq_1743514699] and the fee actually collected during the period beginning on December 1, 2019 and ending on November 30, 2024.
