# Order Transferring from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of Employment and Social Development the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario

SI/2025-76 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-05-13

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-06-04

P.C. 2025-482 2025-05-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-76/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of Employment and Social Development the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Industry in relation to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario.
