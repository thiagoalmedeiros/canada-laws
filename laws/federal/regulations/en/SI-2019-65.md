# Order Designating the Minister of Natural Resources, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the two Acts

SI/2019-65 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-07-09

Enabling authority: [CANADIAN ENERGY REGULATOR ACT](../../acts/en/C-15.1.md)[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2019-07-24

P.C. 2019-1054 2019-07-09

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 8 of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](../../acts/en/C-15.1.md)[^fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25291] and pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[^fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25292] of the definition *appropriate Minister* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25293],

**a)** designates the Minister of Natural Resources, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](../../acts/en/C-15.1.md)[^fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25291]; and

**b)** designates the Minister of Natural Resources as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Canadian Energy Regulator for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-1861_hq_25293].
