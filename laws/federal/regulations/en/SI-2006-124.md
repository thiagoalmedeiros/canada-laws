# Valley Gospel Mission Remission Order

SI/2006-124 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-10-18

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2006-10-18

P.C. 2006-1082 2006-10-05

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-124/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[^footnotea_e] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), hereby remits to Valley Gospel Mission the amount of $1,232.60 being tax paid under Division II of Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md) and related interest in respect of the sale of religious books during the period beginning on April 1, 1997 and ending on September 30, 2001.
