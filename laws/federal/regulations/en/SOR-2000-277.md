# Deposit Insurance Application Fee By-law

SOR/2000-277 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-09-28

Enabling authority: [CANADA DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION ACT](../../acts/en/C-3.md)

Registration: 2000-06-29

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(g)[^Footnote1_e] and subsection 18(1)[^Footnote2_e] of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](../../acts/en/C-3.md), hereby makes the annexed Deposit Insurance Application Fee By-law.

June 29, 2000

## Prescribed Fee

**1** The prescribed fee that is to accompany an application for deposit insurance made by a provincial institution under subsection 17(1.1) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](../../acts/en/C-3.md) is $20,000 and is non-refundable.

<sub>History: SOR/2006-235, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** This By-law comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
