# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2021-83 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-10-26

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2021-11-10

P.C. 2021-938 2021-10-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2021-83/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29972] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2264_hq_29996], transfers from the Minister of Health to the Minister of Mental Health and Addictions and Associate Minister of Health the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Health under the [Canadian Centre on Substance Abuse Act](../../acts/en/C-13.4.md)[^fn_81000-3-2264_hq_29981].
