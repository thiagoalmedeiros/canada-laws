# Office of the Registrar General Fees for Services Order

*Order Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid by Persons for Services in Respect of the Issuance of Documents by the Office of the Registrar General of Canada*

SOR/88-344 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1988-06-27

The Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md) and Order in Council P.C. 1988-672 of April 14, 1988[^fn_SOR-88-344_e_hq_6241], hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fees to be paid by persons for services in respect of the issuance of documents by the Office of the Registrar General of Canada](SOR-88-344.md), effective July 1, 1988.

Ottawa, June 23, 1988

HARVIE ANDRE

*Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Office of the Registrar General Fees for Services Order](SOR-88-344.md).

## Fees

**2** The fees to be paid for the issuance of documents by the Office of the Registrar General of Canada are the following:

**(a)** Certificate of Authentication  …  each $ 100

**(b)** Certificate of Deposit pursuant to the Railway Act, the Canadian National Railway Act and the [Bell Canada Act](../../acts/en/B-3.6.md)

**(i)** for the first certificate  …  100

**(ii)** for each related certificate  …  35

**(c)** Copies of documents deposited under the Railway Act, the Canadian National Railway Act and the [Bell Canada Act](../../acts/en/B-3.6.md), per page  …  each 1

**(d)** Copy of any other document  …  each 10

**(e)** Certified copy of any document  …  each 35
