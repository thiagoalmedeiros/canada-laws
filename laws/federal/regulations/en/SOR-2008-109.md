# Aircraft Objects Regulations

SOR/2008-109 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2013-04-01

Enabling authority: [BANK ACT](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md)

Registration: 2008-04-10

P.C. 2008-651 2008-04-10

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-109/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 436.1[^fn_1572_hq_2710] of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md)[^fn_606849-e_hq_2720], hereby makes the annexed [Aircraft Objects Regulations](SOR-2008-109.md).

## Exemption

**1** Sections 426 to 436 of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md) do not apply to aircraft objects that were not registered under those sections before the day on which these Regulations come into force.

**2** Five years after the day on which these Regulations come into force, sections 426 to 436 of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md) cease to apply to aircraft objects that were registered under those sections before that day.

## Coming into Force

**3**[^fn_Ind11FA_hq_4381] These Regulations come into force on the day on which the Aircraft Protocol, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [International Interests in Mobile Equipment (aircraft equipment) Act](../../acts/en/I-19.6.md), chapter 3 of the Statutes of Canada, 2005, comes into force for Canada.
