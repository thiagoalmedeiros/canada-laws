# Order Designating the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to be the Minister referred to in the Act

SI/2015-62 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-06-30

Enabling authority: [PREVENTION OF TERRORIST TRAVEL ACT](../../acts/en/P-19.45.md)

Registration: 2015-07-15

P.C. 2015-1048 2015-06-30

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-62/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [Prevention of Terrorist Travel Act](../../acts/en/P-19.45.md)[^fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947], designates the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, who is a minister of the Crown, to be the Minister referred to in that Act.
