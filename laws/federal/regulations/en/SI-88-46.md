# Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/88-46 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1988-03-30

P.C. 1988-495 1988-03-17

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-88-46/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby:

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1987-1092 of May 28, 1987[^footnote1star_e]; and

**(b)** to authorize the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by persons for services in respect of registration under the [Industrial Design Act](../../acts/en/I-9.md), notwithstanding section 26 of that Act.
