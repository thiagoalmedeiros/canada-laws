# Order Transferring to the Communications Security Establishment the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Federal Public Administration in the Department of National Defence known as the Communications Security Establishment and the Communications Security Establishment Internal Services Unit

SI/2011-96 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2011-11-16

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2011-12-07

P.C. 2011-1305 2011-11-15

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2011-96/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_a] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_b], hereby transfers to the Communications Security Establishment, effective at 00:00:02 on November 16, 2011, the control and supervision of those portions of the federal public administration in the Department of National Defence known as the Communications Security Establishment and the Communications Security Establishment Internal Services Unit.
