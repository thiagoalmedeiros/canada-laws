# Regulations Adding Perfluorooctane Sulfonate and Its Salts to the Virtual Elimination List

SOR/2009-15 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2009-01-13

Enabling authority: [PERFLUOROOCTANE SULFONATE VIRTUAL ELIMINATION ACT](../../acts/en/P-8.3.md)

Registration: 2009-01-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-15/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Health, pursuant to subsection 3(1) of the [Perfluorooctane Sulfonate Virtual Elimination Act](../../acts/en/P-8.3.md)[^fn_a], hereby make the annexed [Regulations Adding Perfluorooctane Sulfonate and Its Salts to the Virtual Elimination List](SOR-2009-15.md).

Ottawa, December 7, 2008

JIM PRENTICE

*Minister of the Environment*

Ottawa, January 9, 2009

LEONA AGLUKKAQ

*Minister of Health*

## Addition

**1** Perfluorooctane sulfonate and its salts are added to column 1 of the [Virtual Elimination List](SOR-2006-298.md)[^fn_1], compiled under subsection 65(2) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](../../acts/en/C-15.31.md)[^fn_2].

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
