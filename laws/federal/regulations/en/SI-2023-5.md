# Order Dissolving the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office

SI/2023-5 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-03-09

Enabling authority: [CANADIAN SECURITIES REGULATION REGIME TRANSITION OFFICE ACT](../../acts/en/C-23.05.md)

Registration: 2023-03-29

P.C. 2023-191 2023-03-09

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-5/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, under subsection 17(1)[^fn_81000-2-4752_hq_1660592899] of the [Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office Act](../../acts/en/C-23.05.md)[^fn_81000-2-4752_hq_1660045707], dissolves the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office effective March 30, 2023.
