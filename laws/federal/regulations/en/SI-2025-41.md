# Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Official Languages) to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Official Languages Act

SI/2025-41 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-421 2025-03-14

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-41/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the powers, duties and functions under the [Official Languages Act](../../acts/en/O-3.01.md)[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666] that were transferred to the Minister of State (Official Languages) by Order in Council P.C. 2021-946 of October 26, 2021[^fn_81000-3-2853_hq_1741737078].
