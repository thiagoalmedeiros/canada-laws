# Order Designating the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to Provide Support to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience

SI/2025-74 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-05-13

Enabling authority: [SALARIES ACT](../../acts/en/S-3.md)

Registration: 2025-06-04

P.C. 2025-480 2025-05-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-74/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under subsection 5(1)[^fn_81000-3-2906_hq_1746835572] of the [Salaries Act](../../acts/en/S-3.md)[^fn_81000-3-2906_hq_1746835594], designates the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to provide support to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience in the carrying out of that Minister’s responsibilities.
