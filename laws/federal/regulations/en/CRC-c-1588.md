## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Delegation of Powers (VLA) Regulations](CRC-c-1588.md).

## General

**2** The persons named in the schedule may sign the name and affix or cause to be affixed the seal of The Director, The Veterans’ Land Act, to all documents that, under Parts I and II of the [Veterans’ Land Act](../../acts/en/V-1.5.md), require execution by the Director in his corporate capacity.
