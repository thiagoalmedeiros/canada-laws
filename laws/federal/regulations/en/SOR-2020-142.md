The Minister of Employment and Social Development, with the consent of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 8(2) of the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act](../../acts/en/C-3.7.md)[^fn_81000-2-4245_hq_27474], makes the annexed [Income Support Payment (Number of Weeks) Regulations](SOR-2020-142.md).

Gatineau, June 26, 2020

Carla Qualtrough

Minister of Employment and Social Development

## Maximum Number of Weeks

### 28 weeks

**1** For the purpose of subsection 8(1) of the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act](../../acts/en/C-3.7.md), the maximum number of weeks for which income support payments may be made to a worker is 28 weeks.
