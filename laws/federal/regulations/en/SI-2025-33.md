# Order Transferring from the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada to the Minister of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada

SI/2025-33 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-411 2025-03-14

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-33/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2‍(a)[^fn_81000-3-2868_hq_1741753247] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2868_hq_1741753277], transfers from the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada to the Minister of Industry the powers, duties and functions of the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada in relation to the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada.
