# CCRFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order

SOR/2002-400 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2009-08-01

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 49.3(1)[^footnotea_e] of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)[^footnoteb_e], hereby makes the annexed [CCRFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order](SOR-2002-400.md).

## Tariff Rate Quota

**1** The aggregate quantity limit of goods of tariff item Nos. 1701.91.90, 1701.99.90, 1702.90.11, 1702.90.12, 1702.90.13, 1702.90.14, 1702.90.15, 1702.90.16, 1702.90.17, 1702.90.18 and 1702.90.89 that are entitled to the Costa Rica Tariff, for the period set out in column 1 of the schedule, is the applicable aggregate quantity limit set out in column 2.

<sub>History: SOR/2009-228, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2**[^footnote1star_e] This Order comes into force on the day on which section 37 of the [Canada-Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act](../../acts/en/C-1.9.md), chapter 28 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes into force.
