# Order Amending Schedule I.1 to the Financial Administration Act

SOR/2019-350 · Consolidated: 2019-12-22 · Last amended: 2019-12-04

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2019-12-04

P.C. 2019-1380 2019-12-04

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-350/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 3(1.1)[^fn_612480-E_hq_7373] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_612480-E_hq_7372], amends Schedule I.1 to that Act by striking out the reference to the President of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada in column II of that Schedule opposite the name of the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat in column I of that Schedule and by substituting for that reference a reference to the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Intergovernmental Affairs.
