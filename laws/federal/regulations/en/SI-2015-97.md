# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2015-97 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-11-04

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2015-11-18

P.C. 2015-1238 2015-11-04

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-97/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16321] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16322], transfers from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs the powers, duties and functions under the [Bridge To Strengthen Trade Act](../../acts/en/B-8.05.md)[^fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16323], effective November 4, 2015.
