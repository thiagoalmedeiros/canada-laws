# CIFTA Rules of Origin Regulations

SOR/2019-276 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-09-01

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2019-07-30

P.C. 2019-1115 2019-07-26

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-276/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 16(2)[^fn_81000-2-XXXX_hq_24624] of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)[^fn_81000-2-XXXX_hq_24625], makes the annexed [CIFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-2019-276.md).

## Rules of Origin

**1** The following provisions of the Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement have the force of law in Canada:

- **(a)** Article 3.1;

- **(b)** paragraph 1 of Article 3.2;

- **(c)** Articles 3.3 to 3.10;

- **(d)** paragraph 1 of Article 3.11;

- **(e)** Articles 3.12 to 3.14; and

- **(f)** Annexes 3.4 and 3.12.

## Repeal

**2** The [CIFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-2019-276.md)[^fn_81000-2-XXXX_hq_24626] are repealed.

## Coming into Force

**3**[^fn_SOR-2019-276_e_transform_hq_25912] These Regulations come into force on the day on which the Act to amend the Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act and to make related amendments to other Acts, chapter 6 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019, comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
