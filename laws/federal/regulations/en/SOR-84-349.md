# Water Supply and Sewage Disposal Services Charges Order (Peace River-Liard Regional District)

*Order Prescribing the Fees to be Paid by the Peace River-Liard Regional District (British Columbia) for the Water Supply and Sewage Disposal Services Provided to that Regional District by and from the Fort St. John Airport*

SOR/84-349 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1984-05-01

The Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md) and Order in Council P.C. 1983-3759 of 30 November, 1983[^fn_1e], hereby makes the annexed Order prescribing the charges to be paid by the Peace River-Liard Regional District (British Columbia) for the water supply and sewage disposal services provided to that Regional District by and from the Fort St. John Airport.

Ottawa, May 1, 1984

LLOYD AXWORTHY

*Minister of Transport*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Water Supply and Sewage Disposal Services Charges Order (Peace River-Liard Regional District)](SOR-84-349.md).

## Charges

**2** The Peace River-Liard Regional District shall pay for the water supply and sewage disposal services provided by and from the Fort St. John Airport to that Regional District, in accordance with the agreement entered into by the Regional District and Her Majesty in Right of Canada pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1983-3643 of 17 November, 1983[^fn_1en] the fees set out in the schedule.
