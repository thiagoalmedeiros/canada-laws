# Access to Information Act Heads of Government Institutions Designation Order

SI/2024-61 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-12-29

Enabling authority: [ACCESS TO INFORMATION ACT](../../acts/en/A-1.md)

Registration: 2024-12-18

P.C. 2024-1276 2024-11-29

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2024-61/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, makes the annexed [Access to Information Act Heads of Government Institutions Designation Order](SI-83-113.md) under subsection 3.2(2)[^fn_81000-2-5350_hq_1702671860] of the [Access to Information Act](../../acts/en/A-1.md)[^fn_SI_83-113_EN_hq_11116].

## Designation

### Head of government institution

**1** The person holding a position set out in column 2 of the schedule is designated, for the purposes of the [Access to Information Act](../../acts/en/A-1.md), as the head of the government institution set out in column 1.

## Repeal

**2** The [Access to Information Act Heads of Government Institutions Designation Order](SI-83-113.md)[^fn_81000-2-5350_hq_1719410079] is repealed.

## Coming into Force

### 30th day after making

**3** This Order comes into force on the 30th day after the day on which it is made.
