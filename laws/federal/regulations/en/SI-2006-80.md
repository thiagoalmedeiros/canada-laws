# Order Transferring from the Canadian Firearms Centre to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Firearms Centre

SI/2006-80 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-05-31

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2006-05-31

P.C. 2006-388 2006-05-17

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-80/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Canadian Firearms Centre to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, effective May 17, 2006, the control and supervision of all of the portions of the federal public administration within the Canadian Firearms Centre.
