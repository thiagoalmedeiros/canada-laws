# N.H.A. Designated Areas Order

*Order Designating Certain Areas of the Provinces of Newfoundland, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Quebec, Ontario, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta and British Columbia and Certain Areas of the Yukon Territory and the Northwest Territories as Designated Areas*

SOR/84-686 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL HOUSING ACT](../../acts/en/N-11.md)

Registration: 1984-08-23

P.C. 1984-2917 1984-08-23

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Public Works, pursuant to section 60 and to the definition of ***designated area***[^fn_1e] in section 2 of the [National Housing Act](../../acts/en/N-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order designating certain areas of the Provinces of Newfoundland, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Quebec, Ontario, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta and British Columbia and certain areas of the Yukon Territory and the Northwest Territories as designated areas for the purposes of subsections 34.121(2), 34.161(2), 34.17(1) and 55(7) of the said Act.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [N.H.A. Designated Areas Order](SOR-84-686.md).

## Designation of Area

**2** All that portion of the Province of Newfoundland outside the boundaries of an incorporated city, metropolitan area, town or village that has a population in excess of 5,000, based on the most recent decennial census taken pursuant to the [Statistics Act](../../acts/en/S-19.md), is a designated area for the purposes of subsections 34.121(2), 34.161(2), 34.17(1) and 55(7) of the [National Housing Act](../../acts/en/N-11.md).
