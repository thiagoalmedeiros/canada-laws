# Order Designating the Minister of Foreign Affairs as the Minister for the Purposes of the Export and Import Permits Act

SI/2025-85 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-05-13

Enabling authority: [EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](../../acts/en/E-19.md)

Registration: 2025-06-04

P.C. 2025-496 2025-05-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-85/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition of *Minister* in subsection 2(1) of the [Export and Import Permits Act](../../acts/en/E-19.md)[^fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884081],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-430 of March 14, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884029]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Foreign Affairs, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Export and Import Permits Act](../../acts/en/E-19.md)[^fn_81000-3-2925_hq_1741884081].
