# Regulations Prescribing Alternative Means of Publication (Foreign Companies)

SOR/2003-31 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [INSURANCE COMPANIES ACT](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md)

Registration: 2003-01-30

P.C. 2003-61 2003-01-30

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-31/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 1015[^footnotea_e] and paragraph 1021(a)<sup>a</sup> of the [Insurance Companies Act](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md)[^footnoteb_e], hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Prescribing Alternative Means of Publication (Foreign Companies)](SOR-2003-31.md).

## Publication on the Internet

**1** The information concerning foreign companies that is required to be published quarterly in the Canada Gazette pursuant to section 585 of the [Insurance Companies Act](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md) may, instead of being published in that way, be published on the Web site of the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
