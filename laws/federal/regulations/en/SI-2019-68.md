# Order Designating the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act

SI/2019-68 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-07-12

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL SECURITY AND INTELLIGENCE REVIEW AGENCY ACT](../../acts/en/N-16.62.md)

Registration: 2019-07-24

P.C. 2019-1089 2019-07-12

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2019-68/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 55(a) of the [National Security and Intelligence Review Agency Act](../../acts/en/N-16.62.md)[^fn_81000-3-1791_hq_24648], designates the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act.
