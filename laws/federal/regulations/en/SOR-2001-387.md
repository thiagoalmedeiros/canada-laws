Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 978[^footnotea_e] of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md)[^footnoteb_e], 463[^footnotec_e] of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../../acts/en/C-41.01.md)[^footnoted_e], 1021[^footnotee_e] of the [Insurance Companies Act](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md)[^footnotef_e] and 531[^footnoteg_e] of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md)[^footnoteh_e], hereby makes the annexed [Factoring Entity Regulations](SOR-2001-387.md).

## Factoring Entity

### Definition of ***factoring entity***

**1** For the purpose of the definition ***factoring entity*** in subsections 464(1) of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md), 386(1) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../../acts/en/C-41.01.md), 490(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md) and 449(1) of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md), and in subparagraph 522.22(1)(b)(i) of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md), ***factoring entity*** means an entity the activities of which are limited to acting as a factor in respect of accounts receivable, which activities include the raising of money for the purpose of acting as a factor and the lending of money while acting as a factor.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^footnote1_e] These Regulations come into force on the day on which sections 464 and 522.22 of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md), 386 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../../acts/en/C-41.01.md), 490 of the [Insurance Companies Act](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md) and 449 of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md), as enacted by sections 127, 132, 314, 426 and 550, respectively, of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../../acts/en/F-11.1.md), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
