# Order Designating the Minister of Canadian Heritage as the Minister for the Purposes of the Canadian Multiculturalism Act

SI/2025-45 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [CANADIAN MULTICULTURALISM ACT](../../acts/en/C-18.7.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-426 2025-03-14

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-45/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the [Canadian Multiculturalism Act](../../acts/en/C-18.7.md)[^fn_81000-3-2848_hq_1741733506],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2023-771 of July 26, 2023[^fn_81000-3-2848_hq_1741733532]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Canadian Heritage, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
