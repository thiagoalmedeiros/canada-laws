# Order Transferring from the Minister of Canadian Heritage to the Minister of the Environment the Control and Supervision of the Parks Canada Agency

SI/2025-127 · Consolidated: 2025-12-10 · Last amended: 2025-12-03

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-12-17

P.C. 2025-858 2025-12-03

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-127/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Minister of Canadian Heritage to the Minister of the Environment the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration known as the Parks Canada Agency.
