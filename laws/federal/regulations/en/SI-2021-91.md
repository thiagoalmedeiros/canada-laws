# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2021-91 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-10-26

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2021-11-10

P.C. 2021-946 2021-10-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2021-91/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29970] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2263_hq_29995], transfers from the Minister of Economic Development and Official Languages to the Minister of State (Official Languages) the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Economic Development and Official Languages under the [Official Languages Act](../../acts/en/O-3.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-2263_hq_29980] that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2019-1368 of November 20, 2019[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29969].
