# Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/78-65 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1978-04-26

P.C. 1978-1008 1978-04-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-78-65/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, by order, to prescribe fees or charges to recover the costs of providing maps, navigation charts and interpretive publications required by national parks users.
