# Pasta Remission Order 1988

*Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Pasta*

SI/88-106 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1988-07-06

P.C. 1988-1203 1988-06-17

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17[^fn_SI-88-106_e_hq_6457] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duties on pasta](SI-88-106.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Pasta Remission Order 1988](SI-88-106.md).

## Remission

**2** Subject to section 4, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) on pasta containing only flour and water and imported under tariff item 1902.19.90 of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) during the period beginning January 1, 1988 and ending July 31, 1995.

<sub>History: SI/95-89, s. 1</sub>

**3** Subject to section 4, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) on pasta containing only flour and water and imported under tariff item 1902.19.91 of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) on or after August 1, 1995.

<sub>History: SI/95-89, s. 1</sub>

## Condition

**4** The remission referred to in sections 2 and 3 is granted on condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue within three years after the date of importation of the pasta.

<sub>History: SI/95-89, s. 1</sub>
