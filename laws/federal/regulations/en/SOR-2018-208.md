# Critical Habitat of the Misty Lake Lentic Threespine Stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) Order

SOR/2018-208 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-10-12

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2018-10-12

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-208/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Misty Lake Lentic Threespine Stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_a];

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2)[^fn_b] of that Act;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_a], makes the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Misty Lake Lentic Threespine Stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus) Order](SOR-2018-208.md).

Ottawa, October 11, 2018

## Application

**1** Subsection 58(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md) applies to the critical habitat of the Misty Lake Lentic Threespine Stickleback (Gasterosteus aculeatus), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
