# Order According Reciprocal Protection to Switzerland under the Act

SOR/94-27 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [INTEGRATED CIRCUIT TOPOGRAPHY ACT](../../acts/en/I-14.6.md)

Registration: 1994-01-11

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-94-27/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, pursuant to subparagraph 4(1)(c)(iii)[^fn_SOR-94-27_e_hq_5251] of the [Integrated Circuit Topography Act](../../acts/en/I-14.6.md)[^fn_SOR-94-27_e_hq_5252], hereby certifies that Switzerland is a country that confers protection that is substantially equal to the protection conferred by that Act on nationals of Canada and individuals or legal entities that have in Canada a real and effective establishment for the creation of topographies or the manufacture of integrated circuit products.

January 11, 1994

JOHN MANLEY

*Minister of Industry, Science and Technology*
