# Regulations prescribing a territory for the purposes of the definition “country” in the Customs Tariff

SOR/97-61 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 1996-12-30

P.C. 1996-2073 1996-12-30

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-97-61/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 2(3)[^fn_SOR-97-61_e_hq_5663] of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)[^fn_SOR-97-61_e_hq_5664], hereby makes the annexed Regulations prescribing a territory for the purposes of the definition ***country*** in the Customs Tariff.

## Prescription

**1** The territory where the customs laws of Israel are applied in accordance with Article III of the Protocol on Economic Relations set out in Annex V to the Israeli-Palestinian Interim Agreement on the West Bank and the Gaza Strip, dated September 28, 1995, as that Protocol is amended from time to time, is a prescribed territory for the purposes of the definition ***country*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md).

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_IndB9FF_hq_16387] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 41 of the [Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act](../../acts/en/C-6.4.md), being chapter 33 of the Statutes of Canada, 1996, comes into force.
