# Canada Post Systems Management Limited Authorization Order, 1990

*Order Authorizing Canada Post Systems Management Limited to Acquire Shares*

SOR/90-377 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1990-06-21

P.C. 1990-1176 1990-06-21

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is satisfied that Canada Post Systems Management Limited, as a corporation incorporated under the [Canada Business Corporations Act](../../acts/en/C-44.md), is empowered to undertake the transaction described in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Honourable Harvie Andre and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 91(1)(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order authorizing Canada Post Systems Management Limited to acquire shares](SOR-90-377.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Canada Post Systems Management Limited Authorization Order, 1990](SOR-90-377.md).

## Authorization

**2** Canada Post Systems Management Limited is hereby authorized to acquire shares, at an aggregate cost of $120,000, in G.D. Net B.V., a corporation incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands and having its head office located in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
