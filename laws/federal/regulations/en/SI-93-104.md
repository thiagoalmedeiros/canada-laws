# Order Designating the Secretary of State of Canada as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the National Archives, the National Film Board, the National Library, the National Gallery, the National Museum of Science and Technology, the Canadian Museum of Civilization, and the Canadian Museum of Nature

SI/93-104 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-11-04

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1993-07-14

P.C. 1993-1450 1993-06-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-104/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

**(a)** pursuant to subsection 3(1.1) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), to amend Schedule I.1 of that Act by striking out the reference to the Minister of Communications in column II thereof opposite the names of the National Archives of Canada, the National Film Board and the National Library in Column I thereof and by substituting therefor a reference to the Secretary of State of Canada in column II thereof opposite those names.

**(b)** \[Repealed, SI/2015-112\]
