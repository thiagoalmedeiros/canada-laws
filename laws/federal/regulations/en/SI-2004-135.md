# Order Transferring to the Department of Citizenship and Immigration the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions within the Canada Border Services Agency and Transferring from the Deputy Minister and Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration Certain Powers, Duties and Functions

SI/2004-135 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2004-10-20

P.C. 2004-1154 2004-10-08

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2004-135/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), hereby

**(a)** transfers to the Department of Citizenship and Immigration the control and supervision of the portions of the public service within the Canada Border Services Agency that carry out pre-removal risk assessments and that were transferred pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 2003-2063 of December 12, 2003[^footnotea]; and

**(b)** transfers from the Solicitor General of Canada, styled Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration the powers, duties and functions provided for in subsection 77(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](../../acts/en/I-2.5.md) that were transferred pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 2003-2063 of December 12, 2003[^footnotea].
