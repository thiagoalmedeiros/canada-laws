# Defence Maps and Charts Transfer Order

*Order Authorizing the Gratuitous Transfer of Defence Maps, Charts and Other Materials to Other Governments*

C.R.C., c. 688 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._688/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Defence Maps and Charts Transfer Order](CRC-c-688.md).

## Authorization

**2** The Minister of National Defence is hereby authorized, when he considers that the public interest would thereby be served, to transfer gratuitously to other governments maps, charts, map and chart reproduction materials, and related publications and documents, the value of which shall not exceed $75,000 annually.
