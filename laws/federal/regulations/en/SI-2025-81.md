# Order Transferring from the Minister for International Trade to the Minister of State (U.S. Trade) and the Minister of Foreign Affairs the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Subsection 53(2) of the Customs Tariff

SI/2025-81 · Consolidated: 2025-09-01 · Last amended: 2025-05-13

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-06-04

P.C. 2025-492 2025-05-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-81/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Minister for International Trade the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of International Trade under subsection 53‍(2)[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1746831656] of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1746831634],

**(a)** to the Minister of State (U.S. Trade), specifically in relation to Canada-U.S. trade; and

**(b)** to the Minister of Foreign Affairs, in relation to all other trade.
