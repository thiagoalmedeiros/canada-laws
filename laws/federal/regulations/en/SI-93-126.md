# Order Amalgamating and Combining the Privy Council Office with the Federal-Provincial Relations Office under the Prime Minister

SI/93-126 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 1993-07-14

P.C. 1993-1493 1993-06-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-126/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby, pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), to amalgamate and combine the Privy Council Office with the Federal-Provincial Relations Office under the Prime Minister and under the Clerk of the Privy Council and Secretary to the Cabinet.
