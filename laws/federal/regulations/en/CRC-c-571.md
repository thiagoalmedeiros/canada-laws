*Regulations Prescribing Fees for Licences Issued Under the Excise Act*

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Excise Act Licence Fees Regulations](CRC-c-571.md).

## Fees

**2** Every person to whom a licence is granted under the [Excise Act](../../acts/en/E-14.md) for a purpose set out in Column I of an item of the schedule shall pay the annual fee set out in Column II of that item.
