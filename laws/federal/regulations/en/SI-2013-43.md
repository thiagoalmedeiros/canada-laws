# Order Transferring to Shared Services Canada the control and supervision of the portion of the Federal Public Administration in the Acquisitions Branch of the Department of Public Works and Government Services known as the End User Devices Procurement Unit

SI/2013-43 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2013-04-01

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2013-04-24

P.C. 2013-366 2013-03-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2013-43/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092], transfers to Shared Services Canada the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration in the Acquisitions Branch of the Department of Public Works and Government Services known as the End User Devices Procurement Unit, effective April 1, 2013.
