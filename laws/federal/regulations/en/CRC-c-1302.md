# Minister Designation Order (Precious Metals Marking Act)

*Order Designating the Minister for the Purposes of the Precious Metals Marking Act*

C.R.C., c. 1302 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PRECIOUS METALS MARKING ACT](../../acts/en/P-19.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Minister Designation Order (Precious Metals Marking Act)](CRC-c-1302.md).

## Designation

**2** The Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, is hereby designated as the Minister for the purposes of the [Precious Metals Marking Act](../../acts/en/P-19.md).
