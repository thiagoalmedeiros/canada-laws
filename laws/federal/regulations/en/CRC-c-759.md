# Domestic Wine Spirits Remission Order

*Order Respecting the Remission of Excise Duty on Spirits for the Treatment of Domestic Wine*

C.R.C., c. 759 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._759/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Domestic Wine Spirits Remission Order](CRC-c-759.md).

## Remission

**2** Remission of the excise duty set out in subsection 1(1) of Part I of the schedule to the [Excise Act](../../acts/en/E-14.md) imposed and levied pursuant to section 187 of that Act is hereby granted on the spirits used for the treatment of domestic wine after September 18, 1975, in any bonded manufactory.
