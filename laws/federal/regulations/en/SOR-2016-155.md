# Expiry of Sections 10 to 12 and 13 to 15 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations

SOR/2016-155 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-06-17

Enabling authority: [SOFTWOOD LUMBER PRODUCTS EXPORT CHARGE ACT, 2006](../../acts/en/S-12.55.md)

Registration: 2016-06-17

P.C. 2016-572 2016-06-17

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-155/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 101(a) and section 102 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006](../../acts/en/S-12.55.md)[^fn_a], makes the annexed [Expiry of Sections 10 to 12 and 13 to 15 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations](SOR-2016-155.md).

## Ceasing to be in Force

**1** Sections 10 to 12 and 13 to 15 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006](../../acts/en/S-12.55.md) cease to be in force on October 12, 2015.

## Coming into force

**2** These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on October 13, 2015.
