# Designating the Civil Aviation Tribunal as a Department; the Minister of Transport as Appropriate Minister for Purposes of the Financial Administration Act and as Minister for Purposes of Section 31 of the Aeronautics Act

SI/86-64 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[AERONAUTICS ACT](../../acts/en/A-2.md)

Registration: 1986-05-14

P.C. 1986-1068 1986-05-01

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-86-64/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition of ***department*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), to designate the Civil Aviation Tribunal as a department for the purposes of that Act;

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition of ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), to designate the Minister of Transport as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Civil Aviation Tribunal for the purposes of that Act; and

**(c)** pursuant to section 31[^footnote1star_e] of the [Aeronautics Act](../../acts/en/A-2.md), to designate the Minister of Transport, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that section.
