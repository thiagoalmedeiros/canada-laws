Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 253.1[^fn_81000-2-3151-E_hq_21943] of the [Criminal Code](../../acts/en/C-46.md)[^fn_81000-2-3151-E_hq_21944], makes the annexed [Blood Drug Concentration Regulations](SOR-2018-148.md).

## Blood Alcohol Concentration and Blood Drug Concentration

### Summary offence

**1** For the purpose of subsection 320.14(4) of the [Criminal Code](../../acts/en/C-46.md), the prescribed blood drug concentration for tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) is 2 ng of THC per mL of blood.

<sub>History: SOR/2018-149, s. 1</sub>

**2** For the purpose of paragraph 320.14(1)(c) of the [Criminal Code](../../acts/en/C-46.md), the prescribed blood drug concentration for each drug set out in column 1 of the table to this section is set out in column 2.

### Hybrid offence — combination of drugs and alcohol

**3** For the purpose of paragraph 320.14(1)(d) of the [Criminal Code](../../acts/en/C-46.md), the prescribed blood alcohol concentration is 50 mg of alcohol per 100 mL of blood and the prescribed blood drug concentration for tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) is 2.5 ng of THC per mL of blood.

<sub>History: SOR/2018-149, s. 3</sub>
