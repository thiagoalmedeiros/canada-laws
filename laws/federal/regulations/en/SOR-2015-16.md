# Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1[^fn_a], makes the annexed Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1.

## General

### Day for purposes of section 144

**1** The day for the purposes of section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1[^fn_1] is fixed as June 1, 2019.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^fn_Ind3BE3_hq_15215] This Order comes into force on the first day on which sections 111, 139 and 140 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1](../../acts/en/E-0.8.md)[^fn_Ind3BE3_hq_15217] are all in force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
