# Order Transferring to the Department of External Affairs the Control and Supervision of the Investment Development Division in Investment Canada

SI/93-146 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 1993-07-14

P.C. 1993-1492 1993-06-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-146/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), is pleased hereby to transfer to the Department of External Affairs the control and supervision of that portion of the public service in Investment Canada known as the Investment Development Division.
