# Fees in Respect of Mail Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing Fees in Respect of Goods Imported as Mail and the Circumstances in Which the Goods Are not Charged with the Prescribed Fee*

Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 164(4)(a.2)[^footnote1_e] of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)[^footnote2_e], the making of [Regulations prescribing fees in respect of goods imported as mail and the circumstances in which the goods are not charged with the prescribed fee](SOR-92-414.md) that are set out in the schedule hereto give effect in whole or in part to a public announcement made on June 23, 1992;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 147.1(14)[^footnote3_f] of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)[^footnote2_e], is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing fees in respect of goods imported as mail and the circumstances in which the goods are not charged with the prescribed fee](SOR-92-414.md), effective July 1, 1992.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Fees in Respect of Mail Regulations](SOR-92-414.md).

## Definition

<sub>History: SOR/2014-272, s. 1</sub>

**2** In these Regulations, ***commercial goods*** means goods imported into Canada for sale or for any commercial, industrial, occupational, institutional or other similar use.

<sub>History: SOR/2014-272, s. 2</sub>

## Fees

**3** Subject to section 4, the fee charged for goods imported as mail is $5 for each item of mail.

## Exceptions

**4** The fee referred to in section 3 does not apply to goods imported as mail where all the goods in each item of mail are

- **(a)** not charged with duties;

- **(b)** goods in respect of which all duties are remitted;

- **(c)** commercial goods that have an estimated value for duty exceeding $3,300; or

- **(d)** sent by an Express Mail Service (EMS) to Canada from a country that is a member of the Universal Postal Union.

<sub>History: SOR/96-154, s. 1; SOR/2014-272, s. 3; SOR/2024-199, s. 2</sub>
