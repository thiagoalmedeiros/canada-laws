# Order Transferring from the Minister of National Revenue to the Minister of Transport the Control and Supervision of the Royal Canadian Mint

SI/2006-27 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2006-02-22

P.C. 2006-66 2006-02-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-27/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Minister of National Revenue to the Minister of Transport

**(a)** the control and supervision of the Royal Canadian Mint, and

**(b)** the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of National Revenue under the [Royal Canadian Mint Act](../../acts/en/R-9.md),

effective February 6, 2006.
