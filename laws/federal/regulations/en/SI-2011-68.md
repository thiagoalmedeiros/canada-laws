# Order Designating the President of Shared Services Canada as Deputy Head in Respect of that Entity

SI/2011-68 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2011-08-04

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2011-08-17

P.C. 2011-880 2011-08-03

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2011-68/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[^fn_a] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_b], hereby designates the President of Shared Services Canada as deputy head in respect of that entity, effective August 4, 2011.
