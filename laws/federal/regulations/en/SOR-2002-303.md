Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to subsection 74(3) of the [Indian Act](../../acts/en/I-5.md), hereby makes the annexed [Dakota Tipi Band Council Method of Election Regulations](SOR-2002-303.md).

## Method of Election

**1** The chief and the councillors of the Dakota Tipi band shall be elected by a majority of the votes of the electors of the band.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
