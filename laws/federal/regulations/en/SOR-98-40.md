# Order Designating Certain Countries and Military Service Agencies for the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00

SOR/98-40 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 1997-12-29

P.C. 1997-2011 1997-12-29

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-40/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 132(1)(g) of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)[^fn_ae], hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating Certain Countries and Military Service Agencies for the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00](SOR-98-40.md).

**1** The following countries are designated for the purposes of tariff item No. 9810.00.00 of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md):

- Australia

- Belgium

- Denmark

- France

- Germany

- Ghana

- Greece

- Iceland

- India

- Italy

- Jamaica

- Luxembourg

- Netherlands

- New Zealand

- Norway

- Pakistan

- Portugal

- Sri Lanka

- Trinidad and Tobago

- Turkey

- United Kingdom

- United States of America

**2** The following military service agencies are designated for the purposes of tariff item No. 9810.00.00 of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md):

- **(a)** German Air Force Tactical Training Command, Goose Bay, Labrador, Newfoundland

- **(b)** Post Exchanges - AC & W Sites authorized by the Minister of National Revenue

- **(c)** Royal Air Force Detachment, Goose Bay, Labrador, Newfoundland

- **(d)** United States Military Services Agencies at Goose Bay Airport, Labrador, Newfoundland

- **(e)** United States Property Accountability Office, Headquarters ADC Detachment No. 1, Ottawa, Ontario

## Coming into Force

**3** This Order comes into force on January 1, 1998.
