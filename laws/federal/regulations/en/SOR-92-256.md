# Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing the Maximum Fee that a Company May Charge for a Security Certificate Issued in Respect of a Transfer*

SOR/92-256 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md)

Registration: 1992-05-07

P.C. 1992-905 1992-05-07

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 88(2) and section 531 of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md)[^fn_1e], is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the maximum fee that a company may charge for a security certificate issued in respect of a transfer](SOR-92-256.md), effective June 1, 1992.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations](SOR-92-256.md).

## Maximum Fee

**2** A company may charge a fee not exceeding five dollars for a security certificate issued in respect of a transfer.
