# Pensions and Allowances Annual Adjustment Regulations

SOR/2026-226 · Consolidated: 2026-10-29 · Last amended: 2026-10-23

Enabling authority: [PENSION ACT](../../acts/en/P-6.md)

Registration: 2026-10-23

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Veterans Affairs, makes the annexed [Pensions and Allowances Annual Adjustment Regulations](SOR-2026-226.md) under subsections 75(1)[^fn_81000-2-6187_hq_1781105171] and (3)[^fn_81000-2-6187_hq_1778595273] and sections 91.1[^fn_81000-2-6187_hq_1778595291] and 91.3[^fn_81000-2-6187_hq_1778595291] of the [Pension Act](../../acts/en/P-6.md)[^fn_81000-2-6187_hq_1778595311].

## Definition of *Act*

**1** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the [Pension Act](../../acts/en/P-6.md).

## Rounding of amounts

**2** An amount determined under paragraph 75(1)(a) or (b) or subsection 75(3) of the Act must be rounded to the nearest cent or, if the amount is equidistant between two cents, to the higher of them.

## Calculation — paragraph 75(1)(a) of Act

**3** For the purpose of paragraph 75(1)(a) of the Act,

- **(a)** *[not in force]* each of the Consumer Price Indexes referred to in subparagraph 75(1)(a)(ii) of the Act must be rounded to one decimal place, with the digit at the first decimal place being increased by one if the digit at the second decimal place is 5 or more; and

- **(b)** *[not in force]* the ratio between the Consumer Price Indexes must be expressed as a decimal number and rounded to three decimal places, with the digit at the third decimal place being increased by one if the digit at the fourth decimal place is 5 or more.

## Calculation — paragraph 75(1)(b) of Act

**4** For the purpose of paragraph 75(1)(b) of the Act,

- **(a)** *[not in force]* the average annual gross composite wage of the categories of unskilled members of the federal public administration designated by the Minister is equal to the simple arithmetic mean — rounded to the nearest cent or, if it is equidistant between two cents, to the higher of them — of all those categories’ average annual gross wages, each of which is determined in accordance with paragraph (b);

- **(b)** *[not in force]* each category’s average annual gross wage is equal to

  - **(i)** *[not in force]* in the case of a category whose members’ pay is determined on the basis of an annual rate of pay, the simple arithmetic mean — rounded to the nearest cent or, if it is equidistant between two cents, to the higher of them — of the annual rates of pay for all the steps in that category’s pay scale, and

  - **(ii)** *[not in force]* in the case of a category whose members’ pay is determined on the basis of an hourly rate of pay, the product of 2080 and the simple arithmetic mean — rounded to the nearest cent or, if it is equidistant between two cents, to the higher of them — of the hourly rates of pay for all the steps in that category’s pay scale; and

- **(c)** *[not in force]* the income tax for a single person calculated in the province with the lowest combined provincial and federal income tax rate is the income tax for the most recent taxation year ending before the annual adjustment is made and is determined by the formula

  > A × (B − C) + (D × (B − E))

  where

  - **A** is the income tax rate set out in paragraph 117(2)(a) of the [Income Tax Act](../../acts/en/I-3.3.md) for that taxation year,

  - **B** is the average annual gross composite wage determined under paragraph (a),

  - **C** is the *basic personal amount*, as defined in subsection 118(1.1) of the [Income Tax Act](../../acts/en/I-3.3.md) and adjusted in accordance with section 117.1 of that Act, for that taxation year,

  - **D** is the lowest of the provincial personal income tax rates for that taxation year, and

  - **E** is the basic personal amount or equivalent for that taxation year for the province whose personal income tax rate is used for the purpose of D or, if multiple provinces share that rate, the highest basic personal amount or equivalent among those provinces.

## Calculation — subsection 75(3) of Act

**5** For the purpose of subsection 75(3) of the Act, the percentage by which the basic pension is adjusted is to be rounded to four decimal places, with the digit at the fourth decimal place being increased by one if the digit at the fifth decimal place is 5 or more.

**6** The [Pension and Allowance Adjustment Regulations](SOR-91-620.md)[^fn_81000-2-6187_hq_1781538040] are repealed.

## Coming into force

**7** These Regulations come into force, or are deemed to have come into force, on January 1, 2027.
