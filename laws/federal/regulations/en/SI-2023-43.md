# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the Minister for the Purposes of the National Housing Strategy Act

SI/2023-43 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-07-26

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL HOUSING STRATEGY ACT](../../acts/en/N-11.2.md)

Registration: 2023-08-16

P.C. 2023-773 2023-07-26

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-43/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the [National Housing Strategy Act](../../acts/en/N-11.2.md)[^fn_81000-3-25XX_hq_1689942661],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2021-948 of October 26, 2021[^fn_81000-3-25XX_hq_1689942674]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
