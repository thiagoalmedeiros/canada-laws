# Order Authorizing the Minister of Finance to Exercise the Powers of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans Referred to in the Act in Respect of National Sea Products Limited

SOR/89-187 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [ATLANTIC FISHERIES RESTRUCTURING ACT](../../acts/en/A-14.md)

Registration: 1989-03-23

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-89-187/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 7 of the [Atlantic Fisheries Restructuring Act](../../acts/en/A-14.md), is pleased hereby:

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1988-734 of April 21, 1988; and

**(b)** to authorize the Minister of Finance to exercise the powers of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans referred to in that Act in respect of National Sea Products Limited.
