# Order to Remit Part I License Fees Paid or Payable by all Broadcasting Licensees

SI/2020-37 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2020-05-18

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2020-06-10

P.C. 2020-338 2020-05-18

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2020-37/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister of Canadian Heritage and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27206] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27207], remits the Part I licence fees paid or payable by all licensees under paragraph 3(a) of the [Broadcasting Licence Fee Regulations, 1997](SOR-97-144.md)[^fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27210], in respect of the fiscal year 2020–2021.
