# Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development the Control and Supervision of the Aboriginal Affairs Secretariat

SI/2006-51 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2006-03-22

P.C. 2006-119 2006-03-03

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-51/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration within the Plans and Consultation Group in the Privy Council Office known as the Aboriginal Affairs Secretariat, effective April 1, 2006.
