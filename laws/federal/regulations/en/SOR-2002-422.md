# Order Specifying the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for the Purposes of the Employment Equity Act

SOR/2002-422 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [EMPLOYMENT EQUITY ACT](../../acts/en/E-5.401.md)

Registration: 2002-11-21

P.C. 2002-1958 2002-11-21

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity Act](../../acts/en/E-5.401.md)[^footnote1_e], the Treasury Board has consulted the Solicitor General of Canada concerning the application of that Act to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity Act](../../acts/en/E-5.401.md)[^footnote1_e], hereby makes the annexed Order Specifying the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for the Purposes of the Employment Equity Act.

## Designation

**1** The Royal Canadian Mounted Police is specified under paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity Act](../../acts/en/E-5.401.md) as a portion of the public sector employing one hundred or more employees to which that Act applies.

## Coming into Force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
