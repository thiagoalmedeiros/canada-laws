# Customs Accounting Document Error Remission Order

*Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties Imposed under the Customs Tariff and Taxes Imposed under Division III of Part IX and under any other Part of The Excise Tax Act, Underpaid Due to Error on Customs Accounting Document*

C.R.C., c. 754 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Customs Accounting Document Error Remission Order](CRC-c-754.md).

<sub>History: SI/88-18, s. 2</sub>

## Remission

**2** Remission is hereby granted of the amount by which the customs duties imposed under the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) and the taxes imposed under Division III of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md) on goods imported into Canada were underpaid in error, if the amount payable does not amount to more than $7.50 in respect of any one customs accounting document, other than an interim document.

<sub>History: SI/88-18, s. 2; SI/91-8, s. 2</sub>
