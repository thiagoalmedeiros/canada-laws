# Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development To Be the Minister for the Purposes of the Canada Early Learning and Child Care Act

SI/2025-42 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [CANADA EARLY LEARNING AND CHILD CARE ACT](../../acts/en/C-3.55.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-422 2025-03-14

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-42/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the [Canada Early Learning and Child Care Act](../../acts/en/C-3.55.md)[^nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731620],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2024-345 of April 12, 2024[^nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731646]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Employment and Social Development, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
