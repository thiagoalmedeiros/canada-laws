# Order Transferring from the Minister of Mental Health and Addictions and Associate Minister of Health to the Minister of Health the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Centre on Substance Abuse Act

SI/2025-37 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-417 2025-03-14

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-37/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers to the Minister of Health the powers, duties and functions under the [Canadian Centre on Substance Abuse Act](../../acts/en/C-13.4.md)[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666] that were transferred to the Minister of Mental Health and Addictions and Associate Minister of Health by Order in Council P.C. 2021-938 of October 26, 2021[^fn_81000-3-2853_hq_1741737078].
