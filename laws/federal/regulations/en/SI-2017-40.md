# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs, to be the Minister for the purposes of the Act

SI/2017-40 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-07-04

Enabling authority: [CANADA INFRASTRUCTURE BANK ACT](../../acts/en/C-6.18.md)

Registration: 2017-07-26

P.C. 2017-1007 2017-07-04

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2017-40/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [Canada Infrastructure Bank Act](../../acts/en/C-6.18.md)[^fn_81000-3-XXXX-E_hq_20245], designates the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
