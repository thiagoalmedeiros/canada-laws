# Maximum Amounts for Informal Procedure Regulations

*Regulations Increasing the Maximum Amounts that Apply in Respect of Appeals under the Informal Procedure*

SOR/93-295 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 18.27[^fn_SOR-93-295_e_hq_6257] of the [Tax Court of Canada Act](../../acts/en/T-2.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations increasing the maximum amounts that apply in respect of appeals under the informal procedure](SOR-93-295.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Maximum Amounts for Informal Procedure Regulations](SOR-93-295.md).

## Interpretation

**2** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the [Tax Court of Canada Act](../../acts/en/T-2.md). (*Loi*)

## Maximum Amounts

**3** The amount of $7,000 referred to in paragraph 18(1)(a), section 18.1, subsections 18.11(2) and (3) and sections 18.12 and 18.13 of the Act is increased to $12,000.

**4** The amount of $14,000 referred to in paragraph 18(1)(b) and sections 18.1, 18.12 and 18.13 of the Act is increased to $24,000.

## Application

**5** Sections 3 and 4 apply in respect of appeals instituted on or after September 1, 1993.
