# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the Minister for the Purposes of the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation Act

SI/2023-42 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-07-26

Enabling authority: [CANADA MORTGAGE AND HOUSING CORPORATION ACT](../../acts/en/C-7.md)

Registration: 2023-08-16

P.C. 2023-772 2023-07-26

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-42/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the [Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation Act](../../acts/en/C-7.md)[^fn_81000-3-25XX_hq_1689942661],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2021-947 of October 26, 2021[^fn_81000-3-25XX_hq_1689942674]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
