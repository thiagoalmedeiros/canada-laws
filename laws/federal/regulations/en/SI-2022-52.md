# Order Designating the Minister of Industry as the Minister for the Purposes of the Civil Lunar Gateway Agreement Implementation Act

SI/2022-52 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-10-07

Enabling authority: [CIVIL LUNAR GATEWAY AGREEMENT IMPLEMENTATION ACT](../../acts/en/C-31.4.md)

Registration: 2022-10-26

P.C. 2022-1090 2022-10-07

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2022-52/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 5 of the [Civil Lunar Gateway Agreement Implementation Act](../../acts/en/C-31.4.md)[^fn_81000-2-4922-E_hq_1660845335], designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act, effective October 26, 2022.
