# Broadcast Technical Data Services Fees Order

*Order Prescribing the Fees to be Paid by the Private Sector for the Provision of Broadcast Technical Data Services*

SOR/82-281 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1982-03-05

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-82-281/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of Communications, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1981-252 of 29 January, 1981[^fn_SOR-82-281_e_hq_7750], hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fees to be paid by the private sector for the provision of Broadcast Technical Data Services](SOR-82-281.md), effective April 1, 1982.

Dated at Ottawa, this 3rd day of March, 1982

FRANCIS FOX

*Minister of Communications*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Broadcast Technical Data Services Fees Order](SOR-82-281.md).

## Payment of Fees

**2** Every person who applies to the Minister of Communications for the provision of a service set out in Column I of an item of the schedule shall pay to the Minister prior to the provision of that service,

- **(a)** where that service involves an initial subscription, the fee set out in Column II of that item;

- **(b)** where that service involves a renewal or update, the fee set out in Column III of that item; or

- **(c)** where that service involves a special request, the fee set out in Column IV of that item.

<sub>History: SOR/85-461, s. 1</sub>

**3** \[Revoked, SOR/85-461, s. 2\]
