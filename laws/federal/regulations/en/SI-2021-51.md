# Order Designating the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario as a Department and the President as Deputy Head of that Agency for the Purposes of that Act

SI/2021-51 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-08-12

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2021-08-18

P.C. 2021-844 2021-08-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2021-51/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, effective August 12, 2021,

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition “department” in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^fn_608630-E_hq_133], designates the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition “deputy head” in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^fn_608630-E_hq_133], designates the President of the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario as the deputy head of that Agency for the purposes of that Act.
