# Teslin Tlingit Council (GST) Remission Order

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[^footnotea_e] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), hereby makes the annexed [Teslin Tlingit Council (GST) Remission Order](SI-2000-104.md).

## Interpretation

**1** The definitions in this section apply in this Order.

***agreement*** means the Teslin Tlingit Council Self-Government Agreement signed on May 29, 1993, as it read on November 2, 2000. (***accord***)

***person*** has the same meaning as in subsection 123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md). (***personne***)

## Remission

**2** Remission is hereby granted to a person, to the extent and in the manner that a refund of tax paid by the person is provided for in sections 15.7 to 15.11 of the agreement, of tax under Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md) that was paid by the person during the period beginning on October 1, 1997 and ending on October 31, 2000, on condition that no refund of that tax is payable under section 18.1 of the [Yukon First Nations Self-Government Act](../../acts/en/Y-2.6.md).

## Coming into Force

**3** This Order comes into force on November 2, 2000.
