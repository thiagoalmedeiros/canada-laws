# Order Designating the Commission that is Named the Joint Public Inquiry into the Nova Scotia April 2020 Tragedy as a Department for the Purposes of that Act

SI/2020-70 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2020-10-21

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2020-11-11

P.C. 2020-823 2020-10-21

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2020-70/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister,

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b)[^fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27687] of the definition *department* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688], designates the commission that is named the Joint Public Inquiry into the Nova Scotia April 2020 Tragedy as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b)[^fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27689] of the definition *appropriate Minister* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)<sup>b</sup>, designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the commission named in paragraph (a).
