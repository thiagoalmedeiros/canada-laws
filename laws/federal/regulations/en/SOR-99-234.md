His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 132(1)(g) of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby makes the annexed Regulations Designating Certain Countries for the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00, 1999-1.
