# Order Designating the President of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada as the appropriate Minister for the purposes of the Act

SI/2016-40 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-06-30

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2016-07-13

P.C. 2016-656 2016-06-30

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2016-40/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[^fn_4360_hq_18542] of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_4360_hq_18543],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2013-303[^fn_4360_hq_18544] of March 19, 2013; and

**(b)** designates the President of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety Board for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_4360_hq_18543].
