# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2019-123 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-11-20

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2019-12-11

P.C. 2019-1361 2019-11-20

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2019-123/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26069] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26070], transfers from the Minister of Science to the Minister of Industry the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Science under Part I of the [Budget Implementation Act, 1997](../../acts/en/B-9.81.md)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26092] that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2018-1013 of July 18, 2018[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26093].
