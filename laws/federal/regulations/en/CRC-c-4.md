# Air Cushion Vehicle Regulations

*Regulations Respecting Air Cushion Vehicles*

C.R.C., c. 4 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [AERONAUTICS ACT](../../acts/en/A-2.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Air Cushion Vehicle Regulations](CRC-c-4.md).

## Interpretation

**2** In these Regulations, ***air cushion vehicle*** means a machine designed to derive support in the atmosphere primarily from reactions against the earth’s surface of air expelled from the machine.

## Exclusion

**3** Commercial air services provided by means of air cushion vehicles are excluded from the operation of Part II of the [Aeronautics Act](../../acts/en/A-2.md).
