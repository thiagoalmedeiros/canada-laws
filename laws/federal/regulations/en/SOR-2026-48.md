# Order Extending the Application of the Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations

SOR/2026-48 · Consolidated: 2026-03-17 · Last amended: 2026-03-13

Enabling authority: [FREEZING ASSETS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT](../../acts/en/F-31.6.md)

Registration: 2026-03-13

P.C. 2026-212 2026-03-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2026-48/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, under section 6 of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act](../../acts/en/F-31.6.md)[^fn_81000-2-6014_hq_1762807008], extends the application of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations](SOR-2011-78.md)[^fn_81000-2-6014_hq_1762807036] for a period of five years beginning on March 24, 2026.
