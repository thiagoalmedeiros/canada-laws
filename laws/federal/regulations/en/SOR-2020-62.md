## Income Support Payment

### Amount of income support payment

**1** For the purpose of subsection 7(1) of the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act](../../acts/en/C-3.7.md), the amount of an income support payment for any week is $500.

## Coming into Force

### Registration

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
