# Order Designating the Minister of Justice as the Minister for the Purposes of that Act

SI/2021-37 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-06-24

Enabling authority: [UNITED NATIONS DECLARATION ON THE RIGHTS OF INDIGENOUS PEOPLES ACT](../../acts/en/U-2.2.md)

Registration: 2021-07-07

P.C. 2021-635 2021-06-24

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2021-37/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act](../../acts/en/U-2.2.md)[^fn_320_hq_10667], designates the Minister of Justice, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
