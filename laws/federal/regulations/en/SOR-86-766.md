# Delegation of Powers (Customs) Order

*Order Respecting the Delegation of Powers Conferred on the Minister of National Revenue by Sections 131 to 133 of the Customs Act*

SOR/86-766 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS ACT](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)

Registration: 1986-07-15

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-86-766/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section 134 of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)[^footnote1_e], hereby makes the annexed [Order respecting the delegation of powers conferred on the Minister of National Revenue by sections 131 to 133 of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)](SOR-86-766.md), effective on the day that the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)[^footnote1_e], other than paragraph 99(1)(b), subsections 99(2) to (4) and sections 192 to 194 thereof, comes into force.

Ottawa, July 14, 1986

E. M. MacKAY

*Minister of National Revenue*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Delegation of Powers (Customs) Order](SOR-86-766.md).

## General

**2** The officers named in the schedule may exercise the powers conferred on the Minister of National Revenue by sections 131 to 133 of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md).
