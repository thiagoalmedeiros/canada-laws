# Social Insurance Number Disclosure Regulations

*Regulations Respecting the Disclosure of Social Insurance Numbers for the Purpose of Registration under Section 240 of the Excise Tax Act*

SOR/91-41 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [EXCISE TAX ACT](../../acts/en/E-15.md)

Registration: 1990-12-18

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 277(1)(d)[^fn_SOR-91-41_e_hq_6150] of the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the disclosure of Social Insurance Numbers for the purpose of registration under section 240 of the Excise Tax Act](SOR-91-41.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Social Insurance Number Disclosure Regulations](SOR-91-41.md).

## Disclosure Requirement

**2** Every individual who applies to the Minister to be registered pursuant to section 240 of the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md) in the form prescribed by the Minister for that purpose shall disclose therein the individual’s Social Insurance Number.
