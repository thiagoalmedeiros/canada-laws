## Board of Directors

### Number of directors

**1** For the purposes of subsection 13(2) of the [College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents Act](../../acts/en/C-33.7.md), the number of directors is fixed at nine.

### Directors to be appointed

**2** The number of directors that are to be appointed to the Board under subsection 13(3) of the [College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents Act](../../acts/en/C-33.7.md) is fixed at five.

## Coming into Force

### S.C. 2018, c. 27, s. 247

**3**[^fn_SOR-2021-166_e_hq_29407] This Order comes into force on the day on which section 13 of the [College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents Act](../../acts/en/C-33.7.md) comes into force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
