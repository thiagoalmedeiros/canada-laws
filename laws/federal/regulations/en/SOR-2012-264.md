# Regulations for the Redemption of One Cent Coins

SOR/2012-264 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2012-12-07

Enabling authority: [CURRENCY ACT](../../acts/en/C-52.md)

Registration: 2012-12-07

P.C. 2012-1622 2012-12-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-264/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 9.01(1)[^fn_a] of the [Currency Act](../../acts/en/C-52.md)[^fn_b], makes the annexed [Regulations for the Redemption of One Cent Coins](SOR-2012-264.md).

## Authorization

**1** The Minister is authorized to redeem one cent coins that are or that have at any time been current in Canada.

## Coming into force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day of their registration.
