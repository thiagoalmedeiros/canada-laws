# Communications Security Establishment Regulations

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-320-e_hq_10884], hereby makes the annexed [Communications Security Establishment Regulations](SOR-2011-255.md).

## Block Transfer

**1** Subsection 132(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md) applies to the Communications Security Establishment.

## Cession of Effect

**2** These Regulations cease to have effect at 00:00:03 on November 16, 2011.

## Coming into Force

**3** These Regulations come into force at 00:00:01 on November 16, 2011.
