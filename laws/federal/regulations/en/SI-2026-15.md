# Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development to be the Minister for the Purposes of the Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act

SI/2026-15 · Consolidated: 2026-04-28 · Last amended: 2026-04-17

Enabling authority: [CANADIAN SUSTAINABLE JOBS ACT](../../acts/en/C-23.25.md)

Registration: 2026-05-06

P.C. 2026-336 2026-04-17

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2026-15/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the [Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act](../../acts/en/C-23.25.md)[^fn_81000-3-3047_hq_1771968195], effective May 1, 2026,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2024-851 of June 27, 2024[^fn_81000-3-3047_hq_1771968166]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Employment and Social Development, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of the [Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act](../../acts/en/C-23.25.md)[^fn_81000-3-3047_hq_1771968195].
