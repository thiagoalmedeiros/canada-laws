# Government of Jamaica Remission Order

SI/2001-93 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2001-09-12

P.C. 2001-1519 2001-08-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2001-93/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_SI-2001-93_e_hq_6425] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), hereby remits to the Government of Jamaica the amount of $0.2 million, representing accrued interest owing to the Government of Canada on a loan made to the Government of Jamaica.
