# Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness) to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Section 7 of the Emergency Management Act

SI/2025-38 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT ACT](../../acts/en/E-4.56.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-418 2025-03-14

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness) to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the powers, duties and functions under section 7 of the [Emergency Management Act](../../acts/en/E-4.56.md)[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666] that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2024-1183 of November 8, 2024[^fn_81000-3-2844_hq_1741802881].
