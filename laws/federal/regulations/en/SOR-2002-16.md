# Radiocommunication Act (Paragraph 9(1)(c)) Exemption Order (National Defence and Security)

SOR/2002-16 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [RADIOCOMMUNICATION ACT](../../acts/en/R-2.md)

Registration: 2001-12-13

P.C. 2001-2351 2001-12-13

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-16/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 3(2)[^fn_SOR-2002-16_e_hq_5829] of the [Radiocommunication Act](../../acts/en/R-2.md)[^fn_SOR-2002-16_e_hq_5830], hereby makes the annexed [Radiocommunication Act (Paragraph 9(1)(c)) Exemption Order (National Defence and Security)](SOR-2002-16.md).

## Exemption

**1** Subject to section 2, Her Majesty in right of Canada is exempt from the application of paragraph 9(1)(c) of the [Radiocommunication Act](../../acts/en/R-2.md).

## Conditions

**2** The exemption under section 1 applies only if the encrypted subscription programming signal or encrypted network feed

- **(a)** is decoded for the purposes of national defence or security; and

- **(b)** is not available from a lawful distributor.

## Coming into Force

**3** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
