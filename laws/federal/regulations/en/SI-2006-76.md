# Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the Control and Supervision of the Regional Offices in the Communications and Consultations Secretariat

SI/2006-76 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-05-31

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2006-05-31

P.C. 2006-363 2006-05-10

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-76/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the control and supervision of those portions of the federal public administration within the Privy Council Office known as the Regional Offices in the Communications and Consultations Secretariat, effective July 1, 2006.
