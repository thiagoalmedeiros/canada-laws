# Privacy Act Extension Order, No. 3

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 12(3) of the [Privacy Act](../../acts/en/P-21.md)[^fn_81000-2-4561_hq_29053], makes the annexed [Privacy Act Extension Order, No. 3](SOR-2021-174.md).

## Extension of right of access

**1** The right to be given access to personal information under subsection 12(1) of the [Privacy Act](../../acts/en/P-21.md) is extended to include all individuals outside Canada to whom that right has not been extended previously.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the first anniversary of the day on which it is made.
