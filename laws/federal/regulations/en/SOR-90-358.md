# Canada Post Systems Management Limited Incorporation Authorization Order

*Order Authorizing the Canada Post Corporation to Procure the Incorporation of Canada Post Systems Management Limited*

SOR/90-358 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1990-06-15

P.C. 1990-1170 1990-06-15

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is satisfied that the Canada Post Corporation is empowered under section 16 of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](../../acts/en/C-10.md) to undertake the transaction described in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Honourable Harvie Andre and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 91(1)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order authorizing the Canada Post Corporation to procure the incorporation of Canada Post Systems Management Limited](SOR-90-358.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Canada Post Systems Management Limited Incorporation Authorization Order](SOR-90-358.md).

## Authorized Transaction

**2** The Canada Post Corporation is hereby authorized to procure the incorporation, under the [Canada Business Corporations Act](../../acts/en/C-44.md), of a corporation named Canada Post Systems Management Limited the shares of which will be, on incorporation, held by the Canada Post Corporation.
