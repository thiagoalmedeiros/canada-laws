# NAFTA Prescribed Class of Goods Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing a Class of Goods for the Purposes of Subsection 35.02(4) of the Customs Act*

SOR/93-608 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS ACT](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)

Registration: 1993-12-15

P.C. 1993-2109 1993-12-15

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-93-608/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 35.02(4)[^fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6274] and paragraph 164(1)(i)[^fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6275] of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md)[^fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6276], is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing a class of goods for the purposes of subsection 35.02(4) of the Customs Act](SOR-93-608.md), effective on the day on which section 83 of [An Act to implement the North American Free Trade Agreement](../../acts/en/N-23.8.md), being chapter 44 of the Statutes of Canada, 1993, comes into force.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [NAFTA Prescribed Class of Goods Regulations](SOR-93-608.md).

## Class of Goods

**2** The goods in respect of which any regulations made under paragraph 19(1)(a) of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) apply are hereby prescribed as a class of goods for the purposes of subsection 35.02(4) of the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md).
