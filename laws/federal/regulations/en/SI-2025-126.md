# Certain Fees Under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations Remission Order

SI/2025-126 · Consolidated: 2025-12-10 · Last amended: 2025-12-05

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2025-12-17

P.C. 2025-908 2025-12-05

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-126/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the collection of certain amounts is unreasonable, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_81000-2-5825_hq_1743514851] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-2-5825_hq_1743514862], remits the excess from the adjustment, by operation of section 17[^fn_81000-2-5825_hq_1746801845] of the [Service Fees Act](../../acts/en/S-8.4.md)[^fn_81000-2-5825_hq_1746801805], of any fees payable under subsections 298(1) and 306(1) and sections 309 and 310 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations](SOR-2002-227.md)[^fn_81000-2-5825_hq_1743515544] during the period beginning on December 1, 2019 and ending on November 30, 2023.
