# Foreign Institutions Subject to the Canadian Residency Requirements Regulations (Trust and Loan Companies)

SOR/2003-186 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 531[^fn_SOR-2003-186_e_hq_5780] of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md)[^fn_SOR-2003-186_e_hq_5781], hereby makes the annexed [Foreign Institutions Subject to the Canadian Residency Requirements Regulations (Trust and Loan Companies)](SOR-2003-186.md).

## Prescribed Holding Body Corporate

**1** For the purpose of subsection 163(2) of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md), a holding body corporate of a foreign institution is prescribed if

- **(a)** the holding body corporate is incorporated or formed otherwise than by or under an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province; and

- **(b)** the holding body corporate, and entities it controls are, when viewed as a whole, engaged primarily in the business of providing financial services.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
