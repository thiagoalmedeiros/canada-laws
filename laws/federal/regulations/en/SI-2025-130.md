# Adjustment of Certain Costs Paid or Payable Under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations (April 1, 2019 to October 31, 2024) Remission Order

SI/2025-130 · Consolidated: 2025-12-29 · Last amended: 2025-12-18

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2025-12-31

P.C. 2025-977 2025-12-18

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-130/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the collection of the amounts is unreasonable, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, under subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_15952_hq_1702910130] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_15952_hq_1702910221], remits the amounts of the annual adjustments — made under subsection 17(1) of the [Service Fees Act](../../acts/en/S-8.4.md)[^fn_15952_hq_1702912375] — to the costs paid or payable under section 243[^fn_81000-2-5964_hq_1759762446] of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations](SOR-2002-227.md)[^fn_81000-2-5964_hq_1757435941], as it read before April 1, 2025, for the period beginning on April 1, 2019 and ending on October 31, 2024.
