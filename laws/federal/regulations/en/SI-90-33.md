# Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Act

SI/90-33 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [AGRICULTURAL AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT ACT (ARDA)](../../acts/en/A-3.md)

Registration: 1990-03-14

P.C. 1990-392 1990-02-23

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-90-33/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural and Rural Development Act (ARDA)](../../acts/en/A-3.md), is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1984-2290 of June 28, 1984[^footnote1_e]; and

**(b)** to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
