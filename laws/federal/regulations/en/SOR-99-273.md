# Exchange Rate (Authorized Foreign Banks) Regulations

SOR/99-273 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [BANK ACT](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md)

P.C. 1999-1159 1999-06-23

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsections 545(2)[^footnotea_e] and (6)[^footnotea_e] of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md)[^footnoteb_e], hereby makes the annexed [Exchange Rate (Authorized Foreign Banks) Regulations](SOR-99-273.md).

## Rate of Exchange

**1** For the purposes of subsection 545(2) of the [Bank Act](../../acts/en/B-1.01.md), the rate of exchange that shall be applied on any day in determining the amount in Canadian dollars of a deposit with an authorized foreign bank in a currency of a country other than Canada is the latest exchange rate at which the authorized foreign bank offers, before the day of the determination, to buy that currency with Canadian dollars.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on June 28, 1999.
