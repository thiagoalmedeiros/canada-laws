# Order Designating the Minister of Public Works and Government Services as the Minister for the purposes of that Act

SI/2019-116 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-11-20

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL CAPITAL ACT](../../acts/en/N-4.md)

Registration: 2019-12-11

P.C. 2019-1354 2019-11-20

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2019-116/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the [National Capital Act](../../acts/en/N-4.md)[^fn_81000-3-1916_hq_26085],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2015-1252 of November 4, 2015[^fn_81000-3-1926_hq_26086]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
