# National Energy Board Order No. MO-62-69

*Order Respecting the Application of Part IV of the National Energy Board Act*

C.R.C., c. 1055 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL ENERGY BOARD ACT](../../acts/en/N-7.md)

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [National Energy Board Order No. MO-62-69](CRC-c-1055.md).

## Application of part IV of the act

**2** The National Energy Board, pursuant to subsection 97(1)[^footnote1_e] of the [National Energy Board Act](../../acts/en/N-7.md), hereby orders that Part IV of the said Act applies to every person who was operating a pipeline on November 2, 1959.
