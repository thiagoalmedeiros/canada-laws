# Order Transferring to the Public Health Agency of Canada the Control and Supervision of the Portion of the Federal Public Administration in the Department of Health known as the Travelling Public Program Unit

SI/2013-40 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2013-04-01

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2013-04-10

P.C. 2013-341 2013-03-27

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2013-40/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092], transfers from the Department of Health to the Public Health Agency of Canada the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration in the Department of Health known as the Travelling Public Program Unit, effective April 1, 2013.
