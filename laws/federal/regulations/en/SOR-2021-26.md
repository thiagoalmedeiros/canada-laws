# Order Extending the Application of the Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations

SOR/2021-26 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-02-26

Enabling authority: [FREEZING ASSETS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT](../../acts/en/F-31.6.md)

Registration: 2021-02-26

P.C. 2021-94 2021-02-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-26/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section 6 of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act](../../acts/en/F-31.6.md)[^fn_81000-2-4420_hq_28358], extends the application of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations](SOR-2011-78.md)[^fn_81000-2-4420_hq_28359] for a period of five years beginning on March 24, 2021.
