# Regulations Maintaining Certain Reciprocal Transfer Agreements

SOR/2000-376 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Registration: 2000-10-02

T.B. 828458 2000-09-28

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 40.3(3)[^fn_SOR-2000-376_e_hq_5181] of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](../../acts/en/P-36.md) and paragraph 7(2)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Maintaining Certain Reciprocal Transfer Agreements](SOR-2000-376.md).

**1** The Reciprocal Transfer Agreement between Her Majesty in right of Canada and the employer set out in column 1 of an item of the Schedule, made on the date set out in column 2 of that item, is excluded from the application of subsection 40.3(2) of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](../../acts/en/P-36.md).

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
