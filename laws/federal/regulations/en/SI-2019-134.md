# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2019-134 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-12-04

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the President of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada to the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Intergovernmental Affairs the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration known as the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat.
