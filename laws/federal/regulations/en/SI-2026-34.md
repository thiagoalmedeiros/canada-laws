# Order Designating the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada as the Minister Responsible for the Invest in Canada Act

SI/2026-34 · Consolidated: 2026-07-21 · Last amended: 2026-06-25

Enabling authority: [INVEST IN CANADA ACT](../../acts/en/I-21.5.md)

Registration: 2026-07-15

P.C. 2026-676 2026-06-25

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2026-34/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the [Invest in Canada Act](../../acts/en/I-21.5.md)[^fn_81000-3-3069_hq_1776177561],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2018-49 of January 31, 2018[^fn_81000-3-3069_hq_1776184341]; and

**(b)** designates the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purpose of the [Invest in Canada Act](../../acts/en/I-21.5.md)[^fn_81000-3-3069_hq_1776177561].
