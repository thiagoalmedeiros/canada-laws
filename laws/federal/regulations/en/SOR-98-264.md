# General Export Permit No. 37 — Toxic Chemicals and Precursors to the United States

SOR/98-264 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2007-09-18

Enabling authority: [EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](../../acts/en/E-19.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to subsection 7(1.1)[^fna_e] of the [Export and Import Permits Act](../../acts/en/E-19.md), hereby issues the annexed [General Export Permit No. 37 — Toxic Chemicals and Precursors to the United States](SOR-98-264.md).

Ottawa, April 23, 1998

LLOYD AXWORTHY

*Minister of Foreign Affairs*

## Interpretation

**1** The definitions in this section apply in this Permit.

***Export Controls Division*** means the Export Controls Division of the Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade. (***Direction des contrôles à l’exportation***)

***Guide*** has the same meaning as in section 1 of the [Export Control List](SOR-89-202.md). (***Guide***)

***toxic chemicals and precursors*** means the goods referred to in Group 7 of the schedule to the [Export Control List](SOR-89-202.md) that are described in any of items 7-3.3 to 7-3.6 or item 7-4 of the Guide. (***produits chimiques toxiques et précurseurs***)

<sub>History: SOR/2007-210, s. 1</sub>

## General

**2** Any resident of Canada may, under the authority of and in accordance with this Permit, export toxic chemicals and precursors from Canada to the United States.

## Conditions

**3** It is a condition of this Permit that the exporter

- **(a)** keep at the exporter’s place of business or residence the documents in respect of each export made under this Permit for a period of six years after the date of the export;

- **(b)** on request, make the documents referred to in paragraph (a) available to an officer of the Export Controls Division; and

- **(c)** where any toxic chemicals and precursors are required to be reported in the prescribed form under the [Customs Act](../../acts/en/C-52.6.md), insert the statement “GEP-37” or “LGE-37” in the appropriate field of the prescribed form.

## Coming into Force

**4** This Permit comes into force on April 23, 1998.
