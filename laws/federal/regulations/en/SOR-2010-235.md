# Exemption for Public Notices or Documents (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations

SOR/2010-235 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2014-11-21

Enabling authority: [TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md)

Registration: 2010-10-28

P.C. 2010-1332 2010-10-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-235/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 531(1)[^fn_1a] and section 539.14[^fn_1b] of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md)[^fn_1c], hereby makes the annexed [Exemption for Public Notices or Documents (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations](SOR-2010-235.md).

## Interpretation

**1** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../../acts/en/T-19.8.md).

## Exemption Circumstances

**2** For the purpose of section 539.14 of the Act, the prescribed circumstances are that the exemption does not prejudice any of the shareholders or the public interest.

## Coming into Force

**3** These Regulations come into force on June 1, 2011.
