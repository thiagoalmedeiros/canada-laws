# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2020-53 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2020-06-29

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2020-07-22

P.C. 2020-525 2020-06-29

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2020-53/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Department of Public Works and Government Services to the Privy Council Office the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration in the Department of Public Works and Government Services known as the Ministers’ Regional Office Program.
