# National Parks Town, Visitor Centre and Resort Subdivision Designation Regulations

*Regulations Respecting the Designation of Towns, Visitor Centres and Resort Subdivisions in National Parks*

SOR/91-8 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](../../acts/en/N-14.01.md)

Registration: 1990-12-13

P.C. 1990-2680 1990-12-13

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-91-8/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection 7(1)[^fn_SOR-91-8_e_hq_5224] of the National Parks Act, is pleased hereby to revoke the National Parks Townsite and Subdivision Designation Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1133, and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the designation of towns, visitor centres and resort subdivisions in national parks](SOR-91-8.md), in substitution therefor.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [National Parks Town, Visitor Centre and Resort Subdivision Designation Regulations](SOR-91-8.md).

## Surveyed Areas

**2** The surveyed areas shown on the plans of survey listed in the schedule are designated as towns, visitor centres or resort subdivisions as set out in the schedule.
