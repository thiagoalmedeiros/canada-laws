# Order Transferring from the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, to the Minister of State to assist the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development under certain Acts

SI/2018-107 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-11-21

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2018-12-12

P.C. 2018-1421 2018-11-21

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2018-107/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23447] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23448], transfers from the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, to the Minister of State to assist the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development under the following Acts:

**(a)** the [Arctic Waters Pollution Prevention Act](../../acts/en/A-12.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23449];

**(b)** the [Canada Lands Surveys Act](../../acts/en/L-6.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23481];

**(c)** the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations Act](../../acts/en/O-7.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23482];

**(d)** the [Territorial Lands Act](../../acts/en/T-7.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23483];

**(e)** the [Dominion Water Power Act](../../acts/en/W-4.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23484];

**(f)** the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](../../acts/en/C-8.5.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23485];

**(g)** the [Gwich’in Land Claim Settlement Act](../../acts/en/G-11.8.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23486];

**(h)** the [Nunavut Act](../../acts/en/N-28.6.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23487];

**(i)** the [Nunavut Land Claims Agreement Act](../../acts/en/N-28.7.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23488];

**(j)** the [Sahtu Dene and Metis Land Claim Settlement Act](../../acts/en/S-1.5.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23489];

**(k)** the [Yukon First Nations Land Claims Settlement Act](../../acts/en/Y-2.3.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23491];

**(l)** the [Yukon Surface Rights Board Act](../../acts/en/Y-4.3.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23492];

**(m)** the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act](../../acts/en/M-0.2.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23461];

**(n)** the [Yukon Act](../../acts/en/Y-2.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23494];

**(o)** the [Nunavut Waters and Nunavut Surface Rights Tribunal Act](../../acts/en/N-28.8.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23495];

**(p)** the [Tlicho Land Claims and Self-Government Act](../../acts/en/T-11.3.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23496];

**(q)** the [Nunavut Planning and Project Assessment Act](../../acts/en/N-28.75.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23497];

**(r)** the [Northwest Territories Act](../../acts/en/N-27.05.md)[^fn_81000-3-1741_hq_23498].
