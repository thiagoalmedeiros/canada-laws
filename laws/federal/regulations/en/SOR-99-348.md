# Regulations Prescribing Networks (Copyright Act)

SOR/99-348 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [COPYRIGHT ACT](../../acts/en/C-42.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to section 30.8[^fn_SOR-99-348_e_hq_5620] and subsection 62(1)[^fn_SOR-99-348_e_hq_5621] of the [Copyright Act](../../acts/en/C-42.md), hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Prescribing Networks (Copyright Act)](SOR-99-348.md).

## Network

**1** For the purpose of subsection 30.8(9) of the [Copyright Act](../../acts/en/C-42.md), the following are prescribed networks:

- **(a)** networks that are networks within the meaning of the definition ***network*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Broadcasting Act](../../acts/en/B-9.01.md); and

- **(b)** networks that consist of two or more programming undertakings that are owned by the same person or group of persons and that have a practice of common programming or an arrangement by which they share programs, as the case may be.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on October 1, 1999.
