# Order Transferring from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the Department of Transport of Certain Portions of the Directorate-General of Maritime Programs

SI/2003-233 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2003-12-31

P.C. 2003-2090 2003-12-12

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2003-233/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the Department of Transport the control and supervision of those portions of the Directorate-General of Marine Programs that are responsible for policy related to pleasure craft safety, marine navigation services, pollution prevention and response, and navigable waters, effective December 12, 2003.
