# Listed Airport Regulations

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 2 and 84 of the [Air Travellers Security Charge Act](../../acts/en/A-10.5.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby makes the annexed [Listed Airport Regulations](SOR-2004-293.md).

## Interpretation

**1** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the [Air Travellers Security Charge Act](../../acts/en/A-10.5.md).

## Prescribed Airports

**2** The following airports are prescribed airports for the purposes of the definition ***listed airport*** in section 2 of the Act:

- **(a)** Red Deer Regional; and

- **(b)** Rivière-Rouge (Mont-Tremblant International).

## Coming into Force

**3** These Regulations come into force on December 23, 2004.
