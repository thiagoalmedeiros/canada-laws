# Order Designating the Minister for International Trade as Minister for Purposes of Sections 1 to 9 and Parts I and III of the Act

SI/94-8 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [NORTH AMERICAN FREE TRADE AGREEMENT IMPLEMENTATION ACT](../../acts/en/N-23.8.md)

Registration: 1994-01-26

P.C. 1994-5 1994-01-12

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-94-8/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 11 of the [North American Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act](../../acts/en/N-23.8.md)[^fn_SI-94-8_e_hq_6558], is pleased hereby to designate the Minister for International Trade, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of sections 1 to 9 and Parts I and III of that Act, effective January 1, 1994.
