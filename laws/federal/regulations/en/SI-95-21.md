# Income Tax Remission Order (Canada Pension Plan)

SI/95-21 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1995-02-22

P.C. 1995-201 1995-02-07

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-95-21/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the collection of the tax is unjust, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[^fn_SI-95-21_e_hq_6602] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to remit the net total of the taxes payable under Parts I and I.1 of the [Income Tax Act](../../acts/en/I-3.3.md) for the 1987 to 1995 taxation years that, without regard to sections 122.2 and 122.4 to 122.64 thereof as they read at any time, would not be payable by a taxpayer if the part of any amount received by the taxpayer after 1987 and before 1996 by reason of section 63.1 of the [Canada Pension Plan](../../acts/en/C-8.md) that was payable for a month in a year preceding the year in which it was received had been received in that preceding year, and all relevant penalties and interest.
