# Telecommunications and Electronic Maintenance Services Fees Order

*Order Respecting Fees for Provision of Telecommunications and Electronic Maintenance Services Provided by the Department of Transport*

SOR/81-319 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1981-04-09

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-81-319/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of Transport, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1981-928 of 2 April, 1981[^fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6238], hereby makes the annexed Order prescribing fees or charges to be paid for the services provided in accordance with any agreement entered into pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1981-928 of 2 April, 1981[^fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6238], in accordance with schedule hereto.

Dated in Ottawa, April 8, 1981

J. L. PEPIN

*Minister of Transport*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Telecommunications and Electronic Maintenance Services Fees Order](SOR-81-319.md).

## Fees

**2** Fees for telecommunications and electronic maintenance services provided by the Department of Transport as a result of any agreement entered into pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1981-928 of 2 April, 1981[^fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6240] shall be charged in accordance with the schedule.

## SCHEDULE

*(Section 2)*

The following fees for services shall be charged:

| Item | Service | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Labour Rate | Salary rate set out in effective agreement between the Treasury Board and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (I.B.E.W.) Local 2228. |
| 2 | Isolated Post and any other appropriate allowances | As set out in agreement referred to in item 1. |
| 3 | Cost of employee benefit plans | In accordance with effective rates set by Treasury Board. |
| 4 | Vehicle operation | $0.23 per mile. |
| 5 | Parts | Cost plus any applicable shipping charges. |
| 6 | Overhead Rate | 65% to be applied to fees charged pursuant to items 1 to 5. |
