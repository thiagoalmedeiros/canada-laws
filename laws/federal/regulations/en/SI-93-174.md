# Order Designating the Minister of Finance as Minister for Purposes of the Act

SI/93-174 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PETRO-CANADA PUBLIC PARTICIPATION ACT](../../acts/en/P-11.1.md)

Registration: 1993-09-08

P.C. 1993-1674 1993-08-18

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-174/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Petro-Canada Public Participation Act](../../acts/en/P-11.1.md)[^fn_SI-93-174_e_hq_6523], is pleased hereby to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1991-414 of March 1, 1991[^fn_SI-93-174_e_hq_6524] and to designate the Minister of Finance, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
