# Critical Habitat of the Black Redhorse (Moxostoma duquesnei) Order

SOR/2022-235 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-11-08

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2022-11-08

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-235/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Black Redhorse (Moxostoma duquesnei) is a wildlife species that is listed as a threatened species in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954185];

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2)[^fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954411] of that Act;

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is of the opinion that the annexed Order would affect a reserve or other lands that are set apart for the use and benefit of a band and, under subsection 58(7)[^fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954639] of that Act, has consulted with the Minister of Indigenous Services and the band in question with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Black Redhorse (Moxostoma duquesnei) Order](SOR-2022-235.md) under subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954185].

Ottawa, November 4, 2022

## Application

**1** Subsection 58(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md) applies to the critical habitat of the Black Redhorse (Moxostoma duquesnei), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
