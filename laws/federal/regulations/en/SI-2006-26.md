# Order Designating the Minister of Transport as Appropriate Minister for the Queens Quay West Land Corporation for Purposes of the Act

SI/2006-26 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2006-02-22

P.C. 2006-65 2006-02-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-26/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), and pursuant to subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in subsection 83(1) of that Act, hereby

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2004-871 of July 20, 2004[^footnotea_e], and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Transport, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister for the Queens Quay West Land Corporation for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md),

effective February 6, 2006.
