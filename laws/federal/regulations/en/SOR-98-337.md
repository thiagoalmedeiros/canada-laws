The Senate, pursuant to section 59 of the [Parliament of Canada Act](../../acts/en/P-1.md), hereby makes the annexed Senate Sessional Allowance (Deductions for Non-attendance) Regulations.

June 9, 1998

## General

**1** The deduction to be made from the sessional allowance of a senator under subsection 57(1) of the [Parliament of Canada Act](../../acts/en/P-1.md) is increased to $250 per day for every sitting day beyond twenty-one on which the senator does not attend a sitting of the Senate.

<sub>History: SOR/2002-23, s. 1; err.(F), Vol. 136, No. 2</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on July 1, 1998.
