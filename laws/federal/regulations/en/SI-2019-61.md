# Order Designating the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister referred to in that Act

SI/2019-61 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-07-09

Enabling authority: [COLLEGE OF IMMIGRATION AND CITIZENSHIP CONSULTANTS ACT](../../acts/en/C-33.6.md)

Registration: 2019-07-24

P.C. 2019-1049 2019-07-09

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2019-61/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants Act](../../acts/en/C-33.6.md)[^fn_81000-3-1811_hq_24851], designates the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister referred to in that Act.
