# Order Designating the Minister of Supply and Services as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Queens Quay West Land Corporation

SI/93-112 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1993-07-14

P.C. 1993-1458 1993-06-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-112/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition of ***appropriate Minister*** in subsection 83(1) of that Act, is pleased hereby to designate the Minister of Supply and Services, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Queens Quay West Land Corporation for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md).
