# Order Designating the Minister of Communications as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the National Battlefields Commission

SI/93-227 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1993-12-15

P.C. 1993-1979 1993-12-02

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-227/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[^fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6539] of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1452 of June 25, 1993[^fn_SI-93-227_e_hq_6540]; and

**(b)** to designate the Minister of Communications, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister with respect to the National Battlefields Commission for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md).
