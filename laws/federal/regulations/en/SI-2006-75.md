# Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the Control and Supervision of the Regulatory Affairs and Orders in Council Secretariat Except Orders in Council Division

SI/2006-75 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-05-31

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2006-05-31

P.C. 2006-362 2006-05-10

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-75/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration within the Privy Council Office known as the Regulatory Affairs and Orders in Council Secretariat, with the exception of the Orders in Council Division, effective July 1, 2006.
