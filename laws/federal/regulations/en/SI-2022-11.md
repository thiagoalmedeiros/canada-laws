# Remission Order for Certain Fees Under the Bankruptcy and Insolvency General Rules from March 31 to August 31, 2020

SI/2022-11 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-02-10

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2022-03-02

P.C. 2022-103 2022-02-10

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2022-11/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30170] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30171], remits the excess from the adjustment of any fees referred to in paragraphs (a) to (c), by operation of section 17 of the [Service Fees Act](../../acts/en/S-8.4.md)[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30206]:

**(a)** the levies paid under subsection 123(3)[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30280] of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency General Rules](CRC-c-368.md)[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30281] during the period beginning on March 31, 2020 and ending on August 31, 2020;

**(b)** the filing fees paid under subsection 132(1)[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30282], of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency General Rules](CRC-c-368.md)[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30281] during the period beginning on March 31, 2020 and ending on August 31, 2020; and

**(c)** the fee set out in section 136[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30284] of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency General Rules](CRC-c-368.md)[^fn_81000-2-4705_hq_30281] that accompanied notices sent to the Superintendant during the period beginning on March 31, 2020 and ending on August 31, 2020.
