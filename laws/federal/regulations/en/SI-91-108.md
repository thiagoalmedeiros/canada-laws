# Order Giving Notice that a Tax Convention Between Canada and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg Came into Force on July 8, 1991

SI/91-108 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: CANADA-LUXEMBOURG INCOME TAX CONVENTION ACT, 1989[AGREEMENTS AND CONVENTIONS](../../acts/en/Z-02.md)

Registration: 1991-08-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-91-108/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Notice is hereby given, pursuant to section 6 of the Canada-Luxembourg Income Tax Convention Act, 1989[^fnstar_e], that the Convention between Canada and the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg for the Avoidance of Double Taxation and the Prevention of Fiscal Evasion with respect to Taxes on Income and on Capital came into force on July 8, 1991.

Ottawa, August 2, 1991

DON MAZANKOWSKI

*Minister of Finance*
