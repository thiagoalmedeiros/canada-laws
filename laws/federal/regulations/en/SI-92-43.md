# Order Placing Members of the Canadian Forces on Active Service (Cambodia)

SI/92-43 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](../../acts/en/N-5.md)

Registration: 1992-03-11

P.C. 1992-355 1992-02-27

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-92-43/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, by Resolution 717 of October 16, 1991, established a United Nations Advance Mission in Cambodia to assist in the initial implementation of the Agreements on a Comprehensive Political Settlement of the Cambodia Conflict, signed in Paris on October 23, 1991;

And Whereas it appears advisable that Canada participate in that Mission;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to paragraph 31(1)(b)[^fn_SI-92-43_e_hq_6498] of the [National Defence Act](../../acts/en/N-5.md), is pleased hereby to place on active service beyond Canada those

**(a)** officers and non-commissioned members of the regular force of the Canadian Forces, and

**(b)** officers and non-commissioned members of the reserve force of the Canadian Forces

who are part of, or serve in immediate support of, the United Nations Advance Mission in Cambodia.
