# Order Transferring from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of State (Small Business and Tourism) the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Tourism Commission Act

SI/2025-82 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-05-13

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-06-04

P.C. 2025-493 2025-05-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-82/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2‍(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers to the Minister of State (Small Business and Tourism) the powers, duties and functions under the [Canadian Tourism Commission Act](../../acts/en/C-23.3.md)[^fn_81000-3-2908_hq_1746825003] that were transferred to the Minister of Industry by Order in Council P.C. 2025-420 of March 14, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2908_hq_1746842331].
