# Certain Commercial Salmon Fishing Licences Fees Remission Order

SI/99-140 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1999-12-22

P.C. 1999-2192 1999-12-10

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-99-140/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_SI-99-140_e_hq_6454] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), considering that it is in the public interest to do so, hereby remits to the holders of the commercial salmon fishing licences set out below the fees that they paid for those licences for 1999 under section 19 of the [Pacific Fishery Regulations, 1993](SOR-93-54.md):

**(a)** a Category A licence referred to in subitem 3(1) or (2) of Part I of Schedule II to the [Pacific Fishery Regulations, 1993](SOR-93-54.md) that permits salmon fishing by trolling in Salmon Areas F, G or H, as those areas are defined in subsection 2(1) of those Regulations;

**(b)** a Category A licence referred to in those subitems that is subject to conditions specified under paragraph 22(1)(c) of the [Fishery (General) Regulations](SOR-93-53.md) that permit salmon fishing by seine in Salmon Area B (consisting of Areas 11 to 29 and Area 121, as described in Schedule II to the [Pacific Fishery Management Area Regulations](SOR-82-215.md)) or by gillnet in Salmon Area D (consisting of Areas 11 to 15 and Areas 23 to 27, as described in that schedule) or Salmon Area E (consisting of Areas 16 to 22, 28, 29 and 121, as described in that schedule).
