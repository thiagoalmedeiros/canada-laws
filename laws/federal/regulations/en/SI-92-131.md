# Fingerprinting, Palmprinting and Photography Order

SI/92-131 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [IDENTIFICATION OF CRIMINALS ACT](../../acts/en/I-1.md)

Registration: 1992-07-29

P.C. 1992-1354 1992-06-18

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-92-131/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice and pursuant to paragraph 2(1)(b) of the [Identification of Criminals Act](../../acts/en/I-1.md), is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1954-1109 of July 22, 1954[^fn_SI-92-131_e_hq_6502]; and

**(b)** for the purposes of the [Identification of Criminals Act](../../acts/en/I-1.md), to sanction the measurements, processes and operations of fingerprinting, palmprinting and photography.
