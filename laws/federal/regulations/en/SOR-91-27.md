# Equivalent Courses (GST/HST) Regulations

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[^fn_SOR-91-27_e_hq_6136] of the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing equivalents of courses that follow a curriculum designated by a school authority.

**1** \[Repealed, SOR/2002-277, s. 5\]

## Prescribed Courses

**2** For the purpose of section 9 of Part III of Schedule V to the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md), music lessons are prescribed equivalents of music courses that follow a curriculum designated by a school authority.
