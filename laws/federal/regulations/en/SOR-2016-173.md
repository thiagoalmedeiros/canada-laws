His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](../../acts/en/C-1.68.md)[^fn_81200-2-TEXTILE], makes the annexed [Face Protectors for Ice Hockey and Box Lacrosse Players Regulations](SOR-2016-173.md).

## Requirement

### Standard

**1** Face protectors for ice hockey and box lacrosse players must meet the requirements of standard CAN3-Z262.2-M78, entitled Face Protectors for Ice Hockey and Box Lacrosse Players, published by the Canadian Standards Association in English in December 1978 and in French in June 1979.
