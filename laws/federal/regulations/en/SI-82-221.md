# Designation of Place of Inspection and Sale

SI/82-221 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS ACT](../../acts/en/S-22.md)

Registration: 1982-11-10

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-82-221/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

I, P. M. Pitfield, Clerk of the Privy Council, pursuant to paragraphs 24(a) and 25(a) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](../../acts/en/S-22.md), do hereby revoke the [Designation of Place of Inspection and Sale](SI-82-221.md), SI/80-179 of 12 November, 1980[^fn_SI-82-221_e_hq_11257] and designate, effective November 1, 1982, Room 418 in the Blackburn Building, 85 Sparks Street, Ottawa, as the place at which any statutory instrument that has been registered pursuant to section 6 or 32 of the said Act and that is not precluded from inspection or the obtaining of copies thereof by any regulations made under that Act or by any other Act of Parliament may be inspected or copies thereof obtained.

Ottawa, October 26, 1982

P. M. PITFIELD

*Clerk of the Privy Council*
