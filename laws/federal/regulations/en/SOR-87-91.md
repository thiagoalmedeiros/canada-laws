# Order Authorizing the Minister of State (Privatization and Regulatory Affairs) to Exercise the Powers Given to the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans Under the Act in Respect of Certain Companies

SOR/87-91 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [ATLANTIC FISHERIES RESTRUCTURING ACT](../../acts/en/A-14.md)

Registration: 1987-02-12

P.C. 1987-256 1987-02-12

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 7 of the [Atlantic Fisheries Restructuring Act](../../acts/en/A-14.md)[^footnote1_e], is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1985-2003 of June 20, 1985[^footnote2_e]; and

**(b)** to authorize the Minister of State (Privatization and Regulatory Affairs) to exercise the powers given to the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans pursuant to the [Atlantic Fisheries Restructuring Act](../../acts/en/A-14.md) in respect of 125459 Canada Limited, Fishery Products International Limited and 125457 Canada Limited known as NSHOLDCO Limited.
