# Order Transfering to Shared Services Canada the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Federal Public Administration in the Department of Public Works and Government Services

SI/2011-67 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2011-08-04

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 2011-08-17

P.C. 2011-877 2011-08-03

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2011-67/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_a] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md)[^fn_b], transfers to Shared Services Canada the control and supervision of the following portions of the federal public administration in the Department of Public Works and Government Services known as:

**(i)** the Services Transition and Major Projects Unit,

**(ii)** the Chief Technology Officer Unit,

**(iii)** the Enterprise Services Unit,

**(iv)** the Service Management and Delivery Unit,

**(v)** the Email, Data Centre and Network Services Unit, and

**(vi)** the Email, Data Centre and Network Services Support Unit, and

**(b)** orders that the Minister of Public Works and Government Services shall preside over Shared Services Canada,

effective August 4, 2011.
