# Computer Generated Mailing List Remission Order

*Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty and Sales Tax on Computer Generated Mailing Lists*

C.R.C., c. 752 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._752/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Computer Generated Mailing List Remission Order](CRC-c-752.md).

## Remission

**2** Remission is hereby granted of the customs duty paid or payable under Schedule A to the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md) on computer generated mailing lists imported during the period commencing on February 14, 1972 and ending on June 30, 1985.

<sub>History: SI/80-158, s. 1; SI/83-137, s. 1</sub>

**3** Remission is hereby granted of the sales tax paid or payable under the [Excise Tax Act](../../acts/en/E-15.md) on the goods referred to in section 2 in the amount equal to the difference between the sales tax calculated on the duty-paid value of the goods and the sales tax calculated on the value for duty of the goods.
