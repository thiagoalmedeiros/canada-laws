# Critical Habitat of the Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Oncorhynchus clarkii lewisi) Alberta Population Order

SOR/2015-241 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-11-20

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2015-11-20

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-241/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Oncorhynchus clarkii lewisi) Alberta population is a wildlife species that is listed as a threatened species in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_a];

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of that species, entitled Recovery Strategy for the Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Oncorhynchus clarkii lewisi) Alberta Populations in Canada, was included in the public registry on March 28, 2014;

Whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2) of that Act and, under subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must be excluded from the annexed Order;

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 58(5) of that Act, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans has consulted with the Minister responsible for the Parks Canada Agency, namely, the Minister of the Environment, with respect to the annexed Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md)[^fn_a], makes the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Oncorhynchus clarkii lewisi) Alberta Population Order](SOR-2015-241.md).

Ottawa, November 20, 2015

HUNTER TOOTOO

*Minister of Fisheries and Oceans*

## Application

**1** Subsection 58(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../../acts/en/S-15.3.md) applies to the critical habitat of the Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Oncorhynchus clarkii lewisi) Alberta population — which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry — other than the portion of that critical habitat that is already protected under that subsection because it is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2) of that Act, more specifically, in Banff National Park of Canada as described in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Canada National Parks Act](../../acts/en/N-14.01.md).

## Coming into Force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
