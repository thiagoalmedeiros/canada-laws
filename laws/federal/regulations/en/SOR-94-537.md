# Royal Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint Identification System Fees Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid by the Users of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint Identification System*

SOR/94-537 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1994-08-04

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(a)[^fn_SOR-94-537_e_hq_6299] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the fees to be paid by the users of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint Identification System](SOR-94-537.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint Identification System Fees Regulations](SOR-94-537.md).

## Fee

**2** The fee to be paid by law enforcement agencies for the use of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint Identification System is $33.79 annually per sworn police officer.
