# Order Designating Shared Services Canada as a Department and the President as Deputy Head for Purposes of the Act

SI/2011-69 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2011-08-04

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2011-08-17

P.C. 2011-881 2011-08-03

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2011-69/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***department*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^fn_a], designates Shared Services Canada as a department for the purposes of that Act, and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***deputy head*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^fn_a], designates the President of Shared Services Canada as the deputy head of that entity for the purposes of that Act,

effective August 4, 2011.

[^fn_a]: S.C. 2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
