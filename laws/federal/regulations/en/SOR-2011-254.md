# Transfer of the Communications Security Establishment in the Department of National Defence Regulations

SOR/2011-254 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2011-11-16

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2011-11-15

P.C. 2011-1303 2011-11-15

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-254/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^fn_SOR-2011-254_e_hq_11581], hereby makes the annexed [Transfer of the Communications Security Establishment in the Department of National Defence Regulations](SOR-2011-254.md).

## Block Transfer

**1** Subsection 132(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md) applies to all persons employed or engaged in the portion of the federal public administration in the Department of National Defence known as the Communications Security Establishment.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on November 16, 2011.
