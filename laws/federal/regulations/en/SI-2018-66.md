# Order designating the Minister of Status of Women, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, to co-ordinate policy with respect to the status of women and administer related programs

SI/2018-66 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-07-18

Enabling authority: APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 3, 1976

Registration: 2018-08-08

P.C. 2018-1009 2018-07-18

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2018-66/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to National Health and Welfare Vote No. 65 of the Schedule to Appropriation Act No. 3, 1976[^fn_81000-3-1637-E_hq_22303],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2017-4 of January 10, 2017[^fn_81000-3-1637-E_hq_22304]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Status of Women, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, to co-ordinate policy with respect to the status of women and administer related programs.
