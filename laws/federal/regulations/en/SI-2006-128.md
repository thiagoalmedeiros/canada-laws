# Order Designating the Minister of Health as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Assisted Human Reproduction Agency of Canada for Purposes of the Act

SI/2006-128 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-11-15

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2006-11-15

P.C. 2006-1138 2006-10-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-128/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[^footnotea_e] of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), hereby designates the Minister of Health as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Assisted Human Reproduction Agency of Canada for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md).
