# Area Control List

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce, pursuant to sections 4 and 6 of the [Export and Import Permits Act](../../acts/en/E-19.md), is pleased hereby to revoke the [Area Control List](SOR-81-543.md), C.R.C., c. 600 and to establish the annexed [Area Control List](SOR-81-543.md) in substitution therefor.

## Area Control List
