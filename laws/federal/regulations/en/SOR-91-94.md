# Taxation Statistics Diskette Fee Order

*Order Prescribing the Fee To Be Paid by a Person for a Computer Diskette Containing Tables of Taxation Statistics*

SOR/91-94 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1991-01-11

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-91-94/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1988-2270 of September 29, 1988[^fn_SOR-91-94_e_hq_5226], made pursuant to paragraph 19(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee to be paid by a person for a computer diskette containing tables of taxation statistics](SOR-91-94.md).

Ottawa, January 10, 1991

OTTO JELINEK

*Minister of National Revenue*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Taxation Statistics Diskette Fee Order](SOR-91-94.md).

## Fees

**2** A person who makes a request to the Minister of National Revenue for a computer diskette containing taxation statistics shall pay a fee of $27 for each diskette supplied to the person.
