# Order designating the Minister of Small Business and Tourism, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act

SI/2018-74 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-07-18

Enabling authority: [SMALL BUSINESS INVESTMENT GRANTS ACT](../../acts/en/S-10.9.md)

Registration: 2018-08-08

P.C. 2018-1019 2018-07-18

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2018-74/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the [Small Business Investment Grants Act](../../acts/en/S-10.9.md)[^fn_81000-3-1641-E_hq_22313],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2017-73 of February 1, 2017[^fn_81000-3-1641-E_hq_22314]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Small Business and Tourism, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
