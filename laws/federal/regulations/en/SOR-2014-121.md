# Expiry of Section 12.2 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations

SOR/2014-121 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2014-05-16

Enabling authority: [SOFTWOOD LUMBER PRODUCTS EXPORT CHARGE ACT, 2006](../../acts/en/S-12.55.md)

Registration: 2014-05-16

P.C. 2014-577 2014-05-15

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-121/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 101(a) and section 102 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006](../../acts/en/S-12.55.md)[^fn_81000-2-2043-E_hq_13697], makes the annexed [Expiry of Section 12.2 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations](SOR-2014-121.md).

## Ceasing to be in force

**1** Section 12.2 of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006](../../acts/en/S-12.55.md) ceases to be in force on October 12, 2013.

## Coming into force

**2** These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on October 13, 2013.
