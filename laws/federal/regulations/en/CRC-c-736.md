# Transfer of Royal Military College Academic Hoods Order

*Order Authorizing the Minister of National Defence to Transfer Academic Hoods to Recipients of Honorary Degrees*

C.R.C., c. 736 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Transfer of Royal Military College Academic Hoods Order](CRC-c-736.md).

## Authorization

**2** The Minister of National Defence is authorized to transfer academic hoods from time to time to recipients of honorary degrees conferred by the Royal Military College of Canada, Kingston, Ontario.
