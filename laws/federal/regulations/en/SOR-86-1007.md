# One-day Local Employment Seminars Fee or Charge Order

*Order Prescribing the Fees or Charges To Be Paid by Participants to One-Day Local Employer Seminars Hosted by Canada Employment Centres Across Canada*

SOR/86-1007 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1986-09-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1007/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Minister of Employment and Immigration, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1986-2103 of September 11, 1986[^footnote_e], hereby makes the annexed Order prescribing the fees or charges to be paid by participants to one-day local employer seminars hosted by Canada Employment Centres across Canada.

Ottawa, September 23, 1986

BENOIT BOUCHARD

*Minister of Employment and Immigration*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [One-Day Local Employment Seminars Fee or Charge Order](SOR-86-1007.md).

## Fee or Charge

**2** The fee or charge to be paid by each participant to the one-day local employer seminars hosted by Canada Employment Centres across Canada is $25.
