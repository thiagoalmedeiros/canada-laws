# Equity of an Insurance Company or Insurance Holding Company Regulations

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 1021[^fn_SOR-2001-380_e_hq_6000] of the [Insurance Companies Act](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md)[^fn_SOR-2001-380_e_hq_6001], hereby makes the annexed [Equity of an Insurance Company or Insurance Holding Company Regulations](SOR-2001-380.md).

## Equity of a Company or an Insurance Holding Company

### Equity

**1** For the purposes of the [Insurance Companies Act](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md),

- **(a)** the equity of a company is the sum of the shareholders’ equity of the company and the minority interests in entities controlled by the company, as they appear in the consolidated financial statements of the company; and

- **(b)** the equity of an insurance holding company is the sum of the shareholders’ equity of the insurance holding company and the minority interests in entities controlled by the insurance holding company, as they appear in the consolidated financial statements of the insurance holding company.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^fn_SOR-2001-380_e_hq_6002] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 1021 of the [Insurance Companies Act](../../acts/en/I-11.8.md), as enacted by section 465 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../../acts/en/F-11.1.md), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes into force.
