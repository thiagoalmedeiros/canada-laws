# Exemption from Restrictions on Investments (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations

SOR/2001-384 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](../../acts/en/C-41.01.md)

Registration: 2001-10-04

P.C. 2001-1755 2001-10-04

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraphs 396(a)[^fn_SOR-2001-384_e_hq_6003] and (b)[^fn_SOR-2001-384_e_hq_6003] of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../../acts/en/C-41.01.md)[^fn_SOR-2001-384_e_hq_6004], hereby makes the annexed [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations](SOR-2001-384.md).

## Exemption from Restrictions on Investments

### Prescribed circumstances

**1** For the purposes of subparagraph 390(3)(d)(ii) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../../acts/en/C-41.01.md), subsections 390(4) to (6) of the Act do not apply in determining whether an association would be permitted to acquire a substantial investment in an entity under subsection 388(2), paragraph 388(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 388(4) or 390(1) or (2) of the Act.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^fn_SOR-2001-384_e_hq_6006] These Regulations come into force on the day on which paragraphs 396(a) and (b) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../../acts/en/C-41.01.md), as enacted by section 314 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../../acts/en/F-11.1.md), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
