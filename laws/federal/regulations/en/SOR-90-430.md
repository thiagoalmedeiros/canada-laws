# Dundee Estates Limited Sale Authorization Order

*Order Authorizing Dundee Estates Limited to Sell all of the Assets of Dundee Estates Limited to Odyssey Cape Breton Tourist Attractions Limited*

SOR/90-430 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1990-07-27

P.C. 1990-1519 1990-07-27

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-90-430/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is satisfied that Dundee Estates Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Enterprise Cape Breton Corporation, is otherwise empowered to sell all of the assets of Dundee Estates Limited;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister for the purposes of the [Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency Act](../../acts/en/A-13.7.md) and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 91(3) of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order authorizing Dundee Estates Limited to sell all of the assets of Dundee Estates Limited to Odyssey Cape Breton Tourist Attractions Limited.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Dundee Estates Limited Sale Authorization Order](SOR-90-430.md).

## Authorized Sale

**2** Dundee Estates Limited is hereby authorized to sell all of the assets of Dundee Estates Limited to Odyssey Cape Breton Tourist Attractions Limited.
