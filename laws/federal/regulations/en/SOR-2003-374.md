# Organizations in the Province of Quebec Exemption Order

SOR/2003-374 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS ACT](../../acts/en/P-8.6.md)

Registration: 2003-11-19

P.C. 2003-1842 2003-11-19

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-374/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that An Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, R.S.Q., c. P-39.1, of the Province of Quebec which is substantially similar to Part 1 of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](../../acts/en/P-8.6.md)[^footnotea_e], applies to the organizations referred to in the annexed Order;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph 26(2)(b) of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](../../acts/en/P-8.6.md)<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed [Organizations in the Province of Quebec Exemption Order](SOR-2003-374.md).

## Exemption

**1** Any organization, other than a federal work, undertaking or business, that carries on an enterprise within the meaning of section 1525 of the Civil Code of Québec and to which An Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, R.S.Q., c. P-39.1, applies is exempt from the application of Part 1 of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](../../acts/en/P-8.6.md) in respect of the collection, use and disclosure of personal information that occurs within the Province of Quebec.

## Coming into Force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
