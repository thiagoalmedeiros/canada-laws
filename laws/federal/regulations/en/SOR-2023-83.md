# Associate Judges (Federal Court) Regulations

SOR/2023-83 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-05-01

Enabling authority: [FEDERAL COURTS ACT](../../acts/en/F-7.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, makes the annexed [Associate Judges (Federal Court) Regulations](SOR-2023-83.md) under subsections 12(2)[^fn_a] and (5)[^fn_a] of the [Federal Courts Act](../../acts/en/F-7.md)[^fn_b].

## Number of associate judges

**1** For the purposes of subsection 12(2) of the [Federal Courts Act](../../acts/en/F-7.md), the number of associate judges that may be appointed under subsection 12(1) of that Act is fixed at nine.

## Workload — supernumerary associate judges

**2** For the purposes of subsection 12(5) of the [Federal Courts Act](../../acts/en/F-7.md), the workload of a supernumerary associate judge is fixed at a minimum of 50% of the workload of an associate judge.

## Coming into force

**3** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
