# Secretary of State Authority to Prescribe Fees Order (Student Loans)

SI/91-104 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 1991-07-17

P.C. 1991-1210 1991-06-24

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-91-104/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Secretary of State of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19.1(b)[^fn_SI_91-104_EN_hq_20089] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Secretary of State of Canada to prescribe, by order, a fee based on a percentage of the amount of the loan, to be paid by full-time students to whom guaranteed student loans are made after July 31, 1991 under the [Canada Student Loans Act](../../acts/en/S-23.md).
