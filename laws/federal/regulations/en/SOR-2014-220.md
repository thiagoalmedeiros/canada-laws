# CHFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 49.6(9)[^fn_a] of the [Customs Tariff](../../acts/en/C-54.011.md)[^fn_b], makes the annexed [CHFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Order](SOR-2014-220.md).

## Tariff Rate Quota

**1** The aggregate quantity limit of goods of tariff item Nos. 1701.91.10, 1701.99.10, 1702.90.21, 1702.90.61, 1702.90.70 and 1702.90.81 that are entitled to the Honduras Tariff is, for the period set out in column 1 of the schedule, the applicable aggregate quantity limit set out in column 2.

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_Ind4235_hq_14615] This Order comes into force on the day on which section 43 of the [Canada–Honduras Economic Growth and Prosperity Act](../../acts/en/C-6.10.md), chapter 14 of the Statues of Canada, 2014, comes into force, but if these Regulations are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
