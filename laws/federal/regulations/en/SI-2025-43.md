# Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development To Be the Minister for the Purposes of the Poverty Reduction Act

SI/2025-43 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [POVERTY REDUCTION ACT](../../acts/en/P-16.81.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-423 2025-03-14

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the [Poverty Reduction Act](../../acts/en/P-16.81.md)[^nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731620],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2020-123 of March 9, 2020[^nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731646]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Employment and Social Development, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
