# Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry into the Investigation of the Bombing of Air India Flight 182 as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

SI/2006-71 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2006-05-17

P.C. 2006-295 2006-05-01

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-71/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b)[^footnotea_e] of the definition *department* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), designates the Commission of Inquiry into the Investigation of the Bombing of Air India Flight 182 as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b)[^footnoteb_e] of the definition *appropriate Minister* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md), designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Commission referred to in paragraph (a).
