His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](../../acts/en/C-1.68.md)[^fn_81200-2-TEXTILE_hc], makes the annexed [Candles Regulations](SOR-2016-165.md).

## Requirement

### Spontaneous reignition

**1** A candle must not reignite spontaneously after it is extinguished.

## Repeal

**2** \[Repeal\]

## Coming into Force

### Registration

**3** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
