# Order Designating the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc. as a Designated Airport Authority and Designating the Date for the Transfer of the Winnipeg International Airport to the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc.

SI/96-84 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [AIRPORT TRANSFER (MISCELLANEOUS MATTERS) ACT](../../acts/en/A-10.4.md)

Registration: 1996-09-18

P.C. 1996-1388 1996-08-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-96-84/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 2(2) of the [Airport Transfer (Miscellaneous Matters) Act](../../acts/en/A-10.4.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby designates

**(a)** the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc. as a designated airport authority; and

**(b)** the day immediately following the closing date as defined in the Agreement to Transfer entered into under the authority of Order in Council P.C. 1996-1387 of August 28, 1996, as the transfer date for the Winnipeg International Airport to the Winnipeg Airports Authority Inc.
