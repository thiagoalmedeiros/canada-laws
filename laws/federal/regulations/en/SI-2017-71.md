# Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Permit Authorization Order

SI/2017-71 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-11-02

Enabling authority: [JUSTICE FOR VICTIMS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT (SERGEI MAGNITSKY LAW)](../../acts/en/J-2.3.md)

Registration: 2017-11-15

P.C. 2017-1348 2017-11-02

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2017-71/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to subsection 4(4) of the [Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act (Sergei Magnitsky Law)](../../acts/en/J-2.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20732], authorizes the Minister of Foreign Affairs to

**(a)** issue to any person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada a permit to carry out a specified activity or transaction, or class of activity or transaction, that is restricted or prohibited under the Act or the [Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Regulations](SOR-2017-233.md)[^fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20733]; and

**(b)** issue a general permit allowing any person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to carry out a class of activity or transaction that is restricted or prohibited under the Act or the [Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Regulations](SOR-2017-233.md)[^fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20733].
