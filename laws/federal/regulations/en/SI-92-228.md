# Order Transfering the Powers, Duties and Functions of the International Expositions Division to the Minister of Communication

SI/92-228 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../../acts/en/P-34.md)

Registration: 1992-12-30

P.C. 1992-2576 1992-12-10

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-92-228/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../../acts/en/P-34.md), is pleased hereby to transfer from the Secretary of State for External Affairs to the Minister of Communications

**(a)** the powers, duties and functions respecting international expositions under the International Expositions Program; and

**(b)** the control and supervision of that portion of the public service of Canada in the Department of External Affairs known as the International Expositions Division.
