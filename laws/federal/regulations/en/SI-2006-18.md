# Order Designating the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as a Department and the Registrar as Deputy Head

SI/2006-18 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2006-02-22

P.C. 2006-54 2006-02-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-18/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition *department* in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^footnotea_e], designates the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as a department for the purposes of that Act, and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition *deputy head* in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../../acts/en/P-33.01.md)[^footnotea_e], designates the Registrar of the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as the deputy head of that office for the purposes of that Act,

effective February 6, 2006.
