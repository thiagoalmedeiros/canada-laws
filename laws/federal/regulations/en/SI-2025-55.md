# Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons as the Minister for the Purposes of the Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act

SI/2025-55 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [ELECTORAL BOUNDARIES READJUSTMENT ACT](../../acts/en/E-3.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-436 2025-03-14

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-55/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in subsection 2(1) of the [Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act](../../acts/en/E-3.md)[^fn_81000-3-2859_hq_1741747983],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-85 of February 12, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2859_hq_1741748013]; and

**(b)** designates the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
