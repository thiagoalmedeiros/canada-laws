# Order Designating the Province of New Brunswick for the Purposes of the Definition “applicable guidelines” in Subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act

SOR/98-256 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [DIVORCE ACT](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)

Registration: 1998-04-23

P.C. 1998-651 1998-04-23

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-256/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Province of New Brunswick has established comprehensive guidelines for the determination of child support that deal with the matters referred to in section 26.1[^fn_SOR-98-256_e_hq_5570] of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)[^fn_SOR-98-256_e_hq_5573];

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 2(5)[^fn_SOR-98-256_e_hq_5574] of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md)[^fn_SOR-98-256_e_hq_5573], hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating the Province of New Brunswick for the Purposes of the Definition “applicable guidelines” in Subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act](SOR-98-256.md).

## Designation

**1** The Province of New Brunswick is hereby designated for the purposes of the definition ***applicable guidelines*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md).

## Guidelines

**2** For the purposes of subsection 2(5) of the [Divorce Act](../../acts/en/D-3.4.md), the laws that constitute the comprehensive guidelines for the Province of New Brunswick are sections 1 to 3 of the Child Support Guidelines Regulation — Family Services Act, N.B. Reg. 98-27.

## Coming into Force

**3** This Order comes into force on May 1, 1998.
