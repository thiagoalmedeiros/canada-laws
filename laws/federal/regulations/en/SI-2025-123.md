# Fee Paid or Payable for the Acquisition of Permanent Resident Status (Certain Colombian, Haitian and Venezuelan Nationals) Remission Order

SI/2025-123 · Consolidated: 2025-12-10 · Last amended: 2025-12-05

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../../acts/en/F-11.md)

Registration: 2025-12-17

P.C. 2025-905 2025-12-05

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-123/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_81000-2-5836_hq_1744211617] of the [Financial Administration Act](../../acts/en/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-2-5836_hq_1744211632], remits the fee paid or payable for the acquisition of permanent resident status under subsection 303(1)[^fn_81000-2-5836_hq_1744211645] of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations](SOR-2002-227.md)[^fn_81000-2-5836_hq_1744211665] to certain Colombian, Haitian and Venezuelan nationals whose applications for permanent residency were approved under

**(a)** the Temporary public policy to facilitate the issuance of permanent resident visas to certain Colombian, Haitian and Venezuelan nationals with family in Canada signed by the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration on December 4, 2023; or

**(b)** a subsequent public policy issued under section 25.2[^fn_81000-2-5836_hq_1758728661] of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](../../acts/en/I-2.5.md)[^fn_81000-2-5836_hq_1758728675] and signed by the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration on September 20, 2024.
