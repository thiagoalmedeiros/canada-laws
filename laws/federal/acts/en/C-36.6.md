# Condominium Ordinance Validation Act

*An Act to validate certain ordinances of the Yukon Territory and the Northwest Territories*

C-36.6; 1985, c. 46 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2002-12-31

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-36.6/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## Short title

**1** This Act may be cited as the [Condominium Ordinance Validation Act](C-36.6.md).

## Yukon Territory *Condominium Ordinance* validated

**2**[^C-366_en_1] The *Condominium Ordinance* of the Yukon Territory, chapter C-12 of the Revised Ordinances of the Yukon Territory, 1971, and all amendments thereto made before the coming into force of this Act shall be deemed for all purposes to have been validly made under the [Yukon Act](Y-2.01.md) as if the Ordinance as so amended were not in conflict with the Land Titles Act, and everything done, whether before or after the coming into force of this Act, under the Ordinance as so amended shall be given such effect as it may otherwise have as if the Ordinance as so amended were not in conflict with the Land Titles Act.

## Northwest Territories *Condominium Ordinance* validated

**3**[^C-366_en_2] The *Condominium Ordinance* of the Northwest Territories, chapter C-10 of the Revised Ordinances of the Northwest Territories, 1974, and all amendments thereto made before the coming into force of this Act shall be deemed for all purposes to have been validly made under the [Northwest Territories Act](N-27.05.md) as if the Ordinance as so amended were not in conflict with the Land Titles Act, and everything done, whether before or after the coming into force of this Act, under the Ordinance as so amended shall be given such effect as it may otherwise have as if the Ordinance as so amended were not in conflict with the Land Titles Act.
