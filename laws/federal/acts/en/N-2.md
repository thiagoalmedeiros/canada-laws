# National Anthem Act

*An Act respecting the national anthem of Canada*

N-2 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-02-07

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short title

**1** This Act may be cited as the [National Anthem Act](N-2.md).

<sub>History: 1980-81-82-83, c. 5, s. 1</sub>

## National anthem

**2** The words and music of the song “O Canada”, as set out in the schedule, are designated as the national anthem of Canada.

<sub>History: 1980-81-82-83, c. 5, s. 2</sub>

## Public domain

**3** The words and music of the national anthem of Canada are hereby declared to be in the public domain.

<sub>History: 1980-81-82-83, c. 5, s. 3</sub>
