# Regulation respecting the information that must be collected and communicated to the Minister for the exercise of certain of the Minister’s functions

*CQLR c M-15.1.0.1, r 4*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the Ministère de l’Enseignement supérieur, de la Recherche, de la Science et de la Technologie (chapter M-15.1.0.1, s. 4.1)*

*Updated to 2025-12-25*

**1** This Regulation determines the information that must be collected by an educational institution and communicated to the Minister for the exercise of the Minister’s functions provided for in subparagraph 8.1 of the first paragraph of section 4 of the Act respecting the Ministère de l’Enseignement supérieur, de la Recherche, de la Science et de la Technologie (chapter M-15.1.0.1).

In this Regulation:

(1) **educational institution** means any institution that provides college- or university- level education referred to in the Québec Immigration Regulation (chapter I-0.2.1, r. 3);

(2) **foreign national** means any foreign national within the meaning of section 2 of the Québec Immigration Act (chapter I-0.2.1).

**2** Before 1 May of each year, every educational institution must communicate to the Minister, in the manner indicated by the Minister, the total number of applications for admission it has received from foreign nationals and the total number of offers for admission sent to foreign nationals during the previous calendar year.

The applications and offers referred to in the first paragraph must be divided by studies program and by trimester.

**3** Before 1 July of each year, every educational institution must communicate to the Minister, in the manner indicated by the Minister, the total number of preliminary applications for admission it has received from foreign nationals and the total number of preliminary offers for admission sent to foreign nationals as at 1 June for the fall trimester of the current year.

The applications and offers referred to in the first paragraph must be divided by studies program.

**4** Every educational institution must collect the following information from every registered international student:

(1) the number and period of validity of the student’s Québec certificate of acceptance issued under section 3 of the Québec Immigration Regulation (chapter I-0.2.1, r. 3) as part of the international student program; and

(2) the number and period of validity of the student’s study permit issued under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations (SOR/2002-227).

**5** Before the end of each trimester, every educational institution must communicate to the Minister the information collected in accordance with section 4, accompanied by the identification number of each student to which the information corresponds, and enter it, as applicable, in the college-level student enrolment reporting system or in the university enrolment reporting system established by the Minister.

**6** Until the coming into force of section 15.1 of the Québec Immigration Act (chapter I-0.2.1), enacted by section 4 of the Act mainly to improve the regulatory scheme governing international students (2024, chapter 43), subparagraph 1 of the second paragraph of section 1 of this Regulation must be read by replacing “designated in accordance with section 15.1 of the Québec Immigration Act (chapter I-0.2.1)” by “referred to in the Québec Immigration Regulation (chapter I-0.2.1, r. 3)”.

**7** (Omitted).
