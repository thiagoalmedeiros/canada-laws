*CQLR c CCQ, r 2*

*Enabling statute: Civil Code of Québec (Civil Code, art. 1339)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** For the purposes of paragraph 9 of article 1339 of the Civil Code, the market capitalization of a company whose common shares are listed by a stock exchange recognized by the Government shall be at least $75,000,000.

**2** (Omitted).
