*CQLR c C-65.1, r 7*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting contracting by public bodies (chapter C-65.1, s. 23)*

*Updated to 2025-10-28*

**1** This Regulation applies to contracts for leasing immovables of the departments and bodies whose operating budget is voted wholly or in part by the National Assembly, by which the right of occupancy of immovables is acquired for a certain amount of time for rent.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, it does not apply to occupancy agreements concluded with the Société québécoise des infrastructures or to leasing contracts in the field of telecommunications entered into or to be entered into by the Minister of Cybersecurity and Digital Technology.
