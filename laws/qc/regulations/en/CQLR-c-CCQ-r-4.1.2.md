# Regulation respecting the standards for establishing the values of impoverishment and enrichment in order to determine the value of a compensatory allowance

*CQLR c CCQ, r 4.1.2*

*Enabling statute: Civil Code of Québec (Civil Code, a. 521.46, 3rd par.)*

*Updated to 2026-02-26*

**1** The value of impoverishment and of enrichment for the purposes of determining the value of a compensatory allowance are established by considering the elements provided for in this Regulation and taking into account the global context in which that allowance is due.

**2** The value of impoverishment of the spouse that is attributable to his or her contribution in property or services during the parental union is established by taking into account

(1) the value of the economic losses suffered by that spouse by reason of his or her contribution;

(2) if applicable, the value of the economic consequences directly related to the contribution that are or will be suffered by that spouse if those economic consequences are assessable;

(3) the efforts made by the spouses to minimize those losses and consequences.

That value may not be established on the basis of the proportion of the spouse’s contribution to the enrichment of the other spouse.

**3** The value of the losses and economic consequences resulting from the contribution in property of the spouse is established by taking into account

(1) the net value of the property at the time of the spouse’s contribution;

(2) the decrease of the spouse’s assets;

(3) the increase of the spouse’s liabilities;

(4) the absence or decrease of the spouse’s income.

**4** The value of the losses and economic consequences resulting from the contribution in services of the spouse is established by taking into account

(1) the income earned by the spouse from his or her employment, work or occupation, as applicable, prior to his or her contribution, and the absence or decrease of that income;

(2) the benefits to which the spouse was entitled prior to his or her contribution and the absence or decrease of those benefits;

(3) the decrease of the spouse’s assets;

(4) the increase of the spouse’s liabilities;

(5) the absence or decrease of the spouse’s seniority or uninterrupted service;

(6) the absence or decrease of the spouse’s opportunities for advancement;

(7) the absence or decrease of the spouse’s acquisition of skills or expertise;

(8) the actual growth of the spouse’s income compared to the average growth of the income of workers with a similar career path.

**5** The value of the enrichment of the spouse’s patrimony attributable to the contribution in property or services of the other spouse is established by taking into account the net value of that contribution, which is determined by considering both the gains received and the avoided expenses as well as the increase in the net value of the spouse’s enriched patrimony since that contribution.

**6** (Omitted).
