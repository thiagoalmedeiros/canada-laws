*CQLR c V-6.1, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting Northern villages and the Kativik Regional Government (chapter V-6.1, s. 214)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** Under subparagraph 2 of section 214 of the Act respecting Northern villages and the Kativik Regional Government (chapter V-6.1), I (__________*Minister of Municipal Affairs, Regions and Land Occupancy*__________) hereby fix the amount of the tax that a Northern village council may impose and levy annually of all tenants paying rent in the municipality at a maximum of $0.30 per dollar of such rent.
