# Regulation to ensure the integrity of persons during the practice of a recreational activity or a sport

*CQLR c S-3.1, r 3.1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting safety in recreation and sports (chapter S-3.1, s. 55, par. 1)*

*Updated to 2026-03-26*

## I — OBJECT AND SCOPE

**1** The object of this Regulation is to promote a respectful recreation and sports culture that fosters quality experiences in a healthy and safe environment.

To that end, the Regulation establishes standards to ensure the integrity of persons during the practice of a recreational activity or a sport organized by a recreation body, sports body or sports federation.

Non-compliance with one of those standards constitutes a failure with regard to integrity that can be the subject of a complaint or a report to the recreation and sports integrity ombudsman.

## II — INTEGRITY OF PERSONS DURING THE PRACTICE OF A RECREATIONAL ACTIVITY OR A SPORT

### 1 — Persons involved in the practice of a recreational activity or a sport

**2** This Subdivision applies to every person involved in the practice of a recreational activity or a sport organized by a recreation body, sports body or sports federation, in particular the person practising a recreational activity or a sport, a parent of that person, a coach, monitor, instructor, activity leader, an official such as a referee or judge, a volunteer, spectator, or a manager or administrator of such a body or federation.

**3** Every person must act in such a way so as not to compromise the physical or psychological integrity of another person by showing respect, courtesy and moderation, and by striving to create and maintain an environment that is conducive to the practice of a recreational activity or a sport in a healthy and safe environment.

For the purposes of the first paragraph, a person is likely to compromise the integrity of another person in particular where that person

(1) compels another person to commit an act against their will;

(2) uses their influence or authority to incite another person to engage in a practice or perform an act that constitutes a risk to their health;

(3) organizes or tolerates the organization of an initiation or another activity involving degrading, humiliating or dangerous acts;

(4) inflicts bodily injuries on another person;

(5) ridicules, humiliates or disparages another person;

(6) uses language or performs acts that are abusive, offensive or threatening towards another person;

(7) expresses excessive frustration or anger or displays excessive aggressiveness towards another person;

(8) has interactions with another person that are incompatible with the nature of their relationship or exceed its appropriate boundaries;

(9) shows negligence or carelessness in the carrying out of their responsibilities in respect of another person or during the practice of a recreational activity or a sport;

(10) subjects another person to unfair treatment;

(11) engages in a discriminatory behaviour or practice towards another person; or

(12) discourages another person from making a report or filing a complaint with the recreation and sports integrity ombudsman or other competent body.

### 2 — Recreation bodies, sports bodies and sports federations

**4** Recreation bodies, sports bodies and sports federations must take any reasonable measures at their disposal to

(1) promote the behaviours to be adopted to ensure the integrity of persons during the practice of a recreational activity or a sport, in particular by distributing the content of this Regulation;

(2) make the available resources known for persons who wish to make a report or file a compliant in the event of such a failure; and

(3) where a failure with regard to integrity is brought to their attention, protect the integrity of the person identified as the victim by providing that person with the necessary support and preventing further failures from occurring, if applicable.

## III — FINAL

**5** (Omitted).
