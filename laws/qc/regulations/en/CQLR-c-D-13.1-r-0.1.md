*CQLR c D-13.1, r 0.1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting hunting and fishing rights in the James Bay and New Québec territories (chapter D-13.1, s. 94, 1st par., subpar. e)*

*Updated to 2021-12-22*

**1** The right of first refusal provided for in section 48 of the Act respecting hunting and fishing rights in the James Bay and New Québec territories (chapter D-13.1) is renewed for 3 years as of 10 November 2021.

**2** (Omitted).
