*CQLR c C-61.1, r 30*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the conservation and development of wildlife (chapter C-61.1, s. 186)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The territory of the parc de la Gatineau, whose technical description appears in the Schedule to this Regulation, constitutes a game reserve, under the name of Parc de la Gatineau Game Sanctuary.

**2** Hunting is prohibited therein.
