# Regulation to define the terms “robotic devices” and “commercial warehousing”

*CQLR c F-2.1, r 5.1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting municipal taxation (chapter F-2.1, s. 263, 1st par., subpar. 9.1.1)*

*Updated to 2026-01-01*

**1** For the purposes of section 64.3 of the Act respecting municipal taxation (chapter F-2.1),

**robotic device** means

(1) a device or machine that

(a) operates autonomously by means of a computer system;

(b) uses the information transmitted by one or more sensors to analyse its environment in real time in order to adapt to it;

(c) has freedom of movement that allows it to manipulate or move, on at least two axes, products of varying sizes, shapes and weights; and

(d) can carry out more than 2 different tasks, in particular, analyze, pick, transport, pack or sort; or

(2) any accessory that is used exclusively in the operation of a robotic device referred to in paragraph 1, except any base on which the device is placed;

**commercial warehousing** means any product handling, grouping, disposition, storage, picking or distribution operation carried out in a location where the products so stored are intended to be distributed in a retail outlet or sold remotely.

**2** (Omitted).
