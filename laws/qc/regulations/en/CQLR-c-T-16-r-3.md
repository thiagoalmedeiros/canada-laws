*CQLR c T-16, r 3*

*Enabling statute: Courts of Justice Act (chapter T-16, s. 216)*

*Updated to 2026-01-10*

> The amounts prescribed in the Regulation have been indexed and rounded pursuant to the notice published in Part 1 (French) of the Gazette officielle du Québec of 10 January 2026, page 47. (ss. 2, 3) (Effect from 1 January 2026)

**1** The commission for a person appointed to administer oaths, issued under section 214 or 215 of the Courts of Justice Act (chapter T-16), is valid for a 3-year period.

**2** The fees payable for a commission issued under section 214 of the Act to a person residing in Québec to administer oaths in Québec are $68 for a first commission and $46.50 for every renewal of the commission.

If the commission also allows for administration of oaths outside Québec, the fees payable are increased by $33.75.

**3** The fees payable for a commission issued under section 215 of the Act to a person residing outside Québec to administer oaths in the person’s province, territory or country of residence are $68 for a first commission and $46.50 for every renewal of the commission.

If the commission allows for administration of oaths elsewhere than the person’s place of residence, the fees payable are increased by $33.75.

**4** The Regulation respecting commissioners for oaths (O.C. 493-82, 82-03-03) is revoked.

**5** (Omitted).
