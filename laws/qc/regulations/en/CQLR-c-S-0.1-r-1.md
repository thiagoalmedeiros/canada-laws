# Regulation respecting professional acts that may be engaged in by persons other than midwives

*CQLR c S-0.1, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Midwives Act (chapter S-0.1, s. 2)*

*Updated to 2026-04-02*

**1** In this Regulation,

**community-based indigenous midwifery training program** means the theoretical and clinical training unit leading to the achievement of the training objectives of

(1) the community-based midwifery training program of the Inuulitsivik Health Centre and the Ungava Tulattavik Health Centre (Inuulitsiviup Nutarataatitsijingita Ilisarningata Aulagusinga);

(2) the community-based midwifery training program of the Cree Board of Health and Social Services of James Bay (Eeyou Istchee Pimaatisiiwin Chiskutimaachawin);

(3) the community-based midwifery training program of Akwesasne (Kontiwiró:kwas Midwifery Training Program); or

(4) the Innu Midwifery Program of the Innu Round Table Secretariat (Newfoundland and Labrador);

**diploma meeting permit requirements** means a diploma recognized by regulation of the Office des professions du Québec made under the first paragraph of section 12.0.1.1 of the Professional Code (chapter C-26) as meeting the requirements for the permit issued by the Ordre des sages-femmes du Québec;

**midwifery program** means the theoretical and clinical training unit leading to a diploma meeting permit requirements.

**2** A person enrolled in the midwifery program and listed in the Order’s student register may, for the purposes of the program, engage in any professional act that a midwife may engage in on the same conditions but only under the supervision of a midwife or another professional authorized to engage in such act.

**2.1** A person enrolled in a community-based indigenous midwifery training program and listed in the Order’s student register may, for the purposes of the training program, engage in any professional act that a midwife may engage in on the same conditions but only under the supervision of a midwife or another professional authorized to engage in such act.

**3** A person may, during a course, a training period or any other training activity recommended by the board of directors for the purposes of the recognition of a diploma equivalence or training, engage in any professional act that a midwife may engage in on the same conditions but only under the supervision of a midwife. The person must be listed in the Order’s student register.

**4** (Omitted).
