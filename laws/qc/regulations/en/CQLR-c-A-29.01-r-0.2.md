*CQLR c A-29.01, r 0.2*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting prescription drug insurance (chapter A-29.01, s. 62)*

*Updated to 2026-04-02*

**1** An intermediary within the meaning of section 80.1 of the Act respecting prescription drug insurance (chapter A-29.01) may receive from an accredited manufacturer the declared dividend that the shares issued by the manufacturer and held by the intermediary entitle it to.
