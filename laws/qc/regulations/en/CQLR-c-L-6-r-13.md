*CQLR c L-6, r 13*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting lotteries and amusement machines (chapter L-6, s. 119)*

*Updated to 2023-10-27*

**1** For all games offered by the video lottery machines forming part of a video lottery system within the meaning of paragraph *l* of section 1 of the Act respecting lotteries and amusement machines (chapter L-6), the rate of return must not be less than 83%.

**2** (Omitted).
