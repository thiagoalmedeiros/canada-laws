## I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**1** This Regulation applies to road vehicles and combinations of road vehicles within the meaning of the Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2), except vehicles designed to fight fires.

**2** In this Regulation,

**additional load-relieving system device** means an on-board weighing system that continuously measures the load of the axle or axle group of a road motor vehicle, a type 2 semi-trailer or a trailer other than one consisting of a type 1 semi-trailer, or an automatic system that adjusts the number of axles that a type 1 semi-trailer has on the ground according to the load being transported; (*dispositif supplémentaire pour système de délestage*)

**axle group** means a set of axles connected to the road vehicle through one or several suspension systems; (*groupe d’essieux*)

**axle spread** means the longitudinal distance between the centres of the extreme axles of an axle group; (*écartement*)

**box length** means the longitudinal distance between (*longueur de caisse*)

(1) the cargo or the section designed to carry the cargo at the front of a combination of road vehicles, whichever is the foremost point and

(2) the cargo or the rear of the combination of road vehicles whichever is the rearmost point.

**dolly** means a dolly used to convert a type 1 semi-trailer into a type 2 full trailer; (*diabolo*);

**effective rear overhang** means the distance from the effective turn centre to the rearmost point of the vehicle or its cargo; (*porte-à-faux arrière effectif*)

**effective turn centre** means the centre of the rear axle or axle group on a road vehicle, excluding the steerable, self-steering and lift axles; (*centre de virage effectif*)

**fifth wheel and kingpin assembly** means the coupling device between 2 road vehicles consisting of a fifth wheel located on the first road vehicle and a kingpin mounted on the second road vehicle. The contact surface between the fifth wheel and the second vehicle significantly increases the stiffness in roll between the 2 road vehicles and the fifth wheel allows the kingpin at its centre to pivot while keeping it solidly in place; (*attelage par une sellette et un pivot*)

**full trailer** means a self-supporting trailer, equipped with a drawbar dolly, whose load is not transferred to the road vehicle hauling it; (*remorque classique*)

**gross axle weight rating** means the first applicable value in the following order (*poids nominal brut sur l’essieu*):

(1) the value indicated, where applicable, on the inspection certificate issued under the fourth paragraph of section 214 of the Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2) for alterations referred to in subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph of that section;

(2) the value indicated on the additional label applied in accordance with the Motor Vehicle Safety Regulations (C.R.C., c. 1038) where an alteration made to the vehicle results in a change in the gross axle weight rating;

(3) the value specified by the vehicle manufacturer as the load-carrying capacity of a single axle system, indicated on the compliance label as “gross axle weight rating” or the abbreviation “GAWR” and applied to the vehicle in accordance with the Motor Vehicle Safety Regulations;

**gross vehicle weight rating** means the value indicated by the vehicle manufacturer as the cargo-carrying capacity of a single vehicle, indicated on the compliance label as “gross vehicle weight rating” or the abbreviation “GVWR” and applied to the vehicle in accordance with the Motor Vehicle Safety Regulations or, where an alteration to the vehicle results in a change in the gross vehicle weight rating, the value indicated on the additional label applied in accordance with that Regulation; (*poids nominal brut du véhicule*)

**interaxle spacing** means the longitudinal distance between the centre of 2 axles or between axles which are part of different axle groups that are closest to each other or between the centres of axles which are part of the same axle group and are closest to each other; (*entraxe*)

**kingpin setback** means the horizontal distance from the vertical axis through the centre of the kingpin of a type 1 semi-trailer or from the non-removable bearing centre of a type 2 full trailer to the farthest point at the front of the road vehicle, including the cargo but excluding the drawbar dolly and the coupling device, as illustrated below; (*décalage du pivot d’attelage*)

[Image]

**motor home** means a motor vehicle that has been permanently converted into living quarters; (*habitation motorisée*)

**pony trailer** means a trailer equipped with a rigid drawbar attached to the trailer’s structure making it possible for part of the trailer’s load to be transferred to the road vehicle hauling the trailer; (*remorque semi-portée*)

**self-steering axle** means an axle equipped at its ends with a part that can pivot around a vertical axis allowing the wheels to turn automatically in accordance with the vehicle-path or equipped with any other system that allows both wheels, whose tires have a tread of 385 mm or less in width, to turn automatically according to the direction and path of the vehicle; (*essieu autovireur*)

**semi-trailer** means a vehicle fitted with a kingpin designed to be coupled to the fifth wheel of the road vehicle hauling the semi-trailer; (*semi-remorque*)

**steering** is the qualifier used to describe an axle or an axle group located at the front of a road motor vehicle and operated by the vehicle’s steering wheel; (*directeur*)

**steerable axle** means an axle, other than a steering axle, that is steered using a steering system that interprets the direction of the road motor vehicle; (*essieu dirigé*)

**tandem axle group** means an axle group consisting of 2 axles that, as the case may be (*groupe d’essieux tandem*)

(1) is attached to a road vehicle by 2 suspension systems of the same type or one common suspension system designed to automatically balance to within 1,000 kg the load that can be measured under the wheels of each axle, neither of which is an unloadable axle;

(2) is a steering axle attached to the front of a road vehicle whose first suspension is mechanical and the second is pneumatic and includes an operational automatic adjustment device, easily identifiable by a peace officer, that continuously adjusts the pressure of the pneumatic suspension on the basis of the load applied on the mechanical suspension to balance the load that can be measured under the wheels of each axle to within 1,000 kg and whose second axle can be unloaded if equipped with an additional load-relieving system device;

(3) is attached to the road vehicle by 2 suspensions of the same type, automatically balancing to within 1,000 kg the load that can be measured under the wheels of each axle, one of which is an unloadable axle if the vehicle is equipped with an additional load-relieving system device;

**towing vehicle** means a motor vehicle to which is coupled one of the following vehicles: (*véhicule-remorqueur*)

(1) a type 2 semi-trailer and which is equipped with a fifth wheel set elsewhere than on top of the chassis frame or, it is, it is located more than 30 cm behind the centre of the axle located at the rear of the vehicle, not taking into account lift axles, or

(2) a trailer;

**track width** means the length of an axle and its wheels, measured horizontally, above the lowest point of the rim, between points located on the most rounded part of the outside faces of the outer tires, as illustrated below: (*largeur de roulement*)

[Image]

**tractor** means a motor vehicle designed to haul a semi-trailer and equipped with a fifth wheel that is set on top of its chassis frame and 30 cm or less behind the centre of the axle at the rear of the vehicle, not taking into account lift axles; (*tracteur*)

**trailer** means vehicle, other than a semi-trailer, coupled to and hauled by a road vehicle; (*remorque*)

**trailer device setback** means the longitudinal distance from the effective turn centre and the centre of the fifth wheel assembly that serves to couple to another vehicle; (*décalage du dispositif de remorquage*)

**tridem axle group** means a group of 3 equally spaced out axles, attached to the road vehicle by 3 suspensions of the same type linked together, designed to automatically balance to within 1,000 kg the load that can be measured under the wheels of each axle, the tridem having, as the case may be, (*groupe d’essieux tridem*)

(1) no unloadable axle, or

(2) at least one unloadable axle if the vehicle is equipped with an additional load-relieving system device;

**Type A double train** means a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor, a type 1 semi-trailer and a type 2 single drawbar full trailer; (*train double de type A*)

**Type B double train** means a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor and 2 type 1 semi-trailers; (*train double de type B*)

**Type C double train** means a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor, a type 1 semi-trailer and a type 2 double drawbar full trailer; (*train double de type C*)

**type 1 full trailer** means a full trailer whose front rests on a steerable axle or a steering axle group; (*remorque classique de type 1*)

**type 2 full trailer** means a full trailer whose front rests on a kingpin-type axle linked to the chassis frame by a non-removable steering ring or that is composed of a dolly and a type 1 semi-trailer; (*remorque classique de type 2*)

**type 1 semi-trailer** means a semi-trailer coupled to the fifth wheel of the road vehicle hauling it where the fifth wheel is set on top of the chassis frame of the vehicle and 30 cm or less behind the centre of the axle located at the rear of the vehicle, not taking into account lift axles; (*semi-remorque de type 1*)

**type 2 semi-trailer** means a semi-trailer coupled to the fifth wheel of the road vehicle hauling it where the fifth wheel is not set on top of the chassis frame of the vehicle or, if it is, it is located more than 30 cm behind the centre of the axle located at the rear of the vehicle, not taking into account lift axles; (*semi-remorque de type 2*)

**unloadable axle** means an axle equipped with a load-relieving system allowing to vary the load borne by an axle or to raise the axle so as to make its load’s pressure on the ground lighter than the load carried by the other axle or axles in the case of an axle group; (*essieu pouvant être délesté*)

**wheelbase** means (*empattement*)

(1) in reference to a type 2 full trailer comprising a dolly and a semi-trailer, the longitudinal distance from the kingpin to the effective turn centre;

(2) in reference to a type 2 full trailer not covered in the first paragraph, the longitudinal distance from the centre of the non-removable steering ring of the full trailer to the effective turn centre;

(3) in reference to a type 1 full trailer, the longitudinal distance from the steerable axle or axle group to the effective turn centre;

(4) in reference to a trailer other than a type 1 or type 2 full trailer, the longitudinal distance from the centre of the coupling device to the effective turn centre;

(5) in reference to a semi-trailer, the longitudinal distance from the axis of the kingpin to the effective turn centre;

(6) in reference to a tractor, the longitudinal distance from the centre of the first axle to the effective turn centre.

**wide-tread tire** means a tire having a tread whose nominal width is 445 mm or more; (*pneu à bande large*)

**3** Where a road vehicle or a combination of road vehicles is covered by more than one provision of this Regulation, the highest size or load standard will apply.

## II — MAXIMUM DIMENSIONS

### I — GENERAL PRINCIPLES

**4** The standards provided for in Divisions II to IV of this Chapter apply taking into account the general principles provided for in this Division.

**5** The safari bumper guard, the rear-view mirror and the front-view mirror are not included in the calculation of the length of a road vehicle or a combination of vehicles, provided they exceed by less than 30 cm the front of the vehicle.

**6** Auxiliary equipment located in front or at the rear end of a trailer or a semi-trailer are not included in the calculation of the length of the trailer or semi-trailer, box or kingpin setback, provided that equipment does not increase the volume of the cargo of the road vehicle or combination of road vehicles.

**7** The flexible and folding aerodynamic system located at the rear of a road vehicle is not included in the calculation of the overall length, the length of the trailer or semi-trailer, box or effective rear overhang, provided that, as illustrated below,

(1) no part of the system located at more than 1.9 m from the ground exceeds the rear end of the vehicle by more than 1.52 m when the system is extended;

(2) no part of the system located at 1.9 m or less from the ground exceeds, when extended, a virtual line linking

(a) a point located at 1.74 m from the ground and at 1.21 m from the rear end of the vehicle; and

(b) a point located at the rear end and the lowest part of the vehicle’s bumper or, if there is no bumper, a point located at the lowest rearmost point of the vehicle; and

(3) no part of the system exceeds the rear end of the vehicle by more than 30.5 cm when the system is folded.

[Image: C-24.2R31.1_EN_003_001.jpg]

**8** The rearward-facing hoisting system by winch and cable, located immediately behind the driver’s cab of a road motor vehicle, is not included in the calculation of the length of the road motor vehicle and the effective rear overhang, provided that

(1) the length of the vehicle, including the hoisting system, is 14 m or less;

(2) the distance between the effective turn centre and the rear of the hoisting system is 5.15 m or less; and

(3) the jib of the hoisting system is centred widthwise of the vehicle and the folded fly jib is 0.9 m or less from the centre widthwise.

**9** The snow or ice removal equipment at the front of a road vehicle used for snow or ice removal on public infrastructure, except for the transport of snow or abrasives, is not included in the calculation of the length of the road vehicle, provided that

(1) the road vehicle is equipped with an axle group of 2 steering axles at the front;

(2) the road vehicle is equipped with an axle group of 2 axles at the rear; and

(3) the length of the road vehicle, including the snow or ice removal equipment, is 15 m or less.

**10** The impact attenuator at the rear of a road vehicle other than a bus or a motor home is not taken into account in the calculation of the effective rear overhang when the vehicle is used as a protection vehicle and is not part of a combination of road vehicles.

**11** Unless otherwise provided in this Regulation,

(1) the drawbar dolly, the coupling device and the extensions of a road vehicle or a combination of road vehicles are taken into account in the calculation of any of the dimensions of the vehicle or combination of vehicles;

(2) the cargo is taken into account in the calculation of the total length, the width and the height of a road vehicle, except the length of a trailer or semi-trailer.

**12** The limits of the trailer device, wheelbase or effective rear overhang setback do not apply to an axle or an axle group located at the rear of a road vehicle composed of only one or several self-steering or steerable axles which make it impossible to determine the effective turn centre.

### II — MAXIMUM LENGTHS

#### 1 — Maximum length of motor vehicles that are not part of a combination of road vehicles

**13** The maximum length of a motor vehicle that is not part of a combination of road vehicles must be

(1) 19 m for an articulated bus, excluding the bike rack and its cargo if they do not exceed the front of the bus by more than 1 m;

(2) 14 m for a bus or a motor home provided their effective rear overhang is 4 m or less;

(3) 12.5 m for a vehicle not referred to in paragraph 2 whose effective rear overhang is 4 m or less;

(4) 11 m for any vehicle not referred to in paragraphs 1 to 3.

#### 2 — Maximum lengths of combinations of road vehicles

**14** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of a towing vehicle and a single full trailer is 23 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the length of the towing vehicle is 12.5 m or less;

(2) the towing vehicle trailer device setback is 1.8 m or less;

(3) the effective rear overhang of the towing vehicle is 4 m or less;

(4) the trailer length, excluding the drawbar dolly and the coupling device, is 14.65 m or less for a type 2 full trailer and 12.5 m or less for any other full trailer;

(5) the box length is 20 m or less.

**15** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of a towing vehicle and a single type 2 semi-trailer or a trailer other than a full trailer is 23 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the length of the towing vehicle is 12.5 m or less;

(2) the trailer device setback of the towing vehicle, except for towing vehicles, is 1.8 m or less;

(3) the effective rear overhang of the towing vehicle is 4 m or less;

(4) the length of the semi-trailer or trailer is 12.5 m or less;

(5) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer or trailer is 4 m or less;

(6) the length of the box is 20 m or less.

**16** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor equipped with an axle or an axle group of 2 axles at the rear and a type 1 lowered semi-trailer with a removable or folding gooseneck is 23 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the interaxle spacing of the tractor is 3 m or more;

(2) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.2 m or less;

(3) the length of the semi-trailer is 16.2 m or less;

(4) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer is 2 m or less;

(5) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer is 35% or less of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase.

**17** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor equipped with an axle or an axle group of 2 axles at the rear and a type 1 semi-trailer, to the rear of which is attached a forklift supported by its forks, is 23 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the interaxle spacing of the tractor is 3 m or more;

(2) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.2 m or less;

(3) the length of the semi-trailer is 16.2 m or less;

(4) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer is 2 m or less;

(5) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer is 12.5 m or less;

(6) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer is 35% or less of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase, excluding the forklift, and 42% or less including the forklift, without its central rear wheel;

(7) the forklift attached to the rear of the semi-trailer is located at the centre of the semi-trailer’s width;

(8) the width at the rear of the forklift, excluding its central rear wheel, is 2.1 m or less.

**18** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor equipped with an axle or an axle group of 2 axles at the rear and a type 1 semi-trailer not covered by section 16 or 17 is 23 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the interaxle spacing of the tractor is 3 m or more;

(2) the wheelbase of the tractor is 7.2 m or less;

(3) the length of the semi-trailer is 16.2 m or less;

(4) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer is 2 m or less;

(5) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer does not exceed that provided for in Schedule I, which depends on the tractor’s wheelbase;

(6) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer is 35% or less of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase.

**19** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of a farm tractor and 2 trailers is 23 m, provided the length of the trailers is

(1) 14.65 m or less, excluding the drawbar and coupling device, for a type 2 full trailer;

(2) 12.5 m or less for a trailer other than a type 2 full trailer.

**20** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of not more than 4 motorized road vehicles or chassis of motor vehicles coupled according to the saddle-back method is 23 m, provided the length of each vehicle or chassis of motor vehicle does not exceed 12.5 m, including its coupling device.

**21** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor equipped with a tridem axle group at its rear and a type 1 semi-trailer is 23.5 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.6 m to 6.8 m;

(2) the axle spread of the tridem axle group at the rear of the tractor is 2.4 m to 2.8 m;

(3) the length of the semi-trailer is 16.2 m or less;

(4) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer is 2 m or less;

(5) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer is 12 m or less;

(6) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer is 35% or less of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase.

**22** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor equipped with an axle or an axle group of 2 axles at the rear, a type 1 semi-trailer and a dolly is 25 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the length of the combination of road vehicles, excluding the dolly, is 23 m or less;

(2) the interaxle spacing of the tractor is 3 m or more;

(3) the wheelbase of the tractor is 7.2 m or less;

(4) the length of the semi-trailer is 16.2 m or less;

(5) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer is 2 m or less;

(6) the semi-trailer wheelbase is not greater than that provided for in Schedule I, which depends on the tractor wheelbase;

(7) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer is 35% or less of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase.

**23** The maximum length of a Type A double train is 25 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the tractor is equipped with a steering axle at the front and an axle or a tandem axle group at the rear;

(2) the semi-trailer is equipped with an axle or a tandem axle group;

(3) the trailer is equipped with an axle or a tandem axle group at the front and an axle or a tandem axle group at the rear;

(4) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.2 m or less;

(5) the interaxle spacing, other than between the axle at the rear of the semi-trailer and the axle at the front of the trailer, is

(a) 3 m or more between 2 axles or between an axle and a tandem axle group;

(b) 5 m or more between 2 tandem axle groups;

(6) the trailer device setback of the semi-trailer is 1.8 m or less;

(7) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer and the trailer is 2 m or less each;

(8) the length of the box is 20 m or less;

(9) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer and the trailer is 6.25 m or more each;

(10) the axle spread between tandem axle groups, where applicable, is 1.2 m to 1.85 m.

**24** The maximum length of a Type B double train is 27.5 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the tractor is equipped with a steering axle at the front and an axle or a tandem axle group at the rear;

(2) the first semi-trailer is equipped with a tandem or a tridem axle group and the last semi-trailer is equipped with an axle or a tandem or a tridem axle group;

(3) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.8 m or less;

(4) the interaxle spacing is

(a) 3 m or more between 2 axles or between an axle and a tandem or a tridem axle group;

(b) 5 m or more between 2 tandem axle groups;

(c) 5.5 m or more between a tandem axle group and a tridem axle group;

(d) 6 m or more between 2 tridem axle groups;

(5) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailers is 2 m or less;

(6) the length of the box is 20 m or less;

(7) the wheelbase of each semi-trailer is 6.25 m or more;

(8) the sum of the wheelbases of the semi-trailers does not exceed that provided in Schedule II, which depends on the tractor’s wheelbase;

(9) the axle spread is

(a) 1.2 m to 1.85 m for tandem axle groups;

(b) 2.4 m to 3.1 m for tridem axle groups.

**25** The maximum length of a Type C double train is 25 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the tractor is equipped with a steering axle at the front and an axle or a tandem axle group at the rear;

(2) the semi-trailer is equipped with an axle or a tandem axle group;

(3) the trailer is equipped with an axle or a tandem axle group at the front and with an axle or a tandem axle group at the rear;

(4) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.2 m or less;

(5) the interaxle spacing, other than that between the axle at the rear of the semi-trailer and the axle at the front of the trailer is

(a) 3 m or more between 2 axles or between an axle and a tandem axle group;

(b) 5 m or more between 2 tandem axle groups;

(6) the trailer device setback of the semi-trailer is 1.8 m or less;

(7) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer and the trailer is 2 m or less each;

(8) the length of the box is 20 m or less;

(9) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer and that of the trailer are 6.25 m or more each;

(10) the axle spread between tandem axle groups, where applicable, is 1.2 m to 1.85 m;

(11) the trailer dolly, where it is a type C trailer converter dolly within the meaning of the Motor Vehicle Safety Regulations (C.R.C., c. 1038), complies with the requirements set out in section 903 of Schedule IV of the Motor Vehicle Safety Regulations.

**26** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles designed to transport motor vehicles that consists of a tractor equipped with an axle or an axle group of 2 axles at the rear and a type 1 semi-trailer, is 25 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the length of the combination of road vehicles, excluding the cargo and extensions, is 23 m or less;

(2) the interaxle of the tractor is 3 m or more;

(3) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.2 m or more;

(4) the length of the semi-trailer is 16.2 m or less, excluding the extensions;

(5) the width of the cargo and extensions overhang exceeds by 2.3 m or less the length of the semi-trailer, it is centred widthwise on the semi-trailer and it exceeds by 1.2 m or less the front and by 1.2 m or less the rear of the semi-trailer;

(6) any extensions are retracted when not supporting at least one of the wheels of the motor vehicle being transported;

(7) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer is 12.5 m or less;

(8) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer is 42% or less of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase or 4 m, excluding the cargo and extensions, whichever is greater;

(9) where applicable, the cargo consists only of motor vehicles.

**27** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles designed to transport motor vehicles or boats that consists of a towing vehicle equipped with an axle or an axle group of 2 axles at the rear and a type 2 semi-trailer, is 25 m, provided it complies with the following specifications:

(1) the length of the combination of road vehicles, excluding the cargo and extensions, is 23 m or less;

(2) the length of the towing vehicle is 12.5 m or less;

(3) the effective rear overhang of the towing vehicle is 4.6 m or less and, excluding the cargo and extensions, is 4 m or less;

(4) the length of the semi-trailer is 14.65 m or less, excluding the extensions;

(5) the width of the cargo and extensions overhang exceeds by 2.3 m or less the length of the semi-trailer, it is centred widthwise on the semi-trailer and it exceeds by 1.2 m or less the rear of the semi-trailer;

(6) any extensions are retracted when not supporting a cargo;

(7) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer is 12.5 m or less;

(8) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer is 42% or less of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase or 4 m, excluding the cargo and extensions, whichever is greater;

(9) where applicable, the cargo consists only of motor vehicles or boats.

**28** The maximum length of a combination of road vehicles referred to in sections 14 to 27 that does not comply with all the specifications set out in those sections, a combination of road vehicles not covered in those sections and a road vehicle that is part of one of those combinations is

(1) 19 m for the combination of road vehicles;

(2) 14 m for a bus or a motor home if their effective rear overhang is 4 m or less;

(3) 12.5 m for a tractor whose interaxle spacing is 3 m or more and whose wheelbase is 6.2 m or less;

(4) 12.5 m for a motor vehicle, other than a tractor, whose effective rear overhang is 4 m or less;

(5) 16.2 m for a type 1 semi-trailer whose wheelbase is 12.5 m or less;

(6) 12.5 m for a type 2 semi-trailer or a trailer other than a type 2 full trailer;

(7) 14.65 m for a type 2 full trailer, excluding any drawback and coupling device;

(8) 11 m for any road vehicle not referred to in subparagraphs 2 to 7.

### III — MAXIMUM WIDTHS

**29** For the purpose of this Division, the following are not included in the calculation of the width:

(1) rearview mirrors, front-view mirrors and lights;

(2) a cargo covering system using a retractable tarpaulin cover and a securement device, other than walls, posts or side panels, to contain the cargo and which is tied down to one or more points of a vehicle, provided that the system or device exceeds by 10 cm or less the vehicle on each side;

(3) auxiliary equipment that is not a cargo and does not increase the cargo volume of the vehicle, provided it exceeds by 10 cm or less the vehicle on each side;

(4) equipment for grading, clearing or marking roads, including the marking cabin, where the vehicle is used for public infrastructure construction or maintenance;

(5) a device used for the automatic loading of bales of hay;

(6) the wheels and spreading equipment of a farm trailer that is owned by a farmer and designed and used to transport products to be sprayed, chemical fertilizer, manure, liquid manure or manure liquid, travelling elsewhere than on an autoroute, provided the width of the trailer, including the wheels and spreading equipment, is 3.75 m or less.

**30** The maximum width of a trailer or semi-trailer is 2.6 m, provided it complies with the specifications in one of the following paragraphs:

(1) the track width of each of the axles of the trailer or semi-trailer is 2.45 m or more in the case of an axle equipped with 2 wide-tread tires and whose load respects the gross axle weight rating, and 2.5 m or more in other cases;

(2) the trailer or semi-trailer is a step-deck platform trailer whose side rails prevent the track width of each axle to exceed 2.5 m and whose cargo has a total length of 2 m or less and is centred widthwise in the trailer or semi-trailer;

(3) in the case of a pony trailer or type 2 semi-trailer, it must have a gross vehicle weight rating below 10,000 kg.

**31** The maximum width of a farm trailer owned by a farmer is 3.75 m when used to transport grain and travels unloaded elsewhere than on an autoroute, and a maximum width of 2.6 m in other cases.

**32** The maximum width of a farm machine owned by a farmer that is used to transport products to be sprayed or travels unloaded, a seeder or a combine harvester is 7.5 m when those vehicles travel elsewhere than on an autoroute.

**33** The maximum width of a trailer or semi-trailer whose width is not otherwise set out in this Division is 2.5 m. The maximum width of other road vehicles whose width is not otherwise set out in this Division is 2.6 m.

### IV — MAXIMUM HEIGHTS

**34** The maximum height of a road vehicle or a combination of road vehicles is 4.15 m.

Despite the foregoing, the maximum height of a combination of road vehicles designed to transport motor vehicles or boats that complies with all the specifications set out in section 26 or 27 is 4.3 m provided that its height without a cargo is 4.15 m or less.

## III — MAXIMUM LOAD

### I — GENERAL PRINCIPLES

**35** For the purposes of this Regulation,

(1) all the steering axles of a road motor vehicle must belong to the same class of steering axle or steering axle group set out in Schedule III;

(2) all the non-steering axles of a road motor vehicle other than an articulated bus, a full trailer or a semi-trailer must belong to the same class of non-steering axle or non-steering axle group set out in Schedule III;

(3) the wheels that are not attached to an axle but that are attached under the vehicle in a common axis of rotation are included in the classes of axles or axle groups referred to in subparagraphs 1 and 2.

Despite the first paragraph, in the case of type 1 or type 2 full trailers, one class of axles is established for the axle or axle group that are at the front and a second class for the axle or axle group that are at the rear.

**36** An axle group where one is raised or unloaded remains a tandem or tridem axle group for the purpose of establishing the load limits if the following conditions are met:

(1) the road vehicle or combination of road vehicles is travelling on a snow or ice covered road other than a bridge road or overpass;

(2) the road vehicle or combination of road vehicles is losing or is at risk of losing traction on an ascending slope or is about to start from rest;

(3) the road vehicle or combination of road vehicles is travelling at a speed below 60 km/h and the hazard warning lights are activated;

(4) the load imposed on the ground under each axle does not exceed the gross axle weight rating and the sum of the maximum load for the axle tires as indicated on the tire side wall by the manufacturer; and

(5) the trailer device setback, the wheelbase and the effective rear overhang of the road vehicle, combination of road vehicles or the road vehicles composing that combination comply with the specifications set out in this Regulation even where the load-relieving system is activated.

**37** The on-board weighing system of the additional load-relieving system device must display the load of the axles or axle groups on a monitor installed inside the driver’s cab. The system’s margin of error must not exceed 5%.

The load displayed for axles of the same class as set out in Schedule III may be that of the sum of all the loads except the load of unloadable axles, which must be displayed separately for each of those axles.

**38** For the purpose of this Regulation, a road vehicle or a combination of road vehicles is part of one of the classes set out in Schedule IV, which are determined according to the number, type and configuration of their axles.

For a combination of road vehicles consisting of a tractor, a semi-trailer and a dolly, the class of the combination of road vehicles and the total loaded mass limit are established without taking into account the dolly when a semi-trailer is not coupled to the dolly.

### II — STANDARDS FOR MAXIMUM LOADS PER AXLE OR AXLE GROUP

**39** The maximum load per axle or axle group is the lesser of the following values:

(1) the maximum load provided for in Schedule III, adjusted or replaced, as the case may be, in accordance with sections 40 to 44;

(2) the sum of the maximum load indicated by the manufacturer on the side wall of the tires of the axle or axle group, and in the case of a tire that is not a wide-tread tire and that is mounted on a single wheel of an axle or axle group of a class between E.4 and E.20, without exceeding 10 kg per mm of nominal width of its tread;

(3) for a class E.1 axle or a class E.2 or E.3 axle group, the higher of the following values:

(a) 5,500 kg for a class E.1 axle or 11,000 kg for a class E.2 or E.3 group of axles;

(b) the gross axle weight rating or, in the case of an axle group, the sum of the gross axle weight ratings of all the axles of the group.

For the purpose of subparagraph 2 of the first paragraph, in the case of double wheels, the maximum load of the inner tire is, unless proven otherwise, the same as that of the outer tire. The limit of 10 kg per mm of nominal width of the tread provided for in the first paragraph does not apply to class E.13 self-steering axles if the width of the single wheel tires is 365 mm or more, not to class E.14 self-steering axles if the width of the single wheel tires is 385 mm or more.

**40** A towing vehicle hauling on its platform a single unloaded road vehicle that has been in an accident or has broken down, been seized or abandoned has, during a period of thaw, rain, erosion or flooding, the same maximum load per axle or axle group as during a normal period.

**41** The load limit during a period of thaw, rain, erosion or flooding provided for in Schedule III is increased by 1,000 kg for

(1) the class E.6 tandem axle group at the rear of a tractor hauling a tank semi-trailer that is also a type 1 semi-trailer;

(2) the class E.9 tridem axle group of the first semi-trailer of a Type B double train.

**42** The load limit provided for in Schedule III is reduced by 1,000 kg for a class E.4 axle or by 1,000 kg per axle in a class E.5 to E.20 axle group with 2 single wheel tires that are not wide-tread tires, except for a self-steering axle that belongs to

(1) class E.13 and whose single wheel tires have a width of 365 mm or more;

(2) class E.14 and whose single wheel tires have a width of 385 mm or more.

**43** The load limit per axle or axle group applicable to a road vehicle used for snow or ice removal on public infrastructure, except for the transport of snow or abrasives, is

(1) 10,500 kg for class E.1;

(2) 10,000 kg for class E.4;

(3) 18,000 kg for class E.5.

**44** The load limit is 13,500 kg for a class E.4 axle at the rear of a city bus.

The load limit is 15,500 kg for a class 7 axle group at the rear of an intercity bus or a motor home.

### III — STANDARDS FOR MAXIMUM TOTAL LOADED MASS

**45** The maximum total loaded mass of a road vehicle or combination of road vehicles is the lesser of the following values:

(1) the total loaded mass limit provided in Schedule IV by class of road vehicle or combination of road vehicles, adjusted or replaced, as the case may be, in accordance with sections 46 and 47;

(2) the sum of the maximum load per axle or axle group established in accordance with section 39, without taking into account the increase prescribed by section 41 and without exceeding,

(a) for class E.1,

(i) 7,250 kg for a road vehicle other than a tractor;

(ii) 7,700 kg for a tractor equipped with a tridem axle group at the rear;

(iii) 5,500 kg for a tractor not referred to in subparagraph ii;

(b) for class E.2, 14,000 kg;

(c) for class E.3, 13,000 kg.

**46** The total loaded mass limit of a road vehicle used for snow or ice removal on public infrastructure, except for the transport of snow or abrasives, is 20,500 kg for class V.1 and 28,500 kg for class V.3.

The maximum total loaded mass of a class V.1 city bus is 20,500 kg.

**47** The total loaded mass limit of a combination of road vehicles equipped with a self-steering axle located elsewhere than at the front of an axle group of 3 or 4 axles placed under a type 1 semi-trailer or equipped with a group of 3 axles placed under a type 2 semi-trailer or full trailer is 41,500 kg.
