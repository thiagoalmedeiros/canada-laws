*CQLR c P-5.1, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the sectoral parameters of certain fiscal measures (chapter P-5.1, s. 30)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

**1** The fees payable by a corporation or partnership for applications to issue a certificate or qualification certificate provided for in Chapters VIII and X of Schedule E to the Act respecting the sectoral parameters of certain fiscal measures (chapter P-5.1), applications to amend those certificates and applications for review related to an investment project provided for in those chapters are the following:

(1) $10,284 for the issue of an initial qualification certificate;

(2) $12,855 for the issue of an initial annual certificate;

(3) $3,214 for the issue of a subsequent annual certificate;

(4) $10,284 for the amendment of an initial certificate or annual certificate;

(5) $12,855 for an application for review.

The fees are payable to the Minister of Finance in a single payment on the date that the application is filed with the Minister.

**2** (Omitted).
