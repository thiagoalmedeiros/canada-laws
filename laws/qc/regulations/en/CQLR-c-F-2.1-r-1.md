*CQLR c F-2.1, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting municipal taxation (chapter F-2.1, s. 262)*

*Updated to 2026-01-01*

**1** To real estate value used to determine whether a rule provided for in section 148.3 of the Act respecting municipal taxation (chapter F-2.1) or in section 33, 85 or 135 of the Act respecting administrative justice (chapter J-3) applies shall be $500,000.

**2** (Revoked).

**3** (Omitted).
