*CQLR c B-1.2, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec (chapter B-1.2, s. 20.10)*

*Updated to 2022-05-26*

**1** The classes of published documents for which the deposit of a single copy of one edition of the document is required are the following:

(1) maps and charts, including charts of the planets and the skies;

(2) prints;

(3) artists’ books;

(4) digital documents.

**2** A publisher shall deposit 1 copy of a published document where the retail price of the document falls between $249.99 and $5,001.01, in accordance with paragraph 2 of section 20.5 of the Act respecting Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec (chapter B-1.2).

For the purposes of this section, the retail price of a periodical is the retail price of each number.

**3** The classes of published documents exempted from the obligation of deposit provided for in section 20.1 of the Act are the following:

(1) bills smaller than 1,300 cm^2 or larger than 15,800 cm^2;

(2) diaries;

(3) colouring books;

(4) commercial advertisements or catalogues;

(5) telephone directories of towns;

(6) bulletins of local associations containing only transient news without commentary, lists of members or minutes;

(7) calendars;

(8) greeting cards;

(9) press releases;

(10) (paragraph revoked);

(11) slides;

(12) documents without words or pictures;

(13) packaging;

(14) financial statements;

(15) forms;

(16) guides accompanying documents not subject to deposit;

(17) timetables;

(18) circulars;

(19) educational kits, unless they are documents or accompany documents subject to deposit;

(20) menus;

(21) crossword puzzles, word squares or any similar document;

(22) monographs of less than 5 pages not forming part of a series;

(23) patterns or models;

(24) photographs;

(25) programs of activities except show programs;

(26) prospectuses;

(27) reprintings of a document already deposited;

(28) reproductions, by photographic or photomechanical process, other than commercial reproductions of a work of art;

(29) parish leaflets;

(30) bookmarks;

(31) rate or fee schedules;

(32) tests or questionnaires or opinion polls;

(33) offprints without title pages or separate pagination;

(34) students’ works, reports or theses;

(35) school yearbooks;

(36) directories of students or employees;

(37) board games;

(38) microforms;

(39) data banks, databases and raw data;

(40) websites, except those of bodies deemed public bodies referred to in paragraphs 1 to 3 of the Schedule to the Archives Act (chapter A-21.1) and those of the media covering Québec national news.

**4** Any published document with a retail price exceeding $5,000 shall be exempt from the obligation of deposit mentioned in section 20.1 of the Act.

**5** A publisher, in the case of a document mentioned in section 4, shall send in writing the following particulars to Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec within 7 days following the date on which the document is published:

(1) the name of the author of the document;

(2) the title of the document;

(3) the place of publication of the document;

(4) the name of the publisher of the document;

(5) the retail price of the document;

(6) the press run of the document.

**6** A publisher shall enter on a published document that must be deposited with the library or on the container of such document the statement: “*Legal deposit, Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec*”, and the year in which the document was published.

**7** Sections 1 to 6 also apply to any person or body assuming the responsibility of producing a published document.

**8** Contravention of section 1 or 6 is punishable under the terms of section 20.11 of the Act.

**9** Contravention of section 5 is punishable under the terms of section 20.12 of the Act.

**10** (Omitted).

**11** (Omitted).
