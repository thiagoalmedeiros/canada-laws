# Regulation respecting the register, monthly report, notices from employers and the designation of a representative

*CQLR c R-20, r 11*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting labour relations, vocational training and workforce management in the construction industry (chapter R-20, ss. 19.1 and 82)*

*Updated to 2026-01-08*

## I — REGISTRATION OF AN EMPLOYER AND NOTICE TO THE COMMISSION DE LA CONSTRUCTION DU QUÉBEC

**1** Every employer within the meaning of the Act must register with the Commission de la construction du Québec.

**2** Every employer must send to the Commission a written notice containing the following information:

(1) the employer’s name;

(2) in the case of a natural person, the person’s birthdate and contact information;

(3) in the case of a legal person, the reference for the statute under which it was incorporated or continued, the date of incorporation or continuation and the names, birthdates and contact information of its directors;

(4) in the case of a partnership, the date of its formation and the names, birthdates and contact information of its partners;

(5) the employer’s contact information, including the address of its head office as well as, where applicable, the address of its principal establishment in Québec and the address of each of the employer’s establishments in Québec;

(6) the location and contact information where the register provided for in section 8 may be consulted;

(7) the number of the licence the employer holds under the Building Act (chapter B-1.1);

(8) (subparagraph revoked);

(9) the business number that the employer was assigned under the Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises (chapter P-44.1), where applicable;

(10) any additional information requested by the Commission that is of the same type as that provided for in subparagraphs 3 and 4 relating to persons in authority declared pursuant to the Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises.

The employer must update the information referred to in the first paragraph within 30 days of any change to that information. Contact information, however, must be updated before those changes take place.

The employer must ensure that any information declared pursuant to this section is consistent with the information found in the enterprise register pursuant to the Act respecting the legal publicity of enterprises and that the licence number is consistent with the information in the Régie du bâtiment du Québec licence holders register. In the case of discrepancies, the Commission may amend the information that has been declared to it for the purposes of consistency with the information contained in those registers.

**3** The employer must send the notice provided for in section 2 prior to the date on which he undertakes construction work subject to the Act.

**4** An employer must renew its registration with the Commission before resuming its activities by sending the notice provided for in section 2 where

(1) the employer notified the Commission that it will no longer act as employer; or

(2) the employer did not send the Commission its monthly report or the notice of inactivity, if applicable, as well as the sums exigible, in accordance with the conditions and within the deadline prescribed in this Regulation, during 24 consecutive monthly periods of work.

**5** The notice provided for in the first paragraph of section 2 is deemed not to have been received unless it contains all the information required and is accompanied by the fee exigible under the Regulation respecting certain fees required by the Commission de la construction du Québec (chapter R-20, r. 2), where applicable.

## II — DESIGNATION OF A REPRESENTATIVE

**6** Any legal person or partnership referred to in section 19.1 of the Act may designate a representative with a written notice to the Commission on the following conditions:

(1) it must be registered with the Commission as an employer;

(2) it indicates the name, birthdate, contact information of the representative and his capacity in respect of the legal person or partnership;

(3) it indicates the date on which the designation takes effect;

(4) the designated representative holds a journeyman certificate, an occupation competency certificate or an apprentice competency certificate.

**7** A legal person or partnership may, in accordance with the terms and conditions provided for in section 6, designate a new representative to replace the previously designated representative. Such new designation terminates the previous designation as of the date on which the new designation takes effect.

No designation or modification of a designation is deemed to have been received unless it contains the prescribed information and, in the case of a modification, is also accompanied by the fee exigible under the Regulation respecting certain fees required by the Commission de la construction du Québec (chapter R-20, r. 2).

## III — THE KEEPING OF A REGISTER

**8** Every employer must keep a register in which he must enter the following information for each employee:

(1) the name, address of domicile and identification number or social insurance number;

(2) the name of the job: occupation, trade or specialty carried on and the apprenticeship period, where applicable;

(3) for each day of work, the exact times at which the work begins, is interrupted and ends, the hours of work paid at regular, time and a half and double rates, for each job site on which the employees have worked and for each owner for whom the employer is performing work;

(4) the nature of the work, the sector, the type of job site and its location;

(5) the wages paid, the date and the method of payment;

(6) the amounts payable for vacations and paid holidays;

(7) the amount deducted as a levy;

(8) the contribution deducted from wages for complementary social benefits plans;

(9) union dues deducted.

The employer must enter in the register, for itself and, where applicable, for its designated representative, the information provided for in subparagraphs 1, 2 and 4 of the first paragraph and, where applicable, the amounts paid under subparagraphs 5, 6 and 8 of the first paragraph. The employer must also indicate, for each day of work, the hours worked on each job site and for each owner with whom the employer has entered into a contract to perform that work.

Every independent contractor must keep a register in which to enter, for itself and, where applicable, for its designated representative, the information provided for in subparagraphs 1, 2, 4 and 7 of the first paragraph and, where applicable, the amounts paid under subparagraphs 5, 6 and 8 of the first paragraph. The independent contractor must also indicate, for each day of work, the hours worked on each job site and for each owner with whom the independent contractor has entered into a contract to perform that work.

For the purposes of this Regulation, the expression **job site** means the work performed by an employer on a single project.

> As it concerns the declaration of the hours of the designated representative, this section comes into force on 27 December 2026. (O.C. 1568-2025, s. 12)

**9** (Revoked).

**9.1** Every employer must keep the register, together with any document supporting the information it contains, such as the payroll, time cards, contracts, invoices, order forms and any other document related to the construction work carried out by the employer, for 6 years after the last year to which they relate.

**10** (Revoked).

## IV — MONTHLY REPORT

**11** Every employer must send to the Commission a monthly report giving the necessary information allowing to identify each of its employees and indicating, for each monthly period of work and for each employee, their competency, including, as the case may be, the apprenticeship period, the regular and extra hours done each week, including the presentation hours, as the case may be, the number of weeks of work, the nature of such work, the region and the sector in which the work was performed, the wage paid, the paid holidays, the levy and the applicable contributions, assessments and dues. The independent contractor must indicate that information concerning the hours the contractor worked himself.

The employer must also identify, in the monthly report, for itself and its designated representative, where applicable, his competency, the number of work hours, the nature of such work and the sector in which the work was performed and also, as the case may be, the apprenticeship period.

> As it concerns the declaration of the hours of the designated representative, this section comes into force on 27 December 2026. (O.C. 1568-2025, s. 12)

**11.1** (Revoked).

**12** The report must be sent to the Commission not later than the 15th day of each month; it covers the previous monthly working period.

If the employer does not cause any work to be performed by an employee or does not perform any work as an independent contractor during a monthly period of work, the employer must send a notice of inactivity to the Commission not later than the 15th day of each month.

The monthly period of work must consist of not less than 4 and not more than 5 weeks, and must end on the last Saturday of the month. A monthly period begins on the Sunday following the last day of the previous monthly period.

The working week begins at 12:01 a.m. on Sunday and ends at 12:00 p.m. on Saturday.

**13** The employer must pay, by a mode of payment authorized by the Commission not later than on the date provided for in the first paragraph of section 12, the amounts corresponding to

(1) allowances payable for vacations and paid holidays;

(2) employer contributions to the complementary social benefits plans and the applicable sales tax;

(3) union dues;

(4) the employer contributions referred to in section 40 of the Act;

(5) contributions to the Compensation Fund for Employees in the Construction Industry;

(6) contributions deducted as a levy;

(7) contributions to the qualification fund;

(8) contributions to the Training Fund for Employees in the Construction Industry;

(8.1) sector-based contributions;

(9) fees provided for in section 126.0.2 of the Act.

**13.1** The sending of any written notice relating to the registration of the employer or the designation of a representative is done by means of the online services of the Commission, using the prescribed form where applicable.

The sending of a monthly report, a modification of that report or a notice of inactivity is done by means of the online services of the Commission or through any means adapted to the technological environment of the Commission. The receipt of such a document by the Commission is acknowledged by a notice to that effect to the employer.

## V — TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL

**14** A designation made under section 25 of the Act to amend the Act respecting labour relations, vocational training and workforce management in the construction industry (1992, chapter 42) or under section 5 of the Regulation respecting the notice from an employer to the Commission de la construction du Québec and the terms and conditions of the designation of a representative by a legal person or a partnership (O.C. 1364-93, 93-09-22), has the same effect as a designation made under section 6.

**15** This Regulation replaces the Regulation respecting the notice from an employer to the Commission de la construction du Québec and the terms and conditions of the designation of a representative by a legal person or a partnership (O.C. 1364-93, 93-09-22) and the Regulation respcting the keeping of a register and the sending of a monttly report (O.C. 875-93, 93-06-16).

**15.1** Despite the date provided for in the first paragraph of section 12 and in section 13, the monthly reports and the amounts corresponding to the 2 monthly periods of work preceding the date on which the new online service of the Commission becomes available, which date the Commission will communicate, must be sent and paid respectively within 15 days following that date.

**16** (Omitted).
