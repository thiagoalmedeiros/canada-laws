# Regulation respecting the minimum qualifications required to exercise supervision functions within a police force

*CQLR c P-13.1, r 3.1*

*Enabling statute: Police Act (chapter P-13.1, s. 116, 1st and 2nd pars.)*

*Updated to 2026-03-19*

## I — PERSONAL AND PROFESSIONAL QUALITIES REQUIRED TO EXERCISE SUPERVISION FUNCTIONS

**1** A police officer exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis within a police force other than a specialized police force must possess the following personal and professional qualities:

(1) leadership;

(2) sense of ethics;

(3) ability to communicate;

(4) judgment and decisiveness;

(5) ability to coordinate and adapt; and

(6) organizational skills.

## II — TRAINING REQUIRED TO EXERCISE SUPERVISION FUNCTIONS

**2** A police officer exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis within a police force other than a specialized police force must successfully complete the Supervision section of the Basic training program in police management of the École nationale de police du Québec, which includes

(1) a component on fundamental management principles;

(2) a component on police work; and

(3) a component on the implementation and integration of the knowledge acquired.

**3** As of the date on which a police officer begins exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis, the officer has a maximum period of 6 months to begin the training and a maximum period of 24 months to successfully complete it.

Despite the first paragraph, a police officer who begins exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis within 12 months after 19 March 2026 has, as of the date on which the officer begins exercising those functions, a maximum period of 12 months to begin the training and a maximum period of 36 months to successfully complete it.

**4** The director of a police force may, for a valid reason, grant an extension for a maximum period of 4 months to a police officer who did not begin the training within the period provided for in section 3. The director may also grant an extension to an officer who did not successfully complete the training within the period provided for in that section.

The extensions granted under the first paragraph may not exceed a total period of 12 months.

**5** A police officer who has exercised supervision functions on a permanent basis for less than 2 years on 19 March 2026 or a police officer who exercised such functions for less than 2 years before that date and no longer does so,

(1) if the police officer successfully completed the Supervision de patrouille course or the Supervision d’enquêtes course of the École nationale de police du Québec, the officer is deemed to comply with paragraph 2 of section 2 and has a maximum period of 36 months as of 19 March 2026, or as of the date on which the officer resumes exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis, as applicable, to successfully complete the components of the training provided for in paragraphs 1 and 3 of section 2;

(2) if the police officer did not successfully complete the Supervision de patrouille course or the Supervision d’enquêtes course of the École nationale de police du Québec, the officer has a maximum period of 36 months as of 19 March 2026 or as of the date on which the officer resumes exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis, as applicable, to successfully complete the training provided for in section 2.

**6** A police officer who has exercised supervision functions on a permanent basis for more than 2 years but less than 4 years on 19 March 2026, or a police officer who exercised such functions for more than 2 years but less than 4 years before that date and no longer does so,

(1) if the police officer successfully completed the Supervision de patrouille course or the Supervision d’enquêtes course of the École nationale de police du Québec, the officer is deemed to comply with paragraphs 2 and 3 of section 2 and has a maximum period of 36 months as of 19 March 2026, or as of the date on which the officer resumes exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis, as applicable, to successfully complete the component of the training provided for in paragraph 1 of section 2;

(2) if the police officer did not successfully complete the Supervision de patrouille course or the Supervision d’enquêtes course of the École nationale de police du Québec, the officer is deemed to comply with paragraph 3 of section 2 and has a maximum period of 36 months as of 19 March 2026 or as of the date on which the officer resumes exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis, as applicable, to successfully complete the components of the training provided for in paragraphs 1 and 2 of section 2.

**7** A police officer who has exercised supervision functions on a permanent basis for more than 4 years on 19 March 2026 or a police officer who exercised such functions for more than 4 years before that date and no longer does so is deemed to comply with section 2.

**8** A police officer who had never exercised supervision functions on a permanent basis on 19 March 2026 and who successfully completed the Supervision de patrouille course or the Supervision d’enquêtes course of the École nationale de police du Québec within the 5 years preceding the date on which the officer begins exercising such functions is deemed to comply with paragraph 2 of section 2.

**9** Notwithstanding sections 5 to 7, the director of a police force may, for a valid reason, require that a police officer who exercised supervision functions before 19 March 2026 and no longer exercises such functions successfully complete one or more of the components provided for in section 2; the officer is deemed to comply with section 2 within the period indicated therein.

The director may also, for a valid reason, grant an extension for a maximum period of 6 months to a police officer referred to in sections 5 and 6 who did not successfully complete the training within the period provided for in one of those sections.

**10** The director of a police force may, for a valid reason and with the authorization of the Minister of Public Security, grant an extension in addition to the ones granted under sections 4 and 9.

**11** Upon granting an extension under this Regulation, the director of a police force must inform the École nationale de police du Québec. In addition, the director informs the Minister annually of the reasons for and duration of each extension.

**12** A police officer exercising supervision functions on a permanent basis who has not successfully completed the training on the expiry of the maximum period for doing so or on the expiry of an extension granted under section 4, 9 or 10 must be subject to a supervision plan approved by the director of the police force or be reassigned to other functions by the director until the officer successfully completes the training.

**13** An equivalence for a training activity provided for in this Regulation may be granted in accordance with the By-law to establish the Training Plan Regulation of the École nationale de police du Québec (chapter P-13.1, r. 4).
