# Ministerial Order concerning heavy vehicles whose speed limiter must be activated and set at a maximum speed of 105 km/h

*CQLR c C-24.2, r 50*

*Enabling statute: Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2, s. 519.15.3)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** Heavy vehicles whose speed limiter must be activated and set at a maximum speed of 105 km/h are the heavy vehicles assembled after 31 December 1994 and whose gross vehicle weight rating is at least 11,794 kg, except emergency vehicles, tool vehicles, buses, trailers, semi-trailers, detachable axles and vehicles used for personal purposes.

“Gross vehicle weight rating” means the value specified by the manufacturer as the loaded weight of a single vehicle and known as the “gross vehicle weight rating”, “GVWR”, “poids nominal brut du véhicule” or “PNBV”.

**2** (Omitted).
