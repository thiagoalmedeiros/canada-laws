# Regulation respecting the formation of regional committees for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language

*CQLR c S-4.2, r 14*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting health services and social services (chapter S-4.2, s. 510)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The following regional committees for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language are hereby established:

(1) the Bas-Saint-Laurent (01) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(2) the Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean (02) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(3) the Capitale-Nationale (03) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(4) the Mauricie and Centre-du-Québec (04) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(5) the Estrie (05) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(6) the Montréal (06) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(7) the Outaouais (07) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(8) the Abitibi-Témiscamingue (08) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(9) the Côte-Nord (09) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(10) the Nord-du-Québec (10) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(11) the Gaspésie–Îles-de-la-Madeleine (11) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(12) the Chaudière-Appalaches (12) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(13) the Laval (13) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(14) the Lanaudière (14) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(15) the Laurentides (15) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language;

(16) the Montérégie (16) Regional Committee for programs of access to health services and social services in the English language.

**2** (Omitted).
