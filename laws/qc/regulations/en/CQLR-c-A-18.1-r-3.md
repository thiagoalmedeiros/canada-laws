*CQLR c A-18.1, r 3*

*Enabling statute: Sustainable Forest Development Act (chapter A-18.1, ss. 130 and 173)*

*Updated to 2026-01-10*

> The amounts prescribed in the Regulation have been indexed pursuant to the notice published in Part 1 (French) of the Gazette officielle du Québec of 10 January 2026, page 39. (ss. 1, 2, 3, 4) (Effect from 1 January 2026)

> The former alphanumerical designation of this Regulation was: chapter F-4.1, r. 4.

**1** The fees payable for the issue of a certified forest producer’s certificate are $27.75.

**2** Following are the fees payable for an application for a change in the certificate:

(1) $27.75 for an application for registration of an additional forest area made by a certified forest producer during the validity period of his certificate;

(2) $14 for any other application for a change made by the producer in any of the following items appearing on the certificate:

(a) the cadastral designation of an immovable property constituting the site of the registered forest area;

(b) the registered area;

(c) mention as to whether or not the producer has signed an agreement with a common management unit;

(d) the expiry date of the forest management plan;

(e) the unit of assessment;

(f) the name of the office responsible for the registration.

**3** The fees payable for the renewal of a forest producer’s certificate are $27.75.

**4** The fees payable for the issue to a producer of a duplicate or copy of his forest producer’s certificate are $14.

**5** Any person or organization designated by the Minister for the purposes of registering forest areas and certifying forest producers, in accordance with section 130 of the Sustainable Forest Development Act (chapter A-18.1), is authorized to keep the fees he or it collects under this Regulation.

**6** (Omitted).
