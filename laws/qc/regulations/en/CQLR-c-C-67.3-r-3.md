*CQLR c C-67.3, r 3*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting financial services cooperatives (chapter C-67.3, ss. 599 and 723)*

*Updated to 2026-01-03*

> The fees prescribed in the Tariff have been indexed pursuant to the notice published in Part 1 (French) of the Gazette officielle du Québec of 3 January 2026, page 11. (s. 1) (Effect from 1 January 2026)

**1** The fees exigible from a credit union or a federation are:

(1) for an incorporation, an amalgamation, a winding-up, a dissolution, or for the revocation of a dissolution: $547;

(2) for the amendment or updating of articles: $273;

(3) for a certificate of incorporation or the correction of a certificate or for a change of address of the head office in the same judicial district: $79;

(4) ffor the issue of copies of documents that have been registered and of a certificate attesting thereto, and for the issue of certificates signed by the Autorité des marchés financiers: $86.50.

**1.1** (Revoked).

**2** The fees exigible shall be paid upon applying for any formality or procedure provided for in section 1.

**3** (Omitted).
