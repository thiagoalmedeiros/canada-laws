# Order in Council respecting the exclusion of watercourses or parts of watercourses from the jurisdiction of regional county municipalities

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Gouvernement du Québec. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Quebec. Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/C-47.1,%20r.%203) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c C-47.1, r 3*

*Enabling statute: Municipal Powers Act (chapter C-47.1, s. 103)*

*Updated to 2017-11-15*

**1** The following watercourses or parts of watercourses are excluded from the jurisdiction of regional county municipalities:

— the tidal parts of watercourses whose watershed basin is less than 100 km^2 in area;

— the watercourses or parts of watercourses listed in the Schedule attached to this Order in Council.

**2** This Order in Council replace Order in Council respecting the exclusion of watercourses or parts of watercourses from the jurisdiction of regional county municipalities (chapter C-47.1, r. 2).

**3** (Omitted).
