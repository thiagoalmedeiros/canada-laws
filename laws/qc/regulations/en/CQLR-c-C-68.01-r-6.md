*CQLR c C-68.01, r 6*

*Enabling statute: Coroners Act (chapter C-68.01, s. 168)*

*Updated to 2022-11-01*

> The former alphanumerical designation of this Tariff was

> chapter R-0.2, r. 5

> .

**1** The costs exigible by a hospital centre that performs, upon the request of the coroner, the autopsy of a dead body where death has occurred outside a hospital centre shall be $250 per autopsy.

**2** (Omitted).
