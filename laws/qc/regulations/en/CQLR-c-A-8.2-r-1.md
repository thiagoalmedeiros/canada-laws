# Regulation respecting the rules for the distribution of the costs by the Autorité des marchés financiers among the credit assessment agents

*CQLR c A-8.2, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Credit Assessment Agents Act (chapter A-8.2, s. 63)*

*Updated to 2022-08-04*

**1** The costs incurred by the Autorité des marchés financiers for the administration of the Credit Assessment Agents Act (chapter A-8.2) for a year are distributed among the credit assessment agents in proportion with the number of records of persons concerned they hold.

The costs, for each credit assessment agent, correspond to the product of the costs determined by the Government and the proportion of records the credit assessment agent holds, which corresponds to the number of records held by the credit assessment agent in relation to the sum of the records held by all the credit assessment agents.

For the purposes of the first paragraph, the number of records held by each credit assessment agent is recorded at 31 December of the preceding year.

**2** (Omitted).
