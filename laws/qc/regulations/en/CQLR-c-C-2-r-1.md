# Regulation of the Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec respecting deposits by certain public bodies or their pension funds and the conditions of such deposits

*CQLR c C-2, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec (chapter C-2, ss. 20.1 and 23)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec may receive on deposit any sum of money from a public body within the meaning of section 20.2 of the Act respecting the Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec (chapter C-2), from a municipal body within the meaning of section 20.3 of that Act, from a school body within the meaning of section 20.4 of that Act, from a health and social service establishment within the meaning of section 20.5 of that Act or from a pension fund of such bodies.

**2** A body referred to in section 1 or the pension fund of such a body may make deposits with the Fund under the following conditions:

(a) the body or pension fund identifies the sums to be deposited as well as the frequency and conditions of deposits;

(b) the body or pension fund notifies the Fund of its needs concerning deposits to be made or obligations to be met;

(c) the body or pension fund makes such deposits, as provided for by legislative or administrative provisions applicable to it;

(i) where the administration is under the responsibility of a board of directors, a resolution by such a board or by a committee appointed by such a board shall be filed regarding deposits with the Fund;

(ii) where the administration is under another authority, an official attestation of the decision to make a deposit with the Fund shall be filed; and

(d) the board of directors of the Fund shall confirm, by way of a resolution, its acceptance of the body or pension fund as a depositor.

**3** (Omitted).
