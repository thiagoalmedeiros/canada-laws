*CQLR c T-16, r 0.01*

*Enabling statute: Courts of Justice Act (chapter T-16, ss. 224.2 and 224.29)*

*Updated to 2019-06-30*

**1** The rate provided for in the first paragraph of section 224.2 of the Courts of Justice Act (chapter T-16) is 9%.

Despite the first paragraph, that rate is 1% of the annual salary of the judge or presiding justice of the peace when he or she has accumulated 21.7 years of service and continues to hold office.

**2** The interest rate applicable to contributions paid into the pension plan provided for in Part V.1 of the Act and those transferred to it is 6% compounded annually.
