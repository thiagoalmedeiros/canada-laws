## I — PRELIMINARY

**1** The definitions in section 2 of the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation (chapter C-24.2, r. 31.1) apply to this Regulation.

**2** The general principles in sections 5 to 7, 11 and 12 of the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation (chapter C-24.2, r. 31.1) apply to the size calculations provided for this Regulation.

## II — CONDITIONS FOR OBTAINING A SPECIAL PERMIT

### I — CONDITIONS APPLICABLE TO ROAD TRAINS

**3** A special operating permit may be issued for any type of road train referred to in sections 4 to 7 provided that the road train complies with the characteristics prescribed in those sections and with the following:

(1) the width, height and load per axle or group of axles of the road train comply with the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation (chapter C-24.2, r. 31.1);

(2) the engine of the tractor or towing vehicle has a minimum horsepower of 425 hp;

(3) the tractor or towing vehicle is equipped with an air compressor, in good working order, with a capacity of not less than 465 litres per minute (16.5 ft^3/min) supplying the braking system;

(4) the tractor or towing vehicle is equipped with a desiccant air dryer, in good working order, of a capacity sufficient to prevent the accumulation of humidity in the brakes of semi-trailers;

(5) the tractor or towing vehicle is equipped with a speed recorder with geolocation;

(6) the dolly is equipped with an overpressure protection valve and a pilot relay valve designed to boost the braking signal of the second semi-trailer.

For the purposes of subparagraph 5 of the first paragraph, a speed recorder with geolocation is a vehicle-mounted electronic device that is accurate and in good working order that records, at regular intervals of not more than 5 minutes, the speed of the vehicle, the time and the date; it features a global positioning system (GPS) or a similar vehicle-tracking system whose data can be extracted, on demand and for specific dates and times, and presented as a readable table. A tachograph or tachograph charts do not constitute a speed recorder with geolocation.

**4** An A double train must have the following characteristics:

(1) the length of the double train is 41 m or less;

(2) the tractor is equipped with a steering axle at the front and a tandem axle group at the rear;

(3) the interaxle spacing of the tractor is not less than 3.5 m;

(4) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.2 m or less;

(5) the semi-trailer is equipped with a tandem or tridem axle group or a class E.13 or E.14 axle group established in Schedule III to the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation (chapter C-24.2, r. 31.1);

(6) the dolly of the full trailer is equipped with a tandem axle group;

(7) the trailer is equipped with a tandem or tridem axle group at the rear;

(8) the axle spread between tandem axle groups, where applicable, is

(a) 1.2 m to 1.85 m for the tractor and the dolly; and

(b) 1.2 m to 3.1 m for the other vehicles;

(9) the axle spread in a tridem axle group is 2.4 m to 3.7 m;

(10) the length of the semi-trailer is 12.2 m to 16.2 m;

(11) the semi-trailer is equipped with a damper type pintle hook hitch that is free of play and has a secondary locking mechanism;

(12) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer does not exceed 12.5 m and is not less than

(a) 10 m if the semi-trailer is equipped with a tandem axle group and its length is less than 14.5 m;

(b) 9 m if the semi-trailer is equipped with a tridem axle group and its length is less than 14.5 m;

(c) 11.45 m if the semi-trailer is equipped with a tandem axle group and its length is not less than 14.5 m; or

(d) 10.9 m if the semi-trailer is equipped with a tridem axle group and its length is not less than 14.5 m;

(13) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer and of the trailer is 2 m or less;

(14) the length of the trailer, excluding the drawbar and coupling device, is 12.2 m to 16.2 m;

(15) the wheelbase of the trailer does not exceed 12.5 m and is not less than

(a) 10 m if the trailer is equipped with a tandem axle group at the rear and its length is less than 14.5 m, excluding the drawbar and coupling device;

(b) 9 m if the trailer is equipped with a tridem axle group at the rear and its length is less than 14.5 m, excluding the drawbar and coupling device; or

(c) 10.2 m if the length of the trailer is not less than 14.5 m, excluding the drawbar and coupling device;

(16) the trailer device setback of the semi-trailer is

(a) 3.4 m or less if the semi-trailer is equipped with a tridem axle group whose axle spread is 3.6 m to 3.7 m;

(b) 2.8 m or less in other cases;

(17) the effective rear overhang of the trailer is 35% or less of the full trailer’s wheelbase;

(18) the sum of recorded axle loads for the tandem axle group at the rear of the tractor and for the axle group of the semi-trailer is greater than the sum of recorded axle loads for the axle groups of the trailer;

(19) the maximum total loaded mass is the lesser of the following values:

(a) the sum of the maximum load per axle or axle group under section 39 of the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation, without exceeding 5,500 kg for class E.1;

(b) 55,000 kg if the length of the semi-trailer is less than 14.5 m;

(c) 67,500 kg.

**5** A B double train must have the following characteristics:

(1) the length of the double train is 40 m or less;

(2) the tractor is equipped with a steering axle at the front and a tandem axle group at the rear;

(3) the interaxle spacing of the tractor is not less than 3.5 m;

(4) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.2 m or less;

(5) the axle spread of a tandem axle group is 1.2 m to 1.85 m;

(6) the axle spread of a tridem axle group is 2.4 m to 3.7 m;

(7) the semi-trailers are equipped with a tandem or tridem axle group;

(8) the length of the first semi-trailer is 12 m to 16.2 m, excluding the space reserved for the fifth wheel at the rear;

(9) the length of the second semi-trailer is 12 m to 16.2 m;

(10) the kingpin setbacks of the semi-trailers are 2 m or less each;

(11) the wheelbase of the first semi-trailer is 13.5 m or less;

(12) the wheelbase of the second semi-trailer is 12.5 m or less;

(13) the effective rear overhang of the second semi-trailer is 35% or less of the second semi-trailer’s wheelbase;

(14) the recorded axle load for the tandem axle group at the rear of the tractor is greater than the recorded axle load for the axle group of the second semi-trailer;

(15) the maximum total loaded mass is the lesser of the following values:

(a) the sum of the maximum load per axle or axle group under section 39 of the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation (chapter C-24.2, r. 31.1), without exceeding 5,500 kg for class E.1;

(b) 67,500 kg.

**6** A double train designed to transport motor vehicles must have the following characteristics:

(1) the double train consists of a towing vehicle and 2 type 2 semi-trailers;

(2) the length of the double train is 40 m or less;

(3) the interaxle spacing of the towing vehicle is not less than 3.5 m;

(4) the effective rear overhang of the towing vehicle is

(a) 4.6 m or less, including the cargo and extensions;

(b) 4 m or less, excluding the cargo and extensions;

(5) the towing vehicle trailer device setback is 1.9 m or less;

(6) the length of the towing vehicle is 12.5 m or less, including the cargo and extensions;

(7) the towing vehicle is equipped with a steering axle and a tandem axle group whose axle spread is 1.2 m to 1.85 m;

(8) the trailer device setback of the first semi-trailer is 2.4 m or less;

(9) the axle group of the first semi-trailer is a tridem axle group whose axle spread is 2.4 m to 2.8 m;

(10) the axle group of the second semi-trailer is a tandem axle group whose axle spread is 1.2 m to 3.1 m;

(11) the length of each type 2 semi-trailer is 14.65 m or less;

(12) the wheelbase of the first type 2 semi-trailer is 11.35 m to 12.5 m;

(13) the wheelbase of the second semi-trailer is 10 m to 12.5 m;

(14) the effective rear overhang of each semi-trailer, excluding the cargo and extensions, is the higher of the following values:

(a) 42% of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase;

(b) 4 m;

(15) the portion of the cargo and extensions overhang exceeding the length of each semi-trailer is not wider than 2.3 m, is centred widthwise on the semi-trailer and exceeds the rear of the semi-trailer by 1.2 m or less;

(16) any extensions are retracted when not supporting at least one of the wheels of a motor vehicle being transported;

(17) the cargo consists only of motor vehicles;

(18) the recorded axle load for the tandem axle group of the towing vehicle and the recorded axle load for the tridem axle group of the first type 2 semi-trailer are each greater than the recorded axle load for the tandem axle group of the second type 2 semi-trailer;

(19) the maximum total loaded mass is the lesser of the following values:

(a) the sum of the maximum load per axle or axle group under section 39 of the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation (chapter C-24.2, r. 31.1), without exceeding 5,500 kg for class E.1;

(b) 55,000 kg.

**7** A road train consisting of a tractor, a type 1 semi-trailer and a dolly must have the following characteristics:

(1) the length of the road train is 27.5 m or less;

(2) the length of the road train, excluding the dolly, is 23 m or less;

(3) the tractor is equipped with a steering axle at the front and a tandem axle group at the rear;

(4) the interaxle of the tractor is not less than 3.5 m;

(5) the wheelbase of the tractor is 6.2 m or less;

(6) the semi-trailer is equipped with a tandem or tridem axle group or a class E.13 or E.14 axle group established in Schedule III to the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation (chapter C-24.2, r. 31.1);

(7) the dolly is equipped with a tandem axle group;

(8) the length of the first semi-trailer is 16.2 m or less;

(9) the kingpin setback of the semi-trailer is 2 m or less;

(10) the wheelbase of the semi-trailer is 12.5 m or less;

(11) the effective rear overhang of the semi-trailer is 35% or less of the semi-trailer’s wheelbase;

(12) the maximum total loaded mass is the lesser of the following values:

(a) the sum of the maximum load per axle or axle group under section 39 of the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation, without exceeding 5,500 kg for class E.1;

(b) the maximum total loaded mass authorized for the tractor and the semi-trailer by the Vehicle Load and Size Limits Regulation, plus 2,000 kg.

### II — CONDITION APPLICABLE TO THE APPLICANT

**8** To obtain a special permit, the applicant must be registered in the register of owners and operators of heavy vehicles with a “satisfactory” safety rating under the Act respecting owners, operators and drivers of heavy vehicles (chapter P-30.3), or hold a safety fitness certificate or a similar document recognized under the Motor Vehicle Transport Act (R.S.C. 1985, c. 29 (3rd Suppl.)).

## III — FORMALITIES FOR OBTAINING A SPECIAL PERMIT AND PAYABLE FEES

**9** To obtain a special permit, the applicant must provide the information required by section 10 and pay the fees under section 12.

**10** To obtain a special permit, an applicant must provide the following information:

(1) the applicant’s name, address and telephone number;

(2) the applicant’s identification number in the register of owners and operators of heavy vehicles, the number of the applicant’s safety fitness certificate, or the number of a similar document recognized under the Motor Vehicle Transport Act (R.S.C. 1985, c. 29 (3rd Suppl.));

(3) the name of the person responsible for the customer account, and the person’s telephone number and email address;

(4) the licence plate number of any vehicle in the road train or, failing that, the identification number of one of those vehicles;

(5) the period for which the permit is applied for.

**11** The application for a special permit must be made using the form available on the website of the ministère des Transports.

**12** The fees for the issue of a special permit are those obtained by adding

(1) the fees payable under subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph of section 16 of the Regulation respecting special permits (chapter C-24.2, r. 35); and

(2) the product obtained by multiplying the number of months included in the period for which the permit will be applicable by the monthly fees. The monthly fees are those obtained by dividing by 12 the difference between the fees payable under subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph of section 17 of the Regulation respecting special permits and the fees payable under subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph of section 16 of that Regulation.

## IV — CONDITIONS APPLICABLE TO SPECIAL PERMITS

### I — CONDITIONS APPLICABLE AT ALL TIMES

**13** A special permit is valid for a maximum of 12 consecutive months.

**14** When a double train referred to in sections 4 to 6 is travelling, a sign bearing the message shown in Schedule I and meeting the characteristics referred to in Schedule II must be affixed to the rear.

The sign referred to in the first paragraph must be mounted horizontally at an angle of 90° to the semi-trailer longitudinal axis and be free of any object or substance that could impair its legibility. If the sign is a banner, it must be tightly stretched.

If the sign does not respect the requirements of the second paragraph or the presence of dirt on the sign makes it difficult to read, a peace officer may require the driver of a double train to correct the deficiency found or clean the sign. The driver must comply with the requirement of the peace officer.

**15** A special permit authorizes a road train to travel only on the following roads:

(1) divided lane autoroutes and their entrance and exit ramps;

(2) road segments linking the exit and entrance ramps of an autoroute in opposite directions;

(3) roads giving access to a municipal industrial park from an autoroute exit or entrance ramp, for a distance of not more than 2 km;

(4) roads not referred to in subparagraph 3 from an autoroute exit or entrance ramp, for a distance of not more than 500 m;

(5) roads in a municipal industrial park.

The special permit does not authorize the holder of the permit to take Exit 174 or 203 on Autoroute 40 to enter or exit the autoroute.

For the purposes of subparagraphs 3 and 4 of the first paragraph, the distance from the autoroute is measured at the junction of the autoroute exit or entrance ramp with another road.

For the purposes of subparagraphs 3 and 5 of the first paragraph, **municipal industrial park** means an industrial or technological zone designated by a municipality as an industrial or technological park.

**16** A holder of a special permit must

(1) operate the tractor forming part of the road train as an “operator” within the meaning of section 2 of the Act respecting owners, operators and drivers of heavy vehicles (chapter P-30.3);

(2) be registered in the register of owners and operators of heavy vehicles with a “satisfactory” safety rating under the Act respecting owners, operators and drivers of heavy vehicles;

(3) ensure that the road train the permit holder allows to travel meets the characteristics referred to in the first paragraph of section 3;

(4) ensure that the road train the permit holder allows to travel is referred to in any of sections 4 to 7 and that it meets the characteristics referred to in any of those sections;

(5) ensure that the road train is permitted to safely travel on the roads referred to in section 15, having regard in particular to authorized dimensions, weather conditions, and the condition of the road network at the time of the trip;

(6) ensure that the driver of the road train complies with paragraphs 3 and 4 of section 17 and section 22;

(7) notify the Minister within 2 days of any accident or traffic jam caused by the road train, specifying the circumstances of the incident;

(8) keep for 60 days the data recorded by the speed recorder with geolocation referred to in subparagraph 5 of the first paragraph of section 3;

(9) provide, at the request of the Minister, a highway controller or any other peace officer, the data recorded over a period of up to 60 days by the speed recorder with geolocation referred to in subparagraph 5 of the first paragraph of section 3 and with which the vehicles is equipped;

(10) provide, at the request of the Minister, the following data on the carrying out of transportation having occurred within 60 days before the application:

(a) the licence plate number of the tractor of the road train;

(b) the number of the special permit;

(c) the date of the trip;

(d) the place and time of departure;

(e) the place and time of arrival;

(f) the number of each autoroute used.

**17** The driver of a road train must

(1) travel at a maximum speed of 90 km per hour;

(2) travel a distance of at least 150 m from any road vehicle preceding it, except where passing is necessary;

(3) travel only on authorized roads as provided in section 15;

(4) travel only when the roadway is free from snow and ice and when visibility is not less than 500 m; and

(5) take the necessary precautions to ensure that the trip planned by the licence holder can take place safely, having regard in particular to authorized dimensions, weather conditions, and the condition of the road network at the time the road train will travel.

### II — ADDITIONAL CONDITIONS APPLICABLE BETWEEN 1 NOVEMBER AND 31 MARCH

**18** Where a special permit includes a period between 1 November and 31 March, the permit holder must prepare, for each planned trip, a document that contains the safe stopping locations where the road train may stop in the event of unforeseeable deterioration of the weather, road conditions or visibility.

**19** Each stopping location mentioned in section 18 must be identified by using its address or, failing an address, by a description making it easy to find the stopping location in an emergency and the route to follow to get there from the autoroute exit ramp.

A safe stopping location must

(1) allow safe parking or uncoupling of the road train;

(2) be accessible by an authorized road offering the necessary space to safely manoeuver the road train;

(3) be open and accessible at all times; and

(4) be situated 50 km or less from the departure location, another stopping location or the destination.

The following are not safe stopping locations:

(1) stations used for the control of highway transportation of persons and goods;

(2) autoroute exit or access ramps;

(3) autoroute shoulders.

**20** At the request of the Minister, a highway controller or any other peace officer, a permit holder must provide the document on safe stopping locations prepared in accordance with section 18, for each trip planned between 1 November and 31 March.

**21** A permit holder who operates a road train between 1 November and 31 March must update, as of 1 October and not later than 31 October, the list of safe stopping locations prepared as provided in section 18.

The permit holder must also give the driver of a road train a copy of the list of safe stopping locations updated in accordance with the first paragraph.

**22** Between 1 November and 31 March, the driver of a road train must

(1) provide, at the request of a highway controller or any other peace officer, the list of the safe stopping locations given by the permit holder; and

(2) travel only on autoroutes for which safe stopping locations have been identified in the document.

## V — PENAL

**23** A driver of a road train who fails to comply with a request made by a peace officer in accordance with the third paragraph of section 14 is liable to a fine of $90 to $270.

**24** A driver of a road train who travels elsewhere than on the roads listed in subparagraphs 1 to 5 of the first paragraph of section 15 is liable to a fine of $350 to $1,050.

**25** A holder of a special road train operating permit who contravenes any of paragraphs 1 to 10 of section 16 or sections 18, 20 and 21 is liable to a fine of $350 to $1,050.

**26** A driver of a road train who contravenes the first or second paragraph of section 14 or section 17 or 22 is liable to a fine of $175 to $525.

## VI — TRANSITIONAL

**27** Up to 31 December 2029, the tractor of a road train must comply with either the characteristics described in subparagraphs 2 and 3 of the first paragraph of section 3 or with the following:

(1) it has a minimum horsepower of 1 hp per 180 kg of the road train’s total loaded mass and is equipped with an air compressor with a capacity of not less than 425 litres per minute supplying the braking system;

(2) it is equipped with a driver monitoring system that records significant speed variations and relevant data on the date, time and speed.

**28** Up to 31 December 2029, the tractor of a road train is not bound to comply with the characteristics described in subparagraphs 4 and 5 of the first paragraph of section 3.

**29** Up to 31 December 2029, the dolly of a road train is not bound, despite subparagraph 6 of the first paragraph of section 3, to comply with the requirement to be equipped with an overpressure protection valve.

**30** Up to 31 December 2029, an A double train referred to in section 4 is not bound to comply with

(1) paragraphs 1 and 11 of that section;

(2) the minimum wheelbase referred to in paragraphs 12 and 15 of that section if the length of the semi-trailer is not less than 13.5 m;

(3) the trailer device setback referred to in paragraph 16 of that section if the length of the semi-trailer is not less than 13.5 m.

**31** Up to 31 December 2029, the minimum length of the trailer, excluding the drawbar and coupling device, referred to in paragraph 14 of section 4 is 12 m.

**32** Up to 31 December 2029, subparagraph *b* of paragraph 19 of section 4 need not be applied if the length of the semi-trailer is not less than 13.5 m.

**33** Up to 31 December 2029, a B double train referred to in section 5 is not bound to comply with paragraphs 1 and 11 of that section.

**34** Up to 31 December 2029, a road train referred to in section 7 is not bound to comply with paragraph 1 of that section.

## VII — FINAL

**35** Special Road Train Operating Permits Regulation

The Special Road Train Operating Permits Regulation (chapter C-24.2, r. 36) is revoked.

**36** (Omitted).
