*CQLR c CCQ, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Civil Code of Québec (Civil Code, art. 1614)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The discount rates applicable to the calculation of the damages owed to the creditor for the bodily injury he sustains are, as to the future aspects of the injury,

(1) for losses resulting from a decrease in earning capacity and progression of income, salary or wages: 2%; and

(2) for other loss resulting from inflation: 3,25%.

**2** (Omitted).
