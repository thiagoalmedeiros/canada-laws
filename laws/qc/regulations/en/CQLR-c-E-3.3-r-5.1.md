*CQLR c E-3.3, r 5.1*

*Enabling statute: Election Act (chapter E-3.3, ss. 488.1 and 540.1)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** This regulation applies to supply contracts and service contracts of the Commission de la représentation.

**2** The provisions of the Regulation respecting contracts of the Chief Electoral Officer (chapter E-3.3, r. 6.1), approved by the Office of the National Assembly on 24 February 2011 apply, adapted as required, to the contracts of the Commission de la représentation.

**3** (Omitted).
