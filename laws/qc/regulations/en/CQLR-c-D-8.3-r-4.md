*CQLR c D-8.3, r 4*

*Enabling statute: Act to promote workforce skills development and recognition (chapter D-8.3, s. 3)*

*Updated to 2016-05-18*

**1** Every employer whose total payroll for a calendar year exceeds $2,000,000 is required to participate for that year in the development of manpower training as provided in section 3 of the Act to promote workforce skills development and recognition (chapter D-8.3).

**2** This Regulation replaces the Regulation respecting the determination of total payroll (O.C. 1585-95, 95-12-06).

**3** (Omitted).
