*CQLR c S-3.1, r 2*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting safety in recreation and sports (chapter S-3.1, s. 54)*

*Updated to 2024-06-07*

**1** Any person participating in an ice hockey activity with a team from outside Canada is excluded from the application of paragraph 1 of section 1 of the Regulation respecting protective equipment for ice hockey (chapter S-3.1, r. 1) in that the safety helmet that he must wear does not have to comply with the standards mentioned therein.

Such person is also excluded from the application of paragraph 2 of section 1 of that Regulation.

**2** Any person participating in an ice hockey activity with a team from a Canadian province or territory other than Québec is excluded from the application of paragraph 2 of section 1 of that Regulation.

**3** Any person participating in an ice hockey activity with a team from outside Québec is excluded from the application of paragraph 3 of section 1 of that Regulation.

**4** (Revoked).

**5** (Omitted).
