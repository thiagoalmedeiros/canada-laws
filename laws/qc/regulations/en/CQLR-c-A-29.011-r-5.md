*CQLR c A-29.011, r 5*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting parental insurance (chapter A-29.011, s. 6)*

*Updated to 2026-04-08*

**1** The premium rate applicable to an employee and to a person referred to in section 51 of the Act is 0.455%.

The premium rate applicable to a self-employed worker and a family-type resource or intermediate resource is 0.808%.

The premium rate applicable to an employer is 0.636%.

> As of 1 January 2026, and until the next amendment to the rates referred to in this section, the premium rates applicable to the parental insurance plan are those provided for in section 9.1 of the Regulation respecting parental insurance plan premiums (

> chapter A-29.011, r. 3

> ).

**2** (Omitted).
