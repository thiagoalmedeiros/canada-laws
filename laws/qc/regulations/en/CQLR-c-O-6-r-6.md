# Regulation respecting terms and conditions for a permit to be issued by the Ordre des opticiens d’ordonnances du Québec

*Enabling statute: Dispensing Opticians Act (chapter O-6, s. 3)*

*Updated to 2026-04-02*

## I — DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.01** In this Regulation, unless the context indicates otherwise,

(a) **Order** means the Ordre des opticiens d’ordonnances du Québec;

(b) **candidate** means a person who requests his permit, who has complied with the terms and conditions required under this Regulation and who is the holder of a diploma recognized by the Office des professions du Québec under the first paragraph of section 12.0.1.1 of the Professional Code (chapter C-26) or recognized equivalent by the board of directors, or whose training has been recognized equivalent by the board of directors;

(c) **secretary** means the secretary of the Order.

**1.02** The Interpretation Act (chapter I-16) applies to this Regulation.

## II — GENERAL PROVISION

**2.01** Every candidate, in addition to the conditions required by the Dispensing Opticians Act (chapter O-6), must, in order to obtain a permit, comply with the following conditions:

(a) submit an application for a permit made in the form in Schedule 1;

(b) pass the professional examination of the Order.

## III — PROFESSIONAL EXAMINATION

**3.01** The professional examination is held in Montréal, at least once a year, on the dates determined by the board of directors.

**3.02** At least 10 days prior to the date of the professional examination, the candidate must deposit the sum of 50 $ at the office of the Order.

**3.03** The professional examination shall be of a duration of 3 hours and shall deal with professional laws and regulations respecting the practice of the profession of dispensing optician.

**3.04** To pass the professional examination, the candidate must obtain a mark of 60%. Where he does not obtain a mark of 60%, the candidate may take a supplementary examination to be held not later than 3 months after the date of the last examination.

**3.05** The secretary shall communicate the result of the professional examination to the candidate by mail.

**3.06** The secretary shall forward to the board of directors the record of the candidate who has passed the professional examination and recommend that the board of directors issue a permit to the candidate.

**3.07** Where the board of directors has issued a permit to a candidate, the secretary shall notify the latter that, following payment of the fee fixed by the board of directors, he shall make his entry on the roll.
