# Regulation respecting the application of sections 61.2 and 61.3 of the Act respecting the preservation of agricultural land and agricultural activities

*CQLR c P-41.1, r 0.2*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting the preservation of agricultural land and agricultural activities (chapter P-41.1, s. 61.4, 2nd par.)*

*Updated to 2026-02-26*

**1** The following uses are not considered to be an application for exclusion under sections 61.2 and 61.3 of the Act:

(1) development of a parking lot;

(2) holding a festival or event;

(3) commercial storage without infrastructure;

(4) storage of fertilizing residuals at the farm;

(5) operating a bio-methanization plant at the farm, provided the inputs are agricultural products;

(6) operating a sand pit, gravel pit, quarry or mine;

(7) operating a hot mix asphalt plant on an operating site for which an authorization has been obtained or is pending;

(8) carrying out prospecting work, including the drilling of a well;

(9) carrying out filling work;

(10) development of wildlife habitats without infrastructure;

(11) removal of topsoil;

(12) establishment of a servitude of public utility;

(13) operating a snow dump;

(14) development of a wind farm;

(15) construction of a power line;

(16) construction of a control and transformer station as well as an energy storage system;

(17) development of a solar power station;

(18) installation of a wind measurement mast or a meteorological station;

(19) construction of a pipeline;

(20) construction of a navigational marker;

(21) construction of a boat ramp;

(22) implementing several new residential uses in a sub-sector for which applications are admissible identified in a decision of collective scope rendered under section 59 of the Act respecting the preservation of agricultural land and agricultural activities (chapter P-41.1);

(23) use of an equestrian centre not referred to in section 12 of the Regulation respecting the authorization for the alienation or use of a lot without the authorization of the Commission de protection du territoire agricole du Québec (chapter P-41.1, r. 1.1);

(24) expansion of a use for a purpose other than agriculture, for which an authorization has been obtained, on the same lot or a contiguous lot;

(25) continued use for a purpose other than agriculture for which an authorization has already been obtained.

**2** (Omitted).
