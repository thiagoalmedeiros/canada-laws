# Regulation respecting the tuition fees that a general and vocational college must charge

*CQLR c C-29, r 2*

*Enabling statute: General and Vocational Colleges Act (chapter C-29, s. 24.4)*

*Updated to 2024-07-01*

## I — STATUS OF STUDENT

**1** For the purposes of section 24 of the General and Vocational Colleges Act (chapter C-29), a student is deemed to be a full-time student if

(1) the student, in any of his last 2 terms, was registered for at least 4 courses in a program of college studies or for courses totalling at least 180 periods of instruction in such a program and has a maximum of 3 courses remaining to complete the training prescribed by the program;

(2) the student has a major functional deficiency within the meaning of the Regulation respecting financial assistance for education expenses (chapter A-13.3, r. 1) and, for that reason, is in a program of college studies as a part-time student within the meaning of the Act respecting financial assistance for education expenses (chapter A-13.3); or

(3) the student, at the start of the term, was registered for at least 4 courses in a program of college studies or for courses totalling at least 180 periods of instruction in such a program, and remained registered for at least 20% of the duration of that term or those periods of instruction before withdrawing from a course and, as a result, falling below that minimum requirement.

A student deemed to be a full-time student under subparagraph 1 of the first paragraph may be granted such status for 1 term only unless he proves, with supporting documents, that during that term, he was unable to devote himself fully to his studies because of serious reasons such as an illness or the death of his spouse or of a family member, or is unable to complete the program of studies because a required course is not offered until the following term.

## II — TUITION FEES

**2** The tuition fee payable under the first paragraph of section 24.2 of the Act is $2 per period of instruction for a program of studies leading to a Diploma of College Studies.

**3** The tuition fees collected for a course in a program of college studies shall be reimbursed in full where a student withdraws from the course not later than on the date calculated pursuant to subparagraph 3 of the first paragraph of section 1.

## III — PENALTIES

**4** A student who fails to pay all or part of the fee payable under section 2 or who delays payment thereof shall not be awarded credits for any of the courses for which he was registered for as long as the failure to pay or delayed payment persists.

## IV — FINAL

**5** This Regulation replaces the Regulation respecting the tuition fees and special fees which a general and vocational college must charge (O.C. 1016-97, 97-08-13).
