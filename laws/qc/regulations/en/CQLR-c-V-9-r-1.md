# Regulation respecting old car dumps and dumps along roadsides

*CQLR c V-9, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting roads (chapter V-9, s. 42)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** All old car dumps and dumps visible from roadsides maintained by the Minister of Transport shall be surrounded by one of the following fences:

(a) a complete fence made of wood and painted or dyed “redwood”;

(b) a perforated wooden fence, made of vertical or horizontal boards, at least 15 cm wide, and separated by at least 3 cm, painted or dyed “redwood”;

(c) a complete fence of corrugated and galvanized sheet metal panels, painted aluminum;

(d) a complete fence of fiber glass;

(e) a complete fence of asbestos panels.

**2** The top of the fence will be at least 2.40 m above the natural land, and the bottom at a maximum height of 30.50 cm.

**3** No gate or opening will be set in that part of the fence which lies along the road maintained by the Minister of Transport.

**4** In the sense of this Regulation, the word “fence” includes any hedge in a good state, of spruce, cedar or pine as well as any other natural barrier which effectively conceals a dump which might be visible from a road maintained by the Minister of Transport. These hedges or natural barriers must conform, with respect to height, to the standards set forth in section 2.

**5** The fences, hedges or barriers must be maintained in good state.
