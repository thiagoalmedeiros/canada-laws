# Ministerial Order concerning the approval of the cameras used to photograph the registration plates of road vehicles crossing bridge P-15020 on Autoroute 25

Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/C-24.2,%20r.%203.1.1) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). *CQLR c C-24.2, r 3.1.1*

*Enabling statute: Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2, s. 595.1)*

*Updated to 2026-04-01*

**1** The cameras used to photograph the registration plates of road vehicles crossing bridge P-15020 on Autoroute 25 are approved, the main components of which being as follows:

(a) fully-redundant zone controller with detection by suspended laser scanner (LMS511-10100S01 by SICK AG) and by fibre-optic detection loops (SL PUR 350-1-35 PE and SL PUR 350-1-100 PE by Sensor Line GmbH) connected to a detector (SL MA-110 with a single-channel electronic interface by Sensor Line GmbH);

(b) electronic toll system with radio frequency identification, including in particular 15 dBi linear polarization antennas (Avior by STAR Systems International Ltd.), multiprotocol readers (Titan Pro by STAR Systems International Ltd.) with a control board toggle switch (HMS 19037 by STAR Systems International Ltd.) and type B or C transponders meeting ISO/CEI Standard 18000-6, Information technology – Radio frequency identification for item management – Part 6: General Parameters for air interface communications at 860 MHz to 930 MHz General, published jointly by the International Organization for Standardization and the International Electrotechnical Commission;

(c) camera identification system (Smart+ 55 cameras by Tattile S.r.l.) and camera monitoring (P1385-E by Axis Communications AB or second-generation 1440p Quasar Quad HD sWDR mini-dome camera (CM-6204-11-I) operated by the DVTEL system of FLIR Systems Inc.).

**2** The Approval of the cameras used to photograph the registration plates of road vehicles crossing bridge P-15020 on Autoroute 25 (chapter C-24.2, r. 3.1) is revoked.

**3** (Omitted).
