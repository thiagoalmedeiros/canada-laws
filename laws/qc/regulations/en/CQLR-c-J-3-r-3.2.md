# Regulation respecting the Tariff of administrative fees, professional fees and other charges attached to proceedings before the Administrative Tribunal of Québec

*CQLR c J-3, r 3.2*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting administrative justice (chapter J-3, s. 92)*

*Updated to 2026-01-01*

> The amounts prescribed in the Tariff have been indexed as of 1 January 2026 pursuant to the notice published in Part 1 (French) of the Gazette officielle du Québec of 27 December 2025, page 718. (ss. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9)

## I — ADMINISTRATIVE FEES FOR THE FILING OF A MOTION INSTITUTING PROCEEDINGS

### I — IMMOVABLE PROPERTY DIVISION

**1** The fees payable for the presentation of a motion instituting proceedings under Chapter X of the Act respecting municipal taxation (chapter F-2.1) are

(1) (paragraph revoked);

(2) (a) $93.20, where the value entered on the roll is less than or equal to $500,000;

(b) $372.60, where the value entered on the roll is greater than $500,000 but less than or equal to $2,000,000;

(c) $621.10, where the value entered on the roll is greater than $2,000,000 but less than or equal to $5,000,000;

(d) $1,242.50, where the value entered on the roll is greater than $5,000,000.

**2** The fees payable by an expropriating party for the filing of a copy of a notice of expropriation with the Tribunal are $248.50.

**3** The fees payable for the presentation of a motion instituting proceedings under the Act respecting expropriation (chapter E-25) to determine the amount of indemnities arising from the establishment of a reserve for public purposes are $93.20.

**4** The fees payable for the presentation of a motion instituting the proceedings in Schedule II to the Act respecting administrative justice (chapter J-3), other than those referred to in paragraphs 4 and 5, are $93.20.

### II — TERRITORY AND ENVIRONMENT DIVISION

**5** The fees payable for the presentation of a motion instituting proceedings under Schedule III to the Act respecting administrative justice (chapter J-3) are $93.20.

### III — ECONOMIC AFFAIRS DIVISION

**6** The fees payable for the presentation of a motion instituting proceedings under Schedule IV to the Act respecting administrative justice (chapter J-3) are $93.20.

## II — ADMINISTRATIVE FEES FOR CERTAIN ACCESSORY PROCEEDINGS

**7** The fees payable for an application for the taxation of a bill of costs in matters of municipal taxation or expropriation, as well as those related to its contestation, are $31.05.

**8** In the course of proceedings in matters of municipal taxation or expropriation, witnesses are indemnified in accordance with the Regulation respecting indemnities and allowances payable to witnesses summoned before courts of justice (chapter C-25.01, r. 0.5).

**9** The fees payable for the presentation of a motion for a special fee under section 15 of the Tariff of judicial fees of advocates (chapter B-1, r. 22) are $31.05.

## III — PROFESSIONAL FEES

**10** In matters of municipal taxation and expropriation, the fees for the taking down and transcription of depositions of witness, where applicable, are those fixed by the Tariff of fees for the recording and transcription of depositions of witnesses (chapter S-33, r. 1).

## IV — GENERAL

**11** A drawer of a cheque not honoured by the institution on which it is drawn must reimburse the Tribunal for any fees charged by the institution.

**12** (Omis).
