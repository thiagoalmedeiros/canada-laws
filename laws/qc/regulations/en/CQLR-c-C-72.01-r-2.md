*CQLR c C-72.01, r 2*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting municipal courts (chapter C-72.01, ss. 77 and 118)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** The Tariff of court costs in civil matters and court office fees applies to every municipal court for civil matters over which the court has jurisdiction.

**2** The costs and fees established by this Regulation apply to proceedings and documents filed and issued on or after 10 July 2003.

**3** This Regulation replaces the Tariff of court costs in civil matters and court office fees applicable before municipal courts other than the municipal courts of the cities of Laval, Montréal and Québec (O.C. 178-97, 97-02-12).

**4** (Omitted).
