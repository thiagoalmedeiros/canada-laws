*CQLR c A-29.01, r 3*

*Enabling statute: Act respecting prescription drug insurance (chapter A-29.01, s. 60)*

*Updated to 2026-09-24*

**1** The list of insured medications, drawn up by the Minister of Health and Social Services under section 60 of the Act respecting prescription drug insurance (chapter A-29.01), is the list appearing in Schedule 1 to this Regulation.

**2** This Regulation replaces the Regulation respecting the List of medications covered by the basic prescription drug insurance plan (M.O. 2007-002, 07-03-19).

**3** (Omitted).
