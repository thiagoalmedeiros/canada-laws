# Regulation respecting the form and content of the articles, certificates and other documents required to be deposited and filed under Part IA of the Companies Act

*CQLR c S-31.1, r 0.1*

*Enabling statute: Business Corporations Act (chapter S-31.1, s. 725)*

*Updated to 2012-09-01*

**1** In this Regulation, unless the context otherwise requires, the word “documents” means the articles, certificates and other documents required to be deposited under Part IA of the Companies Act (chapter C-38), and that must be filed with the enterprise registrar.

**2** The documents must be:

(a) drawn up on white, No. 7 Bond paper measuring 216 mm by 280 mm and having a gram weight or mass of at least 75 to 90 g/m^2;

(b) printed or typewritten;

(c) legible and made suitable for reproduction by microfilm and photocopy.

**3** Each subject treated must be preceded with an appropriate title.

**4** When annexes are joined to the documents filed with the enterprise registrar, they are deemed to form an integral part of the document.

**5** The following margins must be observed throughout the document:

(a) top and lower margins: 25 mm;

(b) left-hand and right-hand margins: 12 mm.

**6** Nothing must be written on the back of any page in the document.

**7** (Obsolete).
