*CQLR c P-9.002, r 4*

*Enabling statute: Cultural Heritage Act (chapter P-9.002, s. 5 and s. 81, par. 1)*

*Updated to 2012-10-19*

> The former alphanumerical designation of this Regulation was: chapter B-4, r. 4.

**1** (Implicitly revoked, 2011, chapter 21, s. 262).

**2** **Certified extract:** Subject to the second paragraph of section 89 of the Act, the Minister of Culture, Communications and the Status of Women shall issue to any interested person a certified extract of entries of heritage property in the cultural heritage register.

The fee for each certified extract shall be fixed at $1, payable to the order of the Minister of Finance of Québec.
