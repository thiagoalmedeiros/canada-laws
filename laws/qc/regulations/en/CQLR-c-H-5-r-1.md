# Regulation respecting the rates for using the public fast-charging service for electric vehicles

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Gouvernement du Québec. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Quebec. Text obtained from the [official source](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/H-5,%20r.%201) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

*CQLR c H-5, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Hydro-Québec Act (chapter H-5, s. 22.0.2)*

*Updated to 2026-01-01*

> The rates prescribed in the Regulation have been indexed as of 1 January 2026 pursuant to the notice published in Part 1 (French) of the Gazette officielle du Québec of 27 December 2025, page 719. (Sch. I)

**1** The rates for using the public fast-charging service for electric vehicles are those provided for in Schedule I. The rates are fixed, as the case may be, on an hourly basis or based on the quantity of electricity supplied to the user in kilowatt-hours (kWh). The rates vary according to the power of the station used and, as the case may be, according to the conditions determined in the Schedule.

**2** The rates are adjusted by operation of law on 1 January of each year by a rate corresponding to the annual variation in the average all-items Consumer Price Index for Québec excluding alcoholic beverages, tobacco products and recreational cannabis for the 12-month period ending on 30 September of the year preceding the year for which the rates are to be adjusted. That rate may not be less than zero.

The Minister publishes the results of the adjustment, which must be rounded to the nearest whole cent, in the *Gazette officielle du Québec*.

**3** (Omitted).
