## V — SERVICES PROGRAM

**20** A handicapped person who applies to the Office for the preparation of a service program must provide the following information:

(a) his name at birth;

(b) his date of birth;

(c) his sex;

(d) his permanent address;

(e) the nature of the services required.

**21** The handicapped person may in his application recommend measures to facilitate his educational, vocational and social integration.

**22** For a handicapped person to be eligible for a service program, the Office takes into account:

(a) his functional ability;

(b) his degree of self-sufficiency;

(c) his living environment;

(d) his potential for educational integration;

(e) his potential for vocational integration;

(f) his potential for social integration.
