*CQLR c C-24.2, r 27.01*

*Enabling statute: Highway Safety Code (chapter C-24.2, s. 463, 2nd par.; 2008, c. 14, s. 54, pars. 1 and 2)*

*Updated to 2026-01-24*

> The fees prescribed in the Regulation have been indexed pursuant to the notice published in Part 1 (French) of the Gazette officielle du Québec of 24 January 2026, page 61. (s. 1) (Effect from 1 January 2026)

**1** The fee exigible is $13.50 for the issue of a special permit and $5.45 for its replacement.

**2** The fees provided for in section 1 are adjusted by operation of law on 11 February 2019 by the rate provided for in section 83.3 of the Financial Administration Act (chapter A-6.001). The first paragraph of section 83.5 of that Act and the Regulation respecting the rounding off of adjusted fees (chapter A-6.001, r. 0.1) apply to that adjustment.

The Minister of Transport publishes the results of the adjustment in the *Gazette officielle du Québec*.

For the purposes of the first paragraph of section 83.4 of the Financial Administration Act on 1 January 2020, the adjustment provided for in section 2 is deemed to have been made under section 83.3 of that Act.

**3** (Omitted).
