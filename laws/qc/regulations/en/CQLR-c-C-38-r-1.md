*CQLR c C-38, r 1*

*Enabling statute: Companies Act (chapter C-38, s. 123.139.4)*

*Updated to 2025-06-07*

**1** The articles of continuance of a cooperative or a cooperative syndicate must be accompanied by the following documents:

(1) a copy, certified by the applicant company, of the plan of continuance submitted to the Minister of Economy, Innovation and Energy; and

(2) a certified copy of the document recording the approval prescribed in section 259 of the Cooperatives Act (chapter C-67.2).

**2** (Omitted).
