*CQLR c J-1.03*

*Updated to 2024-02-01*

AS, each year, nearly 23,000 families in Québec are affected by the loss of a child during pregnancy, childbirth or the early postnatal period;

AS Québec society remains largely unaware of perinatal bereavement despite the prevalence of perinatal deaths and their psychosocial impacts;

AS, in Québec, elsewhere in Canada and internationally, several civil society stakeholders and States already recognize 15 October as the day for perinatal bereavement awareness;

AS it is appropriate to show support to families affected by perinatal bereavement and to raise awareness about this reality among Quebecers;

THE PARLIAMENT OF QUÉBEC ENACTS AS FOLLOWS:

**1** The fifteenth day of October is proclaimed Québec Perinatal Bereavement Awareness Day.

**2** (Omitted).
