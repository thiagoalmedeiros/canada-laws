*CQLR c D-9.1.01*

*Updated to 2021-06-04*

THE PARLIAMENT OF QUÉBEC ENACTS AS FOLLOWS:

**1** The demise of the Crown does not terminate the activities of the Parliament of Québec, the Government or the courts, nor does it in any manner interrupt those activities.

In addition, the demise of the Crown does not terminate any office or employment.

**2** Oaths of allegiance or office need not be retaken due to the demise of the Crown.

**3** (Amendments integrated into c. E-6, ss. 7 and 8).

**4** (Omitted).
