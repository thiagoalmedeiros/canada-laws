SNWT 2015, c 3

### Preamble

Whereas the Constitution Act, 1982 recognizes

and affirms the existing aboriginal and treaty rights of

the aboriginal peoples of Canada;

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Métis

Comprehensive Land Claim Agreement , signed on

September 6, 1993, provides for the negotiation of

self-government agreements among the Sahtu Dene

and Métis, the Government of the Northwest

Territories and the Government of Canada;

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Métis

Comprehensive Land Claim Agreement provides that

self-government negotiations will address the desire of

the Sahtu Dene and Métis to have self-government

exercised as close to the community level as is

reasonably possible;

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Métis of Délîne , the

Government of the Northwest Territories and the

Government of Canada negotiated a self-government

agreement pursuant to chapter 5 and Appendix B of

the Sahtu Dene and Métis Comprehensive Land Claim

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Métis of Délîne

approved the Agreement through a ratification

vote held on March 10, 11 and 12, 2014;

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Métis of , as

represented by the Délîne First Nation Band and the

Délîne Land Corporation, the Government of the

Northwest Territories and the Government of Canada

signed the Agreement dated February 18, 2015;

Whereas the Délîne Agreement provides that it

will be a treaty within the meaning of sections 25

and 35 of the Constitution Act, 1982 ;

Whereas the Délîne Agreement requires that

legislation be enacted by the Legislature of the

Northwest Territories in order for the agreement to be

ratified;

The Commissioner of the Northwest Territories,

by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative

Assembly, enacts as follows:

## INTERPRETATION

### Definitions

1. (1) In this Act,

" Agreement " means the Final

Délîne Délîne

Self-Government Agreement among the Délîne First

Nation Band and the Délîne Land Corporation, the

Government of the Northwest Territories and the

Government of Canada dated February 18, 2015,

including any amendments made to it from time to

time; ( Accord de Délîne )

" Délîne Got’înê Government" means the government

established pursuant to chapter 3 of the Délîne

Agreement ; ( gouvernement Got’înê de Délîne )

" Délîne law" means laws of the Délîne Got’înê

Government made pursuant to the jurisdictions of that

government set out in the Délîne Agreement and

regulations made pursuant to such laws; ( loi de

Délîne )

" Délîne Tax Treatment Agreement " means the Délîne

Tax Treatment Agreement among the Délîne First

Nation Band and the Land Corporation, the

Délîne

Government of the Northwest Territories and the

Government of Canada, signed on behalf of the

Government of Canada on February 11, 2015, on

behalf of the Government of the Northwest Territories

on February 18, 2015, and on behalf of the Délîne

First Nation Band and the Délîne Land Corporation on

February 18, 2015, including any amendments made to

it from time to time; ( Accord sur le traitement fiscal

de Délîne )

"Northwest Territories law" has the same meaning as

"NWT Law" in 1.1.1 of the Délîne Agreement . (loi

des Territoires du Nord-Ouest )

### Defined terms

(2) Any word or expression defined in the Délîne

Agreement has the same meaning in this Act, unless

the context otherwise requires.

### Interpretation

(3) The Délîne Agreement may be used as an aid

in interpreting this Act or the regulations if there is any

doubt as to the meaning of one of its provisions.

### Binding on Government

2. This Act is binding on the Government of the

Northwest Territories.

### Inconsistency or conflict with land claim agreement

3. (1) In the case of any inconsistency or conflict

between the Sahtu Dene and Métis Comprehensive

Land Claim Agreement and the Délîne Agreement or

a Délîne law, the Sahtu Dene and Métis

Comprehensive Land Claim Agreement prevails to the

extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

### Conflict with Délîne Agreement

(2) In the case of any conflict between the Délîne

Agreement and any Northwest Territories law or

Délîne law, the Délîne Agreement prevails to the

extent of the conflict.

### Conflict with this Act and regulations

(3) In the case of any conflict between this Act

and the regulations and any other Northwest

Territories law, this Act and the regulations prevail to

the extent of the conflict.

## DÉLÎNE AGREEMENT

### Délîne Agreement given effect

4. (1) The Délîne Agreement is approved, given

effect and declared valid, and has the force of law.

### Rights and duties

(2) For greater certainty, any person or body may

exercise the powers, rights, privileges and benefits

conferred on the person or body by the Délîne

Agreement and shall perform the duties, and is subject

to the liabilities, imposed on the person or body by the

Délîne Agreement .

### Third parties

(3) For greater certainty, the Délîne Agreement is

binding on, and can be relied on by, persons and

bodies that are not parties to it.

### Certain portions to have earlier effect

5. Notwithstanding subsection 4(1), 2.5.1, 4.6,

Chapter 31 and 2.1, 2.2, 3.3, 5.1 and 5.2 of schedule C

of the Délîne Agreement are deemed to have effect as

of October 1, 2013.

## DÉLÎNE GOT’ÎNÊ GOVERNMENT

### Capacity

6. The Délîne Got’înê Government is a legal entity

and has the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of

a natural person.

### Force of law

7. Délîne law that is made in accordance with the

Délîne Agreement has the force of law.

### Statutory Instruments Act

8. Délîne laws are not statutory instruments for the

purposes of the Statutory Instruments Act .

## DÉLÎNE TAX TREATMENT AGREEMENT

### Tax agreement given effect

9. (1) The Tax Treatment Agreement is

Délîne

approved, given effect and declared valid, and has the

force of law during the period that it is in effect.

### Not a treaty

(2) The Délîne Tax Treatment Agreement does

not form part of the Délîne Agreement and is not a

treaty within the meaning of section 25 or 35 of the

Constitution Act, 1982 .

## JUDICIAL NOTICE

### Judicial notice of agreements

10. (1) Judicial notice shall be taken of the Délîne

Agreement and the Délîne Tax Treatment Agreement .

### Evidence

(2) A copy of the Délîne Agreement or the

Délîne Tax Treatment Agreement published by the

Queen’s Printer of Canada is evidence of that

agreement and of its contents, and a copy purporting to

be published by the Queen’s Printer of Canada is

deemed to be so published, unless the contrary is

shown.

### Judicial notice of Délîne laws

11. (1) Judicial notice shall be taken of Délîne laws.

### Evidence

(2) A copy of a Délîne law, registered in the

public registry of laws maintained by the

Délîne

Got’înê Government and certified as a true copy by a

duly authorized officer of the Délîne Got’înê

Government is, without proof of the officer’s signature

or official character, evidence of its making on the date

specified in the law.

## LEGAL PROCEEDINGS

### Jurisdiction of the Supreme Court

12. The Supreme Court has exclusive original

jurisdiction to hear and determine applications for

judicial review of the decisions of the Délîne Got’înê

Government, or the institutions of the Délîne Got’înê

Government created pursuant to 3.6.1b) of the

Délîne

Agreement , except in relation to tax matters where an

agreement entered into under 22.2.2 of the Délîne

Agreement provides otherwise.

### Notice of issues

13. (1) If, in any judicial or administrative

proceeding, an issue arises in respect of the

interpretation, validity or applicability of the Délîne

Agreement , this Act or the regulations or any Délîne

law, the issue shall not be decided until the party

raising the issue has properly served notice on the

Délîne Got’înê Government, the Attorney General of

the Northwest Territories and the Attorney General of

Canada.

### Contents of notice

(2) The notice required under subsection (1) must

(a) describe the judicial or administrative

proceeding in which the issue arises;

(b) specify what the issue arises in respect

of;

(c) state the day on which the issue is to be

argued;

(d) give particulars necessary to show the

point to be argued; and

(e) be served at least 14 days before the day

of argument, unless the court or tribunal

authorizes a shorter period.

### Rights of parties

(3) In any judicial or administrative proceeding to

which subsection (1) applies, the Délîne Got’înê

Government, the Attorney General of the Northwest

Territories and the Attorney General of Canada may

appear and participate in the proceeding as parties with

the same rights as any other party.

### Nature of hearing

(4) For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3)

do not require that an oral hearing be held, if one is not

otherwise required.

## ORDERS AND REGULATIONS

### Orders and regulations

14. The Commissioner in Executive Council may

make orders and regulations for the purpose of

carrying out any of the provisions of the Délîne

Agreement or the Délîne Tax Treatment Agreement .

## REFERENCES

### Reference to "municipal bylaw"

15. (1) Unless the context otherwise requires, a

reference in an enactment to a "municipal bylaw"

includes a reference to the following:

(a) a Délîne law that is made pursuant to

9.1.1 or 9.1.3 of the Délîne Agreement ;

(b) a bylaw that is deemed to be a Délîne

law under 30.2.2 of the Délîne

Agreement .

### Reference to "municipal corporation"

(2) Unless the context otherwise requires, a

reference in an enactment to a "municipal corporation"

includes a reference to the Délîne Got’înê

Government.

### Reference to "municipal council" or similar expression

(3) Unless the context otherwise requires, a

reference in an enactment to a "municipal council",

"council of a municipal corporation" or "council of a

municipality" includes a reference to the legislative

branch of the Délîne Got’înê Government described in

3.4.1b) of the Délîne Agreement .

### Reference to "municipal taxing authority"

(4) Unless the context otherwise requires, a

reference in an enactment to a "municipal taxing

authority" includes a reference to the Délîne Got’înê

Government.

### Reference to "municipality"

(5) Unless the context otherwise requires, a

reference in an enactment to a "municipality" includes

a reference to the Délîne Got’înê Government or the

Community of Délîne .

## DÉLÎNE

### Prohibition: use of waters on Délîne lands

12.1. Even if a licence is not required under section 10

for a particular use of waters, no person shall use or

permit the use of waters on Délîne lands, if a Délîne

law made pursuant to 21.1.1d) of the Délîne

Agreement requires a licence or other authorization

from the Board for that type of use.

### Prohibition: deposit of waste on Délîne lands

12.2. Even if a licence is not required under section 11

for a particular deposit of waste, no person shall

deposit or permit the deposit of waste in waters on

Délîne lands, if a Délîne law made pursuant to

21.1.1d) of the Délîne Agreement requires a licence or

other authorization from the Board for that type of

deposit.

### Issuance of licence or other authorization

12.3. (1) The Board may issue, in accordance with

Délîne law, a licence or other authorization required

for the use of waters under section 12.1 or the deposit

of waste under section 12.2.

### Other Acts to be complied with

(2) Nothing in a licence or other authorization

issued under subsection (1) authorizes a person to

contravene or fail to comply with another Act or a

regulation or order made under that Act, except as

provided in that other Act, regulation or order.

### Regulations

12.4. The Commissioner in Executive Council may

make regulations respecting the form of licences or

other authorizations to be issued by the Board under

section 12.3.

### Exemption

12.5. Sections 10 and 11 do not apply in respect of a

use of waters or a deposit of waste in the Community

of Délîne as described in schedule B of the Délîne

Agreement , if Délîne law provides that a licence is not

required for that type of use or deposit.

(6) Section 65 is amended by adding the

### following after subsection (4):

### Notice to Délîne Got’înê Government

(5) An inspector shall, if it is reasonable to do so,

give the Délîne Got’înê Government prior notice of

entry by the inspector on Délîne lands.
