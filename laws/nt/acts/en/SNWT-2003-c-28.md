### Preamble

Whereas the Tåîchô is an aboriginal people of

Canada that has traditionally used and occupied lands

in and adjacent to the Northwest Territories from time

immemorial;

Whereas the Constitution Act, 1982 recognizes

and affirms the existing aboriginal and treaty rights of

the aboriginal peoples of Canada;

Whereas the Tåîchô , the Government of the

Northwest Territories and the Government of Canada

negotiated a land claims and self-government

agreement in order to define and achieve certainty in

respect of rights of the Tåîchô relating to lands,

resources and self-government;

Whereas the Tåîchô approved the Tåîchô

Agreement by a vote held on June 26 and 27, 2003;

Whereas the Tåîchô , the Government of the

Northwest Territories and the Government of Canada

signed the Tåîchô Agreement on August 25, 2003;

Whereas the Tåîchô Agreement provides that it

will be a land claims agreement within the meaning of

section 35 of the Constitution Act, 1982 and that

approval by the Legislative Assembly of the Northwest

Territories is a condition precedent to the validity of

the agreement;

The Commissioner of the Northwest Territories,

by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative

Assembly, enacts as follows:

## INTERPRETATION

### Definitions

1. (1) In this Act,

" Tåîchô Agreement " means the Land Claims and

Self-Government Agreement among the Tåîchô and the

Government of the Northwest Territories and the

Government of Canada, signed on August 25, 2003,

including any amendments made to it from time to

time; ( Accord Tåîchô )

" Tåîchô Government" means the government of the

Tåîchô First Nation established in accordance with the

Tåîchô Agreement ; ( gouvernement tåîchô )

" Tåîchô law" means a law enacted by the Tåîchô

Government. ( loi tåîchô )

" Tax Treatment Agreement " means the

Tåîchô Tåîchô

Tax Treatment Agreement among the Tåîchô First

Nation and the Government of the Northwest

Territories and the Government of Canada, signed on

behalf of the Government of Canada on February 6,

2003, on behalf of the Government of the Northwest

Territories on February 27, 2003, and on behalf of the

Tåîchô First Nation on March 3, 2003, including any

amendments made to it from time to time; ( Accord sur

le traitement fiscal des Tåîchô )

### Defined terms

(2) Any word or expression defined in the Tåîchô

Agreement has the same meaning in this Act, unless

the context otherwise requires.

### Interpretation

(3) The Tåîchô Agreement may be used as an aid

in interpreting this Act or the regulations if there is any

doubt as to the meaning of one of its provisions.

### Binding effect

2. This Act is binding on the Government of the

Northwest Territories.

### Tåîchô Agreement

3. (1) In the case of any inconsistency or conflict

between an enactment and the Tåîchô Agreement , the

Tåîchô Agreement prevails to the extent of the

inconsistency or conflict.

### Other enactments

(2) In the case of any inconsistency or conflict

between this Act and the regulations and any other

enactment, this Act and the regulations prevail to the

extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

Tåîchô Agreement

### Effect of the Tåîchô Agreement

4. (1) The Agreement is approved, given

Tåîchô

effect and declared valid, and has the force of law.

### Rights and obligations

(2) For greater certainty, any person or body may

exercise the powers, rights, privileges and benefits

conferred on the person or body by the Tåîchô

Agreement and shall perform the duties and is subject

to the liabilities imposed on the person or body by the

Tåîchô Agreement .

### Third parties

(3) For greater certainty, the Tåîchô Agreement is

binding on, and can be relied on, by persons and

bodies that are not parties to it.

### Transfers to the Tåîchô Government

5. (1) The Minister shall, by order, identify any

personal property, including cash, assets and liabilities,

that is to be transferred to the Tåîchô Government by

the Government of the Northwest Territories under the

Tåîchô Agreement .

### Transfers on behalf of others

(2) The order referred to in subsection (1) may

include any personal property that was transferred to

the Government of the Northwest Territories by the

Government of Canada, or by a band or a corporation

held by a band, for the purpose of being transferred on

their behalf to the Tåîchô Government under the

Tåîchô Agreement .

### Title to property

(3) The Tåîchô Government shall hold title to

any property identified in the order referred to in

subsection (1) on the date this Act comes into force,

unless the order provides for the vesting of title at a

later date.

### Notice for personal property security interests

6. (1) If a security interest was registered before the

date this Act comes into force in the Personal Property

Registry under the Personal Property Security Act for

personal property that has vested in the Tåîchô

Government under the Tåîchô Agreement , the Tåîchô

Government shall, without delay after the date of the

vesting, send a written notice to the holder of the

security interest advising the holder of the vesting of

title.

### Motor vehicles

(2) The Registrar of Motor Vehicles may cancel

or modify an entry in the registry established under the

Motor Vehicles Act for any motor vehicle that has

vested in the Government under the

Tåîchô Tåîchô

Agreement , if any of the information in the registry

must be changed as a result of the vesting.

Appropriation

### Payments

7. Such sums as are required to meet the monetary

obligations of the Government of the Northwest

Territories under the Tåîchô Agreement shall be paid

from money appropriated for that purpose out of the

Consolidated Revenue Fund.

Tåîchô Tax Treatment Agreement

### Effect of tax agreement

8. The Tåîchô Tax Treatment Agreement is approved,

given effect and declared valid, and has the force of

law during the period it is in effect.

Judicial Notice

### Judicial notice of agreements

9. (1) Judicial notice shall be taken of the Tåîchô

Agreement and the Tåîchô Tax Treatment Agreement .

### Evidence

(2) A copy of the Tåîchô Agreement or the Tåîchô

Tax Treatment Agreement published by the Queen’s

Printer of Canada is evidence of that agreement, and a

copy purporting to be published by the Queen’s Printer

of Canada is deemed to be so published, unless the

contrary is shown.

### Judicial notice of Tåîchô laws

10. (1) Judicial notice shall be taken of Tåîchô laws.

### Evidence

(2) A copy of a Tåîchô law, registered in the

register of laws maintained by the Government

Tåîchô

and certified by a person authorized by the Tåîchô

Government, is evidence of that law without proof of

that person’s signature or official character.

Legal Proceedings

### Notice of issues

11. (1) If, in any judicial or administrative

proceeding, an issue arises in respect of the

interpretation, validity or applicability of the

Tåîchô

Agreement , this Act or the regulations or any Tåîchô

law, the issue shall not be decided until the party

raising the issue has properly served notice on the

Attorney General of the Northwest Territories, the

Attorney General of Canada and the Tåîchô

Government.

### Contents of notice

(2) The notice required under subsection (1) must

(a) describe the judicial or administrative

proceeding in which the issue arises;

(b) state whether the issue arises in respect

of the matters referred to in subsection

(1);

(c) state the day on which the issue is to be

argued;

(d) give particulars necessary to show the

point to be argued; and

(e) be served at least 14 days before the day

of argument, unless the court or tribunal

authorizes a shorter period.

### Rights of parties

(3) In any judicial or administrative proceeding to

which subsection (1) applies, the Attorney General of

the Northwest Territories, the Attorney General of

Canada and the Tåîchô Government may appear and

participate in the proceeding as parties with the same

rights as any other party.

### Nature of hearing

(4) For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3)

do not require that an oral hearing be held, if one is not

otherwise required.

Regulations

### Regulations

12. The Commissioner in Executive Council may, on

the recommendation of the Minister, make regulations

for the purpose of carrying out any of the provisions of

the Tåîchô Agreement or the Tåîchô Tax Treatment

Agreement .

Commencement

### Coming into force

13. This Act comes into force on the day that federal

settlement legislation comes into force pursuant to

4.3.3 of chapter 4 of the Tåîchô Agreement .
