RSNWT 1988, c 16 (Supp)

### Program established

1. The Commissioner may establish, organize and

promote programs to develop and improve the

occupational and other skills of persons.

### Agreements

2. (1) The Commissioner may enter into agreements

on behalf of the Government of the Northwest

Territories with

(a) the Government of Canada,

(b) the government of a province or territory,

or

(c) any municipality, agency, organization,

corporation or person,

for the purpose of arranging for or providing for

programs, research or services relating to

(d) occupational or other skills, or

(e) the improvement of the labour force.

(2) Repealed

### Commissioner empowered to act

3. (1) The Commissioner may do every act and

exercise every power for the purpose of implementing

every obligation assumed by the Government of the

Northwest Territories pursuant to an agreement

entered into pursuant to section 2.

### Specific powers

(2) Without limiting the generality of subsection

(1), the Commissioner may

(a) provide courses of occupational training

in the Territories to adults;

(b) provide courses in the Territories for

apprentices who are adults;

(c) give or undertake to give a course to, or

arrange or undertake to arrange a course

for, adults and to provide services in

connection with such a course;

(d) be remunerated by the Government of

Canada for any courses given, developed,

undertaken or provided;

(e) purchase, erect or convert buildings,

machinery, equipment or other facilities

for use in occupational training including

such facilities as may be required for

training adults, including adults with

special training needs;

(f) provide for the initial operation of the

facilities in paragraph (e); and

(g) provide for the development of courses,

to be given in such facilities.
