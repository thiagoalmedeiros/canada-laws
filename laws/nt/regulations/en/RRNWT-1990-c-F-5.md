RRNWT 1990, c F-5

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

1. These regulations apply to a guarantee or

indemnity made by or on behalf of the Government that

is authorized by an enactment other than the Financial

Administration Act .

2. (1) A guarantee or indemnity may be made by the

Commissioner or the Minister of Finance on behalf of

the Commissioner.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to a guarantee or

indemnity authorized by an enactment in which the

enactment authorizes a specific person to make the

guarantee or indemnity.

3. A guarantee or indemnity made by the

Commissioner or Minister of Finance under section 2

may be executed by the Commissioner or the Minister

of Finance.

4. (1) A guarantee or indemnity may be made

provided such guarantee or indemnity has been

recommended by the Board.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to an indemnity

authorized by an Act in which the maximum liability

under the indemnity is specified in the Act.
