RRNWT 1990, c C-5

*Made under the Cities, Towns and Villages Act*

1. The City of Yellowknife, consisting of that portion

of the Northwest Territories described in the Schedule,

is continued.

## SCHEDULE

All that portion of the Northwest Territories,

according to the Maps of the National Topographic

System distributed by the Department of Energy, Mines

and Resources on a scale of 1:50,000 and designated as

85J7 to 85J10 inclusive, lying within the boundaries

described as follows:

Commencing at the intersection of west longitude

114°31 N with north latitude 62°24 N 30 O ;

thence due east to west longitude 114°20 N ;

thence due north to north latitude 62°27 N 28 O ;

thence due east to west longitude 114°18 N ;

thence due north to north latitude 62°32 N 30 O ;

thence due west to west longitude 114°26 N ;

thence due south to north latitude 62°30 N ;

thence due west to west longitude 114°31 N ;

thence due south to the point of commencement.
