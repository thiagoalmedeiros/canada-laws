RRNWT 1990, c L-43

*Made under the Liquor Act*

Prohibited Area

1. All that portion of the Northwest Territories that

lies within 20 km of the building in Gamètì commonly

known as the Gamètì School is declared to be a

prohibited area. R-053-2008,s.3.

Prohibition

2. No person shall purchase, sell, transport or possess

liquor within the prohibited area described in section 1.

Penalty

3. Every person who violates any provision of these

regulations is guilty of an offence and liable on

summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $500 or to

imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to

both.
