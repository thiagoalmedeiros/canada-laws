RRNWT 1990, c T-3

*Made under the Territorial Court Act*

1. In these regulations, "territorial judge" means a

territorial judge who has been appointed to serve as a

territorial judge on a full-time basis.

2. Every territorial judge shall work at such times as

are required to exercise and perform duly and properly

his or her powers and duties as a territorial judge.

3. Repealed, R-038-2005,s.2.

4. Repealed, R-122-93,s.3.

5. Repealed, R-122-93,s.3.

6. The seal to be used in the Territorial Court shall be

described as follows:

In the centre of the seal shall be the Arms of

the Northwest Territories, and in a circle

around the Arms the words "Territorial Court,

Northwest Territories".
