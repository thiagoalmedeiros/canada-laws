NWT Reg 094-2005

*Made under the Evidence Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

every enabling power, makes the Recording of

1. The following types of devices, machines and

systems are approved as sound recording apparatus as

defined in section 31 of the Act:

(a) any device, machine or system that is

capable of producing digital audio

recordings and that stores the recordings

on a magnetic, optical or other medium

that is suitable for permanent retention;

(b) the Marantz PMD221;

(c) the Optimus CTR-117;

(d) the Radio Shack TCR-200 Telephone

Cassette Recorder;

(e) the Sony Confer/Corder BM-246.

2. The Recording of Evidence by Sound Apparatus

Order , established by instrument numbered R-042-94,

is repealed.
