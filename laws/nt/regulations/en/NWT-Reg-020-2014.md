NWT Reg 020-2014

*Made under the Waters Act*

The Commissioner in Executive Council, under

## AMOUNT AND INTEREST

1. For the purposes of subsection 61(4) of the Act, for

every billable hour that each employee of the

Government of the Northwest Territories assigned to an

expropriation works on the expropriation, fees are

prescribed in the amount of twice the hourly rate of

each employee prorated from the employee’s salary per

diem, based on a productivity rate of 220 days per year,

plus employee benefits (20% of total chargeable

salaries).

2. The rate of interest payable in respect of overdue

fees shall be calculated in accordance with sections 1

and 2 of the Interest Rate Regulations made under the

Financial Administration Act .

## COMING INTO FORCE

3. These regulations come into force April 1, 2014.
