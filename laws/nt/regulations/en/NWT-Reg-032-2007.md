NWT Reg 032-2007

*Made under the Charter Communities Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

Minister, under section 171 of the Charter

Communities Act and every enabling power, makes the

1. The prescribed form for the certification of public

hearings referred to in subsection 133(8) of the Charter

Communities Act is set out in the Schedule.

2. The Charter Communities Forms Regulations ,

### established by regulation numbered R-029-2007, are

repealed.
