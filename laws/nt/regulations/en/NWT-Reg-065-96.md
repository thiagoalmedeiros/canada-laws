NWT Reg 065-96

*Made under the Lotteries Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

enabling power, makes the Norman Wells Delegation

1. The authority to regulate and licence lottery

schemes within the Town of Norman Wells is hereby

delegated to the Town of Norman Wells.

2. The Lotteries Regulations, R.R.N.W.T. 1990,

c.L-49, do not apply to the regulation and licensing of

a lottery scheme within the Town of Norman Wells.

3. These regulations come into force July 1, 1996.
