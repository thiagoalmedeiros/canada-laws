NWT Reg 019-2012

*Made under the Herd and Fencing Act*

The Minister, under subsection 2(4) of the Herd

and Fencing Act and every enabling power, makes the

1. No person shall permit an animal to run at large in

the area comprised of Hay River Highway No. 2 and

lands lying within 8 km of either side of that highway,

excluding from the area any lands that lie within the

boundaries of the Hamlet of Enterprise or the Town of

Hay River.

2. The Animals Running at Large Regulations ,

### R.R.N.W.T. 1990, c.H-38, are repealed.
