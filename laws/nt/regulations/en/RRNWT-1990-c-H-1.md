RRNWT 1990, c H-1

*Made under the Hamlets Act*

1. The Hamlet of Aklavik, consisting of that portion of

the Northwest Territories described in the Schedule, is

continued.

## SCHEDULE

All that portion of the Northwest Territories in the

vicinity of the community of Aklavik as shown on the

1:50,000 scale National Topographic Series Maps 107

B/3W, 107 B/4, 107 B/5E and 107 B/6W Edition 1

produced by the Surveys and Mapping Branch,

Department of Energy, Mines and Resources,

Government of Canada, Ottawa and being more

particularly described as follows:

Commencing at the point of intersection of

northing 7,569,000 m N with easting 495,800 m E;

thence north-easterly in a straight line to the point

of intersection of northing 7,571,400 m N with easting

498,300 m E;

thence south-easterly in a straight line to the point

of intersection of northing 7,567,600 m N with easting

502,100 m E;

thence south-westerly in a straight line to the point

of intersection of northing 7,564,500 m N with easting

499,600 m E;

thence north-westerly in a straight line to the point

of intersection of northing 7,568,000 m N with easting

494,550 m E;

thence north-east in a straight line to the point of

commencement.
