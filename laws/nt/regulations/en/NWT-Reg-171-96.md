NWT Reg 171-96

*Made under the Hamlets Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS the Council of the Hamlet of Fort

Norman has requested the Minister to change the name

of the municipal corporation to the Hamlet of Tulita;

WHEREAS the Minister considers it to be in the

public interest to change the name of the municipal

corporation as requested;

The Minister, on the recommendation of the

and every enabling power, orders as follows:

1. The name of the municipal corporation of the

Hamlet of Fort Norman is hereby changed to the Hamlet

of Tulita.
