RRNWT 1990, c R-1

*Made under the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act*

1. The jurisdictions listed in the Schedule are declared

to be reciprocating jurisdictions for the purposes of the

Act.

2. Province of British Columbia

3. Province of Manitoba

4. Province of Newfoundland and Labrador

5. Province of Nova Scotia

6. Province of Ontario

8. Province of Saskatchewan

10. Yukon
