NSI-007-94

*Made under the Human Rights Act*

The Commissioner, under section 9 of the Fair

Practices Act and every enabling power, hereby

approves the Affirmative Action Policy and Directive

adopted on February 14, 1989 by the Honourable

Dennis Patterson in his capacity as Government Leader

and Chairman of the Executive Council of the

Government of the Northwest Territories.
