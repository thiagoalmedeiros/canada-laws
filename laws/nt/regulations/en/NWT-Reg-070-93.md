NWT Reg 070-93

*Made under the Cities, Towns and Villages Act*

The Minister, with the approval of the Executive

Council, under subsection 150(4) of the Cities, Towns

and Villages Act and every enabling power, orders as

follows:

1. By-law No. 388 of the Village of Fort Simpson, a

long term borrowing by-law, is exempt from the

approval of the ratepayers required by paragraph

150(3)(b) of the Cities, Towns and Villages Act .
