NWT Reg 050-97

*Made under the Guardianship and Trusteeship Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

Minister responsible for the Department of Health and

Social Services, under subsection 64(1) of the

c.29 and every enabling power, makes the Health Care

1. The following types of health care are designated

as types of health care to which a guardian may not

consent, on behalf of a represented person, unless

specifically authorized to do so in a guardianship order:

(a) sterilization that is not medically

necessary to prevent or cure disease;

(b) non-therapeutic abortion;

(c) experimental treatment;

(d) removal of tissue from a represented

person for implantation in another human

being or for the purposes of medical

education or medical research;

(e) psychosurgery;

(f) a procedure or therapy involving the use

of aversive stimuli including non-

prescription drugs for the purpose of

behaviour control or behaviour change.

2. These regulations come into force on July 1, 1997.
