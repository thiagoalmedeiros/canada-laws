NWT Reg 068-96

*Made under the Aurora College Act*

The Minister, under section 4 of the Public

Colleges Act and every enabling power, makes the

1. The Aurora Campus of Aurora College is

established in the Town of Inuvik.

2. The Thebacha Campus of Aurora College is

established in the Town of Fort Smith.

3. The Yellowknife - North Slave Campus of Aurora

College is established in the City of Yellowknife.

4. The Arctic College Inuvik Campus Order ,

R.R.N.W.T. 1990, c.A-12, is repealed.
