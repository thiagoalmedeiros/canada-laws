RRNWT 1990, c J-2

*Made under the Judicature Act*

1. There shall be a Seal of the Supreme Court of the

Northwest Territories, the description of which is as

follows:

Description

In the centre appears the Coat of Arms of the

Northwest Territories, and in a circle around

the Coat of Arms the words "Seal of the

Supreme Court of the Northwest Territories".
