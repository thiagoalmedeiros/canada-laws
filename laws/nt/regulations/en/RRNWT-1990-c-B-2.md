RRNWT 1990, c B-2

*Made under the Business Licence Act*

1. In these regulations, "business establishment"

means any food establishment, drinking establishment,

store, theatre, dance hall, factory or auditorium, and

includes any place of business where the public gathers.

2. No licence shall be issued in respect of a business

establishment to an applicant for a business licence

under the Business Licence Act unless the application

for the licence is accompanied by a certificate of the

Fire Marshal or a local assistant appointed under the

Fire Prevention Act that fire safety precautions in

respect of the business establishment are satisfactory.
