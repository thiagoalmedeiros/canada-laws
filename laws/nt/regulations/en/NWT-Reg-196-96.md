NWT Reg 196-96

*Made under the Education Act*

The Minister, under section 151 of the Education

Act and every enabling power, makes the

1. An education body shall consider the following

factors when making a policy regarding the

transportation of students:

(a) the distance students must travel to attend

school;

(b) any safety concerns that exist regarding

the students;

(c) the climate in the education district;

(d) the age and health of students.
