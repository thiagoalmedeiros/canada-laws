NWT Reg 051-2025

*Made under the Hamlets Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS the council of the Hamlet of Tulita has

requested the variation of the number of councillors on

the council;

and every enabling power, orders as follows:

1. The council of the Hamlet of Tulita is composed of

a mayor and six elected councillors.
