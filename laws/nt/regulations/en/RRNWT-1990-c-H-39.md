RRNWT 1990, c H-39

*Made under the Historical Resources Act*

1. The following sites are declared to be of historical

significance to the Territories:

(a) Repealed, R-012-2006,s.1.

(b) Repealed, R-012-2006,s.1.

(c) Repealed, R-012-2006,s.1.

(d) Repealed, R-012-2006,s.1.

(e) the Old Anglican Church in Tulita

(64°54 N N and 125°34 N W) built in

approximately 1880 by Allan Hardisty, as

described in the Schedule;

(f) Whaler's Graves at Balaena Bay, Cape

Perry, being an area 45.72 m² centred at

70°01 N 54 O N and 124°57 N 00 O W.

## SCHEDULE

The legal description which was filed in the Land

Titles Office on February 19, 1979, under No. 49,

Certificate No. 10722, subject to an amendment due to

a road right-of-way, is as follows:

Commencing at a point on the west boundary of

said lot 16, a distance of 42.6 m from the northwest

corner of said lot;

thence easterly and parallel to the north boundary

of said lot, a distance of 91.4 m to a point;

thence southerly and parallel to the said west

boundary, a distance of 128 m, more or less, to the

water's edge of the Mackenzie River;

thence westerly along the said water's edge of the

Mackenzie River to the southwest corner of said lot;

thence northerly along the said westerly boundary

to the point of commencement.
