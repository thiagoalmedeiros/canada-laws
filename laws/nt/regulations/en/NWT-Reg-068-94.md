NWT Reg 068-94

*Made under the Lotteries Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

enabling power, makes the Inuvik Delegation

1. The authority to regulate and licence lottery

schemes within the Town of Inuvik is hereby delegated

to the Town of Inuvik.

2. The Lotteries Regulations , R.R.N.W.T. 1990, c.L-

49, do not apply to the regulation and licensing of a

lottery scheme within the Town of Inuvik.

3. These regulations come into force on July 1, 1994.
