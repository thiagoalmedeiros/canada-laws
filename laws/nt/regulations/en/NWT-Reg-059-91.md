NWT Reg 059-91

*Made under the Societies Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

R.S.N.W.T. 1988, c. S-11 and every enabling power,

1. The prescribed form of an application referred to in

paragraph 2(a) of the Societies Act is set out in Form 1

of the Schedule.
