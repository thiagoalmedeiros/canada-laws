RRNWT 1990, c L-32

*Made under the Liquor Act*

Application

1. (1) These regulations apply to Whatì.

(2) For the purposes of these regulations, Whatì is

deemed to consist of all that portion of the Northwest

Territories that lies within a radius of 25 km from the

building in Whatì commonly known as the Mezi

Community School. R-055-2008,s.3.

Prohibition

2. No person shall, within Whatì, have in his or her

possession, purchase, sell or transport liquor.

R-055–2008,s.4.

Penalty

3. Every person who contravenes section 2 is guilty of

an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine

not exceeding $500 or to imprisonment for a term not

exceeding six months or to both.
