1. The prescribed form for a writ of election referred

2. The prescribed form for a proclamation referred to

WRIT OF ELECTION

NORTHWEST TERRITORIES

WHEREAS the Legislative Assembly of the Northwest

Territories has terminated or been dissolved and the

Commissioner of the Northwest Territories has by order

instructed the issue of writs for the election of members

of a new Legislative Assembly ( omit the foregoing

preamble in case of a by-election ).

of ....................................... ;

You are hereby directed, that notice of the time, date

and place of election being duly given;

district of ................................. ;

AND you do cause the close of the nominations of

candidates to be ............................ ;

AND you do cause a poll to be held, if necessary, on

.......................................... ;

not later than............................... .
