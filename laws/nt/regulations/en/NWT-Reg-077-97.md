NWT Reg 077-97

*Made under the Lotteries Act*

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

enabling power, makes the Rae-Edzo Delegation

1. The authority to regulate and licence lottery

schemes within the community of Behchokö is

delegated to the Community Government of Behchokö .

2. The Lotteries Regulations, R.R.N.W.T. 1990,

c.L-49, do not apply to the regulation and licensing of

a lottery scheme within Behchokö . R-059-2009,s.4;

R-121-2009, art. 2.

3. Repealed, R-059-2009,s.5.
