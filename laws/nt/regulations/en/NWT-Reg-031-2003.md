NWT Reg 031-2003

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

## ACT

The Commissioner, on the recommendation of the

Minister of Municipal and Community Affairs, under

every enabling power, makes the Fire Prevention Plan

1. (1) A fee shall be paid to the Fire Marshal for the

review of plans and specifications respecting proposed

work in an amount equal to $173 for each hour or part

of an hour required for the review.

(2) The Fire Marshal shall, before reporting in

writing on the review of plans and specifications, advise

the person who submits the plans and specifications of

the fee payable for the review, and receive payment of

the fee.

2. Repealed, R-055-2009,s.4.
