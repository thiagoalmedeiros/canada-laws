RRNWT 1990, c 17 (Supp)

*Made under the Real Estate Professions' Licensing Act*

(Incorporates amendments made by R-074-91)

1. The prescribed form for an application for a licence

as an agent, other than a temporary licence, or for a

renewal of a licence as an agent is set out in Form 1 of

the Schedule.

2. The prescribed form for an application for a licence

as a salesperson, other than a temporary licence, or for

a renewal of a licence as a salesperson is set out in

Form 2 of the Schedule.

3. The prescribed form for a licence for an agent is set

out in Form 3 of the Schedule.

4. The prescribed form for a licence for a salesperson

is set out in Form 4 of the Schedule.
