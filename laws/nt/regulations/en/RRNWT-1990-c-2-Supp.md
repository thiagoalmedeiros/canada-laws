RRNWT 1990, c 2 (Supp)

*Made under the Co-operative Associations Act*

1. Subject to subsection 2(2), fees shall be paid to the

Registrar in accordance with the Schedule.

2. (1) In this section, "public body" means a

department, branch or office of any level of

government, including Crown corporations, boards or

commissions, in Canada.

(2) No fee shall be paid by the following for the

services referred to in item 2 of the Schedule:

(a) a law enforcement agency in Canada;

(b) a public body carrying out an

investigation or proceeding authorized by

an enactment that leads or could lead to

the imposition of a penalty or sanction

under the enactment.

(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), a public body

shall pay a fee for a search referred to in paragraph 2(a)

of the Schedule if the search is conducted using the

online search system provided by the Registrar.

## SCHEDULE ( Section 1 )

Requests, Applications and Filings

1. The following fees are payable for requests, applications and filings:

(a) Request for reservation of name....................................................$ 25

(b) Incorporation of association where the name has been reserved ...........................$ 250

(c) Incorporation of association where the name has not been reserved ........................$ 275

(d) Amalgamation of association where the name has been reserved ..........................$ 250

(e) Amalgamation of association where the name has not been reserved .......................$ 275

(f) Application under section 7 of the Act where the name has been reserved ...................$ 250

(g) Application under section 7 of the Act where the name has not been reserved ................$ 275

(h) Amendment to memorandum of association...........................................$ 100

(i) Amendment to by-laws...........................................................$ 50

(j) Annual return ..................................................................$ 50

(k) Dissolution....................................................................$ NIL

(l) Application, filing, or registration not set out in this Schedule.............................$ NIL

Searches, Certificates and Copies

2. The following fees are payable for searches, certificates and copies:

(a) Searches (for each search) ........................................................$ 4

(b) Certificate of status..............................................................$ 20

(c) Certificate of true copy of an original document .......................................$ 5

(d) Photocopies (for each page).......................................................$ 1

(e) Facsimile transmissions (for each page)..............................................$ 2
