NWT Reg 090-2026

*Made under the Interpretation Act*

The Commissioner in Executive Council, under

enabling power, orders as follows:

1. Standard time is set at the time six hours behind

Coordinated Universal Time.

2. The name of standard time is changed to

"Northwest Territories Time".

3. These regulations come into force

### August 21, 2026.
