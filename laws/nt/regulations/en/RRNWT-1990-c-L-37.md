RRNWT 1990, c L-37

*Made under the Liquor Act*

Prohibited Area

1. All that portion of the Territories that lies within a

radius of 15 km from the building in the community of

Nahanni Butte commonly known as the Old School is

declared to be a prohibited area.

Prohibition

2. No person shall purchase, sell, transport or possess

liquor within the prohibited area described in section 1.

Penalty

3. Every person who violates any provision of these

regulations is guilty of an offence and liable on

summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $500 or to

imprisonment for a term not exceeding 30 days or to

both.
