NWT Reg 075-2009

*Made under the Cities, Towns and Villages Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS the council of the Town of Fort Smith

has requested the variation of the number of elected

councillors on the council;

The Minister, under section 14 of the Cities, Towns

and Villages Act and every enabling power, orders as

follows:

1. The council of the Town of Fort Smith is composed

of a mayor and eight elected councillors.
