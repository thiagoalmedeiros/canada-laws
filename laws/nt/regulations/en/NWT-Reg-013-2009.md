NWT Reg 013-2009

*Made under the Retirement Plan Beneficiaries Act*

The Commissioner in Executive Council, under

paragraph (d) of the definition "plan" in section 1 of the

power, makes the Tax-free Savings Account

1. A tax-free savings account as defined in the Income

Tax Act (Canada) is prescribed as a plan for the

purposes of paragraph (d) of the definition "plan" in

section 1 of the Retirement Plan Beneficiaries Act .
