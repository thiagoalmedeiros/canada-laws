NWT Reg 047-92

*Made under the Public Highways Act*

The Commissioner of the Northwest Territories,

every enabling power, makes the Highway Designation

1. The highways described in Schedule A are

designated as primary highways and classified as

arterial highways.

2. The highways described in Schedule B are

designated as primary highways and classified as

collector highways.

3. The highways described in Schedule C are

designated as primary highways and classified as local

highways.

4. The designations in Schedules A, B and C describe

highways as they exist from time to time.

5. Repealed, R-031-2011,s.2.
