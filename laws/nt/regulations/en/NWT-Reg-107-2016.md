NWT Reg 107-2016

*Made under the Deline Final Self-Government Agreement Act*

The Commissioner in Executive Council, under

section 14 of the Final Self-Government

Agreement Act and every enabling power, makes the

1. If a security interest is registered before the date the

Act comes into force in the Personal Property Registry

under the Personal Property Security Act for personal

property that vests in the Délîne Got’înê Government

under the Délîne Agreement , the Délîne Got’înê

Government shall, without delay after the date of the

vesting, send a written notice to the holder of the

security interest advising the holder of the vesting of

title.

2. The Registrar of Motor Vehicles may cancel or

modify an entry in the registry established under the

Motor Vehicles Act for any motor vehicle that vests in

the Délîne Got’înê Government under the Délîne

Agreement , if any of the information in the registry

must be changed as a result of the vesting.

3. These regulations come into force

### September 1, 2016.
