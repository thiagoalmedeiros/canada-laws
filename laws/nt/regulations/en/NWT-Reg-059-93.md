NWT Reg 059-93

*Made under the Land Titles Act*

The Minister, under paragraph 3(a) of the Land

Titles Act and every enabling power, orders as follows:

1. The whole of the Northwest Territories is

constituted as a registration district to be known as the

"Northwest Territories Registration District".

2. This order comes into force on the day the Land

Titles Act comes into force.
