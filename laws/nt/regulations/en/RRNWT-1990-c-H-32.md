RRNWT 1990, c H-32

*Made under the Hamlets Act*

1. The Hamlet of Sachs Harbour, consisting of that

portion of the Northwest Territories described in the

Schedule, is continued.

2. The council of the Hamlet of Sachs Harbour is

composed of a mayor and six elected councillors.

## SCHEDULE

All that portion of the Northwest Territories, with

reference to the Universal Transverse Mercator Grid in

Zone 10 as shown on Edition 1 of map sheet numbers

97 G/15 and 98 B/2 of the National Topographic Series

produced at a scale of 1:50,000 by the Department of

Energy, Mines and Resources, Canada, lying

to the south of Northing 7,991,000 m N;

to the north of Northing 7,987,000 m N;

to the west of Easting 426,000 m E; and

to the east of Easting 412,000 m E.
