M.R. 115/2026

The Street Weapons Control Act, C.C.S.M. c. S210.5

### Definitions

1 The following definitions apply in this regulation.

"Act" means The Street Weapons Control Act. (« Loi »)

"minister" means the minister appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in Council to administer the Act. (« ministre »)

### How First Nations opt in to Act

2 A First Nation may opt in to the application of the Act by giving the minister a written notice from the band council advising that the council wants the Act to apply to the First Nation.

### Exempt items

3 The Act does not apply to hedge trimmers, pruning shears and any type of saw.

### Permitted possession of street weapons

4 Possession of a street weapon in a public space in a population centre is permitted as follows:

(a) axes and hatchets may be possessed at a business dedicated to axe throwing or at an organized event or exhibition where axe throwing demonstrations or competitions take place;

(b) axes, hatchets and long-bladed weapons may be possessed if they are required for use in

(i) a film, play or historical re-enactment,

(ii) a cultural ceremony, event or festival, or

(iii) an educational activity.

### Transporting street weapons

5 A person who transports a street weapon in the circumstances set out in subsection 3(2) of the Act must

(a) travel by the most direct route to their intended destination without making any unnecessary stops until they arrive at their destination; and

(b) if transporting the street weapon by motor vehicle, store the street weapon

(i) in the trunk of the vehicle,

(ii) in a locked exterior storage compartment on the vehicle, or

(iii) if the vehicle does not have a trunk, in a space behind the last row of seats in the vehicle that is not readily accessible to the person operating the vehicle and cover the street weapon so that it is not visible to any other person.

### Form of request for return of seized street weapon

6 A request for the return of a street weapon must be in writing and must contain the following information:

(a) the name of the person making the request;

(b) the requesting person's address;

(c) the requesting person's telephone number or e-mail address;

(d) a description of the street weapon that was seized;

(e) the date on which the street weapon was seized.

### Submitting request for return

7 A request for the return of a seized street weapon must be submitted

(a) if the street weapon was seized by a police officer, to a police officer at the headquarters or local detachment of the police service of the officer who seized the street weapon;

(b) if the street weapon was seized by a community safety officer, to a community safety officer in the same community safety officer program as the officer who seized the street weapon;

(c) if the street weapon was seized by a First Nation safety officer, to a First Nation safety officer in the same First Nation safety officer program as the officer who seized the street weapon; and

(d) if the street weapon was seized by an institutional safety officer, to an institutional safety officer at the institution where the street weapon was seized.

### Notice of return

8(1) If an order for continued impoundment under section 6 of the Act has not been made, the person who requested the return of their street weapon in accordance with section 5 of the Act must be notified by a person at the location where the seized weapon is stored as to where the street weapon will be returned and who the person who requested the return should contact at that location.

### Return of street weapon

8(2) The contact person specified in the notice under subsection (1) must return the street weapon when the person who requested the return attends to receive the weapon.

### Coming into force

9 This regulation comes into force on the same day that The Street Weapons Control Act, S.M. 2026, c. 8, comes into force.
