# Administrative Penalties Regulation

M.R. 76/2026

The Environment Act, C.C.S.M. c. E125

### Definitions

1 The following definitions apply in this regulation.

"Act" means The Environment Act. (« Loi »)

"administrative penalty" means an administrative penalty issued by the director under section 24.0.1 of the Act for failing to comply with an environmental protection order issued under section 24 of the Act. (« sanction administrative »)

### Determination of administrative penalty amount

2(1) To determine the amount of an administrative penalty, the director is to consider the level of seriousness, the applicable base penalty amount and any mitigating or aggravating factors in accordance with this section.

### Level of seriousness

2(2) A failure to comply with an environmental protection order is considered to be

(a) minor, if the failure is not moderate or major;

(b) moderate, if the failure is not major and it

(i) relates to a requirement to report the release of a pollutant,

(ii) relates to a requirement to collect or analyze samples,

(iii) creates a material risk of the release of a pollutant that would contravene subsection 30.1(1) of the Act if it occurred, or creates a material risk of increasing the amount of pollutant released in such a release, or

(iv) otherwise undermines the purpose of the Act; or

(c) major, if the failure results in the actual release of a pollutant that contravenes subsection 30.1(1) of the Act or increases the amount of pollutant released in such a release.

### Base penalty amount

2(3) Subject to subsection (4), the administrative penalty for a failure to comply of the level of seriousness listed in Column 1 of the following table is the base penalty amount listed opposite in Column 2 if the person who failed to comply is an individual or Column 3 if the person is a corporation:

| COLUMN 1 Level of seriousness | COLUMN 2 Base penalty amount for an individual | COLUMN 3 Base penalty amount for a corporation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| minor | $800 | $4,000 |
| moderate | $4,000 | $20,000 |
| major | $20,000 | $100,000 |

### Mitigating and aggravating factors

2(4) The director may, in their discretion, increase or decrease the administrative penalty for a failure to comply by an amount not exceeding 25% of the base penalty amount, in consideration of the following mitigating and aggravating factors:

Mitigating

(a) the person has made reasonable efforts to promptly remedy the failure;

(b) the person has cooperated with the director or an environment officer;

(c) the person has taken steps to prevent a recurrence of the failure;

Aggravating

(d) the failure was deliberate or reckless or the result of neglect or wilful blindness;

(e) the person did not make reasonable efforts to mitigate the failure;

(f) the person did not cooperate with the director or an environment officer;

(g) within the 10 years immediately preceding the failure, the person was issued one or more administrative penalties for a failure to comply of the same or greater level of seriousness;

(h) within the five years immediately preceding the failure, the person was issued two or more administrative penalties for a failure to comply of a lesser level of seriousness.

### Previous penalties for continued failure

2(5) For the purpose of applying the aggravating factors set out in clauses (4)(g) and (h), two or more administrative penalties that are issued for a failure to comply that occurred or continued on more than one day are to be treated as a single administrative penalty.

### Varying amount if not in public interest

2(6) For certainty, nothing in this regulation prevents the director from varying the amount of the administrative penalty on reconsideration, in accordance with clause 24.0.3(4)(c) of the Act, if the director believes that the amount determined in accordance with this section is not justified in the public interest.

### Multiple failures to comply with single order

3 For certainty, if a person's actions or inactions fail to comply with more than one provision of an environmental protection order, then the director may impose an administrative penalty for each failure.

### Notice of administrative penalty

4 In addition to the information required by subsection 24.0.2(3) of the Act, a notice of administrative penalty must set out the following:

(a) the authority in the Act for imposing the penalty;

(b) a description of how the person failed to comply with the environmental protection order, including the specific provision or provisions of the order that were not complied with;

(c) a description of how the amount of the penalty was determined, including

(i) the director's determination of whether the failure to comply is minor, moderate or major, and

(ii) a description of any mitigating or aggravating factors considered by the director.

### Annual report

5(1) Within six months after the end of each fiscal year of the government, the director must publish a report under section 24.0.6 of the Act on the administrative penalties that were issued during the fiscal year. The report must include the following information for each penalty:

(a) the name of the person required to pay the penalty;

(b) the location where the failure to comply took place;

(c) the date of the failure to comply;

(d) a brief description of the failure to comply, including the provision or provisions of the environmental protection order that were not complied with;

(e) the amount of the penalty;

(f) whether the penalty was subject to a request for reconsideration under section 24.0.3 of the Act and, if so, the outcome of the request;

(g) whether the penalty was subject to an appeal under section 27 of the Act and, if so, the outcome of the appeal.

### Where report published

5(2) The report must be published on the department's website in a conspicuous place that is accessible to the public.

### Disclosing continued failure

5(3) If one or more administrative penalties were issued for a failure to comply that occurred or continued on more than one day, the report must include the amount of the penalty or penalties per day as well as the total amount of the penalty or penalties.

### Additional disclosure permitted

5(4) In addition to the annual report, the director may publish on the department's website the information about an administrative penalty described in subsection (1) at any time after

(a) the period for appealing the penalty expires; or

(b) if the penalty is appealed, the appeal is disposed of.

### Coming into force

6 This regulation comes into force on the same day that The Environment Amendment Act, S.M. 2024, c. 35, Schedule B, comes into force.
