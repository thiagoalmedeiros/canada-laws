M.R. 259/86

The Farm Products Marketing Act, C.C.S.M. c. F47

## Schedule A

1 The minimum prices at which Grade Canada No. 1 White honey may be sold by producers or associations of producers in the Province of Manitoba are hereby fixed at the following prices:

| Sizes | Unit Price | Case Quantity | Case Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 500 g | $1.21 | 24 | $29.04 |
| 750 g | 1.82 | 12 | 21.84 |
| 1 kg | 2.30 | 12 | 27.60 |
| 1.5 kg | 3.45 | 6 | 20.70 |

(The above prices shall apply pro rata to broken case lots.)

2 The above prices shall not apply where Grade Canada No. 1 White honey is sold by a producer to an individual for consumption by himself or the members of his household and delivery is taken by such an individual at the producer's residence, apiary or honey extracting plant.

3 Order 1/85 of the Board, being Manitoba Regulation 25/85 is repealed.

4 This Order comes into force on the date it is filed with the Registrar of Regulations.

November 20, 1986 The Manitoba Honey Marketing Board:

Herb Isaac, Chairman

Kathleen Taylor, Secretary
