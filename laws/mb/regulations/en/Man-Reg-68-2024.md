M.R. 68/2024

The Executive Government Organization Act, C.C.S.M. c. E170

### Access to information and privacy protection services

1 The services for the administration of access to information and privacy protection are prescribed for the purpose of the definition "internal support services" in subsection 11.0.1(1) of The Executive Government Organization Act.
