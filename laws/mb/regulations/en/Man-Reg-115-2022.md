M.R. 115/2022

The Employment Standards Code, C.C.S.M. c. E110

### Minimum wage adjustment — additional amount

1 The minimum wage, as adjusted under section 7 of The Employment Standards Code on October 1, 2022, is hereby increased by an additional amount of $1.15.

### Coming into force

2 This regulation comes into force on October 1, 2022, and takes effect immediately after the minimum wage is adjusted under section 7 of The Employment Standards Code.
