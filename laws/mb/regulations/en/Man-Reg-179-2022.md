M.R. 179/2022

The Workers Compensation Act, C.C.S.M. c. W200

### Temporary annual indexing factor

1 For the purpose of subsection 48(2) of The Workers Compensation Act, the annual indexing factor for 2023 is 1.0944326.

### Temporary biennial indexing factor

2 For the purpose of subsection 48(6) of The Workers Compensation Act, the biennial indexing factor for 2023 is 1.1261783.

### Coming into force

3 This regulation comes into force on January 1, 2023, or on the day it is registered under The Statutes and Regulations Act, whichever is later.
