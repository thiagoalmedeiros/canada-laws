M.R. 61/93

The Health Services Insurance Act, C.C.S.M. c. H35

### Designation

1 The premises listed in the Schedule are designated as institutions for the purposes of section 57 of The Health Services Insurance Act.

### Repeal

2 Manitoba Regulation 219/91 is repealed.

### Coming into force

3 This regulation comes into force on March 31, 1993.
