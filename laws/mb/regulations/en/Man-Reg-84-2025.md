M.R. 84/2025

The Planning Act, C.C.S.M. c. P80

### Members withdrawn

1 For the purposes of The Planning Act and the Capital Planning Region Regulation, Manitoba Regulation 161/2022, the following regional member municipalities are withdrawn from the Capital Planning Region:

(a) the City of Selkirk;

(a.1) the Town of Stonewall;

(b) the Village of Dunnottar;

(c) the Rural Municipalities of East St. Paul, Macdonald, Rockwood, Springfield, St. Andrews, St. Clements, Taché and West St. Paul.
