M.R. 7/2019

The Health Sector Bargaining Unit Review Act, C.C.S.M. c. H29

### Bargaining unit definitions

1 The following definitions apply for the purpose of subsection 2(1) of The Health Sector Bargaining Unit Review Act:

"community sector", in relation to employees, means employees whose work consists primarily of providing direct hands-on or psycho-social patient/resident/client care. (« secteur communautaire »)

"facilities sector", in relation to employees, means employees who provide operational support services, excluding employees of a child care centre that is licensed under The Community Child Care Standards Act. (« secteur des installations »)

### Specified employers for employers organizations

2 For the purpose of clause 3(1)(b) of The Health Sector Bargaining Unit Review Act, the employers listed in the Schedule to this regulation under the name of an employers organization are specified as employers for that organization.
