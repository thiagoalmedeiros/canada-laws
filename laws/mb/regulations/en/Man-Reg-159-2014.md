M.R. 159/2014

The Highway Traffic Act, C.C.S.M. c. H60

### Definition

1 In this regulation, "Act" means The Highway Traffic Act.

### Prescribed offences

2 For the purpose of section 273.6 of the Act, the contravention of a provision of the Criminal Code (Canada) listed in the Schedule is a prescribed offence.

### Coming into force

3 This regulation comes into force on the same day that Part 2 of The Employment and Income Assistance Amendment and Highway Traffic Amendment Act, S.M. 2011, c. 39, comes into force.

(Section 2)
