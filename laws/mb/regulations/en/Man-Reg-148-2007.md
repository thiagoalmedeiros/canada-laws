M.R. 148/2007

The Workers Compensation Act, C.C.S.M. c. W200

### Information re employer

1(1) An employer must provide the following information in writing to the board:

(a) the employer's name, address and telephone number;

(b) the employer's fax number, if the employer has a fax number;

(c) the employer's e-mail address, if the employer has an e-mail address;

(d) the business number assigned to the employer by the Canada Revenue Agency, if the employer has a business number.

1(2) An employer must advise the board in writing of any change in the information provided under subsection (1) as soon as reasonably practicable after the change occurs.

### Coming into force

2 This regulation comes into force on January 1, 2008, or on the day it is registered under The Regulations Act, whichever is later.
