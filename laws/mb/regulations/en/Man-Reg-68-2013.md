M.R. 68/2013

The Farm Products Marketing Act, C.C.S.M. c. F47

This regulation is made under the Dairy Farmers of Manitoba Marketing Plan Regulation, Manitoba Regulation 89/2004, and is Regulation No. 1, 2013 of Dairy Farmers of Manitoba

### Operating levy imposed on producers

1 A levy is imposed on each producer of

(a) $0.63 per kilogram of butterfat in milk marketed by the producer; and

(b) $0.30 per hectolitre of milk marketed by the producer.

### Time of imposition of levy

2 The levy imposed under this regulation is due and payable by the producer to the board at the time and place of marketing of milk by the producer, and must be deducted and withheld by the board from any moneys that are payable to the producer on account of milk marketed to or through the board.

### Non-application of regulation

3 No levy is payable under this regulation on milk to which the Dairy Farmers of Manitoba Marketing Plan Regulation, Manitoba Regulation 89/2004, does not apply.

### Repeal

4 The Milk Operating Levy Regulation, Manitoba Regulation 78/2012, is repealed.

### Coming into force

5 This regulation comes into force on August 1, 2013, or on the day it is registered under The Regulations Act, whichever is later.
