M.R. 173/2004

The Public Schools Act, C.C.S.M. c. P250

### Rolling River First Nation

1 The reserve of the Rolling River First Nation is hereby established as a separate ward of The Rolling River School Division.

### Canupawakpa Dakota Nation

2 The reserve of the Canupawakpa Dakota Nation is hereby established as a separate ward of The Fort La Bosse School Division.

### Waywayseecappo First Nation

3 The reserve of the Waywayseecappo First Nation is hereby established as a separate ward of The Park West School Division.
