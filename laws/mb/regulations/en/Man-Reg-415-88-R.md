### Interpretation

2 Each highway described in the schedules is along a continuous route by road allowance or public road or both, except where otherwise stated.

### Declaration

3 The highways described in the schedules to this regulation are declared to be provincial trunk highways.

### Route number

4 The number specified in the heading of a section in a schedule is prescribed as the route number of the highway described in all the sections of that schedule.
