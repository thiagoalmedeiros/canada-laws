M.R. 1/2000

The Order of Manitoba Act, C.C.S.M. c. O75

### Order

1 The insignia of the Order include

(a) a medal in the form of a stylized crocus bearing a stylized coat of arms and the Crown worn with a ribbon coloured red, white and mauve-blue; and

(b) a lapel pin in the form of a crocus with a buffalo in the centre.
