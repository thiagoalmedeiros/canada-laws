M.R. 278/91

The Workers Compensation Act, C.C.S.M. c. W200

### Designation of employers

1 Pursuant to subsection 76.2(1) of The Workers Compensation Act, the employers listed in the Schedule, and any subsidiaries of the employers, are designated as individually assessed employers.

### Coming into force

2 This regulation comes into force on January 1, 1992 or on registration, whichever is later.

November 26, 1991 The Workers Compensation Board:

Robert L. Kopstein, Chairperson
