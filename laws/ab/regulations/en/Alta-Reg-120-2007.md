Alta Reg 120/2007

*Enabling act: Emblems of Alberta Act*

### Criteria

1 For the purpose of section 11.1 of the Emblems of Alberta Act , the following criteria must be met before a thing, including music and works of art, is prescribed as an Alberta Symbol of Distinction:

(a) must represent a cultural group that has contributed to Alberta’s culturally diverse society;

(b) must not duplicate or significantly resemble the symbol of another cultural group;

(c) must be unique to Alberta or represent Alberta in some manner;

(d) must not be religious in nature;

(e) must be non-partisan;

(f) must not be offensive or promote hatred, racism or divisiveness.
