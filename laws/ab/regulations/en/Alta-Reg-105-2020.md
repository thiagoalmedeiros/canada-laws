Alta Reg 105/2020

*Enabling act: Alberta Senate Election Act*

### Application

1 This Regulation applies to an election under the Alberta Senate Election Act .

### Forms

2 (1) The form in the Schedule to this Regulation is prescribed for the purposes of the section of the Alberta Senate Election Act indicated on the form.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), forms required to be prescribed under the Alberta Senate Election Act that are not set out in the Schedule to this Regulation are to be prescribed by the Chief Electoral Officer.

### Term of a Senate Nominee

3 The term of a Senate nominee commences on the day the person is declared elected in accordance with the Alberta Senate Election Act and expires on the issuance of a writ under section 5(1)(b) of the Alberta Senate Election Act .
