Alta Reg 252/99

*Enabling act: Corrections Act*

### Designated correctional institutions

1 The following facilities are hereby designated as correctional institutions:

(a) Alberta Hospital Edmonton - the Helen Hunley Pavilion, in the City of Edmonton;

(b) repealed AR 208/2009 s2;

(b.1) Calgary Attendance Centre, in the City of Calgary;

(c) Calgary Correctional Centre, in the City of Calgary;

(d) Calgary Remand Centre, in the City of Calgary;

(d.01) Calgary Young Offender Centre and Adult Female Annex, in the City of Calgary;

(d.1) Edmonton Attendance Centre, in the City of Edmonton;

(e) Edmonton Remand Centre, in the City of Edmonton;

(e.1) repealed AR 190/2025 s2;

(f) repealed AR 208/2009 s2;

(g) Fort Saskatchewan Correctional Centre, in the City of Fort Saskatchewan;

(h) repealed AR 44/2012 s2;

(i) Lethbridge Correctional Centre, in the City of Lethbridge;

(j) Medicine Hat Remand Centre, in the City of Medicine Hat;

(k) repealed AR 208/2009 s2;

(l) Peace River Correctional Centre, in the Town of Peace River;

(m) Red Deer Remand Centre, in the City of Red Deer;

(n) Southern Alberta Forensic Psychiatry Centre, in the City of Calgary.

2 Repealed AR 57/2016 s2.

### Repeal

3 The Designated Correctional Institutions Order (AR 72/88) is repealed.
