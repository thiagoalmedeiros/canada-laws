Alta Reg 102/2022

*Enabling act: Captive Insurance Companies Act Insurance Act*

### Interpretation

1 (1) In this Regulation, “insurer” means

(a) a captive insurance company that is licensed under the Captive Insurance Companies Act , and

(b) an insurer that is licensed under section 19(1) or (2) of the Insurance Act and includes a person who exchanges with other persons reciprocal contracts of indemnity or inter-insurance as part of a reciprocal insurance exchange.

(2) A reference to “the Acts and the regulations” includes

(a) the Captive Insurance Companies Act and the regulations under that Act,

(b) the Insurance Act and the regulations under that Act, and

(c) the Automobile Insurance Act and the regulations under that Act.

(3) For the purposes of this Regulation, the annual budget for the fiscal year approved by the Minister for the administration of the Acts and the regulations for the fiscal year includes budgeted

(a) capital costs,

(b) operational costs, and

(c) costs for matters under the Superintendent’s administration.

### Authority to charge fees

2 (1) Each fiscal year the Superintendent is authorized to charge insurers fees to be used to pay for the costs incurred in that year for the administration of the Acts and the regulations, including

(a) the Superintendent’s capital costs, and

(b) the Superintendent’s operational costs.

(2) The Superintendent shall, on or before October 1 of each year, send a notice to each insurer indicating the amount of the administration fee payable under section 3 for the current fiscal year and indicating the date by which the amount is due.

(3) The Superintendent may, on or before March 15 of each year, send a notice to each insurer indicating the amount of the additional fee payable under section 3.1, if any, for the current fiscal year and indicating the date by which the amount is due.

### Calculation of administration fees

3 (1) Subject to section 3.2, the administration fee payable by an insurer in respect of a fiscal year for the administration of all insurance other than the personal accident portion of automobile insurance shall be calculated in accordance with the following formula :

A = B x (D − E) C where

A is the amount of the administration fee payable by the insurer;

B is the insurer’s total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year, other than revenue for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, if any, as reported under section 35 of the Captive Insurance Companies Act and sections 44 and 46 of the Insurance Act ;

C is the total of all insurers’ total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year, other than revenue for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, if any, as reported under section 35 of the Captive Insurance Companies Act and sections 44 and 46 of the Insurance Act ;

D is the amount of the annual budget for the fiscal year estimated by the Minister for the administration of the Acts and the regulations in respect of all insurance other than the personal accident portion of automobile insurance;

E is 1/2 of the amount of the licence fees paid by all insurers for the current calendar year.

(2) Subject to section 3.2, the administration fee payable by an insurer in respect of a fiscal year for the administration of the personal accident portion of automobile insurance shall be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

F = G x J H where

F is the amount of the administration fee payable by the insurer;

G is the insurer’s total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, as reported under sections 44 and 46 of the Insurance Act ;

H is the total of all insurers’ total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, as reported under sections 44 and 46 of the Insurance Act ;

J is the amount of the annual budget for the fiscal year estimated by the Minister for the administration of the Acts and the regulations in respect of the personal accident portion of automobile insurance.

### Calculation of additional fee

3.1 (1) Subject to section 3.2, the additional fee payable by an insurer in respect of a fiscal year for the administration of all insurance other than the personal accident portion of automobile insurance shall be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

K = B x (L − D) C where

K is the amount of the additional fee payable by the insurer;

B is the insurer’s total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year, other than revenue for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, if any, as reported under section 35 of the Captive Insurance Companies Act and sections 44 and 46 of the Insurance Act ;

C is the total of all insurers’ total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year, other than revenue for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, if any, as reported under section 35 of the Captive Insurance Companies Act and sections 44 and 46 of the Insurance Act ;

L is, with respect to the administration of the Acts and the regulations for all insurance other than the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, the Superintendent’s costs incurred, including capital costs, for the fiscal year;

D is the amount of the annual budget for the fiscal year estimated by the Minister for the administration of the Acts and the regulations in respect of all insurance other than the personal accident portion of automobile insurance.

(2) Subject to section 3.2, the additional fee payable by an insurer in respect of a fiscal year for the administration of the personal accident portion of automobile insurance shall be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

M = G x (N − J) H where

M is the amount of the additional fee payable by the insurer;

G is the insurer’s total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, as reported under sections 44 and 46 of the Insurance Act ;

H is the total of all insurers’ total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, as reported under sections 44 and 46 of the Insurance Act ;

N is, with respect to the administration of the Acts and the regulations for the personal accident portion of automobile insurance, the Superintendent’s costs incurred, including capital costs, for the fiscal year;

J is the amount of the annual budget for the fiscal year estimated by the Minister for the administration of the Acts and the regulations in respect of the personal accident portion of automobile insurance.

### Minimum fee

3.2 If the total amount of the administration fees and additional fees calculated in respect of an insurer under sections 3 and 3.1 is less than $250, no administration fee or additional fee is payable by the insurer.
