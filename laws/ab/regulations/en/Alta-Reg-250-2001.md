Alta Reg 250/2001

*Enabling act: Regulations Act*

1 The Lieutenant Governor in Council, being of the opinion that the Regulation to Amend AR 175/2000, filed as AR 215/2001

(a) is available in printed form to all persons who are likely to be interested in it, and

(b) is of such length as to render its publication in The Alberta Gazette undesirable and unnecessary

dispenses with the publication of AR 215/2001.
