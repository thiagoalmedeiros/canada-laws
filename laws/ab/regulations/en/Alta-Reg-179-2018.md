# Court of Justice Civil Forms Regulation

> **Unofficial version, for personal and non-commercial use only.** © Alberta King's Printer. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the Government of Alberta. Text obtained from the [official source](https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/1266.cfm?page=2018_179.cfm&leg_type=Regs&isbncln=9780779842285&display=html) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

Alta Reg 179/2018

*Enabling act: Court of Justice Act*

### Form of documents

1 (1) A civil claim must be in Form 1 of the Schedule.

(2) A dispute note must be in Form 2 of the Schedule.

(3) A request to note in default must be in Form 3 of the Schedule.

(4) A request for default judgment must be in Form 4 of the Schedule.

(5) A dispute note to counterclaim must be in Form 5 of the Schedule.

(6) A third party claim must be in Form 6 of the Schedule.

(7) A dispute note to third party claim must be in Form 7 of the Schedule.

(8) An application must be in Form 8 of the Schedule.

(9) An affidavit must be in Form 9 of the Schedule.

(10) A notice to attend as a witness must be in Form 10 of the Schedule.

(11) A trial statement must be in Form 11 of the Schedule.

### Repeal

2 The Provincial Court Civil Claims Forms Regulation (AR 55/2001) is repealed.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of section 6(2), (3), (4)(a)(i) to (iv), (vi) and (vii), (b) and (c) and (5) to (17) of the Statutes Amendment Act, 2015 .
