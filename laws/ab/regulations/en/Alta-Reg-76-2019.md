Alta Reg 76/2019

*Enabling act: Education Act*

### Disposition of property

1 A reference to a “board” in sections 2 to 7 of the Disposition of Property Regulation does not include an operator of a charter school.

### Charter school application

2 Sections 4 and 7 to 11 of the School Transportation Regulation apply to charter schools.

### Expiry

3 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on August 31, 2028.

### Coming into force

4 This Regulation comes into force on September 1, 2019.
