Alta Reg 100/2021

*Enabling act: Civil Forfeiture Act*

### Notice of objection

1 A notice of objection to be filed with the Minister under section 1.6 of the Act must be in Form 1 of the Schedule.

### Repeal

2 The Victims Restitution and Compensation Payment Forms Regulation (AR 180/2004) is repealed.
