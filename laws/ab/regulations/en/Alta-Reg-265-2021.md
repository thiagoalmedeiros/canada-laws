Alta Reg 265/2021

*Enabling act: Police Officers Collective Bargaining Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation,

(a) “Act” means the Police Officers Collective Bargaining Act ;

(b) “relevant fiscal year” means the fiscal year in respect of which a police association is required to provide a financial statement under section 40.1(1) of the Act.

### Providing financial statements

2 A financial statement must be provided to each member

(a) free of charge, and

(b) in writing in a form that is

(i) accessible by the member so as to be usable for subsequent reference, and

(ii) capable of being retained by the member.

### Requirements for financial statements

3 (1) A financial statement must

(a) be verified by a person with sufficient knowledge of the financial affairs of the police association as accurately disclosing the financial condition and operation of the police association for the relevant fiscal year, and

(b) be signed by the person referred to in clause (a).

(2) The financial statement must include information with respect to the assets, liabilities, income and expenditures of the police association for the relevant fiscal year.

### Expiry

4 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on February 28, 2031.

### Coming into force

5 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of section 12(2) of the Restoring Balance in Alberta’s Workplaces Act, 2020 .
