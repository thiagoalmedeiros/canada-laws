*Enabling act: Education Act*

### Provincial strategic education priorities

1 For the purposes of section 18.01 of the Act, the following provincial strategic priorities for the education system are prescribed:

(a) strong foundations, where

(i) students will develop strong foundations in reading, writing and mathematics to enable future academic and life success, and

(ii) literacy and numeracy screenings will support monitoring students’ development of foundational skills and enable early identification to support appropriate interventions for students;

(b) academic mastery, where

(i) students will demonstrate mastery in reading, writing and mathematics within an environment where striving for improvement and excellence is valued, and

(ii) students are provided with high-quality and academically challenging instruction, rigorous education programs and a continuum of supports and services that enables students to reach their full potential;

(c) engaged and responsible members of society, where students will develop

(i) a comprehensive understanding of their rights and responsibilities as Canadians, Albertans and members of society,

(ii) a strong appreciation and understanding of our shared history and democratic institutions, thereby contributing to a sense of civic pride and responsible citizenship, and

(iii) the habits of mind that support active citizenship, including critical inquiry, respectful dialogue and an understanding of their personal and community responsibilities in contributing to the common good.

### Application to boards

2 The provincial strategic priorities prescribed under section 1 apply to all boards.

### Expiry

3 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on May 31, 2029.

### Coming into force

4 This Regulation comes into force on October 31, 2026.
