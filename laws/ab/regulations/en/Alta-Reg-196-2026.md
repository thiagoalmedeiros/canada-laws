Alta Reg 196/2026

*Enabling act: Mines and Minerals Act Petroleum Marketing Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,

(a) “Commission” means the Alberta Petroleum Marketing Commission;

(b) “gas royalty credit” means a gas royalty credit established under section 2;

(c) “gas royalty credit contract” means a gas royalty credit contract described in section 3;

(d) “gas-stream commodity” means natural gas, gas products and field condensate to which the Royalty Regulation applies under section 3 of that regulation;

(e) “person” includes a firm, trust, partnership, joint venture, government or government agency;

(f) “related person”, with respect to a royalty client, means a person that meets the following criteria:

(i) the royalty client has made arrangements with the person that include the transfer of the royalty client’s interest in a gas-stream commodity to the person;

(ii) the royalty client has directed the Commission to deal with the person on behalf of the royalty client with respect to the gas-stream commodity;

(g) “royalty calculation point” has the same meaning as in the Royalty Regulation;

(h) “royalty client” has the same meaning as in the Royalty Regulation;

(i) “royalty obligations” means money payable to the Crown under the Royalty Regulation, as set out in the statement of account issued to a royalty client by the Minister in accordance with the Alberta Natural Gas Royalty Guidelines , published in January 2009 by the Department, as amended or replaced from time to time;

(j) “Royalty Regulation” means the Natural Gas Royalty Regulation, 2017 (AR 211/2016);

(k) “supplier” means

(i) a royalty client, or

(ii) a related person with whom the Commission enters into a gas royalty credit contract.

### Authority for gas royalty credits

2 The Minister may establish gas royalty credits in relation to royalty obligations payable by royalty clients for gas-stream commodities.

### Gas royalty credit contracts

3 (1) The Commission may enter into a gas royalty credit contract with a supplier.

(2) A gas royalty credit contract must allow the Commission to assign gas royalty credits to a supplier as some or all of the consideration payable for the supply and delivery to the Commission of a gas-stream commodity.

(3) A gas royalty credit contract must

(a) include provisions respecting the volume of the gas-stream commodity to be supplied,

(b) include provisions respecting the pricing of the gas-stream commodity to be supplied,

(c) authorize the Commission to determine the number of gas royalty credits to be assigned in respect of the gas-stream commodity to be supplied,

(d) authorize the Commission to

(i) require the supplier to provide the Commission with the following information:

(A) an estimate of royalty obligations owing in respect of the gas-stream commodity to be supplied;

(B) any other information required for the Commission to determine the number of gas royalty credits to be assigned for each month,

and

(ii) specify the time within which the information must be provided,

and

(e) establish the delivery point, which must be in Alberta and downstream of the royalty calculation point.

(4) A gas royalty credit contract may

(a) specify a method, including the factors to be taken into account, for determining

(i) the consideration to be paid for the gas-stream commodity to be supplied and delivered, and

(ii) the reimbursement for the costs and expenses incurred by the supplier downstream of the royalty calculation point,

(b) allocate gas royalty credits among cost and price components established in the contract,

(c) include provisions respecting the timing of the application of gas royalty credits, which must not be inconsistent with section 5(3), and

(d) include any other terms that the Commission considers necessary.

(5) The Commission must, within the period specified by the Minister, give notice to the Minister that it has entered into a gas royalty credit contract and assigned gas royalty credits under the contract.

(6) A notice under subsection (5) must be in the form and contain the information required by the Minister.

### Reassignment of gas royalty credits

4 (1) A supplier who is a related person to whom gas royalty credits are assigned under a gas royalty credit contract must reassign those gas royalty credits to the royalty client.

(2) A gas royalty credit must be reassigned at a value of $1 per credit.

(3) A gas royalty credit must be reassigned no later than the last day of the month in which the gas royalty credit was assigned to the related person.

(4) The related person must, within the period specified by the Minister, give notice to the Minister of the reassignment in the form and containing the information required by the Minister.

### Application of gas royalty credits

5 (1) The Minister must apply a gas royalty credit as a credit to the account of the royalty client to whom the credit was assigned or reassigned.

(2) A gas royalty credit must be applied at a value of $1 per credit.

(3) A gas royalty credit may be applied only to royalty obligations owing by the royalty client for

(a) the month in which the gas royalty credit was assigned to the royalty client or reassigned to the royalty client, and

(b) the 2 months following the month referred to in clause (a).

(4) The value of gas royalty credits applied in respect of a month must not exceed the amount of the royalty client’s royalty obligations owing for that month.

(5) If the value of the gas royalty credits is not sufficient to pay the royalty client’s royalty obligations, the royalty client must pay the remaining amount owing to the Crown in right of Alberta in accordance with the Royalty Regulation.

### Reconciliation of gas royalty credits by Commission

6 (1) After the end of the 3-month period described in section 5(3), the Minister must give notice to the Commission of gas royalty credits that were assigned or reassigned to the royalty client but whose value exceeded the royalty client’s royalty obligations during that period.

(2) On receiving a notice under subsection (1), the Commission must

(a) pay to the royalty client an amount equal to the value of the unapplied gas royalty credits, and

(b) give notice to the Minister of the value of the unapplied credits paid out under clause (a), in the form required by the Minister.

(3) If the Commission pays a royalty client under subsection (2)(a), the gas royalty credits in respect of which the payment was made are cancelled.

(4) A cancelled gas royalty credit must not be applied to royalty obligations owing by any person.

### Transfer prohibited

7 A person must not

(a) assign or reassign a gas royalty credit except as permitted under this Regulation, or

(b) transfer a gas royalty credit except with the written approval of the Commission.

### Indemnity

8 A supplier to whom gas royalty credits have been assigned or reassigned under this Regulation or a person to whom gas royalty credits have been transferred must indemnify and hold harmless the Government of Alberta and the Commission against all third party claims, demands, actions or costs, including legal costs on a solicitor-client basis, related to, occasioned by or attributable to the supplier or transferee arising from any matter in relation to this Regulation, including the use and application of gas royalty credits.

### Recalculation

9 (1) Nothing in this Regulation restricts the Minister’s ability to make any calculation authorized under section 38 of the Act, including a recalculation, of royalty obligations in respect of a gas-stream commodity to be supplied under a gas royalty credit contract.

(2) If gas royalty credits have been applied to royalty obligations, a recalculation under section 38 of the Act of those royalty obligations does not

(a) affect the gas royalty credits assigned or applied, or

(b) require any additional payment to or by the Commission.
