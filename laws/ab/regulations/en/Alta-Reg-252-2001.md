Alta Reg 252/2001

*Enabling act: Statute Revision Act*

### RSA 2000

1 The revision of the public Acts of Alberta, revised as of December 31, 2000 and deposited with the Clerk of the Legislative Assembly in accordance with the Statute Revision Act , may, on the coming into force of the revision, be cited as the “Revised Statutes of Alberta 2000” or as “RSA 2000”.
