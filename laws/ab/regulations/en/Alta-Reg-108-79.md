Alta Reg 108/79

*Enabling act: Blind Persons ’ Rights Act*

1 The qualifications of a guide dog are prescribed as those qualifications that result from the training of a dog as a guide for a blind person at any guide dog school that is a registered member of the International Guide Dog Federation.

2 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on March 31, 2035.

Schedule Repealed AR 215/2005 s3.
