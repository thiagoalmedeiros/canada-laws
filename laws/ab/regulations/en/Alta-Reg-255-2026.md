Alta Reg 255/2026

*Enabling act: Municipal Government Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,

(a) “Land Use Bylaw” means the City of Calgary’s the Land Use Bylaw 1P2007;

(b) “Zoning Bylaw” means the City of Edmonton’s Zoning Bylaw 20001.

### Required Zoning Bylaw amendments

2 (1) Within 3 months of the coming into force of this Regulation, the City of Edmonton must pass and bring into force a bylaw amending the Zoning Bylaw in accordance with this section.

(2) The City of Edmonton must amend the Zoning Bylaw such that the permitted uses and the regulations respecting density and height for those lots or sites currently zoned as RS – Small Scale Residential Zone are the same as comparable residential lots or sites under the City of Edmonton’s repealed Zoning Bylaw 12800 as it read on December 31, 2023.

(3) The City of Edmonton must amend the Zoning Bylaw such that the parking requirements applicable to the lots or sites currently zoned as RS – Small Scale Residential Zone require the same minimum number of parking spaces as comparable residential lots or sites under the City of Edmonton’s repealed Zoning Bylaw 12800 as it read on July 1, 2020.

### Required Land Use Bylaw amendments

3 (1) Within 3 months of the coming into force of this Regulation, the City of Calgary must pass and bring into force a bylaw amending the Land Use Bylaw in accordance with this section.

(2) The City of Calgary must amend the Land Use Bylaw to establish an overlay that includes all the parcels zoned as residential within the City of Calgary that were rezoned by the reinstatement of low-density residential districts and the subsequent change to the zoning of parcels on the coming into force of the City of Calgary’s bylaw 9P2026.

### Compliance with policy on infill development

4 (1) Within 18 months of the coming into force of this Regulation, the City of Edmonton must

(a) complete a review of the Zoning Bylaw to ensure it complies with the Government’s provincial policy statement on infill development, and

(b) pass and bring into force a bylaw that amends the Zoning Bylaw to comply with the policy statement to the satisfaction of the Minister.

(2) On or before September 1, 2027, the City of Calgary must

(a) complete a review of the Land Use Bylaw to ensure it complies with the Government’s provincial policy statement on infill development, and

(b) pass and bring into force a bylaw that amends the Land Use Bylaw to comply with the policy statement to the satisfaction of the Minister.

(3) The City of Calgary must not pass a bylaw that amends the Land Use Bylaw in respect of the overall zoning of the overlay established under section 3(2) on the coming into force of this Regulation until the requirements of subsection (2) have been met.
