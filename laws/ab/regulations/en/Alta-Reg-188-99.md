Alta Reg 188/99

*Enabling act: Consumer Protection Act*

### Insurance

1 In Parts 2 and 4 of the Consumer Protection Act and in any regulations made under those Parts,

(a) “goods”, “services” and “consumer transaction” do not include insurance to which the Insurance Act applies;

(b) “supplier” does not include an insurer or reciprocal insurance exchange licensed under the Insurance Act or a person acting under a certificate of authority issued under the Insurance Act .

2 Repealed AR 245/2020 s6.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation comes into force on September 1, 1999.
