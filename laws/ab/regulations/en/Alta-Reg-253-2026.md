# Flag Display Regulation

Alta Reg 253/2026

*Enabling act: Education Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,

(a) “display”, with respect to a flag, means to fly, place, affix, erect or otherwise exhibit by a board in a manner that makes the flag clearly visible;

(b) “flag” means a symbolic graphic design that is used as a symbol, signalling device or decoration of a nation, group, organization or movement and rendered on fabric, stickers or printouts, including a digital reproduction, that maintains the proportions and elements of the original distinctive flag design;

(c) “school property” means

(i) a school building,

(ii) the land or other structures outside a school building that are under the control of a board,

(iii) any personal property, including the equipment and furnishings, owned or leased by, or otherwise under the control of, a board and used for the purposes of a school, and

(iv) any website or social media account that is under the control of a board.

### Display of National Flag of Canada and Flag of Alberta

2 (1) In this section, “flag protocol” means the following, as published and amended from time to time, as applicable:

(a) the National Flag of Canada etiquette of the Government of Canada;

(b) the Flag etiquette of the Government of Alberta.

(2) For the purpose of section 61(1) of the Act, the National Flag of Canada and the Flag of Alberta must each be flown on a separate flagpole and displayed outside a school building during a school year in accordance with the applicable flag protocols.

(3) In addition to subsection (2), the National Flag of Canada and the Flag of Alberta may be displayed on school property.

### Display of certain flags on specified dates

3 (1) In this section,

(a) “First Nation” means a band as defined in the Indian Act (Canada);

(b) “Metis settlement” means a corporation established under section 2(1) of the Metis Settlements Act ;

(c) “Metis Settlements General Council” means the corporation established under section 214(1) of the Metis Settlements Act .

(2) The following flags may be displayed inside a school building on the specified dates, or on the school day immediately before or after the specified dates, as applicable:

(a) the flags of First Nations, the Metis Settlements General Council and Metis settlements on

(i) National Indigenous Peoples Day — June 21,

(ii) National Day for Truth and Reconciliation — September 30, and

(iii) Louis Riel Day — November 16;

(b) the Royal Union Flag on

(i) Commonwealth Day — the 2nd Monday in March, and

(ii) Victoria Day — on the date prescribed in the Holidays Act (Canada).

### Display of certain flags on school property

4 The following flags may be displayed on school property:

(a) a flag that depicts an Alberta symbol of distinction under the Emblems of Alberta Act ;

(b) the flag of Vatican City;

(c) civic and municipality flags of Alberta;

(d) a flag that depicts any sports team, including a championship or competition flag.

### Display of flags if related to courses or programs of study under section 18(1)(a) or (e) of Act

5 Flags, including the national flag of a foreign country and flags prescribed in section 3(2)(a), may be displayed inside a school building for a maximum period of 14 consecutive school days at a time if the flags are directly related to the instruction of a course or program of study prescribed or approved under section 18(1)(a) or (e) of the Act.

### Display of flags during formal visit

6 The following flags may be displayed inside a school building for a maximum period of 14 consecutive school days:

(a) the national flag of a foreign country, during a formal visit to the school by a foreign head of state, diplomat or official representative of the foreign country;

(b) one or more of the flags prescribed in section 3(2)(a), during a formal visit to the school by a leader of an Indigenous body referred to in section 3(1).

### Display of flags on celebratory or ceremonial event

7 The following flags may be displayed inside a school building for the duration of a celebratory or ceremonial event:

(a) the national flag of a foreign country;

(b) one or more of the flags prescribed in section 3(2)(a).

### Expiry

8 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on May 29, 2029.

### Coming into force

9 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of section 13 of An Act to Remove Politics and Ideology from Classrooms and Amend the Education Act, 2026 .
