Alta Reg 169/2021

*Enabling act: Critical Infrastructure Defence Act*

### Essential Infrastructure

1 All land in Alberta within 2 km of the United States border is prescribed as essential infrastructure for the purposes of section 1(1)(a)(xxi) of the Act.

### Expiry

2 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on October 31, 2029.
