Alta Reg 79/2018

*Enabling act: Electoral Divisions Act*

### House located In Calgary-McCall

1 Pursuant to section 4 of the Electoral Divisions Act (SA 2017 cE-4.3), the house located at 221 Taradale Dr NE Calgary is located within the electoral division of Calgary-McCall for the purposes of the Election Act .

### House located in Sherwood Park

2 Pursuant to section 4 of the Electoral Divisions Act (SA 2017 cE-4.3), the house located at 21 Rybury Court, Sherwood Park is located within the electoral division of Sherwood Park for the purposes of the Election Act .
