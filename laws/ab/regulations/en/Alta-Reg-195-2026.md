Alta Reg 195/2026

*Enabling act: Mines and Minerals Act Petroleum Marketing Act*

### Definitions

1 (1) In this Regulation,

(a) “actual Crown gas quantity” means the quantity of a gas-stream commodity delivered for a month on the account of the Commission at the royalty calculation point under a natural gas-in-kind direction;

(b) “designated delivery location” means the delivery location designated by the Commission under section 6(1);

(c) “directed Crown gas quantity” means the quantity of a gas-stream commodity that the Commission has directed to be delivered for a month under a natural gas-in-kind direction, as determined in accordance with section 10;

(d) “directed royalty client” means the royalty client to which a natural gas-in-kind direction has been issued;

(e) “estimated consideration” means the estimated amount of the consideration payable under a gas goods-and-services direction referred to in section 24(2)(a);

(f) “estimated operational allowance” means the estimated amount of an operational allowance referred to in section 23(3)(a);

(g) “estimated value” means the estimate of the amounts that constitute the value of the directed Crown gas quantity set out in section 29(3)(a);

(h) “exchange proposal” means an exchange proposal referred to in section 28;

(i) “gas goods-and-services direction” means a direction given by the Commission under section 16 of the Petroleum Marketing Act and in accordance with Part 2 of this Regulation;

(j) “gas-stream commodity” means natural gas, gas products and field condensate to which the Royalty Regulation applies under section 3 of that regulation;

(k) “in-kind gas quantity” means, in respect of a gas-stream commodity, the total for a month of

(i) the actual Crown gas quantity, and

(ii) the quantity of the gas-stream commodity acquired for the month under the Natural Gas Resource Credit Regulation by the Commission as an agent of the Crown;

(l) “lead supplying agent” means a supplying agent designated as the lead supplying agent under a natural gas-in-kind direction;

(m) “natural gas-in-kind direction” means a direction made under section 3(1) for the delivery of directed Crown gas quantity in-kind by a directed royalty client;

(n) “net Crown gas quantity” means the Crown’s royalty share of a gas-stream commodity under section 16 of the Royalty Regulation, adjusted by the allowable costs deducted under section 19 of the Royalty Regulation;

(o) “operational allowance” means an operational allowance referred to in section 21;

(p) “operational goods and services” means the goods and services to be provided under a natural gas-in-kind direction;

(q) “Royalty Regulation” means the Natural Gas Royalty Regulation, 2017 (AR 211/2016);

(r) “supplying agent” means, in relation to the directed Crown gas quantity, a person who is, at a point between the royalty calculation point and the designated delivery location, the owner of the directed royalty client’s share of the gas-stream commodity.

(2) In this Regulation, “facility”, “gas product”, “heat content”, “royalty calculation point”, “royalty client” and “royalty compensation” have the same meanings as in the Royalty Regulation.

## Part 1 Natural Gas-in-kind Directions

### Interpretation

2 When a natural gas-in-kind direction is in effect,

(a) a gas-stream commodity delivered under the direction to a designated delivery location on the account of the Commission is deemed to consist of net Crown gas quantity until proven otherwise, and

(b) a gas-stream commodity delivered under the direction that contains net Crown gas quantity is deemed to be delivered first.

### Natural gas-in-kind direction

3 (1) If an order has been made under section 16.1 of the Royalty Regulation in respect of a quantity of a gas-stream commodity, the Commission may issue a natural gas-in-kind direction in accordance with this section directing the royalty client subject to that order to deliver the directed Crown gas quantity to the Commission.

(2) A natural gas-in-kind direction must specify

(a) that the direction is made under this Regulation,

(b) the royalty client to whom the direction is issued,

(c) the gas-stream commodity subject to the direction,

(d) the period to which the direction applies, which may be expressed as a number of months or as continuing until termination by the Commission,

(e) the designated delivery location,

(f) the royalty calculation point,

(g) any facility subject to the direction,

(h) the operational goods and services to be provided by the directed royalty client, and

(i) any other information the Commission considers necessary to enable the directed royalty client to comply with the direction.

(3) A natural gas-in-kind direction may

(a) identify supplying agents for the purposes of the direction,

(b) designate a lead supplying agent to act on behalf of the directed royalty client and one or more supplying agents, and

(c) direct a supplying agent to provide operational goods and services on behalf of the directed royalty client in respect of the portion of the directed Crown gas quantity that is commingled with the supplying agent’s share of the directed royalty client’s production.

(4) The Commission must provide the natural gas-in-kind direction to the directed royalty client between the following dates:

(a) 30 days after the order is made under section 16.1 of the Royalty Regulation;

(b) 60 days before the first day of the first month to which the natural gas-in-kind direction applies.

### Obligations of directed royalty client and supplying agent

4 (1) A directed royalty client and a supplying agent subject to a natural gas-in-kind direction must

(a) manage the directed Crown gas quantity in a commercially reasonable manner consistent with accepted industry practices for the marketing and transportation of gas-stream commodities,

(b) take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized use of the directed Crown gas quantity,

(c) account to the Crown for any profit, gain or other benefit derived from the directed Crown gas quantity while it is in the possession or control of the directed royalty client or the supplying agent, as the case may be,

(d) coordinate the provision of the operational goods and services required to deliver the directed Crown gas quantity to the Commission at the designated delivery location, and

(e) if costs and charges are incurred in respect of the operational goods and services referred to in clause (d), apply to the Commission for an operational allowance in accordance with section 21 as payment for the operational goods and services provided.

(2) The directed royalty client, lessee and supplying agent must act as agents of the Crown in respect of the directed Crown gas quantity and the provision of operational goods and services unless the Commission directs otherwise.

(3) A directed royalty client must

(a) act as an agent of the lessee in respect of the lessee’s share of the gas-stream commodity subject to the natural gas-in-kind direction, and

(b) give notice to the lessee that the lessee’s share of the gas-stream commodity is subject to the natural gas-in-kind direction.

(4) No person has any of the rights, privileges, prerogatives or immunities of the Crown by reason only of acting as an agent of the Crown under a natural gas-in-kind direction.

(5) Section 86(1) of the Mines and Minerals Act applies to a directed Crown gas quantity.

### Notice of natural gas-in-kind direction

5 (1) The Commission must give notice to the Minister of

(a) the issuance of a natural gas-in-kind direction,

(b) with respect to each gas-stream commodity subject to the natural gas-in-kind direction, the directed Crown gas quantity to be delivered to the Commission under the direction for each month to which the direction applies, and

(c) any other information required by the Minister.

(2) A notice under subsection (1) must be given when the Commission

(a) issues the natural gas-in-kind direction, and

(b) gives notice to the directed royalty client under section 10(3).

### Designation of delivery location

6 (1) The Commission must, for the purposes of a natural gas-in-kind direction, designate a delivery location.

(2) A designated delivery location must be

(a) in Alberta, and

(b) connected to a market hub that is capable of accepting deliveries of the directed Crown gas quantity.

### Delivery of directed Crown gas quantity

7 (1) A directed royalty client must deliver the directed Crown gas quantity to the Commission at the designated delivery location.

(2) If the royalty calculation point is upstream from a processing facility subject to the natural gas-in-kind direction and the directed Crown gas quantity passes through the processing facility before the designated delivery location, the directed royalty client must deliver any gas product obtained from the directed Crown gas quantity as a result of the processing.

### Notice of delivery

8 Within 30 days after a directed royalty client delivers the directed Crown gas quantity to the designated delivery location, the Commission must give notice to the Minister of the actual Crown gas quantity delivered for the month under the natural gas-in-kind direction.

### Forecast Crown gas quantity

9 (1) A directed royalty client must prepare a forecast respecting the quantity of each gas-stream commodity subject to the order made under section 16.1 of the Royalty Regulation that the directed royalty client anticipates recovering and obtaining for a month.

(2) The forecast must include

(a) an estimate of the Crown’s royalty share of the quantity referred to in subsection (1), and

(b) the estimated net Crown gas quantity with respect to the royalty share referred to in clause (a).

(3) The directed royalty client must provide the forecast to the Commission at least 50 days before the first day of the month to which it relates.

(4) A forecast must be in the form required by the Commission.

(5) In preparing a forecast, the directed royalty client

(a) must make reasonable efforts to forecast accurately, and

(b) may use any appropriate methodology, if the methodology

(i) is applied consistently,

(ii) is based on reasonable and supportable assumptions, and

(iii) produces a forecast that is verifiable by the Commission.

(6) The Commission may give notice to the directed royalty client to provide the Commission with

(a) a description of the methodology used to prepare the forecast, and

(b) the data inputs and assumptions used to prepare the forecast.

(7) The directed royalty client must provide the information referred to in a notice given under subsection (6) by the deadline specified in the notice.

(8) If the net Crown gas quantity exceeds or is less than the forecast Crown gas quantity by 10% or more in 3 or more months of a 12-month rolling period, then the Commission may, in respect of any month in the upcoming 12-month period,

(a) require that the forecast for the month be accompanied by detailed supporting calculations, including the data inputs and assumptions used to prepare the forecast,

(b) require the forecast to be accompanied by a statement indicating approval of the forecast by

(i) the chief financial officer of the directed royalty client, or

(ii) another individual, referred to by either the individual’s name or title, who the Minister has approved in advance as an individual who may approve the forecast,

or

(c) substitute all or part of the forecast with a forecast prepared by the Minister.

(9) If the directed royalty client fails to provide a forecast by the deadline specified in subsection (3), the Commission may prepare its own forecast based on any information available to the Commission.

(10) If the directed royalty client fails to provide a forecast by the deadline specified in subsection (3), the directed royalty client is liable to pay to the Commission a penalty of up to $5000 for each day or part of a day after the deadline that it fails to provide the forecast.

(11) If the directed royalty client fails to provide the information required in a notice given under subsection (6) by the deadline specified in the notice, the directed royalty client is liable to pay to the Commission a penalty of up to $5000 for each day or part of a day after the deadline that it fails to provide the information.

### Determination of directed Crown gas quantity

10 (1) On receiving a forecast under section 9, the Commission must determine the directed Crown gas quantity for the month to which the forecast relates.

(2) The directed Crown gas quantity must not exceed the forecast Crown gas quantity for the month determined under section 9(2)(b).

(3) The Commission must give notice of the directed Crown gas quantity to the directed royalty client not less than 30 days before the first day of each month to which the natural gas-in-kind direction applies.

### Delivery under natural gas-in-kind direction

11 (1) A directed royalty client, a lessee of a gas-stream commodity subject to a natural gas-in-kind direction and a supplying agent subject to a natural gas-in-kind direction are not entitled to compensation for

(a) the actual Crown gas quantity,

(b) direct, indirect or consequential losses arising from the natural gas-in-kind direction, or

(c) costs incurred in respect of the directed Crown gas quantity upstream from the royalty calculation point, subject to subsection (3).

(2) The actual Crown gas quantity must be delivered free and clear of all interests, charges and liens.

(3) A directed royalty client, lessee or supplying agent that is acting as an agent of the Crown under a natural gas-in-kind direction is not entitled to compensation other than an operational allowance, if applicable.

(4) A directed royalty client or a supplying agent or other person bound by a natural gas-in-kind direction must mitigate any costs or losses resulting from the direction.

(5) The Commission may determine that consideration applied for in respect of an operational allowance is not just and reasonable if a directed royalty client or a supplying agent or other person bound by a natural gas-in-kind direction has not complied with subsection (4).

### Amendment, extension, suspension and cancellation of directions

12 (1) The Commission may suspend or cancel a natural gas-in-kind direction.

(2) Unless the directed royalty client agrees to a shorter period, the Commission must give notice to the directed royalty client of the suspension or cancellation of the natural gas-in-kind direction not less than 30 days before the first day of the month in which the suspension or cancellation is to take effect.

(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), the Commission may amend, extend, suspend or cancel a natural gas-in-kind direction on giving reasonable notice to the directed royalty client if delivery of the directed Crown gas quantity is prevented or materially impaired by an event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of the Commission, including a natural disaster, war, insurrection, labour dispute, infrastructure failure, regulatory action or market disruption.

### Quality and equalization reporting

13 (1) A directed royalty client must report to the Commission, for each month to which a natural gas-in-kind direction applies,

(a) the heat content and measurements of the actual Crown gas quantity at the royalty calculation point,

(b) the heat content and measurements of the actual Crown gas quantity at the designated delivery location,

(c) any changes to heat content or measurements resulting from any commingling or processing of the actual Crown gas quantity between the royalty calculation point and the designated delivery location, and

(d) the value to the Crown of any equalization applied to the actual Crown gas quantity as a result of the changes referred to in clause (c).

(2) A report under subsection (1) must be provided by the 10th day of the month following the month to which the report relates.

### Origin reporting

14 (1) In this section,

(a) “Canadian origin” means, in respect of a directed Crown gas quantity, that the gas-stream commodities contained in the directed Crown gas quantity are naturally occurring substances recovered and obtained entirely within Canada;

(b) “competent authority” means the applicable regulatory body of the country of destination to which the Commission exports the directed Crown gas quantity from Canada;

(c) “current statement” means a statement referred to in subsection (2) or (3) that is executed not more than 365 days before the delivery of the directed Crown gas quantity to which it relates.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), a directed royalty client must provide a current statement to the Commission certifying that the directed Crown gas quantity is of Canadian origin, if an applicable original certification exists in respect of a directed Crown gas quantity.

(3) The Commission may accept a current statement from a supplying agent certifying that the portion of the directed Crown gas quantity that is commingled with the lessee’s share of production owned by the supplying agent is of Canadian origin.

(4) A current statement must be in the form required by the Commission and contain the information required to establish that the directed Crown gas quantity is of Canadian origin.

(5) A current statement must be provided to the Commission before or concurrently with each delivery of directed Crown gas quantity.

(6) The directed royalty client or supplying agent that provides a current statement must provide any additional documents requested by the Commission for the purposes of this section, which may include

(a) manufacturing process overview documents, including flow charts, schematics and documents relating to production capacity, product identification and product specifications, and

(b) documents relating to the transportation of the directed Crown gas quantity.

(7) The documents requested under subsection (6) must be provided within 20 days after the directed royalty client or supplying agent receives the request from the Commission.

(8) If, after the directed Crown gas quantity is delivered to the Commission and the Commission has made good faith efforts to represent to a competent authority that the directed Crown gas quantity is of Canadian origin based on the current statement and documents provided under this section, the competent authority determines that the directed Crown gas quantity is ineligible for preferential tariff treatment based on a failure to establish to the satisfaction of the competent authority that the directed Crown gas quantity is of Canadian origin, then the declarant is liable to pay to the Commission a penalty equal to any tariffs, customs, duties, taxes, fees, interest and penalties incurred directly or indirectly by the Commission as a result of that determination.

(9) A directed royalty client that fails to provide a current statement or a document requested under subsection (6) by the deadline specified in subsection (5) or (7), as the case may be, is liable to pay to the Commission a penalty of up to $5000 for each day or part of a day after the applicable deadline that it fails to provide the current statement or document.

### Reconciliation of deliveries

15 (1) If the actual Crown gas quantity is less than the net Crown gas quantity owing, the directed royalty client must pay the royalty compensation owing in respect of the portion of the net Crown gas quantity that was not delivered in-kind as required under the natural gas-in-kind direction.

(2) The amount to be paid under subsection (1) is the royalty compensation that would otherwise be payable in respect of that portion of the net Crown gas quantity if that portion were not subject to a natural gas-in-kind direction.

(3) If the actual Crown gas quantity is more than the net Crown gas quantity owing, the Commission must pay the directed royalty client in cash for the value of the actual Crown gas quantity supplied in excess of the net Crown gas quantity.

(4) For the purposes of subsection (3),

(a) the quantity of the excess supply of the net Crown gas quantity for a month is the difference between

(i) the actual Crown gas quantity, and

(ii) the net Crown gas quantity owing,

and

(b) the payment must be determined by multiplying the quantity of the excess supply of the net Crown gas quantity for a month by the applicable reference price under the Royalty Regulation.

(5) A payment made under subsection (3) for the quantity of excess supply must not be considered in any royalty recalculation under the Royalty Regulation.

## Part 2 Gas Goods-and-services Directions

### Direction to provide goods and services

16 (1) For the purposes of section 16 of the Petroleum Marketing Act ,

(a) “goods” means goods required to receive, handle, manage, transmit, store, process, deliver, dispose of or otherwise deal with the in-kind gas quantity downstream from the royalty calculation point to the designated delivery location;

(b) “services” means any services required to receive, handle, manage, transmit, store, process, deliver, dispose of or otherwise deal with in-kind gas quantity;

(c) “supplier” means,

(i) a directed royalty client,

(ii) a lessee,

(iii) a supplying agent,

(iv) a person who provides access to or use of a pipeline or facility used in respect of gas-stream commodities, or

(v) an agent or affiliate of any person referred to in subclauses (i) to (iv).

(2) For greater certainty, a gas goods-and-services direction may direct

(a) a supplier of a pipeline to transmit the in-kind gas quantity in the supplier’s pipeline to a point in Alberta designated by the Commission for that purpose,

(b) a supplier of a facility to

(i) receive the in-kind gas quantity at the facility,

(ii) manage or handle the in-kind gas quantity through the facility, or

(iii) process or store the in-kind gas quantity in the facility,

and

(c) a supplier that is a supplying agent to accept the in-kind gas quantity for any activity identified by the Commission that supports the management, delivery or disposal of the in-kind gas quantity.

(3) A gas goods-and-services direction may be made for an indefinite period or for a specified period.

(4) A gas goods-and-services direction may direct a supplying agent to act as an agent of the Crown for purposes leading directly or indirectly to the disposal of the in-kind gas quantity.

(5) No person has any of the rights, privileges, prerogatives or immunities of the Crown by reason only of acting as an agent of the Crown in respect of a gas goods-and-services direction.

### Content of gas goods-and-services direction

17 Without limiting the generality of section 16(2) of the Petroleum Marketing Act , a gas goods-and-services direction must specify the following, as applicable:

(a) the directed royalty client;

(b) the in-kind gas quantity to be delivered to the Commission;

(c) the goods or services to be provided under the gas goods-and-services direction;

(d) the specific period during which the gas goods-and-services direction is in effect or whether the direction is in effect for an indefinite period;

(e) the location where the goods or services are to be provided;

(f) the person to whom the goods or services are to be provided;

(g) the quantity of the goods to be provided, if readily ascertainable;

(h) the intervals at which the goods or services are to be provided;

(i) with respect to any portion of the in-kind gas quantity that is not directed Crown gas quantity,

(i) the consideration that the Commission is required to pay for the goods or services provided, or

(ii) confirmation that the supplier may apply to the Commission for just and reasonable consideration for the goods or services provided.

### Suspension or cancellation of gas goods-and-services direction

18 The Commission may suspend or cancel a gas goods-and-services direction on giving reasonable notice to the supplier before the suspension or cancellation is to take effect.

## Part 3 Consideration and Operational Allowance

### Definition

19 In this Part, “applicant” means a person who applies for an operational allowance under section 21.

### Just and reasonable consideration

20 (1) For the purposes of section 16 of the Petroleum Marketing Act , the Commission may determine just and reasonable consideration based on one or more of the following:

(a) the amount charged by a supplier in an arm’s length transaction for goods or services;

(b) the actual cost incurred by a supplier to produce or acquire goods or deliver services;

(c) the price of comparable goods or services, if that price is published and generally adopted by buyers and sellers of the goods or services;

(d) the price for comparable goods or services prescribed or determined under an enactment;

(e) the average price paid for comparable goods or services in an arm’s length transaction;

(f) the net book value of goods according to the records of the owner of the goods;

(g) in the case of pipeline transportation, a tariff charged for the service, if

(i) the tariff is fixed or approved for the service by a regulatory authority having jurisdiction to do so, or

(ii) the tariff is generally agreed to and paid by persons who obtain the pipeline transportation service under an arm’s length transaction.

(2) The Commission may publish

(a) a schedule of standard consideration payable for goods and services, or

(b) a standard mechanism or formula for determining the consideration payable for goods and services.

### Application for operational allowance

21 (1) The following persons may apply to the Commission for an operational allowance:

(a) the directed royalty client, on its own behalf or on behalf of a supplying agent;

(b) if the Commission has designated a lead supplying agent, the lead supplying agent, on its own behalf and on behalf of any supplying agent in respect of which it has been designated.

(2) An application for an operational allowance must

(a) be made in the form and manner required by the Commission,

(b) be submitted within one year after the last day of the month in respect of which the operational allowance was incurred,

(c) contain the information and evidence required by the Commission, and

(d) if the Commission requires, be accompanied by a statement indicating that the information contained in the application has been approved by an authorized officer of the applicant, in any manner the Commission directs.

### Payment of operational allowance

22 (1) Subject to subsection (3), the Commission must pay an operational allowance to an applicant in respect of the operational goods and services that are provided to the applicant under a natural gas-in-kind direction for each month to which the direction applies.

(2) An operational allowance may only be paid in respect of the costs of operational goods and services that

(a) were actually incurred by the applicant or a supplying agent on whose behalf the applicant is acting,

(b) are reasonably required to facilitate the handling and delivery of the directed Crown gas quantity between the royalty calculation point and the designated delivery location, including services required to

(i) transport the directed Crown gas quantity from the royalty calculation point to the designated delivery location,

(ii) process the directed Crown gas quantity, if authorized to do so by the Commission, including processing it into a product obtained from the directed Crown gas quantity,

(iii) store the directed Crown gas quantity, if authorized to do so by the Commission, and

(iv) otherwise handle the directed Crown gas quantity between the royalty calculation point and the designated delivery location, including in respect of any authorized processing or storage,

and

(c) are of a similar nature to the goods or services provided to a supplying agent in respect of the delivery of the supplying agent’s share of the directed royalty client’s share of the gas-stream commodities, unless the Commission has agreed in writing to other goods or services.

(3) The Commission is not required to pay an operational allowance if the applicant, or a supplying agent on whose behalf the applicant is acting, fails to satisfy any of the following conditions:

(a) the directed Crown gas quantity was delivered to the designated delivery location on the account of the Commission;

(b) the directed Crown gas quantity was delivered free and clear of all interests, charges and liens;

(c) unless prior written consent for alternative transportation or handling was obtained from the Commission, the directed Crown gas quantity was

(i) transported in an uninterrupted manner to the designated delivery location, and

(ii) handled and transported in a manner that preserved its quality and characteristics at the royalty calculation point;

(d) the directed Crown gas quantity was not altered in quality before its delivery to the designated delivery location, unless prior written consent was obtained from the Commission.

(4) An operational allowance must not be paid in respect of recovery for any costs or charges that are allowable costs as defined in the Royalty Regulation or where the directed royalty client otherwise recovers such costs or charges against the Crown through the calculation of royalty compensation payable under the Royalty Regulation or otherwise.

### Amount of operational allowance

23 (1) Subject to section 22(3), the Commission may, for the purpose of determining the operational allowance for a month, determine

(a) which costs referred to in section 22(2) are to be included in the operational allowance, and

(b) the amount to be paid in respect of each such cost, in accordance with section 20.

(2) The operational allowance for a month must not exceed the difference between

(a) the fair market value at the designated delivery location of

(i) the actual Crown gas quantity, or

(ii) the product obtained from the directed Crown gas quantity,

and

(b) the royalty compensation that would otherwise be payable if the actual Crown gas quantity were not subject to a natural gas-in-kind direction.

(3) For the purposes of this section, the Commission may, with respect to the month for which the operational allowance is being determined,

(a) estimate the operational allowance for a month and, subject to clause (b)(ii), consent to that amount, and

(b) following the final determination of the operational allowance for the month,

(i) if the operational allowance is more than the estimated operational allowance, consent to the higher operational allowance, or

(ii) if the operational allowance is less than the estimated operational allowance,

(A) issue an invoice for the difference to the applicant or the supplying agent on whose behalf the applicant is acting, or

(B) deduct the difference from the estimated operational allowance consented to under clause (a) for the next month or months.

(4) An applicant or supplying agent to whom an invoice is issued under subsection (3)(b)(ii)(A) must pay to the Commission the amount shown in the invoice on or before the last day of the month following the month in which the invoice was issued.

(5) The Commission may pay to the applicant a reasonable share of the estimated operational allowance consented to under subsection (3)(a) or the higher operational allowance consented to under subsection (3)(b)(i) on or before the last day of the month following the month for which the estimated operational allowance was calculated.

(6) If a condition referred to in section 22(3) is not satisfied, the Commission may

(a) reduce or refuse to pay the operational allowance or the estimated operational allowance, in whole or in part,

(b) set off the financial impact on the Crown of the loss of quality or value resulting from the failure to satisfy the condition against any other amount owing under this Regulation by the Commission to the applicant or to the supplying agent on whose behalf the applicant is acting, or

(c) if the operational allowance has been paid, recover the amount of the payment by legal action.

### Amount of consideration payable under gas goods-and-services direction

24 (1) For the purposes of determining the consideration payable to a supplier under a gas goods-and-services direction, the Commission may determine

(a) which costs are eligible for the payment of consideration, and

(b) the amount to be paid in respect of each such cost, in accordance with section 20.

(2) For the purposes of this section, the Commission may, with respect to the month for which the consideration payable is being determined,

(a) estimate the consideration payable for the month and, subject to clause (b)(ii), consent to the estimated consideration, and

(b) following the final determination of the consideration payable for the month,

(i) if the consideration payable is more than the estimated consideration, consent to the higher consideration, or

(ii) if the consideration payable is less than the estimated consideration,

(A) issue an invoice for the difference to the supplier, or

(B) deduct the difference from the estimated consideration consented to under clause (a) for the next month or months.

(3) A supplier to whom an invoice is issued under subsection (2)(b)(ii)(A) must pay to the Commission the amount shown in the invoice on or before the last day of the month following the month in which the invoice was issued.

(4) The Commission may pay to the supplier a reasonable share of the estimated consideration on or before the last day of the month following the month referred to in subsection (2).

### Review of decision

25 (1) The following persons may file a statement of objection with the Commission:

(a) in respect of a decision made under section 23,

(i) the directed royalty client, on its own behalf or on behalf of a supplying agent, or

(ii) if the Commission has designated a lead supplying agent, the lead supplying agent, on its own behalf and on behalf of any supplying agent in respect of which it has been designated;

(b) in respect of a decision made under section 24, a supplier.

(2) A statement of objection must

(a) be filed with the Commission within 60 days after the date of the Commission’s decision, and

(b) specify the grounds of the objection and the requested changes to the decision.

(3) On receiving a statement of objection, the Commission may

(a) decide to review the decision, or

(b) refuse to review the decision.

(4) If the Commission refuses to review a decision, the Commission must, as soon as is reasonably practicable, give notice of the refusal to the person who filed the statement of objection.

(5) If the Commission decides to review the decision, the Commission must, as soon as is reasonably practicable, inform the person who filed the statement of objection whether the person is required to give notice of the filed statement of objection to any person.

(6) If the Commission requires a person to give notice under subsection (5), the Commission must provide the person with the following information:

(a) the identity of each person to whom notice must be given;

(b) the time within which notice must be given;

(c) the manner in which notice must be given;

(d) the form and content of the notice.

(7) A person to whom notice is given under subsection (5) may file a submission in respect of the statement of objection with the Commission within 20 days after the day on which notice was given.

(8) If the Commission decides to review a decision, the Commission must, within 180 days after the statement of objection is filed,

(a) review the original decision, taking into account the statement of objection and any submissions filed under subsection (7), and

(b) confirm, vary or replace the original decision.

(9) The Commission must give notice of the decision to confirm, vary or replace the original decision and provide a copy of that decision to

(a) the person who filed the statement of objection, and

(b) any person who filed a submission under subsection (7).

(10) Notwithstanding subsection (8), the Commission may, on application by a party or on its own motion, extend the period referred to in subsection (8) for a further reasonable period.

### Appeal to Alberta Utilities Commission

26 (1) Subject to subsection (2), a decision of the Commission made under section 25(3)(b) or (8)(b) may be appealed to the Alberta Utilities Commission by the person who filed the statement of objection in respect of the decision.

(2) An appeal

(a) may only relate to the matters included in the statement of objection, and

(b) must be commenced by

(i) filing a notice of appeal with the secretary of the Alberta Utilities Commission within 60 days after the day on which the Commission gives notice under section 25(3)(b) or (8)(b), as the case may be, and

(ii) serving a copy of the notice of appeal on the Commission.

(3) When served with a copy of the notice of appeal, the Commission must provide copies of the following to the Alberta Utilities Commission:

(a) the original decision made under section 23 or 24;

(b) the statement of objection;

(c) any submissions filed with the Commission under section 25(7);

(d) the Commission’s decision made under section 25(3) or (8).

(4) On hearing an appeal, the Alberta Utilities Commission may, by order,

(a) confirm or vary the original decision made under section 23 or 24,

(b) refer the matter back to the Commission with directions to make a new decision under section 23 or 24 in accordance with any instructions given in the order, or

(c) make any other disposition of the appeal that it considers appropriate in the circumstances.

(5) A new decision made by the Commission as directed under subsection (4)(b) may be reviewed by the Commission and appealed to the Alberta Utilities Commission in accordance with this section and section 25, as the case may be.

## Part 4 Product Exchange

### Definitions

27 In this Part,

(a) “delivery month” means the month in which an exchange of the directed Crown gas quantity and an exchanged hydrocarbon product occurs under an exchange proposal;

(b) “exchange point” means the point at which the Commission receives delivery of a hydrocarbon product under an exchange proposal;

(c) “exchanged hydrocarbon product” means a hydrocarbon product to be exchanged for the directed Crown gas quantity under an exchange proposal;

(d) “transfer point” means the point between the royalty calculation point and the designated delivery location where the Commission, under an exchange proposal, transfers ownership of the directed Crown gas quantity to

(i) the directed royalty client, or

(ii) another person, as directed by the directed royalty client.

### Proposal to exchange directed Crown gas quantity

28 (1) A directed royalty client may propose an exchange of the directed Crown gas quantity for an alternative hydrocarbon product.

(2) An exchange proposal must be submitted in writing to the Commission at least 40 days before the first day of the delivery month in which the exchange is proposed to occur.

(3) An exchange proposal must

(a) identify the transfer point and the exchange point,

(b) set out the estimated value of the directed Crown gas quantity for the delivery month in which the exchange is proposed to occur,

(c) identify the exchanged hydrocarbon product, including its quantity, quality and specifications,

(d) specify the actual value of the exchanged hydrocarbon product or propose a mechanism for determining the actual value of the exchanged hydrocarbon product, and

(e) include any other information required by the Commission.

(4) For the purposes of subsection (3)(b), the estimated value of the directed Crown gas quantity must be based on

(a) an estimate of the royalty compensation that would be payable in respect of the directed Crown gas quantity if it were not subject to a natural gas-in-kind direction, and

(b) the estimated operational allowance to be incurred in delivering the directed Crown gas quantity to the transfer point, if the exchange point is not immediately downstream from the royalty calculation point.

### Acceptance of exchange proposal

29 (1) On receiving an exchange proposal, the Commission may

(a) accept the exchange proposal,

(b) reject the exchange proposal, or

(c) propose changes to the exchange proposal or specify further conditions that, if accepted, would result in the acceptance of the exchange proposal.

(2) An exchange proposal is considered to be rejected if, at least 25 days before the first day of the delivery month in which the exchange is proposed to occur,

(a) the Commission fails to respond to an exchange proposal in writing, or

(b) the exchange proposal is otherwise not finalized.

(3) If the Commission accepts an exchange proposal, the Commission is considered to have consented to

(a) the estimated value of the directed Crown gas quantity for the delivery month,

(b) the estimated value of the exchanged hydrocarbon product for the delivery month if the actual value of the exchanged hydrocarbon product has not been determined in the exchange proposal, and

(c) the exchange point modifying the designated delivery location in the natural gas-in-kind direction with respect to the directed Crown gas quantity subject to the exchange proposal.

(4) The acceptance of an exchange proposal is subject to compliance with the following conditions by the directed royalty client:

(a) the accepted quantity of exchanged hydrocarbon product must be delivered on behalf of the Commission at the exchange point;

(b) title to the exchanged hydrocarbon product must be transferred free and clear of all interests, charges and liens to

(i) the Commission, or

(ii) another person, as directed by the Commission;

(c) the exchanged hydrocarbon product must meet the accepted quality and specifications unless prior written authorization has been obtained from the Commission;

(d) the Commission must have been provided the records it requested under section 33 in respect of the directed Crown gas quantity and the exchanged hydrocarbon product;

(e) any other conditions specified in the accepted exchange proposal.

(5) If the directed royalty client fails to comply with a condition referred to in subsection (4), the Commission may

(a) refuse to complete the exchange, in whole or in part,

(b) set off the financial impact on the Crown of the loss of quality or value resulting from the failure to comply against any other amount owing to the directed royalty client by the Commission under this Regulation, or

(c) if the exchange has occurred, recover the amount of the financial impact referred to in clause (b) by legal action.

### Reporting by directed royalty client

30 (1) If the Commission has accepted an exchange proposal, the directed royalty client must, in respect of each delivery month for which the accepted exchange is in effect, provide a report to the Commission with the following information:

(a) the reference price applicable to the directed Crown gas quantity as determined under the Royalty Regulation;

(b) any other information required by the Commission to establish the value of the directed Crown gas quantity or the exchanged hydrocarbon product.

(2) The report must be provided to the Commission by the last day of the month following the delivery month to which the report relates.

### Reconciliation

31 (1) Within 4 months after the last day of the delivery month, the Commission must determine the actual value of the directed Crown gas quantity at the royalty calculation point for the delivery month as if the directed Crown gas quantity were not subject to a natural gas-in-kind direction, and

(a) if the actual value is more than the estimated value consented to by the Commission, either

(i) issue an invoice to the directed royalty client for the difference between the actual value and the estimated value, or

(ii) add the difference between the actual value and the estimated value to the value of the directed Crown gas quantity consented to for the next month or months,

or

(b) if the actual value is less than the estimated value consented to by the Commission, pay to the directed royalty client the difference between the estimated value and the actual value.

(2) If the Commission has consented to an estimated value of the exchanged hydrocarbon product for a month, the Commission must, within a reasonable period after the month following the delivery month, determine the actual value of the exchanged hydrocarbon product received by the Commission based on the accepted pricing mechanism for determining the actual value of the exchanged hydrocarbon product and,

(a) if the actual value is more than the estimated value consented to by the Commission, pay to the directed royalty client the difference between the actual value and the estimated value, or

(b) if the actual value is less than the estimated value, either

(i) issue an invoice to the directed royalty client for the difference between the estimated value and the actual value, or

(ii) deduct the difference between the estimated value and the actual value from the value of the hydrocarbon product consented to for the next month or months.

(3) If the Commission has consented to an estimated operational allowance under section 23(3)(a) with respect to the directed Crown gas quantity to be exchanged, that estimated operational allowance is considered to be the estimated operational allowance for the purposes of section 23(5), subject to any adjustments relating to the exchange that the Commission makes in the final determination of the operational allowance.

## Part 5 General

### Notice to provide information

32 (1) The Commission may give notice to any of the following persons to provide the Commission with the information referred to in subsection (2):

(a) a lessee or a royalty client that is the subject of an order made under section 16.1 of the Royalty Regulation;

(b) a person who is the owner of the directed royalty client’s share of the gas-stream commodities at a point between the royalty calculation point and the designated delivery location;

(c) a supplier or potential supplier.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1), the Commission may require a person to provide the following information:

(a) an estimate of the quantity of gas-stream commodities to be recovered and obtained from each directed royalty client for a specified month or months;

(b) an estimate of the anticipated net Crown gas quantity to be recovered and obtained in a specified month or months;

(c) the identity of each person who is the owner of the directed royalty client’s share of the gas-stream commodities at a point between the royalty calculation point and the designated delivery location;

(d) the share of production owned by each person identified under clause (c);

(e) the identity of each person that provides operational goods and services to or on behalf of each person identified under clause (c);

(f) a description of the operational goods and services that each person referred to in clause (c) or (e) provides;

(g) estimates of the costs of the operational goods and services referred to in clause (f);

(h) a description of the goods or services provided under a gas goods-and-services direction;

(i) estimates of the costs of the goods or services provided under a gas goods-and-services direction;

(j) any other information the Commission considers necessary.

(3) The information referred to in subsection (2) must be provided by the deadline specified in the notice.

(4) A person who fails to provide the required information to the Commission by the deadline specified in the notice is liable to pay to the Commission a penalty of up to $5000 for each day or part of a day after the deadline that it fails to provide the information.

### Records

33 (1) A royalty client that is the subject of an order made under section 16.1 of the Royalty Regulation and a person subject to a gas goods-and-services direction must maintain records in accordance with this section.

(2) Records maintained under this section must include, in respect of each order or direction to which the person is subject, financial, technical and other information respecting the following:

(a) directed Crown gas quantities;

(b) operational goods and services provided under a natural gas-in-kind direction;

(c) goods or services provided under a gas goods-and-services direction;

(d) any just and reasonable consideration paid;

(e) approved exchange proposals;

(f) any other matter that may be relevant to a decision of the Commission under this Regulation or section 16 of the Petroleum Marketing Act .

(3) The records must be kept for at least 6 years after the last day of the year to which the records relate.

(4) The Commission may require a person to provide any of the records referred to in subsection (2) if, in the opinion of the Commission, the information is necessary for the Commission to make a decision under this Regulation or section 16 of the Petroleum Marketing Act .

### Indemnity

34 (1) The directed royalty client is liable and must indemnify and hold harmless the Commission for any costs, losses, damages or liabilities suffered or incurred by the Commission arising from

(a) the failure of the directed royalty client or a supplying agent that is subject to the natural gas-in-kind direction to deliver the directed Crown gas quantity in accordance with the direction, or

(b) any act, omission or operation of the directed royalty client or a supplying agent that is subject to the natural gas-in-kind direction, while the directed Crown gas quantity is in the possession, custody or control of that person, in performing the direction to the extent that the loss is caused by the person’s negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of this Regulation.

(2) The supplier to whom a gas goods-and-services direction has been issued is liable and must indemnify and hold harmless the Commission for any costs, losses, damages or liabilities suffered or incurred by the Commission arising from

(a) the failure of the supplier to deliver the in-kind gas quantity in accordance with the direction, or

(b) any act, omission or operation of the supplier or an agent of the supplier while the in-kind gas quantity is in the possession, custody or control of that person, in performing the direction to the extent that the loss is caused by the person’s negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of this Regulation.

### Readjustment

35 Nothing in this Regulation limits the powers of the Minister under section 38 of the Mines and Minerals Act to audit or examine records or to make calculations or amendments.
