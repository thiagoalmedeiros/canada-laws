Alta Reg 250/2021

*Enabling act: Geothermal Resource Development Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation, “Act” means the Geothermal Resource Development Act .

### Prescribed facility — excluded

2 For the purpose of section 1(1)(c)(v) of the Act, “facility” does not include a facility

(a) that is approved or licensed under another energy resource enactment, and

(b) that does not produce commercial heat or power and the heat or power produced is used entirely at the facility site for the purposes of the site or development of the facility.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation comes into force on January 1, 2022.
