*Enabling act: Public Service Employee Relations Act*

### Application of section 12(1)(e.1) of the Act

1 For the purposes of section 12(1.1) of the Act, section 12(1)(e.1) of the Act applies in respect of a person employed by the Crown in right of Alberta as of January 1, 2020.
