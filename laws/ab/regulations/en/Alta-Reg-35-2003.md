Alta Reg 35/2003

*Enabling act: Electronic Transactions Act*

### Interpretation

1 (1) In this Regulation, “Act” means the Electronic Transactions Act .

(2) For the purposes of section 1(1)(h)(ii) of the Act, an agency, board, commission, corporation, office or other body listed in the Schedule is considered to be a public body.

### Expiry

2 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on June 30, 2028.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation comes into force on the coming into force of the Electronic Transactions Act .
