Alta Reg 51/2002

*Enabling act: Prompt Payment and Construction Lien Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,

(a) “Act” means the Prompt Payment and Construction Lien Act ;

(b) a reference to a Form is to a form in the Schedule to this Regulation.

### Notice of dispute

1.1 A notice of dispute under section 32.2(2) of the Act is to be in Form 1.

### Notice of non-payment

1.2 (1) A notice of non-payment under section 32.3(5)(a) of the Act is to be in Form 2.

(2) A notice of non-payment under section 32.3(6) of the Act is to be in Form 3.

(3) A notice of non-payment under section 32.5(6)(a) of the Act is to be in Form 4.

(4) A notice of non-payment under section 32.5(7) of the Act is to be in Form 5.

### Registration

2 (1) Subject to subsection (2), a statement of lien registered under section 34 of the Act is to be in Form 6.

(2) A statement of lien registered under section 36 of the Act with the Minister of Energy and Minerals is to be in Form 7.

### Affidavit

3 An affidavit verifying the statement of lien and referred to in section 34(6) and (7) of the Act is to be in

(a) Form 8 or Form 9, when the affidavit is personally made by the lienholder, or

(b) Form 10 or Form 11, when the affidavit is made by a person other than the lienholder.

AR 51/2002 s3;46/2021;22/2022

### Notice of change of address

4 A notice of change of address for service referred to in section 39 of the Act is to be in Form 12.

AR 51/2002 s4;22/2022

### Certificate of lis pendens

5 A certificate of lis pendens referred to in section 43 of the Act is to be in Form 13.

AR 51/2002 s5;22/2022

### Discharge of lien

6 A notification referred to in section 47(1) and (2) of the Act that a lien has been satisfied in whole or in part is to be in Form 14.

AR 51/2002 s6;22/2022

### Notice to prove lien

7 A notice to prove lien referred to in section 52(3) of the Act is to be in Form 15.

AR 51/2002 s7;22/2022

7.1 Repealed AR 46/2021 s3.

8 Repealed AR 22/2022 s11.

### Expiry

9 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on August 31, 2030.

### Coming into force

10 This Regulation comes into force on April 1, 2002.
