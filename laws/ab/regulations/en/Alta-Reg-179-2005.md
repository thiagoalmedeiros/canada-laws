# Automobile Insurance Rate Board Fees Regulation

Alta Reg 179/2005

*Enabling act: Insurance Act*

### Definitions

1 (1) In this Regulation,

(a) “Act” means the Insurance Act ;

(b) “Board” means the Automobile Insurance Rate Board;

(c) “insurer” means an insurer that holds a licence authorizing the insurer to undertake the class of automobile insurance.

(2) For the purposes of this Regulation, the Board’s annual budget for the fiscal year approved by the Minister includes budgeted

(a) capital costs,

(b) operational costs, and

(c) costs for matters under the Board’s administration.

### Authority to charge fees

2 (1) Each fiscal year the Board is authorized to charge insurers fees to be used to pay for the following for that year:

(a) the Board’s capital costs;

(b) the Board’s operational costs;

(c) costs for matters under the Board’s administration.

(2) The Board shall, on or before September 1 of each year, send a notice to each insurer indicating the amount of the basic fee payable under section 3 for the current fiscal year and indicating the date by which the amount is due.

(2.1) The Board may, on or before March 15 of each year, send a notice to each insurer indicating the amount of the additional fee payable under section 3.1, if any, for the current fiscal year and indicating the date by which the amount is due.

(3) Repealed AR 118/2008 s3.

### Calculation of basic fee

3 Subject to section 3.2, the basic fee payable by an insurer in respect of a fiscal year shall be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

A = B x D C where

A is the amount of the basic fee payable by the insurer;

B is the insurer’s total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year for automobile insurance, as reported under sections 44 and 46 of the Act;

C is the total of all insurers’ total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year for automobile insurance, as reported under sections 44 and 46 of the Act;

D is the amount of the Board’s annual budget for the fiscal year approved by the Minister, less the estimated amount budgeted for costs recovered under section 4.

### Calculation of additional fee

3.1 Subject to section 3.2, the additional fee payable by an insurer in respect of a fiscal year shall be calculated in accordance with the following formula:

E = B x (F – D) C where

E is the amount of the additional fee payable by the insurer;

B is the insurer’s total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year for automobile insurance, as reported under sections 44 and 46 of the Act;

C is the total of all insurers’ total insurance revenue in the previous calendar year for automobile insurance, as reported under sections 44 and 46 of the Act;

F is the Board’s costs incurred for the fiscal year, including capital costs, operational costs and costs for matters under the Board’s administration;

D is the amount of the Board’s annual budget for the fiscal year approved by the Minister, less the estimated amount budgeted for costs recovered under section 4.

### Minimum fee

3.2 If the total amount of the basic fee and additional fee calculated in respect of an insurer under sections 3 and 3.1 is less than $500, no basic fee or additional fee is payable by the insurer.

### Other costs

4 (1) The Board may, in respect of each application for an approval referred to in the following, charge an insurer the actual costs of engaging the services of an actuary for the purpose of carrying out the approval:

(a) the approval of the insurer’s rating programs for all types of vehicles;

(b) the approval of rating programs with respect to insurers licensed in the current fiscal year or for insurers licensed in the previous fiscal year whose rating program was not approved in the previous fiscal year;

(c) repealed AR 29/2015 s3.

(2) On application by an insurer, the Board may, in its discretion, reduce the costs charged to the insurer for engaging the services of an actuary for the purpose of carrying out an approval under subsection (1).

5 Repealed AR 29/2015 s4.

### Expiry

6 For the purpose of ensuring that this Regulation is reviewed for ongoing relevancy and necessity, with the option that it may be repassed in its present or an amended form following a review, this Regulation expires on April 30, 2038.
