Alta Reg 172/2022

*Enabling act: Gaming, Liquor and Cannabis Act*

### Prescribed things

1 The following are prescribed things for the purposes of section 90.09(1)(b) of the Act that a cannabis licensee may sell:

(a) licensee-branded or premises-branded apparel;

(b) licensee-branded items, including but not limited to water bottles, insulated mugs or candles;

(c) cannabis-themed publications, including but not limited to magazines, books or recipes;

(d) things used in the personal growing of cannabis, including but not limited to lights, soil, plant pots and handheld gardening tools.

### Maximum annual sales

2 In addition to selling cannabis, a cannabis licensee may sell cannabis accessories and prescribed things referred to in section 1 up to a maximum of 15% of the total annual dollar value of all sales by the licensee at the location to which the licensee’s cannabis licence relates.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation comes into force on September 1, 2022.
