Alta Reg 227/2026

*Enabling act: Election Act*

### Contents of list of electors

1 (1) A copy of the list of electors, additions to the list of electors or revised list of electors provided under the Act must not contain an elector’s

(a) first name,

(b) middle name,

(c) surname,

(d) address, except the elector’s municipality of residence,

(e) postal code,

(f) telephone number, or

(g) unique identifier.

(2) A copy of the list of electors, additions to the list of electors or revised list of electors provided to a registered political party may contain the municipality of residence and voting area of each elector in each electoral division.

(3) A copy of the list of electors, additions to the list of electors or revised list of electors provided to a member of the Legislative Assembly may contain the municipality of residence and voting area of each elector in the electoral division that the member represents.

### Expiry

2 This Regulation expires on December 31, 2026.
