Alta Reg 216/2020

*Enabling act: Traffic Safety Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation, “Board” means the Alberta Transportation Safety Board dissolved by section 189.4 of the Act.

### Assets and liabilities transferred

2 The assets and liabilities of the Board are transferred to the Crown in right of Alberta.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation has effect on the coming into force of section 189.4 of the Act.
