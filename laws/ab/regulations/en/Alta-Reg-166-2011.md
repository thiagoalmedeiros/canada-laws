Alta Reg 166/2011

*Enabling act: Provincial Parks Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation,

(a) “ Dispositions Regulation ” means the Provincial Parks (Dispositions) Regulation (AR 241/77);

(b) “ General Regulation ” means the Provincial Parks (General) Regulation (AR 102/85).

### La Biche River

2 The Act, the Dispositions Regulation and the General Regulation apply to the land described in Schedule 1 as if it were a wildland provincial park.

### Dillberry Lake

3 Sections 8, 8.1, 8.2, 9, 9.1, 10 and 10.1 of the Act and the Dispositions Regulation apply to the land described in Schedule 2 as if it were a park.

### Peace River

4 The Act, the Dispositions Regulation and the General Regulation apply to the land described in Schedule 3 as if it were a wildland provincial park.

### Eagle Point

5 The Act, except section 10.1, the Dispositions Regulation, except section 60, and the General Regulation apply to the land described in Schedule 4 as if it were a park.

### Blue Rapids

6 The Act, except section 10.1, the Dispositions Regulation , except section 60, and the General Regulation, except section 46, apply to the land described in Schedule 5 as if it were a recreation area.

### Miquelon Lake

7 The Act, the Dispositions Regulation and the General Regulation apply to the land described in Schedule 6 as if it were a park.

### Aspen Beach

8 The Act, the Dispositions Regulation and the General Regulation apply to the land described in Schedule 7 as if it were a park.

### Dunvegan

9 The Act, the Dispositions Regulation and the General Regulation apply to the land described in Schedule 8 as if it were a park.

### English Bay

10 The Act, the Dispositions Regulation and the General Regulation, except section 46, apply to the land described in Schedule 9 as if it were a recreation area.

### Whitney Lakes

11 The Act, the Dispositions Regulation and the General Regulation apply to the land described in Schedule 10 as if it were a park.

### Repeal

12 The Section 7 Declaration Regulation (AR 195/2001) is repealed.
