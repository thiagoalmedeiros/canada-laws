RSA 2000, c M-3

HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of Alberta, enacts as follows:

### Definitions

1 In this Act,

(a) “Board” means the Labour Relations Board;

(b) “firefighters” means firefighters as defined in the Labour Relations Code .

### Exclusion from bargaining unit

2 (1) The Board may, in accordance with the Labour Relations Code , exclude from a bargaining unit firefighters who, in the opinion of the Board, exercise managerial functions or are employed in a confidential capacity in matters relating to labour relations.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) limits the power and jurisdiction of the Board under the Labour Relations Code or any other enactment.
