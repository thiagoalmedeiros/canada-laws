RSA 2000, c K-2

HER MAJESTY, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of Alberta, enacts as follows:

### Appointment and duties

1 The Lieutenant Governor in Council may appoint a King’s Printer for Alberta, and fix the salary and prescribe the duties appertaining to the office.

### Publication of The Alberta Gazette

2 The Lieutenant Governor in Council may authorize the publication not less than twice in each month of an official gazette, to be called The Alberta Gazette, for the publication of proclamations, official and other notices and any matters that may from time to time be required.

### Printing of statutes, etc.

3 Subject to the direction of the Minister charged with the administration of this Act, the King’s Printer shall print and publish or cause to be printed and published the statutes of Alberta, The Alberta Gazette and any documents and announcements that may be required from time to time.

### Publication of advertisements, etc.

4 Advertisements, notices and documents relating to matters within the control of the Legislative Assembly of Alberta and that are required by any law to be published shall be published in The Alberta Gazette unless some other mode of publication is prescribed by law.

### Conditions of publication, etc. of The Alberta Gazette

5 The Minister charged with the administration of this Act may make regulations

(a) prescribing the conditions of the publication of The Alberta Gazette;

(b) designating the public bodies, officers and persons to whom The Alberta Gazette is to be sent without charge;

(c) prescribing a tariff of charges to be paid for the publication of notices, advertisements and documents in The Alberta Gazette;

(d) prescribing the amount of the subscription to The Alberta Gazette.

### Administration of Act

6 The Minister charged with the administration of this Act is the Minister determined under section 16 of the Government Organization Act as the Minister responsible for this Act.
