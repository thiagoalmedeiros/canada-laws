BC Reg 248/2019

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Environmental Assessment Act.

### Prescribed protected areas

2 The areas set out in Column 2 of the following table, established by the enactments set out opposite in Column 1, are prescribed as protected areas for the purposes of section 16 (2) (c) (ii) of the Act:

| Item | Column 1 Enactment | Column 2 Protected Area |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Creston Valley Wildlife Act | Creston Valley wildlife management area |
| 2 | Ecological Reserve Act | Ecological reserves |
| 3 | Environment and Land Use Act | Protected areas |
| 4 | Heritage Conservation Act | Provincial heritage sites |
| 5 | Park Act | Class A parks |
| 6 | Park Act | Class B parks |
| 7 | Park Act | Class C parks |
| 8 | Park Act | Recreation areas |
| 9 | Protected Areas of British Columbia Act | Conservancies |

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Environmental Assessment Act, S.B.C. 2018, c. 51, ss. 16 (2) and 77 (2).]
