BC Reg 185/2022

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Utilities Commission Act;

*parties* means the Okanagan Nation Alliance and its successors and assigns, and the Upper Nicola Band and its successors and assigns.

### Exemption

2 The authority and the parties are exempt from section 71 of the Act in respect of an energy supply contract that establishes the terms and conditions under which electricity generated at a solar generation facility with a plant capacity output that does not exceed 15 megawatts, located at Nicola Lake on the Nicola Lake 1 Indian Reserve, is sold to the authority.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Utilities Commission Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 473, s. 22.]
