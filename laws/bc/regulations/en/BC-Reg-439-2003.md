BC Reg 439/2003

### Relationship between tax rates for different property classes

1 For taxes imposed under section 388 of the Local Government Act, the relationships between tax rates, expressed as ratios of the rate on each property class to the rate on Class 1, must be as set out in the following Schedule:

Schedule

| Class of Property | Ratio to Class 1 Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 1.0:1 |
| 2 | 3.5:1 |
| 3 | 1.0:1 |
| 4 | 3.4:1 |
| 5 | 3.4:1 |
| 6 | 2.45:1 |
| 7 | 3.0:1 |
| 8 | 1.0:1 |
| 9 | 1.0:1 |

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Local Government Act, R.S.B.C. 2015, c. 1, s. 388 and the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 448, section 20 (2)]
