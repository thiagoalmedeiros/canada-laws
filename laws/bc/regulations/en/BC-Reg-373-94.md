BC Reg 373/94

### Provisions applicable to heritage property

1 For the purposes of section 23 of the Heritage Conservation Act, sections 2, 3 (1), 8 (1) to (4), 9 and 14 to 27 of the Park Act apply to a Provincial heritage property.

### McAbee Fossil Beds Heritage Site

2 (1) For the purposes of section 23 of the Heritage Conservation Act, in addition to the provisions listed in section 1 of this regulation, sections 11, 29.1 and 30 (5) of the Park Act apply to the McAbee Fossil Beds Heritage Site.

(2) Despite section 1 of this regulation, section 9 of the Park Act does not apply to the McAbee Fossil Beds Heritage Site.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Heritage Conservation Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 187, section 23 (2)]
