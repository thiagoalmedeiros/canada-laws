# Kwadacha Station and

Tsay Keh Dene Station Regulation

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Utilities Commission Act;

*KWA Station extension* means the upgrade and expansion of the Kwadacha Diesel Generating Station in the community of Kwadacha to increase the firm capacity by approximately 750 kilowatts, and related works and equipment, including works and equipment to enable the positioning of a spare generator;

*TKD Station extension* means the upgrade and expansion of the Tsay Keh Dene Diesel Generating Station in the community of Tsay Keh Dene to increase the firm capacity by approximately 1 200 kilowatts, and related works and equipment, including works and equipment to enable the positioning of a spare generator.

### Exclusions

2 The KWA Station extension and the TKD Station extension are excluded from the operation of section 45 (1) of the Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Utilities Commission Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 473, s. 45 (4).]
