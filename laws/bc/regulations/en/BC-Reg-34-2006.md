# North Island-Coast Development

 Initiative Trust Regions Regulation

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the North Island-Coast Development Initiative Trust Act;

*electoral area* has the same meaning as in the Local Government Act.

### Regions defined

2 For the purposes of the Act:

*Central South Island region* means that area of British Columbia comprising the following: in the Powell River Regional District, electoral area E; in the Regional District of Nanaimo, electoral areas A, B, C and E, the City of Nanaimo, the City of Parksville and the District of Lantzville; the Cowichan Valley Regional District; in the Capital Regional District, the Juan de Fuca Electoral Area, the Saltspring Island Electoral Area and the Southern Gulf Islands Electoral Area;

*North Island-Sunshine Coast region* means that area of British Columbia comprising the following: the Regional District of Mount Waddington; the Regional District of Comox-Strathcona; the Regional District of Alberni-Clayoquot; in the Regional District of Nanaimo, electoral areas F, G and H, and the Town of Qualicum Beach; the Powell River Regional District, except electoral area E; the Sunshine Coast Regional District.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: North Island-Coast Development Initiative Trust Act, S.B.C. 2005, c. 36, ss. 1 and 25.]
