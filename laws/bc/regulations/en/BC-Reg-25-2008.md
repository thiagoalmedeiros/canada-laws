BC Reg 25/2008

### Simon Fraser University Graduate Student Society

1 The Simon Fraser University Graduate Student Society is a designated student society under the University Act for the graduate student body of Simon Fraser University.

[Provisions of the University Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 468, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: sections 27.1 (1) (b) and 71]
