BC Reg 63/2025

### Direction of seizure — prescribed information

1 For the purposes of section 32 (4) of the Act, the following information is prescribed to be included in a direction of seizure:

(a) the name of the bailiff;

(b) the name of the lien holder;

(c) the name of the person who took possession of the goods from the lien holder;

(d) the date on which the lien holder signed the direction of seizure;

(e) the location of the goods, if known by the lien holder.

### Bailee's undertaking — prescribed information

2 (1) In this section, *base registration* has the same meaning as in the Personal Property Security Regulation.

(2) For the purposes of section 34 (1) (c) , the following information is prescribed to be included in the undertaking:

(a) the name of the bailiff;

(b) the name of the person who is to be designated as the bailee;

(c) the date on which the person who is to be designated as the bailee signs the undertaking;

(d) a description of the goods to be held by the person who is to be designated as the bailee;

(e) the address of the location, or, if no address is available, a description of the location where the person who is to be designated as the bailee will hold the goods;

(f) the base registration number in relation to the lien.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Commercial Liens Act, S.B.C. 2022, c. 9, ss. 61 and 87.]
