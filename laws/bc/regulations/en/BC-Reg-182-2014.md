BC Reg 182/2014

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Utilities Commission Act.

### Exemption

2 The authority is exempt from section 71 of the Act in respect of an energy supply contract with a company listed in column 1 of the table below, and the successors and assigns of each of those companies, for the purchase of electricity generated by the company's project listed in column 2 of the table at the location listed in column 3 of the table, if the total generator nameplate capacity of the project does not exceed 15 MW:

| Column 1 Company Name | Column 2 Project Name | Column 3 Project Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Houweling Nurseries Ltd. | Houweling Nurseries (Delta) Cogeneration | 2776 64th Street, Delta BC V4L 2N7 |
| Quesnel River Pulp, A Division of West Fraser Mills Ltd. | Quesnel River Pulp High Efficiency Cogeneration | 1000 Finning Road, Quesnel, BC V2J 6A1 |
| Conifex MacKenzie Forest Products Inc. | Conifex Mackenzie – Combined Heat and Power Project | FFI Road, Mackenzie, BC V0J 2C0 |

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Utilities Commission Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 473, section 22]
