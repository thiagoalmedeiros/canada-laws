BC Reg 35/2006

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Southern Interior Development Initiative Trust Act;

*electoral area* has the same meaning as in the Local Government Act.

### Regions defined

2 For the purposes of the Act:

*Columbia-Kootenay region* means that area of British Columbia comprising the following: the Regional District of Kootenay Boundary; the Regional District of Central Kootenay; in the Columbia-Shuswap Regional District, electoral areas A and B, the City of Revelstoke and the Town of Golden;

*Thompson-Okanagan region* means that area of British Columbia comprising the following: in the Thompson-Nicola Regional District, electoral areas A, B, J, L, M, N, O and P, the City of Kamloops, the City of Merritt and the Village of Chase; in the Fraser Valley Regional District, electoral areas A and B and the District of Hope; the Regional District of Okanagan-Similkameen; the Regional District of Central Okanagan; the Regional District of North Okanagan; in the Columbia Shuswap Regional District, electoral areas C, D, E and F, the City of Salmon Arm and the District of Sicamous.
