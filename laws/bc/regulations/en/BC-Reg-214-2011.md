BC Reg 214/2011

### Definition

1 For the purposes of the Land Act, *fossil* means the preserved remains, traces or imprints of organisms from the geological past, but does not include

(a) human remains or artifacts, or

(b) the following substances:

(i) limestone;

(ii) dolomite;

(iii) coal;

(iv) petroleum;

(v) natural gas.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Land Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 245, section 1]
