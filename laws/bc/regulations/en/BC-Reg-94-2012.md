BC Reg 94/2012

### Remuneration and expenses of audit council

1 (1) Members of the audit council must be remunerated in accordance with Treasury Board Directive 2/17 as follows:

(a) the chair in accordance with the maximum meeting and per diem fees set for the chair of a board classified as Level 2;

(b) the other members of the audit council in accordance with the maximum meeting and per diem fees set for the director of a board classified as Level 2.

(2) Members of the audit council must be reimbursed for transportation, accommodation, meal and out of pocket expenses in accordance with Treasury Board Directive 2/17.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Auditor General for Local Government Act, S.B.C. 2012, c. 5, s. 27]
