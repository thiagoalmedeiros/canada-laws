BC Reg 210/2025

### Definitions

1 In this regulation:

*land* includes foreshore and land covered by water;

*reserve* means the mineral reserve established under section 2;

*reserve period* means the period beginning on December 1, 2025 and ending on December 1, 2026;

*site number* means the site number given in the registry to a particular area of land shown on an electronic map in the registry.

### Temporary reserve established

2 A mineral reserve for all minerals is established for the reserve period over the area of land identified by site number 1007107 and site name Klappan CLPA — Zone A.

### No registration of claims in reserve

3 A free miner must not register a claim in the reserve.

### Coal land reserve

4 The coal land in the area referred to in section 2 is established as a coal land reserve.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Mineral Tenure Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 292, s. 22 and the Coal Act, S.B.C. 2004, c. 15, s. 21.]
