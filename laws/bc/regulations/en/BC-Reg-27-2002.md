### Exemptions

1 (1) The following organizations are exempted from the application of sections 13 , 14 and 16 of the Budget Transparency and Accountability Act:

552513 British Columbia Ltd.;

BC Transportation Financing Authority;

BCIF Management Ltd.;

British Columbia Enterprise Corporation;

Canadian Blood Services;

Creston Valley Wildlife Management Authority;

Provincial Rental Housing Corporation;

Rapid Transit Project 2000 Ltd.

(2) The following organizations are exempted from the application of sections 13 and 16 of the Budget Transparency and Accountability Act:

(a) Organized Crime Agency of British Columbia;

(b) Nechako-Kitamaat Development Fund Society;

(c) Leading Edge Endowment Fund;

(d) BC PNP Fast Track Fund Ltd.;

(e) to (h) Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 29/2011, Sch. s. 1 (a).]

(i) Health Employers Association of British Columbia;

(j) Community Social Services Employers' Association of British Columbia (CSSEA);

(k) British Columbia Public School Employers' Association;

(l) The Post-Secondary Employers' Association;

(m) Crown Corporations Employers Association;

(n) British Columbia Trade and Invest Ltd.;

(o) Real Estate Foundation of British Columbia.

(3) Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 29/2009, s. 1 (c).]

### Repealed

2 Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 29/2011, Sch. s. 1 (c).]

### BC Rail

3 The British Columbia Railway Company and its subsidiaries, as defined in section 1 of the British Columbia Railway Act,

(a) are exempted from the application of sections 13 and 14 of the Budget Transparency and Accountability Act, and

(b) with respect to the 2010/2011 and subsequent fiscal years, are exempted from the application of section 16 of the Budget Transparency and Accountability Act.

### Limited exemption for Pacific Carbon Trust and Provincial Capital Commission

4 The following organizations are exempted from the application of section 13 of the Budget Transparency and Accountability Act for the 2014/2015 fiscal year:

(a) Pacific Carbon Trust Inc.;

(b) Provincial Capital Commission.

### Limited exemption for Private Career Training Institutions Agency

5 The Private Career Training Institutions Agency is exempted from the application of section 13 of the Budget Transparency and Accountability Act for the 2015/2016 fiscal year.

### Limited exemption for Real Estate Council of British Columbia

6 The Real Estate Council of British Columbia is exempted from the application of section 13 of the Budget Transparency and Accountability Act for the 2017/2018 fiscal year.

### Limited exemption for BC Infrastructure Benefits Inc.

7 BC Infrastructure Benefits Inc. is exempted from the application of sections 13 and 16 of the Budget Transparency and Accountability Act for the 2019/2020 fiscal year.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Budget Transparency and Accountability Act, S.B.C. 2000, c. 23, s. 24.]
