BC Reg 383/88

# Travel Manufactured Home

Exemption Regulation

### Interpretation

1 In this regulation

*travel trailer* means a vehicle that is capable of being towed on its own permanent wheels and undercarriage by a motor vehicle, licensed, or able to be licensed, as a trailer under the Motor Vehicle Act for use on highway, and not used as a principal residence.

### Exemptions

2 The Manufactured Home Tax Act does not apply to campers, motor homes or travel trailers.

[Provisions of the Manufactured Home Tax Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 281, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 4 (d)]
