# Motorcycle Safety Helmet

Exemption Regulation

### Exemption

1 The following persons are exempt from the requirements of section 194 (3) of the Motor Vehicle Act:

(a) a person who

(i) practises the Sikh religion, and

(ii) has unshorn hair and habitually wears a turban composed of 5 or more square metres of cloth.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Motor Vehicle Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 318, section 194 (6) (c)]
