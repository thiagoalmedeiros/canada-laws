BC Reg 106/90

### Grant-in-aid: South Cariboo Home Support Service Association

1 The Cariboo Regional District is granted the additional power to provide a contribution (grant-in-aid) to the South Cariboo Home Support Service Association as an additional extended service.

### Grant-in-aid: Cariboo Tourist Association

2 The Cariboo Regional District is granted the additional power to provide a contribution (grant-in-aid) to the Cariboo Tourist Association as an additional extended service.

### Paratransit

3 The Cariboo Regional District is granted the additional power to provide Paratransit as a local service.

### Economic Development

4 The Cariboo Regional District is granted the additional power to provide Economic Development as an extended service.

### Economic Development Grants-in-aid

5 The Cariboo Regional District is granted the additional power to provide Economic Development Grants-in-aid as an extended service.

### Natural Gas Capital Financing

6 The Cariboo Regional District is granted the additional power to authorize the acquisition of Natural Gas Capital Financing as a local service.
