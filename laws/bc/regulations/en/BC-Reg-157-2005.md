# Special Direction No. 9 to the

British Columbia Utilities Commission

### Definition

1 In this Special Direction, *Act* means the Utilities Commission Act.

### Applications

2 This Special Direction is issued to the commission under section 3 of the Act.

### Repealed

2.1 Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 24/2019, App. A, s. 2.]

### Repealed

3-6 Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 23/2011, s. 2.]

### Storage services must not be required

7 The commission must not, by tariff or otherwise, compel the authority to provide storage services.

1 see B.C. Reg. 180/2005, published in Volume 48, No. 6 of the British Columbia Gazette, Part II.
