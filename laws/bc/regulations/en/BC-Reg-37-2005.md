BC Reg 37/2005

### Definition

1 In this regulation:

*Act* means the Unclaimed Property Act;

*judicial officer* means, in the case of funds in the Court of Appeal, a justice or registrar, in the case of funds in the Supreme Court, a judge, associate judge or registrar, and in the case of funds in the Provincial Court, a judge or registrar.

### Court funds

2 For the purposes of the Act, *unclaimed court funds* means funds, other than judicial interim release funds, that have been paid into court in a proceeding and that

(a) have remained in court for at least 5 years, and

(b) have been determined by a judicial officer for that court to be unclaimed court funds.

[Provisions of the Unclaimed Property Act, S.B.C. 1999, c. 48, s. 17.]
