# British Columbia Farm Industry

 Review Board Regulation

### Application fee

1 The prescribed fee for an application made to the British Columbia Farm Industry Review Board under section 3 of the Farm Practices Protection (Right to Farm) Act is $100.

[Provisions of the Farm Practices Protection (Right to Farm) Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 131, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: section 12 (2) (a)]
