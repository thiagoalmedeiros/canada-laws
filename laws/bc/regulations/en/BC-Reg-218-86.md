BC Reg 218/86

# Railway, Pipeline, Electric Power and

 Telecommunications Corporation

 Rights of Way Valuation Regulation

### Interpretation

1 In this regulation, *gathering pipeline* means a gathering pipeline as defined in the Prescribed Classes of Property Regulation, B.C. Reg. 438/81.

### Application

2 This regulation must be used for determinations of value for the purposes of assessment for the 2026 and subsequent taxation years.

### Determination of value

3 The actual value of the right of way for an item in column 1 of the table must be determined using the rate set out opposite the item in column 2:

**Table**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Column 1 | Column 2 |
| For the track in place of a railway corporation, located on land a) described in section 2 (1) (c) (i) or (ii) of the Railway Corporations Assessment Regulation, B.C. Reg. 51/2016, andb) south of the 59th parallel | $26 630 per acre |
| For the track in place of a railway corporation, located on land a) described in section 2 (1) (c) (i) or (ii) of the Railway Corporations Assessment Regulation, andb) north of the 59th parallel | $1 520 per acre |
| For the track in place of a railway corporation, located on land described in section 2 (1) (c) (iii) of the Railway Corporations Assessment Regulation | $1 350 per acre |
| For the pipelines of a pipeline corporation, other than gathering pipelines | $8 430 per acre |
| For the gathering pipelines of a pipeline corporation | $1 250 per acre |
| For the transmission lines of an electrical power corporation | $8 430 per acre |
| For the metallic or fibre optic cables of a telecommunications corporation | $8 430 per acre |

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Assessment Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 20, s. 21.]
