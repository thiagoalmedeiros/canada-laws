# Environmental Remediation

 Sub-account Expenditures Regulation

### Prescribed Acts

1 For the purposes of section 5 (2) (b) (iv) of the Special Accounts Appropriation and Control Act, the following Acts are prescribed:

(a) Dike Maintenance Act ;

(b) Heritage Conservation Act ;

(c) Land Act ;

(d) Private Managed Forest Land Act ;

(e) Water Sustainability Act ;

(f) Water Protection Act ;

(g) Weed Control Act .

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Special Accounts Appropriation and Control Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 436, section 10 (2)]
