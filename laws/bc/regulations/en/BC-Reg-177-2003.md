### Alpine skiing

2.1 A person requesting a disposition under the Ministry of Lands, Parks and Housing Act in relation to a land use set out under items 15 or 28 of Column 1 of the Schedule to section 1 must pay to the Minister of Finance the fee set out opposite in Column 2.

### Private moorage

2.2 A person applying under the Ministry of Lands, Parks and Housing Act in relation to a land use set out under item 5 of Column 1 of the Schedule to section 1 must pay to the Minister of Finance the fee set out opposite in Column 2.

### British Columbia Energy Regulator

3 No fees are payable in respect of an application described in section 1 and items 2, 5 and 6 of the Schedule to section 2 of this regulation if the British Columbia Energy Regulator is exercising its authority for that application under section 8 of the Energy Resource Activities Act.

### Transition

4 If an applicant

(a) paid the fees applicable on submission of an application under the Land Act Fees Regulation, B.C. Reg. 167/92, the Land Act Fees Regulation, B.C. Reg. 166/2003, the Land Act Fees Regulation, B.C. Reg. 176/2003, the Land Act Shellfish Aquaculture Fee Regulation, B.C. Reg. 232/99, or the Land Act Shellfish Aquaculture Fee Regulation, B.C. Reg. 175/2003, before this section was enacted,

(b) has not paid the fees applicable on approval of an application and issuance of a disposition under Land Act Fees Regulation, B.C. Reg. 167/92, the Land Act Fees Regulation, B.C. Reg. 166/2003, the Land Act Fees Regulation, B.C. Reg. 176/2003, the Land Act Shellfish Aquaculture Fee Regulation, B.C. Reg. 232/99 or the Land Act Shellfish Aquaculture Fee Regulation, B.C. Reg. 175/2003, and

(c) the application referred to in paragraph (a) is in process on the coming into force of this section,

then that applicant must pay the fees referred to in paragraph (b) prior to the issuance of a disposition.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Land Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 245, ss. 6 (3), 7.8, 32 (1) and 110, and the Ministry of Lands, Parks and Housing Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 307, s. 11.]
