### Definitions and interpretation

1 (1) In this regulation:

*Act* means the Wildlife Act;

*ATV* or *all terrain vehicle* means a wheeled or tracked motor vehicle that is capable of travel on or off a highway, including a motorcycle, but not including a snowmobile, or a motor vehicle that is licensed for highway travel under the Motor Vehicle Act;

*electric bicycle* means a wheeled device on which a person may ride, to which pedals or hand cranks are attached that allow the device to be propelled by human power, and to which a motor is attached that allows the device to be propelled by electric power;

*existing road or trail* means a road or trail with a paved surface, a cross-country or downhill ski route marked in a ski area by the owner of the ski area, a road used for logging or mining, a road or trail used for access to fences, power lines, wind generators or communication towers, or a trail on which there is no vegetation except on a strip that, if present, can be straddled by a 4-wheel vehicle;

*highway* has the same meaning as in the Motor Vehicle Act;

*motor vehicle* means a device in, on or by which a person or thing is or may be transported or drawn, and which is designed to be self-propelled, including an electric bicycle, but not including any of the following: another device designed to be propelled by human, animal or wind power; a device used exclusively on stationary rails or tracks; a boat; an aircraft;

*motorcycle* has the same meaning as in the Motor Vehicle Act;

*M.U.* or *management unit* means an area designated as a management unit by the Management Unit Regulation;

*related transportation activity* means the following activities related to hunting: transporting a hunter; transporting game; transporting equipment or supplies, including a firearm or bow, intended to be used for or in support of hunting;

*snowmobile* means a motor vehicle designed for travel primarily on snow or ice, that has one or more steering skis and that uses one or more endless tracks driven in contact with the ground.

(2) In this regulation,

(a) a reference to a specific map is a reference to the map listed in column 1 of Schedule 8, and

(b) a description set out opposite in column 2 of Schedule 8 is listed for ease of reference only.

(3) If a period specified in a schedule to this regulation ends on February 28, the period ends instead on February 29 if the year in which the period ends is a leap year.

### Areas closed to motor vehicles

2 Unless permitted by section 9 or 10 or a section of Schedule 1, a person must not use or operate a motor vehicle in an area described in Schedule 1 during the period specified in that schedule for the area.

### Areas closed to hunting with motor vehicles

3 Unless permitted by section 9 or 10 or a section of Schedule 2, a person must not use or operate a motor vehicle for the purpose of hunting or a related transportation activity in an area described in Schedule 2 during the period specified in that schedule for the area.

### Areas closed to ATVs, electric bicycles and snowmobiles

4 Unless permitted by section 9 or 10 or a section of Schedule 3, a person must not use or operate an ATV, electric bicycle or snowmobile in an area described in Schedule 3 during the period specified in that schedule for the area.

### Areas closed to hunting with ATVs, electric bicycles and snowmobiles

5 Unless permitted by section 9 or 10 or a section of Schedule 4, a person must not use or operate an ATV, electric bicycle or snowmobile for the purpose of hunting or a related transportation activity in an area described in Schedule 4 during the period specified in that schedule for the area.

### Areas closed to snowmobiles

6 Unless permitted by section 9 or 10 or a section of Schedule 5, a person must not use or operate a snowmobile in an area described in Schedule 5 during the period specified in that schedule for the area.

### Areas closed to hunting with snowmobiles

7 Unless permitted by section 9 or 10 or a section of Schedule 6, a person must not use or operate a snowmobile for the purpose of hunting or a related transportation activity in an area described in Schedule 6 during the period specified in that schedule for the area.

### Areas closed to hunting with ATVs and electric bicycles

8 Unless permitted by section 9 or 10 or a section of Schedule 7, a person must not use or operate an ATV or electric bicycle for the purpose of hunting or a related transportation activity in an area described in Schedule 7 during the period specified in that schedule for the area.

### Exceptions for trapping furbearing animals

9 (1) A person who is authorized to trap under the Act may use or operate a motor vehicle for the purpose of trapping furbearing animals in an area described in any of the following sections of Schedule 1 during the period specified in that schedule for the area:

(a) section 59 ;

(b) section 60 ;

(c) section 61 ;

(d) section 63 ;

(d.1) section 76 ;

(e) section 77 ;

(f) section 86 ;

(g) section 103 ;

(h) section 104 ;

(i) section 105 ;

(j) section 133 .

(2) A person who is authorized to trap under the Act may use or operate a motor vehicle for the purpose of trapping furbearing animals in an area described in Schedule 2, 4, 6 or 7 during the period specified for the area in the applicable schedule.

### Other exceptions

10 (1) A person may use or operate a motor vehicle on a highway in an area described in any of Schedules 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7, other than an area described in section 116 of Schedule 1, during the period specified in the applicable schedule for the area.

(2) A person may, for a commercial purpose other than hunting or a related transportation activity or back country recreation, use or operate a snowmobile in an area described in Schedule 5 during the period specified in that schedule for the area.

### Offences

11 A person who contravenes section 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 or 8 commits an offence.
