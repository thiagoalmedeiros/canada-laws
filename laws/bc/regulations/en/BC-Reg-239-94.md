BC Reg 239/94

### Definitions

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Evidence Act.

### Fee for certificate

2 There must be paid, for the issue of a certificate under section 15 (2) of the Act, a fee of $30.

### Fee for certified copy

3 There must be paid for a certified copy of a record provided under section 47 (2) of the Act, the following fees:

(a) for the number of pages, up to and including 10, included in

the record ..................................................................................................................................... $30

(b) for each additional page over 10 included in the record.................................................................... $6

[Provisions of the Evidence Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 124, relevant to the enactment of this regulation: sections 15 (3) and 47 (2)]
