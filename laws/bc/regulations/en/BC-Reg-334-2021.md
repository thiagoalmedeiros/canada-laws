BC Reg 334/2021

### Maximum salaries

1 (1) The salary payable to a person who is appointed Chief of Staff, Office of the Premier, by the Lieutenant Governor in Council, is not to exceed $299 215.05.

(2) The salary payable to a person who is appointed Deputy Chief of Staff, Office of the Premier, by the Lieutenant Governor in Council, is not to exceed $165 000.

(3) The salary payable to a person who is appointed as an Executive Director, Operations and Strategic Initiatives, Office of the Premier (Vancouver), by the Lieutenant Governor in Council, is not to exceed $165 000.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Public Service Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 385, ss. 15 and 25 (3).]
