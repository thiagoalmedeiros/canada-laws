BC Reg 122/2023

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Forest Act.

### Information sharing — prescribed enactments

2 For the purposes of section 142.93 (5) (l) of the Act, the following enactments are prescribed:

(a) the Business Number Act;

(b) section 8 of the Financial Administration Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Forest Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 157, s. 151. (1.01).]
