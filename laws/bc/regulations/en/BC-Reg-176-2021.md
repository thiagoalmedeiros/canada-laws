# Government Body Designation

and Deeming Regulation

### Definition

1 In this regulation, *Act* means the Financial Administration Act.

### Public authorities designated as government bodies

2 Each of the following public authorities is designated, under paragraph (d) of the definition of "government body" in section 1 of the Act, as a government body for the purposes of the Act:

(a) the Greater Vancouver Sewerage and Drainage District;

(b) the Greater Vancouver Water District;

(c) an improvement district as defined in the Local Government Act.

### Organizations deemed to be government bodies

3 The Providence Health Care Society is an organization deemed, under section 88 (2) (b) of the Act, to be a government body for the purposes of Parts 8, 9 and 9.1 of the Act.

[Provisions relevant to the enactment of this regulation: Financial Administration Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 138, ss. 1 and 88.]
