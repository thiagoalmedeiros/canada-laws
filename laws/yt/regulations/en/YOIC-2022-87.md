# Order to provide exemptions from the Public Pool Regulations in respect of Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs

Text obtained from the [official source](https://laws.yukon.ca/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2022/2022-0087/2022-0087_2.pdf) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

YOIC 2022/87

*Made under the Public Health and Safety Act*

### Interpretation

1 In this Order

“ Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs pools ” means collectively

the Eclipse Pool, Onsen Pool, Aurora Pool and Hidden Pool

and Japanese clay tubs, all of which are

(a) situated at the facility identified as the Eclipse

Nordic Hot Springs, located at km 10 of Takhini

Hot Springs Road, Whitehorse, and

(b) identified and described by the operator on the

Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs website as of May 12,

2024; « piscines Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs »

“ operator ”, in relation to the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs

pools, means 837616 Yukon Inc., operating as Eclipse

Nordic Hot Springs. « exploitant »

### Exemptions

2 Subject to section 3, the following exemptions apply in

respect of the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs pools:

(a) the operator is exempt from the Public Pool

Regulations , except sections 1, 14 and 16 to 19;

(b) the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs pools and the

facilities, auxiliary structures, equipment and

other things associated with the use or operation

of the pools are exempt from the Public Pool

Regulations , except sections 1, 14 and 16 to 19.

### Conditions

3 (1) The exemptions in section 2 are subject to the

conditions in this section.

(2) The operator must, at all times, post the following

in accordance with subsections (3) and (4):

(a) a notice, with a font size of at least 36 points,

stating “The Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs is

temporarily exempted from most requirements

of the Public Pool Regulations under the Public

Health and Safety Act. Please see the exemption

order that is available on the Eclipse Nordic Hot

Springs website and at the front entrance door

and front desk of Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs.”;

(b) an electronic copy of this Order;

(c) a paper copy of this Order.

(3) The operator must post the notice set out in

paragraph (2)(a) in a prominent location readily visible

and legible to a customer in each of the following places:

(a) on the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs website,

immediately below the heading “Rates” in the

“Rates & Classes” portion of the website;

(b) immediately beside the front entrance door of

Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs; and

(c) on the customer side of the front desk of Eclipse

Nordic Hot Springs.

(4) The operator must post the copies of this Order as

set out in paragraphs (2)(b) and (c) in a prominent

location readily visible and legible to a customer in the

following manner:

(a) an electronic copy of this Order must be posted

on the Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs website,

immediately below the heading “Rates” in the

“Rates & Classes” portion of the website;

(b) a paper copy of this Order must be posted

immediately beside the front entrance door of

Eclipse Nordic Hot Springs; and

(c) a paper copy of this Order must be posted on

the customer side of the front desk of Eclipse

Nordic Hot Springs.

### Expiry

4 This Order expires on May 11, 2029.
