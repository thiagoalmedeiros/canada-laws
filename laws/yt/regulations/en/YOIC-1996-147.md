YOIC 1996/147

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this special warrant:

1 To authorize an amount of up to $1,624,000 for

Operation and Maintenance and $18,270,000 for Capital to

be paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in

accordance with the attached Schedule A.

2 To stipulate that payments made under it be made:

(a) for the purpose of financing government

operations referred to in an appropriation Act

already enacted for the fiscal year 1996-97 or in

Schedule A to this order;

(b) in the fiscal year 1996-97.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 4th

day of October, 1996.

_____________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
