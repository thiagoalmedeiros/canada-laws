YOIC 2010/161

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The lots in the Ingram Subdivision listed in the

attached Schedule A are classed as shown and shall be sold

(a) in the case of a lot classed as residential, at the

price listed in the Schedule; and

(b) in the case of a lot classed as special, for no

less than the minimum price listed in the

Schedule.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 10 September 2010.
