# Extends the time required for an election in the Town of City of Dawson to a date no later than December 31, 2005

YOIC 2005/61

*Made under the Municipal Act*

Pursuant to subsection 336(16) of the Municipal Act ,

the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The time for an election in the Town of the City of

Dawson required by subsection 336(14) of the Act is hereby

extended to a day which will permit completion of the

election by a date no later than October 19, 2006.

(Section 1 amended by O.I.C. 2005/201)

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this April 15th 2005.
