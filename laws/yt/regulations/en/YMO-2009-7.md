## RULE RESPECTING THE

## IMPLEMENTATION OF CSA

## INSTRUMENTS AND POLICIES (LOCAL

## RULE 11-802)

## PART 1

### Rule 11-801 replaced

1 This Local Rule 11-802 replaces Rule 11-801.

### Definitions

2 “ Act ” means the Securities Act S.Y. 2007, c.16. « Loi »

“ companion policies ” means policies made by the CSA

in relation to a national instrument or multilateral

instrument which help explain, implement or refine that

instrument; « politiques complémentaires »

“ CSA ” means the Canadian Securities Administrators,

about whom information is available at their website:

http://www.csa-acvm.ca. « ACVM »

“ CSA amendment ” means an amendment made by the

CSA to a CSA instrument or policy policy and includes the

remaking of a CSA instrument or policy by the CSA;;

« modification des ACVM »

“ CSA instruments and policies ” means

(a) national instruments, including associated forms,

(b)

(c) multilateral instruments, including associated

forms,

(d)

(e) companion policies;

made, amended or re-made by the CSA from time to

time, but does not include national policies, multilateral

policies, notices or staff notices issued by the CSA;

« normes et politiques des ACVM »

“ CSA jurisdiction ” means the provinces and territories

of Canada; « compétence des ACVM »

“ local amendment ” means an amendment to a CSA

instrument or policy made by order of the Minister and

only having effect in Yukon; « modification locale »

“ multilateral ” in relation to instruments or policies

means those instruments or policies made by the CSA

that are implemented by some but not all provinces and

territories; « multilatérale »

“ national ” in relation to instruments or policies means

those instruments or policies made by the CSA that are

implemented by all provinces and territories;

« canadienne »

“ Superintendent ” means the Superintendent of

Securities appointed under the Act; « surintendant »

“ Rules ” has the same meaning as in the Act. « Règles »

### Numbering System

3 (1) CSA instruments and policies made as rules under

the Act may be referred to by their CSA number

(a) preceded by “national instrument” or the

abbreviation “NI”; or

(b) preceded by “multilateral instrument” or the

abbreviation “MI”;

(c)

(d)

(e) followed by “companion policy” or the

abbreviation “CP”; or

(f) preceded by “form” and followed by the

abbreviation “F” with a number;

as appropriate.

(2) Rules made under the Act which do not implement

a single CSA instrument or policy may be numbered

according to the five-digit numbering system followed

by the CSA and may be referred to by number preceded

by “local rule” or “Yukon rule” or “Yukon local rule”.

## PART 2

### Implementation of certain CSA instruments and policies

4 The CSA instruments and policies listed in the attached

Schedule, as amended or remade by the CSA from time

to time, are made as rules for the purposes of the Act,

with such necessary modifications as are incorporated

in the copies maintained by the Superintendent under

subsection 5(1).

### Superintendent to maintain definitive copies

5 (1) The Superintendent must maintain in the office of

the Superintendent

(a) a copy of each CSA instrument or policy made as

a rule under the Act endorsed with

(i) the number of the Ministerial Order by which

it is made a rule under the Act, and

(ii) its initial effective date in Yukon as a rule

under the Act;

(b) a copy of any CSA amendment made from time

to time to a CSA instrument or policy after it is

made as a rule under the Act, endorsed with the

effective date of the CSA amendment in Yukon;

(c) a copy of any local amendment made from time

to time to a CSA instrument or policy after the

CSA instrument or policy is made as a rule under

the Act, endorsed with

(i) the number of the Ministerial Order by which

the local amendment is made, and

(ii) the effective date of the local amendment;

and

(d) a list of all CSA instruments and policies made

as rules under the Act setting out in respect of

each of them

(i) its initial effective date in Yukon;

(ii) the effective dates in Yukon of any CSA

amendments made to it after its initial

effective date in Yukon, and

(iii) the effective dates in Yukon of any local

amendments made to it after its initial

effective date in Yukon.

(2) The public may inspect the copies and list referred

to in subsection (1) at the office of the Superintendent

without charge and may obtain photocopies or facsimile

copies in accordance with the Securities Fees Regulation .

(3) The CSA instruments and policies adopted as rules

under the Act may not be identical with the CSA

instruments and policies as implemented by other CSA

jurisdictions (which may be found through links to the

various securities regulators in other provinces and

territories on the Government of Yukon website at:

http://www.community.gov.yk.ca/corp/secureinvest.ht

ml) due to

(a) necessary modifications made by each CSA

jurisdiction in order to properly implement the

CSA instrument or policy in their jurisdiction;

(b) amendments made by each CSA jurisdiction

which apply only in that jurisdiction;

(c) CSA amendments which may or may not have

been adopted by each CSA jurisdiction.

(4) If there is any uncertainty

(a) as to which version of a CSA instrument or policy

is in effect in Yukon as a rule made under the

Act; or

(b) as to the effective date in Yukon of a CSA

instrument or policy made as a rule under the

Act, or of any provision or amendment of it,

then the copies of the CSA instruments and policies and

any amendments to them maintained in the office of the

Superintendent and endorsed in accordance with

subsection 5(1) must conclusively be deemed to be the

rule in effect as of the effective dates endorsed on those

copies.

(5) If a CSA instrument or policy has been amended by

the CSA prior to its initial effective date in Yukon as a

rule under the Act but the CSA has not by that date

produced a consolidated version of that instrument or

policy, the Superintendent may produce the

consolidation and use a copy of that consolidation for the

purposes of paragraph (1)(a).

### Necessary modifications and local amendments preserved

6 If the CSA amends or remakes any of the CSA

instruments or policies made as rules under the Act

without incorporating any necessary modifications or

local amendments then in effect in Yukon, those

necessary modifications or local amendments continue

to apply to the CSA instrument or policy as amended or

remade.

### Instruments prevail over companion policies

7 If there is any inconsistency or conflict between a

provision in a national instrument or multilateral

instrument and a provision in the corresponding

companion policy and both are made as rules under the

Act, the provision in the national instrument or

multilateral instrument prevails to the extent of the

inconsistency or conflict.

## PART 3

### Effective Date

8 This Rule comes into effect on September 28, 2009.
