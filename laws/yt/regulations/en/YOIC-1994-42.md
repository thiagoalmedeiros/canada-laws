YOIC 1994/42

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this special warrant:

1 To authorize an amount of up to $5,779,000 for

Operation and Maintenance and $13,000 for Capital to be

paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in accordance

with the attached Schedule A.

2 To stipulate that payments made under it be made:

(a) for the purpose of financing government oper-

ations referred to in an appropriation Act already enacted

for the fiscal year 1993-94 or in Schedule A to this order;

(b) in the fiscal year 1993-94.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

22nd day of March, 1994.

________________________________

Administrator of the Yukon
