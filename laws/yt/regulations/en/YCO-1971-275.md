## REGULATIONS RESPECTING HEALTH

## CARE INSURANCE SERVICES

Pursuant to the provisions of the Yukon Health Care

Insurance Plan Act , the Commissioner of the Yukon

Territory is pleased to and doth hereby order as

follows:

Effective 1st April, 1972 the annexed Regulations

respecting Health Care Insurance Services are hereby

made and established.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 9th

day of July, A.D., 1971.

Commissioner of the Yukon/Commissaire

## INSURED HEALTH SERVICES

### Services not insured

3 The following services shall be deemed not to be

insured health services under the plan:

(a) services that a person is eligible for and

entitled to under any of the statutes listed in

Schedule “A”;

[Paragraph 3(b) repealed by O.I.C. 2022/167]

(c) services of more than one medical practitioner

in the course of the same illness, unless on the

referral of a medical practitioner or the claim is

allowed by the director;

(d) medical-legal services including examinations

and reports relating thereto, and testimony in

court or giving evidence in legal proceedings;

(e) preparation of records, reports, certificates and

communications;

(f) services or examinations required by a third

party, including those related to employment,

industrial or insurance purposes;

(g) services including examinations or reports for

reasons of attending university or camp;

(h) examination or immunization for the purpose

of travel, employment or emigration;

(i) cosmetic service to a person over 15 years of

age except where the director is satisfied on

evidence supplied in advance by the attending

medical practitioner that such service to an

insured person is medically required;

(j) detention time;

(k) laboratory and X-ray procedures performed in

facilities not approved by the Commissioner;

(l) insured services rendered by a medical

practitioner to himself or to his dependants,

except where the director decides otherwise;

(m) service that the director determines on review

of medical evidence, is not an insured service

because the service so rendered was not

medically required by the patient;

(n) premarriage medical examinations;

(o) services provided on a group basis, including

immunizations, examinations, group

treatments and refractions, unless previously

authorized by the director;

(p) giving or writing of prescriptions;

(q) the supply of drugs;

(r) the dispensing by a medical practitioner of

medicines, drugs, or medical appliances;

(s) the fitting and supply of eye-glasses;

(t) refractions by a specialist in ophthamology

more frequently than specified by the director,

unless the director decides otherwise after

reviewing the medical factors;

(u) dental care except for procedures listed in

Schedule “B” to these Regulations performed in

a hospital;

(v) routine dental care including dental X-rays; and

(w) radiology related to dental services except

services pursuant to paragraph (u);

(x) the supply of appliances to a person over

sixteen years of age except that where a person

is sixteen years or less the attending medical

practitioner will make a recommendation to the

director and the director shall, if they are

satisfied with the evidence supporting the

recommendation, approve the supply of the

appliances as an insured service.

## INSURED PERSONS

4 (1) Subject to the Act and these regulations, an

insured person is eligible for and entitled to insured

health services after midnight on the last day of the

second month following the month of arrival to Yukon.

(2) Despite any other provision of these Regulations

(a) an adopted child whose adoptive parent is an

insured person becomes an insured person as

soon as they arrive in Yukon to reside with that

adoptive parent; and

(b) a person who is a protected person within the

meaning of subsection 95(2) of the

Immigration and Refugee Protection Act

(Canada), and who arrives in Yukon through

the special program initiated by the

Government of Canada in 2015 for the

resettlement of Syrian refugees, becomes an

insured person on arriving in Yukon.

(3) In subsection (2), ‘adopted child’ includes a child

whom the insured person is adopting under the

Intercountry Adoption (Hague) Convention Act .

(4)

[Subsection 4(4) repealed by O.I.C. 2015/254]

5 When an insured person is absent from Yukon and

intends to return, they are entitled to insured health

services outside Yukon during a period of twelve

months continuous absence from Yukon.

6 (1) Where an insured person leaves Yukon and takes

up residence in another province participating under

the federal Act, they continue to be an insured person

during normal travelling time, as determined by the

director, and a waiting period not exceeding three

months which may be necessary in order to qualify for

benefits under the medical care insurance legislation of

the other province.

(2) Where an insured person takes up residence

outside of Yukon, but not in a participating province,

they cease to be an insured person three months after

the day they leave Yukon.

### Payment for services

7 (1) Where a medical practitioner renders an

insured health service to an insured person, the

medical practitioner shall, unless the medical

practitioner has made an election under this section,

submit to the director their claim for payment for the

service, in the form required by the director, together

with the information that is required by the director to

substantiate the claim.

(1.01) After a claim is submitted to the director under

subsection (1), subject to these Regulations, payment

under subsection (2) shall be made to the medical

practitioner without undue delay, except if a notice

under subsection 12(3) has been sent to the medical

practitioner or their professional association by either

the director or the medical practitioner, or if the

professional association of a medical practitioner

requests otherwise.

(2) Payment under the plan for insured health services

rendered by a medical practitioner to an insured

person shall be made directly to the medical

practitioner who rendered the services or

(a) to an employer of a medical practitioner to

whom the medical practitioner has assigned

the right to collect the payments that would

otherwise be made to the medical practitioner

under the plan;

(b) to a partnership, association, or group of

medical practitioners who have assigned to the

partnership, association or group their right to

collect their fees under the plan;

(c) to an insured person who submits a

substantiated claim in respect of an insured

health service as provided elsewhere in these

Regulations; and

(d) to a facility listed in Schedule C for such

insured health services as may be approved for

that facility as listed in Schedule D.

[Added by C.O. 1972/149]

(3) An assignment made pursuant to paragraph (a) or

(b) of subsection (2), has no force or effect for

purposes of the plan unless the director approves of the

terms and conditions of the assignment and a copy of

the assignment is filed with the director.

(4) The director may enter into agreements or make

arrangements for payment for insured health services

to a medical practitioner or the employer of a medical

practitioner who renders them on a basis other than a

fee for services rendered.

(5) A medical practitioner may elect to collect their

fees under the plan for the insured health services they

rendered to insured persons otherwise than from the

plan without loss of benefit to insured persons by

giving notice to the director in writing of their election.

(6) Where the director receives notice of an election

pursuant to subsection (5) on or before the

commencement day of the plan, the election shall have

effect from the commencement day of the plan.

(7) Subject to subsection (6), an election pursuant to

subsection (5) shall have effect from and on the first

day beginning after the expiration of thirty days after

the day on which the director receives the notice of the

election, and shall not be adopted unless it is in respect

of all the patients of the medical practitioner.

(8) A medical practitioner who has made an election

pursuant to subsection (5) may at any time, in writing,

notify the director of their revocation of the election.

(9) A revocation of election pursuant to subsection (8)

shall have effect from and on the first day of the first

month beginning after the expiration of sixty days after

the day on which the director receives the notice of

revocation, except where otherwise determined by the

director.

(10) Where a medical practitioner who has made an

election under subsection (5) and has not effectively

revoked such election, renders an insured health

service to an insured person, the medical practitioner

shall complete the claim form approved for that

purpose for the insured person or someone acting on

behalf of the insured person or provide the insured

person with such information as the director may

require so that the insured person may obtain their

benefit under the plan.

(11) Where a medical practitioner has made an

election under subsection (5) and has not effectively

revoked the election, an insured person is entitled to

payment for the costs of insured health services

rendered by the medical practitioner and for which the

insured person is liable in an amount not exceeding the

amount that otherwise would be payable to the medical

practitioner under the payment schedule for Yukon but

only where

(a) the medical practitioner has, before rendering

the service, given the insured person notice in

writing that they have so elected and proposes

to require payment of their fees directly by the

insured person;

(b) there is, between the medical practitioner and

the insured person, an agreement in writing

made prior to the rendering of the services

specifying any amount that exceeds payment

schedule for Yukon; and

(c) the insured person surrenders a substantiated

claim on a form approved by the director or an

itemized receipt signed by the medical

practitioner that shows the services rendered

and the fees paid by the insured person to the

medical practitioner for the services.

(12) Where a medical practitioner renders an insured

health service to an insured person, the medical

practitioner is not entitled to demand nor receive from

the director a payment in an amount greater than the

amount set out in the payment schedule for Yukon.

(13) Insured health services rendered to an insured

person by a specialist without a referral by a general

practitioner, nurse practitioner or registered midwife

to the specialist shall, under the plan, be paid in an

amount equal to the fee that would have been paid had

the services been rendered by a general practitioner,

and the director shall not be responsible for the

difference between the specialist fee and that of a

general practitioner, except where the specialist fee is

the only applicable fee.

(14) An insured person is entitled to payment for the

cost of insured health services rendered outside Yukon

up to an amount not exceeding the amount that would

otherwise be payable therefor under the plan, but only

where

(a) the insured person surrenders an itemized

receipt that shows the services rendered;

(b) the itemized receipt duly completed by the

person who rendered the service is first

received; and

(c) the service or treatment given is covered as an

insured health service in Yukon.

(15) There shall be no responsibility for the payment

for insured health services unless

(a) within six months of the date upon which the

service was rendered, an itemized receipt, duly

completed by the medical practitioner who

rendered the service, is received, or

(b) the director otherwise directs.

(16) In any dispute, the director shall determine if

there is impediment or preclusion to reasonable access

to insured health services by insured persons.

(17) Except as provided in subsection (13), payment to

a medical practitioner for insured health services in

accordance with the payment schedule for Yukon is

payment in full for the insured health services and

there is no further liability on the director respecting

those insured health services.

8 The director shall have the power to require and

receive any and all information that they consider

necessary in order to adjudge the claims for services

rendered to insured persons by medical practitioners.

## PAYMENT SCHEDULE FOR YUKON

9 The director shall if the insured health service is

listed in the payment schedule for Yukon make a

payment to a medical practitioner in Yukon for an

insured health service rendered to an insured person in

an amount up to the maximum amount listed in the

payment schedule for Yukon for the insured health

service.

### Outside Yukon

10 (1) Subject to the Act and these Regulations, the

director shall make payment to medical practitioners

outside Yukon who are licensed or approved by

competent authority of the province, state or country in

which they practice, an amount established by the

director in respect of insured health services rendered

to an insured person.

(2) The rates payable where the medical practitioner is

practising in a province participating under the Federal

Act shall not exceed the prescribed rates of the host

province for the insured services rendered.

(3) The amount payable to a medical practitioner

practising in a non-participating province, state or

country under the federal Act for a service is the

amount the director determines having regard to the

nature of the service is in their opinion fair, but shall

not exceed the maximum amount payable for the

service under section 9.

(4) Where the medical practitioner is practising in a

province where the service rendered is not insured

under the health care insurance plan of the province,

the rates payable shall be determined by the director to

be in the amount which is fair, in their opinion, having

regard to the nature of the service, but shall not exceed

the maximum under section 9.

### Reimbursement of insured persons

11 If an insured person has paid to a medical

practitioner any or all of the costs of the insured health

services rendered to them by the medical practitioner,

the director may reimburse the insured person for the

costs paid, to the maximum that would have been

payable to the medical practitioner under the payment

schedule for Yukon.

12 (1) The director may seek advice regarding any

claim or claims for the purpose of assessing claims and

amounts thereof with particular reference to possible

misuse or abuse of the plan.

(2) Where the director, after receiving such advice,

decides that there has been misuse or abuse of the

plan, or that the fee claimed is not commensurate with

the service rendered, the director may refuse payment

or make payment on a pro-rata basis.

(3) Where the director has reason to withhold payment

or any claim, they shall inform the claimant by notice

in writing to this effect and seek resolution of the

claim.

### Registration

13 (1) Every resident, other than a dependant or

person exempted by the Regulations from so doing,

shall register themselves and their dependants with the

director by submitting a duly completed registration

form to the office of the director immediately on the

coming into force of this Regulation.

[Amended by C.O. 1972/188]

(2) Every resident who fails to comply with

subsection (1) or who willfully withholds information

necessary for the purposes of registration or willfully

gives false information to the person registering them,

is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction

to a fine not exceeding $100.00 and shall be ordered by

the Court to comply with subsection (1).

(3) Where a resident has failed to registered pursuant

to subsection (1), they shall, within 10 days of

notification by the director, deliver by hand or

registered mail, a duly completed registration form to

the director.

[Added by C.O. 1973/447]
