*Made under the Securities Act*

Pursuant to sections 169, 170 and 171 of the Securities

Act , the Minister of Community Services orders as follows

1 The attached Rule to Make Local Amendments to CSA

Instruments Implemented in Yukon (Local Rule 11-804) is

made.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, August 23, 2012.

______________________________________________________

Minister of Community Services

## RULE TO MAKE LOCAL AMENDMENTS TO

## CSA INSTRUMENTS IMPLEMENTED IN

## YUKON (LOCAL RULE 11-804)

1 The CSA instruments listed in Appendix A, which

have been implemented in Yukon under Local Rule 11-802 ,

are amended by replacing the contact information for the

Yukon Superintendent of Securities with the following

wherever it appears

“Office of the Yukon Superintendent of Securities

Government of Yukon

Department of Community Services

307 Black Street, 1st Floor

PO Box 2703 (C-6)

Whitehorse, Yukon Y1A 2C6

Telephone: 867-667-5466

Facsimile: 867-393-6251

http://www.community.gov.yk.ca/corp/securities

_about.html”
