YOIC 1994/78

*Made under the Assessment and Taxation Act*

Pursuant to section 56 of the Assessment and Taxation

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 The attached Rural Electrification and Telephone

Local Improvement Tax Regulation, 1994, Schedule I, is

hereby established.

2 Schedule I of the Rural Electrification and

Telephone Local Improvement Tax Regulations, 1993 is

amended as follows for Region 2 - Henderson Corner:

(a) under the heading “Description” the

description “Lots 15 to 21, Plan 89-32” is

substituted for the description “Lots 15-1, 15-2,

16-1, 16-2, 18-1, 18-2, 20-1, 20-2, 21-1, 21-2 (Lots

17 and 19 remain as in the 1993 submission).

(b) See attached Schedule II for details on the

amendments referred to in clause (a).

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 9th

day of May, 1994.

_______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
