YOIC 2003/147

*Made under the Placer Mining Act*

### Preamble

Whereas in the opinion of the Commissioner in

Executive Council, the land in the Yukon described in the

annexed order is required for the purpose of a National

Historic Site

Pursuant to section 98 of the Placer Mining Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The annexed Placer Mining Prohibition Order, 1979 is

hereby made.

2 This Order comes into force April 1, 2003.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 25th day

of March, 2003.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## PLACER MINING PROHIBITION ORDER,

## 1979

1 Entry on the lands described in the Schedule

the purpose of locating a claim or prospecting for gold

other precious minerals or stones is prohibited.

## SCHEDULE

In the Yukon Territory.

In the vicinity of Bear Creek.

The whole of Lot 1002 Quad 116 B/3 as said lot is shown

on plan of survey number 65024 in the Canada Lands

Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the

Land Titles Office for the Yukon Land Registration District

at Whitehorse under number 55291.
