*Made under the Public Service Labour Relations Act*

## PUBLIC SERVICE STAFF RELATIONS ACT

## PUBLIC SERVICE STAFF RELATIONS ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of the Public Service Staff

Relations Act , the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory

is pleased to and doth hereby order as follows:

1 Authority is delegated to the Chairman of the

Yukon Public Service Staff Relations Board to appoint

such officers and employees as the Board deems

necessary for the performance of its duties under the

Act.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 2nd

day of May, A.D., 1975.

Commissioner of the Yukon/Commissaire
