YOIC 2003/61

*Made under the Placer Mining Act*

Pursuant to section 4 of the Placer Mining Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The annexed Mining Districts Order is hereby made.

2 This Order comes into force April 1, 2003.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 25th day

of March, 2003.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## MINING DISTRICTS ORDER

1 The Proclamation made by the Commissioner of

the Yukon Territory February 19, 1972 under the Yukon

Placer Mining Act (Canada) to establish the Whitehorse

Mining District, the Watson Lake Mining District, the

Dawson Mining District, and the Mayo Mining District is

hereby adopted and continued in force.
