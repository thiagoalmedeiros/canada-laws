YOIC 2011/37

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The following highway is closed

All that portion of the highway situated in the

middle of Lot 1381, Quad 105 D/14, Plan 84093

CLSR, 2000-0202 LTO, comprising approximately

1.97 hectares and shown as hatched on the map

dated February 21, 2011 attached as the Appendix

to this Order.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 30 March 2011.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon
