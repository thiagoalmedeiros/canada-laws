# Establishes that no part of described land (Watson Lake) is deemed to be reserved for the Crown

YOIC 2012/113

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

Pursuant to section 11 of the Territorial Lands (Yukon)

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows

1 If at any time before 2015 notification under

subsection 7(2) of the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act is issued

for the first time in respect of the property described in the

attached schedule, no part of the property (or, if the

notification applies to only a portion of the property, no

part of that portion) is at and after that time to be deemed

under section 11 of the Act to be reserved to the Crown.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, June 21, 2012.

Commissioner of Yukon

## SCHEDULE

In Yukon, in the vicinity of Watson Lake, lying within

Quad 105 A/2, ALL THAT parcel of land more particularly

described as follows; all topographic features hereinafter

referred are according to Edition 4 of the Watson Lake Map

Sheet, produced at a scale of 1:50,000 by Surveys and

Mapping Branch, Department of Energy, Mines and

Resources, Canada; all bearings hereinafter mentioned are

referenced to the meridian through an iron post set on the

centre line of Runway 08-26, Watson Lake Airport, 100 feet

westerly from the Westerly end of the said runway

according to Plan 50948 in the Canada Lands Surveys

Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the Land

Titles Office at Whitehorse as 24758;

COMMENCING at the intersection of a line on a bearing of

135º 39’ from the most easterly corner of Lot 1-11, Group

757, as shown on Plan 53701 in the said Records, a copy of

which is filled in said Office as 29782 and the northerly

Ordinary High Water Mark of Watson Lake;

THENCE north-westerly in a straight line approximately

30.5 metres to said corner of Lot 1-11;

THENCE south-westerly 30.48 metres, more or less, along

the south-easterly boundary of said Lot 1-11 to the most

southerly corner thereof;

THENCE south-westerly 101.52 metres, more or less, along

the south-easterly boundaries of Lots 1-10, 1-9 and 1-8

respectively, to the most southerly corner of said Lot 1-8;

THENCE 112º 30’ a distance of approximately 30 metres to

an intersection with the said Ordinary High Water Mark of

Watson Lake;

THENCE generally northerly along the said Ordinary High

Water Mark of Watson Lake approximately 118 metres to

the point of commencement;

SAID parcel containing approximately 0.37 hectares.
