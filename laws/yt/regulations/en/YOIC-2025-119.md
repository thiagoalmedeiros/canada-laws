# Order to Establish Interim Guidelines for Chasan Chua Territorial Park

YOIC 2025/119

*Made under the Parks and Land Certainty Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Order

“ drone ” means an aircraft that

(a) is not designed to convey a person, and

(b) is operated by any means including one or more

of the following:

(i) remote control,

(ii) pre-programmed software,

(iii) onboard computer autonomy or artificial

intelligence; « drone »

“ organized recreational activity ” means a recreational

activity organized by or on behalf of

(a) one or more individuals for, or with an

expectation of, gain or profit, or

(b) an organization whether or not for, or with an

expectation of, gain or profit,

but does not include a special event; « activité de loisirs

organisée »

“ personal recreational activity ” means a recreational

activity undertaken by an individual or a group of

individuals, including, without limitation, groups of

friends, family members or acquaintances, but does not

include

(a) an organized recreational activity, or

(b) a special event; « activité de loisirs

personnelle »

“ vehicle ” has the same meaning as in the Motor Vehicles

Act . « véhicule »

### Interim guidelines established

2 For the purposes of subsection 20(2) of the Act,

sections 3 to 5 are established as interim guidelines for

Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park.

### Parks General Regulation does not apply

3 The Parks General Regulation does not apply to Chasàn

Chùa Territorial Park.

### Authorized development, use, activity or occupation of land

4 A person may undertake the following development, use,

activity or occupation of land in Chasàn Chùa Territorial

Park without a park permit:

(a) personal recreational activity;

(b) organized recreational activity;

(c) research or educational activity, other than the

creation of research plots and monitoring of

such plots;

(d) harvesting the following for personal

consumption:

(i) wild fruits, berries or fungi,

(ii) renewable parts of plants only if the

harvesting does not kill the plant;

(e) pulling and removing invasive plant species;

(f) taking photographs or videos without an

expectation of gain or profit;

(g) flying a drone;

(h) operating a vehicle;

(i) organizing, hosting or attending a parade,

concert, sports event, wedding, fair or other

special event;

(j) the following activities if they do not involve the

use of a self-propelled or stationary power-

driven machine other than a power saw:

(i) construction of a trail that does not exceed

1.5 m in width, including ground disturbance

and clearing or cutting vegetation,

(ii) construction of structures or features for

trails, such as boardwalks, bridges and

jumps, but not including structures or

features that cross McIntyre Creek,

(iii) maintenance, grooming or enhancement of

existing trails, such as grading, filling holes or

ruts, armouring against erosion, improving

drainage and trimming vegetation;

(k) construction and installation of a bench, picnic

table, dock, interpretive signage or other similar

infrastructure by or on behalf of the City of

Whitehorse;

(l) road maintenance undertaken by or on behalf of

the City of Whitehorse, but not including

rerouting an existing road or installing a culvert;

(m) harvesting a Christmas tree;

(n) vegetation management activities to reduce

wildfire risk, not including the creation of a fuel

break, by or on behalf of the City of Whitehorse

or Government of Yukon;

(o) cleaning up waste, such as removing litter within

the meaning of section 61 of the Act, but not

including any activity that involves the use of a

self-propelled or stationary power-driven

machine;

(p) use and maintenance, by or on behalf of the

Government of Yukon, of the snow survey

station located on approximately 1.1 ha near

Mount McIntyre, north of the Mount McIntyre

Descent Road, in Quad 105D11.

### Bylaws apply

5 (1) The bylaws of the City of Whitehorse continue to

apply in Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park.

(2) For greater certainty, nothing in section 4 affects a

requirement to comply with any bylaw of the City of

Whitehorse relating to undertaking a development, use

or activity, or to the occupation of land, in Chasàn Chùa

Territorial Park for which a park permit is not required.

### Park permit fee

6 The fee payable for a park permit authorizing any

development, use, activity or occupation of land in

Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park is $40.
