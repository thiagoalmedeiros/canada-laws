*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The lots in the Hotsprings Road Rural Residential

Subdivision listed in Schedule A attached hereto are classed

as Rural Residential (RR) and shall be sold at the prices

listed in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, in Yukon,this 15 day of August, 2006.
