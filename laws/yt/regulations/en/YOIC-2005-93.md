YOIC 2005/93

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The portion of Access shown on Plan 68140 CLSR,

25231 LTO Carcross, Yukon as separating Lot 7, Plan 68140

CLSR, 25231 LTO from Lots 8 and 9, Plan 68140 CLSR,

25231 LTO, is hereby closed and declared to no longer be

highway.

2 The portion of Road shown on Plan 65021 CLSR,

54817 LTO immediately adjacent to and southeast of Lot

1000, Quad 105 B/2, Plan 65021 CLSR, 54817 LTO, is

hereby closed and declared to no longer be highway.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this May 26th 2005.

Commissioner of Yukon
