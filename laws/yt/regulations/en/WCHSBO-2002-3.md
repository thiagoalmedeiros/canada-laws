*Made under the Workers’ Safety and Compensation Act*

## W.C.H.S.B.O. 2002/03

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to subsection 101(1) of the Workers’

Compensation Act , the Workers’ Compensation Health &

Safety Board orders as follows:

1 The maximum wage rate for the year 2003 shall be

$66,200 per annum.

2 This order shall come into force on January 1,

2003.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 4th day

of December, 2002.
