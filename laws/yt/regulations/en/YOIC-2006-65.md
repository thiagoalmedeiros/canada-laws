## AN ACT APPROVING YUKON LAND CLAIMS

## AN ACT APPROVING YUKON LAND

## CLAIMS FINAL AGREEMENTS

Pursuant to section 2.3.0 of the Champagne and

Aishihik First Nations Final Agreement , which has been

approved under An Act Approving Yukon Land Claims Final

Agreements , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders

as follows

1 The amendment to Chapter 2, Schedule B of the

Champagne and Aishihik First Nations Final Agreement set out

in Appendix, annexed hereto, is hereby approved.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 6th April 2006.
