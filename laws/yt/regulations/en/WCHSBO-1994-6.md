## W.C.H.S.B.O. 1994/006

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to section 101(1) of the Workers’

Compensation Act , the Board orders as follows:

1 The maximum wage rate for the year 1995 shall be

fifty one thousand, nine hundred dollars (51,900.00) per

annum.

2 This maximum wage rate is to be applied to workers

who are entitled to compensation as a result of a disability

caused after January 1, 1995.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 31st

day of December, 1994.
