YOIC 1997/155

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 7 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders that:

1 Various streets and roads in the unincorporated

community of Carcross are hereby named as shown on the

annexed map comprising:

Sheet 1, Carcross Key Plan;

Sheet A, Carcross/Tagish First Nation;

Sheet B, Developed Downtown Area;

Sheet C, Undeveloped Downtown Area;

Sheet D, Airport Area; and

Sheet E, Choutla Subdivision.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 24

day of September, 1997.

_________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukony
