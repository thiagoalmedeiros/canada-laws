*Made under the Workers’ Safety and Compensation Act*

## W.C.H.S.B.O. 1998/02

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to section 53 of the Workers’ Compensation Act ,

the Board orders as follows:

1 The following assessment rates per one hundred

dollars of payroll shall be:

Class 1 $2.75 2.75%

Class 2 3.25 3.25%

Class 3 0.75 0.75%

Class 4 2.35 2.35%

Class 6 1.60 1.60%

Class 8 1.10 1.10%

2 This order shall be deemed to have come into force

on January 1, 1998.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

21st day of January, 1998.
