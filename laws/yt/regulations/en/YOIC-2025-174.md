YOIC 2025/174

*Made under the Garnishee Act*

### Wage exemption amount

1 (1) Subject to the annual adjustment in section 3, the

minimum monthly amount of wages payable by an

employer to a debtor that is exempt from attachment

under a writ of garnishment under subsection 22(1) of

the Act is

(a) in the case of a debtor who supports no

dependants, $2,000; or

(b) in the case of a debtor who supports one or more

dependants

(i) $3000, if they support at least one dependant,

and

(ii) an additional $500 for each additional

dependant.

### Timing of wage exemption amount

2 The minimum monthly amount of wages payable by an

employer to a debtor that is exempt from attachment

under a writ of garnishment under subsection 22(1) of

the Act is determined on the date that the writ of

garnishment is issued.

### Annual adjustment of limits

3 (1) In this section

“ adjusted amount ” means an amount calculated in

September of the current year by increasing an amount

that is exempt from attachment under a writ of

garnishment in effect in that month by the annual

percentage increase in the All-Items Consumer Price

Index for Whitehorse published by Statistics Canada, if

any, for the period from September 1 of the immediately

preceding year to August 31 of the current year;

« montant indexé »

“ current year ” means, in respect of any particular

calculation being made by the Minister under

subsection (2), the year in which the calculation is made;

« année courante »

(2) In September of each year, the Minister must

calculate the adjusted amount for each prescribed limit

set out in section 1 and publish the adjusted amounts on

a Government of Yukon website or by any other means

that the Minister considers appropriate.

(3) From November 1 in each year until October 31 of

the immediately following year, the prescribed

minimum monthly exempt amounts set out in section 1

are deemed to be replaced by the corresponding

adjusted amounts calculated under subsection (2) and

those adjusted amounts are the prescribed minimum

monthly exempt amounts in effect in those months.

### Forms

4 The following forms, set out in the Schedule to this

Regulation, are prescribed for the purposes of the Act:

(a) Form 1 — Writ of Immediate Garnishment, for

the purposes of section 2 of the Act;

(b) Form 2 — Writ of Continuing Garnishment, for

the purposes of section 3 of the Act;

(c) Form 3 — Writ of Temporary Garnishment, for

the purposes of section 9 of the Act;

(d) Form 4 — Notice of Response, for the purposes

of subsection 25(1) of the Act; and

(e) Form 5 — Demand for Response, for the

purposes of subsection 25(3) of the Act.

### Repeal

5 Order-in-Council 1981/191 is repealed.

### Coming into force

6 This Regulation comes into force on the later of the

following days:

(a) the day on which the Exemptions Act, S.Y. 2024,

c. 5, comes into force;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of

regulations under the Regulations Act .
