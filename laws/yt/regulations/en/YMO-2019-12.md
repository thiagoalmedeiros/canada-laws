## WITHDRAWAL FROM DISPOSITION OF

## CERTAIN YUKON OIL AND GAS LANDS

(FORT SELKIRK HISTORIC SITE BUFFER

## AREA) ORDER

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to withdraw certain

Yukon oil and gas lands from disposition to provide greater

protection to the heritage and environmental resources in

the area surrounding the Fort Selkirk Historic Site.

### Definition

2 In this Order

“Fort Selkirk Historic Site Buffer Area” means the lands

described in Schedule 1 and shown on the map

attached as Schedule 2. « zone »

### Lands withdrawn from disposition

3 The Fort Selkirk Historic Site Buffer Area is

withdrawn from disposition.
