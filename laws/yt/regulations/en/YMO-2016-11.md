Minister of Community Services

## RULE TO MAKE AMENDMENT NO. 13 TO

## THE RULE RESPECTING THE

## IMPLEMENTATION OF CSA INSTRUMENTS

## AND POLICIES (LOCAL RULE 11-802)

### Amends Local Rule 11-802 Schedule

1 This rule amends the Schedule to the Rule Respecting

the Implementation of CSA Instruments and Policies (Local Rule

11-802) .

### Amendments

2 (1) The following entry is added in numerical order

“11-103 Multilateral Failure-to-File Cease

Instrument Trade Orders in

11-103 Multiple Jurisdictions ”

(2) The entry for Multilateral Instrument 62-104 is

amended by replacing the expression “Multilateral” with

the expression “National”.

### Application

3 (1) Subsection 2(1) applies after June 22, 2016.

(2) Subsection 2(2) applies after May 8, 2016.
