YOIC 2021/103

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

## ORDER-IN-COUNCIL 2021/103

Having received the Employment Standards

Order entitled Minimum Wage Order No. 2021/01

is attached (the “Order”);

Therefore, pursuant to the Employment

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders

1 The establishment of the minimum hourly wage

out in section 1 of the Order is approved.

2 Order-in-Council 2020/35 is repealed.

3 This Order-in-Council comes into force

August 1, 2021.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

July 28,

Commissioner of

## ORDER OF THE EMPLYMENT STANDARDS BOARD

## MINIMUM WAGE ORDER NO. 2021/01

Pursuant to subsection 18(1) of the Employment

Standards Act

1 The Employment Standards Board orders that

(a) the minimum hourly wage is fixed at $15.20 per

hour effective August 1, 2021; and

(b) effective April 1, 2022, and on April 1 of each

subsequent year, the minimum hourly wage is

increased by the annual increase, during the

preceding calendar year, in the Consumer Price

Index for Whitehorse maintained by Statistics

Canada.

2 Employment Standards Board Minimum Wage Order

No. 2020/02 is repealed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

July 20, 2021
