YOIC 2023/63

*Made under the Vital Statistics Act*

Pursuant to the Vital Statistics Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council considers it advisable for the

Commissioner in Executive Council to enter into a

memorandum of understanding for information sharing

with the Canada Revenue Agency, substantially in the

form of the draft agreement set out in the attached

Schedule.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

April 20, 2023

Commissioner of

## SCHEDULE

## MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

## FOR INFORMATION SHARING

## BETWEEN

As represented by the Commissioner

Service, Innovation and Integration Branch

(Hereinafter referred to as the “CRA”)

## AND

### THE COMMISSIONER on Behalf of the

### Commissioner in Executive Council for YUKON

As represented by the

Minister of Health and Social Services

## 1. LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITIES

1.1 The Registrar General of Vital Statistics (hereafter

the “Registrar”) is responsible for registration of vital

events which occur in Yukon;

1.2 Section 40 of the Vital Statistics Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council may, on behalf of the

Government of Yukon, enter into agreements with the

Government of Canada in respect of any matter the

Commissioner in Executive Council considers advisable

relating to the purposes and provisions of this Act. S.Y.

2002, c.225, s.40;

1.3 Section 5 of the Canada Revenue Agency Act (CRA

Act) describes the mandate of the CRA as supporting the

administration and enforcement of program legislation.

To meet this mandate, the CRA requires the ongoing

transmission of Yukon vital event information;

1.4 Section 61 of the CRA Act allows the CRA to enter into

agreements or other arrangements with governments,

public or private organizations and agencies or any

person in the name of His Majesty in right of Canada or

in its own name;

1.5 The CRA has implicit authority to collect personal

information based upon the statutory obligation of the

Minister of National Revenue to administer and enforce

the Income Tax Act (ITA) as found in section 220 thereof;

1.6 Subsection 8(2)(b) of the Privacy Act allows the

disclosure of personal information for any purpose in

accordance with any Act of Parliament or any regulation

made thereunder that authorizes its disclosure;

1.7 “ Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy

Act ” means the Freedom of Information and Protection

of Privacy Act (FIPPA), (Chapter F-25) as amended from

time to time;

1.8 “Access to Information and Protection of Privacy Act”

means Yukon’s Access to Information and Protection of

Privacy Act as amended from time to time (SY 2018, c.9)

1.9 Annex A of this Memorandum of Understanding

(MOU) lists the relevant legislative authorities that apply

to this MOU;

1.10 The parties shall establish the conditions and

procedures for the secure, timely and cost effective

transfer, use, storage and destruction of personal

information; and

1.11 Yukon acknowledges that, for the purpose of

collecting the Social Insurance Number (SIN) on behalf

of the CRA, it is acting as an agent of the CRA, and in

this respect, is bound by, and agrees to comply with, all

the confidentiality requirements placed on the CRA with

respect to the receipt of taxpayer information collected

on behalf of the CRA, namely the SIN.

Therefore, the CRA and Yukon have come to the

following administrative understanding:

## 2. PURPOSE

2.1 This MOU sets out the conditions and procedures

under which Yukon will disclose to the CRA the

information identified in Annex D about individuals

whose vital events are registered in Yukon. It provides

the administrative framework for the secure disclosure

of personal information by Yukon to the CRA.

2.2 The CRA requires the information identified in Annex

D for the sole purpose of administering and enforcing

the CRA program legislation described in Annex D, as

that term is defined in section 2 of the CRA Act, including

processing benefit applications and updating CRA

records, as further described in Annex D.

2.3 The initiatives under this MOU will:

a) ensure the security and protection of the identity of

individuals;

b) reduce the inconvenience to citizens by merging

processes dependent on common personal information;

and

c) improve the CRA’s data integrity, processing and

risk management approaches.

The initiatives being developed are described in clauses

6 and 7 of this MOU, and in Annex D to this MOU.

2.4 In carrying out the purpose, the parties will comply

with the requirements of all applicable privacy

legislation. This will include, but is not limited to,

complying with the following fundamental principles

relating to the protection of personal information and

individual privacy:

a) the disclosure of personal information under Annex

D.1 is intended to be based on the voluntary, written,

and informed consent of the newborn’s mother;

b) security measures will be in place to protect

personal information against risks such as unauthorized

access, use, disclosure or destruction at all times,

including during transmission.

c) absent consent, all other disclosures of personal

information made pursuant to this MOU will be based on

legislative authorization;

d) the collection, use, disclosure and retention of

personal information will be limited to the minimum

amount necessary to accomplish the purpose of this

MOU; and

e) access to and use of personal information will be

limited to only those authorized employees and agents

who need to know the personal information in order to

accomplish the purpose of this MOU.

2.5 Unless otherwise specified in an annex, electronic

transmission of personal information between the CRA

and Yukon for the purpose of this MOU will be carried

out using the National Routing System (NRS).

## 3. DEFINITIONS

3.1 The following definitions apply to this MOU and its

annexes:

“Access to Information Act” means the Access to

Information Act , (R.S., 1985, c.A-1), as amended from

time to time and any regulations under that Act;

“Access to Information and Protection of Privacy

Act” means Yukon’s Access to Information and

Protection of Privacy Act as amended from time to time

(SY 2018, c.9);

“Automated Benefits Application” (ABA) means the

enhanced benefit application service as more

particularly described in Annex D of this MOU;

“Canada Revenue Agency Act” (CRA Act) means the

Canada Revenue Agency Act, (1999, c. 17, s. 1, 2005, c.

38, s. 35), as amended from time to time;

“ CRA record ” means a record held by the CRA, which

contains information about a particular individual that is

registered in a CRA database;

“Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy

Act” (FIPPA), means the Freedom of Information and

Protection of Privacy Act (Chapter F-25) as amended

from time to time;

“Income Tax Act (Canada)” (ITA) means the Income

Tax Act , (R.S.C. 1985, c. 1 (5th supp.)), as amended from

time to time;

“ Information ” means any information, in any form,

including personal information, from records respecting

vital eve registrations that are held by the Registrar and

Yukon and provided to the CRA pursuant to the terms of

this MOU;

“ National Routing System ” (NRS) means the secure

communications network that links vital statistics

agencies with each other and with other

provincial/territorial/federal departments and agencies

in order to exchange and authenticate vital event

information;

“ Personal information ” means information about an

individual that is recorded in any form, as defined in

FIPPA. The specific personal information to be disclosed

under this MOU is identified in Annex D;

“Privacy Act” means the Privacy Act , (R.S. 1985, c. P-

21) as amended from time to time;

“ Transmission ” means the provision of the information

to the CRA by any means, and includes, but is not limited

to, providing electronic records;

“ Taxpayer Information ” has the same meaning as

defined in subsection 241(10) of the ITA;

(RSY 2002, c.225) as amended from time to time; and

“ Vital Statistics Regulations ” (VSR) means the Vital

Statistics Regulations, (O.I.C. 1987/188) as amended

from time to time.

## 4. NATURE OF THIS MOU

4.1 This MOU is an administrative understanding

between the parties and is not intended to be

enforceable before the courts.

4.2 A successor to a party to this MOU becomes a party

to this MOU.

4.3 Each annex to this MOU forms an integral part of this

MOU.

## 5. DESIGNATED OFFICIALS

5.1 The CRA and Yukon will designate officials who are

to be the points of contact for the purposes of this MOU.

Annex B of this MOU identifies the CRA officials and their

responsibilities as designated by the CRA. Annex C of this

MOU identifies Yukon officials and their responsibilities

as designated by Yukon.

## 6. INITIATIVES BETWEEN YUKON AND

## THE CRA

6.1 The parties agree to develop, implement and

maintain the initiatives listed in Annex D.

6.2 The parties will work in collaboration to establish the

mechanisms and processes for the secure electronic

disclosure of information. This disclosure will be guided

by agreed-upon protocols, methodologies, standards,

business processes, and the privacy and security

requirements of both parties.

6.3 This collaboration will build upon the technology,

business processes and data exchanges standards

developed under the NRS. The purpose of using such

agreed-upon standards is to promote national

consistency in the sharing of vital event information.

6.4 Each party will maintain its own internal records

containing details of the initiatives implemented under

this MOU.

## 7. INFORMATION MANAGEMENT AND PROTECTION OF

## PERSONAL INFORMATION

7.1 Yukon will disclose the information identified in

Annex D to the CRA using the NRS, as outlined in Annex

E.

7.2 The CRA will ensure that the personal information

disclosed by Yukon under the MOU is used only for the

purposes identified in clause 2.2 and Annex D of this

MOU.

7.3 All of the employees who will be accessing and using

the information disclosed by Yukon are recognized to

have sworn or affirmed, upon commencing employment

in the Federal Public Service, that they will not, without

due authority, disclose the information that comes to

their knowledge through their duties, as per the

Government of Canada Oath or Solemn Affirmation of

Office. If the CRA authorizes an individual who is not an

employee to access and use the information disclosed by

Yukon, the CRA will ensure that the individual is required

to keep the information secure and confidential in the

same way the individual will be required to keep the

CRA`s information secure and confidential.

7.4 The CRA will acknowledge receipt of each electronic

notification disclosed by Yukon under Annex D and

Yukon shall not be held responsible or liable to the CRA

for any information lost or not received by the CRA.

7.5 The CRA will use the personal information disclosed

by Yukon under this MOU for the administration of the

CRA programs and services identified in Annex D,

including processing benefit applications and updating

CRA records, as further described in Annex D. Personal

information disclosed to the CRA by Yukon:

a) under Annex D.1 will be incorporated into CRA

records; and

b) under Annex D.2 will be matched to a CRA record,

and the place of death, date of death and the death

registration number will be incorporated into the

matching CRA record.

7.6 Personal information disclosed to the CRA by Yukon

under Annex D will be incorporated into CRA records.

Once personal information is incorporated, any use,

disclosure and retention by the CRA of the personal

information which has been disclosed to the CRA by

Yukon under this MOU will be protected in accordance

with the processes and procedures outlined in Annex F.

All personal information disclosed to the CRA by Yukon

that is determined to be unmatchable with a CRA record

under clause 7.5 above will be destroyed by the CRA in

a secure manner that protects the privacy of the

individual to whom it pertains, in accordance with

Annex F.

7.7 Subject to clauses 7.8 and 7.9, the CRA may not

disclose to a third party, including any other federal

ministry, agency, or organization, or any other

government or private organization, any information

disclosed to it by Yukon, unless otherwise authorized by

federal statute.

7.8 In the event of an access request under the Access

to Information Act or the Privacy Act or the

corresponding provincial legislation or a court order to

disclose information, obtained by the CRA under this

MOU, the CRA agrees to immediately notify Yukon of the

request, to the designated official as per Annexes B and

C and, consult with Yukon as to appropriate course of

action.

7.9 In the event of an access request under Yukon Access

to Information and Protection of Privacy Act for the SIN

collected pursuant to Annex D of this MOU, Yukon agrees

to immediately notify the CRA of the request, to the

designated official as per Annexes B and C, and consult

with the CRA as to appropriate course of action.

7.10 Where the SIN is collected by Yukon pursuant to

Annex D of this MOU, Yukon agrees to secure, protect,

manage and retain this personal information in

accordance with the Government of Yukon legislation

and the Government of Yukon information management

policies and procedures that are applicable to Yukon.

7.11 All the information disclosed to the CRA under this

MOU shall be considered taxpayer information by the

CRA once received and will be protected by the CRA as

required under the laws of Canada. The information will

only be used in accordance with this MOU.

7.12 The CRA shall ensure that the information will be

safeguarded by applying the security standards that are

described in Annex E of this MOU, including the

requirements in the chart titled “Security Standards for

Handling Protected Canada Revenue Agency

Information.”

7.13 The parties will immediately notify each other, in

writing, with full details when they have reasonable

grounds to believe that any of the requirements

respecting the security or protection of information set

out in this MOU have been or are likely to be breached

as per Annex F. This includes any unauthorized

disclosure or use of information, any circumstances,

incidents or events that have jeopardized or may

jeopardize the security or integrity of their respective

computer systems and networks used to access and

transmit information under this MOU, or any

information contained therein.

7.14 In the event of a security breach of the nature

described in clause 7.12 and as per Annex E, the party

responsible for the security of the information will

promptly take all reasonable steps to prevent

recurrences and advise the other party of the steps

taken.

## 8. MONITORING

8.1 The CRA will conduct monitoring/internal audits with

respect to the confidentiality and security of the

information provided under this MOU and/or any of its

annexes using a risk-based approach to provide a

reasonable assurance that:

• the information is being used, disclosed, retained and

disposed of in accordance with the terms and conditions

set out in this MOU and the federal and

provincial/territorial laws providing for its use,

disclosure, retention and disposition; and

• the security processes and procedures specified in the

MOU and its annexes, or security measures that meet

CRA security standards, are being applied and are

effective.

8.2 The audit/monitoring reports and any related

resulting action plans will be sent to the designated

officials having overall administrative responsibility for

this MOU, as per Annexes B and C, within one month of

final approval of the reports.

## 9. COMMUNICATIONS

9.1 The parties agree that any public communications

material, including news releases, media material, or

similar public documents that are prepared in relation

to this MOU will be submitted to the other party for

concurrence at least 10 days prior to its release.

9.2 All material jointly created by Yukon and the CRA

pursuant to this MOU for use by the public will be

available in French and English.

## 10. EFFECTIVE DATE, AMENDMENTS AND

## TERMINATION

10.1 This MOU shall come into effect on the date of last

signing and shall remain in effect until terminated. It

may be terminated at any time with the mutual written

consent of the parties, or by either party upon a 120 day

written notice from the persons occupying the position

of signatory of the MOU.

10.2 Should notice of termination be given by one of the

parties under clause 10.1 of this MOU, the financial

arrangements under clause 13 and Annex G shall

continue in effect until termination for any transactions

during that period. The provisions in clauses 2.4 and

section 7 shall survive the termination of this MOU.

10.3 Should this MOU, or an annex to this MOU, be

terminated, the confidentiality and security of

information provisions in section 7, and the security

requirements outlined in Annex F, will continue to apply

to the information that has already been provided.

10.4 Subject to clause 10.3 of this MOU, on termination

of this MOU, all rights and obligations of both parties

under this MOU will cease to be in force, except the

obligation for account reconciliation and the issuance of

a final invoice, if appropriate.

10.5 This MOU, and Annexes B and C to this MOU, may

be amended at any time as the parties agree through an

exchange of letters between the persons occupying the

positions of the signatories (or their designate) to this

MOU. The amendment will come into effect on the date

of the last signing.

10.6 Any of the annexes of this MOU, except Annexes B

and C, may be amended at any time with the mutual

consent of the parties, by an exchange of letter between

the authorized officials identified in Annexes B and C and

such amendments will come into effect on the date of

the last signing.

## 11. REVIEW

11.1 The parties agree to complete a review of this

written arrangement in its entirety on or before the

tenth anniversary of the date of its signing or the

effective date, whichever is earlier. Subsequent reviews

will be completed no more than 10 years following the

date of the previous review. Once the parties have

completed the review, the results will be documented in

writing.

11.2 In reviewing this written arrangement, the parties

will consider whether the written arrangement should

continue in its current form, be revised, or terminated.

If either, or both, of the parties determine that this

written arrangement should be terminated, in whole or

in part, the termination will be carried out according to

clause 10 in this arrangement.

11.3 If a review is not conducted by the end of the ten-

year period described above, the written arrangement

may be terminated effective 30 days following the tenth

anniversary of the date of signing of the arrangement,

or the effective date, whichever is earlier, or if both of

these dates precede the previous review date, then

following the tenth anniversary of the previous review

date.

11.4 Notwithstanding the mandatory review in the

tenth year, the parties agree to review periodically, as

needed, the terms, conditions and procedures set out in

this MOU and its annexes.

## 12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

12.1 Annexes B and C identify the authorized officials

responsible for resolving disputes with respect to this

MOU. If those officials are not able to resolve the dispute,

the dispute shall be referred to the persons occupying

the position of signatory of the MOU for resolution

## 13. FINANCIAL ARRANGEMENTS

13.1 The CRA agrees to pay Yukon on-going transaction

fees in accordance with the fee structure set out in

Annex G. One year from the effective date of this MOU,

and every three years thereafter, the designated officials

listed in Annexes B and C agree to an evaluation of the

transaction fee structure based on lessons learned and

any changes in volume. On-going Yukon system

maintenance and upgrades will be the responsibility of

Yukon.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 This MOU, including all annexes and any associated

amendments under section 10 of this MOU, constitutes

the entire MOU for the subject matter outlined herein.

The terms and conditions contained herein apply to all

annexes.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF , this Memorandum of

Understanding, in both official languages.
