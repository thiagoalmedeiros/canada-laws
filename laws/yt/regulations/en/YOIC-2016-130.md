YOIC 2016/130

*Made under the Parks and Land Certainty Act*

Pursuant to sections 8 and 11 of the Parks and Land

Certainty Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows

1 In this Order

“Kusawa Park” means the portion of land shown as

Kusawa Park on Administrative Plan 102874 CLSR.

« Parc Kusawa »

2 Kusawa Park is

(a) established as a settlement agreement park;

and

(b) designated as a natural environment park.

3 The Schedule to the Act is amended by adding the

following in alphabetical order

14.01 Kusawa Park Settlement To protect a

agreement park representative or

designated as a unique landscape

natural that displays

environment ecological

park characteristics

or features of one

or more of the

Yukon’s

ecoregions.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, July 18, 2016.
