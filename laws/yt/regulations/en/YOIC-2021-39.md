YOIC 2021/39

*Made under the Highways Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation

“ public airport ” has the same meaning as in the Public

Airports Act . « aéroport public »

### Public airport not a highway

2 For the purposes of paragraph 1.2(2)(e) of the Act, all

areas that are part of the following public airports that

would, but for this Regulation, be a highway, are

declared not to be a highway:

(a) Braeburn aerodrome;

(b) Carcross aerodrome;

(c) Chapman Lake aerodrome;

(d) Dawson City airport;

(e) Finlayson Lake aerodrome;

(f) Hyland aerodrome;

(g) Mayo airport.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation comes into force on the later of the

following days:

(a) April 1, 2021;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of b)

regulations under the Regulations Act.
