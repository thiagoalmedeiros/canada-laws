YOIC 1985/223

*Made under the Pawnbrokers and Second Hand Dealers Act*

Pursuant to subsection 14(1) of the Pawnbrokers and

Secondhand Dealers Act , the Commissioner in Executive

Council orders as follows:

1 The following “Fee Schedule” is made and

established.

Annual Licence Fee

Pawnbrokers $10.00

Secondhand Dealers $10.00

2 Commissioner’s Order 1976/11 establishing Fees is

revoked.

3 This Order shall come into force on April 1, 1986.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 4th

day of October, A.D., 1985.
