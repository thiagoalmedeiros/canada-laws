YOIC 2006/204

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 7 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 Various roads in the community of Tagish and

surrounding area are hereby named as shown on the

annexed map entitled “Tagish and Area Street Names and

Numbering”.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 27 November 2006.
