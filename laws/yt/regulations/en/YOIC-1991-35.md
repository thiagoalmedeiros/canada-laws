YOIC 1991/35

*Made under the Education Act*

### Duties of the secretary-treasurer

1 The secretary-treasurer of each Council or School

Board shall:

(a) maintain a full and accurate record of all

proceedings and comply with all administrative

procedures specified by the Council or School

Board and by the Minister;

(b) maintain a full and accurate record of the

financial transactions of the Council or School

Board and comply with all accounting

procedures established by the Council or School

Board and by the Minister, and

(c) perform such administrative tasks as may be

directed by the Council or School Board.

### Records open to inspection

2 The administrative and accounting records referred to

in section 1 and all other books, vouchers and

documents of the Council or School Board shall be open

to inspection by the Minister or a designated officer

and, in the case of a School Board, by an auditor

appointed under section 293 of the Act.
