YOIC 2006/111

*Made under the Building Standards Act*

1 The provisions of the National Building Code of

Canada, 1995 apply to the construction by Minto

Explorations Ltd. or its successors of a mill on the

foundation in existence at the date this Regulation is

made located at 62 o 37’9” North, 137o14’20” West, lying

within Selkirk First Nation Settlement Land Parcel R-

6A, being Lot 1011, Quad 115 I/11, Plan 83638 CLSR,

2000-0112 LTO.
