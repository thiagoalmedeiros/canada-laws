YOIC 2025/222

*Made under the Government Organisation Act*

### Cabinet Committee on Legislation continued

1 The Cabinet Committee on Legislation (“CCL”)

established by Order-in-Council 2017/010 to act on

behalf of the Executive Council to provide direction on

legislative proposals and approve draft legislation is

continued.

### Cabinet Committee on Priorities and Planning disestablished

2 The Cabinet Committee on Priorities and Planning

(“CCPP”) established by Order-in-Council 2017/010 is

disestablished.

### Order-in-Council 2017/010 repealed

3 Order-in-Council 2017/010 is repealed.
