YOIC 1992/116

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

Pursuant to subsection 98(1) of the Employment

Standards Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows:

1 For the purpose of Section 98 of the Employment

Standards Act , Manitoba is hereby declared to be a

reciprocating province and the Director of Employment

Standards for Manitoba is hereby designated the

enforcement authority.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 10th

day of August, 1992.
