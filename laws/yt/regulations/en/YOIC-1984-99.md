YOIC 1984/99

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 17 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The roads at Carcross between the British Yukon

Railway right-of-way and Highway Number 2 (the Klondike

Highway) as shown by the plan attached as Schedule A and

derived from survey plan C.L.S.R. 40389 and plan C.L.S.R.

53341 are closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 27th

day of April, A.D., 1984.

______________________________

Administrator of the Yukon
