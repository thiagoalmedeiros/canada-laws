YOIC 2009/249

*Made under the Corrections Act, 2009*

## ORDER

Pursuant to section 12 of the Corrections Act, 2009 the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 (1) Subject to subsection (2), the land described as

follows and every building and other enclosure on that

land is a correctional centre: Lot 509 (Remainder),

Group 804, Land Titles Office Plan 30182, Canada Lands

Survey Records 53002, Yukon Land Registration

District.

(Section 1 replaced by O.I.C. 2020/89)

(Section 1 amended by O.I.C. 2022/171)

(2) The following areas, located on the land described

in subsection (1), are not part of the correctional

centre:

(a) the portion of the Whitehorse Correctional

Centre building known as secure living unit E;

(b) the enclosed walkway that connects the

entrance to secure living unit E with the

parking lot located southwest of the Whitehorse

Correctional Centre building;

(c) the building known as Takhini Haven; and

(Paragraph 1(2)(c) added by O.I.C. 2022/171)

(d) the area of land surrounding Takhini Haven and

shown on the map set out in the Schedule as

the shaded area bounded by a broken bold

outline.

2 This Order comes into force on the day

Corrections Act, 2009 comes into force.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 16 December 2009.
