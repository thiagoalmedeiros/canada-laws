## REGULATIONS ON FEES AND COMMIS SIONS FOR

## COLLECTING, MANAGING OR ACCOUNTING FOR PUBLIC

## MONEY

1 (1) These regulations may be referred to as the Fees

and Commissions Regulations .

2 (1) The fees and commissions set out in Appendix A

shall be paid by cheque to the person collecting,

managing or accounting for public money upon receipt

of an invoice from such person.

3 (1) The fees and commissions set out in Appendix B

shall be paid to the person collecting, managing or

accounting for the public money by deducting the

amount of the fee or commission from the public

money collected by such person.

(2) Every fee or commission paid in accordance with

subsection (1) shall constitute payment in full for the

collection, management and accounting of the public

money for which the fee or commission is paid.

## APPENDIX A

(Appendix A amended by O.I.C. 2023/92)

FEES AND COMMISSIONS TO BE PAID BY CHEQUE

1. Angling Licence Fees.

2. Hunting Licence Fees.

3. Seal Receipts.

4. Campground Permits.

## APPENDIX B

FEES AND COMMISSIONS TO BE PAID BY DEDUCTION

1. Angling Licence Fees.

2. Hunting Licence Fees.

3. Seal Receipts.

4. Campground Permits.

5. Old Crow Water and Sewer Collections.
