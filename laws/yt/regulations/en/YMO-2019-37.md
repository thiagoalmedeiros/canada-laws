# Withdrawal from Disposition of Certain Yukon Oil and Gas Lands (Daadzaii Vàn Territorial Park) Order

YMO 2019/37

*Made under the Oil and Gas Act*

## ORDER

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to withdraw certain Yukon

oil and gas lands from disposition

(a) to support the development of a park

management plan for the Daadzaii Vàn

Territorial Park; and

(b) to conserve the ecological and cultural integrity

of lands in the Daadzaii Vàn Territorial Park

area.

### Definition

2 In this Order

“ Daadzaii Vàn Territorial Park area ” means the portion

of land shown as Daadzaii Vàn Territorial Park on

Administrative Plan 108175 CLSR.

### Lands withdrawn from disposition

3 The Daadzaii Vàn Territorial Park area is withdrawn

from disposition.
