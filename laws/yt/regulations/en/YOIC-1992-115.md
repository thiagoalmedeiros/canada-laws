YOIC 1992/115

*Made under the Criminal Code (Canada)*

Pursuant to subsection 672.38(1) of the Criminal Code

(Canada), the Commissioner in Executive Council orders

as follows:

1 There is hereby established the Yukon Review

Board to make or review dispositions concerning any

accused in respect of whom a verdict of not criminally

responsible by reason of mental disorder or unfit to

stand trial is rendered.

2 (Revoked by O.I.C. 1995/25)

3 The following persons are appointed as members

of the Yukon Review Board, each for a term ending the

17th day of June, 1994:

Dr. Peter Bunton, Vancouver

Dr. Antony Marcus, Vancouver

Stuart Alcock, Vancouver

Barbara Brett, Vancouver

Dr. Olive Johnson, Bowen Island

Joan Bubbs, Kelowna

Marie Anderson, Vancouver

Susan Irwin, New Westminster

4 Commissioner’s Orders 1975/311 and 1978/66 are

revoked.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 10th

day of August, 1992.
