YOIC 1983/176

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of Section 11 of the

Financial Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive

Council is pleased to and doth hereby order as follows:

1 Fees in the amount of $1,467.00 previously paid

into the Yukon Consolidated Revenue Fund shall be

remitted to Beaver Transport Ltd.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

22nd day of August, A.D., 1983.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
