YOIC 2024/120

*Made under the Yukon Act (Canada)*

## CREEK)

### Administration and control relinquished

1 The administration and control of the public real

property described below is relinquished, in perpetuity,

to his Majesty the King in right of Canada as represented

by the Minister of Northern Affairs:

Lot 1039, Quad 115K/7, 112039 CLSR

saving and excepting therefrom and reserving thereout

all mines and minerals, including hydrocarbons, whether

solid, liquid or gaseous, and the right to work them.

### Coming into force

2 Section 1 comes into force on the day the Governor in

Council approves the relinquishment of the public real

property referred to in section 1.
