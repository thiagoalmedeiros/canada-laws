### Close Wind River Trail

2 (1) The following portion of the Wind River Trail is

closed as a highway under the Act:

The portion of the Wind River Trail that is within the

boundaries of Landscape Management Unit 8 (as

referenced in the Peel Watershed Regional Land Use

Plan) and shown for illustrative purposes as the Wind

River Trail on the map attached as the Schedule to this

Order.
