YCO 1974/19

*Made under the Health Care Insurance Plan Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Yukon Health Care

Insurance Plan Act , the Commissioner of the Yukon

Territory is pleased to and doth hereby order as

follows:

The Annual Report of the Yukon Health Care Insurance

Plan for each year ending March 31st, shall be filed in

the Commissioner’s Office no later than December 31st

of that year.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 18th

day of January, A.D., 1974.

Commissioner of the Yukon/Commissaire
