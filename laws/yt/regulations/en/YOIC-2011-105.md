YOIC 2011/105

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

### SPECIAL WARRANT (No. 1), 2011-12

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows

1 An amount of up to $45,769,000 to be paid from

the consolidated revenue fund in accordance with the

attached Schedules A and B is authorized in addition to the

net sum of $1,094,580,000 provided for in the First

Appropriation Act, 2011-12 .

2 Payments made under the special warrant shall be

made for the purpose of financing government operations

for the period July 06, 2011 to March 31, 2012 as set forth

in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 6 July 2011.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon
