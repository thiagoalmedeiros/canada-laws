## PARK STATUS REVOCATION ORDER

(2018)

### Park status revoked

1 The park status of each of Carcross Campground,

Kusawa Lake Campground, Tagish Lake Campground and

Takhini River Campground is revoked by repealing items 4,

14, 32 and 33 of the Schedule to the Act.
