## SPECIAL WARRANT (NO. 2) 2021-22

Pursuant to the Financial Administration Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders

1 An amount of up to $160,694,000 may be paid

from the consolidated revenue fund in accordance

with the attached Schedules A and B, in addition to

the net sum of $401,054,000 provided in Special

Warrant (No. 1), 2021-22 .

2 Payments may be made under this special

warrant for the purpose of financing government

operations for the period April 1, 2021 to

June 30, 2021 as set forth in Schedule A.

3 Payments may be made under this special

warrant by the Yukon Legislative Assembly for the

expenses incurred either before or after April 1, 2021

for the general election held on April 12, 2021.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, May 3, 2021.

Commissioner of Yukon
