*Made under the Highways Act*

### Means of access closed

1 The means of access between the travelled surface of

the Alaska Highway and the following lands adjacent to

that highway are closed:

(a) the lands described in Schedule 1;

(b) the land shown as Roundel Road on Plan 54580

CLSR, Plan 30131 LTO;

(c) the land presently occupied by the Yukon

Transportation Museum, including its parking

area and immediate surrounds, within Lot 2

Whitehorse Airport Subdivision, Plan 105690

CLSR, Plan 2016-0069 LTO;

(d) the land presently occupied by the Yukon

Beringia Interpretive Centre, including its

parking area and immediate surrounds, within

Lot 2 Whitehorse Airport Subdivision, Plan

105690 CLSR, Plan 2016-0069 LTO.

### Means of access — Duncan Drive

2 Any means of access between the travelled surface of

the Alaska Highway and any of the following lots is

closed if the means of access crosses the unsurveyed

portion of Duncan Drive that lies within the highway

right-of-way:

(a) Lot 223 Golden Horn Subdivision, Plan 108744 a)

CLSR, Plan 100040555 LTO;

(b) Lot 214 Golden Horn Subdivison, Plan 77167 b)

CLSR, Plan 95-31 LTO;

(c) Lot 215 Golden Horn Subdivision, Plan 77167 c)

CLSR, Plan 95-31 LTO;

(d) Lot 126 Golden Horn Subdivision, Plan 68769 d)

CLSR, Plan 68723 LTO.
