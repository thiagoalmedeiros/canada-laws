### Amount of Fees

1 (1) The prescribed fee payable for a document or

service listed in a row in Column 2 of the Schedule is set

out in Column 3 of the same row in the Schedule.

(2) The prescribed assurance fund fee is $20.00 for all

applicable transactions up to $10,000 plus $10.00 for

each $10,000, or portion thereof, of additional declared

value since the last transfer.
