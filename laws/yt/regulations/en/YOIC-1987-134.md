YOIC 1987/134

*Made under the Marriage Act*

Pursuant to subsection 58(1) of the Marriage Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The following “Schedule of Fees” is hereby made

and established.

Application for marriage licences $ 20.00

2 Order-In-Council 1983/64 establishing “Schedule

of Fees” is revoked.

3 This Order is effective August 1, 1987.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 2nd

day of July, A.D., 1987.
