YOIC 2022/116

*Made under the Workers’ Safety and Compensation Act*

### Definition

1 In this Regulation,

“ Agreement ” means the Agreement Providing for

Sharing the Cost of Compensation in Respect of the

Injury or Death of Emergency Services Workers entered

into by the Government of Canada and the Government

of Yukon on February 23, 1990; « accord »

“ emergency services worker ” means an emergency

services worker as defined in the Agreement who is

authorized under the Agreement to perform emergency

service work. « travailleur des services d’urgence »

### Designation

2 Emergency services workers are prescribed as persons

who are carrying on an undertaking that the

Government of Yukon considers to be in the public

interest for the purpose of section 82 of the Act

designating workers who are employed by the

Government of Yukon.

### Repeal 3

3 Order in Council 1990/93 is repealed. Le

### Coming into force 4

4 This Regulation comes into force on July 1, 2022. Le
