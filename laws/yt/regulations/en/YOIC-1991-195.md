YOIC 1991/195

*Made under the Canada Student Loans Act*

Pursuant to section 2 of the Canada Student Loans Act ,

the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 The Christian Life College, Stockton, California, is

designated as an eligible institution for the purpose of

the Canada Student Loans Act .

2 Aerokon Aviation Flight Training School of

Whitehorse, Yukon is designated as an eligible

institution for the purpose of the Canada Student Loans

Act .

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 16th

day of October, 1991.
