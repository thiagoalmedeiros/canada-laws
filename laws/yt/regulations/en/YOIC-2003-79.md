YOIC 2003/79

*Made under the Languages Act*

1 The following offices of the Government of Yukon

are prescribed as offices whose nature is such that it is

reasonable that communications with and services

from that office be in English or French

(a) Department of Energy, Mines and Resources,

(i) Client Services and Inspection Branch;

(ii) Forestry Branch;

(iii) Lands Branch;

(iv) Mining Lands Unit;

(v) Yukon Geological Survey;

(b) Department of Environment, Water Resources

Branch;

(c) Yukon Water Board.
