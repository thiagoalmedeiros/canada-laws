YOIC 1998/204

*Made under the Yukon Development Corporation Act*

Pursuant to section 4(d) of the Yukon Development

Corporation Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows:

1 Section 2 of Order-in-Council 1997/91 is hereby

revoked.

2 The Energy Conservation Fund is to be used by the

Corporation for funding electrical power conservation

initiatives in accordance with guidelines established by the

Corporation for:

(a) residential non-government customers of the

Yukon Energy Corporation and The Yukon

Electrical Company Limited consuming in excess

of 1000 kWh/month throughout at least six

months of the year; and

(b) general service non-government and

municipal customers of the Yukon Energy

Corporation and The Yukon Electrical Company

Limited consuming in excess of 2000

kWh/month throughout at least six months of

the year.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

17th day of November, 1998.
