# Hearing Adjudicators Remuneration Regulation

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation

“ full day ” means a period or periods that total more

than three and a half hours in one day; « journée

complète »

“ half day ” means a period or periods that total more

than one hour and a half, but not more than three and a

half hours, in one day. « demi-journée »

### Hearings

2 (1) For presiding over a hearing, a hearing adjudicator

is to be paid

(a) $450 for each day on which the hearing

adjudicator presides over the hearing for a full

day;

(b) $250 for each day on which the hearing

adjudicator presides over the hearing for a half

day; and

(c) $125 for each day on which the hearing

adjudicator presides over the hearing for less

than a half day.

(2) If a hearing adjudicator is required to be and is

available to preside over a hearing on a specific date and

does not preside over a hearing on that date, the hearing

adjudicator is to be paid $125.

### Meetings

3 (1) Subject to subsection (2), for attending a meeting, a

hearing adjudicator is to be paid

(a) $250 for each day on which the hearing

adjudicator attends the meeting for at least a

half day; and

(b) $125 for each day on which the hearing

adjudicator attends the meeting for less than a

half day.

(2) A hearing adjudicator is not entitled to

remuneration for attending a meeting without the prior

approval of the assistant deputy minister.

### Training

4 (1) In this section

“ training event ” , in respect of a hearing adjudicator,

means a conference, workshop, seminar or training

program that the hearing adjudicator attends in

connection with the performance of their duties under

the Act. « activité de formation »

(2) Subject to subsection (3), for attending a training

event, a hearing adjudicator is to be paid

(a) $225 for each day on which the hearing

adjudicator attends the training event for a full

day;

(b) $112.50 for each day on which the hearing

adjudicator attends the training event for a half

day; and

(c) $61.25 for each day on which the hearing

adjudicator attends the training event for less

than a half day.

(3) A hearing adjudicator is not entitled to

remuneration for attending a training event without the

prior approval of the assistant deputy minister.
