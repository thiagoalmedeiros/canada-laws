YOIC 2012/71

*Made under the Territorial Court Act*

## ORDER

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to implement certain

recommendations of a Judicial Compensation

Commission regarding remuneration of justices of the

peace.

### Interpretation

2 In this Order

“ designated paid holiday ” means a designated paid

holiday that a member of the public service appointed

to a position in the management group is entitled to

under the Management Plan; « congé férié »

“ Management Plan ” means the Government of Yukon’s

conditions of employment for managers; « plan de

gestion »

[“Management Plan” replace d by O.I.C. 2014/152]

“ salaried presiding justice of the peace ” means the

justice of the peace appointed on a full-time basis and

paid a salary to perform presiding duties as assigned

by the chief judge; and « juge de paix exerçant les

fonctions de président »

“ supervising judge ” means the supervising judge

appointed pursuant to subsection 61(1) of the

Territorial Court Act . « juge surveillant »

### Hourly rate

3 (1) Except as provided in subsection (2), a justice,

other than the salaried presiding justice of the peace, is

to be paid an hourly rate for time spent holding a

hearing, according to their level of authority designated

by the chief judge, as follows:

(a) as of April 1, 2022,

(i) JP 1 — $49.33 per hour,

(ii) JP 2 — $54.81 per hour, and

(iii) JP 3 — $76.74 per hour;

(b) as of April 1, 2023,

(i) JP 1 — $52.68 per hour,

(ii) JP 2 — $58.54 per hour, and

(iii) JP 3 — $81.96 per hour;

(c) as of April 1, 2024,

(i) JP 1 — $55.26 per hour,

(ii) JP 2 — $61.41 per hour, and

(iii) JP 3 — $85.98 per hour.

(2) If a hearing under subsection (1) is held on a

designated paid holiday, a justice shall be paid 150% of

the hourly rate that would have been payable under

subsection (1).

(3) As of April 1, 2022, a justice at the JP 3 level who is

assigned to hear bail hearings on a weekend or a

designated paid holiday is to be paid as follows:

(a) for each day that the justice is on standby and

does not hold a bail hearing, the amount

payable for four hours of work at the hourly

rate under subsection (1);

(b) for each day on a weekend that the justice is on

standby and holds a bail hearing, the amount

that is the greater of

(i) the amount that would be payable under

paragraph (a) if the justice did not hold a

hearing, and

(ii) the amount payable for the time the justice

spends holding a hearing at the hourly rate

under subsection (1); and

(c) for each designated paid holiday that the

justice is on standby and holds a bail hearing,

the amount that is the greater of

(i) the amount that would be payable under

paragraph (a) if the justice did not hold a

hearing, and

(ii) the amount payable for the time the justice

spends holding a hearing at the hourly rate

under subsection (2).

### Training

4 (1) As of April 1, 2022, a justice, other than the

salaried presiding justice of the peace, is to be paid

(a) for attendance at a training college approved

by the supervising judge

(i) $150 per day for each full day of attendance,

or

(ii) $75 per half day for each half day of

attendance; and

(b) for attendance at a training session, other than

a training college, approved by the supervising

judge, the hourly rate payable to a JP 1 under

subsection 3(1) for each hour of attendance.

(2) Attendance by a justice at a training college or a

training session is subject to the approval of the

supervising judge.

### Exception

5 A justice who is a member of the public service of the

Yukon Government is not eligible to receive

remuneration under sections 3 or 4.
