*Made under the Liquor Act*

Pursuant to subsection 105.1(2) of the Liquor Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council Office orders as

follows:

1 The annexed Old Crow Liquor Prohibition

Regulation is hereby established.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 28th

day of November, 1991.

___________________________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## OLD CROW LIQUOR PROHIBITION

## REGULATIONS

### Definitions

1 In this Act “band community” is called Old Crow

and means the area within 100 kilometres (62 miles) of the

Old Crow Community Centre. “ territoire de la bande Old

Crow ”

### Prohibitions

2 No person shall

(a) possess liquor anywhere in Old Crow,

(b) consume liquor anywhere in Old Crow,

(c) sell liquor anywhere in Old Crow,

(d) buy liquor anywhere in Old Crow, or

(e) transport liquor anywhere in Old Crow.
