YOIC 2007/209

*Made under the Jury Act*

1 For each day while necessarily engaged in serving

as a juror: $80.00

2 For the purpose only of expenses incurred in the

course of their service to the Court, persons serving as

jurors shall receive expenses in respect of meals, travel

and accommodation as follows

(a) for meals, up to $25.00 per day if the juror

resides 50 kilometres or more from the

courthouse where the trial is being conducted;

(b) for travel, at a rate of 10 cents per kilometre if

the juror resides 50 kilometres or more from

the courthouse where the trial is being

conducted;

(c) for accommodation, up to $110.00 per day if

the juror resides 100 kilometres or more from

the courthouse where the trial is being

conducted.

3 In exceptional cases, where no provision is made, or

where the amount allowed, on account of extraordinary

circumstances is deemed inadequate by the Judge, the

Judge may allow such sums as he or she considers just

and reasonable.
