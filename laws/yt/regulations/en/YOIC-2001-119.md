YOIC 2001/119

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(a) of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The lands described in the schedule are withdrawn

from disposal for the period terminating December 31,

2002 or on the Effective date of the Ta’an Kwach’an First

Nation Final Agreement, whichever comes earlier,

excluding sand, gravel and timber that may be disposed of

pursuant to section 30 of the Lands Act . The withdrawal

from disposal of the lands described in the schedule is

without prejudice to the Minister’s authority pursuant to

paragraph 3(1)(b) of the Lands Act to grant any easement

over the lands described in the schedule in favour of the

Yukon Electric Company in respect of any utility lines

existing at the time of this Order.

2 Order-in-Council 2000/220 is revoked.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 30th

day of July, 2001.

_________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
