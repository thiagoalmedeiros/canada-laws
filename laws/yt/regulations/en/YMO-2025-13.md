*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

## ORDER

### Payment of fees

1 Except to the extent that another order made under the

Waste Disposal Sites Regulation provides otherwise, the

charges set out in the Schedule are required to be paid

for the use of the waste disposal sites and for disposing

of the types of waste described in the Schedule at those

sites, in accordance with the Schedule.

### Receipt

2 A receipt must be issued for every payment made to use

a waste disposal site.

### Waste Disposal Sites Charges Order repealed

3 The Waste Disposal Sites Charges Order (M.O. 2020/49)

is repealed.

### Coming into force

4 This Order comes into force on the later of the following

days:

(a) May 1, 2025;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of

regulations under the Regulations Act .
