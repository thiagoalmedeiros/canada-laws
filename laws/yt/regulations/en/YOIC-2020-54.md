## ORDER PROHIBITING ENTRY ON CERTAIN

## LANDS IN YUKON (PEEL WATERSHED

## REGIONAL LAND USE PLAN)

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to prohibit entry on

specified Landscape Management Units in the Peel

Watershed Planning Region as required to implement the

Peel Watershed Regional Land Use Plan.

### Interpretation

2 (1) In this Order,

“Peel Watershed Regional Land Use Plan” means the

regional land use plan developed and recommended

by the Peel Watershed Planning Commission for the

Peel Watershed Planning Region and approved in

August 2019 by Tr’ondëk Hwëch’in, First Nation of

Na-cho Nyäk Dun, Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation,

Gwich’in Tribal Council and Government of Yukon;

« Plan régional d’aménagement du bassin hydrographique

de la rivière Peel »

“recorded claim” means

(a) a recorded placer claim that is in good

standing, acquired under the Yukon Placer Mining

Act (Canada) and continued under the Placer

Mining Act , or acquired under the Placer Mining

Act , or

(b) a recorded mineral claim that is in good

standing, acquired under the Yukon Quartz Mining

Act (Canada) and continued under the Quartz

Mining Act , or acquired under the Quartz Mining

Act . « claim inscrit »

(2) A reference in this Order to a numbered LMU

(Landscape Management Unit) is a reference to the LMU

with that number under the Peel Watershed Regional Land

Use Plan as more particularly shown and described in the

Schedule.

### Prohibition of entry — interim

3 Subject to section 5, a person must not, at any time

before January 1, 2030, enter onto LMU 1, LMU 4, LMU 6,

LMU 10, LMU 15 or LMU 16 (each of which is designated

as a Wilderness Area or a Wilderness Area-Boreal Caribou

under the Peel Watershed Regional Land Use Plan), for the

purpose of

(Section 3 amended by O.I.C. 2020/111)

(a) locating a claim, or prospecting for gold or

other precious minerals or stones, under the

Placer Mining Act ; or

(b) locating a claim, or prospecting or mining for

minerals, under the Quartz Mining Act .

### Prohibition of entry — indeterminate

4 Subject to section 5, a person must not enter on

LMU 2, LMU 8, LMU 9, LMU 11, LMU 12 or LMU 14 (each

of which is designated as a Special Management Area under

the Peel Watershed Regional Land Use Plan), for the

purpose of

(Section 4 amended by O.I.C. 2020/111)

(a) locating a claim, or prospecting for gold or

other precious minerals or stones, under the

Placer Mining Act ; or

(b) locating a claim, or prospecting or mining for

minerals, under the Quartz Mining Act .

### Existing rights and interests

5 Section 3 does not apply to entry on a recorded

claim by the owner or holder of the recorded claim.
