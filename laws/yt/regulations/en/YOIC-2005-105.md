YOIC 2005/105

*Made under the Insurance Act*

### Issuance of licence

1 The superintendent may issue a licence to a municipal

exchange if the superintendent is satisfied that the

municipal exchange is able to meet its contractual

obligations pertaining to the exchange, and on

receiving

(a) an application in a form approved by the

superintendent,

(b) the prescribed fee, and

(c) an undertaking to comply with the Act.

### Documents required

2 The superintendent may require the municipal

exchange to provide information, documents,

verifications or forecasts of business operations

considered necessary for the evaluation of an

application.

### Insurance particulars

3 The municipal exchange, or its members, whenever

required to do so by the superintendent, must provide

information about contracts of insurance or

reinsurance, terms of policies, premiums and other

details of any insurance or reinsurance contracts

entered into by the municipal exchange or by its

members. The municipal exchange, or its members,

must also provide any other information the

superintendent requires in respect of the operation of

the municipal exchange or the declaration of members

of the municipal exchange.

### Licence issued for one year

4 Any licence issued to a municipal exchange pursuant to

this Part expires on June 30 in each year, subject to

renewal by the superintendent before that date.

### Renewal and terms

5 (1) Any licence issued or renewed to a municipal

exchange may be issued or renewed subject to any

limitations or conditions the superintendent considers

appropriate.

(2) Where the superintendent considers it advisable,

the superintendent may at any time add conditions to a

licence or remove conditions from a licence.

### Financial Statements

6 A municipal exchange must file with the superintendent

no later than March 31 in each year

(a) an audited financial statement for the exchange

during the year ending the preceding

December 31, and

(b) any other information in respect of the

municipal exchange which the superintendent

requires.

### Fees

7 The following fees are established as the fees to be

paid for a municipal exchange licence or a renewal of a

municipal exchange licence

(a) examination of documents for licence

$ 2,000.00

(b) initial licence $ 500.00

(c) renewal of licence $ 500.00
