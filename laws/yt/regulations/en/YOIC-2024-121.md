YOIC 2024/121

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

### Exemptions

1 The Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources is exempt

from section 40 of the Financial Administration Act in

respect of any financing agreement made between the

Government of Yukon and PricewaterhouseCoopers Inc.

whereby the Government of Yukon agrees to provide

loans to PricewaterhouseCoopers Inc. as receiver to

Victoria Gold Corp.

### Rate of interest

2 Interest is to be calculated as specified in a financing

agreement referred to in section 1.
