YMO 2024/14

*Made under the Animal Protection and Control Act*

### Manner of service

1 For the purpose of paragraph 7(4)(b) of the Act, an

animal protection and control officer may serve an order

(a) by posting a copy of it in a conspicuous place at

the last known home address of each person to

whom the order is directed; or

(b) by fax or email, to the last known fax number or

email address of each person to whom the order

is directed, if the sender receives confirmation

that the transmission was completed.
