YOIC 1980/204

*Made under the Social Assistance Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Social Assistance Act ,

the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory is pleased to

and doth hereby order as follows:

1 These regulations may be cited as the Seniors

Placement Committee Regulations.

2 All applications for admissions to the Seniors

Facilities including Senior Citizen’s Lodges shall be by

application to the Department of Health and Human

Resources.

3 Upon receipt of an application for admission, the

Department of Health and Human Resources shall

conduct such investigations and prepare such reports

as may be necessary.

4 All applications and necessary reports and

documentations shall be presented to a committee

known as the Seniors Placement Committee.

5 The Seniors Placement Committee shall consist of:

(1) A Chairman as may be designated by the Director

of Health and Human Resources;

(2) The Chief Medical Health Officer of the Yukon

Territory;

(3) The Social Services Supervisor;

(4) The Nurse Supervisor, Whitehorse, Norman D.

Macaulay Lodge;

(5) The Home Supervisor, Dawson, Alexander

McDonald Lodge;

(6) For applications for admission to McDonald Lodge

in Dawson only, a resident of Dawson to be designated

by the Director of Health and Human Resources.

6 The Social Worker for Seniors shall serve as

Secretary to the Committee, prepare the agenda, and

set the dates for Committee meetings.

7 In respect of applications for admission from

Whitehorse or Dawson City respectively, the Nurse or

Home Supervisor respectively for that community shall

be a member of the quorum of the Committee.

8 Subject to Section 7, the quorum shall consist of

three members of the Committee.

9 The findings of the Seniors Placement Committee

are subject to appeal to the Social Assistance Appeal

Committee.
