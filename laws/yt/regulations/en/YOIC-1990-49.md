Administrator of the Yukon

### Rural Electrification and Telephone Local

### Improvement Tax Regulations, 1989

1 These regulations may be cited as the Rural

Electrification and Telephone Local Improvement Tax

Regulations, 1989.

2 In these regulations, “local improvement” means

the extension of an electrical power line or a telephone

service to serve the properties in the regions listed in

Schedule 1.

3 The regions listed in Schedule 1 are declared to

benefit from the local improvement.

4 To recover the cost of the local improvement there

is levied on each property in each region listed in Schedule

1 a local improvement tax consisting of the principal

amount and the interest specified for that property in

Schedule 1.

The local improvement tax

(a) shall be shown as a separate item on the tax

notice for the property;

(b) is due and payable in the annual installments

shown on Schedule 1 at the same time as the

general tax and school tax;

(c) is part of the total tax payable for the

property; and

(d) may be paid in advance by paying the

outstanding balance of principal together with

interest accrued at the time of payment.
