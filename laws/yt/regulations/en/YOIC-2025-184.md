## ORDER

### Remuneration

1 A commissioner is to be paid at the following hourly rate

for performing their duties as a commissioner:

(a) as of April 1, 2025, $200;

(b) as of April 1, 2026, the hourly rate calculated in

accordance with section 2.

### Calculation of rate

2 (1) In this section

“ annual change ” for a calendar year means the

difference between the consumer price index for that

year and the consumer price index for the previous

calendar year, expressed as a percentage; « variation

annuelle »

“ consumer price index ” for a calendar year means the

annual average All-Items Consumer Price Index for

Whitehorse for the year, as published by Statistics

Canada. « indice des prix à la consommation »

(2) The hourly rate referred to in paragraph 1(b) is

calculated annually on April 1 st , beginning in 2026, by

multiplying the previous year’s hourly rate by the

previous year’s annual change and adding th e product

to the pr evious year’s hourly rate.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2), any annual

change that is less than zero is deemed to be zero.
