YOIC 2000/91

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

### SPECIAL WARRANT (No.2)

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this special warrant

1 To authorize an amount of up to $52,647,000 to be

paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in accordance

with the attached Schedules A and B, in addition to the net

sum of $212,370,000 provided for in the Special Warrant

Order-in-Council 2000/78.

2 To stipulate that payments made under the Special

Warrant be made for the purpose of financing government

operations for the period April 1, 2000 to June 30, 2000 as

set forth in Schedules A and B to this order.

3 To stipulate that payments be made under it by the

Yukon Legislative Assembly for the expenses incurred

either before or after April 1, 2000 for the general election

held on April 17, 2000.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 18th

day of May, 2000.

____________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
