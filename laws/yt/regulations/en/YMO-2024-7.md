# Order to extend renewal terms of Chance Oil and Gas Limited permits (2024)

YMO 2024/7

*Made under the Oil and Gas Act*

### Oil and gas permits No. 0012 to No. 0017

1 For oil and gas permits No. 0012, No. 0013, No. 0014,

No. 0015, No. 0016 and No. 0017 held by Chance Oil and

Gas Limited

(a) each renewal term is extended by 30 months so

that the renewal term now ends on

August 30, 2026; and

(b) the resulting total length of the initial term and

the renewal term is 19 years.

### Oil and gas permit No. 0019

2 For oil and gas permit No. 0019 held by Chance Oil and

Gas Limited

(a) the renewal term is extended by 30 months so

that the renewal term now ends on

August 30, 2026; and

(b) the resulting total length of the initial term and

the renewal term is 17 years.

### Oil and gas permit No. 0020

3 For oil and gas permit No. 0020 held by Chance Oil and

Gas Limited

(a) the renewal term is extended by 30 months so

that the renewal term now ends on

August 30, 2026; and

(b) the resulting total length of the initial term and

the renewal term is 16 years.

### Annual reports

4 (1) In this section

“ first nations communication and engagement

plan ” means the plan filed under O.I.C. 2016/169 and

revised in accordance with M.O. 2017/37. « plan de

participation et de communication avec les premières

nations »

(2) It is a condition of this order that Chance Oil and Gas

Limited must file annual reports with the Division Head,

in accordance with subsection (3) and to the satisfaction

of the Division Head, about the implementation of the

first nations communication and engagement plan.

(3) For the purpose of subsection (2), Chance Oil and

Gas Limited must file

(a) the first annual report on or before July 31, 2024

in respect of the period beginning on

June 1, 2023 and ending on May 31, 2024; and

(b) each subsequent annual report on or before

July 31 of each year in respect of the period

beginning on June 1 of the preceding year and

ending on May 31 of the year in which the report

is to be filed.
