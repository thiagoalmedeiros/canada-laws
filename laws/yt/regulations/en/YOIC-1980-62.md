## WORKERS' COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of the Workers’

Compensation Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

is pleased to and doth hereby order as follows:

1 The attached schedule is hereby made and

established for the year 1980.

2 The schedule established by Commissioner’s Order

1979/25 shall remain in force in respect of payments for

the year 1979.

3 This order shall come into force on the day of

signature but have full effect from the first day of January,

1980 and shall govern all payments made in respect of the

year 1980.

Given under my hand, at Whitehorse, in the Yukon

Territory, this 15th day of February, A.D., 1980.

______________________________

Administrator of Yukon
