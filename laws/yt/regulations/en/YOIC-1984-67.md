YOIC 1984/67

*Made under the Municipal Act*

Pursuant to Sections 156 and 190 the Municipal Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council is pleased to and

does hereby order as follows

1 The following forms set out in Schedule A are hereby

made and established

Schedule A – Oath of Office and Allegiance

2 This Order is effective from April 1, 1984.

3 This Order-in-Council may be cited as the Oaths of

Office and Allegiance Regulation.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

March 30, 1984

Commissioner of

## SCHEDULE A

OATH OF OFFICE AND ALLEGIANCE

Oath of Office

___________________________________________________ ,

([Mayor-elect]/[Councillor-elect])

for the municipality of ______________________________ do

[swear]/[affirm]

(1) I am a Canadian citizen;

(2) I am not in any way disqualified from holding the

office of___________________________________________;

([Mayor]/[Councillor])

(3) I have not, nor will I have, any interest, directly or

indirectly, in any contract, services, or conflict connected

with the municipality of ______________________________,

except such as I may lawfully have under the provisions

of the Municipal Act ;

(4) I have not, by myself or any other person, knowingly

employed any bribery, corruption or intimidation to gain

my election;

(5) I will not, without due authority, disclose or make

known any matter that comes to my knowledge by

reason of my office; and

(6) I will faithfully perform the duties of my office, and

will not allow any private interest to influence my

conduct in my role of

___________________________________________;

([Mayor]/[Councillor]).

[So help me God.]/[I so affirm.]

Oath of Allegiance

I, _________________________________, do [swear]/[affirm]

that I will faithfully respect and bear true allegiance

[to the Constitution, the laws of Canada and the laws of

Yukon.]/

[to his Majesty King Charles the Third, his heirs and

successors according to law.]

[So help me God.]/[I so affirm.]
