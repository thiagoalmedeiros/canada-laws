YOIC 1980/186

*Made under the Canada Student Loans Act*

The Commissioner in Executive Council is pleased to

order that such universities and other institutions of

learning as may be designated as “specified

educational institutions” by any Province of Canada for

the purpose of Canada Student Loans, be similarly

designated by the Yukon Territory.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 17th

day of July, A.D., 1980.
