YOIC 2013/112

*Made under the Yukon Act (Canada)*

Pursuant to section 46 of the Yukon Act (Canada), the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The administration and control of the public real

property identified as Lot 1036, Quad 115K/7, Plan 2012-

0086 LTO, 100439 CLSR are relinquished.

2 Section 1 applies on and after the day on which the

Governor in Council approves the relinquishment referred

to in that section.

3 Order-in-Council 2004/195 is revoked.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this May 30, 2013.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon
