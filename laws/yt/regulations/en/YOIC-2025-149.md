YOIC 2025/149

*Made under the Government Organisation Act*

### Assignment of administration

1 This Order assigns responsibility for the administration

of the Clean Energy Act .

### Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources and Minister of Environment

2 Except as otherwise provided by section 3, the Minister

of Energy, Mines and Resources and the Minister of

Environment are assigned common responsibility for the

administration of the Clean Energy Act .

### Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources

3 The Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources is assigned

responsibility for the administration of the following

provisions of the Clean Energy Act :

(a) section 5.01;

(b) section 7;

(c) section 8;

(d) paragraphs 8.01(1)(d) and (e);

(e) subsection 8.01(2) to the extent that a program

or measure relates to the mining sector or a

subsector or operation in the mining sector;

(f) paragraphs 9(1)(b) and (g);

(g) paragraphs 10(c), (c.01), (c.02) and (d) and g)

subparagraph 10(f)(i);

(h) subparagraph 10(f)(ii) to the extent that it h)

applies to subsection 5.01(3);

(i) subparagraph 10(f)(iii) to the extent that it i)

applies to operations in the mining sector.
