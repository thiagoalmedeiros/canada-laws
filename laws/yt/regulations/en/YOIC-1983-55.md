YOIC 1983/55

*Made under the Liquor Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS the Commissioner in Executive Council has

received a resolution of the board of trustees of the Local

Improvement District of Carmacks requesting that Section

103 of the Liquor Act be put into operation in respect of the

Local Improvement District of Carmacks,

NOW THEREFORE, the Commissioner in Executive

Council is pleased to and doth hereby order:

(1) Section 103 of the Liquor Act shall come into force

in the Local Improvement District of Carmacks on April 15,

1983.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 25 day of

March, A.D., 1983.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon
