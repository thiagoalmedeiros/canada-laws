YMO 1996/5

*Made under the Education Act*

Pursuant to section 71 of the Education Act , the

Minister of Education orders as follows:

1 The School Boards described in Schedule A of this

Order are established as a corporation.

2 Ministerial Order 1991/001 is amended by deleting

the expression “Ecole Emilie-Tremblay School Council of

Attendance Area # 23” from Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 5th

day of July, 1996.

Minister of Education/Ministre

## SCHEDULE A

Yukon Francophone School Board, Education Area
