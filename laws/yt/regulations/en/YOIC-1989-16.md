YOIC 1989/16

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

Pursuant to subsection 98(1) of the Employment

Standards Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby orders that:

1 For the purpose of section 98 of the Employment

Standards Act , the Northwest Territories is hereby

declared to be a reciprocating province and the Labour

Standards Board of the Northwest Territories is hereby

designated the enforcement authority.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 31st

day of January, A.D., 1989.
