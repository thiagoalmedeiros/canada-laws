YOIC 2024/34

*Made under the Motor Vehicles Act*

### Alcohol ignition interlock devices prescribed

1 The following alcohol ignition interlock devices are

prescribed for the purpose of the definition “ alcohol

ignition interlock device ” in subsection 1(1) of the Act:

(a) Guardian WR2;

(b) Alcolock WR2;

(c) Alcolock LR;

(d) Smart Start SSI-20/35;

(e) Smart Start SSI-FLEX;

(f) any other device that is

(i) manufactured in accordance with CSA Group

Standard Z627-16, Breath alcohol ignition

interlock devices , and

(ii) approved by the registrar.

### Alcohol Ignition Interlock Device Regulation repealed

2 The Alcohol Ignition Interlock Device Regulation (Order-

in-Council 2015/191) is repealed.
