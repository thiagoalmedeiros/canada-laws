*Made under the Legislative Assembly Act*

## FORM

Pursuant to the provisions of Section 40.7.1 of the

Legislative Assembly Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council is pleased to and doth hereby order

as follows:

1 The annexed Form is made and established.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 14th

day of September, A.D., 1984.

Administrator of Yukon/

## FORM

EXPENSE CLAIM FOR MEMBERS OF THE

LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY WHO RENT OR

LEASE ACCOMMODATION

(Section 40.7.1)

To: Clerk of the Legislative Assembly

I hereby certify that the amount entered below has

been paid by me for the rental or leasing of

accommodation in the City of Whitehorse:

Period Rent Covers:_______________________, 19_______,

to ____________________, 19_______.

Amount of Rent or Lease Cost:

Other Costs: (please specify)

* TOTAL AMOUNT CLAIMED

_______________________ ______________________________

Date Signature of Member of the

Legislative Assembly

*Note : Section 40.7.1 of the Legislative Assembly Act

provides that the maximum amount which may be

claimed for the rental or leasing of accommodation is

$400 per month.
