YOIC 1995/89

*Made under the Liquor Act*

## ROSS RIVER AREA ENFORCEMENT ORDER

### Preamble

Whereas the Commissioner in Executive Council is

satisfied that the making of this area enforcement order has

been approved by the majority of votes cast in a plebiscite

by adults who reside in the unincorporated community of

Ross River, the Commissioner in Executive Council orders

as follows:

1 In this Order,

“unincorporated community of Ross River” means

(a) all of the area within 10 kilometres of the Ross

River Dena Council Band Office; and

(b) all of the area within one kilometre of Coffee

Lake. «agglomération de Ross River non

constituée en municipalité»

2 Effective June 1/95, subsection 105(1) of the Liquor

Act , which prohibits the consumption of liquor in public

places for which an area enforcement order has been made,

applies to all public places in the unincorporated

community of Ross River.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 29th

day of May, 1995.

Commissioner of the Yukon
