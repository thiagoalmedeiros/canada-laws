YOIC 1991/142

*Made under the Canada Student Loans Act*

Pursuant to section 2 of the Canada Student Loans Act ,

the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows:

1 The University of Nations, Kona Coast Campus,

Hawaii, is designated a eligible institution for the

purpose of the Canada Student Loans Act .

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 23

day of July, 1991.
