YOIC 1983/265

*Made under the Territorial Court Act*

Pursuant to the subsections 11(1) and 8.3(1) of the

Territorial Court Act , the Commissioner in Executive

Council is pleased to and does hereby order as follows:

The annexed Regulations respecting the Territorial

Court are made and established to have effect on and from

December 23, 1983.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 23rd

day of December, A.D., 1983.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## REGULATIONS RESPECTING

1 (1) The seal of the court shall be composed of the

armorial bearings of the Yukon Territory encircled by the

following legend or inscription: “The Seal of the Territorial

Court of Yukon”. (pursuant to 11(1) of the Act)

2 (1) The remuneration payable to members of the

judicial council appointed under paragraphs 8.1(1)(c), (d)

or (e) of the Act is $100 per day (pursuant to 8.3(1) of the

Act).
