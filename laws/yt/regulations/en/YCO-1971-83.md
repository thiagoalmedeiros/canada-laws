YCO 1971/83

*Made under the Miners Lien Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Miners’ Lien Act , the

Commissioner of the Yukon Territory is pleased to and

doth hereby order as follows:

The fees for registering any instrument under the

Miners’ Lien Act is $2.50 for the first claim and $1.00

for each additional claim.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 23rd

day of February, A.D., 1971.

Commissioner of the Yukon/Commissaire
