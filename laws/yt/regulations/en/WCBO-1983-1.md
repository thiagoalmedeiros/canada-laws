## WORKERS' COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of Section 79 of the

Workers’ Compensation Act , the Board is pleased to and doth

hereby order as follows:

1 The annexed schedule is hereby established for the

year 1983.

2 This shall be deemed to be in force January 1, 1983.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 1st

day of February, A.D., 1983.

______________________________

Chairperson

## WORKERS' COMPENSATION ACT

## SCHEDULE

WHEREASSection 79 of the Workers’ Compensation Act

requires the Board to proclaim the amount of

compensation to be paid in the year 1983 in proportion to

the percentage change in the level of the average annual

Consumer Price Index established each year.

The amounts of compensation to be paid during 1983

in respect of accidents occurring before January 1, 1983

shall be as set out below.
