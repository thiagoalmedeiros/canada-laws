## JUSTICE OF THE PEACE COURT ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of subsection 24(1) of the

Justice of the Peace Court Act , the Commissioner in Executive

Council is pleased to and doth hereby order, as follows:

1 The Forms-In-Use established under Order-In-

Council 1981/184 are hereby revoked.

2 The attached form be made and established.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 18th

day of June, A.D., 1982.
