YOIC 2024/171

*Made under the Education Labour Relations Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation

“ conciliation board ” means a conciliation board

established under section 52 of the Act; « bureau de

conciliation »

“ National Joint Council ” means the National Joint

Council whose establishment was authorized by Order in

Council P.C. 3676 (Canada), dated May 16, 1944.

« Conseil national mixte »

### Remuneration

2 (1) The chair of a conciliation board shall be paid $600

per hour for performing their duties as the chair of the

conciliation board.

(2) Each member of a conciliation board other than the

chair, shall be paid $400 per hour for performing their

duties as a member of the conciliation board.

### Expenses

3 The chair and other members of a conciliation board

shall be reimbursed for travel and living expenses that

they incur in connection with the performance of their

duties away from their ordinary place of residence in

accordance with the Travel Directive of the National Joint

Council that is in force at the time that the work is

performed.
