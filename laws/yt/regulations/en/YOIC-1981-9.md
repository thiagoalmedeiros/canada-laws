## 2016

Pursuant to the provision of Section 2.(1)c of the

Students Financial Assistance Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council is pleased to and doth hereby order

as follows:

1 College of the Canyons

Santa Clarita Community College District

26455 N. Rockwell Canyon Road

VALENCIA, CALIFORNIA

91355

be prescribed as an approved institution for the

purpose of approving post-secondary grants and

bursaries.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 2nd

day of January, A.D., 1981.
