YOIC 2007/106

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The lots in the Whitehorse Copper Subdivision

listed in Schedule A attached hereto are classed as Country

Residential (CR) and shall be sold at the prices listed in

Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 25 June 2007.
