## PROPERTY TAX EXEMPTIONS

## 2022

Pursuant to the Assessment and Taxation Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders

1 Each society and organization listed in the attached

Schedule is exempt from liability to pay taxes levied

under the Assessment and Taxation Act for the 2022

taxation year in the amounts set out in the Schedule on

the properties listed after the name of the society or

organization.
