YMO 1991/1

*Made under the Education Act*

Pursuant to section 71 of the Education Act , the

Minister of Education orders as follows:

1 The School Councils described in Schedule A of this

Order are established as a corporation.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 30th

day of January, 1991.

Minister of Education/
