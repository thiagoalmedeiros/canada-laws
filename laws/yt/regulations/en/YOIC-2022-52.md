YOIC 2022/52

*Made under the Yukon Government Carbon Price Rebate Act*

(2022)

### Carbon Price Rebate General Regulation amended This Regulation amends the Carbon Price Rebate General Regulation .

1

### Section 1 amended In section 1

2 (a) the expression “ 51.0% ” is replaced with the

expression “ 49.5% ” ; and

(b) the expression “ 1.0% ” is replaced with the

expression “ 2.5% ”.

### Section 5 amended

3 In paragraph 5(c), the expression “ 54 and 55 ” is

replaced with the expression “ 54, 55 and 56 ”.

### Application (1) Section 2 applies after March 31, 2022. (2) Section 3 applies to taxation years that end after 2021.
