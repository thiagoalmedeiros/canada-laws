### Interpretation

1 In this Regulation

“ Act ” « Loi »

[ “Act” repealed by O.I.C. 2020/05)

“ Class 1 project ” means a Class 1 placer land use

operation or a Class 1 exploration program; « projet de

type 1 »

“ designated Class 1 project ” means a designated

Class 1 placer land use operation or a designated Class 1

exploration program; « projet désigné de type 1 »

“ prescribed area ” « zone désignée »

[ “prescribed area” repealed by O.I.C. 2020/05]

“ Schedule ” means the Schedule to this Regulation,

including each map attached to it; « annexe »

[“Schedule” add ed by O.I.C. 2018/46]

“ settlement land ” means land that is Settlement Land

under a land claim final agreement that is approved and

has the force of law under An Act Approving Yukon Land

Claim Final Agreements « terre visée par un

règlement ».

### Prescribed areas

2 (1) For the purpose of subparagraph 116(c.01)(i) of the

Placer Mining Act and subparagraph 149(c.01)(i) of the

Quartz Mining Act , each of the following is prescribed as

an area within which a Class 1 project is a designated

Class 1 project:

(a) each area described in the Schedule;

(b) the remaining area located within Yukon that is

not located within an area referred to in

paragraph (a).

(2) A copy of each map that is included in the Schedule

has been deposited with the Manager, Lands Client

Services, Department of Energy, Mines and Resources at

Whitehorse and with the Mining Recorder at Dawson,

Mayo, Watson Lake and Whitehorse.

(3) The relevant coordinates in respect of each map that

is included in the Schedule may be viewed using the

online tool “Lands Viewer” provided by the Department

of Energy, Mines and Resources.

## 3.

## SCHEDULE

Ross River area

1. In Yukon, the tract of land referred to as the “Ross

River Class 1 Notification Area” shown shaded and

outlined in bold on the attached map with that title,

including for greater certainty any parcel or portion of a

parcel of settlement land that lies within the Ross River

Class 1 Notification Area.

Peel watershed

2 In Yukon, the portion of the watershed of the Peel

River and its tributaries referred to as the “Peel

Watershed Class 1 Notification Area” shown shaded and

outlined in bold on the attached map with that title,

including for greater certainty any parcel or portion of a

parcel of settlement land that lies within the Peel

Watershed Class 1 Notification Area.

(Section 2 added by O.I.C. 2014/08)

South-Western Yukon

3 In Yukon, the tract of land referred to as the ‘South -

Western Yukon Class 1 Notification Area’ shown shaded

and outlined in bold on the attached map with that title.

(Section 3 added by O.I.C. 2014/121, see O.I.C. 2014/121 for the

application of this section prior to July 1, 2015)

Southern Yukon

4 In Yukon, the tract of land referred to as the

‘Southern Yukon Class 1 Notification Are a’ shown shaded

and outlined in bold on the attached map with that title.

(Section 4 added by O.I.C. 2014/121, see O.I.C. 2014/121 for the

application of this section prior to July 1, 2015)

Watson Lake Area

5 In Yukon, the tract of land referred to as the

‘Watson Lake Class 1 Notification Area’ shown shaded

and outlined in bold on the attached map with that title.

(Section 5 added by O.I.C. 2014/121, see O.I.C. 2014/121 for the

application of this section prior to July 1, 2015)

Category A and Category B Settlement Lands

6 In Yukon, each tract of land identified as Category

A or Category B settlement land in Appendix A to any of

the following Final Agreements

(a) Carcross/Tagish First Nation Final Agreement;

(b) Champagne and Aishihik First Nations Final

Agreement;

(c) Kluane First Nation Final Agreement;

(d) Kwanlin Dun First Nation Final Agreement;

(e) Little Salmon/Carmacks First Nation Final

Agreement;

(f) First Nation of Na-Cho Nyak Dun Final

Agreement;

(g) Selkirk First Nation Final Agreement;

(h) Ta’an Kwachan Council Final Agreement;

(i) Teslin Tlingit Council Final Agreement;

(j)

(Paragraph 6(j) repealed by O.I.C. 2018/46)

(k) Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation Final Agreement.

(Section 6 added by O.I.C. 2014/121, see O.I.C. 2014/121 for the

application of this section prior to July 1, 2015)

Dawson Area

7 In Yukon, the tract of land identified as the

traditional territory of the Tr’ondëk Hwëch’in First Nation

on the map referred to in 2.9.1.1 of the Tr’ondëk

Hwëch’in Final Agreement, shown shaded and outlined

in bold on the attached map titled the “Dawson Class 1

Notification Area”, including for greater certainty any

parcel or portion of a parcel of settlement land that lies

within the Dawson Class 1 Notification Area.
