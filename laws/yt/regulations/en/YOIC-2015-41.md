YOIC 2015/41

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

### Interpretation

1 In this Regulation, “grazing agreement” means a lease

or licence that allows a person to graze livestock on

territorial lands.

### Application of Grazing Regulations

2 Any territorial lands to which a grazing agreement

applies are to be dealt with, in respect of the grazing

agreement, as Yukon lands under the Lands Act .

### Exception – existing agreements

3 Section 2 does not apply in respect of any grazing

agreement that was made before the day on which this

Regulation comes into force.
