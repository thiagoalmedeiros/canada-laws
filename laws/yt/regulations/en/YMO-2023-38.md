# Order to extend renewal terms of Chance Oil and Gas Limited permits (2023)

YMO 2023/38

*Made under the Oil and Gas Act*

### Oil and gas permits No. 0012 to No. 0017

1 For oil and gas permits No. 0012, No. 0013, No. 0014,

No. 0015, No. 0016 and No. 0017 held by Chance Oil and

Gas Limited

(a) each renewal term is extended by six months so

that the renewal term now ends on

February 29, 2024; and

(b) the resulting total length of the initial term and

the renewal term is 16 years and 183 days.

### Oil and gas permit No. 0019

2 For oil and gas permit No. 0019 held by Chance Oil and

Gas Limited

(a) the renewal term is extended by six months so

that the renewal term now ends on

February 29, 2024; and

(b) the resulting total length of the initial term and

the renewal term is 14 years and 183 days.

### Oil and gas permit No. 0020

3 For oil and gas permit No. 0020 held by Chance Oil and

Gas Limited

(a) the renewal term is extended by six months so

that the renewal term now ends on

February 29, 2024; and

(b) the resulting total length of the initial term and

the renewal term is 13 years and 183 days.

### Coming into force

4 This Order comes into force on August 15, 2023.
