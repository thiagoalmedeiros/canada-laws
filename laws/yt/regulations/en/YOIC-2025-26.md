# Hospital Privileges Appeal Board Remuneration Regulation

YOIC 2025/26

*Made under the Hospital Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation

“ full day ” means a period or periods that total more

than four hours in one day; « journée complète »

“ member ” means a member of the Hospital Privileges

Appeal Board, appointed under subsection 19(1) of the

Act; « membre »

“ training event ”, in respect of an individual, means a

conference, workshop, seminar or training program that

the individual attends in connection with the

performance of their duties under the Act. « activité de

formation »

### Remuneration of chair

2 The member who is designated as the chair is to be paid

(a) $2000 for a full day for time spent preparing for

a meeting or attending a meeting or a training

event; and

(b) $250 per hour for time spent preparing for a

meeting or attending a meeting or a training

event, for a period or periods less than a full day.

### Remuneration of members

3 (1) A member other than the chair is to be paid

(a) $1800 for a full day for time spent preparing for

a meeting or attending a meeting or a training

event; and

(b) $250 per hour for time spent preparing for a

meeting or attending a meeting or a training

event, for a period or periods less than a full day.

(2) While acting as the chair, a member is to be paid

the remuneration provided for in section 2.

### Travel expenses

4 Travel expenses incurred by a member in connection

with their work and duties as a member, including

training events approved by the Minister, will be

reimbursed as follows:

(a) if there is a directive of the management board

in force concerning payment of travel expenses

to members of boards of the Government of

Yukon, in accordance with the directive, as

amended or replaced from time to time;

(b) otherwise, in accordance with the policy for

payment of travel expenses to employees of the

Government of Yukon, as amended or replaced

from time to time.
