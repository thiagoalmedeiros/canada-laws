YOIC 1986/44

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

1 This regulation may be cited as the Fair Wage

Regulation .

2 In this regulation:

“ contractor ” means a person who has entered into a

contract and includes a subcontractor;

« entrepreneur »

“ fair wage schedule ” means the applicable schedule of

wage rates established pursuant to section 97(1) of the

Employment Standards Act . « annexe des salaires

équitables »

3 Every contractor who is an employer and who has a

contract for the performance of a public work of the

Territory as set out in section 97(3) of the Employment

Standards Act , shall post and keep posted a copy of the

Fair Wage Schedule in a conspicuous place on the work

site where it is most likely to come to the attention of

his employees.
