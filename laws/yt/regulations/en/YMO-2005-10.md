1 The annexed Ministerial Order Respecting the

______________________________________________________

Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources

## MINISTERIAL ORDER RESPECTING THE

## WITHDRAWAL FROM DISPOSITION OF

## YUKON OIL AND GAS LANDS (ASI KEYI

## NATURAL ENVIRONMENT PARK)

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Ministerial Order is to

withdraw certain Yukon oil and gas lands from disposition

to facilitate the establishment of Asi Keyi Natural

Environment Park .

### Lands withdrawn from disposition

2 The Yukon oil and gas lands described in the

schedule to this Order (Asi Keyi Natural Environment Park )

are withdrawn from disposition under the Oil and Gas Act .

## SCHEDULE

Lands withdrawn from disposition(Asi Keyi

Natural Environment Park)

The area of land shown as “Asi Keyi Natural Environment

Park” on the following maps on file at the Federal Claims

Office, Department of Indian Affairs and Northern

Development, at Whitehorse, copies of which have been

deposited with the Manager of Lands, Department of

Energy, Mines and Resources, Government of Yukon at

Whitehorse and with the Mining Recorders at Whitehorse,

Watson Lake, Mayo and Dawson.
