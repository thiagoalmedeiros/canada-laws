YOIC 1995/77

*Made under the Municipal Act*

Pursuant to section 328 of the Municipal Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 Whereas the Council of the Town of the City of

Dawson has adopted a subdivision control bylaw approved

by the Minister of Community and Transportation

Services, the Council shall be an approving authority

within the limits of the Town of the City of Dawson from

the date of this order.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 8th

day of May, 1995.

__________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
