YOIC 2015/109

*Made under the Builders Lien Act*

1 The forms in the Schedule to this Regulation are

prescribed for the purposes of the Act.

2 A claim of lien for materials or machinery furnished

is to be in Form 1.

3 A claim of lien for wages for work done is to be

(a) in the case of a single claimant, in Form 2A; or

(b) in the case of multiple claimants, in Form 2B.

4 A receipt for a discharge of a claim of lien is to be in

Form 3.

5 An affidavit verifying a claim of lien or a discharge of

a lien is to be in Form 4.
