YOIC 2008/109

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 That portion of the Right-of-Way of the Old Alaska

Highway adjacent to Lot 1010, Quad 105 c/6, CLSR Plan

75019, LTO Plan 93-31 as shown “cross-hatched” in the

attached schedule is closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 25 July 2008.
