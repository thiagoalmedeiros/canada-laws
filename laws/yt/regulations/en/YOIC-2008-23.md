YOIC 2008/23

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The portion of the right of way as shown on Plan

92445 CLSR, 2007-0038 LTO shown by cross-hatching on

the attached map is hereby closed and declared to be a

highway no longer.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 08 February 2008.
