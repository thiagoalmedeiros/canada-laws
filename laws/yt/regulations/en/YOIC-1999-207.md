YOIC 1999/207

*Made under the Assessment and Taxation Act*

Pursuant to subsection 11(3) of the Assessment and

Taxation Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows:

1 The attached Improvement Valuation Regulation is

hereby made.

2 Order-in-Council 1987/016 is revoked.

3 This order comes into force on December 15, 1999.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 13

day of December, 1999.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## IMPROVEMENT VALUATION REGULATION

1 The assessment manual to be used for the valuation

of improvements shall be the Marshall Valuation Service

published by Marshall & Swift.

2 In those cases where improvements have not been

valued using the Marshall Valuation Service, the assessment

manual to be used for the valuation of improvements shall

be the “Government of the Province of Alberta Assessment

Manual, 1984 First Edition and amendments thereto”.

3 All references in the Government of Alberta

Assessment Manual to “normal cost”, replacement cost" or

synonymous terms or phrases shall be deemed to refer to

current Whitehorse replacement costs rather than to costs

in Edmonton and surrounding areas.
