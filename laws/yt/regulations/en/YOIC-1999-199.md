YOIC 1999/199

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The lots in the Little Salmon Lake Subdivision listed

in Schedule A attached hereto are classed as recreational

residential and shall be sold at the prices listed in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 23rd

day of November, 1999.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
