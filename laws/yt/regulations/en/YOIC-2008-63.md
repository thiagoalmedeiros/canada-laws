## RURAL ELECTRIFICATION AND

## TELECOMMUNICATIONS LOCAL

## IMPROVEMENT AND RURAL DOMESTIC

## WATER WELL PROGRAM TAX REGULATION,

## 2008

1 These Regulations may be cited as the Rural

Electrification Local Improvement Tax Regulations, 2008 .

2 In these Regulations, “local improvement” means

the extension of a primary electrical power line to serve the

properties in the regions listed in the attached Schedule.

3 The regions listed in the attached Schedule are

declared to benefit from the local improvement.

4 To recover the cost of the local improvement there

is levied on each property in each region listed in the

attached Schedule a local improvement tax consisting of

the principal amount and the interest specified for that

property in the attached Schedule.

The local improvement tax

(a) shall be shown as a separate item on the tax

notice for the property;

(b) is due and payable in the annual instalments

shown on the attached Schedule at the same time

as the general tax and school tax;

(c) is part of the total tax payable for the

property; and

(d) may be paid in advance by paying the

outstanding balance of principal together with

interest accrued at the time of payment.
