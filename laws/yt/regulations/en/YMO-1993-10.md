YMO 1993/10

*Made under the Young Offenders Act (Canada)*

Pursuant to subsection 13.2(1) of the Young Offenders

Act (Canada), the Minister of Health and Social Services

orders as follows:

1 The following be designated as hospitals for the

custody, treatment or assessment of young persons

under the Young Offenders Act (Canada):

Whitehorse General Hospital, Whitehorse,

Yukon Territory

Mental Health Services, Health Canada,

Whitehorse, Yukon Territory

Young Offenders Facility

Whitehorse, Yukon Territory

(Added by O.I.C. 1994/008)

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 25th

day of October, 1993.

Minister of Health and Social Services/Ministre
