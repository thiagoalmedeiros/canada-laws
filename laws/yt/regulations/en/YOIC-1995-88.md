YOIC 1995/88

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The access road shown on Canada Land Survey

Records Plan No. 42239 which is registered in the Yukon

Land Titles Office as Plan No. 20938 is hereby closed.

2 The access road shown on Canada Land Survey

Records Plan No. 43115 which is registered in the Yukon

Land Titles Office as Plan No. 22157 is hereby closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 29th

day of May, 1995.

_________________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
