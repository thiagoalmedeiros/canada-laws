YOIC 2010/7

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The lots in the Hillcrest Industrial Subdivision (in

the Burns Road area) listed in the attached Schedule are

classed as shown and shall be sold for no less than the

minimum price listed in the Schedule.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 14 January 2010.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon
