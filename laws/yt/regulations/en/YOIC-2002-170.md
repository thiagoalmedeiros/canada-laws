YOIC 2002/170

*Made under the Arts Act*

Pursuant to section 14 of the Arts Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The annexed Yukon Arts Advisory Council

Regulation is hereby made.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory,

this 5 th day of August, 2002.

Commissioner of the

## YUKON ARTS ADVISORY COUNCIL

## REGULATION

## 1.

The members of the Yukon Arts Advisory Council shall

each be paid an honorarium of $125 for each day they

perform their functions for at least four hours in

meetings, or other work, approved by the Minister.
