YCO 1970/313

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

The whole of the area known as the Long Lake

Recreational Area, as described on the Schedule attached

hereto and forming part hereof and as shown on the

attached sketch, being required for public purposes, is

hereby withdrawn from disposal.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 18th

day of November A.D., 1970.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon

## SCHEDULE

### Long Lake Recreational Area

Commencing at a point, this point being on the

northerly extension of the north-easterly boundary of Lot

336, Group 804, and a distance of 7,225 feet from the most

northerly corner of said lot. Lot 336, Group 804, being

registered under number 43422 in the Canada Lands

Survey Records of the Department of Energy, Mines and

Resources at Ottawa; thence on a bearing of 249 degrees 21’

for a distance of 500 feet to a point; thence on a bearing of

339 degrees 21’ for a distance of 4,000 feet to a point;

thence on a bearing of 69 degrees 21’ for a distance of

2,000 feet to a point; thence on a bearing of 159 degrees

21’ for a distance of 4,000 feet to a point; thence on a

bearing of 249 degrees 21’ for a distance of 1,500 feet to the

point of commencement.

The above described parcel of land is containing all of

Long Lake and is comprising an area of 183.65 acres, more

or less, as outlined on the attached sketch.
