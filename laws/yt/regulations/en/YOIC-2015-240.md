YOIC 2015/240

*Made under the Criminal Code (Canada)*

1 (1) In this Order, “Yukon Victim Services” means the

office of the Director of Victim Services under the

Victims of Crime Act .

(2) The programs of Yukon Victim Services in relation

to victim impact statements and community impact

statements are designated for the purposes of

subsections 722(4) and 722.2(2), respectively, of the

Criminal Code (Canada).
