YOIC 1995/1

*Made under the First Nations (Yukon) Self-Government Act*

## ORDER

Pursuant to section 3 of the First Nations (Yukon) Self-

Government Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows:

1 Order-in-Council 1994/229 is revoked and this

order is substituted for it.

2 The attached Teslin Tlingit Council Self-

Government Agreement among the Teslin Tlingit

Council, the Government of Canada and the

Government of the Yukon made the 29th of May, 1993

is hereby approved.

3 The attached First Nation of Nacho Nyak Dun Self-

Government Agreement among the First Nation of

Nacho Nyak Dun, the Government of Canada and the

Government of the Yukon made the 29th of May, 1993

is hereby approved.

4 The attached Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation Self-

Government Agreement among the Vuntut Gwitchin

First Nation, the Government of Canada and the

Government of the Yukon made the 29th of May, 1993

is hereby approved.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 5th

day of January, 1995.

Commissioner of the

(Copies of the above-mentioned agreements are

available from the Yukon Government Land Claims

Secretariat Office, 2nd Floor, 204 Main Street, Box

2703, Whitehorse, Yukon Y1A 2C6 Canada)
