YOIC 2016/105

*Made under the Summary Convictions Act*

### Interpretation

1 (1) In this Regulation

“ appearance notice ” means an appearance notice

issued and served on a defendant under section 8;

« citation à comparaître »

“ Option 3 ” means Option 3 as set out in Part B of a ticket

issued under the Act. « option 3 »

(2) A reference in this Regulation to

(a) a schedule is a reference to a schedule attached

to this Regulation; and

(b) a part of a ticket is a reference to that part of

the ticket as shown in Schedule 3.

(3) An abbreviation set out in Schedule 2 for an

enactment, instrument or document may be used to

refer to that enactment, instrument or document in a

ticket issued under the Act.

(4) If Saturday is the day on which the period expires

for a defendant to do one of the options set out in Part

B of a ticket, the last day on which that defendant may

do one of those options is deemed to be the next

business day of the court registry.

### Prescribed offence with set fine

2 An amount set out in Column 3 of Schedule 1 for a

prescribed offence is the set fine for that prescribed

offence.

### Prescribed ticket

3 A ticket issued under the Act must be in the form set out

in Schedule 3.

### Description of offence

4 (1) For the purposes of describing a prescribed offence

under section 10 of the Act, an enforcement officer may,

in entering the description into the ticket, use

(a) the description of the prescribed offence set out

in Column 2 of Schedule 1; or

(b) the abbreviation set out in Schedule 2 instead of

the name of an enactment.

(2) For greater certainty, an enforcement officer is not

required to enter the entirety of the description provided

under Column 2 of Schedule 1 in a ticket that is issued

in respect of that prescribed offence.

### Summons particulars required in limited case

5 For the purposes of section 10 of the Act, an

enforcement officer must set out particulars in the

summons part of the ticket only if the enforcement

officer issues a ticket in accordance with section 25 of

the Act.

### Deposit of ticket within 30 days of service

6 (1) For the purposes of section 19 of the Act, an

enforcement officer, or their agent, must deposit a ticket

with the court registry within 30 days after the date of

service of the ticket.

(2) A ticket that is not deposited within the period set

out in subsection (1) is deemed to be invalid.

(3) For greater certainty, no proceedings may be

commenced in respect of an invalid ticket.

### Payment of set fine before ticket filed

7 (1) A defendant may pay a set fine that is included in

a ticket that has been issued to them at any time before

the ticket is filed with the court registry.

(2) Before a payment is made under subsection (1), the

defendant must file their copy of the ticket with the court

registry.

(3) A copy of a ticket that is filed under subsection (2)

is deemed to be the ticket for which it is a copy.

### Option 3 appearance notice

8 (1) If, when a defendant appears at a court registry in

accordance with Option 3 as set out in Part B of a ticket,

they do not enter a plea to a charge set out in that ticket,

a justice must issue an appearance notice in respect of

each charge for which the defendant did not enter a plea

at that time.

(2) The court registry must, as soon as practicable,

serve an appearance notice issued under subsection (1)

on the defendant.

(3) An appearance notice

(a) must include

(i) the name of the defendant,

(ii) any charge to which the appearance notice

relates,

(iii) the date on and location at which the

defendant is required to appear in court in

respect of a charge,

(iv) the signature of the justice who issued the

appearance notice, and

(v) the date of the signature under subparagraph

(iv); and

(b) may include up to three charges, each of which

is set out in the same ticket.

(4) An appearance notice may be in the form

determined by the court registry.

### If defendant does not appear

9 (1) Subject to subsection (3), if a defendant does not

appear in court in person or by agent at the time and

place specified in an appearance notice, a justice may,

on proof of service of the appearance notice

(a) enter a plea of not guilty on behalf of the

defendant in respect of any charge set out in

that appearance notice; and

(b) upon application of the prosecutor, proceed

immediately, in absence of the defendant, with

a trial of any charge for which a not guilty plea

has been entered; or

(c) issue a warrant for the arrest of the defendant.

(2) If a justice proceeds with a trial under

subsection (1), the justice may

(a) adjourn the proceedings from time to time; and

(b) in all other respects, conduct the trial and

determine any charge that is tried as if the

defendant had appeared in person.

(3) Before a justice may, under this section, proceed

with a trial or issue a warrant, an enforcement officer

must swear, in writing and under oath or solemn

affirmation before a justice, the complaint upon which

the trial or warrant is based.
