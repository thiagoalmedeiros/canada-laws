*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

### SPECIAL WARRANT (No. 2), 2006-07

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this Special Warrant

1 To authorize an amount of up to $12,884,000 to be

paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in accordance

with the attached Schedules A and B in addition to the net

sum of $903,958,000 provided for in the First Appropriation

Act 2006-07 and the Second Appropriation Act 2006-07 .

2 To stipulate that payments made under the Special

Warrant be made for the purpose of financing government

operations for the period March 15, 2007 to March 31,

2007 as set forth in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 16 March 2007.
