Vital Statistics Act .

## BIRTH CERTIFICATES

3 (1) A certificate (referred to in this section as a ‘birth

certificate’) issued by the registrar under

subsection 31(1) of the Act shall contain the particulars

of the registration of birth set out in

paragraphs 31(1)(a) to (f) of the Vital Statistics Act .

(2) A birth certificate may, if requested by the

applicant and approved by the registrar, also contain

the following particulars of the registration of birth in

respect of one or more parents

(a) the parent’s name;

(b) the parent’s place of birth; or

(c) the parent’s name and place of birth.

(3) A form of birth certificate shall be completed with a

unique certificate number, a unique bar code, the date

issued, and descriptions of those particulars to be

contained in the certificate under subsections (1) and

(2), as shown on the following attached samples

(a) sample birth certificate without parental

information; and

(b) sample birth certificate with parental

information.

(4) A description of a particular allowed to be

contained in a birth certificate under subsection (2)

may, if that particular is not requested by the applicant

or is not approved by the Registrar

(a) be omitted from the form of birth certificate; or

(b) be included in the form of birth certificate but

completed with the words “not applicable”.

### Certificate issued using prior forms remains valid

4 Every certificate issued by the registrar in a form

previously established by these regulations remains

valid despite the substitution of a new form in its place.

### Designation of persons

5 The persons listed in Schedule 2 are designated as the

persons who may have access to or may be given

copies of or information from the records in the office

of the registrar.

### Oath of secrecy

6 (1) The oath of secrecy to be taken by the persons

listed in sections 1 to 3 of Schedule 2 and rows 1 and 2

of section 4 of Schedule 2 is prescribed to be the oath

of secrecy set out in Schedule 3.

(2) The oath of secrecy to be taken by the persons

listed in row 3 of section 4 of Schedule 2 is prescribed

to be one of the following:

(a) the oath of secrecy set out in Schedule 3;

(b) the oath set out in section 54 of the Public

Service Employment Act (Canada).
