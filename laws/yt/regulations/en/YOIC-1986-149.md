YOIC 1986/149

*Made under the Public Service Act*

## PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION ACT

Pursuant to subsection 210(1) of the Public Service

Commission Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows:

1 Government of Yukon employees who apply to

contract for the provision of Art Work for the Andrew

Philipsen Law Centre are exempted from subsection 194(1)

of the Public Service Commission Act.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 19th

day of September, A.D.,1986.

Commissioner of the Yukon
