## SPECIAL WARRANT (NO.1) 2022-23

1

An amount of up to $15,348,000 may be paid from the

consolidated revenue fund in accordance with the

attached Schedules A and B, in addition to the net sum

of $2,020,730,000 provided for in the First Appropriation

Act 2022-23 , Second Appropriation Act 2022-23 and

Third Appropriation Act 2022-23 .

2

Payments may be made under this special warrant for

the purpose of financing government operations for the

period April 1, 2022 to March 31, 2023 as set forth in

Schedule A.
