*Made under the Yukon Act (Canada)*

### Administration and control relinquished

1 The administration and control of the following public

real property is relinquished, in perpetuity, to his

Majesty the King in right of Canada as represented by

the Minister of Northern Affairs:

Lots 18, 33 and 34, Ross River, Plan 52922 CLSR, 27954

LTO;

Lots 70 and 71, Ross River, Plan 53407 CLSR, 29803 LTO;

Lots 101 to 104 and 109 to 116, Ross River, Plan 53919

CLSR, 29969 LTO;

Lot 227, Ross River, Plan 67707 CLSR, 63575 LTO;

Lots 249, 250 and 256 to 265, Ross River, Plan 74380

CLSR, 92-66 LTO;

Lot 280, Ross River, Plan 82998 CLSR, 2000-0001 LTO

saving and excepting therefrom and reserving thereout

all mines and minerals, including hydrocarbons, whether

solid, liquid or gaseous, and the right to work them.

### Coming into force

2 Section 1 comes into force on the day the Governor in

Council approves the relinquishment of the public real

property referred to in section 1.
