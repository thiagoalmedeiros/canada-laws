YOIC 1983/134

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 12(4) of the Highways Act, the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The road shown broken and traversing Lot 709

Group 804 on the attached Schedule A is closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 5th

day of July, A.D., 1983.
