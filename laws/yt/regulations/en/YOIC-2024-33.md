## SPECIAL WARRANT (NO.1) 2023-24

Pursuant to the Financial Administration Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders

February 22, 2024

Commissioner of

## SPECIAL WARRANT (NO.1) 2023-24

1

An amount of up to $65,922,000 may be paid from the

consolidated revenue fund in accordance with the

attached Schedules A and B, in addition to the net sum

of $2,070,545,000 provided for in the First Appropriation

Act 2023-24 and Second Appropriation Act 2023-24 .

2

Payments may be made under this special warrant for

the purpose of financing government operations for the

period February 1, 2024 to March 31, 2024 as set forth

in Schedule A.
