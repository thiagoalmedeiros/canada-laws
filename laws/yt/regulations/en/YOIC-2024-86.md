# Order Prohibiting Entry on Certain Lands in Yukon (Selkirk First Nation R-Block Parcels)

YOIC 2024/86

*Made under the Placer Mining Act*

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to prohibit entry to

identified Selkirk First Nation Settlement Parcels to

provide greater protection to heritage and

environmental resources on the parcels and to support

traditional and cultural use of the parcels by the Selkirk

First Nation.

### Definition

2 In this Order

“ recorded claim ” means

(a) a recorded placer claim that is in good standing,

acquired under the Yukon Placer Mining Act

(Canada) and continued under the Placer Mining

Act , or acquired under the Placer Mining Act , or

(b) a recorded mineral claim that is in good

standing, acquired under the Yukon Quartz

Mining Act (Canada) and continued under the

Quartz Mining Act , or acquired under the Quartz

Mining Act . « claim inscrit »

### Prohibition of Entry

3 A person must not enter on the lands described in

Schedule 1, and shown on Schedule 2, for the purpose of

(a) locating a claim or prospecting for gold or other

precious minerals or stones under the Placer

Mining Act ; or

(b) locating a claim or prospecting or mining for

minerals under the Quartz Mining Act .

### Existing rights and interests

4 Section 3 does not apply to entry on a recorded claim by

the owner or holder of the recorded claim.

## SCHEDULE 1

1 Lands

In Yukon, the lands identified as

(a) Selkirk First Nation Settlement Land Parcel R-8B;

(b) Selkirk First Nation Settlement Land Parcel R-

43B;

(c) the “Prohibition of entry area” on the map

attached as Schedule 2 to the Order, more

particularly described as follows:

In Yukon Territory;

In the vicinity of Pelly Crossing;

All that portion of Lot 1008, Quad 115 I/15, being part

of the Selkirk First Nation Settlement Land Selection

R-45B, as shown on a plan recorded in the Canada

Lands Surveys Record at Ottawa as 83230 CLSR, a copy

of which is filed in the Land Titles Office in Whitehorse

as 2000-0058 LTO, which lies east of a straight line

drawn northwesterly from a point in the southern

boundary of said Lot at C.L.S. 77 post marked

“14L1008”, as shown on said plan, to a point in the

northern boundary of said Lot at C.L.S. 77 post marked

“9L1008”, as shown on said plan.

That area containing approximately 310.4 hectares.
