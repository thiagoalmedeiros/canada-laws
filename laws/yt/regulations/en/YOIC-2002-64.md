YOIC 2002/64

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

## 2002-2003

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this special warrant

1 To authorize an amount of up to $279,843,000 to

be paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in

accordance with the attached Schedules A and B, in

addition to the net sum of $118,177,000 provided for in

the First Appropriation Act 2002-03 .

2 To stipulate that payments made under the Special

Warrant be made for the purpose of financing government

operations for the period April 1, 2002 to June 30, 2002 as

set forth in Schedules A and B to this order.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 13th day

of March, 2002.
