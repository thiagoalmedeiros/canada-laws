YOIC 2002/240

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

## 2002-2003

Pursuant to subsections 19(2) and (3) of the Financial

Administration Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby issues this special warrant

1 To authorize an amount of up to $12,729,000 to be

paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund in accordance

with the attached Schedule A and B, in addition to the net

sum of $582,708,000 provided for in the First Appropriation

Act 2002-03 , the Second Appropriation Act 2002-03 , and

Special Warrant (No. 2), 2002-2003.

2 To stipulate that payments made under the Special

Warrant be made for the purpose of financing government

operations for the period October 1, 2002 to March 31,

2003 as set forth in Schedule A.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 23rd day

of December, 2002.
