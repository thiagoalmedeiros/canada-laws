Pursuant to the Traffic Safety Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council orders

1 The attached Regulation to modify the Motor Vehicles

Act by applying certain Traffic Safety Act penalties and

municipal bylaw powers is made.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

April 24, 2025

Commissioner of

## SECTION 1

## REGULATION TO MODIFY THE MOTOR

## VEHICLES ACT BY APPLYING CERTAIN

## TRAFFIC SAFETY ACT PENALTIES AND

## MUNICIPAL BYLAW POWERS

### Municipal bylaws

1 (1) In this section

“ section 42 ” means section 42 of the Traffic Safety Act .

« article 42 »

(2) On the repeal of section 126 of the Motor Vehicles

Act and the coming into force of section 42, subject to

subsection (4) of this section, the Motor Vehicles Act is

to be read as though section 42 were a provision of the

Motor Vehicles Act numbered as section 126.

(3) All bylaws made by a municipality under section 126

of the Motor Vehicles Act that are in force on the coming

into force of this section are continued as if they were

made under section 42.

(4) In section 42

(a) in subsection (1), the reference to “ municipal

highways located in that municipality ” is to be

read as “ highways subject to its jurisdiction ”;

and

## SECTION 2

(b) in subsection (3)

(i) the reference to “ municipal highway located

in that municipality ” is to be read as “ highway

subject to its jurisdiction ”, and

(ii) in paragraph (c), t he reference to “rules of the

road” is to be read as a reference to the

provisions in Part 11 of the Motor Vehicles

Act .

### Application of fines and imprisonment terms in the Traffic Safety Act to specified offences in the Motor Vehicles Act

2 (1) Except for offences referred to in Column 2 of the

Schedule, a fine or term of imprisonment, or both, to

which a person is liable on summary conviction in

respect of an offence under the Motor Vehicles Act is to

be determined under the Motor Vehicles Act .

(2) Where a person referred to in Column 1 of the

Schedule is guilty of an offence under the Motor Vehicles

Act referred to in Column 2 of the Schedule, the fine,

term of imprisonment, or both, applicable under the

provision of the Traffic Safety Act referred to in

Column 3 of the Schedule applies instead of the fine or

term of imprisonment applicable under the provision of

the Motor Vehicles Act referred to in Column 4 of the

Schedule.

(3) Subsection (2) applies to a contravention that occurs

on or after the coming into force of this section and

before the repeal of this section.

### Coming into force

3 This Regulation comes into force on the later of the

following days:

(a) the day section 245 of the Traffic Safety Act

comes into force;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of

regulations under the Regulations Act .
