*Made under the Workers’ Safety and Compensation Act*

## W.C.H.S.B.O. 1998/03

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

## WORKERS’ COMPENSATION ACT

Pursuant to subsection 101(1) of the Workers’

Compensation Act , the Board orders as follows:

1 The maximum wage rate for the year 1998 shall be

fifty four thousand, two hundred dollars ($54,200.00) per

annum.

2 The maximum wage rate is to be applied to workers

who are entitled to compensation as a result of a disability

caused after January 1, 1998.

3 This order shall be deemed to have come into force

on January 1, 1998.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

21st day of January, 1998.
