YOIC 2021/109

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to the Highways Act , the Commissioner

in Executive Council orders

1 That portion of Hunker Road (Plan 10357 CLSR)

comprising approximately 0.22 ha and located

approximately one kilometre from Goldbottom Road,

which is outlined in bold on the map attached as

Schedule 1, is closed.

2 That portion of Hunker Road (Plan 10357 CLSR)

comprising approximately 0.39 ha and located at

Goldbottom Road, which is outlined in bold on the

map attached as Schedule 2, is closed.

3 That portion of Goldbottom Road (Plan

55026 CLSR) comprising approximately 0.01 ha and

located at Hunker Road, which is outlined in bold on

the map attached as Schedule 3, is closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, August 5 2021.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon
