Burns Road in Whitehorse, Yukon. The maps are

designated as Department of Environment (ENV)

2 Where the boundary of an outfitting concession area

appears to follow the line represented by a road, creek,

river, lake or the channel of a river, it shall, unless

otherwise clearly indicated on the map, be deemed to

follow the center of that line.
