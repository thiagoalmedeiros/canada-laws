YOIC 1999/33

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

Pursuant to subsection 98(1) of the Employment

Standards Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

hereby orders that:

1 For the purpose of section 98 of the Employment

Standards Act , Nunavut is hereby declared to be a

reciprocating province and the Director of Court

Services of Nunavut is hereby designated the

enforcement authority, on and from the 1st day of

April, A.D., 1999.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this

31 day of March 1999.
