# Waste Disposal Sites Non-Payable Charges Order (2026)

YMO 2026/13

*Made under the Financial Administration Act*

### Charges not payable

1 The charges otherwise required to be paid under

items 1 to 12 of the Schedule to the Waste Disposal Sites

Charges Order are not payable from May 21 to

May 25, 2026 if

(a) waste is transported from the property of an

individual to a waste disposal site by that

individual or by another individual on their

behalf;

(b) loads are transported for no financial gain; and

(c) loads are transported in one of the following

manners:

(i) in a car,

(ii) in the cargo box of a pick-up truck,

(iii) in a utility trainler measuring less than

10 feet in length.

### Application

2 This Order applies only to non-commercial waste and

transportation.

### Repeal

3 The Third Waste Disposal Sites Non-Payable Charges

Order (2025) is repealed.
