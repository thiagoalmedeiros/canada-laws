## ORDER TO ESTABLISH CHASÀN CHÙA

### Definitions

1 In this Order

“ authorization ” means any of the following granted

under the Lands Act or Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act :

(a) a lease, right-of-way, easement or other interest

in land,

(b) a licence, permit or other form of approval;

« autorisation »

“ pre-existing authorization ” means an authorization

(a) that is valid and subsisting immediately before

this order comes into force, and

(b) that, in the area established by this order as

Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park, authorizes a

development, use or activity or an occupation of

land; « autorisation préexistante »

“ recorded claim ” means a recorded mineral claim that

is in good standing, acquired under the Yukon Quartz

Mining Act (Canada) and continued under the Quartz

Mining Act , or acquired under the Quartz Mining Act .

« claim enregistré »

### Park established

2 Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park is established as a natural

environment park.

### Park purpose

3 The purpose of the park is to protect a representative or

unique landscape that displays ecological characteristics

or features of one or more of the Yukon’s ecoregions .

### Park boundaries

4 The land to be included in Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park

is the area shown as Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park on

Administrative Plan 113990 CLSR.

### Existing rights and interests

5 (1) Subject to subsection (2), the rights of an owner or

a holder of a recorded claim in Chasàn Chùa Territorial

Park under the Quartz Mining Act continue in accordance

with that Act.

(2) The owner or holder of a recorded claim in Chasàn

Chùa Territorial Park must not undertake the

development of mines and minerals on the claim or of

infrastructure, or any land use or activity associated

with such development, except as authorized by a park

permit.

(3) A pre-existing authorization remains in effect until

the day on which the authorization is cancelled in

accordance with the terms of the authorization or

expires.

(4) A park permit is not required to undertake a

development, use or activity or to occupy land in Chasàn

Chùa Territorial Park if

(a) the development, use or activity or occupation is

authorized under a pre-existing authorization;

and

(b) the development, use or activity is undertaken

or the land is occupied in accordance with the

terms of the authorization.

### Industrial development prior to completion of management plan

6 For the purpose of section 31 of the Act, the Minister

may issue a park permit authorizing any of the following

industrial developments, including any land use, activity

or infrastructure associated with such developments, in

Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park prior to the coming into

force of the management plan for the park:

(a) the development of mines and minerals on a

recorded claim;

(b) the development of new transmission or

distribution lines;

(c) the harvesting of timber resources, including

deadfall;

(d) the creation of a fuel break;

(e) activities to operate, maintain or enhance public

infrastructure, such as establishing a snow

dump site, undertaken by or on behalf of the City

of Whitehorse.

### Planning committee

7 (1) The Minister must establish a planning committee

for Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park in accordance with this

section.

(2) The role of the planning committee is to prepare a

management plan for Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park for

recommendation to the Minister.

(3) The membership of the planning committee is to

include at least one representative of each of the

following:

(a) City of Whitehorse;

(b) Government of Yukon;

(c) Kwanlin Dün First Nation;

(d) Ta’an Kwäch’än Council.

### Recommendation of management plan

8 (1) The members of the planning committee must make

reasonable efforts to reach consensus on a management

plan for Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park before

recommending the plan to the Minister.

(2) The Minister must consider a management plan

recommended by the planning committee and whether

the members of the committee reached consensus on

the plan.

### Management committee

9 (1) As soon as practicable after a management plan is

approved under section 23 of the Act for Chasàn Chùa

Territorial Park, the Minister must establish a

management committee for the park in accordance with

this section.

(2) The role of the management committee is to advise

and make recommendations to the Minister with respect

to the implementation of the management plan for

Chasàn Chùa Territorial Park.

(3) The membership of the management committee is

to include at least one representative of each of the

following:

(a) City of Whitehorse;

(b) Government of Yukon;

(c) Kwanlin Dün First Nation;

(d) Ta’an Kwäch’än Council.
