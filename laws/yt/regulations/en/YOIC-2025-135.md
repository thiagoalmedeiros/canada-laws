### Definition

1 In this Regulation

“ applicant ” includes, in the case of an application for

registration as a collector, the individual in respect of

whom the application is made by a collection agency.

« demandeur »

### Classes of collection agent

2 (1) The following classes of collection agent are

established:

(a) collection agency;

(b) collector.

(2) The collection agency class of collection agent

consists of those persons that

(a) conduct the business of collection agent as a

corporation, partnership or sole proprietorship;

and

(b) employ or use one or more collectors to carry

out the activities of a collection agent.

(3) The collector class of collection agent consists of

(a) those individuals employed or used by a

collection agency, other than a sole

proprietorship, to carry out the activities of a

collection agent; and

(b) those individuals whose sole proprietorships are

registered as collection agencies when the

individuals are carrying out the activities of a

collection agent.

### Exemption from application of certain provisions of the Act — collectors

3 A collector is exempt from the application of the

following provisions of the Act:

(a) section 79;

(b) section 80;

(c) section 81;

(d) section 83.

### Application for registration as collection agency

4 An application for the registration of a person as a

collection agency

(a) must be made to the Minister in the form

approved by the Minister and signed by the

applicant or an authorized representative of the

applicant;

(b) must include a declaration of the applicant or the

authorized representative of the applicant that

contains the statements and information

specified in the form respecting the suitability of

the applicant for registration as a collection

agency; and

(c) must be accompanied by the fee set out in

paragraph 7(a).

### Application for registration as collector

5 (1) An application for the registration of an individual

as a collector

(a) must be made to the Minister in the form

approved by the Minister;

(b) must be made and signed by the collection

agency or an authorized representative of the

collection agency that intends to employ or use

the individual to carry out the activities of a

collection agent while the individual is

registered;

(c) must include a declaration of the collection

agency or an authorized representative of the

collection agency that contains the statements

and information specified in the form respecting

the suitability of the individual for registration

as a collector; and

(d) must be accompanied by the fee set out in

paragraph 7(b).

(2) The Minister may rely on a declaration referred to in

paragraph (1)(c) to determine whether an individual is

suitable to be registered as a collector.

### Actions by Minister respecting registrations

6 (1) Subject to subsection (2), the Minister may take one

or more of the following actions in respect of an

application for registration, or an existing registration,

as the case may be:

(a) issue or renew a registration;

(b) refuse to issue or renew a registration;

(c) suspend or terminate a registration;

(d) impose conditions on a registration and amend

the conditions;

(e) rescind conditions on a registration.

(2) Unless the Minister or an authorized officer of the

Minister considers it appropriate to consent, and

consents, to an applicant or registrant acting as a

collection agent or being employed or used as a

collection agent, the Minister must refuse to issue or

renew a registration under paragraph (1)(b) and must

terminate a registration under paragraph (1)(c), as the

case may be, if

(a) the applicant or registrant has been convicted of

an offence referred to in paragraph 78(a) or (b)

of the Act; or

(b) the applicant or registrant has, within the

previous 10 years, been bankrupt or has been an

officer or director of a corporation or a member

of a partnership that became bankrupt during

the period of the applicant’s or registrant’s

involvement unless, in each case, the creditors

in the bankruptcy have been paid in full.

(3) Without limiting the reasons for which the Minister

may take any action referred to in subsection (1), the

Minister may take an action under paragraph (1)(b) to

(d) if

(a) in the case of a registrant, the registrant has

contravened a condition of their registration; or

(b) based on the past or present conduct in any

jurisdiction of the applicant or registrant,

including, in the case of an application for

registration as a collection agency, and of the

existing registration of a collection agency, the

conduct of the officers and directors or partners

or sole proprietor or other interested persons in

respect of the agency, the Minister has

reasonable grounds to believe

(i) that the applicant is not suitable to be

registered or the registrant is not suitable to

remain registered, or

(ii) that it is in the public interest for conditions

to be imposed on the applicant’s or

registrant’s registration.

(4) For the purpose of determining whether a person is

suitable to be registered or remain registered as a

collection agency or whether an individual is suitable to

be registered or remain registered as a collector, the

Minister may request any document or information

(a) in the case of the registration of a collection

agency, from the applicant or registrant or an

authorized representative of the applicant or

registrant; or

(b) in the case of the registration of an individual as

a collector,

(i) from the collection agency that intends to

employ or use, or that employs or uses, the

individual to carry out the activities of a

collection agent, or

(ii) from the individual.

(5) The Minister may determine whether a person or

individual is suitable

(a) to be registered on receiving an application

under section 4 or 5; or

(b) to remain registered at any time while the

person or individual is registered.

(6) Before taking an action under paragraphs (1)(b) to

(d), the Minister must give the applicant or registrant an

opportunity to make representations unless the Minister

considers that it would be prejudicial to the public

interest to delay taking the action.

(7) The Minister must give written notice of an action

taken under subsection (1), together with reasons

(a) to the applicant or registrant; and

(b) in the case of an application for registration, or

the existing registration, of an individual as a

collector, to

(i) the collection agency that intends to employ

or use, or that employs or uses, the individual

to carry out the activities of a collection agent,

and

(ii) the individual.

(8) If the applicant or registrant has not been given an

opportunity to make representations, the Minister must

include in the written notice under subsection (7) notice

that the applicant or registrant may request in writing,

within 30 days of the date of the notice, an opportunity

to make representations and that the action be

reconsidered.

(9) The Minister may reconsider an action taken under

subsection (1).

### Fees

7 The following fees are payable for registration:

(a) for registration as a collection agency, $350;

(b) for registration as a collector, $75.

### Expiry of registrations

8 The registration of a person as a collection agency, and

the registration of an individual as a collector, expire on

June 30 following the day of registration.

### Termination of collector registrations

9 (1) The registration of an individual as a collector is

terminated immediately on the occurrence of either of

the following events:

(a) the person that employs or uses the collector

ceases to be registered as a collection agency;

(b) the collection agency that employs or uses the

individual as a collector ceases to employ or use

the individual as a collector.

(2) If a collection agency determines that an individual

that it employs or uses as a collector is not suitable to

remain registered as a collector, the collection agency

must immediately cease employing or using the

individual as a collector.

(3) A collection agency must, within 10 days after

ceasing to employ or use an individual as a collector for

any reason,

(a) give written notice to the Minister that it has

ceased to employ or use the individual as a

collector; and

(b) if it ceases to employ or use the individual as a

collector because of the application of

subsection (2), include the reason in the written

notice.

### C.O. 1978/235 repealed

10 Commissioner’s Order 1978/235 is repealed.

### Coming into force

11 This Regulation comes into force on the later of the

following days:

(a) the day on which section 7 of the Community

Services Statute Law Amendment Act (2023)

comes into force;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of

regulations under the Regulations Act .
