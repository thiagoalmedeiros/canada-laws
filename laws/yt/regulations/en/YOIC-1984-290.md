YOIC 1984/290

*Made under the Public Service Labour Relations Act*

## PUBLIC SERVICE STAFF RELATIONS ACT

## STAFF RELATIONS ACT

Pursuant to subsection 10(1) of the Public Service Staff

Relations Act and Commissioner’s Order 1970/196, the

Commissioner in Executive Council is pleased to and doth

hereby order as follows:

1 Authority is delegated to the Chairman of the

Yukon Public Service Staff Relations Board to fix the rate of

remuneration to Mr. V. Ready, appointed as Arbitrator in

the current dispute between the Yukon Territorial

Government and the Yukon Government Employees’

Union, at the rate of $500.00 per day for each day he is

engaged on work in connection with his appointment as

Arbitrator.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 19th

day of October, A.D., 1984.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
