# Temporary Authorization to Continue to Use Waters or Deposit Waste (Specified Quartz Mining Operators) Regulation

YOIC 2025/97

*Made under the Waters Act*

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation

“ decision document ” means a decision document within

the meaning of the Yukon Environmental and Socio-

economic Assessment Act (Canada) issued in respect of

a proposal referred to in paragraph 7.01(3)(c) of the

Quartz Mining Land Use Regulation ; « décision écrite »

“ exploration program ” means an exploration program

governed by a preexisting approved operating plan

within the meaning of subsection 7.01(1) of the Quartz

Mining Land Use Regulation ; « programme

d'exploration »

“ preexisting approved operating plan ” has the same

meaning as in subsection 7.01(1) of the Quartz Mining

Land Use Regulation ; « plan d'exploitation approuvé

préexistant »

“ preexisting licence ” means a licence that authorizes,

or authorized, a specified quartz mining operator to use

or permit the use of waters or to deposit or permit the

deposit of waste, or both, while carrying out exploration

activities under an exploration program, where the

licence expires during the period beginning on

January 1, 2025 and ending on December 31, 2026;

« permis préexistant »

“ specified quartz mining operator ” means a person to

whom this regulation applies under section 3.

« exploitant désigné pour l’exploitation du quartz »

### Purpose

2 The purposes of this Regulation are

(a) to promote continuity for specified quartz

mining operators by temporarily authorizing

them to continue to use or permit the use of

waters or to deposit or permit the deposit of

waste, or both, while carrying out exploration

activities that are within the scope of their

preexisting approved operating plans for their

exploration programs until decisions made on

their applications for renewals of their

preexisting licences are in effect;

(b) to mitigate harmful effects caused by

extraordinary delays affecting applications for

renewals of preexisting licences;

(c) to support the viability of a sustainable,

competitive and healthy quartz mining industry;

(d) to support the ability of decision makers to

undertake appropriate assessment,

consultation, regulatory and other measures in

a manner that upholds the essential socio-

economic and environmental values of Yukon

and respects the aboriginal and treaty rights

referred to in section 35 of the Constitution Act,

1982 ; and

(e) to recognize the importance of the Board’s

objects of providing for the conservation,

development and utilization of waters in a

manner that provides the optimum benefit from

them for all Canadians and for the residents of

Yukon in particular, by incorporating the

provisions of the preexisting licences issued by

the Board in the authorization.

### Application

3 This Regulation applies to the operator of an exploration

program

(a) to whom section 7.01 of the Quartz Mining Land

Use Regulation applies; and

(b) who has applied for the renewal of their

preexisting licence for the exploration program.

### Temporary authorization to continue to use waters or to deposit waste

4 (1) A specified quartz mining operator is temporarily

authorized to continue to use or permit the use of waters

or to deposit or permit the deposit of waste, or both, in

accordance with this section while carrying out

exploration activities within the scope of their

preexisting approved operating plan for their

exploration program.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), the authorization is

effective after the operator’s preexisting licence has

expired for any period during which section 7.01 of the

Quartz Mining Land Use Regulation allows the operator

to carry out exploration activities within the scope of

their preexisting approved operating plan for that

exploration program.

(3) Unless the authorization is cancelled under

section 6, the authorization expires immediately on the

earlier of the following days:

(a) the effective date of a decision set out in a

decision document issued in respect of the

operator’s proposal in relation to exploration

activities, referred to in paragraph 7.01(3)(c) of

the Quartz Mining Land Use Regulation , that the

project not proceed;

(b) the effective date of the decision of the Board on

the application for the renewal of the operator’s

preexisting licence.

(4) The authorization is subject to the following

conditions:

(a) despite the expiry of their preexisting licence,

the operator must, in continuing to use or

permit the use of waters or in continuing to

deposit or permit the deposit of waste, or both,

under the authorization, act in accordance with

the provisions of their preexisting licence,

including terms, conditions or restrictions set

out in the licence, taking into account any

modifications effected under section 5 or any

amendments effected under section 6, except to

the extent that to do so would conflict with

paragraph (b);

(b) if applicable to that operator, beginning at the

time the operator is required to carry out the

activities in conformity with the decision

document referred to in

subparagraph 7.01(3)(d)(ii) of the Quartz Mining

Land Use Regulation , the operator must, in

continuing to use or permit the use of waters or

in continuing to deposit or permit the deposit of

waste, or both, under the authorization, act in

conformity with that decision document,

including any terms, conditions and restrictions

set out in that decision document.

### Modification of conditions

5 Despite the expiry of a preexisting licence, if the

conditions of the licence would have been amended

under subsection 13(7) of the Act had the licence

remained in effect, then, for the purposes of

paragraph 4(4)(a), the conditions of the licence must be

read as though the conditions had been amended under

subsection 13(7) of the Act.

### Amendment or cancellation of authorization

6 The Minister may, after consulting with the Board, by

written notice to a specified quartz mining operator,

amend a condition of the operator’s authorization under

paragraph 4(4)(a), or cancel the operator’s authorization

under subsection 4(1),

(a) to deal with a water shortage in any water

management area; or

(b) where the amendment or cancellation appears

to the Minister to be in the public interest.

### Information to be included in public register

7 For each specified quartz mining operator, the following

shall be included in the file for the application for the

renewal of their licence that is in the register maintained

under section 23 of the Act:

(a) a notice that the operator is temporarily

authorized to continue to use or permit the use

of waters or to deposit or permit the deposit of

waste, or both, in accordance with this

Regulation;

(b) a notice of an amendment to a condition of, or

of the cancellation of, the authorization that is

effected under section 6.

### Conflict or inconsistency with Waters Regulation

8 This Regulation prevails to the extent of any conflict or

inconsistency between a provision of this Regulation and

a provision of the Waters Regulation .

### Authorization does not affect renewal of licence

9 Nothing in this Regulation is to be interpreted as

affecting

(a) an application for the renewal of a preexisting

licence; or

(b) the renewal of a preexisting licence.

### Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment Act (Canada)

10 Nothing in this Regulation is to be interpreted as

authorizing the use of waters or deposit of waste where

to do so would be inconsistent with the Yukon

Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment Act

(Canada).

### Repeal

11 This Regulation is repealed on December 31, 2028.
