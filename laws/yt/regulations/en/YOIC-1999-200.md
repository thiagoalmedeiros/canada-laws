YOIC 1999/200

*Made under the Lands Act*

Pursuant to section 12 of the Lands Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The lots in the Spruce Hill Subdivision listed in

Schedule A attached hereto are classed as country

residential and shall be sold at the prices listed in Schedule

A.

2 Lot 1119 on Cowley Lake Road is classed as rural

residential and may be sold for the price listed in Schedule A.

3 Notwithstanding section 48 of the Lands

Regulations, Lot 1119 shall be sold under a five year

agreement for sale.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 23rd

day of November, 1999.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
