## YUKON REGIONAL RELIEF LOAN PROGRAM

## REGULATION

### Definitions

1 In this Regulation

“agreement” means the following:

(a) the Funding Agreement between Her Majesty

the Queen in Right of Canada and Government

of Yukon that is attached as Schedule 1,

(b) the amendment to the agreement referred to

in paragraph (a) that is dated September 30, 2020

and is attached as Schedule 2,

(c) any further amendments to the agreement

referred to in paragraph (a) that are made

(i) by written approval, authorization or

consent as provided for in that agreement, or

(ii) under its clause 10.3,

(d) any further agreement that the Minister enters

into with Her Majesty the Queen in Right of

Canada respecting the program and the funds

provided for the program; « accord »

“Applicant” has the same meaning as in the

agreement; « demandeur »

“Loan” has the same meaning as in the agreement;

« prêt »

“program” means the Yukon Regional Relief Loan

Program as described in the agreement; « programme »

“RRRF Pool” has the same meaning as in the

agreement; « Fonds FARR »

“SME” has the same meaning as in the agreement;

« PME »

“Social Enterprise” has the same meaning as in the

agreement. « entreprise à vocation sociale »

### Objective

2 The objective of this Regulation is to establish the

Yukon Regional Relief Loan Program to provide for loans to

be made to eligible SMEs and Social Enterprises in

accordance with the agreement.

### Revolving fund

3 (1) The RRRF Pool is established as a revolving fund

whose limit is the amount provided for under the

agreement.

(2) The money in the RRRF Pool

(a) is the money received under the agreement

and the money received from Applicants in full

or partial repayment of their Loans under the

agreement; and

(b) may be paid from the RRRF Pool only as

provided for in the agreement.

### Minister to administer program

4 The administration of the program is assigned to the

Minister, who must administer it in accordance with the

agreement.

### Schedules 3 and 4

5 (1) A French translation of the agreement referred to

in paragraph (a) of the definition “agreement” in section 1

is attached as Schedule 3.

(2) A French translation of the agreement referred to

in paragraph (b) of the definition “agreement” in section 1

is attached as Schedule 4.
