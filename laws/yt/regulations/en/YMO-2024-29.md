YMO 2024/29

*Made under the Wildlife Act*

### Preamble

Whereas the Minister of Environment is of the opinion

that a prohibition on sheep hunting in the Donjek

management unit is urgently required for purposes of

conservation;

Therefore, pursuant to section 194 of the Wildlife Act ,

the Minister of Environment orders

1 The attached Sheep Hunting Prohibition Order —

Donjek is made.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

June 19, 2024

Minister of Environment/Ministre
