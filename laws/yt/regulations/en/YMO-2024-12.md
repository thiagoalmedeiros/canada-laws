## ANIMALS AND SPECIES REGULATION

### Animals

1 For the purpose of paragraph (b) of the definition

“animal” in section 1 of the Act, the species listed in

Schedule 1 are prescribed.

### Livestock

2 For the purpose of paragraph (b) of the definition

“livestock” in section 1 of the Act, the following are

prescribed as livestock:

(a) species of animals listed in Schedule 2;

(b) species of animals listed in Schedule A of the

Game Farm Regulations , in respect of an animal

of that species that is held in captivity for

commercial purposes.

### High-risk animals

3 For the purpose of the definition “high - risk animal” in

section 1 of the Act, an animal that belongs to a species

of animals listed in Schedule 3 is prescribed as a high-

risk animal.

### Allowed species

4 For the purpose of the definition “allowed species” in

section 1 of the Act, the following are prescribed as

allowed species:

(a) species of animals listed in Schedule 4;

(b) species of fish that are commercially available to

be purchased and possessed as aquarium fish.

### Restricted species

5 For the purpose of the definition “restricted species” in

section 1 of the Act, the following are prescribed as

restricted species:

(a) species of animals listed in Schedule 5;

(b) any animal that is the offspring resulting from

the crossing of two animals that do not belong

to the same species, if

(i) one of the two animals belongs to a species of

animal referred to in paragraph (a), and

(ii) neither of the two animals belongs to a

prohibited species.

### Prohibited species

6 For the purpose of the definition “prohibited species” in

section 1 of the Act, the following are prescribed as

prohibited species:

(a) Eurasian boar ( Sus scrofa except Sus scrofa

domesticus );

(b) an animal that is not livestock and does not

belong to an allowed species or a restricted

species;

(c) any animal that is the offspring resulting from

the crossing of two animals that do not belong

to the same species, if one of the two animals

belongs to a species referred to in paragraph (a)

or (b).
