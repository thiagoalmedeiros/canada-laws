*Made under the Assessment and Taxation Act*

## ORDER-IN-COUNCIL 2025/185

Pursuant to section 19 and subsection 23(1) of the

Assessment and Taxation Act , the Commissioner in

Executive Council orders

1 An Assessment Review Board, to be known as the

Yukon Assessment Review Board, is established for

all of Yukon.

2 The following persons are appointed as members

of the Yukon Assessment Review Board:

Christopher Alway – chair

Jacob Huffman – vice-chair

John Berg, Stacy Mitro, (Peter) Matthew Wilkinson

and Wesley Wirth – members.

3 The Yukon Assessment Review Board shall

commence sitting to hear complaints against the

assessment roll prior to February 15, 2026.

4 This order

(a) comes into force on the later of

(i) January 1, 2026, and

(ii) the day on which it is filed with the

registrar of regulation under the

Regulations Act ; and

(b) expires on December 31, 2026.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

September 25, 2025
