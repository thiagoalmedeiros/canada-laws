YOIC 2023/15

*Made under the Public Health and Safety Act*

## YUKON

## CANADA

## ORDER-IN-COUNCIL 2023/15

### Preamble

Whereas, on March 18, 2020, the chief medical officer

of health declared the existence of a public health

emergency related to the COVID-19 pandemic;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 4.3(6) of the Public

Health and Safety Act , the Minister has recommended

that the Commissioner in Executive Council declare the

end of the public health emergency related to the

COVID-19 pandemic;

Therefore, pursuant to subsection 4.3(7) of the Public

Health and Safety Act , the Commissioner in Executive

Council declares that:

the public health emergency related to the COVID-19

pandemic, declared to exist on March 18, 2020, has

ended.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

January 14, 2023
