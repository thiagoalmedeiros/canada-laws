YOIC 2003/209

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 The portion of the old Alaska Highway, Plan 40861

CLSR, 19296 LTO lying easterly of monument H1498

shown on the said plan and southerly of Block 4, Plan

43170 CLSR, 22370 LTO to the southeasterly extension of

the said Block 4 and northerly of the boundary of the

Alaska Highway, Plan 56901 CLSR, 34791 LTO, is hereby

closed and declared to no longer be highway.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 9th July 2003.
