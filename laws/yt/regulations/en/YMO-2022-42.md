YMO 2022/42

*Made under the Education Act*

Pursuant to the Education Act , the Minister of Education

orders

1 The attached Order to Amend Ministerial

Order 1991/01 is made.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,

August 11, 2022

Minister of Education/Ministre

## ORDER TO AMEND MINISTERIAL

## ORDER 1991/01

### Name change

1 Vanier Catholic Secondary School Council of Attendance

Area #9 is continued under the name of St. Francis of

Assisi Catholic Secondary School Council of Attendance

Area #9.

### Schedule amended

2 In Schedule A to Ministerial Order 1991/01

(a) the expression

(i) “ Vanier Catholic Secondary School Council of

Attendance Area #9 ” is repealed , and

(ii) “ St. Francis of Assisi Catholic Secondary

School Council of Attendance Area #9 ” is

### added in alphabetical order,

(b) the expression “ Whistle Bend Elementary

School Council of Attendance Area #30 ” is

### added in alphabetical order; and

(c) the following expressions are repealed:

(i) “ Chief Zzeh Gittlit School Council of

Attendance Area #15 ” ,

(ii) “ Grey Mountain Primary School Council of

Attendance Area #12 ” ,

(iii) “ Watson Lake School Council of Attendance

Area #11 ” ,

(iv) “ Nelnah Bessie John School Council of

Attendance Area #20 ” ,

(v) “ Ross River School Council of Attendance

Area #17 ” ,

(vi) “ St. Elias Community School Council of

Attendance Area #4 ” , and

(vii) “ Takhini Elementary School Council of

Attendance Area #18 ”.

### Dissolution of school councils

3 The school councils listed in paragraph 2(c) are

dissolved.

### Coming into force

4 This Order comes into force on the later of the

following days:

(a) August 18, 2022;

(b) the day on which it is filed with the registrar of

regulations under the Regulations Act .
