# Establishes that no part of Lot 1021, Quad 115 I/14, Plan 94836 CLSR, 2008-0123 LTO is deemed to be reserved to the Crown (Ft. Selkirk)

YOIC 2010/92

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

Pursuant to section 11 of the Territorial Lands (Yukon)

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows

1 No part of Lot 1021, Quad 115 I/14, Plan 94836

CLSR, 2008-0123 LTO is deemed under section 11 of the

Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act to be reserved to the Crown.

2 Section 1 applies only while the lot described in

that section is held by the Commissioner and the Selkirk

First Nation as tenants in common.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 07 May 2010.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon
