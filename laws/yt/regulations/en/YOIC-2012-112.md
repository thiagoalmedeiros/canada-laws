# Establishes that no part of described land (Quiet Lake) is deemed to be reserved for the Crown

YOIC 2012/112

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

Pursuant to section 11 of the Territorial Lands (Yukon)

Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders as

follows

1 1 If at any time before 2015 notification under

subsection 7(2) of the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act is

issued for the first time in respect of the property described

in the attached schedule, no part of the property (or, if the

notification applies to only a portion of the property, no

part of that portion) is at and after that time to be deemed

under section 11 of the Act to be reserved to the Crown.

2 Order-in-Council 2011/46 is revoked.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, June 21, 2012.

______________________________________________________

Commissioner of Yukon

## SCHEDULE

In the Yukon, in the vicinity of Quiet Lake, lying within

Quad 105 C/14, ALL THAT parcel of land more particularly

described as follows; all topographic features hereinafter

referred are according to Edition 1 of the Iron Creek Map

Sheet, produced at a scale of 1:50,000 by Surveys and

Mapping Branch, Department of Energy, Mines and

Resources, Canada; all bearings hereinafter mentioned are

astronomic and are referenced to the meridian through

“station 8” as shown on Plan 55624 in the Canada Lands

Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the

Land Titles Office at Whitehorse as 31693:

Parcel ID. No. Lease 105C14-4-720

COMMENCING at the intersection of the Ordinary High

Water Mark of the east shore of Quiet Lake and the

westerly production of the southerly boundary of Lease

105C14-4-720 (unsurveyed) according to documents in the

Lands Branch; Energy Mines & Resources at Whitehorse;

THENCE in a general northerly direction along the said

Ordinary High Water Mark, a distance of approximately

63.5 metres, to its intersection with the westerly

production of the northerly boundary of said Lease

105C14-4-720;

THENCE easterly in a straight line along the said

production of the northerly boundary of Lease 105C14-4-

720 and the northerly boundary of said Lease 105C14-4-

720 to a point, said point being 30.48 metres

perpendicularly distant from the said Ordinary High Water

Mark;

THENCE in a general southerly direction along a line

parallel to and 30.48 metres perpendicularly distant from

the said Ordinary High Water Mark, a distance of

approximately 63.5 metres, to its intersection with the said

southerly boundary of Lease 105C14-4-720;

THENCE westerly in a straight line along the said southerly

boundary of Lease 105C14-4-720 and the production

thereof, a distance of approximately 30.5 metres, to the

point of commencement;

SAID parcel containing approximately 0.19 hectares.

Parcel ID. No. Application 2003-0223

COMMENCING at the most westerly corner of Lot 38,

Group 855, marked by a C.L.S. ’69 Post, as shown on Plan

56931 in the said Records, a copy of which is filed in the

said Office as 34727;

THENCE 16º 57’ 15” according to said Plan, a distance of

64.53 meters, more or less, along the northwesterly

boundary of said Lot 38, Group 855, to the northwesterly

corner thereof, which is marked by a C.L.S. ’69 Post;

THENCE 277º 34’ 30”, a distance of approximately 31

metres, to an intersection with the Ordinary High Water

Mark of the east shore of Quiet Lake;

THENCE in a general southerly direction along the said

Ordinary High Water Mark, a distance of approximately 70

metres, to its intersection with a line, said line having a

bearing of 88º 29’ 00” and passing through the point of

commencement;

THENCE 88º 29’ 00”, a distance of approximately 32

metres, to the point of commencement;

SAID parcel containing approximately 0.20 hectares.
