YOIC 1985/228

*Made under the Medical Profession Act*

Pursuant to the provisions of subsections 18(6) and

62(1) of the Medical Profession Act , the Commissioner

in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 That for the purposes of subsection 18(5) of the

Act the penalty shall be $200.00.

(Section 1 amended by O.I.C. 2011/130)

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 11

day of October, A.D., 1985.
