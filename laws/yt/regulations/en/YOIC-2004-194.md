## YUKON ACT (CANADA)

### and

## YUKON ACT (CANADA)

Pursuant to sections 12 and 31 of the Lands Act a n d

section 46 of the Yukon Act (Canada), the Commissioner in

Executive Council orders as follows

1 The administration and control of the lands in

Yukon herein described is hereby relinquished to Her

Majesty the Queen in right of Canada as represented by the

Minister of Indian and Northern Affairs Canada

Block 312, Plan 69768 CLSR, Plan 75794 LTO,

Whitehorse;

Lots 30 and 31, Group 951, Plan 52352 CLSR,

Plan 27158 LTO;

Lot 35, Group 757, Plan 51337 CLSR, 25446 LTO;

and

Lot 61, Group 757, Plan 57794 CLSR, 37002 LTO.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this September 29th 2004.

Commissioner of Yukon
