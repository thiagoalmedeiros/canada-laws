YOIC 2006/13

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 13 of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows

1 All portions of any highway located within the area

near Johnson's Crossing shown by cross-hatching on the

attached map are hereby closed and declared to be

highways no longer.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 13th January 2006.

Minister of Highways and Public Works
