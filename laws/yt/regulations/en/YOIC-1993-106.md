YOIC 1993/106

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

1 Part 2 of the Employment Standards Act does not

apply to a firefighter employed by the City of

Whitehorse whose contract of employment is governed

by the collective agreement between the City of

Whitehorse and the International Association of

Firefighters, Local 2217.
