# Order Prohibiting Entry on Certain Lands (Category B Settlement Land)

Text obtained from the [official source](https://laws.yukon.ca/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2020/2020-0028/2020-0028_1.pdf) via the [A2AJ Canadian Laws dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/a2aj/canadian-laws). Always check the official version before relying on it.

YOIC 2020/28

*Made under the Placer Mining Act*

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to prohibit entry to each

Category B Settlement Land parcel that is listed in a

Schedule to this order to provide greater protection to

heritage and environmental resources on the parcel and

to support traditional and cultural use of the parcel by

the First Nation whose Settlement Land parcel it is.

### Definition

2 In this Order

“recorded claim” means

(a) a recorded placer claim that is in good standing,

acquired under the Yukon Placer Mining Act

(Canada) and continued under the Placer Mining

Act , or acquired under the Placer Mining Act ; or

(b) a recorded mineral claim that is in good

standing, acquired under the Yukon Quartz

Mining Act (Canada) and continued under the

Quartz Mining Act , or acquired under the Quartz

Mining Act ; « claim inscrit »

### Prohibition of Entry

3 A person must not enter on a Settlement Land Parcel

described in Schedules 1 to 11, for the purpose of

(a) locating a claim or prospecting for gold or other

precious minerals or stones under the Placer

Mining Act ; or

(b) locating a claim or prospecting or mining for

minerals under the Quartz Mining Act .

### Existing rights and interests

4 Section 3 does not apply to entry on a recorded claim by

the owner or holder of the recorded claim.
