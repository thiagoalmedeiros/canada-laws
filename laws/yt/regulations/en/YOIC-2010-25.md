## AMENDMENTS TO SELF-GOVERNING

## SCHEDULE

## LITTLE SALMON/CARMACKS FIRST NATION SELF-

### Section 13.6.6 of the Little Salmon/Carmacks First Nation Self-Government Agreement is repealed and replaced with the following

1 “13.6.6 The provisions in 13.6.4 are interim

provisions and shall expire March 31, 2012 or on

the effective date of the agreement concluded

pursuant to 13.6.1 and 13.6.2, whichever is earlier.

This interim period may be extended for such

further time as may be agreed to in writing by the

Minister on behalf of Canada, the Minister on behalf

of the Yukon and the Chief on behalf of the Little

Salmon/Carmacks First Nation.”
