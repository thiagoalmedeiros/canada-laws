## SITES)

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Order is to prohibit entry on the

Alligator Lake site, Deuces Wild Creek site, Gladstone site

and Thulsoo Mountain site for the purposes described in

section 3 to protect the heritage and archaeological

significance of these ice patch sites.

### Definitions

2 In this Order

“ Alligator Lake site ” means the area outlined in bold on

the map attached as Schedule 1; « site d’Alligator

Lake »

“ Deuces Wild Creek site ” means the area outlined in

bold on the map attached as Schedule 2; « site de

Deuces Wild Creek »

“ Gladstone site ” means the area outlined in bold on the

map attached as Schedule 3; « site Gladstone »

“ recorded claim ” means

(a) a recorded placer claim that is in good standing,

acquired under the Yukon Placer Mining Act

(Canada) and continued under the Placer Mining

Act , or acquired under the Placer Mining Act , or

(b) a recorded mineral claim that is in good

standing, acquired under the Yukon Quartz

Mining Act (Canada) and continued under the

Quartz Mining Act , or acquired under the Quartz

Mining Act ; « claim inscrit »

“ Thulsoo Mountain site ” means the area outlined in bold

on the map attached as Schedule 4. « site de Thulsoo

Mountain »

### Prohibition of entry

3 Subject to section 4, a person must not enter on the

Alligator Lake site, Deuces Wild Creek site, Gladstone site

or Thulsoo Mountain site for the purpose of

(a) locating a claim, or prospecting for gold or other

precious minerals or stones, under the Placer

Mining Act ; or

(b) locating a claim, or prospecting or mining for

minerals, under the Quartz Mining Act .

### Existing rights and interests

4 Section 3 does not apply to entry on a recorded claim by

the owner or holder of the recorded claim.
