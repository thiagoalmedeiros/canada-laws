## LOCAL IMPROVEMENT TAX REGULATION,

## 2010

1 To recover the cost of local improvements with

respect to the regions and projects listed in the attached

Schedule, a local improvement tax is levied on each

property for the local improvement associated with that

property consisting of the principal amount and the

interest specified for that property in the attached

Schedule.

2 The local improvement tax

(a) shall be shown as a separate item on the tax

notice for the property;

(b) is due and payable in the annual instalments

shown on the attached Schedule at the same time

as the property taxes under the Act are due and

payable;

(c) is part of the total tax payable for the

property; and

(d) may be paid in advance by paying the

outstanding balance of principal together with

interest accrued at the time of payment.
