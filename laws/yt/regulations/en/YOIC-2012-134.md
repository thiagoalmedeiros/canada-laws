*Made under the Corrections (Young Offenders) Act*

## YOUNG PERSONS OFFENCES ACT

## YOUNG PERSONS OFFENCES ACT

Pursuant to section 19 of the Corrections (Young

Offenders) Act and section 38 of the Young Persons

Offences Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders as follows

1 The Whitehorse Correctional Center is designated

as a place of temporary detention.

2 The Young Offenders Facility located at 728 Range

Road, Whitehorse, Yukon is designated as a place of

temporary detention, open custody and secure custody.

3 Any place of confinement under the control of the

Royal Canadian Mounted Police is designated as a place

of temporary detention and secure custody.

4 Orders in Council 1984/075, 1985/200, 1989/156,

1994/164 and 2003/155 are repealed.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, July 5, , 2012.
