YOIC 2019/149

*Made under the Criminal Code (Canada)*

## ORDER

Pursuant to the Criminal Code (Canada), the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders

### Approved program

1 For the purposes of section 802.1 of the Criminal

Code (Canada), the regulation of articled students by the

Law Society of Yukon, including its determination of the

extent to which articled students may appear or examine

or cross examine witnesses as an agent on summary

conviction offences, is an approved program.

(Section 1 amended by O.I.C. 2019/149)

### Section 1 amended

2 On the coming into force of the Legal Profession

Act, 2017, in section 1 of this Order, the expression

"students-at-law" is replaced, wherever it occurs, with

the expression "articled students".

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, September 19, 2019.
