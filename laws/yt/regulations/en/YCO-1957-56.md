YCO 1957/56

*Made under the Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgments Act*

### Preamble

WHEREAS by subsection (1) of the Reciprocal

Enforcement of Judgements Act , chapter 4 of the Ordinances

of the Yukon Territory, 1956 (2nd session), the

Commissioner of the Yukon Territory, when satisfied that

reciprocal provisions have been or will be made by a

province of Canada for the enforcement therein of

judgements given in the Territory, he may by order declare

it to be a reciprocating jurisdiction for the purposes of the

aforesaid Act; and

WHEREAS the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory is

satisfied that the Province of Saskatchewan will make

reciprocal provisions for the enforcement therein of

judgements within the meaning of the aforesaid Act given

in the Territory;

Now, THEREFORE, by virtue and in exercise of the

powers by the said Act vested in the Commissioner of the

Yukon Territory, the Commissioner is pleased to declare

and doth hereby order as follows:

1 The Province of Saskatchewan is hereby declared to

be a reciprocating jurisdiction for the purpose of the

Reciprocal Enforcement of Judgements Act.

2 This Order shall come into force and effect on and

from the 1st day of August, A.D. 1957.

IN TESTIMONY WHEREOF I, Frederick Howard

Collins, Commissioner of the Yukon Territory, have this

16th day of July, A.D. 1957, set my hand and caused the

seal of the Government of the Yukon Territory to be

hereunto affixed at the City of Whitehorse, in the Yukon

Territory.

______________________________

Commissioner of the Yukon
