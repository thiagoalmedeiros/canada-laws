YOIC 1986/91

*Made under the Employment Standards Act*

1 The Provinces of Alberta, British Columbia and

Saskatchewan are hereby declared to be reciprocating

jurisdictions for the purposes of the Employment

Standards Act .

2 The Director, Employment Standards, Department

of Labour of the province of Alberta, Director,

Employment Standards, Ministry of Labour of the

province of British Columbia and the Director, Labour

Standards, Department of Labour of the province of

Saskatchewan are designated the enforcement

authorities of those provinces respectively.

3 This Order is effective June 1, 1986.
