YOIC 2018/128

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

Pursuant to the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders

1 All of the land shown within Lots 2 to 7, Lot 12 on

CLSR Plan 50394, LTO Plan 24094 that, before the making

of this Order, was deemed under section 11 of the Act to be

reserved to the Crown is no longer deemed to be reserved

to the Crown under that section.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, June 28, 2018.
