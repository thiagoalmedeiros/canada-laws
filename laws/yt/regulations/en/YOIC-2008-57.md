YOIC 2008/57

*Made under the Territorial Lands (Yukon) Act*

Pursuant to paragraph 11(b) of the Territorial Lands

(Yukon) Act , the Commissioner in Executive Council orders

as follows

1 Any part of Lot 1000, Quad 95 D/12, Plan 90766

CLSR, 2006-0002 LTO lying within 30.48 metres (100 feet)

of the ordinary high water mark along the shore of Hulse

Lake in Yukon is to be included in the grant of this Lot.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 17 April 2008.
