YOIC 2019/127

*Made under the Education Labour Relations Act*

1 In this regulation

“ adjudicator ” means a person appointed under

section 65 of the Act; « arbitre de griefs »

“ National Joint Council ” means the National Joint

Council whose establishment was authorised by Order-

in-Council P.C. 3676 (Canada) dated May 16, 1944.

« Conseil national mixte »

2 An adjudicator is to be paid $730 per day for

performing their duties as an adjudicator.

3 An adjudicator is to be reimbursed for expenses that

are incurred in the performance of their duties as an

adjudicator in accordance with the Travel Directive of

the National Joint Council that is in force at the time

that the duties are performed.
