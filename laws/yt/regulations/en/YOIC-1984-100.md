YOIC 1984/100

*Made under the Highways Act*

Pursuant to section 12(4) of the Highways Act , the

Commissioner in Executive Council orders as follows:

1 The road shown shaded and traversing Lot 1084

Extension Quad 105D/14 on the attached Schedule A is

closed.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 27th

day of April, A.D., 1984.

______________________________

Administrator of the Yukon
