YCO 1970/225

*Made under the Public Service Labour Relations Act*

## PUBLIC SERVICE STAFF RELATIONS ACT

Pursuant to the provisions of the Public Service Staff

Relations Act , the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory

is pleased to and doth hereby order as follows:

The annexed “ Oath of Fidelity ” is hereby made and

established.

Dated at Whitehorse, in the Yukon Territory, this 22nd

day of July, A.D., 1970.

Commissioner of the Yukon/Commissaire

I, solemnly and

sincerely swear that I will faithfully and honestly and to

the best of my skill and knowledge fulfill the duties

which devolve upon me under the Public Service Staff

Relations Act by reason of my

(Chairman )

duties as (Vice Chairman ) of the Yukon Public Service

Staff

(a member )

Relations Board and where the person taking the oath

is a Board Member add “representing the interests of

(employee)

the (employer) “.
