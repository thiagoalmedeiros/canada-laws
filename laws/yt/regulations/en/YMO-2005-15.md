# Supreme Court and Territorial Court are courts for the purpose of proceedings under the Act

YMO 2005/15

*Made under the Interjurisdictional Support Orders Act*

Pursuant to section 2 of the Interjurisdictional Support

Orders Act , the Minister of Justice orders as follows

1 The Supreme Court and the Territorial Court are

designated as courts for the purpose of proceedings

under the Act.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this December 15 2005.

Minister of Justice/Ministre
