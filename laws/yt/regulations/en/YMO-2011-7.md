YMO 2011/7

*Made under the Oil and Gas Act*

Pursuant to paragraph 17(1)(b) of the Oil and Gas Act ,

the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources orders as

follows

1 The attached Ministerial Order Respecting the

Withdrawal from Disposition of Yukon Oil and Gas Lands

(Lhutsaw Wetland Habitat Protection Area) is made.

2 Ministerial Order 2007/07 is revoked.

3 This Order comes into force on the later of the day

it is made and September 1, 2011.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon, this 31 August 2011.

______________________________________________________

Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources

## MINISTERIAL ORDER RESPECTING THE

## WITHDRAWAL FROM DISPOSITION OF

## YUKON OIL AND GAS LANDS (LHUTSAW

## WETLAND HABITAT PROTECTION AREA)

### Purpose

1 The purpose of this Ministerial Order is to withdraw

certain Yukon oil and gas lands from disposition to

facilitate the maintenance of the Lhutsaw Wetland Habitat

Protection Area.

### Interpretation

2 In this Order, “Lhutsaw Wetland Habitat Protection

Area” means the area shown by that name on

Administrative Plan CLSR 86458, being

(a) part of Lot 1006, Quad 115 1/15 (Selkirk First

Nation Land Selection R-3A), Plan 87217 CLSR,

2003-0071 LTO, and

(b) Lot 1017, Quad 115 1/10 (Selkirk First Nation

Land Selection S-122B), Plan 83799 CLSR, 2000-

0168 LTO and certain vacant land. « Habitat

protégé du marais Lhutsaw »

### Lands withdrawn from disposition

3 The Yukon oil and gas lands described in paragraph

(b) of the definition “Lhutsaw Wetland Habitat Protection

Area” in section 2 are withdrawn from disposition under

the Oil and Gas Act .

(Section 3 amended by M.O. 2016/24)
