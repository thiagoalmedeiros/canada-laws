## ORDER APPROVING AN AMENDMENT

## TO THE LITTLE SALMON/CARMACKS

Pursuant to section 3 of the First Nations (Yukon) Self-

Government Act and clause 6.2.2 of the Little

Salmon/Carmacks First Nation Self-Government

Agreement, the Commissioner in Executive Council

orders

1 The amendment to the Little Salmon/Carmacks First

Nation Self-Government Agreement set out in the

attached Schedule is approved.

Dated at Whitehorse, Yukon,
