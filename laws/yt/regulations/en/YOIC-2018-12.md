## ORDER TO ESTABLISH CONRAD

## CAMPGROUND

### Interpretation

1 In this Order

“Conrad Campground” means land disposition

number 2008-0009 comprising approximately 46.9

hectares, more particularly described in the attached

Schedule, of Lot 17 (REM) and Lot 18 (REM), Group

754, LTO FB 7868, CLSR FB 7868. « terrain de camping

Conrad »

### Establishment of park

2 Conrad Campground is established as a recreation

park.

### Purpose of park

3 The purpose of Conrad Campground is to provide a

destination campground.

### Schedule amended

4 The Schedule to the Act is amended by adding the

following:

5.01 Conrad Campground Recreation park To provide a

destination

campground
