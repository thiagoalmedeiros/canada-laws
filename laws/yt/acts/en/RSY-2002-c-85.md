## FARO MINE ACT

### Loans to Curragh Inc.

1 (1) The Minister of Finance is hereby authorized to

lend sums not exceeding $5,000,000 in the aggregate

to Curragh Inc.

(2) On behalf of the Government of the Yukon, the

Minister may execute agreements and other documents

for making the loans referred to in subsection (1) and

for securing their repayment.
