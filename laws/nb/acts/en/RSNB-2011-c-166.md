2011, c.166

Deposited May 13, 2011

### Great Seal of Province

1 There shall be a Great Seal of the Province, which the Lieutenant-Governor in Council may from time to time change.

### Changing of Great Seal

2 Whenever the Lieutenant-Governor in Council changes the Great Seal, he or she shall issue a proclamation under his or her hand and seal directing when the change takes effect, specifying as far as possible the changes made and describing the Seal to be used from then on.

### Continued use of present Great Seal

3 Until changed under the provisions of this Act, the Great Seal currently in use shall continue to be the Great Seal of the Province.
