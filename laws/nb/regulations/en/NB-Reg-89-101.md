NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 89-101

under the Motor Vehicle Act (O.C. 89-564)

Filed August 1, 1989

Under section 169.3 of the Motor Vehicle Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

### Citation

1 This Regulation may be cited as the School Crossing Guards and Flagpersons Regulation - Motor Vehicle Act.

### Signs – cross walks

2 The design, specifications and placement of signs to be placed at cross walks at which school crossing guards direct children across a roadway shall be those set out in the manual and specifications referred to in section 115 of the Motor Vehicle Act.

### Stop sign of school crossing guards

3 A stop sign used by school crossing guards shall be a hand-held octagon shape with a minimum size of 30 cm by 30 cm.

### Traffic control device

4 A traffic control device used by a flagperson shall be a paddle of a minimum size of 60 cm by 60 cm that displays the word “Stop” on one side and “Slow” on the other side and that is reflectorized at night.

### Blaze orange clothing

5 School crossing guards and flagpersons shall wear a blaze orange vest, shirt or jacket that is not covered by any other clothing or article and that is reflectorized at night.

### Commencement

6 This Regulation comes into force on August 15, 1989.
