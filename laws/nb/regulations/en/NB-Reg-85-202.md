NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 85-202

under the Off-Road Vehicle Act (O.C. 85-1011)

Filed December 23, 1985

Under section 38 of the Off-Road Vehicle Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Fees Regulation - Off-Road Vehicle Act.

2 The fees payable under the Act are:

| (a) for registration of a dirt bike, a dune buggy, a snow bike or an amphibious machine | $18 |
| --- | --- |
| (b) for registration of all other off-road vehicles | $45 |
| (c) for the issuance or renewal of a dealer’s licence | $38 |
