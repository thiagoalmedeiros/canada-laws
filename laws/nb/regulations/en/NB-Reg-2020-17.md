NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 2020-17

under the Off-Road Vehicle Act (O.C. 2020-47)

Filed March 16, 2020

Under section 38 of the Off-Road Vehicle Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

### Citation

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Mount Carleton Grooming Hub Regulation – Off-Road Vehicle Act.

### Definition of “Act”

2 In this Regulation, “Act” means the Off-Road Vehicle Act.

### Mount Carleton Grooming Hub

3 For the purposes of subsection 7.1(3) of the Act, the area known as the Mount Carleton Grooming Hub is described and identified on the map attached as Schedule A.
