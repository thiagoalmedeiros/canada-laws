NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 83-62

under the Financial Administration Act (O. C. 83-275)

Filed April 15, 1983

Under section 56 of the Financial Administration Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Board, makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Residence Rental Regulation - Financial Administration Act.

2 Rental agreements with employees for residential property of the Crown shall be entered into by the Department of Transportation and Infrastructure.

3 Agreements under section 2 shall be signed on behalf of the Department of Transportation and Infrastructure by the Minister of Transportation and Infrastructure or an employee of the Department of Transportation and Infrastructure designated by the Minister.

4 Regulation 77-75 under the Financial Administration Act is repealed.
