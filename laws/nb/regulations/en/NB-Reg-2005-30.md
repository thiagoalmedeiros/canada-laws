NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 2005-30

under the King’s Printer Act (O.C. 2005-180)

Filed June 29, 2005

Under section 10 of the King’s Printer Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

### Citation

1 This Regulation may be cited as the General Regulation - King’s Printer Act.

### Fees payable for purchase of items
