NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 94-91

under the Highway Act (O.C. 94-495)

Filed July 27, 1994

Under section 67 of the Highway Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Controlled Access Highways in Kent County Regulation - Highway Act.

2 The highways or portions of highways in Kent County in Schedule A are designated as controlled access highways under the categories specified in the Schedule.

3 This Regulation comes into force on August 15, 1994.

## SCHEDULE A

1 All that portion of Route 11 from the boundary between Westmorland County and Kent County to Route 134 at Kouchibouguac located in Dundas Parish, Wellington Parish, Richibucto Parish, Saint-Charles Parish, Saint-Louis Parish and Carleton Parish, Kent County, designated as a level II controlled access highway and being more particularly bounded and described as follows:
