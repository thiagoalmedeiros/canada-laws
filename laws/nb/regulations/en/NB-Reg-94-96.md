NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 94-96

under the Highway Act (O.C. 94-500)

Filed July 27, 1994

Under section 67 of the Highway Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Controlled Access Highways in Restigouche County Regulation - Highway Act.

2 The highways or portions of highways in Restigouche County in Schedule A are designated as controlled access highways under the categories specified in the Schedule.

3 This Regulation comes into force on August 15, 1994.
