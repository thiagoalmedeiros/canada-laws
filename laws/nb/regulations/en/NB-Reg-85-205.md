NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 85-205

under the Financial Administration Act (O.C. 85-1014)

Filed December 23, 1985

Under section 56 of the Financial Administration Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Board, makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Fees for Services Provided under the Off-Road Vehicle Act Regulation - Financial Administration Act.

2 In this Regulation

“Division” means the Motor Vehicle Division as constituted under the Motor Vehicle Act.(Direction)

2.1 The fee for the issuance of a number plate of an off-road vehicle is $25.

3 The fee for replacement of a number plate of an off-road vehicle is $25.

4 The fee for replacement of a registration certificate or decal of an off-road vehicle is $20.

5 The fee for a search of a record of the Division relating to an off-road vehicle is eight dollars.

6 The fee for providing a certified copy of a record of the Division relating to an off-road vehicle is eight dollars.
