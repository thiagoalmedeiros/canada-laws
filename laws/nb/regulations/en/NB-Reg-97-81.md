NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 97-81

under the Financial Administration Act (O.C. 97-562)

Filed June 26, 1997

Under section 56 of the Financial Administration Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Board, makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Fees for Assessment Services under the Judicature Act Regulation - Financial Administration Act.

2 The fees for the provision of services by the Minister of Families and Children for a report under section 11.4 of the Judicature Act are as follows:

(a) for social assessments - fifty dollars per hour plus expenses;

(b) for psychological assessments - eighty dollars per hour plus expenses;

(c) for psychiatric assessments - one hundred and twenty dollars per hour plus expenses.

3 This Regulation comes into force on July 1, 1997.
