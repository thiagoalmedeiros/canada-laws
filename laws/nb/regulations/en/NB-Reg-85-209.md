NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 85-209

under the Financial Administration Act (O.C. 85-1016)

Filed December 23, 1985

Under section 56 of the Financial Administration Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Board, makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Fees for Services Provided under the Limited Partnership Act Regulation - Financial Administration Act.

2 A fee of forty dollars shall be paid for a search of a name, other than a search of a name referred to in subsection 11(2) of New Brunswick Regulation 84-196 under the Limited Partnership Act.

2.1 A fee of ten dollars shall be paid for a photocopy provided by the registrar of a document under the Limited Partnership Act.

2.2 A fee of twenty dollars shall be paid for a certificate of fact provided by the registrar under the Limited Partnership Act.

3 This Regulation comes into force on January 1, 1986.
