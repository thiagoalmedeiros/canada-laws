NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 2013-38

under the Species at Risk Act (O.C. 2013-143)

Filed May 22, 2013

Under section 18 of the Species at Risk Act, the Minister makes the following Regulation:

### Citation

1 This Regulation may be cited as the List of Species at Risk Regulation - Species at Risk Act.

### Listing

2 The wildlife species listed in Schedule A are classified as set out in Schedule A.

### Commencement

3 This Regulation comes into force on June 3, 2013.
