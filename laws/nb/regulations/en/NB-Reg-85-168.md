NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 85-168

under the Registry Act (O.C. 85-822)

Filed October 11, 1985

Under section 71 of the Registry Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Location of Registry Offices Regulation - Registry Act.

2 The registry office for each county is located in The Town of St. Stephen.
