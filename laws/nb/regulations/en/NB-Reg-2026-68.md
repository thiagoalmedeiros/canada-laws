NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 2026-68

under the Enforcement of Canadian Judgments Act (O.C. 2026-251)

Filed November 18, 2026

Under section 17 of the Enforcement of Canadian Judgments Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the following Regulation:

### Citation

1 This Regulation may be cited as the General Regulation – Enforcement of Canadian Judgments Act.

### Definition of “Act”

2 In this Regulation, “Act” means the Enforcement of Canadian Judgments Act.

### Prescribed orders

3 For the purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition “Canadian civil protection order” in section 1 of the Act, an order or a portion of an order concerning an intimate image made in a civil proceeding by a court of a province or territory of Canada other than New Brunswick that requires a person to do any of the following is prescribed:

(a) refrain from distributing the intimate image or threatening to distribute the intimate image;

(b) destroy or delete all copies of the intimate image in the person’s possession or control;

(c) remove the intimate image or have the intimate image removed from any online platform;

(d) de-index the intimate image or have the intimate image de-indexed from any search engine; and

(e) do or refrain from doing any other thing for the purpose of making the intimate image unavailable to others.

### Affidavit

4 An affidavit referred to in paragraph 5(1)(b) of the Act shall contain the following statements and information:

(a) the name and address of the deponent;

(b) a statement indicating that the deponent is the enforcing party or the solicitor, agent or employee of the enforcing party, as the case may be;

(c) a statement indicating that the judgment is a Canadian judgment and a statement of the facts that establish that the judgment is a Canadian judgment;

(d) if applicable, a statement indicating that the judgment is a Canadian tax judgment and a statement of the facts that establish that the judgment is a Canadian tax judgment;

(e) if applicable, a statement indicating that the judgment is a Canadian civil protection order and a statement of the facts that establish that the judgment is a Canadian civil protection order;

(f) if applicable, a statement indicating that the judgment is against an individual who resides in New Brunswick and indicating whether the individual took part in the proceeding or did not take part in the proceeding;

(g) if the judgment is against an individual who resides in New Brunswick and the individual did not take part in the proceeding, a statement indicating that the judgment does not enforce either of the following:

(i) a contract for the purchase of consumer goods or services within New Brunswick; or

(ii) a contract of employment under which the individual’s place of employment is in New Brunswick;

(h) if the judgment requires a person to pay money, a statement indicating that the judgment is a final judgment;

(i) the date on which the judgment became enforceable in the province or territory where it was made;

(j) if applicable, the date on which the time limit for enforcement applicable in the province or territory where the judgment was made expires; and

(k) if applicable, a statement indicating that an order staying or limiting the enforcement of the judgment has been made in the province or territory where the judgment was made.

### Fee

5 For the purposes of subsection 5(1) of the Act, the fee for submitting a Canadian judgment for registration is $50.

### Commencement

6 This Regulation comes into force on December 1, 2026.
