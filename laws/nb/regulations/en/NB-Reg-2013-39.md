NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 2013-39

under the Species at Risk Act (O.C. 2013-144)

Filed May 22, 2013

Under section 76 of the Species at Risk Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, makes the following Regulation:

### Citation

1 This Regulation may be cited as the Prohibitions Regulation - Species at Risk Act.

### Prohibitions

2 The prohibitions under section 28 of the Species at Risk Act apply to the listed wildlife species set out in Schedule A.

### Commencement

3 This Regulation comes into force on June 3, 2013.

## SCHEDULE A

BIRDS

Duck, Harlequin (Histrionicus histrionicus) Eastern population

Eagle, Bald (Haliaeetus leucocephalus)

Falcon anatum/tundrius, Peregrine (Falco peregrinus anatum/tundrius)

Plover melodus subspecies, Piping (Charadrius melodus melodus)

REPTILES

Sea Turtle, Leatherback (Dermochelys coriacea) Atlantic population

ARTHROPODS

Ringlet, Maritime (Coenonympha nipisiquit)

VASCULAR PLANTS

Aster, Gulf of St. Lawrence (Symphyotrichum laurentianum)

Lousewort, Furbish’s (Pedicularis furbishiae)

Pinedrops (Pterospora andromedea)

Quillwort, Prototype (Isoetes prototypus)

Twayblade, Southern (Listera australis)
