NEW BRUNSWICK REGULATION 82-73

under the Judicature Act (O.C. 82-340) and the Provincial Offences Procedure Act (O.C. 82-340)

Filed April 29, 1982

1 Under section 73 of the Judicature Act, and section 147 of the Provincial Offences Procedure Act, the Lieutenant-Governor in Council makes the Rules of Court of New Brunswick, as attached, excepting

(a) Rule 62.01(b)

(b) Rule 63

(c) Rule 64.01(a)

(d) Rule 65

(e) Rule 72

and the Forms relating thereto.

2 Regulation 90 under the Judicature Act is repealed.

3 This Regulation comes into force on June 1, 1982.
