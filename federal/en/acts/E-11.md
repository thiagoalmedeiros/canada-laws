# Canadian Environment Week Act

*An Act respecting Canadian Environment Week*

E-11 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2002-12-31

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-11/)

## Short title

**1** This Act may be cited as the [Canadian Environment Week Act](E-11.md).

<sub>History: 1970-71-72, c. 28, s. 1</sub>

## Canadian Environment Week

**2** Throughout Canada, in every year, the week that includes June 5 shall be kept and observed under the name of Canadian Environment Week.

<sub>History: R.S., 1985, c. E-11, s. 2; R.S., 1985, c. 10 (1st Supp.), s. 2</sub>
