# Language Skills Act

*An Act respecting language skills*

L-6.2; 362013 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-09-21

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-6.2/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## Short Title

### Short title

**1** This act may be cited as the [Language Skills Act](L-6.2.md).

## Language Skills

### Requirements

**2** Any person appointed to any of the following offices must, at the time of his or her appointment, be able to speak and understand clearly both official languages:

- **(a)** the Auditor General of Canada, appointed pursuant to subsection 3(1) of the [Auditor General Act](A-17.md);

- **(b)** the Chief Electoral Officer, appointed pursuant to subsection 13(1) of the [Canada Elections Act](E-2.01.md);

- **(c)** the Commissioner of Official Languages for Canada, appointed pursuant to subsection 49(1) of the [Official Languages Act](O-3.01.md);

- **(d)** the Privacy Commissioner, appointed pursuant to subsection 53(1) of the [Privacy Act](P-21.md);

- **(e)** the Information Commissioner, appointed pursuant to subsection 54(1) of the [Access to Information Act](A-1.md);

- **(f)** the Senate Ethics Officer, appointed pursuant to section 20.1 of the [Parliament of Canada Act](P-1.md);

- **(g)** the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner, appointed pursuant to subsection 81(1) of the [Parliament of Canada Act](P-1.md);

- **(h)** the Commissioner of Lobbying, appointed pursuant to subsection 4.1(1) of the [Lobbying Act](L-12.4.md);

- **(i)** the Public Sector Integrity Commissioner, appointed pursuant to subsection 39(1) of the [Public Servants Disclosure Protection Act](P-31.9.md);

- **(j)** the President of the Public Service Commission, appointed pursuant to subsection 4(5) of the [Public Service Employment Act](P-33.01.md);

- **(k)** the Parliamentary Budget Officer, appointed under subsection 79.1(1) of the [Parliament of Canada Act](P-1.md).
