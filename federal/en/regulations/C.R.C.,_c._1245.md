# Grosse Isle, P.Q., Prohibited Place Order

*Order Declaring that Grosse Isle, P.Q., Is a Prohibited Place*

C.R.C., c. 1245 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-08-19

Enabling authority: [SECURITY OF INFORMATION ACT](../acts/O-5.md)

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1245/)

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as [Grosse Isle, P.Q., Prohibited Place Order](C.R.C.,_c._1245.md).

## Declaration

**2** Grosse Isle, County of Montmagny, Province of Quebec is hereby declared to be a prohibited place within the meaning of the [Foreign Interference and Security of Information Act](../acts/O-5.md).

<sub>History: 2001, c. 41, s. 39; 2024, c. 16, s. 57</sub>

## Application

**3** This Order does not apply to over-flying aircraft.
