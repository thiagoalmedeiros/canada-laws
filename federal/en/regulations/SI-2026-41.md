# Order Transferring from the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Department of Employment and Social Development the Control and Supervision of the Supply Chains Act Taskforce

SI/2026-41 · Consolidated: 2026-07-21 · Last amended: 2026-07-09

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2026-07-29

P.C. 2026-702 2026-07-09

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2026-41/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-_hq_1781706633] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-_hq_1781706654], transfers from the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Department of Employment and Social Development the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration in the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness known as the Supply Chains Act Taskforce.
