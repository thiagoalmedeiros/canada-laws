# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the Appropriate Minister for the Canada Lands Company Limited for the Purposes of the Financial Administration Act

SI/2025-92 · Consolidated: 2025-08-11 · Last amended: 2025-07-25

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2025-08-13

P.C. 2025-575 2025-07-25

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-92/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph (d) of the definition *appropriate Minister* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752161143] and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition *appropriate Minister* in subsection 83(1) of that Act,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2011-585 of May 18, 2011[^fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752160954]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister for the Canada Lands Company Limited for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752161143].
