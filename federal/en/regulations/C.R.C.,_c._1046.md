# Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels Direction

*Direction Made Pursuant to Section 224 of the [National Defence Act](../acts/N-5.md) Respecting Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels*

C.R.C., c. 1046 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Direction may be cited as the Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels Direction.

## Application

**2** It is hereby directed that the Government Vessels Discipline Act shall apply to civilian crews engaged for service on auxiliary vessels of the Canadian Forces.
