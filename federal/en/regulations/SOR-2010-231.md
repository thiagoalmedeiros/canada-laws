# Annual Statement (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations

SOR/2010-231 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-02-06

Enabling authority: [COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](../acts/C-41.01.md)

Registration: 2010-10-28

P.C. 2010-1328 2010-10-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-231/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance pursuant to subsections 292(2)[^fn_1a] and 463(1)[^fn_1b] of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../acts/C-41.01.md)[^fn_1c], hereby makes the annexed [Annual Statement (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations](SOR-2010-231.md).

## Contents of Annual Statement

**1** The prescribed statements referred to in subsection 292(2) of the Cooperative Credit Associations Act are

- **(a)** a balance sheet as at the end of the financial year;

- **(b)** a statement of income for the financial year;

- **(c)** a statement of cash flows for the financial year;

- **(d)** a statement of changes in equity for the financial year; and

- **(e)** a statement of comprehensive income for the financial year.

<sub>History: SOR/2014-273, s. 25(F); SOR/2015-30, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on June 1, 2011.
