# Prohibited Activities Respecting Real Property (Foreign Banks) Regulations

SOR/2001-411 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [BANK ACT](../acts/B-1.01.md)

Registration: 2001-10-04

P.C. 2001-1782 2001-10-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-411/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 978[^footnotea_e]of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md)[^footnoteb_e], hereby makes the annexed [Prohibited Activities Respecting Real Property (Foreign Banks) Regulations](SOR-2001-411.md).

## Prohibited Activities

### Prohibited activities

**1** Paragraphs 510(1)(a) and (b) of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md) apply in respect of the holding, managing and other dealing with real property in Canada by a foreign bank or an entity associated with a foreign bank if that holding, managing or other dealing involves the making or acquiring of loans or advances on security of real property in Canada.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^footnote1star_e] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 514 of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md), as enacted by section 132 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../acts/F-11.1.md), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes into force.
