# Excise Act Licence Fees Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing Fees for Licences Issued Under the Excise Act*

C.R.C., c. 571 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [EXCISE ACT](../acts/E-14.md)

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._571/)

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Excise Act Licence Fees Regulations](C.R.C.,_c._571.md).

## Fees

**2** Every person to whom a licence is granted under the [Excise Act](../acts/E-14.md) for a purpose set out in Column I of an item of the schedule shall pay the annual fee set out in Column II of that item.
