# Nova BUS Corporation Exemption Order

SOR/96-446 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY ACT](../acts/M-10.01.md)

Registration: 1996-09-17

P.C. 1996-1455 1996-09-17

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-446/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 9(1)(c) of the [Motor Vehicle Safety Act](../acts/M-10.01.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby makes the annexed [Nova BUS Corporation Exemption Order](SOR-96-446.md), being of the opinion that conformity with certain prescribed standards would impede the development of new kinds of buses.

## Exemption

**1** Six hundred and fifty units of Nova LFS low-floor model buses, manufactured by Nova BUS Corporation, are exempt from the requirements in subsections 217(11), (14), (17) and (19) of Schedule IV to the [Motor Vehicle Safety Regulations](C.R.C.,_c._1038.md) that are applicable to push-out windows, for a period of two years beginning on the day on which this Order is registered under the [Statutory Instruments Act](../acts/S-22.md).

## Coming into Force

**2** This Order comes into force on September 17, 1996.
