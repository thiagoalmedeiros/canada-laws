# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the Minister for the Purposes of the National Housing Act

SI/2023-44 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-07-26

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL HOUSING ACT](../acts/N-11.md)

Registration: 2023-08-16

P.C. 2023-774 2023-07-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-44/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the [National Housing Act](../acts/N-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-25XX_hq_1689942661],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2021-949 of October 26, 2021[^fn_81000-3-25XX_hq_1689942674]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
