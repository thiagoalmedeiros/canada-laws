# Privacy Act Extension Order, No. 3

SOR/2021-174 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-07-13

Enabling authority: [PRIVACY ACT](../acts/P-21.md)

Registration: 2021-07-14

P.C. 2021-725 2021-07-13

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-174/)

His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 12(3) of the [Privacy Act](../acts/P-21.md)[^fn_81000-2-4561_hq_29053], makes the annexed [Privacy Act Extension Order, No. 3](SOR-2021-174.md).

## Extension of right of access

**1** The right to be given access to personal information under subsection 12(1) of the [Privacy Act](../acts/P-21.md) is extended to include all individuals outside Canada to whom that right has not been extended previously.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the first anniversary of the day on which it is made.
