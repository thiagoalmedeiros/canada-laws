# Statistics Canada Census-Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order

SI/2010-45 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2010-06-17

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../acts/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2010-07-07

P.C. 2010-773 2010-06-17

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2010-45/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 20 of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8010], hereby approves the exclusion from the application of the definitions ***deployment*** and ***internal appointment process*** in subsection 2(1), paragraphs 22(2)(a) to (c)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8011] and sections 40, 41[^fn_612624-e_hq_8012], 48, 51 to 53[^fn_612624-e_hq_8013], 57, 59 and 62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8014] and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term basis, other than other persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of that Act.

Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply certain provisions of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8010] to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8014] and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term basis, except for other persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of that Act;

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(2) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8010], the Public Service Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8010], hereby excludes from the application of the definitions ***deployment*** and ***internal appointment process*** in subsection 2(1), and of paragraphs 22(2)(a) to (c)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8011], and sections 40, 41[^fn_612624-e_hq_8012], 48, 51 to 53[^fn_612624-e_hq_8013], 57, 59 and 62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_612624-e_hq_8014] and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term basis, except for other persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of that Act.

This Order may be cited as the Statistics Canada Census-Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order.

Ottawa, May 11, 2010

MARIA BARRADOS
