# Solicitor General of Canada Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/95-59 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1995-05-17

P.C. 1995-698 1995-04-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-95-59/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)[^fn_SI-95-59_e_hq_6610] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Solicitor General of Canada to prescribe by order the fee to be paid by any user to whom any pardon services are provided by the National Parole Board.
