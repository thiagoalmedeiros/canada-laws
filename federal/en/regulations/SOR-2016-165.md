# Candles Regulations

SOR/2016-165 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-06-22

Enabling authority: [CANADA CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY ACT](../acts/C-1.68.md)

Registration: 2016-06-22

P.C. 2016-592 2016-06-21

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-165/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](../acts/C-1.68.md)[^fn_81200-2-TEXTILE_hc], makes the annexed [Candles Regulations](SOR-2016-165.md).

## Requirement

### Spontaneous reignition

**1** A candle must not reignite spontaneously after it is extinguished.

## Repeal

**2** \[Repeal\]

## Coming into Force

### Registration

**3** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
