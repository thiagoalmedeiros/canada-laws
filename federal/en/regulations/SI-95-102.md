# Order Transferring to the Minister of National Revenue, the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Industry under the Tax Rebate Discounting Act

SI/95-102 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 1995-09-20

P.C. 1995-1481 1995-08-30

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-95-102/)

Whereas the expression “Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs” in the definition ***Minister*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](../acts/T-3.md) was replaced by the expression “Minister of Industry” pursuant to paragraph 62(1)(t) of the Department of Industry Act[^fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6600], which Act was brought into force by Order in Council P.C. 1995-536 of March 29, 1995[^fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6601];

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), is pleased hereby to transfer from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of National Revenue the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Industry under the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](../acts/T-3.md).
