# Order Designating the Minister of Canadian Heritage as Minister for purposes of the Act

SI/99-128 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FOREIGN PUBLISHERS ADVERTISING SERVICES ACT](../acts/F-29.6.md)

Registration: 1999-11-10

P.C. 1999-1863 1999-10-21

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-99-128/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in section 2 of the [Foreign Publishers Advertising Services Act](../acts/F-29.6.md)[^fn_SI-99-128_e_hq_6453], hereby designates the Minister of Canadian Heritage, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
