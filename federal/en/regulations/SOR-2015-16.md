# Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1

SOR/2015-16 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-02-11

Enabling authority: [ECONOMIC ACTION PLAN 2014 ACT, NO. 1](../acts/E-0.8.md)

Registration: 2015-01-30

P.C. 2015-39 2015-01-29

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-16/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1[^fn_a], makes the annexed Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1.

## General

### Day for purposes of section 144

**1** The day for the purposes of section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1[^fn_1] is fixed as June 1, 2019.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^fn_Ind3BE3_hq_15215] This Order comes into force on the first day on which sections 111, 139 and 140 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1](../acts/E-0.8.md)[^fn_Ind3BE3_hq_15217] are all in force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
