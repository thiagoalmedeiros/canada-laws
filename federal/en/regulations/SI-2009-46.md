# Order Designating the Indian Residential Schools Truth and Reconciliation Commission as a Department and the Chairperson as the Deputy Head for Purposes of the Act

SI/2009-46 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-01-01

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../acts/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2009-06-24

P.C. 2009-935 2009-06-09

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2009-46/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***department*** and paragraph (b) of the definition ***deputy head*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^footnotea_e] hereby

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2008-799 of April 25, 2008[^fn_SI-2009-45_e_hq_13550], effective July 1, 2009;

**(b) and (c)** \[Repealed, SI/2009-46\]

**(d)** repeals paragraphs (b) and (c), effective January 1, 2016.
