# Order Designating the Minister of Transport as the Minister for the purposes of the Act

SI/2016-8 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-02-16

Enabling authority: [AIR CANADA PUBLIC PARTICIPATION ACT](../acts/A-10.1.md)

Registration: 2016-03-09

P.C. 2016-62 2016-02-16

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2016-8/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Air Canada Public Participation Act](../acts/A-10.1.md)[^fn_a],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 1989-516 of March 23, 1989[^fn_b]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Transport, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Air Canada Public Participation Act](../acts/A-10.1.md)[^fn_a].
