# Order Amending the Canadian Chicken Marketing Levies Order

SOR/2020-103 · Consolidated: 2020-05-17 · Last amended: 2020-05-10

Enabling authority: [FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](../acts/F-4.md)

Registration: 2020-05-08

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-103/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Governor in Council has, by the [Chicken Farmers of Canada Proclamation](SOR-79-158.md)[^fn_609262-E_hq_1253], established Chicken Farmers of Canada (“CFC”) pursuant to subsection 16(1)[^fn_609262-E_hq_1254] of the [Farm Products Agencies Act](../acts/F-4.md)[^fn_609262-E_hq_1267];

Whereas CFC has been empowered to implement a marketing plan pursuant to that Proclamation;

Whereas the proposed [Order Amending the Canadian Chicken Marketing Levies Order](SOR-2020-103.md) is an order of a class to which paragraph 7(1)(d)[^fn_609262-E_hq_1268] of that Act applies by reason of section 2 of the [Agencies’ Orders and Regulations Approval Order](C.R.C.,_c._648.md)[^fn_609262-E_hq_1270], and has been submitted to the National Farm Products Council pursuant to paragraph 22(1)(f) of that Act;

And whereas, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(d)[^fn_609262-E_hq_1268] of that Act, the National Farm Products Council has approved the proposed Order, after being satisfied that it is necessary for the implementation of the marketing plan that CFC is authorized to implement;

Therefore, Chicken Farmers of Canada, pursuant to paragraphs 22(1)(f) and (g)[^fn_81000-2-3136-E_hq_20420] of the [Farm Products Agencies Act](../acts/F-4.md)[^fn_609262-E_hq_1267] and section 12[^fn_609262-E_hq_1269] of the schedule to the [Chicken Farmers of Canada Proclamation](SOR-79-158.md)[^fn_609262-E_hq_1253], makes the annexed [Order Amending the Canadian Chicken Marketing Levies Order](SOR-2020-103.md).

Ottawa, May 6, 2020
