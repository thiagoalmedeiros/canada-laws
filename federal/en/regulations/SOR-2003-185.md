# Foreign Institutions Subject to the Canadian Residency Requirements Regulations (Insurance Companies)

SOR/2003-185 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [INSURANCE COMPANIES ACT](../acts/I-11.8.md)

Registration: 2003-05-29

P.C. 2003-766 2003-05-29

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-185/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 1021[^fn_SOR-2003-185_e_hq_5778] of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md)[^fn_SOR-2003-185_e_hq_5779], hereby makes the annexed [Foreign Institutions Subject to the Canadian Residency Requirements Regulations (Insurance Companies)](SOR-2003-185.md).

## Prescribed Holding Body Corporate

**1** For the purpose of subsection 167(2) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md), a holding body corporate of a foreign institution is prescribed if

- **(a)** the holding body corporate is incorporated or formed otherwise than by or under an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province; and

- **(b)** the holding body corporate and entities it controls are, when viewed as a whole, engaged primarily in the business of providing financial services.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
