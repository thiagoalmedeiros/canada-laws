# Order Transferring from the Department of Natural Resources to the Department of the Environment the Control and Supervision of the Large Final Emitters Group

SI/2005-48 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2005-06-01

P.C. 2005-909 2005-05-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2005-48/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Department of Natural Resources to the Department of the Environment the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration in the Department of Natural Resources known as the Large Final Emitters Group.
