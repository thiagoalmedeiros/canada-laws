# Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff Extension of Benefit Order — Ferro-chromium

SOR/90-303 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 1990-05-24

P.C. 1990-938 1990-05-24

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-90-303/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 23 of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_SOR-90-303_e_hq_6124], is pleased hereby to extend the benefit of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff to ferro-chromium of tariff item No. 7202.41.00 or 7202.49.00 that originates in a country to which the General Tariff applies, effective April 15, 1990.
