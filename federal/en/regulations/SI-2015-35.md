# Order Designating the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to be the Minister referred to in the Act

SI/2015-35 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-05-12

Enabling authority: [CANADIAN HIGH ARCTIC RESEARCH STATION ACT](../acts/C-17.8.md)

Registration: 2015-06-03

P.C. 2015-581 2015-05-12

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-35/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [Canadian High Arctic Research Station Act](../acts/C-17.8.md)[^fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947], designates the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to be the Minister referred to in that Act.
