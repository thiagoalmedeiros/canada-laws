# Order Transferring from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the Department of Employment and Social Development the Control and Supervision of the Anti-Racism Secretariat

SI/2023-53 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-07-26

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2023-08-16

P.C. 2023-783 2023-07-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-53/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-2604_hq_1690307956] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2604_hq_1690307970] transfers from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the Department of Employment and Social Development the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration in the Department of Canadian Heritage known as the Anti-Racism Secretariat.
