# Regulations Prescribing Physical Injuries

SOR/2019-260 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-06-25

Enabling authority: [ROYAL CANADIAN MOUNTED POLICE ACT](../acts/R-10.md)

Registration: 2019-06-25

P.C. 2019-930 2019-06-22

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-260/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, pursuant to subsection 45.79(3)[^fn_81000-2-1641-E_hq_14060] of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](../acts/R-10.md)[^fn_81000-2-1641-E_hq_14059], makes the annexed [Regulations Prescribing Physical Injuries](SOR-2019-260.md).

## Physical injuries

**1** For the purposes of the definition *serious injury* in subsection 45.79(1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](../acts/R-10.md), the following physical injuries — that are not transient or trifling in nature and for which medical attention was received from a person who is entitled to practice medicine or practice as a nurse under the laws of a province — are prescribed:

- **(a)** any physical injury that results in

  - **(i)** a substantial loss or impairment of mobility of the body,

  - **(ii)** a loss of function, in whole or in part, of any limb, organ or other body member,

  - **(iii)** a loss of vision or hearing in whole or in part, or

  - **(iv)** a substantial disfigurement;

- **(b)** the fracture of a limb, a rib, a vertebra or the skull; or

- **(c)** any burns, cuts or lacerations to a major portion of the body.

## Coming into force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
