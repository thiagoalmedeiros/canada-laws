# Order Designating the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act

SI/2019-68 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-07-12

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL SECURITY AND INTELLIGENCE REVIEW AGENCY ACT](../acts/N-16.62.md)

Registration: 2019-07-24

P.C. 2019-1089 2019-07-12

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 55(a) of the [National Security and Intelligence Review Agency Act](../acts/N-16.62.md)[^fn_81000-3-1791_hq_24648], designates the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act.
