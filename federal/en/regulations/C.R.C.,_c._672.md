# Advance Payments for Crops Guarantee Assignments Regulations

*Regulations Respecting the Assignment of the Right of Organizations under Subsection 12(1) of the Advance Payments for Crops Act*

C.R.C., c. 672 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Advance Payments for Crops Guarantee Assignments Regulations](C.R.C.,_c._672.md).

## Designation

**2** Any person occupying the position of Assistant Deputy Minister, Food Production and Marketing Branch, Department of Agriculture, is hereby designated as the paying officer for the purposes of Part IX of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md) in respect of an assignment of the rights of an organization under subsection 12(1) of the [Advance Payments for Crops Act](../acts/C-49.md).

## Prescription

**3** For the purposes of paragraph 81(1)(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), the rights of an organization under subsection 12(1) of the [Advance Payments for Crops Act](../acts/C-49.md) is a prescribed class of Crown debts.
