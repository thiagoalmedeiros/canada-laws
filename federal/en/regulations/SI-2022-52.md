# Order Designating the Minister of Industry as the Minister for the Purposes of the Civil Lunar Gateway Agreement Implementation Act

SI/2022-52 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-10-07

Enabling authority: [CIVIL LUNAR GATEWAY AGREEMENT IMPLEMENTATION ACT](../acts/C-31.4.md)

Registration: 2022-10-26

P.C. 2022-1090 2022-10-07

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2022-52/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 5 of the [Civil Lunar Gateway Agreement Implementation Act](../acts/C-31.4.md)[^fn_81000-2-4922-E_hq_1660845335], designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act, effective October 26, 2022.
