# Exemption from Approval for Certain Investments in Intragroup Service Entities (Life Companies and Insurance Holding Companies) Regulations

SOR/2003-244 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [INSURANCE COMPANIES ACT](../acts/I-11.8.md)

Registration: 2003-06-18

P.C. 2003-995 2003-06-18

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-244/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraphs 501(b)[^fn_SOR-2003-244_e_hq_5797] and 977(b)[^fn_SOR-2003-244_e_hq_5798] and section 1021[^fn_SOR-2003-244_e_hq_5798] of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md)[^fn_SOR-2003-244_e_hq_5799], hereby makes the annexed [Exemption from Approval for Certain Investments in Intragroup Service Entities (Life Companies and Insurance Holding Companies) Regulations](SOR-2003-244.md).

## Exemption — life companies

**1** Subsections 495(7) and (8) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md) do not apply to a life company’s acquisition of control of, or a life company’s acquisition or increase of a substantial investment in, an entity referred to in paragraph 495(6)(b) of that Act if the financial intermediary activities of the entity that expose the entity to material market or credit risk are limited to providing services exclusively to the life company or members of the life company’s group.

## Exemption — insurance holding companies

**2** Subsections 971(5) and (6) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md) do not apply to an insurance holding company’s acquisition of control of, or an insurance holding company’s acquisition or increase of a substantial investment in, an entity referred to in paragraph 971(4)(b) of that Act if the financial intermediary activities of the entity that expose the entity to material market or credit risk are limited to providing services exclusively to the insurance holding company or members of the insurance holding company’s group.

## Coming into force

**3** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
