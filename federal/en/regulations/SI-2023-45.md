# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the Minister for the Purposes of the Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non-Canadians Act

SI/2023-45 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-07-26

Enabling authority: [PROHIBITION ON THE PURCHASE OF RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY BY NON-CANADIANS ACT](../acts/P-25.2.md)

Registration: 2023-08-16

P.C. 2023-775 2023-07-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-45/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the [Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non-Canadians Act](../acts/P-25.2.md)[^fn_81000-3-25XX_hq_1689941929],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2022-1104 of October 19, 2022[^fn_81000-3-25XX_hq_1689941871]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
