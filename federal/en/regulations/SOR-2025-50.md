Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, makes the annexed [Special Economic Measures Permit Authorization Order](SOR-2025-50.md) under subsection 4(4)[^fn_81000-2-5751_hq_1738000630] of the [Special Economic Measures Act](../acts/S-14.5.md)[^fn_81000-2-5751_hq_1738000617].

## Authorizations

### Authorization

**1** The Minister of Foreign Affairs may

- **(a)** issue to any person in Canada or Canadian outside Canada a permit to carry out a specified activity or transaction, or class of activity or transaction, that is restricted or prohibited under a regulation listed in the schedule; and

- **(b)** issue a general permit allowing any person in Canada or Canadian outside Canada to carry out a specified activity or transaction, or class of activity or transaction, that is restricted or prohibited under a regulation listed in the schedule.

### Authorization — Syria

**2** The Minister of Foreign Affairs may

- **(a)** issue to any person in Canada or Canadian outside Canada a permit to carry out a specified activity or transaction, or class of activity or transaction, that is restricted or prohibited under the [Special Economic Measures (Syria) Regulations](SOR-2011-114.md) other than an activity or class of activity that is set out in section 3.4 of those Regulations; and

- **(b)** issue a general permit allowing any person in Canada or Canadian outside Canada to carry out a specified activity or transaction, or class of activity or transaction, that is restricted or prohibited under the [Special Economic Measures (Syria) Regulations](SOR-2011-114.md) other than an activity or class of activity that is set out in section 3.4 of those Regulations.

## Coming into Force

### Registration

**3** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
