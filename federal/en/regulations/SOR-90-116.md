# Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Police Information Retrieval System Fees Order

*Order Prescribing the Fee or Charge To Be Paid by a Province or Municipality for the Use, by a Police Force for which the Province or Municipality is Responsible, of the Police Information Retrieval System Owned by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police*

SOR/90-116 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1990-02-02

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-90-116/)

The Solicitor General of Canada, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1989-1899 of September 21, 1989[^fn_SOR-90-116_e_hq_5205], hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee or charge to be paid by a province or municipality for the use, by a police force for which the province or municipality is responsible, of the Police Information Retrieval System owned by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police](SOR-90-116.md).

Ottawa, January 26, 1990

PIERRE BLAIS

*Solicitor General of Canada*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Police Information Retrieval System Fees Order](SOR-90-116.md).

## Fee

**2** A province or municipality shall pay to the Receiver General for the use, by a police force for which the province or municipality is responsible, of the Police Information Retrieval System owned by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police a fee of $450 per year in respect of each sworn police officer in the police force.
