# Order Transferring from Shared Services Canada to the Department of Public Works and Government Services the Control and Supervision of the Next Generation Human Resources and Pay System Initiative

SI/2023-76 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-11-15

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2023-12-06

P.C. 2023-1140 2023-11-15

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-76/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-2462_hq_1699018962] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2462_hq_1699018977], transfers from Shared Services Canada to the Department of Public Works and Government Services the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration in Shared Services Canada known as the Next Generation Human Resources and Pay System Initiative.
