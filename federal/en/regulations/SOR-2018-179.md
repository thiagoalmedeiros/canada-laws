## Drug Screening Equipment

### Approved equipment

**1** For the purpose of the definition *approved drug screening equipment* in section 320.11 of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md), the following equipment that is designed to ascertain the presence of a drug in a person’s body is approved:

- **(a)** a Dräger DrugTest® 5000 and a Dräger DrugTest® 5000 STK-CA, when used together; and

- **(b)** a SoToxa™, an Abbott SoToxa™ Test Cartridge and an Abbott SoToxa™ Oral Fluid Collection Device, when used together.

<sub>History: SOR/2019-237, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

### Day made

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is made.
