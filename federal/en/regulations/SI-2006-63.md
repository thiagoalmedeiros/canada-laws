# Order Designating the Executive Director of the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as Deputy Head

SI/2006-63 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-05-03

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2006-05-03

P.C. 2006-229 2006-04-21

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-63/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), hereby designates the Executive Director of the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as deputy head in respect of that Secretariat.
