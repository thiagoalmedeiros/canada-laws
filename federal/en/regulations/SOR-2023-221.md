# Critical Habitat of the Atlantic Mud-piddock (Barnea truncata) Order

SOR/2023-221 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-10-20

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../acts/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2023-10-20

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-221/)

Whereas the Atlantic Mud-piddock (Barnea truncata) is a wildlife species that is listed as a threatened species in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-5152_E_hq_1684344285];

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2)[^fn_81000-2-5152_E_hq_1684344343] of that Act;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed Critical Habitat of the Atlantic Mud-piddock (Barnea truncata) Order under subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-5152_E_hq_1684344285].

Ottawa, October 18, 2023

## Application

**1** Subsection 58(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md) applies to the critical habitat of the Atlantic Mud-piddock (Barnea truncata), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
