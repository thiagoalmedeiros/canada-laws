# Time Limit for the Application of Subsection 118(1) of the Customs Tariff Regulations

SOR/98-59 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 1997-12-29

P.C. 1997-2031 1997-12-29

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-59/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 118(4)(a) of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fna_e], hereby makes the annexed [Time Limit for the Application of Subsection 118(1) of the Customs Tariff Regulations](SOR-98-59.md).

## Time Limit for Application

**1** Subsection 118(1) of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) applies in respect of any imported goods for a period of six years after the day on which the goods are released.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on January 1, 1998.
