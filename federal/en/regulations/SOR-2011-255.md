# Communications Security Establishment Regulations

SOR/2011-255 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2011-11-16

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../acts/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2011-11-15

P.C. 2011-1304 2011-11-15

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-255/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-320-e_hq_10884], hereby makes the annexed [Communications Security Establishment Regulations](SOR-2011-255.md).

## Block Transfer

**1** Subsection 132(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md) applies to the Communications Security Establishment.

## Cession of Effect

**2** These Regulations cease to have effect at 00:00:03 on November 16, 2011.

## Coming into Force

**3** These Regulations come into force at 00:00:01 on November 16, 2011.
