# Order Designating the Minister for International Development as the Minister for the purposes of the Act

SI/2015-2 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-01-22

Enabling authority: [INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT RESEARCH CENTRE ACT](../acts/I-19.md)

Registration: 2015-02-11

P.C. 2015-22 2015-01-22

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-2/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in section 2[^fn_81000-3-955-E_hq_14995] of the [International Development Research Centre Act](../acts/I-19.md)[^fn_81000-3-955-E_hq_14996],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 1979-1625[^fn_81000-3-955-E_hq_15056] of June 5, 1979; and

**(b)** designates the Minister for International Development, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [International Development Research Centre Act](../acts/I-19.md)[^fn_81000-3-955-E_hq_14996].
