# Order Designating the Minister for Purposes of the Act

SI/93-228 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CULTURAL PROPERTY EXPORT AND IMPORT ACT](../acts/C-51.md)

Registration: 1993-12-15

P.C. 1993-1980 1993-12-02

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-228/)

[^fn_SI_93-228_EN_hq_12211]His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in section 2 of the [Cultural Property Export and Import Act](../acts/C-51.md), is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1453 of June 25, 1993[^fn_SI-93-228_e_hq_6541]; and

**(b)** to designate the Minister of Communications, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
