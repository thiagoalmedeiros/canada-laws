# Order Designating Alberta for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code

SOR/2010-21 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2010-03-01

Enabling authority: [CRIMINAL CODE](../acts/C-46.md)

Registration: 2010-02-02

P.C. 2010-100 2010-02-02

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-21/)

Whereas Alberta has legislative measures that protect recipients of payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost of borrowing under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Alberta has requested that the Governor in Council designate that province for the purposes of section 347.1[^fn_610628-e_hq_3365] of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3366];

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3365] of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3366], hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating Alberta for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code](SOR-2010-21.md).

## Province Designated

**1** Alberta is designated for the purposes of section 347.1 of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md).

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_Ind521_hq_8482] This Order comes into force at 12:00 a.m. Mountain time on the first day on which section 17 of the Payday Loans Regulation, Alta. Reg. 157/2009, is in force.
