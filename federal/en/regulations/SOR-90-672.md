His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 50.1(3)(b)[^fn_SOR-90-672_e_hq_6132] of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the manner of adjusting the Industrial Product Price Indices for Motor Gasoline and Diesel Oil](SOR-90-672.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Industrial Product Price Indices Adjustment Regulations](SOR-90-672.md).

## Adjustment of the Industrial Product Price Index for Motor Gasoline

**2** For the purposes of paragraph 50.1(2)(a) of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), the Industrial Product Price Index for Motor Gasoline released by Statistics Canada for each of the twelve months immediately preceding October 1985 shall be adjusted by

- **(a)** multiplying the index by 0.8804; and

- **(b)** rounding the result obtained under paragraph (a) to the nearest one-tenth or, if the result obtained is equidistant from two one-tenths, to the greater thereof.

## Adjustment of the Industrial Product Price Index for Diesel Oil

**3** For the purposes of paragraph 50.1(2)(a) of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), the Industrial Product Price Index for Diesel Oil released by Statistics Canada for each of the twelve months immediately preceding October 1985 shall be adjusted by

- **(a)** multiplying the index by 0.9681; and

- **(b)** rounding the result obtained under paragraph (a) to the nearest one-tenth or, if the result obtained is equidistant from two one-tenths, to the greater thereof.
