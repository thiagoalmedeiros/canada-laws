# Order Designating the Minister of State (Small Business and Tourism) as the Minister for the Purposes of the Canada Small Business Financing Act

SI/2025-84 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-05-13

Enabling authority: [CANADA SMALL BUSINESS FINANCING ACT](../acts/C-10.2.md)

Registration: 2025-06-04

P.C. 2025-495 2025-05-13

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-84/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the [Canada Small Business Financing Act](../acts/C-10.2.md)[^fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-428 of March 14, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2910_hq_1746840045]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of State (Small Business and Tourism), a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Canada Small Business Financing Act](../acts/C-10.2.md)[^fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484].
