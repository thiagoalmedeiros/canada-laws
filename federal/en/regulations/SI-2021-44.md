# Order Designating the President of the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada as Deputy Head in Respect of that Agency

SI/2021-44 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-08-06

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2021-08-18

P.C. 2021-831 2021-08-06

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2021-44/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[^fn_608651-E_hq_152] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_610261-E_hq_4314], designates the President of the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada as deputy head in respect of that Agency, effective August 6, 2021.
