# Secretary of State Authority to Prescribe Fees Order (Student Loans)

SI/91-104 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1991-07-17

P.C. 1991-1210 1991-06-24

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-91-104/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Secretary of State of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19.1(b)[^fn_SI_91-104_EN_hq_20089] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Secretary of State of Canada to prescribe, by order, a fee based on a percentage of the amount of the loan, to be paid by full-time students to whom guaranteed student loans are made after July 31, 1991 under the [Canada Student Loans Act](../acts/S-23.md).
