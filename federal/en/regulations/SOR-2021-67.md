# CUKTCA Rules of Origin Regulations

SOR/2021-67 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-04-01

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2021-03-29

P.C. 2021-238 2021-03-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-67/)

His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 16(2)(a)[^fn_81000-2-4397_hq_28258] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_81000-2-4397_hq_28259], makes the annexed [CUKTCA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-2021-67.md).

## Force of law

**1** The following provisions of the Protocol on Rules of Origin and Origin Procedures of the Canada–European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement, as incorporated by reference in the Canada–United Kingdom Trade Continuity Agreement, have the force of law in Canada:

- **(a)** Articles 1 and 2;

- **(b)** paragraphs 1 to 2C and 3 of Article 3;

- **(c)** Articles 4 to 17; and

- **(d)** Annexes 1, 4, 5, 5‑A and 7.

## Coming into force

**2**[^fn_SOR-2021-67_e_transform_hq_28950] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 37 of the [Canada–United Kingdom Trade Continuity Agreement Implementation Act](../acts/C-10.56.md), chapter 1 of the Statutes of Canada, 2021, comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
