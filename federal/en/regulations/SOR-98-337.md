# Senate Sessional Allowance (Deductions for Non-attendance) Regulations

SOR/98-337 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PARLIAMENT OF CANADA ACT](../acts/P-1.md)

Registration: 1998-06-10

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-337/)

The Senate, pursuant to section 59 of the [Parliament of Canada Act](../acts/P-1.md), hereby makes the annexed Senate Sessional Allowance (Deductions for Non-attendance) Regulations.

June 9, 1998

## General

**1** The deduction to be made from the sessional allowance of a senator under subsection 57(1) of the [Parliament of Canada Act](../acts/P-1.md) is increased to $250 per day for every sitting day beyond twenty-one on which the senator does not attend a sitting of the Senate.

<sub>History: SOR/2002-23, s. 1; err.(F), Vol. 136, No. 2</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on July 1, 1998.
