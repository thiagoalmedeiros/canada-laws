# Exemption for Public Notices or Documents (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations

SOR/2010-237 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2014-11-21

Enabling authority: [COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](../acts/C-41.01.md)

Registration: 2010-10-28

P.C. 2010-1334 2010-10-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-237/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 463(1)[^fn_1a] and section 487.14[^fn_1b] of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../acts/C-41.01.md)[^fn_1c], hereby makes the annexed Exemption for Public Notices or Documents Regulations (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations.

## Interpretation

**1** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../acts/C-41.01.md).

## Exemption Circumstances

**2** For the purpose of section 487.14 of the Act, the prescribed circumstances are that the exemption does not prejudice any of the members or the shareholders or the public interest.

## Coming into Force

**3** These Regulations come into force on June 1, 2011.
