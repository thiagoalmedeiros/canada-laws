# Income Support Payment (Number of Weeks) Regulations

SOR/2020-142 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2020-08-28

Enabling authority: [CANADA EMERGENCY RESPONSE BENEFIT ACT](../acts/C-3.7.md)

Registration: 2020-06-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-142/)

The Minister of Employment and Social Development, with the consent of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 8(2) of the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act](../acts/C-3.7.md)[^fn_81000-2-4245_hq_27474], makes the annexed [Income Support Payment (Number of Weeks) Regulations](SOR-2020-142.md).

Gatineau, June 26, 2020

Carla Qualtrough

Minister of Employment and Social Development

## Maximum Number of Weeks

### 28 weeks

**1** For the purpose of subsection 8(1) of the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act](../acts/C-3.7.md), the maximum number of weeks for which income support payments may be made to a worker is 28 weeks.

<sub>History: SOR/2020-189, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

### Registration

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
