# Order Extending the Application of the Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Ukraine) Regulations

SOR/2019-69 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-03-04

Enabling authority: [FREEZING ASSETS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT](../acts/F-31.6.md)

Registration: 2019-03-04

P.C. 2019-158 2019-02-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-69/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section 6 of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act](../acts/F-31.6.md)[^fn_81000-2-2665-E_hq_16607], extends the application of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Ukraine) Regulations](SOR-2014-44.md)[^fn_81000-2-2665-E_hq_16608] for a period of five years beginning on March 6, 2019.
