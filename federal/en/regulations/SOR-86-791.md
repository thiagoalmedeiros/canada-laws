# Government Employees Compensation Place of Employment Regulations

*Regulations for Determining the Place Where a Government Employee is Usually Employed*

SOR/86-791 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES COMPENSATION ACT](../acts/G-5.md)

Registration: 1986-07-24

P.C. 1986-1666 1986-07-23

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-86-791/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 10 of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](../acts/G-5.md), is pleased hereby to approve the annexed [Regulations for determining the place where a government employee is usually employed](SOR-86-791.md), made by the Minister of Labour.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Government Employees Compensation Place of Employment Regulations](SOR-86-791.md).

## Place of Employment

**2** For the purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](../acts/G-5.md), the place where an employee is usually employed is the place where the employee is appointed or engaged to work.
