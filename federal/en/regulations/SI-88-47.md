# Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/88-47 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1988-03-30

P.C. 1988-496 1988-03-17

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-88-47/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby:

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1987-1093 of May 28, 1987[^fn_SI-88-47_e_hq_6456]; and

**(b)** to authorize the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by persons for services in respect of registration under the [Copyright Act](../acts/C-42.md), notwithstanding section 41 of that Act.
