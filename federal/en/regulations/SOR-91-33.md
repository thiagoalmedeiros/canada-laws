# Amalgamations and Windings-Up Continuation (GST/HST) Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing Purposes for Which New Successor Corporations Shall Be Deemed to Be the Same Corporations as and Continuations of Predecessor Amalgamated or Wound-Up Corporations*

SOR/91-33 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-03-04

Enabling authority: [EXCISE TAX ACT](../acts/E-15.md)

Registration: 1990-12-18

P.C. 1990-2742 1990-12-18

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-91-33/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[^fn_SOR_91-33_EN_hq_5507] of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing purposes for which new successor corporations shall be deemed to be the same corporations as and continuations of predecessor amalgamated or wound-up corporations.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Amalgamations and Windings-Up Continuation (GST/HST) Regulations](SOR-91-33.md).

<sub>History: SOR/99-173, s. 1</sub>

## Prescribed Purpose

**2** For the purposes of sections 271 and 272 of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), “prescribed purpose” means for the purpose of applying any provision of that Act set out in the schedule.
