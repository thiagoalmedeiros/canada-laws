# Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons To Be the Minister Referred to in the Federal Public Sector Labour Relations and Employment Board Act and as the Minister for the Purposes of the Federal Public Sector Labour Relations Act

SI/2025-51 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [FEDERAL PUBLIC SECTOR LABOUR RELATIONS AND EMPLOYMENT BOARD ACT](../acts/P-33.35.md)[FEDERAL PUBLIC SECTOR LABOUR RELATIONS ACT](../acts/P-33.3.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-432 2025-03-14

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-51/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-87 of February 12, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743279];

**(b)** designates, under section 3 of the [Federal Public Sector Labour Relations and Employment Board Act](../acts/P-33.35.md)[^fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743327], the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, who is not a member of the Treasury Board, to be the Minister referred to in that Act; and

**(c)** designates, under the definition *Minister* in subsection 2‍(1) of the [Federal Public Sector Labour Relations Act](../acts/P-33.3.md)[^fn_81000-3-2614_hq_1741743361], the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, who is a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada and is not a member of the Treasury Board, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
