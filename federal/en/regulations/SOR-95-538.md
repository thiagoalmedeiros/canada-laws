# Consular Services Fees Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing the Travel Documents for Which Fees are Payable and the Amount of Those Fees, with a View to the Recovery of Costs of Providing Consular Services*

SOR/95-538 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2010-11-26

Enabling authority: [DEPARTMENT OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS, TRADE AND DEVELOPMENT ACT](../acts/F-27.5.md)

Registration: 1995-11-08

P.C. 1995-1858 1995-11-06

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 10.1[^footnotea_e] of the [Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade Act](../acts/E-22.md)[^footnoteb_e], is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the travel documents for which fees are payable and the amount of those fees, with a view to the recovery of costs of providing consular services](SOR-95-538.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Consular Services Fees Regulations](SOR-95-538.md).

## Interpretation

**2** In these Regulations,

***travel document*** means any of the following documents that are issued by the Minister for travel purposes:

- **(a)** a passport issued pursuant to the [Canadian Passport Order](SI-81-86.md);

- **(b)** a certificate of identity; and

- **(c)** refugee travel papers. (***document de voyage***)

## Fee

**3** A travel document is subject to the payment of the fee set out in section 4.

**4** The fee for a travel document that is issued to a person of at least 16 years of age is $25 and shall be paid when the application for the document is made.

<sub>History: SOR/2001-536, s. 1; SOR/2010-271, s. 1</sub>

## RELATED PROVISIONS

###### — SOR/2001-536, s. 2

**2** Section 4 of the Regulations, as it read immediately before the coming into force of these Regulations, continues to apply in respect of an application to a passport issuing office for a travel document that is

- **(a)** made in person before that coming into force; or

- **(b)** sent by mail or courier

  - **(i)** before that coming into force, or

  - **(ii)** within five days after that coming into force if the application is accompanied by the fee referred to in that section.
