# Minister of Communications (National Archives of Canada) Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

*Order Authorizing the Minister of Communications to Prescribe By Order the Fees to be Paid by Users or Classes of Users of a Service or Facility of the National Archives of Canada*

SI/94-106 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1994-08-24

P.C. 1994-1346 1994-08-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-94-106/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Communications and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)[^footnote1_e] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke the [Minister of Communications (National Archives of Canada) Authority to Prescribe Fees Order](SI-94-106.md), made by Order in Council P.C. 1989-1595 of August 24, 1989[^footnote2_e]; and

**(b)** to make the annexed Order authorizing the Minister of Communications to prescribe by order the fees to be paid by users or classes of users of a service or facility of the National Archives of Canada, in substitution therefor.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the Minister of Communications (Library and Archives of Canada) Authority to Prescribe Fees Order.

<sub>History: 2004, c. 11, subpar. 52(5) and (6)</sub>

## Authorization

**2** The Minister of Communications is hereby authorized to prescribe by order the fees to be paid by users or classes of users of a service or facility of the Library and Archives of Canada.

<sub>History: 2004, c. 11, subpar. 52(5) and (6)</sub>

## Terms and Conditions

**3** The fees referred to in section 2 shall be determined in accordance with the Treasury Board Policy on External User Charges for Goods, Services, Property Rights and Privileges, issued in August 1986 and revised in December 1989.
