# Order Directing that the Document Set Out in the Schedule be Discontinued

SI/2022-18 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-03-03

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2022-03-16

P.C. 2022-201 2022-03-03

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2022-18/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas it appears to the Governor in Council that the document set out in the schedule, required by an Act of Parliament to be laid before both Houses of Parliament, contains the same information as or less information than is contained in the Public Accounts or in any estimates of expenditures submitted to Parliament;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to section 157[^fn_81000-3-1155-E_hq_16356] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-1155-E_hq_16358], directs that the document set out in the schedule be discontinued.

## SCHEDULE

- Northern Pipeline Agency Annual Report (section 14 of the [Northern Pipeline Act](../acts/N-26.md), R.S., c. N-26)
