# Order Designating the National Transportation Act Review Commission as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

SI/92-39 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1992-03-11

P.C. 1992-293 1992-02-20

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-92-39/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***department*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), to designate the National Transportation Act Review Commission as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), to designate the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the National Transportation Act Review Commission.
