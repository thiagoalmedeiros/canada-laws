# CCFTA Rules of Origin Regulations

SOR/2021-144 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-10-15

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS ACT](../acts/C-52.6.md)[CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2021-06-17

P.C. 2021-586 2021-06-17

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-144/)

His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness with respect to the provisions of the annexed Regulations other than section 1, and on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance with respect to that section 1, makes the annexed [CCFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-97-340.md) pursuant to

**(a)** subsection 164(1.1)[^fn_81000-2-4441_hq_28689] of the [Customs Act](../acts/C-52.6.md)[^fn_81000-2-4441_hq_28690]; and

**(b)** subsections 16(2)[^fn_81000-2-4441_hq_28691] and 16(4)[^fn_81000-2-4441_hq_28692] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_81000-2-4441_hq_28693].

## Rules of origin

**1** The following provisions of the Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement have the force of law in Canada:

- **(a)** Articles D-01 to D-03;

- **(b)** paragraph 1 of Article D-04;

- **(c)** Articles D-05 to D-13;

- **(d)** Articles D-16 and D-17; and

- **(e)** Annexes D-01, D-03.1 and D-03.2.

**2** The [CCFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-97-340.md)[^fn_81000-2-4442_hq_28601] are repealed.

## Coming into force

**3** These Regulations come into force on the 120th day after the day on which they are registered.
