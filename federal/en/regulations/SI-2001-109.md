# Visitor Visa Fee (World Youth Day 2002) Remission Order

SI/2001-109 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2001-11-07

P.C. 2001-1913 2001-10-19

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2001-109/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[^footnotea_e] of [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), hereby makes the annexed [Visitor Visa Fee (World Youth Day 2002) Remission Order](SI-2001-109.md).

**1** Remission is hereby granted

- **(a)** of the fee of $75, paid or payable under item 3 of the schedule to the Immigration Act Fees Regulations, for the processing of a duly completed application for a visitor visa to come into Canada once, on condition that the applicant is a participant in World Youth Day 2002; and

- **(b)** of the fee of $150, paid or payable under item 4 of the schedule to the Immigration Act Fees Regulations, for the processing of a duly completed application for a visitor visa to come into Canada more than once, on condition that the applicant is a volunteer organizer of World Youth Day 2002 invited by the Canadian Conference of Catholic Bishops to facilitate and administer the activities of that event.
