His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115[^fn_a] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_b], makes the annexed Ferry-Boats Remission Order, 2016.

## Remission

### Ferry-boats

**1** Remission is granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) in respect of ferry-boats, classified under subheading No. 8901.10 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md), excluding those produced in Canada that have been exported and then subsequently re-imported into Canada.

### Conditions

**2** The remission is granted on the following conditions:

- **(a)** the ferry-boat was permanently imported into Canada for the first time on or after October 1, 2015;

- **(b)** a claim for remission is made by the importer to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the date of importation; and

- **(c)** the importer files, on request, all evidence that is required by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility for remission.
