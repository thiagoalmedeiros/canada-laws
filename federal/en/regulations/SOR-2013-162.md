# Least Developed Country Tariff Withdrawal Order (2013 GPT Review)

SOR/2013-162 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-01-01

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2013-09-30

P.C. 2013-968 2013-09-27

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2013-162/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 38[^fn_a] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_b], makes the annexed [Least Developed Country Tariff Withdrawal Order (2013 GPT Review)](SOR-2013-162.md).

## Withdrawal of Entitlement

**1** Entitlement to the benefit of the Least Developed Country Tariff is withdrawn in respect of all goods that originate in Equatorial Guinea and Maldives.

## Exemption

**2** Section 1 does not apply to goods that were in transit to Canada before January 1, 2015.

## Amendments to the Customs Tariff Schedule

**3** \[Amendment\]

## Coming into Force

**4** This Order comes into force on January 1, 2015.
