# Order Designating (1) the Commission that is Named Public Inquiry into the 2022 Public Order Emergency as a Department for the Purposes of that Act; and (2) the Prime Minister as the Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Commission

SI/2022-23 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-04-25

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2022-05-11

P.C. 2022-393 2022-04-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2022-23/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister,

**(a)** under paragraph (b)[^fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27687] of the definition “department” in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688], designates the commission that is named Public Inquiry into the 2022 Public Order Emergency as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** under paragraph (b)[^fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27689] of the definition “appropriate Minister” in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688], designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the commission referred to in paragraph (a).
