# Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations

*Regulations Delegating Powers Conferred by Section 163 of the Customs Act Upon the Minister of National Revenue*

The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 163(2) of the [Customs Act](../acts/C-52.6.md), hereby revokes the [Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations](SOR-86-359.md) made on the 12th of October, 1984[^footnotea] and, in substitution therefor, makes the annexed [Regulations delegating powers conferred by section 163 of the [Customs Act](../acts/C-52.6.md) upon the Minister of National Revenue](SOR-86-359.md).

Ottawa, March 25, 1986

ELMER MacKAY

*Minister of National Revenue*

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations](SOR-86-359.md).

## General

**2** The officers named in the schedule may exercise the powers conferred upon the Minister of National Revenue by section 163 of the [Customs Act](../acts/C-52.6.md).
