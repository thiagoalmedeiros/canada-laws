# Critical Habitat of the Lilliput (Toxolasma parvum) Order

SOR/2022-215 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-10-20

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../acts/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2022-10-20

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Lilliput (Toxolasma parvum) is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_91000-2-4879_hq_31146];

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2)[^fn_81000-2-4879_hq_31143] of that Act;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Lilliput (Toxolasma parvum) Order](SOR-2022-215.md) under subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_91000-2-4879_hq_31146].

Ottawa, October 17, 2022

## Application

**1** Subsection 58(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md) applies to the critical habitat of the Lilliput (Toxolasma parvum), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
