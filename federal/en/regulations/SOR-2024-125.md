# CTMA Ranger Remission Order

SOR/2024-125 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-06-10

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2024-06-10

P.C. 2024-658 2024-06-10

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-125/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, makes the annexed [CTMA Ranger Remission Order](SOR-2024-125.md) under section 115[^fn_81000-2-5281_E_hq_1695937895] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_81000-2-5281_E_hq_1695938111].

## Remission

**1** Remission is granted to Gestion C.T.M.A. Inc., of Cap-aux-Meules, Quebec, in an amount equal to the difference between the customs duty paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) at the General Tariff and those that would have been payable if the vessel *CTMA Ranger* that was imported in 2022 had been entitled to the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff.

## Conditions

**2** Remission is granted on the following conditions:

- **(a)** the importer files any evidence that may be required by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility for remission; and

- **(b)** the importer makes a claim for remission to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the day on which this Order comes into force.

## Coming into force

**3** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
