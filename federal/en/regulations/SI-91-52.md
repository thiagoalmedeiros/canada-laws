# Order Giving Notice that a Tax Agreement Between Canada and the Polish People’s Republic Came into Force on November 30, 1989

SI/91-52 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: CANADA-POLAND INCOME TAX CONVENTION ACT, 1989[AGREEMENTS AND CONVENTIONS](../acts/Z-02.md)

Registration: 1991-04-10

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-91-52/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Notice is hereby given, pursuant to section 12 of the Canada-Poland Income Tax Convention Act, 1989[^fn_SI-91-52_e_hq_6213], that the Convention between the Government of Canada and the Government of the Polish People’s Republic for the Avoidance of Double Taxation with respect to Taxes on Income and on Capital came into force on November 30, 1989.

Ottawa, February 2, 1991

MICHAEL WILSON

*Minister of Finance*
