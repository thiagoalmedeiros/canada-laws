# Order Designating the Minister of Industry (1) as the Minister for the purposes of the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council Act; and (2) as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council for the purposes of the Financial Administration Act

SI/2019-122 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-11-20

Enabling authority: [NATURAL SCIENCES AND ENGINEERING RESEARCH COUNCIL ACT](../acts/N-21.md)[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2019-12-11

P.C. 2019-1360 2019-11-20

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2019-122/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the [Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council Act](../acts/N-21.md)[^fn_81000-3-1928_hq_26090] and paragraph (c.1)[^fn_81000-3-1928_hq_26091] of the definition *appropriate Minister* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_3256_hq_10807],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2018-1011 of July 18, 2018[^fn_81000-3-1928_hq_26089];

**(b)** designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council Act](../acts/N-21.md)[^fn_81000-3-1928_hq_26090]; and

**(c)** designates the Minister of Industry as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_3256_hq_10807].
