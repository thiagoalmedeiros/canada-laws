# Order Designating the Department of Industry to Provide Support to the Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation

SI/2025-91 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-07-03

Enabling authority: [SALARIES ACT](../acts/S-3.md)

Registration: 2024-07-16

P.C. 2025-571 2025-07-03

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-91/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under subsections 5(1)[^fn_81000-3-2944-E_hq_22309] of the [Salaries Act](../acts/S-3.md)[^fn_81000-3-2944-E_hq_22310],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-481 of May 13, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2944_hq_1741746879]; and

**(b)** designates the Department of Industry to provide support to the Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation in the carrying out of that Minister’s responsibilities.
