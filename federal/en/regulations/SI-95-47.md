# Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry into the Deployment of Canadian Forces to Somalia as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

SI/95-47 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1995-04-19

P.C. 1995-529 1995-03-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-95-47/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b)[^fn_SI-95-47_e_hq_6607] of the definition ***department*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), to designate the Commission of Inquiry into the Deployment of Canadian Forces to Somalia as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b)[^fn_SI-95-47_e_hq_6607] of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), to designate the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Commission of Inquiry into the deployment of Canadian Forces to Somalia.
