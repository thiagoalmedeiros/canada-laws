# Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 25 (RCMP)

*Order Respecting the Designation of the National Security Investigations Records, No. CMP/P-PU-025, as an Exempt Personal Information Bank*

SOR/93-272 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-08-19

Enabling authority: [PRIVACY ACT](../acts/P-21.md)

Registration: 1993-05-25

P.C. 1993-1034 1993-05-25

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-93-272/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Solicitor General of Canada and the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 18(1) of the [Privacy Act](../acts/P-21.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order respecting the designation of the National Security Investigations Records, No. CMP/P-PU-025, as an exempt personal information bank.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 25 (RCMP)](SOR-93-272.md).

## General

**2** The National Security Investigations Records, No. CMP/P-PU-025, a personal information bank under the control of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, is hereby designated as an exempt bank on the basis of sections 21 and 22 of the [Privacy Act](../acts/P-21.md).

**3** For the purposes of paragraph 18(3)(b) of the [Privacy Act](../acts/P-21.md), the laws concerned, in relation to those files that are contained in the exempt bank referred to in section 2 that consist predominantly of personal information described in subparagraph 22(1)(a)(ii) of that Act, are the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md), the [Foreign Interference and Security of Information Act](../acts/O-5.md), the [Security Offences Act](../acts/S-7.md), the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](../acts/R-10.md) and the [Canadian Security Intelligence Service Act](../acts/C-23.md).
