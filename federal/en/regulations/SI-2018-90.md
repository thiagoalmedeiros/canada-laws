# Order Designating the President of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada as the Minister for the purposes of section 23 of the Act

SI/2018-90 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-09-25

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../acts/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2018-10-17

P.C. 2018-1202 2018-09-25

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2018-90/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 23(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-1716-E_hq_23011],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2016-4 of January 13, 2016[^fn_81000-3-1716-E_hq_23010]; and

**(b)** designates the President of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada as the minister for the purposes of section 23 of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-1716-E_hq_23011].
