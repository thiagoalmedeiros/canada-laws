# L-5 hydroxytryptophan Remission Order

*Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on L-5 Hydroxytryptophan*

C.R.C., c. 773 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [L-5 hydroxytryptophan Remission Order](C.R.C.,_c._773.md).

## Remission

**2** Remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) on L-5 hydroxytryptophan imported on or after October 1, 1975.
