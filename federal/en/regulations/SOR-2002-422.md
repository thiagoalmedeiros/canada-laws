Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity Act](../acts/E-5.401.md)[^footnote1_e], the Treasury Board has consulted the Solicitor General of Canada concerning the application of that Act to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity Act](../acts/E-5.401.md)[^footnote1_e], hereby makes the annexed Order Specifying the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for the Purposes of the Employment Equity Act.

## Designation

**1** The Royal Canadian Mounted Police is specified under paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity Act](../acts/E-5.401.md) as a portion of the public sector employing one hundred or more employees to which that Act applies.

## Coming into Force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
