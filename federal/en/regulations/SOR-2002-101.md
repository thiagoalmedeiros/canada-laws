# Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Designated Violations Regulations

SOR/2002-101 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL CONSUMER AGENCY OF CANADA ACT](../acts/F-11.1.md)

Registration: 2002-02-28

P.C. 2002-272 2002-02-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-101/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(a) of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../acts/F-11.1.md)[^fn_SOR-2002-101_e_hq_5844], hereby makes the annexed [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Designated Violations Regulations](SOR-2002-101.md).

## Interpretation

### Definition of Act

**1** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../acts/F-11.1.md).

## Designated Violations

### Designation

**2** The following are designated as violations that may be proceeded with under sections 20 to 31 of the Act:

- **(a)** the contravention of any consumer provision; and

- **(b)** the non-compliance with any compliance agreement entered into under an Act listed in Schedule 1 to the Act.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**3** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
