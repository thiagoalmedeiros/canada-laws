# NAFTA Prescribed Class of Goods Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing a Class of Goods for the Purposes of Subsection 35.02(4) of the Customs Act*

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 35.02(4)[^fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6274] and paragraph 164(1)(i)[^fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6275] of the [Customs Act](../acts/C-52.6.md)[^fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6276], is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing a class of goods for the purposes of subsection 35.02(4) of the Customs Act](SOR-93-608.md), effective on the day on which section 83 of [An Act to implement the North American Free Trade Agreement](../acts/N-23.8.md), being chapter 44 of the Statutes of Canada, 1993, comes into force.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [NAFTA Prescribed Class of Goods Regulations](SOR-93-608.md).

## Class of Goods

**2** The goods in respect of which any regulations made under paragraph 19(1)(a) of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) apply are hereby prescribed as a class of goods for the purposes of subsection 35.02(4) of the [Customs Act](../acts/C-52.6.md).
