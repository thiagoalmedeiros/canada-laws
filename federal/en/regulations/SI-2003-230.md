# Order Transferring from the Department of Justice to the Department of the Solicitor General the Control and Supervision of the National Crime Prevention Centre

SI/2003-230 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2003-12-31

P.C. 2003-2087 2003-12-12

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2003-230/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Department of Justice to the Department of the Solicitor General the control and supervision of the portion of the public service in the Department of Justice known as the National Crime Prevention Centre, effective December 12, 2003.
