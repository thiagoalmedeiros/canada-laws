# Transfer of Duties Order (International Experience Canada Program Unit)

SI/2013-96 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2013-08-31

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2013-09-11

P.C. 2013-904 2013-08-29

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2013-96/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development to the Department of Citizenship and Immigration the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration, in the Global Business Opportunities Bureau, known as the International Experience Canada Program Unit, effective August 31, 2013.
