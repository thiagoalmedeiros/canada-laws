# Order Declaring that the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not Apply in Yukon

SOR/2014-279 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2014-11-28

Enabling authority: [FISHERIES ACT](../acts/F-14.md)

Registration: 2014-11-28

P.C. 2014-1300 2014-11-27

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-279/)

Whereas the Minister of the Environment has caused to be published in the Canada Gazette, Part I, on June 28, 2014, a copy of the proposed [Order Declaring that the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not Apply in Yukon](SOR-2014-279.md), substantially in the annexed form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with respect to the proposed order;

And whereas on October 29, 2014, the Minister of the Environment and the Government of Yukon entered into an equivalency agreement under section 4.1[^fn_81000-2-1955-E_hq_13359] of the [Fisheries Act](../acts/F-14.md)[^fn_81000-2-1955-E_hq_13369] to the effect that there are in force by or under the laws of Yukon provisions in the Waters Act[^fn_81000-2-1955-E_hq_13360] and in the Waters Regulation[^fn_81000-2-1955-E_hq_13361] that are equivalent in effect to provisions in the [Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations](SOR-2012-139.md)[^fn_81000-2-1955-E_hq_13362];

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection 4.2(1)[^fn_81000-2-1955-E_hq_13359] of the [Fisheries Act](../acts/F-14.md)[^fn_81000-2-1955-E_hq_13369], makes the annexed [Order Declaring that the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not Apply in Yukon](SOR-2014-279.md).

## Declaration

### Definitions

**1** For the purposes of sections 2 and 3, ***effluent***, ***final discharge point*** and ***wastewater system*** have the same meanings as in section 1 of the [Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations](SOR-2012-139.md).

### Non-application — Regulations

**2** The [Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations](SOR-2012-139.md), made under subsection 36(5) of the [Fisheries Act](../acts/F-14.md), do not apply in respect of a wastewater system in Yukon that would otherwise have been subject to those regulations and that is subject to the Waters Act, S.Y. 2003, c. 19; 2007, c. 6 and the Waters Regulation, Yukon O.I.C. 2003/58.

### Non-application — subsection 36(3) of the Act

**3** Subsection 36(3) of the [Fisheries Act](../acts/F-14.md) does not apply in respect of any deposit of effluent from the final discharge point of a wastewater system referred to in section 2 if the effluent would otherwise have been regulated under the [Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations](SOR-2012-139.md).

## Coming into Force

### Registration

**4** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
