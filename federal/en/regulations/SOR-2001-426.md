# Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Banks, Bank Holding Companies, Insurance Companies and Insurance Holding Companies) Regulations

SOR/2001-426 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [BANK ACT](../acts/B-1.01.md)[INSURANCE COMPANIES ACT](../acts/I-11.8.md)

Registration: 2001-10-04

P.C. 2001-1797 2001-10-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-426/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 978[^footnotea_e] of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md)[^footnoteb_e] and 1021[^footnotec_e] of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md)[^footnoted_e], hereby makes the annexed [Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Banks, Bank Holding Companies, Insurance Companies and Insurance Holding Companies) Regulations](SOR-2001-426.md).

## Maximum Fee

### Maximum fee

**1** For the purposes of subsections 85(2) of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md) and 89(2) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md), the maximum fee that may be charged for a security certificate issued in respect of a transfer is $5.
