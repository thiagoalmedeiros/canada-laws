# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2021-9 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-03-17

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2021-03-31

P.C. 2021-170 2021-03-17

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2021-9/)

His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-2159_hq_28733] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2159_hq_28734], transfers from the Minister of National Revenue to the Minister of the Environment the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of National Revenue under section 188 of the [Greenhouse Gas Pollution Pricing Act](../acts/G-11.55.md)[^fn_81000-3-2159_hq_28735].
