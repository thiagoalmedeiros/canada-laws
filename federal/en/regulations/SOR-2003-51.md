# Regulations for the Purposes of the Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act

SOR/2003-51 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CANADA STRATEGIC INFRASTRUCTURE FUND ACT](../acts/C-10.3.md)

Registration: 2003-01-30

P.C. 2003-112 2003-01-30

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-51/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph 5(a) of the [Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](../acts/C-10.3.md)[^footnotea], hereby makes the annexed Regulations Prescribing Capital Assets for the Purpose of the Definition ***Strategic Infrastructure*** in the [Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](../acts/C-10.3.md).

## Capital Assets

**1** The following are prescribed as other fixed capital assets for the purpose of paragraph (f) of the definition ***strategic infrastructure*** in section 2 of the [Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](../acts/C-10.3.md):

- **(a)** advanced telecommunications and high-speed broadband infrastructure; and

- **(b)** all infrastructure situated in Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut not otherwise prescribed in paragraph (a) of this subsection or set out in any of paragraphs (a) to (e) of that definition.

<sub>History: SOR/2004-200, s. 2</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day they are registered.
