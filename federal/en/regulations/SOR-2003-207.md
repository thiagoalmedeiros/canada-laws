Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(j) of the [Excise Act, 2001](../acts/E-14.1.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Respecting the Fees for the Examination of Instruments and the Provision of Tables](SOR-2003-207.md).

## Fees

**1** The fee to be paid for the examination of an instrument under subsection 148(2) of the [Excise Act, 2001](../acts/E-14.1.md) is $25.

**2** The fee for each copy of the tables set out in column 1 of the chart to this section is set out in column 2 of the chart. No fee is payable for copies of tables provided to an alcohol licensee or licensed user at the time their licence is issued.
