# Order Designating the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada as the Minister for the Purposes of the Building Canada Act

SI/2025-89 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-07-03

Enabling authority: [BUILDING CANADA ACT](../acts/B-9.89.md)

Registration: 2025-07-16

P.C. 2025-567 2025-07-03

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-89/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the [Building Canada Act](../acts/B-9.89.md)[^fn_81000-3-2939_hq_1750341153], designates the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Building Canada Act](../acts/B-9.89.md).
