# Minister of Agriculture Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/86-138 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1986-08-06

P.C. 1986-1647 1986-07-23

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-86-138/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Agriculture and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to revoke the [Minister of Agriculture Authority to Prescribe Fees Order](C.R.C.,_c._712.md) made by Order in Council P.C. 1972-1/1430 of 27th June, 1972 and to authorize the Minister of Agriculture to prescribe by order the fee to be charged to persons requesting the blood typing of animals.
