# Order Transferring from the Minister for International Development to the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada

SI/2023-38 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-07-26

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2023-08-16

P.C. 2023-763 2023-07-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-38/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Minister for International Development to the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada the powers, duties and functions of the Minister for International Development in relation to the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada.
