Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, pursuant to paragraphs 11(2)(g)[^fn_SOR-99-382_e_hq_4296] and 26.03(1)(e)[^fn_SOR-99-382_e_hq_4297] of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](../acts/C-3.md), hereby makes the annexed Exemption from Deposit Insurance By-law (Exemption Fee).

September 29, 1999

The Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 26.05[^fn_SOR-99-382_e_hq_4297] of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](../acts/C-3.md), hereby approves the annexed Exemption from Deposit Insurance By-law (Exemption Fee) made by the Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation.

Ottawa, October 4, 1999

## Fee Payable

**1** The fee payable under paragraph 26.03(1)(e) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](../acts/C-3.md) by a federal member institution that applies under section 26.02 of that Act for authorization to accept deposits without being a member institution is $20,000.

<sub>History: SOR/2008-105, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** This By-law comes into force on October 15, 1999.
