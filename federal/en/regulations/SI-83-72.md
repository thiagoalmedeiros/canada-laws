# Pari-Mutuel Payments Order

*Order Respecting Payments to Be Made for the Supervision of Pari-Mutuel Systems of Betting*

SI/83-72 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CRIMINAL CODE](../acts/C-46.md)

Registration: 1983-04-13

P.C. 1983-846 1983-03-24

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-83-72/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Agriculture and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 188(4) of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md), is pleased hereby to revoke the [Pari-Mutuel Payments Order](SI-83-72.md) made by Order in Council P.C. 1980-3512 of 19th December, 1980[^footnote_e], and to make the annexed Order respecting payments to be made for the supervision of pari-mutuel systems of betting, effective April 1, 1983.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Pari-Mutuel Payments Order](SI-83-72.md).

## Payments

**2** For the purposes of subsection 188(4) of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md) the fraction fixed is eight-tenths of one per cent.
