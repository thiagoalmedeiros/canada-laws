# Order Transferring from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the Department of Human Resources Development the Control and Supervision of the Voluntary Sector Affairs Directorate

SI/2003-231 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2003-12-31

P.C. 2003-2088 2003-12-12

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2003-231/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the Department of Human Resources Development the control and supervision of the portion of the public service in the Department of Canadian Heritage known as the Voluntary Sector Affairs Directorate, effective December 12, 2003.
