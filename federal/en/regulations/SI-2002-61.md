# Gross Revenue Insurance Program Account for the Province of Alberta Remission Order

SI/2002-61 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2002-04-10

P.C. 2002-485 2002-03-22

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food and the Treasury Board, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, hereby remits to the province of Alberta, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), any interest accrued until March 31, 2002 on the federal share of the Gross Revenue Insurance Program Account for the Province of Alberta.
