# Order Designating the Inquiry under the name of the Public Inquiry into Foreign Interference in Federal Electoral Processes and Democratic Institutions as a Department and the Prime Minister as the Appropriate Minister

SI/2023-63 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-09-07

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2023-10-11

P.C. 2023-883 2023-09-07

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-63/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister,

**(a)** under paragraph (b)[^fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080400] of the definition *department* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080424], designates the commission that is named the Inquiry under the name of the Public Inquiry into Foreign Interference in Federal Electoral Processes and Democratic Institutions as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** under paragraph (b)[^fn_81000-3-2566_hq_1689080463] of the definition *appropriate Minister* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)<sup>b</sup>, designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the commission named in paragraph (a).
