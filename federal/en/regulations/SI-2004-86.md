# Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development the Control and Supervision of the Aboriginal Affairs Secretariat

SI/2004-86 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2004-08-11

P.C. 2004-849 2004-07-20

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2004-86/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development the control and supervision of the portion of the public service within the Intergovernmental Affairs Branch in the Privy Council Office known as the Aboriginal Affairs Secretariat, effective July 20, 2004.
