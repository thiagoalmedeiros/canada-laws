Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 49.9[^fn_a] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_b], makes the annexed Regulations Defining ***EU country or other CETA beneficiary***.

## Interpretation

**1** For the purposes of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md), ***EU country or other CETA beneficiary*** means a country or territory set out in the schedule to these Regulations.

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_Ind8411_hq_20657] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 97 of the [Canada–European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement Implementation Act](../acts/C-4.8.md), chapter 6 of the Statutes of Canada, 2017, comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
