# Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development as the Minister for the Purposes of the Accessible Canada Act

SI/2025-44 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [ACCESSIBLE CANADA ACT](../acts/A-0.6.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-424 2025-03-14

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the [Accessible Canada Act](../acts/A-0.6.md)[^fn_81000-3-2849_hq_1741734725],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2023-1064 of October 27, 2023[^fn_81000-3-2849_hq_1741734704]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Employment and Social Development, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
