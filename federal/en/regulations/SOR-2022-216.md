# Critical Habitat of the Fawnsfoot (Truncilla donaciformis) Order

SOR/2022-216 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-10-20

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../acts/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2022-10-20

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-216/)

Whereas the Fawnsfoot (Truncilla donaciformis) is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31137];

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2)[^fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31138] of that Act;

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is of the opinion that the annexed Order would affect a reserve or other lands that are set apart for the use and benefit of a band and, under subsection 58(7)[^fn_81000-2-4877_hq_1657539797] of that Act, has consulted with the Minister of Indigenous Services and the band in question with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Fawnsfoot (Truncilla donaciformis) Order](SOR-2022-216.md) under subsections 58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-4877_hq_31137].

Ottawa, October 17, 2022

## Application

**1** Subsection 58(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md) applies to the critical habitat of the Fawnsfoot (Truncilla donaciformis), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
