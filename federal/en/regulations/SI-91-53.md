# Order Giving Notice that a Tax Agreement Between Canada and Papua New Guinea Came into Force on December 21, 1989

SI/91-53 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: CANADA-PAPUA NEW GUINEA INCOME TAX AGREEMENT ACT, 1989[AGREEMENTS AND CONVENTIONS](../acts/Z-02.md)

Registration: 1991-04-10

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-91-53/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Notice is hereby given, pursuant to section 18 of the Canada-Papua New Guinea Income Tax Agreement Act, 1989[^fn_SI-91-53_e_hq_6214], that the Agreement between Canada and Papua New Guinea for the Avoidance of Double Taxation and the Prevention of Fiscal Evasion with respect to Taxes on Income came into force on December 21, 1989.

Ottawa, February 2, 1991

MICHAEL WILSON

*Minister of Finance*
