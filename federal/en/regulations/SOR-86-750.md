# Pacific Hake Exemption Notice

*Notice Excepting Certain Pacific Hake from the Operation of Section 29 of the Fisheries Act*

The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to section 29 of the [Fisheries Act](../acts/F-14.md), hereby makes the annexed Notice excepting certain Pacific hake from operation of section 29 of the [Fisheries Act](../acts/F-14.md).

Ottawa, July 3, 1986

TOM SIDDON

*Minister of Fisheries and Oceans*

## Short Title

**1** This Notice may be cited as the [Pacific Hake Exemption Notice](SOR-86-750.md).

## Exemption

**2** Pacific hake caught in those waters described in the [Pacific Fishery Management Area Regulations](SOR-82-215.md) as Areas 121, 123, 124, 125 and 126 are hereby excepted from the operation of section 29 of the [Fisheries Act](../acts/F-14.md).
