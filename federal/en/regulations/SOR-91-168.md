# Canadian Heraldic Authority Fee Order

*Order Prescribing the Fee for Processing Applications Submitted to the Canadian Heraldic Authority for the Grant of Coats of Arms*

SOR/91-168 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1991-02-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-91-168/)

The Prime Minister, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1990-2224 of October 11, 1990[^fn_SOR-91-168_e_hq_5227], hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee for processing applications submitted to the Canadian Heraldic Authority for the grant of coats of arms](SOR-91-168.md).

Ottawa, February 27, 1991

BRIAN MULRONEY

*Prime Minister*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Canadian Heraldic Authority Fee Order](SOR-91-168.md).

## Fee

**2** The fee payable by an applicant for the processing of an application submitted to the Canadian Heraldic Authority for the grant of a coat of arms is $435.

**3** The fee referred to in section 2 shall be paid by cheque payable to the Receiver General for Canada and shall be included with a letter acknowledging receipt of notice from the Canadian Heraldic Authority that a warrant has issued authorizing the grant of a coat of arms to the applicant.
