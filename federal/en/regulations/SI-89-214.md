# Solicitor General of Canada Authority to Prescribe Fees or Charges Order (RCMP Training Academy-Regina)

SI/89-214 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1989-10-11

P.C. 1989-1898 1989-09-21

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-89-214/)

Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the cost of providing services or the use of facilities to persons by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police should be borne by the persons to whom those services or facilities are provided.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby:

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1985-137 of January 17, 1985[^fn_SI-89-214_e_hq_6458]; and

**(b)** to authorize the Solicitor General of Canada to prescribe, by order, the fee or charge to be paid by persons to whom services or the use of facilities is provided by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police at its Training Academy (Depot Division) in Regina, Saskatchewan.
