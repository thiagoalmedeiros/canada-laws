# Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons for the Purposes of the Canada Elections Act

SI/2025-53 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [CANADA ELECTIONS ACT](../acts/E-2.01.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-434 2025-03-14

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-53/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Elections Act](../acts/E-2.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699900],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-86 of February 12, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699962]; and

**(b)** designates the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, for the purposes of that Act.
