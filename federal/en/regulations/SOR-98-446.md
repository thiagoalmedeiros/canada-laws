# Divestiture of Service Transitional Coverage Regulations

SOR/98-446 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](../acts/P-36.md)[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1998-08-28

T.B. 826504 1998-08-25

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-98-446/)

The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(v.5)[^fn_SOR-98-446_e_hq_4101] of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](../acts/P-36.md) and paragraph 7(2)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), hereby makes the annexed [Divestiture of Service Transitional Coverage Regulations](SOR-98-446.md).

## Interpretation

**1** The definitions in this section apply in these Regulations.

***Act*** means the [Public Service Superannuation Act](../acts/P-36.md). (***Loi***)

***person or body*** means a person or body to whom the administration of a service is transferred or otherwise divested under subsection 40.1(1) of the Act. (***cessionnaire***)

## Maximum Period

**2** For the purpose of paragraph 40.1(2)(a) of the Act, the maximum period during which a person or body may form part of the Public Service is three years.

<sub>History: SOR/2001-213, s. 1</sub>

## Condition

**3** As a condition of remaining part of the Public Service in accordance with a direction of Treasury Board made under paragraph 40.1(2)(a) of the Act, a person or body shall make monthly payments into the Superannuation Account or the Public Service Pension Fund of the amounts determined in accordance with section 9 of the [Public Service Superannuation Regulations](C.R.C.,_c._1358.md).

<sub>History: SOR/2001-213, s. 2</sub>

## Coming into Force

**4** These Regulations come into force on August 28, 1998.
