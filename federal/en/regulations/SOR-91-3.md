# Export Inspection and Certification Exemption Regulations

*Regulations Exempting Animals from the Application of Section 19 of the Health of Animals Act*

SOR/91-3 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [HEALTH OF ANIMALS ACT](../acts/H-3.3.md)

Registration: 1990-12-13

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-91-3/)

The Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to subsection 19(6) of the [Health of Animals Act](../acts/H-3.3.md)[^fn_SOR-91-3_e_hq_5222], hereby makes the annexed [Regulations exempting animals from the application of section 19 of the Health of Animals Act](SOR-91-3.md).

Dated at Ottawa, the 10th day of December, 1990

DON MAZANKOWSKI

*Minister of Agriculture*

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Export Inspection and Certification Exemption Regulations](SOR-91-3.md).

## Exemptions

**2** All animals are exempt from the application of section 19 of the [Health of Animals Act](../acts/H-3.3.md) except for horses, swine, foxes, mink, members of the suborder rumenatiae and members of the family camelidae.
