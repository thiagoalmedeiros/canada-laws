# Designating the Civil Aviation Tribunal as a Department; the Minister of Transport as Appropriate Minister for Purposes of the Financial Administration Act and as Minister for Purposes of Section 31 of the Aeronautics Act

SI/86-64 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)[AERONAUTICS ACT](../acts/A-2.md)

Registration: 1986-05-14

P.C. 1986-1068 1986-05-01

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-86-64/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition of ***department*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), to designate the Civil Aviation Tribunal as a department for the purposes of that Act;

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition of ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), to designate the Minister of Transport as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Civil Aviation Tribunal for the purposes of that Act; and

**(c)** pursuant to section 31[^footnote1star_e] of the [Aeronautics Act](../acts/A-2.md), to designate the Minister of Transport, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that section.

[^footnote1star_e]: S.C. 1985, c. 28, s. 5
