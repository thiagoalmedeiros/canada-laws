The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1988-440 of March 10, 1988[^footnote1star_e], hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee to be paid for bait services provided by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans](SOR-96-180.md).

Ottawa, March 29, 1996

FRED MIFFLIN

*Minister of Fisheries and Oceans*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Bait Services Fee Order](SOR-96-180.md).

## Fee

**2** The fee to be paid by a person to whom bait services are provided by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans is $ 0.60 per kilogram of bait.
