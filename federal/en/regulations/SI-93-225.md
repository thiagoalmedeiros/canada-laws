# Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

SI/93-225 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1993-12-15

P.C. 1993-1977 1993-12-02

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-225/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General In Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***department*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), to designate the Commission of Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), to designate the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Commission of Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada.
