# Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Standards Council of Canada Act

SI/93-117 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [STANDARDS COUNCIL OF CANADA ACT](../acts/S-16.md)

Registration: 1993-07-14

P.C. 1993-1463 1993-06-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-117/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in section 2 of the [Standards Council of Canada Act](../acts/S-16.md), is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1982-15 of January 12, 1982[^fn_SI-93-117_e_hq_6517]; and

**(b)** to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Standards Council of Canada Act](../acts/S-16.md).
