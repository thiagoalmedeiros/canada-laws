# Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Tourism) to the Minister of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Tourism Commission Act

SI/2025-40 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-420 2025-03-14

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-40/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers to the Minister of Industry the powers, duties and functions under the [Canadian Tourism Commission Act](../acts/C-23.3.md)[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741736111] that were transferred to the Minister of State (Tourism) by Order in Council P.C. 2023-769 of July 26, 2023[^fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741736266].
