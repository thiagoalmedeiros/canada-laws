# Exemption from Restrictions on Investments (Insurance Companies, Insurance Holding Companies and Societies) Regulations

SOR/2001-385 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2010-01-01

Enabling authority: [INSURANCE COMPANIES ACT](../acts/I-11.8.md)

Registration: 2001-10-04

P.C. 2001-1756 2001-10-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-385/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraphs 501(a)[^fn_SOR-2001-385_e_hq_6007] and (b)[^fn_SOR-2001-385_e_hq_6007] and 554(9)(a)[^fn_SOR-2001-385_e_hq_6009] and (b)[^fn_SOR-2001-385_e_hq_6009] of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md)[^fn_SOR-2001-385_e_hq_6011], hereby makes the annexed [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments (Insurance Companies, Insurance Holding Companies and Societies) Regulations](SOR-2001-385.md).

## Exemption from Restrictions on Investments

### Prescribed circumstances — life companies and insurance holding companies

**1** For the purposes of subparagraph 495(3)(d)(ii) and 971(3)(d)(ii) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md), subsections 495(6) to (8) of the Act do not apply in determining whether a company would be permitted to acquire a substantial investment in an entity under subsection 493(2), paragraph 493(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 493(4) or 495(1) or (2) of the Act.

### Prescribed circumstances — property and casualty companies and marine companies

**2** For the purpose of subparagraph 495(5)(d)(ii) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md), subsections 495(6) to (8) of the Act do not apply in determining whether a company would be permitted to acquire a substantial investment in an entity under subsection 493(2), paragraph 493(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 495(1) or (4) of the Act.

<sub>History: SOR/2009-296, s. 29</sub>

### Prescribed circumstances — societies

**3** For the purpose of subparagraph 554(3)(d)(ii) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md), subsections 554(4) and (5) of the Act do not apply in determining whether a society would be permitted to acquire a substantial investment in an entity under subsection 552(2), paragraph 552(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 554(1) or (2) of the Act.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**4**[^fn_SOR-2001-385_e_hq_6012] These Regulations come into force on the day on which paragraphs 501(a) and (b) and 554(9)(a) and (b) of the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md), as enacted by sections 426 and 437 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../acts/F-11.1.md), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
