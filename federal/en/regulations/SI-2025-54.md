# Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons as the Minister for the Purposes of the Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Suspension Act, 1994

SI/2025-54 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [ELECTORAL BOUNDARIES READJUSTMENT SUSPENSION ACT, 1994](../acts/E-3.1.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-435 2025-03-14

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-54/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-84 of February 12, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2860_hq_1741752760]; and

**(b)** designates the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Suspension Act, 1994](../acts/E-3.1.md)[^fn_81000-3-2860_hq_1741752788].
