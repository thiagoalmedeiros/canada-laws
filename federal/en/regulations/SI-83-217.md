# Minister of Transport Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/83-217 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1983-12-14

P.C. 1983-3759 1983-11-30

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-83-217/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Transport to prescribe, by order, the fees and charges to be paid by the Regional District of Peace River-Liard in the Province of British Columbia for the water supply service and sewage disposal service provided by the Fort St. John Airport to the residential community of that District.
