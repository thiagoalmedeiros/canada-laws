# Order Authorizing the Minister of Foreign Affairs to Prescribe the Fee

SI/2000-111 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

P.C. 2000-1723 2000-11-30

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)[^fn_5496_hq_19986] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), hereby authorizes the Minister of Foreign Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by foreign nationals to participate in an international youth exchange program in Canada.
