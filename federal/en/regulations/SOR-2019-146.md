Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 9(1)(b)[^fn_81000-2-3766_hq_24502] of the [Currency Act](../acts/C-52.md)[^fn_81000-2-3766_hq_24503], makes the annexed [Order Calling in Certain Notes](SOR-2019-146.md).

## Notes called in

**1** Notes issued under the [Bank of Canada Act](../acts/B-2.md) in the following denominations are called in:

- **(a)** one dollar;

- **(b)** two dollars;

- **(c)** twenty-five dollars;

- **(d)** five hundred dollars; and

- **(e)** one thousand dollars.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on January 1, 2021.
