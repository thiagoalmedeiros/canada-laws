# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs to be the Minister for the purposes of the Act

SI/2015-99 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-11-04

Enabling authority: [NEW BRIDGE FOR THE ST. LAWRENCE ACT](../acts/N-22.6.md)

Registration: 2015-11-18

P.C. 2015-1242 2015-11-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-99/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [New Bridge for the St. Lawrence Act](../acts/N-22.6.md)[^fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947], effective November 4, 2015,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2014-857 of June 26, 2014[^fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of the [New Bridge for the St. Lawrence Act](../acts/N-22.6.md)[^fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947].
