# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2019-123 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-11-20

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2019-12-11

P.C. 2019-1361 2019-11-20

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26069] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26070], transfers from the Minister of Science to the Minister of Industry the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Science under Part I of the [Budget Implementation Act, 1997](../acts/B-9.81.md)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26092] that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2018-1013 of July 18, 2018[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26093].
