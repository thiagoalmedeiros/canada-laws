His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[^fn_SI-98-8_e_hq_6439] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), hereby makes the annexed [Charitable Goods Remission Order](SI-98-8.md).

## Remission

**1** Remission is hereby granted of all the taxes paid or payable under Division III of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), and of the customs duties paid or payable under section 21 of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md), on goods donated by a non-resident of Canada to religious, charitable or educational institutions in Canada.

## Repeal

**2** \[Repeal\]

## Coming into Force

**3** This Order comes into force on January 1, 1998.
