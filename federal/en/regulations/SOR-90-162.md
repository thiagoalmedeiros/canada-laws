# Nordion Europe S.A. Incorporation Authorization Order

*Order Authorizing Nordion International Inc. to Incorporate Nordion Europe S.A. under the Laws of Belgium*

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is satisfied that Nordion International Inc., as a corporation incorporated under the [Canada Business Corporations Act](../acts/C-44.md), is empowered to undertake the transaction described in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of State (Privatization and Regulatory Affairs) and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 91(1)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order authorizing Nordion International Inc. to incorporate Nordion Europe S.A. under the laws of Belgium](SOR-90-162.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Nordion Europe S.A. Incorporation Authorization Order](SOR-90-162.md).

## Authorized Transaction

**2** Nordion International Inc. is hereby authorized to incorporate, under the laws of Belgium, Nordion Europe S.A., some of the shares of which, on incorporation, will be held by, on behalf of or in trust for Nordion International Inc.
