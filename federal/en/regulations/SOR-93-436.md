# Programming Undertaking Regulations

*Regulations Defining Programming Undertaking*

SOR/93-436 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [COPYRIGHT ACT](../acts/C-42.md)

Registration: 1993-08-26

P.C. 1993-1684 1993-08-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-93-436/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology and the Secretary of State of Canada, pursuant to subsection 3(1.41)[^fn_SOR-93-436_e_hq_6262] of the [Copyright Act](../acts/C-42.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations defining programming undertaking](SOR-93-436.md), effective August 31, 1993.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Programming Undertaking Regulations](SOR-93-436.md).

## Programming Undertaking

**2** For the purpose of subsection 3(1.4) of the [Copyright Act](../acts/C-42.md), ***programming undertaking*** means a network, other than a network within the meaning of the [Broadcasting Act](../acts/B-9.01.md), consisting of

- **(a)** a person who transmits by telecommunication all or part of the person’s programs or programming directly or indirectly to the person referred to in paragraph (b); and

- **(b)** a person who communicates all or part of the programs or programming referred to in paragraph (a) to the public by telecommunication.
