# Refined Sugar Anti-dumping Duty Remission Order, 2026

SOR/2026-108 · Consolidated: 2026-06-14 · Last amended: 2026-06-04

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2026-06-04

P.C. 2026-548 2026-06-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2026-108/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, makes the annexed Refined Sugar Anti-dumping Duty Remission Order, 2026 under section 115[^fn_81000-2-5747_hq_1737645423] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_81000-2-5747_hq_1737645443].

## Remission

**1** **(1)** Subject to subsection (2), remission is granted of the anti-dumping duties that were paid under the [Special Import Measures Act](../acts/S-15.md) in respect of refined sugar products that were imported by a company set out in column 1 of the schedule and recorded under a transaction number (B3-3 or B2 Form) set out in column 2.

*Conditions*

**(2)** Remission is granted on the following conditions:

- **(a)** a claim for remission is made to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the day on which this Order comes into force; and

- **(b)** on request of the Canada Border Services Agency, the claimant files all evidence that is required by the Agency to determine eligibility for remission.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
