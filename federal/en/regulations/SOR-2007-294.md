# Olympic and Paralympic Marks Regulations

SOR/2007-294 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2007-12-17

Enabling authority: [OLYMPIC AND PARALYMPIC MARKS ACT](../acts/O-9.2.md)

Registration: 2007-12-13

P.C. 2007-1924 2007-12-13

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-294/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 12(1) of the [Olympic and Paralympic Marks Act](../acts/O-9.2.md)[^fn_Olympic2_hq_2651], hereby makes the annexed [Olympic and Paralympic Marks Regulations](SOR-2007-294.md).

## Prescribed Period

**1** For the purposes of paragraph 3(4)(a), subsection 4(1), paragraph 5(2)(a), section 6 and paragraph 8(2)(a) of the [Olympic and Paralympic Marks Act](../acts/O-9.2.md), the prescribed period begins on the day on which these Regulations come into force and ends on December 31st, 2010.

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_Ind1494_hq_3622] These Regulations come into force on the day on which subsection 12(1) of the [Olympic and Paralympic Marks Act](../acts/O-9.2.md) comes into force.
