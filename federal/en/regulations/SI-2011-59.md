# Order Transferring the Internal E-staffing Unit from Human Resources and Skills Development to the Public Service Commission

SI/2011-59 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2011-06-30

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2011-07-20

P.C. 2011-796 2011-06-30

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2011-59/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], hereby transfers to the Public Service Commission from the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration in the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development known as the Internal E-staffing Unit, effective July 1, 2011.
