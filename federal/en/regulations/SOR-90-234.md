# Advance Income Tax Ruling Fees Order

*Order Prescribing the Fee To Be Paid by a Person to whom a Service is Provided in Respect of a Request for an Advance Income Tax Ruling from the Department of National Revenue*

SOR/90-234 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2022-04-01

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1990-04-19

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-90-234/)

The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 19(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md) and Order in Council P.C. 1989-1949 of September 28, 1989[^fn_SOR-90-234_e_hq_5206], hereby revokes the Department of National Revenue (Taxation) Advance Rulings Fees Order, made on May 25, 1983[^fn_SOR-90-234_e_hq_5207], and makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee to be paid by a person to whom a service is provided in respect of a request for an advance income tax ruling from the Department of National Revenue](SOR-90-234.md) in substitution therefor.

Ottawa, Ontario, April 18, 1990

OTTO JELINEK

*Minister of National Revenue*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Advance Income Tax Ruling Fees Order](SOR-90-234.md).

## Fee

**2** The fee payable by a person who requests the Minister of National Revenue to provide an advance income tax ruling, whether or not the request is withdrawn, is $221.24 during the period beginning on April 1, 2022 and ending on March 31, 2023 and $281.22 as of April 1, 2023, for each hour or part of an hour that is spent in preparing the ruling.
