# Order Designating the Minister of Health as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Assisted Human Reproduction Agency of Canada for Purposes of the Act

SI/2006-128 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-11-15

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2006-11-15

P.C. 2006-1138 2006-10-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-128/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[^footnotea_e] of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), hereby designates the Minister of Health as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Assisted Human Reproduction Agency of Canada for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md).

[^footnotea_e]: S.C. 1992, c. 1, s. 69(2)
