# CUFTA Rules of Origin Regulations

SOR/2024-68 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-07-01

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2024-04-19

P.C. 2024-385 2024-04-19

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-68/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, makes the annexed [CUFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-2024-68.md) under subsection 16(2)[^fn_81000-2-5203_hq_1697222553] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_81000-2-5203_hq_1697222565].

## Rules of Origin

### Force of law

**1** The following provisions of the Canada–Ukraine Free Trade Agreement have the force of law in Canada:

- **(a)** Articles 3.1 to 3.14; and

- **(b)** Annexes 3-A and 3-C.

## Repeal

**2** The [CUFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-2024-68.md)[^fn_81000-2-5203_hq_1697224346] are repealed.

## Coming into Force

### S.C. 2024, c. 3

**3**[^fn_SOR-2024-68_e_transform_hq_1715189715] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 9 of the [Canada–Ukraine Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act, 2023](../acts/C-10.541.md) comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
