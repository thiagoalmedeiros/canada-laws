Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 978[^fn_610299-E_hq_2866] of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md)[^fn_610299-E_hq_2867], hereby makes the annexed [Material Banking Group Percentage Regulations](SOR-2008-163.md).

## Prescribed Percentage

### Prescribed percentage

**1** For the purposes of subsection 508(2) of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md), the percentage is 35%.

### Prescribed percentage

**2** For the purposes of subsection 508(3) of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md), the percentage is 50%.
