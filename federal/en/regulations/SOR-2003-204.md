Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(o) of the [Excise Act, 2001](../acts/E-14.1.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby makes the annexed [Continuation of Amalgamated or Merged Corporations Regulations](SOR-2003-204.md).

## Prescribed Purposes

**1** For the purposes of section 213 of the [Excise Act, 2001](../acts/E-14.1.md), the purpose of applying any provision of Parts 5 and 6, other than section 213, of that Act is a prescribed purpose.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations take effect on July 1, 2003.
