# Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Office of Infrastructure of Canada the Control and Supervision of the Cities Secretariat

SI/2004-103 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2004-08-11

P.C. 2004-870 2004-07-20

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2004-103/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Office of Infrastructure of Canada the control and supervision of the portion of the public service in the Privy Council Office known as the Cities Secretariat, effective July 20, 2004.
