His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to sections 130, 133 and 135 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1](../acts/E-0.8.md)[^fn_a], makes the annexed [Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Sections 130, 133 and 135 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1](SOR-2015-13.md).

## General

### Day for purposes of certain sections

**1** The day for the purposes of sections 130, 133 and 135 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1](../acts/E-0.8.md)[^fn_8656_hq_19931] is fixed as June 1, 2018.

<sub>History: SOR/2017-92, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^fn_IndABEE_hq_15209] This Order comes into force on the first day on which both sections 114 and 115 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1](../acts/E-0.8.md)[^fn_IndAC54_hq_15210] are in force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
