# Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Control and Supervision of the Emergency Preparedness Secretariat

SI/2024-57 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-11-08

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2024-11-20

P.C. 2024-1182 2024-11-08

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2024-57/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-2784_hq_1729277451] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2784_hq_1729277469], transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration in the Privy Council Office known as the Emergency Preparedness Secretariat, effective November 21, 2024.
