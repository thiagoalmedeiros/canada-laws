# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2018-72 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-07-18

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2018-08-08

P.C. 2018-1017 2018-07-18

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2018-72/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22381] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22382], transfers from the Minister of Canadian Heritage to the Minister of La Francophonie the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Canadian Heritage under the [Official Languages Act](../acts/O-3.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-1662-E_hq_22383].
