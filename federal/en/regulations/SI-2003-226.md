# Order Transferring from the Minister of Canadian Heritage to the Minister of the Environment the Control and Supervision of the Parks Canada Agency

SI/2003-226 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2003-12-31

P.C. 2003-2081 2003-12-12

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2003-226/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Minister of Canadian Heritage to the Minister of the Environment the control and supervision of that portion of the public service known as the Parks Canada Agency, effective December 12, 2003.
