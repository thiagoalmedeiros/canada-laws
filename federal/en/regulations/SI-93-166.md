# Indian Income Tax Remission Order

SI/93-166 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1993-08-25

P.C. 1993-1649 1993-08-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-166/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[^fn_SI-93-166_e_hq_6518] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to remit amounts that would be remitted if the reference to subsection 87(1) of the [Indian Act](../acts/I-5.md) in the [Indian Income Tax Remission Order](SI-93-44.md) included a reference to a provision similar to that subsection in an Act cited in that Order.
