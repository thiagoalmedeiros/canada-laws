# Order Designating Ontario for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code

SOR/2009-277 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2009-12-15

Enabling authority: [CRIMINAL CODE](../acts/C-46.md)

Registration: 2009-10-01

P.C. 2009-1628 2009-10-01

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-277/)

Whereas Ontario has legislative measures that protect recipients of payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost of borrowing under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Ontario has requested that the Governor in Council designate that province for the purposes of section 347.1[^fn_610628-e_hq_3365] of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3366];

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3365] of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3366], hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating Ontario for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md)](SOR-2009-277.md).

## Province Designated

**1** Ontario is designated for the purposes of section 347.1 of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md).

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_IndE901_hq_8059] This Order comes into force at 12:00 a.m., Eastern time or — in that part of Ontario in the Central time zone — Central time, on the first day on which both of the following are in force:

- **(a)** the Payday Loans Act, 2008, S.O. 2008, c. 9, except for sections 52 and 66 to 74; and

- **(b)** Ontario Regulation 98/09, except for sections 37 and 38.
