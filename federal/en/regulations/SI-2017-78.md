# Order Designating the Deputy Minister of the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as Deputy Head in respect of that Entity

SI/2017-78 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-11-29

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2017-12-13

P.C. 2017-1468 2017-11-29

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2017-78/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[^fn_608651-E_hq_152] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_610261-E_hq_4314], designates the Deputy Minister of the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as deputy head in respect of that entity, effective November 30, 2017.
