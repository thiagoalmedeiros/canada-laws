# Order Transferring to the Department of Agriculture and Agri-Food the Control and Supervision of the Portion of the Public Service known as the Retail Food Program of the Department of Industry

SI/95-85 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 1995-08-09

P.C. 1995-1245 1995-07-26

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-95-85/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), is pleased hereby to transfer to the Department of Agriculture and Agri-Food the control and supervision of the portion of the public service in the Department of Industry known as the Retail Food Program.
