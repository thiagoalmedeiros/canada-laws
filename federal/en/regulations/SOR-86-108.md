# Tax Rebate Discounting Regulations

*Regulations Concerning the Statement Describing the Discounting Transaction and the Notice of the Actual Amount of the Refund of Tax Received by the Discounter*

SOR/86-108 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [TAX REBATE DISCOUNTING ACT](../acts/T-3.md)

Registration: 1986-01-10

P.C. 1986-103 1986-01-10

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-86-108/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs, pursuant to section 10[^fn_SOR-86-108_e_hq_3778] of the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](../acts/T-3.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations concerning the statement describing the discounting transaction and the notice of the actual amount of the refund of tax received by the discounter](SOR-86-108.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Tax Rebate Discounting Regulations](SOR-86-108.md).

## General

**2** The statement describing the discounting transaction that is required by subparagraph 4(1)(b)(i) of the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](../acts/T-3.md) shall be as prescribed in Schedule I.

<sub>History: SOR/92-509, s. 1</sub>

**3** The notice of the actual amount of the refund of tax received by the discounter that is required by paragraph 5(b) of the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](../acts/T-3.md) shall be as prescribed in Schedule II.

<sub>History: SOR/92-509, s. 1</sub>
