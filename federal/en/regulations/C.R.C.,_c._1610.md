## Declaration

**1** The game listed in the schedule is hereby declared to be game in danger of becoming extinct pursuant to subsection 17(3) of the [Yukon Act](../acts/Y-2.01.md).

## SCHEDULE

- Bison (buffalo)

- Black-tailed deer

- Elk

- Musk-oxen
