# Regulations Establishing the Period Within Which Owners of Copyright not Represented by Collective Societies Can Claim Retransmission Royalties

SOR/97-164 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [COPYRIGHT ACT](../acts/C-42.md)

Registration: 1997-03-19

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

The Copyright Board, pursuant to paragraph 70.66(3)(b)[^footnotea] of the [Copyright Act](../acts/C-42.md), hereby makes the annexed Regulations Establishing the Period for Royalty Entitlements of Non-members of Collecting Bodies.

Ottawa, March 18, 1997

**1** The owner of copyright who does not authorize a collective society to collect, for that person's benefit, the royalties referred to in paragraph 31(2)(d) of the [Copyright Act](../acts/C-42.md) and whose work is so retransmitted has a period of two years following the end of the calendar year in which the retransmission occurred to claim the royalties payable under subsection 76(1) of that Act.

<sub>History: SOR/2004-152, s. 2</sub>

**2** These Regulations come into force on March 19, 1997.
