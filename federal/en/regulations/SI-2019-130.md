# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2019-130 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-11-20

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2019-12-11

P.C. 2019-1368 2019-11-20

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2019-130/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26069] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26070], transfers from the Minister of La Francophonie to the Minister of Economic Development and Official Languages

**(a)** the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of La Francophonie under the [Canadian Tourism Commission Act](../acts/C-23.3.md)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26071] that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2018-1016 of July 18, 2018[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26072]; and

**(b)** the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of La Francophonie under the [Official Languages Act](../acts/O-3.01.md)[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26081] that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2018-1017 of July 18, 2018[^fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26082].
