# Order Transferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness) the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness under Section 7 of the Emergency Management Act

SI/2024-58 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-11-08

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)[EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT ACT](../acts/E-4.56.md)

Registration: 2024-11-20

P.C. 2024-1183 2024-11-08

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2024-58/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1725647994] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1725648015], transfers from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness) the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness under section 7 of the [Emergency Management Act](../acts/E-4.56.md)[^fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1726161363].
