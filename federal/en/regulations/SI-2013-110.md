# Order Repealing Order in Council P.C. 2008-12 and Designating the Minister of Health as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Canadian Food Inspection Agency for the purposes of the Act

SI/2013-110 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2013-10-07

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2013-10-23

P.C. 2013-1053 2013-10-04

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2013-110/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[^fn_81000-3-754-E_hq_12658] of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-754-E_hq_12659],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2008-12 of January 16, 2008[^fn_81000-3-754-E_hq_12660], and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Health as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Canadian Food Inspection Agency for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-754-E_hq_12659],

effective October 7, 2013.
