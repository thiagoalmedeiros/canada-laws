# Satellites and Satellite Subsystems Remission Order, 1988

*Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Satellites and Satellite Subsystems for Testing and on Goods for Use in the Manufacture of Satellites and Satellite Subsystems*

SOR/88-532 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 1988-10-18

P.C. 1988-2374 1988-10-17

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-88-532/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_1e], to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duties on satellites and satellite subsystems for testing and on goods for use in the manufacture of satellites and satellite subsystems](SOR-88-532.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Satellites and Satellite Subsystems Remission Order, 1988](SOR-88-532.md).

## Remission

**2** Subject to section 3, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) on satellites and satellite subsystems for testing and on goods for use in the manufacture of satellites and satellite subsystems, imported on or after January 1, 1988.

## Condition

**3** The remission referred to in section 2 is granted on condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue within three years of the date of importation.
