Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(f) of the [Excise Act, 2001](../acts/E-14.1.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Respecting the Information to be Displayed on Alcohol Containers and their Packaging](SOR-2003-201.md).

## Information

**1** For the purpose of section 87 of the [Excise Act, 2001](../acts/E-14.1.md), the prescribed information to be displayed is

- **(a)** the name and address of the alcohol licensee who packaged the alcohol; or

- **(b)** their licence number.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations take effect on July 1, 2003.
