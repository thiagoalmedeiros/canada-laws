His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 60.01(7)[^fn_SOR-95-380_e_hq_6357] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_SOR-95-380_e_hq_6358], is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing agricultural goods in respect of any country for the purpose of imposing a safeguard surtax, 1995-2, effective August 1, 1995.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Safeguard Surtax Regulations, 1995-2](SOR-95-380.md).

## Goods Subject to Safeguard Surtax

**2** For the purposes of section 68 of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md), the following agricultural goods, classified under the tariff item numbers that follow them, are prescribed in respect of any country:

- **(a)** cream (0401.30.10, 0401.30.20);

- **(b)** powdered whey (0404.10.21, 0404.10.22); and

- **(c)** butter and other fats and oils derived from milk (0405.10.10, 0405.10.20, 0405.90.10, 0405.90.20).
