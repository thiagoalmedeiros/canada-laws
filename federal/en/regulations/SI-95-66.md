# Minister of Foreign Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/95-66 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1995-05-31

P.C. 1995-783 1995-05-16

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-95-66/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraphs 19(1)(b)[^fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20089] and 19.1(b)[^fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20089] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to repeal Order in Council P.C. 1989-1700 of August 24, 1989[^fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20103] and to authorize the Minister of Foreign Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by any person to whom a permit or a certificate is issued pursuant to the [Export and Import Permits Act](../acts/E-19.md).
