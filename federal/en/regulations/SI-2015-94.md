# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs as the Minister for the purposes of the Act

SI/2015-94 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-11-04

Enabling authority: [CANADA STRATEGIC INFRASTRUCTURE FUND ACT](../acts/C-10.3.md)

Registration: 2015-11-18

P.C. 2015-1235 2015-11-04

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-94/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in section 2 of the [Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](../acts/C-10.3.md)[^fn_320_hq_10667], effective November 4, 2015,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2014-376 of April 7, 2014[^fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](../acts/C-10.3.md)[^fn_320_hq_10667].
