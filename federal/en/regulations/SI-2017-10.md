# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2017-10 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-02-01

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2017-02-22

P.C. 2017-76 2017-02-01

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2017-10/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612481-E_hq_7374] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612481-E_hq_7375], transfers from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of State styled the Minister of Science the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Industry under Part I of the [Budget Implementation Act, 1997](../acts/B-9.81.md)[^fn_81000-3-1338-E_hq_19113].
