His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Acting Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 42.1(1) of the [Canadian Human Rights Act](../acts/H-6.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the manner and form for making an appeal from the decision or order of a Human Rights Tribunal](SOR-80-394.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Human Rights Tribunal Appeal Regulations](SOR-80-394.md).
