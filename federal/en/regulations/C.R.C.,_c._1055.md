## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [National Energy Board Order No. MO-62-69](C.R.C.,_c._1055.md).

## Application of part IV of the act

**2** The National Energy Board, pursuant to subsection 97(1)[^footnote1_e] of the [National Energy Board Act](../acts/N-7.md), hereby orders that Part IV of the said Act applies to every person who was operating a pipeline on November 2, 1959.
