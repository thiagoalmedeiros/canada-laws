# Order Designating the Minister of Public Works and Government Services as the Minister for the purposes of the Act

SI/2015-100 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-11-04

Enabling authority: [CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](../acts/C-10.md)

Registration: 2015-11-18

P.C. 2015-1243 2015-11-04

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-100/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](../acts/C-10.md)[^fn_81000-3-1110-E_hq_16222], effective November 4, 2015,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2006-67 of February 6, 2006[^fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](../acts/C-10.md)[^fn_81000-3-1110-E_hq_16222].
