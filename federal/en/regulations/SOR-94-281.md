# Nautical Charts and Related Publications Fees Order

*Order Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid for the Provision of Nautical Charts and Related Publications*

SOR/94-281 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1994-04-13

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-94-281/)

The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1988-440 of March 10, 1988[^fn_SOR-94-281_e_hq_5261], hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fees to be paid for the provision of nautical charts and related publications](SOR-94-281.md).

Ottawa, Ontario, April 13, 1994

BRIAN TOBIN

*Minister of Fisheries and Oceans*

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Nautical Charts and Related Publications Fees Order](SOR-94-281.md).

## Interpretation

**2** In this Order,

***chart sheet*** means each side of a sheet of paper on which a nautical chart is printed; (***feuille de carte***)

***GEBCO*** means General Bathymetric Charts of the Oceans. (***GEBCO***)

## Application

**3** This Order applies in respect of retail sales only.

## Fees

**4** Where the Department of Fisheries and Oceans provides a person with a nautical chart or related publication set out in column I of an item of the schedule, that person shall pay to the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans the fee set out in column II of that item.
