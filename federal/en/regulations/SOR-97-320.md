# Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Fruit and Vegetable Aggregate Quantity Limit Order

SOR/97-320 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 1997-07-05

P.C. 1997-951 1997-07-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-97-320/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 25.8[^footnotea_e] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^footnoteb_e], hereby makes the annexed Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Fruit and Vegetable Aggregate Quantity Limit Order.

## Tariff Rate Quota

**1** The quantity limit of goods of tariff item No. 0703.10.92 that are entitled to the Chile Tariff is, for the period set out in column 1 of item 1 of the schedule, the applicable quantity limit set out in column 2 of that item.

<sub>History: SOR/98-28, s. 30</sub>

**2** The aggregate quantity limit of goods of tariff item Nos. 0810.10.11 and 0810.10.92 that are entitled to the Chile Tariff is, for the period set out in column 1 of item 2 of the schedule, the applicable aggregate quantity limit set out in column 2 of that item.

<sub>History: SOR/98-28, s. 30</sub>

## Coming into Force

**3**[^fn_Ind79F1_hq_16411] This Order comes into force on the day on which section 51 of the [Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act](../acts/C-1.6.md), chapter 14 of the Statutes of Canada, 1997, comes into force.
