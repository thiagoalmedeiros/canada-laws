# Order Exempting Kapisikama Lake, Located in Quebec, from the Application of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act

SOR/2024-102 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-06-05

Enabling authority: [CANADIAN NAVIGABLE WATERS ACT](../acts/N-22.md)

Registration: 2024-05-27

P.C. 2024-578 2024-05-24

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-102/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Minister of Transport has received an application to exempt Kapisikama Lake, located in Quebec, from the application of section 23[^fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276193] of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](../acts/N-22.md)[^fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276236];

And whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that this exemption would be in the public interest;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, makes the annexed [Order Exempting Kapisikama Lake, Located in Quebec, from the Application of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act](SOR-2024-102.md) under subsection 24(1)[^fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276193] of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](../acts/N-22.md)[^fn_81100-2-391_hq_1689276236].

## Exemption

### Kapisikama Lake

**1** Kapisikama Lake, which is located in Quebec and whose location and description are set out in the table to this section, is exempt from the application of section 23 of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](../acts/N-22.md).
