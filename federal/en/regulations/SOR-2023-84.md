# Associate Judges (Tax Court of Canada) Regulations

SOR/2023-84 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-05-01

Enabling authority: [TAX COURT OF CANADA ACT](../acts/T-2.md)

Registration: 2023-05-01

P.C. 2023-373 2023-04-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-84/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, makes the annexed [Associate Judges (Tax Court of Canada) Regulations](SOR-2023-84.md) under subsections 11.1(2)[^fn_a] and (6)[^fn_a] of the [Tax Court of Canada Act](../acts/T-2.md)[^fn_b].

## Number of associate judges

**1** For the purposes of subsection 11.1(2) of the [Tax Court of Canada Act](../acts/T-2.md), the number of associate judges that may be appointed under subsection 11.1(1) of that Act is fixed at two.

## Workload — supernumerary associate judges

**2** For the purposes of subsection 11.1(6) of the [Tax Court of Canada Act](../acts/T-2.md), the workload of a supernumerary associate judge is fixed at a minimum of 50% of the workload of an associate judge.

## Coming into force

**3** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
