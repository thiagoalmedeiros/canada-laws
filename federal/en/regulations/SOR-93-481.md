His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(a)[^fn_SOR-93-481_e_hq_6265] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the fee for fingerprinting for the purposes of a visa, licence or security clearance](SOR-93-481.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Fingerprinting for Visa, Licensing or Security Clearance Purposes Fee Regulations.

## Fee

**2** The fee to be paid by an individual for fingerprinting for the purposes of obtaining a visa, licence or security clearance is $25.
