# Oil Product Designation Regulations

SOR/88-216 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2020-03-16

Enabling authority: [NATIONAL ENERGY BOARD ACT](../acts/N-7.md)

Registration: 1988-03-24

P.C. 1988-581 1988-03-24

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-88-216/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to section 89[^fn_4040_hq_8098] of the [National Energy Board Act](../acts/N-7.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations designating certain substances as oil products and exempting certain kinds of oil from the operation of certain provisions of the National Energy Board Act](SOR-88-216.md).

**1** \[Repealed, SOR/2020-52, s. 1\]

## Designation

**2** The following substances are designated as oil products:

- **(a)** methanol; and

- **(b)** methyl tertiary butyl ether.

## Exemption

**3** Methanol and methyl tertiary butyl ether are exempt from the operation of Part 9 of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](../acts/C-15.1.md).
