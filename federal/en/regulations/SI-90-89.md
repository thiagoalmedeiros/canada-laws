# Order Specifying the National Capital Region as the Place in Which Shall be Located the Head Office of Certain Museums

SI/90-89 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [MUSEUMS ACT](../acts/M-13.4.md)

Registration: 1990-07-18

P.C. 1990-1353 1990-06-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-90-89/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 25 of the [Museums Act](../acts/M-13.4.md)[^fn_SI-90-89_e_hq_6468], is pleased hereby to specify the National Capital Region, as described in the schedule to the [National Capital Act](../acts/N-4.md), as the place in which shall be located the head office of each of the following museums, namely,

**(a)** the National Gallery of Canada,

**(b)** the Canadian Museum of Civilization,

**(c)** the Canadian Museum of Nature, and

**(d)** the National Museum of Science and Technology,

effective July 1, 1990.
