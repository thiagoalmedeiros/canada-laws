# Order Transferring from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of National Revenue the Control and Supervision of the Royal Canadian Mint and the Powers, Duties and Functions under the Royal Canadian Mint Act

SI/2003-240 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2003-12-31

P.C. 2003-2098 2003-12-12

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2003-240/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of National Revenue

**(a)** the control and supervision of the Royal Canadian Mint, and

**(b)** the powers, duties and functions under the [Royal Canadian Mint Act](../acts/R-9.md),

effective December 12, 2003.
