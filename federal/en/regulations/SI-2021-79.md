# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2021-79 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-10-26

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2021-11-10

P.C. 2021-928 2021-10-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2021-79/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Minister of Economic Development and Official Languages to the Minister of Indigenous Services the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Economic Development and Official Languages with respect to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario.
