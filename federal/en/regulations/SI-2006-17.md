# Order Designating the Registrar of the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as Deputy Head

SI/2006-17 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2006-02-22

P.C. 2006-53 2006-02-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-17/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), hereby designates the Registrar of the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as deputy head in respect of that Office, effective February 6, 2006.
