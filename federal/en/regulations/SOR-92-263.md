# Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing the Maximum Fee that an Assocition May Charge for a Security Certificate Issued in Respect of a Transfer*

SOR/92-263 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](../acts/C-41.01.md)

Registration: 1992-05-07

P.C. 1992-912 1992-05-07

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-92-263/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 92(2) and section 463 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../acts/C-41.01.md)[^fn_1e], is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing the maximum fee that an association may charge for a security certificate issued in respect of a transfer, effective June 1, 1992.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations](SOR-92-263.md).

## Maximum Fee

**2** An association may charge a fee not exceeding five dollars for a security certificate issued in respect of a transfer.
