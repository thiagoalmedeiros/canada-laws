# Order Specifying that the Canadian Forces are a Portion of the Public Sector Employing One Hundred or more Employees for the Purpose of Subsection 4(1) of that Act

SOR/2002-420 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [EMPLOYMENT EQUITY ACT](../acts/E-5.401.md)

Registration: 2002-11-21

P.C. 2002-1956 2002-11-21

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity Act](../acts/E-5.401.md)[^footnote1_e], the Treasury Board has consulted the Minister of Labour and the Minister of National Defence concerning the application of that Act to the Canadian Forces;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board, and the Minister of Labour, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity Act](../acts/E-5.401.md)<sup>a</sup>, hereby specifies that the Canadian Forces are a portion of the public sector employing one hundred or more employees for the purpose of subsection 4(1) of that Act.
