# Order Extending the Application of the Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations

SOR/2016-42 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-03-11

Enabling authority: [FREEZING ASSETS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT](../acts/F-31.6.md)

Registration: 2016-03-11

P.C. 2016-125 2016-03-11

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-42/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section 6 of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act](../acts/F-31.6.md)[^fn_81000-2-2665-E_hq_16607], extends the application of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations](SOR-2011-78.md)[^fn_81000-2-2665-E_hq_16608] for a period of five years beginning on March 24, 2016.
