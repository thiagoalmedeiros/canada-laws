# Order Designating the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration as the Minister responsible for the administration of that Act

SI/2001-120 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [IMMIGRATION AND REFUGEE PROTECTION ACT](../acts/I-2.5.md)

Registration: 2001-12-19

P.C. 2001-2265 2001-12-06

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2001-120/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 4 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](../acts/I-2.5.md), assented to on November 1, 2001, being chapter 27 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, hereby designates the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister responsible for the administration of that Act, effective December 6, 2001.
