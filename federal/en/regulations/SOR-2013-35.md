# Exemption Order for Certain Licences, Authorizations and Documents (Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Alberta Population))

SOR/2013-35 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2013-03-08

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../acts/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2013-03-08

P.C. 2013-267 2013-03-07

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2013-35/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to section 76 of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-1571-E-GC2_hq_13832], makes the annexed [Exemption Order for Certain Licences, Authorizations and Documents (Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Alberta Population))](SOR-2013-35.md).

## Exemption

**1** Section 32 of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md) does not apply to the following licences, authorizations and documents for a period of one year from the date that the Westslope Cutthroat Trout (Alberta population) is added to the List of Wildlife Species at Risk set out in Schedule 1 to that Act:

- **(a)** the [Alberta Fishery Regulations, 1998](SOR-98-246.md) to the extent that they authorize sportfishing;

- **(b)** the licences issued under subsection 13(3) of the [Alberta Fishery Regulations, 1998](SOR-98-246.md); and

- **(c)** the [National Parks of Canada Fishing Regulations](C.R.C.,_c._1120.md) to the extent that they authorize fishing.

## Coming into Force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
