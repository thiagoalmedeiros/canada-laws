# Order Transferring from the Prime Minister to the President of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat

SI/2006-46 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2006-02-22

P.C. 2006-87 2006-02-06

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2006-46/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, hereby pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), transfers from the Prime Minister to the President of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada the control and supervision of the portion of the federal public administration known as the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat, effective February 6, 2006.
