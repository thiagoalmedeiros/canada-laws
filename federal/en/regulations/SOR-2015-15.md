His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 143 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1[^fn_a], makes the annexed Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 143 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1.

## General

### Day for purposes of section 143

**1** The day for the purposes of section 143 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1[^fn_1] is fixed as December 1, 2018.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^fn_IndE9B3_hq_15213] This Order comes into force on the first day on which sections 111, 139 and 140 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1](../acts/E-0.8.md)[^fn_IndE9B3_hq_15214] are all in force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
