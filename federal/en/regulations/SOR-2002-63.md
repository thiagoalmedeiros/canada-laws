Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 462.3(2)[^fn_SOR-2002-63_e_hq_5889] of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md), hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Excluding Certain Indictable Offences from the Definition of “Designated Offence”](SOR-2002-63.md).

## Exclusion

**1** The indictable offences under the following Acts are excluded from the definition ***designated offence*** in subsection 462.3(1) of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md):

- **(a)** \[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 36\]

- **(b)** \[Repealed, SOR/2018-108, s. 391\]

- **(c)** \[Repealed, SOR/2010-74, s. 1\]

- **(d) and (e)** \[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 36\]

- **(f)** [Feeds Act](../acts/F-9.md);

- **(g)** [Fertilizers Act](../acts/F-10.md);

- **(h)** [Foreign Publishers Advertising Services Act](../acts/F-29.6.md);

- **(i)** [Health of Animals Act](../acts/H-3.3.md);

- **(j)** \[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 36\]

- **(k)** \[Repealed, SOR/2018-108, s. 391\]

- **(l)** [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](../acts/N-28.3.md), except for the indictable offence under section 50;

- **(m)** [Plant Protection Act](../acts/P-14.8.md);

- **(m.1)** [Safe Food for Canadians Act](../acts/S-1.1.md); and

- **(n)** [Seeds Act](../acts/S-8.md).

<sub>History: 2010, c. 12, s. 36; SOR/2010-74, s. 1; SOR/2018-108, s. 391</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on February 1, 2002.
