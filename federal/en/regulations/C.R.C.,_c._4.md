# Air Cushion Vehicle Regulations

*Regulations Respecting Air Cushion Vehicles*

C.R.C., c. 4 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [AERONAUTICS ACT](../acts/A-2.md)

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._4/)

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Air Cushion Vehicle Regulations](C.R.C.,_c._4.md).

## Interpretation

**2** In these Regulations, ***air cushion vehicle*** means a machine designed to derive support in the atmosphere primarily from reactions against the earth’s surface of air expelled from the machine.

## Exclusion

**3** Commercial air services provided by means of air cushion vehicles are excluded from the operation of Part II of the [Aeronautics Act](../acts/A-2.md).
