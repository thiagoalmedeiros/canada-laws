Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 226(2)(b)[^fna_e] and section 277[^fnb_e] of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), hereby makes the annexed [Returnable Beverage Container (GST/HST) Regulations](SOR-2008-48.md).

## Prescribed Acts

**1** For the purposes of paragraph 226(2)(b) of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), the following Acts are prescribed:

- **(a)** the Environment Act, S.N.S. 1994-95, c. 1;

- **(b)** the Beverage Containers Act, R.S.N.B. 2011, c. 121;

- **(c)** the Beverage Containers Act, R.S.P.E.I. 1988, c. B-2.1; and

- **(d)** the Environmental Protection Act, S.N.L. 2002, c. E-14.2.
