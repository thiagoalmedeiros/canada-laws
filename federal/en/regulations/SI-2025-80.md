# Order Transferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Section 7 of the Emergency Management Act

SI/2025-80 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-05-13

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-06-04

P.C. 2025-491 2025-05-13

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-80/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2‍(a)[^fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839215] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839239], transfers from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience the powers, duties and functions under section 7 of the [Emergency Management Act](../acts/E-4.56.md)[^fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839271] that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2025-418 of March 14, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2909_hq_1746839825].
