His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 978[^fn_610575-E_hq_3466] of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md)[^fn_610575-E_hq_3469], section 463[^fn_610575-E_hq_3470] of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../acts/C-41.01.md)[^fn_610575-E_hq_3471] and section 531[^fn_610575-E_hq_3472] of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../acts/T-19.8.md)[^fn_610575-E_hq_3473], hereby makes the annexed [Prescribed Products Regulations](SOR-2011-100.md).

## Prescribed Products

**1** For the purposes of sections 385.131 and 385.241 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../acts/C-41.01.md) and sections 434.1 and 442.1 of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../acts/T-19.8.md), the following products are prescribed:

- **(a)** deposit type instruments as defined in the [Deposit Type Instruments Regulations](SOR-2011-98.md); and

- **(b)** principal protected notes as defined in the [Principal Protected Notes Regulations](SOR-2008-180.md).

<sub>History: SOR/2021-181, s. 115</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on November 1, 2011.
