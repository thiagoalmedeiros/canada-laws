# CJFTA Rules of Origin Regulations

SOR/2012-179 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2012-10-01

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2012-09-20

P.C. 2012-1080 2012-09-20

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-179/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 16(2)[^fn_3352_hq_9259] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_3352_hq_9260], hereby makes the annexed [CJFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-2012-179.md).

## Rules of Origin

**1** The following provisions of the Free Trade Agreement between Canada and the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan, signed on June 28, 2009, have the force of law in Canada:

- **(a)** Articles 4-1 and 4-2;

- **(b)** Article 4-3, paragraph 1;

- **(c)** Articles 4-4 to 4-13;

- **(d)** Article 4-15; and

- **(e)** Annex 4-1.

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_IndCE58_hq_13231] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 32 of the [Canada–Jordan Economic Growth and Prosperity Act](../acts/C-6.45.md), chapter 18 of the Statutes of Canada, 2012, comes into force.
