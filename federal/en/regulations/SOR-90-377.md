Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is satisfied that Canada Post Systems Management Limited, as a corporation incorporated under the [Canada Business Corporations Act](../acts/C-44.md), is empowered to undertake the transaction described in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Honourable Harvie Andre and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 91(1)(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order authorizing Canada Post Systems Management Limited to acquire shares](SOR-90-377.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Canada Post Systems Management Limited Authorization Order, 1990](SOR-90-377.md).

## Authorization

**2** Canada Post Systems Management Limited is hereby authorized to acquire shares, at an aggregate cost of $120,000, in G.D. Net B.V., a corporation incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands and having its head office located in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
