# Order Designating the Minister of Industry as the Designated Minister for the purposes of the Business Development Bank of Canada Act and as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Business Development Bank of Canada for the purposes of the Financial Administration Act

SI/2025-48 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT BANK OF CANADA ACT](../acts/B-9.9.md)[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-429 2025-03-14

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Designated Minister* in section 2 of the [Business Development Bank of Canada Act](../acts/B-9.9.md)[^fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740274], paragraph (d) of the definition *appropriate Minister* in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740307] and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition *appropriate Minister* in subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740307],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2023-1066 of October 27, 2023[^fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740340];

**(b)** designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Designated Minister for the purposes of the [Business Development Bank of Canada Act](../acts/B-9.9.md)[^fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740274]; and

**(c)** designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Business Development Bank of Canada for the purposes of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-3-2851_hq_1741740307].
