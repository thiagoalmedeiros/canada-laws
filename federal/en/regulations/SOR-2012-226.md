His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to sections 79[^fn_a] and 87[^fn_b] of the [Copyright Act](../acts/C-42.md)[^fn_c], makes the annexed [MicroSD Cards Exclusion Regulations (Copyright Act)](SOR-2012-226.md).

## Microsd Cards

**1** Memory cards in microSD form factor, including microSD, microSDHC and microSDXC cards, are excluded from the definition ***audio recording medium*** in section 79 of the [Copyright Act](../acts/C-42.md).

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
