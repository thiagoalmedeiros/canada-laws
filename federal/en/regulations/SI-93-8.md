# Minister of National Revenue Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/93-8 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1993-01-27

P.C. 1992-2597 1992-12-14

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-8/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)[^fn_SI-93-8_e_hq_6510] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of National Revenue to prescribe, by order, the fees or charges to be paid by persons who request that publications of the Department of National Revenue be distributed to them.
