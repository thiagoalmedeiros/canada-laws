# Shared Premises Regulations (Banks)

SOR/2002-339 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2008-03-06

Enabling authority: [BANK ACT](../acts/B-1.01.md)

Registration: 2002-09-24

P.C. 2002-1563 2002-09-24

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-339/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 413.3(4)(a)[^footnotea_e] of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md)[^footnoteb_e], hereby makes the annexed [Shared Premises Regulations (Banks)](SOR-2002-339.md).

## Definition of ***member institution***

**1** **(1)** In this section, ***member institution*** has the same meaning as in section 2 of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](../acts/C-3.md).

*Shared premises*

**(2)** A bank to which paragraph 413(1)(b) or (c) of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md) applies may carry on business in Canada on premises that are shared with those of a member institution that is affiliated with the bank if, on those premises, whenever a request is made in person to open a deposit account with the bank, the bank opens the account at a work space that is separate and distinct from every work space at which the member institution carries on business.

<sub>History: SOR/2008-63, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
