His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Employment and Immigration and the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 48.1[^fn_SOR-95-588_e_hq_6368] of the Unemployment Insurance Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order fixing the rate of premium that persons employed in insurable employment are required to pay for the year 1996](SOR-95-588.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Unemployment Insurance Rate of Premium, 1996, Order](SOR-95-588.md).

## Rate of Premium

**2** The rate of premium that persons employed in insurable employment are required to pay for the year 1996 is 2.95 per cent of insurable earnings in that year.
