# Order Designating the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act

SI/2019-60 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-07-09

Enabling authority: [FEDERAL PROMPT PAYMENT FOR CONSTRUCTION WORK ACT](../acts/F-7.7.md)

Registration: 2019-07-24

P.C. 2019-1048 2019-07-09

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2019-60/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [Federal Prompt Payment for Construction Work Act](../acts/F-7.7.md)[^fn_81000-3-1811_hq_24851], designates the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, who is a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purpose of that Act.
