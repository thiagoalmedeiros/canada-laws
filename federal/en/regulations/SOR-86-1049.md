# Energy Administration Act Sections 53 to 65 Non-application Order, 1986

*Order Declaring Sections 53 to 65 of the Energy Administration Act To Be Non-Applicable in Respect of any Producer-Province*

SOR/86-1049 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [ENERGY ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/E-6.md)

Registration: 1986-10-30

P.C. 1986-2427 1986-10-30

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1049/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to subsection 52(1.1)[^footnote_ea] of the [Energy Administration Act](../acts/E-6.md)[^footnote_eb], is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order declaring sections 53 to 65 of the Energy Administration Act to be non-applicable in respect of any producer-province, effective November 1, 1986.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the Energy Administration Act Sections 53 to 65 Non-application Order, 1986.

## Declaration

**2** Sections 53 to 65 of the [Energy Administration Act](../acts/E-6.md) do not apply in respect of any producer-province.
