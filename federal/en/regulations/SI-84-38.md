# Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/84-38 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1984-02-22

P.C. 1984-398 1984-02-02

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-84-38/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs to prescribe by order the fees to be paid for Laboratory Registration under the Urea Formaldehyde Insulation Act.
