Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 637[^footnotea_e] and paragraph 668(a)[^footnoteb_e] of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md)[^footnotec_e], hereby makes the annexed [Supervisory Information (Banks) Regulations](SOR-2001-59.md).

## Prescribed Supervisory Information

**1** **(1)** For the purposes of section 637 of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md), the following information or any component of that information is prescribed supervisory information in relation to a bank:

- **(a)** any rating assigned by the Superintendent to the bank to assess its financial condition and any other such rating that is substantially based on information obtained from the Superintendent;

- **(b)** any stage of intervention assigned to the bank under the Guide to Intervention for Federal Financial Institutions;

- **(c)** any order made in respect of the bank under subsection 485(3) of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md), any prudential agreement entered into by the bank under section 644.1 of that Act or any direction issued to it under section 645 of that Act;

- **(d)** any report prepared by or at the request of the Superintendent or any recommendation made by the Superintendent as a result of an annual or special examination or other supervisory review of the bank, including any related correspondence to or from the directors or officers of the bank; and

- **(e)** \[Repealed, SOR/2011-196, s. 4\]

- **(f)** any category in which the bank is classified under the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Differential Premiums By-law and any premium rate assigned to it and any annual premium determined for it under that By-law.

**(2)** For the purposes of section 637 of the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md), the information prescribed by the following regulations is prescribed supervisory information in relation to an affiliate of a bank:

- **(a)** the [Supervisory Information (Authorized Foreign Banks) Regulations](SOR-2001-58.md), if the affiliate is an authorized foreign bank to which the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md) applies;

- **(b)** the [Supervisory Information (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations](SOR-2001-57.md), if the affiliate is an association to which the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../acts/C-41.01.md) applies;

- **(c)** the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Companies) Regulations](SOR-2001-56.md), if the affiliate is a company, society, foreign company or provincial company to which the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md) applies;

- **(d)** the [Supervisory Information (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations](SOR-2001-55.md), if the affiliate is a company to which the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../acts/T-19.8.md) applies;

- **(e)** the [Supervisory Information (Bank Holding Companies) Regulations](SOR-2001-480.md), if the affiliate is a bank holding company to which the [Bank Act](../acts/B-1.01.md) applies; and

- **(f)** the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Holding Companies) Regulations](SOR-2001-484.md), if the affiliate is an insurance holding company to which the [Insurance Companies Act](../acts/I-11.8.md) applies.

<sub>History: SOR/2001-481, s. 1; SOR/2011-196, s. 4</sub>

## Prohibited Disclosure

**2** Subject to sections 3 and 4, a bank shall not, directly or indirectly, disclose information referred to in section 1.

<sub>History: SOR/2011-196, s. 5</sub>

## Limited Disclosure

**3** A bank may disclose information referred to in section 1 to its affiliates or to its directors, officers, employees, auditors, securities underwriters or legal advisors, or to those of its affiliates, if the bank ensures that the information remains confidential.

<sub>History: SOR/2011-196, s. 5</sub>

**4** A bank or any of its affiliates may disclose information referred to in paragraph 1(1)(c) if the bank or affiliate considers the information to contain a material fact or material change that is required by the securities laws of the relevant jurisdiction to be disclosed.

<sub>History: SOR/2011-196, s. 6(E)</sub>

## Coming into Force

**5** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
