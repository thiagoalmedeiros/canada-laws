Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_SOR-2011-254_e_hq_11581], hereby makes the annexed [Transfer of the Communications Security Establishment in the Department of National Defence Regulations](SOR-2011-254.md).

## Block Transfer

**1** Subsection 132(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md) applies to all persons employed or engaged in the portion of the federal public administration in the Department of National Defence known as the Communications Security Establishment.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on November 16, 2011.
