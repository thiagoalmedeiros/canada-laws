# Order Designating the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as a Department for the purposes of the Act

SI/2017-79 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-11-29

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../acts/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2017-12-13

P.C. 2017-1469 2017-11-29

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2017-79/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, effective November 30, 2017,

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition *department* in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_608630-E_hq_133], designates the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition *deputy head* in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_608630-E_hq_133], designates the Deputy Minister of the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as the deputy head of that entity for the purposes of that Act.
