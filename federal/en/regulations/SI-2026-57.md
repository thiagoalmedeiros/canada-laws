# Order Transferring from the Treasury Board Secretariat to Shared Services Canada the Control and Supervision of the Financial Management Transformation Sector and the Enterprise Resource Planning Solutions Division

SI/2026-57 · Consolidated: 2026-09-30 · Last amended: 2026-09-28

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2026-10-07

P.C. 2026-924 2026-09-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2026-57/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-3128_hq_1785877236] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-3128_hq_1785877254], transfers from the Treasury Board Secretariat to Shared Services Canada the control and supervision of those portions of the federal public administration in the Treasury Board Secretariat known as the Financial Management Transformation Sector and the Enterprise Resource Planning Solutions Division, effective October 1, 2026.
