His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 31 and paragraph 32.1(1)(b) of the [Canada Labour Code](../acts/L-2.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting hours of work for the class of employees known as commission salesmen who are employed in connection with the Radio and Television Broadcasting Industry in Canada](SOR-79-430.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Broadcasting Industry Commission Salesmen Hours of Work Regulations](SOR-79-430.md).

## Interpretation

**2** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means Part III of the [Canada Labour Code](../acts/L-2.md).

## Exemption

**3** The class of employees known as commission salesmen who are employed in connection with the radio and television broadcasting industry in Canada and paid on the basis of

- **(a)** a commission, or

- **(b)** a salary plus commission

are exempt from the application of sections 169, 171, 173 and 174 of the Act.
