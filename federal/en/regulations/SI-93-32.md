# Minister of Communications Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/93-32 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1993-03-24

P.C. 1993-428 1993-03-09

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-32/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Communications and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)[^fn_SI-93-32_e_hq_6512] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Communications to prescribe, by order, the fees or charges to be paid by a user for whom the Department of Communications provides any of the following services :

**(a)** the technical assessment, reassessment, testing or certification of telecommunications apparatus; and

**(b)** the labelling or marking of telecommunications apparatus as evidence of ongoing compliance with the technical requirements, specifications, standards or procedures of the Department of Communications.
