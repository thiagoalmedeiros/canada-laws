# Order Designating the Minister of Industry, who is a Federal Minister, to be the Minister for the Purposes of that Act

SI/2020-25 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2020-03-06

Enabling authority: [COLLEGE OF PATENT AGENTS AND TRADEMARK AGENTS ACT](../acts/C-33.7.md)

Registration: 2020-04-01

P.C. 2020-119 2020-03-06

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2020-25/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 4 of the [College of Patent Agents and Trade-mark Agents Act](../acts/C-33.7.md)[^fn_320_hq_10667], designates the Minister of Industry, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
