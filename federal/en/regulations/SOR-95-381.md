His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 60.01(7)[^fn_SOR-95-381_e_hq_6359] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_SOR-95-381_e_hq_6360], is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing agricultural goods in respect of any country for the purpose of imposing a safeguard surtax, 1995-3, effective August 1, 1995.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Safeguard Surtax Regulations, 1995-3](SOR-95-381.md).

## Goods Subject to Safeguard Surtax

**2** For the purposes of section 68 of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md), the following agricultural goods, classified under the tariff item numbers that follow them, are prescribed in respect of any country:
