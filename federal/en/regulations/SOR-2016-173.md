# Face Protectors for Ice Hockey and Box Lacrosse Players Regulations

SOR/2016-173 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-06-22

Enabling authority: [CANADA CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY ACT](../acts/C-1.68.md)

Registration: 2016-06-22

P.C. 2016-600 2016-06-21

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-173/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](../acts/C-1.68.md)[^fn_81200-2-TEXTILE], makes the annexed [Face Protectors for Ice Hockey and Box Lacrosse Players Regulations](SOR-2016-173.md).

## Requirement

### Standard

**1** Face protectors for ice hockey and box lacrosse players must meet the requirements of standard CAN3-Z262.2-M78, entitled Face Protectors for Ice Hockey and Box Lacrosse Players, published by the Canadian Standards Association in English in December 1978 and in French in June 1979.

## Repeal

**2** \[Repeal\]

## Coming into Force

### Registration

**3** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
