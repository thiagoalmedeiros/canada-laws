# Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order

SI/2010-81 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2010-10-28

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../acts/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2010-11-10

P.C. 2010-1341 2010-10-28

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2010-81/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 20 of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_1a], hereby approves

**(a)** the re-application of the provisions of that Act to the positions excluded pursuant to the Statistics Canada Census-Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order[^fn_1b] and the persons occupying them, and

**(b)** the exclusion from the application of the definitions *deployment* and *internal appointment process* in subsection 2(1) and of paragraphs 22(2)(a) to (c)[^fn_1c], and sections 40, 41[^fn_1d], 48, 51 to 53[^fn_1e], 57, 59 and 62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_1f], or the National Household Survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_1f] when the survey replaces the population census long-form questionnaire and is conducted in conjunction with the population census and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term basis, except for other persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the Statistic Act[^fn_1f].

Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply certain provisions of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_1a] to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_1f], or the National Household Survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_1f] when it replaces the population census long-form questionnaire and is conducted in conjunction with the population census and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term basis, except for other persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_1f];

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(2) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_1a], the Public Service Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_1a], hereby

**(a)** reapplies the provisions of that Act to the positions excluded pursuant to the Statistics Canada Census-Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order[^fn_1b] and the persons occupying them, and

**(b)** excludes from the application of the definitions *deployment* and *internal appointment process* in subsection 2(1) and of paragraphs 22(2)(a) to (c)[^fn_1c], and sections 40, 41[^fn_1d], 48, 51 to 53[^fn_1e], 57, 59 and 62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_1f], or the National Household Survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_1f] when the survey replaces the population census long-form questionnaire and is conducted in conjunction with the population census and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term basis, except for other persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the [Statistics Act](../acts/S-19.md)[^fn_1f].

This Order may be cited as the [Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order](SI-2017-75.md).

Ottawa, September 29, 2010
