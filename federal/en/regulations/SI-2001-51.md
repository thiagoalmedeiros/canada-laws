# Order transferring from the Prime Minister to the President of the Treasury Board the control and supervision of The Leadership Network

SI/2001-51 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2001-04-25

P.C. 2001-609 2001-04-11

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2001-51/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), hereby transfers from the Prime Minister to the President of the Treasury Board the control and supervision of that portion of the public service known as The Leadership Network.
