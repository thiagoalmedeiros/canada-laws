# Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of the Citizenship Act

SI/2023-24 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2023-06-27

Enabling authority: [CITIZENSHIP ACT](../acts/C-29.md)

Registration: 2023-07-19

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2023-24/)

Whereas, by the [Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of that Act for a Period of Five Years](SOR-2019-82.md)[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371464], the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection 28.1(1)[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333] of the [Citizenship Act](../acts/C-29.md)[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371523], extended the application of section 14[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300] of that Act for a period of five years, ending on July 31, 2024;

And whereas, under subsection 28.1(2)[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333] of that Act, the Minister may, before the expiry of each extended period, extend the application of section 14[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300] for up to five years;

Therefore, the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection 28.1(2)[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673372333] of the [Citizenship Act](../acts/C-29.md)[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1673371523], extends the application of section 14[^fn_81000-2-5043_hq_1675278300] of that Act until July 31, 2027.

Ottawa, June 27, 2023
