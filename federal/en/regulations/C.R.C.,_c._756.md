# Demonstration Aircraft Remission Order

*Order Respecting Remission of Sales Tax on Domestically Manufactured Aircraft Used for Demonstration to Prospective Customers*

C.R.C., c. 756 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._756/)

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Demonstration Aircraft Remission Order](C.R.C.,_c._756.md).

## Interpretation

**2** In this Order, “aircraft” includes helicopters.

## Remission of Sales Tax

**3** Subject to section 4, remission is hereby granted of the sales tax payable under the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md) on any aircraft manufactured in Canada at the time of its first use in Canada.

<sub>History: SI/86-58, s. 1</sub>

## Conditions

**4** The remission referred to in section 3 is granted on condition that

- **(a)** the aircraft is used only by the manufacturer of the aircraft for the purpose of demonstrating the aircraft to prospective customers of the manufacturer;

- **(b)** the manufacturer keeps complete records of the use of the aircraft; and

- **(c)** the manufacturer produces the records for inspection when requested to do so by an officer of the Department of National Revenue administering or enforcing the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md).

<sub>History: SI/86-58, s. 1</sub>
