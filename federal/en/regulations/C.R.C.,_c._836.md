# Dogfish Exemption Notice

*Notice Excepting Dogfish from the Operation of Section 29 of the Fisheries Act*

C.R.C., c. 836 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FISHERIES ACT](../acts/F-14.md)

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._836/)

## Short Title

**1** This Notice may be cited as the [Dogfish Exemption Notice](C.R.C.,_c._836.md).

## Exemption

**2** Dogfish and other fish caught incidental to fishing for dogfish in the Goose Island Banks of Hecate Straits are hereby excepted from the operation of section 29 of the [Fisheries Act](../acts/F-14.md).
