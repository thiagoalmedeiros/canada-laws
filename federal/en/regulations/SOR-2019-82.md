# Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of that Act for a Period of Five Years

SOR/2019-82 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-03-28

Enabling authority: [CITIZENSHIP ACT](../acts/C-29.md)

Registration: 2019-03-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-82/)

The Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, pursuant to subsection 28.1(1)[^fn_81000-2-3663_hq_23655] of the [Citizenship Act](../acts/C-29.md)[^fn_81000-2-3663_hq_23656], extends the application of section 14 of that Act for a period of five years, ending on July 31, 2024.

Ottawa, March 25, 2019
