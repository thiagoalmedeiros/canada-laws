His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](../acts/C-1.68.md)[^fn_81200-2-TEXTILE_hc], makes the annexed [Ice Hockey Helmet Regulations](SOR-2016-186.md).

## Requirement

### Standard

**1** Ice hockey helmets must meet the requirements of standard Z262.1, entitled Ice hockey helmets, published by the Canadian Standards Association, as amended from time to time.
