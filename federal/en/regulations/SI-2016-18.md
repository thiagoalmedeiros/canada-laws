# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2016-18 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-03-31

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2016-04-20

P.C. 2016-209 2016-03-31

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2016-18/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612481-E_hq_7374] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612481-E_hq_7375], transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration in the Privy Council Office known as the Canadian Secretariat to the Canada-U.S. Regulatory Cooperation Council, effective April 1, 2016.
