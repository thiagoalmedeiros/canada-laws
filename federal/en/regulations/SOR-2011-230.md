# Prescribed Entities and Classes of Mortgages and Hypothecs Regulations

SOR/2011-230 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2012-01-01

Enabling authority: [INTEREST ACT](../acts/I-15.md)

Registration: 2011-10-21

C.P. 2011-1235 2011-10-20

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-230/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 10(3)[^fn_81000-2-800-E_hq_10377] of the [Interest Act](../acts/I-15.md)[^fn_81000-2-800-E_hq_10378], hereby makes the annexed [Prescribed Entities and Classes of Mortgages and Hypothecs Regulations](SOR-2011-230.md).

## Prescribed entities, mortgages and hypothecs

**1** For the purposes of paragraph 10(2)(b) of the [Interest Act](../acts/I-15.md),

- **(a)** the following entities are prescribed:

  - **(i)** partnerships,

  - **(ii)** trusts settled for business or commercial purposes,

  - **(iii)** unlimited liability corporations as defined in the Business Corporations Act, R.S.A. 2000, c. B-9,

  - **(iv)** unlimited liability companies as defined in the Business Corporations Act, S.B.C. 2002, c. 57, and

  - **(v)** unlimited companies as defined in the Companies Act, R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 81; and

- **(b)** the prescribed class of mortgages and hypothecs consists of those issued after January 1, 2012.

## Coming into force

**2** These Regulations come into force on January 1, 2012.
