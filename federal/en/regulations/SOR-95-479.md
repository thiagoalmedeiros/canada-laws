His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to section 7 of the Department of Natural Resources Act[^footnote1star_e], is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting the report on the state of Canada’s forests.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Report on the State of Canada’s Forests Regulations](SOR-95-479.md).

## Report

**2** By June 30 of each year, the Minister of Natural Resources shall prepare a report respecting the state of the forests in Canada and their contribution to the economy and environment of Canada and to the social well-being of Canadians.
