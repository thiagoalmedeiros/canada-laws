His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance pursuant to subsections 313(2)[^fn_1a] and 531(1)[^fn_1b] of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../acts/T-19.8.md)[^fn_1c], hereby makes the annexed [Annual Statement (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations](SOR-2010-232.md).

## Contents of Annual Statement

**1** The prescribed statements referred to in subsection 313(2) of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../acts/T-19.8.md) are

- **(a)** a balance sheet as at the end of the financial year;

- **(b)** a statement of income for the financial year;

- **(c)** a statement of cash flows for the financial year;

- **(d)** a statement of changes in shareholders’ equity for the financial year; and

- **(e)** a statement of comprehensive income for the financial year.

<sub>History: SOR/2014-273, s. 46(F); SOR/2015-32, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on June 1, 2011.
