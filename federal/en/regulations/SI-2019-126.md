# Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development, a Minister of the Crown, to be “the Minister” for the purposes of that Act

SI/2019-126 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-11-20

Enabling authority: [CANADA DISABILITY SAVINGS ACT](../acts/C-3.5.md)

Registration: 2019-12-11

P.C. 2019-1364 2019-11-20

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2019-126/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 4 of the [Canada Disability Savings Act](../acts/C-3.5.md)[^fn_320_hq_10667],

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2018-1021 of July 18, 2018[^fn_81000-3-1906_hq_26064]; and

**(b)** designates the Minister of Employment and Social Development, a Minister of the Crown, to be “the Minister” for the purposes of that Act.
