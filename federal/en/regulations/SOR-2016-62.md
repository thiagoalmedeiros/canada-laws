# Personal Health Information Custodians in Nova Scotia Exemption Order

SOR/2016-62 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2016-03-29

Enabling authority: [PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS ACT](../acts/P-8.6.md)

Registration: 2016-03-29

P.C. 2016-161 2016-03-24

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-62/)

Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that the Personal Health Information Act, SNS 2010, c. 41, of Nova Scotia, which is substantially similar to Part 1 of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](../acts/P-8.6.md)[^fn_81000-2-2452-E_hq_15413], applies to the personal health information custodians referred to in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph 26(2)(b) of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](../acts/P-8.6.md)[^fn_81000-2-2452-E_hq_15413], makes the annexed [Personal Health Information Custodians in Nova Scotia Exemption Order](SOR-2016-62.md).

## Exemption

**1** Any personal health information custodian to which the Personal Health Information Act, SNS 2010, c. 41, of Nova Scotia applies is exempt from the application of Part 1 of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](../acts/P-8.6.md) in respect of the collection, use and disclosure of personal health information that occurs in Nova Scotia.

## Coming into Force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
