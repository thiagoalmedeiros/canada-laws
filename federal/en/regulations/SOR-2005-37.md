The Attorney General of Canada, pursuant to paragraph (b)[^footnotea_e] of the definition ***approved container*** in subsection 254(1) of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md), hereby makes the annexed [Order Approving Blood Sample Containers](SOR-2005-37.md).

Ottawa, February 11, 2005

Irwin Cotler

Attorney General of Canada

## Approved Containers

**1** The following containers, being containers of a kind that is designed to receive a sample of blood of a person for analysis, are hereby approved as suitable, in respect of blood samples, for the purposes of section 258 of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md):

- **(a)** Vacutainer® XF947;

- **(b)** BD Vacutainer™ 367001;

- **(c)** Vacutainer® 367001;

- **(d)** Tri-Tech Inc. TUG10;

- **(e)** BD Vacutainer® REF 367001; and

- **(f)** TRITECHFORENSICS TUG10.
