# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2017-5 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-02-01

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2017-02-22

P.C. 2017-71 2017-02-01

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612481-E_hq_7374] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612481-E_hq_7375], transfers from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of State styled the Minister of Small Business and Tourism the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Industry under the [Canadian Tourism Commission Act](../acts/C-23.3.md)[^fn_81000-3-1332-E_hq_19122].
