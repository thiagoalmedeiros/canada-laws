# Fitness and Amateur Sport Regulations

*Regulations Defining Fitness and Amateur Sport*

C.R.C., c. 868 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PHYSICAL ACTIVITY AND SPORT ACT](../acts/P-13.4.md)

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._868/)

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Fitness and Amateur Sport Regulations](C.R.C.,_c._868.md).

## Interpretation

**2** For the purposes of the [Fitness and Amateur Sport Act](../acts/F-25.md),

***amateur sport*** means any athletic activity when engaged in solely for recreation, fitness or pleasure and not as a means of livelihood; (***sport amateur***)

***fitness*** means the state in which a person is able to function at his physical and mental optimum. (***santé***)
