His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(a)[^fn_SOR-93-484_e_hq_6267] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the fee to be paid for the issuance of licences for the possession or sale of devices used for the surreptitious interception of private communications](SOR-93-484.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Private Communications Interception Fee Regulations](SOR-93-484.md).

## Fee

**2** The fee to be paid by an individual, for the issuance of a licence by the Solicitor General of Canada for the possession or sale of devices used for the surreptitious interception of private communications, is $56.
