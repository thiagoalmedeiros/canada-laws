# First Nations Tax Commissioner Appointment Regulations

SOR/2006-243 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-12-16

Enabling authority: FIRST NATIONS FISCAL AND STATISTICAL MANAGEMENT ACT

Registration: 2006-10-05

P.C. 2006-1078 2006-10-05

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-243/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to subsection 20(3) and paragraph 140(a) of the First Nations Fiscal and Statistical Management Act[^fn_SOR-2006-243_e_hq_1448]<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed [First Nations Tax Commissioner Appointment Regulations](SOR-2006-243.md).

## Body to Appoint

### Named body

**1** For the purposes of subsection 20(3) of the [First Nations Fiscal Management Act](../acts/F-11.67.md), a commissioner shall be appointed by the Indigenous Law Centre at the University of Saskatchewan.

<sub>History: SOR/2024-277, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
