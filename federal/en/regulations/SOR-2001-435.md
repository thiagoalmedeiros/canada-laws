Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 531[^fn_SOR-2001-435_e_hq_5924] of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../acts/T-19.8.md)[^fn_SOR-2001-435_e_hq_5925], hereby makes the annexed [Total Assets for Public Holding Requirements (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations](SOR-2001-435.md).

## Total Assets

### Definition of ***total assets***

**1** For the purpose of subsection 380(3) of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](../acts/T-19.8.md), ***total assets***, in respect of a company, at a particular time, means the total value of the assets that would be reported on its balance sheet, prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in subsection 313(4) of that Act.

## Coming into Force

### Coming into force

**2**[^fn_SOR-2001-435_e_hq_5926] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 3 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](../acts/F-11.1.md), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes into force.
