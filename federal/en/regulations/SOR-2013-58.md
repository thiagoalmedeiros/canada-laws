# Transfer of a Portion of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency Regulations

SOR/2013-58 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2013-03-31

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](../acts/P-33.01.md)

Registration: 2013-03-27

P.C. 2013-342 2013-03-27

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2013-58/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md)[^fn_a], makes the annexed [Transfer of a Portion of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency Regulations](SOR-2013-58.md).

## Block Transfer

**1** Subsection 132(1) of the [Public Service Employment Act](../acts/P-33.01.md) applies to all persons employed or engaged in the portion of the federal public administration in the Canadian Food Inspection Agency known as the Domestic Terrestrial Animal Pathogen Unit.

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on March 31, 2013.
