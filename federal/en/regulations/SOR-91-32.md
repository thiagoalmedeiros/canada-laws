# Mail and Courier Imports (GST/HST) Regulations

*Regulations Prescribing Taxable Goods Imported by Mail or Courier*

SOR/91-32 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [EXCISE TAX ACT](../acts/E-15.md)

Registration: 1990-12-18

P.C. 1990-2741 1990-12-18

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-91-32/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[^fn_SOR-91-32_e_hq_6140] of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing taxable goods imported by mail or courier](SOR-91-32.md).

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Mail and Courier Imports (GST/HST) Regulations](SOR-91-32.md).

<sub>History: SOR/2001-63, s. 1</sub>

## Interpretation

**2** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md). (*Loi*)

## Prescribed Goods

**3** For the purposes of section 7 of Schedule VII to the Act, the following imported goods are prescribed goods:

- **(a)** excisable goods and wine;

- **(b)** goods that are prescribed property for the purposes of section 143.1 of the Act, if the supplier of the goods is required to register under Subdivision d of Division V of Part IX of the Act and is not so registered;

- **(c)** goods for which the value for duty is reduced by the application of section 85 of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md);

- **(d)** goods that are purchased from a retailer in Canada and mailed or transported from outside Canada directly to the purchaser; and

- **(e)** goods that are purchased or ordered through or from a person in Canada acting for or on behalf of a person outside Canada who is selling the goods.

<sub>History: SOR/2001-63, s. 2</sub>

## RELATED PROVISIONS

###### — SOR/2001-63, s. 3(2)

**3.** **(2)** Subsection 2(1) applies to goods imported after November 26, 1997.
