# Order Designating the Minister of Labour as the Minister for the Purposes of that Act

SI/2020-24 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2020-03-06

Enabling authority: [PAY EQUITY ACT](../acts/P-4.2.md)

Registration: 2020-04-01

P.C. 2020-118 2020-03-06

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2020-24/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 5 of the [Pay Equity Act](../acts/P-4.2.md)[^fn_320_hq_10667], designates the Minister of Labour, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
