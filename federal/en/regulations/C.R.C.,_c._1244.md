# C.N.R. Company Exemption Order

*Order Exempting the C.N.R. Company from Subsection 10(2) of the Official Languages Act*

C.R.C., c. 1244 · Consolidated: 2025-09-15 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [OFFICIAL LANGUAGES ACT](../acts/O-3.01.md)

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1244/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [C.N.R. Company Exemption Order](C.R.C.,_c._1244.md).

## Exemption

**2** The Canadian National Railway Company is hereby exempted from the application of subsection 10(2) of the [Official Languages Act](../acts/O-3.01.md) in respect of services provided or made available by it to the public, other than to the travelling public, at places elsewhere than in Canada.
