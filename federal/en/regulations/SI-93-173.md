# Order Designating the Minister of Finance as Minister for Purposes of the Act

SI/93-173 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [NORDION AND THERATRONICS DIVESTITURE AUTHORIZATION ACT](../acts/N-23.7.md)

Registration: 1993-09-08

P.C. 1993-1673 1993-08-18

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-93-173/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in subsection 2(1) of the [Nordion and Theratronics Divestiture Authorization Act](../acts/N-23.7.md)[^fn_SI-93-173_e_hq_6521], is pleased hereby to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1991-680 of April 11, 1991[^fn_SI-93-173_e_hq_6522] and to designate the Minister of Finance, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
