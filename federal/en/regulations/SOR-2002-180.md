Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 34(a) of the [Canadian Air Transport Security Authority Act](../acts/C-11.2.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby makes the annexed [CATSA Aerodrome Designation Regulations](SOR-2002-180.md).

## Designation

**1** The aerodromes set out in the schedule are designated for the purposes of the [Canadian Air Transport Security Authority Act](../acts/C-11.2.md).

## Coming into Force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
