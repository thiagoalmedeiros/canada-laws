His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](../acts/C-1.68.md)[^fn_81200-2-TEXTILE_hc], makes the annexed [Restraint Systems and Booster Seats for Motor Vehicles Regulations](SOR-2016-191.md).

## Requirement

### General

**1** Restraint systems and booster seats for motor vehicles described in column 1 of the table to this section must meet the requirements set out in the Parts of the [Motor Vehicle Restraint Systems and Booster Seats Safety Regulations](SOR-2010-90.md) that are mentioned in column 2.
