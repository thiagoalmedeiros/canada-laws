# CPTPP Remission Order

SOR/2019-293 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-08-08

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2019-08-08

P.C. 2019-1138 2019-08-07

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-293/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115[^fn_81000-2-39172222_hq_25181] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_81000-2-39172222_hq_25182], makes the annexed [CPTPP Remission Order](SOR-2019-293.md).

## Remission

**1** Remission is granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) in respect of goods classified under tariff item No. 4011.90.90 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) for which the CPTPT treatment is claimed and that were imported during the period commencing on December 30, 2018 and ending on the coming into force of this Order.

## Condition

**2** The remission is granted on the condition that the importer makes a claim for remission to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the date of importation.

## Coming into force

**3** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
