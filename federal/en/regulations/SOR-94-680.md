# Guitar Parts Remission Order, 1994

*Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Certain Guitar Parts*

SOR/94-680 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 1994-11-01

P.C. 1994-1836 1994-11-01

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-94-680/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_SOR-94-680_e_hq_6306], is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duties on certain guitar parts](SOR-94-680.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Guitar Parts Remission Order, 1994](SOR-94-680.md).

## Remission

**2** Subject to section 3, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) on certain parts of guitars.

## Conditions

**3** The remission is granted pursuant to section 2 on the condition that:

- **(a)** the guitar parts are imported into Canada during the period commencing on September 23, 1993 and ending on March 10, 1994;

- **(b)** the guitar parts are for use in the manufacture of guitars; and

- **(c)** a claim for remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue within two years of the date of importation of the goods for which remission is claimed.
