The Minister of Foreign Affairs issues the annexed [General Permit Allowing Specified Activities and Transactions that Are Prohibited Under the Special Economic Measures (Syria) Regulations](SOR-2025-61.md) under paragraph 2(b) of the [Special Economic Measures Permit Authorization Order](SOR-2025-50.md)[^fn_81000-2-5772_hq_1738693373] and subsection 4(5)[^fn_81000-2-5772_hq_1740071487] of the [Special Economic Measures Act](../acts/S-14.5.md)[^fn_81000-2-5772_hq_1740071346].

## General Permit

### Authorized activities and transactions

**1** Any person in Canada or Canadian outside Canada may, under this General Permit, carry out any of the following activities and transactions that are otherwise prohibited under the [Special Economic Measures (Syria) Regulations](SOR-2011-114.md) if the activities and transactions are aimed at the democratization and stabilization of Syria or the delivery of humanitarian assistance to or within Syria:

- **(a)** provide any financial or related service to or for the benefit of an entity listed in the schedule;

- **(b)** provide or acquire financial or other related services to, from or for the benefit of or on the direction or order of Syria or any person in Syria.

## Validity Period

### 360 days

**2** This General Permit ceases to have effect on the 360th day after the day on which it comes into force.

<sub>History: SOR/2025-164, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

### Registration

**3** This General Permit comes into force on the day on which it is registered.

## SCHEDULE — Entities

*(Paragraph 1(a))*

- **1** Commercial Bank of Syria

- **2** Real Estate Bank

- **3** Industrial Bank

- **4** Popular Credit Bank

- **5** Saving Bank

- **6** Agricultural Cooperative Bank

- **7** Central Bank of Syria
