# In-Transit Steel Goods Remission Order

SOR/2018-288 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-12-17

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2018-12-17

P.C. 2018-1610 2018-12-14

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-288/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115[^fn_a] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_b], makes the annexed In-Transit Steel Goods Remission Order.

## Remission

**1** Subject to subsection (2), remission is granted of the surtax paid or payable under the [Order Imposing a Surtax on the Importation of Certain Steel Goods](SOR-2025-148.md)[^fn_1] in respect of goods that were in transit to Canada before October 25, 2018.

## Conditions

**2** The remission is granted if

- **(a)** the good is imported into Canada on or after October 25, 2018; and

- **(b)** a claim for remission is made to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the date of importation.

## Coming into force

**3** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
