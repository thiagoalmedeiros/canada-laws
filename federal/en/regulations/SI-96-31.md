# Order Transferring to the Department of National Revenue the Control and Supervision of that Portion of the Public Service in the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions known as the Pension Advice Section

SI/96-31 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 1996-04-17

P.C. 1996-467 1996-03-29

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-96-31/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), is pleased hereby to transfer to the Department of National Revenue the control and supervision of that portion of the public service in the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions known as the Pension Advice Section, effective April 1, 1996.
