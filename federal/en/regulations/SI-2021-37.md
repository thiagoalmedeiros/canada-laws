# Order Designating the Minister of Justice as the Minister for the Purposes of that Act

SI/2021-37 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2021-06-24

Enabling authority: [UNITED NATIONS DECLARATION ON THE RIGHTS OF INDIGENOUS PEOPLES ACT](../acts/U-2.2.md)

Registration: 2021-07-07

P.C. 2021-635 2021-06-24

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2021-37/)

His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act](../acts/U-2.2.md)[^fn_320_hq_10667], designates the Minister of Justice, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
