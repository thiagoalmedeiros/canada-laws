# Order in Council Directing that the Annual Report of the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission and the Annual Report of the Canada Energy Regulator Board of Directors Be Discontinued

SI/2026-55 · Consolidated: 2026-09-30 · Last amended: 2026-09-25

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2026-10-07

P.C. 2026-867 2026-09-25

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2026-55/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas it appears to the Governor in Council that the annual report of the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission, required by section 72 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](../acts/N-28.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-6252_hq_1783025560] to be laid before both Houses of Parliament, and the annual report of the Canada Energy Regulator Board of Directors, required by subsection 18(1) of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](../acts/C-15.1.md)[^fn_81000-2-6252_hq_1783025605] to be laid before both Houses of Parliament, contain the same information as or less information than is contained in the Public Accounts or in any estimates of expenditures submitted to Parliament;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources, under section 157[^fn_81000-2-6252_hq_1783025859] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_81000-2-6252_hq_1783025892], directs that the annual report of the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission, required by section 72 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](../acts/N-28.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-6252_hq_1783025560] to be laid before both Houses of Parliament, and the annual report of the Canada Energy Regulator Board of Directors, required by subsection 18(1) of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](../acts/C-15.1.md)[^fn_81000-2-6252_hq_1783025605] to be laid before both Houses of Parliament, be discontinued.
