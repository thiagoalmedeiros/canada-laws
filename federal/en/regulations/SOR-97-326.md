# Regulations Exempting Goods of Chile from the Application of Anti-dumping Measures

SOR/97-326 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [SPECIAL IMPORT MEASURES ACT](../acts/S-15.md)

Registration: 1997-07-05

P.C. 1997-957 1997-07-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-97-326/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 14(2)[^fn_SOR-97-326_e_hq_5750] of the [Special Import Measures Act](../acts/S-15.md), hereby makes the annexed Regulations Exempting Goods of Chile from the Application of Anti-dumping Measures.

## Exemption

**1** Goods of Chile that qualify as originating goods under the [CCFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](SOR-97-340.md) and that are entitled to the benefit of the Chile Tariff under the [CCFTA Tariff Preference Regulations](SOR-97-322.md) are exempt from the application of anti-dumping measures under the [Special Import Measures Act](../acts/S-15.md) as of the earlier of

- **(a)** the date on which the rate for all goods covered by the subheading that includes the tariff item under which the goods are classified is free under the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md) for goods of Chile that are originating goods and under the Arancel Aduanero for goods of Canada that are originating goods; and

- **(b)** January 1, 2003.

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_SOR-97-326_e_hq_5751] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 89 of the [Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act](../acts/C-1.6.md), chapter 14 of the Statutes of Canada, 1997, comes into force.
