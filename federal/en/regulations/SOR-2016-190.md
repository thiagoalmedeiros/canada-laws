# Residential Detectors Regulations

SOR/2016-190 · Consolidated: 2026-06-14 · Last amended: 2026-06-04

Enabling authority: [CANADA CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY ACT](../acts/C-1.68.md)

Registration: 2016-06-22

P.C. 2016-617 2016-06-21

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-190/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](../acts/C-1.68.md)[^fn_SOR-2016-190_e_hq_18685], makes the annexed [Residential Detectors Regulations](SOR-2016-190.md).

## Requirements

### Residential detectors

**1** A residential detector set out in column 1 of the table to this section must comply with the standard set out in column 2, without regard to any other standard to which it refers.

### Official languages

**3** The information required under these Regulations to be provided on the label of a residential detector or in instructions intended for consumers must appear in English and in French.
