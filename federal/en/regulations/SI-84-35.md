# Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/84-35 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1984-02-22

P.C. 1984-324 1984-02-02

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs to prescribe by order the fees to be paid for Contractor and Homeowners Registration Courses under the Urea Formaldehyde Insulation Act[^footnote1star_e].
