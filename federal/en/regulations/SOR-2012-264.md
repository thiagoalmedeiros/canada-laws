Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 9.01(1)[^fn_a] of the [Currency Act](../acts/C-52.md)[^fn_b], makes the annexed [Regulations for the Redemption of One Cent Coins](SOR-2012-264.md).

## Authorization

**1** The Minister is authorized to redeem one cent coins that are or that have at any time been current in Canada.

## Coming into force

**2** These Regulations come into force on the day of their registration.
