# Listed Airport Regulations

SOR/2004-293 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [AIR TRAVELLERS SECURITY CHARGE ACT](../acts/A-10.5.md)

Registration: 2004-12-06

P.C. 2004-1489 2004-12-06

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-293/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 2 and 84 of the [Air Travellers Security Charge Act](../acts/A-10.5.md)[^footnotea_e], hereby makes the annexed [Listed Airport Regulations](SOR-2004-293.md).

## Interpretation

**1** In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the [Air Travellers Security Charge Act](../acts/A-10.5.md).

## Prescribed Airports

**2** The following airports are prescribed airports for the purposes of the definition ***listed airport*** in section 2 of the Act:

- **(a)** Red Deer Regional; and

- **(b)** Rivière-Rouge (Mont-Tremblant International).

## Coming into Force

**3** These Regulations come into force on December 23, 2004.
