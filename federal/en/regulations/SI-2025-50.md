# Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons as the Minister for the Purposes of the Parliamentary Employment and Staff Relations Act

SI/2025-50 · Consolidated: 2025-10-14 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: OTHER THAN STATUTORY AUTHORITY

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-431 2025-03-14

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-50/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister,

**(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-88 of February 12, 2025[^fn_81000-3-2826_hq_1738700942]; and

**(b)** designates the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons as the Minister for the purposes of the [Parliamentary Employment and Staff Relations Act](../acts/P-1.3.md)[^fn_81000-3-2826_hq_1738700976].
