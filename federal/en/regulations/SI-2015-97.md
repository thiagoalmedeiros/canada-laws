# Transfer of Duties Order

SI/2015-97 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2015-11-04

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2015-11-18

P.C. 2015-1238 2015-11-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2015-97/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[^fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16321] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16322], transfers from the Minister of Transport to the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs the powers, duties and functions under the [Bridge To Strengthen Trade Act](../acts/B-8.05.md)[^fn_SI-2015-97_e_hq_16323], effective November 4, 2015.
