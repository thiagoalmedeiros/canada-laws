# Order Designating the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

SI/2001-60 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2001-05-09

P.C. 2001-742 2001-04-26

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2001-60/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, hereby

**(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b)[^footnotea_e] of the definition ***department*** in section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), designates the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

**(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b)[^footnoteb_e] of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of that Act, designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada.

[^footnotea_e]: S.C. 1992, c. 1, s. 69(3)

[^footnoteb_e]: S.C. 1992, c. 1, s. 69(1)
