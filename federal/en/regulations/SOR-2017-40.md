# Order Designating New Brunswick for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code

SOR/2017-40 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2018-01-01

Enabling authority: [CRIMINAL CODE](../acts/C-46.md)

Registration: 2017-03-24

P.C. 2017-251 2017-03-24

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-40/)

Whereas New Brunswick has legislative measures that protect recipients of payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost of borrowing under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of New Brunswick has requested that the Governor in Council designate that province for the purposes of section 347.1[^fn_610628-e_hq_3365] of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3366];

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3365] of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md)[^fn_610628-e_hq_3366], makes the annexed [Order Designating New Brunswick for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md)](SOR-2017-40.md).

## Province Designated

**1** New Brunswick is designated for the purposes of section 347.1 of the [Criminal Code](../acts/C-46.md).

## Coming into Force

**2**[^fn_Ind7E94_hq_19927] This Order comes into force at 12:00 a.m. Atlantic time on the first day on which the following are both in force:

- **(a)** An Act Respecting Payday Loans, S.N.B. 2008, c. 3; and

- **(b)** Payday Lending Regulation – Cost of Credit Disclosure and Payday Loans Act.
