The Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(g)[^Footnote1_e] and subsection 18(1)[^Footnote2_e] of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](../acts/C-3.md), hereby makes the annexed Deposit Insurance Application Fee By-law.

June 29, 2000

## Prescribed Fee

**1** The prescribed fee that is to accompany an application for deposit insurance made by a provincial institution under subsection 17(1.1) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](../acts/C-3.md) is $20,000 and is non-refundable.

<sub>History: SOR/2006-235, s. 1</sub>

## Coming into Force

**2** This By-law comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
