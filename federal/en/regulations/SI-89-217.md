# Minister of National Revenue Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/89-217 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1989-10-25

P.C. 1989-1949 1989-09-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-89-217/)

Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the cost of providing a service in respect of a request for an advance income tax ruling should be borne by the person to whom it is provided.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1983-653 of March 3, 1983[^fn_SI_89-217_EN_hq_20107]; and

**(b)** to authorize the Minister of National Revenue to prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by a person to whom a service is provided in respect of a request for an advance income tax ruling.
