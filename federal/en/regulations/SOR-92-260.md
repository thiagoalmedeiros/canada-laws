# Name Use (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations

*Regulations Permitting the Use of the Name of an Association in a Document in Connection with a Transaction Related to Securities*

SOR/92-260 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](../acts/C-41.01.md)

Registration: 1992-05-07

P.C. 1992-909 1992-05-07

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-92-260/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 463 and subsection 465(4) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](../acts/C-41.01.md)[^fn_1e], is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations permitting the use of the name of an association in a document in connection with a transaction related to securities](SOR-92-260.md), effective June 1, 1992.

## Short Title

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the Name Use (Cooperative Credit Association) Regulations.

## Permitted Use of Name

**2** A person may use the name of an association in a prospectus, an offering memorandum, a takeover bid circular, an advertisement for a transaction related to securities or in any other document in connection with a transaction related to securities where the use is required by law or the association has given its express written permission for the use.
