# Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Act

SI/93-229 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CANADIAN SPACE AGENCY ACT](../acts/C-23.2.md)

Registration: 1993-12-15

P.C. 1993-1981 1993-12-02

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in section 2 of the [Canadian Space Agency Act](../acts/C-23.2.md)[^fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6542], is pleased hereby

**(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1672 of August 18, 1993[^fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6543];

**(b)** to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
