# Minister Responsible for the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

SI/81-81 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 1981-06-10

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-81-81/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister responsible for the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister responsible for the said Corporation to prescribe by order the fees to be charged for services provided by Her Majesty in respect of the issuance by the Corporation of certificates of multiple-unit residential building construction starts.
