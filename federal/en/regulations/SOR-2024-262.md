# Regulations Amending Certain Regulations Made Under the Canada Post Corporation Act

SOR/2024-262 · Consolidated: 2024-12-15 · Last amended: 2024-12-09

Enabling authority: [CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](../acts/C-10.md)

Registration: 2024-12-09

P.C. 2024-1278 2024-11-29

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-262/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas, under subsection 20(1) of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](../acts/C-10.md)[^fn_81000-2-5589_hq_1729793120], a copy of the proposed [Regulations Amending Certain Regulations Made Under the Canada Post Corporation Act](SOR-2024-262.md), in the annexed form, was published in the Canada Gazette, Part I, on September 7, 2024 and a reasonable opportunity was afforded to interested persons to make representations to the Minister of Public Works and Government Services with respect to the proposed Regulations;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, under subsection 19(1)[^fn_81000-2-5589_hq_1729793188] of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](../acts/C-10.md)[^fn_81000-2-5589_hq_1729793120], approves the annexed [Regulations Amending Certain Regulations Made Under the Canada Post Corporation Act](SOR-2024-262.md) made on October 23, 2024 by the Canada Post Corporation.
