# Critical Habitat of the Plains Minnow (Hybognathus placitus) Order

SOR/2026-56 · Consolidated: 2026-03-31 · Last amended: 2026-03-23

Enabling authority: [SPECIES AT RISK ACT](../acts/S-15.3.md)

Registration: 2026-03-23

Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas the Plains Minnow (Hybognathus placitus) is a wildlife species that is listed as a threatened species in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-6029_hq_1764099066];

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2)[^fn_81000-2-6029_hq_1764099090] of that Act;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Plains Minnow (Hybognathus placitus) Order](SOR-2026-56.md) under subsection 58(4) and paragraph (5)(a) of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md)[^fn_81000-2-6029_hq_1764099066].

Ottawa, March 19, 2026

## Application

**1** Subsection 58(1) of the [Species at Risk Act](../acts/S-15.3.md) applies to the critical habitat of the Plains Minnow (Hybognathus placitus), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## Coming into force

**2** This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
