# CCFTA Rules of Origin for Casual Goods Regulations

SOR/97-323 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 1997-07-05

P.C. 1997-954 1997-07-04

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-97-323/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 13(2)[^fn_SOR-97-323_e_hq_5747] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_SOR-97-323_e_hq_5748], hereby makes the annexed [CCFTA Rules of Origin for Casual Goods Regulations](SOR-97-323.md).

## Interpretation

**1** In these Regulations, ***casual goods*** means goods other than goods imported for sale or for an industrial, occupational, commercial or institutional or other like use.

## Casual Goods

**2** Casual goods that are acquired in Chile are deemed to originate in Chile and are entitled to the benefit of the Chile Tariff if

- **(a)** the marking of the goods is in accordance with the marking laws of Chile and indicates that the goods are the product of Chile or Canada; or

- **(b)** the goods do not bear a mark and there is no evidence to indicate that the goods are not the product of Chile or Canada.

## Coming into Force

**3**[^fn_SOR-97-323_e_hq_5749] These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 9 of the [Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act](../acts/C-1.6.md), chapter 14 of the Statutes of Canada, 1997, comes into force.
