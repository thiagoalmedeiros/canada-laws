# Access to Information Act Extension Order, No. 1

*Order Extending the Right To Be Given Access under Subsection 4(1) of the Access to Information Act to Records under the Control of a Government Institution*

Enabling authority: [ACCESS TO INFORMATION ACT](../acts/A-1.md)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 4(2) of the [Access to Information Act](../acts/A-1.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order extending the right to be given access under subsection 4(1) of the Access to Information Act to records under the control of a government institution](SOR-89-207.md).

## Short Title

**1** This Order may be cited as the [Access to Information Act Extension Order, No. 1](SOR-89-207.md).

## Extension

**2** The right to be given access under subsection 4(1) of the [Access to Information Act](../acts/A-1.md) to records under the control of a government institution is hereby extended to include all individuals who are present in Canada but who are not Canadian citizens or permanent residents within the meaning of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](../acts/I-2.5.md) and all corporations that are present in Canada.
