# Dominion Succession Duty (1957) Regulations

*Regulations Respecting Section 12 of the Dominion Succession Duty Act*

SOR/57-216 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [DOMINION SUCCESSION DUTY ACT](../acts/Z-0.98.md)

Registration: 1957-06-13

P.C. 1957-814 1957-06-13

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-57-216/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 12 of the [Dominion Succession Duty Act](../acts/Z-0.98.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations Respecting section 12 of the Dominion Succession Duty Act.

**1** These Regulations may be cited as the [Dominion Succession Duty (1957) Regulations](SOR-57-216.md).

**2** The Province of Ontario and the Province of Quebec are hereby prescribed for the purposes of section 12 of the [Dominion Succession Duty Act](../acts/Z-0.98.md).
