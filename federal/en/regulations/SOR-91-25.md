# Artists’ Representatives (GST/HST) Regulations

SOR/91-25 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2019-03-04

Enabling authority: [EXCISE TAX ACT](../acts/E-15.md)

Registration: 1990-12-18

P.C. 1990-2734 1990-12-18

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-91-25/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[^footnote1_e] of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing registrants who make supplies of intangible personal property on behalf of artists.

**1** \[Repealed, SOR/2014-248, s. 2\]

## Prescribed Registrants

**2** Any registrant listed in the schedule is prescribed for the purposes of subsection 177(2) of the [Excise Tax Act](../acts/E-15.md).
