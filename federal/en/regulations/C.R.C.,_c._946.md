# Proclamation Fixing Valuation Days

C.R.C., c. 946 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [INCOME TAX ACT](../acts/I-3.3.md)

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._946/)

Know You that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for Canada, do by this Our Proclamation

**(a)** fix December 22, 1971 as the day in relation to any property referred to in paragraph 24(a) of the Income Tax Application Rules, 1971 for the purposes of subdivision c of Division B of Part I of the [Income Tax Act](../acts/I-3.3.md); and

**(b)** fix December 31, 1971 as the day in relation to any property referred to in paragraph 24(b) of those Rules for the purposes of that subdivision.
