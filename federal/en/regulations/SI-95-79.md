# Order Designating the Appropriate Authority for a Province with Respect to the Act

Enabling authority: [CANADA STUDENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE ACT](../acts/S-22.7.md)

Registration: 1995-08-09

Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-95-79/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). The Minister of Employment and Immigration, pursuant to subsection 3(1) of the [Canada Student Financial Assistance Act](../acts/S-22.7.md)[^fn_SI-95-79_e_hq_5157], hereby designates, for the purposes of that Act, the appropriate authority for a province as set out in the schedule hereto.

Ottawa, July 17, 1995

LLOYD AXWORTHY

*Minister of Employment and Immigration*
