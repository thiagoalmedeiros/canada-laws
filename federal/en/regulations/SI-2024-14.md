# Adjustment of Certain Fees in Respect of Publication in the Canada Gazette for the 2019–2020 and 2020–2021 Fiscal Years Remission Order

SI/2024-14 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-03-01

Enabling authority: [FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](../acts/F-11.md)

Registration: 2024-03-13

P.C. 2024-196 2024-03-01

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2024-14/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the collection of the amounts is unreasonable, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, under subsection 23(2.1)[^fn_15952_hq_1702910130] of the [Financial Administration Act](../acts/F-11.md)[^fn_15952_hq_1702910221], remits the following amounts representing annual adjustments — that should have been made under subsection 17(1) of the [Service Fees Act](../acts/S-8.4.md)[^fn_15952_hq_1702912375] — to the Canada Gazette insertion fees charged under section 17 of the [Department of Public Works and Government Services Act](../acts/P-38.2.md)[^fn_81000-2-5354-EN_V2_hq_1703194252]:

**(a)** $3,181.38 for the 2019–2020 fiscal year; and

**(b)** $4,488.69 for the 2020–2021 fiscal year.
