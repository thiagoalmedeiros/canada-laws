# Area Control List

SOR/81-543 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2017-06-20

Enabling authority: [EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](../acts/E-19.md)

Registration: 1981-07-03

P.C. 1981-1793 1981-07-02

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce, pursuant to sections 4 and 6 of the [Export and Import Permits Act](../acts/E-19.md), is pleased hereby to revoke the [Area Control List](SOR-81-543.md), C.R.C., c. 600 and to establish the annexed [Area Control List](SOR-81-543.md) in substitution therefor.

## Area Control List
