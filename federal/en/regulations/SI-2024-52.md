# Order Transferring from the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans to the Minister of Foreign Affairs the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Great Lakes Fishery Commission

SI/2024-52 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2024-10-18

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2024-11-06

P.C. 2024-1114 2024-10-18

> **Unofficial version.** © His Majesty the King in Right of Canada. Reproduced from the [official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2024-52/) under the [Reproduction of Federal Law Order](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-97-5/). Only the version on the Justice Laws Website is official.

Whereas, under section 33 of the Government Organization Act, 1979[^fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725994925], the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is responsible for the administration of the [Great Lakes Fisheries Convention Act](../acts/F-17.md)[^fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725995010];

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647930] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647952], transfers from the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans to the Minister of Foreign Affairs the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans in relation to the Great Lakes Fishery Commission.
