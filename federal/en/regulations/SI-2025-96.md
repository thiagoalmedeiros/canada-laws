# Order Transferring from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the Department of National Defence the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Coast Guard and the Canadian Coast Guard Support Services Group

SI/2025-96 · Consolidated: 2025-09-15 · Last amended: 2025-09-02

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 2025-09-24

P.C. 2025-639 2025-09-02

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-96/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the Department of National Defence the control and supervision of those portions of the federal public administration in the Department of Fisheries and Oceans known as the Canadian Coast Guard and the Canadian Coast Guard Support Services Group.
