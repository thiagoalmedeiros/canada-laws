# Order Declaring May 1, 1989 as the Day on Which the Third Supplement to the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 Comes into Force

SI/89-123 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [REVISED STATUTES OF CANADA, 1985 ACT](../acts/R-7.7.md)

Registration: 1989-05-10

P.C. 1989-735 1989-04-28

Whereas the Statute Revision Commission has reported in writing the completion of the 3rd Supplement to the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985;

And Whereas the Governor General has caused a printed copy of that Supplement, attested under the signature of the Governor General, countersigned by the Minister of Justice and certified correct by the Chairman of the Statute Revision Commission, to be deposited in the office of the Clerk of the Parliaments on April 4, 1989.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 13(2) of the [Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 Act](../acts/R-7.7.md)[^fn_1e], is pleased hereby to declare May 1, 1989 as the day on and from which the 3rd Supplement to the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 shall come into force and have operation and effect as law as though the enactments contained therein were expressly embodied in and enacted by an Act of Parliament.
