# Order Transferring from the Minister of Foreign Affairs to the Minister for International Trade the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Subsection 53(2) of the Customs Tariff

SI/2025-39 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2025-03-14

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)[CUSTOMS TARIFF](../acts/C-54.011.md)

Registration: 2025-03-26

P.C. 2025-419 2025-03-14

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-2025-39/)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[^fn_612645E_hq_7867] of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md)[^fn_612645E_hq_7868], transfers from the Minister of Foreign Affairs to the Minister for International Trade the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Foreign Affairs under subsection 53(2)[^fn_81000-3-2839_hq_1741793772] of the [Customs Tariff](../acts/C-54.011.md)[^fn_81000-3-2839_hq_1741720736].
