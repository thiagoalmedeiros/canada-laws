# Order Transferring from the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food to the Minister of National Health and Welfare, the Powers, Duties and Functions under Certain Acts

SI/95-44 · Consolidated: 2025-07-24 · Last amended: 2006-03-22

Enabling authority: [PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES ACT](../acts/P-34.md)

Registration: 1995-04-19

P.C. 1995-525 1995-03-28

[Official source](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SI-95-44/)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](../acts/P-34.md), is pleased hereby to transfer to the Minister of National Health and Welfare the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food under the [Pest Control Products Act](../acts/P-9.01.md) and the [Pesticide Residue Compensation Act](../acts/P-10.md), effective April 1, 1995.
